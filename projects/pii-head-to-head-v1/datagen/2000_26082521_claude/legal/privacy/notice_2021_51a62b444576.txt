MERIDIAN VALLEY MUTUAL INSURANCE COMPANY
RECORDS AND PERSONAL INFORMATION HANDLING STANDARD
Standard No. RIM-2021-03
Effective: 1 March 2021. Supersedes Standard RIM-2018-01.
Issued by: Office of the Records Custodian, in consultation with the Compliance Committee

I. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This Standard governs the personal information held by the Company in the course of underwriting, servicing, and adjusting policies of insurance issued within the Company's licensed territory. It applies to all records in the Company's custody or control, without regard to medium, and includes paper files held at the branch offices, electronic records maintained in the policy administration system and the claims system, records held by third-party administrators under contract, and working copies created by staff in the ordinary course of business. Departmental procedures may be more restrictive than this Standard but may not be less restrictive.

II. CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

The Company maintains eight categories of personal information. The first category, Identity and Contact Information, comprises the fields Full Name, Former Name, Date of Birth, Residential Address, Mailing Address, Telephone Number, and Electronic Mail Address. The second, Government-Issued Identifiers, comprises Social Security Number, Taxpayer Identification Number, Driver Licence Number, and Passport Number. The third, Policy and Coverage Information, comprises Policy Number, Certificate Number, Named Insured, Additional Insured, Coverage Effective Date, Coverage Termination Date, Premium Amount, and Deductible Amount. The fourth, Claims Information, comprises Claim Number, Date of Loss, Description of Loss, Adjuster Assignment, Reserve Amount, Settlement Amount, and Subrogation Status. The fifth, Health and Medical Information, comprises Diagnosis Code, Procedure Code, Treating Provider Name, Treatment Date, Prescription Record, and Independent Medical Examination Report, and is held only in respect of lines of business for which such information is material to the risk or to the adjustment of a claim. The sixth, Financial Information, comprises Bank Routing Number, Bank Account Number, Payment Card Number, Payment Card Expiration Date, and Billing History. The seventh, Underwriting and Risk Information, comprises Credit-Based Insurance Score, Motor Vehicle Record, Loss History Report, Property Inspection Report, and Occupation. The eighth, System and Access Information, comprises User Identifier, Internet Protocol Address, Device Identifier, Authentication Log, and Session Log.

III. PERMITTED ACCESS

Access is granted by role and is limited to the categories necessary to the performance of the role. Underwriting Analysts and Underwriting Managers may access Categories One, Two, Three, Six, and Seven. Claims Adjusters and Claims Supervisors may access Categories One, Two, Three, Four, and, where the line of business requires it, Category Five. Members of the Special Investigations Unit may access all categories on the written authorisation of the Chief Claims Officer, such authorisation to be recorded in the case file. Billing Services Representatives may access Categories One, Three, and Six. The Information Security Officer and designated system administrators may access Category Eight. The Records Custodian may access any category for the limited purposes of inventory, retention scheduling, and disposal. Contracted third-party administrators may access only those categories enumerated in their governing agreement and its data-handling appendix.

IV. RETENTION

Policy administration records are retained for seven years following the termination or non-renewal of the policy. Claim files are retained for ten years following final disposition, and claim files involving a minor claimant are retained for ten years following the claimant's attainment of majority. Records within Category Five are retained in accordance with the longer of this Standard or the applicable state insurance and health information statute. Underwriting declination and rating records are retained for five years from the date of the decision. Recorded telephone calls are retained for twenty-four months. Authentication and session logs are retained for eighteen months. Litigation holds issued by the General Counsel suspend all retention periods for the records identified in the hold until the hold is released in writing.

V. DISPOSAL

Upon expiry of the retention period and in the absence of a hold, the Records Custodian authorises disposal. Paper records are destroyed by cross-cut shredding on Company premises or by a bonded destruction vendor operating under a written agreement that requires a certificate of destruction. Electronic records are destroyed by secure deletion, and media leaving Company control are subject to cryptographic erasure or physical destruction. Each disposal event is entered in the Destruction Register, which records the columns Record Series, Retention Authority, Volume or Item Count, Custodian of Record, Method of Destruction, Date of Destruction, Vendor Reference, and Witnessing Officer. The Destruction Register is itself retained permanently.
