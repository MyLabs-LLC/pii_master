NOTES

1. The table above is the controlling schedule for this office. Where a category

2. Access is by role, not by individual. Role assignment is maintained by the
   Records Custodian and reviewed each September.

3. On expiry of the retention period the Records Custodian schedules disposal
   within the following quarter. Paper is cross-cut shredded on site. Magnetic
   and optical media are destroyed by an approved vendor under purchase order,
   with the certificate of destruction returned to this office. Records under
   litigation hold, audit hold, or open grievance are excluded from disposal
   until the hold is lifted in writing by the Office of General Counsel.
