who may see it, how long it is kept, and how it is destroyed. It takes effect
on the first day of the spring term. Unit supervisors shall confirm in writing
that their staff have read it.

PART II. ACCESS AUTHORITY

  R1 Registrar and Associate Registrar   R6 International Student Adviser
  R2 Records Custodian                   R7 Institutional Research, extract
  R3 Transcript Unit staff                  de-identified, aggregate only
  R4 Academic Adviser of record          R8 Disability Services Liaison
  R5 Residency Officer                   R9 Certifying Official, benefits

  Access is granted by role and is limited to the categories listed against
  that role in Part I. Requests from any other University unit, from an
  external agency, or from a third party shall be routed to the Registrar and
  logged in the disclosure register before any category is released.

PART III. DISPOSAL METHODS

  M1  Logical deletion followed by overwrite of the storage allocation and
      removal from all mirrored and archival copies at the quarterly cycle.
  M2  Cross-cut shred on site by unit personnel, in the shredding room, on a
      scheduled disposal day, under the eye of the Records Custodian.
  M3  Destruction by an approved vendor under purchase order, or on site with
      two witnesses; certificate of destruction filed and held seven years.

PART IV. PROCEDURE

  1. The Records Custodian produces an eligibility list each quarter from the
     schedule in Part I.
  2. The list is checked against the hold register. Any category subject to
     litigation, audit, grievance or open records request is struck from the
     list and carried forward.
  3. The Registrar authorises the remaining list by signature.
  4. Destruction is performed by the stated method within thirty days of
     authorisation.
  5. The disposition register is completed with the following fields: category
     reference, volume, method, date of destruction, authorising role,
     witnessing role, certificate reference.
  6. The register is reconciled annually against the schedule and reported to
     the Office of General Counsel.

Departure from this memorandum requires the prior written approval of the
Registrar. Unauthorised copying of any category to removable media, personal
equipment, or a non-University service is a disciplinary matter.
