PART II — ACCESS TIER DEFINITIONS
Tier 1  Public and directory material. No restriction.
Tier 2  Branch and member-facing operations. Member Services Representatives, Head Tellers, Branch Managers.
Tier 3  Underwriting and compliance. Lending Officers, Collections staff, Compliance Analysts.
Tier 4  Restricted technical. Information Security Administrator and designated systems staff only.
Tier 5  Personnel confidential. Human Resources staff and the Chief Executive Officer only.

Internal Audit holds standing read-only access to all tiers. Access to a tier does not confer authority to export, print, or transmit records outside the Credit Union.

PART III — NOTES ON THE SCHEDULE
1. Retention runs from the trigger event stated in the table, not from the date the record was created.
2. Where two periods appear in the retention column, the shorter applies to transient log material and the longer applies to audit trail entries.
3. Categories held in more than one system are governed by the longest applicable period.
4. Requests to vary a retention period require written approval from the Chief Compliance Officer and are recorded in the exception register.
5. Records under legal hold are flagged in the system of record and suppressed from the quarterly eligibility list until the hold is released.
