RECORDS HANDLING STANDARD RH-11: CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION, PERMITTED ACCESS, RETENTION AND SECURE DISPOSAL

Approved by the Information Governance Committee on 12 September 2016. Supersedes RH-11 v1.4 in its entirety. Owner: the Records Custodian. Applicable to all operating divisions, sub-contracted haulage partners and agency labour suppliers engaged by the Company.

1. SCOPE AND CONSTRUCTION

- This Standard is to be construed in accordance with the Data Protection Act 1998 and, from such date as it shall come into force, Regulation (EU) 2016/679, and nothing herein shall be taken to authorise any processing not otherwise lawful.
- Where any provision of this Standard conflicts with a statutory retention obligation, the statutory obligation shall prevail and the Records Custodian shall be notified in writing forthwith.

2. DECLARED CATEGORIES

- Category A — Recruitment and Pre-Engagement Vetting Files, comprising the field labels: Applicant Reference; Surname; Forename(s); Date of Birth; Correspondence Address; Telephone Number; Email Address; Previous Employer; Reference Outcome; Right to Work Document Type; Document Expiry Date.
- Category B — Contractual and Personnel Records, comprising: Employee Number; Job Title; Depot Code; Start Date; Termination Date; Notice Period.
- Category C — Remuneration and Deduction Records, comprising: National Insurance Number; Tax Code; Bank Sort Code; Bank Account Number; Gross Pay; Net Pay; Pension Scheme Reference.
- Category D — Driver Compliance Records, comprising: Driving Licence Number; Licence Categories Held; Endorsement Code; Driver CPC Card Number; Tachograph Card Number; Infringement Code.
- Category E — Health, Absence and Welfare Records, comprising: Medical Questionnaire Reference; Examining Practitioner; Fitness Determination; Absence Reason Code; Return to Work Interview Date.
- Category F — Security and Access Control Records, comprising: Access Card Number; Biometric Template Reference; Turnstile Event Timestamp; Site Code.
- Category G — Third Party Contact Particulars, comprising: Contact Name; Trading Name; Delivery Contact Telephone Number; Delivery Instruction Free Text.

3. PERMITTED ACCESS

- Categories A, B and E shall be accessible only to the Head of Human Resources and to such HR Advisers as the Head of Human Resources shall designate in writing.
- Category C shall be accessible only to the Payroll Supervisor and, for audit purposes only, to the Finance Director.
- Category D shall be accessible to the Transport Manager (CPC holder), the Fleet Compliance Manager and the Records Custodian.
- Category F shall be accessible to the Site Security Supervisor, provided that biometric template references shall not be exported from the access control system under any circumstances.
- Category G shall be accessible to Traffic Planners and Customer Service Advisers on a need-to-know basis only.

4. RETENTION PERIODS

- Category A: twelve months from notification of outcome, where the applicant was unsuccessful.
- Categories B and C: six years from the end of the tax year in which engagement ceased.
- Category D: four years from expiry of the relevant entitlement; tachograph records twelve months from date of recording.
- Category E: forty years from the date of the last relevant examination.
- Category F: thirty-one days for event logs; termination of engagement for enrolment records.
- Category G: twenty-four months from completion of the relevant consignment.

5. DISPOSAL PROCEDURE

- Paper records shall be placed in sealed confidential waste consoles and destroyed to DIN 66399 level P-4 or higher.
- Magnetic media shall be degaussed and thereafter physically deformed; solid state media shall be granulated.
- Every disposal event shall be entered in the Disposal Register against the fields: Disposal Reference; Category; Volume; Method; Authorising Officer; Witnessing Officer; Date of Destruction; Certificate Number.
