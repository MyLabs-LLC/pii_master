SUNRIDGE VALLEY UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

STANDARD AS-114.2
DISPOSAL AUTHORIZATION AND CERTIFICATION PROCEDURE

Issued: 1 August 2021
Authority: Administrative Standard AS-114, clause 5
Responsible Office: Records Management, Office of the Superintendent

────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1.  APPLICABILITY

1.1  This procedure governs every disposal of District records containing personal
     information, in any medium, under any of Categories A through J as defined
     in AS-114 clause 2.

1.2  No record within scope may be destroyed except under an authorization issued
     pursuant to this procedure.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────

2.  DISPOSAL ELIGIBILITY REPORT

2.1  The Records Custodian generates the eligibility report semi-annually, in the
     first two weeks of January and of July.

2.2  The report is organized by category and by originating department, and lists
     for each eligible group:

         •  Category designation
         •  Originating department
         •  System of record
         •  Date range covered
         •  Record count or linear volume
         •  Retention rule applied
         •  Eligibility date

2.3  The report reproduces no recorded values from any field within the records
     identified. Identification is by category, date range and system only.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3.  DEPARTMENTAL REVIEW AND HOLD CERTIFICATION

3.1  Each originating department has twenty calendar days from receipt to review
     its section of the report.

3.2  The departmental custodian shall confirm in writing:

         (a)  that no litigation hold applies;
         (b)  that no audit hold applies;
         (c)  that no public records request is pending against the group;
         (d)  that no records within the group have been reclassified into a
              longer-retention category since the last report.

3.3  Where any condition in 3.2 cannot be confirmed, the affected group is
     withheld from the authorization and carried forward to the next report cycle
     with a written note of the reason.

3.4  Silence is not certification. A department that does not respond within
     twenty days has its entire section withheld.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.  AUTHORIZATION

4.1  The Records Custodian issues a written disposal authorization identifying the
     approved groups by category and date range.

4.2  Authorizations for Categories C, D, G, H and I require countersignature by
     the responsible director of the originating function.

4.3  An authorization is valid for ninety days from issue. Unexecuted
     authorizations lapse and must be reissued.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.  EXECUTION

5.1  Paper — cross-cut shred, maximum particle 4mm x 40mm. On-site shredding is
     witnessed by a District employee. Off-site shredding requires a bonded
     vendor under an executed confidentiality agreement, with sealed transport
     containers and a certificate of destruction returned within ten business
     days.

5.2  Production systems — purge executed through the application's records
     function by the system administrator. Ad hoc database deletion is
     prohibited.

5.3  Backups — associated backup sets are purged at the next scheduled rotation
     following production purge. The rotation date is recorded on the certificate.

5.4  Removable media, decommissioned drives, retired workstations — cryptographic
     erasure where the device supports it; otherwise physical destruction by
     shredding or degaussing. Media leaving District custody intact is prohibited.

5.5  Surveillance media — automated overwrite at the retention interval. Manual
     export for investigation requires a preservation notice, which suspends the
     overwrite for the exported segment only.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.  CERTIFICATE OF DISPOSAL

6.1  A certificate is completed for each execution event and records:

         Certificate number
         Authorization reference
         Category designation
         Originating department
         Date range covered
         Record count or linear volume
         Medium
         Method of destruction
         Date of execution
         Executing role title
         Witnessing role title
         Vendor certificate reference (if applicable)
         Backup rotation date (if applicable)

6.2  Certificates are signed by role title. Certificates are retained
     permanently and are not themselves subject to any disposal schedule.

6.3  The Records Custodian reconciles executed certificates against issued
     authorizations at the close of each cycle and reports unexecuted or
     partially executed authorizations to the Superintendent.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7.  EXCEPTIONS

7.1  Emergency destruction — where a record poses an immediate health or safety
     hazard, the site administrator may authorize destruction and shall file a
     retrospective certificate within two business days.

7.2  No other exception to this procedure exists.
