KESTREL DRUG STORES, INC.
OFFICE OF THE CHIEF PRIVACY OFFICER
Standard RIM-04 — Categories of Personal Information: Custody, Access, Retention and Disposal
Effective 1 April 2016. Supersedes RIM-04 rev. 3 (2012). Review cycle: 24 months.

1. Purpose and Scope. This Standard establishes the authoritative inventory of the categories of personal information created, received or maintained by the Company in the course of retail pharmacy, front-store, clinical services and employment operations, and prescribes for each category the permitted user population, the minimum and maximum retention periods, and the required method of destruction. It applies to all records irrespective of medium, including the dispensing system, the point-of-sale environment, the central data warehouse, paper files held at store level, and any information transferred to or held by a business associate or service provider under contract. It applies to every colleague, contractor, licensed intern and temporary worker who acts on the Company's behalf.

2. Categories of Personal Information. The Company recognises nine categories. Category A, Patient Identity Information, comprises the fields Patient Name, Date of Birth, Sex, Home Address, Mailing Address, Telephone Number, Electronic Mail Address, Patient Identifier and Preferred Language. Category B, Clinical and Dispensing Information, comprises Prescription Number, Fill Date, Drug Product Identifier, Directions for Use, Quantity Dispensed, Days Supply, Refills Remaining, Prescriber Name, Prescriber Identifier, Allergy Notation, Diagnosis Code and Counselling Record. Category C, Third-Party Payer Information, comprises Payer Name, Member Identifier, Group Number, Person Code, Plan Effective Date and Adjudication Response Code. Category D, Payment Information, comprises Cardholder Name, Primary Account Number, Expiration Date, Authorisation Code and Transaction Identifier. Category E, Loyalty and Marketing Information, comprises Loyalty Account Number, Enrolment Date, Communication Preference and Purchase History. Category F, Colleague Information, comprises Colleague Identifier, Social Security Number, Compensation Data, Licence and Certification Data, Background Screening Result and Benefit Election. Category G, Controlled Substance Register Information, comprises the fields prescribed by the applicable state board and the federal register requirements. Category H, Clinical Services Information, comprises Immunisation Record, Screening Result, Consent Record and Medication Therapy Management Note. Category I, Physical and Electronic Security Information, comprises Video Surveillance Recording, Badge Access Log and System Audit Log. No category may be extended, merged or created except by written amendment to this Standard approved by the Privacy Official.

3. Authorised Access. Access is granted on the principle of least privilege and is provisioned by role, not by individual request. Categories A, B, G and H are accessible to the Pharmacist-in-Charge, staff pharmacists, certified pharmacy technicians and pharmacy interns assigned to the location, and, for the purpose of central operations, to designated colleagues of the Pharmacy Services, Clinical Programmes and Quality Assurance departments. Category C is additionally accessible to the Third-Party Reconciliation team. Category D is restricted to the Payment Operations team and to the Information Security Officer for the purpose of investigation; no other population may retrieve the full field set. Categories E is accessible to the Customer Insights team in aggregated form and to the Loyalty Administration team in identified form. Category F is restricted to Human Resources, Payroll and the Office of General Counsel. Category I is restricted to Asset Protection and the Information Security Officer. The Records Custodian maintains the access matrix and certifies it quarterly; the Store Manager has no standing access to Categories A, B, G or H.

4. Retention. Categories A, B, G and H are retained for the period required by the state of dispensing and in no event fewer than ten years from the date of last activity. Category C is retained for seven years from adjudication. Category D is retained only for the interval necessary to complete settlement, chargeback and reconciliation, and the account field is rendered unreadable in storage at all times. Category E is retained for the duration of enrolment and thirty-six months thereafter. Category F is retained for the term of employment and seven years thereafter, except where a longer statutory period applies to the licensure or screening fields. Category I is retained for ninety days unless preserved under legal hold.

5. Disposal. Upon expiry of the applicable period the Records Custodian issues a destruction authorisation identifying the category, the medium and the location. Paper is cross-cut shredded on site or consigned to a bonded destruction vendor under a certificate of destruction. Electronic records are purged by cryptographic erasure or by overwrite in accordance with the Information Security Media Sanitisation Procedure. Destruction is suspended in its entirety for any record subject to legal hold. The Records Custodian retains the destruction log, which records category, volume, method, date and authorising role, for six years.
