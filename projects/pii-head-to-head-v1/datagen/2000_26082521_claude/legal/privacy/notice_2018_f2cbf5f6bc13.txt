BRIAR HOLLOW COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
INTERNAL STANDARD 09-97: HANDLING OF PERSONAL INFORMATION
Issued 3 March 1997 by the Health Officer. Administered by the Records Custodian. Applies to all Divisions, all media, and all contracted service providers holding Department records.

I. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
* Establishes the categories of personal information held by the Department, the positions authorised to access each, the period each is retained, and the manner of disposal.
* Applies equally to paper files, microform, magnetic media, and records held on the clinic minicomputer and the environmental health permit system.
* Contracted providers are bound by this standard through the confidentiality rider to the service agreement.

II. CATEGORIES HELD

* Category 1 — Identity. Name fields, date of birth field, sex code, race and ethnicity codes, place of birth field, mother's maiden name field.
* Category 2 — Locating. Residence address fields, mailing address fields, telephone fields, employer name and work telephone fields, emergency contact name and relationship fields.
* Category 3 — Assigned numbers. Medical record number, clinic account number, state programme case number, insurer subscriber and group numbers, laboratory accession number, permit application number.
* Category 4 — Clinical. Chief complaint narrative, history and physical fields, diagnosis code fields, procedure code fields, medication, dosage and administration fields, allergy and adverse reaction fields, laboratory and imaging result fields, immunisation type, lot and date fields, referral and consultation fields.
* Category 5 — Investigative. Disease report fields, onset and exposure fields, contact locating fields, interview narrative, isolation and quarantine order fields, animal bite and rabies exposure fields.
* Category 6 — Environmental. Complainant name and locating fields, premises owner and operator fields, inspection findings, violation and abatement fields, water and septic permit applicant fields.
* Category 7 — Financial. Income declaration fields, household size field, sliding fee determination, charge, payment and adjustment ledger fields, collection referral fields.
* Category 8 — Personnel. Application and reference fields, licensure and certification fields, performance evaluation fields, leave and attendance fields, occupational exposure and post-exposure fields.

III. ACCESS BY POSITION
* Categories 1, 2 and 4 — the assigned clinician; the Nursing Supervisor; the Medical Director; the Records Custodian for filing purposes only.
* Category 3 — the Records Custodian; the Data Entry Clerk; the Billing Clerk.
* Category 5 — the Communicable Disease Investigator; the Health Officer. Access by any other position requires the written direction of the Health Officer.
* Category 6 — the Sanitarian of record; the Environmental Health Supervisor. Complainant identity within Category 6 is withheld from all other positions and from public inspection requests.
* Category 7 — the Billing Clerk; the Fiscal Officer; the Department Director on request.
* Category 8 — the Personnel Officer; the Department Director.
* Every disclosure outside the Department is entered in the Disclosure Register, which bears the fields: date, category disclosed, receiving agency, statutory or authorisation basis, position releasing.

IV. RETENTION PERIODS
* Categories 1 through 4, adult subject: seven years from last encounter.
* Categories 1 through 4, minor subject: to the twenty-first birthday, or seven years from last encounter, whichever is the later.
* Category 5: ten years from case closure.
* Category 6: ten years for permitted premises; three years for unfounded complaints.
* Category 7: five years from close of the fiscal year in which the account was settled or written off.
* Category 8: term of employment plus six years.
* All periods are suspended on notice of litigation, audit, or pending public records request, and resume on written release by the Health Officer.

V. DISPOSAL PROCEDURE
* Verify expiry of the retention period against the schedule at Section IV; record the verification on the destruction schedule.
* Obtain the countersignature of the Division Head owning the series.
* Destroy paper by cross-cut shredding on premises, or by incineration under vendor manifest with a Department employee present throughout.
* Destroy microform by chemical means; destroy magnetic media by degaussing followed by physical destruction of the medium.
* Complete the destruction log for each action, bearing the fields: series title, category, inclusive dates, volume, method, date of destruction, witness title, custodian title.
* File the log permanently in the Records Office. Produce it at the annual audit and on demand of the County Auditor.
