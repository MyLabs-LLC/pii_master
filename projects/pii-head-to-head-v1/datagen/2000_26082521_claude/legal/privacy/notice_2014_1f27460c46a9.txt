RIVERBEND COMMUNITY CREDIT UNION

SCHEDULE B TO ADMINISTRATIVE STANDARD AP-114
CONSOLIDATED CATEGORY, CUSTODY AND RETENTION SCHEDULE

Maintained by: The Records Custodian
Revision: 3
Date of Revision: 4 November 1998

1. This Schedule is incorporated by reference into AP-114 and shall be read in conjunction therewith. In the event of inconsistency between this Schedule and the body of AP-114, the body of AP-114 shall prevail.

2. The columns of the Schedule bear the following meanings:

   (a) "Category Code" denotes the classification assigned pursuant to Clause 2.1 of AP-114;
   (b) "Category Designation" denotes the descriptive title of the category;
   (c) "Custodian of Record" denotes the officer accountable for the integrity, currency and security of the category;
   (d) "Access Class" denotes the access tier defined at Clause 3.2 of AP-114;
   (e) "Minimum Retention" denotes the period below which no record within the category may be destroyed;
   (f) "Disposal Method" denotes the method prescribed at Clause 5.2 of AP-114.

4. Where a single physical or logical record contains data elements belonging to more than one category, the record shall be governed in its entirety by the category bearing the longest Minimum Retention and the most restrictive Access Class.

5. The Custodian of Record shall certify to the Compliance Officer, semi-annually and in writing, that the inventory of records within that officer's custody has been reconciled against this Schedule and that no record has been retained beyond its Minimum Retention save under an active litigation hold, examination hold, or subpoena.

6. No amendment to this Schedule shall take effect until it has been (a) reduced to writing, (b) assigned a revision number, and (c) circulated to every Custodian of Record named herein.
