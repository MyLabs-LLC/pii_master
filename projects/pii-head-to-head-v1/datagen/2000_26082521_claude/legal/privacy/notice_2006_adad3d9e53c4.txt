CROSSHAUL FREIGHT SERVICES (NORTHERN) LTD
STANDING INSTRUCTION 22 — DEPOT PERSONAL DATA HOLDINGS
Issued to all Depot Managers. Effective from the first Monday following receipt.

1. Depots shall hold personal data in three classes only: the Depot Personnel File (Class 1), the Driver Compliance File (Class 2), and the Visitor and Contractor Log (Class 3). No fourth class may be created at depot level.

2. Class 1 comprises the following fields: Surname; Given Names; Payroll Number; Home Address; Home Telephone Number; Date of Birth; National Insurance Number; Emergency Contact Name; Emergency Contact Telephone Number; Shift Pattern Code.

3. Class 2 comprises the following fields: Payroll Number; Driver Licence Number; Licence Expiry Date; Endorsement Codes; Tachograph Analysis Reference; Infringement Code; Medical Review Date.

4. Class 3 comprises the following fields: Visitor Name; Employer Name; Vehicle Registration Mark; Person Visited; Time of Arrival; Time of Departure; Pass Number Issued.

5. Class 1 and Class 2 files shall be held in a locked steel cabinet in the Depot Manager's office. Keys are held by the Depot Manager and the Deputy Depot Manager only. Duplicate keys shall not be cut at depot level.

6. Traffic Office staff may consult Class 2 for the purpose of vehicle allocation. They may not consult Class 1.

7. Class 3 is held at the gatehouse and is accessible to the gatehouse attendant on duty and to the Depot Manager.

8. Retention: Class 1, six years after leaving; Class 2, four years after expiry of the entry; Class 3, three months.

9. Time-expired records shall be entered on the Depot Withdrawal Sheet and forwarded to Head Office in sealed sacks for destruction. Depots shall not destroy personal data locally, and shall not place any part of Class 1, 2 or 3 in general waste or in recycling collections.

10. Compliance with this Instruction is verified at the annual depot audit.
