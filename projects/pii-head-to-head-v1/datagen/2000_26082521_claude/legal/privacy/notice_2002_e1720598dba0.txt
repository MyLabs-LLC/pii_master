MEMORANDUM

TO:      All Personnel, Patient Accounts and Billing Office
FROM:    Records Custodian, Patient Financial Services
CC:      Administrator, Health Information Management; Compliance Coordinator
DATE:    March 12, 1997
RE:      Standard PFS-04 — Categories of Personal Information, Access, Retention and Disposal

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This standard identifies the categories of personal information maintained by the Patient Accounts and Billing Office, states which positions may access each category, fixes the period each category is retained, and prescribes the method of disposal at the end of that period. It governs information held in paper folders, on microfiche, and in the patient accounting system, and applies equally to working copies, screen printouts, worklists, and extracts drawn from those sources.

2. CATEGORIES MAINTAINED

2.1 Patient identification data. Fields in this category include Patient Name, Date of Birth, Sex, Social Security Number, Medical Record Number, and Patient Account Number.

2.2 Contact and residence data. Fields include Street Address, City, State, ZIP Code, Home Telephone, Work Telephone, and Employer Name.

2.3 Guarantor and responsible party data. Fields include Guarantor Name, Relationship to Patient, Guarantor Address, Guarantor Telephone, and Guarantor Employer.

2.4 Coverage data. Fields include Payer Name, Plan Type, Group Number, Subscriber Name, Subscriber Identification Number, Policy Effective Date, and Coordination of Benefits Indicator.

2.5 Charge and adjudication data. Fields include Admission Date, Discharge Date, Attending Physician, Diagnosis Code, Procedure Code, Revenue Code, Total Charges, Allowed Amount, Adjustment Reason Code, and Patient Balance.

2.6 Payment instrument data. Fields include Cardholder Name, Card Number, Card Expiration Date, Check Number, and Bank Routing Number.

2.7 Collection and financial assistance data. Fields include Dunning Cycle, Telephone Contact Log, Payment Plan Terms, Household Size, Household Income, Assistance Determination, and Placement Agency.

3. ACCESS

Access is granted by position, not by individual request, and only to the categories required by the duties of that position.

3.1 Billing Clerks and Claim Editors: categories 2.1 through 2.5.
3.2 Insurance Follow-Up Representatives: categories 2.1, 2.4, and 2.5.
3.3 Cashiers: categories 2.1, 2.6, and the Patient Balance field only from 2.5.
3.4 Collection Specialists and Financial Counselors: categories 2.1, 2.3, 2.5, and 2.7.
3.5 Billing Office Supervisor, Records Custodian, and Internal Audit: all categories.
3.6 Contracted review firms: the categories named in the written agreement, for the term of that agreement only.

Category 2.6 is masked on all reports except the Cashier's daily reconciliation.

4. RETENTION

4.1 Categories 2.1 through 2.5: ten years from final resolution of the account. For accounts of minors, ten years from final resolution or until the patient reaches twenty-one years of age, whichever is later.
4.2 Category 2.6: eighteen months from posting of the payment.
4.3 Category 2.7: seven years from closure of the collection file.
4.4 Worklists, printouts, and extracts: destroy at the end of the shift on which they were produced.

5. DISPOSAL

Paper and microfiche are destroyed by cross-cut shredding in the presence of the Records Custodian or a designated Supervisor. Electronic records are purged by the patient accounting system's archive utility; magnetic media are degaussed before release from the building. Each destruction is entered in the Destruction Log under the columns Date of Destruction, Category, Volume, Method, Witness Title, and Custodian Initials. The log is retained permanently.

6. HOLDS

No record subject to audit, litigation, or open payer dispute may be destroyed. The Compliance Coordinator issues and releases holds in writing.

Direct questions to the Records Custodian, extension listed on the departmental telephone roster.
