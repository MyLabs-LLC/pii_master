COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
DIVISION OF ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES

STANDARD RA-12
CLASSIFICATION, ACCESS, RETENTION AND DISPOSITION OF IDENTIFIABLE RECORD CONTENT

Issued 14 October 1996. Revision 2 effective 3 February 1998.
Supersedes RA-9 and RA-9.1.

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
   1.1 This standard governs each record series held by the Department in which content identifying, or capable of identifying, an individual is present, without regard to medium or division of origin.
   1.2 Media within scope: paper folders, bound registers, index cards, microfiche, magnetic tape, diskette, fixed disk on Department-owned equipment, and facsimile output.
   1.3 Excluded: aggregate statistical extracts from which all Category A content has been removed before storage.

2. CATEGORIES OF IDENTIFIABLE CONTENT
   2.1 Category A - Registration and Demographic Content
       2.1.1 Field labels: Surname; Given Name; Middle Initial; Former Name; Date of Birth; Sex; Race/Ethnicity; Residence Address; Mailing Address; Telephone Number; Social Security Number; Medical Record Number; Case Number; Head of Household.
   2.2 Category B - Clinical Encounter Content
       2.2.1 Field labels: Encounter Date; Presenting Complaint; Diagnosis Code; Procedure Code; Immunization Series; Laboratory Result; Prescribed Medication; Referring Provider; Disposition.
   2.3 Category C - Communicable Disease and Surveillance Content
       2.3.1 Field labels: Reportable Condition; Report Source; Onset Date; Contact Name; Contact Relationship; Exposure Site; Investigation Status.
   2.4 Category D - Eligibility and Financial Content
       2.4.1 Field labels: Household Size; Declared Income; Sliding Fee Class; Insurance Carrier; Policy Identifier; Guarantor; Account Balance; Remittance Method.
   2.5 Category E - Employee and Occupational Health Content
       2.5.1 Field labels: Position Title; Payroll Identifier; Hire Date; Tuberculin Screening Result; Exposure Incident Report; Disciplinary Action.
   2.6 Category F - Complaint and Investigative Content
       2.6.1 Field labels: Complainant Status; Subject of Complaint; Premises Type; Allegation Class; Finding; Enforcement Action.

3. ACCESS
   3.1 Access is assigned by category and by role. Individual grants of access outside an approved role are not made.
   3.2 Category assignments:
       Category A - Registration Clerk; Public Health Nurse; Records Custodian; Fiscal Officer (billing function only).
       Category B - Public Health Nurse; Clinic Supervisor; Health Officer; Records Custodian.
       Category C - Communicable Disease Investigator; Health Officer; Records Custodian.
       Category D - Fiscal Officer; Eligibility Technician; County Auditor (audit period only).
       Category E - Personnel Officer; Division Director of the affected division; Department Counsel.
       Category F - Environmental Health Supervisor; Department Counsel; Health Officer.
   3.3 The Records Custodian maintains the Access Authorization Register, bearing the columns: Role Title; Category Code; Permission Level; Authorizing Signature Block; Effective Date; Revocation Date.
   3.4 Disclosure beyond the Department is entered in the Disclosure Register under the columns: Entry Number; Category Code; Requesting Body; Statutory Basis; Extent of Content Released; Date Released; Releasing Role Title.

4. RETENTION
   4.1 Categories A and B: ten years from date of last encounter. Where the subject was a minor at last encounter, until attainment of majority plus ten years.
   4.2 Category C: twenty-five years from close of investigation.
   4.3 Category D: seven years from close of the fiscal year in which the account was settled.
   4.4 Category E: duration of employment plus thirty years for occupational health content; duration of employment plus seven years for all other content.
   4.5 Category F: five years from the date of final enforcement action.
   4.6 Destruction is suspended in its entirety upon written notice from Department Counsel and resumes only upon written release.

5. DISPOSITION
   5.1 The Records Custodian issues a list of holdings eligible for destruction each quarter and obtains the countersignature of the Health Officer before any action.
   5.2 Paper and index media: cross-cut shredding to a residue strip not exceeding six millimetres, or incineration by bonded contractor under continuous observation by the Records Custodian or designee.
   5.3 Magnetic media: degaussing followed by physical deformation. Microfiche: incineration only.
   5.4 A Certificate of Destruction is completed for each action under the columns: Certificate Number; Category Code; Record Series Title; Inclusive Dates of Series; Volume in Cubic Feet; Method; Date Performed; Witness Role Title.
   5.5 Certificates of Destruction are retained permanently and are not subject to Section 4.
