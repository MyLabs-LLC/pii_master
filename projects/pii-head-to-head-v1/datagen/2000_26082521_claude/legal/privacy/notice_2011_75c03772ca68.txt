PIONEER LAND TITLE COMPANY
BRANCH OPERATING BULLETIN NO. 22
FILE CONTENT AND CLEAN DESK REQUIREMENTS

Effective 1 June 1998. Post at each work station.

1. Escrow files contain protected particulars. The categories are: identification particulars; residence and contact particulars; banking and disbursement particulars; loan and payoff particulars; transaction and vesting particulars.

2. No file shall be left open, face up, or unattended on any desk, counter, credenza or conference table outside the presence of the responsible officer.

3. Signature cards, wire instruction sheets, buyer and seller information sheets and lender payoff demands shall be filed in the jacket immediately upon use and never left in the copier, facsimile machine or printer tray.

4. Working copies of any document bearing a Social Security Number, Taxpayer Identification Number, Account Number or Date of Birth field shall be placed in the confidential destruct bin, not the wastebasket.

5. Terminals shall be logged off whenever the operator leaves the work station.

6. Requests for file information from any person other than the Escrow Officer of record shall be referred to the Branch Manager without comment.

7. Closed files shall be forwarded to the Records Custodian within ten business days of final disbursement and shall not be retained at the branch.

8. Questions concerning this Bulletin shall be directed to the Branch Manager.
