MERIDIAN TITLE & ESCROW COMPANY
CORPORATE ADMINISTRATION DIVISION

STANDARD PRACTICE INSTRUCTION SPI-114
CUSTODY, ACCESS AND DISPOSITION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD IN FILE

Issued: October 1997
Supersedes: SPI-114 (Rev. 2), March 1994
Approving Authority: Vice President, Corporate Administration
Distribution: Branch Managers; Escrow Officers; Title Officers; Records Custodian; Internal Audit

1. PURPOSE

1.1 This Instruction prescribes the categories of personal information maintained by the Company, the persons authorised to access each category, the minimum period for which each category shall be retained, and the manner of its destruction.

1.2 This Instruction is mandatory upon all officers, employees, temporary staff and contract abstractors.

2. SCOPE

2.1 This Instruction applies to information held in any medium, including paper file jackets, ledger cards, microfilm, microfiche, magnetic tape, fixed disk, diskette and any printed working copy thereof.

2.2 This Instruction does not govern public records obtained from a county recorder, assessor or clerk, which are governed by SPI-121.

3. CATEGORIES

3.1 Category A — Identification Particulars. Fields: Full Legal Name; Former or Alternate Name; Date of Birth; Social Security Number; Taxpayer Identification Number; Driver's Licence Number; Marital Status; Citizenship Status.

3.2 Category B — Contact Particulars. Fields: Mailing Address; Property Address; Home Telephone; Business Telephone; Facsimile Number; Forwarding Address.

3.3 Category C — Financial Particulars. Fields: Depository Institution Name; Account Number; Routing Number; Wire Instruction Reference; Loan Number; Payoff Balance; Earnest Money Amount; Source of Funds Declaration.

3.4 Category D — Transaction Particulars. Fields: Escrow Number; Title Order Number; Policy Number; Vesting; Consideration; Lien Position; Commission Disbursement.

3.5 Category E — Underwriting and Search Particulars. Fields: Judgment Search Result; Bankruptcy Search Result; Name Variance Note; Indemnity Reference.

3.6 Category F — Personnel Particulars, held by the Human Resources Department only. Fields: Employee Number; Compensation Grade; Bond Application Reference; Withholding Election.

4. ACCESS

4.1 Access to Categories A through E is limited to the Escrow Officer of record, the assigned Escrow Assistant, the Title Officer, the Branch Manager, the Records Custodian and Internal Audit.

4.2 Access to Category F is limited to the Director of Human Resources and such officers as the President may designate in writing.

4.3 Access by any other person, including a party to the transaction, shall be granted only upon written request approved by the Branch Manager and recorded in the Access Register.

4.4 No category shall be disclosed to an outside party except as required by statute, court order, underwriter audit, or the express written instruction of the party to whom the information relates.

5. RETENTION

5.1 Closed escrow files: seven (7) years from date of final disbursement.

5.2 Cancelled escrow files: three (3) years from date of cancellation.

5.3 Title search and examination files: ten (10) years from date of policy issuance.

5.4 Trust account records: seven (7) years, or such longer period as the Department of Insurance may require.

5.5 Personnel records: four (4) years following separation.

6. STORAGE

6.1 Active files shall be secured in locking cabinets at close of business.

6.2 Inactive files shall be transferred to the off-site vault under transmittal receipt.

7. DISPOSAL

7.1 Upon expiry of the retention period the Records Custodian shall prepare a Destruction Schedule identifying each file by number and category and submit it to the Branch Manager for authorisation.

7.2 Paper shall be destroyed by cross-cut shredding or by witnessed pulping. Magnetic media shall be overwritten in full and thereafter physically defaced. Microform shall be incinerated.

7.3 Destruction shall be witnessed by two employees, one of whom shall be an officer. Both shall sign the Certificate of Destruction, which shall be retained permanently.

8. LEGAL HOLD

8.1 No record shall be destroyed while it is the subject of a claim, demand, audit or subpoena, notwithstanding Clause 5.

9. NON-COMPLIANCE

9.1 Departure from this Instruction is a matter for disciplinary action and shall be reported to Internal Audit within one business day of discovery.
