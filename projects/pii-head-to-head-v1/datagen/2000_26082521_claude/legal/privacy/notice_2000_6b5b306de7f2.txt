MEMORANDUM

TO:      All Escrow, Title, and Branch Staff
FROM:    Office of the Records Custodian
DATE:    February 2011
SUBJECT: Standard RM-02 — Handling of Nonpublic Personal Information

1. Scope. This standard applies to every file, working paper, exhibit, and electronic record created or received in connection with a title order or an escrow settlement, whether held at a branch, at the imaging center, or in off-site storage.

2. Categories held. The Company maintains four categories of nonpublic personal information: (a) identifying particulars, comprising the fields Full Legal Name, Former or Maiden Name, Date of Birth, and Marital Status; (b) government identifiers, comprising the fields Social Security Number, Taxpayer Identification Number, and Driver's License or State Identification Number; (c) financial particulars, comprising the fields Deposit Account Number, ABA Routing Number, Wire Instruction Detail, Loan Number, and Source of Funds Statement; and (d) transaction particulars, comprising the fields Property Address, Assessor's Parcel Number, Vesting Instruction, and Order Number.

3. Access. Access to a live file is limited to the assigned escrow officer, the assigned title examiner, the branch manager of record, and the Records Custodian. Underwriting counsel and the funding desk may be granted access for the duration of a specific order upon written request to the branch manager.

4. Retention. A closed file is retained five years from the date of final disbursement. Where an insured closing protection letter was issued, or where the file was the subject of a claim, notice of claim, or audit exception, retention is extended to seven years from the same trigger.

5. Disposal. Upon expiry of the retention period the Records Custodian schedules the file range for destruction. Paper is cross-cut shredded on premises or surrendered to the bonded vendor against a certificate of destruction. Electronic media is overwritten in accordance with the Information Systems Manager's procedure, or physically destroyed where overwriting is impracticable. Microfilm is incinerated.

6. Log. Each disposal event is recorded in the Destruction Register under the column headings Order Number Range, Category, Retention Trigger Date, Method, Certificate Number, and Custodian Initials. The Register is retained permanently.

7. Direct all questions to the Records Custodian.
