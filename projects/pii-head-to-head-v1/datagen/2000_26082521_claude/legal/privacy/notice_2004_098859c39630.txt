MERIDIAN DISTRIBUTION SERVICES LIMITED
INTERNAL STANDARD PS-04

HOLDING, ACCESS AND DISPOSAL OF PERSONAL DATA

Issue 2 — Effective 1 April 1997
Supersedes Issue 1 (June 1995)
Custodian: Company Secretary's Department

1. SCOPE

1.1 This Standard applies to all personal data held by the Company in manual filing systems or on automated equipment, at Head Office, at all depots, and at any third-party site operated under contract.

1.2 This Standard applies irrespective of the medium of storage, including paper, microfiche, magnetic tape, and fixed or removable disc.

1.3 Nothing in this Standard reduces any obligation arising under statute, under an operator's licence undertaking, or under the terms of a customer contract.

2. CATEGORIES OF DATA HELD

2.1 Category A — Employment Records. Fields: Surname; Given Names; Title; Payroll Number; National Insurance Number; Date of Birth; Home Address; Home Telephone Number; Marital Status; Next of Kin Name; Next of Kin Telephone Number; Bank Sort Code; Bank Account Number; Grade; Date of Engagement; Date of Leaving; Reason for Leaving.

2.2 Category B — Driver Qualification Records. Fields: Payroll Number; Driver Licence Number; Licence Categories Held; Endorsement Codes; Endorsement Expiry Date; Tachograph Card Number; Medical Examination Date; Medical Review Date; Training Course Codes; Assessment Result Code.

2.3 Category C — Occupational Health Records. Fields: Payroll Number; Referral Reference; Assessment Outcome Code; Fitness Restriction Code; Review Date. Clinical correspondence is held under separate cover and is not incorporated into Category A.

2.4 Category D — Disciplinary and Grievance Records. Fields: Payroll Number; Case Reference; Category of Matter; Stage Reached; Outcome Code; Expiry Date of Warning.

2.5 Category E — Recruitment Records. Fields: Applicant Reference; Surname; Given Names; Correspondence Address; Daytime Telephone Number; Position Applied For; Referee Name; Referee Address; Selection Decision Code.

2.6 Category F — Customer and Consignee Contact Records. Fields: Contact Name; Job Title; Site Name; Site Address; Site Telephone Number; Site Facsimile Number; Delivery Instruction Code.

2.7 Category G — Incident and Claims Records. Fields: Payroll Number; Vehicle Fleet Number; Third Party Name; Third Party Address; Third Party Insurer; Claim Reference; Witness Name; Witness Address.

2.8 Category H — Site Access Records. Fields: Pass Number; Surname; Given Names; Photograph; Vehicle Registration Mark; Time of Entry; Time of Exit.

3. ACCESS

3.1 Categories A, C and D are accessible only to the Personnel Manager, the Records Custodian, and the line manager of the individual concerned. Category C is further restricted such that the line manager receives the Fitness Restriction Code alone.

3.2 Category B is accessible to the Transport Manager, depot Traffic Office staff, and the Records Custodian.

3.3 Category E is accessible to the Personnel Manager and the recruiting manager only.

3.4 Categories F and G are accessible to Commercial Department staff and to the Claims Handler.

3.5 Category H is accessible to the Security Supervisor at the site of collection.

3.6 Access by any other person requires the written authority of the Records Custodian, recorded in the Access Authorisation Register.

4. RETENTION

4.1 Category A: six years from the date of leaving.
4.2 Category B: four years from expiry of the qualification.
4.3 Category C: forty years from the date of the last entry.
4.4 Category D: twelve months from the expiry date of the warning.
4.5 Category E, unsuccessful applicants: six months from the selection decision.
4.6 Category F: for the duration of the trading relationship and two years thereafter.
4.7 Category G: six years from settlement or closure of the claim.
4.8 Category H: three months from the date of entry.

5. DISPOSAL

5.1 Records reaching the end of the retention period shall be withdrawn at the quarterly review and listed on Form PS-04/1.

5.2 Paper records shall be destroyed by cross-cut shredding to a residue particle not exceeding 4 millimetres in width, or by pulping under supervision.

5.3 Magnetic media shall be overwritten in full three times, or physically destroyed where overwriting is not practicable.

5.4 Destruction shall be witnessed by two members of staff, one of whom shall be the Records Custodian or a person nominated in writing by the Records Custodian.

5.5 The Destruction Register shall record: Category Reference; Range of Record References; Volume in Linear Metres; Method of Destruction; Date of Destruction; Witness Signatures; Contractor Certificate Number where applicable. The Register is retained indefinitely.

6. REVIEW

6.1 This Standard shall be reviewed at intervals not exceeding twenty-four months by the Records Custodian.
