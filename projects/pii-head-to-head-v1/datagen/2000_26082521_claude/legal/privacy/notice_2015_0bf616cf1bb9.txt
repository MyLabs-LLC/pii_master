PRIVACY CONTROL SELF-AUDIT
Standard RIM-114, Attachment D. To be completed quarterly by the department Records Custodian and countersigned by the Department Head. Return to the Office of the Privacy Officer within ten business days of quarter end.

DEPARTMENT: ____________________   QUARTER: ____________________
CUSTODIAN (ROLE TITLE): ____________________

INVENTORY
[ ] Category listing reconciled against the Control Grid; no uncoded category held.
[ ] Field labels in active use match those authorized for each category held.
[ ] Field labels added to any form, screen, or report since last audit reported to the Privacy Officer.
[ ] No category held in personal drawers, home files, or portable media.

ACCESS
[ ] Access roster reviewed; entries limited to authorized role titles.
[ ] Terminated and transferred associates removed from all category access within one business day.
[ ] Temporary access grants expired or renewed in writing.
[ ] Health and Medical category segregated; no Underwriting or Marketing role holds access.
[ ] Masking active on Social Security Number, Account Number, and Credit Card Number fields for all roles not expressly authorized.
[ ] Vendor and adjusting firm access covered by executed confidentiality agreement.

RETENTION
[ ] Retention trigger dates recorded for all closed files.
[ ] Eligible files identified for destruction and listed on the Destruction Register.
[ ] Litigation and examination holds checked before any file is listed.
[ ] No file retained beyond schedule without a documented hold.

DISPOSAL
[ ] Shredders operable; consoles locked; keys held by the Custodian only.
[ ] Vendor pickups witnessed and logged.
[ ] Certificates of Destruction received and filed for all prior-quarter pickups.
[ ] Magnetic and optical media overwritten three times prior to physical destruction.
[ ] Destruction Register complete: Category Code, Volume, Method, Date, Custodian Signature, Witness Signature.

INCIDENTS
[ ] Misdirected mail, facsimile, or electronic mail incidents logged and reported.
[ ] Documents received in error returned or destroyed and recorded.
[ ] No open incident older than 30 days.
