MEMORANDUM

FROM:    Records Custodian
DATE:    October 21, 1996
SUBJECT: Standing Instruction on Personal Information Held by This Office

(a) Inventory. The office maintains six categories of personal information. Identification data carries the labels Patient Name, Date of Birth, Social Security Number, Medical Record Number, and Account Number. Contact data carries Mailing Address, Home Telephone, and Employer Name. Responsible party data carries Guarantor Name, Relationship, and Guarantor Address. Coverage data carries Payer Name, Group Number, and Subscriber Identification Number. Financial transaction data carries Total Charges, Allowed Amount, Patient Balance, Check Number, and Card Number. Hardship data carries Household Income, Household Size, and Determination Code. No further categories may be created without the Manager's written authorization and an amendment to this instruction.

(b) Access. Each supervisor certifies annually, on the Access Certification form, which of the six categories each position under their direction requires. Access is issued against that certification and withdrawn on the day a position changes or an employee separates. The Records Custodian and Internal Audit hold standing access to all categories for the purpose of review. Outside parties obtain access only under a signed agreement naming the categories, the purpose, and the term.

(c) Retention. Identification, contact, responsible party, coverage, and financial transaction data are retained for ten years measured from the date the account reaches zero balance or is written off, whichever occurs first. Card and bank fields within financial transaction data are purged at eighteen months regardless of account status. Hardship data is retained seven years from determination. Transitory material — worklists, screen prints, batch reports — is destroyed on the day it is produced.

(d) Disposal. Destruction is performed in the department, not by the loading dock or general housekeeping. Paper and microfiche are cross-cut shredded; magnetic media are degaussed. Two staff are present, one of whom must be a Supervisor or the Records Custodian. The Destruction Log entry records Date of Destruction, Category, Volume, Method, Witness Title, and Custodian Initials, and the log itself is kept permanently in the file room.

(e) Holds. Nothing is destroyed while an audit, a payer dispute, or a legal matter is open. The Compliance Coordinator maintains the hold list and reviews it quarterly with this office.
