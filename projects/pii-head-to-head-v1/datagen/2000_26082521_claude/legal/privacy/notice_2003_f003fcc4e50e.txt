PRIVACY AND CONFIDENTIALITY STANDARD — SCHEDULE 1
INVENTORY OF PERSONAL INFORMATION HOLDINGS
Maintained by the Records Custodian pursuant to Standard RM-04, Clause 2.10
Reviewed annually by the Supervisory Committee. Revision 4, June 1999.

PART ONE — GENERAL PROVISIONS

1. Every holding of personal information maintained anywhere within the Credit Union shall be entered in this Schedule. A holding not entered in this Schedule is an unauthorised holding and shall be surrendered to the Records Custodian upon discovery.

2. The Records Custodian shall verify the accuracy of this Schedule not less than once in each calendar year and shall certify such verification in writing to the Supervisory Committee.

3. The abbreviations employed in Part Two bear the following meanings: RC, Records Custodian; MSR, Member Services Representative; LOR, Loan Officer of Record; DPS, Data Processing Supervisor; HRO, Human Resources Officer; CO, Compliance Officer; IA, Internal Auditor; SO, Security Officer.
