HOSPITAL BUSINESS OFFICE — RECORDS STANDARD
                   PERSONAL INFORMATION HOLDINGS: REGISTER,
                    CUSTODY, RETENTION, AND DESTRUCTION
                          Revision 3 — calendar year 2016
                 ------------------------------------------------

I.   PREAMBLE
     A.   The billing office maintains personal information in furtherance of
          treatment, payment, and health care operations, and for no other
          purpose except as compelled by law or as permitted upon valid
          authorization.
     B.   Every holding shall be entered in the Category Register maintained by
          the Records Custodian, which Register shall bear, and only bear, the
          following column headings:
          1.   Category Name
          2.   Constituent Field Labels
          3.   Source System of Record
          4.   Custodial Unit
          5.   Authorized Roles
          6.   Retention Trigger Event
          7.   Minimum Retention Period
          8.   Disposition Method
     C.   The Register shall at no time contain a specimen, illustration, or
          extract of the information it describes; it is an index of labels
          and of nothing further.

II.  ENUMERATION OF CATEGORIES
     A.   Account Master Category
          1.   Field labels: Account Number; Guarantor Name; Guarantor Mailing
               Address; Telephone Number; Electronic Mail Address; Employer
               Name; Responsible Party Relationship.
     B.   Clinical-Financial Linkage Category
          1.   Field labels: Medical Record Number; Encounter Number; Date of
               Service; Attending Provider Identifier; Diagnosis Code;
               Procedure Code; Revenue Code; Place of Service Code.
     C.   Remittance Category
          1.   Field labels: Payer Name; Claim Number; Remittance Advice
               Number; Allowed Amount; Contractual Adjustment; Patient
               Responsibility; Denial Reason Code.
     D.   Correspondence Category
          1.   Field labels: Notice Type; Date Mailed; Return Mail Indicator;
               Interpreter Requested Indicator; Complaint Category; Resolution
               Code.
     E.   Workforce Category, being information concerning personnel of the
          billing office and not concerning patients
          1.   Field labels: Employee Identification Number; Position Title;
               System Role Assignment; Date of Access Certification.

III. CUSTODY AND ACCESS
     A.   Custody of each category vests in the Records Custodian, who may
          delegate day-to-day stewardship but may not delegate accountability.
     B.   Access to categories II.A through II.D is confined to those roles
          enumerated in column 5 of the Register, and is further confined,
          within each such role, to the minimum necessary to accomplish the
          purpose of the access.
     C.   Access to category II.E is confined to the Revenue Cycle Supervisor,
          the Information Security Officer, and Human Resources.
     D.   Quarterly, the Information Security Officer shall recertify each role
          assignment; an assignment not affirmatively recertified shall lapse
          at the close of the quarter.

IV.  RETENTION PERIODS
     A.   Categories II.A, II.B, and II.C: seven (7) years measured from the
          Retention Trigger Event recorded in column 6, ordinarily the date
          the account balance is extinguished.
     B.   Category II.D: three (3) years from the date of final resolution,
          provided that any item constituting a privacy complaint shall be
          retained six (6) years.
     C.   Category II.E: the term of engagement plus six (6) years.
     D.   No holding shall be retained beyond its stated period merely for
          administrative convenience; over-retention is a deviation reportable
          to the Privacy Officer.

V.   DESTRUCTION PROCEDURE
     A.   The Records Custodian shall generate a destruction proposal listing
          the affected Record Series and the applicable Trigger Event dates.
     B.   The proposal shall be circulated to the Office of General Counsel for
          confirmation that no hold subsists.
     C.   Upon confirmation, paper shall be shredded and electronic media
          purged or physically destroyed, in each case under continuous
          observation by a member of the workforce holding a custodial role.
     D.   The completed Certificate of Destruction shall be retained
          permanently, notwithstanding the destruction of the records to which
          it relates.
