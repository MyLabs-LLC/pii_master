METROPOLITAN REGIONAL TRANSIT AUTHORITY — OFFICE OF THE GENERAL COUNSEL
STANDARD RIG-04, PART VII: DISPOSITION, CERTIFICATION AND LEGAL HOLD
Adopted 14 March 2011. Amended 3 October 2011 to insert clause (f).

(a) No record containing personal information of any category enumerated in paragraph 2 shall be destroyed, deleted, overwritten, or otherwise rendered irretrievable except in accordance with this Part, and any destruction effected otherwise than in accordance with this Part shall be reported forthwith to the General Counsel notwithstanding that the applicable retention period may in fact have expired.

(b) The Designated Custodian shall, not less frequently than quarterly, cause a Disposition Review to be conducted, at which there shall be identified every holding whose applicable retention period has expired, and there shall be prepared a Disposition Proposal reciting, in respect of each such holding, the Category Code, the Volume Description, the System of Record or Physical Location, the Date of Expiry, and the Proposed Method.

(c) The Disposition Proposal shall be transmitted to the Records Custodian, who shall cause it to be checked against the Legal Hold Register maintained by the Office of the General Counsel, and shall strike from the Proposal any holding to which a subsisting Legal Hold Notice, litigation obligation, audit obligation, or outstanding access request under the applicable public records statute pertains.

(d) Destruction shall be effected only by cross-cut shredding to a residue of not greater than the dimensions prescribed by the applicable national standard, by secure overwriting in not fewer than three passes, by verified cryptographic key destruction, or by degaussing of magnetic media, and shall be witnessed by an officer other than the officer effecting it.

(e) Upon completion, the witnessing officer and the officer effecting destruction shall jointly certify the entry made in the Disposition Log, which entry shall recite the Category Code, the Volume Description, the Method, the Date of Destruction, and the Titles of the certifying officers. The Disposition Log is itself a permanent record of the Authority and is not subject to destruction.

(f) Where destruction is performed by a contracted disposal vendor, the vendor shall be bound by a written instrument imposing obligations no less protective than those imposed by this Part, shall furnish a certificate of destruction in respect of each consignment, and shall permit inspection of its facilities by the Records Custodian upon reasonable notice.
