REGIONAL TRANSIT AUTHORITY
WORK INSTRUCTION WI-114.4
CERTIFIED DISPOSAL OF PERSONAL INFORMATION HOLDINGS
Effective 1 September 2004. Issued under Section 5 of Administrative Standard AS-114.

1. The custodian identifies, from the Retention and Disposition Schedule, those record series for which the retention period has expired, and prepares a disposal list stating the series title, the category designation, the medium, the inclusive period covered, and the estimated volume. The disposal list records series and volumes only; it does not reproduce any data element from the holdings.

2. The custodian forwards the disposal list to the General Counsel, who confirms in writing that no legal hold subsists over any listed series. A list returned without that confirmation is void and may not be acted upon.

3. The Records Custodian reviews the confirmed list, verifies the computation of each retention period against the trigger event recorded in the authoritative system, and certifies the list for disposal. Certification is entered in the disposal register under a sequential control number.

4. Physical records are transferred to the secure holding area pending destruction. Custody during transfer is continuous; records are not left unattended in corridors, loading areas, or unlocked offices, and are not placed in general refuse or recycling collection at any stage.

5. Destruction is carried out on Authority premises under the observation of the Records Custodian or a delegate of that office. Paper is cross-cut shredded to a particle size not exceeding the standard specified in the current contract, or pulped. Magnetic media are degaussed and then physically deformed. Optical media are shredded. Where a contractor performs the destruction, the observing officer remains present throughout and does not delegate observation to contractor personnel.

6. Electronic holdings are destroyed by deletion followed by overwriting of the affected storage, and by the corresponding purge of backup sets on their next scheduled cycle. Where a backup set cannot be purged selectively, the custodian records the date on which the set expires in the ordinary rotation and the disposal is treated as complete on that date.

7. The observing officer completes a Certificate of Destruction recording the control number, the series titles, the medium, the volume, the method, the date, the location, and the signatures of the observing officer and the operator. Certificates are retained permanently and are produced to auditors on request.
