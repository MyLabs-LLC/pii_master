BAYSHORE PHARMACY HOLDINGS
Privacy Standard PS-02 — Custody of Personal Information
Rev. 2016.1

(a) This Standard identifies, by category, the personal information held by the Company and fixes the conditions of its custody. It is issued under the authority of the Privacy Official and is binding on all operating units, including the retail division, the specialty division, the central fill facility and the mail service facility.

(b) The Company holds seven categories. Patient Identity Information comprises the fields Patient Name, Date of Birth, Sex, Address, Telephone Number and Patient Identifier. Dispensing Information comprises Prescription Number, Fill Date, Drug Product Identifier, Quantity Dispensed, Days Supply and Prescriber Identifier. Coverage Information comprises Payer Name, Member Identifier and Group Number. Financial Information comprises Cardholder Name, Primary Account Number and Expiration Date. Workforce Information comprises Colleague Identifier, Social Security Number, Licence Number and Compensation Data. Clinical Programme Information comprises Consent Record, Immunisation Record and Screening Result. Monitoring Information comprises Video Surveillance Recording, Badge Access Log and System Audit Log.

(c) Custody of the first, second, third and sixth categories vests in the Pharmacist-in-Charge at the location of origin and, centrally, in the Director of Pharmacy Services. Custody of the fourth vests in the Treasurer. Custody of the fifth vests in the Director of Human Resources. Custody of the seventh vests in the Asset Protection Manager. A custodian may not delegate certification of the user population, though operational tasks may be assigned to the Records Custodian.

(d) Retention follows the schedule appended to this Standard and is measured, in the case of clinical and dispensing categories, from the date of last activity, and otherwise from the close of the transaction, enrolment or employment relationship to which the record pertains. Retention beyond the scheduled period is permitted only under a preservation notice.

(e) Disposal is effected by cross-cut shredding for paper, by certified destruction for portable media, and by purge or cryptographic erasure for records held in Company systems. The Records Custodian records each disposal event in the register, noting category, medium, volume, method, date and authorising role, and reconciles the register against the schedule semi-annually. Failure to dispose within ninety days of the scheduled date is reported to the Privacy Official as a control exception.
