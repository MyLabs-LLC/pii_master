RECORDS HANDLING STANDARD — TITLE AND ESCROW OPERATIONS
Section 4: Personal Information Inventory and Custody

☐ 1. GENERAL

☐ 1.1 This Standard is issued under the authority of the Compliance Officer and applies to every custodial file, index, and abstract held by the company.
☐ 1.2 Compliance is a condition of continued terminal access.

☐ 2. INVENTORY OF CATEGORIES

☐ 2.1 Class I — Party Identity. Field labels: Name of Record; Prior Name; Date of Birth; Taxpayer Identification Number; Identification Document Number.
☐ 2.2 Class II — Locators. Field labels: Mailing Address; Subject Property Address; Home Telephone; Work Telephone; Electronic Mail Address.
☐ 2.3 Class III — Funds and Credit. Field labels: Lender; Loan Number; Account Number; Routing Number; Wire Beneficiary; Payoff Amount.
☐ 2.4 Class IV — Order Reference. Field labels: File Number; Escrow Number; Commitment Number; Policy Number; Legal Description; Assessor Parcel Number.
☐ 2.5 Class V — Status and Encumbrance. Field labels: Marital Status; Vesting; Judgment Debtor; Lien Party; Estate Reference; Guardianship Reference.

☐ 3. CUSTODY AND ACCESS

☐ 3.1 Each class is assigned a custody role at the point of file opening.
☐ 3.2 Class III is restricted to the Escrow Officer and the Trust Accountant.
☐ 3.3 Class V is restricted to the Title Examiner and Underwriting Counsel.
☐ 3.4 All other classes follow the standard escrow team profile.
☐ 3.5 Requests from outside the assigned team are logged and referred to the Branch Manager.

☐ 4. RETENTION

☐ 4.1 Classes I, II, and IV — seven years from disbursement.
☐ 4.2 Class III — seven years from trust reconciliation.
☐ 4.3 Class V — policy liability period plus three years.
☐ 4.4 Duplicates and working notes — destroy at closing.

☐ 5. DISPOSAL

☐ 5.1 Verify the Hold Register.
☐ 5.2 Obtain the Branch Manager's certification.
☐ 5.3 Shred paper on premises before two witnesses.
☐ 5.4 Overwrite and destroy electronic media.
☐ 5.5 Execute and file the Certificate of Destruction.

☐ 6. ATTESTATION

☐ 6.1 The Branch Manager signs this checklist each quarter and forwards it to the Records Custodian.
