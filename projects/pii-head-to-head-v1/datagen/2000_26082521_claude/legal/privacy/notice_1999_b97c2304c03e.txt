SUNRIDGE VALLEY UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT
STANDARD AS-114.1 — QUICK REFERENCE: CATEGORY ACCESS AND RETENTION SUMMARY
Adopted 1 August 2021 | Companion to Administrative Standard AS-114

1. This quick reference summarizes AS-114 for daily operational use. Where this summary and AS-114 differ, AS-114 governs.

2. SUMMARY TABLE

   CATEGORY                     | STANDING ACCESS            | RETENTION
   -----------------------------|----------------------------|---------------------------
   A  Student Directory         | Site admin, office mgr     | Enrollment + 1 yr
   B  Education Records         | Teachers, counselors, reg. | Transcript permanent;
                                |                            | other 5 yrs post-exit
   C  Health & Immunization     | Nurse, health assistant    | Age 19 or +3 yrs
   D  Special Education         | Case manager, program spec.| 5 yrs; evals 10 yrs
   E  Family & Emergency Contact| Office mgr, site admin     | Enrollment + 1 yr
   F  Meal Eligibility          | Nutrition Svcs, Business   | 3 yrs post fiscal year
   G  HR Records                | HR Director, HR analysts   | Employment + 7 yrs
   H  Payroll & Benefits        | Payroll Supervisor         | 7 yrs from tax year
   I  Background Screening      | HR Director only           | Employment + 7 yrs
   J  Transportation & Access   | Transportation Supervisor  | Logs 45 days

3. FIELD LABEL REMINDERS

3.1 Category C comprises the labels: immunization series; dates administered; medication authorization; allergy classification; medical condition; individual health plan; nurse visit log.

3.2 Category H comprises the labels: bank routing designation; account designation; withholding elections; benefit enrollment; dependent name; dependent relationship; retirement member number.

3.3 Staff shall not transcribe recorded values from these labels into email, chat, spreadsheets outside the system of record, or printed working documents.

4. THREE RULES FOR EVERY DAY

4.1 Ask whether you need the record for your assigned duty. If not, do not open it.

4.2 Never combine categories in a single working file. Category B and Category C shall not appear in the same document.

4.3 When a record's retention period has elapsed, route it to the Records Custodian. Do not dispose of it yourself.

5. Questions regarding classification, access exceptions, or holds are directed to the Records Custodian, Office of the Superintendent, extension listed in the internal directory.
