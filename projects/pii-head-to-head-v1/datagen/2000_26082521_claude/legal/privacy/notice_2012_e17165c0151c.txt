OPERATIONS STANDARD NO. RM-11
PERSONAL INFORMATION MAINTAINED IN MEMBER RECORDS:
CLASSIFICATION, ACCESS AUTHORITY, AND DISPOSITION

Issued by the Office of the Records Custodian
Effective 1 March 1997 — Supersedes Operations Bulletin 94-7

1. PURPOSE. This Standard is promulgated pursuant to the authority delegated to the Records Custodian by the Board of Directors and establishes the mandatory classification of personal information maintained by the Credit Union in any medium, whether paper, microfiche, magnetic tape, or on-line file.

2. CLASSIFICATION. All personal information shall be assigned to one of the following classes:

   Class I — Identification Data, comprising the fields Member Legal Name, Former Name, Date of Birth, Social Security Number, Mother's Maiden Name, Residential Address, Mailing Address, and Home Telephone Number.

   Class II — Relationship Data, comprising the fields Share Account Number, Share Draft Account Number, Certificate Number, Date of Admission to Membership, and Signature Card Reference.

   Class III — Credit Data, comprising the fields Employer Name, Occupation Code, Stated Annual Income, Consumer Reporting Agency Score, Loan Account Number, and Delinquency Status Code.

3. ACCESS. Class I and Class II shall be accessible to the Member Services Representative, the Branch Manager, and the Operations Supervisor. Class III shall be accessible solely to the Loan Officer, the Collections Officer, and the Vice President of Lending, and to the Internal Auditor in the discharge of audit duties.

4. RETENTION. Class I and Class II shall be retained for the duration of the membership relationship and for six (6) years following its termination. Class III shall be retained for six (6) years following satisfaction, charge-off, or denial, whichever occurs first.

5. DISPOSITION. Expired records shall be cross-cut shredded, and magnetic media degaussed, in the presence of two (2) employees, one of whom shall be the Records Custodian or a designee. A Certificate of Destruction identifying only the class, volume, and date shall be executed and retained permanently.

6. REVIEW. This Standard shall be reviewed annually by the Records Custodian and the General Counsel.
