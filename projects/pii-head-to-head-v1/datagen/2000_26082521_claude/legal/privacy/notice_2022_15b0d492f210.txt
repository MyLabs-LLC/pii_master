TRANSIT AUTHORITY INFORMATION GOVERNANCE MANUAL
PART 4: PERSONAL INFORMATION — CATEGORIES, CUSTODY, RETENTION AND DISPOSAL
Effective 1 July 2011. Review due 1 July 2014.
Approved by the Information Governance Committee.

4.1 PURPOSE AND STATUS
 • 4.1.1 This Part establishes the mandatory controls governing every category of personal information held by the Authority.
 • 4.1.2 Compliance is a condition of continued system access. Departures require written dispensation from the Privacy Officer.
 • 4.1.3 Divisional Heads shall confirm compliance in writing each financial year.

4.2 REGISTER OF CATEGORIES
 • 4.2.1 Workforce Administration
   – Fields: full legal name, former name(s), date of birth, gender, home address, personal mobile number, personal e-mail address, employee reference number, national insurance number, nationality, right-to-work document type and expiry date, next-of-kin name and telephone number.
 • 4.2.2 Remuneration and Deductions
   – Fields: grade, hourly rate, bank name, sort code, account number, building society roll number, tax reference, pension member number, court order reference, union subscription indicator.
 • 4.2.3 Occupational Health and Safety
   – Fields: employee reference number, medical practitioner name, fitness-for-duty classification, restriction code, date of assessment, injury description, absence start and end dates.
 • 4.2.4 Driver and Operator Licensing
   – Fields: name, driving licence number, licence categories, endorsement codes, medical certificate expiry date, competency assessment score.
 • 4.2.5 Customer Fare Accounts
   – Fields: title, name, postal address, contact telephone number, contact e-mail address, smartcard serial number, autoload payment instrument token, concession type, eligibility evidence reference.
 • 4.2.6 Concessionary Travel Eligibility
   – Fields: applicant name, date of birth, residential address, disability category, awarding body reference, photograph.
 • 4.2.7 Journey and Validation Records
   – Fields: smartcard serial number, vehicle or gateline identifier, location code, date and time of validation, fare deducted.
 • 4.2.8 Surveillance and Onboard Recording
   – Fields: camera identifier, location, recording start and end time, image and audio content, retrieval request reference.
 • 4.2.9 Incidents, Claims and Complaints
   – Fields: complainant or claimant name, contact details, correspondence content, witness names, injury details, settlement value.
 • 4.2.10 Recruitment and Unsuccessful Applicants
   – Fields: name, contact details, curriculum vitae content, referee name and contact details, interview scores.

4.3 AUTHORISED ACCESS
 • 4.3.1 Categories 4.2.1 to 4.2.3 — Human Resources Records Custodian; the individual's reporting manager; Occupational Health Adviser for 4.2.3 only.
 • 4.3.2 Category 4.2.4 — Depot Operations Manager and the Driver Standards Assessor.
 • 4.3.3 Categories 4.2.5 to 4.2.7 — Revenue Systems Administrator; Customer Services Team Leader (read-only, excluding payment instrument tokens).
 • 4.3.4 Category 4.2.8 — Security Control Room Supervisor; disclosure beyond that post only on a signed Police or Insurer Request Form.
 • 4.3.5 Category 4.2.9 — Claims Handler and Legal Services Manager.
 • 4.3.6 Category 4.2.10 — Recruitment Co-ordinator only.
 • 4.3.7 No holding may be accessed by the Information Technology Service Desk except under a logged break-glass authorisation.

4.4 RETENTION PERIODS
 • 4.4.1 Workforce Administration — six years after the end of employment.
 • 4.4.2 Remuneration and Deductions — seven years after the end of the relevant tax year.
 • 4.4.3 Occupational Health — forty years from date of assessment where exposure monitoring applies; otherwise six years.
 • 4.4.4 Driver and Operator Licensing — four years after licence surrender.
 • 4.4.5 Customer Fare Accounts — two years after account closure.
 • 4.4.6 Journey and Validation Records — fifty-six days, save where held under an active investigation hold.
 • 4.4.7 Surveillance Recordings — thirty-one days, save where lawfully exported.
 • 4.4.8 Incidents, Claims and Complaints — six years after final settlement.
 • 4.4.9 Recruitment (unsuccessful) — twelve months from notification of outcome.

4.5 DISPOSAL PROCEDURE
 • 4.5.1 The Records Custodian shall run a quarterly expiry report against each category.
 • 4.5.2 Records subject to litigation hold shall be extracted before any disposal run.
 • 4.5.3 Paper shall be destroyed by cross-cut shredder or by an accredited contractor under a Certificate of Destruction.
 • 4.5.4 Electronic records shall be securely overwritten; decommissioned media shall be degaussed and physically destroyed.
 • 4.5.5 Surveillance media shall be overwritten in situ; no manual deletion is permitted.
 • 4.5.6 Every disposal shall be recorded in the Destruction Register showing category, volume, method, date and authorising post.
 • 4.5.7 The Destruction Register is itself retained permanently.
