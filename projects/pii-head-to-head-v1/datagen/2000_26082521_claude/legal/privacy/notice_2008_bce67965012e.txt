STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
SOP RM-04.3 — Destruction of Personal Information Records
Owner: Office of the Records Custodian
Effective: November 1, 2016

A. APPLICABILITY
   This procedure governs the destruction of all records containing personal
   information in the categories listed at Section 3 of Policy RM-04, namely
   Identification Data, Contact Data, Financial Relationship Data, Credit
   Assessment Data, Payment Instrument Data, Related Party Data, Employment
   Data, and Electronic Access Data.

B. AUTHORITY TO INITIATE
   (i)   Only the Records Custodian may initiate a destruction event.
   (ii)  Only the Chief Compliance Officer may clear a destruction event.
   (iii) No other role, including Branch Managers and department heads, holds
         either authority.

C. QUARTERLY CYCLE
   (i)   The Records Custodian extracts the eligibility list from the systems
         of record on the first business day of each quarter.
   (ii)  The list identifies records by category, system, volume, and the
         retention trigger that has expired. It does not reproduce record
         content.
   (iii) The list is circulated for hold clearance and must be returned within
         ten business days.

D. EXECUTION
   (i)   Paper and microform: cross-cut shredding, either on premises with two
         staff present or by the bonded vendor under a signed chain of custody.
   (ii)  Magnetic and solid-state media: overwriting to the approved standard,
         or physical destruction where the media is failed or unreadable.
   (iii) Records held by a service provider: destruction performed by the
         provider under the confidentiality agreement, with written
         confirmation returned to the Records Custodian.
   (iv)  Deletion from an application interface is not destruction and does not
         satisfy this procedure.

E. DOCUMENTATION
   (i)   A Certificate of Destruction is completed for each event and records
         the date, categories, volume, method, vendor, and witnessing staff by
         role title.
   (ii)  Certificates are filed in the destruction register and retained ten
         years.
   (iii) Internal Audit samples the register annually and reports exceptions to
         the Board Audit Committee.

F. FAILURE TO COMPLY
   Destruction outside this procedure, or retention beyond the scheduled
   period without an approved exception, is reported as an incident under the
   Credit Union's incident response standard.
