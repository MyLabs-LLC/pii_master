METROPOLITAN REGIONAL TRANSIT AUTHORITY — RECORDS AND INFORMATION GOVERNANCE UNIT
PRACTICE NOTE RIG-04/PN-2: DEPARTMENTAL EXTRACTS AND SECONDARY HOLDINGS
Issued 22 August 2011 for the attention of all Division Heads and Designated Custodians.

It has come to the attention of this Unit that extracts drawn from the systems of record are, with some frequency, retained locally in spreadsheet form beyond the operational purpose for which they were drawn. Division Heads are reminded that a secondary holding attracts the whole of the obligation imposed by Standard RIG-04 upon the primary holding from which it derives, and that the retention clock applicable to such an extract is that of the originating category and not that of the departmental project which occasioned its creation. An extract containing the elements labelled Surname, Given Name(s), Fare Card Serial Number, or Tap-On Timestamp is, for all purposes of the Standard, a record of the corresponding category. Accordingly, every such extract shall be registered with the Designated Custodian within five working days of its creation, shall bear the Category Code and Extraction Purpose in its file properties, and shall be destroyed by secure overwriting immediately upon the conclusion of the purpose recited. Where the purpose has concluded and destruction has occurred, the responsible officer shall so notify the Records Custodian, who shall record the disposition in the Disposition Log in the ordinary manner. Non-registration will be treated as a control deficiency reportable to the Audit Committee.
