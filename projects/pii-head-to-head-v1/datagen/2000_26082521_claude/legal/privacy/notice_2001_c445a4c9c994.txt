NORTHVALE DRUG COMPANY — CORPORATE POLICY MANUAL
Section 14.3: Personal Information Inventory, Custodianship and Schedule
Approved by the Privacy Steering Committee, March 2016

14.3.1 General. The Company maintains the following inventory of personal information categories. Each category is assigned a single accountable custodian role. Custodianship carries responsibility for the accuracy of the entry, for the certification of the user population, and for the initiation of disposal at the close of the retention interval. Categories are described by their constituent field labels only; the Company does not reproduce field contents in policy documentation, training material or system documentation of any kind.

Category                          Custodian Role                    Retention Interval
Patient Identity                  Privacy Official                  10 years from last activity
Dispensing and Clinical           Director of Pharmacy Services     10 years from last fill
Prescriber Reference              Director of Pharmacy Services     Life of relationship
Third-Party Payer                 Manager, Reconciliation           7 years from adjudication
Payment Instrument                Manager, Payment Operations       Settlement cycle only
Loyalty and Preference            Manager, Loyalty Administration   Enrolment plus 36 months
Colleague and Payroll             Director, Human Resources         Employment plus 7 years
Surveillance and Access           Asset Protection Manager          90 days
Complaint and Incident            Privacy Official                  6 years from closure

14.3.2 Field Composition. The Patient Identity category consists of Patient Name, Date of Birth, Sex, Address, Telephone Number, Electronic Mail Address and Internal Patient Identifier. The Dispensing and Clinical category consists of Prescription Number, Fill Date, Drug Product Identifier, Quantity, Directions, Refill Authorisation, Allergy Notation and Counselling Record. The Third-Party Payer category consists of Payer Name, Member Identifier, Group Number and Person Code. The Payment Instrument category consists of Cardholder Name, Primary Account Number, Expiration Date and Authorisation Code. The Colleague and Payroll category consists of Colleague Identifier, Social Security Number, Compensation Data, Licence Number and Benefit Election. Where a system field is not enumerated above it is presumed to fall outside the inventory and may not be populated with personal information without amendment of this Section.

14.3.3 Access. Access is provisioned by role through the identity management system and reviewed by each custodian on a quarterly cycle. Requests for access outside the certified population are directed to the Privacy Official and are granted, if at all, for a defined period and a stated purpose, and are logged. Bulk extraction of any category to a local file, removable medium or personal account is prohibited without the written concurrence of both the custodian and the Information Security Officer.

14.3.4 Disposal. At the close of the retention interval the custodian issues a disposal instruction. Electronic records are purged by the platform owner and the purge is evidenced in the disposal register; paper records are destroyed by a bonded vendor against a certificate. Media leaving Company control is sanitised beforehand. Any record identified in a preservation notice is exempted from disposal until the notice is lifted in writing by the Office of General Counsel.
