STANDARD PRACTICE INSTRUCTION 22-B
MEMBER INFORMATION: CATEGORIES OF RECORD, PERMITTED ACCESS,
RETENTION PERIODS, AND PROCEDURE FOR DESTRUCTION

Adopted by resolution of the Board of Directors, 14 October 1998
Custodian of Record: the Records Custodian, Administrative Services Division
Distribution: Officers; Branch Managers; Department Heads (Controlled Copy)

ARTICLE I — AUTHORITY AND SCOPE

1.1 This Instruction is adopted in furtherance of the Credit Union's obligations under the Right to Financial Privacy Act, the Fair Credit Reporting Act, the Bank Secrecy Act and implementing regulations thereunder, and such further privacy obligations as may be imposed by Title V of the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act and the implementing regulations anticipated to be prescribed by the National Credit Union Administration.

1.2 This Instruction applies to every record containing personal information concerning a member, a former member, a joint owner, a beneficiary, a guarantor, a co-maker, or an applicant, without regard to the medium in which such record is maintained, and expressly including printed reports, ledger cards, signature cards, microfilm, microfiche, magnetic tape, optical platter, diskette, and records resident upon the host system or upon any workstation.

1.3 No employee, officer, director, committee member, or agent shall access, reproduce, transmit, remove from the premises, or disclose any personal information except as expressly permitted by this Instruction.

ARTICLE II — CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

2.1 Category A (Identifying Particulars) consists of the data fields Member Legal Name, Prior Legal Name, Date of Birth, Place of Birth, Taxpayer Identification Number, Driver's Licence Number, Street Address, Mailing Address, Home Telephone Number, Business Telephone Number, and Mother's Maiden Name.

2.2 Category B (Account Particulars) consists of the data fields Member Number, Share Suffix, Loan Suffix, Certificate Number, Automated Teller Card Number, Personal Identification Number Hash, Date of Membership, Signature Card Locator, and Account Status Code.

2.3 Category C (Credit and Underwriting Particulars) consists of the data fields Employer Name, Length of Employment, Position Held, Gross Monthly Income, Housing Payment, Consumer Report Identifier, Credit Score, Debt-to-Income Ratio, Collateral Description, Lien Position, and Adverse Action Reason Code.

2.4 Category D (Transaction Particulars) consists of the data fields Posting Date, Effective Date, Transaction Code, Amount, Payee or Originator Reference, Branch or Terminal Identifier, and Teller Identifier.

2.5 Category E (Regulatory and Investigative Particulars) consists of the data fields Currency Transaction Report Reference, Suspicious Activity Report Reference, Levy or Garnishment Reference, Subpoena Reference, and Fraud Case Number.

ARTICLE III — PERMITTED ACCESS

3.1 Access shall be granted upon the principle of least privilege, that is, only to the extent required for the performance of assigned duties, and shall be evidenced by a User Access Authorisation Form approved by the Department Head and countersigned by the Data Processing Manager.

3.2 The following access classes are hereby established:

   Access Class    Categories Permitted    Approving Authority
   ------------    --------------------    -------------------
   Teller          A (view), B (view), D   Branch Manager
   Member Services A, B, D                 Branch Manager
   Lending         A, B, C, D              Vice President of Lending
   Collections     A, B, C, D              Vice President of Lending
   Compliance      A, B, C, D, E           Compliance Officer
   Internal Audit  A, B, C, D, E (view)    Supervisory Committee
   Data Processing All (technical)         Data Processing Manager

3.3 Access to Category E shall in every instance be logged, and the log shall be reviewed monthly by the Compliance Officer. No employee shall disclose to any member, or to any other person, the existence of a record within Category E.

ARTICLE IV — RETENTION

4.1 Category A and Category B shall be retained during the relationship and for six (6) years after closure of the last account.

4.2 Category C shall be retained for twenty-five (25) months from the date of adverse action, or for six (6) years from satisfaction of the obligation, whichever period is longer.

4.3 Category D shall be retained for five (5) years, save that items subject to Bank Secrecy Act recordkeeping shall be retained for the period prescribed thereby.

4.4 Category E shall be retained for five (5) years from the date of filing and shall not be destroyed while any investigation remains open.

ARTICLE V — DESTRUCTION

5.1 Upon expiry of the applicable retention period, the Records Custodian shall prepare a Destruction Schedule bearing the columns Category, Volume Identifier, Retention Expiry Date, Method, and Witness Signature.

5.2 Paper shall be destroyed by cross-cut shredding or by pulping. Microfiche shall be incinerated or chemically destroyed. Magnetic and optical media shall be degaussed and thereafter physically destroyed; overwriting alone shall not be deemed sufficient.

5.3 Destruction shall be witnessed by two employees, and a Certificate of Destruction shall be executed and retained permanently. Where destruction is performed by a contracted vendor, the vendor shall be bound by written confidentiality undertaking and destruction shall be witnessed on the vendor's premises.

5.4 Notwithstanding any provision of this Instruction, destruction shall be suspended immediately upon notice of litigation, examination, subpoena, or investigation, and shall not resume until written release is issued by the General Counsel.
