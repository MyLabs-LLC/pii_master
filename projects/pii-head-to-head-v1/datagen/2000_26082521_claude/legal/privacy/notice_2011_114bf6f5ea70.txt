GULFLINE MUTUAL ASSURANCE
RECORDS AND PRIVACY DIRECTIVE 2021-03

SECTION I — IDENTIFICATION
Directive Title: Custody, Access and Destruction of Personal Information
Issuing Authority: Chief Privacy Officer
Concurrence: General Counsel; Chief Information Security Officer
Effective Date: 12 July 2021
Next Review: July 2022
Applies To: All employees, temporary staff, appointed agents and vendors

SECTION II — CATEGORIES IN CUSTODY

(a) Identity — legal name, date of birth, government identification number, taxpayer identification number.
(b) Locator — residential address, mailing address, telephone numbers, electronic mail address.
(c) Contract — policy number, coverage terms, endorsements, beneficiary designations.
(d) Payment — bank account details, card details, premium ledger, disbursement instructions.
(e) Risk — inspection reports, prior loss history, insurance score, driving record extract.
(f) Loss — claim number, incident narrative, medical and treatment records, expert reports, reserve history.
(g) Personnel — employment file, licensing records, disciplinary record, benefits elections.
(h) Technical — account credentials, session logs, telematics data, recorded interactions.

SECTION IV — RETENTION

1. Categories (a) through (c): seven years from contract termination.
2. Category (d): seven years from the transaction posting date.
3. Category (e): five years from the underwriting decision.
4. Category (f): ten years from final closure; longer where statute or a reservation of rights requires.
5. Category (g): seven years from separation.
6. Category (h): twenty-four months, save for recorded interactions at thirty-six months.

SECTION V — DESTRUCTION

1. No record may be destroyed outside the quarterly cycle administered by the Records Custodian.
2. Paper is destroyed by cross-cut shredding under witness; electronic records are purged to the standard adopted by the Chief Information Security Officer; portable media is physically destroyed.
3. Each destruction event is entered in the Destruction Log under the headings: Log Number; Category Letter; Record Series; Volume; Method; Witness Role; Vendor Certificate Reference; Date.
4. Destruction of any record subject to hold is prohibited. Holds are released only in writing by the General Counsel.
5. Failure to observe this Directive is a disciplinary matter and, for vendors, a material breach of the services agreement.
