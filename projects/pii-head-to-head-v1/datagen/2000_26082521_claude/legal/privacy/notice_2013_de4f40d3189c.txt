CALDERMOOR LOGISTICS LIMITED
RECORDS HANDLING STANDARD RHS-2 — PERSONAL INFORMATION
Approved 22 June 2021 by the Information Governance Group
Owner: Records Custodian | Version 2.0 | Next review June 2022

A. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This standard identifies each category of personal information processed by the company, the field labels recorded within each category, the role permitted to access it and the period for which it is retained. It applies to all operating depots, the central office and any third party processing on the company's instruction.

C. ACCESS

The role named in column three holds standing access to the whole category. The Records Custodian holds standing read access to all categories for audit purposes only. All other access is by exception and must be authorised in writing before it is granted. Exception access is time-limited to the stated purpose and expires automatically after 30 days unless renewed. System access rights are reconciled against this register each quarter; rights not supported by the register are revoked without notice to the holder.

D. RETENTION

Retention periods run from the trigger stated and are enforced by the holding system where technically possible and by scheduled manual review where not. A category may be held beyond its stated period only under a legal hold recorded by the Records Custodian. Holds are reviewed every six months and released in writing.

E. DISPOSAL

On expiry the Records Custodian issues a disposal instruction identifying the category, the medium and the volume. Paper is destroyed by cross-cut shredding under supervision. Electronic records are erased at primary, replica and backup level, and portable media are physically destroyed. Disposal is witnessed by a second officer of at least supervisory grade. The destruction log records the category reference, the volume, the date of disposal, the method applied, the role of the operative, the role of the witness and the certificate reference. The log is retained for six years and is subject to internal audit.
