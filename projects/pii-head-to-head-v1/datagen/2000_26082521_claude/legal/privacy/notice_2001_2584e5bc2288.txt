DISTRICT RECORDS OFFICE
STANDARD RH-06: HANDLING OF PERSONAL INFORMATION IN DISTRICT SYSTEMS
Effective 1 August 2004. Applies to all central office and building staff.

1. INVENTORY — complete annually by the system owner

  [ ] Category of information held is named on the inventory sheet
  [ ] Field labels are listed (identity fields, contact fields, benefit status fields, health alert fields, payroll and withholding fields, account fields for direct deposit)
  [ ] Source of the information is named
  [ ] System of record is named, so that duplicates can be identified and retired
  [ ] Sheet is signed by the system owner and filed with the Records Custodian

2. ACCESS CONTROL

  [ ] Access is granted by role, not by individual request
  [ ] Role assignments are reviewed each August and each January
  [ ] Health alert fields and special education fields are suppressed from general staff views
  [ ] Payroll fields, withholding fields, and account fields are visible to Payroll staff only
  [ ] Accounts are disabled the same working day a staff member separates
  [ ] Shared logins are prohibited without exception
  [ ] Contractors receive access only under a signed confidentiality undertaking, time-limited to the engagement

3. EXTRACTS AND REPORTS

  [ ] Extracts containing identity or contact fields require the Records Custodian's approval
  [ ] Reports for research or planning use aggregate counts; cells below ten are suppressed
  [ ] Extracts are deleted within 30 days of the purpose being met
  [ ] No extract is carried off site on removable media without encryption and written authorisation
  [ ] Every extract is entered in the extract register

4. RETENTION

  [ ] Transcript and permanent record — indefinite
  [ ] Cumulative folder contents — 5 years after exit
  [ ] Special education file — 5 years after last service
  [ ] Conduct records — 3 years
  [ ] Benefit and eligibility records — 3 years after programme year
  [ ] Personnel and payroll — 7 years after separation
  [ ] Application materials of candidates not hired — 2 years
  [ ] System backups — 90 days rolling, then overwritten

5. DISPOSAL

  [ ] Destruction list issued semi-annually by the Records Custodian
  [ ] Litigation holds and open requests checked and cleared before any deletion
  [ ] Records purged from the system of record and from every downstream copy identified in the inventory
  [ ] Paper cross-cut shredded; tape degaussed; disks wiped and physically destroyed before surplus
  [ ] Two witnesses present; certificate of destruction completed and retained 7 years
  [ ] Backup expiry noted on the certificate where a purge depends on the rolling window

Questions on this standard are directed to the Records Custodian before, not after, a record is created, copied, or released.
