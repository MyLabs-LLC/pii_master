POLICY RM-2004-07 — PROCEDURAL ANNEX B
DISCLOSURE, TRANSPORT AND THIRD-PARTY HANDLING

1. Disclosure to persons outside the Credit Union.

    (a) No category of personal information shall be disclosed to any person outside the Credit Union except (i) with the written and dated authorisation of the member, (ii) to a service provider engaged under a written contract containing the confidentiality and safeguards clauses approved by counsel, (iii) pursuant to a subpoena, summons, or lawful demand of a supervisory authority, or (iv) as otherwise permitted by the exceptions set out in the Credit Union's annual privacy notice.

    (b) Every disclosure made under sub-clause (a)(iii) shall be referred to the Compliance Officer before any record is produced. Under no circumstances shall a branch employee respond directly to such a demand, nor confirm or deny the existence of a relationship with the person named in it.

    (c) A record of every disclosure made under sub-clause (a) shall be entered in the Disclosure Log, stating the category disclosed, the authority relied upon, the recipient's organisation, the date and the authorising officer.

2. Transport of records between premises.

    (a) Records in transit between branches, to the Central Operations facility, or to off-site storage shall travel in a sealed and numbered container, accompanied by a manifest stating the category designations and the container count.

    (b) Transport shall be effected by a bonded courier under contract or by a Credit Union officer. Records shall not be left in an unattended vehicle, taken to a private residence, or removed from Credit Union control for any purpose not authorised in advance by the Records Custodian in writing.

3. Third-party destruction vendors.

    (a) Where destruction is performed by a contracted vendor rather than on Credit Union premises, the vendor shall be selected following due diligence documented by the Compliance Officer, shall be bonded and insured, and shall permit unannounced observation of the destruction process by a Credit Union officer.

    (b) The vendor shall furnish a Certificate of Destruction within five business days of collection. Custody of the records passes to the vendor only upon signature of the manifest by both parties, and the Credit Union's obligations under this Annex are not discharged by that transfer.

4. Reporting of loss.

    (a) Any employee who becomes aware that a record has been lost, misdirected, accessed without authorisation or destroyed otherwise than in accordance with this Policy shall report the circumstances to the Compliance Officer on the same business day, whether or not the employee believes the matter to be trivial. No employee shall be subject to adverse treatment by reason of making such a report in good faith.
