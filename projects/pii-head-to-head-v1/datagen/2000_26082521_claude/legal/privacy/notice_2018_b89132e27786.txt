CASCADE TITLE GUARANTY & ESCROW, INC.
RECORDS MANAGEMENT MANUAL — SECTION 9

INFORMATION CATEGORY SCHEDULE, ACCESS AUTHORITY AND DISPOSITION

Revision 4. Issued by the Records Custodian under authority of the Executive Committee, dated 12 February 1999. Prior revisions withdrawn and to be destroyed.

1. GENERAL

1.1 Every item of personal information held by the Company shall be assigned to a category set out in the Schedule at Clause 4. Information not falling within a listed category shall be referred to the Records Custodian for classification before filing.

1.2 The Schedule states the minimum retention. It does not authorise retention beyond that period, and files shall not be held indefinitely for convenience.

2. ACCESS AUTHORITY

2.1 The access roles named in the Schedule are exhaustive. A role confers access only in respect of files assigned to that officer.

2.2 Every instance of access by Internal Audit, by an underwriter examiner, or by any person outside the named roles shall be entered in the Access Register showing: Date of Access; File Number; Category Accessed; Requesting Role; Approving Officer; Purpose Code.

3. DISPOSITION AUTHORITY

3.1 Disposition shall not commence without a Destruction Schedule bearing the counter-signature of an officer.

3.2 A Certificate of Destruction shall be raised for each Destruction Schedule and shall be retained permanently in the Records Custodian's binder.

5. EXCEPTIONS

5.1 Records under legal hold, and records of a transaction in which the Company has received notice of claim, are suspended from disposition until released in writing by Underwriting Counsel.

5.2 Category D-1 is not subject to routine disposition and shall be transferred to the permanent vault upon closure of the matter.
