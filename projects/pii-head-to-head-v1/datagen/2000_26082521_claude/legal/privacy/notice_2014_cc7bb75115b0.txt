SUBJECT: Summary of the Records Handling Standard

The full standard is held in the depot office. This summary is displayed for
the information of all staff.

What we hold about you:

  - Identification and contact details (name, home address, telephone number,
    date of birth, national insurance number, next of kin details)
  - Licence and qualification details (licence number, categories, expiry
    date, endorsements, training dates)
  - Pay and banking details (grade, rate, sort code, account number, tax code)
  - Working time and absence details (hours, rest periods, absence dates and
    reason codes)
  - Medical fitness details (examination date, outcome, any restriction)
  - Disciplinary and grievance details (dates, stage, outcome)

Who may see it:

  - Your Depot Manager sees identification, working time and disciplinary
    details for staff they supervise.
  - The Personnel Officer and the Data Protection Coordinator see all
    categories.
  - The Payroll Officer sees pay and banking details only.
  - Medical fitness details are seen by the Personnel Officer and the company
    doctor. Your manager is told only whether you are fit and what, if any,
    restriction applies.

How long we keep it:

  Pay, identification and working time records — six years after you leave.
  Licence and training records — four years after you leave.
  Medical fitness records — six years, or forty years where exposure applies.
  Disciplinary warnings — removed at the expiry date stated in the warning.

How it is destroyed:

  All of the above is shredded, or the disk destroyed, by the Records
  Custodian. It is never put in ordinary waste.

If you wish to see what is held about you, write to the Data Protection
Coordinator at Head Office. A response will be given within forty days.
