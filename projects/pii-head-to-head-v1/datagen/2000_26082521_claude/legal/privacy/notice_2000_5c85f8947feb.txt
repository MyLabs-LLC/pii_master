RECORDS AND INFORMATION PRIVACY STANDARD
Policy RM-14 · Office of the Compliance Officer
Effective 3 March 1997 · Supersedes RM-11 (September 1993)
Review cycle: biennial

1. This Standard applies to all information concerning members, joint owners, guarantors, applicants and employees held by the Credit Union in any medium whatsoever, including original paper instruments, microfiche, host-system printouts, magnetic cartridge and diskette. It applies at the main office, at every branch office, and at any location where records are held on the Credit Union's behalf by a servicer under written agreement.

2. Information is classified in four categories. Category A, Identification Data, comprises the fields MEMBER-NO, SURNAME, GIVEN-NAME, DOB, TIN, ID-DOC-TYPE and ID-DOC-EXPIRY. Category B, Relationship Data, comprises SHARE-ID, LOAN-ID, OPEN-DATE, CLOSE-DATE, BALANCE-CURR and the signature card reference SIG-CARD-SEQ. Category C, Underwriting and Employment Data, comprises EMPLOYER, POSITION, INCOME-GROSS-MO, BUREAU-FILE-NO, BUREAU-SCORE, DEBT-RATIO and ADVERSE-ACTION-CODE. Category D, Authentication Data, comprises PIN-OFFSET, AUDIO-RESPONSE-CODE, VERIFICATION-PHRASE and TELLER-OVERRIDE-KEY.

3. Access to Category A and Category B is granted by profile to the teller line, the member services desk and the Branch Manager. Category C is restricted to the Lending Department, the Collections Officer and the Internal Auditor. Category D is accessible to no employee in cleartext; the Security Administrator may reset but may not display these fields. All profiles are reviewed each quarter by the Records Custodian, who reports exceptions to the Supervisory Committee.

4. Retention runs from the date of account closure or final disposition: Category A and B, seven years; Category C, twenty-five months for declined applications and seven years for booked loans; Category D, purged at closure.

5. Disposal is by cross-cut shredding or bonded incineration for paper and microfiche, and by degaussing for magnetic media, in each case witnessed by two officers and entered in the Destruction Register.
