REGIONAL TRANSIT AUTHORITY
OFFICE OF THE GENERAL COUNSEL — RECORDS MANAGEMENT PROGRAMME

ADMINISTRATIVE STANDARD AS-114
PERSONAL INFORMATION HOLDINGS: CATEGORIES, CUSTODY, RETENTION AND DISPOSAL
Issued: March 2004. Supersedes AS-114 (Rev. 3, 1998). Standard owner: the Records Custodian.

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This Standard establishes the categories of personal information maintained by the Authority in the course of its statutory functions, identifies the classes of personnel authorised to access each category, fixes the minimum and maximum retention periods applicable to each, and prescribes the manner of disposal at the expiry of those periods. It applies to information held in any medium, whether in operational systems, departmental working files, microform, or backup media, and binds all divisions, all contracted service providers acting on the Authority's behalf, and all seconded personnel. It does not extend to records of corporate finance, procurement, or engineering that contain no personal information.

2. CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

Category A — Employment and payroll records. This category comprises information created or received in connection with the employment relationship. The data elements maintained include Employee Number, Position Classification, Bargaining Unit Code, Date of Hire, Pay Grade, Deduction Category, Leave Entitlement Type, Leave Balance, Disciplinary Action Class, and Emergency Contact Designation. Custody rests with the Director of Human Resources.

Category B — Operator qualification and fitness-for-duty records. The data elements maintained include Licence Class Held, Endorsement Type, Medical Examiner Certification Status, Re-examination Interval, Restriction Code, Refresher Training Completion Status, and Testing Programme Participation Indicator. Custody rests with the Superintendent of Transportation.

Category C — Customer and fare media records. The data elements maintained include Fare Media Serial Reference, Account Status Code, Concession Category, Eligibility Verification Status, Transaction Timestamp, Boarding Location Code, Autoload Authorisation Indicator, and Correspondence Reference. Custody rests with the Manager of Revenue Systems.

Category D — Incident, claim, and security records. The data elements maintained include Incident Reference Number, Party Role Designation, Injury Classification, Statement Status, Vehicle Assignment Identifier, Route and Run Identifier, and Recorded Image Retrieval Reference. Custody rests with the Chief of Transit Police and, for civil matters, the General Counsel.

Category E — Applicant and recruitment records. The data elements maintained include Requisition Number, Applicant Reference Number, Qualification Screening Outcome, Interview Panel Score, and Disposition Code. Custody rests with the Director of Human Resources.

Category F — Accessible services eligibility records. The data elements maintained include Eligibility Determination Category, Functional Assessment Outcome Code, Certification Expiry Interval, Attendant Authorisation Indicator, and Trip Booking Reference. Custody rests with the Manager of Accessible Services.

3. ACCESS

Access to each category is restricted to the custodian named in Section 2, to personnel within that custodian's division whose assigned duties require access, and to the Records Custodian for the purpose of programme audit. Access by any other division requires the written authorisation of the custodian, recorded on the access register. Categories B and F are further restricted: fitness and functional assessment elements are segregated from general personnel and service files and are not visible to supervisory staff, who receive only the derived Restriction Code or Eligibility Determination Category. Contracted service providers receive access only to the extent specified in the confidentiality schedule of their agreement, and only for the term of that agreement.

4. RETENTION

Category A is retained for the term of employment plus seven years. Category B is retained for five years from the date of the superseding certification. Category C is retained for twenty-four months from the last transaction posted to the account, save that correspondence is retained for three years. Category D is retained for seven years from closure, or, where litigation is pending or reasonably anticipated, until released in writing by the General Counsel. Category E is retained for three years from the disposition date. Category F is retained for the certification term plus three years.

5. DISPOSAL

At expiry, the custodian shall submit the disposal list to the Records Custodian for certification. Paper is cross-cut shredded or pulped under supervision; magnetic media are degaussed and physically destroyed; optical media are shredded. Overwriting alone does not satisfy this Standard. A Certificate of Destruction bearing the record series, the volume disposed, the method, the date, and the signature of the witnessing officer is retained permanently.
