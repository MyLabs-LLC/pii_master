INFORMATION GOVERNANCE STANDARD IG-04
PERSONAL DATA CATEGORY AND RETENTION SCHEDULE
Issuing Authority: Office of the Records Custodian
Version 2.1 — Effective 1 April 2016 — Next Review 1 April 2018
Classification: Internal Use Only — Not for Onward Circulation

The following provisions shall be observed by all depots, transport offices and shared service functions of the Company without exception or local variation.

- The categories of personal information held by the Company, and no others, are hereby declared to be: (i) Employee Identification Particulars; (ii) Driver Licensing and Entitlement Records; (iii) Payroll and Statutory Deduction Records; (iv) Occupational Health Surveillance Outcomes; (v) Emergency Contact Particulars; (vi) Vehicle Telematics and Positional Records; (vii) Consignee and Consignor Contact Particulars; (viii) Disciplinary and Grievance Case Files.
- Each category shall be recorded against the field labels prescribed in Appendix A, being, inter alia: Surname; Given Name; Employee Number; Date of Birth; National Insurance Number; Home Address Line 1; Postcode; Contact Telephone Number; Bank Sort Code; Bank Account Number; Licence Number; Licence Expiry Date; Digital Tachograph Card Number.
- Access to the categories at (i) to (v) inclusive is restricted to the Head of Human Resources, the Payroll Supervisor and the Records Custodian.
- Access to the categories at (vi) and (vii) is restricted to the Fleet Compliance Manager and the duty Traffic Planner.
- Retention shall be six years from cessation of engagement, save that Occupational Health Surveillance Outcomes shall be retained forty years from date of last examination and Vehicle Telematics and Positional Records twelve months from date of capture.
- Disposal shall be effected by cross-cut shredding to DIN 66399 security level P-4, witnessed by two authorised officers, and evidenced by a Certificate of Destruction retained in the Disposal Register.
