The Depot Records Officer shall verify each of the following and shall record any exception in the Exception Log, together with the remedial action proposed and the date by which it shall be completed.

[ ] Every personal information holding at this location falls within one of the declared categories, namely Employee Identification Particulars, Driver Licensing and Entitlement Records, Payroll and Statutory Deduction Records, Occupational Health Surveillance Outcomes, Emergency Contact Particulars, Vehicle Telematics and Positional Records, Consignee and Consignor Contact Particulars, or Disciplinary and Grievance Case Files.

[ ] The field labels in use correspond to those prescribed in the master schedule and have not been locally extended, in particular: Employee Number; Surname; Given Name; Date of Birth; National Insurance Number; Home Address; Postcode; Mobile Telephone Number; Next of Kin Name; Next of Kin Telephone Number; Licence Number; Bank Account Number.

[ ] Access permissions have been reconciled against the establishment list, and no user retains access to a category otherwise than by reason of their current role.

[ ] Leavers have been removed from all category permissions within five working days of their last day of service.

[ ] All holdings which have passed their prescribed retention period have been identified, listed and marked for destruction.

[ ] Retention periods applied at this location correspond to those in the master schedule: six years for personnel and payroll; forty years for health surveillance; twelve months for tachograph and telematics; thirty-one days for access control event logs.

[ ] Confidential waste consoles are locked, sited within a controlled area, and emptied by the approved destruction contractor at intervals not exceeding fourteen days.

[ ] A Certificate of Destruction has been obtained for every collection and filed in the Disposal Register.
