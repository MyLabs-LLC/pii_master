METROPOLITAN TRANSIT AUTHORITY

STANDARD RIM-STD-12
HANDLING OF PERSONAL INFORMATION: ACCESS, RETENTION AND DISPOSAL

Issue 3.  Effective 1 July 1997.  Supersedes Issue 2 in full.
Authority: Corporate Services Instruction 9/96.
Custodian of this standard: Manager, Records and Information Management.

1.  DEFINITIONS

1.1  "Personal information" means information about an individual whose identity is apparent, or can reasonably be ascertained, from the information or from the information together with other holdings of the Authority.
1.2  "Category" means a class of holding listed in the Schedule at clause 5.
1.3  "Custodian" means the officer nominated under RIM-STD-11 clause 4.
1.4  "Access level" means the level assigned in the Authorised Access Schedule, RIM-STD-12 Attachment B.

2.  GENERAL REQUIREMENTS

2.1  A holding of personal information shall not be created, acquired or maintained except for a function of the Authority set out in its enabling legislation or in an approved corporate plan.
2.2  Each holding shall be entered in the Register against one category only. Where a file contains material falling in more than one category, it shall be classed at the more restrictive access level.
2.3  Access shall be granted on the basis of duties performed, not of grade or seniority.
2.4  Personal information shall not be released outside the Authority except under clause 6.

3.  ACCESS CONTROL

3.1  The Custodian shall maintain a written list of officers holding access to each category, and shall review that list at not more than six-monthly intervals.
3.2  Removal of access on transfer, secondment or separation is effective from the last day of duty and shall not await the review cycle.
3.3  Requests for access outside the standing list shall be made in writing to the Custodian and shall state the function to which the access relates. Approvals are valid for the period stated and for no longer.

4.  RETENTION

4.1  Minimum retention periods are stated in the Schedule. A record shall not be destroyed before the minimum period has run.
4.2  A record subject to current or foreshadowed legal proceedings, audit, investigation or a pending request for access by the individual concerned shall be quarantined and shall not be destroyed while the matter is open, notwithstanding clause 4.1.
4.3  Retention beyond the stated maximum requires written approval of the Branch Manager and endorsement by the Branch Privacy Officer.

6.  DISCLOSURE OUTSIDE THE AUTHORITY

6.1  Disclosure is permitted only where required by law, where authorised in writing by the individual concerned, or on the written direction of the Director of Corporate Services.
6.2  Every disclosure shall be entered in the Disclosure Log maintained by the Custodian, recording the category, the recipient class, the ground relied on, and the date.

7.  DISPOSAL

Disposal is governed by RIM-PROC-12A. No holding listed in the Schedule may be destroyed otherwise than under that procedure.
