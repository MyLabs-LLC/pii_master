MEMORANDUM

TO:        Purchasing; Accounts Payable; Information Systems
FROM:      Records Custodian
DATE:      December 2011
SUBJECT:   Standard PRS-04, Supplement 3 — Personal Information in the Custody of Contracted Service Providers

Scope. This Supplement applies wherever a contracted service provider creates, receives, stores, transmits or destroys personal information on the Utility's behalf. Current arrangements of this kind include print and mail production, lockbox and payment processing, collection agency services, off-site records storage, records destruction, and hosted software services.

Categories transferred. The contract file must identify, by category and by data element, exactly what is transferred. Transfers are limited to the elements the provider requires to perform the service. Category B payment card elements are transferred only to providers holding current card industry attestation, and only in the truncated form permitted by that attestation.

Contract requirements. Every agreement under which personal information is transferred must contain: a statement that the Utility retains ownership of the information; a prohibition on use for any purpose other than performance of the contract; a prohibition on further disclosure without the Utility's prior written consent; a requirement to notify the Records Custodian within twenty-four hours of any suspected unauthorised access, loss or disclosure; a right of the Utility to audit the provider's handling on reasonable notice; a defined retention period not exceeding that of the parent category; and a requirement to return or destroy the information on termination and to certify the same in writing.

Access. Provider personnel are granted access by the provider under an access matrix supplied to the Records Custodian before the transfer commences and updated on change. Where a provider connects to a Utility system, accounts are individually named, are not shared, and are disabled by the Information Systems Manager within one business day of the provider's notice that a person no longer requires access.

Retention and disposal. The provider destroys the information at the earlier of the contractual retention period or the parent category retention period, using the methods specified in Section 6 of the parent Standard. A Certificate of Destruction naming the categories, volumes, methods and dates is furnished to the Records Custodian. Records held in off-site storage are destroyed only on written instruction from the Records Custodian; providers are not authorised to destroy on their own schedule.

Register. Purchasing maintains a register of all such arrangements, recording the provider, the service, the categories and elements transferred, the contract retention period, the date of the most recent access matrix, and the date of the most recent destruction certificate. The register is furnished to the Records Custodian each June and December and is subject to review by the Internal Auditor.
