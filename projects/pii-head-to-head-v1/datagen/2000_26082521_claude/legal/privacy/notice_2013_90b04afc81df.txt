PERSONAL DATA ACCESS AND RETENTION SCHEDULE

Doc No. DP-SCH-07 | Rev. B | Approved 19 October 2021 | Custodian: Records Custodian

CLAUSE 1 — APPLICATION
This Schedule is subordinate to the Personal Information Handling Standard and is to be read with it. Where a conflict arises, the Standard prevails.

CLAUSE 2 — CATEGORY REGISTER
The following categories are recognised. No further category may be created without written approval of the Data Protection Lead.

C1  Worker Identity Record — Full Name; Date of Birth; Home Address; Personal Telephone Number; Personal Email Address; National Insurance Number
C2  Engagement Record — Candidate Reference; Contract Type; Assignment Start Date; Assignment End Date; Client Account Code; Job Title
C3  Remuneration Record — Payroll Number; Bank Sort Code; Bank Account Number; Tax Code; Gross Pay; Deductions; Pension Scheme Reference
C4  Eligibility Record — Nationality; Document Type; Document Number; Document Expiry Date; Check Date
C5  Competence Record — Qualification Title; Awarding Body; Certificate Number; Expiry Date; Training Completion Date
C6  Health Record — Fitness Declaration Status; Occupational Health Referral Reference; Adjustment Requirement; Absence Reason Code
C7  Disclosure Record — Certificate Number; Issue Date; Outcome Code; Risk Assessment Reference
C8  Case Record — Case Reference; Case Type; Opened Date; Closed Date; Outcome Code

CLAUSE 4 — RECORDS CUSTODIAN
The Records Custodian holds no content access. Custodian permissions extend to category label, file location, retention status and disposal status only.

CLAUSE 5 — RETENTION
C1, C2, C3, C5 — six years from end of final engagement or end of relevant tax year, whichever is later.
C4 — two years from end of final engagement.
C6 — two years from date of collection; sickness absence fields purged at twelve months.
C7 — six months from outcome decision, save where a longer period is required by the client contract and approved in writing.
C8 — six years from closed date.

CLAUSE 6 — REVIEW AND DISPOSAL
6.1 A retention review is run on the first working day of each quarter.
6.2 Records reaching end of retention are placed in the disposal queue and destroyed within 30 days.
6.3 Destruction is by cross-cut shredding or certified electronic erasure. Deletion is confirmed against live systems, archive stores and backup media.
6.4 Objection, erasure or restriction requests suspend automatic disposal pending determination by the Data Protection Lead.
