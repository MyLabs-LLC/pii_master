REDBANK COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
INTERNAL STANDARD 21-11: PERSONAL INFORMATION — HOLDINGS, ACCESS, RETENTION, DISPOSAL
Issued: 04 November 2021

WHAT IS HELD
  - Identity data
      - Fields held: Name; Former Name; Date of Birth; Sex; Gender; Race; Ethnicity; Primary Language.
  - Identifier data
      - Fields held: Social Security Number; Medical Record Number; Client Case Number; Insurance Member Number; Vital Record File Number.
  - Contact data
      - Fields held: Home Address; Mailing Address; Telephone Number; Email Address; Emergency Contact.
  - Health data
      - Fields held: Diagnosis; Procedure; Immunisation; Laboratory Result; Prescription; Clinical Narrative; Service Date.
  - Financial data
      - Fields held: Income; Household Composition; Fee Determination; Payer; Account Number.
  - Sensitive-subset data
      - Fields held: Communicable Disease Status; HIV-Related Information; Substance Use Treatment Record; Behavioural Health Record; Reproductive Health Service Indicator.
  - Workforce data
      - Fields held: Employee Identifier; Position; Licensure; Screening Result; Disciplinary Action.

WHO MAY ACCESS
  - Registration and eligibility staff — identity, identifier, contact, financial data for clients of their assigned clinic.
  - Licensed clinical staff — health data for clients under their care.
  - Sensitive-subset data — designated programme staff only; designation in writing by the Programme Manager; access reviewed monthly.
  - Workforce data — Human Resources Manager and the employee's Division Director.
  - Privacy Officer and Internal Auditor — read-only across all categories for audit purposes.
  - Information Technology staff — administrative access to systems, not to record content; content access requires a ticketed, time-boxed grant.

HOW LONG IT IS KEPT
  - Clinical series — 10 years from last service; minors until age 22.
  - Sensitive-subset series — 25 years from case closure.
  - Financial series — 7 years from fiscal year close.
  - Workforce series — 7 years from separation; exposure records 30 years.
  - Audit and access logs — 6 years.
  - Registry entries — permanent.

HOW IT IS DESTROYED
  - The Records Custodian issues a disposition list each January and July.
  - Programme managers confirm no hold, audit, or pending request attaches.
  - The Privacy Officer signs the disposition authorisation.
  - Paper is cross-cut shredded; electronic media is purged or physically destroyed; backups age out on the standard rotation.
  - A certificate of destruction is filed and kept permanently. It records series, category, volume, method, date, and certifying role title.
