MERIDIAN COMMUNITY CREDIT UNION
STANDARD 7.3 — CLASSIFICATION AND DISPOSITION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION
Issue 2, dated 12 July 2004. Review due July 2006.

1. PURPOSE

To establish a single classification of the personal information held by the credit union, to fix the custodial responsibility for each class, to state the minimum period for which each class is preserved, and to prescribe the method by which each class is destroyed at the end of that period.

2. AUTHORITY

This standard is issued by the Compliance Officer under delegated authority of the Board of Directors. It gives effect to the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act, Regulation P, NCUA Part 748 and Part 749, the Bank Secrecy Act and its implementing rules, and the record disposal requirements of the Fair and Accurate Credit Transactions Act.

3. DEFINITIONS

"Personal information" means information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person that is held in a system of record of the credit union, whether that person is a member, a former member, an applicant, a joint owner, an authorized signer, a beneficiary, a guarantor, or an employee. "System of record" means any file, application, or repository from which such information is retrieved by an identifier assigned to the person. "Access group" means the set of positions to which the Security Administrator has assigned entitlement to a system of record.

SEP = date of separation from employment.

5. ACCESS CONTROL

Entitlement to a class is granted only to the access group named above. Requests for entitlement outside the named group are submitted to the Compliance Officer and are approved only where a documented business need exists and the requester's supervisor concurs in writing. Entitlement lists are produced quarterly by the Security Administrator and certified by each department head within ten business days of issue.

6. RETENTION

The periods stated are minimums. No class may be destroyed before the stated period elapses. Where two periods apply to the same physical file, the longer governs the entire file.

7. DISPOSITION

D1 Cross-cut shred at the branch. D2 Locked collection bin, bonded vendor, certificate of destruction returned to the Records Custodian. D3 Degauss and destroy media. D4 Retain permanently in the restricted vault; no disposal is authorized.

8. HOLDS

A hold issued by the Compliance Officer, by counsel, or by an examiner suspends all disposition for the named class until the hold is released in writing.

9. EXCEPTIONS

Exceptions are granted only by the Compliance Officer and are recorded in the exception register maintained by the Records Custodian.
