COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT
RECORDS MANAGEMENT PROCEDURE 22-B — DISPOSAL CHECKLIST

To be completed by the Divisional Records Officer for every disposal lot. Return the completed sheet to the Records Custodian within five working days of destruction.

[ ] Disposal lot number assigned from the Disposal Register.
[ ] Category of personal information identified against Standard 22-A. Categories carried on this lot recorded in the field marked Category Code.
[ ] Retention period confirmed as expired for every file in the lot. Files with differing expiry dates are not to be lotted together.
[ ] Legal hold search completed against the Litigation Hold Index; the fields checked are Case Reference, Category Code, and Hold Expiry.
[ ] Subject access requests outstanding against any file in the lot checked and cleared.
[ ] Lot inspected item by item; extracts, working copies, and duplicate microfiche located and added.
[ ] Method of destruction selected: cross-cut shredding; incineration; three-pass overwrite; carrier destruction.
[ ] Destruction witnessed by a second officer of the Division.
[ ] Certificate of Destruction raised. Fields completed: Lot Number; Category Code; Volume; Method; Date of Destruction; Officer; Witnessing Officer.
[ ] Index cards and finding aids annotated "destroyed under lot number", not erased.
[ ] Contracted destruction agent, where used, has returned its own certificate before the lot is signed off.
[ ] Completed sheet and certificate forwarded to the Records Custodian for filing.

Certificates are retained six years. Incomplete sheets are returned to the Division and the disposal is treated as unauthorised.
