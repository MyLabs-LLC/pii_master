BILLING OFFICE BULLETIN NO. 22
DATE: August 4, 1998
ISSUED BY: Records Custodian
TO: Patient Accounting Staff — Post at Each Workstation

SUBJECT: Reminder — Information Categories and Their Handling Rules

The following categories are held in this office. Handle each according to the rule beside it.

* Demographic — Patient Name, Date of Birth, Social Security Number, Medical Record Number. Viewable by all billing staff. Retain ten years past account closure.

* Address and Telephone — Mailing Address, Home Telephone, Employer Name. Viewable by all billing staff. Retain with the account.

* Guarantor — Guarantor Name, Relationship, Address, Telephone. Viewable by Collection Specialists and Financial Counselors. Retain with the account.

* Insurance — Payer Name, Group Number, Subscriber Identification Number, Certification Number. Viewable by Follow-Up Representatives. Retain with the account.

* Payment Instrument — Cardholder Name, Card Number, Expiration Date, Check Number, Bank Routing Number. Viewable by Cashiers only. Purge at eighteen months.

* Hardship — Household Income, Household Size, Determination Code. Viewable by Financial Counselors only. Retain seven years, then destroy.

Nothing in these categories leaves the department without the Supervisor's written approval. Screen prints are shredded at the cross-cut shredder in the file room before the end of shift; they are not placed in wastebaskets or recycling bins. Sign the Destruction Log when you shred.

Questions go to the Records Custodian.
