WESTBROOK COUNTY SCHOOLS
CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION — DEPARTMENT CHECKLIST
Revision 2, 2004. To be worked through by each department head at the start of the school year.

WHAT WE HOLD
- Identity fields for pupils and staff
- Contact fields for pupils, guardians, and staff
- Academic history and assessment results
- Special education referral, eligibility, and service documentation
- Health category items, including the immunisation record and the health alert code
- Benefit eligibility indicators
- Conduct and investigation files
- Personnel, evaluation, payroll, withholding, and direct deposit account fields
- Background check dispositions

WHO GETS TO SEE IT
- Staff see only the categories their duties require, for only the individuals their duties cover
- Health and special education categories move on a named-list basis; the list is signed by the principal and refreshed each year
- Payroll and personnel categories stay within Human Resources and Payroll
- Outside release happens on written consent, on a logged subpoena, or in a documented emergency, and never verbally at a counter or over the telephone

HOW LONG WE KEEP IT
- Permanent academic record: indefinitely
- Cumulative folder: 5 years past exit
- Special education file: 5 years past last service
- Health items: 2 years past exit
- Conduct file: 3 years
- Benefit eligibility: 3 years past programme year
- Personnel and payroll: 7 years past separation
- Unsuccessful applications: 2 years

GETTING RID OF IT
- Do not destroy anything on your own judgement; wait for the destruction list
- Confirm no hold applies, then initial the list and return it
- Shred or bin-and-lock; nothing goes to recycling intact
- Wipe and destroy media rather than reassigning it
- Two witnesses, one certificate, filed for 7 years

BEFORE YOU SIGN
- Every filing cabinet holding these categories locks, and is locked overnight
- Every screen showing them is positioned away from public counters
- Every departing staff member returns keys and has system access closed the same day
- Every new staff member has read this checklist and signed the confidentiality acknowledgement
- Any suspected loss or improper release is reported to the Records Custodian the same day
