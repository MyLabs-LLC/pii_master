HERITAGE ESCROW SERVICES CORPORATION
INTERNAL STANDARD IS-7
HANDLING OF CUSTOMER INFORMATION IN ELECTRONIC AND MICROFORM SYSTEMS

Adopted by the Operations Committee, September 1999. Applies to all divisions.

1. INFORMATION HELD

1.1 The escrow production system maintains, for each order, the following data elements: Party Role; Full Legal Name; Alternate Name; Date of Birth; Social Security Number; Home Telephone; Business Telephone; Mailing Address; Property Address; Marital Status; Vesting Instruction.

1.2 The trust accounting system maintains: Escrow Number; Depository Name; Account Number; Routing Number; Wire Confirmation Reference; Disbursement Payee Role; Amount Field; Reconciliation Status.

1.3 The title plant interface maintains: Order Number; Search Name Field; Judgment Search Result; Tax Status Field; Prior Policy Reference.

1.4 The imaging system maintains microform and optical images of the documents supporting the above.

2. ACCESS

2.1 Access is granted by function code, not by individual discretion. Function codes are assigned by the Data Processing Manager upon the written requisition of a department head.

2.2 The following function codes are recognised: ESC (Escrow Officer and Assistant); TTL (Title Officer and Examiner); TRA (Trust Accountant); CTL (Controller); AUD (Internal Audit, read only); CUS (Records Custodian); ADM (Data Processing Manager, administrative only).

2.3 Function code ADM shall not be used to view customer data except in the course of a documented system fault, which shall be logged.

2.4 Function codes shall be revoked on the effective date of transfer, suspension or separation. The Director of Human Resources shall notify the Data Processing Manager in advance of that date.

2.5 Passwords shall not be shared, posted, or held by a supervisor.

3. RETENTION

3.1 Production records shall be purged from live tables seven (7) years after final disbursement and three (3) years after cancellation.

3.2 Title plant search records shall be retained ten (10) years from policy issuance.

3.3 Back-up tapes shall be recycled on a rotation of not more than one hundred eighty (180) days, save for the annual archive volume, which follows the retention of its source category.

4. DISPOSAL

4.1 Purge runs shall be scheduled quarterly and shall be executed only against a purge list approved by the Records Custodian.

4.2 Deletion from a live table is not disposal. The corresponding back-up volumes shall be overwritten in full before release.

4.3 Diskettes, fixed disks and tapes leaving Company custody, whether for repair, trade-in or scrap, shall be overwritten and thereafter physically defaced under the witness of two employees.

4.4 Microform shall be incinerated by the approved vendor under a bonded transmittal, and the vendor's certificate filed with the Certificate of Destruction.

5. REPORTING

5.1 Any loss, theft or unauthorised viewing of the categories in Clause 1 shall be reported by the discovering employee to the Records Custodian the same business day, and by the Records Custodian to the Operations Committee within three business days.
