PERSONAL INFORMATION HOLDINGS STANDARD
Standard No. RM-07 — Issued 14 March 2011 — Supersedes RM-03 (2007)
Owner: Office of the Privacy Officer

1. SCOPE
 - Applies to all personal information held by the Authority in any medium, whether operational, administrative or archival.
 - Binds all divisions, contracted operators and secondees.

2. CATEGORIES HELD
 - Employee Identification Data — surname, given name(s), date of birth, employee number, national insurance number, home address, personal telephone number, emergency contact name and relationship.
 - Payroll and Banking Data — salary band, bank sort code, bank account number, tax code, pension scheme membership number.
 - Fare Account Data — cardholder name, smartcard serial number, registered postal address, contact e-mail address, payment card token, concession category.
 - Travel History Data — card serial number, entry station, exit station, journey timestamp.

3. PERMITTED ACCESS
 - Clause 2.1 holdings: the Human Resources Records Custodian and the line manager of record only.
 - Clause 2.2 holdings: the Payroll Supervisor and the Head of Finance only.
 - Clause 2.3 and 2.4 holdings: the Revenue Protection Analyst on written request countersigned by the Privacy Officer.

4. RETENTION
 - Employee categories: six years from termination of engagement.
 - Fare account categories: two years from account closure.
 - Travel history: eight weeks from date of journey.

5. DISPOSAL
 - Paper: cross-cut shredding, witnessed by the Records Custodian.
 - Magnetic and solid-state media: degaussing or physical destruction under certificate.
 - A Destruction Register entry shall be raised for every disposal event and retained permanently.
