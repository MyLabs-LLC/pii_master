STANDARD ADM-4412 — SECTION G
PROCEDURE FOR RESPONDING TO REQUESTS FOR ACCESS AND AMENDMENT

Issued: 12 January 2004

G.1 RECEIPT OF REQUEST

G.1.1 A request for inspection of records shall be submitted in writing to the Building Principal of the school at which the records are maintained.

G.1.2 The receiving office shall date-stamp the request on the day of receipt and forward a copy to the Records Custodian within two (2) working days.

G.1.3 The Records Custodian shall open a Request File containing the following labelled fields: Request Control Number; Date Received; Category or Categories Requested; Requesting Party Relationship; Responding Office; Target Response Date; Disposition; Date Closed.

G.2 VERIFICATION OF STANDING

G.2.1 Before any record is produced, the responding office shall verify that the requesting party is a parent, guardian, eligible student, or authorised representative.

G.2.2 Verification shall be recorded in the Request File by noting the method of verification used and the title of the employee performing it. The identity documentation itself shall not be photocopied or retained.

G.3 PRODUCTION

G.3.1 Inspection shall be arranged within forty-five (45) days of receipt.

G.3.2 Inspection shall take place on District premises in the presence of an employee designated by the Building Principal.

G.3.3 Where a record contains information concerning more than one individual, the responding office shall produce only that portion pertaining to the requesting party. Portions pertaining to others shall be masked prior to inspection.

G.3.4 Copies may be furnished at the schedule of fees adopted by the Board. Fees shall be waived where the fee would effectively prevent inspection.

G.4 AMENDMENT

G.4.1 A party who believes a record is inaccurate, misleading, or in violation of privacy rights may request amendment in writing.

G.4.2 The Building Principal shall decide the request within thirty (30) days and shall notify the requesting party in writing of the decision and of the right to a hearing.

G.4.3 Where a hearing is requested, the Superintendent shall appoint a Hearing Officer who has no direct interest in the outcome.

G.4.4 Where amendment is refused, the requesting party may place a statement of disagreement in the record. The statement shall be retained for as long as the contested portion is retained and shall be disclosed whenever the contested portion is disclosed.

G.5 CLOSURE

G.5.1 The Records Custodian shall close the Request File upon completion of inspection, amendment, or hearing.

G.5.2 Request Files shall be retained for five (5) years from date of closure and shall then be disposed of under Section E.
