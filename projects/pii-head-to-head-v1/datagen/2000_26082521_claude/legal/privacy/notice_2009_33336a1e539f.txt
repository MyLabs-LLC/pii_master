ASHGROVE PHARMACY GROUP
RECORDS MANAGEMENT PROCEDURE RM-02
INFORMATION HOLDINGS, CUSTODY AND END OF LIFE

Effective from 1 September 2016. Applies group wide. Issued under Privacy
Standard PS-04. Owner: the Records Custodian.

1. HOLDINGS
1.1 The Group maintains a register of every category of personal information it
    holds. The register records, for each category: the category code, the
    category name, the data elements contained, the lawful basis, the system of
    record, the roles entitled to access, the retention trigger, the retention
    period and the approved disposal method.
1.2 The register is maintained by the Records Custodian and reconciled against
    the system inventory each quarter.
1.3 No new collection of personal information may commence until the category has
    been entered in the register and a retention rule assigned. Collection
    outside the register is a reportable control failure.

2. CUSTODY
2.1 Each category is assigned a named system owner by role title. The system
    owner is accountable for entitlement decisions, for the accuracy of the
    register entry and for the quarterly reconciliation.
2.2 Records must be held only in the system of record identified in the register.
    Extracts to local drives, removable media, personal accounts or unmanaged
    spreadsheets are prohibited.
2.3 Paper holdings at site level must be kept in a locked cabinet in a
    non public area and must not be left unattended at the counter, in the
    consultation room or in the dispensary at close of trade.
2.4 Transfer of any category between sites requires a numbered transfer note
    recording the category code, the volume, the despatching role, the receiving
    role and the date of receipt.

3. ACCESS
3.1 Access is provisioned by role and never by individual exception.
3.2 Requests are raised through the access workflow, approved by the line manager
    and the system owner, and logged with the business justification.
3.3 Entitlement is withdrawn on the day a role change or leaving date takes
    effect. The line manager is accountable for notifying the service desk in
    advance of that date.
3.4 All access to Categories A, B, C and G is written to an audit trail. Audit
    trails are sampled monthly by the Information Security Analyst and reviewed
    in full where a concern is raised.

4. RETENTION
4.1 Retention runs from the trigger event stated in the register.
4.2 Retention periods are minimum periods for legal and clinical purposes and
    maximum periods for privacy purposes. Records must not be kept beyond the
    stated period unless a hold applies.
4.3 Holds are applied only by Legal Counsel, are recorded in the exception log
    with the reason and the expected release date, and are reviewed every six
    months.

5. END OF LIFE
5.1 The retention register produces a quarterly disposal report listing every lot
    that has reached expiry.
5.2 The Records Custodian performs the hold check on Form RM-11 before any lot is
    released for destruction.
5.3 Destruction is witnessed by a second role. Paper is cross cut shredded or
    collected under seal by the approved contractor. Electronic records are
    purged from production and from every replica and backup set within the next
    full backup cycle. Media is degaussed or physically destroyed.
5.4 A destruction certificate is obtained for every lot, filed against the
    disposal report and retained permanently. The certificate records the
    category codes, the volume, the method, the date and the witnessing roles.
5.5 Failure to obtain a certificate is treated as an incomplete disposal and the
    lot is re entered on the next disposal report.

6. NON COMPLIANCE
Departure from this procedure is reported through the incident process to the
Group Privacy Officer within one working day of discovery.
