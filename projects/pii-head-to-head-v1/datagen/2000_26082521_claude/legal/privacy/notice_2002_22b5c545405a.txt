REGIONAL TRANSIT AUTHORITY
OFFICE OF THE RECORDS CUSTODIAN

STANDARD RTA-ADM-114
PERSONAL INFORMATION HOLDINGS — CLASSIFICATION, ACCESS, RETENTION AND DISPOSAL

Issued: April 2004
Supersedes: RTA-ADM-097 (1998)
Review cycle: 24 months
Applies to: all divisions, contracted operators, and appointed processors

1. SCOPE
- Applies to every holding of personal information under the control of the Authority in any medium, including paper file, microfiche, magnetic tape, and networked file store.
- Applies whether the holding is a system of record or a working extract taken from one.
- Does not displace statutory retention obligations where those are longer.

2. CATEGORIES HELD
- Category A — Establishment and identification records of staff. Fields maintained: Employee Number; Surname; Given Names; Job Title; Grade; Division; Date of Engagement; Date of Separation.
- Category B — Remuneration and deductions. Fields maintained: Employee Number; Pay Band; Bank Sort Reference; Account Reference; Tax Reference; Deduction Code; Period Identifier.
- Category C — Attendance and occupational health. Fields maintained: Employee Number; Absence Code; Certifying Practitioner Reference; Fitness Determination; Review Date.
- Category D — Operator licensing and route competence. Fields maintained: Employee Number; Licence Class; Endorsement Code; Assessment Outcome; Expiry Date.
- Category E — Concession and stored-value scheme registrations. Fields maintained: Scheme Member Number; Entitlement Class; Card Serial; Correspondence Address; Contact Telephone; Photograph Reference.
- Category F — Customer correspondence, claims and complaints. Fields maintained: Case Reference; Complainant Name; Correspondence Address; Service Line; Disposition Code.
- Category G — Incident reports and recorded images from vehicle and station surveillance. Fields maintained: Incident Reference; Location Code; Time Reference; Camera Identifier; Subject Description.
- Category H — Premises access control and identity passes. Fields maintained: Pass Number; Holder Name; Zone Permission; Issue Date; Surrender Date.
- Category I — Recruitment files, including files of unsuccessful applicants. Fields maintained: Application Reference; Applicant Name; Post Applied For; Referee Name; Selection Outcome.
- Category J — Superannuation and survivor benefit records. Fields maintained: Scheme Number; Member Name; Nominated Beneficiary Name; Service Reckoning; Commencement Date.

3. ACCESS
- Access is by role, not by grade, and is granted only on demonstrated operational need.
- Categories A, B, H, I, J — Human Resources Division and the Payroll Section only.
- Category C — the Occupational Health Officer only; line managers receive the fitness determination and nothing further.
- Category D — the Licensing Registrar and the Depot Manager of record.
- Categories E and F — Customer Services Division; extract access for the Revenue Protection Section.
- Category G — the Security Controller; release beyond the Authority only on written instruction of the Records Custodian.
- All access is logged. Logs record the requesting role, the record class, and the purpose code.
- No holding is to be copied to portable media without the countersignature of the Records Custodian.

4. RETENTION
- Category A — six years from Date of Separation.
- Category B — six complete tax years.
- Category C — forty years from last entry.
- Category D — four years from Expiry Date.
- Category E — three years from lapse of entitlement.
- Category F — two years from Disposition Code entry.
- Category G — thirty-one days, except where an incident is under investigation or claim.
- Categories H and I — twelve months.
- Category J — permanent.

5. DISPOSAL
- Disposal is authorised in writing by the Records Custodian; no division may dispose on its own motion.
- Paper and microfiche: cross-cut shredding to particle standard, on Authority premises, witnessed by two officers.
- Magnetic media: three-pass overwrite, then physical destruction of the carrier.
- A Certificate of Destruction is raised for each disposal lot and is itself retained for six years.
- Entries in indices and finding aids are struck through, not erased.
