MEMORANDUM

TO: All Branch Managers; Department Heads
FROM: The Records Custodian
SUBJECT: Standard RM-16, Custody of Categorised Member Information
DATE: 12 October 1999

1. Standard RM-16 takes effect on the first business day of November and replaces the interim instruction circulated in August. It is to be read to every employee at the next branch meeting and the reading minuted.

2. The Standard recognises five categories. Identification Data covers MEMBER-NO, SURNAME, GIVEN-NAME, DOB, TIN and the address block. Relationship Data covers SHARE-ID, LOAN-ID, OPEN-DATE, BALANCE-CURR and SIG-CARD-SEQ. Underwriting Data covers EMPLOYER, INCOME-GROSS-MO, BUREAU-SCORE, DEBT-RATIO and ADVERSE-ACTION-CODE. Authentication Data covers PIN-OFFSET, AUDIO-RESPONSE-CODE and VERIFICATION-PHRASE. Correspondence Data covers COMPLAINT-REF, INQUIRY-DATE, DISPUTE-CODE and RESOLUTION-CODE.

3. The change of substance is in access. Underwriting Data is no longer available from the branch inquiry screen. Where a member asks at the counter why an application was declined, the enquiry is to be referred to the Lending Department, which alone may read ADVERSE-ACTION-CODE and alone may issue the notice. Authentication Data has never been displayable and remains so; where a member has forgotten a code, the Security Administrator resets it and the member is advised by mail to the address of record.

4. Retention is unchanged: seven years from closure for Identification and Relationship Data, twenty-five months for declined applications, three years for Correspondence Data, and purge at closure for Authentication Data.

5. Disposal remains centralised. Branches are not to shred. Expired files are to be boxed, labelled with the fields BOX-SEQ, CATEGORY, PERIOD-COVERED and BRANCH-ID, and consigned to the Operations Division, which will arrange destruction by the bonded contractor and record the certificate in the Destruction Register. Boxes awaiting collection are to be held in the vault and not in the back corridor.

6. Questions of interpretation are to be put to this office in writing.
