RECORDS MANAGEMENT STANDARD RM-04
MEMBER AND EMPLOYEE INFORMATION: CLASSIFICATION, ACCESS, RETENTION AND DISPOSITION
Issued under authority of the Board of Directors, Resolution of the Regular Meeting
Effective 1 October 1998. Supersedes Standard RM-04 (Rev. 2, 1994) in its entirety.

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 This Standard prescribes the mandatory classification, custody, permitted access, minimum retention and authorised disposition of all information pertaining to identifiable natural persons which is created, received, compiled or maintained by the Credit Union in any medium whatsoever, including paper, microform, magnetic tape, direct access storage device, and optical platter.

1.2 This Standard applies without exception to every employee, officer, director, committee member, volunteer, and contracted service provider having access to the premises or systems of the Credit Union.

1.3 Nothing in this Standard shall be construed to diminish any obligation imposed by the Federal Credit Union Act, Parts 748 and 749 of the Rules and Regulations of the National Credit Union Administration, the Fair Credit Reporting Act, the Right to Financial Privacy Act, or the Bank Secrecy Act and its implementing regulations. Where a conflict arises, the more restrictive requirement shall govern.

2. CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

2.1 Category A — Identification Particulars. Comprises the fields designated Surname, Given Name, Middle Initial, Former Name, Date of Birth, Taxpayer Identification Number, Member Number, and Government-Issued Identification Type and Expiry.

2.2 Category B — Contact Particulars. Comprises the fields designated Residential Street Address, Mailing Address, Home Telephone Number, Business Telephone Number, and Electronic Mail Address where voluntarily furnished.

2.3 Category C — Membership Eligibility Records. Comprises the fields designated Field of Membership Qualifier, Sponsoring Employer, Immediate Family Relationship, Date of Admission, and Date of Expulsion or Withdrawal.

2.4 Category D — Share and Deposit Records. Comprises the fields designated Account Number, Account Type Code, Ownership Designation, Beneficiary Designation, Signature Card Image, Balance, and Transaction History.

2.5 Category E — Credit and Lending Files. Comprises the fields designated Loan Number, Application Date, Stated Annual Income, Employer Name, Length of Employment, Debt-to-Income Ratio, Collateral Description, Payment History, and Delinquency Status.

2.6 Category F — Consumer Report Information. Comprises information obtained from a consumer reporting agency, including the fields designated Credit Score, Public Record Indicator, and Inquiry History.

2.7 Category G — Electronic Access Credentials. Comprises the fields designated Personal Identification Number Offset, Audio Response Passcode, Card Number, and Card Expiry.

2.8 Category H — Personnel and Payroll Records. Comprises the fields designated Employee Number, Position Title, Rate of Compensation, Withholding Elections, Benefit Elections, Performance Evaluation, and Disciplinary Notation.

2.9 Category I — Premises Surveillance Media and Category J — Correspondence, Complaint and Dispute Files.

3. AUTHORISED ACCESS

3.1 Access shall be granted upon the principle of least privilege, that is, only to the extent demonstrably necessary to the discharge of an assigned duty.

3.2 Categories A, B, C and D: the Member Services Representative, the Branch Operations Officer, and the Records Custodian.

3.3 Categories E and F: the Loan Officer of Record, the Collections Representative, the Chairman of the Credit Committee, and the Records Custodian. Access to Category F is further conditioned upon a permissible purpose as defined by statute.

3.4 Category G: the Data Processing Supervisor only, and in no case shall the plaintext form of any credential be displayed, printed or spoken.

3.5 Category H: the Human Resources Officer and the Chief Executive Officer. No supervisor shall access the file of any employee outside their direct reporting line.

3.6 Category I: the Security Officer, and the Chief Executive Officer upon written request.

3.7 The Internal Auditor and the Compliance Officer shall possess read-only access to all Categories for the sole purpose of examination, such access to be logged in the Access Register.

4. RETENTION PERIODS

4.1 Categories A, B and C: for the duration of membership and seven (7) years thereafter.

4.2 Category D: signature cards, for the life of the account and six (6) years following closure; transaction history, five (5) years.

4.3 Category E: seven (7) years from the date of final payment, charge-off, or written denial, whichever is later.

4.4 Category F: two (2) years from the date of receipt, save where incorporated into an approved credit file.

4.5 Category G: purged within thirty (30) days of the closure of the associated account.

4.6 Category H: seven (7) years following separation of employment.

4.7 Category I: ninety (90) days, unless retained pursuant to Clause 6.

5. DISPOSITION

5.1 No record shall be destroyed except pursuant to the Approved Disposition Schedule and upon the written authorisation of the Records Custodian, countersigned by the Compliance Officer.

5.2 Paper records shall be reduced by cross-cut shredding to a particle not exceeding one-quarter inch in width, or incinerated by a bonded destruction contractor under the direct observation of a designated witness.

5.3 Magnetic media shall be degaussed and thereafter physically deformed. Overwriting alone shall not constitute disposition.

5.4 The Certificate of Destruction shall record, at minimum, the columns headed Record Series Code, Category Designation, Volume in Cubic Feet, Method of Destruction, Date of Destruction, Custodian Signature, and Witness Signature, and shall itself be retained permanently.

6. SUSPENSION OF DISPOSITION

6.1 Upon notice of litigation, subpoena, administrative summons, or examination by any regulatory authority, all disposition of the affected series shall cease immediately and shall not resume until released in writing by counsel.

7. ENFORCEMENT

7.1 Violation of this Standard shall constitute grounds for disciplinary action up to and including summary dismissal, and where warranted, referral to the appropriate authorities.
