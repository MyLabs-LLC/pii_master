RECORDS RETENTION SCHEDULE — EXTRACT
Companion to Administrative Standard AS-4120 | Maintained by the Records Custodian

Schedule columns: Series Code — Series Title — Responsible Position — Minimum Retention — Trigger Event — Disposition Method

  STUDENT SERIES
    - ST-01  Cumulative Education Record — Registrar — 5 years — withdrawal, transfer or graduation — shred
    - ST-02  Academic Transcript — Registrar — permanent — none — retain
    - ST-03  Enrolment and Residency Verification — Registrar — 3 years — end of enrolment — shred
    - ST-04  Attendance Detail — Attendance Clerk — 3 years — end of school year — shred
    - ST-05  Disciplinary File — Principal — 5 years — end of enrolment — shred
    - ST-06  Special Education File — Director of Student Services — 5 years — exit from services — shred
    - ST-07  Health Office File — School Nurse — retain with ST-01 — end of enrolment — shred
    - ST-08  Immunisation Record — School Nurse — 2 years past ST-01 expiry — end of enrolment — shred

  EMPLOYMENT SERIES
    - HR-01  Personnel File — Director of Human Resources — 7 years — separation — shred
    - HR-02  Application and Selection Record, unsuccessful — Director of Human Resources — 2 years — position closed — shred
    - HR-03  Background Check Disposition — Director of Human Resources — duration of service plus 3 years — separation — shred
    - HR-04  Licensure and Endorsement — Director of Human Resources — permanent — none — retain

  OPERATIONAL SERIES
    - OP-01  Nutrition Eligibility Determination — Nutrition Services Manager — 3 years — end of fiscal year — shred
    - OP-02  Transportation Ridership Record — Transportation Supervisor — 1 year — end of school year — shred
    - OP-03  Visitor Sign-In Register — Building Principal — 1 year — book closed — shred
    - OP-04  Authentication and Filter Logs — Director of Technology — 2 years — log rotation — cryptographic erase
    - OP-05  Surveillance Footage — Director of Safety and Security — 30 days — recording overwritten in sequence — overwrite
    - OP-06  Certificate of Destruction — Records Custodian — permanent — none — retain

  NOTES
    - Retention runs from the close of the fiscal year in which the trigger event occurs.
    - A series under litigation hold is exempt from disposition until the hold is released in writing.
    - Media that cannot be verifiably erased is physically destroyed and recorded under OP-06.
    - Amendments to this extract take effect only on republication by the Records Custodian.
