UNIVERSITY REGISTRAR — RECORDS HANDLING STANDARD RM-2004-9
CONFIDENTIALITY OF CATEGORY E (RESTRICTED-SENSITIVITY) DATA
Adopted 14 October 2004

1. DEFINITION. Category E comprises those fields identified in clause 3.5 of RM-2004-1, being determinations and status indicators the disclosure of which would be materially adverse to the individual concerned irrespective of the accuracy of the field.

2. SEGREGATION. Category E fields shall be held in tables logically separate from Categories A through D and shall not be reproduced in any standard report, roster, roll, or advising screen. Where a downstream system requires knowledge that a determination exists, only a Boolean indicator shall be passed; the substance of the determination shall not be passed.

3. AUTHORISED ROLES. Access is limited to the Records Custodian and to the Data Steward designated in writing for the sub-series concerned. No delegation of Category E access to student employees, graduate assistants, temporary staff, or contracted personnel is permitted under any circumstance.

4. DISCLOSURE OUTSIDE THE OFFICE. Disclosure to another University office shall be made only on the written request of that office's head, shall state the field or fields sought and the purpose, and shall be limited to the minimum fields necessary. Disclosure to a party outside the University shall be made only on the individual's signed consent, on lawful process, or under an exception recognised in the annual public notice.

5. LOGGING. Every access and every disclosure shall generate a Category G entry recording the role code, the date, the fields touched, and the purpose code. Logs shall be reviewed monthly by the Records Custodian and quarterly by Internal Audit.

6. WORKING COPIES. Extraction of Category E fields to a working file, spreadsheet, or printout is prohibited except on the prior written approval of the Registrar. Approved working copies shall be marked, held in a locked container when not in use, and destroyed by the approving deadline.

7. RETENTION AND DISPOSITION. Category E records shall be retained seven years following separation and shall then be destroyed under RM-2004-7, clause 6, with a witness present and a certificate of destruction executed.

8. NON-COMPLIANCE. Any handling of Category E data contrary to this Standard shall be reported to the Registrar within one business day of discovery and shall be treated as a security incident under University policy.
