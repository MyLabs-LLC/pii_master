STONEBRIDGE COUNTY HEALTH DISTRICT
RECORDS MANAGEMENT PROGRAM

APPENDIX B TO POLICY 300 — SCHEDULE OF PERSONAL INFORMATION CATEGORIES
Approved 8 February 2021 | Custodial Authority: Records Custodian | Version 3.0

B.1  CONSTRUCTION OF THIS APPENDIX. The schedule set out at paragraph B.3 is descriptive of categories only. It identifies the nature of the information held, the role or roles competent to access it, the event from which the retention period runs, the duration of that period, and the method by which the information is to be destroyed at the end of it. Nothing in this Appendix authorizes the creation or acquisition of information not otherwise authorized.

B.2  PRECEDENCE. Where a category is governed by a statute, regulation, grant condition, or order imposing a longer retention period or a more restrictive condition of access than that stated below, the external requirement governs and the Records Custodian shall annotate the schedule accordingly.

B.4  DISPOSAL PROCEDURE.
     (a)  In January of each year the Records Custodian shall compile the disposition list for all series whose retention has expired in the preceding calendar year.
     (b)  The Privacy Officer shall certify in writing that no litigation hold, audit, investigation, or pending access request affects any listed series.
     (c)  The District Director shall thereupon authorize destruction. Authorization shall be specific to the series and inclusive period and shall not be given in general terms.
     (d)  Paper shall be destroyed by cross-cut shredding, save for series 300-04 and 300-06, which shall be incinerated. Electronic records shall be sanitized in accordance with the media sanitization procedure and the medium destroyed if retired from service.
     (e)  Two employees shall witness each event and execute the Certificate of Destruction, reciting series, inclusive period, volume, method, and date. Certificates are retained permanently as series 300-12.

B.5  AMENDMENT. This Appendix shall be reviewed biennially by the Records Custodian in consultation with the Privacy Officer, and any amendment shall be submitted to the District Director for approval before taking effect.
