GREAT LAKES REGIONAL INSURANCE GROUP
PRIVACY STANDARD PS-9: HOLDINGS, ACCESS, RETENTION, AND DISPOSAL
Version 2.1 — adopted 2 September 2021

1. Application. This Standard applies to every business unit of the Group and to every vendor processing personal information on the Group's behalf. It is administered by the Records Custodian and enforced through the annual departmental privacy review.

2. Description of holdings. The Group's holdings are described by category rather than by system, so that the same rules follow a record when it is copied between systems. The categories are as follows.

2.1 Personal Identifiers. The fields Full Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Marital Status, Residential Address, Mailing Address, Telephone Number, and Electronic Mail Address.

2.2 Regulated Identifiers. The fields Social Security Number, Taxpayer Identification Number, Driver Licence Number, and Vehicle Identification Number.

2.3 Contract Data. The fields Policy Number, Endorsement Number, Named Insured, Beneficiary Designation, Coverage Limit, Deductible Amount, Premium Amount, and Billing Cycle.

2.4 Loss Data. The fields Claim Number, Date of Loss, Cause of Loss, Adjuster Identifier, Reserve Amount, Indemnity Paid, Expense Paid, and Litigation Status.

2.5 Health Data. The fields Diagnosis Code, Procedure Code, Treatment Date, Provider Name, Provider Identifier, and Disability Determination, held only where the line of business or the claim requires them.

2.6 Payment Data. The fields Bank Routing Number, Bank Account Number, Payment Card Number, Payment Card Expiration Date, and Refund Instruction.

2.7 Technical Data. The fields User Identifier, Internet Protocol Address, Device Identifier, Login Timestamp, and Audit Event Type.

3. Access. Access follows the role, not the individual, and is provisioned only through the standard request form countersigned by the requester's manager. Policy Service Representatives receive 2.1 and 2.3. Underwriters receive 2.1, 2.2, 2.3, and the rating elements of 2.4. Claims Adjusters receive 2.1, 2.2, 2.3, and 2.4; access to 2.5 is added only for adjusters assigned to the health and disability lines. Payment Operations receives 2.1, 2.3, and 2.6. Systems Administration receives 2.7. The Records Custodian and the Privacy Officer receive read access to all categories for oversight purposes and may not use that access to make underwriting or claims decisions. Any access outside these allocations requires the written approval of the Privacy Officer and lapses after ninety days unless renewed.

4. Retention. Contract Data is retained for seven years after the contract ends. Loss Data is retained for ten years after the claim closes. Health Data is retained for the period required by the governing state statute and, absent a statutory period, for ten years. Payment Data is purged of the Bank Account Number and Payment Card Number fields twelve months after the final transaction, the remainder being retained with the Contract Data. Technical Data is retained for eighteen months. Regulated Identifiers are retained only for as long as the record they identify is retained.

5. Disposal. Disposal is authorised in writing by the Records Custodian following a review for legal holds. Paper is destroyed by cross-cut shredding through the contracted vendor, which returns a certificate of destruction for each collection. Electronic records are removed from production, from reporting extracts, and from backup at the next media rotation. The Disposal Log records the columns Record Series, Retention Rule, Hold Check Completed, Volume, Method, Date of Destruction, and Authorising Officer, and is reviewed quarterly by Internal Audit.
