GROUP INFORMATION GOVERNANCE MANUAL
SECTION 9 — PERSONAL INFORMATION: CATEGORY REGISTER, ACCESS CONTROL, RETENTION AND DESTRUCTION
Issue 3, dated 1 November 2016. Custodian of this Section: the Records Custodian. Distribution: Directors, Depot Managers, Transport Managers, Human Resources, Payroll, Fleet Compliance, Site Security.

9.1 GENERAL OBLIGATIONS

  * Every officer, employee and agency worker of the Company is required, as a condition of engagement, to observe this Section and to report any suspected departure from it to the Records Custodian without delay.
  * No category of personal information may be created, acquired, extended or repurposed save upon the prior written determination of the Records Custodian, such determination to be recorded in the Category Register.
  * The Category Register shall record, for each category: Category Reference; Category Title; Lawful Basis Relied Upon; Business Owner; System of Record; Field Labels Held; Permitted Roles; Retention Trigger; Retention Period; Disposal Method.

9.2 DECLARED CATEGORIES AND FIELD LABELS

  * Personnel and Establishment Records — Employee Number; Surname; Forename(s); Preferred Name; Date of Birth; Home Address; Postcode; Home Telephone Number; Mobile Telephone Number; Personal Email Address; Emergency Contact Name; Emergency Contact Telephone Number; Relationship to Employee.
  * Remuneration Records — National Insurance Number; Tax Code; Payroll Reference; Bank Sort Code; Bank Account Number; Building Society Roll Number; Pension Scheme Member Number; Attachment of Earnings Order Reference.
  * Driver and Vehicle Compliance Records — Driving Licence Number; Licence Issuing Authority; Categories Held; Endorsement Codes; Points Recorded; Driver CPC Card Number; Periodic Training Hours; Digital Tachograph Card Number; Infringement Type; Working Time Reference Period.
  * Occupational Health Records — Health Questionnaire Reference; Screening Type; Date of Examination; Fitness Determination; Restriction Applied; Review Due Date.
  * Security and Site Access Records — Access Card Number; Biometric Template Reference; Vehicle Registration Presented; Gate Event Timestamp; Site Code; Visitor Host Role.
  * Surveillance Records — Camera Identifier; Recording Start Timestamp; Recording End Timestamp; In-Cab Event Code; Retrieval Request Reference.
  * Commercial Contact Records — Contact Name; Contact Job Title; Trading Name; Delivery Contact Telephone Number; Delivery Contact Email Address; Special Delivery Instruction.

9.3 PERMITTED ACCESS

  * Access shall be granted upon the principle of least privilege, shall be role-based, and shall in every case be recorded in the Access Authorisation Log against the fields: Role Title; Category Reference; Basis of Access; Authorising Manager; Date Granted; Date Reviewed; Date Revoked.
  * Personnel, Remuneration and Occupational Health categories are reserved to the Head of Human Resources, the Payroll Supervisor and the Occupational Health Coordinator respectively; no operational manager shall be granted access to Occupational Health Records beyond the Fitness Determination and Restriction Applied fields.
  * Driver and Vehicle Compliance categories are reserved to the Transport Manager, the Fleet Compliance Manager and the Records Custodian.
  * Surveillance Records may be retrieved only upon a written Retrieval Request countersigned by the Depot Manager and the Records Custodian.
  * Access rights shall be reviewed not less than quarterly and revoked within five working days of any change of role or cessation of engagement.

9.4 RETENTION

  * Personnel and Remuneration Records: six years from the end of the tax year in which the engagement ceased.
  * Driver and Vehicle Compliance Records: four years from expiry of entitlement; tachograph and working time material, twelve months from recording.
  * Occupational Health Records: forty years from date of last examination.
  * Security and Site Access Records: thirty-one days for event data; cessation of engagement for enrolment data.
  * Surveillance Records: thirty-one days, save where lawfully retained for the purposes of an investigation or claim.
  * Commercial Contact Records: twenty-four months from completion of the relevant consignment.

9.5 DESTRUCTION

  * Destruction shall be authorised in writing by the Records Custodian upon production of the Due for Destruction Report.
  * Paper shall be cross-cut shredded to DIN 66399 level P-4 or destroyed by the approved contractor under continuous supervision.
  * Magnetic media shall be degaussed and physically deformed; solid state media shall be granulated to a particle size not exceeding two millimetres.
  * A Certificate of Destruction shall be obtained in every case and retained for a period of seven years from the date of destruction.
