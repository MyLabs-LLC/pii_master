NORTHGATE COMMUNITY CREDIT UNION
PRIVACY AND RECORDS STANDARD — SECTION 6
DATA CATEGORIES, CUSTODY, RETENTION AND DESTRUCTION
Revision 2, October 2011

APPLICABILITY

» All branches, the operations centre, the call centre and remote workers.
» All media: core system tables, imaging repository, loan origination system, paper files, backup tapes and courier bags.
» All persons: employees, volunteers, board members, temporary staff and contracted service providers.

CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD

» Direct identifiers — labels: Full Legal Name; Alias; Date of Birth; Social Security Number; Taxpayer Identification Number; Government Identification Number; Mother's Maiden Name.
» Contact identifiers — labels: Residence Address; Mailing Address; Telephone Number (Home, Mobile, Work); Electronic Mail Address; Preferred Contact Method.
» Membership identifiers — labels: Member Number; Account Suffix; Certificate Number; Safe Deposit Box Number; Date of Admission; Field-of-Membership Basis.
» Payment instruments — labels: Debit Card Number; Credit Card Number; Card Verification Value; Card Expiry; Personal Identification Number; Cheque Serial Number; ACH Routing Reference.
» Authentication secrets — labels: Online Banking Identifier; Password Hash; Security Question; Security Answer; Voice Passphrase; Token Serial.
» Financial behaviour — labels: Balance; Transaction Date; Transaction Amount; Merchant Category; Payee; Deposit Source; Overdraft Occurrence.
» Underwriting information — labels: Income; Employer; Occupation; Consumer Report; Credit Score; Score Factors; Obligations; Collateral Description; Appraised Value; Adverse Action Reason.
» Workforce information — labels: Employee Number; Position; Compensation; Withholding Election; Benefit Election; Background Check Disposition; Bond Status.
» Regulatory information — labels: Customer Identification Program Outcome; Sanctions Screening Outcome; Currency Transaction Report Reference; Suspicious Activity Report Reference; Subpoena Reference.

CUSTODY AND PERMITTED ACCESS

» The Records Custodian is accountable for the inventory and the retention schedule.
» The Information Security Officer provisions and revokes access strictly against approved role definitions.
» Frontline roles (Teller, Member Services Representative) access direct identifiers, contact identifiers, membership identifiers and financial behaviour only.
» Lending roles (Loan Processor, Lending Officer, Underwriter) additionally access underwriting information for the duration of the application and the life of the resulting obligation.
» Authentication secrets are never visible to any role; reset only, never retrieval.
» Payment instrument numbers are displayed truncated to all roles; full display requires dual authorisation and generates an alert to the Information Security Officer.
» Workforce information is confined to the Human Resources Manager and the Chief Executive Officer.
» Regulatory information is confined to the BSA Officer and, on documented need, the Compliance Officer. Disclosure of a Suspicious Activity Report Reference outside these roles is prohibited by law.

RETENTION

» Life of membership plus seven years — membership, identification and signature records.
» Five years from satisfaction, charge-off or closure — loan and open-end credit records.
» Twenty-five months — declined applications and adverse action notices.
» Seven years — statements, item images and personnel files after separation.
» Five years — regulatory filings and supporting workpapers.
» Permanent — charter, bylaws, minutes, general ledger and audit reports.

DESTRUCTION

» Destruction is scheduled monthly and executed only after hold clearance by the Compliance Officer.
» Paper: cross-cut shredded on site or by attended mobile service.
» Electronic: multi-pass overwrite of production records; degauss and physical destruction of retired media.
» Two persons attend every event; the Destruction Log records Category, Medium, Date Range, Volume, Method, Date, Custodian Role and Witness Role.
» Logs and vendor certificates are retained seven years and produced to examiners on request.
