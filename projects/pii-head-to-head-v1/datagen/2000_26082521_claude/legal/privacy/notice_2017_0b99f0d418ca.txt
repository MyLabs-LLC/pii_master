METROPOLITAN TRANSIT COMMISSION
ADMINISTRATIVE BULLETIN 2004/11
PERSONAL INFORMATION — HOLDINGS AND RETENTION

I. CLASSES OF HOLDING
  o Employment and establishment records
  o Remuneration, deductions and superannuation records
  o Health, attendance and welfare records
  o Vocational licensing and safety competence records
  o Passenger scheme registrations and entitlement records
  o Correspondence, complaint and claims records
  o Incident, accident and surveillance records
  o Site access, pass issue and vehicle permit records
  o Recruitment and selection records, including unsuccessful applications

II. CUSTODY
  o Each class is assigned to a single Custodial Division. Joint custody is not recognised.
  o The Custodial Division maintains the index; all other divisions work from notified extracts.
  o Extracts inherit the classification, access restriction and retention period of the parent holding.

III. ACCESS
  o Granted by role and purpose code, revoked on transfer, reviewed half-yearly.
  o Health records are accessible to the Occupational Health Officer alone.
  o Surveillance records are accessible to the Security Controller alone.
  o Disclosure outside the Commission requires written authority and a Disclosure Log entry recording Requesting Body, Class, Purpose and Authorising Officer.

IV. RETENTION
  o Employment records — six years from separation
  o Remuneration records — six tax years
  o Health records — forty years from last entry
  o Licensing records — four years from expiry
  o Scheme registrations — three years from lapse
  o Correspondence and claims — two years from closure
  o Surveillance records — thirty-one days, save under investigation
  o Access and permit records — twelve months
  o Recruitment records — twelve months
  o Superannuation records — permanent

V. DISPOSAL
  o Authorised only by the Records Custodian, lot by lot.
  o Legal hold and outstanding subject access searches completed before authorisation.
  o Paper and film destroyed by cross-cut shredding or incineration on Commission premises.
  o Magnetic and optical media overwritten three times and the carrier destroyed.
  o Certificate of Destruction raised and retained six years.
  o Index entries annotated, never erased.

VI. NON-COMPLIANCE
  o Unauthorised disposal, unauthorised copying to portable media, and removal of holdings from Commission premises without a movement docket are reportable to the Records Custodian within one working day and are disciplinary matters.
