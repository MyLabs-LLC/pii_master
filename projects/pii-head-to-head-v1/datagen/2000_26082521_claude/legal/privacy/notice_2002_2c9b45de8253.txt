PATIENT FINANCIAL SERVICES DEPARTMENT
STANDARD PFS-STD-04: PERSONAL INFORMATION CATEGORY INVENTORY
Effective 14 March 2016 | Supersedes rev. 2 (2013) | Review cycle: 24 months
Owner: the Records Custodian, Patient Financial Services

    1.  SCOPE
        1.1  This Standard applies to all information holdings of the billing
             office, in any medium, whether originated internally or received
             from a treating department, a business associate, or a payer.

    2.  CATEGORIES HELD
        2.1  Patient identification data elements, comprising the fields
             Patient Full Name; Date of Birth; Social Security Number;
             Medical Record Number; Guarantor Name; Guarantor Mailing
             Address; Telephone Number; Electronic Mail Address.
        2.2  Coverage data elements, comprising Payer Name; Subscriber
             Identifier; Group Number; Plan Effective Date; Claim Number.
        2.3  Transactional data elements, comprising Date of Service;
             Procedure Code; Diagnosis Code; Amount Billed; Amount Paid;
             Balance Due; Adjustment Reason Code.

    3.  PERMITTED ACCESS
        3.1  Read access is limited to the Billing Representative, the
             Financial Counselor, the Collections Representative, and the
             Revenue Cycle Supervisor.
        3.2  Amendment access is limited to the Revenue Cycle Supervisor and
             the Charge Integrity Analyst, and each amendment shall be
             recorded in the audit log.
        3.3  Audit access is reserved to the Privacy Officer and to Internal
             Audit, exercisable without prior notice to the custodial unit.

    4.  RETENTION
        4.1  Seven (7) years from the date of final adjudication of the
             account.
        4.2  Accounts of minor patients: until the age of majority plus three
             (3) years, notwithstanding paragraph 4.1.

    5.  DISPOSAL
        5.1  Cross-cut shredding to the particle size specified from time to
             time by the Information Security Officer, followed by entry in
             the Destruction Register under the headings Record Series;
             Volume; Method; Date of Destruction; Custodian Title.
