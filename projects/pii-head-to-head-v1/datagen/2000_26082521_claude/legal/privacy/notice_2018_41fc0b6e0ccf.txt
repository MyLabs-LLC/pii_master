PIEDMONT VALLEY CREDIT UNION
INFORMATION HANDLING STANDARD IH-2011.1
Issued by the Office of the Compliance Officer
Review cycle: annual, each first quarter

1.0 PURPOSE AND AUTHORITY

1.1 This standard implements the credit union's obligations under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act privacy and safeguards provisions, NCUA Rules and Regulations Part 748 and Part 749, and the disposal rule at 16 CFR Part 682.
1.2 It establishes the inventory of information categories, the access matrix, the retention schedule and the mandatory disposal procedure.
1.3 Departures from this standard require written waiver from the Chief Executive Officer countersigned by the Supervisory Committee Chair.

2.0 INVENTORY OF CATEGORIES

2.1 Category A — Identity. Field labels in scope: Legal Name; Prior Legal Name; Date of Birth; Place of Birth; Social Security Number; Individual Taxpayer Identification Number; Driver Licence Number; Passport Number; Photograph.
2.2 Category B — Location and Contact. Field labels in scope: Residence Street Address; Mailing Address; Prior Address; Home Telephone; Mobile Telephone; Work Telephone; Electronic Mail Address; Emergency Contact Name; Emergency Contact Telephone.
2.3 Category C — Account and Relationship. Field labels in scope: Member Number; Share Account Number; Share Draft Account Number; Certificate Number; Debit Card Number; Credit Card Number; Card Expiry; Card Verification Value; Personal Identification Number; Online Banking User Identifier; Password Hash; Security Question; Security Answer.
2.4 Category D — Financial and Transactional. Field labels in scope: Ledger Balance; Available Balance; Transaction Date; Transaction Amount; Merchant Descriptor; Payee Name; ACH Company Identifier; Wire Beneficiary; Overdraft Count; Non-Sufficient Funds Count.
2.5 Category E — Credit and Underwriting. Field labels in scope: Gross Monthly Income; Employer Name; Length of Employment; Consumer Report; Credit Score; Score Factor Code; Existing Obligations; Collateral Description; Vehicle Identification Number; Appraised Value; Loan-to-Value Ratio; Adverse Action Reason.
2.6 Category F — Employment. Field labels in scope: Employee Number; Position Title; Compensation Rate; Withholding Election; Benefit Election; Disciplinary Record; Background Check Result; Bond Eligibility.
2.7 Category G — Regulatory and Investigative. Field labels in scope: Customer Identification Program Result; Office of Foreign Assets Control Screening Result; Currency Transaction Report; Suspicious Activity Report; Subpoena Reference; Litigation Hold Reference.

3.0 ACCESS MATRIX

3.1 Access is granted by role, not by individual, and is provisioned by the Information Security Officer on written request of the department head.
3.2 Role permissions:
    - Teller: read Categories A (name only), B, C (masked), D.
    - Member Services Representative: read and write Categories A, B, C (masked), D.
    - Lending Officer: read Categories A, B, C, D, E; write Category E.
    - Collections Officer: read Categories A, B, D, E.
    - Human Resources Generalist: read and write Category F.
    - BSA Officer: read all categories; write Category G.
    - Compliance Officer: read all categories.
    - Information Systems Administrator: administrative rights to systems; no business-purpose read authority; all access logged.
    - External Auditor: read access, time-boxed, under executed confidentiality agreement.
3.3 Unmasked display of Category C card and credential fields is restricted to the Card Services Supervisor and requires dual authorisation.
3.4 Quarterly entitlement recertification is performed by each department head and evidenced to the Compliance Officer.

4.0 RETENTION SCHEDULE

4.1 Membership record: life of membership plus seven years.
4.2 Closed-end loan file: five years after final payment, release of lien or charge-off, whichever is later.
4.3 Open-end credit file: five years after account closure.
4.4 Declined application and related notice: twenty-five months.
4.5 Deposit account statements and item images: seven years.
4.6 Wire and ACH records: five years.
4.7 Category G filings and supporting documentation: five years from date of filing.
4.8 Category F records: seven years after separation; claims files longer where applicable.
4.9 Charter, bylaws, board minutes and general ledger: permanent.

5.0 DISPOSAL PROCEDURE

5.1 Records reaching end of retention are identified by the Records Custodian in the monthly Eligible-for-Destruction Report.
5.2 The Compliance Officer clears the report against all open litigation holds and examination requests before release.
5.3 Paper is shredded on premises to cross-cut particle size not exceeding 4mm by 40mm, or collected in locked consoles for destruction by the approved vendor under attendance.
5.4 Microform is incinerated or pulverised. Optical media is shredded. Magnetic media is degaussed and then physically destroyed.
5.5 Electronic records held in production systems are purged by the Database Administrator using an approved purge routine; backup media containing purged records expires on the normal rotation cycle and is not restored for business use.
5.6 The Destruction Log is completed for every event and retains: Record Category; Medium; Date Range Covered; Volume; Method; Date of Destruction; Custodian Role; Witness Role; Vendor Certificate Reference.
5.7 Destruction Logs and vendor certificates are retained for seven years.
