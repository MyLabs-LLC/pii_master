MATRIX NOTES
1. Primary Access Role denotes standing entitlement provisioned at role assignment.
   Secondary Access Role denotes entitlement granted per assignment and revoked on
   completion of that assignment.
2. Marketing Analyst entitlement against Category F is limited to aggregated
   extracts. Row level export is not provisioned to this role.
3. Payment Operations Analyst entitlement against Category E is limited to the
   tokenised view. Cleartext account data is not retained in any system of record.
4. Any role not listed against a category has no entitlement to that category,
   including read entitlement, and must submit a documented business case to the
   system owner.
5. Third party processors inherit the entitlement of the role they support and are
   bound by the confidentiality schedule of the services agreement. Processor
   access is reviewed each quarter by the system owner.
6. Entitlement recertification is performed twice yearly. Entitlements not
   recertified within fifteen working days of the review opening are revoked
   automatically.
7. Where a record falls into more than one category, the longest applicable
   retention period governs the whole record and the most restrictive access
   entitlement applies.
8. Retention periods run from the trigger event, not from the date of creation or
   the date of last access.
9. Legal hold suspends the retention clock for the affected lot until the hold is
   released in writing by Legal Counsel.
10. Amendments to this matrix require version control through PS-04 and may not be
    made locally at site level.
