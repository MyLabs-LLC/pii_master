COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT — MEMORANDUM OF STANDARD PRACTICE
Subject: Handling of Personal Information Categories
File: PRIV-2016-03

* Categories held: Identity (Name; Date of Birth; Sex); Identifiers (Social Security Number; Client Number; Member Number); Contact (Address; Telephone Number; Electronic Mail Address); Clinical (Diagnosis; Service Date; Result); Financial (Account Number; Charge; Payment); Personnel (Employee Number; Credential Number).

* Who may access: the Health Officer; the Privacy Officer; the Records Custodian; the Clinical Program Supervisor; the Public Health Nurse; the Eligibility Technician; the Human Resources Manager. Each such role holder is confined to the categories assigned to that role in the Access Control Register, and to no other.

* How long held: clinical categories, seven years from last service; minors' clinical categories, majority plus seven years; financial categories, seven years; personnel categories, employment plus six years; vital records categories, permanently.

* How disposed: by shredding or media sanitization only; witnessed; certified in writing by the Records Custodian; entered in the Destruction Register; certificates retained permanently.

* Deviations from the foregoing shall be reported forthwith to the Privacy Officer and shall be documented in the Incident Log.
