CONFIDENTIAL RECORDS HANDLING STANDARD
ARTICLE VII — CUSTODY, ACCESS AND DESTRUCTION
(Extract from the Operations Manual, Section 7, as amended November 1997)

7.01 Declaration. All information in the possession of the Credit Union which identifies or is capable of identifying a natural person is declared confidential, is the property of the Credit Union, and is held in a fiduciary capacity for the benefit of the person to whom it relates.

7.02 Enumeration of Holdings. The confidential holdings of the Credit Union are enumerated as follows: (a) identification particulars; (b) contact particulars; (c) eligibility and membership status; (d) share, draft and certificate account records; (e) loan applications, credit files and consumer report extracts; (f) payment and delinquency histories; (g) safe deposit box rental records; (h) wire transfer instructions; (i) electronic access credentials; (j) personnel, payroll and benefit records; (k) premises surveillance media; and (l) correspondence, complaint and dispute files.

7.03 Field Labels. Each holding enumerated in Clause 7.02 shall be documented in the Data Dictionary by field label only, such labels to include but not be limited to: Surname, Given Name, Date of Birth, Taxpayer Identification Number, Member Number, Residential Address, Home Telephone Number, Employer Name, Stated Annual Income, Account Number, Account Type Code, Loan Number, Collateral Description, Credit Score, Card Number, Personal Identification Number Offset, Employee Number, Rate of Compensation, Box Number, and Date of Last Access.

7.04 Custody. The Records Custodian is the sole custodian of record for all holdings save that the Data Processing Supervisor is custodian of holdings resident on machine-readable media and the Human Resources Officer is custodian of holdings enumerated at Clause 7.02(j).

7.05 Access. Access is permitted only upon a demonstrated business necessity. The following are entitled to access as stated: the Member Services Representative, to holdings (a) through (d); the Loan Officer of Record, to holdings (e) and (f); the Vault Attendant, to holding (g), and to the Date of Last Access field only; the Wire Room Operator, to holding (h), during the business day of execution only; the Data Processing Supervisor, to holding (i); the Human Resources Officer, to holding (j); the Security Officer, to holding (k); and the Compliance Officer and Internal Auditor, to all holdings for examination purposes.

7.06 Prohibitions. No officer or employee shall remove any confidential holding from the premises, transmit any such holding by facsimile to an unattended machine, discuss any such holding in a public area of the office, or disclose any such holding to a member of their own family, without the prior written authorisation of the Chief Executive Officer.

7.07 Retention. Retention periods are as prescribed in Schedule 1 to this Article. Where no period is prescribed, the holding shall be retained for six (6) years and thereafter destroyed.

7.08 Destruction. Destruction shall be witnessed, shall render the information wholly irrecoverable, and shall be recorded in the Destruction Register. Confidential holdings shall in no circumstances be placed in ordinary refuse, deposited in a recycling receptacle, or sold as scrap.

7.09 Certification. The Records Custodian shall certify to the Supervisory Committee, semi-annually, that the requirements of this Article have been observed in all material respects.
