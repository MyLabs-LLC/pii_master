METROPOLITAN REGIONAL TRANSIT AUTHORITY
OFFICE OF THE GENERAL COUNSEL — RECORDS AND INFORMATION GOVERNANCE UNIT
STANDARD RIG-04: CLASSIFICATION, CUSTODY, RETENTION AND DISPOSITION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION HOLDINGS
Issued 14 March 2011. Effective 1 April 2011. Supersedes Administrative Bulletin 2004-11 in its entirety.

1. Purpose and Application. This Standard is promulgated under the authority delegated to the General Counsel by Resolution of the Board and applies, without exception, to every record in the custody or under the control of the Authority which contains personal information, irrespective of the medium in which such record is fixed, the department by which it was created, or the system of record in which it is maintained. Where any departmental practice, local procedure, or vendor-supplied system configuration conflicts with the provisions set forth herein, the provisions of this Standard shall govern, and the responsible Division Head shall procure conformity without undue delay.

2. Categories of Personal Information Held. The Authority recognises the following enumerated categories, and no personal information shall be created, acquired, or ingested unless it is capable of assignment to one of them: (a) Identity Particulars, comprising the elements labelled Surname, Given Name(s), Former Name(s), Date of Birth, Gender, and Government-Issued Identifier Type and Number; (b) Contact Particulars, comprising Residential Address, Mailing Address, Home Telephone Number, Mobile Telephone Number, and Electronic Mail Address; (c) Fare Media and Account Particulars, comprising Fare Card Serial Number, Customer Account Reference, Autoload Instrument Type, Instrument Terminal Digits, and Billing Postal Code; (d) Travel History Particulars, comprising Tap-On Station or Stop Code, Tap-On Timestamp, Tap-Off Station or Stop Code, Route Identifier, Vehicle Identifier, and Concession Class Applied; (e) Concession Eligibility Particulars, comprising Concession Category, Evidence Type Submitted, Eligibility Expiry Date, and Certifying Body Reference; (f) Personnel and Occupational Particulars, comprising Payroll Number, Position Title, Bargaining Unit, Commercial Licence Class and Endorsements, Medical Certification Status, and Regulated Testing Result Status; (g) Complaint, Claim and Incident Particulars, comprising Incident Reference Number, Complainant Role, Narrative Statement, Injury Description Class, and Insurer Claim Reference; and (h) Surveillance and Recorded Media Particulars, comprising Camera Location Code, Recording Commencement Timestamp, and Retrieval Request Reference.

3. Custody and Permitted Access. Each category is assigned to a Designated Custodian, being respectively the Fare Systems Administrator for categories (c) and (d), the Human Resources Records Officer for category (f), the Chief Safety Officer for categories (g) and (h), and the Records Custodian for all residual categories. Access is granted upon the principle of demonstrated operational necessity and not upon seniority, organisational proximity, or convenience. No officer, agent, contractor, or seconded employee shall access any category for which an access authorisation has not been recorded in the Access Register by the Designated Custodian, and no such authorisation shall be granted for an indefinite term.

4. Retention. Categories (a) through (c) shall be retained for the duration of the account relationship and for a further period of three years from its closure. Category (d) shall be retained for twenty-four months from the date of the journey to which it relates, save that aggregated and irreversibly de-identified derivatives may be retained indefinitely. Category (f) shall be retained for seven years following separation. Categories (g) and (h) shall be retained for the applicable limitation period plus one year, or, in the case of category (h) where no request has been received, for thirty days.

5. Disposition. Upon expiry of the applicable period, and absent a Legal Hold Notice issued by the General Counsel, the Designated Custodian shall effect destruction by cross-cut shredding, secure overwriting, or cryptographic key destruction, and shall certify the same by entry in the Disposition Log recording the Category Code, Volume Description, Method, and Date of Destruction.
