INTER-OFFICE MEMORANDUM

TO:      All Branch Records Custodians
FROM:    Office of the Privacy Officer
DATE:    March 2004
SUBJECT: Addendum 2 to RIM-114 — Branch Office Holdings

Effective immediately, branch offices shall hold only the categories listed at 1 through 4 below. All other categories are Home Office custody.

1. Applicant Identification. Field labels permitted at branch: Full Legal Name; Date of Birth; Social Security Number; Driver's License Number.
2. Contact and Locator Data. Field labels permitted at branch: Mailing Address; Home Telephone; Work Telephone.
3. Underwriting Risk Data. Field labels permitted at branch: Prior Carrier; Prior Policy Number; Motor Vehicle Record Code.
4. Producer Data. Field labels permitted at branch: Producer Name; Agency Name; National Producer Number.

5. Access at branch level is limited to the Branch Manager, the Underwriting Technician, and the Policy Services Clerk. Producers hold no branch file access.
6. Health and Medical Data is prohibited at branch premises in any medium. Documents received in error shall be sealed, logged, and couriered to the Home Office Medical Unit the same business day.
7. Retention at branch: 90 days from application disposition. Files are then transferred to the Home Office vault or shredded per the retention schedule, whichever the schedule directs.
8. Disposal at branch: cross-cut shredder only. Wastebasket disposal of any listed field label is a reportable incident.
9. Confirm compliance to the Privacy Officer in writing within 30 days.
