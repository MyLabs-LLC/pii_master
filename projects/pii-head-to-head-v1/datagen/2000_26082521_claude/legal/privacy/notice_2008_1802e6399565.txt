COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT — DIVISION OF HEALTH INFORMATION MANAGEMENT
RECORDS-HANDLING COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST
Reference: Policy 2016-09 (Protection of Confidential Personal Information)
Applicable to: all Divisions, Clinics, and Contracted Program Sites
Review Cycle: Annual, or upon material change to the Department's information systems

SECTION A — INVENTORY OF CATEGORIES

[ ] A.1 The Division has confirmed that its holdings of personal information fall wholly within the categories enumerated below, and has reported any category not so enumerated to the Privacy Officer within five (5) business days of discovery:
    [ ] Demographic and Identity Category — fields: Full Legal Name; Maiden or Prior Name; Date of Birth; Place of Birth; Race; Ethnicity; Marital Status.
    [ ] Unique Identifier Category — fields: Social Security Number; Driver License Number; Client Master Index Number; Case File Number; Health Plan Beneficiary Number.
    [ ] Locator Category — fields: Street Address; Apartment or Unit Designator; City; ZIP Code; Home Telephone Number; Mobile Telephone Number; Emergency Contact Name; Emergency Contact Telephone Number; Electronic Mail Address.
    [ ] Protected Health Information Category — fields: Admission Date; Discharge Date; Service Date; Diagnosis; Procedure Code; Medication and Dosage; Communicable Disease Report Number; Laboratory Specimen Identifier.
    [ ] Payment Category — fields: Guarantor Name; Insurance Group Number; Insurance Policy Number; Payment Card Number; Bank Account Number; Amount Billed; Amount Adjusted.
    [ ] Vital Records Category — fields: Certificate Number; Registrant Name; Informant Name; Parent Name; Date of Event; Cause of Death.
    [ ] Personnel and Volunteer Category — fields: Employee Number; Home Address; Emergency Contact; Immunization Status; Licensure Number; Disciplinary Action Record.

SECTION B — AUTHORIZATION OF ACCESS

[ ] B.1 Every individual with access holds a current role assignment in the Access Control Register, such register to identify the role title and not the individual, the permitted category or categories, and the permitted operation (view, amend, extract, or transmit).
[ ] B.2 Access to the Unique Identifier Category and the Payment Category is restricted, without exception, to the Billing Supervisor, the Eligibility Technician, and the Privacy Officer.
[ ] B.3 Access to the Vital Records Category is restricted to the Local Registrar and to Deputy Registrars appointed in writing.
[ ] B.4 Access to the Personnel and Volunteer Category is restricted to the Human Resources Manager and to the supervising Division Director as to that Director's own direct reports.
[ ] B.5 Contracted parties access records solely pursuant to an executed Business Associate Agreement, and solely for the categories specified therein.
[ ] B.6 Access privileges are revoked not later than the close of business on the effective date of separation, transfer, or expiration of contract.

SECTION C — RETENTION

[ ] C.1 Retention periods are applied as follows and are computed from the date of last activity unless otherwise stated:
    [ ] Adult clinical records — seven (7) years.
    [ ] Minor clinical records — until the client attains majority plus seven (7) years.
    [ ] Communicable disease investigation records — ten (10) years.
    [ ] Vital records registers — permanent.
    [ ] Billing and payment records — seven (7) years from final adjudication.
    [ ] Personnel records — the term of employment plus six (6) years.
    [ ] Volunteer and student placement records — three (3) years.
[ ] C.2 No record subject to a litigation hold, audit hold, or public records request has been destroyed, notwithstanding the expiration of its retention period.

SECTION D — DISPOSAL

[ ] D.1 Paper records are cross-cut shredded to a particle size not exceeding that specified in the Department's contract for confidential destruction services.
[ ] D.2 Electronic media are sanitized by overwriting or degaussing, or are physically destroyed, prior to release from Department custody.
[ ] D.3 A Certificate of Destruction has been executed by the Records Custodian and countersigned by a witness, and states the categories destroyed, the volume, the method, and the date.
[ ] D.4 Certificates of Destruction are retained permanently and are produced upon the request of the Health Officer, County Counsel, or an authorized auditor.

Certified by: the Division Director
Reviewed by: the Privacy Officer
