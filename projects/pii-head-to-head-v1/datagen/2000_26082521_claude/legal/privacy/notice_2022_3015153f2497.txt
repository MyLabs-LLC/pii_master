ELM RIDGE ELEMENTARY — SITE RECORDS HANDLING STANDARD
Issued by the Records Custodian, September 2004. Post at the main office counter.

Categories kept at this site:
  - Enrolment and identity fields
  - Emergency contact and guardian fields
  - Attendance and tardy records
  - Health office log, medication authorisations, immunisation record
  - Meal benefit status indicator
  - Referral and conduct incident forms
  - Working copies of report cards

Who may open a cumulative folder:
  - The principal and assistant principal
  - The office manager and attendance clerk
  - The classroom teacher of record, for pupils on their roster only
  - The school nurse, for the health portions only
  - A parent or guardian of that pupil, by appointment, in the office, in the presence of a staff member

How long we keep it here:
  - Active cumulative folder — while the pupil is enrolled
  - Attendance registers — 3 years
  - Health office daily log — 2 years
  - Meal benefit status — 3 years after the programme year
  - Working copies of report cards — destroy at the end of the school year; the permanent record stays with the transcript at the District office

When a pupil leaves:
  - Forward the cumulative folder to the receiving school within 10 working days of the records request
  - Keep no copy at this site except the transfer receipt

Disposal:
  - Nothing leaves this office in an unshredded state
  - Shred at the counter machine or use the locked bin only
  - Log every destruction on the site destruction sheet and send a copy to the Records Custodian in July
