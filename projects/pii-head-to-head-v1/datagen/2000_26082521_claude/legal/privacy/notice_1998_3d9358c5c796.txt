BULLETIN NO. 99-07
TO ALL BRANCH MANAGERS AND DEPARTMENT HEADS
RE: ADDENDUM TO STANDARD RM-04 — TREATMENT OF THE COMPLAINT AND DISPUTE FILE (CATEGORY J)

1. The Category J file is hereby confirmed as a distinct record series and shall not be interfiled with the Credit and Lending File or with the Personnel File.

2. Category J comprises the fields designated Complainant Role, Member Number, Date of Receipt, Nature of Grievance Code, Account Reference, Investigating Officer, Disposition Code, and Date of Closure.

3. Access to Category J is restricted to the Compliance Officer, the Supervisory Committee, and the officer assigned to investigate the individual matter. A branch manager may inspect only those items originating at their own branch.

4. The minimum retention period is three (3) years from the Date of Closure, extended to seven (7) years where the Nature of Grievance Code denotes an allegation of discrimination, unfair collection practice, or error in a consumer report.

5. Disposition shall be effected in accordance with Clause 5 of Standard RM-04. No branch shall dispose of Category J material locally; all such material shall be transmitted under seal to the Records Custodian for centralised destruction.

6. Direct all questions to the Compliance Officer. This Bulletin takes effect immediately.
