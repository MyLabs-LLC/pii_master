RIVERBEND COMMUNITY CREDIT UNION
ADMINISTRATIVE STANDARD AP-114
MEMBER INFORMATION: CLASSIFICATION, ACCESS, RETENTION AND DISPOSAL

Issued by: Office of the Corporate Secretary
Approved by: Board Audit and Compliance Committee, Resolution 97-22
Effective Date: 1 September 1997
Supersedes: AP-109 (Records Retention and Destruction), dated 14 March 1994
Review Cycle: Biennial, or upon material amendment of applicable law

1.0 PURPOSE AND AUTHORITY

1.1 This Standard is promulgated under the general records authority vested in the Corporate Secretary by Article IX of the Bylaws, and is binding upon all officers, employees, temporary staff, volunteer directors, committee members, and contracted service providers of the Credit Union (collectively, "Covered Persons").

1.2 Nothing herein shall be construed to diminish any obligation arising under federal or state law, examination directive, or the terms of any executed confidentiality undertaking; where a conflict arises, the more restrictive requirement shall govern.

2.0 CLASSIFICATION OF MEMBER INFORMATION

2.1 All information concerning a natural person maintained by the Credit Union in any medium, whether originated internally or received from a third party, shall be assigned to one and only one of the categories enumerated in Clauses 2.2 through 2.6.

2.2 Category I — Identity and Verification Data. Comprising the data elements captured under the field labels Surname; Given Name; Middle Initial; Former or Maiden Surname; Date of Birth; Taxpayer Identification Number; Government-Issued Identification Type; Government-Issued Identification Number; Mother's Maiden Surname.

2.3 Category II — Contact and Location Data. Comprising the data elements captured under the field labels Residence Street Address; Mailing Address; Postal Code; Home Telephone Number; Business Telephone Number; Electronic Mail Address.

2.4 Category III — Financial Relationship Data. Comprising the data elements captured under the field labels Member Number; Share Account Number; Share Draft Account Number; Certificate Number; Loan Account Number; Card Account Number; Current Balance; Transaction History; Delinquency Status.

2.5 Category IV — Underwriting and Assessment Data. Comprising the data elements captured under the field labels Employer Name; Occupation; Gross Monthly Income; Consumer Reporting Agency Score; Debt-to-Income Ratio; Collateral Description; Appraised Value.

2.6 Category V — Employment and Benefits Data pertaining to Covered Persons, comprising the data elements captured under the field labels Personnel Number; Position Title; Compensation Rate; Performance Rating; Beneficiary Designation; Payroll Deduction Authorization.

3.0 ACCESS AUTHORIZATION

3.1 Access to any category shall be granted solely upon the concurrent satisfaction of (a) a documented business necessity, and (b) a written authorization recorded by the Records Custodian in the Access Authorization Register.

3.2 Categories I and II may be accessed by the Member Services Representative, the Branch Manager, and the Compliance Officer. Category III may be accessed by the foregoing and by the Accounting Supervisor. Category IV may be accessed only by the Loan Officer, the Credit Committee, and the Compliance Officer. Category V may be accessed only by the Human Resources Manager and the Chief Executive Officer.

3.3 No Covered Person shall access any record pertaining to that Covered Person's own account, or to the account of any relative within the second degree, without the prior written concurrence of the Compliance Officer.

4.0 RETENTION PERIODS

4.1 Category I and Category II records shall be retained for the life of the membership relationship plus five (5) years.

4.2 Category III records shall be retained for seven (7) years following closure of the applicable account, save that records subject to litigation hold shall be retained until the hold is released in writing.

4.3 Category IV records shall be retained for twenty-five (25) months following final disposition of the credit application, whether approved, denied, or withdrawn.

4.4 Category V records shall be retained for seven (7) years following separation from service.

5.0 DISPOSAL

5.1 Upon expiry of the applicable retention period, the Records Custodian shall schedule the affected records for destruction and shall obtain the countersignature of the Compliance Officer upon the Certificate of Destruction.

5.2 Paper media shall be cross-cut shredded to a particle size not exceeding 5/32 inch by 1-1/2 inches. Magnetic media shall be degaussed and thereafter physically deformed. Microfiche shall be incinerated.

5.3 Destruction shall be witnessed by two Covered Persons, neither of whom may be the Records Custodian, and the Certificate of Destruction shall be retained permanently.

6.0 EXCEPTIONS

6.1 Exceptions to this Standard require the written approval of the Board Audit and Compliance Committee and shall be reported at the next regular meeting of the Board of Directors.
