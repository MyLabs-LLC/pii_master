ASHCROFT LOGISTICS GROUP
OFFICE MEMORANDUM

To:      All Administrative and Supervisory Staff
From:    The Records Custodian, Head Office
Date:    23 June 2004
Ref:     ALG/OM/2004/31
Subject: Personal Information — Categories, Custody, Retention, Destruction

(a) Categories held. The Group holds the following categories and no others.
Employment Identification (Surname; Forenames; Home Address; Home Telephone
Number; Date of Birth; National Insurance Number; Payroll Number). Contract
Terms (Job Title; Location; Start Date; Notice Period; Salary Band). Banking
(Bank Name; Sort Code; Account Number). Statutory Deductions (Tax Code; Tax
District Reference; Pension Scheme Number). Driving Entitlement (Licence
Number; Categories; Expiry Date; Endorsements). Working Time (Duty Start; Duty
End; Break Taken; Weekly Total). Absence (First Day; Last Day; Reason Code;
Certificate Received). Medical (Examination Date; Outcome; Restriction).
Discipline (Date; Category of Conduct; Sanction; Expiry). Security Vetting
(Reference Requested; Reference Received; Result). Customer Contact (Contact
Name; Position; Telephone Number; Delivery Site).

(b) Custody. Each category is assigned to a single custodian role, and the
paper file and any system record are held in that custodian's charge. The
Personnel Officer holds Employment Identification, Contract Terms, Absence,
Discipline and Security Vetting. The Payroll Officer holds Banking and
Statutory Deductions. The Transport Manager holds Driving Entitlement and
Working Time. The Personnel Officer holds Medical in a separate locked cabinet.
The Customer Services Supervisor holds Customer Contact.

(c) Access by others. A supervisor may see only what is necessary for a stated
task, on application to the custodian, and the application is recorded. Banking
and Medical are not disclosed to supervisors in any circumstances. Nothing in
any category is disclosed outside the Group other than to the Inland Revenue,
the Traffic Commissioner, the Group's insurers, or on the written instruction
of the Company Secretary.

(d) Retention. Employment Identification, Contract Terms, Banking, Statutory
Deductions, Working Time and Absence are kept for six years from the end of the
tax year in which employment ends. Driving Entitlement is kept for four years
after employment ends. Medical is kept for six years unless a longer statutory
period applies. Discipline is destroyed at the expiry stated in the sanction.
Security Vetting is destroyed twelve months after appointment or refusal.
Customer Contact is kept for two years from the last transaction.

(e) Destruction. On the expiry of the period the custodian passes the file to
the Records Custodian, who destroys it by cross-cut shredder or, for magnetic
media, by physical destruction, and enters the category, quantity and date in
the Destruction Register. No member of staff is to destroy any of the above
categories on their own authority, and no such record is to be removed from
company premises.
