NORTHGATE DISTRIBUTION (UK) LIMITED
RECORDS STANDARD RS-11: PERSONAL INFORMATION CATEGORIES, ACCESS AND RETENTION
Effective 06 September 2021 | Supersedes RS-11 v1.0 | Owner: Records Custodian

1. SCOPE

This standard applies to all personal information processed by the company in the course of transport, warehousing and support operations, in any medium, at any site and on any device issued by the company. It applies to permanent staff, agency staff and subcontracted drivers.

3. ACCESS CONTROL

Access is granted by category, not by individual record. The access group named against each code is the sole group entitled to standing access. Any other access requires a written request to the Records Custodian stating the code, the purpose and the period required. Standing access is reviewed at each quarter end and withdrawn on change of role.

4. RETENTION

Retention is measured from the trigger event stated in column four. Categories PD3 and PD7 are subject to fixed statutory or scheme limits and may not be extended. Extension of any other category requires the approval of the Records Custodian and must be recorded against the code in the retention schedule.

5. DISPOSAL

Disposal is initiated by the Records Custodian on expiry of the retention period and executed by the access group holding the category. Confidential waste is uplifted under sealed consignment. Secure erasure is applied at file and backup level. Completion is certified within ten working days and the certificate reference entered against the code.
