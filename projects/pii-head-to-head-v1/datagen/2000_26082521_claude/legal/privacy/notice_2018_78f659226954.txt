OFFICE OF THE UNIVERSITY REGISTRAR
INTERNAL NOTICE — RECORDS HANDLING
Effective January 4, 2016

Personal information held by this office falls into four handling classes.

CLASS 1 — DIRECTORY
  Legal Name, Program of Study, Enrollment Status, Dates of Attendance, Degree Awarded, Honors Designation.
  Access: any university employee; released externally unless the Directory Information Suppression Flag is set.
  Retention: permanent.

CLASS 2 — RESTRICTED ACADEMIC
  Student Identification Number, Course Registration Record, Term Grade, Cumulative Grade Point Average, Academic Standing Action.
  Access: Registrar staff and Designated School Officials with a legitimate educational interest.
  Retention: permanent.

CLASS 3 — RESTRICTED PERSONAL
  Date of Birth, Permanent Mailing Address, Telephone Number, University Electronic Mail Address, Emergency Contact Name.
  Access: Registrar staff only.
  Retention: five years after last term of attendance.

CLASS 4 — CONFIDENTIAL
  Consent to Release Form, Disclosure Log Entry, Protective Order Flag, Judicial Order Entry.
  Access: Records Custodian; University Counsel on written request.
  Retention: permanent, stored separately from the academic file.

Disposal of Class 3 records is carried out annually in July by the Records Custodian. Paper is shredded on premises; electronic records are deleted and the deletion entered in the disposal log. Staff must not create local copies of Class 2, 3, or 4 records on desktop drives or removable media.
