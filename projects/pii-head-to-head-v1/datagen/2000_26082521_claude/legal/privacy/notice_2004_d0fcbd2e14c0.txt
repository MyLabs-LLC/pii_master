MEMORANDUM — INTERNAL

TO:           Division Managers; Escrow Officers; Title Officers; Information Systems Manager
FROM:         Chief Compliance Officer
DATE:         11 July 2011
SUBJECT:      Standard RM-07, Revision 2 — Personal Information Inventory, Access Control, Retention, and Disposal
DISTRIBUTION: All offices; posted to the compliance intranet
SUPERSEDES:   Standard RM-07, Revision 1 (issued 2009)

1.0  PURPOSE AND AUTHORITY

1.1  This standard establishes the authoritative inventory of the categories of personal information held by the Company, fixes the permissible access population for each, and prescribes retention periods and disposal method.

1.2  It is issued under the Company's Information Security Program and gives effect to the safeguards obligations at 16 C.F.R. Part 314, the disposal obligations at 16 C.F.R. Part 682, and the privacy regulations of each state insurance department in which the Company is licensed. Where a state rule imposes a longer period than Section 4.0, the state rule controls.

2.0  INVENTORY OF CATEGORIES

2.1  Category A — Identity Data. Fields maintained: Full Legal Name; Former or Maiden Name; Date of Birth; Marital Status; Specimen Signature; Notarial Identification Description.

2.2  Category B — Government Identifiers. Fields maintained: Social Security Number; Individual Taxpayer Identification Number; Driver's License or State Identification Number; Passport Number; Employer Identification Number of a party-entity.

2.3  Category C — Financial Data. Fields maintained: Deposit Account Number; ABA Routing Number; Wire Instruction Detail; Loan Number; Payoff Balance; Earnest Money Amount; Source of Funds Statement; Seller Proceeds Instruction.

2.4  Category D — Transaction Data. Fields maintained: Property Address; Assessor's Parcel Number; Legal Description; Vesting Instruction; Order Number; Policy Number.

2.5  Category E — Personnel Data. Fields maintained: Home Address; Personal Telephone Number; Compensation Rate; Withholding Election; Beneficiary Designation; Background Check Disposition.

3.0  ACCESS CONTROL

3.1  Categories A through D are accessible to the assigned escrow officer, the assigned title officer, the branch manager of record, the funding desk, and the Records Custodian.

3.2  Category B is additionally restricted: the field values are masked in all screen views and reports except the reporting module operated by the tax reporting clerk, and except upon written authorization of the Chief Compliance Officer.

3.3  Category E is accessible only to the Human Resources Manager, the payroll administrator, and the officer to whom the subject reports.

3.4  No category may be transmitted outside the Company network except through the approved secure delivery portal. Removable media is prohibited.

4.0  RETENTION SCHEDULE

  Category | Record Series             | Retention Trigger        | Period   | Disposal Method
  ---------|---------------------------|--------------------------|----------|------------------
  A        | Closed escrow file        | Final disbursement       | 5 years  | Shred / wipe
  A        | Closing protection file   | Final disbursement       | 7 years  | Shred / wipe
  B        | Tax reporting workpapers  | End of reporting year    | 4 years  | Shred

  C        | Wire authorisation forms  | Date of wire             | 7 years  | Shred
  D        | Title evidence and policy | Date of policy issuance  | Permanent| Not applicable
  E        | Personnel file            | Separation of employment | 7 years  | Shred

5.0  DISPOSAL PROCEDURE

5.1  The Records Custodian produces a quarterly Eligible-for-Destruction list from the records management system.

5.2  The list is circulated to the Chief Compliance Officer and to General Counsel for legal hold clearance. No item proceeds without both clearances.

5.3  Cleared paper is cross-cut shredded on premises, or collected by the bonded vendor in locked consoles and destroyed under certificate. Cleared electronic records are purged from production and from backup rotation at the next scheduled cycle.

5.4  The Destruction Register is completed under the headings Batch Number, Category, Record Series, Volume, Method, Vendor Certificate Number, Date, and Approving Officer, and is retained permanently.

6.0  LEGAL HOLD

6.1  A hold notice issued by General Counsel suspends all destruction for the identified population until released in writing.

7.0  REVIEW

7.1  This standard is reviewed annually by the Chief Compliance Officer and the Records Custodian, and upon any material change to licensing jurisdiction or systems architecture.
