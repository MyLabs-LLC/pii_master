B.  ACCESS GRADING

Grade 1 — Teller line. IDENT and REGISTRY, display only, no printing.
Grade 2 — Member Services. Grades 1 plus CONTACT and AUTH, amendment allowed
          on CONTACT only.
Grade 3 — Lending. Grades 1 and 2 plus EMPLOY and CREDIT.
Grade 4 — Collections. Grades 1 and 2 plus COLLECT.
Grade 5 — Compliance, Internal Audit, Records Custodian. All categories,
          display and print, logged by the host.

Grades are assigned in writing by the Branch Manager and are withdrawn on the
last working day of an employee. The Data Processing Supervisor produces a
grade register on the first business day of each month for the Compliance
Officer to countersign.

C.  RETENTION

IDENT, CONTACT, AUTH and REGISTRY: six years from closure of the final share.
EMPLOY and CREDIT: twenty-five months from decision where credit is declined;
life of loan plus six years where credit is granted.
COLLECT: seven years from charge-off or final recovery, whichever is later.

D.  DISPOSAL

At term, the Records Custodian withdraws the file, strikes the record from the
inventory index, and places the material in the locked destruction bin. Bins
are emptied fortnightly under the observation of two employees. Screen prints
and working notes bearing any labelled field above are treated as records of
their own category and are placed in the same bins; they may not be discarded
in ordinary waste. Destruction is recorded by category, volume and date.
