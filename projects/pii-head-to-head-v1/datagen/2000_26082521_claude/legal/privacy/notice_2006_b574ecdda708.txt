REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSION — RECORDS HANDLING STANDARD 04-16
ANNUAL CERTIFICATION CHECKLIST FOR DIVISIONAL RECORDS OFFICERS
Return to the Privacy Coordinator by 31 December 2004.

Inventory verification
[ ] Every holding of personal information in the division is listed on the divisional Inventory extract.
[ ] Each entry states its category, its custodial unit, and its retention trigger.
[ ] Each entry lists the field labels held — including Full Name, Date of Birth, Social Security Number, Residential Address, Telephone Number, Employee Number, Account Number, Card Serial Number, Licence Number, and Determination Code where applicable.
[ ] Holdings created since the last certification are added; discontinued holdings are marked closed with the closure date.
[ ] Shadow files, desk copies, and spreadsheets held outside the system of record are identified or eliminated.

Access verification
[ ] Access to each category is restricted to the role titles named in Standard PR-04 or a divisional annexe approved by the Privacy Coordinator.
[ ] The access list has been reconciled against the current establishment; leavers and transfers are removed.
[ ] Clinical and regulated testing content is held apart from personnel content and is not visible to supervisory roles.
[ ] Payment instrument fields are masked in all customer-facing screens.
[ ] Contractor access is limited to assigned files and is covered by a confidentiality undertaking on file.
[ ] Disclosures to external bodies during the year are recorded in the Disclosure Log with the columns: Date; Category; Recipient Body; Legal Basis; Approving Officer Title.

Retention verification
[ ] Records past their retention period are identified and queued for destruction.
[ ] Records under litigation hold are flagged and excluded from the destruction queue.
[ ] Off-site storage manifests agree with the divisional Inventory.

Disposal verification
[ ] Paper destroyed by cross-cut shredding or by certified contractor under manifest.
[ ] Magnetic media degaussed; optical media shredded; no medium released for reuse or resale.
[ ] Certificates of destruction filed and cross-referenced to the Disposal Register.
[ ] Disposal Register entries are complete and signed by the Records Custodian.
