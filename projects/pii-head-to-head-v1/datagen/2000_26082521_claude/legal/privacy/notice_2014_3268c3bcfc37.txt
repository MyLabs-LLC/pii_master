BOARD POLICY 5-018 (ADOPTED 2016)
ACCESS TO AND DISCLOSURE OF PERSONAL INFORMATION CATEGORIES

    ARTICLE 1 — GENERAL RULE
        (a)  Personal information held by the District is confidential and is disclosed only as this policy permits.
        (b)  Classification of a record into a category is determined by content, not by the office holding it.
        (c)  Aggregation of two or more categories creates a compound record governed by the most restrictive component.

    ARTICLE 2 — INTERNAL ACCESS
        (a)  Access is assigned to a position, never to an individual, and lapses automatically on reassignment or separation.
        (b)  Permitted positions by category:
             (i)    Directory Information — Registrar; front-office administrative staff.
             (ii)   Education Record — Principal; Assistant Principal; Registrar; assigned instructional staff limited to the pupils they serve.
             (iii)  Special Education Record — Case Manager; Director of Student Services; assessment personnel named on the evaluation team.
             (iv)   Health Record — School Nurse; substitute nurse under supervision.
             (v)    Nutrition Eligibility Record — Nutrition Services Manager; determining official.
             (vi)   Personnel Record — Director of Human Resources; payroll designee; the supervising administrator, limited to the evaluation and leave elements.
             (vii)  Network and Device Records — Director of Technology; systems administrator designee.
             (viii) Surveillance Footage — Director of Safety and Security.
        (c)  The Records Custodian holds read access to all categories for the sole purpose of inventory, retention and disposition administration.

    ARTICLE 3 — EXTERNAL DISCLOSURE
        (a)  Disclosure to a receiving school district upon transfer is permitted without consent.
        (b)  Disclosure in response to lawful process is permitted following notice to the parent or eligible pupil, except where notice is prohibited by the instrument.
        (c)  Disclosure to a contracted service provider is permitted only where the contract contains the approved data-protection schedule, restricts onward transfer, and requires return or destruction at termination.
        (d)  All other disclosures require prior written consent.

    ARTICLE 4 — RECORD OF DISCLOSURE
        (a)  Every external disclosure is entered in the Disclosure Register.
        (b)  Register columns: Entry Number; Category Reference; Requesting Party Type; Legal Basis; Scope Released; Approving Officer Title; Date of Release.
        (c)  The Register accompanies the underlying record for the life of that record.

    ARTICLE 5 — RESPONSIBILITY
        (a)  The Records Custodian reports annually to the Board on access exceptions, disclosure volumes, and completed dispositions.
        (b)  Unauthorised access is a disciplinary matter and is reported to the Superintendent within one working day of detection.
