SUBJECT: Standard for the Holding, Access, Retention and Disposal of
         Personal Information

1. PURPOSE

1.1 This standard sets out the categories of personal information held by the
company, the roles permitted to see each category, the period for which each
category is kept, and the manner in which it is to be destroyed. It applies to
information held on paper, on the personnel and payroll systems, on depot
terminals, and on removable media.

1.2 The standard is issued under the company's obligations as a data controller
and is to be read alongside the Employee Handbook and the Depot Security
Instruction.

2. CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD

2.1 Employee Identification Category. Fields: Surname; Forenames; Former Name;
Date of Birth; Home Address; Home Telephone Number; National Insurance Number;
Emergency Contact Name; Emergency Contact Telephone Number; Works Number.

2.2 Driver Licensing and Competence Category. Fields: Driving Licence Number;
Licence Categories Held; Licence Expiry Date; Endorsement Code; Endorsement
Date; Tachograph Card Number; Dangerous Goods Certificate Number; Fork Lift
Truck Authorisation Reference; Induction Date; Refresher Training Date.

2.3 Remuneration Category. Fields: Grade; Hourly Rate; Overtime Rate; Bank
Name; Sort Code; Account Number; Tax Code; Student Loan Indicator; Pension
Scheme Membership Number; Attachment of Earnings Reference.

2.4 Attendance and Working Time Category. Fields: Shift Pattern; Hours Worked;
Rest Period Taken; Absence Start Date; Absence End Date; Absence Reason Code;
Holiday Entitlement Balance.

2.5 Occupational Health Category. Fields: Medical Examination Date; Fitness
Outcome; Restriction Noted; Review Date; Eyesight Test Date.

2.6 Conduct and Grievance Category. Fields: Incident Date; Incident Type Code;
Stage of Procedure; Outcome; Expiry Date of Warning.

2.7 Recruitment Category. Fields: Applicant Reference; Post Applied For;
Previous Employer; Referee Name; Referee Address; Interview Score; Decision.

2.8 External Contact Category. Fields: Consignee Contact Name; Consignee
Telephone Number; Delivery Instruction; Signature Image; Proof of Delivery
Reference.

3. AUTHORISED ACCESS

3.1 Categories 2.1, 2.4, 2.6 and 2.7 may be accessed by the Personnel Officer
and, for staff under their direct supervision only, the relevant Depot Manager.

3.2 Category 2.2 may be accessed by the Transport Manager, the Traffic
Supervisor on duty, and the Records Custodian.

3.3 Category 2.3 may be accessed by the Payroll Officer and the Company
Secretary. Depot management have no access to this category.

3.4 Category 2.5 may be accessed only by the Personnel Officer and the retained
occupational health adviser. Line management receive the fitness outcome and
any restriction, and nothing further.

3.5 Category 2.8 may be accessed by depot clerical staff and the Customer
Services Supervisor.

3.6 Any access outside the above must be authorised in writing by the Records
Custodian and entered in the Access Register.

4. RETENTION PERIODS

4.1 Employee Identification, Remuneration, Attendance: six years from the end
of the tax year in which employment ends.

4.2 Driver Licensing and Competence: for the duration of employment plus four
years.

4.3 Occupational Health: forty years from the date of the last examination
where exposure records apply; otherwise six years from termination.

4.4 Conduct and Grievance: warnings are removed from the file at the expiry
date shown; grievance papers are kept six years from closure.

4.5 Recruitment (unsuccessful applicants): twelve months from the decision.

4.6 External Contact: two years from the date of delivery.

5. DISPOSAL

5.1 Paper is to be placed in the locked confidential waste cabinet at each
depot and destroyed by cross-cut shredding. General waste bins must not be
used for any category listed at section 2.

5.2 Magnetic media are to be degaussed or physically destroyed. Deletion of a
file from a terminal is not sufficient disposal.

5.3 The Records Custodian maintains a Destruction Log recording the category,
the volume, the date of destruction and the authorising role. The log is kept
indefinitely.

6. REVIEW

This standard is reviewed annually by the Records Custodian. Queries are to be
directed to the Records Custodian at Head Office.
