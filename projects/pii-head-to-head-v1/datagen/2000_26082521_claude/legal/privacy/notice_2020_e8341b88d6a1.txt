STANDING INSTRUCTION SI-2011/09
CUSTODY, ACCESS AND DESTRUCTION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION
Issuing Authority: Corporate Secretariat. Circulation: All Directorates. Classification: Internal.

PART I — CATEGORIES OF HOLDING
 » C-1 Establishment Records. Fields: full name, previous name, date of birth, employee number, national insurance number, home address, personal contact number, emergency contact name.
 » C-2 Financial Instruction Records. Fields: bank sort code, bank account number, tax reference, pension scheme number, deduction order reference.
 » C-3 Fitness and Occupational Health Records. Fields: employee number, assessment date, fitness classification, restriction code, absence dates.
 » C-4 Vocational Licensing Records. Fields: name, licence number, licence category, endorsement code, medical expiry date.
 » C-5 Customer Account Records. Fields: name, postal address, e-mail address, telephone number, smartcard serial number, stored payment token, concession class.
 » C-6 Travel Movement Records. Fields: card serial number, station or vehicle identifier, timestamp, fare value.
 » C-7 Image and Audio Capture Records. Fields: device identifier, location, capture period, recorded content.
 » C-8 Correspondence, Claims and Litigation Records. Fields: name, contact details, narrative content, witness name, medical report reference, settlement figure.
 » C-9 Candidate Records. Fields: name, contact details, employment history, referee name, referee contact details, assessment score.

PART II — CUSTODY AND PERMITTED ACCESS
 » 1. Each category is assigned a single accountable post, recorded in the Custodianship Schedule.
 » 2. C-1, C-3 and C-9 are held by the Human Resources Records Custodian.
 » 3. C-2 is held by the Payroll Supervisor; access is restricted to two named posts at any time.
 » 4. C-4 is held by the Driver Standards Assessor.
 » 5. C-5 and C-6 are held by the Revenue Systems Administrator.
 » 6. C-7 is held by the Security Control Room Supervisor.
 » 7. C-8 is held by the Legal Services Manager.
 » 8. Access outside the assigned post requires a completed Information Access Request approved by the Privacy Officer, retained for the life of the record.
 » 9. Bulk extraction of any category to portable media is prohibited without written dispensation.

PART III — RETENTION
 » 10. C-1: six years from separation. C-2: seven complete tax years.
 » 11. C-3: forty years where statutory exposure monitoring applies; otherwise six years from assessment.
 » 12. C-4: four years from licence surrender or expiry.
 » 13. C-5: two years from account closure. C-6: fifty-six days from journey.
 » 14. C-7: thirty-one days from capture. C-8: six years from final settlement. C-9: twelve months from outcome.
 » 15. Any hold notice issued by the Legal Services Manager suspends the applicable clock until formally lifted in writing.

PART IV — DESTRUCTION
 » 16. Destruction is initiated only on the quarterly expiry report countersigned by the accountable post.
 » 17. Approved methods: cross-cut shredding; pulping under supervision; secure overwrite; degaussing; physical comminution of solid-state media.
 » 18. Contractor collections are conducted under sealed consignment with a Certificate of Destruction returned within ten working days.
 » 19. Image and audio holdings are overwritten by automatic cycle; manual deletion is a reportable event.
 » 20. Every destruction event is entered in the Destruction Register recording category reference, volume, method, date, authorising post and certificate number.
 » 21. The Privacy Officer audits the Destruction Register twice yearly and reports exceptions to the Information Governance Committee.
