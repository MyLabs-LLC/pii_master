MEMORANDUM

TO:        All Department Heads; All Staff with Systems Access
FROM:      Office of the Records Custodian
DATE:      March 2011
SUBJECT:   Standard PRS-04 — Classification, Access, Retention and Disposal of Personal Information Held by the Utility

1. PURPOSE

This Standard establishes uniform handling requirements for records containing personal information created or received by the Utility in the course of providing water, wastewater and electric service. It supersedes Standard PRS-03 and all departmental practice notes inconsistent with it.

2. SCOPE

This Standard applies to personal information in any medium — paper, microform, magnetic, optical or electronic — regardless of the system of record in which it resides, and regardless of whether the record is held by the Utility directly or by a contracted service provider acting on the Utility's behalf.

3. CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

The Utility recognises five categories. Each category is defined by the data elements it contains, not by the department that collects it.

Category A — Customer Identification.
Data elements: full name; service address; mailing address where different; primary telephone number; secondary telephone number; electronic mail address; customer account number; date of account establishment.

Category B — Customer Financial.
Data elements: bank routing and deposit account numbers held for automatic payment authorisation; payment card number and expiry, where retained; deposit amount and refund status; arrears balance; payment arrangement terms; collection referral status; credit assessment score obtained at account opening.

Category C — Consumption and Premises.
Data elements: meter identifier; meter read values and read dates; interval consumption data where advanced metering is installed; service connection and disconnection dates; premises access notes; medical necessity flag and its supporting certification date.

Category D — Employee and Applicant.
Data elements: full name; home address; home and mobile telephone numbers; social security number; date of birth; dependant names and dates of birth; job classification and salary grade; performance evaluation ratings; disciplinary action records; leave balances and absence reasons; benefit enrolment elections; direct deposit account number; emergency contact name and telephone number; pre-employment background screening result.

Category E — Vendor and Contractor Principals.
Data elements: principal's name; business and personal telephone numbers; taxpayer identification number; insurance certificate holder details; licence or certification number and expiry.

4. ACCESS AUTHORISATION

Access is granted by category, by role, on the principle of least privilege. The following table records the authorised roles.

Category A    Customer Service Representative (read/write); Billing Clerk (read); Field Service Technician (read, address and access notes only); Records Custodian (read/write).

Category B    Billing Supervisor (read/write); Treasury Clerk (read); Collections Officer (read); Internal Auditor (read). Payment card fields are masked to all roles except Treasury Clerk.

Category C    Meter Operations Supervisor (read/write); Billing Clerk (read); Engineering Analyst (read, de-identified extracts only); Customer Service Representative (read, medical necessity flag only).

Category D    Human Resources Director (read/write); Human Resources Generalist (read/write); Payroll Administrator (read, compensation and deposit fields); Department Head (read, own reports only, excluding social security number and dependant elements); General Counsel (read, on written request).

Category E    Purchasing Manager (read/write); Accounts Payable Clerk (read); Internal Auditor (read).

Requests for access outside the above matrix must be submitted in writing to the Records Custodian and are granted only for a defined purpose and a defined period, and are logged.

5. RETENTION PERIODS

Retention runs from the trigger event stated for each category.

Category A — six years from closure of the account.
Category B — six years from closure of the account; payment card fields purged upon completion of the authorised transaction and in no case retained beyond ninety days.
Category C — five years from the read date; interval consumption data thirteen months from the read date, except where subject to a billing dispute, in which case until final resolution plus one year; medical necessity certifications for the duration of the flag plus three years.
Category D — personnel files, seven years from separation; payroll records, seven years from the tax year to which they relate; pre-employment screening results, three years from the date of the hiring decision; unsuccessful applicant records, two years from the date of the decision.
Category E — seven years from the expiry or termination of the contract.

Where a record is subject to litigation hold, audit, open records request or regulatory inquiry, the retention period is suspended and the record is not destroyed until the Records Custodian issues a written release.

6. DISPOSAL

Paper records are destroyed by cross-cut shredding to a particle size not exceeding 5 mm by 30 mm, performed on Utility premises or by a bonded contractor under supervision. Microform is incinerated. Magnetic media are degaussed and then physically destroyed. Optical media are shredded or abraded across the full recording surface. Electronic records held in production systems are purged by the assigned system administrator and the purge confirmed in writing to the Records Custodian; backup copies age out under the standard backup rotation and are not restored for the purpose of retrieving purged records.

A Certificate of Destruction is prepared for every disposal event. It records the category, the volume, the medium, the destruction method, the date, and the signatures of the Records Custodian and the witnessing supervisor. Certificates are retained permanently.

7. RESPONSIBILITIES

Department Heads maintain a current inventory of the categories held by their department and report changes to the Records Custodian within thirty days. The Records Custodian maintains the master inventory, administers the access matrix, and conducts an annual review of this Standard. The Information Systems Manager implements technical access controls consistent with Section 4 and produces access logs on request.

8. NON-COMPLIANCE

Failure to observe this Standard is grounds for withdrawal of systems access and for disciplinary action under the applicable personnel policy.

Questions regarding interpretation should be directed to the Office of the Records Custodian.
