CRESTLINE PHARMACY GROUP
RECORDS HANDLING STANDARD RHS-04, REV. 2
INFORMATION CATEGORIES, ACCESS, RETENTION AND DISPOSAL

Issued: 14 June 2004
Owner: Office of the Privacy Officer
Applies to: All retail locations, Central Fill, and Corporate Services

This standard fixes the categories of personal information held by the Group, the roles
permitted to view each category, the retention period, and the approved disposal route.
No category may be created, merged, or extended without written approval of the Records
Custodian. Field labels comprising each category are enumerated in Data Dictionary DD-1.1.

+------+---------------------------+----------------------------+----------------------+------------------+
| CODE | INFORMATION CATEGORY      | ACCESS PERMITTED TO        | RETENTION PERIOD     | DISPOSAL METHOD  |
+------+---------------------------+----------------------------+----------------------+------------------+
| A-1  | Patient Identification    | Pharmacist-in-Charge       | 10 yrs from last fill | Cross-cut shred |
| A-2  | Prescription Record       | Licensed Pharmacy Staff    | 10 yrs from last fill | Cross-cut shred |
| A-3  | Third-Party Benefit Data  | Claims Adjudication Clerk  | 7 yrs from plan close | Cross-cut shred |
| A-4  | Payment Authorisation Data| Store Manager (audit only) | 18 months from batch  | Secure media purge |
| A-5  | Employee Personnel Record | HR Records Administrator   | 7 yrs from separation | Cross-cut shred |
| A-6  | Loyalty Programme Profile | Category Marketing Analyst | 3 yrs from last visit | Logical delete  |
+------+---------------------------+----------------------------+----------------------+------------------+

Access is granted on the minimum necessary principle. Requests for access outside the
role shown above are logged by the Records Custodian and expire after thirty days.

Disposal is performed only into locked collection containers serviced by the contracted
destruction vendor, or by the approved overwrite utility for electronic media. Each
disposal event is recorded in the Destruction Log by category code, volume, and date.
