COUNTY OF WESTBROOK
DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC HEALTH

STANDARD 7.2 — CONFIDENTIALITY OF PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD BY THE DEPARTMENT
Adopted by the Board of Health, 14 January 1998
Review cycle: biennial
Custodian of record: the Departmental Records Custodian

1. CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD

   1.1 Registration data
       o name fields
       o date of birth field
       o sex code
       o residence address fields
       o telephone contact field
       o preferred language code
       o next-of-kin name and relationship fields

   1.2 Programme participation data
       o clinic enrolment number
       o eligibility determination fields
       o household composition fields
       o income verification fields
       o benefit issuance fields

   1.3 Health service data
       o encounter date fields
       o chief complaint narrative
       o diagnosis and procedure code fields
       o prescription and dispensing fields
       o referral and consultation fields
       o laboratory order and result fields

   1.4 Regulatory and investigative data
       o communicable disease report fields
       o contact tracing name and locating fields
       o food service establishment complaint fields
       o septic and well permit applicant fields

   1.5 Employment data
       o personnel file identifiers
       o payroll deduction fields
       o occupational health screening fields

2. PERMITTED ACCESS

   2.1 Categories 1.1 and 1.3 — the assigned clinician, the Nursing Supervisor, the Medical Director.
   2.2 Category 1.2 — the Eligibility Worker, the Programme Coordinator.
   2.3 Category 1.4 — the Communicable Disease Investigator, the Health Officer; Sanitarians for environmental subsets only.
   2.4 Category 1.5 — the Personnel Officer, the Department Director.
   2.5 External disclosure — permitted only on statutory demand, subpoena, or signed authorisation of the individual of record. Each instance is entered in the Disclosure Register.

3. RETENTION

   3.1 Registration and health service data — seven years from last service; longer where a minor is the subject of the record.
   3.2 Programme participation data — three years from close of the certification period.
   3.3 Regulatory and investigative data — ten years from closure.
   3.4 Employment data — retained for the period of employment plus six years.

4. DISPOSAL

   4.1 No record is destroyed before the Records Custodian certifies expiry of the retention period.
   4.2 Paper is destroyed by cross-cut shredder; magnetic media by degaussing and physical destruction of the platter or cartridge.
   4.3 A Certificate of Destruction is completed for each disposal action and bears the fields: series title, date range, volume, method, date, certifying officer title.
   4.4 Certificates are filed permanently in the Records Office.
