SECTION A — CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION MAINTAINED

[ ] A-1  Individual identity set
         name fields; date of birth field; sex code; race and
         ethnicity codes; marital status code; language code
[ ] A-2  Locating set
         residence address fields; mailing address fields; telephone
         fields; employer name field; emergency contact fields
[ ] A-3  Identifier set
         departmental medical record number; clinic visit number;
         state programme identifier; insurer certificate number;
         specimen accession number
[ ] A-4  Clinical set
         encounter notes; diagnosis code fields; procedure code
         fields; medication and dosage fields; allergy fields;
         laboratory and radiology result fields; immunisation record
         fields
[ ] A-5  Investigative set
         disease report fields; exposure history fields; contact
         locating fields; interview narrative
[ ] A-6  Financial set
         income declaration fields; household size field; fee scale
         determination; charge and payment ledger fields
[ ] A-7  Personnel set
         application and reference fields; performance evaluation
         fields; leave and attendance fields; occupational exposure
         report fields

SECTION B — ACCESS CONTROL CHECKLIST

[ ] B-1  Access is granted by category, not by individual record.
[ ] B-2  Category assignments to positions are recorded on Form
         PR-3a and countersigned by the Division Head.
[ ] B-3  Clinical and investigative sets (A-4, A-5) are open to the
         assigned clinician or investigator, the Nursing Supervisor,
         and the Medical Director.
[ ] B-4  Financial set (A-6) is open to the Billing Clerk and the
         Fiscal Officer; clinical narrative is masked in the billing
         extract.
[ ] B-5  Personnel set (A-7) is open to the Personnel Officer and
         the Department Director only.
[ ] B-6  Data Processing Unit staff hold system-level access for
         maintenance; content access is logged and reviewed monthly
         by the Records Custodian.
[ ] B-7  Terminal sessions are closed when the workstation is left
         unattended. Screens are positioned away from public areas.
[ ] B-8  Files removed from the Records Office are signed out on the
         charge-out card bearing fields: series, date out, position
         of borrower, date returned.

SECTION C — RETENTION

[ ] C-1  A-1 through A-4, adult subject ........... 7 yr from last
         encounter
[ ] C-2  A-1 through A-4, minor subject ........... to age 21, or
         7 yr from last encounter, whichever is later
[ ] C-3  A-5 ....................................... 10 yr from
         case closure
[ ] C-4  A-6 ....................................... 5 yr from
         fiscal year close
[ ] C-5  A-7 ....................................... employment
         period plus 6 yr
[ ] C-6  Retention is suspended in full on notice of litigation,
         audit, or open public records request.

SECTION D — DISPOSAL

[ ] D-1  Custodian certifies expiry of the applicable period.
[ ] D-2  Division Head countersigns the destruction schedule.
[ ] D-3  Paper destroyed by cross-cut shredding; microfiche by
         chemical destruction; magnetic media by degaussing followed
         by physical destruction.
[ ] D-4  Vendor destruction performed under manifest, witnessed by a
         Department employee.
[ ] D-5  Destruction log completed with fields: series title,
         inclusive dates, volume, method, date, witness title.
[ ] D-6  Log retained permanently; reviewed at annual audit.
