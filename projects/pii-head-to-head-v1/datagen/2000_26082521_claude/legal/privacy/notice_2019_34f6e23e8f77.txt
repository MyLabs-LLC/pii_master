MEMORANDUM

TO:        Meter Operations; Billing; Customer Service
FROM:      Records Custodian
DATE:      August 2011
SUBJECT:   Standard PRS-04, Supplement 1 — Interval Consumption Data from Advanced Metering

This Supplement addresses Category C data elements generated by advanced metering infrastructure and is issued because the granularity of interval data differs materially from that of the monthly register read it replaces.

Elements in scope. Meter identifier; interval timestamp; interval consumption value; voltage and power quality event flags; tamper and outage event flags; communication node identifier; premises linkage key.

Sensitivity determination. Interval data at intervals shorter than one hour permits inference of occupancy and of activity within the premises. It is therefore handled as Category C-restricted, a subclass of Category C, and is not disclosed outside the roles listed below without the written authorisation of the Records Custodian or a valid legal process reviewed by General Counsel.

Access. Meter Operations Supervisor (read/write). Billing Clerk (read, aggregated to the billing period only). Engineering Analyst (read, de-identified extracts in which the premises linkage key is replaced by a study identifier held in a separate register). Customer Service Representative (read, most recent seven days only, and only while a customer enquiry is open). No other role has access.

Retention. Interval values are retained thirteen months from the interval timestamp and are then purged from the meter data management system. Aggregated billing-period totals migrate to the customer account record and follow the Category A retention period. Event flags are retained twenty-four months. De-identified study extracts held by Engineering are retained for the duration of the study plus one year, after which both the extract and the corresponding study identifier register are destroyed.

Disposal. Purge is executed monthly by the meter data management system administrator against records exceeding the retention period. The administrator confirms the purge in writing to the Records Custodian, stating the record count and the interval date range purged. Extracts held on Engineering workstations are deleted and the workstation storage subjected to a single-pass overwrite; removable media used to transport extracts are destroyed rather than reused.

Litigation hold. Where a hold is in effect the monthly purge is suspended for the affected premises linkage keys. Meter Operations maintains the suspension list and reconciles it with the Records Custodian each quarter.

This Supplement takes effect upon distribution and is reviewed annually with the parent Standard.
