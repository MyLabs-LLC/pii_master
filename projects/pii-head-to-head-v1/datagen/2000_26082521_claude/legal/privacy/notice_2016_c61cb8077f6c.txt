NOTES

1. Category F is displayed in masked form on every screen and report other than the daily cash reconciliation.
2. A position not listed for a category has no business need for it. Requests for an exception are made in writing to the Records Custodian and are granted for a stated period only.
3. Records under audit, litigation, or payer dispute are exempt from the schedule until the Compliance Coordinator releases the hold in writing.
4. Every destruction is recorded in the Destruction Log: Date of Destruction, Category Letter, Volume in Inches or Records, Method, Witness Title, Custodian Initials. Microfilm and magnetic media are degaussed before removal from the building.
5. Off-site storage cartons carry the Category Letter and Eligible Destruction Year on the label. No other marking is permitted on the exterior of a carton.

Effective date of this revision: February 1, 1999.
