REGIONAL TRANSIT AUTHORITY
INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

TO: All Division Directors and Designated Custodians
FROM: The Records Custodian
DATE: 14 June 2004
SUBJECT: Semi-annual re-certification of access to personal information holdings

Under Section 3 of Administrative Standard AS-114, access to each category of personal information is confined to those personnel whose assigned duties require it. The Standard contemplates that requirement will change as duties change, and that the access register will be corrected accordingly. Experience since the March issuance indicates that entries are added promptly on transfer or promotion but are seldom removed on the corresponding departure, with the result that the register overstates the population of authorised users.

Each custodian is therefore directed to review the register entries pertaining to the categories in that custodian's charge and to certify, for each entry, the position title, the category to which access is granted, the business justification, and whether the access remains required. Where an entry cannot be justified by reference to current assigned duties, it is to be marked for withdrawal. Certification is due by 30 July 2004 and thereafter on 31 January and 31 July of each year.

Particular attention is drawn to the segregated elements of Categories B and F. Supervisory access to those elements is not authorised in any circumstance, and any entry conferring it is to be withdrawn immediately rather than referred for review.
