MEMORANDUM

TO:       All Branch Managers; Operations Supervisors; Records Custodian
FROM:     The Compliance Officer
SUBJECT:  Amendment No. 1 to Policy RM-2004-07 — Custody of Credit and Underwriting Data
DATE:     19 November 2004

1. Effective immediately, the third category of personal information described at clause 2.2 of Policy RM-2004-07, being Credit and Underwriting Data, is reclassified from Restricted to Highly Restricted.

2. In consequence of clause 1, working files containing the fields Credit Bureau Score, Debt-to-Income Ratio, Collateral Description, Employer Name, Length of Employment or Gross Monthly Income shall not remain on any desk, credenza or counter outside the physical presence of the assigned Lending Officer. Such files shall be returned to the locked lending cabinet at each interruption of work and without exception at the close of business.

3. Consumer reports obtained in connection with a declined application are subject to a distinct retention period and shall be segregated at the point of decision into the Adverse Action file maintained by the Lending Manager. They shall not be interfiled with Member Identification Data.

4. Access previously extended to Member Services Representatives for the purpose of quoting indicative loan terms is withdrawn. Requests of that nature shall be referred to a Lending Officer.

5. Branch Managers shall confirm receipt and staff briefing in writing to the undersigned within ten business days. Amendment No. 1 shall be affixed to each controlled copy of the Policy.
