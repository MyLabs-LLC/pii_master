INTERNAL MEMORANDUM — PATIENT FINANCIAL SERVICES
RIVERBEND REGIONAL MEDICAL CENTER

TO:    Supervisors, Billing Office
FROM:  Records Custodian
DATE:  June 2, 2016
RE:    Addendum 1 to Standard PFS-04 — Treatment of Scanned Correspondence

Effective immediately, incoming correspondence scanned into the document imaging queue shall be indexed under the same category framework set forth in Section 2.0 of Standard PFS-04, and no separate or parallel index shall be maintained by any work unit.

Indexers are directed to observe the following:

-  Each imaged item shall be tagged with the Billing Account Number field and with the Correspondence Type field only. No free-text summary of the item's contents shall be entered in the index.

-  Where an imaged item contains fields within the Financial Instrument Category, the item shall be routed to the secure queue and shall not remain in the general imaging repository beyond the close of the business day on which it was received.

-  Where an imaged item contains fields within the Financial Assistance Category, access shall be restricted to the Financial Counselor role notwithstanding any broader departmental permission.

Retention of imaged correspondence follows the retention period of the account to which it is indexed, and disposal shall proceed by the electronic purge method described in Section 5.0. Physical originals shall be shredded no later than thirty (30) days following successful quality review of the image.

Questions regarding the classification of an item not readily assignable to an enumerated category shall be directed to the Records Custodian and shall not be resolved by the indexer.
