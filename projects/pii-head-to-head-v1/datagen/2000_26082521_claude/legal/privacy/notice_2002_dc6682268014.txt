COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
RECORDS CENTRE

INSTRUCTION RC-4: PREPARATION OF THE ANNUAL RECORDS INVENTORY
Issued 9 September 1999

[1] Purpose
    - To establish, for each division, a current inventory of holdings containing identifiable content, as the basis for the retention determinations in Standard RA-12.

[2] Frequency
    - One inventory each fiscal year, returned to the Records Custodian not later than the last working day of the second month of the year.

[3] Unit of Inventory
    - The record series, not the individual folder or volume.
    - A series is entered separately where its category assignment, its medium, or its retention period differs from adjacent holdings.

[4] Worksheet Columns
    - Series Number
    - Series Title
    - Division of Origin
    - Category Code (A through F)
    - Field Labels Present
    - Medium
    - Inclusive Dates
    - Volume in Cubic Feet
    - Storage Location Code
    - Retention Period Claimed
    - Authority Cited
    - Disposition Action Due

[5] Completion Notes
    - The Field Labels Present column is completed by naming the labels carried on the form or screen comprising the series. Content drawn from those fields is not transcribed onto the worksheet.
    - Where a series carries labels drawn from more than one category, the most restrictive category code governs the whole series.
    - The Authority Cited column carries the section of Standard RA-12, or the statutory citation, on which the claimed period rests. An entry lacking an authority is returned to the division for completion.
    - Storage Location Code is taken from the current Records Centre shelving key and not from any descriptive designation used locally.

[6] Review
    - The Records Custodian consolidates divisional worksheets into the Departmental Records Inventory and forwards a summary to the Health Officer.
    - Series shown as due for disposition are scheduled under Section 5 of Standard RA-12 in the next quarterly cycle.

[7] Retention of Inventory Papers
    - Divisional worksheets: three years.
    - Consolidated Departmental Records Inventory: permanent.
