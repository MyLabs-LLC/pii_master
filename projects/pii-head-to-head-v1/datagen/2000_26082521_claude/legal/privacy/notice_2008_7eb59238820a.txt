ADMINISTRATIVE STANDARD AS-4120
CLASSIFICATION, ACCESS, RETENTION AND DISPOSITION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD BY THE DISTRICT

Issuing authority:  Office of the Superintendent
Standard owner:     Records Custodian
Effective:          1 July 2016
Supersedes:         AS-4120 (rev. 2011)
Review cycle:       36 months

    1.  PURPOSE AND SCOPE
        1.1  This standard classifies every category of personal information created or received by the District, fixes the authorised access level for each, and prescribes minimum retention and terminal disposition.
        1.2  Applies to all schools, central departments, contracted service providers, and volunteers. Applies to paper, electronic, microform, and backup media without distinction.
        1.3  Does not displace statutory retention obligations. Where this standard and a statutory schedule conflict, the longer period governs.

    2.  CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION
        2.1  Category A — Student Directory Information
             Fields: Legal Name; Preferred Name; Grade Level; Enrolment Status; Dates of Attendance; Participation in Recognised Activities; Awards Received; Most Recent School Attended.
        2.2  Category B — Student Education Record (Non-Directory)
             Fields: Student Identification Number; Date of Birth; Place of Birth; Home Language; Address of Record; Parent/Guardian Name; Guardianship Status; Emergency Contact Particulars; Transcript; Assessment Results; Attendance Detail; Disciplinary Action Record.
        2.3  Category C — Special Education and Related Services
             Fields: Referral Record; Evaluation Report; Eligibility Determination; Individualised Education Programme; Service Minutes Log; Progress Report; Parental Consent Record.
        2.4  Category D — Student Health Information
             Fields: Immunisation Record; Medication Authorisation; Administration Log; Screening Result; Condition and Allergy Flag; Health Office Visit Note; Accident Report.
        2.5  Category E — Nutrition Benefit Eligibility
             Fields: Household Size; Household Income Band; Benefit Case Reference; Eligibility Determination Code; Verification Outcome.
        2.6  Category F — Employee and Applicant Records
             Fields: Employee Reference Number; Taxpayer Identification Number; Date of Birth; Address of Record; Licensure and Endorsement; Compensation Band; Evaluation Record; Leave and Absence Record; Grievance File; Background Check Disposition.
        2.7  Category G — Transportation
             Fields: Route Assignment; Stop Assignment; Pupil Ridership Record; Vehicle Camera Footage.
        2.8  Category H — Technology and Network
             Fields: Account Identifier; Device Assignment; Authentication Log; Content Filter Log; Learning Platform Activity Record.
        2.9  Category I — Visitor, Volunteer and Contractor
             Fields: Visitor Sign-In Entry; Volunteer Application; Criminal History Check Disposition; Facility Access Credential Record.
        2.10 Category J — Facility Surveillance
             Fields: Camera Location Identifier; Recording Segment Reference; Retrieval Request Log.

    3.  AUTHORISED ACCESS
        3.1  Access is granted by role, on the legitimate educational interest test, and is the minimum necessary to perform assigned duties.
        3.2  Category A — school-based administrative staff; release to third parties permitted only after the annual opt-out window closes.
        3.3  Categories B, C, E — the Principal, assigned Counsellor, assigned Case Manager, Registrar, and Records Custodian.
        3.4  Category D — the School Nurse and, on a need-to-know basis, the Principal.
        3.5  Category F — the Director of Human Resources and designated payroll personnel.
        3.6  Categories H, J — the Director of Technology and the Director of Safety and Security respectively; retrieval requires a logged written request approved by the Records Custodian.
        3.7  Every access outside the assigned role requires prior written authorisation, recorded in the Disclosure Register.

    4.  RETENTION
        4.1  Categories B and C: five years after graduation, transfer, or withdrawal; transcript element permanent.
        4.2  Category D: retained with Category B; medication authorisation destroyed at close of the school year.
        4.3  Category E: three years after the end of the fiscal year to which the determination relates.
        4.4  Category F: seven years after separation; licensure element permanent.
        4.5  Categories H and I: two years. Category J: thirty days unless placed under hold.

    5.  DISPOSITION
        5.1  Paper: cross-cut shredding or supervised pulping.
        5.2  Electronic: cryptographic erasure or overwrite to the approved standard; failed media physically destroyed.
        5.3  A Certificate of Destruction is executed by the Records Custodian and retained permanently.
        5.4  Disposition is suspended in full for any series subject to litigation hold, audit, or open public records request.
