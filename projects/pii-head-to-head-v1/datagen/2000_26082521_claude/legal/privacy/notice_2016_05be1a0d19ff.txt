MEMORANDUM

TO:      Unit Heads receiving student record extracts
FROM:    Office of the University Registrar
DATE:    October 19, 2016
RE:      Standard RG-11, Handling of Registrar Extracts

A. BACKGROUND

Several units now receive recurring extracts from the student information system. This standard states what those extracts may contain, who in the receiving unit may open them, how long the unit may keep them, and how the unit must dispose of them. It takes effect with the spring term extract cycle.

B. PERMITTED CONTENT BY EXTRACT TYPE

   1. Roster extract
      Student Identification Number, Legal Name, Preferred Name, Course Section, Credit Hours, Enrollment Status.

   2. Advising extract
      Student Identification Number, Legal Name, Program of Study, Plan, Cumulative Grade Point Average, Academic Standing Action, Advising Hold Indicator.

   3. Outreach extract
      Student Identification Number, Preferred Name, University Electronic Mail Address, Local Mailing Address, Admit Term.

   4. Compliance extract
      Student Identification Number, Legal Name, Date of Birth, Country of Citizenship, Visa Classification, Immigration Reporting Status.

No extract may include the Consent to Release Form, the Disclosure Log Entry, the Protective Order Flag, or any element carrying the Directory Information Suppression Flag. Requests for elements outside the lists above are submitted in writing to the Associate Registrar and are approved case by case.

C. ACCESS WITHIN THE RECEIVING UNIT

The unit head names one Extract Steward and one alternate. Only the Steward, the alternate, and staff named on the unit's access roster may open an extract. The roster is filed with the Records Custodian and refreshed each fall. Extracts are stored on unit network shares with restricted permissions. Storage on personal devices, personal cloud accounts, or removable media is prohibited. Extracts are not forwarded outside the unit.

D. RETENTION

   Roster extract        — end of the term to which it relates
   Advising extract      — end of the academic year to which it relates
   Outreach extract      — 90 days from delivery
   Compliance extract    — six years from the close of the reporting year

E. DISPOSAL

At the end of the retention period the Extract Steward deletes the file from the unit share and from any working copies, and confirms deletion to the Records Custodian on the quarterly extract certificate. Printed extracts are cross-cut shredded. The Records Custodian may suspend a unit's extract feed where a certificate is not returned within thirty days.

F. QUESTIONS

Direct questions on scope, content, or approval to the Associate Registrar.
