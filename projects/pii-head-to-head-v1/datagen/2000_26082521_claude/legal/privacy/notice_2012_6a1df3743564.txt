BAYLINE DRUG STORES, INC.
PRIVACY STANDARD PS-11: INVENTORY OF PERSONAL INFORMATION CATEGORIES

Effective 01 September 2004. Supersedes PS-11 dated 03 February 2003.
Approved by the Privacy Steering Committee. Review cycle: annual.

1. PURPOSE

This standard records what categories of personal information the Company maintains,
which role holds custody of each, how long each is kept, and how each is destroyed. It
supports the Notice of Privacy Practices and the record retention obligations of the
applicable state board of pharmacy.

2. SCOPE

All information collected in the course of dispensing, front-store retail, clinical
services, and employment, in paper or electronic form, at any location or in the care of
a business associate.

3. CATEGORY INVENTORY

INFORMATION CATEGORY          CUSTODIAN ROLE            RETENTION               DISPOSAL ROUTE
----------------------------  ------------------------  ----------------------  --------------------
01 Patient Master Record      Pharmacy Operations       10 yrs, last activity   Shred - Bin B
02 Prescription History       Pharmacy Operations       10 yrs, last activity   Shred - Bin B
03 Insurance Eligibility      Third-Party Payer Desk    7 yrs, close of claim   Shred - Bin B
04 Retail Payment Record      Store Accounting          3 yrs, transaction      Purge - Batch job
05 Immunisation Register      Clinical Services         20 yrs, administration  Shred - Bin B
06 Complaint and Incident     Privacy Officer           6 yrs, case closure     Shred - Bin A
07 Associate File             Human Resources           7 yrs, separation       Shred - Bin A
----------------------------  ------------------------  ----------------------  --------------------

4. FIELD LABELS

Category 01 holds the labels Full Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Residential Address, and
Home Telephone Number. Category 02 holds Prescriber Identifier, Drug Name, Strength,
Quantity Dispensed, Days Supply, and Refill Count. Category 03 holds Plan Name, Member
Identifier, Group Number, Person Code, and Relationship Code. Category 04 holds
Cardholder Name, Primary Account Number, Expiration Date, and Authorisation Code.
Category 07 holds Associate Name, Social Security Number, Hire Date, Home Address, Pay
Band, and Licence Number. No other labels may be captured into these categories.

5. ACCESS

Custody does not confer unlimited access. Within each custodian function, access is
assigned by profile and reviewed quarterly by the Records Custodian. Bin A containers
are restricted to corporate floors; Bin B containers are placed behind the pharmacy
counter and are never left in the retail area.

6. DISPOSAL

Destruction is witnessed and certificated. Certificates are filed by category number and
retained for six years. Electronic purge jobs run monthly against categories 04 and any
expired record set, and produce a machine log filed with the certificates.
