===============================================================================
BILLING OFFICE PRIVACY STANDARD 2016-02
CLASSIFICATION, ACCESS, AND DISPOSITION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION MAINTAINED BY
THE HOSPITAL BILLING OFFICE
===============================================================================
Issued by:      the Chief Privacy Officer
Concurrence:    the Director of Revenue Cycle; the Office of General Counsel
Adopted:        the first business day of July, 2016
Distribution:   all personnel assigned to the billing office; all supervisors
                of business associates performing extended business office
                functions

ARTICLE I. AUTHORITY AND CONSTRUCTION
    (a)  This Standard is promulgated pursuant to the administrative
         requirements of 45 C.F.R. § 164.530 and the records retention
         provisions of applicable state law, and shall be construed so as to
         give effect to the more protective of the two wherever they differ.
    (b)  Nothing herein shall be construed to enlarge any right of access that
         does not otherwise exist by reason of assigned job function.

ARTICLE II. CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION
    (a)  Demographic category. The fields Patient Full Name; Former Name;
         Date of Birth; Marital Status; Preferred Language; Race and Ethnicity
         Designation; Emergency Contact Name; Next of Kin Name.
    (b)  Governmental identifier category. The fields Social Security Number;
         Driver License or State Identification Number; Medicare Beneficiary
         Identifier; Medicaid Case Number.
    (c)  Financial instrument category. The fields Payment Card Number
         (truncated); Card Expiration Month and Year; Bank Routing Number;
         Bank Account Number; Payment Plan Term.
    (d)  Means-testing category, being those fields solicited on the financial
         assistance application, namely Household Size; Household Gross
         Income; Proof of Income Document Type; Asset Declaration; Prior
         Determination Outcome.
    (e)  Adverse action category. The fields Collection Agency Placement Date;
         Bankruptcy Petition Number; Judgment Status; Deceased Indicator.

ARTICLE III. AUTHORIZED ACCESS
    (a)  Access shall be granted on the principle of least privilege, and
         shall be evidenced by a role assignment rather than by an individual
         grant, save in the circumstances described in subsection (c).
    (b)  The following roles are authorized to the categories indicated:
         (1)  the Billing Representative and the Cashier, to categories (a)
              and (c), the latter in truncated form only;
         (2)  the Financial Counselor, to categories (a), (b), and (d);
         (3)  the Collections Representative, to categories (a) and (e);
         (4)  the Privacy Officer and the Office of General Counsel, to all
              categories, for the purpose of investigation or the defense of
              a claim.
    (c)  Temporary elevation may be authorized in writing by the Records
         Custodian for a term not exceeding thirty (30) days.

ARTICLE IV. RETENTION
    (a)  Categories (a) through (c): seven (7) years from final payment,
         write-off, or adjudication, whichever occurs last.
    (b)  Category (d): seven (7) years from the date of determination.
    (c)  Documentation required by 45 C.F.R. § 164.530(j): six (6) years.
    (d)  Any record subject to a litigation hold notice: preserved without
         limitation until the hold is released in writing.

ARTICLE V. DISPOSITION
    (a)  Paper is destroyed by cross-cut shredding at a secured facility;
         electronic media are purged or destroyed in conformity with the
         media sanitization guideline then in force.
    (b)  A Certificate of Destruction shall be obtained and filed, bearing the
         headings Record Series; Media Type; Container Count; Method; Date of
         Destruction; Vendor Name; Witnessing Role.
