MEMORANDUM

To:        All Records Custodians; Branch Privacy Officers
From:      Manager, Records and Information Management
Subject:   RIM-STD-12 Attachment B — Authorised Access Schedule, revision of
File:      RIM/12/B
Date:      2 September 1998

The Authorised Access Schedule is revised as set out below and takes effect from 1 October 1998. Custodians are to reconcile standing access lists against this Schedule and report exceptions to this Branch by 31 October 1998.

LEVELS

 R1  Operational.  Officers performing the function to which the category relates,
     within the responsible Branch only.
 R2  Restricted.  Named officers only, on the standing list held by the Custodian.
     Access is by duty, not by grade.
 R3  Controlled.  Named officers only, by written approval of the Branch Manager,
     endorsed by the Branch Privacy Officer, for a stated period.

STANDING NOTES

1.  Internal Audit holds access to all categories for audit purposes on production of
    a signed audit instruction. Access is logged by the Custodian in each instance.
2.  Access held by a position does not travel with an officer on transfer. The losing
    Branch Custodian cancels; the gaining Branch Custodian grants afresh.
3.  Terminal enquiry privileges on the mainframe personnel and payroll systems are
    assigned by category profile only. Profiles are not to be varied at the terminal.
4.  Where a category is held in both paper and machine-readable form, the same level
    applies to both. Extracts and working copies inherit the level of the source.
5.  Requests to add a position to this Schedule are to be made in writing to this
    Branch and are considered at the quarterly Records Committee.
