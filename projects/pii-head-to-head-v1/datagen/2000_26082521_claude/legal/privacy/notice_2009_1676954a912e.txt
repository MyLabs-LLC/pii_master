RECORDS CLASSIFICATION AND RETENTION STANDARD
Office of the University Registrar
Standard No. REG-STD-04   Effective 01 March 2011   Supersedes REG-STD-03

Applies to all student records held by this office in any medium, active or inactive.

CATEGORY                         ACCESS            RETENTION              DISP
-------------------------------  ----------------  ---------------------  ----
Name and Former Name             REG, RC           Permanent              n/a
Institutional Identifier         REG, RC, TU       Permanent              n/a
Government Identification No.    RC                5 yr after separation  D2
Date of Birth                    REG, RC, TU       Permanent              n/a
Mailing Address, Perm and Local  REG, RC, ADV      3 yr after separation  D1
Telephone Number                 REG, RC           3 yr after separation  D1
Institutional Mail Account       REG, RC, ADV      3 yr after separation  D1
Emergency Contact Designation    REG, RC           1 yr after separation  D1
Registration and Grade Detail    REG, RC, TU, DAA  Permanent              n/a
Degree and Award Record          REG, RC, TU, DAA  Permanent              n/a
Disciplinary Notation            REG               7 yr after separation  D2
Directory Suppression Election   REG, RC, TU       Permanent              n/a

ACCESS LEGEND
  REG  University Registrar and Associate Registrar
  RC   Records Custodian
  TU   Transcript Unit staff, transaction scope only
  ADV  Academic Adviser of record, need-to-know basis
  DAA  Degree Audit Analyst

DISPOSAL CODES
  D1   Cross-cut shred on site; electronic copies purged from active and
       backup media at the next quarterly purge cycle.
  D2   Witnessed destruction by the Records Custodian and one other unit
       officer; Certificate of Destruction filed and held seven years.

Access outside the legend requires written authorisation of the University
Registrar. Categories bearing a suppression election are withheld from all
external release irrespective of access level.
