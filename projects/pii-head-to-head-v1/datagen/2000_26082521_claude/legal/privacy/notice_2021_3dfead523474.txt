OFFICE OF THE UNIVERSITY REGISTRAR
RECORDS MANAGEMENT STANDARD RM-2004-1
STUDENT RECORD DATA CATEGORIES, ACCESS AUTHORISATION, RETENTION AND DISPOSITION
Issued: March 2004
Supersedes: RM-1998-3
Approving authority: The University Registrar
Review cycle: Biennial

1.0 PURPOSE
1.1 This Standard establishes the categories of personally identifiable information maintained by the Office of the University Registrar, designates the offices and role holders authorised to access each category, prescribes minimum and maximum retention periods, and specifies the method of disposition at the expiry of retention.
1.2 This Standard is issued under the authority delegated to the Registrar for the custody of the education record.

2.0 SCOPE
2.1 This Standard applies to all record series held by the Office in any medium, including paper, microform, magnetic tape, optical disc, and data held within the student information system and its extract files.
2.2 Record series maintained by other units and to which the Office holds only query access are governed by the standard of the originating custodian.

3.0 DATA CATEGORIES
3.1 Category A — Identification Data. Fields: Institutional Identification Number; legal name fields; former name fields; government-issued identification number where lawfully collected; date of birth; sex.
3.2 Category B — Contact Data. Fields: permanent mailing address; local mailing address; billing address; telephone number fields; electronic mail address; emergency contact name and telephone number.
3.3 Category C — Enrolment and Progress Data. Fields: term of admission; classification; declared major and minor; enrolment status; course registration records; grade records; grade point average; academic standing; degree conferral and honours.
3.4 Category D — Admissions Source Data. Fields: prior institution codes; secondary school code; transfer credit evaluations; standardised examination score fields; application status indicators.
3.5 Category E — Restricted-Sensitivity Data. Fields: disability accommodation determinations; disciplinary action indicators; non-immigrant visa classification and related status fields; residency-for-tuition determinations; financial aid need indicators.
3.6 Category F — Directory Data. The subset of fields designated by annual public notice as directory information, together with the non-disclosure flag governing each record.
3.7 Category G — Access and Audit Data. Fields: requestor role code; date of access; purpose code; disclosure log entries.

4.0 ACCESS AUTHORISATION
4.1 Access shall be granted by role, not by individual, and only upon written certification of legitimate educational interest by the requesting unit head.
4.2 Categories A, C, and F: the Records Custodian; Assistant Registrars; Degree Audit Analysts; academic advising staff of the student's college of record.
4.3 Category B: the Records Custodian; the Office of Student Accounts; the Office of Admissions for applicant records only.
4.4 Category D: the Records Custodian; transfer credit evaluators; the Office of Institutional Research in de-identified aggregate form only.
4.5 Category E: the Records Custodian and the designated Data Steward for the sub-series concerned. No general advising access is granted. Each disclosure shall generate a Category G entry.
4.6 Access privileges shall be reviewed each academic term and revoked upon change of role.

5.0 RETENTION
5.1 Categories A and C are retained permanently for students who matriculate.
5.2 Category B is retained for five years following the last term of enrolment or the date of degree conferral, whichever is later.
5.3 Category D is retained for five years following matriculation; for applicants who do not matriculate, one year following the term applied for.
5.4 Category E is retained for seven years following separation, unless a longer period is required by the originating unit.
5.5 Category G is retained for the life of the record to which it pertains.

6.0 DISPOSITION
6.1 Paper records shall be destroyed by cross-cut shredding under the observation of the Records Custodian or a designee.
6.2 Magnetic and optical media shall be degaussed or physically destroyed; deletion of files alone is not an acceptable method.
6.3 A certificate of destruction identifying the record series, the volume, the method, and the date shall be executed and retained permanently.
6.4 Disposition shall be suspended upon notice of litigation, audit, or open records request affecting the series.
