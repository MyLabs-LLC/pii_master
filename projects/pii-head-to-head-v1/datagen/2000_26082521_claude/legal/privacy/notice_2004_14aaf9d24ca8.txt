SCHEDULE A TO POLICY RM-2004-07
RETENTION AND DISPOSAL SCHEDULE FOR PERSONAL INFORMATION
Maintained by the Records Custodian; reviewed annually by the Compliance Officer

1. The periods stated in the table below are minimum periods. No record shall be destroyed before the expiry of the stated period, and no record shall be retained beyond that period by more than one hundred and eighty days save where a Litigation Hold, an examination notice or a lawful information request has been issued in writing by the Compliance Officer. A Litigation Hold suspends the operation of this Schedule in respect of the records identified in it and remains in force until released in writing by the officer who issued it.

2. Where a single physical file contains records falling within more than one category, the longest applicable period shall govern the whole file unless the file is separated at the point of closure by the Records Custodian.

CATEGORY DESIGNATION            CUSTODIAL UNIT           MINIMUM RETENTION         DISPOSAL METHOD
------------------------------  -----------------------  ------------------------  -------------------------
Member Identification Data      Branch Operations        5 years from closure      Cross-cut shred
Account and Share Data          Central Operations       7 years from closure      Cross-cut shred
Credit and Underwriting Data    Lending Department       7 years from final         Cross-cut shred
                                                         payment or charge-off
Adverse Action Records          Lending Department       25 months from decision   Cross-cut shred
Transactional Data              Central Operations       5 years from posting      Degauss and destroy
Electronic Access Data          Information Systems      Purge at closure          Secure overwrite
Correspondence and Servicing    Member Services          3 years from resolution   Cross-cut shred
Currency Transaction Records    BSA Compliance           5 years from filing       Cross-cut shred
Suspicious Activity Records     BSA Compliance           5 years from filing       Cross-cut shred
Personnel Data                  Human Resources          7 years from separation   Cross-cut shred
Premises Surveillance Media     Security Administration  90 days from recording    Degauss and destroy

3. The fields comprised within each category designation are those enumerated at clause 2 of the Policy. Where a new field is added to the core processing system, the Information Security Coordinator shall notify the Records Custodian in advance of implementation and the field shall be assigned to an existing category, or a new category shall be proposed to the Board Audit and Compliance Committee.

4. Quarterly, the Records Custodian shall produce a Destruction Eligibility Listing identifying every file and media unit whose period has expired. Destruction shall proceed only upon countersignature by the Compliance Officer, and the resulting Certificate of Destruction shall record the category designation, the volume destroyed, the method employed, the date and the witnessing officer.
