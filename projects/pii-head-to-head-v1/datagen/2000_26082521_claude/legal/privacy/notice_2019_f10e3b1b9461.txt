COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT

STANDING INSTRUCTION SI-7
DESTRUCTION OF SUPERSEDED WORKING COPIES

Effective 1 April 1997

I. APPLICATION
     A. Duplicates, drafts, working extracts and printer output bearing content of Category A, B, C or D as defined in Standard RA-12.
     B. The instruction does not apply to the record copy of any series.

II. HOLDING PENDING DESTRUCTION
     A. Working copies are held in a locked collection receptacle in the division of origin.
     B. Receptacles are emptied not less than weekly.
     C. Working copies are not placed in general waste, recycling collection or unattended corridors at any time.

III. METHOD
     A. Cross-cut shredding at the point of collection.
     B. Where volume exceeds two cubic feet, transfer under seal to the Records Centre for contractor incineration.

IV. LOG
     A. Each emptying of a receptacle is entered in the Working Copy Destruction Log under the columns: Entry Number; Division; Category Codes Present; Method; Date; Performed By (Role Title); Verified By (Role Title).
     B. The log is retained for three years and is then destroyed under Section III.A.

V. RESPONSIBILITY
     A. Division Directors ensure receptacle security within their areas.
     B. The Records Custodian audits compliance semi-annually and reports exceptions to the Health Officer in writing.
