SCOPE

This memorandum lists every category of personal information held by the
company, the role accountable for it, the period of retention and the route of
disposal. No category may be created, copied to another system, or sent
outside the company without the prior written authority of the Records
Custodian.

NOTES ON ACCESS

Access is confined to the role named in the Authorised Holder column and to the
Records Custodian. A Depot Manager may request an extract of category A1 or B1
for a member of their own depot; the request is logged and the extract is
returned for destruction when the purpose has been met. Category D1 is held in
a separate locked cabinet and is not accessible to line management. Category F1
is examined only where a specific operational or disciplinary purpose has been
recorded in advance.

NOTES ON DISPOSAL

Records reaching the end of the retention period are drawn down each quarter by
the Records Custodian, checked against the schedule, and destroyed by the
approved contractor under witness. A certificate of destruction is obtained and
filed with the quarterly Destruction Return.
