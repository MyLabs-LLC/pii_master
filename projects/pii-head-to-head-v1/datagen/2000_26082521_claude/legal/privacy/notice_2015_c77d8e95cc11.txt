OFFICE OF THE UNIVERSITY REGISTRAR
STANDARD RM-2004-4: ACCESS AUTHORISATION MATRIX FOR REGISTRAR DATA CATEGORIES
Effective 1 September 2004

§1. This Standard supplements RM-2004-1 and governs the assignment of query and update privileges within the student information system.

§2. Privileges are assigned in three grades: (a) view; (b) view and update; (c) view, update, and extract to file.

§3. The following assignments apply:

    Identification Data ................ Records Custodian (c); Assistant Registrar (b);
                                         college advising staff (a)
    Contact Data ....................... Records Custodian (c); Student Accounts (a)
    Enrolment and Progress Data ........ Records Custodian (c); Degree Audit Analyst (b);
                                         college advising staff (a)
    Restricted-Sensitivity Data ........ Records Custodian (b); Data Steward (b)
    Directory Data and non-disclosure
      flag ............................. Records Custodian (b); Information Desk (a)
    Access and Audit Data .............. Records Custodian (a); Internal Audit (a)

§4. Grade (c) privileges permit extraction of files containing the fields listed in the relevant category. Extract files shall be stored on institutionally controlled media, shall not be transmitted by unencrypted means, and shall be destroyed within thirty days of the purpose for which they were created.

§5. No role holder shall query a record in which the role holder has a personal interest.

§6. Requests for privilege assignment shall be submitted on the Access Authorisation Form and countersigned by the unit head. Assignments lapse at the close of each academic year unless recertified.

§7. Violations of this Standard shall be reported to the Registrar and may result in immediate revocation of all privileges.
