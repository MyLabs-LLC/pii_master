STANDARD 11-06: INFORMATION CATEGORIES, ACCESS, RETENTION AND DISPOSITION
Office of the University Registrar
Issued 06 June 2011. Mandatory for all unit personnel and temporary appointees.

1. PURPOSE
   To fix, for each category of personal information held by this office, the
   roles permitted access, the period of retention, and the method of
   disposition. This standard implements University Policy 3.14 and applicable
   federal requirements governing education records.

2. SCOPE
   All categories below, in paper, microform, magnetic, optical or hosted form,
   including working copies and extracts held on unit workstations.

4. ACCESS ROLES
   1 University Registrar and Associate Registrar
   2 Records Custodian
   3 Transcript Unit staff
   4 Academic Adviser of record, need-to-know
   5 Degree Audit Analyst
   6 International Student Adviser, categories A06 only
   7 Institutional Research, aggregate or de-identified extract only
   8 Disability Services Liaison, categories D01 only

   Roles are assigned in writing by the University Registrar and revoked on
   the effective date of transfer or separation. No role confers authority to
   copy a category to portable media without prior written approval.

5. DISPOSITION METHODS
   X1  Cross-cut shred on site by unit personnel; electronic instances deleted
       and overwritten under the quarterly purge procedure.
   X2  Destruction witnessed by two officers of the unit, one of whom shall be
       the Records Custodian; certificate retained seven years.

6. HOLDS
   Litigation, audit, grievance or public-records holds suspend disposition for
   all affected categories until released in writing by the Office of General
   Counsel. Suspension is recorded in the disposition register.

7. REVIEW
   This standard is reviewed triennially or upon material change to federal or
   state requirements, whichever occurs first.
