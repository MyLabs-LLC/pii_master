NORTHRIDGE CASUALTY INSURANCE ASSOCIATION
Information Handling Bulletin 21-07
Personal Information Categories, Access, and Disposal
Approved by the Records Custodian, 14 June 2021

The Association holds personal information in four broad categories, and every member of staff is expected to know which of them their role permits them to open. The Member and Applicant category consists of the fields Full Name, Date of Birth, Home Address, Telephone Number, and Electronic Mail Address. The Coverage category consists of Policy Number, Coverage Type, Effective Date, and Premium Amount. The Claim category consists of Claim Number, Date of Loss, Loss Description, Reserve Amount, and Payment Status. The Sensitive category consists of Social Security Number, Bank Account Number, Payment Card Number, and, in the health lines only, Diagnosis Code and Provider Name.

Service Representatives may open the first three categories. Claims Adjusters may open all four for files assigned to them. The Fraud Review Officer may open all four for any file, subject to a note in the file explaining the reason. Everyone else, including staff in Marketing and Actuarial, receives extracts from which the Sensitive category has been removed at the point of extraction.

Files are kept for seven years after the coverage ends, or ten years after a claim closes, whichever is later. When the period runs out, the Records Custodian releases the file for disposal, paper goes to the locked destruction bins for collection by the approved vendor, electronic copies are deleted from primary and backup storage on the next scheduled cycle, and the disposal is entered in the log against the columns Record Series, Volume, Method, Date, and Authorising Officer.
