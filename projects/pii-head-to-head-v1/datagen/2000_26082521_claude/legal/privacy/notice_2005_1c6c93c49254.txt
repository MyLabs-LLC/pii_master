RIVERBEND COMMUNITY CREDIT UNION
OPERATING PROCEDURE OP-114.5
DISPOSAL OF MEMBER AND PERSONNEL RECORDS

Issued: 22 June 1999
Authority: Administrative Standard AP-114, Clause 5.0
Applicable to: All branches, the Operations Center, and the off-site storage facility

1. INITIATION

   1.1 On the first business day of each calendar quarter, the Records Custodian shall generate the Disposal Eligibility Listing, showing for each eligible record the Category Code, the Custodian of Record, the Retention Expiry Date, and the Storage Location Code.

   1.2 The Records Custodian shall transmit the Listing to each affected Custodian of Record for review.

2. HOLD REVIEW

   2.1 Each Custodian of Record shall, within ten (10) business days, either certify that no hold subsists or identify by Category Code and Storage Location Code those records to be withheld.

   2.2 The Compliance Officer shall independently verify the Listing against the Litigation Hold Register, the Examination Correspondence File, and the Subpoena Log, and shall strike from the Listing any record so encumbered.

3. AUTHORIZATION

   3.1 The Listing, as amended, shall be approved in writing by the Compliance Officer and countersigned by the Corporate Secretary.

   3.2 No record shall be removed from storage prior to the affixing of both signatures.

4. EXECUTION

   4.1 Destruction shall be performed on Credit Union premises. Removal of records to a third-party facility for destruction is prohibited absent the written authorization of the Chief Executive Officer.

   4.2 Two witnesses, neither of whom is the Records Custodian, shall observe the destruction in its entirety and shall not depart the destruction area until every item on the Listing has been rendered irrecoverable.

5. CERTIFICATION

   5.1 The Certificate of Destruction shall record: Certificate Number; Date of Destruction; Category Codes Destroyed; Volume Destroyed; Method Employed; Name of Records Custodian; Names of Witnesses; Signature of Compliance Officer.

   5.2 The completed Certificate shall be filed permanently with the Office of the Corporate Secretary and a duplicate furnished to Internal Audit.

6. NON-COMPLIANCE

   6.1 Any deviation from this Procedure shall be reported forthwith to the Compliance Officer and shall be entered upon the Exception Log for review by Internal Audit.
