ASHGROVE COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
Records Custodian Working Checklist — Standard RC-08 (Personal Information)
Version 2.1, November 2021

Confirm the inventory is current
[ ] Every system holding personal information appears on the Records Inventory.
[ ] Each entry names its categories: Identity Fields, Contact Fields, Government Identifier Fields, Clinical Content, Eligibility and Financial Fields, Workforce Fields.
[ ] Each entry names the responsible programme supervisor by role title.
[ ] Shadow files, spreadsheets, and desk copies have been identified and either registered or destroyed.

Confirm access is correct
[ ] Clinical Content is limited to the treating team roster and the Health Officer.
[ ] Government Identifier Fields are limited to Billing, Vital Records, and the Records Custodian.
[ ] Workforce Fields are limited to Human Resources and the direct supervisor.
[ ] Contractors hold the mapping of their equivalent Department role and nothing beyond it.
[ ] Accounts of separated staff and ended placements are disabled the same working day.
[ ] Quarterly access review is signed by the Privacy Officer.
[ ] Exceptions are recorded in the Access Exception Log with the approving role and expiry date.

Confirm retention is applied
[ ] Clinical files: ten years from last encounter; minors, ten years past majority.
[ ] Immunization registry entries: permanent.
[ ] Billing and claims: seven years from fiscal year close.
[ ] Eligibility determinations: five years.
[ ] Environmental Health case files: six years from closure.
[ ] Workforce files: seven years from separation; occupational exposure records, thirty years.
[ ] Audit and access logs: six years.
[ ] Backup sets rotate out on the ninety-day cycle.

Confirm holds before destroying anything
[ ] Litigation hold list checked with the County Attorney's office.
[ ] Open public records requests checked with the Public Information Coordinator.
[ ] Open audits checked with the Finance Director.
[ ] Open investigations checked with the State Epidemiologist where communicable disease files are involved.
[ ] Hold clearance signed by the programme supervisor.

Carry out disposal
[ ] Paper cross-cut shredded on site, or collected by the contracted vendor under continuous staff observation.
[ ] Electronic records deleted from primary storage, then replicas, then confirmed absent by the Information Technology Administrator.
[ ] Removable media and end-of-life drives degaussed and physically destroyed.
[ ] Vendor certificate of destruction received and filed.

Close the cycle
[ ] Destruction Register completed with Category, Programme Unit, Date Range Covered, Volume, Method, Authorising Role, Witnessing Role.
[ ] Inventory updated to show the disposed date range.
[ ] Quarterly summary submitted to the Health Officer.
[ ] Any deviation reported to the Privacy Officer within one working day as a privacy incident.
