SABLEWOOD COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
Division of Health Information Management

PERSONAL INFORMATION HANDLING STANDARD HIM-07
Issued 22 June 2021. Approved: Health Officer. Custodian of record: the Records Custodian.

§1  DEFINITIONS AND STRUCTURE
    (a)  "Category" means a grouping of data elements sharing a common sensitivity classification and handling rule.
    (b)  Every category carries a two-character code used in the master index and on all disposition certificates.
    (c)  Data elements are enumerated by field label only. No element value appears in this standard or in any register derived from it.

§2  MASTER INDEX COLUMN NAMES
    (a)  The master index maintained by the Records Custodian carries the following columns, and no others:
         (1)  RECORD_SERIES_CODE
         (2)  CATEGORY_CODE
         (3)  SYSTEM_OF_RECORD
         (4)  CUSTODIAN_ROLE
         (5)  LAWFUL_BASIS
         (6)  RETENTION_TRIGGER
         (7)  RETENTION_PERIOD
         (8)  DISPOSITION_METHOD
         (9)  HOLD_FLAG
        (10)  LAST_REVIEW_DATE
    (b)  The master index is itself a public record and is disclosable in full.

§3  CATEGORIES
    (a)  ID — Identity elements: Legal Name; Alias; Date of Birth; Place of Birth; Sex; Gender Identity; Race; Ethnicity.
    (b)  GN — Government identifiers: Social Security Number; State Identification Number; Alien Registration Number; Vital Record Certificate Number.
    (c)  LC — Locator elements: Residence Address; Mailing Address; Telephone Number; Electronic Mail Address; Employer Name.
    (d)  PH — Health elements: Chief Complaint; Diagnosis; Laboratory Result; Medication; Encounter Date; Referring Provider.
    (e)  FN — Financial elements: Household Income; Payer; Account Balance; Bank Account Number; Payment Card Number.
    (f)  SN — Elevated-sensitivity elements: HIV Status; STI Test Result; Tuberculosis Case Data; Substance Use Treatment Data; Behavioural Health Note; Minor Consent Indicator.
    (g)  WF — Workforce elements: Employee Number; Position; Compensation; Licence Number; Screening Result.

§4  ACCESS CONTROL
    (a)  Assignment of a category code to a role is made only by the Health Information Manager, on written request of a Division Director, and only where the requesting role's duties require the category in full.
    (b)  SN is not aggregated with any other category in any report, extract, or dashboard.
    (c)  External disclosure of any category requires either a valid authorisation, a court order, or a statutory reporting mandate recorded in the disclosure accounting log.
    (d)  The disclosure accounting log is retained for six years and is available to the subject on request.

§5  RETENTION AND DISPOSITION
    (a)  Retention runs from the RETENTION_TRIGGER stated in the master index, not from record creation.
         (1)  ID, LC, PH — ten years from last encounter; minors to age twenty-two.
         (2)  GN — coterminous with the parent clinical or eligibility series.
         (3)  FN — seven years from fiscal year close.
         (4)  SN — twenty-five years from case closure.
         (5)  WF — seven years from separation; exposure records thirty years.
    (b)  Disposition proceeds only after the HOLD_FLAG is cleared by the County Attorney's designee.
    (c)  Approved methods: cross-cut shredding; pulping; degaussing; disintegration; cryptographic erase with verified key destruction.
    (d)  Vendor destruction is witnessed by departmental staff or performed under continuous video record retained one year.
    (e)  Certificates of destruction record series code, category codes, volume, method, and date only.
