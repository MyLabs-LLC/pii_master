SUNRIDGE VALLEY UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT
ADMINISTRATIVE STANDARD AS-114
PERSONAL INFORMATION CATEGORIES, ACCESS, RETENTION AND DISPOSAL
Issued by the Office of the Superintendent — Effective 1 August 2021
Supersedes AS-107 (2016)

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 This Standard establishes uniform requirements for the classification, access control, retention and disposal of records containing personal information held by the District in any medium, including paper, electronic database records, removable media and cloud-hosted systems.

1.2 This Standard applies to all District employees, substitute and temporary staff, contracted service providers, volunteers, and any third party granted access to District record systems.

1.3 Where a conflict arises between this Standard and applicable federal or state law, the legal requirement governs and the Records Custodian shall be notified in writing within five business days.

2. CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

2.1 The District maintains the following categories. Each category is defined by the field labels it comprises; no category description in this Standard shall reproduce recorded values.

2.2 Category A — Student Directory Information. Field labels: full legal name; preferred name; grade level; homeroom assignment; enrollment status; date of enrollment; participation in officially recognized activities; height and weight of athletic team members; awards received.

2.3 Category B — Student Education Records. Field labels: student identification number; course schedule; assignment scores; term grades; standardized assessment results; attendance record; disciplinary incident log; counselor conference notes; transcript.

2.4 Category C — Student Health and Immunization. Field labels: immunization series and dates administered; medication administration authorization; allergy classification; documented medical condition; individual health plan; school nurse visit log; emergency treatment consent status.

2.5 Category D — Special Education and Related Services. Field labels: eligibility determination; evaluation report; individualized education program goals and services; related service minutes; annual review date; procedural safeguards acknowledgment.

2.6 Category E — Family and Emergency Contact. Field labels: parent or guardian name; relationship to student; residential address; mailing address; primary and alternate telephone number; electronic mail address; custody or court order status; authorized pickup designee.

2.7 Category F — Free and Reduced-Price Meal Eligibility. Field labels: household size; household income band; eligibility determination code; benefit program participation indicator; application date.

2.8 Category G — Employee Human Resources Records. Field labels: employee identification number; taxpayer identification number; date of birth; home address; credential and certification number; employment classification; salary schedule placement; performance evaluation rating; disciplinary action record; leave balance.

2.9 Category H — Payroll and Benefits. Field labels: bank routing and account designation; withholding elections; benefit plan enrollment; dependent name and relationship; retirement system member number; garnishment order status.

2.10 Category I — Background Screening. Field labels: fingerprint submission date; clearance status; subsequent arrest notification indicator; screening expiration date.

2.11 Category J — Transportation and Facility Access. Field labels: bus route assignment; stop assignment; badge or fob identifier; access point log entry; video surveillance recording identifier.

3. AUTHORIZED ACCESS

3.1 Access is granted on the principle of least privilege and only to personnel with a demonstrated educational, administrative or legal need.

3.2 The following access matrix governs standing access:

    Category A — Site administrators; office managers; communications office.
    Category B — Site administrators; teachers of record; counselors; registrar; Student Information System administrators.
    Category C — Credentialed school nurse; health assistant under nurse supervision; site administrator for emergency response only.
    Category D — Special education case manager; program specialist; Director of Special Education; service providers named in the program.
    Category E — Office managers; site administrators; transportation dispatch for route contacts only.
    Category F — Nutrition Services Director; eligibility clerk; Business Office for reimbursement claims.
    Categories G, H, I — Human Resources Director; assigned HR analysts; Payroll Supervisor; Superintendent.
    Category J — Transportation Supervisor; Director of Facilities; School Resource Officer upon documented request.

3.3 All access is provisioned through role-based security groups. Individual account-level grants are prohibited except by written exception approved by the Records Custodian and reviewed at each quarter's end.

3.4 Third-party vendor access requires an executed data privacy agreement and is limited to the categories and field labels enumerated in that agreement's data inventory schedule.

3.5 Access logs for Categories C, D, G, H and I are reviewed monthly by the Records Custodian.

4. RETENTION PERIODS

4.1 Category A — Retain for the duration of enrollment plus one year.

4.2 Category B — Cumulative transcript retained permanently. All other Category B records retained five years following graduation, transfer or withdrawal.

4.3 Category C — Retain until the student reaches age 19 or three years after last District enrollment, whichever is later.

4.4 Category D — Retain five years following termination of services, except that eligibility determinations and final evaluation reports are retained for ten years.

4.5 Category E — Retain for the duration of enrollment plus one year, except custody and court order documentation, which follows the Category B schedule.

4.6 Category F — Retain three years following the close of the applicable federal fiscal year, or until the resolution of any audit finding, whichever is later.

4.7 Categories G and I — Retain for the duration of employment plus seven years.

4.8 Category H — Retain seven years from the close of the applicable tax year.

4.9 Category J — Access point logs and surveillance recordings retained forty-five days unless preserved for an active investigation. Route and stop assignments retained for the current and prior school year.

4.10 Any record subject to a litigation hold, audit hold or public records request is exempt from scheduled disposal until the hold is released in writing by the General Counsel.

5. DISPOSAL PROCEDURE

5.1 The Records Custodian shall generate a disposal eligibility report each January and July identifying records that have met their retention period.

5.2 The custodian of the originating department shall review the report and certify, in writing, that no hold under clause 4.10 applies.

5.3 Upon certification, disposal shall proceed as follows:

    (a) Paper records — cross-cut shredding to a maximum particle size of 4mm x 40mm, performed on District premises or by a bonded vendor providing a certificate of destruction.
    (b) Electronic records in production systems — deletion through the application's records purge function, followed by purge of associated backup sets at the next scheduled backup rotation.
    (c) Removable media and decommissioned drives — cryptographic erasure where supported, otherwise physical destruction by shredding or degaussing.
    (d) Surveillance media — automated overwrite at the end of the retention interval.

5.4 A disposal certificate shall be completed for each disposal event recording: category disposed; date range covered; volume or record count; method of destruction; name and title of the certifying custodian; vendor certificate reference where applicable.

5.5 Disposal certificates are retained permanently.

5.6 Destruction of any record outside this procedure, including disposal in general waste or recycling streams, constitutes a policy violation reportable under Board Policy 4119.21.

6. RESPONSIBILITIES AND REVIEW

6.1 The Records Custodian maintains the master data inventory, administers this Standard, and reports annually to the Board of Trustees.

6.2 Site administrators are responsible for departmental compliance and for the annual attestation due each 30 September.

6.3 This Standard shall be reviewed no less than every three years, and upon any material change to law or to the District's record systems.
