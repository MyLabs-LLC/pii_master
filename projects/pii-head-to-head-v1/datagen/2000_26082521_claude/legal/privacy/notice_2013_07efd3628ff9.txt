MEMORANDUM OF STANDARD PRACTICE — PRIVACY OF MEMBER RECORDS
File Reference: ADM/PRIV/03 — Issued 7 July 1996 — Revision 2

(1) The Credit Union holds personal information concerning its members in four distinct groupings, hereinafter referred to as the "Protected Groupings," which are defined as follows:

    (a) Identity Particulars, being those fields captured upon the Membership Application, namely Applicant Legal Name, Date of Birth, Social Security Number, Residence Address, Length of Residence, Home Telephone Number, and Employer Name;

    (b) Deposit Particulars, being the fields Account Number, Account Type Code, Date Opened, Ownership Designation, Beneficiary Designation, and Balance;

    (c) Borrowing Particulars, being the fields Loan Number, Purpose Code, Original Principal, Payment Amount, Consumer Report Reference, Score, Collateral Description, and Payment History Grid;

    (d) Correspondence Particulars, being the fields Notice Type, Date Mailed, and Return Reason Code.

(2) Access to the Protected Groupings shall be limited as follows: to grouping (a) and (b), the Member Services Representative and the Branch Manager; to grouping (c), the Loan Officer, the Collection Clerk, and the Vice President of Lending; to grouping (d), the Operations Clerk. The Internal Auditor and the Supervisory Committee shall have inspection rights over all groupings without limitation, provided that such inspection be recorded in the Access Register.

(3) It shall be the duty of every employee to refrain from discussing the contents of any Protected Grouping within the hearing of members of the public, to secure terminals by sign-off whenever leaving a workstation unattended, to place all working papers in locked storage at the close of business, and to refer every third-party request for information to the Compliance Officer without exception.

(4) Retention shall be as follows: grouping (a), six years after the relationship terminates; grouping (b), six years after the account is closed; grouping (c), six years after the loan is paid, charged off, or declined; grouping (d), two years from the date of mailing.

(5) Records that have attained the end of their retention period shall be listed by the Records Custodian upon the Quarterly Purge List, which shall be circulated to Department Heads for objection, and, thirty days having elapsed without objection, shall be destroyed by cross-cut shredding under dual control, whereupon a Certificate of Destruction shall be executed.

(6) Departures from this Memorandum shall be authorised in writing by the President and reported to the Board at its next regular meeting.
