RIVER OAKS FEDERAL CREDIT UNION
RECORDS HANDLING STANDARD RH-04-02
PERSONAL INFORMATION INVENTORY AND RETENTION SCHEDULE

Effective 01 March 2004. Supersedes RH-02-01.
Issued by the Office of the Records Custodian. Approved by the Supervisory Committee.

1. SCOPE

This standard governs each category of personal information maintained by the credit union in any medium, including paper, microfiche, magnetic tape, and on-line files. It is issued under Title V of the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act, Regulation P, and NCUA Rules and Regulations Part 748, Appendix A. Departmental procedures may impose stricter controls but may not shorten a retention period stated below.

3. ACCESS

Access is granted on a least-privilege basis by the Security Administrator upon written request of the department head. Grants are reviewed semi-annually by the Internal Auditor and revoked on transfer or separation.

4. DISPOSAL CODES

D-1  Cross-cut shredding on premises, witnessed by two staff members.
D-2  Bonded destruction vendor; certificate of destruction filed.
D-3  Degaussing followed by physical destruction of the media.
D-4  Overwrite of the electronic record; index entry retained.

Destruction is suspended for any category subject to a litigation hold or to a pending examination request, and does not resume until released in writing by the Records Custodian.
