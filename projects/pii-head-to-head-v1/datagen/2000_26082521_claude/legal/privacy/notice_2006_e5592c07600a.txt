METROPOLITAN TRANSIT AUTHORITY
Office of the Records Custodian
Standard RC-2004-03 — Personal Information Holdings: Categories, Access, Disposal
Effective 1 April 2004. Supersedes RC-1998-07.

Scope
- Applies to every record series held by an operating division, a support division, or a contracted agent acting on Authority instruction.
- Applies irrespective of medium: paper, microform, magnetic tape, optical disc, or online database table.

Categories held
- Category A — Workforce administration. Field labels: Employee Number; Full Legal Name; Date of Birth; Social Security Number; Home Mailing Address; Home Telephone Number; Emergency Contact Name; Bank Routing Number; Deposit Account Number.
- Category B — Operator qualification. Field labels: Commercial Driver Licence Number; Endorsement Code; Medical Certification Expiry; Restriction Code.
- Category C — Fare media accounts. Field labels: Card Serial Number; Registrant Name; Billing Address; Payment Instrument Reference; Autoload Threshold.
- Category D — Reduced-fare and paratransit eligibility. Field labels: Applicant Name; Eligibility Determination Code; Functional Assessment Code; Certifying Practitioner Identifier.
- Category E — Incident, claim, and surveillance. Field labels: Claimant Name; Incident Reference; Camera Identifier; Recording Interval.

Access
- Category A: Human Resources Director; Payroll Supervisor; Internal Audit on written requisition.
- Category B: Operations Superintendent; Safety Officer.
- Category C: Fare Systems Administrator; Customer Service Manager.
- Category D: Eligibility Coordinator; Occupational Health Nurse.
- Category E: Security Operations Supervisor; General Counsel.

Retention and disposal
- Categories A and B: six years following separation, then cross-cut shred.
- Category C: three years following account closure, then secure erasure.
- Category D: two years following expiry of determination, then cross-cut shred.
- Category E: thirty days for routine recordings; five years where a claim is opened.
- All destruction is entered in the Disposal Register by the Records Custodian.
