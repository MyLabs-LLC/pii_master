OFFICE OF THE UNIVERSITY REGISTRAR
RECORDS DISPOSITION SCHEDULE AND PROCEDURE
Standard RM-2004-7 — Issued June 2004

I. APPLICATION

    1. This Standard prescribes the retention period and disposition method for each record series in the custody of the Office. It is to be read with the category definitions in RM-2004-1.
    2. Periods stated are minimum periods. No series shall be destroyed in advance of its stated period.

III. PROCEDURE

    3. In July of each year the Records Custodian shall produce a disposition list identifying each box, file, or data set that has reached the end of its retention period. The list shall state the series title, the inclusive terms, the volume, and the proposed method.
    4. The disposition list shall be transmitted to the Registrar and to the Office of the General Counsel. Destruction shall not proceed until both have returned written concurrence or thirty days have elapsed without objection.
    5. Where a litigation hold, audit notice, or open records request affects any series on the list, that series shall be struck from the list and the hold recorded in the disposition file.
    6. Paper shall be reduced by cross-cut shredding to a residue particle not exceeding the dimensions specified by the University's information security guideline. Bulk destruction by a contracted vendor is permitted only where the vendor performs the reduction on University premises in the presence of a designee of the Records Custodian.
    7. Magnetic media shall be degaussed and then physically deformed. Optical media shall be shredded or abraded across the full recording surface. Return of media to inventory following simple erasure is prohibited.
    8. A certificate of destruction shall be executed for each disposition event, shall be signed by the Records Custodian and the witness, and shall be retained permanently in the disposition file.
    9. The disposition file, the annual disposition lists, and the certificates of destruction are themselves permanent records of the Office.
