MEMORANDUM

TO:      ALL PERSONNEL
FROM:    RECORDS CUSTODIAN, ON BEHALF OF THE COMPLIANCE COMMITTEE
DATE:    NOVEMBER 2011
RE:      STANDARD RM-14 — DISPOSAL OF PERSONAL INFORMATION AND CERTIFICATION OF DESTRUCTION

SECTION 1 — APPLICATION

1.1  This standard governs the disposal of every medium on which the Company holds personal information, including paper files, imaged records, database extracts, backup tapes, facsimile transmissions, working notes, and the hard drives of retired workstations, copiers, and multifunction devices.

SECTION 2 — CATEGORIES SUBJECT TO CONTROLLED DISPOSAL

2.1  Identity Data — fields: Full Legal Name; Former or Maiden Name; Date of Birth; Marital Status; Specimen Signature.

2.2  Government Identifiers — fields: Social Security Number; Taxpayer Identification Number; Driver's License or State Identification Number.

2.3  Financial Data — fields: Deposit Account Number; ABA Routing Number; Wire Instruction Detail; Loan Number; Payoff Balance.

2.4  Transaction Data — fields: Property Address; Parcel Number; Legal Description; Order Number; Policy Number.

2.5  Personnel Data — fields: Home Address; Personal Telephone Number; Compensation Rate; Beneficiary Designation.

SECTION 3 — WHO MAY AUTHORISE AND WHO MAY PERFORM

3.1  Only the Records Custodian may authorise a destruction batch. Only the Records Custodian, the branch manager of record, or the bonded vendor's attendant under supervision may perform one.

3.2  No employee may dispose of a record containing any category listed in Section 2 by placing it in a waste receptacle, recycling stream, or unattended bin. Interim material awaiting destruction is deposited in a locked console only.

SECTION 4 — RETENTION TRIGGERS

4.1  Closed escrow file: five years from final disbursement; seven years where a closing protection letter was issued or a claim was noticed.

4.2  Trust account and wire records: seven years from reconciliation or from the date of the wire.

4.3  Tax reporting workpapers: four years from the close of the reporting year.

4.4  Personnel file: seven years from separation.

4.5  Title evidence and issued policy: permanent; not eligible for destruction under this standard.

SECTION 5 — METHOD

5.1  Paper: cross-cut shred to a particle width not exceeding one-quarter inch, or pulp.

5.2  Magnetic media: overwrite in full; where overwriting fails or the medium is defective, degauss and then physically destroy.

5.3  Optical media and microform: shred or incinerate.

5.4  Devices with embedded storage: the Information Systems Manager removes and destroys the storage component before the device leaves Company custody.

SECTION 6 — CERTIFICATION AND RECORD

6.1  Every batch is entered in the Destruction Register under the headings Batch Number, Branch, Category, Record Series, Retention Trigger Date, Volume, Method, Vendor Certificate Number, Date Performed, and Authorising Officer.

6.2  Vendor certificates are affixed to the Register entry. The Register is retained permanently and is produced to auditors and to underwriters on request.

SECTION 7 — SUSPENSION

7.1  A legal hold notice from General Counsel suspends destruction for the identified population. Suspension is noted against the affected batch and is lifted only in writing.

SECTION 8 — BREACH OF THIS STANDARD

8.1  Failure to observe Sections 3.2 or 5 is reported immediately to the Records Custodian and the Chief Compliance Officer and is treated as a matter for disciplinary action.
