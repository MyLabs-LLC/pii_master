*  PATIENT ACCOUNTING / BILLING OFFICE                                        *
*  INFORMATION HANDLING STANDARD IHS-16                                       *
*  Subject: Categories of Personal Information; Custodianship; Retention      *
*           Schedules; Authorized Methods of Disposal                         *
*  Status: In force. Reviewed and re-issued this calendar year 2016.          *
*  Enquiries: the Records Custodian (billing office) or the Privacy Officer.  *
*******************************************************************************

1.0   DEFINITIONS
      1.1   "Personal information" means any information, in any medium, that
            identifies or reasonably may be used to identify a natural person,
            together with any information appended to or derived from it.
      1.2   "Category" means a grouping of field labels administered as a
            single unit for purposes of access control, retention, and
            disposal, irrespective of the systems in which the constituent
            fields physically reside.
      1.3   "Custodial unit" means the organisational unit charged with the
            day-to-day integrity of a category; custodianship is a function of
            office and does not attach to any individual personally.
      1.4   "Trigger event" means the occurrence from which a retention period
            is computed, and not the date on which a record was created,
            unless the schedule expressly so provides.

2.0   CATEGORIES ADMINISTERED BY THE BILLING OFFICE
      2.1   Identity and contact category
            2.1.1   Field labels: Patient Full Name; Alias or Former Name;
                    Date of Birth; Sex; Mailing Address; Alternate Address;
                    Telephone Number; Alternate Telephone Number; Electronic
                    Mail Address; Preferred Method of Contact.
      2.2   Sensitive identifier category
            2.2.1   Field labels: Social Security Number; Medicare Beneficiary
                    Identifier; Medicaid Case Number; Immigration or Visa
                    Status Indicator; Military Service Indicator.
            2.2.2   This category is subject to encryption at rest and in
                    transit and to the masking rule at paragraph 3.4.
      2.3   Payment instrument category
            2.3.1   Field labels: Payment Card Brand; Payment Card Number
                    (truncated to the permissible digits only); Expiration
                    Month and Year; Cardholder Name; Bank Routing Number;
                    Bank Account Number; Authorisation Response Code.
            2.3.2   Sensitive authentication data shall not be stored
                    subsequent to authorisation in any circumstance.
      2.4   Account and adjudication category
            2.4.1   Field labels: Account Number; Encounter Number; Date of
                    Service; Amount Billed; Amount Paid; Balance Due; Claim
                    Number; Denial Reason Code; Appeal Status.
      2.5   Hardship and legal status category
            2.5.1   Field labels: Household Size; Household Gross Income;
                    Income Verification Document Type; Charity Determination
                    Outcome; Bankruptcy Petition Number; Estate Claim Status;
                    Power of Attorney Designation; Deceased Indicator.

3.0   AUTHORISED ACCESS
      3.1   Access is granted by role and is reviewed not less than quarterly.
      3.2   Schedule of authorisations:
            3.2.1   Billing Representative I — categories 2.1 and 2.4.
            3.2.2   Billing Representative II and Revenue Cycle Supervisor —
                    categories 2.1, 2.3 (truncated), and 2.4.
            3.2.3   Financial Counsellor — categories 2.1, 2.2, and 2.5.
            3.2.4   Collections Representative — categories 2.1, 2.4, and so
                    much of 2.5 as concerns legal status.
            3.2.5   Privacy Officer, Information Security Officer, Internal
                    Audit, and the Office of General Counsel — all categories,
                    for oversight, investigative, or defensive purposes.
      3.3   External auditors and contracted collection agencies obtain access
            only under an executed business associate agreement and only to
            the categories enumerated in the schedule appended thereto.
      3.4   Category 2.2 is displayed in masked form by default; unmasking is
            logged with the fields User Role; Account Number; Timestamp;
            Business Justification Code.

4.0   RETENTION SCHEDULE
      4.1   Categories 2.1, 2.3, and 2.4 — seven (7) years from the trigger
            event "account closed and balance extinguished".
      4.2   Category 2.2 — coterminous with the account record to which it is
            appended, and purged from all secondary extracts at ninety (90)
            days.
      4.3   Category 2.5 — seven (7) years from determination or from release
            of the estate claim, whichever is later.
      4.4   Records of a minor patient — until majority plus the period at 4.1.
      4.5   Records under legal hold — retained until written release.

5.0   DISPOSAL
      5.1   Disposal is authorised only by the Records Custodian, upon written
            confirmation from the Office of General Counsel that no hold
            subsists.
      5.2   Paper is destroyed by cross-cut shredding; electronic media are
            cryptographically erased, purged, or physically destroyed.
      5.3   The Certificate of Destruction shall record, under these headings
            and no others, Record Series; Category Reference; Media Type;
            Container Count; Method Applied; Date of Destruction; Vendor Name;
            Witnessing Role; Custodian Approval.
      5.4   Certificates are retained permanently and are produced on demand to
            the Privacy Officer.
