MERIDIAN FREIGHT SERVICES LIMITED
INFORMATION HANDLING STANDARD IHS-04
PERSONAL INFORMATION INVENTORY, ACCESS AND RETENTION

Issued 11 March 2021    Version 1.2    Owner: Records Custodian    Review cycle: annual

1. This standard fixes the categories of personal information held by the company, the role groups permitted to access each category, the applicable retention period and the approved disposal route. No category may be created, extended, exported or copied outside the register below without the written authority of the Records Custodian.

2. Register of categories:

REF   CATEGORY OF PERSONAL DATA          AUTHORISED ROLE GROUP     RETENTION PERIOD          DISPOSAL METHOD
----  --------------------------------  ------------------------  ------------------------  ---------------
A-01  Employee identification records   Personnel Administrator   6 years from leaving date Cross-cut shredding
A-02  Driver entitlement records        Fleet Compliance Officer  5 years from expiry date  Cross-cut shredding
A-03  Tachograph and hours records      Transport Manager         24 months from entry      Secure erasure
A-04  Payroll and deductions data       Payroll Administrator     7 years from tax year end Secure erasure
A-05  Occupational health records       Occupational Health Lead  40 years from last entry  Confidential uplift
A-06  Consignee contact records         Customer Service Lead     3 years from delivery     Secure erasure
A-07  Vehicle telematics and CCTV       Security Supervisor       31 days rolling           Scheduled overwrite
A-08  Unsuccessful applicant records    HR Coordinator            6 months from decision    Cross-cut shredding

3. Retention periods run from the trigger stated in the register. On expiry the Records Custodian issues a disposal instruction to the holding function. Disposal must be completed within 30 days of that instruction and entered in the destruction log. Where a legal hold or an open claim is notified, the retention period is suspended until the hold is formally lifted by the Records Custodian.
