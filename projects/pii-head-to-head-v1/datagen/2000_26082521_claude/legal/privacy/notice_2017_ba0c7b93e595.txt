INFORMATION PRACTICES STANDARD 5.00
CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION, CUSTODY, AND DISPOSITION

Approved: Executive Committee, 19 January 1999
Owner: Records Custodian · Concurrence: Compliance Officer, Data Processing Manager
Review Cycle: Annual · Classification of this document: Internal Use Only

5.01 GENERAL

5.01.1 This Standard governs the custody of personal information without regard to medium and shall be read together with the Employee Confidentiality Undertaking executed by each employee upon hire and reaffirmed annually.

5.01.2 The Records Custodian is the sole authority for the interpretation of this Standard and for the maintenance of the Master Record Inventory.

5.02 CATEGORIES

5.02.1 CATEGORY 1 — MEMBER IDENTITY. Fields: Legal Name; Alternate Name; Date of Birth; Social Security Number; Residence Address; Mailing Address; Home Telephone Number; Employment Telephone Number; Identification Document Type; Identification Document Number.

5.02.2 CATEGORY 2 — ACCOUNT AND SERVICE. Fields: Member Number; Share Suffix; Loan Suffix; Card Number; Personal Identification Number Reference; Date Established; Ownership Type; Beneficiary Name; Statement Cycle Code.

5.02.3 CATEGORY 3 — FINANCIAL CONDITION. Fields: Employer Name; Gross Income; Monthly Obligations; Consumer Reporting Agency Name; Report Date; Score; Public Record Indicator; Bankruptcy Indicator; Charge-Off Indicator.

5.02.4 CATEGORY 4 — PERSONNEL. Fields: Employee Legal Name; Employee Number; Social Security Number; Date of Hire; Position Title; Salary Grade; Performance Rating; Bonding Status; Beneficiary Designation.

5.02.5 CATEGORY 5 — INVESTIGATIVE. Fields: Case Number; Case Type; Referring Officer; Regulatory Filing Reference; Status.

5.03 CUSTODY AND ACCESS

5.03.1 Each category is assigned a single Custodian Department, as set out below. Access outside the Custodian Department requires the written concurrence of the Department Head and the Compliance Officer.

     Category   Custodian Department        Permitted Roles
     --------   --------------------        ---------------
     1          Member Services             Member Services Representative; Branch Manager
     2          Operations                  Operations Clerk; Operations Supervisor
     3          Lending                     Loan Officer; Collections Officer; VP Lending
     4          Human Resources             Human Resources Manager; President
     5          Compliance                  Compliance Officer; General Counsel

5.03.2 Category 4 shall be maintained in locked cabinets physically separate from member records, and Category 5 shall be maintained in a locked cabinet within the office of the Compliance Officer. Neither shall be maintained upon any shared network volume.

5.03.3 Terminal access rights shall be revoked by the Data Processing Manager not later than the close of business on an employee's final day of service, and a quarterly reconciliation of active user profiles against the payroll register shall be performed by the Internal Auditor.

5.04 RETENTION

5.04.1 Category 1 and Category 2: six (6) years after termination of the relationship.
5.04.2 Category 3: six (6) years after satisfaction, charge-off, or denial; twenty-five (25) months where the record consists solely of a declined application.
5.04.3 Category 4: seven (7) years after separation from service.
5.04.4 Category 5: five (5) years after closure of the case.

5.05 DISPOSITION

5.05.1 Destruction shall proceed only upon a Disposition Authorisation bearing the columns Category, Inventory Locator, Retention Expiry, Method, Date Destroyed, and the signatures of two witnesses.

5.05.2 Approved methods are cross-cut shredding for paper, incineration for microfiche, and degaussing followed by physical destruction for magnetic and optical media.

5.05.3 Disposition is suspended in respect of any record subject to examination, subpoena, litigation, or open investigation, and may resume only upon written release by the General Counsel.
