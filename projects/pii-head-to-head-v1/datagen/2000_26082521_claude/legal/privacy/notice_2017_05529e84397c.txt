RIVERSTONE FEDERAL CREDIT UNION
Standard PR-04 — Member Information Categories, Access and Retention
Effective 1 March 2011. Supersedes PR-03 (June 2008).
Standard Owner: Records Custodian. Approving Authority: Supervisory Committee.

Scope
- Applies to all member, applicant, employee and vendor-principal information held by the credit union in paper, microform, magnetic or electronic media.
- Applies to information held by third-party servicers under contract.

Categories of Information Held
- Identification data: legal name, former name, date of birth, government-issued identification number, taxpayer identification number, citizenship indicator.
- Contact data: mailing address, residence address, primary telephone, alternate telephone, electronic mail address.
- Relationship data: membership number, share and share-draft account numbers, date of admission to membership, eligibility basis, joint owner designation, beneficiary designation.
- Financial data: balance history, transaction history, direct deposit source, automated clearing house originator, wire instruction record.
- Credit data: consumer report, credit score, income declaration, employment declaration, debt-to-income calculation, collateral description, adverse action reason code.
- Security data: authentication credential, challenge response, signature card image, image capture of government identification.

Access Authorisation
- Member Services Representative: identification, contact and relationship categories.
- Lending Officer: credit and collateral categories, for active applications only.
- Collections Officer: financial and credit categories, delinquent obligations only.
- BSA Officer and Compliance Officer: all categories, on documented investigative need.
- Information Systems Administrator: system-level access to storage media without authority to read content for business purposes.

Retention
- Membership application and signature card: life of membership plus seven years.
- Closed loan file: five years from final payment or charge-off.
- Denied credit application and adverse action notice: twenty-five months.
- Currency transaction and suspicious activity documentation: five years from filing.
- Board and committee minutes: permanent.

Disposal
- Paper: cross-cut shredding at 4mm or finer, witnessed by the Records Custodian, entered on the Destruction Log (fields: category, medium, volume, date, witness role).
- Electronic: three-pass overwrite or physical destruction of platters; certificate of destruction retained seven years.
- No disposal during any active litigation hold issued by the General Counsel.
