NORTHVALE UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT
ADMINISTRATIVE REGULATION AR-4180
PERSONAL INFORMATION HOLDINGS: CLASSIFICATION, ACCESS, RETENTION, DISPOSAL
Adopted March 2004 — supersedes AR-4180 (1997) — review cycle 36 months
Office of primary responsibility: the Records Custodian, Student Services

SECTION 1 — CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD
The District holds the following categories only. Any holding not listed below must be reported to the Records Custodian before it is created.

  * Student directory category — legal name fields, grade level, enrollment status, dates of attendance, school of assignment.
  * Student identity category — assigned student identifier, date of birth field, place of birth field, gender field, home language field.
  * Student contact category — residence address fields, mailing address fields, home and emergency telephone fields, parent or guardian name fields, custody status indicator.
  * Academic record category — course enrolment, marks, standardised assessment scores, promotion and retention decisions, transcript.
  * Special education category — referral records, assessment reports, eligibility determinations, individualised education programme documents, service logs.
  * Health category — immunisation record, medication authorisation, health alert code, nurse visit log, screening result.
  * Eligibility and benefit category — meal benefit status indicator, transportation eligibility, supplemental programme participation flag.
  * Conduct category — incident report, investigation notes, disciplinary action taken, appeal outcome.
  * Employee category — personnel file, certification and credential fields, tax withholding fields, direct deposit account fields, leave balance, evaluation record, background check disposition.
  * Applicant category — application form, references, interview notes for candidates not hired.

SECTION 2 — WHO MAY ACCESS
  * School officials with a legitimate educational interest may access the academic, conduct, contact and identity categories for the students assigned to them, and no others.
  * Only the school nurse, the Records Custodian, and staff designated in writing on the health alert distribution list may access the health category.
  * Only special education case managers assigned to the pupil, the programme supervisor, and the Records Custodian may access the special education category.
  * Only Payroll and Human Resources staff may access the employee and applicant categories. Building administrators may access evaluation records for staff they supervise.
  * Parents, guardians, and eligible students may inspect the records of that student on written request, within 45 days.
  * Disclosure to any party outside the District requires written consent, a subpoena logged by the Records Custodian, or a documented health or safety emergency.
  * Every disclosure outside the District is entered in the access log kept with the file.

SECTION 3 — RETENTION PERIODS
  * Transcript and permanent academic record — retain indefinitely.
  * Cumulative student folder (non-transcript contents) — 5 years after graduation, transfer, or withdrawal.
  * Special education category — 5 years after the last date of service, then destroyed after notice to the parent or eligible student.
  * Health category — 2 years after the pupil leaves the District, except immunisation record, which follows the cumulative folder.
  * Conduct category — 3 years after the close of the school year in which the incident occurred.
  * Eligibility and benefit category — 3 years after the end of the programme year.
  * Employee personnel file — 7 years after separation. Payroll and withholding records — 7 years.
  * Applicant category (not hired) — 2 years from the closing date of the posting.

SECTION 4 — DISPOSAL PROCEDURE
  * The Records Custodian issues a destruction list each July and each January.
  * Building secretaries verify that no listed file is subject to a litigation hold or an open records request, and initial the list.
  * Paper is cross-cut shredded on site, or placed in locked bins for the contracted destruction vendor, never in general waste or recycling.
  * Microfiche and magnetic media are shredded or degaussed; hard drives are wiped to Department standard and physically destroyed before surplus.
  * Two staff witness each destruction run. The certificate of destruction records the category, date range, volume, method, and both signatures, and is retained for 7 years.
