SECURE DISPOSAL OF PERSONAL INFORMATION — OPERATING STANDARD

Ref: IG-STD-009
Issued: 3 March 2021
Owner: Records Custodian
Authorised by: Data Protection Lead

1. SCOPE

1.1 This Standard governs the destruction of records containing personal information in any format, including:
   (a) paper worker files and correspondence;
   (b) records held in the applicant tracking and payroll systems;
   (c) removable media, including portable drives and optical media;
   (d) end-of-life laptops, desktops, mobile handsets and multifunction devices holding stored images.

2. TRIGGER

2.1 Disposal is triggered only by expiry of the retention period recorded against the category in DP-SCH-07, or by a determination of the Data Protection Lead.

2.2 Disposal must not be triggered by a request from a hiring manager, a client, or a branch, and must not be used to remove records that are the subject of a complaint, claim, investigation or regulatory enquiry.

3. AUTHORISATION

3.1 Each disposal batch requires authorisation by two roles: the Records Custodian and either the Compliance Officer or the Data Protection Lead.

3.2 Batches containing Special Category Data or Criminal Offence Data require authorisation by the Data Protection Lead in every case.

4. METHOD

4.1 Paper: cross-cut shredding to DIN 66399 level P-4 minimum, on site or by an approved contractor under lockable console collection.

4.2 Electronic records in live systems: logical deletion followed by purge of the deleted-items store within 30 days, and confirmation of expiry of the corresponding backup generation.

4.3 Removable media and end-of-life hardware: cryptographic erase where supported, otherwise overwrite to NIST SP 800-88 Purge, otherwise physical destruction of the storage device.

4.4 Multifunction devices: internal storage purged before removal from the premises. Removal without purge confirmation is prohibited.

5. RECORD OF DISPOSAL

5.1 The Records Custodian records each batch in the Disposal Register with the following columns:
   (a) Batch Reference
   (b) Record Category
   (c) Retention Rule Applied
   (d) Format
   (e) Item Count or Volume
   (f) Method
   (g) Date of Destruction
   (h) Authorising Role (first)
   (i) Authorising Role (second)
   (j) Contractor Name and Certificate Number, where applicable

5.2 The Disposal Register contains no worker-level identifiers. Entries are made at category and batch level only.

5.3 The Register is retained for six years and reviewed annually by the Compliance Officer. Contractor certificates are filed against the batch reference.

6. NON-COMPLIANCE

6.1 Destruction outside this Standard, including disposal of paper records in general waste or recycling, is a disciplinary matter and is reportable to the Data Protection Lead as an incident.
