RIVERBEND MUTUAL CREDIT UNION
BOARD-APPROVED STANDARD 14: PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD BY THE CREDIT UNION
Adopted by resolution of the Board, 3 November 1996. Revision 2, 5 May 1999.
Responsible officer: the Records Custodian, reporting to the Compliance Officer.

ARTICLE I — APPLICATION

This standard applies to every record about a natural person created or
received by the credit union, whatever its medium and wherever it is held,
including records held at the service bureau under contract. It binds every
employee, every volunteer official, and every contractor granted access.

ARTICLE III — ACCESS

3.1  Access is granted to a position, in writing, by the head of the custodian
department, and is limited to those categories the position requires in the
ordinary course of its duties.

3.2  Categories 7 through 14 and 21 are closed to the teller line at all times.

3.3  Volunteer officials and members of the Supervisory Committee may view any
category in the course of their duties but may not amend, remove, or copy any
record.

3.4  Requests from outside the credit union are referred without exception to
the Compliance Officer, who determines whether the request is answerable under
the Right to Financial Privacy Act, the Fair Credit Reporting Act, or a valid
legal process, and who records the disposition of every such request.

3.5  Terminals displaying any category must be positioned so that the screen is
not visible from the member side of the counter, and must be cleared before the
workstation is left unattended.

ARTICLE IV — DISPOSAL

4.1  Twice yearly the Records Custodian produces an eligibility list of records
that have reached term, showing category, volume, and location.

4.2  The list is reviewed by the Compliance Officer, who removes from it any
record subject to examination, litigation, subpoena, or an unresolved member
dispute. The remainder is approved for destruction over signature.

4.3  Destruction is carried out by the method shown in Article II, witnessed by
two employees of different departments. Where an outside contractor is engaged,
the contractor operates under a written confidentiality undertaking and returns
a certificate of destruction.

4.4  The Records Custodian maintains a destruction log showing category, volume,
method, date, and the two witnessing positions. The log is retained permanently
and is furnished to the Supervisory Committee and to the examiner on request.

ARTICLE V — REVIEW

This standard is reviewed annually by the Compliance Officer and re-approved by
the Board. Departures require prior written authorisation, which is filed with
this standard.
