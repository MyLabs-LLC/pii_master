MERIDIAN PHARMACY GROUP
Records Handling Bulletin 2016-07: Store-Level Disposal of Personal Information
Issued by the Records Custodian. Applies to all retail locations and clinic sites.

This Bulletin governs the disposal of personal information held in paper form at store level and supplements, but does not displace, the retention schedule set out in the Privacy and Records Standard. Personal information held at store level falls within four handling classes: patient identity fields, comprising Patient Name, Date of Birth, Address and Telephone Number; dispensing fields, comprising Prescription Number, Drug Product Identifier, Prescriber Name and Fill Date; payer fields, comprising Member Identifier and Group Number; and payment fields, comprising Cardholder Name and the truncated Primary Account Number as it appears on the customer transaction record.

No record within any handling class may be placed in general refuse, in recycling, or in any receptacle other than a locked destruction console. Consoles are keyed to the Pharmacist-in-Charge and the Store Manager jointly and are serviced on a scheduled interval by the bonded destruction vendor. Where the retention period has expired, the Pharmacist-in-Charge verifies against the schedule before deposit; where the period is unexpired, the record is returned to the secured file room. Printed material generated in error, including misprinted labels and duplicate summaries bearing any field of the identity or dispensing classes, is deposited immediately at the point of generation.

Colleagues encountering personal information outside a controlled receptacle are to secure it and report the circumstance to the Privacy Official the same business day.
