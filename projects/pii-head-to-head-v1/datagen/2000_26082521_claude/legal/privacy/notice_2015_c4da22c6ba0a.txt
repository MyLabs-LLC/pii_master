COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
RECORDS AND INFORMATION MANAGEMENT STANDARD RIM-STD-04
PERSONAL INFORMATION: CLASSIFICATION, ACCESS, RETENTION, DISPOSITION
Effective 15 March 2021. Supersedes RIM-STD-04 (rev. 2016). Review cycle 24 months.
Approval: Director of Public Health. Concurrence: Privacy Officer; Office of County Counsel.

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
   1.1 This standard establishes classification, access control, retention, and disposition requirements for personal information held by the Department in any medium, including paper, electronic, imaged, and removable media.
   1.2 Scope extends to records held by contracted entities and business associates acting on the Department's behalf.

2. CLASSIFICATION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION
   2.1 Category A — Direct Identifiers. Sensitivity tier: Restricted.
       2.1.1 Field labels: Last Name; First Name; Middle Initial; Suffix; Prior or Alias Name; Medical Record Number; Master Person Index Number; Case Number.
   2.2 Category B — Contact Attributes. Sensitivity tier: Restricted.
       2.2.1 Field labels: Residential Street Address; Mailing Address; Municipality; Postal Code; Primary Telephone Number; Secondary Telephone Number; Electronic Mail Address; Emergency Contact Name; Emergency Contact Relationship.
   2.3 Category C — Demographic Attributes. Sensitivity tier: Confidential.
       2.3.1 Field labels: Date of Birth; Sex Assigned at Birth; Gender Identity; Race; Ethnicity; Preferred Language; Interpreter Required Indicator; Marital Status; Household Size.
   2.4 Category D — Government-Issued Identifiers. Sensitivity tier: Restricted.
       2.4.1 Field labels: Social Security Number; Driver License Number; State Identification Card Number; Alien Registration Number; Birth Certificate File Number.
   2.5 Category E — Health and Clinical Information. Sensitivity tier: Restricted.
       2.5.1 Field labels: Reportable Condition Code; Diagnosis Code; Immunization Series Record; Laboratory Result; Specimen Collection Site; Treatment Plan; Case Investigation Narrative; Contact Linkage Identifier.
   2.6 Category F — Financial and Payer Information. Sensitivity tier: Restricted.
       2.6.1 Field labels: Payer Name; Subscriber Identification Number; Group Number; Sliding-Fee Determination Tier; Gross Household Income; Bank Routing Number; Bank Account Number.
   2.7 Category G — Workforce and Volunteer Information. Sensitivity tier: Confidential.
       2.7.1 Field labels: Employee Identification Number; Position Classification; Professional Licensure Number; Immunization Attestation; Background Screening Disposition Code; Payroll Deduction Code.
   2.8 Category H — System and Access Metadata. Sensitivity tier: Internal.
       2.8.1 Field labels: User Account Identifier; Authentication Timestamp; Source Network Address; Record Access Audit Entry; Device Identifier.

3. AUTHORIZED ACCESS
   3.1 Access is granted by role, on the minimum-necessary principle, and is provisioned only by the Systems Access Administrator upon written authorization of the applicable Division Director.
   3.2 Role assignments:
       3.2.1 Categories A, B, C, E — Clinical Services staff in a treatment relationship; the Health Information Management Supervisor; the Communicable Disease Investigator assigned to the case.
       3.2.2 Category D — the Eligibility Determination Specialist; the Vital Records Registrar. Display is masked in all other contexts.
       3.2.3 Category F — the Billing and Reimbursement Analyst; the Fiscal Officer.
       3.2.4 Category G — the Human Resources Manager; the Employee Health Coordinator.
       3.2.5 Category H — the Information Security Officer; the Internal Auditor. Read-only.
   3.3 The Privacy Officer holds audit access to all categories for compliance review purposes.

4. RETENTION PERIODS
   4.1 Clinical service records: ten years from the date of last encounter; for a minor, until attainment of the age of majority plus ten years, whichever is later.
   4.2 Communicable disease investigation records: seven years from case closure.
   4.3 Eligibility and sliding-fee determination records: five years from determination.
   4.4 Billing and payer records: seven years from final adjudication.
   4.5 Workforce records: seven years from separation; exposure-related records, thirty years.
   4.6 System access audit entries: six years from creation.
   4.7 Any record subject to a Litigation Hold Notice is exempt from destruction until the hold is released in writing by County Counsel.

5. DISPOSITION
   5.1 Eligible records are identified quarterly by the Records Custodian from the Retention Disposition Report.
   5.2 Paper is destroyed by cross-cut shredding to a residue particle no greater than the standard specified in the vendor agreement, witnessed by a second staff member.
   5.3 Electronic media are sanitized by cryptographic erase or purge in accordance with NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 1; media that cannot be purged are physically destroyed.
   5.4 Each destruction event is entered in the Certificate of Destruction Register with the columns: Record Series Code; Category Codes; Media Type; Volume; Destruction Method; Destruction Date; Performing Role; Witnessing Role; Vendor Certificate Number.
   5.5 The register is retained permanently.
