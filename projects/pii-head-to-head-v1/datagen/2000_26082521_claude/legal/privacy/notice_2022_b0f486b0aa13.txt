HALBROOK COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
INTERNAL MEMORANDUM — RECORDS AND PRIVACY

TO:       All Division Supervisors
FROM:     Office of the Privacy Officer
SUBJECT:  Standing Instruction PI-7, Categories of Personal Information
DATE:     19 October 2021

Effective immediately, the following instruction governs the holding, access, and disposal of personal information in every division of this Department, and supersedes all divisional practice inconsistent with it. Supervisors shall cause a copy to be posted in each records area.

CATEGORY                          CUSTODIAN OF RECORD    PERMITTED ACCESS     RETENTION            DISPOSITION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Identifying particulars           Records Custodian      Intake; Custodian    7 yrs from closure   Shred, witnessed
Contact particulars               Records Custodian      Intake; Custodian    7 yrs from closure   Shred, witnessed
Demographic descriptors           Program Supervisor     Program staff        10 yrs from entry    Shred, witnessed
Clinical and treatment content    Health Officer         Clinical staff       10 yrs from care     Incinerate
Screening and test result data    Health Officer         Clinical staff       10 yrs from care     Incinerate
Case investigation content        Health Officer         Investigators        25 yrs from report   Incinerate
Eligibility and income content    Fiscal Officer         Fiscal staff         6 fiscal years       Shred, witnessed
Identifier reference content      Privacy Officer        Privacy Officer      6 fiscal years       Erase, then shred
Personnel and payroll content     HR Officer             HR Officer           5 yrs from exit      Shred, witnessed
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Three points require emphasis. First, the categories above are exhaustive; where a division believes it holds information not falling within any of them, the Privacy Officer shall be notified in writing within ten working days and the inventory amended accordingly. Second, permitted access is a ceiling and not an entitlement: a role appearing in the third column may consult a category only where consultation is necessary to a duty presently assigned to that role, and every consultation of clinical or identifier content shall be recorded in the access log. Third, no record shall be destroyed except upon the written authorization of the undersigned, countersigned by the Records Custodian, and no authorization shall issue while a hold subsists.

Disposal shall be conducted quarterly. Materials awaiting disposal shall be held in locked containers in the records area and shall not be commingled with ordinary refuse at any stage. Two employees shall witness each destruction event and shall execute the Certificate of Destruction, which is retained permanently. Contractors performing destruction shall furnish a certificate in the same form.

Questions of interpretation shall be referred to this Office.
