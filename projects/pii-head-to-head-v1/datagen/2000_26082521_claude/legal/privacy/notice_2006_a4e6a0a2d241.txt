PELL COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
Personal Information Handling Standard — PI-11
Adopted September 2021 by the Health Officer

1. Categories held

- Identity and contact: Full Name, Date of Birth, Residential Address, Telephone Number, Email Address, Head of Household Name.
- Regulated identifiers: Social Security Number, Medical Record Number, Insurance Policy Number, Programme Case Number.
- Health content: Diagnosis, Test Results, Immunization Record, Treatment Plan, Referral Notes.
- Eligibility and financial: Household Income Band, Sliding-Fee Tier, Benefit Programme Enrolment Status.
- Workforce: Payroll Number, Licence Number, Training Record, Leave Record.

2. Who may access

- Access is granted by category, by role, and only for assigned work.
- The Privacy Officer approves every role-to-category mapping before the Information Technology Administrator provisions it.
- Mappings are reviewed each January and on any change of duty.
- Contracted staff receive the same mapping as the equivalent Department role and no more.
- Viewing one's own record, or the record of a relative or acquaintance, is prohibited without written authorisation.

3. Retention schedule

Category                    Retention trigger           Period
Clinical file               Last encounter              10 years
Minor's clinical file       Age of majority             10 years
Immunization registry entry Entry created               Permanent
Billing and claims          Fiscal year close           7 years
Eligibility determination   Determination date          5 years
Environmental complaint     Case closure                6 years
Workforce file              Separation                  7 years
Access and audit logs       Log date                    6 years

4. Disposal

- Destruction is performed only on records past the period above and free of any legal hold.
- The Records Custodian issues the destruction authorisation; no staff member may destroy records on personal judgement.
- Paper: cross-cut shred, minimum particle standard as specified in the vendor contract.
- Electronic: secure delete, replica purge, then confirmation by the Information Technology Administrator.
- Media: degauss and physically destroy.
- Complete a Destruction Register entry showing Category, Date Range, Volume, Method, Authorising Role, Witnessing Role.

5. Reporting

- The Records Custodian reports destruction volumes by category to the Health Officer each quarter.
- Any destruction performed outside this standard is reported as a privacy incident within one working day.
