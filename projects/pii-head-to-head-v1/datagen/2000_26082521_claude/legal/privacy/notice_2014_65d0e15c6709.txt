1. PURPOSE

This procedure states, for each category of personal information processed by the group, its security classification, the lawful basis relied upon, the retention period applied and the roles permitted to access it. It is binding on all depots and on all functions holding personal information on behalf of the group.

4. DISPOSAL

MEDIUM            METHOD                          AUTHORISED BY         EVIDENCE RECORDED
----------------  ------------------------------  --------------------  -----------------
Paper             Cross-cut shredding to DIN P-4  Records Custodian     Destruction certificate
Optical media     Physical destruction            IT Operations Lead    Asset disposal log entry
Portable media    Physical destruction            IT Operations Lead    Asset disposal log entry
Server storage    Cryptographic erasure           IT Operations Lead    Erasure job identifier
Telematics feed   Scheduled overwrite             Security Supervisor   Overwrite cycle log

Disposal is initiated only on a written instruction from the Records Custodian and must be completed within 20 working days of that instruction.

5. DESTRUCTION LOG

The following fields are recorded for every disposal event: category reference; classification; medium; volume disposed; retention trigger date; date of disposal; method applied; role of operative; role of witness; certificate or job reference; date of entry into the log. The log is held for six years from the date of the last entry and is examined at each internal audit of information governance.
