RIDGEWAY DRUG MART
OPERATIONS BULLETIN OB-2004-19
SUBJECT: PERSONAL INFORMATION CATEGORIES HELD AT STORE LEVEL

To: All Store Managers and Pharmacists-in-Charge
From: Records Custodian
Date: 08 November 2004
Action required: Post at the pharmacy workstation and acknowledge by district roll-up

The Company holds six categories of personal information at store level. The table below
states who may access each category, how long it is kept, and how it is destroyed. No
other category may be created at a store, and no category may be copied to a personal
file, a home computer, or an unapproved spreadsheet.

================================================================================================
INFORMATION CATEGORY            ACCESS PERMITTED TO           RETENTION AND DISPOSAL
================================================================================================
Customer Identification         Front-Store Supervisor        3 years, then cross-cut shred
Prescription and Clinical       Pharmacist-in-Charge          10 years, then cross-cut shred
Benefit and Eligibility         Claims Desk (read only)       7 years, then cross-cut shred
Payment Authorisation           Store Accounting              18 months, then secure purge
Associate Personnel             HR Records Administrator      7 years, then cross-cut shred
Marketing Preference            Marketing Analyst             3 years, then logical delete
================================================================================================

The field labels making up each category are listed in the store copy of the Data
Dictionary held in the pharmacy binder. Labels may not be added at store level.

Retention is counted from the last activity date on the record, not the date the record
was opened. Where a record spans two categories, the longer retention governs.

Destruction is by locked collection container only. Containers are emptied by the
contracted vendor on the scheduled service visit, and the vendor certificate is filed in
the pharmacy binder behind the destruction tab. Records awaiting destruction are held in
the locked back-office cabinet and are never staged on the counter, in the receiving area,
or in the general waste.

Questions on category assignment, and all requests for access outside the roles shown
above, are directed to the Records Custodian through the district office.
