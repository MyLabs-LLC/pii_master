MARROWFIELD COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
ADMINISTRATIVE STANDARD AS-11 (2021)
PERSONAL INFORMATION INVENTORY, ACCESS TIERS AND DISPOSITION

Adopted by the Board of Health on the 3rd day of June, 2021.

SECTION 1. AUTHORITY AND PURPOSE.
1.1  This Standard is adopted pursuant to the general records authority conferred upon the Department and shall be construed consistently with, and as subordinate to, any statute, regulation, or grant condition imposing a longer period of retention or a narrower condition of access.
1.2  The purpose of this Standard is to fix, for each category of personal information maintained by the Department in any medium whatsoever, the roles competent to access it, the period for which it shall be retained, and the manner in which it shall be destroyed upon expiry of that period.

SECTION 2. DEFINITIONS.
2.1  "Category" means a class of personal information described by reference to its nature and function, and not by reference to any particular record or the individual to whom it pertains.
2.2  "Access Tier" means the graduated level of authorization prescribed in Section 4.
2.3  "Disposition" means the destruction of a record or its transfer to the County Archive, as the schedule may require.

SECTION 4. ACCESS TIERS.
4.1  Tier 1 is available to the Records Custodian, the Registrar, and clerical staff assigned to intake.
4.2  Tier 2 is available to program supervisors, the Fiscal Officer, and the Epidemiology Analyst, in each case upon written designation.
4.3  Tier 3 is available to licensed clinical personnel engaged in the care of the individual concerned and to the Health Officer.
4.4  Tier 4 is available to the Records Custodian and to the Privacy Officer, and to no other role.
4.5  Tier 5 is available to the Human Resources Officer and the Department Director.
4.6  No role shall access a category beyond the minimum necessary to discharge the duty for which the access is sought, and every access to a Tier 3 or Tier 4 category shall be logged.

SECTION 5. RETENTION.
5.1  Periods stated in Section 3 are minima and shall be computed from the trigger stated in the same row.
5.2  Where a record contains information falling within more than one category, the longest applicable period governs the whole.

SECTION 6. DISPOSAL PROCEDURE.
6.1  The Records Custodian shall, not less than annually, prepare a disposition list identifying each series whose retention has expired.
6.2  The Privacy Officer shall certify that no hold subsists, whereupon the Department Director shall authorize destruction in writing.
6.3  Paper shall be destroyed by cross-cut shredding or, where so scheduled, by incineration, in each case witnessed by two employees. Electronic media shall be sanitized by cryptographic erasure or overwriting, and thereafter physically destroyed if the medium is to be retired.
6.4  A Certificate of Destruction shall be executed for each disposition event, reciting the series, the inclusive period covered, the method, and the witnesses. Certificates are retained permanently.

SECTION 7. HOLDS AND EXCEPTIONS.
7.1  Upon notice of litigation, audit, investigation, or a pending request for access, disposition of the affected series shall be suspended until the notice is lifted in writing by the Privacy Officer.

SECTION 8. REVIEW.
8.1  This Standard shall be reviewed not less than once in every two years and upon any material change in law.
