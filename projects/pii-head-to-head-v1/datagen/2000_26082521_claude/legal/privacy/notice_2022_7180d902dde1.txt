STUDENT AND EMPLOYEE INFORMATION HOLDINGS STANDARD

Issuing Authority: Office of the Superintendent
Standard Reference: ADM-4412
Effective: 12 January 2004
Review Cycle: Triennial
Custodian of Record: District Records Custodian

SECTION A — PURPOSE AND SCOPE

A.1 This Standard establishes uniform requirements for the identification, custody, access authorisation, retention, and destruction of records containing personal information held by the District.

A.2 This Standard applies to all schools, central office departments, transportation services, food services, and contracted service providers acting on the District's behalf.

A.3 This Standard applies to records in all media, including paper files, microfiche, magnetic tape, optical media, and records held within the Student Information System.

SECTION B — CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD

B.1 Category I — Student Identification Data. Comprises given name and surname fields, district-assigned learner identifier, date of birth field, gender field, and enrolment status indicator.

B.2 Category II — Household and Contact Data. Comprises residential address fields, mailing address fields, home and alternate telephone number fields, parent or guardian name fields, custodial arrangement indicator, and emergency contact fields.

B.3 Category III — Academic Record Data. Comprises course enrolment history, term and final grade fields, standardised assessment score fields, attendance and tardy counts, promotion and retention decisions, and graduation credit tallies.

B.4 Category IV — Special Education and Related Services Data. Comprises eligibility determination fields, individualised education programme documents, evaluation reports, service minute logs, and related-services provider notes.

B.5 Category V — Health and Immunisation Data. Comprises immunisation compliance fields, medication administration logs, health screening result fields, allergy and condition indicators, and nurse visit entries.

B.6 Category VI — Discipline and Conduct Data. Comprises incident classification codes, investigation summaries, suspension and expulsion determinations, and appeal disposition fields.

B.7 Category VII — Free and Reduced Price Meal Eligibility Data. Comprises household size field, household income field, eligibility determination code, and certification method indicator.

B.8 Category VIII — Employee Personnel Data. Comprises legal name fields, taxpayer identification number field, date of birth field, home address and telephone fields, certification and licensure fields, evaluation ratings, leave balances, and disciplinary action records.

B.9 Category IX — Employee Compensation and Benefit Data. Comprises salary schedule placement, gross and net pay fields, withholding election fields, direct deposit routing and account fields, and dependent enrolment fields.

B.10 Category X — Transportation Data. Comprises route assignment fields, stop assignment fields, and pupil ridership records.

SECTION C — AUTHORISED ACCESS

C.1 Access shall be granted on the principle of least privilege and only where a legitimate educational or administrative interest exists.

C.2 The following access matrix governs routine access:

  Category I ......... Building Principal; Registrar; Attendance Clerk; Records Custodian
  Category II ........ Building Principal; Registrar; School Secretary; Transportation Coordinator
  Category III ....... Building Principal; Assigned Classroom Teacher; Guidance Counsellor; Registrar
  Category IV ........ Director of Special Education; Case Manager; Building Principal; Related Services Provider
  Category V ......... School Nurse; Director of Health Services; Building Principal (indicator fields only)
  Category VI ........ Building Principal; Assistant Superintendent for Student Services; Hearing Officer
  Category VII ....... Food Services Director; Eligibility Clerk; Business Manager
  Category VIII ...... Director of Human Resources; Personnel Analyst; Supervising Administrator
  Category IX ........ Payroll Supervisor; Benefits Coordinator; Business Manager
  Category X ......... Transportation Coordinator; Route Planner; Building Principal

C.3 Parents, guardians, and eligible students may inspect records pertaining to themselves upon written request to the Building Principal. Inspection shall be arranged within forty-five (45) days of receipt.

C.4 Disclosure to any party not listed in C.2 requires written consent of the parent, guardian, or eligible student, except where disclosure is permitted or required by statute.

C.5 Each instance of disclosure to an external party shall be entered in the Disclosure Log maintained by the Records Custodian, recording the requesting party, the category disclosed, the stated purpose, and the authorising signature.

SECTION D — RETENTION PERIODS

D.1 Retention is calculated from the trigger event stated for each category.

  Category I ......... 100 years from date of last enrolment (permanent record fields only); all other fields, 5 years
  Category II ........ 5 years from date of last enrolment
  Category III ....... Transcript fields, 100 years; supporting fields, 5 years from date of last enrolment
  Category IV ........ 7 years from termination of services or from age of majority, whichever is later
  Category V ......... 7 years from date of last enrolment
  Category VI ........ 5 years from date of final disposition
  Category VII ....... 3 years from close of the applicable programme year
  Category VIII ...... 7 years from date of separation
  Category IX ........ 7 years from close of the applicable fiscal year
  Category X ......... 3 years from close of the applicable school year

D.2 Where a record is subject to litigation hold, audit, or open records request, the applicable retention period is suspended until written release is issued by the Office of the Superintendent.

D.3 No record shall be destroyed prior to the expiry of its stated retention period.

SECTION E — DISPOSAL PROCEDURE

E.1 The Records Custodian shall generate a Disposal Eligibility Report each quarter listing records that have reached the end of their retention period.

E.2 The Report shall be circulated to the responsible department head, who shall confirm in writing that no hold applies.

E.3 Paper records shall be destroyed by cross-cut shredding or witnessed pulping. Microfiche shall be incinerated. Magnetic and optical media shall be degaussed and physically destroyed.

E.4 Disposal shall be witnessed by two employees, one of whom shall be the Records Custodian or a designee.

E.5 A Certificate of Destruction shall be completed for each disposal event and shall record: the category disposed, the volume disposed, the destruction method, the date of destruction, and the signatures of both witnesses.

E.6 Certificates of Destruction shall be retained permanently.
