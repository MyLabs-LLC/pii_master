PALLETLINE CONTRACT LOGISTICS PLC
GROUP RECORDS STANDARD GRS/9
PERSONAL INFORMATION: CATEGORIES, CUSTODY AND DESTRUCTION

Approved by the Audit Committee. Applicable Group-wide from the commencement of the 1998/99 financial year.

CLAUSE 1 — PURPOSE
1.1 To define the categories of personal information held by Group companies, to fix the period of retention for each, and to specify the manner of destruction.
1.2 To provide a single register against which the Group's notification under the Data Protection Acts is maintained.

CLAUSE 2 — REGISTER OF CATEGORIES
2.1 The Records Custodian maintains the Register of Categories. No category may be established, amended or withdrawn except by entry in that Register.
2.2 Each entry states the category reference, the constituent field labels, the custodian function, the permitted user functions, the retention period, and the destruction method.
2.3 The Register is reproduced in summary at Schedule 1.

CLAUSE 3 — CUSTODY
3.1 Custody of a category rests with the function named in the Register and may not be delegated below the grade of Department Manager.
3.2 Where a category is held in more than one location, one location shall be designated the master and all others working copies. Working copies carry the retention period of the master and are destroyed with it.
3.3 Extraction of fields from a category to form a working list is permitted only where the list is destroyed at the conclusion of the immediate purpose and in any event within twenty-eight days.

CLAUSE 4 — DESTRUCTION
4.1 Destruction is by cross-cut shredding, pulping, or incineration for paper; by degaussing or physical destruction for magnetic media; and by physical destruction for microfiche.
4.2 Destruction is performed by the Records Custodian or by an approved contractor operating under a written confidentiality undertaking.
4.3 A certificate of destruction is obtained from any contractor and filed against the Destruction Register entry.
4.4 Where litigation, an insurance claim, or an enquiry by a regulatory body is pending or reasonably anticipated, destruction is suspended until the Company Secretary confirms in writing that the suspension is lifted.
