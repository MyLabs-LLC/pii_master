NORTHVALE PHARMACY HOLDINGS
CORPORATE RECORDS STANDARD CRS-2004/07
PERSONAL INFORMATION: CATEGORIES, CUSTODY, RETENTION AND DESTRUCTION

Issue date: 22 March 2004
Issuing authority: Records Custodian, on behalf of the Privacy Steering Committee
Distribution: District Managers, Pharmacists-in-Charge, Store Managers, Corporate Services

SECTION 1 - APPLICATION

This standard applies to every record containing personal information that Northvale
Pharmacy Holdings creates, receives, or maintains, whether held in the dispensing system,
the point-of-sale system, the personnel system, or in paper form at store or corporate
level. Business associates handling any category below are bound to the same retention
and destruction terms by written agreement.

SECTION 2 - DEFINITIONS

"Category" means a defined grouping of personal information field labels. "Custody" means
accountability for accuracy, security, retention, and destruction. "Access" means the
ability to view or extract, and is separate from custody. "Disposal" means destruction
rendering reconstruction infeasible.

SECTION 5 - CONTROL OF ACCESS

Access is assigned by role profile only. Individual grants outside a role profile require
written authorisation from the Records Custodian, are limited to a named category, and
lapse automatically after thirty days unless renewed. Extract and report functions against
categories C-01 through C-06 are logged and the logs reviewed monthly by the District
Manager. Category C-07 may not be joined to categories C-02 or C-03 for any purpose.

SECTION 6 - SUSPENSION OF DISPOSAL

Where a category is subject to a board inquiry, an audit, or anticipated litigation, the
Records Custodian issues a hold notice naming the category reference. Disposal of the held
category ceases at every location until the notice is lifted in writing. Hold notices are
registered and reviewed at each quarterly Committee meeting.

SECTION 7 - DESTRUCTION PROCEDURE

Paper is placed in locked collection containers and destroyed by the contracted vendor,
which issues a certificate of destruction identifying the category reference, the volume,
and the date. Electronic records are removed by the approved purge utility and the
resulting log is filed with the certificate. Certificates and logs are retained for six
years from the date of destruction. Destruction by ordinary waste stream, recycling, or
store dumpster is prohibited for every category in this standard.
