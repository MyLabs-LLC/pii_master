TRANSIT DISTRICT AUTHORITY
PRIVACY AND RECORDS STANDARD PR-04 (ISSUE 2, SEPTEMBER 2004)
PERSONAL INFORMATION INVENTORY, ACCESS CONTROL, RETENTION AND DESTRUCTION

1. PURPOSE AND APPLICATION

   1.1 This Standard establishes the mandatory controls governing personal information under the custody or control of the Authority.
   1.2 Divisional procedures may impose stricter controls; they may not relax the controls set out here.
   1.3 The Privacy Coordinator maintains the master Inventory. Divisional Records Officers maintain divisional extracts.

2. INVENTORY OF CATEGORIES

   2.1 Category 100 — Personnel and Payroll
       o Field labels held: Employee Number; Surname; Given Names; Date of Birth; Social Security Number; Residential Address; Home Telephone Number; Union Local; Grade and Step; Gross Periodic Earnings; Deduction Codes; Bank Routing Number; Deposit Account Number.
       o Custodial unit: Human Resources Division.
       o Authorised access: Human Resources Director; Compensation Analyst; Payroll Supervisor; Internal Audit (requisition required); General Counsel (litigation hold only).

   2.2 Category 200 — Occupational Health and Regulated Testing
       o Field labels held: Employee Number; Examination Date; Fitness Determination Code; Restriction Code; Specimen Identifier; Testing Panel Code; Result Classification; Medical Review Officer Identifier.
       o Custodial unit: Occupational Health Services, in a locked file separate from Category 100.
       o Authorised access: Occupational Health Nurse; Medical Review Officer; Substance Abuse Programme Manager. No supervisory access to clinical content; supervisors receive the Fitness Determination Code only.

   2.3 Category 300 — Customer Fare Accounts
       o Field labels held: Card Serial Number; Account Number; Registrant Name; Billing Address; Electronic Mail Address; Contact Telephone Number; Payment Instrument Reference; Transaction Timestamp; Boarding Location Code.
       o Custodial unit: Revenue Systems Division.
       o Authorised access: Fare Systems Administrator; Customer Service Representatives (account view only, no payment instrument display); Revenue Accountant.

   2.4 Category 400 — Accessibility Eligibility
       o Field labels held: Applicant Name; Applicant Reference; Date of Birth; Residential Address; Functional Assessment Code; Mobility Aid Code; Determination Code; Determination Expiry; Certifying Practitioner Identifier.
       o Custodial unit: Accessible Services Office.
       o Authorised access: Eligibility Coordinator; Assessment Contractor (assigned files only); Appeals Officer.

   2.5 Category 500 — Security, Incident and Claim
       o Field labels held: Incident Reference; Reporting Employee Number; Involved Party Name; Contact Telephone Number; Injury Classification; Camera Identifier; Vehicle Number; Recording Interval; Claim Reference; Settlement Classification.
       o Custodial unit: Security Operations Centre; Risk Management for claim files.
       o Authorised access: Security Operations Supervisor; Claims Adjuster; General Counsel; law enforcement on written demand recorded in the Disclosure Log.

3. RETENTION SCHEDULE

   o Category 100 — six years after separation; pension entitlement extracts retained permanently.
   o Category 200 — five years from date of test or examination; exposure records thirty years.
   o Category 300 — three years after account closure; transaction detail eighteen months.
   o Category 400 — two years after determination expiry, or after final appeal, whichever is later.
   o Category 500 — routine recordings thirty days; incident-flagged recordings and claim files five years after closure.

4. DESTRUCTION

   4.1 Destruction proceeds only on the written authorisation of the Records Custodian and only where no litigation hold is recorded.
   4.2 Paper is cross-cut shredded on premises or consigned to a certified destruction contractor under a chain-of-custody manifest.
   4.3 Magnetic media are degaussed and physically deformed; optical media are shredded.
   4.4 Database records are purged by the scheduled expiry job; backup copies age out under the standard rotation.
   4.5 The Disposal Register records the columns: Record Series Code; Category; Volume; Destruction Method; Destruction Date; Authorising Officer Title; Witness Title; Certificate Number.
