CEDAR MILL CREDIT UNION
Records Handling Checklist — RH/2011/07
For use by the Records Custodian and all department heads
Approved: Operations Committee, 12 September 2011

Complete one checklist per department per quarter. Mark each line. Unmarked lines constitute an exception and are reported to the Compliance Officer.

INVENTORY VERIFICATION

[ ] Identity category confirmed in inventory (labels: Legal Name, Date of Birth, Social Security Number, Identification Document Type, Identification Document Number, Expiry Date)
[ ] Contact category confirmed (labels: Street Address, City, State, Postal Code, Home Telephone, Mobile Telephone, Electronic Mail Address)
[ ] Account category confirmed (labels: Member Number, Suffix, Account Type, Card Number, Card Expiry, Personal Identification Number, Online Credential)
[ ] Financial category confirmed (labels: Balance, Transaction Date, Transaction Amount, Counterparty, Routing Reference)
[ ] Credit category confirmed (labels: Income, Employer, Consumer Report, Credit Score, Collateral Description, Lien Position)
[ ] Employment category confirmed (labels: Employee Number, Title, Rate, Withholding, Review Rating)
[ ] No category exists outside the inventory. Shadow files, personal spreadsheets and desk copies eliminated.

ACCESS VERIFICATION

[ ] Role list for the department is current
[ ] Every entitlement traced to an approved role; no individual grants
[ ] Departed and transferred staff de-provisioned within one business day
[ ] Card and credential fields displayed masked to all roles except Card Services Supervisor
[ ] Access log reviewed for out-of-pattern queries by the Information Security Officer

RETENTION VERIFICATION

[ ] Membership records: life of membership plus seven years
[ ] Loan files: five years from satisfaction
[ ] Declined applications: twenty-five months
[ ] Bank Secrecy Act filings: five years
[ ] Personnel files: seven years post-separation
[ ] Permanent records segregated in the fire-rated vault
[ ] Nothing retained beyond schedule without a documented hold

DISPOSAL VERIFICATION

[ ] Eligible-for-destruction listing produced and countersigned
[ ] Litigation and examination holds cleared by the Compliance Officer
[ ] Paper cross-cut shredded; witness present
[ ] Media degaussed and physically destroyed
[ ] Destruction Log completed (fields: Category, Medium, Date Range, Volume, Method, Date, Custodian Role, Witness Role)
[ ] Vendor certificate filed
