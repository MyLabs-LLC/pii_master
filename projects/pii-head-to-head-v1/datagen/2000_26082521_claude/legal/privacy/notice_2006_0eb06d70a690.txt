MEMBER INFORMATION AND RECORDS HANDLING STANDARD
Policy Reference RM-2004-07
Issued under authority of the Board Audit and Compliance Committee
Effective 1 September 2004; supersedes RM-1999-03 in its entirety

1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 This Standard governs the classification, custody, permitted use, retention and destruction of all records containing personal information held by the Credit Union in any medium, whether paper, microfiche, magnetic tape, optical disc or data held within the core processing system. It applies to every employee, officer, director, volunteer, temporary worker and contracted service provider who obtains access to such records in the course of their duties.

1.2 This Standard is issued in furtherance of the Credit Union's obligations under Part 748 of the NCUA Rules and Regulations, the privacy provisions of Regulation P, and the safeguards obligations arising under Title V of the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act. Where any provision of this Standard conflicts with an obligation imposed by law, regulation or a lawful order of a supervisory authority, the external obligation shall prevail and the Compliance Officer shall be notified in writing without delay.

2. Categories of Personal Information

2.1 The Credit Union recognises seven categories of personal information. The first category, Member Identification Data, comprises those elements collected to establish and verify identity at the point of membership application and thereafter, and is constituted by the fields Surname, Given Name, Middle Initial, Former Name, Date of Birth, Taxpayer Identification Number, Residential Address, Mailing Address, Home Telephone Number, Business Telephone Number and Electronic Mail Address.

2.2 The second category, Account and Share Data, comprises the fields Share Account Number, Share Draft Account Number, Certificate Number, Account Open Date, Account Status Code, Signatory Designation and Beneficiary Designation. The third category, Credit and Underwriting Data, comprises the fields Loan Account Number, Loan Product Code, Credit Bureau Score, Debt-to-Income Ratio, Collateral Description, Employer Name, Length of Employment and Gross Monthly Income, together with any consumer report obtained in connection with an application.

2.3 The fourth category, Transactional Data, records the movement of funds and comprises the fields Transaction Date, Transaction Code, Amount, Posting Branch, Teller Identifier and Counterparty Description. The fifth category, Electronic Access Data, comprises the fields Personal Identification Number, Audio Response Passcode, Home Banking User Identifier and Security Question Response, each of which shall at all times be stored in irreversibly enciphered form.

2.4 The sixth category, Correspondence and Servicing Data, comprises member complaints, service requests, recorded telephone contacts and collection notes. The seventh category, Personnel Data, comprises records relating to employees and applicants for employment and is held separately from all member records under the exclusive custody of the Human Resources Manager.

3. Authorised Access

3.1 Access to any category enumerated in clause 2 is granted upon the principle of least privilege and only for the discharge of an assigned duty. Member Services Representatives may access categories one, two, four and six. Lending Officers may additionally access category three. The Information Security Coordinator may access category five for administrative purposes only and may not view enciphered values. The Records Custodian holds custodial access to all categories for the purpose of storage, retrieval and destruction, and may not use such access for member servicing.

3.2 Every grant of access shall be recorded in the Access Authorisation Register maintained by the Records Custodian, reviewed semi-annually, and revoked on the effective date of any transfer or separation.

4. Retention

4.1 Categories one through four shall be retained for the period specified in Schedule A, calculated in each case from the date of account closure and not from the date of collection. Category five shall be purged upon closure. Categories six and seven shall be retained in accordance with the periods separately prescribed by the Compliance Officer.

5. Disposal

5.1 No record within scope of this Standard may be discarded, recycled or transferred to a third party for destruction except upon a Destruction Authorisation signed by the Records Custodian and countersigned by the Compliance Officer. Paper and microfiche shall be cross-cut shredded on Credit Union premises. Magnetic media shall be degaussed and thereafter physically rendered unreadable. A Certificate of Destruction shall be filed and retained for six years.
