MEMORANDUM — RECORDS ADMINISTRATION

To: All Branch Managers, Escrow Officers, Trust Accountants
From: The Records Custodian
Subject: Standard 4.2 — Personal Information: Categories, Access, Retention, Disposal
Classification: Internal — Operations

1. PURPOSE

1.1 Standard 4.2 establishes a single company-wide vocabulary for the personal information contained in custodial files, and fixes the access, retention, and disposal rules attaching to each category. It replaces the varying local practices previously in use at branch level.

2. CATEGORY SCHEDULE

2.1 Category 1 — Identity. Comprises the fields Name, Prior Name, Date of Birth, Social Security Number, and Driver Licence Number.

2.2 Category 2 — Address and Contact. Comprises the fields Mailing Address, Property Address, Telephone, and Facsimile.

2.3 Category 3 — Financial. Comprises the fields Lender Name, Loan Number, Bank Account Number, Wire Instructions, and Payoff Statement Amount.

2.4 Category 4 — Order and Title. Comprises the fields Order Number, Escrow Number, Policy Number, Legal Description, and Parcel Number.

2.5 Category 5 — Legal Status. Comprises the fields Marital Status, Vesting, Judgment Reference, Lien Reference, and Estate or Probate Reference.

3. ACCESS

3.1 Categories 1, 2, and 4 are open to the assigned escrow team.
3.2 Category 3 is closed except to the Escrow Officer and the Trust Accountant.
3.3 Category 5 is closed except to the Title Examiner and Underwriting Counsel.
3.4 Every disclosure outside the company is entered in the Disclosure Log.

4. RETENTION

4.1 Categories 1, 2, and 4 — seven years from disbursement.
4.2 Category 3 — seven years from trust reconciliation.
4.3 Category 5 — policy liability period plus three years.
4.4 Litigation and audit holds override all periods.

5. DISPOSAL

5.1 Cross-cut shredding of paper, witnessed by two officers.
5.2 Overwriting followed by physical destruction of electronic media.
5.3 Certificate of Destruction executed and retained permanently.

6. EFFECT

6.1 Branch Managers will circulate this memorandum to all staff holding terminal access and will confirm receipt to the Records Custodian within thirty days.
