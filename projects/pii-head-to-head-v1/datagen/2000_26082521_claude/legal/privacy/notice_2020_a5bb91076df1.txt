COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
ADMINISTRATIVE STANDARD AS-2021-09
CLASSIFICATION AND HANDLING OF PERSONAL INFORMATION IN PROGRAM DATA SYSTEMS
Adopted 19 May 2021

A.  DEFINITIONS AND AUTHORITY
    A.1  "Personal information" means any data element in Section B, alone or in
         combination, that identifies or can reasonably be used to identify an
         individual.
    A.2  Authority: HIPAA Privacy and Security Rules; state public health records
         statute; the County Records Retention Schedule as amended.

B.  DATA ELEMENT CATEGORIES BY PROGRAM SYSTEM
    B.1  Immunization Registry
         B.1.a  Identity elements: Client Last Name; Client First Name; Client
                Identifier; Guardian Name; Guardian Relationship Code.
         B.1.b  Clinical elements: Vaccine Type Code; Administration Date;
                Lot Number Reference; Administering Site Code; Exemption Type.
    B.2  Maternal and Child Health Case Management System
         B.2.a  Identity elements: Participant Identifier; Household Identifier.
         B.2.b  Demographic elements: Date of Birth; Primary Language; Housing
                Status; Insurance Status.
         B.2.c  Assessment elements: Risk Screening Score; Referral Disposition;
                Care Plan Goal Text.
    B.3  Behavioral Health Contract Monitoring File
         B.3.a  Elements: Client Identifier; Service Authorization Number;
                Encounter Type Code; Units Authorized; Units Rendered.
    B.4  Vital Records Working File
         B.4.a  Elements: Registrant Name; Certificate Number; Informant Name;
                Informant Relationship; Attending Certifier Credential Number.

C.  ACCESS AUTHORIZATION
    C.1  Access is provisioned by role and reviewed semi-annually by the Privacy
         Officer against the Role-to-Category Access Matrix.
    C.2  B.1 — Immunization Program Nurses; the Registry Administrator.
    C.3  B.2 — Assigned Public Health Nurse; Case Management Supervisor.
    C.4  B.3 — Contract Monitor; Fiscal Officer. Clinical narrative fields are
         suppressed for the Fiscal Officer role.
    C.5  B.4 — Deputy Registrar; Vital Records Registrar.
    C.6  No role receives standing access to more than one program system without
         written justification retained in the Access Authorization File.

D.  RETENTION
    D.1  B.1 — permanent, consistent with statewide registry requirements.
    D.2  B.2 — six years from case closure; minors, to age of majority plus six years.
    D.3  B.3 — seven years from contract term end.
    D.4  B.4 — working file destroyed at ninety days; the register of record is permanent.

E.  DISPOSAL PROCEDURE
    E.1  The Records Custodian generates the disposition queue and obtains written
         release from the Program Manager of record and from County Counsel.
    E.2  Destruction methods: cross-cut shredding for paper; secure incineration
         for microform; cryptographic erase or degaussing for electronic media.
    E.3  Third-party destruction is performed only under an executed business
         associate agreement by a certified vendor, with a certificate returned
         within ten business days.
    E.4  Log columns: System of Origin; Category References; Record Count;
         Media Type; Method; Release Approval Role; Execution Date;
         Certificate Reference.
