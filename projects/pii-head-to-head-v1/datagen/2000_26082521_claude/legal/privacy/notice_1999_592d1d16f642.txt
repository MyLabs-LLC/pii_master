COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
STANDARD 6.0 — CUSTODY, ACCESS, AND LIFECYCLE OF CONFIDENTIAL PERSONAL INFORMATION
Adopted by the Board of Health, 2016

6.1 Scope of Holdings
   (a) The Department is custodian of the following categories, each of which is deemed confidential in its entirety irrespective of the sensitivity of any constituent field:
       • Category I — Client Identity: fields Name, Alias, Date of Birth, Sex, Race, Ethnicity, Primary Language.
       • Category II — Client Identifiers: fields Social Security Number, Client Index Number, Encounter Number, Insurance Member Number.
       • Category III — Client Contact and Household: fields Street Address, Mailing Address, Telephone Number, Electronic Mail Address, Household Size, Head of Household Name.
       • Category IV — Health and Treatment: fields Presenting Complaint, Diagnosis, Test Result, Treatment Plan, Referral Destination, Provider of Record.
       • Category V — Financial: fields Income Verification Source, Fee Determination, Charge Amount, Payment Method, Account Number.
       • Category VI — Investigative: fields Case Number, Contact Trace Identifier, Exposure Location, Investigating Officer of Record.
       • Category VII — Employment: fields Employee Number, Compensation Grade, Credential Number, Health Screening Result.

6.2 Access Determinations
   (a) Access shall be determined by role and by category, and never by individual request alone.
   (b) The following role holders are the only authorized custodians of the categories indicated:
       • the Health Officer — all categories, for purposes of statutory duty.
       • the Privacy Officer — all categories, for purposes of audit and investigation.
       • the Records Custodian — all categories, for purposes of retention and disposal only.
       • the Clinical Program Supervisor — Categories I, III, and IV within the assigned program.
       • the Public Health Nurse — Categories I, III, IV, and VI within the assigned caseload.
       • the Communicable Disease Investigator — Categories I, III, and VI.
       • the Eligibility Technician — Categories I, III, and V.
       • the Human Resources Manager — Category VII.
   (c) All access, whether granted or denied, shall be logged, and the log shall record the role title, the category accessed, the operation performed, and the timestamp.

6.3 Retention
   (a) Retention shall be governed by the schedule then in force, provided that no category shall be retained longer than is necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.
   (b) Where a single record contains fields drawn from more than one category, the longest applicable retention period shall govern the record in its entirety.

6.4 Disposal
   (a) Eligible records shall be identified quarterly by the Records Custodian and presented to the Privacy Officer for concurrence.
   (b) Upon concurrence, destruction shall proceed by shredding, pulping, incineration, or media sanitization, as appropriate to the medium.
   (c) Destruction shall be witnessed, certified, and entered in the Destruction Register.
   (d) Under no circumstances shall records within any category be placed in ordinary refuse, recycling, or surplus property channels.
