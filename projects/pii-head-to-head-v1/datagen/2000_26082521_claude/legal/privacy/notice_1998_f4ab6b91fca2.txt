MIDLAND VALLEY MUTUAL INSURANCE COMPANY
CORPORATE RECORDS AND PRIVACY STANDARD
Standard No. RIM-114 — Categories of Personal Information: Custody, Access, Retention, Disposal
Issued: 12 January 2004   Supersedes: RIM-109 (Rev. 3)
Owner: Office of the Privacy Officer   Review Cycle: Annual
Scope: All departments; all media (paper, microfiche, magnetic tape, optical platter, electronic).

SECTION 1 — CATEGORIES HELD
Each department Records Custodian shall confirm the inventory below at each annual review.

* Category A — Applicant and Policyholder Identification. Field labels held: Full Legal Name; Prior Name; Date of Birth; Gender; Social Security Number; Driver's License Number; Marital Status.
* Category B — Contact and Locator Data. Field labels held: Mailing Address; Garaging Address; Home Telephone; Work Telephone; Facsimile Number; Electronic Mail Address.
* Category C — Financial and Premium Data. Field labels held: Bank Name; Routing Number; Demand Deposit Account Number; Credit Card Number; Card Expiration; Billing Mode; Premium Balance; Collection Status.
* Category D — Underwriting Risk Data. Field labels held: Loss History Indicator; Motor Vehicle Record Code; Credit-Based Insurance Score; Prior Carrier; Prior Policy Number; Inspection Report Reference.
* Category E — Claim File Data. Field labels held: Claim Number; Date of Loss; Loss Description; Reserve Amount; Payee Name; Draft Number; Attorney of Record; Investigative Report Reference.
* Category F — Health and Medical Data. Field labels held: Treating Provider Name; Diagnosis Code; Procedure Code; Attending Physician Statement Reference; Independent Medical Examination Reference; Impairment Rating.
* Category G — Producer and Agency Data. Field labels held: Producer Name; Agency Name; National Producer Number; Appointment Date; Commission Account Number.
* Category H — Associate Personnel Data. Field labels held: Associate Name; Payroll Identifier; Position Title; Compensation Grade; Benefit Election Codes; Performance Rating; Disciplinary Action Code.
* Category I — Marketing and Preference Data. Field labels held: Solicitation History; Opt-Out Election; Affiliate Sharing Election; Telephone Preference Flag.

SECTION 2 — AUTHORIZED ACCESS
Access is granted by role, not by individual request. Access outside the roles listed requires written approval of the Privacy Officer.

* Categories A, B, D — Underwriting Technician; Underwriting Supervisor; Policy Services Clerk; Appointed Producer of record.
* Category C — Premium Accounting Clerk; Treasury Supervisor; Collections Analyst. Card fields are masked at the terminal for all roles except Treasury Supervisor.
* Category E — Claims Representative assigned; Claims Supervisor; Special Investigations Analyst; Subrogation Analyst; Coverage Counsel.
* Category F — Medical Case Manager; Claims Supervisor; Special Investigations Analyst. Segregated from Categories A–E in a locked sub-file. No access by Underwriting or Marketing under any circumstance.
* Category G — Agency Services Coordinator; Commission Accounting Clerk; Regional Sales Manager.
* Category H — Human Resources Generalist; Human Resources Director; the associate's direct supervisor for Position Title and Performance Rating fields only.
* Category I — Marketing Analyst; Compliance Analyst. Opt-Out Election field is read-only for all roles.

SECTION 3 — RETENTION PERIODS
Measured from the trigger event stated; no destruction while litigation or Department of Insurance hold is in force.

* Categories A, B, D — 7 years from policy expiration or cancellation.
* Category C — 7 years from close of accounting year.
* Category E — 10 years from claim closure; 21 years from date of loss where the claimant was a minor at loss.
* Category F — 10 years from claim closure, then destruction irrespective of other categories.
* Category G — 5 years from termination of appointment.
* Category H — 7 years from separation; benefit and pension fields, permanent.
* Category I — 3 years from last solicitation; Opt-Out Election, permanent.

SECTION 4 — DISPOSAL
* Paper: cross-cut shredding on premises, or transfer to the bonded destruction vendor under sealed console. Certificate of Destruction filed with the Records Custodian.
* Microfiche and film: incineration by vendor only.
* Magnetic and optical media: three-pass overwrite, then physical destruction. Reformatting alone is not disposal.
* Log entries: Category Code; Volume; Method; Date of Destruction; Custodian Signature; Witness Signature. Log retained permanently.
