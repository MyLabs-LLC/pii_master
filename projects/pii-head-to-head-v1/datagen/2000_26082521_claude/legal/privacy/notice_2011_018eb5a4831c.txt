STANDARD FOR THE CLASSIFICATION AND DISPOSAL OF PERSONAL RECORDS
Issued under the authority of the Supervisory Committee
Reference: SC-PRIV-2 · Effective 1 July 1998

1.0 GENERAL

1.1 The Credit Union holds personal information as a necessary incident of its business and holds it in trust. The obligations set out below attach to the information itself and follow it into every copy, extract, report and archive medium in which it comes to rest.

1.2 The Records Custodian maintains the master inventory. No department may create a new file, register, spreadsheet or standing report containing any field named in clause 2.0 without the written concurrence of the Records Custodian, who shall enter it in the inventory against the columns INV-SEQ, OWNER-DEPT, CATEGORY, MEDIUM, LOCATION and RETENTION-CLASS.

2.0 CATEGORIES

2.1 Category I — Identification. Fields: MEMBER-NO, SURNAME, GIVEN-NAME, DOB, TIN, ID-DOC-TYPE, ID-DOC-NO, ELIGIBILITY-BASIS.

2.2 Category II — Contact. Fields: ADDR-LINE-1, ADDR-LINE-2, CITY, STATE, ZIP, PHONE-HOME, PHONE-WORK, MAIL-PREF-CODE.

2.3 Category III — Financial Relationship. Fields: SHARE-ID, LOAN-ID, OPEN-DATE, CLOSE-DATE, BALANCE-CURR, DELINQ-DAYS, CHARGE-OFF-DATE, JOINT-OWNER-SEQ, BENEFICIARY-SEQ.

2.4 Category IV — Assessment. Fields: EMPLOYER, INCOME-GROSS-MO, BUREAU-FILE-NO, BUREAU-SCORE, DEBT-RATIO, DECISION-CODE, ADVERSE-ACTION-CODE.

2.5 Category V — Authentication. Fields: PIN-OFFSET, AUDIO-RESPONSE-CODE, VERIFICATION-PHRASE, TELLER-OVERRIDE-KEY.

3.0 ACCESS

3.1 Access is granted by role and not by seniority. A Vice President has no entitlement to a category merely by reason of office.

3.2 Categories I to III are available to the teller line, member services and the Branch Manager. Category IV is available to the Lending Department, the Collections Officer and the Internal Auditor. Category V is available to no person in displayable form; the Security Administrator holds reset authority only.

3.3 The examining authority and the external auditor are afforded access to all categories on presentation of credentials to the Chief Executive Officer, and each such access is logged.

4.0 RETENTION

4.1 Categories I, II and III: seven years from CLOSE-DATE, or from CHARGE-OFF-DATE where later.

4.2 Category IV: twenty-five months where DECISION-CODE records a denial or withdrawal; otherwise seven years from CLOSE-DATE.

4.3 Category V: purged upon closure of the last associated relationship.

5.0 DISPOSAL

5.1 Paper and microfiche are destroyed by cross-cut shredding on the premises or by a bonded contractor under certificate. Magnetic cartridges are degaussed and then cut. No medium is sold, donated or placed in ordinary refuse.

5.2 Each destruction is witnessed by two officers, one of whom shall be independent of the owning department, and entered in the Destruction Register.

5.3 A record identified in a legal hold notice, a subpoena, or a pending dispute under Category IV is withheld from destruction until the Compliance Officer releases it in writing.
