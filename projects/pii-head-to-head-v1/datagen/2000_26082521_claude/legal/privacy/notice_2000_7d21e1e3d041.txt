CEDAR RIDGE CASUALTY GROUP — CORPORATE RECORDS UNIT

STANDING INSTRUCTION SI-07
QUARTERLY DISPOSAL OF EXPIRED PERSONAL INFORMATION

Issued .................. 6 May 2021
Issued Under ............ Privacy and Records Policy, clause 9.2
Responsible Role ........ Records Custodian
Counter-signing Role .... Deputy General Counsel
Frequency ............... Quarterly, within ten business days of quarter end
Distribution ............ Unit Managers; Privacy Stewards; Vault Services

1. The Records Custodian shall extract, for each category held — Identification, Contact, Policy Administration, Financial, Underwriting, Claims, Workforce and Digital — the record series whose retention trigger date has passed the period prescribed in Schedule 2 of the Policy.

2. The extract shall be circulated to the Privacy Steward of each holding unit. Access to the extract is limited to the Records Custodian, the Privacy Stewards, and Internal Audit.

3. A Privacy Steward may withhold a series from disposal only by recording an objection code and the retention basis relied upon.

4. Sensitive elements within the Claims and Underwriting categories require the Deputy General Counsel's counter-signature before release.

5. Destruction is carried out by the bonded vendor. Paper is shredded on site; electronic media is degaussed or physically destroyed. A certificate of destruction is filed against the register entry.

6. The Records Custodian closes the cycle by recording, for each entry: Series Title; Category Code; Volume; Method; Certificate Reference; Approving Role; Completion Date.
