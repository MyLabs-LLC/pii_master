CITY TRANSIT AUTHORITY
ADMINISTRATIVE STANDARD AS-2004/09
PERSONAL INFORMATION: CUSTODY, ACCESS, RETENTION AND DISPOSAL
Approved by the Executive Director. Review due September 2007.

* SECTION 1 — CUSTODY

  * The Records Custodian holds overall custody of all categories listed below.
  * A Divisional Records Officer is designated for each operating and support division.
  * No category may be replicated to a portable medium without the written approval of the Records Custodian.

* SECTION 2 — CATEGORIES AND FIELD LABELS

  * Personnel Master
    - Employee Number, Surname, Given Names, Date of Birth, Social Security Number, Residential Address, Home Telephone Number, Hire Date, Grade, Emergency Contact Name, Emergency Contact Telephone Number.
  * Payroll and Benefit
    - Employee Number, Earnings Code, Deduction Code, Bank Routing Number, Deposit Account Number, Dependant Name, Beneficiary Name, Plan Enrolment Code.
  * Operator Qualification
    - Employee Number, Commercial Driver Licence Number, Issuing Jurisdiction, Endorsement Code, Medical Certificate Expiry, Restriction Code, Violation Code.
  * Regulated Testing
    - Employee Number, Specimen Identifier, Collection Date, Panel Code, Result Classification, Medical Review Officer Identifier.
  * Customer Account
    - Account Number, Card Serial Number, Registrant Name, Billing Address, Electronic Mail Address, Contact Telephone Number, Payment Instrument Reference, Balance, Transaction Timestamp, Boarding Location Code.
  * Accessibility Eligibility
    - Applicant Reference, Applicant Name, Date of Birth, Residential Address, Functional Assessment Code, Determination Code, Determination Expiry, Certifying Practitioner Identifier.
  * Security and Incident
    - Incident Reference, Involved Party Name, Contact Telephone Number, Injury Classification, Vehicle Number, Camera Identifier, Recording Interval, Claim Reference.

* SECTION 3 — AUTHORISED ACCESS BY ROLE TITLE

  * Human Resources Director — Personnel Master, Payroll and Benefit.
  * Payroll Supervisor — Payroll and Benefit.
  * Operations Superintendent — Operator Qualification.
  * Occupational Health Nurse and Medical Review Officer — Regulated Testing.
  * Fare Systems Administrator and Revenue Accountant — Customer Account.
  * Eligibility Coordinator and Appeals Officer — Accessibility Eligibility.
  * Security Operations Supervisor and Claims Adjuster — Security and Incident.
  * Internal Audit — any category, on written requisition, read-only, logged.
  * General Counsel — any category under litigation hold.

* SECTION 4 — RETENTION

  * Personnel Master — six years after separation.
  * Payroll and Benefit — seven years after the tax year to which the record relates.
  * Operator Qualification — three years after expiry or separation, whichever is later.
  * Regulated Testing — five years from collection.
  * Customer Account — three years after closure; transaction detail eighteen months.
  * Accessibility Eligibility — two years after determination expiry.
  * Security and Incident — thirty days routine; five years where a claim is opened.

* SECTION 5 — DISPOSAL PROCEDURE

  * Step 1 — The Divisional Records Officer produces the expiry list from the retention trigger.
  * Step 2 — The list is screened against the Litigation Hold Register; held items are removed.
  * Step 3 — The Records Custodian authorises destruction in writing.
  * Step 4 — Paper is cross-cut shredded; magnetic media degaussed and deformed; optical media shredded.
  * Step 5 — The contractor returns a certificate of destruction within ten working days.
  * Step 6 — The Disposal Register is completed: Record Series Code; Category; Volume; Method; Destruction Date; Authorising Officer Title; Witness Title; Certificate Number.
  * Step 7 — The Privacy Coordinator samples five per cent of entries each quarter for verification.
