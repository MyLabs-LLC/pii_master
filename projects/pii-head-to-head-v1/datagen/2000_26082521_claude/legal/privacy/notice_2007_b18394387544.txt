METROPOLITAN TRANSIT AUTHORITY
RECORDS AND INFORMATION MANAGEMENT BRANCH

PROCEDURE RIM-PROC-12A — DISPOSAL OF RECORDS CONTAINING PERSONAL INFORMATION

Effective 1 July 1997.  To be read with RIM-STD-12.

A.  PREPARATION

A1  The Custodian identifies holdings that have passed the minimum retention period stated in the RIM-STD-12 Schedule.
A2  The Custodian confirms against the Quarantine List that no item is the subject of proceedings, audit, investigation or an outstanding access request.
A3  A disposal proposal is prepared listing, by category, the item numbers, the medium, the volume, and the earliest and latest creation dates of the material.
A4  The proposal is countersigned by the Branch Privacy Officer before any material is moved from its normal storage.

B.  METHOD BY MEDIUM

B1  Paper and microfiche: cross-cut shredding to a residue width not exceeding 6 mm, or secure incineration.
B2  Magnetic tape and cartridge: bulk degaussing, followed by physical destruction of the cartridge shell.
B3  Diskette and fixed disk: overwriting of the whole surface in not fewer than three passes, or physical destruction where overwriting is impracticable.
B4  Surveillance recordings: erasure by the recording equipment in normal cycle, or bulk degaussing where the medium is withdrawn from the cycle.
B5  Bulk transfer to a disposal contractor is permitted only where the contract contains the confidentiality undertaking at Attachment C, and only where the Authority provides a witnessing officer.

C.  WITNESSING AND CERTIFICATION

C1  Destruction shall be witnessed throughout by an officer holding access at the level assigned to the category, who shall not be the officer performing the destruction.
C2  A Certificate of Destruction shall be completed at the point of destruction and shall record:
      - disposal proposal reference
      - category and item numbers disposed of
      - medium and method applied
      - volume disposed of
      - place of destruction
      - date and time of commencement and completion
      - position title and signature of the officer performing destruction
      - position title and signature of the witnessing officer
C3  The Certificate shall NOT reproduce, quote or attach any content of the material destroyed.

D.  RECORD OF THE DISPOSAL

D1  The Certificate is retained by the Custodian for seven years and is produced on request to Internal Audit.
D2  The Register entry for the category is annotated with the Certificate reference at the next monthly update.
D3  Failure to obtain a Certificate renders the disposal unauthorised and is reportable to the Branch Manager within one working day.
