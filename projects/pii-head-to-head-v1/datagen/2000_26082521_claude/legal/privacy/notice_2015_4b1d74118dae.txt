COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT — PRIVACY PRACTICE DIRECTIVE PD-11
PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD BY THE ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH DIVISION
Issued 2 August 2021 by the Division Director. Countersigned by the Privacy Officer.

I. CATEGORIES HELD
   A. Applicant and Permit Holder Identity
      1. Field labels: Applicant Last Name; Applicant First Name; Business Ownership Name; Permit Number; Parcel Identification Number.
   B. Contact Attributes
      1. Field labels: Service Address; Billing Address; Contact Telephone Number; Contact Electronic Mail Address.
   C. Regulatory and Enforcement Content
      1. Field labels: Inspection Score; Violation Code; Corrective Action Due Date; Complainant Name; Complainant Contact Method; Confidentiality Requested Indicator.
   D. Financial Attributes
      1. Field labels: Fee Schedule Tier; Invoice Number; Payment Instrument Type.

II. ACCESS
   A. Categories A, B, and D: Environmental Health Specialists; the Permit Clerk; the Fiscal Officer.
   B. Category C complainant fields: the assigned Investigator and the Division Director only. These fields are withheld from public records disclosure where the Confidentiality Requested Indicator is set.

III. RETENTION
   A. Permit and inspection files: five years from permit expiration.
   B. Enforcement and abatement files: ten years from final order.
   C. Complaint intake records: three years from closure.

IV. DISPOSITION
   A. The Records Custodian issues a Destruction Eligibility List each January.
   B. Paper is shredded on site; electronic files are purged from the permitting system and from all backup sets at the next retention sweep.
   C. Completion is logged with the columns: File Series; Volume; Method; Date; Authorizing Role.
