OFFICE OF THE UNIVERSITY REGISTRAR
RECORDS AND INFORMATION MANAGEMENT STANDARD
Standard No. REG-2016-02
Issued: August 1, 2016
Supersedes: REG-2011-07
Review cycle: three years
Approved by: University Registrar; concurrence, Office of University Counsel

SECTION I — SCOPE

This standard governs personal information created, received, or maintained by the Office of the University Registrar in the ordinary course of student records administration. It binds all Registrar employees, student employees, and temporary staff, and it binds academic and administrative units to the extent that they receive record extracts from this office. Where a unit maintains its own copy of an extract, that unit assumes the retention and disposal obligations set out in Section IV for as long as the copy exists.

SECTION II — CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

The office maintains information in the following categories. Categories are defined by data element, not by system of origin; the same element may appear in more than one system.

A. Core Identity
   Student Identification Number; Legal Name; Preferred Name; Prior Legal Name; Date of Birth; Sex or Gender Designation; Country of Citizenship; Visa Classification.

B. Contact and Emergency
   Permanent Mailing Address; Local Mailing Address; Billing Address; Telephone Number; Alternate Telephone Number; University Electronic Mail Address; Emergency Contact Name; Emergency Contact Telephone Number; Emergency Contact Relationship.

C. Enrollment and Academic Progress
   Admit Term; Program of Study; Plan and Subplan; Course Registration Record; Credit Hours Attempted and Earned; Term Grade; Grade Change Authorization; Term Grade Point Average; Cumulative Grade Point Average; Academic Standing Action; Leave of Absence Record; Withdrawal Record; Degree Awarded; Date of Conferral; Honors Designation.

D. Verification and Reporting
   Enrollment Verification Request; National Clearinghouse Reporting Status; Veterans Benefit Certification Record; Athletic Eligibility Certification Record; Immigration Reporting Status.

E. Access Control and Disclosure
   Directory Information Suppression Flag; Consent to Release Form; Designated Third-Party Identifier; Disclosure Log Entry; Judicial Order or Subpoena Entry; Health or Safety Emergency Disclosure Entry.

F. Accommodation and Restriction
   Testing Accommodation Flag; Registration Restriction Flag; Protective Order Flag.

SECTION III — AUTHORIZED ACCESS

Access is granted by role, not by individual request, and is reviewed each fall term by the Records Custodian.

Category    Role with standing access                              Role with access on approval
A           Registrar staff; Admissions processing staff           Institutional Research (de-identified extract only)
B           Registrar staff; Student Accounts staff                Dean of Students Office
C           Registrar staff; Academic Advisors in the student's     Faculty of record, limited to own course sections
            own college
D           Certification Officer; Registrar staff                 External agency named in the certification
E           Records Custodian only                                 University Counsel; Public Records Officer
F           Records Custodian; Disability Services liaison         None

No person may access a record concerning their own enrollment, a household member's enrollment, or the enrollment of a person with whom they have a personal relationship. Such requests are routed to the Associate Registrar.

SECTION IV — RETENTION

Categories A, C, and E are retained permanently as the official academic record. Category B is retained five years after the last term of attendance. Category D is retained six years after the reporting year closes. Category F is retained until the underlying restriction is lifted, then one year.

SECTION V — DISPOSAL

Eligible records are listed on a quarterly destruction certificate prepared by the Records Custodian and signed by the Associate Registrar. Paper is cross-cut shredded on premises. Imaged files are deleted from primary and backup media on the next scheduled cycle. Portable media are degaussed and physically destroyed. The signed certificate is retained permanently.
