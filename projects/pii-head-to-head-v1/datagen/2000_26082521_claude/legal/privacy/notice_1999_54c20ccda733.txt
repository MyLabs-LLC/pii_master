ALLEGHENY VALLEY CREDIT UNION
PRIVACY AND RECORDS STANDARD 11
CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION: CUSTODY, ACCESS, RETENTION, DISPOSAL

Adopted 22 April 2004 by resolution of the Board of Directors.
Administered by the Records Custodian. Distribution: all managers, all branches.

1.0  APPLICATION

1.1  This standard applies to personal information in every medium and at every
     location under the control of the credit union, including branch files,
     the records room, the service bureau, and off-site storage.
1.2  It applies to information about members, former members, applicants,
     joint owners, authorized signers, guarantors, beneficiaries, vendors'
     personnel, and employees of the credit union.

2.0  PRINCIPLES

2.1  Personal information is collected and held only in the categories listed
     in Schedule A. A category not listed is not an authorized holding.
2.2  Access is limited to the role stated for the category. A role entitlement
     is personal to the position, not to the individual occupying it, and
     lapses on transfer out of that position.
2.3  Information is retained for the period stated and no longer, except where
     a hold is in force.

5.0  HOLDS AND SUSPENSION

5.1  A hold is issued in writing by the Compliance Officer and identifies the
     category codes affected and the reason.
5.2  While a hold is in force, no record in the affected category is destroyed,
     archived off site, or purged from the host system.
5.3  Holds are reviewed at each quarterly meeting of the Compliance Committee.

6.0  ASSURANCE

6.1  The Internal Auditor tests entitlement lists against Schedule A annually
     and reports exceptions to the Supervisory Committee.
6.2  The Records Custodian maintains the destruction register and the vendor
     certificates for six years from the date of each destruction event.
6.3  Failure to observe this standard is a matter for disciplinary action under
     the personnel policy.
