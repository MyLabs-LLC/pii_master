FAIRBRIDGE COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
STANDARD 7.4 — CLASSIFICATION, ACCESS, RETENTION AND DISPOSAL OF PERSONAL INFORMATION
Issued June 2021. Supersedes Standard 7.3 (2017). Custodian of record: Departmental Records Custodian.

1. PURPOSE AND APPLICATION
- Establishes a single classification of the personal information categories held by the Department.
- Binds all programme units: Clinical Services, Environmental Health, Vital Records, Community Nutrition, Emergency Preparedness, and Administration.
- Applies equally to source documents, working copies, extracts, exports, and backups.

2. CLASSIFICATION OF CATEGORIES
- Tier 1 — Directory information
  - Full Legal Name
  - Mailing Address
  - Telephone Number
  - Email Address
- Tier 2 — Identifying attributes
  - Date of Birth
  - Sex
  - Race and Ethnicity
  - Household Size
  - Preferred Language and Interpreter Need
- Tier 3 — Regulated identifiers
  - Social Security Number
  - Medical Record Number
  - Medicaid or Managed Care Member Number
  - Birth or Death Certificate File Number
  - Immigration or Refugee Case Number
- Tier 4 — Health and investigative content
  - Diagnosis and Procedure Codes
  - Laboratory and Screening Results
  - Immunization History
  - Contact Tracing Interview Notes
  - Behavioural Health and Substance Use Notes
  - Child and Adult Protective Referral Content
- Tier 5 — Employment and workforce
  - Payroll Number
  - Licence and Certification Number
  - Background Check Disposition
  - Occupational Health Clearance Status

3. PERMITTED ACCESS BY ROLE
- Intake Clerk: Tier 1 and Tier 2 only.
- Public Health Nurse: Tiers 1, 2 and 4 for assigned caseload.
- Communicable Disease Investigator: Tiers 1, 2 and 4 for assigned outbreak only; access closes when the investigation closes.
- Billing Technician: Tiers 1, 2 and 3.
- Vital Records Registrar: Tiers 1, 2 and 3 for registration functions.
- Human Resources Manager: Tier 5 and Tier 1.
- Information Technology Administrator: system-level access without content review; content viewing requires a ticket approved by the Privacy Officer.
- Tier 4 behavioural health content is closed to all roles not named in the treating team roster.

4. RETENTION PERIODS
- Tier 1 and Tier 2 held in a client index: retained for the life of the associated programme file.
- Tier 3 identifiers: purged from working systems at the retention date of the parent record; never carried into extracts or reporting sets.
- Clinical files: ten years after the final encounter, or ten years past majority for minors.
- Communicable disease investigation files: five years after closure unless the State Epidemiologist directs longer holding.
- Environmental Health complaint files: six years from resolution.
- Vital Records source documents: permanent, held in the fire-rated vault.
- Employment files: seven years from separation; occupational exposure records thirty years.
- Backups and disaster copies: overwritten on a ninety-day cycle irrespective of category.

5. DISPOSAL PROCEDURE
- Quarterly, the Records Custodian produces the Eligible for Destruction list by category and programme unit.
- The programme supervisor reviews for open litigation, audit, or public records request holds and signs the hold clearance.
- The Privacy Officer countersigns for any list containing Tier 3 or Tier 4 categories.
- Paper is cross-cut shredded on site, or collected by the contracted destruction vendor under continuous staff observation.
- Electronic records are deleted from primary storage, then from replicas, then confirmed absent by the Information Technology Administrator.
- Removable media and end-of-life drives are degaussed and physically destroyed.
- Every destruction event is entered in the Destruction Register: Category, Programme Unit, Date Range Covered, Volume, Method, Authorising Role, Witnessing Role.
- The register itself is retained permanently.

6. EXCEPTIONS
- No category may be exported to a personal device, personal account, or unapproved cloud service.
- Requests for research or surveillance use must be reduced to the minimum categories necessary and approved in writing by the Health Officer.
