TIDEWATER MUTUAL INSURANCE COMPANY
Interoffice Memorandum

To:       All Department Managers
From:     Office of the Records Custodian
Date:     11 November 2021
Subject:  Standing Instruction on Personal Information — categories, access, retention, disposal
Ref:      SI-2021-14

This instruction restates, in one place, what the Company holds about the people it insures, who may look at it, how long it is kept, and how it is destroyed. It takes effect immediately and replaces the departmental notes previously circulated on the same subject.

The Company holds five categories. Identification holds the fields Full Name, Date of Birth, Home Address, Telephone Number, and Electronic Mail Address. Regulated Numbers holds Social Security Number and Driver Licence Number. Policy holds Policy Number, Coverage Type, Effective Date, Termination Date, and Premium Amount. Claim holds Claim Number, Date of Loss, Loss Description, Reserve Amount, and Settlement Amount. Payment holds Bank Routing Number, Bank Account Number, and Payment Card Number. Where a claim requires medical substantiation, the fields Diagnosis Code, Treatment Date, and Provider Name are held within the Claim category and are marked restricted in the claims system.

Access is set by role. Customer Service Representatives see Identification and Policy. Underwriters see Identification, Regulated Numbers, and Policy. Adjusters see Identification, Policy, and Claim, including the restricted medical fields on files assigned to them. Accounts Receivable sees Identification, Policy, and Payment. Nobody outside these roles receives a copy containing Regulated Numbers or Payment fields; requests for reporting data are met with an extract from which those fields have been removed before release. Managers are responsible for notifying Information Security within one business day when a member of their staff changes role or leaves the Company, so that the access can be adjusted or withdrawn.

Policy records are kept seven years past termination. Claim records are kept ten years past closure. Payment fields are cleared twelve months after the last transaction on the account. Any record named in a legal hold is kept until the General Counsel lifts the hold, whatever the schedule otherwise says.

When the period expires, send the request to this office. We will confirm there is no hold and authorise destruction. Paper goes to the locked bins only, never to the general waste or the recycling stream, and is collected by the contracted vendor against a certificate of destruction. Electronic records are deleted by Information Technology from production and from the reporting environment, with backup copies aging out on the standard rotation. Each destruction is entered in the Destruction Register under the columns Record Series, Volume, Method, Date, Vendor Reference, and Authorising Officer. Departments should not maintain their own parallel destruction records.
