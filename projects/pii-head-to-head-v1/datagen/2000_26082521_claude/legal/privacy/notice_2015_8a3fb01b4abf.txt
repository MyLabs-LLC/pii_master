METROPOLITAN REGIONAL TRANSIT AUTHORITY
STANDARD RIG-04 — SCHEDULE A (as amended, November 2011)
CATEGORY REGISTER, CUSTODY ASSIGNMENT AND DISPOSITION SCHEDULE

The Schedule appended hereto is issued pursuant to paragraph 2 of the Standard and is to be read as forming part of it. Where the Schedule and the body of the Standard are capable of reconciliation they shall be reconciled; where they are not, the body of the Standard prevails. The Access Tier column denotes the maximum tier at which an authorisation may be recorded in the Access Register, and does not itself constitute an authorisation; the tiers are, in ascending order of restriction, Tier 3 (Operational), Tier 2 (Restricted), and Tier 1 (Controlled). No authorisation at Tier 1 shall be recorded otherwise than upon the written concurrence of the General Counsel, and every Tier 1 authorisation shall be reviewed at intervals not exceeding six months by the Records Custodian, who shall revoke any authorisation the necessity for which is no longer demonstrated.

The elements comprised within each category are those enumerated in paragraph 2 of the Standard and are not restated here. Where an element is capable of assignment to more than one category, the Designated Custodian of the more restricted category shall be deemed the custodian of the record in its entirety, and the longer of the competing retention periods shall apply. Disposition shall in every case be certified in the Disposition Log, and the certification shall recite the Code, the Volume Description, the Method employed, and the Date of Destruction.
