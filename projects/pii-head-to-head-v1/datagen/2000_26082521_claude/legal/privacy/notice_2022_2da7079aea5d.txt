WINSLOW COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
Records Handling Standard RH-02 — Personal Information Categories
Effective: 1 February 2021 | Owner: Records Custodian | Review: Annual

Scope
- Applies to every division, contracted provider, and volunteer placement that creates, receives, or stores records on Department systems.
- Covers paper, electronic, and imaged records without distinction.

Categories held
- Identity: Full Legal Name, Preferred Name, Date of Birth, Sex, Race/Ethnicity, Preferred Language.
- Contact: Residential Address, Mailing Address, Telephone Number, Email Address, Emergency Contact Name and Relationship.
- Government identifiers: Social Security Number, State Identification Number, Medicaid Number, Medical Record Number, Case Number.
- Clinical: Diagnosis Codes, Immunization History, Laboratory Results, Treatment Notes, Medication List.
- Financial: Sliding-Fee Determination, Insurance Carrier, Policy Number, Payment Method Type.
- Employment (staff files only): Job Title, Hire Date, Payroll Number, Leave Balances, Disciplinary Record.

Access
- Clinical categories: assigned clinicians and the Health Officer only.
- Government identifiers: Billing Unit staff and the Records Custodian only.
- Employment categories: the Human Resources Manager and the employee's direct supervisor.
- Any access outside the above requires written approval from the Privacy Officer, recorded in the Access Exception Log.

Retention
- Clinical records: ten years from last encounter; for minors, ten years past the age of majority.
- Billing and financial records: seven years from close of the fiscal year.
- Employment records: seven years from separation.
- Communicable disease investigation files: as directed by the State Registrar.

Disposal
- Cross-cut shred all paper; the Records Custodian files the certificate of destruction.
- Wipe electronic media to state standard, then physically destroy the media.
- Record each disposal in the Destruction Register under the headings: Category, Volume, Method, Date, Witnessing Role.
