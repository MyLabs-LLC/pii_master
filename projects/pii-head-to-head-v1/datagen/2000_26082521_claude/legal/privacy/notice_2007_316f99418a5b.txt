COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
ADMINISTRATIVE STANDARD HD-ADM-14.2
PERSONALLY IDENTIFIABLE INFORMATION: CATEGORIES, PERMITTED ACCESS, RETENTION, AND DISPOSAL
Issued: First Quarter 2016 — Supersedes HD-ADM-14.1
Standard Owner: the Records Custodian, Office of the Health Officer

- Categories of personal information held. The Department maintains, in both electronic and physical form, records containing the following enumerated categories of information, and no others without the prior written authorization of the Privacy Officer:
  - Identity Data — comprising the fields Full Legal Name, Former or Alternate Name, Date of Birth, Sex Assigned at Birth, Gender Identity, and Preferred Language.
  - Government Identifier Data — comprising the fields Social Security Number, State Identification Number, Medicaid Member Identifier, and Medicare Beneficiary Identifier.
  - Contact Data — comprising the fields Residential Street Address, Mailing Address, Primary Telephone Number, Secondary Telephone Number, and Electronic Mail Address.
  - Clinical Data — comprising the fields Client Chart Number, Encounter Date, Diagnosis Code, Immunization Record, Laboratory Result, and Treating Clinician of Record.
  - Financial and Eligibility Data — comprising the fields Household Income Bracket, Sliding-Fee Category, Insurance Carrier, Policy Number, and Bank Routing and Account Number where direct reimbursement is elected.
  - Workforce Data — comprising the fields Employee Identification Number, Position Classification, Licensure Number, and Background Screening Disposition.
- Permitted access. Access shall be granted upon the principle of minimum necessity and shall be limited to those role holders designated in the Access Control Register maintained by the Records Custodian, namely the Health Officer, the Privacy Officer, assigned Clinical Program Supervisors, credentialed Public Health Nurses within their assigned program, the Eligibility Technician for Financial and Eligibility Data only, and the Human Resources Manager for Workforce Data only.
- Retention. Each category shall be retained for the period prescribed in the State Records Retention Schedule as adopted by resolution of the Board of Health, and shall not be destroyed prior to the expiration of that period nor retained beyond it absent a litigation hold issued by County Counsel.
- Disposal. Disposal shall be effected only by the methods set forth in Section 7 of this Standard and shall be witnessed and certified in writing.
