INTEROFFICE MEMORANDUM

TO:      All Department Managers
FROM:    Office of the Compliance Officer
SUBJECT: Standard PI-3, Custody and Disposition of Member Information
DATE:    14 September 2004
FILE:    ADM/PI-3

Standard PI-3 takes effect on 01 October 2004 and applies to every department that originates, receives, or stores personal information in the course of credit union business. The schedule below assigns a single custodian of record to each category. The custodian is accountable for the accuracy of the entitlement list for that category, for the physical or logical security of the storage location, and for initiating disposition when the retention period expires.

Retention is expressed in whole months and runs from the closing event applicable to the category: closure of the relationship, payoff of the obligation, separation from employment, or the date of the entry, as the case may be. Where a category exists in both paper and electronic form, the period applies to each form independently.

Managers are to reconcile their holdings against this schedule by 30 November 2004 and to report to the Records Custodian any category held by their department that does not appear above. Holdings that cannot be assigned a reference number are to be sequestered in the records room pending classification.

Disposition is carried out quarterly. The Records Custodian issues an eligibility list to each custodian of record fourteen days before the destruction date. The custodian reviews the list, marks any item subject to hold, signs the list, and returns it. Unreturned lists suspend destruction for that department until the following quarter. Paper is destroyed by cross-cut shredding under the observation of two employees; electronic media are degaussed and then physically destroyed. The signed eligibility list and the vendor certificate are retained by the Records Custodian for six years.

Questions concerning classification are directed to the Compliance Officer. Questions concerning entitlement are directed to the Security Administrator.
