SECTION 1 - PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This standard defines the categories of personal information created, received or
held by the Group, the roles permitted to access each category, the period for
which each category is retained, and the method by which each category is
disposed of at end of life. It applies to all dispensing sites, distribution
centres, the central pharmacy support office, and to third parties processing
information under a written services agreement. It applies to records in every
medium, including paper, imaged copy, application database, backup media and
voice recording.

SECTION 2 - CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD
  Category A - Patient Identification Data
    Data elements: given name, family name, date of birth, sex, residential
    address, telephone contact, electronic contact, internal patient identifier.
  Category B - Prescription and Dispensing Data
    Data elements: prescriber identifier, product code, product description,
    quantity, directions for use, dispensing date, refill sequence, substitution
    indicator, counselling indicator.
  Category C - Clinical and Consultation Data
    Data elements: allergy code, adverse reaction note, immunisation record,
    screening result, pharmacist consultation note.
  Category D - Payer and Reimbursement Data
    Data elements: plan sponsor code, member enrolment identifier, group
    identifier, adjudication response code, copayment amount, prior authorisation
    reference.
  Category E - Payment Instrument Data
    Data elements: cardholder name, primary account number, expiry, authorisation
    reference, terminal identifier.
  Category F - Loyalty and Marketing Data
    Data elements: programme enrolment identifier, contact preference flag,
    consent status, transaction history summary.
  Category G - Colleague and Contractor Data
    Data elements: employee identifier, national insurance or tax identifier,
    licence or registration number, payroll banking details, absence record,
    disciplinary record.
  Category H - Surveillance and Access Data
    Data elements: closed circuit television image, badge swipe record,
    application logon identifier, audit trail entry.

SECTION 3 - PERMITTED ACCESS
Access is granted by role, on the minimum necessary principle, and is provisioned
only through the standard access request workflow countersigned by the line
manager and the system owner.
  Categories A, B, C ...... Registered Pharmacist; Pharmacy Technician;
                            Pharmacy Practice Manager; Clinical Audit Reviewer.
  Category D .............. Third Party Billing Analyst; Reimbursement Manager.
  Category E .............. Payment Operations Analyst (tokenised view only);
                            Card Scheme Compliance Lead.
  Category F .............. Loyalty Programme Administrator; Marketing Analyst
                            (aggregated extract only).
  Category G .............. Human Resources Business Partner; Payroll
                            Administrator; Occupational Health Adviser.
  Category H .............. Asset Protection Investigator; Information Security
                            Analyst.
No colleague may access a record relating to themselves, a household member or a
colleague at the same site other than through the formal subject access route.

SECTION 4 - RETENTION PERIODS
  Category A .... Ten years from the date of the last dispensing event.
  Category B .... Ten years from the dispensing date; controlled drug entries,
                  eleven years from the register closing date.
  Category C .... Ten years from the date the note was recorded.
  Category D .... Seven years from adjudication or from final appeal closure.
  Category E .... Authorisation data eighteen months; account number truncated at
                  point of capture and never retained in cleartext.
  Category F .... Twenty four months from the last qualifying transaction, or
                  thirty days from withdrawal of consent, whichever is earlier.
  Category G .... Six years from the end of the engagement; registration records,
                  the duration of the engagement plus ten years.
  Category H .... Thirty one days for image data; twelve months for logon and
                  audit trail entries.

SECTION 5 - DISPOSAL PROCEDURE
5.1 Eligible records are identified by the quarterly disposal report produced from
    the retention register.
5.2 The Records Custodian confirms that no legal hold, audit hold or open
    complaint applies.
5.3 Paper is cross cut shredded to the approved particle size on site, or
    collected in locked consoles for destruction by the approved contractor.
5.4 Electronic records are purged from the production system and from all
    replicas; media is degaussed or physically destroyed.
5.5 A destruction certificate is filed against the disposal report and retained
    permanently.

SECTION 6 - HOLDS AND EXCEPTIONS
Any extension of a retention period requires written approval from the Group
Privacy Officer and Legal Counsel and is recorded in the exception log.
