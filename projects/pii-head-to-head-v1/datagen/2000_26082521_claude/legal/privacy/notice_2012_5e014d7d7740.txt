MERIDIAN VALLEY CREDIT UNION
STANDARD RM-06 — CLASSIFICATION, ACCESS AND RETENTION OF MEMBER INFORMATION
Issued 12 January 1998.  Owner: Records Custodian.  Review: annual.

1. SCOPE

This standard governs information held about natural persons in any medium,
including paper files, microfiche, tape cartridges and diskettes.

2. CLASSIFICATION

+------+----------------------------------+----------------------+-------------+------+
| Code | Category of Personal Information | Access Authority     | Retention   | Disp |
+------+----------------------------------+----------------------+-------------+------+
| A-1  | Identity particulars             | Member Services      | CL + 6 yrs  | S    |
| A-2  | Contact particulars              | Member Services      | CL + 6 yrs  | S    |
| B-1  | Share and deposit registry data  | Member Services      | CL + 6 yrs  | S    |
| C-1  | Credit application data          | Lending Officer      | DEC + 25 mo | S    |
| C-2  | Consumer report data (external)  | Lending Officer      | DEC + 25 mo | S    |
| D-1  | Delinquency and collection notes | Collections Unit     | CHG + 7 yrs | S    |
| E-1  | Signature specimens              | Branch Manager       | CL + 6 yrs  | S    |
| F-1  | Marketing preference register    | Records Custodian    | Permanent   | n/a  |
+------+----------------------------------+----------------------+-------------+------+

Key: CL = account closure; DEC = credit decision; CHG = charge-off;
S = cross-cut shredding.

3. ACCESS

Access follows the position held, not the individual holding it. A position not
named against a category in the table above may not view that category without
the written concurrence of the Compliance Officer, filed with the request.

4. DISPOSAL

Records reaching term are entered on the quarterly destruction schedule,
approved by the Compliance Officer, and destroyed in the presence of two
employees. The Records Custodian enters the category code, the volume destroyed
and the date of destruction in the destruction log. The log itself is retained
permanently and is produced to the examiner on request.
