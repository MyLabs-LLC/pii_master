Date of issue ......... 14 March 1997
Supersedes ............ RIM-STD-11 (Rev. 1), 4 August 1994
Next review due ....... March 2000

Approved by ........... Director of Corporate Services
Distribution .......... List A (Branch Managers); List C (Depot Superintendents)

SECTION 1 — PURPOSE
To record in one place the categories of personal information held by the Authority, the officer accountable for each category, the authorised access level, and the minimum retention period.

SECTION 2 — APPLICATION
Applies to all records containing personal information, in any medium, held at any Authority premises, including material held in depot administration offices and in commercial off-site storage.

SECTION 3 — CATEGORY GROUPS CARRIED IN THE REGISTER
3.1  Employment and personnel
3.2  Payroll, deductions and superannuation
3.3  Occupational health, injury and rehabilitation
3.4  Customer entitlement and concession
3.5  Customer correspondence and complaint
3.6  Revenue protection and infringement
3.7  Incident, claim and witness
3.8  Security, access control and surveillance

SECTION 4 — RESPONSIBILITY
Each Branch Manager shall nominate a Records Custodian in writing. The Custodian maintains the Branch entry in the Register and certifies its accuracy at each annual review. Nominations lapse on transfer or separation of the nominated officer and must be renewed within ten working days.

SECTION 5 — RELATED INSTRUCTIONS
Access, retention and disposal requirements are set out at RIM-STD-12. Disposal procedure and certification are set out at RIM-PROC-12A.
