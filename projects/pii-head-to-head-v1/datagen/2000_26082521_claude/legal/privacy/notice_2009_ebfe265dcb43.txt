RIDGEWATER COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
OFFICE OF THE RECORDS CUSTODIAN

STANDARD RH-04: CLASSIFICATION, ACCESS AND DISPOSITION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION
Issued 12 April 2021  |  Supersedes RH-04 (2017)  |  Review cycle: biennial

1. APPLICATION. This Standard is promulgated under the retention authority vested in the Records Custodian and binds every division, contractor and seconded worker of the Department. Access to a category enumerated below is confined to the roles set opposite it, and then only to the extent necessary for the discharge of an assigned duty.

2. SCHEDULE OF CATEGORIES.

+------+----------------------------------------+--------------------------------+------------------------------+------------------+
| CODE | CATEGORY OF PERSONAL INFORMATION       | ROLES AUTHORIZED TO ACCESS     | MINIMUM RETENTION            | DISPOSAL METHOD  |
+------+----------------------------------------+--------------------------------+------------------------------+------------------+
| A-1  | Identifying particulars                | Records Custodian; Registrar   | 7 yrs after case closure     | Cross-cut shred  |
| A-2  | Contact particulars                    | Program Clerk; Case Manager    | 7 yrs after case closure     | Cross-cut shred  |
| B-1  | Demographic descriptors                | Epidemiology Analyst           | 10 yrs from creation         | Cross-cut shred  |
| B-2  | Clinical and diagnostic content        | Attending Clinician; Nurse Sup | 10 yrs after last encounter  | Incineration     |
| C-1  | Communicable disease investigation     | Disease Investigator           | 25 yrs from report date      | Incineration     |
| C-2  | Immunization history                   | Immunization Registrar         | Permanent                    | Not applicable   |
| D-1  | Eligibility and financial content      | Fiscal Officer                 | 6 fiscal years               | Cross-cut shred  |
| D-2  | Government-issued identifier refs      | Records Custodian only         | 6 fiscal years               | Secure erasure   |
| E-1  | Employment and personnel content       | Human Resources Officer        | 5 yrs after separation       | Cross-cut shred  |
+------+----------------------------------------+--------------------------------+------------------------------+------------------+

3. DISPOSITION. No series shall be destroyed before expiry of the minimum retention period, nor while it is subject to a litigation hold, an audit hold, or a pending request for access. Destruction shall be effected under the observation of the Records Custodian or a designated deputy and shall be entered in the Certificate of Destruction Register, which shall record the series code, the volume disposed of, the method applied, the date of destruction, and the signature of the attesting officer. The Register is itself retained permanently.
