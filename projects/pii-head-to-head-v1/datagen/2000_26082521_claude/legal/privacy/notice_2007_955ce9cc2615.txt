ANNEX C — DATA CATEGORY, ACCESS, RETENTION AND DISPOSAL SCHEDULE
Records Handling Standard, 2016 revision
Maintained by the Records Custodian. Endorsed by Counsel.

Column headings used throughout this Annex:

  CATEGORY | SYSTEM OF RECORD | AUTHORISED ROLES | RETENTION TRIGGER | PERIOD | DISPOSAL METHOD

Categories

  Identity attributes | escrow production system | Escrow Officer, Escrow Assistant, Title Examiner, Counsel | policy issuance or cancellation | 7 years | cryptographic erasure
  Government identifiers | escrow production system, encrypted archive | Escrow Officer, Funding Clerk, Counsel | confirmed disbursement | 90 days production, statutory period in archive | cryptographic erasure
  Contact attributes | escrow production system | Escrow Officer, Escrow Assistant, Post-Closing Clerk, Counsel | policy issuance or cancellation | 7 years | cryptographic erasure
  Disbursement account attributes | funding module | Escrow Officer, Funding Clerk, Counsel | wire confirmation and reconciliation | 45 days | cryptographic erasure
  Source-of-funds evidence | imaging repository | Escrow Officer, Escrow Assistant, Counsel | disbursement | 5 years | cryptographic erasure
  Transaction and property attributes | escrow production system | all operational roles | policy issuance or cancellation | 7 years | cryptographic erasure
  Adverse-matter search results | title plant interface, working file | Title Examiner, Escrow Officer, Counsel | file closing | 3 years | shredding or cryptographic erasure
  Notarised instrument images | imaging repository | Post-Closing Clerk, Escrow Officer, Counsel | recording confirmation | 7 years | cryptographic erasure
  Correspondence and call records | messaging archive | Escrow Officer, Escrow Assistant, Counsel | last entry | 3 years | cryptographic erasure

Standing conditions

  1. Roles listed are ceilings, not entitlements. Actual access is limited to files assigned to the individual.
  2. The Information Technology Administrator holds no content access under this Annex.
  3. External examiners are admitted under Section 4 of the Standard only.
  4. Any category not listed above is treated as Restricted until the Records Custodian assigns it an entry.
  5. Retention periods run from the trigger, not from the date of collection.
  6. Holds suspend all periods. Release is by written instrument from Counsel only.
  7. Disposal is executed monthly against the eligibility list and recorded in the destruction log.
  8. This Annex is reviewed annually and reissued in full rather than amended in part.
