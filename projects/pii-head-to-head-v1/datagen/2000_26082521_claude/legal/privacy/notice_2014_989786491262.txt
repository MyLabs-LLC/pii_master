CONTROL NOTES
- Codes are assigned by the Privacy Officer. Departments may not create category codes.
- A record bearing field labels from two or more categories takes the longest retention of those categories.
- Category F is filed separately at all times and is never merged into the Category E jacket.
- Roles listed are exhaustive. Temporary access requires a signed Form RIM-7 and expires in 30 days.
- Contractors, adjusting firms, and independent inspectors receive category access only under executed confidentiality agreement, and only for the codes named in that agreement.
- No category shall be transmitted by facsimile without a cover sheet and telephoned confirmation of receipt.
- Destruction is suspended in its entirety upon receipt of a litigation hold or an examination notice from the Department of Insurance.
- The Certificate of Destruction shall reference the code above, not a description in free text.
