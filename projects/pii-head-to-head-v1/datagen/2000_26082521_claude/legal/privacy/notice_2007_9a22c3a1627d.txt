SUBJECT: Records Handling Standard RG-04 — Categories, Access, Retention, Disposal

1. PURPOSE

This standard sets out how the Office of the University Registrar classifies the personal information it maintains, who may access each classification, how long each classification is kept, and how records are destroyed at the end of the retention period. It applies to records in all media, including paper files, imaged files, and data held in the student information system.

2. CATEGORIES MAINTAINED

2.1  Identifiers — Student Identification Number, Legal Name, Preferred Name, Former Name, Date of Birth, Country of Citizenship.
2.2  Contact Data — Permanent Mailing Address, Local Mailing Address, Telephone Number, University Electronic Mail Address, Emergency Contact Name, Emergency Contact Relationship.
2.3  Academic Record — Term Enrollment Status, Course Registration History, Term Grade, Term and Cumulative Grade Point Average, Transfer Credit Evaluation, Degree Conferred, Date of Conferral, Academic Standing Action.
2.4  Administrative Holds — Financial Hold Indicator, Conduct Hold Indicator, Advising Hold Indicator, Immunization Hold Indicator.
2.5  Disclosure Records — Directory Information Suppression Flag, Consent to Release Form, Third-Party Disclosure Entry, Subpoena Notification Entry.

3. ACCESS

Categories 2.1 through 2.4 are available to Registrar staff holding an active system role and to Designated School Officials with a legitimate educational interest in the specific record. Category 2.5 is maintained by the Records Custodian and is available on request to the student of record and to the Office of University Counsel. Access by any other office requires written authorization from the Registrar.

4. RETENTION AND DISPOSAL

Categories 2.2 and 2.4 are purged five years after the last term of attendance. Categories 2.1, 2.3, and 2.5 are permanent. Paper records approved for destruction are cross-cut shredded by the Records Custodian; electronic records are overwritten and the deletion entered in the disposal log.
