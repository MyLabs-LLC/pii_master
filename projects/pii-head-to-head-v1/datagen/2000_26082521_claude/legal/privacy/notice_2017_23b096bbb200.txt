CEDAR POINT FEDERAL CREDIT UNION
OPERATIONS MANUAL, SECTION 9 — HANDLING OF MEMBER INFORMATION
Standard 9.3 (Revision C)                          Effective: 1 July 1997

9.3.1  PURPOSE

This standard states the categories of personal information maintained by the
credit union, the positions authorised to see each category, the period for
which each category is held, and the manner of its destruction. It is issued
under the authority of the Board and is examinable under Section 748 of the
NCUA Rules and Regulations.

9.3.2  CATEGORIES MAINTAINED

Ref  Category of Personal Information         Medium of Record
---  ---------------------------------------  --------------------------
 1   Identity and verification particulars    Paper, host file
 2   Residence and contact particulars        Host file
 3   Membership eligibility evidence          Paper
 4   Share, draft and certificate registry    Host file, microfiche
 5   Loan application and underwriting file   Paper, microfiche
 6   Consumer reporting agency data           Paper
 7   Employment and income verification       Paper
 8   Payroll deduction authorisations         Paper
 9   Beneficiary and joint owner designations Paper
10   Delinquency, workout and charge-off      Paper, host file
11   Currency transaction filings             Paper
12   Branch surveillance images               Videotape
13   Audio response and telephone service log Host file
14   Complaint and correspondence file        Paper

Compliance Officer          All               11
Internal Auditor            All               None
Data Processing Supervisor  1-4, 10, 13       None
Supervisory Committee       All               None

9.3.4  RETENTION

Categories 1 to 4 and 9 are held for six years from closure of the last
relationship. Categories 5 to 8 are held for twenty-five months from the credit
decision, or for the life of the loan plus six years where credit is extended.
Category 10 is held for seven years from charge-off. Category 11 is held for
five years from filing. Category 12 is overwritten on a ninety-day cycle.
Categories 13 and 14 are held for two years.

9.3.5  DISPOSAL

Paper is destroyed by cross-cut shredder on the premises, or by a bonded
contractor returning a certificate of destruction. Microfiche is incinerated.
Magnetic media are degaussed before release or disposal; erasure by deletion is
not sufficient. No category may be destroyed while it is the subject of an
examination, a claim, or a written request from a member.
