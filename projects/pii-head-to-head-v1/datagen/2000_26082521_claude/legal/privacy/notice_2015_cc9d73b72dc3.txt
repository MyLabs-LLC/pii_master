PERSONAL INFORMATION HANDLING STANDARD — RECORDS ADMINISTRATION SERIES, ISSUE 3

Applicability: All branch offices, closing centres, and the central plant. Effective on issue. Supersedes Issue 2.

1. SCOPE AND DEFINITIONS

- 1.1 "Personal information" means any recorded item that identifies, or can reasonably be linked to, a natural person party to a transaction handled by this company.
- 1.2 "Custodial file" means the assembled record of a single order, whether held in paper jacket, microform, or electronic image.
- 1.3 The Records Custodian is the officer accountable for this Standard. The Branch Manager is accountable for local compliance.

2. CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD

The company recognises the following categories. Each is defined by the class of field it contains, not by any particular file.

- 2.1 Category A — Identity Particulars. Fields: full legal name; former or maiden name; date of birth; government-issued identification number; identification document type and issuing authority.
- 2.2 Category B — Contact Particulars. Fields: mailing address; property address; residence telephone; business telephone; facsimile number; electronic mail address.
- 2.3 Category C — Financial Particulars. Fields: lender name; loan number; deposit account number; wire instruction detail; earnest money amount; payoff figure; credit report indicator.
- 2.4 Category D — Transaction Particulars. Fields: order number; escrow number; policy number; commitment number; legal description; parcel identifier; recording reference.
- 2.5 Category E — Sensitive Particulars. Fields: marital status; divorce decree reference; probate or estate reference; bankruptcy reference; judgment or lien party detail; power of attorney reference.
- 2.6 Category F — Employment and Vendor Particulars. Fields: employee number; payroll classification; licence number; bond or errors-and-omissions carrier reference.

3. ACCESS AUTHORITY

- 3.1 Category A, B, and D may be accessed by the assigned Escrow Officer, the Escrow Assistant of record, the Title Examiner, and the Recording Clerk.
- 3.2 Category C may be accessed by the assigned Escrow Officer and the Trust Accountant only. The Escrow Assistant may enter but not retrieve.
- 3.3 Category E may be accessed by the Title Examiner and the Underwriting Counsel. All other access requires written authorisation of the Branch Manager.
- 3.4 Category F is held exclusively by the Personnel Administrator and the Compliance Officer.
- 3.5 No category may be disclosed to an outside party except upon lawful process, upon written instruction of the party of record, or as required to complete the transaction. Each disclosure is entered in the Disclosure Log with date, category, recipient class, and authorising officer title.
- 3.6 Terminal access is granted by role profile. Profiles are reviewed by the Compliance Officer each calendar quarter. Shared sign-on identifiers are prohibited.

4. RETENTION PERIODS

- 4.1 Category A, B, and D within a closed custodial file — seven years from date of disbursement, then transfer to the central plant.
- 4.2 Category C — seven years from date of final reconciliation of the trust account to which the item relates.
- 4.3 Category E — retained for the life of the policy liability, plus three years.
- 4.4 Category F — six years from separation of the individual, except licence and bond references, which are held for the statutory period applicable to the state of issue.
- 4.5 Working papers, drafts, and duplicate copies not forming part of the custodial file — destroyed upon closing.
- 4.6 No record subject to a litigation hold, audit hold, or regulatory examination notice may be destroyed, irrespective of the periods above. Holds are recorded on the Hold Register.

5. DISPOSAL PROCEDURE

- 5.1 The Records Custodian issues a Disposal Schedule listing eligible file ranges by order number series and category.
- 5.2 The Branch Manager certifies against the Hold Register before release.
- 5.3 Paper is cross-cut shredded to a particle width not exceeding three-eighths of one inch, on premises, witnessed by two officers.
- 5.4 Microform is incinerated or chemically destroyed by the approved vendor under certificate.
- 5.5 Magnetic and optical media are overwritten in full and thereafter physically destroyed; erasure alone is not sufficient.
- 5.6 A Certificate of Destruction is executed, listing date, method, category, file range, and the titles of the witnessing officers. Certificates are retained permanently.
- 5.7 The Compliance Officer audits disposal certificates semi-annually and reports exceptions to the Executive Committee.
