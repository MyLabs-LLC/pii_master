Issue Date: 14 April 2021
Supersedes: Version 2.4 (September 2019)

Document Owner: Data Protection Lead

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 This Standard defines the categories of personal information processed by the Company, the roles permitted to access each category, the applicable retention period, and the approved method of disposal.

1.2 This Standard applies to all branches, to the central payroll function, and to any third party processing personal information on the Company's behalf under written instruction.

1.3 This Standard applies to information held in the applicant tracking system, the payroll system, branch shared drives, and physical files held in branch storage.

2. CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

2.1 Category A — Identity and Contact Data. Fields: Full Name; Former Name; Date of Birth; Gender; Home Address; Personal Telephone Number; Personal Email Address; Emergency Contact Name; Emergency Contact Telephone Number.

2.2 Category B — Right to Work and Immigration Data. Fields: Nationality; Passport Number; Passport Expiry Date; Visa Category; Visa Expiry Date; Share Code; Right to Work Check Date; Checking Officer Role.

2.3 Category C — Payroll and Financial Data. Fields: Payroll Number; National Insurance Number; Tax Code; Student Loan Plan Type; Bank Sort Code; Bank Account Number; Account Holder Name; Pension Scheme Reference; Salary Sacrifice Indicator.

2.4 Category D — Assignment and Placement Data. Fields: Candidate Reference; Client Account Code; Assignment Start Date; Assignment End Date; Job Title; Hourly Rate; Timesheet Reference; Shift Pattern; Placement Outcome Code.

2.5 Category E — Vetting and Screening Data. Fields: Reference Provider Organisation; Reference Provider Role; Qualification Title; Awarding Body; Certificate Number; Professional Registration Number; Registration Expiry Date.

2.6 Category F — Special Category Data. Fields: Racial or Ethnic Origin (diversity monitoring); Religion or Belief (diversity monitoring); Health and Disability Status; Reasonable Adjustment Requirement; Occupational Health Outcome; Trade Union Membership.

2.7 Category G — Criminal Offence Data. Fields: Disclosure Certificate Number; Disclosure Issue Date; Disclosure Outcome Code; Self-Declaration Status; Barred List Check Date.

2.8 Category H — Workplace Conduct Data. Fields: Incident Reference; Incident Date; Incident Category; Grievance Reference; Disciplinary Outcome Code; Appeal Status.

3. ACCESS

3.1 Access is granted on the principle of least privilege and by role, not by individual request.

3.2 Categories A, D and E may be accessed by Recruitment Consultants, Branch Managers and the Compliance Officer.

3.3 Category B may be accessed by the Compliance Officer and Branch Managers only.

3.4 Category C may be accessed by the Payroll Controller and Payroll Administrators only. Branch staff have no access to Category C.

3.5 Categories F and G may be accessed by the Compliance Officer and the Data Protection Lead only, and are stored in a restricted partition segregated from the main worker record.

3.6 Category H may be accessed by the Human Resources Manager and the Data Protection Lead.

3.7 The Records Custodian holds administrative access for retention and disposal purposes only and must not access record content except where required to identify a category.

4. RETENTION PERIODS

Category A — 6 years from the end of the final assignment
Category B — 2 years from the end of the final assignment
Category C — 6 years from the end of the relevant tax year
Category D — 6 years from assignment end date
Category E — 6 years from the end of the final assignment
Category F — 2 years from the date of collection; diversity monitoring fields aggregated at 12 months
Category G — 6 months from the disclosure outcome decision
Category H — 6 years from case closure

4.1 Records relating to unsuccessful applicants are retained for 12 months from the placement outcome decision and are then destroyed in full.

4.2 Where a retention period has expired but the record is subject to legal hold, disposal is suspended and the Records Custodian records the hold reference against the file.

5. DISPOSAL

5.1 Physical records are destroyed by cross-cut shredding to DIN 66399 security level P-4 or higher, or by an approved contractor providing a certificate of destruction.

5.2 Electronic records are erased in accordance with NIST SP 800-88 guidance. Deletion from the live system must be followed by removal from backup media at the next scheduled backup expiry.

5.3 Disposal is performed quarterly. The Records Custodian maintains a Disposal Log capturing: Log Entry Reference; Record Category; Volume; Disposal Method; Date of Destruction; Authorising Role; Certificate Reference.

5.4 The Disposal Log is retained for 6 years and is subject to annual audit by the Compliance Officer.
