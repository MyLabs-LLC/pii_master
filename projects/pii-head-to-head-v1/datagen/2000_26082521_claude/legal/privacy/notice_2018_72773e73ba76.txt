NOTES

(a)  The designation "masked" signifies that the application shall render the field with all but the terminal characters suppressed, and that no user bearing the designated role shall have the capability to unsuppress the field.

(b)  Minimum retention runs from the date of final account resolution except in the case of the Collection category, which runs from the date of account release by the external agency.

(c)  A record subject to a preservation directive shall be flagged in the account header and shall be excluded from the automated purge job until the directive is lifted in writing.

(d)  Role assignments are reviewed semi-annually by the Compliance Officer. Any role not appearing above possesses no entitlement to the category by implication.
