MEMORANDUM

TO:        Human Resources; Payroll; Department Heads
FROM:      Office of the Records Custodian
DATE:      November 2011
SUBJECT:   Standard PRS-04, Supplement 2 — Category D Handling in Departmental Custody

Department Heads increasingly hold working copies of Category D material outside the personnel file system. This Supplement states what may be held departmentally, for how long, and how it is to be destroyed.

PERMITTED DEPARTMENTAL HOLDINGS

A Department Head may hold, for direct reports only, the following elements:

    Employee name
    Employee number
    Job classification
    Work telephone extension
    Leave balance
    Performance evaluation rating and evaluation date
    Training completion record
    Documented coaching or counselling notes

PROHIBITED DEPARTMENTAL HOLDINGS

The following elements are held solely by Human Resources and Payroll and are not to be copied to departmental files, shared drives, electronic mail folders or personal notebooks:

    Social security number
    Date of birth
    Home address and home telephone number
    Dependant names and dates of birth
    Benefit enrolment elections
    Direct deposit account and routing numbers
    Wage garnishment orders
    Medical certifications and accommodation records
    Pre-employment background screening results
    Emergency contact details

Where a Department Head has legitimate need of a prohibited element for a specific purpose, the element is viewed in the Human Resources system of record and is not extracted.

RETENTION OF DEPARTMENTAL WORKING COPIES

    Performance evaluation copies — until the succeeding evaluation is finalised, then destroy.
    Coaching and counselling notes — three years from the date of the note, or until the employee separates, whichever is earlier; a copy is transmitted to Human Resources for the personnel file at the time the note is made.
    Leave and training records — twelve months, the system of record being authoritative.
    All departmental holdings for a separated employee — destroy within thirty days of separation.

DISPOSAL

Paper is placed in the locked destruction consoles and not in general waste or open recycling. Electronic copies on network shares are deleted by the Department Head and the deletion confirmed to the Records Custodian on the quarterly attestation form. Copies held in electronic mail, including sent items and locally cached folders, are deleted at the same time; Department Heads should note that a message deleted from the client remains recoverable from the mail archive until the archive retention period expires, and should therefore avoid transmitting Category D elements by electronic mail in the first instance.

ATTESTATION

Each Department Head submits a quarterly attestation to the Records Custodian confirming (a) the categories of Category D material held departmentally, (b) that no prohibited element is held, and (c) that material exceeding its retention period has been destroyed. The attestation form is obtained from the Office of the Records Custodian. Non-submission is reported to the General Manager.
