BRANCH RECORDS HANDLING NOTE

Reference: OPS-NOTE-11
Effective: 2 June 2021
Issued by: Head of Compliance
Applies to: All branch offices

1. Branch offices hold personal information in three categories only: Identity and Contact Data; Assignment and Placement Data; Right to Work Evidence.

2. Field sets are as recorded in IG-STD-004. Branches must not create additional fields, spreadsheets or notebooks holding personal information outside the applicant tracking system.

3. Payroll and Financial Data, Special Category Data and Criminal Offence Data are not held at branch level under any circumstances. Any such material received at a branch must be forwarded to the Compliance Officer the same working day and the branch copy destroyed.

4. Access to branch records is limited to Recruitment Consultants and the Branch Manager. Access is removed on the day a role change or leaver notification is processed.

5. Physical files awaiting collection are stored in the locked cabinet in the back office. The key is held by the Branch Manager. Files must not be left in unlocked drawers, on desks, or in vehicles.

6. Right to Work Evidence is retained for 2 years from the end of the final assignment. All other branch-held categories are retained for 6 years from the end of the final assignment.

7. Branches do not perform disposal. Expired files are listed on the quarterly return and collected by the Records Custodian for destruction under certificate.

8. Suspected loss, misdirection or unauthorised disclosure is reported to the Data Protection Lead within 24 hours using the incident form.
