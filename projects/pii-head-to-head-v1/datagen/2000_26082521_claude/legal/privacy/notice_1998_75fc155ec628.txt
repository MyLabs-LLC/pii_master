RIVERBEND COMMUNITY CREDIT UNION
INTERIM DIRECTIVE ID-98/03
SUPPLEMENT TO ADMINISTRATIVE STANDARD AP-114

To: All Branch Managers; All Department Heads
From: The Compliance Officer
Effective: 12 January 1998, pending incorporation into AP-114 at next revision

I. Effective immediately, the categories defined at Clause 2.0 of AP-114 shall be extended to include a sixth category, designated Category VI — Electronic Channel Data, comprising the data elements captured under the field labels Audio Response Personal Identification Number; Home Banking User Identifier; Home Banking Password Hash; Session Log Identifier; Terminal Location Code.

II. Category VI shall be accessible only to the Data Processing Manager and to such personnel as that officer shall designate in writing, and in no circumstance shall Category VI be accessible from any branch teller platform.

III. The retention period for Category VI shall be twenty-four (24) months from the date of last member use, notwithstanding the general periods prescribed at Clause 4.0 of AP-114.

IV. Disposal of Category VI records shall be effected by secure overwrite of the storage medium in not fewer than three passes, and shall be evidenced upon the Certificate of Destruction in the manner prescribed at Clause 5.3 of AP-114.

V. Department Heads shall acknowledge receipt of this Directive to the Office of the Corporate Secretary within ten (10) business days.
