H.3 UNDERTAKINGS OF THE PROVIDER

H.3.1 The provider shall use the categories marked at H.2 solely for the purposes of the contracted service and for no other purpose.

H.3.2 The provider shall not disclose, sell, transfer, or make available any category, in whole or in part, to any third party without the prior written authorisation of the Superintendent.

H.3.3 The provider shall restrict access within its own organisation to those personnel whose duties require it, and shall maintain a current list of such personnel by title.

H.3.4 The provider shall store all District records separately from records of other clients and shall apply physical and electronic safeguards no less protective than those described at Section E of this Standard.

H.3.5 The provider shall notify the District Contract Administrator in writing within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of any loss, theft, or unauthorised disclosure.

H.3.6 The provider shall not transfer any category outside the continental United States.

H.4 RETURN AND DESTRUCTION AT TERMINATION

H.4.1 Within thirty (30) days of expiry or termination of the engagement, the provider shall return all District records in its possession or destroy them in accordance with Section E, at the District's election.

H.4.2 Where destruction is elected, the provider shall furnish a Certificate of Destruction recording: the category destroyed, the volume destroyed, the destruction method, the date of destruction, and the title and signature of the certifying officer.

H.4.3 The District reserves the right to inspect the provider's premises and records-handling practices on ten (10) days' notice during the term of the engagement and for one year following its close.
