ALDER COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
RECORDS HANDLING STANDARD RH-12
EFFECTIVE 01 OCT 1996 — SUPERSEDES RH-11
ISSUING AUTHORITY: RECORDS CUSTODIAN

Applies to every record series maintained by the Divisions of Nursing, Environmental Health, and Vital Statistics.

Categories maintained
- Identity elements: surname field, given name field, middle initial field, date of birth field, sex code, race/ethnicity code.
- Contact elements: mailing address fields, alternate mailing address fields, residence telephone field.
- Assigned identifiers: departmental case number, state program enrollment number, third-party payer subscriber number, laboratory accession number.
- Clinical elements: presenting complaint, diagnosis code field, treatment and disposition notes, laboratory result fields, immunization lot and administration fields.
- Financial elements: sliding-fee determination worksheet, household size field, billing ledger fields, adjustment and write-off fields.

Access authorisation
- Clinical elements: licensed clinical staff assigned to the case; the Medical Director.
- Financial elements: the Billing Clerk; the Fiscal Officer.
- Assigned identifiers: the Records Custodian; the Data Entry Clerk, for entry only.
- Environmental Health complaint files: the Sanitarian of record; the Environmental Health Supervisor.
- All other access requires written authorisation of the Health Officer, filed with the request.

Retention
- Adult clinical files: seven years from date of last encounter.
- Minor clinical files: until the twenty-first birthday, or seven years from last encounter, whichever is later.
- Communicable disease investigation files: ten years from case closure.
- Immunisation registry entries: retained indefinitely pending State schedule revision.
- Fee determination and billing ledgers: five years from close of the fiscal year.
- Correspondence not filed to a case: two years.

Disposal
- Approved methods: cross-cut shredding on Department premises; witnessed incineration by the contracted vendor.
- Prohibited: placement in general refuse; recycling in uncut form; removal from premises other than under vendor manifest.
- Destruction log entry required before disposal: record series, inclusive dates, volume in cubic feet, method, date of destruction, title of witnessing officer.
- The destruction log is retained permanently by the Records Custodian and is subject to inspection by the County Auditor.
