MEMBER INFORMATION HANDLING STANDARD
Document Control No. 98-004
Approved by the Board of Directors, 19 January 1998
Custodian of Record: the Records Custodian, Operations Division

PART ONE — APPLICATION

1. The Credit Union collects, holds and uses information about its members and about persons who apply for membership, for credit, or for ancillary services, and about its own employees and volunteer officials. This Standard states, by category, what is held, who may see it, for how long it is kept, and how it is destroyed. Nothing in this Standard authorises a use of information that is not permitted by the Right to Financial Privacy Act, the Fair Credit Reporting Act, the Bank Secrecy Act, or the Credit Union's own disclosure notices.

2. Every category described in Part Two exists in at least two forms: the record of the record on the host system, and one or more derived forms — the daily and monthly report suite, the microfiche archive produced by the service bureau, and the working files maintained at the branch. Employees are reminded, in the plainest terms the Compliance Officer can put them, that a derived form carries exactly the same restrictions as the record from which it was derived. A report left on a desk overnight is a breach of this Standard whether or not it is later filed correctly.

PART TWO — CATEGORIES OF INFORMATION

3. Membership and Identification Data. The fields maintained are MEMBER-NO, MEMBER-SINCE, SURNAME, GIVEN-NAME, MIDDLE-INIT, DOB, TIN, SPONSOR-GROUP-CODE, ELIGIBILITY-BASIS and the address and telephone block comprising ADDR-LINE-1, ADDR-LINE-2, CITY, STATE, ZIP, PHONE-HOME and PHONE-WORK.

4. Account Relationship Data. The fields maintained are SHARE-ID, SHARE-TYPE, DRAFT-ID, CERT-ID, MATURITY-DATE, LOAN-ID, LOAN-TYPE, ORIG-AMT, BALANCE-CURR, PAYMENT-DUE, DELINQ-DAYS, JOINT-OWNER-SEQ, BENEFICIARY-SEQ and SIG-CARD-SEQ.

5. Credit and Underwriting Data. The fields maintained are EMPLOYER, POSITION, YEARS-SERVICE, INCOME-GROSS-MO, OTHER-INCOME, HOUSING-EXPENSE, BUREAU-FILE-NO, BUREAU-SCORE, DEBT-RATIO, COLLATERAL-DESC, LTV-PCT, DECISION-CODE and ADVERSE-ACTION-CODE.

6. Transaction and Compliance Data. The fields maintained are TRAN-SEQ, TRAN-DATE, TRAN-CODE, TRAN-AMT, TELLER-ID, BRANCH-ID, CTR-FILED-FLAG, EXEMPT-FLAG, OFAC-MATCH-FLAG and SAR-REF.

7. Authentication and Security Data. The fields maintained are PIN-OFFSET, AUDIO-RESPONSE-CODE, VERIFICATION-PHRASE, CARD-STATUS and TELLER-OVERRIDE-KEY.

8. Employee and Volunteer Data. The fields maintained are EMP-NO, POSITION-CODE, HIRE-DATE, PAY-GRADE, W-4-STATUS, BOND-COVERAGE-FLAG, BACKGROUND-CHECK-DATE and DISCIPLINARY-REF.

PART THREE — ACCESS, RETENTION AND DISPOSAL

9. Access is by system profile only. Clauses 3 and 4 are open to the teller line and member services; clause 5 to the Lending Department, the Collections Officer, the Internal Auditor and the examining authority; clause 6 to the BSA Officer and the Internal Auditor, save that SAR-REF is restricted to the BSA Officer alone; clause 7 to no person in displayable form; clause 8 to the Personnel Officer and the Chief Executive Officer.

10. Retention periods are: identification and relationship records, seven years from closure; declined applications, twenty-five months; transaction and compliance records, five years; authentication records, purged at closure; personnel records, seven years from separation, save that bond and background records are held permanently.

11. Disposal of paper and microfiche is by cross-cut shredder on the premises or by a bonded destruction contractor supplying a certificate of destruction; disposal of magnetic media is by degaussing followed by physical destruction of the cartridge. Two officers shall witness each disposal and shall sign the Destruction Register against the columns DESTR-SEQ, DESTR-DATE, CATEGORY, MEDIUM, VOLUME, METHOD, WITNESS-1 and WITNESS-2. No record subject to a legal hold notified by the Compliance Officer may be destroyed, notwithstanding the expiry of its retention period.
