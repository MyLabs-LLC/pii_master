PERSONAL INFORMATION HANDLING STANDARD
Policy Reference: RM-04
Issuing Function: Compliance and Risk Management
Effective Date: April 1, 2016
Supersedes: RM-04, issued October 2013
Review Cycle: Biennial
Approving Authority: Board Audit Committee

SECTION 1 — PURPOSE
This standard establishes uniform requirements for the classification, access control, retention, and destruction of personal information held by the Credit Union in any medium, including paper, microform, imaged, and electronic records.

SECTION 2 — SCOPE
This standard applies to all employees, temporary staff, contracted service providers, and volunteer officials who create, receive, handle, or store personal information. It applies at all branch locations, the operations center, the disaster recovery site, and any off-site storage facility held under contract.

SECTION 3 — CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION MAINTAINED
3.1 Identification Data. Legal name, date of birth, and government-issued identifier fields collected to satisfy member verification requirements.
3.2 Contact Data. Mailing address, residence address, telephone, and electronic mail fields maintained for correspondence and notice delivery.
3.3 Financial Relationship Data. Share and loan account identifiers, balances, transaction histories, and periodic statements.
3.4 Credit Assessment Data. Consumer report content, income and employment fields, debt obligation schedules, collateral valuations, and internal scoring outputs.
3.5 Payment Instrument Data. Card and draft identifiers, authentication credentials, and dispute case files.
3.6 Related Party Data. Beneficiary designations, powers of attorney, custodial and fiduciary arrangements, and joint owner records.
3.7 Employment Data. Personnel files, compensation records, benefit elections, background screening results, and disciplinary documentation.
3.8 Electronic Access Data. Online and telephone banking credentials, device registrations, session logs, and system audit trail entries.

SECTION 4 — ACCESS AUTHORIZATION
4.1 Access is granted only on a documented business-need basis and only to the categories required for the assigned duty.
4.2 Standing access by category is assigned as follows: Member Services Representatives — 3.1, 3.2, 3.3; Lending Officers — 3.1 through 3.6; Collections staff — 3.1, 3.2, 3.3, 3.6; Human Resources Generalists — 3.7 only; Information Security Administrator — 3.8 only, plus emergency access to any category under the break-glass procedure; Internal Audit — read-only access to all categories.
4.3 Service provider access is limited to the categories enumerated in the executed confidentiality agreement.
4.4 The Information Security Administrator recertifies all access assignments quarterly. Unrecertified assignments are revoked.

SECTION 5 — RETENTION PERIODS
5.1 Identification and Contact Data: five years after closure of the last account relationship.
5.2 Financial Relationship Data: seven years from the date of entry.
5.3 Credit Assessment Data: twenty-five months from the date of the credit decision.
5.4 Payment Instrument Data: seven years for dispute files; authentication credentials purged ninety days after deactivation.
5.5 Related Party Data: six years after termination of the designation.
5.6 Employment Data: seven years after separation, except benefit records, which are retained for the period required by the governing plan document.
5.7 Electronic Access Data: eighteen months for session logs; seven years for audit trail entries.

SECTION 6 — DISPOSAL PROCEDURE
6.1 The Records Custodian generates a disposal eligibility list each calendar quarter.
6.2 The list is circulated to the Chief Compliance Officer and outside counsel for legal hold clearance. No item proceeds without written clearance.
6.3 Cleared paper and microform records are destroyed by cross-cut shredding, either on premises under witness or by the bonded vendor of record.
6.4 Cleared electronic media are overwritten to the approved standard or physically destroyed. Logical deletion alone is not acceptable disposal.
6.5 A Certificate of Destruction is executed for each disposal event and retained for ten years.
6.6 Departments may not dispose of records independently of this procedure.

SECTION 7 — HOLDS AND EXCEPTIONS
Records subject to litigation hold, regulatory examination, or open dispute are exempt from disposal until released in writing by the Chief Compliance Officer.
