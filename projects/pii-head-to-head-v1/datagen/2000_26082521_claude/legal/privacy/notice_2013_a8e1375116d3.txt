RECORDS HANDLING STANDARD — BRANCH SUMMARY SHEET
Form RM-04A | Effective January 15, 2016 | Post at each teller station

SECTION A — CATEGORIES HELD AT BRANCH LEVEL
A1  Identification Data
A2  Contact Data
A3  Financial Relationship Data
A4  Signature and Authorization Cards
A5  Related Party Data

SECTION B — WHO MAY ACCESS
Member Services Representative: A1, A2, A3, A4
Head Teller: A1 through A5
Branch Manager: A1 through A5
Lending Officer: A1 through A5
Internal Audit: all categories, read-only
All other staff: no standing access; request through the Branch Manager

SECTION C — RETENTION AT BRANCH
Working files: current year plus one. Transfer to the operations center thereafter.
Signature and Authorization Cards: retain while the relationship is open; transfer at closure.
Teller proof work: ninety days, then transfer.

SECTION D — DISPOSAL
Branches do not destroy records. Place eligible material in the locked destruction console. Consoles are collected by the bonded vendor on the published schedule. Loose disposal in waste or recycling containers is prohibited. Report any console overflow or lock failure to the Branch Manager the same business day.

SECTION E — QUESTIONS
Direct all questions to the Records Custodian at the extension published in the internal directory.
