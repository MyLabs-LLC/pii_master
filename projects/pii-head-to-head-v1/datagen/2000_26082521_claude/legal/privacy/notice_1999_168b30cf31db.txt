SUNRIDGE VALLEY UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT
BULLETIN AS-114.3 — VENDOR AND CONTRACTOR DATA ACCESS CONDITIONS
Records Management, Office of the Superintendent
1 August 2021

1. SCOPE

1.1 This bulletin sets the conditions under which a vendor, contractor or other external party may be granted access to any category of personal information held by the District.

2. PRECONDITIONS TO ACCESS

2.1 No access is provisioned before all of the following are complete:

    (a) an executed data privacy agreement incorporating the District's standard terms;
    (b) a completed data inventory schedule enumerating, by category and by field label, every element the vendor will access, store or transmit;
    (c) evidence of current background screening for each individual requiring access, where the engagement involves Categories B, C, D or E;
    (d) written designation of the vendor's own data protection contact by role title;
    (e) approval by the Records Custodian and by the sponsoring District department head.

3. LIMITS ON ACCESS

3.1 Vendor access is limited strictly to the categories and field labels listed in the data inventory schedule. Any expansion requires an amended schedule and fresh approval.

3.2 Bulk export is prohibited absent express written authorization specifying the categories, field labels, date range and destination.

3.3 Vendors shall not use District personal information for product development, benchmarking, model training, marketing or any purpose beyond the contracted service.

3.4 Subcontracting of any function involving access requires prior written approval and flows down every condition in this bulletin.

3.5 Storage outside the United States is prohibited without written approval from the Superintendent.

4. TERM AND TERMINATION

4.1 Vendor accounts are provisioned with an expiration date matching the contract term and are reviewed at each quarter's end.

4.2 Within thirty days of contract termination or expiry, the vendor shall return or destroy all District personal information and furnish a certificate of destruction identifying the categories, field labels and date ranges covered.

4.3 The District may audit vendor compliance on ten business days' notice.

5. INCIDENTS

5.1 The vendor shall notify the Records Custodian within twenty-four hours of discovering any unauthorized access, disclosure or loss, and shall provide a written account within five business days identifying the categories and field labels involved and the number of individuals affected.

6. ENFORCEMENT

6.1 Failure to meet any condition in this bulletin is grounds for immediate suspension of access and is a material breach of the data privacy agreement.
