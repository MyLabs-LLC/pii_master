TRANSHOLD CARRIAGE SERVICES LIMITED

STANDARD 11: CLASSIFICATION AND HANDLING OF PERSONAL RECORDS

Issue 4. Replaces Issue 3 in its entirety. Distribution: controlled copies 1 to 14.

1. Classification

  (a) Personal records are classified as Restricted, Confidential, or Confidential — Medical in Confidence.

  (b) Restricted records comprise: Site Access Log (fields: Pass Number, Surname, Given Names, Vehicle Registration Mark, Time of Entry, Time of Exit) and Internal Directory (fields: Surname, Given Names, Job Title, Extension Number, Location Code).

  (c) Confidential records comprise: Personnel File (fields: Surname, Given Names, Payroll Number, Home Address, Home Telephone Number, Date of Birth, National Insurance Number, Next of Kin Name, Next of Kin Telephone Number); Payroll File (fields: Payroll Number, Tax Reference, Tax Code, Bank Sort Code, Bank Account Number); Driver File (fields: Payroll Number, Driver Licence Number, Endorsement Codes, Licence Expiry Date, Tachograph Card Number); Recruitment File (fields: Applicant Reference, Surname, Given Names, Correspondence Address, Referee Name, Referee Address).

  (d) Confidential — Medical in Confidence records comprise the Health Surveillance File (fields: Payroll Number, Referral Reference, Assessment Outcome Code, Restriction Code, Review Date).

2. Handling

  (a) Restricted records may be held in unlocked furniture within a controlled area but shall not leave the site.

  (b) Confidential records shall be held in locked furniture and returned to that furniture at the close of each working day.

  (c) Confidential — Medical in Confidence records shall be held in a separate locked container to which only the Occupational Health Adviser and the Records Custodian hold keys. These records shall not be copied.

  (d) Transmission of Confidential records by facsimile is permitted only where the receiving machine is attended and the receipt is confirmed by telephone.

3. Access

  (a) Access is by function, not by grade. The functions permitted access to each classification are listed on the Access Schedule held by the Records Custodian.

  (b) Requests from outside the Company shall be referred to the Company Secretary without exception, and shall not be answered by the receiving department.

4. Retention and disposal

  (a) Restricted records: three months from creation.

  (b) Confidential records: six years from the date of leaving, save for the Recruitment File in respect of unsuccessful applicants, which is six months from the selection decision.

  (c) Confidential — Medical in Confidence records: forty years from the last entry.

  (d) Disposal of Restricted records is by shredding at departmental level. Disposal of all Confidential records is by supervised shredding or incineration and is recorded in the Destruction Register under the headings: Classification, Record Range, Method, Date, Witnesses, Certificate Number.

5. Departure from this Standard requires the prior written approval of the Records Custodian, which shall be time-limited and recorded as a numbered concession.
