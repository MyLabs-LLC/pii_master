COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
OFFICE OF THE RECORDS CUSTODIAN

ACCESS AUTHORIZATION SCHEDULE AAS-3
Adopted 22 May 1998. Reviewed annually.

A. BASIS
   1. This schedule implements Section 3 of Standard RA-12 and fixes the permission level attaching to each role for each category of identifiable content.
   2. Permission levels are defined as follows:
      a. Level 1 - View of the registration face sheet only.
      b. Level 2 - View of the full record within the category.
      c. Level 3 - View and entry.
      d. Level 4 - View, entry, correction and authority to release content outside the Department.

B. SCHEDULE BY ROLE
   1. Registration Clerk
      a. Category A, Level 3.
      b. Category D, Level 1.
   2. Public Health Nurse
      a. Category A, Level 2.
      b. Category B, Level 3.
      c. Category C, Level 1.
   3. Clinic Supervisor
      a. Categories A and B, Level 3.
      b. Category D, Level 2.
   4. Communicable Disease Investigator
      a. Category A, Level 2.
      b. Category C, Level 3.
   5. Eligibility Technician
      a. Category A, Level 1.
      b. Category D, Level 3.
   6. Fiscal Officer
      a. Category D, Level 4.
   7. Personnel Officer
      a. Category E, Level 4.
   8. Environmental Health Supervisor
      a. Category F, Level 3.
   9. Department Counsel
      a. All categories, Level 2, on written statement of need entered in the Access Authorization Register.
   10. Health Officer
      a. All categories, Level 4.
   11. Records Custodian
      a. All categories, Level 4, for custody, inventory and disposition purposes only.

C. GRANT AND WITHDRAWAL
   1. A grant takes effect on the date the Division Director signs the authorization block and the Records Custodian countersigns.
   2. Withdrawal is immediate upon separation, transfer, or suspension of the holder of the role, and is recorded in the Revocation Date column of the Access Authorization Register the same working day.
   3. Terminal passwords are reissued whenever a Level 4 authorization is withdrawn.

D. AUDIT
   1. The Records Custodian reconciles the Access Authorization Register against the current position roster each quarter.
   2. Terminal access reports are examined for entries falling outside authorized categories. Exceptions are reported to the Division Director concerned and to the Health Officer.
   3. The reconciliation worksheet bears the columns: Role Title; Positions Filled; Authorizations Active; Authorizations Withdrawn; Exceptions Noted; Date of Reconciliation.

E. RETENTION OF THIS SCHEDULE AND ITS RECORDS
   1. Superseded editions of this schedule: retained ten years.
   2. Access Authorization Register entries: retained six years from the Revocation Date, then destroyed by cross-cut shredding under Standing Instruction SI-7.
   3. Quarterly reconciliation worksheets: retained three years.
