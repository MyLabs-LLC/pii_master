COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
OPERATING PROCEDURE OP-7.3 — PERSONAL INFORMATION INVENTORY AND DISPOSAL
Rev. 1, effective 4 October 2021. Owner: Office of the Privacy Officer.

1  INVENTORY OF CATEGORIES
   - Category 1: Registration and Identity
       - Labels: Last Name, First Name, Middle Name, Preferred Name,
         Patient Identifier, Household Identifier, Photograph on File Indicator.
   - Category 2: Locator Attributes
       - Labels: Street Address, Apartment or Unit, Municipality, Postal Code,
         Mobile Telephone Number, Alternate Telephone Number, Electronic Mail
         Address, Preferred Contact Method.
   - Category 3: Sensitive Special Categories
       - Labels: Communicable Disease Status, Substance Use Treatment Indicator,
         Reproductive Health Service Indicator, Genetic Test Result,
         Biometric Template Reference, Immigration Status Indicator.
   - Category 4: Program Eligibility and Finance
       - Labels: Income Verification Type, Household Size, Benefit Program Code,
         Subscriber Identification Number, Copayment Amount Due.
   - Category 5: Consent and Disclosure Tracking
       - Labels: Authorization Form Type, Authorization Expiration Date,
         Revocation Indicator, Disclosure Recipient Class, Purpose of Disclosure.

2  ACCESS
   - Categories 1, 2, 5: Front Desk Registration staff (create and amend only);
     the Health Information Management Supervisor (full).
   - Category 3: restricted to the treating clinician of record, the Communicable
     Disease Program Manager, and the Privacy Officer. Access generates a
     mandatory audit entry reviewed monthly.
   - Category 4: the Eligibility Determination Specialist and the Fiscal Officer.
   - No contractor role is granted Category 3 access.

3  RETENTION
   - Categories 1 through 3: ten years from last service date; minors, age of
     majority plus ten years.
   - Category 4: five years from determination or benefit end, whichever is later.
   - Category 5: six years from the later of the authorization date or expiration.

4  DISPOSAL
   - Step 1: Records Custodian produces the eligibility listing.
   - Step 2: Program Manager confirms no active hold, in writing.
   - Step 3: Paper placed in locked destruction consoles; consoles are emptied
     only in the presence of a Department witness.
   - Step 4: Electronic records purged from production, replicas, and backup
     media at the next scheduled sweep.
   - Step 5: Entry made in the Disposal Register: Series, Category, Volume,
     Method, Date, Witness Role, Certificate Number. Register retained permanently.
