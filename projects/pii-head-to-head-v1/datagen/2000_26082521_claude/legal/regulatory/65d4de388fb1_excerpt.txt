ADMINISTRATIVE RULES OF THE DEPARTMENT OF FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS
RULE 4-19 — IDENTIFIER CATEGORIES AND SAFEGUARD TIERS
(promulgated under Title 12 § 402.302; excerpt)

4-19-1.  SCOPE.
This rule classifies data elements held by a Covered Entity and prescribes the
minimum safeguard applicable to each classification. Classification is by field
label, not by the content of any individual entry.

4-19-3.  MINIMUM SAFEGUARD BY TIER.

    TIER 1 .... Access on business need; no encryption mandated.
    TIER 2 .... Role-based access; encryption in transit.
    TIER 3 .... Role-based access; encryption in transit and at rest.
    TIER 4 .... Tier 3, plus masking in all screens, reports, and extracts;
                dual authorisation for bulk export; access logging retained
                24 months.
    TIER 5 .... Tier 4, plus prohibition on export to any medium not under
                the direct control of the Covered Entity.

4-19-4.  COMBINATION RULE.
Where two or more categories appear in the same record set, the higher tier
governs the whole set. A field label may not be reclassified downward absent
written approval of the Records Custodian, recorded in the classification
exception register.
