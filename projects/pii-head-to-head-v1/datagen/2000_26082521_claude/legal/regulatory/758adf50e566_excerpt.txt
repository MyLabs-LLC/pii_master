205.115  CIVIL PENALTY.

    (i)    A record holder that fails to comply with Sections 205.107
           through 205.110 is subject to a civil penalty assessed by the
           Attorney General as follows:

           First violation ..................................... not to exceed $2,500
           Second violation within 24 months ................... not to exceed $10,000
           Each subsequent violation within 24 months .......... not to exceed $25,000

    (ii)   Each affected system of records constitutes a separate violation.
           Multiple failures within a single system arising from the same act
           or omission shall be treated as one violation.

    (iii)  A penalty shall not be assessed where the record holder demonstrates
           that the failure was not intentional and resulted from a bona fide
           error made notwithstanding the maintenance of procedures reasonably
           adapted to avoid such error.

205.116  KNOWING DISCLOSURE; MISDEMEANOR.

    An officer or employee of an agency who, knowing that disclosure is
    prohibited, willfully discloses personal information to any person not
    entitled to receive it is guilty of a misdemeanor and, upon conviction,
    shall be fined not more than $5,000, imprisoned for not more than one year,
    or both. Willful disclosure of any Category I field for pecuniary gain is
    punishable as a felony of the fourth degree.

205.117  ADMINISTRATIVE SANCTIONS.

    Nothing in this subchapter limits the authority of an agency to impose
    discipline, up to and including dismissal, upon an employee for a violation
    of this chapter or of any policy adopted under it.

205.118  PRIVATE RIGHT OF ACTION; LIMITATION.

    An individual injured by a violation of this chapter may bring an action for
    actual damages and reasonable attorney fees. An action shall be commenced
    within two years of the date the violation was discovered or reasonably
    should have been discovered. This chapter creates no right of action against
    an individual employee acting within the scope of assigned duties.
