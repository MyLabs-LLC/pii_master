PART 121 — CONFIDENTIALITY OF PERSONALLY IDENTIFIABLE INFORMATION
MAINTAINED BY LOCAL EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES

§ 121.1  Scope and application.
This part applies to every local educational agency receiving funds administered by the Department, and to every contractor, evaluator, research organization, or data processing service acting on behalf of such an agency.

§ 121.2  Definitions.
 (a) Personally identifiable information includes, but is not limited to —
  (1) The name of the pupil, the pupil's parent, or another family member;
  (2) The address of the pupil or of the pupil's family;
  (3) A personal identifier, such as the pupil's social security number, state-assigned pupil identification number, or locally assigned file number;
  (4) A list of personal characteristics, including height, weight, hair color, eye color, and distinguishing marks, that would make the pupil's identity easily traceable; and
  (5) Other information that would make the pupil's identity easily traceable.
 (b) Disclosure means to permit access to, or the release, transfer, or other communication of, a record by any means, including oral, written, electronic, facsimile, and microform.
 (c) Record means any information recorded in any way, including handwriting, print, tape, film, microfilm, microfiche, and machine-readable media.

§ 121.3  Obligations of the agency.
 (a) Each agency shall designate in writing an official, by position title, responsible for ensuring the confidentiality of personally identifiable information.
 (b) Each agency shall maintain, for public inspection, a current listing of the position titles of all employees within the agency who may have access to personally identifiable information, together with the record series each such position is authorized to access.
 (c) Each agency shall provide instruction to all persons collecting or using personally identifiable information regarding the requirements of this part and of applicable State law. A record of instruction shall be kept showing the date of instruction and the position titles of attendees.
 (d) Except as provided in § 121.4, an agency shall obtain the written consent of the parent, or of the pupil upon attaining the age of majority, before disclosing personally identifiable information to a party other than an official of a participating agency. The consent instrument shall specify the records to be disclosed, the purpose of the disclosure, and the party to whom the disclosure may be made.

§ 121.5  Destruction of information.
 (a) The agency shall inform the parent when personally identifiable information collected, maintained, or used under this part is no longer needed to provide educational services to the pupil.
 (b) The information shall be destroyed at the request of the parent, except that a permanent record of the pupil's name, address, telephone listing, grades, attendance record, classes attended, grade level completed, and year completed may be maintained without time limitation.

§ 121.6  Enforcement.
The Department may withhold payments, in whole or in part, to any agency that fails to comply with this part. A finding of noncompliance shall be issued in writing, shall state the provisions violated, and shall afford the agency not less than thirty days to submit a corrective action plan.
