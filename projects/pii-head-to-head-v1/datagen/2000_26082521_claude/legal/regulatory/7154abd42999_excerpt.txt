================================================================================

================================================================================

 SEC. 8.42.010  CLASSIFICATION OF IDENTIFIERS

 CODE  IDENTIFIER CLASS      FIELD LABEL IN CLASS            RETENTION  ACCESS
 ----  --------------------  ------------------------------  ---------  ------
 A     Nominal               Full Legal Name                 7 years    LVL 1
 A     Nominal               Former or Maiden Name           7 years    LVL 1
 B     Locational            Residence Address               7 years    LVL 2
 B     Locational            Telephone Number                3 years    LVL 2
 C     Numerical             Social Security Number          4 years    LVL 3
 C     Numerical             Driver License Number           4 years    LVL 3
 D     Personal              Date of Birth                   4 years    LVL 3
 D     Personal              Emergency Contact Name          2 years    LVL 3
 E     Financial             Bank Routing Designation        4 years    LVL 4
 E     Financial             Payroll Account Number          4 years    LVL 4
 ----  --------------------  ------------------------------  ---------  ------

 SEC. 8.42.020  The Records Custodian shall hold the sole key to Class C, D and
 E files, shall verify the Access Class of each requester before release, and
 shall purge each class on the retention interval shown above.

 SEC. 8.42.030  SCHEDULE OF PENALTIES

 ITEM  VIOLATION                       FIRST        SECOND       SUBSEQUENT
 ----  ------------------------------  -----------  -----------  ------------
 1     Failure to designate Custodian  $250         $500         $1,000
 2     Storage outside secured area    $250         $750         $1,500
 3     Disclosure without authority    $500         $1,500       $3,000 + susp.
 4     Failure to purge on schedule    $100         $300         $750
 5     Refusal of subject inspection   $250         $500         $1,000
 ----  ------------------------------  -----------  -----------  ------------

 Penalties cumulate per record. Appeal lies to the Hearing Officer within
 twenty days of service of the citation.
