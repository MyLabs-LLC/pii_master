OFFICE OF ADMINISTRATIVE RULES
TITLE 114, CHAPTER 8 -- SAFEGUARDING OF PERSONAL INFORMATION BY EMPLOYERS
RULE 114-8-.03  IDENTIFIER SCHEDULE; CLASSIFICATION; MINIMUM RETENTION
Authority: § 21-4-102(d).

(1) Every covered holder shall classify each element of personal identifying
    information in its custody according to the schedule set out in subsection
    (3). Classification shall govern the storage, transmission, and destruction
    of the record in which the element appears.

(2) Class I elements shall be stored only in a locked receptacle or in an
    access-controlled electronic repository; Class II elements may be stored in
    the general personnel file; Class III elements shall be segregated from the
    general personnel file and shall not be disclosed to a client except upon
    the written authorization of the individual to whom they pertain.

(3) Schedule of identifiers:

(4) The minimum retention period stated in the schedule runs from the date of
    separation, or from the date of final action upon an application, whichever
    is later. Upon expiration the holder shall dispose of the element in the
    manner prescribed by Rule 114-8-.05.
