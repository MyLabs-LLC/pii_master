ARTICLE 7 — SAFEGUARDING OF NONPUBLIC PERSONAL INFORMATION

§ 7-101. Definitions.
As used in this Article:
(a) "Licensee" means any person or entity licensed, authorized, or registered under the Insurance Code, including an insurer, producer, third-party administrator, or independent adjuster.
(b) "Individual" means a natural person who is a policyholder, applicant, insured, member, claimant, or beneficiary.
(c) "Nonpublic personal information" means personally identifiable financial information and personally identifiable health information, together with any list, description, or grouping derived therefrom that is not lawfully made available to the general public.
(d) "Personal identifier" means any of the following data elements when maintained in association with an individual's name: social security number; taxpayer identification number; date of birth; driver's license or state identification number; financial account number; credit or debit card number, alone or in combination with any required security code, access code, or password; member or subscriber identification number; policy or certificate number; claim number; and medical record number.
(e) "Record" means any material, regardless of physical form or characteristic, on which information is recorded or preserved, including paper, microform, magnetic media, optical media, and electronic files.
(f) "Holder of records" means a licensee, or an affiliate or service provider acting for a licensee, that maintains custody or control of records containing nonpublic personal information.

§ 7-102. General obligations of a holder of records.
(a) A holder of records shall implement and maintain a comprehensive written information security program appropriate to (1) the size and complexity of the holder, (2) the nature and scope of its activities, and (3) the sensitivity of the information maintained.
(b) The program required by subsection (a) shall be reviewed not less than annually and following any material change in business practice affecting the collection, use, or retention of nonpublic personal information.
(c) The holder shall designate one or more employees to coordinate the program. The designation shall be recorded in the minutes of the governing body or in a written delegation maintained with the program documentation.

§ 7-103. Administrative, technical, and physical safeguards.
(a) Access to records shall be restricted to those persons whose duties reasonably require access.
(b) Records maintained in electronic form shall be protected by access controls that uniquely identify each authorized user and that log each instance of access to a personal identifier field.
(c) Records maintained in paper or microform shall be stored in locked containers, or in areas secured against entry by unauthorized persons during non-business hours.
(d) Transmission of a personal identifier across a public network shall employ encryption or an equivalent control approved by the Commissioner by rule.

§ 7-104. Limitation on disclosure.
No holder of records shall disclose nonpublic personal information to a nonaffiliated third party except (1) as necessary to effect, administer, or enforce a transaction requested or authorized by the individual; (2) pursuant to the written authorization of the individual, which authorization shall specify the classes of information to be disclosed and the period of its validity; (3) to a regulatory authority in the exercise of lawful oversight; or (4) as otherwise required by law.
