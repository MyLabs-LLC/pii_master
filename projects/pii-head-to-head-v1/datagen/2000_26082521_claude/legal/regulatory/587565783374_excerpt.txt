MEMORANDUM

TO:       Deputy Director, Administration
FROM:     The Records Custodian
DATE:     September 9, 2004
SUBJECT:  Penalty and enforcement provisions — excerpt, Personal Information
          Protection Act, Article 9, as requested for the compliance file

     Sec. 9-201. Civil penalties; assessment.
       (a) The Commissioner, upon a finding that a holder of records has
     disclosed personal information otherwise than as permitted by Section
     7-102(c), may assess a civil penalty in an amount not exceeding two thousand
     five hundred dollars for each occurrence, and, where the disclosure was made
     for pecuniary gain or with intent to cause harm to the subject of the
     record, in an amount not exceeding ten thousand dollars for each occurrence.
       (b) In determining the amount of the penalty, the Commissioner shall
     consider the nature and number of the identifier categories disclosed, the
     number of subjects affected, whether the holder of records reported the
     occurrence in accordance with the applicable rule, the adequacy of the
     safeguards then in effect, and any prior finding entered against the covered
     agency within the preceding thirty-six months.
       (c) A penalty assessed under this section shall be in addition to, and not
     in lieu of, any criminal penalty imposed under Section 7-103 and any
     discipline imposed under the personnel rules of the covered agency.

     Sec. 9-202. Liability of the covered agency.
     A covered agency shall be liable for a penalty assessed against a holder of
     records acting within the scope of official duty, unless the agency
     demonstrates that it had adopted and enforced safeguards conforming to the
     applicable rule and that the act complained of was contrary to instruction.

     Sec. 9-203. Notice and hearing.
     No penalty shall be assessed until the covered agency has been served with
     written notice specifying the occurrence, the provision alleged to have been
     violated, and the penalty proposed, and has been afforded not fewer than
     thirty days within which to request a hearing.

The compliance file should note that Section 9-202 conditions agency liability upon the enforcement, and not merely the adoption, of safeguards. This office recommends that the annual acknowledgment required by Section 7-102(b)(iv) and the access log be preserved for the full thirty-six month lookback period specified in Section 9-201(b).
