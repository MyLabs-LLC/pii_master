TITLE 6 — EDUCATION RECORDS
CHAPTER 41. PROTECTION OF PERSONALLY IDENTIFIABLE INFORMATION HELD BY LOCAL EDUCATION AGENCIES
Adopted by the State Board of Education under delegated rulemaking authority. Effective July 1, 2021.

§ 41.101. Definitions. As used in this chapter:
- "Covered record" means any record, file, register, log, image, or discrete data element maintained by or on behalf of a local education agency, in any medium, that is capable of being associated with an enrolled or formerly enrolled student.
- "Holder of records" means the local education agency, its schools, its contracted service providers, and any subcontractor thereof.
- "Records Custodian" means the employee designated under § 41.103(a) as accountable for compliance with this chapter.
- "Direct identifier" means a field that identifies a natural person without reference to other data.
- "Indirect identifier" means a field that, alone or in combination, permits identification of a natural person in a reasonably small population.
- "Sensitive category identifier" means a field describing health, disability, discipline, benefit eligibility, or legal status.

§ 41.102. Identifier categories. The following field labels, wherever occurring in a covered record, are identifiers for purposes of this chapter:
- Direct identifier fields: Student Full Name; Parent or Guardian Name; Social Security Number; State Student Identification Number; Local Student Identification Number; Residential Street Address; Home Telephone Number; Personal Electronic Mail Address; Biometric Template Reference.
- Indirect identifier fields: Date of Birth; Place of Birth; Grade Level; Homeroom Assignment; Transportation Route Number; Photograph or Video Still; Enrollment Entry Date; Enrollment Exit Date; Parent Employer Name.
- Sensitive category identifier fields: Individualized Education Program Status; Disability Classification Code; Section 504 Plan Indicator; Free and Reduced Price Meal Eligibility; Immunization Record; Health Condition Code; Medication Administration Authorization; Disciplinary Action Type; Custody Order Flag; Homeless or Foster Care Status Indicator.

§ 41.103. Obligations on holders of records. Each holder of records shall:
- Designate a Records Custodian in writing and file the designation with the Superintendent and the Department not later than thirty (30) days after the position is filled or vacated.
- Maintain a current data inventory recording, for each system of records: System Name; Owning Department; Field Labels Collected; Lawful Basis for Collection; Retention Period; Disposition Method; Hosting Location.
- Restrict access to the minimum necessary, granted by role rather than by individual, and recertify all role assignments not less than annually.
- Require every employee, volunteer, and contractor with access to complete confidentiality training before access is provisioned and annually thereafter.
- Execute a written data protection addendum with each service provider prohibiting secondary use, requiring deletion or return at contract termination, and forbidding onward transfer without written authorization.
- Encrypt covered records at rest and in transit using algorithms then recognized by the Department; prohibit storage on unmanaged removable media.
- Maintain a disclosure log containing the columns: Date of Disclosure; Requesting Party Category; Legal Basis; Field Labels Released; Approving Official Title; Method of Transmission. Retain the log for five (5) years.
- Dispose of covered records by cross-cut shredding, pulping, incineration, or media sanitization conforming to Department guidance, and record the disposition on a Certificate of Destruction identifying the record series and volume only.

§ 41.104. Penalties.
- A civil penalty of not more than $2,500 per violation, and not more than $50,000 in the aggregate for violations arising from a single course of conduct, may be assessed by the Department.
- Each day a required designation, inventory, or log remains absent or materially incomplete constitutes a separate violation.
- A holder assessed two or more penalties within thirty-six (36) months is ineligible for state data-system grants for five (5) years.
- Willful unauthorized disclosure by an employee is grounds for suspension, dismissal, and referral for certificate revocation, in addition to any liability imposed by general law.
