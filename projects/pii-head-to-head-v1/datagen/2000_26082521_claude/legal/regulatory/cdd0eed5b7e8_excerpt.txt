MEMORANDUM — ENFORCEMENT PROVISIONS
Excerpt, Article 11, Sections 8 and 9, with the schedule adopted thereunder

SECTION 8. PENALTIES.

  (a) Any holder of records who knowingly and willfully discloses personal
  information in violation of this Article, or who knowingly and willfully
  requests or obtains such information under false pretenses, shall be guilty of
  a misdemeanor and, upon conviction, shall be fined not more than one thousand
  dollars for each such disclosure, or imprisoned not more than one year, or
  both.

  (b) Any holder of records who, through negligence, permits personal
  information to be accessed by a person not entitled thereto shall be subject to
  such disciplinary action as the governing board may impose, up to and including
  dismissal, and shall be subject to the civil penalties set forth in the
  schedule below.

  (c) The penalties prescribed by this Section are cumulative and are in addition
  to, and not in lieu of, any remedy available to an aggrieved individual at law
  or in equity.
