STATE ADMINISTRATIVE CODE — TITLE 45, HEALTH INFORMATION PRACTICES
Chapter 7, Subchapter C: Safeguards Applicable to Billing and Collection Units
§ 7.402 Minimum Compliance Actions — Office Copy, Patient Financial Services

Posting required. Do not remove from the department binder.

  [ ] 7.402(a) The facility shall designate in writing a Records Custodian for each unit that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits protected information in the course of billing.

  [ ] 7.402(b) The Records Custodian shall maintain a current inventory of every system, ledger, worklist, and paper file in which protected information is held, indexed by system name and data steward role.

  [ ] 7.402(c) Access to billing records shall be granted on the basis of assigned role only, and shall be reviewed by the Records Custodian not less than once each calendar quarter.

  [ ] 7.402(d) Statements, remittance advices, and collection notices shall display the minimum identifier set necessary to effect payment, and shall not display the Social Security Number in whole or in truncated form.

  [ ] 7.402(e) Facsimile and electronic transmission of protected information shall be preceded by verification of the recipient endpoint against the approved-recipient register.

  [ ] 7.402(f) Records scheduled for destruction shall be destroyed by a method rendering reconstruction infeasible, and the Records Custodian shall retain a certificate of destruction for six years.

  [ ] 7.402(g) Each workforce member with billing system access shall complete annual instruction in this Subchapter, and attendance shall be recorded by role title and employee number field.
