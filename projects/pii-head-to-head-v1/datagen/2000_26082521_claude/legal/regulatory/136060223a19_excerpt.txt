ARTICLE VII
DISCLOSURE, CONSENT, AND THE RECORD OF ACCESS

Sec. 7.01. Consent Required. Except as this Article otherwise provides, no district shall disclose personally identifiable information contained in the education record of a pupil to any person, agency, or organization without the prior written consent of the parent or of the eligible pupil. Such consent shall specify the records to be disclosed, shall state the purpose of the disclosure, shall identify the party or class of parties to whom the disclosure may be made, and shall bear the signature and date of execution of the consenting party. A consent obtained by means of a form which does not conform to the requirements of this Section shall be of no force or effect, and a disclosure made in reliance thereupon shall be deemed an unauthorized disclosure for all purposes of this Code.

Sec. 7.02. Exceptions. Consent shall not be required where the disclosure is made to a school official of the district having a legitimate educational interest in the record; to officials of another school or postsecondary institution in which the pupil seeks or intends to enroll, upon condition that the parent or eligible pupil be notified of the transfer and afforded an opportunity to receive a copy and to challenge the contents thereof; to authorized representatives of the Comptroller General, the Attorney General, the Secretary of Education, or state educational authorities in connection with an audit or evaluation of a federally or state supported program; in connection with financial assistance for which the pupil has applied; to organizations conducting studies for or on behalf of the district, pursuant to a written agreement restricting redisclosure and requiring destruction of the information upon completion of the study; to accrediting organizations in carrying out accrediting functions; to a parent of a dependent pupil as that term is defined in the Internal Revenue Code; in compliance with a judicial order or lawfully issued subpoena, upon reasonable prior notice to the parent or eligible pupil except where such notice is prohibited by the issuing authority; or in connection with a health or safety emergency where knowledge of the information is necessary to protect the health or safety of the pupil or other persons.

Sec. 7.03. Record of Access. Each district shall maintain, with the education record of each pupil, a record of each request for access to and each disclosure of personally identifiable information from that record. The record of access shall be maintained for so long as the education record itself is maintained, and shall be available for inspection by the parent, the eligible pupil, the Records Custodian, and any officer authorized by law to audit the district's compliance with this Code. The record of access shall be kept upon a form containing the following columns and no others: Sequence Number; Date of Request; Date of Disclosure; Requesting Party (Name and Title); Organization Represented; Legitimate Interest Asserted; Elements Disclosed; Method of Transmission; Authorizing Official; Redisclosure Permitted (Y/N); and Notification to Parent (Date). Entries shall be made contemporaneously in ink, and no entry shall be erased, obliterated, or removed; corrections shall be made by a single ruled line through the erroneous entry, initialed and dated by the person making the correction.

Sec. 7.04. Limitation on Redisclosure. A party to whom personally identifiable information has been disclosed under Section 7.02 shall not redisclose that information to any other party without the prior written consent of the parent or eligible pupil, except that a party may redisclose on behalf of the district where the district has recorded the intended recipients and the legitimate interests of each in the record of access maintained under Section 7.03. A district which learns that a recipient has redisclosed information in violation of this Section shall prohibit access by that recipient to personally identifiable information from education records for a period of not less than five years.

Sec. 7.05. Penalty. A district which is found, after notice and an opportunity for hearing, to have a policy or practice of permitting the release of education records in violation of this Article shall be subject to suspension of eligibility for state categorical funding until compliance is demonstrated, and, where the violation is willful, to a civil penalty not exceeding ten thousand dollars per occurrence assessed against the district and payable to the general fund.
