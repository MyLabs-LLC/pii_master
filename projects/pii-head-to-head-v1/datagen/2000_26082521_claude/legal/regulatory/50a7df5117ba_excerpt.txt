TITLE 22. DEPARTMENT OF COMMUNITY SERVICES
CHAPTER 8. SAFEGUARDING OF PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD BY MUNICIPAL ENTERPRISE FUNDS

    RULE 22-8.01  SCOPE.

        (A) This chapter applies to every municipal enterprise fund that furnishes
            water, sewer, electric, gas, sanitation, or stormwater service, and to every
            third party performing billing, collection, meter reading, credit reporting,
            or data processing services on behalf of such a fund under written contract.

        (B) Compliance with this chapter does not relieve a Holder of any obligation
            imposed by Title 14 (Public Records) or Title 31 (Records Retention).

    RULE 22-8.02  DEFINITIONS.

        As used in this chapter, unless the context requires otherwise:

        (A) "Holder" means any municipal enterprise fund, or agent thereof, that
            collects, maintains, transmits, or destroys a Protected Record.

        (B) "Protected Record" means any record containing one or more Category I
            identifiers, or containing a Category II identifier in combination with a
            Category III identifier.

        (C) "Category I identifier" means:

            (1) Social Security Number or any truncation thereof exceeding four digits;
            (2) Taxpayer Identification Number;
            (3) Financial Account Number, including Depositor Account Number, Bank
                Routing Number, Credit Card Number, and Card Expiration Date;
            (4) Driver's License Number or State Identification Card Number;
            (5) Password, Personal Identification Number, or Security Answer used to
                authenticate a customer by telephone or at a walk-in station.

        (D) "Category II identifier" means:

            (1) Customer Name, in any of the forms Last Name, First Name, Middle
                Initial, or Name of Business Entity;
            (2) Service Address or Mailing Address;
            (3) Telephone Number;
            (4) Utility Account Number.

        (E) "Category III identifier" means:

            (1) Date of Birth;
            (2) Mother's Maiden Name;
            (3) Employer Name and Employment Status;
            (4) Medical Certification Code submitted in support of a life-support or
                medical-necessity service designation;
            (5) Income Verification Code submitted in support of an assistance rate.

        (F) "Records Custodian" means the officer designated in writing by the governing
            body to discharge the duties imposed by Rule 22-8.03.

    RULE 22-8.03  DUTIES OF THE HOLDER.

        (A) Designation. Each Holder shall designate a Records Custodian and shall file
            the designation with the Department within thirty (30) days of appointment or
            vacancy.

        (B) Inventory. The Records Custodian shall maintain a current inventory of
            systems containing Protected Records, showing for each system:

            (1) System Name and Responsible Division;
            (2) Field Name and Identifier Category for each field;
            (3) Media Type and Storage Location;
            (4) Retention Period and Authorized Disposal Method.

        (C) Access. Access to Protected Records shall be limited to employees whose
            assigned duties require such access. The Records Custodian shall review the
            access authorization roster not less than semiannually and shall revoke
            authorization within one (1) business day of separation or transfer.

        (D) Transmission. A Category I identifier shall not be transmitted by facsimile
            to an unattended device, nor printed on the exterior of any mailed item,
            nor used as an account number, customer number, or login identifier.

        (E) Contractors. No Protected Record shall be furnished to a third party except
            under a written agreement containing the confidentiality provisions
            prescribed in Appendix B.

    RULE 22-8.04  PENALTIES.

        (A) A Holder that violates this chapter is subject to an administrative penalty
            of not more than five hundred dollars ($500) for each violation, each day of
            continuing violation constituting a separate offense.

        (B) An employee who knowingly discloses a Protected Record for purposes not
            authorized by this chapter is subject to disciplinary action up to and
            including dismissal, and, where the disclosure was made for pecuniary gain,
            to referral to the prosecuting attorney under § 44-19 of the State Code.
