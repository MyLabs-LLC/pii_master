NOTES TO THE SCHEDULE

  1. Classification. Class A denotes an identifier the disclosure of which,
     standing alone, is sufficient to identify the individual and to expose
     that individual to a risk of financial detriment. Class B denotes an
     identifier sufficient to identify the individual when combined with any
     other item of this schedule. Class C denotes an identifier of a device,
     network resource, or conveyance associated with the individual.

  2. Masking. Where a Class A identifier appears upon a statement, remittance
     advice, coupon, envelope window, or collection notice, the holder shall
     cause all but the terminal four characters of the field to be suppressed,
     and shall not employ any Class A identifier as the sole reference number
     of a statement mailed to an address of record.

  3. Retention. The periods stated in the fourth column run from the date of
     final disposition of the account. Upon expiration, the Records Custodian
     shall cause the record to be destroyed in the manner prescribed by
     § 4-107 and shall enter the fact of destruction upon the disposal log,
     which log shall show the field labels destroyed, the method employed,
     the date, and the signature of the witnessing employee, but shall not
     reproduce the contents of any field.

  4. Amendment. The Department may add to, or remove from, this schedule any
     category of data upon publication of notice in the Register and the
     expiration of sixty days thereafter.
