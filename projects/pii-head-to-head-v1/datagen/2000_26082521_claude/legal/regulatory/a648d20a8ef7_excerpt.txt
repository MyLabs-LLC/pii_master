ADMINISTRATIVE CODE OF THE DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION
Part 47 — Confidentiality of Pupil and Personnel Data
Subpart C — Duties of the Custodian; Inventory and Classification

§ 47.30 Designation of the Records Custodian.

(a) The governing board of each district shall, by resolution entered upon its minutes at the organizational meeting held annually, designate one officer to serve as Records Custodian and one officer to serve as Alternate Records Custodian. The resolution shall identify the designees by position title only and shall be transmitted to the Department within thirty (30) days of adoption. No designation shall be effective until the designee has executed and filed with the district's central office the acknowledgment of duties prescribed by § 47.36.

(b) The Records Custodian shall exercise general supervision over all repositories of personally identifiable information maintained by or on behalf of the district, whether such repositories are situated in the central administrative office, in a school building, in the office of a contracted service provider, or upon computing infrastructure leased or licensed from a vendor. Supervision under this subsection is not delegable in whole, but the Custodian may appoint building-level designees to perform ministerial functions under the Custodian's direction and subject to the Custodian's review.

§ 47.31 Data Inventory.

(a) Not later than the fifteenth day of September in each year, the Records Custodian shall cause to be prepared, and shall certify as accurate to the best of the Custodian's knowledge and belief, a written inventory of every system of records maintained by the district. The inventory shall be arranged by system and shall set forth, with respect to each system, the following particulars:

(1) The designation of the system and the operating unit having primary responsibility therefor;

(2) The statutory or regulatory authority for the collection and maintenance of the information;

(3) The categories of individuals concerning whom records are maintained, stated by class and not by individual, such as "enrolled pupils," "applicants for enrollment," "parents and guardians of record," "certificated personnel," "classified personnel," "applicants for employment," "substitute personnel," "volunteers subject to background clearance," "contractors and their assigned personnel," and "recipients of vendor payments";

(4) The categories of data elements maintained within the system, stated by field label, which labels shall include as applicable: Surname; Given Name; Middle Initial; Preferred Name; Residence Address Line 1; Residence Address Line 2; Mailing Address; Municipality; State; Postal Code; Home Telephone; Mobile Telephone; Work Telephone; Electronic Mail Address; Date of Birth; Place of Birth; Gender Code; Race and Ethnicity Codes; Home Language Code; State Assigned Student Identifier; Local Student Number; Grade Level; Homeroom Assignment; Enrollment Date; Withdrawal Date and Reason Code; Special Education Eligibility Category; Section 504 Plan Indicator; Free and Reduced Price Meal Eligibility Status; Immunization Compliance Status; Health Condition Codes; Medication Administration Authorization; Emergency Contact Name; Emergency Contact Relationship; Emergency Contact Telephone; Custody Restriction Indicator; Bus Route Number; Employee Number; Position Control Number; Bargaining Unit Code; Hire Date; Certification Number and Expiration; Social Security Number; Direct Deposit Routing Number; Direct Deposit Account Number; Federal Withholding Allowances; Retirement System Membership Number; and Health Plan Subscriber Number;

(5) The medium of storage and the physical location of the primary and backup copies;

(6) The retention period prescribed by the applicable records retention schedule and the method of disposition;

(7) The routine uses of the system, including each recurring transfer to a state agency, testing contractor, transportation contractor, food service management company, insurance carrier, or third-party administrator; and

(8) The name of the position responsible for authorizing access to the system.

(b) The inventory shall not incorporate, reproduce, or append any actual data value drawn from any system of records. Sample records shall not be attached. Where illustration of a field's format is necessary, the Custodian shall describe the format by reference to length and character class.

§ 47.32 Classification Levels.

Each system of records enumerated in the inventory shall be assigned one of the following classification levels, and the assignment shall be reviewed whenever the composition of the system materially changes:

Level I — Public. Contains no personally identifiable information, or contains only elements properly designated as directory information under district policy.

Level II — Internal. Contains personally identifiable information the disclosure of which would not reasonably be expected to cause substantial harm, including class rosters, seating charts, and building assignments.

Level III — Confidential. Contains personally identifiable information the disclosure of which could reasonably be expected to cause harm, including disciplinary records, attendance intervention files, and counselor case notes.

Level IV — Restricted. Contains any unique identifying number, financial account information, biometric identifier, or information relating to health, disability, or eligibility for assistance programs. Level IV systems shall be encrypted at rest, shall require multifactor authentication for remote access, and shall be subject to quarterly access review by the Records Custodian.

§ 47.33 Penalty for Failure to Inventory.

Failure to prepare, certify, and retain the inventory required by § 47.31 shall constitute a deficiency reportable in the district's annual compliance audit and may result in the withholding of administrative reimbursement, in whole or in part, until the deficiency is cured to the satisfaction of the Department.
