STATE ADMINISTRATIVE CODE
TITLE 12 — EDUCATION
CHAPTER 40 — PROTECTION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION IN INSTITUTIONAL RECORDS
Adopted by the Board of Regents of the State University System; effective July 1, 1997
(Excerpt: Rules .101 through .104. Retain in the Registrar's rule file. Post Schedule A at each point of collection.)

RULE 12-40-.101  PURPOSE AND APPLICATION

  (a) This chapter governs the collection, retention, disclosure, and disposal of
      personal information maintained by any institution under the governance of
      the Board.
  (b) This chapter applies to records in any form, including paper files, index
      cards, microform, magnetic tape, disk storage, and printed output, and
      whether held in a central office or in a departmental office.
  (c) Where a provision of this chapter conflicts with a federal requirement
      applicable to education records, the federal requirement governs. The
      Records Custodian shall note the conflict in the rule file kept under
      Rule .104 and shall report it to the Office of the General Counsel.

RULE 12-40-.102  DEFINITIONS

  As used in this chapter, unless the context otherwise requires:

  (1) "Board" means the Board of Regents of the State University System.

  (2) "Direct identifier" means a data element that identifies an individual on
      its face without reference to other information, including full name,
      social security number, student identification number, employee payroll
      number, driver license number, passport number, financial account number,
      residence address, telephone number, electronic mail address, and
      biometric print.

  (3) "Indirect identifier" means a data element that does not identify an
      individual on its face but that may identify an individual when combined
      with other data elements reasonably available to the recipient, including
      date of birth, place of birth, sex, race or ethnicity designation, term of
      first enrollment, major field or curriculum code, and job classification.

  (4) "Holder of record" means any officer, employee, agent, student assistant,
      or contractor of the institution who has custody of, access to, or control
      over a record containing personal information, whether or not that person
      created the record.

  (5) "Personal information" means any one direct identifier, or any two or more
      indirect identifiers, associated with an individual who is or has been a
      student, an applicant for admission, an employee, or an applicant for
      employment.

  (6) "Records Custodian" means the officer designated in writing by the
      president of each institution to administer this chapter.

  (7) "Routine use" means a disclosure for a purpose compatible with the purpose
      for which the information was collected, as published in the notice
      required by Rule .103(d).

RULE 12-40-.103  OBLIGATIONS OF HOLDERS OF RECORD

  (a) Collection. A holder shall collect only those data elements necessary to a
      stated institutional purpose and shall collect them, where practicable,
      from the individual to whom they pertain.
  (b) Accuracy. A holder shall make a reasonable effort to assure that personal
      information is accurate, complete, and current before it is used to make a
      determination affecting the individual.
  (c) Access control. Access shall be limited to holders whose assigned duties
      require it. Each grant of access shall be recorded on the authorization
      roster by title of position.
  (d) Notice. No system of record shall be established or materially altered
      until notice of its routine uses has been filed with the Records Custodian.
  (e) Disclosure log. Every disclosure outside the institution, other than a
      routine use, shall be entered in the disclosure log showing: Date of
      Request; Requesting Party (name of entity); Statutory or Rule Basis; Data
      Elements Released; Form of Release; Approving Officer (title).
  (f) Disposal. Records shall be destroyed only under the retention class shown
      on Schedule A and only by an approved method.

RULE 12-40-.104  RULE FILE

  Each Records Custodian shall keep a rule file containing the current chapter
  text, all schedules, the systems inventory, the authorization roster, the
  disclosure log for the current and two preceding fiscal years, and copies of
  all certifications filed under Rule .105.
