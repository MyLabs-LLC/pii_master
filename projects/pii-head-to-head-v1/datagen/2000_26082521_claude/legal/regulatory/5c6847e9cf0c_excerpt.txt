EXCERPT — PERSONAL INFORMATION PROTECTION ACT
Chapter 1387, Article II — Definitions and Construction
Compliance file copy — Patient Financial Services / Billing Office

§ 2.01. Definitions. As used in this Chapter, unless the context clearly requires otherwise, the following terms shall have the meanings hereinafter ascribed to them:

- "Personal information" means an individual's first name or first initial and last name, in combination with any one or more of the following unencrypted and unredacted data elements: Social Security Number; Driver's License Number or State Identification Card Number; Financial Account Number, Credit Card Number, or Debit Card Number, alone or in combination with any required security code, access code, or password permitting access thereto; Medical Record Number; Health Plan Beneficiary Number; Date of Birth.

- "Holder of records" means any person, firm, partnership, corporation, association, or public body that owns, licenses, maintains, stores, or otherwise has custody of personal information in the course of business, whether in electronic, paper, microform, or other medium.

- "Records Custodian" means the individual designated in writing by a holder of records to receive process, respond to inquiries, and discharge the duties imposed by Articles III and IV of this Chapter.

- "Redact" means the alteration or truncation of data such that no more than the last four digits of an account or identification number remain accessible.

§ 2.02. Construction. Nothing in this Article shall be construed to relieve a holder of records of any obligation arising under federal law, and where federal law imposes a stricter standard, the stricter standard shall govern.
