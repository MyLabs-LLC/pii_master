§ 14.08 — CATEGORIES OF IDENTIFIERS AND CORRESPONDING HANDLING REQUIREMENTS

The following table sets forth, for each category of identifier defined in § 14.02(a), the minimum handling requirement imposed by this Article. Nothing herein shall be construed to authorize a lesser standard where any other law, rule, or contractual undertaking imposes a greater one.

IDENTIFIER CATEGORY          | STATUTORY CITE  | MIN. HANDLING STANDARD        | RETENTION
-----------------------------|-----------------|-------------------------------|----------
Full Legal Name              | § 14.02(a)      | Restricted Access             | 7 yrs
Social Security Number       | § 14.02(a)(i)   | Encrypt at Rest and in Transit| 7 yrs
Driver's License Number      | § 14.02(a)(ii)  | Encrypt at Rest               | 7 yrs
State ID Card Number         | § 14.02(a)(ii)  | Encrypt at Rest               | 7 yrs
Financial Account Number     | § 14.02(a)(iii) | Encrypt; Truncate on Display  | 3 yrs
Credit or Debit Card Number  | § 14.02(a)(iii) | Encrypt; Truncate on Display  | 3 yrs
Card Security / Access Code  | § 14.02(a)(iii) | Prohibited from Storage       | None
Diagnosis or Treatment Code  | § 14.02(a)(iv)  | Restricted Access; Audit Log  | 10 yrs
Prescription History         | § 14.02(a)(iv)  | Restricted Access; Audit Log  | 10 yrs
Health Insurance Policy No.  | § 14.02(a)(v)   | Restricted Access             | 7 yrs
Subscriber Identification No.| § 14.02(a)(v)   | Restricted Access             | 7 yrs
Date of Birth                | § 14.02(a)       | Restricted Access             | 7 yrs

NOTES TO THE TABLE:

(1) "Restricted Access" shall mean access limited to those persons whose duties reasonably require the same, as provided in § 14.04(c).

(2) "Truncate on Display" shall mean the masking of all but the final four characters of the identifier in any screen display, printed receipt, or report generated for routine business purposes.

(3) The retention periods set forth above are minimum periods. No Protected Record shall be retained beyond the applicable minimum period absent a documented legal hold, and every record so held shall be entered upon the Legal Hold Register maintained by the Records Custodian with the following fields: Record Series, Basis for Hold, Date Imposed, Position Title of Requesting Officer, Anticipated Release Date.

(4) Where a single record contains identifiers falling within more than one category, the most protective handling standard and the longest retention period applicable to any identifier therein shall govern the record in its entirety.
