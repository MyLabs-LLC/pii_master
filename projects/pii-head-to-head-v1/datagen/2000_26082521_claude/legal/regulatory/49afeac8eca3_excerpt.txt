TITLE 8, ARTICLE IV — CONSTRUCTION AND DEFINITIONS
Sec. 8.410. Definitions applicable to Articles IV through VII.

Unless the context clearly requires otherwise, the following terms shall have the meanings given:

  "Aggregate data" — data reported at a group level from which no individual can be re-identified by any means reasonably likely to be used, and in which no cell represents fewer than eleven individuals.

  "Authorized purpose" — a use of personal information that is (i) within the statutory mandate of the Department, (ii) documented in the applicable record inventory entry, and (iii) approved in writing by the program manager of the custodial unit.

  "Custodial unit" — the organizational unit designated in the record inventory as bearing primary responsibility for a record series.

  "De-identified" — stripped of all fields enumerated in the Identifier Schedule at Sec. 8.412, with no actual knowledge that the residual information could be used alone or in combination to identify a person.

  "Identifier Schedule" — the enumeration of identifier categories maintained under Sec. 8.412, comprising, at minimum: Name; Geographic Subdivision Smaller Than a State; All Elements of Dates Except Year; Telephone Number; Facsimile Number; Electronic Mail Address; Social Security Number; Medical Record Number; Health Plan Beneficiary Number; Account Number; Certificate or License Number; Vehicle Identifier and Serial Number; Device Identifier and Serial Number; Web Universal Resource Locator; Internet Protocol Address; Biometric Identifier; Full-Face Photographic Image; Any Other Unique Identifying Number, Characteristic, or Code.

  "Limited data set" — personal information from which the Identifier Schedule fields have been removed except Dates, Town or City, State, and Postal Code, and which is disclosed only under a data use agreement.

  "Re-identification" — any process by which de-identified or aggregate data is linked, matched, or combined with other information so as to identify a natural person.

  "Records Custodian" — the officer designated by the Health Officer to hold the master record inventory, the identifier crosswalks, the disclosure log, and the certificates of destruction.

  "Sensitive record series" — a record series flagged in the inventory as containing special category information, including but not limited to: Communicable Disease Case Reports; Sexually Transmitted Infection Registers; Tuberculosis Contact Investigations; Immunization Registry Extracts; Behavioral Health Referrals; Vital Records Amendments.

  "Working copy" — any extract, export, printout, or derivative of a record series held outside the system of record, whether temporary or otherwise. A working copy is a record for all purposes of this Title and is subject to the same retention, security, and destruction requirements as the source.

Sec. 8.411. Construction. The enumeration of a term in this Article does not by itself authorize the collection of the data element named. Authority to collect must be established independently under Sec. 8.420.
