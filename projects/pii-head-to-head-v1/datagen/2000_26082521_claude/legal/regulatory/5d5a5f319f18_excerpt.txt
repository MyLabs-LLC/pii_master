ARTICLE 3. DEFINITIONS.

3.1 As used in this Act, unless the context requires otherwise:

3.2 "Personal information" means an individual's first name or first initial and last name in combination with any one or more of the following data elements, when either the name or the data element is not encrypted or redacted:

 (a) Social Security number;
 (b) Driver's license number or State-issued identification card number;
 (c) Financial account number, credit card number, or debit card number, with or without any required security code, access code, personal identification number, or password permitting access to the account;
 (d) Medical information or health insurance identification number;
 (e) Taxpayer identification number;
 (f) Date of birth, where combined with any element in subsections (a) through (e).

3.3 "Personal information" does not include publicly available information lawfully made available to the general public from federal, State, or local government records, including recorded instruments of conveyance, recorded liens, and recorded plats.

3.4 "Holder of records" means any person or entity that, for any business purpose, collects, maintains, transmits, discards, or otherwise possesses personal information concerning a resident of this State, whether or not the entity is domiciled in this State. The term includes title insurers, title insurance agents, escrow agents, closing agents, and settlement service providers.

3.5 "Records Custodian" means the individual designated in writing by a holder of records to administer the information security program required under Article 5.

3.6 "Breach of the security of the system" means unauthorized acquisition of computerized or physical data that compromises the security, confidentiality, or integrity of personal information maintained by a holder of records. Good faith acquisition by an employee or agent of the holder of records for a legitimate business purpose is not a breach, provided the personal information is not used or subject to further unauthorized disclosure.

3.7 "Encrypted" means rendered unusable, unreadable, or indecipherable through a technology or methodology generally accepted in the field of information security, where the confidential process or key is not acquired in the same incident.

3.8 "Discard" means to abandon, dispose of, or relinquish control of a record, whether in paper, electronic, or other form.
