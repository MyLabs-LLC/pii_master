RULE 220.14 — CUSTODY OF CONSIGNEE INFORMATION AT CROSS-DOCK AND BREAK-BULK FACILITIES

(a) A permitted motor carrier shall not permit a bill of lading, delivery manifest, waybill, driver qualification file or claims folder bearing any identifier category listed at Rule 220.03(b) to remain upon an open dock, sorting table, staging lane or unattended vehicle cab outside the hours during which the facility is under the supervision of the shift supervisor of record.

(b) Documents awaiting keying, imaging or transmittal shall be held in a locked receptacle bearing an exterior label limited to the following entries: Facility Number; Document Series; Count of Pieces; Date Deposited; Depositing Role Title; Collecting Role Title. No identifier category shall appear upon the exterior of the receptacle.

(c) Facsimile equipment used to receive documents described in subsection (a) shall be located in an area to which access is restricted to the role titles entered upon the facility access roster, and the transmission log of such equipment shall itself be treated as a record subject to this rule.

(d) A carrier who has designated a Records Custodian under Chapter 44 of the Commerce and Trade Title may satisfy subsections (a) through (c) by procedures adopted under that chapter, provided such procedures are reduced to writing and produced upon demand of an inspector of the Department.

Authority: Transportation Code § 12.014; § 12.031. Effective 1 July 2004.
