SUBCHAPTER E — RIGHTS OF INDIVIDUALS AND ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURE

    § 12.212  Right of Access.

        (a)  An individual shall have a right of access to inspect and obtain a copy
             of a protected record pertaining to that individual, in the form and
             format requested if readily producible.

        (b)  The holder may impose a reasonable, cost-based fee limited to the cost
             of labor for copying, supplies, postage, and, where an electronic copy
             is requested, the cost of portable media.

        (c)  Access may be denied, in whole or in part, only as to psychotherapy
             notes, information compiled in reasonable anticipation of a legal
             proceeding, and information the disclosure of which is reasonably likely
             to endanger the life or physical safety of any person. Denials shall be
             stated in writing and shall advise the individual of the right of review
             set forth at § 12.214.

    § 12.213  Right of Amendment.

        (a)  An individual may request amendment of any identifier element or
             substantive entry alleged to be inaccurate or incomplete.

        (b)  The holder shall act within sixty (60) days. If the amendment is
             accepted, the holder shall append the correction, shall not obliterate
             the original entry, and shall notify such persons as the individual
             identifies as having received the erroneous information.

        (c)  If the amendment is denied, the individual may submit a statement of
             disagreement, which shall thereafter accompany all disclosures of the
             disputed portion of the record.

    § 12.214  Review and Appeal.

        (a)  A request for review shall be submitted to the Records Custodian and
             shall be assigned to a licensed professional who did not participate in
             the original determination.

        (b)  The reviewing officer shall issue a written determination within thirty
             (30) days, which determination shall be final as to the holder and shall
             not preclude the filing of a complaint with the Department.

    § 12.215  Effective Date.

        The provisions of this subchapter apply to protected records created or
        maintained on or after the effective date established by the Department's
        implementing notice, provided that §§ 12.206 and 12.207 apply to all
        protected records regardless of the date of creation.
