TITLE 22 - EDUCATION
CHAPTER 14 - PROTECTION OF PERSONALLY IDENTIFIABLE INFORMATION
ARTICLE 2 - DEFINITIONS AND CLASSIFICATION OF IDENTIFIERS (EXCERPT)

Sec. 14-201.  Definitions.

As used in this chapter, and in any rule adopted under it, unless the
context clearly requires otherwise:

TERM                   MEANING
---------------------  ---------------------------------------------------
(1) Agency Record      Any item, collection, or grouping of information
                       about an individual that is maintained by a local
                       education agency and is retrievable by the name of
                       that individual or by an identifier assigned to
                       that individual.
(2) Covered Holder     A local education agency, the governing board of
                       such an agency, a chartered school, a cooperative
                       service unit, or a contractor acting on behalf of
                       any of the foregoing.
(3) Records Custodian  The officer designated by a Covered Holder under
                       Sec. 14-310 to receive requests, to maintain the
                       disclosure log, and to answer for compliance with
                       this chapter.
(4) Direct Identifier  A data element that, standing alone, identifies a
                       specific individual.
(5) Indirect           A data element that does not identify an individual
    Identifier         standing alone but that may identify an individual
                       when combined with other reasonably available
                       information.

Sec. 14-202.  Classification of identifier categories.

Each data element held by a Covered Holder shall be classified in the
system of records inventory according to the following categories.

CODE   CATEGORY LABEL                    CLASS     RETENTION TIER
-----  --------------------------------  --------  --------------
D-01   Full Legal Name                   Direct    Tier I
D-02   Social Security Number            Direct    Tier I
D-03   State Student Identification No.  Direct    Tier I
D-04   Date of Birth                     Direct    Tier I
D-05   Residential Street Address        Direct    Tier II
D-06   Telephone Number                  Direct    Tier II
D-07   Electronic Mail Address           Direct    Tier II
D-08   Parent or Guardian Legal Name     Direct    Tier I
D-09   Biometric Template Reference      Direct    Tier I
I-01   Place of Birth                    Indirect  Tier II
I-02   Grade Level and Homeroom          Indirect  Tier III
I-03   Free or Reduced Meal Status       Indirect  Tier I
I-04   Disciplinary Action Code          Indirect  Tier I
I-05   Individualized Program Status     Indirect  Tier I
I-06   Immunization and Health Screening Indirect  Tier I

Sec. 14-203.  Effect of classification.

Classification under Sec. 14-202 does not of itself authorize collection
of a data element. No Covered Holder may collect a data element unless
collection is required by statute or is necessary to a lawful function
described in the agency's published notice of systems of records.
