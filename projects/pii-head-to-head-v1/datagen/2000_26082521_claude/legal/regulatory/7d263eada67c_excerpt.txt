ADMINISTRATIVE CODE TITLE 22 — EDUCATION
CHAPTER 14. SECURITY AND CONFIDENTIALITY OF PUPIL RECORDS
(Excerpt: §§ 14-201 through 14-205; effective July 1, 2011)

§ 14-201. Definitions.
As used in this chapter, unless the context otherwise requires:

• "Agency of record" means a local education agency, charter school, or regional service center that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits pupil records.
• "Custodian of records" means the employee designated in writing by the governing board under § 14-202(a), and any employee acting under that designation.
• "Pupil record" means information directly related to an identified or identifiable pupil that is maintained by an agency of record in any medium, including paper, microform, magnetic, optical, or electronic media.
• "Personal identifier" means any of the following data elements, when maintained in association with a pupil's name or with any other element enumerated in this definition:
   • Social Security number, or any four or more sequential digits thereof;
   • state-assigned pupil identification number or local student number;
   • date of birth; place of birth;
   • parent or guardian name; mother's maiden name;
   • residence address; mailing address;
   • home, mobile, or facsimile number;
   • electronic mail address;
   • financial account number, or credit or debit card number, with or without any required security code, access code, or password;
   • driver's license number or state identification card number;
   • biometric record, including fingerprint, retina image, hand geometry, or voice print;
   • health, disability, or immunization information, including special education classification and evaluation results;
   • eligibility status for free or reduced-price meals;
   • photograph or video image in which the pupil is individually identifiable.
• "Directory information" means the following elements only: name; grade level; dates of enrollment; participation in officially recognized activities; height and weight of members of athletic teams; diplomas and awards received.
• "Breach" means the unauthorized acquisition, access, use, or disclosure of one or more personal identifiers that compromises the security or confidentiality of a pupil record.

§ 14-202. Duties of the agency of record.
Each agency of record shall:

• designate one custodian of records for each attendance center and file the designation with the chief school administrator;
• maintain a written inventory of every system of records containing personal identifiers, stating the system name, physical or logical location, categories of identifiers held, and retention period;
• restrict access to personal identifiers to employees having a legitimate educational interest, documented by position title;
• store paper records in a locked cabinet within a locked room during non-business hours;
• render electronic personal identifiers unusable, unreadable, or indecipherable when stored on a portable electronic device or transmitted across a public network;
• require annual confidentiality training of all employees and contractors with access, and retain attendance rosters for three years.

§ 14-203. Disclosure log.
The custodian of records shall maintain, with each pupil record, a log recording each disclosure to a party other than the parent, eligible pupil, or an employee with a legitimate educational interest. The log shall contain the following columns:

• Date of request
• Name of requesting party
• Relationship to pupil
• Statutory or regulatory basis for disclosure
• Records or elements released
• Method of transmittal
• Signature of custodian

§ 14-204. Retention and destruction.

• Permanent records shall be retained not less than one hundred years from date of withdrawal or graduation.
• Temporary records shall be destroyed not later than five years after the pupil ceases enrollment.
• Destruction shall be by cross-cut shredding, incineration, pulping, or degaussing, and shall be witnessed and documented on a certificate of destruction.

§ 14-205. Penalties.

• A violation of § 14-202 or § 14-203 constitutes grounds for withholding of state aid in an amount not exceeding two percent of the annual entitlement.
• A knowing or reckless disclosure of a personal identifier by an employee is a civil violation subject to a penalty of not more than $1,000 for each pupil whose record is disclosed, and constitutes cause for dismissal under the applicable tenure or classified service statute.
