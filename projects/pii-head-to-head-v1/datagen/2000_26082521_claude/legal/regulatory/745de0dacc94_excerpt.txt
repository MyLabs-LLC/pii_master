TITLE 22 - EDUCATION
CHAPTER 14, ARTICLE 5 - ACCOUNTING OF DISCLOSURES (EXCERPT)

Sec. 14-512.  Required contents of the disclosure log.

For every disclosure of an Agency Record to a party outside the Covered
Holder, the Records Custodian shall enter the following fields, and no
field beyond those listed, in the disclosure log.

FIELD NO. | FIELD LABEL                  | FORMAT      | REQUIRED
----------+------------------------------+-------------+------------
01        | Log Entry Sequence Number    | Integer     | Yes
02        | Date of Disclosure           | Date        | Yes
03        | Requesting Party Category    | Code List   | Yes
04        | Legal Basis Citation         | Text        | Yes
05        | Record Series Identifier     | Code        | Yes
06        | Identifier Category Codes    | Code List   | Yes
07        | Method of Transmission       | Code List   | Yes
08        | Releasing Role Title         | Text        | Yes
09        | Consent Reference Number     | Text        | Conditional
10        | Redaction Applied            | Boolean     | Yes
11        | Date of Custodian Review     | Date        | Yes

Sec. 14-513.  Retention and inspection of the log.

  (a)  The disclosure log shall be retained for five years after the
       date of the last entry, or for the life of the underlying record,
       whichever is longer.

  (b)  The log shall be made available for inspection by the affected
       individual, by the parent or guardian of a minor, and by the
       Department, upon request and without charge.

  (c)  Field 09 is required only where the disclosure rests on written
       consent rather than on a statutory exception.
