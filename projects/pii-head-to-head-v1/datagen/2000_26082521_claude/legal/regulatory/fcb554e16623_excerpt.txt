BOARD OF REGENTS OF THE STATE UNIVERSITY SYSTEM
OFFICE OF THE GENERAL COUNSEL

RULE 12-40-.105  DUTIES OF THE RECORDS CUSTODIAN; ANNUAL CERTIFICATION
(Excerpt as amended, effective January 1, 1999. Form RC-1 attached.)

.105(1)  DESIGNATION
         The president of each institution shall designate a Records Custodian and
         an alternate in writing and shall file the designation with the Board
         within thirty days of any vacancy.

.105(2)  DUTIES
         The Records Custodian shall:
         (a) maintain the inventory of systems of record described in .105(3);
         (b) approve in writing each routine use before it takes effect;
         (c) cause every holder of record to sign the acknowledgment of duties
             prescribed by the Board and retain the signed acknowledgment;
         (d) review the disclosure log not less than quarterly;
         (e) report each unauthorized disclosure to the president and to the Board
             within ten working days of discovery;
         (f) certify compliance annually on Form RC-1.

.105(3)  INVENTORY OF SYSTEMS OF RECORD
         The inventory shall show, for each system: system name; office of record;
         medium; categories of data subjects; data elements by class; routine
         uses; retention class; method of disposal; and title of the responsible
         officer.

.105(4)  ANNUAL CERTIFICATION
         On or before the fifteenth day of September each year, the certification
         below shall be filed for the fiscal year then ended.
