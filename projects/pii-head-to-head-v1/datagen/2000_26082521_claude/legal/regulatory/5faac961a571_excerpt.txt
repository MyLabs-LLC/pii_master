SUBCHAPTER F — PENALTIES AND ENFORCEMENT
(Excerpt; §§ 812–815)

§ 812. Civil penalty. Any holder of records who violates a provision of this Subchapter shall be liable for a civil penalty:
 [ ] Not exceeding five hundred dollars ($500) for a first violation;
 [ ] Not exceeding two thousand dollars ($2,000) for a second violation occurring within twenty-four months of the first;
 [ ] Not exceeding five thousand dollars ($5,000) for each subsequent violation, each day of continuing violation being deemed a separate offense.

§ 813. Willful conduct. Any officer or employee who willfully discloses personal information knowing that disclosure is prohibited, or who willfully maintains a system of records without filing the notice required by § 5-3.6, shall be guilty of a misdemeanor.

§ 814. Obtaining records under false pretenses. Any person who requests or obtains a record concerning another under false pretenses shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and shall be liable to the subject of the record for actual damages.

§ 815. Administrative action. The Health Officer may, independent of the foregoing:
 [ ] Suspend or revoke system access;
 [ ] Impose discipline under the applicable personnel rules;
 [ ] Refer the matter to the County Attorney or the District Attorney.
