PART 412 — SAFEGUARDING OF INDIVIDUALLY IDENTIFIABLE DISPENSING INFORMATION

§ 412.11  Categories of identifiers; classification of holdings.

(a) Every holder of records shall classify each data element maintained in its patient profile system, claims processing system, and hard-copy prescription file into one of the three categories described in this section, and shall record the classification in the system data dictionary maintained by the Records Custodian.

(b) Category I identifiers are those field designations by which an individual may be identified without reference to any other source, and comprise: patient surname; patient given name; residential street address; residential telephone number; social security account number; and state-issued identification number.

(c) Category II identifiers are those field designations by which an individual may be identified only in combination with one or more additional elements, and comprise: date of birth; sex; postal code; health plan member identification number; group number; person code; prescription serial number; internal patient file number; and prescriber identification number where the prescriber's practice is limited to a single individual.

(d) Category III elements are those which do not identify an individual, and comprise: national drug code; quantity dispensed; days supply; dosage form; therapeutic class code; refill sequence number; date of service; acquisition cost; and dispensing fee.

§ 412.12  Handling requirements by category.

(a) Category I identifiers shall not be displayed on any document posted, affixed, or otherwise made visible in an area accessible to the general public, and shall not be transmitted by facsimile except to a number verified by the Records Custodian against the entry maintained in the authorized recipient register.

(b) Category I and Category II identifiers shall be purged or rendered unreadable prior to the transfer of any storage medium to a vendor for maintenance, refurbishment, or disposal.

(c) Category III elements may be extracted, aggregated, and reported for inventory control, utilization review, and quality assurance purposes without the authorization otherwise required by § 412.9, provided that no Category I or Category II identifier accompanies the extract and that the extract is not of such small cell size as to permit indirect identification.
