ADMINISTRATIVE ENFORCEMENT UNDER § 14.09 — GUIDANCE FOR STORE AND DISTRICT MANAGEMENT

1. Investigatory Authority. The Board, by its duly authorized inspectors, may enter any premises of a Covered Establishment during ordinary business hours and may examine, copy, and take custody of any book, record, log, or register required to be maintained under this Article. Refusal to permit such examination shall itself constitute a violation under § 14.07 and shall be reported forthwith to the Office of the Records Custodian.

2. Notice of Apparent Violation. Prior to the imposition of any penalty, the Board shall serve upon the Covered Establishment a written Notice of Apparent Violation specifying the section alleged to have been violated, the facts constituting the alleged violation, and the maximum penalty to which the Covered Establishment is exposed. Such notice shall be served upon the registered agent of the Covered Establishment.

3. Opportunity to Cure. Where the alleged violation is of a recordkeeping or procedural character and has not resulted in the unauthorized acquisition of Personal Identifying Information, the Covered Establishment shall be afforded a period of thirty (30) days within which to cure the same. Timely and complete cure shall abate the penalty in full, save that a second violation of the same section within a period of twenty-four (24) months shall not be subject to abatement.

4. Hearing. A Covered Establishment aggrieved by a Notice of Apparent Violation may, within twenty (20) days of service, file a written request for hearing. Such request shall specify with particularity each finding contested and shall be accompanied by such documentary evidence as the Covered Establishment intends to rely upon. Failure to request a hearing within the period prescribed shall constitute a waiver of all defenses and shall render the Notice final and unappealable.

5. Aggravating and Mitigating Factors. In determining the amount of any penalty, the Board shall consider: (a) the number of individuals whose Personal Identifying Information was affected; (b) the sensitivity of the identifier categories involved, as classified in § 14.08; (c) the presence or absence of a written information security program in conformity with § 14.04(b); (d) the promptness of notification under § 14.06; (e) the history of prior violations; and (f) the good faith of the Covered Establishment in remediating the conditions giving rise to the violation.

6. Internal Referral. Any employee receiving a Notice of Apparent Violation, an inspector's request for records, or any correspondence from the Board shall transmit the same to the Office of the Records Custodian on the day of receipt and shall make no response, admission, or production of records except at the direction of that Office.
