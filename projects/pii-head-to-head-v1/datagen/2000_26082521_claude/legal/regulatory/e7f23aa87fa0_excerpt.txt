MEMORANDUM

TO:    Pharmacists-in-Charge, All Regions
FROM:  Corporate Compliance
RE:    Board of Pharmacy Rule 44-2.03 — Verification of Requesting Party
DATE:  August 4, 1997

Effective October 1, 1997, the following administrative rule governs release of patient-identifiable information at the counter or by telephone. Post at the will-call station.

Rule 44-2.03. Verification Prior to Release.

   (a) Before releasing any covered record or any direct identifier contained therein, the Records Custodian or designee shall verify the identity and authority of the requesting party.

   (b) Verification shall be documented on Form BP-212 (Request for Release of Pharmacy Record), which shall capture the following fields: Requesting Party Category; Relationship to Patient; Authority Asserted; Identifier Elements Requested; Verification Method; Date of Verification; Custodian Signature.

   (c) Acceptable verification methods are limited to: (i) inspection of government-issued photographic identification; (ii) callback to a number of record obtained independently of the request; (iii) written authorization bearing an original signature; or (iv) lawful process served upon the licensee.

   (d) A request that cannot be verified under subsection (c) shall be refused and logged as such. Refusal is not a reportable incident.

   (e) Failure to complete Form BP-212 constitutes a recordkeeping violation subject to citation under Rule 44-6.01.
