MEMORANDUM

TO:       Regulatory File — Privacy and Information Security
FROM:     Office of the General Counsel
SUBJECT:  Excerpt of Administrative Order 2016-04, Articles I–V, concerning the
          protection of nonpublic personal information held by settlement agents
ACTION:   File; circulate to Records Custodian and Branch Compliance Liaisons

ARTICLE I — AUTHORITY AND PURPOSE

  1.1  This Order is issued under the general supervisory authority of the
       Commissioner over title insurance agents, agencies, and settlement
       service providers.
  1.2  The purpose of this Order is to establish minimum standards for the
       collection, retention, transmission, and destruction of nonpublic
       personal information obtained in connection with a real estate
       settlement or escrow transaction.

ARTICLE II — DEFINITIONS

  2.1  "Covered entity" means a licensee, and any affiliate acting on the
       licensee's behalf, that maintains one or more files containing
       nonpublic personal information.
  2.2  "Nonpublic personal information" means personally identifiable
       financial information and any list, description, or grouping of
       consumers derived using such information, excluding publicly
       available information.
  2.3  "Transaction file" means the aggregation of records assembled for a
       single settlement, including the settlement statement, the escrow
       instructions, the title commitment, and all supporting instruments.
  2.4  "Unauthorized acquisition" means acquisition of a record by a person
       not entitled to access, without regard to whether the record was
       subsequently used.

ARTICLE III — IDENTIFIER CATEGORIES AND HANDLING

  3.1  Each element listed below shall be classified and handled according
       to the assigned category code.

       CODE   IDENTIFIER ELEMENT (FIELD LABEL)      HANDLING CLASS   RETENTION
       ----   ------------------------------------  --------------   ---------
       A-1    Social Security Number                Restricted       Close + 5
       A-2    Individual Taxpayer Identification No. Restricted      Close + 5
       B-1    Bank Routing Number                   Restricted       Close + 7
       B-2    Deposit Account Number                Restricted       Close + 7
       B-3    Wire Instruction Beneficiary Detail   Restricted       Close + 7
       C-1    Driver's License / State ID Number    Controlled       Close + 3
       C-2    Passport Number                       Controlled       Close + 3
       D-1    Date of Birth                         Controlled       Close + 3
       D-2    Residence Address                     Internal         Close + 3
       E-1    Loan Number                           Internal         Close + 7
       E-2    Escrow / File Number                  Internal         Permanent
       F-1    Signature Image                       Controlled       Close + 7

  3.2  Restricted elements shall not be transmitted by unencrypted
       electronic mail, facsimile to an unattended device, or courier
       without signature receipt.
  3.3  Restricted elements appearing on a document produced for a party's
       file copy shall be truncated to the final four characters where
       truncation does not defeat the legal sufficiency of the instrument.

ARTICLE IV — OBLIGATIONS OF THE HOLDER

  4.1  The covered entity shall maintain a written information security
       program, approved by an officer of the entity, and shall designate
       by title a Records Custodian responsible for its administration.
  4.2  The covered entity shall maintain an access register identifying,
       for each transaction file, the following columns: File Number;
       Custodian of Record (title); Date Opened; Date of Last Access;
       Authorization Basis.
  4.3  Vault, cabinet, and server room access shall be logged. Logs shall
       be retained not less than twenty-four months.
  4.4  Upon separation of any employee, access credentials shall be revoked
       not later than the close of business on the effective date of
       separation.

ARTICLE V — ENFORCEMENT

  5.1  Violation of any provision of this Order subjects the licensee to
       suspension or revocation of licensure, or to a monetary penalty, or
       both.
  5.2  The Commissioner shall consider, in mitigation, the promptness of
       self-reporting and the adequacy of remedial measures undertaken.
  5.3  This Order does not create a private right of action.
