REGULATION 08-14
DATA CLASSIFICATION AND DISCLOSURE SCHEDULE
Adopted pursuant to § 15-206 of the pupil records chapter

Section 1. Effect of schedule. The schedule set out in Section 3 is the exclusive classification of pupil data elements for purposes of disclosure determinations. An element not listed is presumed to be Restricted until classified by the Records Custodian in writing.

Section 2. Classification definitions.
  2.1 "Directory" — releasable without prior written consent, subject to a standing objection filed by a parent or eligible pupil.
  2.2 "Restricted" — releasable only on prior written consent or under an enumerated exception.
  2.3 "Confidential-Sensitive" — releasable only on prior written consent, and only in the minimum quantity necessary; aggregation with any other element requires custodial approval.

Section 4. Special limitation. A social security number shall not be collected, requested, or stored except where required by state or federal law. Where a lawful requirement exists, the number shall be stored in the designated field of the student information system, shall not be printed on any roster, identification card, class list, or correspondence, and shall not be used as an identifier for any internal purpose.

Section 5. Objection to directory disclosure. A parent or eligible pupil may file an objection on the form prescribed by the Records Custodian. The objection remains effective until withdrawn in writing and shall be honored not later than 5 business days after receipt.
