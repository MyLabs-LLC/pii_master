DEPARTMENT OF LABOR AND WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT
CHAPTER 800 -- PRIVATE EMPLOYMENT AND STAFFING SERVICES
RULE 800-14-.06  SECURITY OF PERSONNEL RECORDS; ACCESS LOG; NOTIFICATION
Authority: § 21-4-102(d), § 21-4-105.  Effective the first day of the quarter
following adoption.

PARA.    │ PROVISION
─────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
(1)      │ The purpose of this rule is to prescribe the minimum
         │ administrative, technical, and physical measures by which a
         │ licensed private employment agency shall safeguard records
         │ containing personal identifying information of applicants,
         │ assigned workers, and former assigned workers.
         │
(2)      │ This rule applies to every licensee, and to every agent,
         │ contractor, or subcontractor of a licensee who is afforded
         │ access to a personnel record. Terms not defined herein carry
         │ the meanings given them in § 21-4-101.
         │
(3)(a)   │ The licensee shall designate in writing a Records Custodian
         │ and at least one alternate, and shall record the designation
         │ in the license file kept at the principal place of business.
         │ The designation shall state the position title of each
         │ designee and shall be reviewed upon any change in that
         │ position.
         │
(3)(b)   │ Records containing Class I identifiers shall be maintained in
         │ a locked cabinet, vault, or room, or in an electronic
         │ repository requiring unique user authentication, and shall be
         │ accessible only to the Records Custodian, the alternate, and
         │ such other position titles as the licensee may designate in
         │ writing.
         │
(3)(c)   │ No record containing personal identifying information shall be
         │ removed from the licensee's premises except upon the written
         │ authorization of the Records Custodian, and no such record
         │ shall be transmitted by facsimile or by unencrypted
         │ electronic mail.
         │
(3)(d)   │ The licensee shall not disclose a Class III identifier to a
         │ client, prospective client, or other third party except as
         │ required by law or upon the specific written authorization of
         │ the individual to whom the identifier pertains.
         │
(3)(e)   │ The licensee shall cause each employee having access to
         │ personnel records to receive instruction in this rule upon
         │ hire and annually thereafter, and shall retain a record of
         │ such instruction showing the position title of the attendee
         │ and the date of completion.
         │
(4)      │ The Records Custodian shall maintain a contemporaneous access
         │ log, in bound or tamper-evident electronic form, containing
         │ the columns set out in Schedule A. An entry shall be made at
         │ the time of each retrieval and at the time of each return or
         │ disposal. The log shall be retained three years after the
         │ last entry.
         │
(5)(a)   │ Upon discovery of unauthorized acquisition of or access to a
         │ record containing personal identifying information, the
         │ Records Custodian shall commence an inquiry without
         │ unreasonable delay and shall report to the Department upon
         │ the prescribed form.
         │
(5)(b)   │ The report shall state the record series affected, the
         │ identifier categories involved, the number of individuals
         │ affected, the date of discovery, and the remedial measures
         │ undertaken; it shall not set out the identifiers themselves.
         │
(6)      │ A licensee failing to comply with this rule is subject to the
         │ civil penalties prescribed by § 21-4-106 and to suspension or
         │ revocation of licensure.
─────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

SCHEDULE A -- REQUIRED COLUMNS OF THE ACCESS LOG

+-----+---------------------------+--------------------+
| SEQ | LOG COLUMN                | ENTRY REQUIRED BY  |
+-----+---------------------------+--------------------+
| 01  | Date of access            | Custodian          |
| 02  | Time of access            | Custodian          |
| 03  | Record series code        | Custodian          |
| 04  | Requesting position title | Requesting party   |
| 05  | Purpose-of-access code    | Requesting party   |
| 06  | Record medium             | Custodian          |
| 07  | Return or disposal date   | Custodian or agent |
| 08  | Custodian initials        | Custodian          |
