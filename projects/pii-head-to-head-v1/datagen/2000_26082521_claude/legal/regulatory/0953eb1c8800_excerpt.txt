DEPARTMENT OF LABOR — DIVISION OF EMPLOYMENT SERVICES
RULE 60-3.09  SAFEGUARDING OF APPLICANT DATA BY LICENSED AGENCIES

    ADOPTED.............. 11/14/1996
    AMENDED.............. 03/02/1998
    AUTHORITY............ 14 Admin. Code ch. 22, sec. 22-103(a)
    APPLIES TO........... Class A and Class C agency licensees

  .09(1)  Each licensee shall maintain a current Access Roster listing, by
          position title only, every individual authorized to retrieve
          Class II, Class III, or Class VI information. Entry by name is
          prohibited.

  .09(2)  The Records Custodian shall reconcile the Access Roster against
          the active payroll register not less than quarterly and shall
          note the reconciliation upon Form ES-114, blocks 4 through 9.

  .09(3)  Terminals capable of displaying personal information shall be
          positioned so that the display is not visible from any public
          counter, and shall be secured by password of not fewer than six
          characters, changed not less than every ninety days.

  .09(4)  Personal information carried off premises for the purpose of a
          client-site placement shall be limited to Class I and to the
          skills summary. Removal of any other identifier category
          requires the prior written approval of the Records Custodian,
          recorded upon the Off-Premises Log.

  .09(5)  Failure to maintain the Access Roster or the Off-Premises Log is
          grounds for suspension of licensure under Rule 60-1.22.
