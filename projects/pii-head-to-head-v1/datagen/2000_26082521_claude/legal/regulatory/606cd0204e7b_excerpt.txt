ADMINISTRATIVE REGULATION AR-114
SAFEGUARDING OF PERSONAL INFORMATION IN AUTHORITY RECORD SYSTEMS
Adopted by resolution of the Board; issued under the authority of Title 9, Chapter 4 and Rule 17-3.012

1.0 PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 This regulation prescribes the administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to be applied by every department of the Authority to record systems containing personal information, and assigns responsibility for the discharge of the statutory duties of the holder of records.

1.2 This regulation applies to the record systems inventoried in Appendix A, including the Reduced Fare Program file, the Paratransit Eligibility file, the Registered Fare Media Account file, the Employee Personnel and Payroll file, the Claims and Incident file, the Contractor Access Credential file, and the Closed Circuit Surveillance archive.

2.0 RESPONSIBILITY

2.1 The Records Custodian is accountable to the Chief Executive Officer for compliance with this regulation and shall maintain the register of record systems required by § 9-405, recording for each system the System Name, System Owner (by position title), Categories of Individuals Covered, Categories of Data Elements Maintained, Routine Uses, Retention Period, Storage Location, and Access Authority.

2.2 Each System Owner shall certify annually to the Records Custodian that the entries pertaining to that owner's system remain accurate and that access authorizations have been reviewed.

3.0 COLLECTION AND MINIMIZATION

3.1 No department shall collect a data element unless the element is necessary to a function the department is expressly authorized to perform. In particular, the field labelled Social Security Number shall not be collected, displayed, or printed except where its collection is required by federal or State law, and where so required only the last four characters shall be displayed on any screen, report, or correspondence.

3.2 Application forms and enrolment screens shall bear the notice prescribed by § 9-406, stating the authority for collection, the principal purposes, the routine uses, and whether the supply of each data element is mandatory or voluntary.

4.0 ACCESS CONTROL

4.1 Access to a record system shall be granted on the principle of least privilege, upon the written approval of the System Owner, and shall be withdrawn within one business day of the separation or transfer of the person concerned.

4.2 Each access to the fields ACCOUNT_REF, CARD_SERIAL, TRIP_BOOKING_REF, and PICKUP_ZONE shall be recorded in an audit log capturing User Identifier, Terminal Identifier, Function Invoked, Record Accessed, and Date and Time of Access. Audit logs shall be reviewed monthly by the Deputy Records Custodian and retained for three years.

4.3 No record containing a direct identifier shall be removed from Authority premises, transmitted over a public network, or written to portable media otherwise than in encrypted form and pursuant to a written authorization filed with the Records Custodian.

5.0 DISCLOSURE

5.1 Disclosure to a person other than the individual concerned shall be made only pursuant to a routine use, a written consent, a court order, or a lawful demand of a law enforcement agency made in writing and signed by an officer of appropriate rank. The Records Custodian shall maintain a disclosure accounting for each such disclosure, recording the Date of Disclosure, Nature of Information Disclosed, Purpose, and Recipient Agency or Class of Recipient.
