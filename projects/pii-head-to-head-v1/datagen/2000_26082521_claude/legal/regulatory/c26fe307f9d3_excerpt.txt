TITLE 12 — TRADE AND CONSUMER PROTECTION
CHAPTER 118 — PROTECTION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD BY DISPENSING ESTABLISHMENTS

Sec. 8. Prohibited practices; enforcement; penalties.

(a) Prohibited practices. No holder of records subject to this chapter shall (1) disclose a protected record, or permit the inspection of a protected record, except as authorized by section 5 of this chapter; (2) use any identifying particular for the purpose of soliciting the purchase of any product or service, whether by mail, telephone, or electronic means, unless the individual has given prior written authorization upon a form separate from any other document and revocable at will; (3) sell, lease, license, barter, or otherwise transfer for consideration any list, index, or extract derived from protected records other than aggregate data; or (4) require, as a condition of dispensing, that an individual furnish a social security account number, where an alternative internal patient file number is sufficient for the establishment's operational purposes.

(b) Duty of the holder. Every holder shall establish and maintain administrative, physical, and technical safeguards reasonably designed to protect against the unauthorized access, alteration, destruction, or disclosure of protected records. Such safeguards shall include, at a minimum, the assignment of individual terminal access codes to each employee whose duties require access; the periodic revocation of access codes upon termination or reassignment; the physical securing of hard-copy files and of media containing archived data during hours in which the establishment is not staffed by a licensed pharmacist; and the destruction of discarded printouts by shredding, pulping, or incineration.

(c) Civil penalty. Any holder who violates subsection (a) of this section shall be liable for a civil penalty of not more than two thousand five hundred dollars for each violation. Each protected record disclosed in violation of this chapter shall constitute a separate violation, except that where a single act of transmission results in the disclosure of multiple records maintained under a common processing system, the maximum aggregate penalty for that act shall not exceed fifty thousand dollars.

(d) Willful violation. Any officer, employee, or agent of a holder who knowingly and for the purpose of pecuniary gain discloses a protected record in violation of this chapter, or who obtains a protected record under false pretenses, shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and upon conviction shall be fined not more than ten thousand dollars, or imprisoned for not more than one year, or both.

(e) Private right of action. An individual aggrieved by a violation of this chapter may bring a civil action in a court of competent jurisdiction for actual damages, or five hundred dollars per violation, whichever is greater, together with reasonable attorney fees and costs. The remedies provided by this subsection are in addition to, and not in lieu of, any other remedy available at law or in equity.

(f) Limitation. No action under this section shall be commenced more than two years after the date on which the aggrieved individual discovered, or in the exercise of reasonable diligence should have discovered, the violation complained of.
