ADMINISTRATIVE CODE
DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC INSTRUCTION
RULE SERIES 340 — SAFEGUARDING OF EDUCATION RECORDS
Rules 340.1710 through 340.1716 (excerpt)

R 340.1710 Scope; construction.
  (1) These rules prescribe the minimum administrative, physical, and technical safeguards required of a covered agency that holds protected education information.
  (2) These rules shall be construed to impose a continuing duty. A single act of compliance does not discharge the duty for subsequent periods.

R 340.1711 Definitions.
  (1) "Covered agency" means a school district, public school academy, regional educational service agency, or a vendor performing an institutional function under written contract with any of the foregoing.
  (2) "Protected education information" means a pupil record that contains one or more elements in any of the following identifier categories:
      (a) Category A — Direct identifiers: legal surname; legal given name; former or alternate name; state assigned student identifier; local student identification number; social security number or any part of a social security number; taxpayer identification number.
      (b) Category B — Contact identifiers: residential street address; mailing address; household telephone number; mobile telephone number; personal electronic mail address; emergency contact name and telephone number.
      (c) Category C — Demographic identifiers: date of birth; place of birth; sex; race and ethnicity code; primary language of the household; immigration or visa classification.
      (d) Category D — Program and status identifiers: free and reduced-price meal eligibility flag; special education eligibility category; Section 504 accommodation code; English learner status code; migrant status code; foster care placement indicator; homeless status indicator; disciplinary incident number.
      (e) Category E — Health and biometric identifiers: immunization record; medication administration log entry; health condition or diagnosis code; individual health plan number; fingerprint, retinal, or facial geometry template; voice print.
      (f) Category F — Credential and account identifiers: network account username; authentication credential or password hash; device identifier assigned to a pupil; bank account or routing number held for activity refunds; payment card account number.
  (3) "Breach" means the acquisition, access, use, or disclosure of protected education information in a manner not permitted by these rules that compromises the security or privacy of that information.

R 340.1712 Minimum safeguards.
  (1) A covered agency shall do all of the following:
      (a) Maintain a written inventory identifying each system of record, the identifier categories stored in it, and the position title of the individual accountable for it.
      (b) Restrict access to protected education information to employees whose assigned duties require the access, and document the basis for each grant of access by position title.
      (c) Require unique authentication credentials for each user account. Shared or generic accounts are prohibited on systems containing Category A, E, or F elements.
      (d) Encrypt Category A, E, and F elements at rest and in transit, using a method conforming to the standard published by the department.
      (e) Review access rights not less than semiannually and terminate access within 1 business day after separation or reassignment.
  (2) A covered agency shall obtain from each vendor a written undertaking that the vendor will implement safeguards not less protective than those required by this rule and will return or destroy protected education information at the conclusion of the engagement.

R 340.1713 Retention and destruction.
  (1) Protected education information shall be retained only for the period prescribed in the applicable retention and disposal schedule.
  (2) Destruction shall render the information unreadable and unreconstructable. A certificate of destruction shall be executed by the Records Custodian and retained for 3 years, and shall identify the record series, the identifier categories destroyed, the volume, and the method employed. The certificate shall not reproduce any element of the destroyed information.
