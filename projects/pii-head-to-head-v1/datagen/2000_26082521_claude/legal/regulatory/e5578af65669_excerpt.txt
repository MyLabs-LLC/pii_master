MEMORANDUM

TO:      Records Custodians, All Offices
FROM:    Office of Corporate Compliance
RE:      Rule 214, Subchapter E — Retention and Destruction (excerpt), with
         Company retention grid
CC:      Underwriting Counsel; Internal Audit

Subchapter E text:

  214.70  Minimum retention. A holder of record shall retain each covered record
          for the period prescribed by the Department. No covered record shall be
          destroyed before expiration of the applicable period, nor while subject
          to litigation hold, examination or subpoena.

  214.71  Method of destruction. Covered records containing identifiers under
          Section 214.22(a), (b) or (c) shall be destroyed by cross-cut
          shredding, pulping, incineration, or, in the case of magnetic media,
          by degaussing or overwriting. Discard in ordinary refuse is
          prohibited.

  214.72  Certificate of destruction. Destruction shall be evidenced by a
          certificate executed by the Records Custodian and retained for six
          years. The certificate shall show only: file series; inclusive file
          number range; volume destroyed; method; date; officer title; witness
          title. It shall not reproduce any identifier.

  214.73  Third-party contractors. A contractor performing destruction shall be
          bound by written agreement to this subchapter and shall furnish the
          certificate required by Section 214.72.

Notes:
  1. Periods run from the later of file closing or final disbursement.
  2. Series not listed default to seven years pending grid amendment.
  3. Draft, working and duplicate copies are destroyed at file closing and are
     not separately scheduled. They remain covered records until destroyed.
  4. Requests to extend retention beyond schedule shall be made in writing to
     this office and shall state the file series and the reason. Do not annotate
     the grid at the branch.
