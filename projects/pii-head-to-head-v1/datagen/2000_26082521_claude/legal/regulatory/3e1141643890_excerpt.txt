RULE 12-40-.106  UNAUTHORIZED DISCLOSURE; PENALTIES
(Excerpt. Read in conjunction with Rules .103 and .105.)

A.  PROHIBITED ACTS
    A holder of record commits a violation of this chapter who:
      1. discloses personal information to a person not entitled to receive it;
      2. obtains personal information under false pretenses or for a purpose
         unrelated to assigned duties;
      3. removes a record containing personal information from the office of
         record without written authorization;
      4. fails to record a disclosure in the log required by Rule .103(e);
      5. destroys a record before expiration of its retention class; or
      6. permits another person to use an access authorization issued to the
         holder.

B.  ADMINISTRATIVE PENALTIES
    1. A first violation shall be grounds for written reprimand and withdrawal of
       access authorization pending retraining.
    2. A second violation, or a first violation committed knowingly, shall be
       grounds for suspension without pay or for dismissal under the applicable
       personnel rule.
    3. A contractor in violation shall be barred from further access, and the
       contracting officer shall be notified for action under the contract's
       compliance clause.
    4. Penalties under this subsection are in addition to, and not in lieu of,
       liability under subsections C and D.

C.  CIVIL LIABILITY
    An individual aggrieved by a violation may bring an action for actual damages,
    for reasonable attorney fees, and for injunctive relief. An action shall be
    commenced within two years after the date on which the violation was
    discovered or reasonably should have been discovered.

D.  CRIMINAL PENALTY
    A person who knowingly discloses personal information in violation of
    subsection A(1) or who obtains personal information in violation of
    subsection A(2) is guilty of a misdemeanor punishable by a fine of not more
    than five thousand dollars, by imprisonment for not more than one year, or by
    both.

E.  POSTING
    The text of this rule shall be posted in each office of record in a place
    visible to holders of record and shall be furnished to each holder at the time
    the acknowledgment required by Rule .105(2)(c) is signed.
