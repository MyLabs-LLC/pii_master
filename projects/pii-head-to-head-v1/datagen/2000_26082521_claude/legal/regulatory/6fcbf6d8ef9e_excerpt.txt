COUNTY BOARD OF HEALTH — RULE 7
RETENTION, DISPOSITION, AND DESTRUCTION OF RECORDS CONTAINING PERSONAL IDENTIFYING INFORMATION

7.1  APPLICABILITY

  » This Rule applies to all records identified in the inventory required by Administrative Rule 40-11.02(1), without regard to the medium in which such records are maintained.

7.2  RETENTION SCHEDULE — MINIMUM PERIODS

  » Communicable Disease Investigation File .......... 10 years from case closure
  » Immunization Register Entry ...................... until individual attains age 26
  » Environmental Health Complaint File .............. 6 years from disposition
  » Vital Records Application File ................... 7 years from date of application
  » Clinic Encounter File ............................ 10 years from last encounter
  » Disclosure Log ................................... 6 years from last entry
  » Confidentiality Acknowledgment ................... 3 years after separation

  » No record shall be destroyed prior to the expiration of its retention period, nor while the same is subject to a pending request for access, an audit, an investigation, or any litigation hold of which the Records Custodian has received notice.

7.3  METHOD OF DESTRUCTION

  » Paper records shall be destroyed by cross-cut shredding, pulping, or incineration. Disposal in ordinary refuse or recycling receptacles, whether or not the records have been torn, is expressly prohibited.
  » Magnetic media shall be degaussed or overwritten not fewer than three times; optical media shall be physically destroyed.
  » Where destruction is performed by a contractor, the contract shall contain a confidentiality clause, and the contractor shall furnish a Certificate of Destruction stating the date, the volume, the method employed, and the location of destruction.

7.4  CERTIFICATION AND LOG

  » The Records Custodian shall maintain a Destruction Log recording, for each destruction event: Record Series Title; Record System of Origin; Categories of Data Elements Contained (by label only); Inclusive Dates of the Series; Volume; Authorizing Schedule Item; Method of Destruction; Date of Destruction; Title of Witnessing Official.
  » The Destruction Log shall be retained permanently.

7.5  TRANSFER OF CUSTODY

  » Upon reorganization, program transfer, or contract termination, custody shall not pass to any successor until the successor has executed a written undertaking to observe this Rule and Administrative Rule 40-11 in their entirety.
  » An itemized transfer manifest shall be prepared in duplicate, signed by the transferring Records Custodian and the receiving official, and retained by each for six years.
