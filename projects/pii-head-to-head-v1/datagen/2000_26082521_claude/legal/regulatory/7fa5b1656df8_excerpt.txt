MUNICIPAL CODE OF ORDINANCES
CHAPTER 2.68 — PROTECTION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION IN UTILITY RECORDS
(Adopted by Ordinance; codified 2016)

2.68.010  PURPOSE.
  To implement Ch. 812, Art. 3 of the State Code within the utility
  enterprise funds of the City and to fix responsibility for the
  security of customer records.

2.68.020  DEFINITIONS.
  A. "Utility record" means any record generated or maintained in
     connection with water, sewer, electric, or solid waste service,
     including applications for service, billing records, payment
     records, service orders, meter data, and collection files.
  B. "Identifier category" has the meaning given in Ch. 812,
     § 812.301(b), Categories A through H.
  C. "Custodian" means the Records Custodian designated under
     2.68.030.
  D. "Authorized user" means an employee, temporary worker, or
     contractor whose assigned duties require access, as shown on the
     access authorization roster maintained by position title.

2.68.030  DESIGNATION OF CUSTODIAN.
  The Utility Director shall designate in writing a Records Custodian
  and one alternate. The designation shall be filed with the City
  Clerk and reviewed each fiscal year.

2.68.040  DUTIES OF THE CUSTODIAN.
  A. Maintain the record inventory required by 2.68.050.
  B. Approve or deny each request for access to Category A, C, E, F,
     or G elements, and record the disposition of each request.
  C. Cause an annual review of the access authorization roster and
     remove positions no longer requiring access.
  D. Report any suspected unauthorized acquisition to the Utility
     Director and the City Attorney not later than the next business
     day.
  E. Cause each authorized user to complete annual training and
     execute a confidentiality acknowledgment.

2.68.050  RECORD INVENTORY.
  The inventory shall be maintained in the following columnar form:

  Record Series ID | Series Title | Source System | Identifier
  Categories | Custodian Position Title | Storage Location Type |
  Encryption Status | Retention Period | Disposition Method | Last
  Review Date

  No column shall be omitted. Where an identifier category is not
  present in a series, the entry shall read "None."

2.68.060  DISCLOSURE RESTRICTED.
  A. Personal information in utility records shall not be released
     except: (1) to the customer of record or a person holding
     written authorization; (2) pursuant to lawful subpoena, warrant,
     or court order; (3) to a collection agent under contract
     containing the assurances required by Ch. 812, § 812.303(e);
     or (4) as otherwise required by law.
  B. Aggregate or de-identified data may be released for planning or
     research purposes upon approval of the Utility Director.
  C. A log of disclosures under subsection A(2) and A(3) shall be
     kept for five (5) years.

2.68.070  VIOLATION — PENALTY.
  A. Any employee violating this Chapter is subject to discipline up
     to and including termination.
  B. Any person violating 2.68.060 shall be subject to a fine of not
     less than two hundred dollars ($200) nor more than one thousand
     dollars ($1,000) for each offense. Each day a violation
     continues is a separate offense.
  C. Penalties imposed under this Chapter are in addition to any
     civil penalty assessed by the State under Ch. 812, § 812.308.
