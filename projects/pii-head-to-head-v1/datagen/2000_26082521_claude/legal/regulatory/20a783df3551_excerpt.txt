INTERNAL MEMORANDUM — REGULATORY REFERENCE
DEPARTMENT OF FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS
ADMINISTRATIVE RULE 4-2-13.03 — SAFEGUARDING OF CUSTOMER INFORMATION
(EXCERPT — SUBSECTIONS .03(1) THROUGH .03(4))

.03(1) SCOPE. This rule applies to every licensed escrow agent and every title insurance agency maintaining customer records within the State.

.03(2) PROTECTED IDENTIFIER CATEGORIES. The following are protected identifier categories:

    [ ] Government-issued numeric identifiers
    [ ] Financial account and payment card identifiers
    [ ] Access credentials and authentication elements
    [ ] Biometric and physiological identifiers
    [ ] Health and insurance coverage identifiers
    [ ] Geolocation and residence identifiers
    [ ] Employment and compensation identifiers

.03(3) OBLIGATIONS OF THE HOLDER. Each licensee shall:
    (a) designate an employee, by title, to coordinate the information security program;
    (b) identify reasonably foreseeable internal and external risks;
    (c) restrict access to files, whether physical or electronic, to personnel having a business need;
    (d) review the program not less than annually and upon any material change in business practices.

.03(4) EXAMINATION. Records demonstrating compliance shall be produced upon demand of an examiner. Failure to produce is itself a violation, separately assessable under Rule 4-2-19.
