MEMO — FOR THE FILE

ARTICLE 9. ENFORCEMENT; PENALTIES.

9.1 The Commissioner may examine the books and records of any holder of records subject to licensure under the Title Insurance Act to determine compliance with Articles 5 through 7.

9.2 A violation of this Act constitutes an unfair or deceptive act or practice and is enforceable as such.

9.3 Civil penalties shall be assessed as follows:

 (a) For each violation, not more than $2,500.
 (b) For a knowing or willful violation, not more than $10,000 per violation.
 (c) For purposes of this Section, each record containing personal information that is subject to a violation constitutes a separate violation, provided that the aggregate penalty for violations arising from a single incident shall not exceed $500,000.

9.4 In addition to civil penalties, the Commissioner may suspend, revoke, or refuse to renew the license or appointment of any title insurance agent or escrow agent found to be in violation.

9.5 In determining the amount of any penalty, the Commissioner shall consider: the degree of culpability; any history of prior violations; the extent of actual harm; the good faith of the holder of records; the adequacy of the written program maintained under Section 5.1; and the promptness of notification under Article 7.

9.6 A holder of records that establishes it maintained and complied with a written program conforming to Section 5.3 at the time of the incident shall be entitled to a rebuttable presumption of reasonable conduct.

9.7 Nothing in this Article limits the authority of the Attorney General.

Note for the file: Section 9.6 presumption is the operative reason Compliance requires the annual program review and dated training rosters. Absent current documentation, the presumption is unavailable. Route questions to the Records Custodian.
