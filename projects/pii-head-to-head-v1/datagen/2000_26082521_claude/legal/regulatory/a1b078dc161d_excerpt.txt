TITLE 45, CHAPTER 7, SUBCHAPTER A — DEFINITIONS
§ 7.101 Construction of Terms
§ 7.102 Categories of Identifying Information

Compliance reference sheet — Billing Office. Terms below are controlling for all Subchapters of this Chapter.

§ 7.101 — General
  • (1) "Protected information" means information, in any medium, that relates to the past, present, or future physical or mental condition of an individual, to the provision of care to an individual, or to payment for such care, and that identifies the individual or with respect to which there is a reasonable basis to believe the individual can be identified.
  • (2) "Holder of records" means any facility, practitioner, clearinghouse, billing agent, collection agent, or contractor that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits protected information, whether or not compensation is received.
  • (3) "Disclosure" means the release, transfer, provision of access to, or divulging in any manner of protected information outside the entity holding the information.
  • (4) "Records Custodian" means the individual designated under § 7.402(a) who is accountable for the safeguards required by this Chapter.
  • (5) "Workforce member" means an employee, volunteer, trainee, agency staff member, or other person whose conduct in the performance of work is under the direct control of the holder of records, whether or not paid.

§ 7.102 — Identifier Categories
Information falling within any of the following categories shall be treated as identifying for purposes of §§ 7.301 through 7.612:
  • (a) Name, including maiden, former, alias, and guarantor name fields.
  • (b) Geographic subdivisions smaller than a State, including street address, city, county, precinct, and postal code.
  • (c) All elements of date directly related to an individual, including Date of Birth, Date of Admission, Date of Discharge, Date of Service, and Date of Death.
  • (d) Telephone Number, Facsimile Number, and Electronic Mail Address.
  • (e) Social Security Number and Taxpayer Identification Number.
  • (f) Medical Record Number, Encounter Number, and Account Number.
  • (g) Health Plan Beneficiary Number, Subscriber Identification Number, Group Number, and Claim Control Number.
  • (h) Certificate or License Number; Vehicle or Device Serial Number.
  • (i) Web Universal Resource Locator, Internet Protocol Address, and biometric template.
  • (j) Any other unique identifying number, characteristic, or code assigned by the holder of records.
