TITLE 45, CHAPTER 7, SUBCHAPTER D — OBLIGATIONS OF HOLDERS OF RECORDS
§§ 7.501 – 7.508
Excerpt maintained for reference by the Patient Financial Services Records Custodian.

§ 7.501 Duty of Care
  - (1) A holder of records shall exercise reasonable care to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of all protected information within its custody, without regard to the medium in which the information is held.
  - (2) The obligations of this Subchapter attach at the point the information is received and continue until lawful destruction is certified.

§ 7.502 Written Program
  - (1) Each holder of records shall maintain a written information protection program, approved by the governing body and reviewed not less than annually.
  - (2) The program shall address, at a minimum:
      -- (i) administrative controls, including role assignment, workforce sanction, and vendor oversight;
      -- (ii) physical controls, including facility access, workstation placement, and secure storage of paper ledgers and remittance batches;
      -- (iii) technical controls, including unique user identification, automatic session termination, audit logging, and encryption of protected information at rest and in transit.

§ 7.503 Minimum Necessary
  - (1) A holder of records shall limit any use, disclosure, or request of protected information to the minimum necessary to accomplish the intended purpose.
  - (2) Paragraph (1) shall not apply to disclosures to the individual who is the subject of the record, to disclosures required by law, or to disclosures for treatment purposes.

§ 7.504 Business Associates and Collection Agents
  - (1) A holder of records shall not disclose protected information to a billing agent, collection agent, or other contractor absent a written agreement obligating that party to safeguards not less protective than those required by this Chapter.
  - (2) The agreement shall require the contractor to report to the Records Custodian any acquisition, access, use, or disclosure not permitted by the agreement, without unreasonable delay and in no case later than five business days after discovery.

§ 7.505 Accounting of Disclosures
  - (1) A holder of records shall maintain, for six years, a record of each disclosure not made for treatment, payment, or health care operations, capturing the following fields: Date of Disclosure; Recipient Name and Address Field; Description of Information Disclosed; Statement of Purpose; and Authorizing Role.
  - (2) The record required by paragraph (1) shall be furnished to the individual on request, at no charge for the first request in any twelve-month period.

§ 7.506 Notification of Unauthorized Acquisition
  - (1) Upon discovery of the unauthorized acquisition of unencrypted protected information, the holder of records shall notify each affected individual without unreasonable delay and in no case later than sixty days after discovery.
  - (2) Where the number of affected individuals exceeds five hundred, the holder of records shall additionally notify the Department and prominent media serving the affected area within the same period.
  - (3) Notification shall describe the categories of information involved by reference to § 7.102 and shall not itself contain protected information beyond that necessary to identify the recipient.

§ 7.507 Retention
  - (1) Billing records shall be retained for not less than seven years from the date of final adjudication of the account.

§ 7.508 Non-Waiver
  - (1) No agreement between a holder of records and an individual shall operate to waive the requirements of this Subchapter.
