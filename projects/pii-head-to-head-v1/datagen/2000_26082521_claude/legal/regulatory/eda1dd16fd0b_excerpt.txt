PART 2 — APPLICABLE PROVISIONS
  § 1  This General Order is issued under Ch. 812, Art. 3, and applies
       to every municipally owned utility system subject to the
       Commission's record-keeping jurisdiction.
  § 2  Definitions in Ch. 812, § 812.301 are incorporated by
       reference. "Reportable incident" means any unauthorized
       acquisition of, or unauthorized access to, unencrypted personal
       information that compromises its security or confidentiality.

PART 5 — ENFORCEMENT
  § 5.1  Failure to file this certification within thirty (30) days of
         the close of the reporting period shall subject the utility
         to a forfeiture of one hundred dollars ($100) per day.
  § 5.2  A false certification is a violation of Ch. 812, § 812.308(b)
         and may be referred to the Attorney General.
  § 5.3  The Commission may direct a compliance audit at the
         utility's expense upon two (2) or more items marked "No" in
         successive periods.
