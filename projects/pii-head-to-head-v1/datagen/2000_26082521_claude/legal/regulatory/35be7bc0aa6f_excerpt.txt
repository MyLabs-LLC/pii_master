TITLE 44 — PUBLIC RECORDS AND PERSONAL INFORMATION
CHAPTER 9 — MUNICIPAL AND PUBLIC UTILITIES

§ 44-9-203.  Notice of unauthorized acquisition of computerized records.

     (a)  A municipal utility that owns or licenses computerized data
          including protected identifiers shall disclose any breach of
          the security of the system to any resident whose unencrypted
          protected identifier was, or is reasonably believed to have
          been, acquired by an unauthorized person.

     (b)  Disclosure shall be made in the most expedient time possible
          and without unreasonable delay, consistent with the
          legitimate needs of law enforcement and with any measures
          necessary to determine the scope of the breach and restore
          the reasonable integrity of the system.

     (c)  The notice shall state, at a minimum:
          (1)  the categories of protected identifiers involved;
          (2)  the date or range of dates of the acquisition, if known;
          (3)  the steps the utility has taken in response;
          (4)  a telephone number and mailing point at which the
               recipient may obtain further information; and
          (5)  advice that the recipient may place a fraud alert with a
               consumer reporting agency.

     (d)  Notice may be delayed if a law enforcement agency determines
          in writing that notice will impede a criminal investigation.

     (e)  A utility that maintains its own notification procedures as
          part of an information security policy is in compliance with
          this section if it notifies affected residents in accordance
          with that policy.

     (f)  A person injured by a violation of this section may institute
          a civil action to recover damages.
