ARTICLE IX. SECURITY OF PERSONAL INFORMATION; NOTIFICATION OF UNAUTHORIZED ACQUISITION

    I.  APPLICATION

        A.  This Article governs the electronic and physical security of Personal
            Information maintained by the Municipal Utility and by any depository,
            service bureau, or microfilm vendor acting on its behalf.

        B.  For purposes of this Article, "Personal Information" bears the meaning
            assigned in § 14-701(b) of the City Code.

    II. SAFEGUARDS REQUIRED

        A.  Physical.

            1.  Paper records containing Personal Information shall be stored in locked
                cabinets or in a room secured after business hours.
            2.  Terminals affording access to the customer master file shall not be
                positioned so that the display is visible from a public counter.
            3.  Magnetic media removed from the data center shall be logged out and in,
                the log recording Media Serial Number, Date Removed, Destination, and
                Name of Authorized Employee.

        B.  Logical.

            1.  Each authorized user shall be assigned a unique User Identifier, and
                shared or group identifiers are prohibited.
            2.  Passwords shall be changed not less frequently than every ninety (90)
                days and shall not be posted, shared, or stored in plain text.
            3.  The system shall record, for each inquiry into a field bearing a
                restriction code, the User Identifier, Terminal Identifier, Date and
                Time of Access, and Account Number Accessed. Such access logs shall be
                retained for two (2) years.

    III. NOTIFICATION

        A.  The Records Custodian shall report to the Director of Utilities, in writing
            and within twenty-four (24) hours of discovery, any loss, theft, or
            unauthorized acquisition of Personal Information.

        B.  The report shall state the System Name, the Fields Affected, the Estimated
            Number of Accounts Affected, the Date of Discovery, and the corrective
            measures taken or proposed.

        C.  The Director shall determine whether notice to affected customers is
            warranted and shall report each determination to the governing body at its
            next regular meeting.
