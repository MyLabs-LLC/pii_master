RULE 900 — PERSONAL INFORMATION SAFEGUARDS
OFFICE OF THE COUNTY SUPERINTENDENT
ANNUAL SELF-CERTIFICATION INSTRUMENT — SCHOOL DISTRICT LEVEL
Return to the Office of the County Superintendent not later than October 15.

SECTION A — DESIGNATIONS AND NOTICES
[ ] 900.01  Records Custodian designated; designation on file with the County Office.
[ ] 900.02  Annual notice of rights published in the student handbook and in the language of the home where required.
[ ] 900.03  Directory information fields enumerated in the annual notice; opt-out form furnished (Fields: Name, Grade Level, Participation in Activities, Weight and Height of Athletic Team Members, Dates of Attendance, Awards Received).
[ ] 900.04  Notice of Record Systems posted at the district office during business hours.

SECTION B — PHYSICAL AND TECHNICAL CONTROLS
[ ] 900.10  Record storage areas locked outside business hours; unescorted access limited to authorized position titles.
[ ] 900.11  Terminals displaying personal information positioned so that screens are not visible from public counters.
[ ] 900.12  Passwords are unique to the individual, changed not less than every ninety (90) days, and not shared, written, or posted.
[ ] 900.13  Access credentials revoked on the effective date of separation, transfer, or expiration of a volunteer clearance.
[ ] 900.14  Backup media stored offsite in a locked container; transport log maintained (Media Identifier, Date Out, Date In, Carrier, Receiving Title).
[ ] 900.15  Printers used for reports containing Category I fields are located in a controlled area; output retrieved immediately.
[ ] 900.16  Discarded paper bearing personal information is cross-cut shredded or placed in a locked destruction container.

SECTION C — DISCLOSURE CONTROL
[ ] 900.20  Disclosure Log maintained and current for each record system.
[ ] 900.21  Written consents filed with the corresponding record; consent form states Data Elements to Be Released, Recipient, Purpose, Expiration Date.
[ ] 900.22  Requests for personal information from law enforcement referred to the Superintendent or designee before any release.
[ ] 900.23  Transfer of records to a receiving school effected by certified mail or bonded courier; transmittal receipt filed.
[ ] 900.24  Research requests reviewed for aggregation thresholds; no cell reported with fewer than ten (10) individuals.

SECTION D — TRAINING AND REVIEW
[ ] 900.30  All covered persons completed annual confidentiality training; roster retained (Position Title, Date Completed, Instructor Title).
[ ] 900.31  Confidentiality acknowledgment executed by each covered person at hire and annually thereafter.
[ ] 900.32  Internal review of two (2) record systems completed during the fiscal year; findings and corrective actions documented.
[ ] 900.33  Prior year deficiencies closed, or a corrective action plan with completion dates is attached.

SECTION E — CERTIFICATION
[ ] 900.40  Each item above is marked complete, or an exception statement is attached identifying the item number, the reason, and the projected date of compliance.
[ ] 900.41  Instrument signed by the Superintendent and the Records Custodian and dated.

Failure to file this instrument by the stated date shall be reported to the State Board and may result in withholding of apportionment pending compliance.
