ADMINISTRATIVE RULE 12-04
CUSTODIAL DUTIES FOR STUDENT AND EMPLOYEE PERSONAL INFORMATION
Compliance Checklist — Building Level

Section 1. Applicability.
[ ] Rule applies to every school building, central office unit, and off-site storage facility operated by the district.
[ ] Rule applies to paper, electronic, and microform holdings without distinction.

Section 2. Custodial duties. The building administrator shall verify that:
[ ] A Building Records Liaison has been named and the name of the position, not the incumbent, is posted in the main office.
[ ] Cumulative folders are stored in locking cabinets in a room secured outside business hours.
[ ] Screens displaying fields labeled Social Security Number, Date of Birth, or Health Condition Code are positioned away from public sightlines.
[ ] Visitor sign-in sheets do not request the purpose of a records visit in narrative form.
[ ] Records awaiting destruction are held in a locked receptacle marked "Confidential Disposal."

Section 3. Access requests.
[ ] Each request is entered in the Records Access Register with columns: Request Date; Requester Category; Record Series; Field Labels Sought; Disposition; Response Date.
[ ] Inspection by a parent or eligible student is scheduled within forty-five (45) days.
[ ] Redaction is applied where a record contains identifiers of a person other than the subject.

Section 4. Enforcement.
[ ] Deficiencies noted at inspection are corrected within thirty (30) days.
[ ] Uncorrected deficiencies are reported to the Superintendent and may result in withholding of the building's discretionary allocation.
