§ 12-104. Definitions.

As used in this chapter, unless the context clearly requires otherwise, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them in this section. (a) "Personal information" means an individual's first name or first initial and last name in combination with any one or more of the following data elements, where either the name or the data element is not encrypted, redacted, or otherwise rendered unusable: Social Security number; taxpayer identification number; driver's license number or state identification card number; passport number; financial account number, credit card number, or debit card number, alone or in combination with any required security code, access code, personal identification number, or password permitting access to the account; medical record number; health plan member identifier or group number; biometric identifier, including fingerprint, voice print, retina image, or iris image; and date of birth. (b) "Holder of records" means any insurer, producer, third-party administrator, or affiliate thereof that acquires, maintains, uses, or disposes of records containing personal information in the ordinary course of business, whether such records are maintained in paper, electronic, or other form. (c) "Redacted" means the alteration or truncation of a data element such that no more than the last four digits or characters remain accessible. (d) "Records Custodian" means the officer or employee designated by a holder of records under § 12-107(b).
