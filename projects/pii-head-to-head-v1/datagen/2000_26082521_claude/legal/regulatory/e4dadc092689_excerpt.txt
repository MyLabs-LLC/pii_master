ARTICLE 4A — PROTECTION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

§ 4A-101. Definitions.

As used in this Article, and in any rule adopted under it, the following terms carry the meanings stated in this section unless the context plainly requires otherwise. "Personal information" means an individual's first name or first initial and last name in combination with one or more of the following data elements, where either the name or the data element is not encrypted, redacted, or otherwise rendered unreadable: Social Security number; driver's license number or state identification card number; passport number or alien registration number; financial account number, credit card number, or debit card number, alone or in combination with any security code, access code, personal identification number, or password permitting access to the account; and health insurance member or subscriber number. Date of birth, standing alone, is not personal information for purposes of this Article, but is personal information when combined with any element listed above.

"Holder of records" means any person, partnership, corporation, association, or other legal entity that receives, maintains, processes, or otherwise has access to personal information in connection with the provision of goods or services or in connection with employment, including an employment agency, a temporary help service, and a professional employer organization. "Records Custodian" means the individual designated under § 4A-104 to maintain and supervise the program required by this Article.
