TITLE 41 — PUBLIC UTILITIES AND CARRIERS
ARTICLE 3 — CONFIDENTIALITY OF CUSTOMER RECORDS
(Excerpt: §§ 41-3-101 through 41-3-105)

    § 41-3-101. Definitions.
        As used in this article, unless the context otherwise requires:
            (1) "Customer record" means any writing, drawing, map, print, card, tape, magnetic or optical medium, or other documentary material, regardless of physical form, that is created or received by a municipal utility in connection with an application for service, the provision of service, or the collection of charges for service.
            (2) "Personal identifier" means any of the following data elements when appearing in a customer record in combination with the customer's first name or first initial and last name:
                (a) Social security number;
                (b) Driver's license number or state identification card number;
                (c) Financial account number, credit card number, or debit card number, alone or in combination with any required security code, access code, or password permitting access to the account;
                (d) Date of birth;
                (e) Mother's maiden name or other authentication response of record.
            (3) "Holder of record" means the municipal utility, its governing board, its officers, its employees, and any agent or contractor to whom customer records are entrusted.
            (4) "Records Custodian" means the officer designated under § 41-3-103(a).
    § 41-3-102. General duty.
        (a) A holder of record shall not disclose a personal identifier except as authorized by this article or by order of a court of competent jurisdiction.
        (b) The duty imposed by subsection (a) survives termination of service and termination of employment.
    § 41-3-103. Administration.
        (a) Each municipal utility shall designate in writing a Records Custodian, who shall maintain the inventory of record series required by § 41-3-104.
        (b) The designation shall be filed with the clerk of the governing body and shall state the position title only.
    § 41-3-105. Penalties.
        (a) Willful disclosure in violation of § 41-3-102 is a misdemeanor punishable by a fine of not more than one thousand dollars ($1,000) per occurrence.
        (b) Each customer record disclosed constitutes a separate occurrence.
        (c) Penalties under this section are in addition to any civil liability and to any disciplinary action available to the appointing authority.
