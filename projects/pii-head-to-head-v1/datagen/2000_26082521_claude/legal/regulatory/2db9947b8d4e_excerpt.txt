ARTICLE 27B. CONFIDENTIALITY OF PHARMACY PATIENT RECORDS

§ 27B-101. Declaration of policy; construction.
It is declared to be the policy of this State that information acquired by a pharmacy permit holder in the course of receiving, compounding, dispensing, or transferring a prescription order, or in the course of submitting a claim for reimbursement to a third-party payor, is acquired in confidence and is held by the permit holder in a fiduciary capacity for the benefit of the individual to whom it pertains. This Article shall be liberally construed to effectuate that policy, and any waiver of the protections afforded herein shall be strictly construed against the party asserting the waiver. Nothing in this Article shall be deemed to abrogate any duty of confidentiality arising under the common law, under the rules of the Board of Pharmacy, or under any contract to which the permit holder is a party.

§ 27B-102. Definitions.
As used in this Article, unless the context clearly requires a different meaning, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them in this section.

(1) "Permit holder" means any person, partnership, corporation, or other legal entity holding a permit to operate a pharmacy issued under Article 4 of this Chapter, and includes each retail outlet operated under a common permit series where a chain of outlets maintains records in a centralized or shared processing system.

(2) "Records custodian" means the individual designated in writing by the permit holder as responsible for the maintenance, security, retrieval, and lawful disclosure of protected records at a given location or within a given processing system, together with any deputy or alternate so designated during the absence of the primary designee.

(3) "Protected record" means any writing, entry, printout, microform, magnetic or optical medium, or electronically stored data set that contains one or more identifying particulars in association with a prescription order, a refill authorization, a therapeutic substitution, a compounding record, a patient profile entry, a counseling notation, a claim for reimbursement, or a record of payment or denial thereof.

(4) "Identifying particulars" means data elements recorded under the following field designations or under substantially equivalent designations, whether or not so captioned in the permit holder's system: patient surname; patient given name; patient middle initial; residential street address; mailing address; residential telephone number; date of birth; sex; social security account number; state driver license number; health plan member identification number; group number; cardholder identification number; person code; prescription serial number; internal patient file number; and any other number, code, or characteristic assigned or applied by the permit holder or by a third-party payor by which the identity of an individual may be ascertained, directly or by reference to other information reasonably available to the recipient.

(5) "Disclosure" means the transfer, release, transmission, display, or provision of access to a protected record, or to any identifying particular contained therein, to any person other than the individual to whom the record pertains or the personal representative of that individual, and includes transmission by facsimile machine, by electronic data interchange, by remote terminal inquiry, and by the physical transfer of magnetic media.

(6) "Aggregate data" means information derived from protected records from which all identifying particulars enumerated in subdivision (4) of this section have been removed, and which cannot reasonably be used, alone or in combination with other information, to identify any individual.
