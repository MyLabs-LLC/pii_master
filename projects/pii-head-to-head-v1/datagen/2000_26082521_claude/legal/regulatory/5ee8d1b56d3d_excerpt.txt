MUNICIPAL UTILITY BOARD
ADMINISTRATIVE RULE — ADOPTED

RULE NO. ................ AR-2004-07
SUBJECT ................. Handling of Customer Personal Identifiers
AUTHORITY ............... State Code Ann. Title 9, ch. 42, § 42.105;
                          Utility Board Resolution 04-22
SUPERSEDES .............. AR-1997-13
ADOPTED ................. April 2004
REVIEW CYCLE ............ Biennial
RESPONSIBLE OFFICER ..... Records Custodian

1.  PURPOSE. To implement § 42.105 within the operating divisions of the
    utility.

2.  SCOPE. All divisions. All media. Includes records held by the collection
    contractor under the master services agreement.

3.  RULE.
    3.1  No personal identifier shall be spoken aloud at a public counter where
         it may be overheard by a person not a party to the transaction.
    3.2  Customer verification at the counter shall be by presentation of
         photographic identification. The clerk shall record verification by
         initialling the service order. The identification number shall not be
         transcribed.
    3.3  Working papers bearing personal identifiers shall be placed in the
         locked shred bin at the end of each shift. Wastebaskets shall not be
         used.
    3.4  Field service orders shall bear the service premises and meter number
         only. Account holder identifiers shall be omitted from the printed
         route sheet.

4.  ENFORCEMENT. Violations shall be reported to the Records Custodian and are
    subject to § 42.108(c).
