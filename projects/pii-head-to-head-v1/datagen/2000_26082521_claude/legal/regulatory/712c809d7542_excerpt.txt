ADMINISTRATIVE CODE — TITLE 22, PART 4
PROTECTION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION IN EDUCATION RECORDS
Excerpt: § 4.11 — Standing Duties of the Records Custodian
Effective July 1, 2004

Prior to the close of each reporting period, the local education agency shall verify each item below and retain the completed verification for three (3) years:

- § 4.11(a) — A Records Custodian has been designated in writing by the Superintendent. An Alternate Custodian has been designated for any absence exceeding ten (10) working days.
- § 4.11(b) — The Master Index of Education Records is current and states, for each file: Student Identification Number, File Location Code, Retention Class, Date of Last Review, Custodian Initials.
- § 4.11(c) — Cumulative folders are stored in a locking cabinet or vault. Keys are logged on the Key Issuance Register (Key Number, Position Title, Date Issued, Date Returned).
- § 4.11(d) — The Disclosure Log records, for every release outside the agency: Date of Request, Requesting Party Category, Statutory Basis, Data Elements Released, Method of Transmittal, Authorizing Signature Block.
- § 4.11(e) — No record containing a Category I identifier has been transmitted by facsimile absent prior telephone confirmation that the receiving device is in a secured area.
- § 4.11(f) — Magnetic media and diskettes bearing personal information are degaussed or physically destroyed before disposal, and the destruction is entered on Form RC-9 (Media Type, Serial or Lot Number, Method, Witness Title).
- § 4.11(g) — Any deficiency identified under this section has been reported to the Superintendent within five (5) working days.
