CHAPTER 41, SUBCHAPTER B — DEFINITIONS AND CONSTRUCTION
§ 41-201. Definitions.
      For purposes of this chapter, unless the context clearly requires
      otherwise —

      (a) "Nonpublic personal information" means information —
            (1) furnished by a natural person to a licensee in connection with
                the solicitation, underwriting, servicing, or adjustment of a
                contract of insurance;
            (2) derived by the licensee from a transaction or examination; or
            (3) obtained from a consumer reporting agency, motor vehicle
                registry, medical provider, or other third-party source.
          The term excludes information lawfully obtained from a publicly
          available source and not combined with any element enumerated in
          subsection (c).

      (b) "Holder of records" means a licensee, an affiliate, a producer of
          record, a managing general agent, a third-party administrator, or an
          independent contractor, that has custody or control of a record.

      (c) "Identifier category" means one of the following classes of data
          element:
            (1) IDENTITY ELEMENTS —
                  (A) Full Name;
                  (B) Former or Maiden Name;
                  (C) Social Security Number;
                  (D) Date of Birth;
                  (E) Place of Birth.
            (2) CONTACT ELEMENTS —
                  (A) Residence Address;
                  (B) Mailing Address;
                  (C) Telephone Number;
                  (D) Electronic Mail Address.
            (3) POLICY ELEMENTS —
                  (A) Policy Number;
                  (B) Certificate or Group Number;
                  (C) Claim Number;
                  (D) Beneficiary Designation.
            (4) FINANCIAL ELEMENTS —
                  (A) Deposit Account Number;
                  (B) Bank Routing Number;
                  (C) Payment Card Number;
                  (D) Income or Net Worth Statement.
            (5) HEALTH ELEMENTS —
                  (A) Diagnosis or Condition;
                  (B) Treatment or Procedure;
                  (C) Prescription Record;
                  (D) Result of Physical or Laboratory Examination.

      (d) "Control" exists where the holder determines the purposes and means
          of processing, irrespective of the physical location of the medium.

      (e) "Authorized purpose" means underwriting, rating, claims adjustment,
          reinsurance, fraud detection, actuarial study, regulatory
          examination, or a purpose to which the individual has consented in
          writing.

§ 41-202. Construction.
      Nothing in this subchapter shall be construed to create a private right
      of action or to limit the examination authority of the Commissioner.
