ARTICLE IV — AFFIRMATIVE DUTIES OF HOLDERS OF RECORDS
Administrative compliance checklist, retained per § 4.14 (Recordkeeping)

§ 4.01. General Duty of Care. Every holder of records shall implement and maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the size and complexity of its operations, the nature and scope of its activities, and the sensitivity of the personal information maintained. In furtherance of the foregoing, each holder of records shall:

( ) Designate a Records Custodian, and one alternate, and record such designation in the register maintained under § 4.14, said register to bear the columns: Designation Date; Role Title; Department; Scope of Authority; Revocation Date.

( ) Conduct, not less frequently than annually, an inventory of all systems, files, and repositories in which personal information is maintained, and record for each the following fields:
    (i) System or File Name;
    (ii) Identifier Categories Present (enumerated by reference to § 2.01);
    (iii) Medium of Storage;
    (iv) Retention Period;
    (v) Access Control Method;
    (vi) Date of Last Review.

( ) Restrict access to personal information to those workforce members whose duties reasonably require such access, and maintain an access authorization log bearing the columns: Role Title; System Accessed; Authorization Granted; Authorization Reviewed; Authorization Terminated.

( ) Require, by written contract, that any third-party service provider to whom personal information is disclosed shall implement and maintain safeguards no less protective than those required by this Article, and shall promptly notify the Records Custodian of any suspected unauthorized acquisition.

( ) Provide, upon commencement of employment and annually thereafter, training to all workforce members having access to personal information, and document such training by the fields: Role Title; Curriculum Version; Completion Status; Attestation Received.

( ) Refrain from transmitting personal information across any public network, or to any portable or removable device, unless such information is encrypted by a method generally accepted within the industry.

( ) Cause all records containing personal information to be disposed of by shredding, erasure, or such other means as renders the personal information unreadable and irretrievable, and to memorialize each disposition in a log bearing the columns: Disposition Date; Record Series; Volume; Method Employed; Role Title of Attesting Witness.

( ) Post in a conspicuous location within each area in which personal information is processed a notice stating the title and departmental contact channel of the Records Custodian.

§ 4.02. Duty Upon Request of an Individual. A holder of records shall, upon receipt of a verified written request from an individual who is the subject of records maintained by it, disclose the categories of personal information maintained, the categories of sources from which such information was collected, and the categories of third parties to whom such information has been disclosed within the twelve (12) months preceding the request. Such disclosure shall be made within thirty (30) days, which period may be extended once by an additional thirty (30) days upon written notice stating the reason for extension.

§ 4.03. Non-Delegation. The duties imposed by this Article shall not be delegated to any third party in a manner that relieves the holder of records of ultimate responsibility for compliance.
