SUBCHAPTER C — RETENTION, DESTRUCTION, AND ACCOUNTING

§ 12.206  Retention Periods.

     (a)  Minimum Retention.  A holder of records shall retain each protected record
          for a period of not less than six (6) years measured from the later of:

               (i)   the date of the last entry made in the record; or

               (ii)  the date on which the final payment or final adjustment
                     relating to the billed episode of care was posted to the
                     account ledger.

     (b)  Records of Minors.  Where the individual to whom a protected record
          pertains was a minor at the time of service, the period prescribed in
          paragraph (a) shall not commence until such individual attains the age of
          majority under the law of this jurisdiction.

     (c)  Tolling.  The periods prescribed in paragraphs (a) and (b) shall be tolled
          during the pendency of any audit, investigation, administrative
          proceeding, or litigation of which the holder has received notice, and for
          two (2) years following the final resolution thereof.

§ 12.207  Destruction.

     (a)  Method.  Upon expiration of the applicable retention period, a protected
          record shall be destroyed by a method rendering the identifier elements
          unreadable, indecipherable, and incapable of reconstruction. Acceptable
          methods include cross-cut shredding to a particle size not exceeding
          specified tolerance, pulping, incineration, degaussing, and cryptographic
          erasure of the governing key material.

     (b)  Certificate.  Each act of destruction shall be evidenced by a certificate
          bearing the following fields, and no others:

               Destruction Order Number ........... _______________
               Record Series Designation .......... _______________
               Volume (linear feet or gigabytes) .. _______________
               Method Employed .................... _______________
               Date of Destruction ................ _______________
               Witnessing Role Title ............. _______________
               Custodian Signature Block ......... _______________

     (c)  The certificate required by paragraph (b) shall itself be retained
          permanently and shall not be treated as a protected record.

§ 12.208  Accounting of Disclosures.

     (a)  Upon written request, an individual shall be entitled to an accounting of
          all disclosures of that individual's protected record made during the six
          (6) years preceding the date of the request, exclusive of disclosures made
          for treatment, payment, or health care operations.

     (b)  The accounting shall state, as to each disclosure, the Date of Disclosure,
          the Name of the Receiving Entity, a Brief Description of the Identifier
          Elements Disclosed, and the Statutory or Contractual Basis for Disclosure.

     (c)  The holder shall furnish the accounting without charge for the first
          request in any twelve-month period and may impose a reasonable,
          cost-based fee for subsequent requests, provided that the individual is
          informed of such fee in advance and afforded an opportunity to withdraw or
          modify the request.

     (d)  The holder shall act upon a request under this section within sixty (60)
          days, subject to a single extension of not more than thirty (30) days upon
          written notice stating the reason for delay and the date by which the
          accounting will be provided.
