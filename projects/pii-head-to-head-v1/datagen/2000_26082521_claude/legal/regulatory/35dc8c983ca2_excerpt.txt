MEMORANDUM
COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT / RECORDS ADMINISTRATION

TO:   All Clinic Supervisors
FROM: The Records Custodian
RE:   Administrative Rule 40-3-11, subd. 4 (Safeguarding of Identifiers) — text for posting
DATE: April 14, 2004

Post the following at each intake station and each terminal from which a system of records may be queried.

     40-3-11 SAFEGUARDING OF IDENTIFIERS.
     Subd. 4. Physical and procedural safeguards. Every holder of records shall:
        A. secure all record media containing an identifier category enumerated
           in subdivision 2 within a locked receptacle, room, or cabinet at the
           close of each business day and at any time the work area is left
           unattended;
        B. position display terminals so that the fields Patient Name, Date of
           Birth, Medical Record Number, and Social Security Number are not
           visible to persons in the waiting area or public corridor;
        C. transmit no record containing an identifier category by facsimile
           except to a receiving device situated in a secured area and confirmed
           in advance by telephone to the receiving custodian;
        D. destroy record media scheduled for disposal by shredding, pulping, or
           incineration, and record such destruction on the Certificate of
           Destruction, which shall bear the fields Series Title, Volume,
           Inclusive Dates, Method of Destruction, and Witnessing Official; and
        E. report any suspected unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure to the
           Records Custodian not later than the close of the next business day.

     Failure of a holder of records to observe this subdivision constitutes
     grounds for corrective action under the applicable personnel rule, without
     prejudice to any penalty imposed under Section 7-103 of the Act.
