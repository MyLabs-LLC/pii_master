MEMORANDUM — COMPLIANCE SERIES No. 4 of 2004

FROM:  Office of the Records Custodian
TO:    Data Stewards; Systems Analysts, Policy Administration; Supervisor, Claims Quality Review
RE:    Regulation 33, Part IV — Classification of Data Elements
DATE:  June 18, 2004

Part IV is reproduced below in relevant part. Every data dictionary maintained by this company shall carry the tier designation prescribed by § 4.2 for each field enumerated, and the designation shall be reflected in screen masking rules at the next scheduled release.

§ 4.1  Construction.
(a) A data element is classified by the sensitivity of the element itself and not by the sensitivity of the record in which it appears.
(b) Where a single field is capable of holding elements of more than one tier, the field takes the higher tier.
(c) A derived value, index key, or hash computed from a Tier I element takes the tier of the element from which it is derived, unless the Commissioner determines the derivation to be irreversible.

§ 4.3  Obligations attaching to tier.
(a) A Tier I element shall not appear in full on any printed report, screen display, item of correspondence, or extract file, except where the recipient's business function requires the full element and that requirement is documented in the access authorization.
(b) A Tier I element shall not be used as a primary or secondary index key in any system placed in service after the effective date of this Part.
(c) A Tier II element may be displayed in full to authorized personnel but shall not be transmitted outside the holder's network except in conformity with § 7-103(d) of the Act.
(d) Tier III elements are not personal identifiers for purposes of the Act but remain subject to the general confidentiality obligations of the holder.

§ 4.4  Data dictionary.
The holder shall maintain a current data dictionary listing, for each field: field label, source system, tier, retention period, and the position title of the responsible data steward. The dictionary shall be produced to the Department on request.
