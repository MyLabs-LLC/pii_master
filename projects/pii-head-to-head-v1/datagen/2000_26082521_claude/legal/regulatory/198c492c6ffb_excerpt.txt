DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION
REGULATION 7 CSR 20-100.400 — DIRECTORY INFORMATION AND LIMITS ON DISCLOSURE

(1) Designation of directory information.
• A district electing to designate directory information shall adopt the designation by board resolution and publish it annually.
• Only the following field labels may be designated:
   – Student Name
   – Grade Level
   – Participation in Officially Recognized Activities and Sports
   – Weight and Height of Members of Athletic Teams
   – Dates of Attendance
   – Diplomas, Certificates, Honors, and Awards Received
   – Most Recent Educational Agency Attended
• The following field labels shall not be designated under any circumstance:
   – Social Security Number
   – State or Local Student Identification Number used alone to access an education record
   – Residential Street Address
   – Personal Electronic Mail Address
   – Date of Birth
   – Disability or Health Status

(2) Opt-out.
• The annual notice shall inform parents and eligible students of the right to refuse designation as to any or all field labels, and shall state the deadline for filing the refusal.
• A refusal on file shall be honored until revoked in writing and shall be flagged in the student information system by a suppression indicator visible to every user with disclosure authority.

(3) Duties of the Records Custodian.
• Verify suppression status before any release, including class lists, yearbook submissions, honor rolls, athletic programs, and media requests.
• Record every directory release in the Disclosure Log.
• Deny requests for directory information made for commercial solicitation purposes.

(4) Sanctions.
• Release of a suppressed field label is a reportable violation.
• The Department may direct corrective action, require retraining of all staff with disclosure authority, and place the district on conditional accreditation status for repeated violations within a single school year.
• Nothing in this regulation authorizes release of an education record otherwise protected by federal law.
