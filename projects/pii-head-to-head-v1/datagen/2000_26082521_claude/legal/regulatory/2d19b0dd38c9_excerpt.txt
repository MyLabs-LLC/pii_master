TITLE 22 — EDUCATION
CHAPTER 14. CONFIDENTIALITY OF PUPIL RECORDS
SUBCHAPTER B — DEFINITIONS (excerpt)

Sec. 14.201. Categories of Personal Identifiers.

(a) For purposes of this subchapter, "personally identifiable
    information" means any datum enumerated in the schedule set out
    in subsection (d), together with any combination of data which
    would permit a reasonable member of the school community to
    identify the individual with reasonable certainty.

(b) The Records Custodian shall classify each entry appearing in a
    pupil file according to the code assigned in column one of the
    schedule, and shall cause that code to appear on every index,
    transmittal sheet, and disclosure register maintained under
    Sec. 14.310.

(c) Classification under this section is a continuing duty. Where a
    single entry contains identifiers of more than one class, the
    entry takes the most restrictive classification applicable.

(d) Schedule of Categories:

+------+----------------------------------+------------------+
| Code | Identifier Category              | Governing Clause |
+------+----------------------------------+------------------+
| A-1  | Legal Surname and Given Name     | 14.201(a)(1)     |
| A-2  | Student Identification Number    | 14.201(a)(2)     |
| A-3  | Date and Place of Birth          | 14.201(a)(3)     |
| A-4  | Parent or Guardian Name          | 14.201(a)(4)     |
| A-5  | Residential Mailing Address      | 14.201(a)(5)     |
| B-1  | Enrollment and Attendance Record | 14.201(b)(1)     |
| B-2  | Grade and Assessment History     | 14.201(b)(2)     |
| B-3  | Health and Immunization Entry    | 14.201(b)(3)     |
| C-1  | Disciplinary Action Summary      | 14.201(c)(1)     |
| C-2  | Special Education Eligibility    | 14.201(c)(2)     |
+------+----------------------------------+------------------+

(e) Class C identifiers may not be released under the directory
    information exception provided by Sec. 14.240, notwithstanding
    any annual notice given by the district.
