KEY
  CLASS      D = direct identifier, Rule .102(2); I = indirect identifier, Rule .102(3)
  CONSENT    Written consent required under Rule .103(d) before release to a party
             outside the institution, absent a published routine use
  RETENTION  P = permanent; year figures run from end of last term of attendance
             or from separation from employment, whichever applies

Elements not listed above shall be classified by the Records Custodian and the
classification reported to the Office of the General Counsel before collection begins.
