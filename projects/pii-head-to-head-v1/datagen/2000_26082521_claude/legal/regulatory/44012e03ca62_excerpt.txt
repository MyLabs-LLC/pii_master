TITLE 12, CHAPTER 47, SUBCHAPTER D — EXAMINATION PROCEDURES

§ 47.401  Scope of Examination.

    (a) At each safety and soundness examination, and at such other times as the
        Commissioner directs, examiners shall review the covered institution's
        compliance with Subchapters A through C.

    (b) The review shall cover, at a minimum:

            (1)  the written designation of the Records Custodian and the date
                 of the most recent board review under § 47.201(b);

            (2)  the access register required by § 47.202(b), tested against a
                 sample of position titles drawn from the current payroll
                 register by title only;

            (3)  the disposal log required by § 47.203(c), tested against
                 vendor certificates of destruction;

            (4)  service provider contracts executed or renewed since the prior
                 examination, tested for the clauses required by § 47.204(b);

            (5)  any breach notifications given since the prior examination and
                 the supporting investigation file.

§ 47.402  Workpapers.

    (a) Examiners shall not transcribe personal information into workpapers.
        Where a finding requires reference to a particular record, the workpaper
        shall identify the record by record series, medium, and sequence number
        assigned by the institution for examination purposes.

    (b) Workpapers containing identifier categories, but no values, are exempt
        from the disposal requirements of § 47.203(b) and shall be retained by
        the Department for six years.

§ 47.403  Findings and Corrective Action.

    (a) Findings shall be classified as follows:

            Level 1  —  Deficiency correctable before the close of examination.
            Level 2  —  Deficiency requiring a written corrective action plan.
            Level 3  —  Deficiency indicating a systemic failure of the program
                        required by § 47.201, referable for penalty proceedings
                        under § 47.302.

    (b) A corrective action plan shall be submitted within thirty days of the
        transmittal of the report of examination and shall state, for each
        finding: the section violated; the corrective step; the title of the
        officer accountable; and the completion date.

    (c) The Commissioner may extend the period under subsection (b) for good
        cause shown in writing.

§ 47.404  Referral.

    (a) A Level 3 finding shall be referred to the Enforcement Division not
        later than fifteen days after transmittal of the report.

    (b) Referral does not stay the obligation to submit a corrective action
        plan.
