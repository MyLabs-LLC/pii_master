CHAPTER 2.44
PROTECTION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD BY THE MUNICIPAL UTILITY

2.44.010  Purpose and application.
This chapter governs the collection, storage, use, disclosure, and destruction of personal information contained in the records of the water, wastewater, and electric utilities operated by the City. It applies to every officer, employee, contractor, and agent having access to such records, without regard to the medium in which the record is kept.

2.44.020  Definitions.
As used in this chapter:
(a) "Covered record" means a record created or maintained in connection with an application for utility service, the furnishing of utility service, metering, billing, collection, or discontinuance of service, and which contains one or more protected identifiers.
(b) "Holder of record" means a department, division, office, contractor, or agent of the City having custody or control of a covered record, whether or not that entity created the record.
(c) "Protected identifier" means an item of information falling within a category listed in Section 2.44.030.
(d) "Records Custodian" means the officer designated under Section 2.44.040(a).
(e) "Disclosure" means release, transfer, provision of access to, or divulging in any manner of a covered record to a person other than the individual to whom the record pertains.
(f) "Breach of security" means acquisition of a covered record by a person not authorized to acquire it.

2.44.030  Categories of protected identifiers.
The following are protected identifiers when appearing in a covered record, alone or in combination:
Category I — Government-issued identification numbers, including social security number, taxpayer identification number, driver's license number, and state identification card number.
Category II — Financial account information, including bank account number, payment card number, and any associated security code, access code, or personal identification number.
Category III — Contact information, including service address, mailing address, telephone number, and electronic mail address, where the account bears a confidentiality designation under Section 2.44.050(c).
Category IV — Consumption and premises data, including interval meter readings recorded at intervals of less than one month, load profile data, and premises access codes.
Category V — Household and eligibility data, including date of birth, household size, income verification, medical certification for life-support equipment, and enrollment in a low-income or hardship rate program.

2.44.040  Duties of holders of records.
(a) Each holder shall designate in writing a Records Custodian and one alternate, and shall file the designation with the City Clerk.
(b) Each holder shall maintain a field inventory of every record series in its custody, bearing the column headings: Record Series; Field Label; Identifier Category; Retention Period; Access Level.
(c) Access shall be limited to those persons whose assigned duties require it.
(d) Protected identifiers within Categories I and II shall be masked or truncated on any document printed, mailed, or displayed at a public counter.
(e) Each disclosure shall be entered in a disclosure log bearing the column headings: Date of Request; Class of Requesting Party; Statutory Basis; Record Series Released; Approving Officer.
(f) Each person with access shall complete annual instruction on this chapter.

2.44.050  Penalties.
Violation of this chapter is punishable by a civil penalty of not more than five hundred dollars for each violation, each day of continuing violation constituting a separate offense. Knowing disclosure for pecuniary gain is a misdemeanor. Nothing in this section limits disciplinary action under the personnel rules.
