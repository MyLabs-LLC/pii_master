§ 12-4-909. VIOLATIONS; PENALTIES; ENFORCEMENT.

(a) Administrative sanctions. Any holder of records who violates any provision of this Article or of any rule promulgated hereunder shall be subject to the following, which shall be cumulative and not exclusive:

    * written reprimand placed in the personnel file;
    * immediate suspension of access to any record system pending investigation;
    * suspension without pay for a period not exceeding thirty calendar days;
    * dismissal from employment or termination of contract; and
    * referral to the appropriate licensing authority.

(b) Civil penalty. The Board of Health may assess against any person who knowingly and without lawful authority discloses personal identifying information a civil penalty of not more than one thousand dollars ($1,000) for each record so disclosed, provided that the aggregate penalty assessed for violations arising out of a single occurrence shall not exceed twenty-five thousand dollars ($25,000).

(c) Criminal penalty. Any person who, with intent to obtain pecuniary gain or to cause harm to another, obtains, sells, transfers, or uses personal identifying information held by the Agency shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and, upon conviction, shall be punished by a fine of not more than five thousand dollars ($5,000), by imprisonment for not more than one year, or by both such fine and imprisonment.

(d) Private right of action preserved. Nothing in this section shall be construed to abridge, limit, or supersede any remedy at law or in equity otherwise available to an aggrieved individual.
