TITLE 45, CHAPTER 12 — PROTECTION OF INDIVIDUALLY IDENTIFIABLE HEALTH AND FINANCIAL INFORMATION
SUBCHAPTER B — DUTIES OF HOLDERS OF PROTECTED RECORDS

    § 12.201  Scope and Applicability.

        (a)  General Rule.  The provisions of this subchapter shall apply to every
             covered entity, and to every business associate acting on behalf of a
             covered entity, that creates, receives, maintains, transmits, or
             otherwise comes into possession of a protected record in the course of
             furnishing, administering, adjudicating, or billing for health care
             services within the jurisdiction.

        (b)  Exclusions.  Nothing in this subchapter shall be construed to apply to:

            (1)  aggregate statistical compilations from which all identifier
                 elements enumerated at § 12.202(c) have been permanently removed,
                 provided that no key, code, or mechanism capable of effecting
                 re-identification is retained by the holder or by any agent of the
                 holder; or

            (2)  records of employment maintained by a covered entity solely in its
                 capacity as an employer, except where such records incorporate
                 information derived from the provision of health care.

    § 12.202  Definitions.

        As used in this subchapter, unless the context clearly requires otherwise:

        (a)  "Holder of records" means any covered entity, business associate, or
             subcontractor thereof having actual or constructive custody of a
             protected record, irrespective of whether such custody is exercised
             directly or through a contracted intermediary.

        (b)  "Records Custodian" means the individual designated in writing by the
             governing body of a covered entity to discharge the obligations imposed
             by §§ 12.204 through 12.209, and any successor lawfully appointed to
             that office.

        (c)  "Identifier element" means any of the following data fields, whether
             appearing alone or in combination, and whether stored in electronic,
             paper, microform, or other medium:

            (1)  Full Name; Surname; Given Name; Middle Initial; Maiden Name;
                 Alias or Former Name;

            (2)  Street Address; Post Office Box; Municipality; County; Postal
                 Code (all digits); Country of Residence;

            (3)  Date of Birth; Date of Admission; Date of Discharge; Date of
                 Death; Age, where the individual is aged eighty-nine (89) years
                 or older;

            (4)  Telephone Number; Facsimile Number; Electronic Mail Address;
                 Uniform Resource Locator; Internet Protocol Address;

            (5)  Social Security Number; Taxpayer Identification Number; Driver's
                 License Number; Passport Number; State Identification Number;

            (6)  Medical Record Number; Patient Account Number; Health Plan
                 Beneficiary Number; Member Identification Number; Group Number;
                 Certificate Number; Prior Authorization Number; Claim Control
                 Number;

            (7)  Financial Account Number; Payment Card Primary Account Number;
                 Card Verification Value; Card Expiration Date; Routing Transit
                 Number; Guarantor Account Number;

            (8)  Device Serial Number; Implant Identifier; Vehicle Identification
                 Number; License Plate Number;

            (9)  Biometric Identifier, including without limitation fingerprint,
                 voice print, retinal or iris image, and full-face photographic
                 image or any comparable image; and

            (10) any other unique identifying number, characteristic, or code
                 assigned by the holder or by any third party, the disclosure of
                 which would permit identification of the individual to whom the
                 record pertains.

        (d)  "Protected record" means any record containing one or more identifier
             elements in conjunction with information relating to the past, present,
             or future physical or mental health of an individual, the provision of
             health care to an individual, or the past, present, or future payment
             for the provision of health care to an individual.

        (e)  "Disclosure" means the release, transfer, provision of access to, or
             divulging in any manner of a protected record or any portion thereof
             to any person other than the individual who is the subject of the
             record.

    § 12.203  Designation of the Records Custodian.

        (a)  Every holder of records shall designate a Records Custodian and shall
             cause the fact of such designation, together with the office, mailing
             location, and business telephone line of that officer, to be documented
             in the compliance register maintained pursuant to § 12.209(b).

        (b)  The designation required by paragraph (a) of this section shall be
             reviewed not less frequently than annually and shall be amended within
             ten (10) business days of any vacancy in the office.

    § 12.204  Minimum Necessary Standard.

        (a)  A holder of records shall make reasonable efforts to limit the use,
             disclosure of, and request for a protected record to the minimum
             identifier elements necessary to accomplish the intended purpose of
             such use, disclosure, or request.

        (b)  The standard set forth in paragraph (a) shall not apply to:

            (1)  disclosures to or requests by a health care provider for purposes
                 of treatment;

            (2)  disclosures made to the individual who is the subject of the
                 record, or to that individual's personal representative;

            (3)  disclosures made pursuant to a valid authorization executed by the
                 individual; or

            (4)  disclosures required by law, provided that such disclosures are
                 limited to the relevant requirements of the law compelling them.

    § 12.205  Administrative, Physical, and Technical Safeguards.

        (a)  Administrative.  The holder shall implement written policies governing
             workforce authorization, role-based access provisioning, periodic
             access recertification, and termination of access privileges.

        (b)  Physical.  The holder shall secure all workstations, storage cabinets,
             printing devices, and portable media on which protected records reside
             against unauthorized physical access, and shall maintain a log of media
             removed from the secured premises, recording the fields: Media Type;
             Control Tag; Date Removed; Authorizing Role; Date Returned.

        (c)  Technical.  The holder shall employ access controls, unique user
             identification, automatic session termination, audit controls,
             integrity controls, and encryption of protected records both at rest
             and in transmission across any open network.
