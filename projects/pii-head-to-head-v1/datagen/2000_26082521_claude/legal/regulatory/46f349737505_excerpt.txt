RULE 17-3.012 RETENTION, MASKING, AND DISPOSITION OF RECORDS CONTAINING PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS

(1) A transit operating agency shall retain a record containing one or more direct identifiers only for so long as is necessary to accomplish the purpose for which the record was collected, and in no event beyond the period fixed in the retention schedule approved by the State Archivist for the applicable record series.

(2) Upon expiration of the applicable retention period, the agency shall either destroy the record or render it non-identifying. A record is rendered non-identifying only where every direct identifier has been irreversibly removed or overwritten and the residual data elements cannot, by any means reasonably likely to be employed, be re-associated with the individual concerned.

(3) Where an operational need requires continued retention of transactional data beyond the retention period, the agency shall mask the fields ACCOUNT_REF and CARD_SERIAL by one-way transformation and shall generalize the fields TAP_DATETIME to calendar date and POSTAL_CODE_OF_RESIDENCE to the first three characters.

(4) Destruction shall be effected by shredding, pulping, incineration, degaussing, or multiple-pass overwriting, as appropriate to the medium. The Records Custodian shall cause a certificate of destruction to be prepared recording the record series, the volume destroyed, the method employed, and the date of destruction, and shall retain such certificate for six years.
