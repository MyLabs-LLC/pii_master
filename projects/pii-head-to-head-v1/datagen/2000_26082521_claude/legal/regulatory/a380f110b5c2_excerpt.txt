MUNICIPAL CODE — TITLE 2, ADMINISTRATION AND PERSONNEL
CHAPTER 2.44 — CUSTOMER INFORMATION SECURITY

    2.44.010  Purpose.
        The purpose of this chapter is to secure personal information held by the Department of Public Utilities and to conform Department practice to Chapter 220-14 of the Public Utilities Commission rules.

    2.44.020  Definitions.
        Terms used in this chapter have the meanings assigned in R 220-14.02. In addition:
            A.  "Department" means the Department of Public Utilities.
            B.  "Business office" means any facility at which applications for service are accepted or payments are received.
            C.  "Third-party servicer" means any person under contract to perform billing, printing, collection, meter reading, or data processing.

    2.44.030  Duties of the Director.
        The Director shall:
            A.  Appoint a Records Custodian from among the classified staff of the Department;
            B.  Cause the Authorized Position Roster to be posted in each business office;
            C.  Report to the Council, not later than March 1 of each year, on compliance during the preceding calendar year.

    2.44.040  Applications for service.
        A.  The application form shall collect only the following fields: Applicant Name; Service Address; Mailing Address; Telephone Number; Social Security Number or Taxpayer Identification Number; Driver's License Number; Employer Name; Prior Service Address; Deposit Amount Tendered.
        B.  The Social Security Number field shall bear a notice stating the authority for collection and the uses to be made of the number.
        C.  Completed applications shall be removed from public counters at the close of each business day.

    2.44.050  Third-party servicers.
        A.  No contract shall be executed unless it contains a confidentiality clause conforming to R 220-14.03.
        B.  The contract shall identify by title the servicer's employee responsible for compliance.
        C.  Upon expiration of the contract the servicer shall return or destroy all records and shall furnish a Certificate of Destruction.

    2.44.060  Violation — penalty.
        A.  Violation of this chapter by an employee is cause for disciplinary action up to and including dismissal.
        B.  Violation by a third-party servicer is cause for termination of the contract and for debarment for a period not exceeding three years.
        C.  Nothing in this section limits prosecution under § 41-3-105.
