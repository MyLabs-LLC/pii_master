ADMINISTRATIVE RULE 11-07
DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION — DIVISION OF MOTOR CARRIER SERVICES
Subject: Retention and Handling of Consignment Records Containing Personal Information
Authority: Chapter 24-9. Adopted: March 2011.

1. Scope. This rule governs records generated in connection with the tender, carriage and delivery of freight where such records contain personal information of a shipper, consignee, driver or employee.

2. Classification. Each record series shall be classified and coded as follows:

 Code | Record Series                | Identifier Categories Present            | Retention | Disposal Method
 ---- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | --------- | ---------------
 A-1  | Bill of lading, consignee copy | Name; delivery address                  | 3 years   | Cross-cut shred
 A-4  | Proof of delivery, signed     | Name; signature image                    | 3 years   | Cross-cut shred
 B-2  | Driver qualification file     | Name; driver's license number; date of birth | 3 years after separation | Cross-cut shred
 B-7  | Payroll and withholding       | Name; Social Security number; account number | 7 years | Cross-cut shred
 C-3  | Customs entry documentation   | Name; passport number; alien registration number | 5 years | Incinerate
 D-1  | Claims and cargo loss files   | Name; account number; medical information | 6 years  | Cross-cut shred

3. Access. Access to a record series listed in section 2 shall be limited to personnel whose duties require it. The Records Custodian shall maintain an access register recording, for each grant of access: position title; record series code; date of grant; date of revocation; and authorizing official.

4. Transmission. Personal information shall not be transmitted over a public network unless encrypted. Facsimile transmission of a record in series B-2, B-7 or C-3 is prohibited except to a machine located in a controlled area.

5. Portable media. Personal information shall not be stored on a laptop computer or other portable device unless encrypted and unless the device is entered on the equipment register.

6. Cross-border transfer. A holder shall not transfer a record in series C-3 outside the jurisdiction except pursuant to a written agreement imposing safeguards not less protective than those required by this rule.

7. Reporting. Non-compliance discovered in the course of an internal audit shall be reported to the Division within thirty (30) days on Form MCS-114.
