COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
ADMINISTRATIVE RULE HD-114
SAFEGUARDING OF IDENTIFIABLE HEALTH AND SERVICE RECORDS
Adopted by the Board of Health; supersedes Rule HD-97 in its entirety

ARTICLE I. OBLIGATIONS OF HOLDERS OF RECORDS

Section 101. Custody and Accountability. Each holder of records shall:
 • Designate in writing a Records Custodian and at least one Alternate Records Custodian for every system of records, and file the designation with the Health Officer.
 • Maintain a current System Inventory listing, for each system: System Title; Program Unit; Statutory Authority; Categories of Individuals Covered; Categories of Data Elements; Retention Period; Physical Location; Media Type.
 • Post the designation at each location where records are stored, together with the telephone extension of the Records Unit.

Section 102. Physical Safeguards. Each holder of records shall:
 • Store paper records in locking cabinets or in a room secured by a keyed or combination lock during non-business hours.
 • Restrict keys and combinations to persons named on the Access Roster, and record each issuance on Form HD-114-A (Key Issuance Log), which shall capture: Employee Number; Position Title; Key Identifier; Date Issued; Date Returned.
 • Site terminals, printers, and facsimile machines so that displayed or printed personal information is not visible to members of the public.
 • Transport records between facilities only in sealed, opaque containers bearing the notation "Confidential — Health Records" and a transmittal number.

Section 103. Administrative Safeguards. Each holder of records shall:
 • Obtain from every employee, volunteer, student intern, and contractor a signed Confidentiality Acknowledgment before granting access, and retain the acknowledgment for six years following separation.
 • Review the Access Roster quarterly and revoke access within one business day of separation, transfer, or change in assigned duties.
 • Conduct annual instruction in the requirements of this Rule and record attendance in the Training Register, showing: Employee Number; Program Unit; Course Code; Date Completed.

Section 104. Technical Safeguards. Each holder of records shall:
 • Assign a unique user identifier to each authorized user; group, shared, and generic identifiers are prohibited.
 • Require password changes not less frequently than every ninety (90) days and suspend the identifier after five (5) consecutive failed logon attempts.
 • Enable audit logging capturing, at minimum: User Identifier; Terminal Identifier; Date and Time of Access; Record Identifier Accessed; Transaction Type.
 • Retain audit logs for three (3) years and review them not less than monthly for irregular access patterns.
 • Overwrite or physically destroy magnetic media before reassignment or surplus disposition; reformatting alone shall not be deemed sufficient.

ARTICLE II. DISCLOSURE

Section 201. Each disclosure outside the department shall be entered in the Disclosure Log with the following columns completed: Log Sequence Number; Date of Disclosure; System Title; Recipient Organization; Recipient Title; Purpose Code; Authority Cited; Data Elements Released; Approving Official Title.

Section 202. Disclosure is permitted only where:
 • The subject of the record, or a person authorized to act for the subject, has given specific written consent identifying the recipient and the purpose;
 • The disclosure is compelled by court order or lawful subpoena, in which case the Records Custodian shall notify the County Attorney before production;
 • The disclosure is to a public health authority for the control of communicable disease, pursuant to the reporting provisions of the Sanitary Code; or
 • The information has been rendered non-identifiable by removal of all direct identifiers and by suppression of any cell in a tabulation representing fewer than five (5) persons.

Section 203. No disclosure shall be made by telephone unless the identity and authority of the requester have been independently verified by return call to a published number, and the verification noted in the Disclosure Log.
