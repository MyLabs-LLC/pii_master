§ 99.60 Applicability.
   (1) This subpart applies to every recipient of state or federal education funds that maintains records containing personal identifiers.

§ 99.61 Complaint.
   (1) A complaint may be filed by a parent, guardian, or eligible pupil.
   (2) The complaint shall be in writing and shall state:
       (a) the identity of the agency alleged to be in violation;
       (b) the record or identifier category concerned;
       (c) the date on which the alleged violation became known;
       (d) the remedy sought.
   (3) A complaint filed more than one hundred eighty (180) days after the complainant knew or should have known of the alleged violation shall be dismissed as untimely.

§ 99.62 Investigation.
   (1) The Office shall notify the agency in writing and shall specify the documents to be produced.
   (2) The agency shall respond within thirty (30) days.
   (3) The Records Custodian shall produce the access log and the current identifier schedule.

§ 99.63 Findings.
   (1) The Office shall issue written findings stating whether a violation occurred, the corrective action required, and the period for compliance.

§ 99.64 Penalties.
   (1) An agency that fails to come into compliance within the period specified is subject to:
       (a) withholding of further payments;
       (b) issuance of a cease-and-desist order;
       (c) termination of eligibility to receive funds.
   (2) An officer or employee who knowingly discloses a personal identifier in violation of this part is subject to a civil penalty for each disclosure, and each record disclosed constitutes a separate violation.
   (3) A person who obtains a personal identifier by false pretense is subject to referral for prosecution.
   (4) Penalties under this section are in addition to, and not in substitution for, remedies available under other law.

§ 99.65 Posting.
   (1) A copy of this subpart shall be posted at each attendance site and maintained in the district policy binder.
