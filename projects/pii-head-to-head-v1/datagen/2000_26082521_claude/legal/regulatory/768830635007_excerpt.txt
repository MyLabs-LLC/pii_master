MEMORANDUM

TO:       All Building Principals; Office Managers; Registrars
FROM:     Office of the Records Custodian
RE:       Transmittal of Statutory Excerpt — Protection of Personally
          Identifiable Student and Employee Information
DATE:     March 14, 2011
FILE:     ADM-1140.02 / Retain permanently

Pursuant to the standing directive of the Superintendent, there is transmitted
herewith, for placement in the administrative reference binder maintained at each
attendance center, a verbatim excerpt of the enactment cited below, together with
the implementing rule promulgated thereunder. Building administrators are
directed to review Sections 3 and 7 with all clerical personnel having custody
of, or routine access to, cumulative folders, health files, transportation
rosters, food-service eligibility applications, and payroll records.

Nothing in this transmittal shall be construed to enlarge, diminish, or otherwise
modify the obligations imposed by the federal statute governing education records
or by the collective bargaining agreements presently in force. Where the
requirements of state and federal law differ, the more protective requirement
shall govern.

Questions concerning the application of the excerpt to a particular record series
shall be directed in writing to the Records Custodian and shall not be resolved at
the building level.

Attachment: Excerpt (4 pp.)
