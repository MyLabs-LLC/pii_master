REGULATION 5125.1 — SCHEDULE OF IDENTIFIER CATEGORIES
Adopted by the Governing Board. Filed with the County Superintendent.
Review cycle: annual.        Schedule No. ________  Revision ________

PART A. PURPOSE
This regulation classifies each category of personal identifier maintained by the District and states the disclosure tier applicable to it. No category may be released at a tier higher than that stated.

PART B. SCHEDULE

  IDENTIFIER CATEGORY          | CLASS | PERMITTED INTERNAL USE      | TIER
  -----------------------------|-------|-----------------------------|-----
  Pupil name                   |   A   | Enrollment, attendance      |  2
  Parent/guardian name         |   A   | Notification                |  2
  Residence address            |   A   | Residency verification      |  1
  Mailing address              |   A   | Notification                |  1
  Home telephone number        |   A   | Emergency contact           |  1
  Date of birth                |   B   | Age eligibility             |  1
  State pupil ID number        |   B   | Reporting, transcript        |  1
  Federal taxpayer ID number   |   C   | Prohibited except by statute|  0
  Health condition code        |   C   | Nurse, designated staff     |  0
  Program eligibility code     |   C   | Program administration      |  0
  Photograph or likeness       |   B   | Identification card         |  2
  Grade level and homeroom     |   A   | Scheduling                  |  2

PART C. TIER DEFINITIONS
Tier 0 — No disclosure absent written consent or court order.
Tier 1 — Disclosure to officials with a demonstrated legitimate educational interest.
Tier 2 — Disclosure permitted as directory information unless objection is on file.

PART D. OBLIGATIONS OF HOLDERS
1. The Records Custodian shall verify the classification of each new data element before it is entered into any file, index, or system.
2. Class C identifiers shall not be reproduced, extracted to a working list, or transmitted electronically without the written authorization of the Superintendent or designee.
3. Requests for combined extracts drawing on two or more Class B identifiers shall be treated as Class C.
4. A quarterly inventory shall be conducted and certified on the form bearing the headings: SITE; FILE SERIES; CLASS; VOLUME; LOCATION; CUSTODIAN INITIALS; DATE CERTIFIED.
5. Objections to directory release shall be logged and honored for the full school year.

PART E. NONCOMPLIANCE
Unauthorized extraction, sale, or disclosure of any Class B or Class C identifier shall be reported to the Superintendent and to the Department within five (5) working days and shall subject the responsible employee to disciplinary action and to the civil and criminal penalties prescribed by statute.
