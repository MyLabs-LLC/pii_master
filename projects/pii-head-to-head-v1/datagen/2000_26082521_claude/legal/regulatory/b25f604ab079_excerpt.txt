4.0  RETENTION, DISPOSAL, AND ENFORCEMENT
     (Administrative Rule adopted pursuant to Ordinance No. 99-031)

     4.1  Retention.

          4.1.1  A record containing Personal Information shall be retained only for the
                 period specified in the approved Records Retention Schedule and shall be
                 destroyed promptly upon expiration of that period, unless subject to a
                 litigation hold issued by the City Attorney.

          4.1.2  The following record series are subject to the retention periods shown
                 in the Schedule, and each shall bear the restriction code indicated
                 therein:

                 (a)  Service Application and Credit Authorization File — fields:
                      Applicant Name, Service Address, Social Security Number,
                      Date of Birth, Driver's License Number, Employer Name,
                      Deposit Amount, Deposit Receipt Number.

                 (b)  Automatic Payment Authorization File — fields: Account Holder
                      Name, Bank Routing Number, Depositor Account Number, Account
                      Type, Authorization Date, Revocation Date.

                 (c)  Medical Necessity Designation File — fields: Customer Name,
                      Account Number, Certification Code, Certifying Practitioner
                      License Number, Expiration Date.

                 (d)  Assistance Program Eligibility File — fields: Applicant Name,
                      Household Size, Income Verification Code, Determination Code,
                      Review Date.

     4.2  Disposal.

          4.2.1  Paper records shall be destroyed by cross-cut shredding or by
                 incineration witnessed by the Records Custodian or a designee.

          4.2.2  Magnetic media shall be degaussed or overwritten not fewer than three
                 times before reuse, transfer, or surplus disposition. Simple deletion
                 of files does not satisfy this paragraph.

          4.2.3  A Certificate of Destruction shall be executed for each disposal action
                 and shall recite the Record Series, Inclusive Retention Dates, Volume,
                 Method of Destruction, and the signature and title of the witnessing
                 officer. Certificates shall be retained permanently.

     4.3  Audit.

          4.3.1  The Internal Auditor shall test compliance with this Rule not less than
                 once in each fiscal year and shall report findings to the Director of
                 Utilities and to the Audit Committee.

          4.3.2  The scope of the test shall include the access authorization roster, the
                 media removal log, the access logs required by Article IX, and a sample
                 of Certificates of Destruction.

     4.4  Enforcement.

          4.4.1  Failure to execute or retain a Certificate of Destruction constitutes a
                 violation of Rule 22-8.03 and is subject to the administrative penalty
                 prescribed in Rule 22-8.04(A).

          4.4.2  Nothing in this Rule creates a private right of action, and nothing
                 herein waives the immunity of the City under the Governmental Tort
                 Claims Act.
