TITLE 9, PART 22 — DISPOSAL OF RECORDS CONTAINING CONSUMER INFORMATION; PENALTIES

22.1 DUTY OF PROPER DISPOSAL

(a) Any person or institution that maintains, or otherwise possesses, consumer information for a business purpose shall properly dispose of that information by taking reasonable measures to protect against unauthorized access to or use of the information in connection with its disposal.

(b) Reasonable measures include:
(1) implementing and monitoring compliance with written policies requiring the burning, pulverizing, or shredding of papers containing consumer information so that the information cannot practicably be read or reconstructed;
(2) implementing and monitoring compliance with written policies requiring the destruction or erasure of electronic media containing consumer information so that the information cannot practicably be read or reconstructed; and
(3) after due diligence, entering into and monitoring compliance with a written contract with a disposal company engaged in the business of record destruction.

22.2 RECORD SERIES SUBJECT TO THIS PART

The following fields, wherever they appear in a record series, bring that series within this Part:

     Field Label                          Retention Class
     -----------------------------        ---------------
     Member Name                          A
     Residential Address                  A
     Mailing Address                      A
     Telephone Number                     A
     Social Security Number               B
     Taxpayer Identification Number       B
     Date of Birth                        B
     Driver's License Number              B
     Share Account Number                 B
     Loan Account Number                  B
     Card Number                          B
     Personal Identification Number       C
     Online Banking User Identification   C
     Credit Report Data                   C
     Mother's Maiden Name                 C

Class B and Class C fields shall be destroyed by a method listed at 22.1(b) and shall not be placed in general waste or recycling streams under any circumstances.

22.3 CERTIFICATES OF DESTRUCTION

The Records Custodian shall obtain from the disposal company a certificate of destruction identifying the pickup location, the number and type of containers, the record series destroyed, the method of destruction, and the name of the witnessing officer. Certificates shall be retained for three years.

22.4 PENALTIES

(a) A violation of this Part is subject to a civil money penalty of not more than $1,000 for each day the violation continues, not to exceed $50,000 for any single continuing violation.

(b) A knowing or willful violation, or a pattern of violations demonstrating reckless disregard for this Part, is subject to a civil money penalty of not more than $10,000 for each day the violation continues, and the Commissioner may issue an order of removal or prohibition against any institution-affiliated party responsible for the violation.

(c) Penalties assessed under this section are in addition to, and not in lieu of, any remedy available to an injured person under other law.
