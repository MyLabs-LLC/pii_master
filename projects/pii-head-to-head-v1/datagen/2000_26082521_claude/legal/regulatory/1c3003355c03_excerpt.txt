TITLE 8 — INSURANCE
CHAPTER 41 — SAFEGUARDING OF NONPUBLIC PERSONAL INFORMATION
Excerpt: §§ 41-101 through 41-104

  § 41-101. Application.
      I.  This chapter binds each licensee, and each affiliate of a licensee,
          that collects, receives, maintains, or transmits records of
          applicants, insureds, claimants, or beneficiaries within this State.
      II. Compliance is not excused by delegation of any function to a
          service provider, vendor, or independent adjuster.

  § 41-102. Records subject to this chapter.
      I.  A record is subject to this chapter if it contains, in any medium,
          one or more of the following data elements in combination with the
          name or policy designation of a natural person:
              A. Social Security Number;
              B. Driver's License Number or State Identification Number;
              C. Financial Account Number, Credit Card Number, or Debit Card
                 Number, with or without security code or access code;
              D. Date of Birth;
              E. Medical History, Diagnosis Code, or Treatment Record;
              F. Biometric Identifier.
      II. Aggregate data from which every element listed in subdivision I has
          been permanently removed is not a record for purposes of this chapter.

  § 41-103. Designation of custodian.
      I.  Each licensee shall designate a Records Custodian and shall file the
          designation with the Commissioner within thirty (30) days of
          appointment. The filing shall state the title of the position only.
      II. The Records Custodian shall maintain a current inventory of record
          systems, stating for each: System Name; Element Categories Held;
          Retention Period; Custodial Unit; Offsite Copy (Y/N).

  § 41-104. Penalty.
      I.  A violation is punishable by a civil penalty of not more than five
          thousand dollars ($5,000) per record system, per occurrence.
      II. Continued noncompliance after written notice of deficiency
          constitutes a separate violation for each day it persists.
