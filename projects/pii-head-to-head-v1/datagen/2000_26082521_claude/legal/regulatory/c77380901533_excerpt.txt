TITLE 12, CHAPTER 4, ARTICLE 9 — PROTECTION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION MAINTAINED BY LOCAL HEALTH AGENCIES

§ 12-4-901. Definitions.

As used in this Article, and unless the context in which a term appears plainly requires a different construction, the following words and phrases shall be given the meanings hereinafter ascribed to them:

• "Agency" means a county health department, a district health department, a board of health, or any subordinate office, clinic, program, or contractor thereof that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits records concerning identifiable persons.

• "Record" means any writing, drawing, map, photograph, card, tape, recording, microform, magnetic medium, or electronically stored information, regardless of physical form or characteristics, made or received by the Agency in connection with the transaction of public business.

• "Personal identifying information" means any one or more of the following data elements, alone or in combination, when the same are associated with or reasonably capable of association with a particular individual:

    – Full Legal Name; Former Name; Alias; Maiden Name
    – Social Security Number, whether stated in full or in truncated form
    – Date of Birth; Date of Death
    – Residential Street Address; Mailing Address; Prior Address
    – Home Telephone Number; Work Telephone Number; Facsimile Number
    – Electronic Mail Address
    – Driver's License Number or State Identification Card Number
    – Financial Account Number; Credit Card Number; Debit Card Number
    – Medical Record Number; Patient Identifier; Client Case Number
    – Health Plan Beneficiary Number; Member Identification Number
    – Biometric Identifier, including finger and voice prints
    – Full-Face Photographic Image and any comparable image

• "Holder of records" means the Agency and every officer, employee, volunteer, student intern, or agent thereof having custody or control of a record, and includes without limitation the Records Custodian designated pursuant to § 12-4-904.

• "Disclosure" means the release, transfer, provision of access to, or divulging in any manner of information outside the entity holding the information.
