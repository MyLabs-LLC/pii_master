BOARD OF PHARMACY
CHAPTER 46 — PHARMACY PRACTICE
SUBCHAPTER 46B — PATIENT RECORDS AND CONFIDENTIALITY

RULE .0403  DESIGNATION AND DUTIES OF THE RECORDS CUSTODIAN

(a) Each permit holder shall designate in writing a Records Custodian for every location at which protected records are created, stored, or accessed. The designation shall be entered upon the permit file maintained at the location and shall state the designee's position title, the date of designation, and the systems to which the designation extends. The designation shall not state the residential address or home telephone number of the designee.

(b) The Records Custodian shall maintain a disclosure log in which shall be entered, for each disclosure made otherwise than to the individual to whom the record pertains: date of disclosure; time of disclosure; identity of requesting entity by business name; statutory or contractual basis asserted; description of the field categories released; medium of transmission; and position title of the releasing employee.

(c) The disclosure log shall be retained for not less than three years from the date of the last entry and shall be produced upon demand of an inspector of the Board.

(d) A Records Custodian shall report any unauthorized access to, or loss of, protected records to the permit holder's central compliance office within twenty-four hours of discovery.
