CHAPTER 8.44, ARTICLE VIII — ENFORCEMENT, PENALTIES, AND REPORTING

8.44.810 Violations.
Each of the following constitutes a separate violation, and each day on which a violation continues constitutes a separate offense:
 (1) Collection of a data element without documented authority.
 (2) Access to a record series outside an authorized purpose.
 (3) Disclosure without a legal basis or without entry in the disclosure log.
 (4) Failure to encrypt personal information in transit outside the County network.
 (5) Retention beyond the approved retention period.
 (6) Destruction by a method other than the approved disposition method.
 (7) Attempted or completed re-identification of de-identified or aggregate data.
 (8) Failure to report a suspected unauthorized acquisition within the period prescribed.

8.44.820 Schedule of Penalties.

  Tier | Conduct                              | Administrative Penalty      | Referral
  -----|--------------------------------------|-----------------------------|----------------------
  I    | Negligent, no disclosure resulted    | Written reprimand           | Program manager
  II   | Negligent, disclosure resulted       | Up to $500 per offense      | Records Custodian
  III  | Knowing, no personal gain            | Up to $2,500 per offense    | Health Officer
  IV   | Knowing, for personal gain or malice | Up to $10,000 per offense   | County Counsel
  V    | Re-identification, any state of mind | Up to $10,000 per offense   | County Counsel

8.44.830 Aggravating and Mitigating Factors.
In setting a penalty within a tier, the Health Officer shall consider: the number of individuals affected; the identifier categories involved; whether special category information was implicated; whether the conduct was self-reported; the sufficiency of the unit's prior training records; and the existence of prior findings against the same custodial unit within the preceding thirty-six months.

8.44.840 Notice of Determination.
A notice of determination shall be issued in writing and shall state, at minimum: Case Reference Code; Custodial Unit; Provision Violated; Tier Assigned; Identifier Categories Involved; Number of Individuals Affected; Corrective Action Required; Date of Determination; Deadline for Appeal; Determining Role.

8.44.850 Appeal.
A determination may be appealed to the Board of Health within fifteen calendar days of service. The appeal shall be heard on the record. The filing of an appeal does not stay any corrective action ordered under 8.44.840.

8.44.860 Annual Report.
The Records Custodian shall report annually to the Board of Health the number of determinations by tier, the number of disclosures logged by requesting entity type, the number of record series overdue for inventory review, and the number of certificates of destruction issued. The report shall contain aggregate data only.
