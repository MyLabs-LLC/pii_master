ADMINISTRATIVE GUIDELINES FOR THE SECURITY OF NONPUBLIC PERSONAL INFORMATION
Part 704, Subpart C

I. SCOPE AND CONSTRUCTION

704.30 These Guidelines establish standards for the safeguarding of member information and member information systems. They are to be read together with the privacy provisions of Subpart B. Where a provision of these Guidelines conflicts with a provision of Subpart B, the more protective provision governs.

II. DEFINITIONS

704.31 For purposes of this Subpart —

(a) MEMBER INFORMATION means any record containing nonpublic personal information about a member, whether in paper, electronic, or other form, that is maintained by or on behalf of the credit union.

(b) NONPUBLIC PERSONAL INFORMATION includes, without limitation, the following data elements when associated with an identified or identifiable natural person: name; residential address; telephone number; Social Security number; date of birth; account number; loan number; card number; account balance; transaction history; credit report information; income; employment status; and any list, description, or grouping of members derived from any of the foregoing.

(c) MEMBER INFORMATION SYSTEM means any method used to access, collect, store, use, transmit, protect, or dispose of member information, including the systems maintained by service providers.

(d) SERVICE PROVIDER means any person or entity that maintains, processes, or otherwise is permitted access to member information through the provision of services directly to the credit union.

III. OBLIGATIONS OF THE CREDIT UNION

704.32 (a) The board of directors shall approve a written information security program and shall oversee its development, implementation, and maintenance, including assigning specific responsibility for its implementation to a Records Custodian or comparable officer.

(b) The credit union shall assess risk by —
(1) identifying reasonably foreseeable internal and external threats that could result in unauthorized disclosure, misuse, alteration, or destruction of member information;
(2) assessing the likelihood and potential damage of those threats, taking into consideration the sensitivity of the data elements listed at 704.31(b); and
(3) assessing the sufficiency of policies, procedures, member information systems, and other arrangements in place to control those risks.

(c) The credit union shall adopt controls commensurate with assessed risk, including access controls, physical restrictions at facilities where member information is stored, encryption of member information in transit over public networks and, where appropriate, in storage, dual control procedures for high-risk transactions, monitoring systems to detect actual and attempted attacks, and measures to protect against destruction or damage due to environmental hazards.

(d) The credit union shall train staff to implement the program and shall regularly test the key controls, systems, and procedures. Tests shall be conducted or reviewed by independent third parties or by staff independent of those who develop or maintain the program.

704.33 The Records Custodian shall report to the board not less than annually. The report shall address material matters relating to the program, including risk assessment, risk management and control decisions, service provider arrangements, results of testing, security breaches or violations and management's responses, and recommendations for changes to the program.
