SUBCHAPTER B — DEFINITIONS

214.20  Application. The definitions in this subchapter apply to Sections 214.30
        through 214.90 and to any form, log or register prescribed thereunder.

214.21  "Covered record" means any writing, card, microform, magnetic tape,
        diskette or electronic file maintained by a regulated entity that
        contains one or more personal identifiers, without regard to medium of
        storage or purpose of collection.

214.22  "Personal identifier" means any of the following data elements when
        recorded in association with a natural person:

        (a) Primary identifiers —
            Full Legal Name; Former Name; Maiden Name; Date of Birth;
            Social Security Number; Taxpayer Identification Number.

        (b) Financial identifiers —
            Deposit Account Number; Loan Number; Escrow File Number when linked
            to a named party; Credit Card or Charge Card Number; Wire
            Instruction Beneficiary Number.

        (c) Governmental identifiers —
            Driver's License Number; State Identification Card Number;
            Passport Number; Alien Registration Number.

        (d) Locational identifiers —
            Residence Street Address; Mailing Address; Home Telephone Number;
            Facsimile Number; Electronic Mail Address.

214.23  "Holder of record" means the regulated entity in possession or control of
        a covered record, and includes a successor, assignee, receiver or
        custodian of the entity's files.

214.24  "Disclosure" means the transfer, transmission, display, oral recitation
        or making available of a covered record or any part thereof to a person
        other than the individual to whom the identifiers pertain.

214.25  "Records Custodian" means the officer designated under Section 214.31 and
        any deputy acting under written delegation.
