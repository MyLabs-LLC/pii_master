CHAPTER 812, ARTICLE 3 — STATUTORY EXCERPT
RECORDS SERIES ADM-014 / FORM ADM-014A

  Enacting Authority ......... State Legislature

SECTION II — APPLICABILITY
  Applies to every public agency, municipal corporation, and utility
  district that acquires, maintains, transmits, or disposes of records
  containing personal information of residents of this State.

SECTION III — DEFINITIONS
§ 812.301. As used in this Article:
  (a) "Personal information" means an individual's first name or first
      initial and last name in combination with any one or more of the
      identifier categories enumerated in subsection (b), where either
      the name or the identifier is not encrypted or redacted.
  (b) "Identifier categories" means the following data elements:
      Category A — Social Security number.
      Category B — Driver's license number or State identification
                   card number.
      Category C — Financial account number, credit card number, or
                   debit card number, with or without any required
                   security code, access code, or password.
      Category D — Date of birth.
      Category E — Medical or health insurance information, including
                   member identification number.
      Category F — Biometric identifier.
      Category G — Username or electronic mail address in combination
                   with a password or security question answer.
      Category H — Utility account number, meter identification
                   number, or premises service identifier when
                   associated with a named individual.
  (c) "Record" means information inscribed on a tangible medium or
      stored in an electronic or other medium and retrievable in
      perceivable form, without regard to physical location.
  (d) "Holder of records" means any officer, employee, agent, vendor,
      or contractor in possession or control of records containing
      personal information.
  (e) "Records Custodian" means the officer designated under
      § 812.303(a) as accountable for compliance.

SECTION IV — OBLIGATIONS ON HOLDERS OF RECORDS
§ 812.303. Each holder of records shall:
  (a) designate a Records Custodian in writing and file the
      designation with the governing body;
  (b) maintain a written information security program describing
      administrative, technical, and physical safeguards;
  (c) restrict access to personal information to those persons whose
      duties require access, and maintain an access authorization
      roster by position title;
  (d) inventory all record series containing identifier categories
      and review the inventory not less than annually;
  (e) obtain written assurances from each vendor with access to
      personal information;
  (f) encrypt Category A, B, C, E, F, and G elements at rest and in
      transit across public networks.

§ 812.304. Retention and disposal.
  (a) Personal information shall be retained no longer than the
      period prescribed by the applicable retention schedule.
  (b) Disposal shall render the information unreadable and
      unreconstructable. A certificate of destruction shall be
      executed and retained for three (3) years.

SECTION V — PENALTIES
§ 812.308. (a) A violation of § 812.303 or § 812.304 constitutes a
  civil offense punishable by a penalty of not more than five thousand
  dollars ($5,000) for each violation. Each record improperly
  disclosed or disposed of constitutes a separate violation.
  (b) Where the violation is willful or the result of gross
  negligence, the penalty shall be not more than fifteen thousand
  dollars ($15,000) per violation, and the Attorney General may seek
  injunctive relief.
  (c) An officer who knowingly discloses personal information for
  pecuniary gain commits a misdemeanor of the first degree.
  (d) Penalties under this Section are cumulative and not exclusive of
  remedies available under any other law.
