ARTICLE VII — ENFORCEMENT, PENALTIES, AND CORRECTIVE ORDERS
(Excerpt from the Insurance Information Protection Act, as amended 2016)

  Section 701.  Examination.
      1.  The Commissioner may examine the records, systems, and procedures of
          any holder of records at any time, with or without prior notice.
      2.  The holder shall produce, upon demand: the record system inventory;
          the disclosure log; access grant registers by position title; vendor
          agreements; and certificates of destruction.
      3.  Refusal to produce is itself a violation of this Article.

  Section 702.  Corrective order.
      1.  Upon a finding of deficiency, the Commissioner shall issue a written
          order specifying the provision violated, the record systems affected,
          and the period allowed for cure, which shall not exceed ninety (90)
          days.
      2.  The holder shall file a plan of correction, signed by the Records
          Custodian and by the officer responsible for compliance, within
          fifteen (15) days of service of the order.

  Section 703.  Schedule of civil penalties.
      Penalties are assessed per violation and are cumulative:

          Failure to designate a Records Custodian ............. $  1,000
          Failure to maintain record system inventory ......... $  2,500
          Failure to log a disclosure ......................... $    500
          Disclosure of Identity or Financial Elements
            outside an authorized purpose ..................... $ 10,000
          Disclosure of Health Elements outside an
            authorized purpose ................................ $ 15,000
          Failure to encrypt portable media ................... $  5,000
          Improper disposal of records ........................ $  5,000
          Failure to give notice of unauthorized acquisition
            within the prescribed period ...................... $ 10,000
          Obstruction of examination .......................... $ 25,000

  Section 704.  Aggregate limits.
      1.  Penalties assessed against a single holder for violations arising
          from a single event shall not exceed five hundred thousand dollars
          ($500,000), except as provided in subsection 2.
      2.  The limit in subsection 1 does not apply where the violation was
          willful, or where the holder concealed the violation from the
          Commissioner.

  Section 705.  Willful violation.
      1.  A willful violation is a misdemeanor and, in addition to civil
          penalty, is punishable as provided by general law.
      2.  Sale or barter of any element within the Identity, Financial, or
          Health categories for consideration, absent written authorization,
          is deemed willful.

  Section 706.  License action.
      The Commissioner may suspend, revoke, or refuse to renew a certificate
      of authority or producer license upon a second finding of violation
      within any twenty-four (24) month period.

  Section 707.  Appeal.
      An order under this Article is subject to review upon written request
      filed within thirty (30) days of service. Filing does not stay the
      operation of a corrective order absent express direction of the
      Commissioner.
