TITLE 9 — COMMERCE AND TRADE
CHAPTER 44 — PROTECTION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD BY CARRIERS, FREIGHT FORWARDERS AND WAREHOUSE OPERATORS
(Excerpt: §§ 44-101 to 44-104)

§ 44-101. Short title; scope of application. This chapter may be cited as the Personal Information Custody Act. It applies to every person or entity that, in the ordinary course of transporting, forwarding, brokering, consolidating, cross-docking or storing goods for hire within this State, comes into possession of a record containing one or more protected identifier categories, whether that record is maintained in paper form, on microfiche, on magnetic or optical media, or within a computerised database. The application of this chapter is not defeated by the circumstance that the record was created by a shipper, a consignee, a customs broker or a connecting carrier, nor by the circumstance that the entity in possession acts solely as agent, bailee or subcontractor of the party by whom the information was first furnished.

§ 44-102. Definitions. As used in this chapter, unless the context plainly requires otherwise:

(a) "Protected identifier category" means any of the following data elements when appearing in a record in association with the surname or given name of a natural person: residential or mailing address; telephone or facsimile number; electronic mail address; date of birth; social security or taxpayer identification number; operator's licence or State identification card number; passport or alien registration number; financial account number, or credit or debit card number, whether or not accompanied by a security code, access code, personal identification number or password; employee or payroll number; and any unique biometric datum, including a fingerprint image or a voiceprint template.

(b) "Record holder" means the entity in actual or constructive possession of a record described in § 44-101, and includes an entity that has delegated custody of such a record to a service bureau, an off-site storage vendor or a data processing contractor, the delegation being without effect upon the obligations imposed by this chapter.

(c) "Records Custodian" means the individual designated in writing pursuant to § 44-103(a), by role title and not otherwise, and charged with the duties enumerated in that section.

(d) "Disposal" means the discarding, abandonment, sale or transfer of a record, or the release of a medium upon which a record is stored, in circumstances in which the record holder no longer exercises control over the record.

§ 44-103. Duties of the record holder. (a) Every record holder shall designate a Records Custodian and shall maintain, at each terminal, depot or administrative office at which protected identifier categories are held, a register bearing the column headings: Record Series; Medium of Storage; Identifier Categories Present; Originating Party Role; Date of Receipt; Retention Expiry; Authorised Access Role Titles; Date of Disposal; Method of Disposal; and Attesting Role Title.

(b) The Records Custodian shall restrict access to protected identifier categories to those role titles whose duties in respect of the movement, storage or billing of freight cannot reasonably be discharged without such access, and shall cause the register required by subsection (a) to be reviewed not less often than semi-annually.

(c) Where a record is furnished to a subcontractor, the record holder shall obtain from that subcontractor a written undertaking, executed by role title, that the subcontractor will observe safeguards not less protective than those required by this chapter.
