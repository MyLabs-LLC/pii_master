STATE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH
ADMINISTRATIVE DIRECTIVE 98-06
RETENTION, AMENDMENT, AND DISPOSITION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION
To: County Health Officers and Records Custodians

PART A — RIGHTS OF THE SUBJECT OF A RECORD

* Upon written request, the Records Custodian shall permit the subject of a record, or the parent or legal guardian of a subject who is a minor, to inspect the record.
    1. Verification of identity shall be obtained before inspection and noted on Form 98-06-B, which shall capture: Request Number; Date of Request; Verification Method; Date of Inspection; Custodian Title.
    2. Access shall be granted within twenty (20) business days of receipt of the request.
    3. Where the responsible clinician certifies in writing that disclosure would be reasonably likely to cause substantial harm, access may be denied in whole or in part; the certification shall be filed with the record.

* Upon written request, the subject may seek amendment of information alleged to be inaccurate, irrelevant, incomplete, or out of date.
    1. The Records Custodian shall grant or deny the request within thirty (30) days.
    2. A denial shall state the reason, the title of the reviewing official, and the procedure for filing a statement of disagreement.
    3. A statement of disagreement, if filed, shall be attached to the record and transmitted with any subsequent disclosure of the disputed portion.

PART B — RETENTION

* Minimum retention periods shall be observed as follows:
    1. Communicable disease case investigation files — six (6) years from case closure.
    2. Immunization records — until the subject attains twenty-six (26) years of age, or ten (10) years, whichever is longer.
    3. Environmental health complaint files bearing complainant identifiers — three (3) years.
    4. Vital records worksheets — permanent.
    5. Audit logs generated under Rule HD-114 § 104 — three (3) years.

* No record shall be destroyed while it is the subject of a pending request, appeal, audit, litigation hold, or open investigation.

PART C — DISPOSITION

* Destruction shall be by shredding, pulping, or incineration. Placement in ordinary refuse or recycling streams is prohibited.
* Destruction performed by a contractor shall be witnessed by a departmental employee or evidenced by a certificate of destruction retained for six (6) years.
* Each disposition shall be entered in the Disposition Register, completing the columns: Register Entry Number; System Title; Volume or Box Identifier; Inclusive Dates of Material; Method of Destruction; Date of Destruction; Witnessing Official Title.
* The Records Custodian shall certify annually to the Health Officer that dispositions during the preceding year conformed to the approved retention schedule.
