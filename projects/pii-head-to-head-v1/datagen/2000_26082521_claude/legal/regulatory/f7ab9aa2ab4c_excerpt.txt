EXCERPT OF STATUTE — EDUCATION CODE, TITLE VII, CHAPTER 4
CONFIDENTIALITY OF PUPIL RECORDS
Enacted 1995. Amended 1997. Effective July 1, 1997.

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.
This chapter may be cited as the Pupil Records Confidentiality Act.

SECTION 2. DEFINITIONS.
As used in this chapter, unless the context otherwise requires:
(a) "Pupil record" means any item of information, in any medium, maintained by a local education agency and directly related to an identifiable pupil, excluding notes of instructional personnel retained in the sole possession of the maker.
(b) "Personal identifier" means any of the following categories of data element: pupil surname and given name; parent or guardian surname and given name; residence address; mailing address; home telephone number; date of birth; place of birth; state-assigned pupil identification number; federal taxpayer identification number; photograph or likeness; and any other data element that, alone or in combination with other elements reasonably available, permits the identity of the pupil to be traced with reasonable certainty.
(c) "Directory information" means those data elements designated by resolution of the governing board as subject to release without prior written consent, provided that annual public notice of the designation has been given and the period for written objection has closed.
(d) "Holder of record" means a local education agency, a contractor acting on its behalf, or any officer or employee thereof having custody or control of a pupil record.
(e) "Records Custodian" means the officer designated in writing by the governing board as responsible for the security, accuracy, and lawful disclosure of pupil records at each attendance site.
(f) "Disclosure" means permitting access to, release of, transfer of, or other communication of a pupil record, or of the personal identifiers contained therein, by any means.

SECTION 3. OBLIGATIONS OF HOLDERS OF RECORDS.
(a) Each holder of record shall maintain pupil records in a locked file cabinet or in an access-controlled electronic system.
(b) Each holder of record shall obtain the prior written consent of the parent, guardian, or eligible pupil before any disclosure, except as authorized by Section 5.
(c) A Records Custodian shall be designated at each attendance site and the designation filed with the Superintendent. A vacancy in the designation shall be filled within ten (10) working days.
(d) Each holder of record shall amend, upon substantiated written request, any personal identifier shown to be inaccurate, misleading, or in violation of the privacy rights of the pupil.
(e) No holder of record shall use a personal identifier for a purpose other than that for which it was collected.

SECTION 4. ACCESS LOG.
A log shall be maintained with each pupil record, in the form prescribed by the Department, bearing the following column headings: DATE OF ACCESS; NAME OF REQUESTING PARTY; AGENCY OR RELATIONSHIP; LEGITIMATE INTEREST ASSERTED; RECORD SEGMENT EXAMINED; INITIALS OF CUSTODIAN. The log shall be available for inspection by the parent, guardian, or eligible pupil during regular business hours.

SECTION 5. PENALTIES.
Violation of Section 3 or Section 4 constitutes a misdemeanor punishable by fine. Each unlawful disclosure is a separate offense. The Department may withhold apportionment funds from an agency found in repeated violation.
