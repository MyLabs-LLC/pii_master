SECTION B — APPLICABILITY
This rule applies to every municipally owned utility serving more than two thousand accounts. A utility below that threshold shall file the abbreviated certification on Form PUC-1123-S.

SECTION F — PENALTIES
Failure to file within thirty days of the close of the reporting period, or filing a certification known to be inaccurate, subjects the utility to an administrative penalty of not more than one thousand dollars for each day the deficiency continues. The Commission may direct an examination of the utility's records at the utility's expense.
