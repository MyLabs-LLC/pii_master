I.  APPLICABILITY

    § 909.14(a). This section applies to every holder of records operating a retail
    pharmacy permit, and to any contractor performing data processing, mailing, or
    claims adjudication on the holder's behalf.

II. CLASSIFICATION SCHEDULE

    § 909.14(b). Each data element maintained in a covered record shall be assigned
    to a class in accordance with the following schedule. The schedule shall be
    reproduced in the holder's written information security program.

    Class | Data Element                    | Disclosure Tier | Retention | Masking
    ------+---------------------------------+-----------------+-----------+---------
      A   | Patient surname and given name  | Restricted      | 7 yrs     | Required
      A   | Social security number          | Prohibited      | 7 yrs     | Required
      A   | Health plan member number       | Restricted      | 7 yrs     | Required
      A   | Patient account number          | Restricted      | 7 yrs     | Required
      B   | Residence address               | Restricted      | 7 yrs     | Required
      B   | Telephone number of record      | Restricted      | 7 yrs     | Required
      B   | Date of birth                   | Limited         | 7 yrs     | Required
      C   | Date of dispensing              | Limited         | 10 yrs    | Optional
      C   | Prescriber license number       | Limited         | 10 yrs    | Optional
      C   | National drug code              | Unrestricted    | 10 yrs    | None
      D   | Postal code, first three digits | Unrestricted    | 10 yrs    | None

III. TIER DEFINITIONS

    § 909.14(c)(1). "Prohibited" — the element shall not be released to any party
    except pursuant to lawful process, and shall not be printed on any label,
    receipt, or shipping document.

    § 909.14(c)(2). "Restricted" — the element may be released only upon
    verification under Rule 44-2.03 and only to a requesting party within an
    authorized category.

    § 909.14(c)(3). "Limited" — the element may be released in combination with
    Class C or Class D elements only, and never in combination with a Class A
    element absent written authorization.

    § 909.14(c)(4). "Unrestricted" — the element may be released without
    verification, provided no Class A or Class B element accompanies it.

IV. OBLIGATIONS

    § 909.14(d). The Records Custodian shall review the classification of each new
    data element introduced by a system upgrade, vendor interface, or form revision
    prior to production use, and shall record the review on the Element
    Classification Register (columns: Element Name; Source System; Assigned Class;
    Reviewer Title; Review Date).

    § 909.14(e). Where a data element is capable of more than one classification,
    the more restrictive class shall govern.
