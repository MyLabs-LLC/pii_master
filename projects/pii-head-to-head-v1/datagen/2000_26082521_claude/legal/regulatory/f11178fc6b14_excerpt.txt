RULE 12.03 — MINIMUM STANDARDS FOR THE PROTECTION OF MEMBER RECORDS

12.03(1) Purpose. This rule prescribes minimum standards for the protection of member records maintained by credit unions subject to the Department's supervision.

12.03(2) Definitions.
12.03(2)(a) "Member record" means any record containing an identifier drawn from Category I or Category II.
12.03(2)(b) "Category I identifier" means: Social Security or taxpayer identification number; account number; share or loan number; card number; personal identification number; online banking user identification or password.
12.03(2)(c) "Category II identifier" means: full name; residential or mailing address; telephone number; electronic mail address; date of birth; employer name; signature image.
12.03(2)(d) "Access" means the ability to view, copy, alter, transmit, or destroy a member record.

12.03(3) Standards.
12.03(3)(a) A credit union shall assign access rights by job function and shall review each assignment upon transfer or separation of the employee.
12.03(3)(b) Member records containing a Category I identifier shall not be transmitted by unsecured facsimile or unencrypted electronic mail.
12.03(3)(c) Workstations from which member records may be viewed shall be positioned or shielded so that the display is not visible from any area open to the public.
12.03(3)(d) A log of access to archived member records shall be retained for two years and shall record the date, the requesting office, the record series, and the purpose of access.

12.03(4) Examination. Compliance with this rule shall be assessed at each regular examination.
