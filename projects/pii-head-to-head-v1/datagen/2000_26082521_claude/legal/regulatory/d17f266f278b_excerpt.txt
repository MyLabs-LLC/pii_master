TITLE 22 - EDUCATION
CHAPTER 14, ARTICLE 6 - ENFORCEMENT, PENALTIES, AND REMEDIES (EXCERPT)

Sec. 14-601.  Prohibited acts.

  (a)  No Covered Holder, and no officer, employee, or contractor of a
       Covered Holder, shall:

       (1)  disclose an Agency Record, or any identifier category
            contained in an Agency Record, except as authorized by
            Sec. 14-501 through Sec. 14-509;

       (2)  collect or retain a data element that has not been
            classified under Sec. 14-202 and entered in the published
            notice of systems of records;

       (3)  sell, barter, lease, or offer for commercial advantage any
            Agency Record or any compilation derived from one;

       (4)  fail to maintain the disclosure log required by
            Sec. 14-512; or

       (5)  obstruct, delay, or provide false information in response
            to an audit conducted by the Department.

  (b)  A violation of subsection (a) is complete upon the act and does
       not require proof of injury to any individual.

Sec. 14-602.  Schedule of civil penalties.

  The Department may assess a civil penalty against a Covered Holder in
  accordance with the following schedule. Each affected record is a
  separate violation for purposes of codes P-03 and P-07.

CODE   DESCRIPTION OF VIOLATION                TIER  FIRST    REPEAT
-----  --------------------------------------  ----  -------  -------
P-01   Failure to appoint a Records Custodian  C     $   250  $   750
P-02   Failure to maintain a disclosure log    B     $   500  $ 1,500
P-03   Disclosure without lawful authority     A     $ 2,500  $ 7,500
P-04   Collection of an unclassified element   B     $ 1,000  $ 3,000
P-05   Retention beyond the approved tier      C     $   500  $ 1,500
P-06   Failure to notify of a breach event     A     $ 2,500  $ 7,500
P-07   Sale or barter of a covered record      A     $ 5,000  $15,000
P-08   Obstruction of a Department audit       A     $ 2,500  $ 7,500

Sec. 14-603.  Aggregate limitation.

  Penalties assessed against a single Covered Holder arising from a
  single course of conduct shall not exceed two hundred fifty thousand
  dollars in any one fiscal year, except where the conduct falls under
  code P-07, in which case no aggregate limitation applies.

Sec. 14-604.  Aggravating and mitigating factors.

  In setting a penalty within the range prescribed by Sec. 14-602, the
  Department shall consider the following factors, weighted as shown:

  Number of individuals whose records were affected .......... 30 pct
  Sensitivity of the identifier categories involved .......... 25 pct
  Whether the violation was knowing or reckless .............. 20 pct
  Adequacy of controls in place at the time of the act ....... 15 pct
  Promptness and completeness of self-reporting .............. 10 pct

Sec. 14-605.  Individual liability.

  An officer, employee, or contractor who knowingly commits an act
  described in Sec. 14-601(a)(1) or (a)(3) for the purpose of personal
  gain is guilty of a misdemeanor and, upon conviction, is subject to a
  fine of not more than five thousand dollars, imprisonment for not more
  than one year, or both. Conviction under this section is grounds for
  revocation of any certificate issued by the State Board of Education.

Sec. 14-606.  Remedies of the individual.

  An individual whose Agency Record has been disclosed in violation of
  this chapter may bring a civil action for actual damages, injunctive
  relief, and reasonable attorney fees. The remedies in this section are
  cumulative and do not bar any penalty assessed under Sec. 14-602.
