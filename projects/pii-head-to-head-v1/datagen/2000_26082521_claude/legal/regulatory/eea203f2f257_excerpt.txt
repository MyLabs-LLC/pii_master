STATE BOARD RULE 5-9.104
NOTIFICATION OF BREACH OF SECURITY OF PERSONAL INFORMATION MAINTAINED BY SCHOOL DISTRICTS

(1) Discovery and internal reporting. Upon discovery or notification of a breach, the district shall —
  (a) — notify the Records Custodian and the Superintendent within twenty-four hours;
  (b) — suspend routine destruction of any record relevant to the incident;
  (c) — open an incident file bearing an incident number assigned in sequence.

(2) Assessment. The Records Custodian shall determine, and record in the incident file, each of the following —
  (a) — the date of the breach and the date of discovery;
  (b) — the number of individuals affected, stated by category (currently enrolled pupils; former pupils; parents or guardians; employees; applicants);
  (c) — the personal identifier categories involved, selected from: name; Social Security number; state pupil identification number; date of birth; residence address; telephone number; electronic mail address; financial account number; payment card number; driver's license or state identification card number; health or disability information; biometric record; username and password or security question and answer;
  (d) — whether the information was encrypted, and if so, whether the encryption key was also acquired;
  (e) — the reasonable likelihood of harm to the affected individuals.

(3) Notification to individuals. Where subsection (2)(e) is answered in the affirmative, written notice shall be given in the most expedient time possible and without unreasonable delay, and in no event later than forty-five days after discovery, except where a law enforcement agency determines in writing that notice will impede a criminal investigation. Each notice shall state —
  (a) — the date or estimated date range of the breach;
  (b) — a description of the personal identifier categories involved;
  (c) — the toll-free telephone number and mailing address of the district office designated to receive inquiries;
  (d) — the toll-free numbers, addresses, and Internet addresses of the consumer reporting agencies and of the Federal Trade Commission;
  (e) — advice to review account statements and to request a security freeze.

(4) Notification to the Department. Where more than five hundred individuals are affected, the district shall file with the Department, on the form prescribed, a report stating the incident number, the assessment required by subsection (2), the date and method of individual notification, and the corrective measures adopted.

(5) Substitute notice. Substitute notice is permitted only where the cost of individual notice exceeds $250,000, the affected class exceeds five hundred thousand individuals, or the district lacks sufficient contact information. Substitute notice shall consist of electronic mail where an address is available, conspicuous posting on the district website for not less than thirty days, and notification to major statewide media.

(6) Records. The incident file shall be retained for five years and shall be produced to the Department upon request.
