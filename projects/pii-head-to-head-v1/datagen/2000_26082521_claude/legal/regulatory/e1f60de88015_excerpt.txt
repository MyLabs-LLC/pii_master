RULE 340. PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD BY THE COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT

340.1 Authority. Promulgated by the Board of Health.

340.2 Definitions.
 (1) "Record" — any item, collection, or grouping of personal information maintained by the Department, whether in paper, electronic, photographic, or audio form.
 (2) "System of record" — a group of records from which information is retrieved by the name of an individual or by an identifying number, symbol, or other particular assigned to the individual.
 (3) "Routine use" — a disclosure compatible with the purpose for which the information was collected.
 (4) "De-identified" — stripped of all elements enumerated in Rule 340.4 such that the remaining information cannot reasonably be used to identify an individual.

340.3 Notice. At the point of collection the Department shall provide notice stating: the authority for collection; whether disclosure is mandatory or voluntary; the principal purpose; the routine uses; and the consequences of refusing to supply the information.

340.4 Enumerated elements. Name; street address; city; ZIP code; county of residence; telephone number; fax number; electronic mail address; social security number; medical record number; health plan number; account number; certificate or license number; vehicle identifier; device identifier; web address; internet protocol address; biometric identifier; photographic image; date of birth; admission date; discharge date; date of death; and any other unique identifying number or code.

340.5 Duties of the holder.
 (a) Verify the identity and authority of any person requesting a record before disclosure.
 (b) Log each disclosure in the Disclosure Register, recording: date; recipient organization; recipient role; purpose; identifier categories released; and authority relied upon.
 (c) Furnish the Disclosure Register to the Records Custodian upon request and in any event annually.

340.6 Individual rights. An individual may inspect a record pertaining to that individual, request amendment, and receive an accounting of disclosures for the preceding six years. Requests shall be submitted on the Department's request form and acted upon within thirty days.

340.7 Contractors. Every contract involving access to personal information shall incorporate the Department's Business Associate Addendum and shall require the contractor to indemnify the County for costs of breach notification.

340.8 Penalties. Any person who knowingly obtains or discloses a record in violation of this Rule shall be liable for a civil penalty not exceeding the amount fixed by statute for each violation, in addition to any discipline imposed under the County Personnel Rules.
