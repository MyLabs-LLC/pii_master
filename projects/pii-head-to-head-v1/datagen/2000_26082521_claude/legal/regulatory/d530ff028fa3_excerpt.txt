MEMORANDUM

TO:      District Managers; Pharmacists-in-Charge
FROM:    Office of Regulatory Compliance
RE:      Excerpt — Chapter 14, Article 6: Enforcement and Penalties
DATE:    November 9, 1998

The Department has issued the enforcement article in final form. It is set out below
in full and takes effect on the first day of the calendar quarter following adoption.

ARTICLE 6 — ENFORCEMENT AND PENALTIES

6.01  GROUNDS FOR CITATION. The Department may issue a citation to a holder of
      records upon a finding of any of the following:
      6.01.1  Failure to designate a Records Custodian, or failure to file or amend
              the designation within the period prescribed.
      6.01.2  Release of a Class A or Class B identifier without verification.
      6.01.3  Failure to maintain the disclosure log, or maintenance of a log that
              omits any required column.
      6.01.4  Storage of covered records awaiting destruction other than in a locked
              receptacle within the prescription department.
      6.01.5  Destruction of covered records prior to expiration of the retention
              period.

6.02  SCHEDULE OF CIVIL PENALTIES.
      6.02.1  First citation within a rolling twenty-four (24) month period:
              written reprimand and a corrective action plan filed within thirty
              (30) days.
      6.02.2  Second citation: civil penalty of not less than two hundred fifty
              dollars ($250) and not more than one thousand dollars ($1,000) per
              violation.
      6.02.3  Third and subsequent citations: civil penalty of not more than five
              thousand dollars ($5,000) per violation, and referral to the Board for
              action against the permit.

6.03  AGGREGATION. Violations arising from a single act affecting multiple covered
      records shall be aggregated and treated as one violation for purposes of
      section 6.02, provided the holder reports the act to the Department within
      five (5) business days of discovery.

6.04  MITIGATION. In assessing a penalty the Department shall consider the number
      of identifier elements involved, the disclosure tier of those elements, the
      holder's compliance history, and the promptness of self-reporting.

6.05  APPEAL. A holder may contest a citation by filing a written request for
      hearing with the Department within twenty (20) days of service. Filing stays
      collection of the penalty but does not stay a corrective action plan.
