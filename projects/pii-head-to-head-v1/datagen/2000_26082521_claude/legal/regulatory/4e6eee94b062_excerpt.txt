Subdivision 2 of the rule enumerates the identifier categories to which the safeguards of subdivisions 3 through 6 attach. The enumeration is reproduced verbatim in the left-hand column. The remaining columns are the departmental crosswalk maintained by this office and are advisory only; where the crosswalk and the rule are in conflict, the rule governs.

     CLASS DEFINITIONS. R-1: record may not leave the secured area except under
     transmittal receipt; screen display restricted to assigned staff. R-2:
     record may be used in the ordinary course of program business; disclosure
     outside the Department requires the written concurrence of the program
     manager. R-3: record is subject to public inspection in whole or in part
     under the public records law, and the holder of records shall consult this
     office before releasing any field not expressly declared public.

The rule requires that this enumeration be reviewed annually. Divisions shall report to this office, on or before September 30, any field in local use that in the judgment of the program manager falls within category (14) and is not presently listed above.
