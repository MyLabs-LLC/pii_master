TITLE 12 — FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS AND FIDUCIARIES
CHAPTER 44 — SAFEGUARDING OF PERSONAL INFORMATION BY LICENSED TITLE AND ESCROW ENTITIES

§ 44.101. Purpose.
This chapter establishes minimum standards for the collection, retention, transmission, and disposal of personal information obtained by licensees in the course of title search, examination, closing, escrow, and settlement services.

§ 44.102. Definitions.
As used in this chapter, unless the context requires otherwise:

(a) "Licensee" means any title insurance agent, title insurance agency, escrow agent, or independent settlement service provider holding a certificate of authority under Title 12, Chapter 9.

(b) "Personal information" means an individual's first name or first initial and last name in combination with any one or more of the following data elements, when either the name or the data element is not encrypted or redacted:
 (1) Social Security number;
 (2) driver's license number or state identification card number;
 (3) financial account number, credit card number, or debit card number, with or without any required security code, access code, personal identification number, or password;
 (4) taxpayer identification number;
 (5) passport number or other government-issued identification number;
 (6) date of birth;
 (7) mother's maiden name or other knowledge-based authentication response;
 (8) biometric identifier, including fingerprint, voiceprint, or retina or iris image;
 (9) health insurance policy number or subscriber identification number;
 (10) username or email address in combination with a password or security question answer permitting access to an online account.

(c) "Personal information" does not include information lawfully made available to the general public from federal, state, or local government records, including instruments recorded in the office of the county recorder.

(d) "Record" means any material, regardless of physical form or characteristics, on which personal information is inscribed or stored, including paper documents, microform, magnetic media, optical media, and electronic files maintained on servers, workstations, portable devices, or third-party hosted systems.

(e) "Records Custodian" means the officer or employee designated by a licensee under § 44.201(a) as responsible for the information security program.

(f) "Disposal" means the destruction, erasure, or other modification of personal information contained in a record so as to render the information unreadable, indecipherable, and incapable of reconstruction by ordinary means.

(g) "Breach of the security of the system" means the unauthorized acquisition of unencrypted computerized data that compromises the security, confidentiality, or integrity of personal information maintained by a licensee. Good-faith acquisition by an employee or agent of the licensee for a legitimate business purpose is not a breach, provided the personal information is not used or subject to further unauthorized disclosure.

§ 44.103. Scope.
This chapter applies to all personal information in a licensee's custody or control, without regard to whether the individual to whom the information pertains is a party to the transaction, and without regard to whether the record is maintained within or outside the geographic boundaries of this state.

§ 44.104. Construction.
Nothing in this chapter relieves a licensee of any obligation arising under federal law, including the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act, 15 U.S.C. § 6801 et seq., and the regulations promulgated thereunder. Where a conflict exists, the more protective requirement governs.
