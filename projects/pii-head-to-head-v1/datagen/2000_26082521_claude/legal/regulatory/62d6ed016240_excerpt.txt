INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

  TO:      Registrar
  FROM:    University Data Governance Office
  SUBJECT: Board of Regents Administrative Rule 8-4-3 — Data Element Inventory
  CITE:    Reg. Code tit. 8, ch. 4, r. 8-4-3 (adopted Mar. 2011)

Text of the rule as adopted:

  (a) Each institution shall file with the Board an inventory of every system of records containing personal information as defined at Chapter 205.102(2).

  (b) The inventory shall be submitted on the prescribed form and shall report, for each system, the following columns only:

        SYSTEM OF RECORDS NAME | RECORDS CUSTODIAN (TITLE) | DATA ELEMENTS MAINTAINED |
        CATEGORY | LEGAL BASIS FOR COLLECTION | RETENTION PERIOD | DISPOSITION METHOD

  (c) No individual record, extract, or specimen entry shall accompany the inventory. The inventory reports field names and counts only.

  (d) The inventory shall be revised within sixty (60) days of any addition or deletion of a Category I or Category II field, and shall be recertified annually by the institution's designated compliance officer.

  (e) Failure to file a conforming inventory shall be reported to the Board under r. 8-4-9.

Action required: initial filing due September 1, 2011. Direct questions to the Data Governance Office.
