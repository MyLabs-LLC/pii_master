SUBCHAPTER D — EXAMINATION, ENFORCEMENT AND PENALTIES

§ 44.401. Examination.
The Commissioner may examine the books, records, and information systems of any licensee at any time to determine compliance with this chapter. The licensee shall make the Records Custodian, or the alternate, available for interview.

§ 44.402. Production of Documents.
Upon written request, a licensee shall produce within ten (10) business days:
 (1) the written designation required by § 44.201(b);
 (2) the current written information security program;
 (3) the record inventory required by § 44.202(b)(1);
 (4) disposal logs for the preceding twenty-four (24) months;
 (5) training attendance records;
 (6) service provider contracts and audit reports;
 (7) investigation records maintained under § 44.307.

§ 44.403. Administrative Penalties.
(a) A licensee who violates any provision of this chapter shall be subject to an administrative penalty of not more than five thousand dollars ($5,000) for each violation.

(b) Each day a violation continues after written notice from the Commissioner constitutes a separate violation.

(c) A violation involving the records of more than one individual shall be assessed as a single violation unless the Commissioner finds the licensee acted knowingly or with reckless disregard, in which case each affected individual's record constitutes a separate violation, provided that the aggregate penalty for a single incident shall not exceed five hundred thousand dollars ($500,000).

§ 44.404. Suspension and Revocation.
The Commissioner may suspend or revoke a certificate of authority upon a finding that the licensee has:
 (1) knowingly falsified any record required under this chapter;
 (2) failed to designate a Records Custodian for a period exceeding ninety (90) days;
 (3) failed to give notice required under Subchapter C; or
 (4) been subject to three or more penalty assessments under § 44.403 within any thirty-six (36) month period.

§ 44.405. Criminal Referral.
Nothing in this subchapter limits the authority of the Attorney General to refer for prosecution any person who knowingly obtains, transfers, or uses personal information held by a licensee without authorization.

§ 44.406. No Private Right of Action.
This chapter does not create a private right of action. Remedies available under other law are not abridged.

§ 44.407. Hearing and Appeal.
A licensee assessed a penalty under § 44.403 may request a hearing before the Commissioner within twenty (20) days of service of the notice of assessment. Final orders are appealable to the district court of the county in which the licensee maintains its principal place of business.
