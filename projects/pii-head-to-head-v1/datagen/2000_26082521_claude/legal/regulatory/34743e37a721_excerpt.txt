MEMORANDUM

TO:        All Records Custodians, Office of the Registrar
FROM:      Records Compliance Coordinator
RE:        Chapter 205, Sections 205.107 and 205.109 — Duties of Record Holders
POSTED:    August 3, 2011

205.107  SAFEGUARDS; DUTIES OF THE RECORD HOLDER.

     A record holder shall implement and maintain reasonable administrative, technical,
     and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the personal information
     maintained. At minimum, the record holder shall:

     (1)  limit access to Category I and Category II fields to those employees whose
          assigned duties require access, and document each authorization by position
          title rather than by individual assignment memorandum;

     (2)  prohibit the display of the Social Security Number on any identification
          card, roster, posted grade list, mailing label, or transmittal document;

     (3)  substitute the Student Identification Number for the Social Security Number
          in all internal correspondence, worklists, and system-generated reports;

     (4)  encrypt Category I fields at rest and in transmission across any public
          network;

     (5)  destroy records at the end of the approved retention period by shredding,
          incineration, or verified electronic erasure, and record the disposition in
          the destruction register;

     (6)  review access authorizations not less than semiannually and revoke access
          upon transfer or separation of the assigned position.

205.109  DISCLOSURE LOG; CONTENTS.

     A record holder shall maintain, for each system of records, a log of every
     disclosure of personal information made to a party other than the individual or
     an employee acting within the scope of assigned duties. The log shall be retained
     for the life of the record and shall contain the following columns:

     DATE OF DISCLOSURE | REQUESTING PARTY CLASSIFICATION | DATA ELEMENTS RELEASED |
     STATUTORY OR REGULATORY BASIS | CONSENT ON FILE (Y/N) | RELEASING CUSTODIAN TITLE

     The log shall be made available to the individual who is the subject of the record
     upon written request, and to the Attorney General upon demand. A disclosure made
     under a lawfully issued subpoena shall be logged, and the individual notified in
     advance of compliance, unless the issuing authority has ordered otherwise.

205.110  NOTIFICATION OF BREACH.

     Upon discovery of a breach of the security of the system, the record holder shall
     notify the Records Custodian and the agency head without unreasonable delay, and
     shall notify each affected individual in the most expedient time possible and
     without unreasonable delay, consistent with the legitimate needs of law
     enforcement. Notification shall describe the categories of data elements involved,
     the approximate date of the breach, and the measures the record holder has taken.
