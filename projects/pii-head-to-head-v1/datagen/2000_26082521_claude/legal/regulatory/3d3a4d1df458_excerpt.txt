ADMINISTRATIVE DIRECTIVE HD-2011-06
SUBJECT: Handling of Personal Information — Supplemental Requirements
ISSUED BY: Office of the Health Officer
SUPERSEDES: HD-2008-14

1. APPLICABILITY. All Department personnel and all persons granted access to Department systems.

2. WORKSTATION CONTROLS.
 2.1 Displays shall be positioned so that personal information is not visible from public corridors or waiting areas.
 2.2 Sessions shall lock automatically after ten minutes of inactivity.
 2.3 Printing of records containing identifier Category II or Category V shall occur only on printers located in secured areas.

3. TRANSPORT OF RECORDS.
 3.1 Records removed from a Department facility shall be logged out with the Records Custodian, recording: requesting program unit; number of files; identifier categories; destination facility; date out; and date returned.
 3.2 Records in transit shall be carried in an opaque, closed container.
 3.3 Records shall not be left unattended in a vehicle.

4. ELECTRONIC MAIL. Personal information shall not be placed in the subject line of any message. Message bodies containing identifier Categories I through VII shall be sent only through the Department's secure messaging gateway.

5. REPORTING. Suspected loss, theft, or improper disclosure shall be reported immediately to the supervisor and to the Records Custodian. Personnel shall not attempt independent investigation or recovery.

6. ENFORCEMENT. Non-compliance shall be documented on the Corrective Action form and forwarded to Human Resources. Repeated non-compliance shall result in withdrawal of system access.
