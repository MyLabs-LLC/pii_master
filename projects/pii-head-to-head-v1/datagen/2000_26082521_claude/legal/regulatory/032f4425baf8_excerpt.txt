EXCERPT — PROTECTION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD BY EDUCATIONAL AGENCIES
Article 11, Sections 1 through 9, as amended effective July 1, 2010

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE AND PURPOSE.

     (a) This Article shall be known and may be cited as the Personal
     Information Protection Article.

     (b) It is the declared policy of this State that personal information
     assembled, collected, or maintained by an educational agency in the
     ordinary course of its administrative operations is held in trust for the
     individual to whom the information pertains, and that such information
     shall not be disclosed, disseminated, sold, transferred, or otherwise made
     available except as expressly authorized herein.

SECTION 2. DEFINITIONS.

     As used in this Article, unless the context clearly requires otherwise:

     (1) "Educational agency" means any school district, cooperative,
     intermediate unit, charter school, or governing board thereof, and includes
     any officer, employee, agent, contractor, or subcontractor acting on behalf
     of the foregoing.

     (2) "Holder of records" means any officer or employee of an educational
     agency who, by reason of the duties of the position held, creates, receives,
     stores, transmits, or disposes of personal information, whether in paper,
     microform, magnetic, optical, or other electronic medium.

     (3) "Personal information" means an individual's first name or first
     initial and last name in combination with any one or more of the following
     data elements, when either the name or the data element is not encrypted or
     redacted:

          (A) Social Security Number;
          (B) Driver's License Number or State Identification Card Number;
          (C) Financial Account Number, Credit Card Number, or Debit Card
              Number, in combination with any required security code, access
              code, or password permitting access to the account;
          (D) Medical Record Number or Health Insurance Identification Number;
          (E) Biometric Identifier, including fingerprint, retina scan, or
              voiceprint;
          (F) Date of Birth in combination with Place of Birth or Mother's
              Maiden Name.

     (4) "Directory information" means those data elements enumerated by rule of
     the Commissioner and does not include any element listed in paragraph (3).

     (5) "Breach of the security of the system" means the unauthorized
     acquisition of computerized data that compromises the security,
     confidentiality, or integrity of personal information maintained by an
     educational agency. Good-faith acquisition by a holder of records for a
     purpose of the agency is not a breach, provided the information is not used
     or subject to further unauthorized disclosure.

SECTION 3. DUTIES OF HOLDERS OF RECORDS.

     Every holder of records shall (i) limit the collection of personal
     information to that which is reasonably necessary to the administration of
     the educational program; (ii) maintain such information in a secure manner
     appropriate to the medium in which it is kept; (iii) restrict access to
     those persons having a legitimate educational interest; and (iv) maintain a
     contemporaneous log of each disclosure, which log shall record the field
     labels Requestor, Relationship to Individual, Records Requested, Statutory
     Basis, Date of Release, and Authorizing Signature.
