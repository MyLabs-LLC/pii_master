TITLE 12, CHAPTER 47 — APPENDIX B
MINIMUM CONTENT OF THE MEMBER RECORD INVENTORY

    Authority: § 47.201(b).

    A covered institution shall maintain a current inventory of the record
    series in which personal information is held. The inventory is a schedule
    of series; it does not contain member data. For each series the inventory
    shall carry the following columns:

        Column 1    Record series name
        Column 2    Originating department
        Column 3    Business purpose
        Column 4    Identifier categories present (I, II, III, IV)
        Column 5    Medium (paper / microform / magnetic / optical / hosted)
        Column 6    Location or system of record
        Column 7    Retention period and statutory citation
        Column 8    Disposal method under § 47.203(b)
        Column 9    Title of the officer accountable for the series
        Column 10   Date of last verification

    Notes on completion:

        1.  Column 4 carries category designations only. An entry that
            reproduces a Social Security number, account number, date of birth,
            or any other value described at § 47.102(b) is prohibited.

        2.  A series held by a service provider shall be shown in Column 6 by
            the provider's system name, with the governing contract referenced
            in a footnote to the inventory.

        3.  Column 10 shall be updated not less than annually, and upon any
            migration of the series to a different medium or system.

        4.  The inventory shall be presented to examiners at the opening
            conference. Failure to produce a current inventory is a Level 2
            finding under § 47.403(a).
