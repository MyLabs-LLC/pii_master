RULE 12-4.007 — NOTIFICATION OF BREACH OF THE SECURITY OF A SYSTEM
Promulgated under the authority of § 1387.11, Personal Information Protection Act

• Any holder of records that discovers, or is notified of, a breach of the security of a system containing personal information shall commence a good-faith investigation forthwith, and in no event later than the close of the third (3rd) business day following discovery.

• The Records Custodian shall cause to be prepared an incident record setting forth, at a minimum, the following fields, and no such record shall be deemed complete unless each field is populated or affirmatively marked "not determined":
      – Incident Reference Number
      – Date of Discovery
      – Date Range of Exposure
      – Method of Discovery
      – Identifier Categories Implicated
      – Count of Affected Individuals
      – Medium Affected
      – Containment Measures Taken
      – Role Title of Investigator
      – Date Investigation Closed

• Where the investigation determines that personal information was, or is reasonably believed to have been, acquired by an unauthorized person, the holder of records shall give notice to each affected individual in the most expedient time possible and without unreasonable delay, consistent with the legitimate needs of law enforcement and any measures necessary to determine the scope of the breach and restore the reasonable integrity of the system.

• Notice under this Rule shall be given by one or more of the following methods, and the method employed shall be recorded: written notice by first-class mail to the last known mailing address of record; electronic notice, where the affected individual has affirmatively consented to electronic communication; or substitute notice, where the cost of providing notice would exceed two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000), the class to be notified exceeds five hundred thousand (500,000) persons, or the holder of records lacks sufficient contact information.

• Substitute notice shall consist of each of the following: conspicuous posting upon the internet home page of the holder of records for a period of not less than thirty (30) days; notification to major statewide media; and electronic mail notice where an electronic mail address of record exists.

• The notice shall describe, in plain language, the categories of personal information involved, the approximate date of the breach, the measures taken by the holder of records, the steps an individual may take to protect themselves, and the departmental contact channel of the Records Custodian. The notice shall not be delayed on account of the holder of records having not yet ascertained the total number of affected individuals.

• Where more than five hundred (500) residents of this State are affected, written notice shall be provided contemporaneously to the Office of the Attorney General.
