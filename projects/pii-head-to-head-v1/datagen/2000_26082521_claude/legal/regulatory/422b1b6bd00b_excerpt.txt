CHAPTER 44, SUBCHAPTER B — DUTIES OF HOLDERS OF RECORDS

§ 44.201. Designation of a Records Custodian.

(a) Each licensee shall designate in writing one officer or employee as Records Custodian. The designation shall be maintained in the licensee's principal place of business and produced upon demand of the Commissioner.

(b) The written designation shall state, at minimum, the following fields:
 — Designated Role Title
 — Business Unit
 — Effective Date of Designation
 — Reporting Line
 — Alternate Custodian Role Title
 — Signature of Designating Officer

(c) No licensee shall operate for more than thirty (30) days without a Records Custodian of record.

§ 44.202. Written Information Security Program.

(a) The Records Custodian shall develop, implement, and maintain a comprehensive written information security program appropriate to the size and complexity of the licensee, the nature and scope of its activities, and the sensitivity of the personal information at issue.

(b) The program shall address, at minimum:
 (1) inventory of records by category of personal information held;
 (2) restriction of access to personal information to those employees whose duties require it;
 (3) authentication controls for all systems containing personal information;
 (4) encryption of personal information transmitted across public networks and stored on portable devices;
 (5) supervision of service providers under § 44.204;
 (6) disposal procedures under § 44.205;
 (7) incident response procedures under Subchapter C;
 (8) annual review and documented revision.

§ 44.203. Wire and Funds Transfer Instructions.

(a) A licensee shall not accept a change to previously issued disbursement instructions transmitted by electronic mail without independent verbal verification using contact information obtained from a source other than the message requesting the change.

(b) The licensee shall record each verification attempt in the transaction file, noting: Date of Verification, Method of Verification, Role Title of Verifying Employee, and Outcome.

§ 44.204. Service Providers.

(a) A licensee shall take reasonable steps to select and retain service providers capable of maintaining appropriate safeguards.

(b) Contracts executed or renewed after the effective date of this chapter shall require the service provider to implement and maintain such safeguards, to permit audit upon reasonable notice, and to notify the licensee of any breach without unreasonable delay.

§ 44.205. Retention and Disposal.

(a) Records shall be retained for not less than five (5) years following the date of closing or the date the file is otherwise concluded, whichever is later.

(b) Upon expiration of the retention period, records shall be disposed of in accordance with § 44.102(f). Paper records shall be cross-cut shredded, pulped, or incinerated. Electronic media shall be overwritten, degaussed, or physically destroyed.

(c) A disposal log shall be maintained recording: Batch Identifier, Record Category, Volume, Disposal Method, Date of Disposal, and Role Title of Witnessing Employee.

§ 44.206. Employee Training.
Each employee with access to personal information shall receive training upon hire and annually thereafter. Attendance records shall be retained for three (3) years.
