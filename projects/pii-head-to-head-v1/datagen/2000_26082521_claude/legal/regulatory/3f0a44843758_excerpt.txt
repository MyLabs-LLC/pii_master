CHAPTER 14. PUBLIC UTILITIES
ARTICLE VII. CONFIDENTIALITY OF CUSTOMER RECORDS

    § 14-701. Records Declared Confidential.

        (a) Every record of the Utility Department that contains Customer Identifying
            Information is confidential and is not a public record open to inspection
            under Chapter 3, Article II of this Code.

        (b) "Customer Identifying Information" means information recorded in any of the
            following fields of the customer master file, whether maintained in
            machine-readable or paper form:

            (1) Customer Name; Co-Applicant Name;
            (2) Service Address; Mailing Address, where different;
            (3) Telephone Number (Residence); Telephone Number (Business);
            (4) Social Security Number; Taxpayer Identification Number;
            (5) Date of Birth;
            (6) Driver's License Number and issuing jurisdiction;
            (7) Account Number; Meter Number; Deposit Receipt Number;
            (8) Bank Routing Number and Depositor Account Number retained pursuant to a
                written automatic payment authorization;
            (9) Consumption History, Billing History, and Payment History, but only when
                stored or transmitted in association with a field enumerated in
                paragraphs (1) through (8) of this subsection.

        (c) The Records Custodian shall cause each field enumerated in subsection (b) to
            be marked in the data dictionary with the restriction code prescribed by the
            Director of Utilities, and shall re-verify such markings not less than
            annually.

    § 14-702. Disclosure Prohibited.

        (a) No officer, employee, agent, or contractor of the Utility Department shall
            disclose Customer Identifying Information except as authorized by § 14-703.

        (b) Aggregate consumption data from which all fields enumerated in
            § 14-701(b)(1) through (8) have been removed, and which describes not fewer
            than five service connections, is not subject to this section.

(Ord. No. 97-114, § 3; Ord. No. 98-052, § 1.)
