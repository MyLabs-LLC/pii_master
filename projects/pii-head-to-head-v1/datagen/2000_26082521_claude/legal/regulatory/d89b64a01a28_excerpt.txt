§ 8.44 PROTECTION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION IN DEPARTMENTAL RECORDS
(Adopted by ordinance; amendments effective July 1, 2021)

§ 8.44.010 Purpose and Scope.
(a) This chapter governs the collection, retention, use, disclosure, and destruction of personal information contained in records held by the Department of Public Health of the County and by any contractor, subrecipient, or agent acting on the Department's behalf.
(b) This chapter applies to records in any medium, including paper, microform, electronic database, removable media, facsimile transmission, and voice recording.
(c) Where a provision of this chapter conflicts with a federal or state requirement imposing a stricter standard, the stricter standard controls.

§ 8.44.020 Definitions.
For purposes of this chapter:
(1) "Personal information" means information that identifies, relates to, describes, or is reasonably capable of being associated with a particular natural person, whether alone or in combination with other information.
(2) "Direct identifier" means any of the following data elements: Full Name; Social Security Number; Driver's License or State Identification Number; Passport Number; Financial Account Number; Payment Card Number; Medical Record Number; Health Plan Beneficiary Number; Certificate or License Number; Biometric Identifier; Full-Face Photographic Image.
(3) "Indirect identifier" means any of the following data elements, which alone are not sufficient to identify a person but which may be sufficient in combination: Date of Birth; Date of Admission; Date of Discharge; Date of Death; Residential Street Address; Postal Code; Telephone Number; Electronic Mail Address; Internet Protocol Address; Device Serial Number; Vehicle Identification Number; Employer Name; Occupation.
(4) "Special category information" means personal information concerning communicable disease status, reportable condition, immunization history, substance use treatment, reproductive health services, behavioral health services, genetic testing, or immigration status.
(5) "Holder of records" means any person or unit with custody or control of departmental records, including the Records Custodian, program managers, and authorized contractors.
(6) "Disclosure" means the release, transfer, provision of access to, or divulging in any manner of personal information outside the unit that maintains it.

§ 8.44.030 Obligations of Holders of Records.
(a) Minimum necessary. A holder of records shall collect, access, use, and disclose only the minimum personal information necessary to accomplish the authorized public health purpose.
(b) Inventory. Each program unit shall maintain a current record inventory listing, for each record series: Record Series Title; Custodial Unit; Statutory Authority for Collection; Identifier Categories Present; Retention Period; Approved Disposition Method; Date of Last Review.
(c) Access control. Access shall be granted by role and reviewed not less than semiannually. The access register shall record: Role Title; Unit; Systems Authorized; Basis for Access; Date Granted; Date Last Reviewed; Date Terminated.
(d) Disclosure log. Every disclosure not expressly excepted under § 8.44.040 shall be logged with: Record Series; Requesting Entity Type; Legal Basis; Identifier Categories Released; Number of Individuals Affected; Date of Disclosure; Approving Role.
(e) Transmission. Personal information transmitted outside the County network shall be encrypted in transit. Special category information shall not be transmitted by facsimile except to a verified dedicated line.
(f) Destruction. Records past their approved retention period shall be destroyed by cross-cut shredding, pulping, or cryptographic erasure, and a certificate of destruction retained for six years.
(g) Notification. A holder of records shall report any suspected unauthorized acquisition of personal information to the Records Custodian within twenty-four hours of discovery.

§ 8.44.040 Permitted Disclosures Without Authorization.
Disclosure is permitted, without individual authorization, only where required by mandatory reporting statute, ordered by a court of competent jurisdiction, requested by the state health officer for outbreak control, or made to a business associate under a written agreement satisfying § 8.44.050.
