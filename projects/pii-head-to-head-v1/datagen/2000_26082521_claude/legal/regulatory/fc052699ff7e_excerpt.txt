CHAPTER 603, ACTS OF 2010 — PROTECTION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD BY PUBLIC BODIES
Section 11. Enforcement and Penalties (excerpt)

Compliance determinations under this section shall be made against the following items:

[ ] 11(a) — Written information security program on file, dated within the preceding twelve months, and approved by the governing body.
    - Program identifies the officer responsible for its implementation by position title.
    - Program lists each category of personal information held, by data element label.
[ ] 11(b) — Annual risk assessment completed, with findings and remediation dates recorded.
[ ] 11(c) — Contracts with third-party service providers containing the clause required by Section 8.
[ ] 11(d) — Incident response documentation retained for five years from the date of the incident.

Penalties. A public body that fails any item above shall be subject to the following, cumulatively:

[ ] Civil penalty of not more than $5,000 for each violation, each affected individual constituting a separate violation.
[ ] Reimbursement to the Attorney General of reasonable costs of investigation and litigation.
[ ] Injunctive relief compelling compliance within a period fixed by the court, not to exceed one hundred eighty days.
[ ] Referral of any employee who obtained or disclosed personal information for personal gain to the appropriate prosecuting authority under Section 12.

Nothing in this section limits any remedy available under other law, nor creates a private right of action.
