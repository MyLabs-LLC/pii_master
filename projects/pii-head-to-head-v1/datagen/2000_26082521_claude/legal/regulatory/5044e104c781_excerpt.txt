ADMINISTRATIVE RULE 27-4 — SAFEGUARDING OF IDENTIFYING PARTICULARS
Promulgated by the Commissioner; filed with the Secretary of State

27-4.010  APPLICABILITY.

  This Rule applies to every record series maintained by an educational agency
  that contains one or more identifying particulars, without regard to whether
  the record series is designated an education record, a personnel record, a
  medical record, or a record of a business or transportation function.

27-4.020  CATEGORIES OF IDENTIFYING PARTICULARS.

  Identifying particulars are classified into three tiers. The tier assigned to a
  field shall determine the safeguards required by 27-4.030.

  TIER I — DIRECT IDENTIFIERS
      Full Legal Name .................. Student Identification Number
      Social Security Number .......... State Assigned Unique Learner Number
      Employee Payroll Number ......... Biometric Identifier
      Financial Account Number ........ Health Insurance Identification Number

  TIER II — QUASI-IDENTIFIERS
      Date of Birth ................... Place of Birth
      Residential Street Address ...... Mailing Address
      Home Telephone Number ........... Mobile Telephone Number
      Electronic Mail Address ......... Parent or Guardian Legal Name
      Mother's Maiden Name ............ Photographic Likeness

  TIER III — SENSITIVE PROGRAM PARTICULARS
      Disability Classification ....... Individualized Program Identifier
      Free or Reduced Meal Status ..... Immunization Record Number
      Disciplinary Incident Number .... Immigration or Residency Status
      Custody or Guardianship Order Number

27-4.030  MINIMUM SAFEGUARDS.

  (a) Physical. Records containing Tier I or Tier III particulars shall be
      maintained in a locking cabinet or a room secured by key or coded entry.
      Keys shall be issued only upon written authorization of the Records
      Custodian, and a key issuance register shall be maintained showing the
      columns Key Number, Position Title of Holder, Date Issued, and Date
      Returned.

  (b) Technical. Personal information transmitted across a public network shall
      be encrypted. Portable storage media, including magnetic tape, optical
      disc, and removable flash memory, shall not be used to transport Tier I
      particulars off agency premises absent the prior written approval of the
      Records Custodian.

  (c) Facsimile and Telephonic Disclosure. No identifying particular of Tier I
      or Tier III shall be released by facsimile or by telephone unless the
      identity and authority of the recipient have first been independently
      verified and the verification noted in the disclosure log.

  (d) Administrative. Each holder of records shall complete annual instruction
      in the requirements of this Rule and shall execute an acknowledgment
      bearing the field labels Position Title, Building Assignment, Date of
      Instruction, and Signature of Holder.

27-4.040  RETENTION AND DESTRUCTION.

  (a) No record containing an identifying particular shall be destroyed except
      in accordance with the approved retention schedule.

  (b) Destruction shall be by shredding, pulping, or incineration for paper
      media, and by overwriting or physical destruction for electronic media.
      Deposit of intact records in a refuse or recycling receptacle is
      prohibited and shall constitute a violation of 27-4.030(a).

  (c) A certificate of destruction shall be executed and retained for seven
      years, and shall show Record Series Title, Inclusive Dates of Series,
      Volume Destroyed, Method of Destruction, and Position Title of Witness.

27-4.050  NOTIFICATION UPON BREACH.

  Upon discovery of a breach of the security of the system, the holder of records
  shall notify the Records Custodian without unreasonable delay and in no event
  later than twenty-four hours after discovery. The Records Custodian shall
  thereafter cause written notification to be made to each affected individual,
  or to the parent or guardian of a minor, in the most expedient time possible,
  consistent with the legitimate needs of law enforcement and with any measures
  necessary to determine the scope of the breach and restore the integrity of the
  system.
