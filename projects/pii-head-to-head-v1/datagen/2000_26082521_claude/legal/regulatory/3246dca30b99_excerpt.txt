SUBPART D — DESTRUCTION OF RECORDS CONTAINING PERSONAL INFORMATION

§ 61.401 Standard for disposal.
 (a) A holder of records shall dispose of a record containing personal information only by a method that renders the personal information unreadable and incapable of reconstruction.
 (1) For records in paper form, acceptable methods are:
  (i) cross-cut shredding;
  (ii) pulping;
  (iii) incineration.
 (2) For records in electronic form, acceptable methods are:
  (i) overwriting of the storage medium in accordance with a recognized standard;
  (ii) degaussing;
  (iii) physical destruction of the storage medium.
 (b) Deletion of a file, reformatting of a volume, or removal of an index entry does not, standing alone, satisfy paragraph (a) of this section.

§ 61.402 Certificate of destruction.
 (a) Each disposal event shall be recorded on a certificate of destruction bearing the following fields:
  (1) Certificate number;
  (2) Record series and retention code;
  (3) Volume disposed (linear feet or media count);
  (4) Method of destruction;
  (5) Date and place of destruction;
  (6) Position title of the witnessing official;
  (7) Signature block of the Records Custodian.
 (b) Certificates shall be retained for six years and produced on demand of the Commission.

§ 61.403 Disposal by contractor.
 A holder that engages a disposal contractor remains responsible for compliance with § 61.401 and shall obtain the certificate required by § 61.402 within ten business days of each collection.
