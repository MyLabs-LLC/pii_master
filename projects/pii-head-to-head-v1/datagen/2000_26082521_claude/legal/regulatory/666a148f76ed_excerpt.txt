ADMINISTRATIVE RULE 40-11 — DUTIES OF HOLDERS OF RECORDS CONTAINING PERSONAL IDENTIFYING INFORMATION
Promulgated pursuant to § 12-4-903; effective July 1, 2004

40-11.02  General Duty of Care.

Every holder of records shall, at all times and without exception, observe each of the following requirements, and the Records Custodian shall verify compliance therewith not less frequently than semiannually:

(1) DESIGNATION AND ACCOUNTABILITY
    — The Health Officer shall designate in writing a Records Custodian, and shall designate an alternate to act during the absence or incapacity of the Records Custodian.
    — The Records Custodian shall maintain a current inventory of every record system, whether manual or automated, identifying for each system the categories of personal identifying information contained therein, the statutory authority for collection, the retention period, and the responsible program supervisor.

(2) LIMITATION UPON COLLECTION
    — No holder of records shall solicit, collect, or cause to be recorded any data element enumerated in § 12-4-901 unless the element is demonstrably necessary to a lawful function of the Agency.
    — Where a Social Security Number is solicited, the form or screen upon which it is solicited shall bear a notice stating the authority for the request, the principal purposes for which the number is to be used, and whether disclosure by the individual is mandatory or voluntary.

(3) ACCESS CONTROL
    — Access shall be granted upon the principle of least privilege, that is to say, only to those holders of records whose assigned duties require such access.
    — Each user of an automated record system shall be assigned a unique user identifier; group, shared, or generic identifiers are prohibited.
    — Passwords shall be of not fewer than eight characters, shall be changed at intervals not exceeding ninety days, shall not be reused for four successive cycles, and shall not be written, posted, or otherwise displayed in any work area.
    — Terminals, workstations, and printers situated in areas accessible to the public shall be positioned so that displayed information cannot be read by persons other than the operator, and shall invoke a password-protected screen lock after ten minutes of inactivity.

(4) PHYSICAL SAFEGUARDS
    — Paper records shall be secured in locked cabinets or in a locked file room at the close of each business day and whenever the responsible holder of records is absent from the immediate vicinity.
    — Keys and access cards shall be issued by the Records Custodian, logged upon issuance, and surrendered upon separation, transfer, or expiration of assignment.
    — Records shall not be removed from Agency premises except upon written authorization specifying the records removed, the purpose, and the date of required return.

(5) TRANSMISSION
    — Facsimile transmission of records containing any data element enumerated in § 12-4-901 shall be preceded by telephonic confirmation that an authorized recipient is present at the receiving device.
    — Records transmitted over any public network shall be encrypted by a method approved in writing by the Records Custodian.
    — Portable media, including diskettes, compact discs, and magnetic tape, shall be logged out and logged in, and shall be transported in a sealed, opaque container.

(6) DISCLOSURE AND ACCOUNTING
    — No disclosure shall be made except as authorized by law, and every disclosure to a person or entity outside the Agency shall be entered in the Disclosure Log, which shall record the date of disclosure, the record system, the categories of data elements disclosed, the identity and address of the recipient by office or title, and the legal basis relied upon.
    — The Disclosure Log shall be retained for six years from the date of the last entry and shall be produced upon lawful demand.

(7) TRAINING
    — Each holder of records shall complete instruction in this Rule prior to being granted access and annually thereafter, and shall sign a Confidentiality Acknowledgment, which the Records Custodian shall retain for the duration of employment and three years thereafter.
