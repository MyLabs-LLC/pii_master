MEMO — REGULATORY EXCERPT
Chapter 14, Part 3: Destruction of Records Containing Personal Identifiers
Circulated to: Records Custodian; File Room Supervisor; Vendor Management

1.  APPLICATION. This Part governs any holder of records that maintains
    records containing one or more personal identifiers, defined in Part 1
    as: Social Security Number; taxpayer identification number; financial
    account number; payment card number and expiration field; driver's
    license number; passport number; date of birth; mother's maiden name;
    biometric template; and health plan identifier.

2.  STANDARD. Destruction shall render the identifier permanently
    irretrievable. Recycling without prior destruction does not satisfy
    this Part. Placement in an unsecured receptacle constitutes
    abandonment.

3.  VENDOR DESTRUCTION. Where destruction is performed by a contracted
    vendor, the holder shall obtain a certificate of destruction stating:
    Certificate Number; Pickup Date; Container Count; Weight; Method;
    Destruction Facility; Authorized Signatory (title).

4.  RECORDKEEPING. The disposal log required by Section 3 shall be
    retained for six years following the last entry and shall be produced
    on demand.

5.  PENALTIES.

      Violation                              First      Subsequent
      -------------------------------------  ---------  ----------
      Failure to maintain disposal log       $  250     $  1,000
      Improper method of destruction         $  500     $  2,500
      Abandonment of records                 $1,000     $  5,000
      Failure to produce upon demand         $  500     $  2,500

    Penalties accrue per occurrence. A single container of abandoned
    records constitutes one occurrence for each record series contained
    therein.

6.  SAVINGS. Compliance with this Part does not relieve a holder of any
    obligation imposed by federal law or by contract.
