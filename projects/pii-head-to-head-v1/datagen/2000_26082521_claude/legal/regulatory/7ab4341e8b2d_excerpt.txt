COMPLIANCE BULLETIN — POST AT WORK STATION

RULE 214, SUBCHAPTER F: PENALTIES AND ENFORCEMENT (EXCERPT)

214.90 CIVIL PENALTY. A holder of record who violates Section 214.33, 214.34 or
214.35 is subject to an administrative penalty of not more than $2,500 for each
covered record involved, and not more than $50,000 for all violations arising out
of a single course of conduct.

214.91 CONTINUING VIOLATION. Each day a violation of Section 214.31 or 214.32
continues after written notice from the Department is a separate violation.

214.92 KNOWING DISCLOSURE. Disclosure of a personal identifier by an officer or
employee, with knowledge that the disclosure is unauthorized and for pecuniary
gain, is a misdemeanor and, on conviction, is punishable as provided by general
law. Referral does not abate administrative proceedings.

214.93 LICENSE ACTION. The Department may suspend, condition or revoke a license
or certificate of authority for a pattern of violation. Failure to produce the
access log on demand of an examiner is prima facie evidence of violation of
Section 214.33(b).

214.94 MITIGATION. In assessing penalty the Department shall consider prior
history, the number of identifier categories exposed, the promptness of report to
the Department, and corrective measures taken.

214.95 NO PRIVATE RIGHT. Nothing in this subchapter creates a private cause of
action.
