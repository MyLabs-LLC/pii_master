TITLE 22 — EDUCATION
CHAPTER 15. CONFIDENTIALITY OF PUPIL RECORDS
(Excerpt; as amended effective July 1, 2015)

§ 15-201. Application.
(a) This chapter applies to each local education agency, intermediate unit, charter authorizer, and contracted service provider that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits a pupil record.
(b) Compliance with this chapter does not relieve an agency of an obligation arising under federal law, and where a federal requirement affords greater protection, the federal requirement governs.

§ 15-202. Definitions. As used in this chapter, unless the context requires otherwise:
(1) "Pupil record" means information directly related to an identified or identifiable pupil that is maintained by an agency, or by a party acting for the agency, in any medium, including paper, electronic, photographic, and audio media.
(2) "Personally identifiable information" means any of the following, alone or in combination: the pupil's name; the name of a parent, guardian, or other family member; the residential address of the pupil or of the pupil's household; a direct personal identifier, including the state assigned student identifier, a social security number, a federal or state benefit enrollment number, or a biometric record; and an indirect identifier, including date of birth, place of birth, mother's maiden name, telephone number, and personal electronic mail address.
(3) "Records Custodian" means the officer designated by the board under § 15-203(a).
(4) "Directory information" means those data elements listed in the schedule adopted under § 15-206 that the board has determined would not, if disclosed, constitute an unwarranted invasion of personal privacy.

§ 15-203. Designation; duties.
(a) The board of each agency shall designate in writing a Records Custodian and at least one alternate, and shall record the designation in the minutes of a public meeting.
(b) The Records Custodian shall maintain the disclosure log required by § 15-208, respond to inspection requests, and certify annually to the board that the inventory required by § 15-205 is current.
