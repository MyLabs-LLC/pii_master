RE:      Excerpt placed on file — Personal Information Protection Act, Article 7

The text reproduced below is the operative portion of Article 7 as enacted and as amended effective January 1, 2004, and is placed on departmental file pursuant to the standing instruction that each division maintain the governing text at the point of use.

     Sec. 7-101. Definitions.
     As used in this Article, unless the context clearly requires otherwise:
       (a) "Holder of records" means any officer, employee, agent, contractor or
     subcontractor of a covered agency who, in the ordinary course of the
     performance of official duties, creates, receives, maintains, transmits,
     discloses, or otherwise has custody or control of a record containing
     personal information, without regard to whether such person is the
     originator of the record and without regard to whether such record is
     maintained in paper, microform, magnetic, optical, or other electronic form.
       (b) "Personal information" means information which identifies, or which in
     combination with other information reasonably available to the holder of
     records is capable of identifying, a natural person, and includes without
     limitation the following categories of identifier: full name or any part
     thereof; residential or mailing address; telephone, facsimile, or pager
     number; electronic mail address; date of birth; social security number;
     driver's license or state identification number; financial account, credit
     card, or debit card number; medical record number; health plan beneficiary
     or member number; certificate or license number; vehicle identifier or
     license plate number; device identifier or serial number; biometric
     identifier, including finger or voice print; full face photographic image or
     comparable image; and any other unique identifying number, characteristic,
     or code.
       (c) "Disclosure" means the release, transfer, provision of access to, or
     divulging in any manner of personal information to any person or entity
     other than the holder of records.
       (d) "Covered agency" means a State agency, a county or municipal agency, a
     district board of health, and any political subdivision or instrumentality
     of the foregoing.

     Sec. 7-102. Duties of the holder of records; standard of care.
       (a) A holder of records shall maintain personal information with that
     degree of care which a reasonably prudent custodian would exercise under
     like circumstances, and shall not access, examine, or reproduce such
     information except as strictly necessary to the discharge of an assigned
     official duty.
       (b) Each covered agency shall designate in writing a Records Custodian,
     who shall: (i) maintain a current inventory of all systems of records;
     (ii) restrict access to the minimum necessary to accomplish the purpose of
     the access; (iii) cause to be maintained an access log capturing, at
     minimum, the fields Requestor Title, Date of Access, System of Record,
     Purpose Code, and Authorizing Official; and (iv) require of each person
     granted access an annual written acknowledgment of the duties imposed by
     this Article.
       (c) No holder of records shall disclose personal information except
     (1) upon the written authorization of the subject of the record or the
     subject's lawful representative; (2) to a person or agency lawfully entitled
     to receive the same in the performance of a statutory function; (3) pursuant
     to lawful process issued by a court of competent jurisdiction; or (4) in
     aggregate or summary form from which every identifier enumerated in
     Section 7-101(b) has been removed.

     Sec. 7-103. Penalty.
     Any holder of records who knowingly and willfully discloses personal
     information in violation of Section 7-102(c) shall be guilty of a
     misdemeanor and shall in addition be liable for a civil penalty not
     exceeding two thousand five hundred dollars per occurrence. Each disclosure
     to each unauthorized recipient shall constitute a separate occurrence.
