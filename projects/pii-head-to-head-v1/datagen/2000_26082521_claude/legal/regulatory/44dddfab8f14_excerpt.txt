NOTES.

  (a) Each record containing the personal identifying information of a
      separate individual constitutes a separate affected record.

  (b) The aggregate penalty assessed against a single holder for violations
      arising out of a single act or omission shall not exceed one hundred
      fifty thousand dollars.

  (c) The Department may, in lieu of a civil penalty, accept a written
      assurance of voluntary compliance, which shall recite the corrective
      measures undertaken and the position title of the officer responsible
      for their execution.
