REGULATION 173 — STANDARDS FOR SAFEGUARDING CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Section 5.01. Scope and Application. This regulation applies to every licensee of the Department, including domestic and foreign insurers transacting business in this State, insurance producers, adjusters, and third-party administrators, with respect to all customer information maintained by or on behalf of the licensee. Compliance with this regulation does not relieve a licensee of any obligation arising under other provisions of the Insurance Code or under federal law.

Section 5.02. Information Security Program.

(A) Each licensee shall implement, maintain, and document a comprehensive written information security program that contains administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the size and complexity of the licensee, the nature and scope of its activities, and the sensitivity of the customer information at issue. The program shall be reasonably designed to (1) ensure the security and confidentiality of customer information; (2) protect against any anticipated threats or hazards to the security or integrity of such information; and (3) protect against unauthorized access to or use of such information that could result in substantial harm or inconvenience to a customer.

(B) The program shall be approved by the board of directors or an appropriate committee thereof, or, where the licensee has no board, by the senior officer exercising equivalent authority. The board or committee shall receive a written report not less than annually describing the overall status of the program, the results of testing performed under subsection (D), material matters relating to service provider oversight, and any recommendations for change.

(C) Each licensee shall designate one or more employees, who may hold the title of Records Custodian or such other title as the licensee assigns, to coordinate the program. The designation shall be recorded in the licensee's minute book or equivalent record and shall identify the position title, reporting line, and date of designation.

(D) Each licensee shall conduct a risk assessment that identifies reasonably foreseeable internal and external threats to the security, confidentiality, and integrity of customer information; assesses the likelihood and potential damage of such threats, taking into consideration the sensitivity of the data elements enumerated in § 12-104(a); and assesses the sufficiency of policies, procedures, information systems, and other arrangements in place to control such risks. The risk assessment shall be documented and shall be reviewed and updated not less than annually and upon any material change in business operations, systems architecture, or third-party arrangements.

(E) Access to customer information shall be restricted to those persons whose duties require such access. Each licensee shall maintain an access control register recording, for each authorized person, the following fields: position title, business unit, systems or record series authorized, basis of authorization, date access granted, date of last review, and date access revoked. Entitlement reviews shall be performed not less than semiannually.

Section 5.03. Oversight of Service Providers. A licensee shall exercise appropriate due diligence in selecting service providers, shall require by written contract that each service provider implement appropriate measures designed to meet the objectives of this regulation, and shall monitor performance under such contracts. Contracts executed or renewed on or after the effective date of this regulation shall include provisions governing the return or destruction of customer information upon termination.
