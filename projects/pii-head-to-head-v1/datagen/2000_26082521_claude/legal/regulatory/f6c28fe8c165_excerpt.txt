INTEROFFICE MEMORANDUM
=====================================================================
DEPARTMENT:  Compliance and Records Management
DISTRIBUTION: Branch Compliance Binders, Sections 3 and 4
SUBJECT:     Rule 214, Subchapter C — Duties of Holders of Record
EFFECTIVE:   First day of the calendar quarter following filing
SUPERSEDES:  Bulletin 214-C (prior printing)
=====================================================================

Text follows verbatim. Where the rule imposes a duty on "the holder," the duty is discharged through the Records Custodian.

214.31  Designation of Records Custodian.
   (a) Each holder of record shall designate in writing one Records Custodian for
       each place of business at which covered records are maintained.
   (b) The designation shall be filed with the Department on the prescribed form
       and shall recite, by title only, the following fields:
       (1) Office of Designated Custodian;
       (2) Office of Alternate Custodian;
       (3) Categories of Covered Records Held;
       (4) Physical Location of Storage;
       (5) Date of Designation.
   (c) A designation remains effective until superseded. Vacancy in the office
       does not suspend any duty under this subchapter.

214.32  Inventory and Classification.
   (a) The holder shall maintain a current inventory of covered records, indexed
       by file series and not by the identity of any party.
   (b) The inventory shall state for each series:
       (1) Series title;
       (2) Identifier categories present, by reference to Section 214.22(a)–(d);
       (3) Medium;
       (4) Volume, expressed in linear feet or in bytes;
       (5) Scheduled disposition.
   (c) The inventory shall be reviewed not less than annually and the review
       noted on the inventory face sheet.

214.33  Restriction on Access.
   (a) Access to a covered record shall be limited to personnel whose assigned
       duties require it for the completion, examination or audit of a
       transaction.
   (b) The holder shall maintain an access log recording, for each instance of
       access by a person not regularly assigned to the file:
       (1) Employee number of person accessing;
       (2) File series and file number;
       (3) Date and time of access;
       (4) Stated business purpose;
       (5) Approving officer.
   (c) Logs are covered records for all purposes of this subchapter.

214.34  Restriction on Disclosure.
   (a) A holder shall not disclose a covered record except:
       (1) to a party to the transaction or that party's authorized agent;
       (2) to an underwriter, lender or settlement agent to the extent required
           to close, insure or fund the transaction;
       (3) pursuant to subpoena, court order or written demand of an examiner;
       (4) upon written authorization of the individual, which authorization
           shall recite the identifier categories released and the recipient.
   (b) Disclosure under subsection (a)(3) shall be reported to the Records
       Custodian not later than the next business day.
   (c) No holder shall furnish covered records in bulk to any person for
       marketing, list compilation or resale.

214.35  Safeguards.
   (a) Covered records not in active use shall be secured in locked cabinets or
       locked rooms during non-business hours.
   (b) Electronic files containing identifiers under Section 214.22(a) or (b)
       shall be protected by individual user credentials. Shared or generic
       credentials are prohibited.
   (c) Facsimile transmission of identifiers under Section 214.22(a) shall be
       preceded by telephone confirmation of the receiving unit.

214.36  Accuracy. Upon written request, the holder shall permit the individual to
        inspect identifiers of record and shall correct demonstrated error within
        thirty days. A record of correction shall be retained with the file.
