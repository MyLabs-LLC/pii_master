SECTION II. ATTESTATIONS

The undersigned, in the capacity indicated above, hereby attests as follows:

    1. That a written information security program has been developed,
       implemented, and maintained in conformity with § 14.04(b), and that
       the same has been reviewed not less than annually.

    2. That the designation of a Records Custodian required by § 14.04(a)
       remains in full force and effect, no vacancy in said office having
       persisted for more than ten (10) business days during the period.

    3. That the access authorization log required by § 14.04(c) has been
       maintained continuously, and that all revocations of access were
       recorded within twenty-four (24) hours of the effective separation
       or reassignment of the affected employee.

    4. That no Protected Record was disclosed to any third-party service
       provider except pursuant to a written contract satisfying § 14.05.

    5. That all destruction of Protected Records was effected by a method
       enumerated in § 14.04(e), and that Certificates of Destruction are
       on file for each such event.
