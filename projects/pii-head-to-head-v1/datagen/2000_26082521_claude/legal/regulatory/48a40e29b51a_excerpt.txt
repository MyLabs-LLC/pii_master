ADMINISTRATIVE RULE AR-118
HANDLING OF PROTECTED IDENTIFIERS IN CUSTOMER SERVICE RECORDS
Issued by the Department of Public Utilities — Office of the Records Custodian
Effective: first day of the fiscal quarter following adoption

SECTION 1.  AUTHORITY
This rule is issued under Chapter 2.44 of the Municipal Code and supersedes Office Memorandum 97-4.

SECTION 2.  SCOPE
This rule applies to the customer information system, the field service order system, the collections file, and any working paper, worksheet, spreadsheet, or extract derived from them.

SECTION 3.  CLASSIFICATION TABLE
Each field in a record series shall be assigned to one class. Where a field could fall in more than one class, the more restrictive class governs.

Class | Identifier Category                  | Storage Restriction        | Retention Limit | Release Requires
------|--------------------------------------|----------------------------|-----------------|------------------
  A   | Government identification numbers    | Encrypted field only       | 3 years         | Custodian approval
  B   | Financial account and card numbers   | Truncated; last 4 digits   | 18 months       | Custodian approval
  C   | Confidential-flag contact information| Restricted view           | Term of service | Supervisor approval
  D   | Interval consumption and access codes| Restricted view           | 24 months       | Supervisor approval
  E   | Eligibility and medical certification| Segregated folder         | Term of program | Custodian approval
  F   | General account data                 | Standard                  | 7 years         | None

SECTION 4.  WORKING COPIES
No extract containing a Class A or Class B field shall be written to removable media, transmitted by facsimile, or carried off Department premises. Extracts approved for analysis shall substitute the internal reference number for the identifier field.

SECTION 5.  PUBLIC COUNTER PROCEDURE
Staff at the public counter shall verify the identity of a requesting party before displaying any record. Verification shall be recorded on the counter log under the headings: Log Sequence; Record Series Displayed; Verification Method; Staff Initials.

SECTION 6.  DESTRUCTION
Records reaching the retention limit in Section 3 shall be destroyed by shredding, pulping, or certified electronic overwrite. A certificate of destruction shall be prepared bearing the headings: Certificate Number; Record Series; Volume; Method; Witnessing Officer.

SECTION 7.  REPORTING OF SUSPECTED LOSS
Any employee who suspects that a covered record has been lost, taken, or accessed without authorization shall report the matter to the Records Custodian before the end of the same work shift. The Custodian shall open a file and notify the Director of Public Utilities within one business day.

SECTION 8.  NONCOMPLIANCE
Failure to observe this rule is grounds for suspension of system access pending review, and for discipline under Article XII of the personnel rules.
