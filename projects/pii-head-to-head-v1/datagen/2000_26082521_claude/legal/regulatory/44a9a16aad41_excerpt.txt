TITLE 12 — PUBLIC HEALTH RECORDS
CHAPTER 4 — SAFEGUARDING OF INDIVIDUALLY IDENTIFIABLE DATA

12-4-1. Scope.
This Chapter applies to all records created or received by the Department in the course of communicable disease investigation, vital registration, environmental health permitting, maternal and child health services, and clinic operations.

12-4-2. Categories of identifiers.
For purposes of this Chapter, identifiers are classified as follows:

Category I — Name, alias, and maiden name.
Category II — Government-issued numbers, including social security number, alien registration number, and driver's license number.
Category III — Contact elements, including street address, mailing address, telephone number, and electronic mail address.
Category IV — Clinical elements, including medical record number, laboratory accession number, case investigation number, and immunization registry identifier.
Category V — Financial elements, including insurance policy number, health plan identifier, account number, and payment card number.
Category VI — Date elements, including date of birth, date of death, date of service, and date of test.
Category VII — Biometric and image elements.

12-4-3. Marking.
Any file, folder, screen, or report containing an element of Category I through Category VII shall bear the legend CONFIDENTIAL — PUBLIC HEALTH RECORD.

12-4-4. Custody.
The Records Custodian shall maintain an inventory of systems of record. The inventory shall state, for each system: system name; owning program unit; identifier categories present; medium; access control method; retention period; and date of last review.

12-4-5. Access.
Access shall be granted by role, not by individual request. The Records Custodian shall review the access roster quarterly and shall revoke access within one business day of separation, transfer, or expiration of contract.

12-4-6. Prohibited practices.
No holder of record shall: place personal information in interoffice mail without a sealed envelope; discuss personal information in a public area; leave personal information visible on an unattended display; or transmit Category II or Category V elements by facsimile absent confirmation of the receiving number.

12-4-7. Sanctions.
First offense: written reprimand and retraining. Second offense: suspension without pay. Third offense: dismissal. Willful disclosure for gain shall be referred for prosecution without regard to prior offenses.
