ARTICLE 9. NOTIFICATION OF UNAUTHORIZED ACQUISITION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION
(Excerpt — as amended, 2021 session)

Sec. 9.01. Scope.
* This article governs any holder of records, including a school district, charter school, education service agency, or contracted operator, that owns or licenses computerized data containing personal information of a resident of this state.

Sec. 9.02. Definition of personal information.
* "Personal information" means a first name or first initial and last name in combination with any one or more of the following data elements, when the elements are not encrypted or redacted:
  * Social Security Number;
  * Driver License Number or State Identification Card Number;
  * Financial Account Number, Credit Card Number, or Debit Card Number, in combination with any required security code, access code, or password;
  * Medical Record Number, Health Insurance Policy Number, or health treatment information;
  * Biometric Identifier;
  * Username or Electronic Mail Address in combination with a password or security question answer.
* "Personal information" does not include information lawfully made available to the general public from federal, state, or local government records.

Sec. 9.03. Duty to investigate.
* Upon becoming aware of an incident, the holder shall commence a good faith investigation within seventy-two (72) hours.
* The investigation shall determine: the categories of field labels involved; the number of records affected; whether acquisition by an unauthorized person occurred or is reasonably believed to have occurred; and whether the data were encrypted and the decryption key unaffected.
* The holder shall document the determination in an Incident Determination Record bearing the fields: Incident Reference Number; Date Discovered; Systems Involved; Field Labels Involved; Record Count; Encryption Status; Determination; Determining Official Title.

Sec. 9.04. Duty to notify.
* Notice to affected individuals shall be given in the most expedient time possible and without unreasonable delay, and in no case later than sixty (60) days after determination, except where a law enforcement agency states in writing that notice will impede a criminal investigation.
* Notice shall be given in writing, or by electronic means where the holder's primary method of communication with the individual is electronic.
* Notice shall state, at minimum: the approximate date of the incident; the categories of personal information involved, described by field label; the steps taken by the holder; the steps the individual may take; and a telephone number and mailing address of the office designated to receive inquiries.
* Where more than five hundred (500) residents are affected, the holder shall notify the Attorney General and the Department of Education concurrently and shall provide the Incident Determination Record on request.

Sec. 9.05. Penalties and remedies.
* A violation constitutes an unfair or deceptive practice enforceable by the Attorney General.
* The Attorney General may seek injunctive relief and civil penalties not to exceed $10,000 per day for continuing failure to notify.
* A public body assessed a penalty under this section shall report the assessment to its governing board at the next regular meeting.
* Nothing in this article limits any remedy otherwise available at law or in equity.
