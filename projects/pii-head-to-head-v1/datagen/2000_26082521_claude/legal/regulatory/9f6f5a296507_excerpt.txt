ADMINISTRATIVE RULES OF THE STATE BOARD OF EDUCATION
CHAPTER 704 — PUPIL PERSONNEL SERVICES
RULE 704-3.12  Safeguarding of Personal Information in Pupil Files

(1) Each district shall maintain the cumulative pupil file in three physically or logically separated parts:
 (a) Part A, the permanent record, limited to: surname; given name; date of birth; parent or guardian name field; residence address field; date of entry; date of withdrawal; grades earned; units of credit; and attendance summary.
 (b) Part B, the temporary record, containing:
  1. standardized test result fields;
  2. health record fields, including immunization series code and screening result;
  3. discipline entry fields, including incident code and disposition code;
  4. correspondence bearing a direct identifier.
 (c) Part C, the special education record, containing evaluation reports, eligibility determinations, and program placement documents.

(2) Part A shall be retained permanently. Part B shall be destroyed not later than five years after the pupil's withdrawal or graduation, whichever occurs first. Part C shall be retained in accordance with Rule 704-3.14.

(3) No pupil file or portion thereof shall be transmitted to a receiving district, institution, or agency except upon:
 (a) written request identifying the pupil by surname and state-assigned pupil identification number;
 (b) certification of enrollment or intent to enroll; and
 (c) entry of the transmittal in the access log.

(4) A district employee who releases information contrary to this rule shall be reported by the Records Custodian to the superintendent, and the report shall be placed in the employee's personnel file. Repeated violation constitutes grounds for suspension of certification under Rule 704-9.02.
