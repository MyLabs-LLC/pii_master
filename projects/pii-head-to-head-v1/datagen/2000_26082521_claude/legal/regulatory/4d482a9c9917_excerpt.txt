TITLE 45, CHAPTER 7, SUBCHAPTER F — ENFORCEMENT, PENALTIES, AND ADMINISTRATIVE REVIEW
§§ 7.601 – 7.612

Excerpt supplied by the Office of Compliance. Retain with the departmental policy set. Supersedes the prior Subchapter F excerpt in this binder.

§ 7.601 Investigative Authority
  * (1) The Department may, on complaint or on its own motion, investigate any holder of records to determine compliance with this Chapter.
  * (2) A holder of records shall, upon written demand, produce to the Department:
        - the written program required by § 7.502;
        - the system and file inventory required by § 7.402(b);
        - the quarterly access review records required by § 7.402(c);
        - the accounting of disclosures required by § 7.505;
        - contractor agreements executed under § 7.504.
  * (3) Records produced under paragraph (2) shall be produced within twenty business days and shall be maintained by the Department as confidential and exempt from public inspection.

§ 7.602 Culpability Tiers
  * (1) Each violation shall be assigned to one of the following tiers by the Commissioner or a designee:
        - Tier I — the holder of records did not know, and by exercising reasonable diligence would not have known, of the violation;
        - Tier II — the violation was due to reasonable cause and not to willful neglect;
        - Tier III — the violation was due to willful neglect and was corrected within thirty days of discovery;
        - Tier IV — the violation was due to willful neglect and was not timely corrected.
  * (2) Each day on which a continuing violation persists constitutes a separate violation for purposes of this Subchapter.

§ 7.603 Schedule of Civil Money Penalties
  * (1) Penalties shall be assessed in accordance with the following schedule:

        Tier | Minimum Per Violation | Maximum Per Violation | Annual Cap Per Provision
        ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
        I    |                  $120 |               $60,000 |              $30,000
        II   |                $1,200 |               $60,000 |             $120,000
        III  |               $12,000 |               $60,000 |             $300,000
        IV   |               $60,000 |               $60,000 |           $1,800,000

  * (2) The amounts in paragraph (1) shall be adjusted annually for inflation and published by the Department not later than the first business day of each calendar year.

§ 7.604 Factors in Assessment
  * (1) In determining the amount of a penalty within the applicable range, the Commissioner shall consider:
        - the nature, number, and duration of the violations;
        - the number of individuals whose information was affected;
        - the categories of identifying information involved, by reference to § 7.102;
        - whether the violation caused financial harm, reputational harm, or delay in the delivery of care;
        - the history of prior compliance, including any corrective action plan then in effect;
        - the size, complexity, and financial condition of the holder of records;
        - the degree of cooperation extended during the investigation.

§ 7.606 Criminal Referral
  * (1) A person who knowingly obtains or discloses protected information in violation of this Chapter, and who does so for commercial advantage, personal gain, or malicious harm, shall be referred to the Attorney General for prosecution.
  * (2) Referral under paragraph (1) does not bar the concurrent assessment of civil money penalties under § 7.603.

§ 7.609 Corrective Action in Lieu of Penalty
  * (1) The Commissioner may, for a Tier I or Tier II violation, accept a corrective action plan in lieu of a penalty where the holder of records demonstrates that the deficiency has been remediated and that recurrence is unlikely.
  * (2) A corrective action plan shall specify remedial measures, responsible role titles, and completion milestones, and shall be subject to Department verification.

§ 7.612 Administrative Review
  * (1) A holder of records may request a hearing before the Office of Administrative Hearings within thirty days of service of a notice of proposed determination.
  * (2) A timely request stays collection of the penalty pending final agency action.
  * (3) Failure to request a hearing within the period prescribed renders the proposed determination final and not subject to judicial review.
