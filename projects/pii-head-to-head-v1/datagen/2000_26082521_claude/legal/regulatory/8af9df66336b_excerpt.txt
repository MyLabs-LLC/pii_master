SUBJECT:  Penalties and enforcement — Article 7, Part III (excerpt)

PART III.  ENFORCEMENT AND PENALTIES

I.   Examination.
     The Commissioner may examine the records, systems, and information security program of a holder of records at any time to determine compliance with this Article. The holder shall bear the reasonable cost of the examination.

II.  Administrative penalties.
     Upon a finding after hearing that a holder has violated this Article, the Commissioner may impose the following:

       VIOLATION                                  PENALTY PER OCCURRENCE
       ------------------------------------------------------------------
       Failure to maintain written program        not more than  $2,500
       Failure to restrict access                 not more than  $5,000
       Improper disposal of records               not more than  $5,000
       Unauthorized disclosure, negligent         not more than $10,000
       Unauthorized disclosure, knowing           not more than $25,000

     Each individual whose nonpublic personal information is affected constitutes a separate occurrence, provided that the aggregate penalty for violations arising out of a single event shall not exceed $500,000 in any calendar year.

III. Suspension or revocation.
     In addition to any penalty imposed under paragraph II, the Commissioner may suspend or revoke a certificate of authority or license where the violation is knowing and material, or where the holder has been the subject of two or more final orders under this Article within a period of thirty-six months.

IV.  Individual liability.
     An officer, director, or employee who knowingly discloses a personal identifier in violation of § 7-104, or who requests such disclosure under false pretenses, commits a misdemeanor punishable by fine, by imprisonment for not more than one year, or by both.

V.   Report to the Commissioner.
     A holder that discovers unauthorized acquisition of a record containing a personal identifier shall notify the Commissioner in writing not later than the tenth business day following discovery, stating the record series affected, the identifier categories involved, the approximate number of individuals, and the corrective measures taken.

VI.  Preservation of other remedies.
     The remedies provided in this Part are cumulative and do not limit any other remedy available at law or in equity.
