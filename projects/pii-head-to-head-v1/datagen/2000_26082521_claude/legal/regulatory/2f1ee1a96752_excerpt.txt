TITLE 9 — PUBLIC RECORDS AND PERSONAL INFORMATION
CHAPTER 4. PROTECTION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD BY PUBLIC AUTHORITIES
(Excerpt: §§ 9-401 through 9-404)

§ 9-401. Short title and application. This chapter may be cited as the Personal Information Practices Act. It applies to every agency, department, board, commission, district, and public authority of this State and of its political subdivisions, including without limitation any regional transportation authority, transit district, or transit operating agency created by special act, and to every officer, employee, agent, contractor, and subcontractor thereof who in the course of official duties creates, receives, maintains, uses, discloses, transfers, or destroys a record containing personal information. Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to diminish any greater protection afforded by other law, nor to authorize the withholding of a record from the individual to whom it pertains except as expressly provided in § 9-409.

§ 9-402. Definitions. As used in this chapter, unless the context clearly requires otherwise:

(a) "Personal information" means any information concerning a natural person which, alone or in combination with other information held or reasonably available to the holder, identifies or describes that person, or is capable of being associated with that person, and which is not lawfully available to the general public from federal, State, or local government records. The term includes information recorded in any medium, whether written, printed, photographic, magnetic, optical, electronic, or otherwise, and whether stored in an active file, a backup file, a microform, or a transactional log.

(b) "Direct identifier" means any data element the sole or principal function of which is to designate a particular individual, including the data elements customarily captured under the field labels Full Name, Former or Maiden Name, Social Security Number, Taxpayer Identification Number, Driver License or State Identification Number, Passport Number, Employee Number, Reduced Fare Permit Number, Paratransit Client Number, Registered Fare Media Serial Number, Bank Account Number, Payment Card Number, Residence Address, Mailing Address, Home Telephone Number, Electronic Mail Address, Digital Photograph, and Signature Image.

(c) "Indirect identifier" means any data element which does not by itself designate a particular individual but which, when combined with one or more other data elements, is reasonably capable of doing so, including the data elements customarily captured under the field labels Date of Birth, Place of Birth, Gender, Race or Ethnic Origin, Employer Name, Job Title, Work Location, Postal Code of Residence, Household Size, Household Income Band, Disability Category, Medical Certification Code, and Institutional Affiliation.

(d) "Transactional identifier" means any data element generated by the operation of an automated fare collection, scheduling, dispatch, surveillance, or access control system which is linked or linkable to an individual, including the data elements customarily captured under the column names CARD_SERIAL, ACCOUNT_REF, TAP_DATETIME, ENTRY_STATION_CODE, EXIT_STATION_CODE, VEHICLE_ID, ROUTE_ID, FARE_PRODUCT_CODE, AUTOLOAD_AUTHORIZATION_REF, TRIP_BOOKING_REF, PICKUP_ZONE, DROPOFF_ZONE, and OPERATOR_BADGE_REF.

(e) "Holder of records" means the agency in whose custody or control a record containing personal information is maintained, irrespective of the physical location of the record or the identity of the person by whom it was created.

(f) "Records Custodian" means the officer designated under § 9-405 by the chief executive of the holder of records to discharge the duties imposed by this chapter, and includes any Deputy Records Custodian to whom such duties are lawfully delegated in writing.

(g) "Routine use" means a disclosure of a record for a purpose which is compatible with the purpose for which the record was collected and which has been described in the notice published pursuant to § 9-406.
