ADMINISTRATIVE RULE DPH-AR-2021-04
SUBJECT: HANDLING OF IDENTIFIER CATEGORIES IN CASE INVESTIGATION FILES
ISSUING AUTHORITY: Office of the Health Officer
EFFECTIVE: 15 March 2021
SUPERSEDES: DPH-AR-2018-11

1. AUTHORITY
   1.1 This rule is issued under § 8.44.060 of the County Code and implements the obligations of holders of records set out therein.

2. APPLICATION
   2.1 This rule binds all staff assigned to communicable disease investigation, environmental health complaint intake, and vital records processing.
   2.2 Volunteers and temporary staff are bound to the same extent as permanent staff.

3. SEGREGATION REQUIREMENT
   3.1 Case files shall be maintained in two parts:
       (a) the identified part, containing Direct Identifier fields — Full Name, Residential Street Address, Telephone Number, Date of Birth, Medical Record Number; and
       (b) the analytic part, containing Case Reference Code, Jurisdiction Code, Onset Interval, Exposure Setting Category, Outcome Category.
   3.2 The two parts shall be linked only by the Case Reference Code. The crosswalk linking Case Reference Code to Direct Identifier fields shall be held solely by the Records Custodian.

4. PROHIBITED PRACTICES
   4.1 Staff shall not transcribe Direct Identifier fields into working notebooks, whiteboards, shared spreadsheets, or unmanaged electronic mail folders.
   4.2 Staff shall not photograph any screen or document displaying a Direct Identifier field.
   4.3 Staff shall not remove identified case material from departmental premises except under a signed field authorization recording: Role Title; Record Series; Purpose; Date Out; Date Returned.

5. REVIEW
   5.1 The Records Custodian shall audit a sample of case files each quarter and report exceptions to the Health Officer.
