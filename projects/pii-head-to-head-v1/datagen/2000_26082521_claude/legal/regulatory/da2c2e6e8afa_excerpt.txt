TITLE 24 — COMMERCE, TRADE AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT
CHAPTER 9 — SAFEGUARDING OF PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD BY CONTRACTORS AND CARRIERS
(Excerpt: §§ 24-9-101 through 24-9-105. Effective January 1, 2011.)

§ 24-9-101. Purpose; application.
(a) This chapter prescribes minimum standards for the collection, retention, transmission and disposal of personal information by a person who, in the course of commercial activity, holds records containing personal information concerning a resident of this State.
(b) This chapter applies to motor carriers, freight forwarders, warehousemen, brokers, customs agents, and their subcontractors, without regard to whether a record is maintained in paper, electronic, magnetic or optical form.
(c) Compliance with this chapter does not relieve a holder of records of any obligation arising under federal law, under a tariff, or under contract.

§ 24-9-102. Definitions.
As used in this chapter, unless the context requires otherwise:
(1) "Holder of records" means a person that owns, licenses, stores, processes or maintains personal information, whether or not that person collected the information.
(2) "Personal information" means an individual's first name or first initial and last name in combination with one or more of the following data elements, where either the name or the data element is not encrypted, redacted or truncated:
 (A) Social Security number;
 (B) driver's license number or State identification card number;
 (C) financial account number, credit card number or debit card number, with or without any required security code, access code, personal identification number or password;
 (D) passport number, alien registration number, or other identification number issued by a government body;
 (E) medical information or health insurance information, including subscriber number, group number, and treatment or diagnosis code;
 (F) biometric identifier, including fingerprint, voice print, retina or iris image, or hand geometry template;
 (G) date of birth in combination with mother's maiden name.
(3) "Personal information" does not include information lawfully made available to the general public from federal, State or local government records.
(4) "Records Custodian" means the individual designated under § 24-9-104(a).
(5) "Disposal" means the discarding, abandonment, sale, donation, transfer or destruction of a record.
(6) "Encrypted" means rendered unusable, unreadable or indecipherable through a security technology or methodology generally accepted in the field of information security.

§ 24-9-103. Duty to protect.
(a) A holder of records shall implement and maintain reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to:
 (1) the size, scope and type of business of the holder;
 (2) the amount of resources available to the holder;
 (3) the volume of records maintained; and
 (4) the need for security and confidentiality of both consumer and employee information.
(b) Safeguards shall be documented in a written information security program and shall be reviewed not less than annually, and whenever a material change in business practice may reasonably implicate the security of personal information.

§ 24-9-104. Records Custodian.
(a) Each holder of records shall designate in writing one or more employees to coordinate the information security program.
(b) The designation shall be recorded on the register maintained under § 24-9-105(c) and shall state the position title, the department, and the effective date of designation.
(c) The Records Custodian shall report annually to the governing body of the holder concerning incidents, audits and corrective actions.

§ 24-9-105. Third-party service providers.
(a) A holder of records shall take reasonable steps to select and retain service providers capable of maintaining appropriate safeguards.
(b) A holder shall require by written contract that a service provider implement and maintain such safeguards.
