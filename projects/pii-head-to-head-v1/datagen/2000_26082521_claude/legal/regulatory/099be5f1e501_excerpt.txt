TITLE 14 — LABOR AND EMPLOYMENT
CHAPTER 22 — PROTECTION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION IN EMPLOYMENT AND PLACEMENT RECORDS
(Excerpt — sections 22-101 through 22-106)

CITATION.................. 14 Admin. Code ch. 22
EFFECTIVE................. July 1, 1997
SUPERSEDES................ 14 Admin. Code ch. 22 (1991 revision)
FILE LOCATION............. Agency Compliance Binder, Tab 3
RETAINED BY............... the Records Custodian

SEC. 22-101. SHORT TITLE.
This chapter may be cited as the Employment Records Confidentiality Act.

SEC. 22-102. DEFINITIONS.
As used in this chapter, unless the context otherwise requires:
(a) "Holder of records" means any employment agency, temporary help service, employee leasing firm, or private personnel service that collects, receives, maintains, or transmits personal information in the ordinary course of placement activity.
(b) "Personal information" means information capable of identifying a natural person, whether recorded on paper, microfiche, magnetic media, or otherwise, and includes without limitation the identifier categories enumerated in subsection (c).
(c) "Identifier category" means any of the following classes of data element:
    (1) Class I — Name, former name, and any assumed or professional name.
    (2) Class II — Government-issued numeric identifiers, including social security account number, taxpayer identification number, alien registration number, and operator's license number.
    (3) Class III — Financial designations, including bank routing and deposit account designations, payment card numbers, and wage garnishment references.
    (4) Class IV — Residential and mailing designations, telephone and facsimile numbers, and electronic mail addresses.
    (5) Class V — Date and place of birth, marital status, dependent counts, and citizenship or work-authorization status.
    (6) Class VI — Medical, disability, workers' compensation, and substance-screening results.
    (7) Class VII — Criminal history, credit history, and prior-employment verification results.
(d) "Disclosure" means the transfer of personal information, by any means, to a person other than the data subject or an employee of the holder acting within the scope of assigned duties.
(e) "Authorization" means a dated, signed instrument executed by the data subject specifying the identifier categories to be disclosed and the recipient class.

SEC. 22-103. OBLIGATIONS OF HOLDERS OF RECORDS.
Every holder of records shall:
(a) Designate in writing a Records Custodian responsible for compliance with this chapter, and post the designation at each place of business;
(b) Collect only those identifier categories reasonably necessary to the placement, payroll, or statutory reporting function for which collection is made;
(c) Maintain Class II, Class III, and Class VI information in locked storage separate from the general applicant file, and restrict access to personnel listed on the Access Roster;
(d) Log each disclosure upon the Disclosure Register, showing date, identifier categories released, recipient class, authorization reference, and initials of the releasing clerk;
(e) Destroy records by shredding, pulping, or magnetic erasure not later than the expiration of the retention period prescribed in section 22-105;
(f) Transmit personal information by facsimile only to a device under the control of the recipient, and only where the transmittal cover sheet bears the restricted-handling legend.

SEC. 22-104. PROHIBITED PRACTICES.
No holder of records shall (1) condition placement upon the applicant's consent to disclosure beyond that required by law; (2) use a Class II identifier as a file control number, badge number, or timekeeping number; or (3) sell, lease, or trade personal information for consideration.

SEC. 22-106. PENALTIES.
Violation of section 22-103 or 22-104 constitutes a Class B administrative offense, punishable by civil penalty of not more than five hundred dollars for each record affected, not to exceed twenty-five thousand dollars in the aggregate for any single inspection period. A knowing and willful violation for pecuniary gain is a misdemeanor. Each day a required register is not maintained constitutes a separate violation.
