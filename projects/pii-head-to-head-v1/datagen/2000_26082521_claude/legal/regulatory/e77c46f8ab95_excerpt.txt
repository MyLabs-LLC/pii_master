RULE 17.03: DUTY TO PROTECT AND STANDARDS FOR PROTECTING PERSONAL INFORMATION

(1) Every holder of records shall develop, implement, and maintain a comprehensive written information security program that is applicable to any records containing personal information. The program shall be reasonably consistent with industry standards and shall contain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the size, scope, and type of business of the holder, the resources available to the holder, the amount of stored data, and the need for security and confidentiality of both consumer and employee information. The program shall be maintained in writing, whether in paper or electronic form, and shall be produced to the Department upon request.

(2) Without limiting the generality of subsection (1), every such program shall include, at a minimum:

(a) Designation of one or more employees to maintain the program. The designated employee shall be identified in the program by role title as the Records Custodian, and the program shall record the reporting line, the effective date of designation, and the date of the most recent review.

(3) Every holder of records shall maintain a current inventory of records containing personal information. The inventory shall be organized by record series and shall include, for each series, the following columns: Record Series Title; Business Purpose; Data Elements Collected; Source of Collection; Storage Medium; Physical or Network Location; Retention Period; Disposal Method; and Authorized Roles. Where a record series contains applicant or assignment files, the Data Elements Collected column shall separately identify each of the following fields where present: Full Name; Former Name; Date of Birth; Social Security Number; Work Authorization Document Type and Number; Driver's License Number and Issuing State; Direct Deposit Routing Number; Direct Deposit Account Number; Emergency Contact Name and Telephone Number; and Background Check Reference Number.

(4) The holder of records shall take reasonable steps to select and retain third-party service providers that are capable of maintaining appropriate security measures, and shall require such providers by written contract to implement and maintain such measures. Contracts entered into after the effective date of this Rule shall include a provision requiring the service provider to return or destroy all personal information upon termination of services and to certify the disposition of such information to the Records Custodian in writing.

(5) The holder of records shall limit the amount of personal information collected to that reasonably necessary to accomplish the legitimate purpose for which it is collected, shall limit the time such information is retained to that reasonably necessary to accomplish such purpose, and shall limit access to those persons who are reasonably required to know such information in order to accomplish such purpose or to comply with state or federal record retention requirements.
