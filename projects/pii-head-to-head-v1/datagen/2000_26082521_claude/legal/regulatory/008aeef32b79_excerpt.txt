ARTICLE 5. DUTIES OF HOLDERS OF RECORDS.

Section 5.1. Written Program Required.
A holder of records shall implement and maintain a written information security program containing administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to (i) the size and complexity of the holder of records, (ii) the nature and scope of its activities, and (iii) the sensitivity of the personal information maintained. The program shall be reviewed not less than annually and upon any material change in business practice.

Section 5.2. Designation.
A holder of records shall designate one or more employees to coordinate the program. The designation shall be in writing and shall be maintained with the program. The designated employee shall be titled the Records Custodian.

Section 5.3. Minimum Content.
The program shall, at a minimum, address:
 (1) identification and assessment of reasonably foreseeable internal and external risks;
 (2) restriction of physical access to records containing personal information, including locked storage of closing files, title commitments, and settlement statements outside business hours;
 (3) restriction of logical access to the least privilege necessary to perform assigned duties;
 (4) encryption of personal information transmitted across public networks or stored on portable devices or removable media;
 (5) oversight of service providers, including title plant vendors, document couriers, off-site storage facilities, and shredding contractors;
 (6) procedures for the secure discard of records under Article 6;
 (7) documented employee training upon hire and not less than annually thereafter;
 (8) incident response procedures, including the notification duties of Article 7.

Section 5.4. Service Provider Contracts.
On and after the effective date of this Act, a holder of records shall not disclose personal information to a service provider unless the contract requires the service provider to implement and maintain safeguards consistent with this Article. Contracts in effect prior to the effective date shall be brought into conformity at renewal or within twenty-four (24) months, whichever occurs first.

Section 5.5. Records of Compliance.
Documentation of risk assessments, training rosters, program reviews, service provider certifications, and destruction certificates shall be retained for a period of not less than five (5) years and shall be produced to the Commissioner upon request.

Section 5.6. No Private Right of Action.
Nothing in this Article shall be construed to create a private right of action.
