PART 312 — SAFEGUARDING AND DISPOSAL OF PERSONAL INFORMATION BY MOTOR CARRIERS, FORWARDERS AND THEIR AGENTS

SUBPART B — OBLIGATIONS OF HOLDERS OF RECORDS

§ 312.11 GENERAL STANDARD.

Each holder of records shall develop, implement and maintain a written information security programme that contains administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the size and complexity of the holder's operations, to the nature and scope of its activities, and to the sensitivity of the identifier categories held. A programme shall not be deemed adequate under this part merely because it recites the obligations of this section; the holder must be able to demonstrate, by reference to its own registers, training records and inspection reports, that the safeguards described are in fact observed at each terminal, agency office and contracted facility at which records are handled.

§ 312.12 ELEMENTS OF THE PROGRAMME.

(i) Designation. The holder shall designate, by role title, one or more employees to coordinate the programme. The designation shall be recorded in the programme document and shall be reviewed upon any change in the duties attaching to that role title.

(ii) Risk identification. The holder shall identify reasonably foreseeable internal and external risks to the security, confidentiality and integrity of records containing the identifier categories enumerated at § 312.3, being: name; postal address; telephone number; electronic mail address; date of birth; government-issued identification number; account, card or routing number; and access credential.

(iii) Safeguards. The holder shall design and implement safeguards to control the risks so identified, and shall regularly test or otherwise monitor the effectiveness of those safeguards. Testing shall extend to the transmittal of documents between terminals, to the storage of imaged documents upon removable media, and to the retrieval of archived cartons from any off-site facility.

(iv) Oversight of service providers. The holder shall take reasonable steps to retain only service providers capable of maintaining appropriate safeguards, shall require such providers by contract to implement and maintain them, and shall retain in its files the executed contract together with the provider's certificate of compliance, each subscribed by role title.

(v) Adjustment. The holder shall evaluate and adjust the programme in the light of the results of the testing and monitoring required by paragraph (iii), of material changes to its operations, and of any incident of unauthorised acquisition or disclosure of which it becomes aware.

§ 312.13 DISPOSAL.

Upon expiry of the retention period applicable to a record series, the holder shall dispose of records containing any identifier category by burning, pulverising or shredding papers so that the information cannot practicably be read or reconstructed, and by destroying or erasing electronic media so that the information cannot practicably be read or reconstructed. Disposal shall be attested upon the disposal register under the column headings: Record Series; Volume; Method; Date of Destruction; Supervising Role Title; Witnessing Role Title.
