PART 402 — NOTIFICATION, ENFORCEMENT AND PENALTIES

Sec. 402.1 Notice of unauthorized acquisition.
(a) A holder of records that discovers or is notified of a breach of the security of the system shall give notice to each affected individual in the most expedient time possible and without unreasonable delay, consistent with the legitimate needs of law enforcement and with measures necessary to determine the scope of the breach and to restore the reasonable integrity of the data system.
(b) Notice shall not be delayed more than forty-five (45) days from the date of discovery except upon written request of a law enforcement agency.
(c) The notice shall state, at minimum: the categories of personal information involved; the approximate date of the incident; the measures taken by the holder; the contact channel established for inquiries; and the toll-free numbers and addresses of the consumer reporting agencies.

Sec. 402.2 Notice to the Attorney General.
(a) Where a single incident affects more than five hundred (500) residents of this State, the holder shall furnish to the Attorney General, on the prescribed form, the following particulars:
 (1) Legal name and registered agent of the holder;
 (2) Docket or incident reference number;
 (3) Number of residents affected;
 (4) Identifier categories compromised, by class as enumerated in § 24-9-102(2);
 (5) Date of discovery and date of notice;
 (6) Whether the personal information was encrypted at the time of the incident;
 (7) Position title of the Records Custodian and the certifying officer.

Sec. 402.3 Civil penalties.
Violations shall be assessed according to the following schedule:

 Tier | Description of Violation                                  | Penalty per Violation | Aggregate Cap
 ---- | --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- | -------------
 I    | Failure to maintain written information security program  | $1,000                | $50,000
 II   | Failure to designate a Records Custodian                   | $2,500                | $75,000
 III  | Improper disposal of records                               | $5,000                | $150,000
 IV   | Failure to give notice within the period fixed by § 402.1  | $10,000               | $250,000
 V    | Knowing or willful concealment of a breach                 | $25,000               | No cap

Sec. 402.4 Factors in assessment.
In determining the amount of a penalty the Commission shall consider the gravity of the violation, the good faith of the holder, any history of prior violations, the deterrent effect of the penalty, and such other matters as justice may require.

Sec. 402.5 Private right of action.
Nothing in this part shall be construed to create a private right of action.
