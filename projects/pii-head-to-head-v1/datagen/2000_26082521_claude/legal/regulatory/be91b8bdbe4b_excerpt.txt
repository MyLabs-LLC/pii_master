ARTICLE VII — RECORD RETENTION AND IDENTIFIER CLASSIFICATION SCHEDULE
Promulgated pursuant to 14 Admin. Code sec. 22-105. Post conspicuously in the file room.

SEC. 22-105.1 GENERAL RULE.
No record containing personal information shall be retained beyond the period specified in the schedule at subsection .4, except where a litigation hold, wage claim, or audit notice is in effect. The Records Custodian shall enter the basis for any extension in the Retention Exception Log and shall review all extensions annually.

SEC. 22-105.2 CLASSIFICATION DUTY.
Upon intake, the assigned clerk shall classify every incoming document by identifier category and shall affix the corresponding class code to the file jacket. Documents bearing more than one class shall be governed by the most restrictive class present.

SEC. 22-105.3 DEFINITIONS SUPPLEMENTAL TO SEC. 22-102.
(a) "Intake document" — any application, referral slip, resume, verification form, or screening result received from or concerning an applicant.
(b) "Active period" — the interval commencing at intake and terminating twelve months after the last placement or last recorded contact, whichever is later.
(c) "Dormant file" — a file for which the active period has lapsed and no statutory retention obligation remains.

SEC. 22-105.4 SCHEDULE.

  CLASS | IDENTIFIER CATEGORY                    | STORAGE  | RETENTION      | DISCLOSURE
  ------+----------------------------------------+----------+----------------+-----------
   I    | Name and assumed name                  | General  | Active + 3 yr  | Routine
   II   | Government numeric identifiers         | Locked   | Active + 4 yr  | Restricted
   III  | Financial account designations         | Locked   | Active + 4 yr  | Restricted
   IV   | Contact designations                   | General  | Active + 3 yr  | Routine
   V    | Birth data, status, work authorization | Locked   | Active + 3 yr  | Restricted
   VI   | Medical and screening results          | Sealed   | Active + 5 yr  | Prohibited
   VII  | Criminal, credit, prior-employment     | Locked   | Active + 2 yr  | Restricted

SEC. 22-105.5 DESTRUCTION.
Dormant files shall be purged not less than semiannually. The Records Custodian shall complete a Certificate of Destruction bearing the file control numbers destroyed, the identifier categories involved, the method employed, and the position titles of the two witnesses. Certificates shall be retained seven years and are subject to inspection without notice.

SEC. 22-105.6 CONVERSION TO OTHER MEDIA.
Conversion of paper records to microfiche or magnetic media does not shorten the retention period. Converted media inherit the storage designation of the highest class converted. Source documents may be destroyed upon verification of legibility of the converted image and entry of the verification upon the Conversion Log.
