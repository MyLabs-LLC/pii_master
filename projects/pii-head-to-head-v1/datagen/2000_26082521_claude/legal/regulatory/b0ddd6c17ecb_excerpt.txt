ADMINISTRATIVE CODE OF THE STATE
CHAPTER 118-6 — IDENTIFIER CLASSIFICATION AND HANDLING SCHEDULE
Promulgated by the Public Utility Commission under Title 9, ch. 42, § 42.106

118-6-.01  SCOPE.
This rule establishes the classification of personal identifiers held by an
Agency, the handling tier assigned to each class, and the minimum and maximum
retention period applicable to each. Where a record contains identifiers of more
than one class, the highest applicable tier governs the entire record.

118-6-.03  TIER DEFINITIONS.
 (1) TIER A. Restricted. Access by named position only. Encrypted at rest in any
     electronic information system. Not to be printed except upon written
     authorization of the Records Custodian. Not to be included in any extract,
     report, or file furnished to a third party.
 (2) TIER B. Confidential. Access limited by division. May be printed for
     operational necessity; printed copy is a Tier B record.
 (3) TIER C. Internal. Access by employees of the Agency in the course of their
     duties.
 (4) TIER D. Operational. No access restriction. Not a personal identifier for
     purposes of § 42.102(b) unless linked to a record of Tier A, B, or C.

118-6-.04  MEDIA CONVERSION.
Conversion of a Tier A or Tier B record to microfilm, microfiche, or optical
disk does not alter the tier assignment. The source document shall be destroyed
by the method prescribed above upon verification of the image, and the
verification entered in the destruction log.

118-6-.05  DESTRUCTION LOG.
The Agency shall maintain a log recording, for each destruction event: the
record series, the inclusive dates of the records destroyed, the volume, the
method, the position title of the witnessing officer, and the date. The log is
a permanent record and is not subject to the retention limits in .02.
