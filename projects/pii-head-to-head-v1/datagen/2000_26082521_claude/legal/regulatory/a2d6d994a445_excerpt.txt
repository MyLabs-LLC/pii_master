ADMINISTRATIVE CODE  --  TITLE 8, DEPARTMENT OF HIGHER EDUCATION
CHAPTER 22   PROTECTION OF PERSONALLY IDENTIFIABLE INFORMATION
Rule adopted 03/14/2016; filed with the Secretary of State 03/28/2016
Copy of record: Office of the University Registrar, file 8-22-A
____________________________________________________________________

8-22.01  SCOPE
  This chapter applies to every education record, admissions file,
  financial aid file, and directory listing held by an institution
  of the state system, in any medium.

8-22.02  DEFINITIONS
  TERM                    CODE   MEANING
  ----------------------  -----  -------------------------------------
  Direct identifier       DI-1   Data element that identifies an
                                 individual on its own, including:
                                 legal name; Social Security number;
                                 student identification number;
                                 driver license number; passport
                                 number; biometric template.
  Indirect identifier     DI-2   Data element that identifies an
                                 individual in combination, including:
                                 date of birth; place of birth;
                                 residential address; telephone
                                 number; electronic mail address;
                                 parent or guardian name.
  Directory item          DR-3   Element releasable absent a filed
                                 suppression request, including:
                                 enrollment status; major field;
                                 dates of attendance; degrees awarded.
  Holder of record        HR-4   Unit or agent in custody of any
                                 element in class DI-1 or DI-2.

8-22.03  OBLIGATIONS OF HOLDERS
  NO.   RESPONSIBLE ROLE      REQUIREMENT                    FREQUENCY
  ----  --------------------  -----------------------------  ---------
  .01   Records Custodian     Maintain element inventory     Annual
  .02   Records Custodian     Review access authorizations   Quarterly
  .03   Unit Head             Certify staff training         Annual
  .04   Registrar             File suppression register      Continuous
  .05   Information Security  Encrypt DI-1 at rest and in    Continuous
        Officer               transit
  .06   Records Custodian     Report suspected disclosure    Within 24
                              to the Registrar               hours

8-22.04  ACCESS LOG
  The log shall record, for each access to a DI-1 element, the role
  of the person accessing, the record system, the purpose code, and
  the time of access. The log shall be retained three years and shall
  be produced on demand of the Registrar or the state auditor.

8-22.05  PENALTY
  Failure to comply is a Class B administrative violation. Penalty:
  written reprimand upon a first finding; suspension of system access
  upon a second finding; civil penalty of $1,000 per record and
  referral to the Attorney General upon a third or knowing finding.
  Penalties under this rule are cumulative with any imposed by law.
