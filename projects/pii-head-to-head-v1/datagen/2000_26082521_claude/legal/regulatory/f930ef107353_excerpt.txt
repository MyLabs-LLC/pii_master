DISTRICT ADMINISTRATIVE REGULATION 5125.1-AR
PROTECTION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION IN PUPIL AND EMPLOYEE RECORDS
Adopted pursuant to Title 22, Chapter 14, and Rule 704-3.12

1.0 PURPOSE
1.1 This regulation prescribes the minimum handling standards applicable to every record series in which a direct, indirect, or sensitive identifier appears.

2.0 CLASSIFICATION OF FIELDS
2.1 Each field maintained in a district record series shall be classified in the index of record series according to the following schedule. The index shall show, for each field, the columns: FIELD LABEL, CLASS, AUTHORIZED POSITION TITLES, MEDIUM, and RETENTION PERIOD.

  CLASS I  (Restricted): social security account number; state-assigned pupil identification number; medication administration entry; disability classification code; free or reduced-price meal eligibility status; juvenile justice referral notation.
  CLASS II (Confidential): surname; given name; residence address; mailing address; home telephone listing; date of birth; parent or guardian name field; emergency contact name field.
  CLASS III (Directory): grade level; participation in officially recognized activities; height and weight of members of athletic teams; dates of attendance; diplomas and awards received.

3.0 HANDLING STANDARDS
3.1 Class I fields shall not be entered on any form, roster, list, or label distributed outside the office of the Records Custodian.
3.2 Class I and Class II fields shall not be transmitted by facsimile except to a receiving machine situated in a room to which access is controlled, and only after telephone confirmation that an authorized recipient is present at the machine.
3.3 Printouts containing Class I or Class II fields shall be shredded, not discarded intact, upon the completion of their administrative use.
3.4 Magnetic media containing Class I fields shall be stored in the vault when not in use and shall be degaussed before reissue or disposal.
3.5 Class III fields may be released without consent only if the annual notice required by Section 4.0 has been published and the applicable opt-out period has closed.

4.0 ANNUAL NOTICE
4.1 The Records Custodian shall cause the annual notice of rights to be distributed to each household at the opening of the school year, and shall retain a certification of distribution stating the method, the quantity distributed, and the position title of the certifying officer.

5.0 VIOLATIONS
5.1 Any employee who becomes aware of a disclosure contrary to this regulation shall notify the Records Custodian in writing on the same business day.
5.2 Violation of Sections 3.1 through 3.4 shall be reported to the superintendent and may result in disciplinary action up to and including dismissal, and in referral to the Commissioner for action under Rule 704-9.02.
