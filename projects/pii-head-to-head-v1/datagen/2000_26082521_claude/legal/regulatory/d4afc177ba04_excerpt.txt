TITLE 27 — PUBLIC HEALTH AND SAFETY
CHAPTER 14 — CONFIDENTIALITY OF PHARMACY RECORDS

§ 27-14-101. Purpose.
The purpose of this chapter is to establish minimum standards for the collection, storage, transmission, and destruction of records containing personal information held by pharmacies, wholesalers, and third-party administrators operating within the State.

§ 27-14-102. Definitions.
As used in this chapter, unless the context requires otherwise:
   (1) "Covered record" means any writing, print, microform, magnetic medium, or electronic file that contains one or more direct identifiers in combination with prescription, diagnostic, or payment information.
   (2) "Direct identifier" means a data element that identifies an individual without reference to other information, including but not limited to: full legal name; residence address; mailing address; telephone number; facsimile number; social security number; driver license or state identification number; health plan member number; subscriber number; patient account number; prescription serial number.
   (3) "Indirect identifier" means a data element that identifies an individual only in combination with other elements, including but not limited to: date of birth; gender; five-digit postal code; date of dispensing; prescriber license number; employer group code.
   (4) "Holder of records" means any person or entity that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits a covered record in the ordinary course of business, and includes the licensee, its officers, and its agents.
   (5) "Records Custodian" means the individual designated in writing by the holder of records under § 27-14-103(b) as responsible for compliance with this chapter.
   (6) "Authorized disclosure" means a disclosure made pursuant to written authorization, to a treating practitioner, to a payor for adjudication of a claim, or as compelled by lawful process.

§ 27-14-103. Obligations of holders of records.
   (a) A holder of records shall limit access to covered records to personnel whose duties require such access.
   (b) A holder of records shall designate a Records Custodian for each licensed location and shall file the designation with the Board within thirty (30) days of the appointment or of any change thereto.
   (c) A holder of records shall maintain a disclosure log bearing, at minimum, the following column headings: Date of Request; Requesting Party Category; Identifier Elements Released; Statutory Basis; Custodian Initials.
   (d) Covered records awaiting destruction shall be stored in a locked receptacle within the prescription department.

§ 27-14-104. Retention.
Covered records shall be retained not less than seven (7) years from the date of last dispensing, after which they shall be shredded, incinerated, or degaussed.

§ 27-14-105. Penalties.
   (a) A civil penalty not to exceed one thousand dollars ($1,000) per violation, each day of continuing violation constituting a separate offense, and not to exceed twenty-five thousand dollars ($25,000) in the aggregate per calendar year.
   (b) Suspension or revocation of the pharmacy permit.
   (c) Knowing disclosure for pecuniary gain is a misdemeanor.
