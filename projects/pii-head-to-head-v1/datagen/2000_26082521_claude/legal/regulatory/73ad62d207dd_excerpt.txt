TITLE 22, CHAPTER 14, SUBCHAPTER D
SECURITY OF PUPIL RECORDS AND THE DISCLOSURE REGISTER

Sec. 14.310.  Register Required.

     (a)  Every holder of pupil records shall maintain a disclosure
          register in bound form, or in an electronic form producing
          an equivalent permanent record, for each pupil file.

     (b)  An entry shall be made in the register upon each inspection
          or release of an identifier of any class, excepting only
          inspection by the Records Custodian in the ordinary course
          of maintaining the file.

     (c)  The register shall be kept in the same secure location as
          the file to which it relates and shall be available to the
          parent or eligible student on the same terms as the file.

Sec. 14.311.  Required Columns.

     (a)  The register shall bear the following column headings, in
          the order given:

NO.  COLUMN HEADING                  CONTENT REQUIRED
---  ------------------------------  --------------------------------------
1    Date of Entry                   Month, day, and year of release
2    Category Code                   Code from Sec. 14.201 of this title
3    Requesting Party by Role Title  Office or title only; no proper name
4    Statutory Basis for Release     Section and subsection cited
5    Method of Transmittal           Hand, mail, facsimile, or courier
6    Custodian Initials              Entered at the time of release
7    Date of Parent Notice           Month, day, and year of mailing

     (b)  No column may be omitted. Where a column is inapplicable,
          the Records Custodian shall enter the notation "N/A" and
          state the reason in the margin.

Sec. 14.312.  Storage and Retention.

     (a)  Files containing Class A or Class C identifiers shall be
          kept in a locked cabinet or in a room secured by key or
          combination, access to which is limited to the Records
          Custodian and the alternate.

     (b)  Data processing media bearing such identifiers shall be
          stored under equivalent control, and any copy carried off
          school premises shall be logged out and logged in on the
          same business day.

     (c)  Records shall be retained not less than the periods shown:

CLASS  RECORD SERIES                   MINIMUM RETENTION PERIOD
-----  ------------------------------  --------------------------------
I      Permanent Pupil Record Card     Retained without limit
II     Attendance Register             Five years after withdrawal
III    Health and Immunization Entry   Until the pupil attains age 21
IV     Disciplinary Action Summary     Three years after graduation
V      Disclosure Register             Five years after final entry

     (d)  Destruction at the expiration of a retention period shall be
          by shredding, pulping, or incineration, and shall be
          witnessed by an employee other than the Records Custodian.
          A certificate of destruction identifying the series and the
          inclusive years destroyed shall be filed with the
          superintendent.

Sec. 14.313.  Unauthorized Access.

     (a)  The holder shall report to the Department any loss, theft,
          or unauthorized inspection of a file or medium containing a
          Class A or Class C identifier.

     (b)  The report shall state the categories involved, the number
          of pupils affected, the date of discovery, and the remedial
          measures undertaken. It shall not be delayed pending
          completion of an internal investigation.

     (c)  Failure to report within the period fixed by Administrative
          Rule 6.14 is subject to assessment under Item C-7 of
          Schedule C.
