STATE STUDENT RECORDS CONFIDENTIALITY ACT
ARTICLE 3 — DEFINITIONS, IDENTIFIER CATEGORIES, AND OBLIGATIONS OF HOLDERS
Excerpt, §§ 301–307. As amended, 2004 Session.

§ 301. DEFINITIONS. For purposes of this Article:

- "Holder of records" means any agency, contractor, or agent that maintains, processes, or has custody of personal information concerning a student, applicant, or employee, whether in paper, microform, magnetic, or electronic form.
- "Personal information" means information that identifies, describes, or is capable of being associated with a natural person, including but not limited to the following labeled fields: Full Name, Former Name, Date of Birth, Place of Birth, Social Security Number, State Identification Number, Home Address, Mailing Address, Home Telephone Number, Guardian Name, Guardian Employer, Free and Reduced Price Meal Status, Immunization Record, Disciplinary Action Code, Special Education Eligibility Code.
- "Directory information" means the limited fields designated by the governing board under § 305 and published in the annual notice.
- "Disclosure" means the release, transfer, provision of access to, or divulging in any manner of personal information outside the holder of records.

§ 302. IDENTIFIER CATEGORIES. The following categories shall govern handling requirements:

- Category I (Direct Identifiers): Full Name, Social Security Number, State Identification Number, Student Identification Number, Photograph, Biometric Record, Home Address, Telephone Number, Electronic Mail Address.
- Category II (Indirect Identifiers): Date of Birth, Grade Level, Homeroom Assignment, Bus Route Number, Guardian Name, Enrollment Date, Withdrawal Date. A Category II identifier assumes Category I status when combined with two or more other Category II identifiers.
- Category III (Protected Attributes): Health Condition, Medication Administration Record, Counseling Referral, Disciplinary Action Code, Custody Order Reference, Special Education Eligibility Code, Public Assistance Status.

§ 303. OBLIGATIONS OF HOLDERS. Each holder of records shall:

- Collect only such personal information as is relevant and necessary to a purpose required or authorized by law, and record that purpose on the collection instrument.
- Maintain a Notice of Record Systems stating: System Title, Categories of Individuals, Categories of Data Elements, Routine Uses, Retention Period, Title of System Manager.
- Restrict internal access to employees having a legitimate educational interest, documented on the Access Authorization Roster (Position Title, System Title, Level of Access, Effective Date, Termination Date).
- Obtain written consent before disclosing Category I or Category III information, except as permitted by § 304, and file the consent with the record.
- Provide the individual, or the parent or guardian of a minor, opportunity to inspect and to request amendment, and afford a hearing upon denial of amendment.
- Require by contract that any agent or contractor observe this Article, and terminate the contract upon violation.
- Maintain the Disclosure Log for the life of the record and make it available for inspection by the individual.

§ 304. PERMITTED DISCLOSURES WITHOUT CONSENT. Disclosure without consent is limited to:

- Officials of a school in which the student seeks or intends to enroll, upon notice to the parent or guardian.
- Authorized representatives of state or federal audit authorities, for audit or evaluation only.
- Compliance with a lawful subpoena or judicial order, after reasonable effort to notify.
- An emergency involving the health or safety of any person, limited to the data elements necessary to address the emergency, and logged within twenty-four (24) hours.

§ 307. PENALTIES.

- Negligent disclosure of Category I or Category III information: civil penalty not exceeding two thousand five hundred dollars ($2,500) per occurrence.
- Knowing or willful disclosure for pecuniary gain: misdemeanor, and civil penalty not exceeding ten thousand dollars ($10,000) per occurrence.
- Failure to maintain the Disclosure Log or the Notice of Record Systems: administrative penalty not exceeding five hundred dollars ($500) per record system per month of noncompliance.
- Penalties under this section are cumulative and do not limit any remedy otherwise available.
