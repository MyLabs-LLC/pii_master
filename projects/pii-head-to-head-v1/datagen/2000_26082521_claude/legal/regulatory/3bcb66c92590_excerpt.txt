TITLE 12, CHAPTER 47 — INTERPRETIVE BULLETIN 2011-3
SUBJECT: Identifier Categories and the Access Register Under § 47.202(b)

    1. Occasion for This Bulletin.
        1.1 The Department has received inquiries from examined institutions concerning the level of detail required in the access register maintained under § 47.202(b), and in particular the meaning of the phrase "identifier categories accessible."
        1.2 This bulletin states the Department's interpretation. It does not amend the chapter.

    2. Identifier Categories.
        2.1 For purposes of the access register, an institution shall classify each system or file into one or more of the following categories:
            (a) Category I — Direct identifiers as defined at § 47.102(c).
            (b) Category II — Account balances, transaction histories, and loan terms, whether or not accompanied by a Category I element.
            (c) Category III — Indirect identifiers as defined at § 47.102(d).
            (d) Category IV — Correspondence, notes of member contact, and complaint files.
        2.2 The register records the category, not the underlying value. An entry that reproduces a direct identifier is itself a record subject to Subchapter B and defeats the purpose of the register.

    3. Level of Detail.
        3.1 The field "position title" is satisfied by the title as it appears in the institution's organizational chart. The name of the incumbent is not required and should not be entered.
        3.2 Where a position has access to a system containing multiple categories, each category shall be listed separately, so that revocation may be recorded at the category level.
        3.3 Institutions operating role-based access control may substitute the role name for the position title, provided a current mapping of roles to position titles is retained with the register.

    4. Semiannual Review.
        4.1 The review required by § 47.202(c) shall be evidenced by a dated attestation signed by the Records Custodian and by the head of each affected department.
        4.2 The attestation shall state the number of access grants reviewed, the number continued, and the number revoked. Examiners will test a sample against the register.

    5. Effective Date.
        5.1 This bulletin is effective upon publication and applies to examinations commencing on or after the first day of the next calendar quarter.
