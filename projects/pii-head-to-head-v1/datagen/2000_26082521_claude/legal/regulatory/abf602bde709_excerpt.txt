205.101  SHORT TITLE.
Sections 205.101 through 205.118 may be cited as the Personal Information Protection Act.

205.102  DEFINITIONS.
As used in this chapter, unless the context otherwise requires:

  (1) "Agency" means any department, board, commission, authority, or institution of higher education supported in whole or in part by state funds, and any officer or employee thereof acting within the scope of official duties.

  (2) "Personal information" means an individual's first name or first initial and last name in combination with any one or more of the following data elements, where either the name or the data element is not encrypted, redacted, or otherwise rendered unreadable:
      (a) Social Security Number;
      (b) driver's license number or state identification card number;
      (c) financial account number, credit card number, or debit card number, alone or in combination with any required security code, access code, or password that would permit access to an account;
      (d) medical information or health insurance information, including any health plan member identification number;
      (e) any unique biometric identifier, including a fingerprint template, voiceprint template, or retina or iris image.

  (3) "Directory information" means those data elements designated by the agency under Section 205.106 that may be disclosed without prior written consent, provided the individual has not filed a request to restrict disclosure.

  (4) "Record holder" means an agency that acquires, maintains, stores, transmits, or disposes of personal information, whether in electronic, magnetic, optical, or paper form.

  (5) "Records Custodian" means the officer designated in writing by the head of the agency as responsible for the operation and integrity of a system of records. A record holder may designate more than one Records Custodian by system of records.

  (6) "Breach of the security of the system" means the unauthorized acquisition of unencrypted computerized data that compromises the security, confidentiality, or integrity of personal information maintained by a record holder. Good faith acquisition of personal information by an employee or agent of the record holder for a lawful purpose is not a breach, provided the personal information is not subject to further unauthorized disclosure.

205.103  CATEGORIES OF IDENTIFIERS; CLASSIFICATION REQUIRED.

  (1) Each record holder shall classify every data element maintained in a system of records into one of the categories established by this section, and shall maintain a written inventory of that classification.

  (2) Category I (Restricted) comprises the following fields: Social Security Number; Taxpayer Identification Number; Financial Account Number; Payment Card Number; Card Security Code; Driver's License Number; State Identification Card Number; Passport Number; Biometric Template Identifier; Authentication Credential.

  (3) Category II (Confidential) comprises the following fields: Date of Birth; Place of Birth; Student Identification Number; Permanent Mailing Address; Local Mailing Address; Telephone Number; Electronic Mail Address; Parent or Guardian Name; Health Insurance Member Number; Disability Accommodation Code; Immigration Status Code.

  (4) Category III (Directory) comprises the following fields: Full Legal Name; Enrollment Status; Dates of Attendance; Classification by Year; Major Field of Study; Degrees and Awards Conferred; Participation in Officially Recognized Activities.

  (5) A data element not expressly enumerated shall be classified by the Records Custodian at the most restrictive category reasonably applicable, and the classification reported to the agency head within thirty (30) days.
