DEPARTMENT OF INSURANCE AND FINANCIAL REGULATION
ADMINISTRATIVE RULE 114-57
STANDARDS FOR THE PROTECTION OF RECORD INFORMATION HELD BY INSURERS
Adopted under authority of Ins. Code § 41-110. Effective January 1, 2016.
Excerpt: Rules 57.010 — 57.080

57.010  Purpose.
        This rule prescribes minimum administrative, technical, and physical
        standards for the protection of record information held by an insurer,
        producer, third-party administrator, or reinsurer licensed in this
        State. It does not displace any stricter obligation imposed by federal
        law or by contract.

57.020  Definitions.
        As used in this rule —
        57.020.1  "Holder" means any entity in actual or constructive
                  possession of a record, including a successor in interest.
        57.020.2  "Record" means information relating to an identifiable
                  natural person, recorded in any form, obtained in connection
                  with an application, policy, claim, or appeal.
        57.020.3  "Custodial Unit" means the organizational unit designated as
                  accountable for a record system.
        57.020.4  "Disclosure" means any transfer, display, or grant of access
                  outside the Custodial Unit, whether or not a copy is made.

57.030  Identifier categories.
        Holders shall classify each data element in each record system into one
        and only one of the following categories:
        57.030.1  Direct Identifier — an element sufficient by itself to
                  identify a natural person. Includes: Full Name; Social
                  Security Number; Taxpayer Identification Number; Member
                  Identification Number; Policy Number; Claim Number;
                  Electronic Mail Address; Telephone Number; Residence Address.
        57.030.2  Indirect Identifier — an element sufficient to identify a
                  natural person only in combination with another element.
                  Includes: Date of Birth; Sex; Postal Code; Occupation Code;
                  Employer Group Number; Vehicle Identification Number.
        57.030.3  Sensitive Financial Identifier — Includes: Bank Routing
                  Number; Deposit Account Number; Payment Card Number;
                  Card Verification Value; Premium Draft Authorization.
        57.030.4  Health Identifier — Includes: Diagnosis Code; Procedure
                  Code; Provider Name; Prescription Record; Laboratory Result;
                  Disability Determination.
        57.030.5  Non-Identifying — all other elements.

57.040  Duties of the holder.
        57.040.1  Maintain a written information security program, reviewed at
                  least annually by the Records Custodian.
        57.040.2  Restrict access to Direct Identifiers, Sensitive Financial
                  Identifiers, and Health Identifiers to personnel whose
                  assigned duties require such access, and record each grant of
                  access by position title, date of grant, and date of removal.
        57.040.3  Encrypt records in transmission over public networks and at
                  rest on portable media.
        57.040.4  Log every Disclosure. The log shall state: Requesting Party
                  Category; Purpose Code; Element Categories Released;
                  Authorization Basis; Date.
        57.040.5  Retain the Disclosure log for not less than six (6) years.

57.050  Third-party access.
        No holder shall permit access to a record system by a vendor absent a
        written agreement obligating the vendor to standards not less
        protective than those of this rule and permitting audit by the holder.

57.060  Disposal.
        Records shall be destroyed at the expiration of the applicable
        retention period by shredding, pulping, incineration, or verified
        cryptographic erasure. A certificate of destruction shall be retained,
        stating System Name, Element Categories, Volume, Method, and Date.

57.070  Notice of unauthorized acquisition.
        The holder shall notify the Commissioner within seventy-two (72) hours
        of determining that a record has been acquired without authorization.

57.080  Sanctions.
        Violation of any provision of this rule constitutes grounds for
        suspension or revocation of certificate of authority, in addition to
        the civil penalties prescribed by statute.
