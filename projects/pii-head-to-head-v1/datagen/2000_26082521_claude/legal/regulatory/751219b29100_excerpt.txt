TITLE 9, CHAPTER 2 — PENALTIES AND ENFORCEMENT
Schedule of penalties applicable to holders of records and their agents

SEC.      NATURE OF VIOLATION                     CIVIL PENALTY           CRIMINAL CLASS
========  ======================================  ======================  ==============
§ 9-201   Failure to designate a Custodian        Not more than $500      None
§ 9-201   Failure to maintain field schedule      Not more than $500      None
§ 9-202   Failure to train workforce annually     Not more than $1,000    None
§ 9-202   Failure to log a required disclosure    $100 for each record    None
§ 9-203   Improper disposal of covered record     Not more than $2,500    Misdemeanor B
§ 9-203   Disclosure without authorization        Not more than $10,000   Misdemeanor A
§ 9-204   Obtaining a record by false pretense    Not more than $25,000   Misdemeanor A
§ 9-204   Sale or barter of identifiers           Not more than $25,000   Felony E

§ 9-205   MITIGATION AND WAIVER

          In fixing the amount of any civil penalty the Commissioner shall
          consider the number of individuals affected, the classification of
          the identifiers disclosed as shown upon Schedule 2(c), the history
          of prior violations by the holder, the promptness with which the
          Records Custodian gave notice of the occurrence, and the measures
          undertaken to prevent recurrence. Where the holder demonstrates
          that the violation was neither willful nor the product of gross
          neglect, and that it was cured within thirty days of discovery,
          the Commissioner may waive the penalty in whole or in part.

§ 9-206   CONTINUING VIOLATIONS

          Each day upon which a violation of § 9-201 or § 9-202 continues
          after written notice from the Department shall constitute a
          separate and distinct offense.

§ 9-207   PROCEEDINGS

          No penalty shall be imposed except after notice served upon the
          holder at its principal place of business and an opportunity for
          hearing before the Commissioner or a designated hearing officer.
          A holder aggrieved by a final determination may seek review in
          the district court of the county in which it maintains its
          business office, within thirty days of service of the order.
          Penalties recovered hereunder shall be paid into the Health
          Information Protection Fund.
