TITLE 10, CHAPTER V, SUBPART 5-3
PROTECTION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD BY LOCAL HEALTH AGENCIES
(Filed with the Secretary of State; effective January 1, 1997)

§ 5-3.1 Application
 — This Subpart applies to every record system operated by or on behalf of a county health department, whether maintained in paper, microform, magnetic, or optical media.
 — Compliance shall be verified by the Records Custodian not less than annually against the checklist set out in § 5-3.4.

§ 5-3.2 Definitions
As used in this Subpart, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:
 — "Personal information" means any information concerning a natural person which, because of name, number, symbol, mark, or other identifier, can be used to identify that person.
 — "Direct identifier" means a data element that identifies a person without reference to other data, including the labeled fields: Full Legal Name; Social Security Number; Medical Record Number; Case File Number; Residence Address; Telephone Number; Facsimile Number; Electronic Mail Address.
 — "Indirect identifier" means a data element that identifies a person only in combination with other data, including the labeled fields: Date of Birth; Sex; Race/Ethnicity; ZIP Code of Residence; Date of Admission; Date of Discharge; Date of Death; Occupation; Employer.
 — "Record" means any item, collection, or grouping of personal information maintained in a system of records.
 — "System of records" means a group of records from which information is retrieved by the name of a person or by an identifier assigned to that person.
 — "Holder of records" means the county health department and any officer, employee, contractor, or agent thereof having custody of or access to a system of records.
 — "Disclosure" means the release, transfer, provision of access to, or divulging in any manner of a record outside the holder of records.

§ 5-3.3 General duty
 — No holder of records shall collect, retain, or disclose personal information except as authorized by statute, by this Subpart, or by the written consent of the subject of the record.
 — Collection shall be limited to those data elements demonstrably necessary to the program purpose stated in the system notice filed under § 5-3.6.
