ADMINISTRATIVE RULE 6.14
DUTIES OF THE RECORDS CUSTODIAN
Adopted by the State Board of Education; effective July 1, 2004

Section 1.  Designation.

  (1.1)  Each district shall designate in writing one Records
         Custodian and not fewer than one alternate. The designation
         shall be filed with the Department and posted in the
         administrative office of each attendance center.

  (1.2)  The designation shall state the office or title held. It
         shall be renewed at the opening of each school year and
         upon any vacancy in the office.

Section 2.  Enumerated Duties.

  (2.1)  The duties set out in the following schedule are mandatory
         and may not be delegated except to an alternate designated
         under clause (1.1).

CLAUSE   DUTY IMPOSED UPON THE RECORDS CUSTODIAN        TIME FOR PERFORMANCE
-------  --------------------------------------------  ------------------------
2.1      Verify the identity of the requesting party    Before any release
2.2      Record the purpose stated for the inspection   At time of request
2.3      Enter category code of identifier disclosed    Same business day
2.4      Notify parent or eligible student of release   Within 10 school days
2.5      Amend an entry determined to be inaccurate     Within 30 calendar days
2.6      Purge identifiers no longer required           At close of school year
2.7      Report unauthorized access to the Department   Within 5 business days
2.8      Certify the annual inventory of categories     On or before August 15

Section 3.  Manner of Performance.

  (3.1)  Verification under clause 2.1 shall be accomplished by
         examination of a government-issued credential, by comparison
         with the signature card on file, or by such other means as
         the superintendent shall approve in writing.

  (3.2)  Entries made under clauses 2.2 and 2.3 shall be made in
         permanent ink or by a data processing system which prevents
         alteration of a completed entry without an audit notation.

  (3.3)  Notice under clause 2.4 shall be directed to the address of
         record then appearing in the pupil file. Failure of delivery
         does not excuse the duty to make the entry.

Section 4.  Records of Compliance.

  (4.1)  The Records Custodian shall retain proof of performance of
         each duty enumerated in Section 2 and shall produce it on
         demand of the Department, the district auditor, or a court
         of competent jurisdiction.
