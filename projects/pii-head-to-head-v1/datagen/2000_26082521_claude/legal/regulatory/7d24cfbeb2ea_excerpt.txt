INTEROFFICE MEMORANDUM
OFFICE OF THE RECORDS CUSTODIAN

To the attention of: Branch Managers; Supervisor, Central Files; Vendor Management Coordinator
Subject: Disposal of records — Administrative Rule 214-4
Effective on receipt

Rule 214-4.03  Disposal of records containing personal identifiers.

(1) A holder of records shall not dispose of a record containing a personal identifier except by a method that renders the identifier unreadable and incapable of reconstruction.

(2) Acceptable methods are cross-cut shredding, pulping, and incineration; and, for electronic media, degaussing or physical destruction of the storage component. Deletion of a file, reformatting of a medium, or return of a leased device to the lessor without destruction of the storage component does not satisfy this Rule.

(3) Where disposal is performed by a contractor, the holder shall obtain a written certificate of destruction stating the date of destruction, the method employed, the volume or item count, and the record series destroyed. Certificates shall be retained for five years and produced to the Department on request.

(4) A violation of this Rule constitutes an unfair trade practice under the Insurance Code and is subject to the penalties prescribed by Article 7, Part III.

Post at each central file station and at each satellite shredding console.
