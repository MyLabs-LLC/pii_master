STATE ADMINISTRATIVE CODE, TITLE 7, PART 210
PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD BY CONTRACT CARRIERS AND THIRD-PARTY LOGISTICS FIRMS
================================================================================

SEC. 210.02  DEFINITIONS

SEC.       TERM DEFINED             MEANING ASSIGNED
---------- ------------------------ --------------------------------------------
210.02(a)  Personal Information     Any information that identifies, relates to,
                                    or describes a natural person and is held in
                                    connection with a shipment or employment.
210.02(b)  Holder of Record         A person who maintains, processes, or stores
                                    personal information in the course of
                                    transportation, warehousing, or brokerage.
210.02(c)  Records Custodian        The individual designated in writing to hold
                                    custody of the master file and its indexes.
210.02(d)  Identifier, Direct       A field that alone names or numbers a party,
                                    namely: Full Name; Social Security Number;
                                    Driver License Number; Passport Number.
210.02(e)  Identifier, Indirect     A field identifying a party when combined
                                    with another field, namely: Date of Birth;
                                    Postal Code; Employer Name; Route Number.
---------- ------------------------ --------------------------------------------

SECS. 210.03 TO 210.09  OBLIGATIONS AND PENALTY

SEC.       OBLIGATION OF HOLDER     REQUIREMENT
---------- ------------------------ --------------------------------------------
210.03     Security Program         Adopt and maintain a written program naming
                                    the Records Custodian, the Alternate
                                    Custodian, and each Access Class.
210.04     Access Control           Restrict terminal access by Access Class and
                                    log each entry: User Identification Code;
                                    Date of Access; Purpose Code; File Class.
210.05     Transfer Outside State   No direct identifier shall be transmitted to
                                    a point outside the State except under a
                                    filed Data Transfer Undertaking, Form 210-T.
210.06     Loss or Compromise       Report loss, theft, or unauthorized access
                                    to the Commission within five business days,
                                    stating File Class and Record Count only.
210.07     Disclosure Register      Enter every disclosure in a bound register
                                    showing: Recipient Class; Authority Cited;
                                    Fields Released; Date; Custodian Initials.
210.09     Penalty                  First offense: written reprimand and order
                                    to cure. Second offense: $1,000. Third and
                                    later: $5,000 and revocation of permit.
---------- ------------------------ --------------------------------------------
