CHAPTER 402, SUBCHAPTER D — ENFORCEMENT, NOTIFICATION, AND PENALTIES
(excerpt; consolidated text as of 01 JUL 2021)

§ 402.401  CIVIL PENALTIES.
The Commissioner may assess a civil penalty against a Covered Entity, or
against a director, officer, or employee thereof, for each violation of this
chapter in accordance with the following schedule. Each Record affected
constitutes a separate violation, subject to the aggregate limits stated.

  CITATION    VIOLATION                                   PER VIOLATION   AGGREGATE
  ---------   -----------------------------------------   -------------   ----------
  § 402.201   Failure to maintain safeguards ..........        $ 1,000     $ 250,000
  § 402.202   Omission of a program element ...........        $   500     $ 100,000
  § 402.203   Retention beyond schedule ...............        $   250     $  75,000
  § 402.204   Failure to designate a Custodian ........        $ 2,500     $   2,500
  § 402.205   Unauthorised third-party disclosure .....        $ 5,000     $ 500,000
  § 402.206   Failure to produce on examination .......        $ 1,000     $  50,000
  § 402.302   Misclassification of identifier category         $   250     $  50,000
  § 402.403   Late or deficient notification ..........        $ 2,500     $ 300,000
  § 402.405   Obstruction of an investigation .........        $10,000     $ 500,000

§ 402.402  FACTORS IN ASSESSMENT.
In fixing the amount of a penalty the Commissioner shall consider: (1) the
gravity of the violation; (2) the number of Records affected; (3) the tier of
the identifier categories involved; (4) prior findings against the Covered
Entity within the preceding sixty (60) months; (5) the promptness of
self-reporting; and (6) remedial measures undertaken before issuance of the
notice of charges.

§ 402.403  NOTIFICATION OF BREACH.
(a) Upon determination that a Breach of Security has occurred, the Covered
    Entity shall give notice in accordance with the following deadlines,
    computed from the date of determination.

    RECIPIENT                        DEADLINE          FORM
    ------------------------------   ---------------   -------------------------
    Commissioner                     72 hours          Form DFI-407, filed
                                                       electronically
    Affected natural persons         30 calendar days  Written notice, first
                                                       class or electronic where
                                                       consented
    Consumer reporting agencies      30 calendar days  Written notice, if more
                                                       than 1,000 Records
    Board of directors               At next meeting   Minuted report

(b) Notice to affected natural persons shall state, at a minimum: the date or
    date range of the Breach; the categories of field labels involved by code
    under Rule 4-19-2; the measures taken by the Covered Entity; the steps the
    recipient may take; and a toll-free line for enquiries maintained for not
    less than ninety (90) days.
(c) Notice may be delayed only upon written request of a law enforcement agency
    stating that notice would impede a criminal investigation. The delay
    terminates on the date specified by that agency.

§ 402.404  NOTICE OF CHARGES; HEARING.
No penalty shall be assessed until the Covered Entity has been served with a
notice of charges and afforded not less than twenty (20) days to request a
hearing. A request for hearing stays assessment but does not stay any
cease-and-desist order issued under § 402.406.

§ 402.405  OBSTRUCTION.
Any alteration, concealment, or destruction of a Record, log, or register with
intent to impede an examination or investigation is a violation of this
subchapter and may be referred to the Attorney General.

§ 402.406  OTHER REMEDIES.
Penalties under this subchapter are in addition to, and not in substitution
for, any order of restitution, any condition imposed on a charter, and any
remedy available under federal law.
