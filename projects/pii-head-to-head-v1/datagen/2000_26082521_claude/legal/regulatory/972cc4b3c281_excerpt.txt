  19-1  APPLICABILITY.  This chapter governs every record system operated
        by or for the institution in which an element listed at 19-3 is
        stored, whether the system is electronic, paper, or microform,
        and whether operated on premises or by a contracted processor.

  19-2  DEFINITIONS.  "Element" means a single labeled field. "Class"
        means the grouping assigned to an element by this chapter.
        "Records Custodian" means the officer of record for a system.
        "Processor" means a contracted party acting on institutional
        instruction. "Unauthorized acquisition" means access by a party
        without a purpose code assigned under 19-4.

  19-3  IDENTIFIER INVENTORY.  Each record system shall list, by label,
        every element it contains.  The label and its class are entered
        on this schedule; contents of the fields are not entered.

        ELEMENT LABEL                CLASS   SOURCE FORM       RETENTION
        ===========================  ======  ================  ==========
        Legal name                   DI-1    Application       Permanent
        Former or preferred name     DI-1    Change request    Permanent
        Social Security number       DI-1    Federal aid form  5 years
        Student identification no.   DI-1    Registration      Permanent
        Date of birth                DI-2    Application       Permanent
        Place of birth               DI-2    Application       10 years
        Residential address          DI-2    Address form      3 years
        Mailing address              DI-2    Address form      3 years
        Telephone number             DI-2    Address form      3 years
        Electronic mail address      DI-2    Account form      3 years
        Parent or guardian name      DI-2    Aid application   5 years
        Bank account number          FN-5    Refund form       3 years
        Payment card last segment    FN-5    Cashier record    18 months
        Photograph                   BI-6    Identity card     Permanent
        Fingerprint template         BI-6    Access control    Term of use
        Immigration document number  GV-7    Visa file         5 years

  19-4  DUTIES OF THE RECORDS CUSTODIAN.  The Custodian shall assign a
        purpose code to each authorized role; certify the inventory at
        19-3 annually; withdraw access upon separation or transfer; and
        require encryption of class DI-1, FN-5, and GV-7 elements at
        rest and in transit.

  19-5  DISCLOSURE AND REDISCLOSURE.  Disclosure outside the institution
        requires a written agreement stating the purpose, the elements
        released by label, the term, and the manner of destruction.
        Redisclosure by a processor is prohibited absent written consent
        of the Registrar.

  19-6  NOTIFICATION OF UNAUTHORIZED ACQUISITION.

        EVENT                          RESPONSIBLE ROLE      DEADLINE
        =============================  ====================  ===============
        Discovery of unauthorized      Records Custodian     Same day
        acquisition
        Report to the Registrar        Records Custodian     1 business day
        Assessment of scope            Information Security  5 business days
        Notice to affected persons     Registrar             30 days
        Report to the Attorney         General Counsel       45 days
        General

  19-7  RETENTION AND DISPOSITION.  An element shall be destroyed at the
        close of the period stated at 19-3.  Destruction shall be by
        cross-cut shredding, pulping, or certified media sanitization,
        and shall be entered in the disposition register.

  19-8  PENALTIES.

        TIER  FINDING                        CIVIL PENALTY   ACTION
        ====  =============================  ==============  ==================
        I     Failure to file inventory      None            Written notice
        II    Negligent disclosure           $500/record     Retraining
        III   Knowing disclosure             $5,000/record   Access suspended
        IV    Disclosure for personal gain   $10,000/record  Dismissal

        A finding under tier III or IV shall be referred to the General
        Counsel.  Appeal lies to the Board within twenty days of notice.

  19-9  SEVERABILITY.  If any provision of this chapter is held invalid,
        the remaining provisions continue in force.
