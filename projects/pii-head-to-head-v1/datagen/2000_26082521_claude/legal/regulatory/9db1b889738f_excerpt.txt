ADMINISTRATIVE RULE 22-104(b)
SECURITY OF PUPIL IDENTIFIER FILES
Promulgated under authority of Education Code § 7-4-11

.01 SCOPE.
This rule governs every file, index, card, tape, diskette, or listing in which a personal identifier is recorded.

.02 CLASSIFICATION.
Files shall be classified as Tier I (identifier plus program or health data), Tier II (identifier only), or Tier III (directory information only).

.03 STORAGE.
Tier I and Tier II files shall be secured at the close of each business day. Keys shall be signed out on the form prescribed by the Department.

.04 TRANSMITTAL.
No Tier I file shall leave the attendance site except under transmittal receipt bearing: SENDING SITE; RECEIVING SITE; NUMBER OF FOLDERS; SEAL NUMBER; RELEASING SIGNATURE.

.05 DISPOSAL.
Superseded media shall be destroyed by shredding or degaussing. A certificate of destruction shall be retained three (3) years.

.06 SANCTION.
Failure to comply shall be reported by the Records Custodian to the Superintendent within two (2) working days and may constitute grounds for disciplinary action.
