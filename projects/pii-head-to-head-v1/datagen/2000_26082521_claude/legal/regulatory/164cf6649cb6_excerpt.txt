TITLE 22 — EDUCATION
CHAPTER 14 — PUPIL RECORDS AND PERSONAL INFORMATION PRIVACY
(as amended; operative July 1, 1997)

§ 14-201. Short title.
This chapter may be cited as the Pupil Records and Personal Information Privacy Act.

§ 14-202. Definitions.
As used in this chapter, unless the context clearly requires otherwise:
 (1) "Agency" means a school district, board of cooperative educational services, intermediate unit, or other local educational agency that collects, maintains, transmits, or destroys pupil records, and includes any officer, employee, contractor, or agent thereof.
 (2) "Direct identifier" means a data element that of itself identifies a pupil, a parent, or a guardian, and includes the following field labels: surname; given name; former or maiden surname; residence address; mailing address; home telephone listing; social security account number; state-assigned pupil identification number; locally assigned enrollment number; photographic likeness; and signature specimen.
 (3) "Indirect identifier" means a data element that does not of itself identify a pupil but which, in combination with other elements reasonably available to a recipient, permits identification, and includes: date of birth; place of birth; mother's maiden surname; grade level; homeroom assignment; transportation route number; attendance zone code; date of first enrollment; and date of withdrawal.
 (4) "Sensitive identifier" means a data element pertaining to the physical, mental, behavioral, or economic condition of a pupil, and includes: health screening result; medication administration entry; immunization series code; disability classification code; individualized education program status; free or reduced-price meal eligibility status; and juvenile justice referral notation.
 (5) "Directory information" means those elements designated by the governing board under § 14-205 and published in advance of designation.
 (6) "Holder of record" means any person having custody, possession, or control of a pupil record, whether in paper, microform, magnetic, or optical medium.
 (7) "Records Custodian" means the officer designated under § 14-203.

§ 14-203. Designation of Records Custodian.
Each agency shall designate one officer as the Records Custodian and shall file the designation, stating position title only, with the Commissioner not later than the fifteenth instructional day of each school year. The Records Custodian shall maintain the index of record series required by § 14-207 and shall be the sole officer authorized to approve releases under § 14-205.

§ 14-204. Obligations of holders of records.
Every holder of record shall:
 (a) store records containing a direct or sensitive identifier in a locked cabinet or in an electronic system requiring individual password authentication;
 (b) restrict access to persons demonstrating a legitimate educational interest, determined in each instance and not by class of employment;
 (c) enter in the access log, for each inspection or disclosure, the following fields: date of access; position title of the person accessing; statutory or regulatory basis; categories of identifiers disclosed; and medium of transmittal;
 (d) verify the identity and authority of a requesting party before release by facsimile transmission or by magnetic media, and confirm receipt in writing;
 (e) not remove an original record from agency premises except upon the written authorization of the Records Custodian;
 (f) report a known or suspected unauthorized disclosure to the Records Custodian not later than five business days after discovery.

§ 14-208. Penalties.
 (a) A holder of record who negligently discloses a record in violation of this chapter is subject to a civil penalty not exceeding five hundred dollars for each violation.
 (b) A knowing or willful disclosure is subject to a civil penalty not exceeding one thousand dollars for each violation and constitutes a misdemeanor of the second degree.
 (c) An agency found by the Commissioner to have engaged in a pattern of violation may have state aid withheld, in whole or in part, until compliance is certified.
 (d) Penalties under this section are cumulative and are not in lieu of any remedy available under 20 U.S.C. § 1232g.
