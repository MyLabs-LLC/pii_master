CHAPTER 402, SUBCHAPTER B — OBLIGATIONS ON HOLDERS OF RECORDS
(excerpt as amended 14 APR 2021)

§ 402.201  GENERAL DUTY.
A Covered Entity that holds a Record shall implement and maintain reasonable
administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to (i) the size
and complexity of the entity, (ii) the nature and scope of its activities, and
(iii) the sensitivity of the Record held.

§ 402.202  MINIMUM PROGRAM ELEMENTS.
The written information security program shall address, at a minimum, the
elements set out below.

 REF     PROGRAM ELEMENT              MINIMUM FREQUENCY   ACCOUNTABLE ROLE
 -----   --------------------------   -----------------   ----------------------
 202.1   Records inventory            Annual              Records Custodian
 202.2   Risk assessment              Annual              Chief Risk Officer
 202.3   Access review                Semiannual          Information Security Officer
 202.4   Vendor due diligence         At onboarding       Vendor Management Officer
 202.5   Vendor re-certification      Annual              Vendor Management Officer
 202.6   Staff privacy training       Annual              Human Resources Director
 202.7   Disposal log reconciliation  Quarterly           Records Custodian
 202.8   Incident response test       Annual              Information Security Officer
 202.9   Board report on program      Annual              Chief Executive Officer

§ 402.203  LIMITATION ON COLLECTION AND RETENTION.
(a) A Covered Entity shall collect only those data elements necessary for a
    permissible purpose stated in its published notice.
(b) A Record shall not be retained beyond the applicable period in the schedule
    filed with the Department, except where retention is required by court
    order, litigation hold, or examination request.
(c) Retention beyond the scheduled period shall be documented in the retention
    exception register, stating the field label affected, the authority relied
    upon, and the review date.

§ 402.204  DESIGNATION OF CUSTODIAN.
The board of directors shall designate, by resolution, a Records Custodian and
one alternate. The designation shall be recorded by title only in the board
minutes and reported to the Department within thirty (30) days of any change.

§ 402.205  ACCESS BY THIRD PARTIES.
No Record shall be disclosed to a third party absent (1) written contract
imposing duties equivalent to this subchapter, (2) an executed confidentiality
undertaking, and (3) an entry in the disclosure log identifying the recipient
organization, the categories of fields disclosed, the legal basis, and the date
of disclosure. The disclosure log shall be retained for six (6) years.

§ 402.206  EXAMINATION.
The Department may examine any Record, log, or register required by this
subchapter without prior notice. Failure to produce within five (5) business
days constitutes a separate violation under § 402.401.
