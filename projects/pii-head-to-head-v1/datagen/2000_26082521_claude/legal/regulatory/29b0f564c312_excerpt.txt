STATE ADMINISTRATIVE CODE
TITLE 44 — PUBLIC UTILITIES
CHAPTER 9 — CUSTOMER RECORD SECURITY
RULE 44-9-2 — DISPOSAL OF RECORDS CONTAINING PERSONAL INFORMATION

SECTION A. AUTHORITY.
  Adopted under Ch. 812, Art. 3, § 812.309 (rulemaking).

SECTION B. SCOPE.
  All municipal utility systems, whether operated as a department,
  authority, or district.

SECTION C. REQUIREMENT.
  (1) Paper records shall be cross-cut shredded to particles not
      exceeding 5/32 inch by 1-1/2 inches, incinerated, or pulped.
  (2) Electronic media shall be purged or destroyed in accordance
      with a recognized media sanitization standard. Reformatting
      alone does not satisfy this paragraph.
  (3) Disposal shall be witnessed by an employee other than the
      person performing the disposal.
  (4) A third-party disposal vendor shall be bonded and shall furnish
      a certificate of destruction within ten (10) business days.

SECTION D. DISPOSAL LOG.
  The Records Custodian shall maintain a log bearing the following
  fields for each disposal event:
      Log Entry No.
      Date of Destruction
      Record Series Code
      Identifier Categories Present
      Volume (boxes / media units)
      Method of Destruction
      Vendor Name and Certificate No.
      Title of Witnessing Employee
      Title of Approving Officer

SECTION E. RETENTION OF LOG.
  Three (3) years from date of entry. The log is subject to
  inspection by the Commission upon request.
