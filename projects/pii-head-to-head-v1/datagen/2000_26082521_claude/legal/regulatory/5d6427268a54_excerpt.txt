STATE BOARD OF EDUCATION
ADMINISTRATIVE CODE, CHAPTER 8 - RECORDS AND INFORMATION SECURITY
RULE 8.14 - DUTIES OF COVERED HOLDERS
(Excerpt; adopted under the authority of Sec. 14-401, Title 22)

8.14.1  General duty.

        A Covered Holder shall protect every Agency Record in its
        custody against unauthorized access, use, alteration, loss,
        and disclosure, whether the record is held in paper form,
        electronic form, or any other form.

8.14.3  Designation of the Records Custodian.

        (a)  Each Covered Holder shall designate one Records Custodian
             and at least one alternate.
        (b)  The designation shall be by role title and shall be filed
             with the Department within thirty days of any change.
        (c)  The Records Custodian shall not delegate the duties under
             control C-104 or control C-105 to a person who is not an
             employee of the Covered Holder.

8.14.4  Minimum necessary principle.

        A Covered Holder shall grant a staff member access only to
        those identifier categories that are necessary to the duties
        of that staff member's assigned role. Access granted by reason
        of convenience, seniority, or general administrative interest
        does not satisfy this rule.

8.14.5  Contractors.

        A contractor that receives an Agency Record is subject to this
        rule to the same extent as the Covered Holder that engaged it.
        A Covered Holder remains answerable for the acts of its
        contractors and shall terminate the engagement upon a material
        failure of any control listed in 8.14.2.
