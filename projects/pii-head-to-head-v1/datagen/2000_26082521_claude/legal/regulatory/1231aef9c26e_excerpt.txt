BOARD RULE 6-12 (SUPERSEDES RULE 6-08)
UNAUTHORIZED RELEASE OF PERSONAL INFORMATION — ENFORCEMENT
Adopted: 09/2004

• 6-12.1 SCOPE. This Rule applies to all certificated staff, classified staff, substitutes, volunteers, student teachers, and vendors granted access to any student or personnel record system.

• 6-12.2 PROHIBITED ACTS. No covered person shall:
   • Access a record absent a legitimate educational or administrative interest;
   • Remove an original record from district premises without the written authorization of the Records Custodian;
   • Transmit a Social Security Number, Date of Birth, or Home Address by unsecured electronic mail;
   • Post, display, or announce a Student Identification Number in any public area, roster, or posted grade list;
   • Store personal information on a personally owned computer, diskette, or portable drive;
   • Retain a record beyond the Retention Class assigned in the Master Index.

• 6-12.3 REPORTING. A covered person with knowledge of a suspected unauthorized release shall notify the Records Custodian by the close of the next business day. The Custodian shall complete Form IR-2, stating: Date of Discovery, Record System Title, Data Elements Involved, Estimated Number of Individuals Affected, Containment Action, Title of Reporting Employee.

• 6-12.4 NOTIFICATION. Where an unauthorized acquisition of unencrypted Category I information is confirmed, the Superintendent shall cause written notice to be given to each affected individual, or the parent or guardian of a minor, in the most expedient time possible and without unreasonable delay, subject only to the legitimate needs of law enforcement.

• 6-12.5 SANCTIONS. Violations shall be addressed progressively as follows:
   • First violation, non-willful — written reprimand, mandatory retraining within thirty (30) days;
   • Second violation, or first willful violation — suspension without pay, and revocation of system access;
   • Third violation, or any violation for pecuniary gain — recommendation for dismissal and referral to the licensing authority and to the prosecuting attorney.

• 6-12.6 VENDORS. Vendor violations shall result in immediate suspension of access credentials, return or certified destruction of all records held, and referral to Purchasing for debarment proceedings.
