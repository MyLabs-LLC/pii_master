SCHEDULE C — CIVIL PENALTIES AND
                       ADMINISTRATIVE SANCTIONS
              (Promulgated under Sec. 14.410, Title 22)
==============================================================================

Clause 1.  Application.

  1.1  This schedule applies to any holder of pupil records, including
       a district, a cooperative service agency, a contractor
       performing an institutional function, and any employee or agent
       of the foregoing.

  1.2  A penalty imposed under this schedule is in addition to, and
       not in substitution for, any remedy available to a parent or
       eligible student under Chapter 14 or under federal law.

Clause 2.  Assessment.

  2.1  Penalties are assessed per act, per entry, or per occurrence as
       stated in the schedule below. Each calendar day on which a
       continuing violation persists constitutes a separate act.

  2.2  The Commissioner may reduce an assessed penalty by not more
       than one-half upon a showing that the holder self-reported the
       violation before the Department received a complaint, and that
       corrective measures were completed within thirty days.

Clause 3.  Schedule.

ITEM   VIOLATION DESCRIBED                           PENALTY RANGE
=====  ============================================  ==========================
C-1    Disclosure of an identifier without consent   $250 to $1,000 per act
C-2    Failure to maintain the disclosure register   $100 per school month
C-3    Refusal to permit inspection by a parent      $500 and writ of mandamus
C-4    Failure to amend an entry after final order   $500 per uncorrected entry
C-5    Willful destruction of a record under appeal  $2,500 and referral
C-6    Retention beyond the approved schedule        $150 per school month
C-7    Failure to report unauthorized access         $1,000 per occurrence
C-8    Repeat violation within twenty-four months    Twice the base amount
=====  ============================================  ==========================

Clause 4.  Administrative Sanctions.

  4.1  In addition to a monetary penalty, the Commissioner may:

       (a)  suspend the district's authority to release directory
            information for a period not exceeding one school year;

       (b)  require the appointment of a substitute Records Custodian
            approved by the Department;

       (c)  require completion of a training course in the handling of
            confidential pupil information by every employee having
            access to Class A or Class C identifiers; or

       (d)  place the district on reporting status, requiring monthly
            filing of the disclosure register with the Department.

Clause 5.  Referral.

  5.1  Where the Commissioner finds that a disclosure was made for
       pecuniary gain, the matter shall be referred to the prosecuting
       attorney of the county in which the record is kept.

  5.2  Referral under clause 5.1 does not stay the assessment of a
       civil penalty under Clause 3.

Clause 6.  Review.

  6.1  A holder aggrieved by an assessment may request a hearing
       within twenty days of service of the notice of assessment.
       Failure to request a hearing renders the assessment final and
       enforceable as a civil judgment.
