3) NOTES ON INDIVIDUAL CODES
   3.1) ERR-VAL-207 arises where the postcode field fails the length rule before
        truncation. Rows were repaired at source and reloaded under job id
        ETL-FC-0311, run id RUN-20210609-0400, rows_written 9,884.
   3.2) ERR-VAL-211 arises where the date-of-birth column is populated with a
        placeholder rather than a parseable date. The transform suppresses the
        column in all cases; the exception is raised for source-quality tracking
        only and does not indicate a disclosure.
   3.3) ERR-VAL-214 and ERR-VAL-218 arise where the contact columns are populated
        in a free-text field intended for operational notes. Both columns are
        Tier A and were suppressed before the exception was written.
   3.4) ERR-VAL-233 indicates a tokenisation collision check failure on the
        concession card column. Affected rows are held pending key epoch review.
   3.5) ERR-VAL-240 is raised whenever the national identity column is present in
        an extract that is not authorised to carry it. Three occurrences were
        escalated to the Records Custodian on 2021-06-11, 2021-06-17 and
        2021-06-24.
