MAPPING RULE SET MRS-0417
STUDENT MASTER TO STATE PUPIL ACCOUNTING EXTRACT
REVISION 4 — EFFECTIVE 01-SEP-1998 — SUPERSEDES REVISION 3 OF 14-MAR-1998

1.  SCOPE
1.1 This rule set governs field-level translation from STUDENT.MASTER (VSAM KSDS, library SISPROD) to the fixed-length submission file PUPACCT.EXTRACT transmitted to the State Department of Education under the quarterly pupil accounting cycle.
1.2 The rule set is binding on job stream ETLSTU10 and on any ad hoc extract executed by the Data Processing Supervisor.
1.3 Field labels enumerated at clause 3 constitute the complete authorised source inventory. Any source field not enumerated shall not be read, staged, or written.

2.  SOURCE AND TARGET DECLARATION
2.1 Source: SISPROD.STUDENT.MASTER, record length 812, key STU_LOCAL_ID.
2.2 Target: PUPACCT.EXTRACT, record length 300, unlabelled, EBCDIC, blocked 6000.
2.3 Staging: SISWRK.STG.PUPACCT, retained 3 generations.

4.  TRANSFORMATION RULES
4.1 R01 — Right-justify, zero-fill. Where STU_STATE_ID is unpopulated, substitute the token issued by the identity broker in the form TOK-nnnnnn and log at severity W.
4.2 R02 — Trim, fold to upper case, truncate at declared length. Embedded apostrophes and hyphens are retained; all other punctuation is stripped.
4.3 R03 — Convert from packed decimal to CCYYMMDD. Century determination follows the district Year 2000 windowing standard: values 00 through 30 resolve to 20nn, values 31 through 99 resolve to 19nn.
4.4 R04 — Validate against the corresponding entry in CODES.MASTER. Reject the record on failure; do not default.
4.5 R05 — Retain the first five positions only. Positions 6 through 9 of the postal field are discarded at read time and are not staged.
4.6 R06 — Translate the eligibility indicator to the state single-character domain. The underlying application record and income declaration fields are not carried forward.
4.7 R07 — Pass through unchanged.
4.8 R08 — Numeric, right-justify, zero-fill; must not exceed the enrolled day count for the reporting period.

5.  SUPPRESSION
5.1 R09 — Fields marked SUPPRESS are excluded at the SELECT stage. They are not written to SISWRK.STG.PUPACCT and are not present in any checkpoint dataset.
5.2 The Records Custodian shall countersign any request to alter the suppression list. Requests are logged under change register CR-98.
5.3 Extracts produced prior to 01-SEP-1998 remain governed by Revision 3 until purge.

6.  CHANGE CONTROL
6.1 Amendments take effect at the first scheduled run following approval by the Director of Management Information Services.
6.2 This revision was approved 24-AUG-1998 and distributed to the Records Custodian, the Data Processing Supervisor, and the Pupil Accounting Clerk of each attendance centre.
