*
*  This member is read at bind time by the transform module and again by the
*  report writer.  It carries no data of its own; it names columns and states
*  the disposition applied to them before any extract leaves the production
*  region.  Each stanza is keyed by column name as it appears in the copybook,
*  not by the display label used on the escrow worksheet, and where the two
*  differ the display label is carried in the LABEL keyword for the benefit of
*  the exception listings.  Where a column is named here and does not appear
*  in the source, the transform module raises an informational message and
*  continues; where a column appears in the source and is not named here, the
*  module abends.  That default is deliberate and is not to be relaxed.
*
