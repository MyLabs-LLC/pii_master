Four notes for the record.

1. PTY_SSN and ESC_ACCT tokenise against vault TVLT01. The same
   input yields the same token within a run and across runs.
   Reversal is available to the Records Custodian only.

2. VEST_TXT is free-form and is suppressed in whole. Requests to
   parse it for party names were declined on 24 February 2004.

3. Source columns PTY_MAIDEN, PTY_EMPLOYER and PTY_DL have no
   target and are dropped at the extract. Under DMASK.CFG 4.2 an
   unmapped column aborts the job, so all three are listed
   explicitly in the drop stanza of the job control.

4. Trial run of 6 April 2004: rows read 402,877; rows written
   402,730; rejects 147; elapsed 01:22:16; RC=4. Reject detail is
   with Data Quality Control.

Sign-off is requested from the Records Custodian and from Escrow
