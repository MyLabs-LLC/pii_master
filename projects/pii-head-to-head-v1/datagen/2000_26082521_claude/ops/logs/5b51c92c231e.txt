Version 4.2 supersedes version 4.1 in all non-production regions
and in all warehouse extracts. Version 4.1 remains in force for the
February close only. The configuration text is reproduced below for
the record. Changes from 4.1 are marked at the right margin with an
asterisk.

Three items require action. First, RETAIN=LAST0 removes the
trailing-digit retention previously allowed on account columns;
reports that keyed on those digits must be rekeyed to the token.
Second, VESTING_TEXT is now suppressed in whole, which will empty
approximately 61,000 warehouse rows in the March load. Third, any
column not appearing above will now abort the job rather than load
suppressed; departments adding columns must file a request through
this office not less than five business days before the cycle.

Direct questions to this office. Do not alter the file locally.
