VALIDATION EXCEPTION DIGEST — CYCLE 1998-11-14 — JOB ESC0410P — SEQ 03

The digest covers the 1,304 rows held from the escrow warehouse feed and is circulated to the branch supervisors and to the Records Custodian. It is a listing of column names and counts; the exception dataset itself carries surrogate keys and rule identifiers only, and the underlying values are neither printed here nor displayed on the correction screen, which presents restricted columns as <MASK> pending release by the vault.

The largest category, 1,204 rows, arises on BORROWER_DOB under rule V-002, century indeterminate. These are branch-entered records still held in a six-digit picture clause, and the pivot at 30 assigns them to a century the edit routine will not accept without confirmation. The correction path is the same as in the two preceding cycles: the branch confirms the century from the file jacket and re-keys through the maintenance transaction, which writes a full eight-digit value to the master.
