3. Rule set B — quasi-identifiers retained for analysis
   3.1 county_fips ................ PASS
   3.2 census_tract ............... PASS
   3.3 age_band ................... DERIVE from date_of_birth
   3.4 sex_at_birth ............... PASS
   3.5 race_code, ethnicity_code .. PASS
   3.6 specimen_collection_date ... SHIFT ±0 days (no shift in surveillance feed)
4. Enforcement
   4.1 Masking runs before any write to the reporting schema.
   4.2 Any field present in source but absent from sections 2 and 3 halts the load.
   4.3 Rule changes require sign-off from the Records Custodian and the Privacy Officer.
   4.4 Vault namespaces are not shared across programs; cross-namespace joins are blocked at the view layer.
