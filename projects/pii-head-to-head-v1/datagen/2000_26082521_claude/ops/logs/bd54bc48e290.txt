4.1 Tokenisation is performed at the extract stage by module TKNSVC v2.3. The clear value is not written to the staging area at any point.

4.2 Masked placeholders appearing in operator logs and reject listings shall use the following forms only:

    RIDER_SURNAME_TKN      ->  TKN:################
    RIDER_GIVEN_NAME_TKN   ->  TKN:################
    FARE_CARD_TKN          ->  TKN:################
    BIRTH_YEAR_ONLY        ->  ####
    POSTAL_SECTOR          ->  ***

4.3 Operators are reminded that the reject listing (clause 5) prints field labels and rule codes only. No clear value is to be reproduced in any incident ticket, electronic mail, or telephone escalation.
