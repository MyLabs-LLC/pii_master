2. OPERATING CLAUSES

2.01  Any field label enumerated at Clause 1 shall be resolved by the masking
      agent before the staging commit. No unmasked column may persist beyond
      the load buffer.
2.02  Token issuance under TOKENIZE_FPE and TOKENIZE_REF is format-preserving
      and deterministic within a single ruleset revision. Revision increments
      invalidate prior token equivalence.
2.03  Vault-backed rules (VLT = Y) require a successful handshake with the
      token vault. On handshake failure the agent shall fail closed and the
      dependent session shall abort.
2.04  Fields carrying CLS of SEN or DIR shall not be written to any extract
      destined for an outside vendor, notwithstanding a PASS_THROUGH entry
      appearing elsewhere in the mapping.
2.05  RETAIN_LAST_4 preserves terminal characters only. The leading segment is
      replaced with the fill character and is not recoverable from the target.
2.06  GENERALIZE_YEAR discards day and month components at read time. Downstream
      age derivation shall use the generalized value.
2.07  Requests to add, retire, or reclassify a field label are submitted to the
      Records Custodian on form DPS-CR-11 and take effect only on publication
      of a new revision.
2.08  The Data Steward for each subject area attests to this table quarterly.
      Attestation for the current quarter is recorded in DPS-ATT-2011-Q1.
