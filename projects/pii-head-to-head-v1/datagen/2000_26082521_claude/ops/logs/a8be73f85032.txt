EFFECTIVE 19980406 AT 0001 HRS. SUPERSEDES SCHEDULE B DATED 19971117.

PURSUANT TO SECTION 4(b) OF THE DIVISION DATA HANDLING DIRECTIVE, AND
SUBJECT TO THE WRITTEN CONCURRENCE OF THE RECORDS CUSTODIAN AND THE
UTILITY BILLING SUPERVISOR, THE FOLLOWING MAPPING RULES SHALL GOVERN ALL
EXTRACTS TAKEN FROM CUSTMAST.VSAM AND ALL LOADS DIRECTED TO THE CIS.PARTY,
CIS.ACCT AND CIS.PREM TABLESPACES. NO FIELD OMITTED FROM THIS SCHEDULE MAY
BE CARRIED FORWARD BY ANY MEANS, WHETHER BY DIRECT MOVE, BY REDEFINES
CLAUSE, OR BY INCLUSION IN A FILLER AREA. WHERE A DISPOSITION OF SUPPRESS
OR TOKENISE IS INDICATED, THE UNDERLYING VALUE SHALL NOT BE WRITTEN TO ANY
INTERMEDIATE DATASET, SPOOL FILE, OR OPERATOR LISTING, AND ANY SUCH
WRITING SHALL CONSTITUTE A REPORTABLE HANDLING EXCEPTION UNDER SECTION 9.
