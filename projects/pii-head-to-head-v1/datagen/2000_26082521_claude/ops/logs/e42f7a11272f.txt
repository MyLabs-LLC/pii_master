TO:      Privacy Officer

SUBJECT: Masking profile in effect for the reporting extract — file mask_profile_dw.conf

Attached below is the current contents of the masking configuration applied to all outbound extracts from the reporting warehouse. It was last modified 2016-01-26 and is under change control. Any addition of a column to a warehouse table requires a corresponding entry here before that table may be extracted.

--- begin mask_profile_dw.conf ---

[profile]

owner_role      = Database Administrator

default_action  = deny
# Columns with no rule below are not emitted. Do not change default_action.

[actions]

Three items require your decision before the next quarterly review.

First, RESIDENCE_ZIP is truncated to three characters. The Epidemiology Unit has asked for five for two low-population ZIP ranges. That request is outstanding and has not been implemented.

Second, DATE_OF_BIRTH is currently converted to a five-year age bucket with a cap at 89. The cap is applied after bucketing, which produces a final bucket of unequal width. This is working as configured but may not be what was intended when the rule was written.

Third, the profile contains no rule for the newly added column HOUSEHOLD_SIZE. Under default_action the column is simply not emitted, so no extract is currently at risk, but the Epidemiology Unit has noticed its absence and will raise a ticket.

Please return a marked-up copy with your determinations by February 12.
