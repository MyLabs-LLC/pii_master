MEMORANDUM — METROPOLITAN TRANSIT AUTHORITY
OFFICE OF INFORMATION SYSTEMS / DATA SERVICES DIVISION

TO:      Manager, ETL Operations
FROM:    Records Custodian
DATE:    16 September 2004
RE:      Manifest Exception, Job RDRLOAD_0914 — Emergency Contact Columns
FILE:    OIS-DS-2004-0916-M

1. The log for job RDRLOAD_0914, sub-job RDR_XFM_02, records two warnings at 2004-09-14 02:03:46 in respect of the columns EMERGENCY_CONTACT_NAME and EMERGENCY_CONTACT_PHONE. Both were present in the source column set and neither was matched against the drop manifest hash held in rdrload.cfg revision 11.

2. The default mask was applied and no clear value entered the staging area or the target structure RDS.DIM_RIDER. The target column set recorded at 03:09:44 confirms neither column was materialised. This office accepts that no exposure arose from the cycle of 14 September 2004.

3. The defect is nonetheless a control failure. The two columns were added to the [drop] section of rdrload.cfg at revision 11 but the manifest hash was not regenerated, so the drop list and the hash disagreed. Reliance on the default mask as a fallback is not an acceptable control.

4. The following is directed:

   4.1 The manifest hash is to be regenerated and rdrload.cfg advanced to revision 12 before the cycle of 20 September 2004.

   4.2 The change control record is to note both column labels explicitly.

   4.3 A verification run against the September cycle is to confirm that neither EMERGENCY_CONTACT_NAME nor EMERGENCY_CONTACT_PHONE appears in the source column set enumerated at extract, and the result is to be reported to this office.

   4.4 Reject queue RJQ.RDRLOAD for cycle 2004-09-14, holding 2,553 rows, is to be retained on its ordinary 30-day schedule. No extension is granted.

5. Confirmation of clauses 4.1 through 4.3 is required in writing by 22 September 2004.

END OF MEMORANDUM
