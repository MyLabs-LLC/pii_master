1.  SCOPE

1.1  This specification governs the movement of member master attributes from
     copybook MBRMAST01 into the operational data store dimension table
     ODS_MEMBER_DIM. It applies to nightly job CUEXT410 and to any ad hoc
     re-extract authorised under clause 4.3.

1.2  No column enumerated in the table at clause 2 may be propagated to a
     downstream mart, reporting extract, or third-party file transfer except in
     the transformed state prescribed in the TRANSFORM RULE column.

3.  TRANSFORMATION CLAUSES

3.1  TOKENISE-FF1 shall resolve against the token vault instance TKNVLT1 prior
     to write. Where the vault does not return a reference within the session
     timeout, the row shall be rejected to CUEXT410.REJECT and not defaulted.

3.2  YEAR-ONLY shall retain the four-digit year component only. The month and
     day components are discarded at the reader and are not written to any
     staging area, sort work file, or session log.

3.3  SUPPRESS directs the reader to bypass the field entirely. The target table
     carries no column for sequence 014 and none shall be added.

3.4  LEFT-3 retains the first three positions of the postal code. Rows carrying
     fewer than three positions are counted under rule VR-0110.

4.  CHANGE CONTROL

4.1  Amendments to this table require joint sign-off by the Records Custodian
     and the Information Security Officer.

4.2  Revision 6 adds sequences 015 and 020 and reclassifies sequence 010 from
     PASS-THRU to LEFT-3.

4.3  Ad hoc re-extracts require a written request retained for seven years.
