MEMORANDUM

TO:       Data Services Group; Fare Systems Operations
FROM:     the Records Custodian, Information Governance Office
DATE:     14 September 2021
SUBJECT:  Deployment of masking profile MASK_PROF_07 to the fare data warehouse

Effective 20 September 2021 at 02:00, masking profile MASK_PROF_07 replaces MASK_PROF_05 on all extracts leaving the fare collection database. The change was requested by the Deputy General Manager for Administration following the annual controls walkthrough.

What changes:

The profile applies column-level suppression to the following fields at read time: rider_given_name, rider_family_name, date_of_birth, national_id_number, residential_street_line_1, residential_street_line_2, postal_code, primary_contact_number, contact_email_address, and payment_instrument_number. Under MASK_PROF_05 the last four characters of payment_instrument_number were retained for reconciliation. Under MASK_PROF_07 the full field is replaced with a surrogate token drawn from the vault (format TKN_PAY_<32>), and reconciliation is performed on the token rather than on the underlying value.

The fields eligibility_category, concession_type, service_area_zone, and account_open_date are not masked and remain available for reporting.

Action required:

1. Report owners must re-point any query that filters on payment_instrument_number to payment_instrument_token by 18 September. Queries not converted will return an empty result set rather than an error.
2. The Paratransit Registration team must confirm in writing that the eligibility review workbook no longer joins on national_id_number. The replacement join key is registrant_surrogate_id.
3. Extract requests submitted through the Data Request Register must state the business purpose for each unmasked field. Requests naming a masked field without a stated purpose will be returned.

Questions to the Information Governance Office through the standard ticket queue. A follow-up notice will issue after the first full nightly cycle completes.
