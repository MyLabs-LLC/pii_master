1. Application

  1.1 This Schedule binds every mapping executed by jobs PHRXETL0 through PHRXETL7 inclusive, and every ad hoc extract requested under work order procedure WO-11.

  1.2 A mapping rule not appearing in this Schedule confers no authority to move a field, and the field shall be treated as suppressed by default.

3. Derivation of PAT_AGE_BAND_CD

  3.1 The derivation shall be performed within the extract step, in storage, and the source field shall not be written to any work file. Bands are: 01 (under 18), 02 (18–34), 03 (35–49), 04 (50–64), 05 (65 and over), 99 (indeterminate).

  3.2 Where the derivation cannot be performed, band 99 shall be assigned and error V-0113 raised; under no circumstance shall the source field be carried forward for later derivation.
