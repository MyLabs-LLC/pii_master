PART II — CLAUSES
1.  Basis of register
1.1 Each row failing a rule of the set at I.4 is written to the reject store with the failing column name, the rule code and the run identifier. Column content is not written to the reject store and is not reproduced in this register.
1.2 A row failing more than one rule is counted once against the rule of highest severity.

2.  Severity
2.1 Severity 1 suspends the load for the affected subject area.
2.2 Severity 2 permits the load to complete and requires remediation within five business days.
2.3 Severity 3 is recorded for trend purposes only.

3.  Remediation
3.1 Remediation is effected at source by the originating branch or department; correction within the warehouse is not permitted.
3.2 A rule producing exceptions in excess of one per cent of rows read for three consecutive cycles shall be referred for rule review rather than data correction.
