SECTION D — RESTRICTED ELEMENT HANDLING
S-001 ... NATIONAL_ID_TOKEN is write-once. No downstream job may request detokenisation without a signed access authorisation on Form RG-208.
S-002 ... GRADE_CODE and CREDIT_HOURS are directory-excluded and require role REG_ODS_ACADEMIC.
S-003 ... FERPA confidentiality indicator SPBPERS_CONFID_IND is carried to CONFID_FLAG and evaluated before any extract to a non-registrar consumer.
S-004 ... LAST_NAME and FIRST_NAME are suppressed to placeholder in all consumer views except REG_ODS_FULL.
S-005 ... Masked outputs are fixed-width placeholder characters. Reversal is not supported by design.
