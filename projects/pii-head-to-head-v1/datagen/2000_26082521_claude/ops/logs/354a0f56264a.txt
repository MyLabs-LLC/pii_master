Three points require attention.

First, UNLISTED_COLUMN is set to DENY. Any column added to CANDMAST or PLACEMENT without a corresponding entry here will not render. Development is to file the masking entry with the schema change, not after it.

Second, PAY_ACCT_REF and PAY_ROUTING_NBR carry REVEAL=NONE at every role including AUDITOR. Reconciliation against bank returns is performed on the token, not the underlying number. Requests to relax this were considered on 19 February and declined.

Third, EXPORT_INHERITS=Y closes the practice, observed in two districts during the January review, of extracting to diskette and reading the extract outside the masking layer. Extracts now carry the mask. Branches requiring unmasked extracts for tax filing will route the request through Payroll Systems.

Revision 7 is scheduled for the June maintenance window and will add the CLIENT_CONTACT table.
