Three changes from the prior version are worth calling out. MOTHERS_MAIDEN_NAME is now suppressed outright rather than tokenised; nothing downstream consumed the token. POSTAL_CODE moved from full suppression to first-three retention at the request of the branch analytics group. And unbound_field_action is now reject_job rather than warn, so a new personal-data column appearing in a source table will stop the load instead of flowing through unmasked. Expect that to bite the first time a source system adds a column without notice. If a job fails on that check, do not override it — open a ticket with this office and we will bind the field.
