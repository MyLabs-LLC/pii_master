SECTION C — NOTES
  1. Token domain is per-field. Cross-field token equality is not guaranteed and
     must not be relied upon for linkage.
  2. R-122 reduces all calendar dates to year or year-month. Day-of-month is not
     emitted to the mart under any condition.
  3. R-123 truncates ZIP_CD to first three positions; populations under the
     threshold in Appendix B are rolled to "000".
  4. Fields marked SUPP are excluded at the extract SELECT list. They are not
     nulled downstream.
  5. Any new source column defaults to SUPP until a rule reference is assigned.
