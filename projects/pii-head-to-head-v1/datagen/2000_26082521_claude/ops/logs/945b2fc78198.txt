VALIDATION EXCEPTION REGISTER — RXDW STAGING
Register period: 2004-04-14 01:00 CST through 2004-04-14 06:00 CST
Jobs in scope: J_RXDW_0410, J_RXDW_0420, J_RXDW_0435
Total rows rejected in period: 11,193

EXCEPTION CLASS VAL-1042 (mandatory identifier absent after transform) — 4,006 rows, all attributable to job J_RXDW_0410. The condition arises where the column CARDHOLDER_ID_NBR is presented empty or space-filled at the source interface, with the consequence that the tokenizing transform produces no surrogate and the not-null constraint upon the corresponding warehouse column is thereby violated. It is the settled position of the Records Custodian, reaffirmed in the determination of 2003-11-06 and not since disturbed, that no default, filler, or synthesized value may be substituted for an absent identifier, and that the affected row shall accordingly be rejected in whole rather than admitted in part. The rejected rows have been written to the register in masked condition and the originating store batches noted for referral to the Third Party Billing group.

EXCEPTION CLASS VAL-1077 (date of birth fails format edit) — 2,915 rows, of which 2,780 arise in job J_RXDW_0410 and 135 in job J_RXDW_0435. The column PAT_DOB_DT is received in a form not conformable to the century-explicit edit mask imposed at revision 09, the residual defect being understood to originate in the legacy point-of-sale conversion of certain acquired locations. Because the column is in any event overwritten by the literal placeholder prescribed in the masking policy, no identifying content is present in the register entry, and the derived column PAT_BIRTH_YR_DERIVED is set to null rather than inferred.

EXCEPTION CLASS VAL-1103 (prescriber identifier fails checkdigit) — 1,882 rows across jobs J_RXDW_0410 and J_RXDW_0435. The column PRSCBR_DEA_NBR does not satisfy the arithmetic verification prescribed by the governing standard. Rows are rejected notwithstanding that the clinical columns NDC_CD, GPI_CD, QTY_DISPENSED and DAYS_SUPPLY_QTY are individually valid, the Company holding the view that a dispensing record unattributable to a verified prescriber is not a record fit for retention in the analytic environment.

EXCEPTION CLASS VAL-1150 (free-text column populated where suppression required) — 1,946 rows, job J_RXDW_0420. The column SIG_TXT and the column MBR_NOTE_TXT were found populated downstream of the suppression transform, the cause being traced to a mapping variant deployed to node ETLPRD03 without the countersignature required by the configuration of record. The variant has been withdrawn, the affected rows purged from staging, and the matter reported to the Records Custodian for such further action as may be appropriate.

EXCEPTION CLASS VAL-1188 (tender column present in inbound feed) — 444 rows, job J_RXDW_0435. The columns TENDER_ACCT_NBR, TENDER_EXP_DT and TENDER_HOLDER_NM were transmitted by an interface not authorized to convey them; all three were overwritten at the boundary in accordance with the tender stanza and the rows rejected.
