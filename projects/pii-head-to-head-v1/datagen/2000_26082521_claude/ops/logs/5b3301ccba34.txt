Two points for the group's attention.

First, the 427 length failures on MEMBER_NBR all originated in the branch-conversion segment loaded last spring. The source system pads that field differently. Operations has raised a change request with the core vendor; until it is answered, the transform rule will continue to reject rather than pad, which is the correct behaviour.

Second, the EMAIL_ADDR suppression count is lower than the loaded row count because roughly 15,000 records carry no value in that field. That is expected and is not a defect in the masking layer.

Questions on the counts should go to ETL Operations. Questions on the disposition of quarantined rows should go to the Records Custodian.
