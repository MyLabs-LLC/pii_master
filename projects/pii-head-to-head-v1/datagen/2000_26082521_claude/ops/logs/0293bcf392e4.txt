SECTION B — NUMBERED CLAUSES

1.  Scope
1.1 This record authorises amendment of the masking rule set identified at A.5, applied at load time to the staging schema STG_MBR and propagated to the presentation schema DW_MBR.
1.2 The amendment is confined to the columns enumerated at Section C. No other column of the schema is varied by this record.
1.3 Masking is applied prior to commit. Unmasked column content shall not persist beyond the extract landing directory, which is purged on completion of each cycle.

2.  Masking obligations
2.1 SSN_TXT shall be transformed by method FPE_TOKEN and rendered in all downstream views as the fixed placeholder XXX-XX-XXXX. The referential token is held in the vault schema and is not exposed to reporting roles.
2.2 DATE_OF_BIRTH shall be generalised to DOB_YEAR_BUCKET (five-year interval). The source column shall be dropped from DW_MBR at load.
2.3 ACCT_NBR and CARD_PAN shall be transformed by method LAST4_SUPPRESS. CARD_PAN shall render as **** **** **** ****; no positional digits are retained in any environment other than production core.
2.4 The columns MEMBER_NAME_FIRST, MEMBER_NAME_LAST, MEMBER_NAME_MIDDLE_INIT, ADDR_LINE_1, ADDR_LINE_2, CITY_TXT, STATE_CD, POSTAL_CD, PHONE_HOME, PHONE_MOBILE and EMAIL_ADDR shall be transformed by method NULL_OUT unless the consuming report is listed on the Exception Schedule held by the Records Custodian.
2.5 MEMBER_NBR shall be substituted by SURROGATE_MBR_KEY. The natural key shall not appear in any extract file leaving the Information Services perimeter.

3.  Testing and evidence
3.1 The amendment shall be exercised in MDW_TST_01 and the row counts recorded at Section D shall be reconciled to the source cycle control totals before promotion.
3.2 Any variance exceeding 0.10 per cent of the source count shall be referred to the Data Steward and shall suspend promotion.

4.  Retention and review
4.1 This record is retained for six years from the date of promotion.
4.2 The rule set is reviewed annually, and on any change to the core extract layout.
