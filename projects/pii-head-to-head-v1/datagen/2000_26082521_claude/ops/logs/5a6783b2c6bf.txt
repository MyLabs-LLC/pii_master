  1. SCOPE AND APPLICATION
     1.1 This ruleset shall govern, exclusively and without exception, the transformation of records originating in the store-level dispensing platform (source system code PDX) and the central membership registry (source system code MBR) into the enterprise analytic schema rx_analytics.*, as that schema stands constituted on the effective date first written above.
     1.2 Nothing herein shall be construed to authorize the persistence, in any target column, of any source value classified at sensitivity tier T1 (Directly Identifying) or tier T2 (Indirectly Identifying), save as expressly permitted under clause 4.3.
     1.3 Where this ruleset and the masking configuration referenced at clause 4.1 are in conflict, the masking configuration shall control, and the conflicting rule shall be quarantined at parse time.

  2. DEFINITIONS
     2.1 "Disposition" means the mandatory treatment assigned to a mapped field, being one of: PASS, TOKENIZE, MASK, GENERALIZE, SUPPRESS, or DROP.
     2.2 "Emitted form" means the literal representation written to the target column after the disposition has been applied.
     2.3 "Vault reference" means the identifier of the keyring under which a surrogate is minted; no vault reference shall of itself be deemed an identifier of a natural person.
