1. A caller cannot provide the verifiers on file but insists
   the balance is wrong and becomes agitated.
                                PROCEED / STOP / ESCALATE

2. An itemized statement must go to a payer. The fax number
   was confirmed against last year's directory.
                                PROCEED / STOP / ESCALATE

3. A colleague in another department asks for a screenshot
   of an account to "settle a question."
                                PROCEED / STOP / ESCALATE

4. A worklist export is needed for a productivity report.
   The export includes Member ID and Diagnosis Code columns.
                                PROCEED / STOP / ESCALATE

5. A patient is standing at the window and asks to see their
   own itemized bill. Identity verifiers match.
                                PROCEED / STOP / ESCALATE

   audit and requests a batch of claim images by end of day.
                                PROCEED / STOP / ESCALATE

7. A record carries a Legal Hold Flag. A correction is
   requested to a transposed figure.
                                PROCEED / STOP / ESCALATE

8. A printed remittance advice is found on the break room
   table, origin unknown.
                                PROCEED / STOP / ESCALATE
