PRIVACY AND SAFEGUARDS REFRESHER — SLIDE SCRIPT
Presenter: Compliance Officer or designee
Runtime: 25 minutes | Deck revision: Spring 2004

=== SLIDE 3: WHAT WE PROTECT ===
Read aloud:
• 1. Restricted information falls into four groups. Group one is identity: Full Legal Name, Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Driver's License Number, Residential Address.
• 2. Group two is access: Member Number, Account Number, Card Number, PIN Offset, Audio Response Access Code.
• 3. Group three is financial condition: Credit Score, Loan Application Status, Balance, Transaction History, Delinquency Status.
• 4. Group four is institutional: report filings, screening results, audit workpapers, system credentials.

Presenter note: hold up the blank application. The printed field names are public. The moment ink is in them, everything on Slide 3 applies.

=== SLIDE 4: WHERE IT LEAKS ===
Read aloud:
• 5. The fax cover sheet sent to the wrong stored number.
• 6. The printout left in the shared printer overnight.
• 7. The screen left visible to the lobby line.
• 8. The balance confirmed to a caller who was never authenticated.
• 9. The file copied to a diskette and carried home to finish over the weekend.

=== SLIDE 5: WHAT HAPPENS ===
Read aloud:
• 10. To the member: unauthorized transfers, card reissue, months of correspondence.
• 11. To the employee: written warning, credential suspension, termination, and in willful cases referral for prosecution.
• 12. To the credit union: examiner findings, penalties under Regulation P, and the cost of notifying every affected household.

=== SLIDE 6: THREE SCENARIOS — TABLE DISCUSSION ===
Read aloud, allow two minutes each:
• 13. A caller identifies themselves as an accountant preparing a tax return and asks for the year-to-date dividend total on a member's certificate. They cannot supply the Member Number. What do you do?
• 14. A teller notices a coworker viewing the account history of a household that is the subject of local gossip. No transaction is posted. Is anything wrong, and what is owed to whom?
• 15. A loan officer needs to send a signed application containing the Social Security Number field to the indirect dealer who originated it. The dealer asks for it by return fax to a number written on a business card. What are the two required steps before any transmission?

=== SLIDE 7: CLOSE ===
• 16. Verify before you disclose.
• 17. Secure before you step away.
• 18. Report the same day you discover.

Presenter note: collect the signed acknowledgment sheets before dismissing the room. Forward to the Human Resources Administrator.
