SLIDE SCRIPT — RESTRICTED INFORMATION AWARENESS
BILLING OFFICE / REVENUE CYCLE
RUNTIME: 22 MINUTES
PRESENTER: PRIVACY OFFICER OR DESIGNEE

=====================================================
SLIDE 1 — TITLE
=====================================================
ON SCREEN: "What You May Not Say, Send, or Store"
SAY: Sign the roster before you sit. Attendance is auditable.

=====================================================
SLIDE 2 — WHY THIS MODULE EXISTS
=====================================================
ON SCREEN: Three bullets.
SAY: Last cycle produced incidents in three recurring shapes — misdirected fax, unattended printout, and disclosure to an unverified caller. All three are preventable at the desk. None required a sophisticated attacker.

=====================================================
SLIDE 3 — THE FOUR BUCKETS
=====================================================
ON SCREEN: Table, two columns.

BUCKET                  | FIELDS THAT LIVE THERE
------------------------|--------------------------------
Identity                | Patient Name; Date of Birth;
                        | Social Security Number; Address
Health                  | Medical Record Number; Diagnosis
                        | Code; Procedure Code; Date of
                        | Service; Attending Provider
Money                   | Guarantor Account Number; Primary
                        | Account Number; Routing Number;
                        | Payment Plan ID
Institutional           | Payer Contract Rate; Employee ID;
                        | Audit Finding; Legal Hold Flag

SAY: If a field appears in this table, it does not leave the system of record without a documented business reason and an approved channel.

=====================================================
SLIDE 4 — THE THREE QUESTIONS
=====================================================
ON SCREEN: 1. Do I need it? 2. Is the recipient verified? 3. Is the channel approved?
SAY: Any "no" stops the action. Escalate to the Revenue Cycle Supervisor. There is no penalty for stopping. There is a penalty for guessing.

=====================================================
SLIDE 5 — CONSEQUENCE LADDER
=====================================================
ON SCREEN: Ascending steps.
SAY, reading upward: Coaching note. Written warning. Suspension without pay. Termination. Referral to law enforcement. Personal criminal liability attaches where disclosure is knowing and for gain. The organization cannot indemnify you against that.
SAY: Separately — notification letters, regulator reporting, penalty assessment per violation, and a multi-year corrective action plan. Departmental budget absorbs none of that quietly.

=====================================================
SLIDE 6 — SCENARIO DRILL (INTERACTIVE)
=====================================================
PRESENTER: Read each aloud. Poll the room. Do not advance until a rationale is stated.

DRILL 1. A caller identifies as an attorney representing a patient and demands the itemized bill immediately, citing a filing deadline.

DRILL 2. A biller's screen is visible from a public corridor. A visitor pauses and reads it.

DRILL 3. An unencrypted spreadsheet of aged receivables is attached to a message addressed to a vendor. The message has not yet been sent.

DRILL 4. A staff member's own family member is admitted. The staff member has system access to that account.

DRILL 5. A fax confirmation shows the transmission completed to a number one digit different from the intended destination.

DRILL 6. A departing employee asks to copy their work-in-progress folder to personal storage "to finish loose ends."

=====================================================
SLIDE 7 — REPORTING
=====================================================
ON SCREEN: One hour. One number. No exceptions.
SAY: Report to the Privacy Officer within one hour of discovery. Reporting does not require certainty. It requires suspicion.

=====================================================
SLIDE 8 — CLOSE
=====================================================
SAY: Attestation is due in fourteen days. Unfinished attestations are reported to the department director on day fifteen.

END SCRIPT.
