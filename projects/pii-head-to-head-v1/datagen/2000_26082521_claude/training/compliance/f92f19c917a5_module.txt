ITEM 1
An intake clerk in the immunization program receives an internal spreadsheet
carrying the columns Patient Name, Date of Birth, and Immunization Lot No.
The clerk's assigned duties cover lot reconciliation only.
  A. Open the file and delete the columns not required.
  B. Open the file, use the lot column, disregard the others.
  C. Return the file unopened and request a lot-only extract.
  D. Forward the file to the division supervisor for a decision.

ITEM 2
Two staff members discuss a pending case investigation while riding the
public elevator in the administrative building. No name is spoken, but the
Reportable Condition and the Exposure Site are stated aloud.
  A. Permissible, because no name was spoken.
  B. Not permissible; the condition and site are Tier 3 identifiers.
  C. Permissible if no member of the public is visibly present.
  D. Permissible during a declared outbreak response.

ITEM 3
A clinic supervisor is asked by an outside laboratory to send a specimen
listing by unencrypted attachment because the laboratory's secure portal is
unavailable that afternoon.
  A. Send it; the laboratory is a known business associate.
  B. Send it with the Specimen No. column removed.
  C. Send it and note the exception in the access log.
  D. Decline and hold the listing until the portal is restored.

ITEM 4
A workforce member discovers that a vital records printout containing
Certificate No. and Informant Name was left overnight in an unsecured
common-area printer tray.
  A. Report to the Privacy Officer the same business day.
  B. Shred the printout and take no further action.
  C. Report at the next scheduled division meeting.
  D. Return it to the Records Custodian without a report.

ITEM 5
An epidemiologist copies a Tier 1 extract to a personal flash drive to
continue analysis at home over the weekend.
  A. Permitted if the drive is kept in the employee's possession.
  B. Permitted if the extract is deleted on Monday.
  C. Not permitted; removable media must be Department-issued and encrypted.
  D. Permitted with verbal approval from the division supervisor.

ITEM 6
A supervisor receives the annual access recertification list and returns it
unchanged for a division in which two positions were reclassified.
  A. Acceptable; reclassification does not affect access.
  B. Not acceptable; each role must be reviewed against current duties.
  C. Acceptable if no separations occurred during the year.
  D. Not acceptable, but correction may wait until the next cycle.
