SLIDE SCRIPT — CT-114
Presenter notes for Compliance Coordinator. Read aloud text in quotes. Timing 45 minutes.

SLIDE 1 — TITLE
    "Information Classification and Handling. Annual refresher. This is required training; the roster will circulate at the end."
    Do not begin until the room is settled. Confirm no vendors present.

SLIDE 2 — WHY THIS MATTERS
    "Three regulators can examine how we handle data: the state Department of Insurance, the FTC under the Safeguards Rule, and HHS for our health lines."
    "Penalties are assessed per record, not per incident. A single misdirected file can carry six-figure exposure."
    Pause. Do not cite specific carrier incidents.

SLIDE 3 — THE FOUR TIERS
    "Public. Internal. Confidential. Restricted."
    "Everything on your desk today is at least Internal. Most of it is Restricted."
    Ask the room: name one Public item. Expected answer: the published rate filing summary.

SLIDE 4 — RESTRICTED FIELD LABELS
    Read the label list; do not read example values.
    "Social Security Number. Date of Birth. Driver's License Number. Member ID. Group Number. Diagnosis Code. Bank Routing Number. Account Number. Payment Card Number."
    "If a field carries one of these labels, the record is Restricted. There is no judgment call."

SLIDE 5 — THE THREE FAILURES
    "Nearly every incident we see is one of three things: sent to the wrong recipient, saved to the wrong place, or looked at without a reason."
    "The third one is the hardest for people to accept. Curiosity is misuse."

SLIDE 6 — CONSEQUENCES
    "For the company: fines, notification cost, examination, lost agents."
    "For you: corrective action, license referral, and in health lines, criminal exposure."
    Do not soften this slide.

SLIDE 7 — REPORTING
    "One business hour. You are not asked to investigate. You are asked to report."
    "There is no retaliation. There is a penalty for silence."

SLIDE 8 — SCENARIO WALKTHROUGH
    Distribute the handout quiz. Work Scenario 1 aloud; assign 2 through 4 to table groups.
    Allow eight minutes. Collect nothing.

SLIDE 9 — CLOSE
    "Questions go to the Privacy Office. Sign the roster on your way out."
    Return the roster to the Records Custodian same day.
