MEMORANDUM

TO:      Training Facilitators
FROM:    Compliance Department
DATE:    March 8, 2021
RE:      Part 2 Slide Script — Why the Rules Exist (approx. 8 minutes)

SLIDE 1 — TITLE
On screen: "Restricted Information: What We Hold and Why It Matters"
Say: "Every file we open collects more sensitive information about a household than almost any other business they will deal with this year. That is the reason for the rules we are about to review."

SLIDE 2 — WHAT WE COLLECT
On screen: bulleted field labels — Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Deposit Account Number, Bank Routing Number, Driver's License Number, Payoff Statement Balance.
Say: "Read that list. Any one of these is enough to open credit in someone else's name. We collect all six in a routine purchase file."

SLIDE 3 — WHO WANTS IT
On screen: three icons — external fraud, insider misuse, accidental disclosure.
Say: "Most losses in our industry are not sophisticated intrusions. They are a wire redirected after an email thread was read, or a file emailed to the wrong recipient because autocomplete filled the address."

SLIDE 4 — THE RULE IN ONE LINE
On screen: "Right person, right channel, right amount."
Say: "Before you send anything, ask three questions. Is this person entitled to it? Is this channel approved for that tier? Am I sending only the fields they actually need?"

SLIDE 5 — HANDOFF
Say: "Part 3 covers what happens when the rule is broken. Part 4 is the scenario quiz. Both are required for credit."
