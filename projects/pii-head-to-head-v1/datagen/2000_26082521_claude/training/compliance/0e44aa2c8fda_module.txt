PRIVACY AND DATA PROTECTION REFRESHER — FACILITATOR SLIDE SCRIPT
Deck Reference: PDP-2021-Q3 | Runtime: 12 minutes | Section 1 of 4

SLIDE 1 — TITLE
 ON SCREEN: "Four Categories. One Standard."
 SCRIPT: 1.1 Ask the room to keep the handout closed until Slide 5.

SLIDE 2 — WHAT IS RESTRICTED
 ON SCREEN: Category table headers — Category | Example Field Labels | Storage Rule
 SCRIPT: 2.1 Read the four category names aloud: Health Information, Payment Card Data, Government Identifiers, Colleague Records.
 2.2 State that the field labels on screen — Patient Name, Date of Birth, Prescription Number, Primary Account Number, Social Security Number, Employee Identification Number — are what the team will see daily, and that seeing a field is not authorization to use it.

SLIDE 3 — THE RULE
 ON SCREEN: "Minimum necessary. Every time."
 SCRIPT: 3.1 Emphasize that curiosity is not a permitted purpose.
 3.2 Note the shred bin location and that label waste never enters the recycling stream.

SLIDE 4 — WHAT IT COSTS
 ON SCREEN: Termination | Civil penalty tiers | Criminal referral | License action
 SCRIPT: 4.1 State plainly that a single willful lookup has ended employment in this chain and has cost individuals their license.

SLIDE 5 — SCENARIO CHECK
 SCRIPT: 5.1 Read aloud, take a show of hands, do not debate:
 (i) A caller asks a technician to confirm whether a household member picked up a controlled substance yesterday.
 (ii) An associate leaves a will-call bag on the counter, label facing outward, while assisting the next customer.
 (iii) A colleague asks for a login because their own account is locked and the queue is backing up.
 5.2 Correct response for all three: decline, escalate, document.
