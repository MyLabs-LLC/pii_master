MEMORANDUM

TO: All Staff
FROM: Privacy Officer / Records Custodian
SUBJECT: Restricted Information Categories — Quick Reference Card
DISTRIBUTION: Post at each workstation and at the front counter

WHAT IS RESTRICTED

Protected Health Information — clinic charts, immunization histories, family planning records, WIC certification files. Restricted fields: Client Name, Date of Birth, Address, Telephone, Social Security Number, Medical Record Number, Diagnosis, Service Date.

Vital Records — birth and death registrations and the indexes that support them. Restricted fields: Registrant Name, Certificate Number, Date and Place of Event, Parent Names, Informant Name.

Case and Contact Data — disease reports, investigation notes, contact tracing worksheets. Restricted fields: Case Name, Contact Name, Onset Date, Exposure Location, Provider Name.

Personnel and Procurement — employee files, applicant packets, payroll, bid submissions before opening. Restricted fields: Employee Name, Employee Number, Home Address, Compensation Rate, Bank Account Number.

Not restricted: aggregate counts published in the annual report, public health advisories, inspection scores posted to the Department website, and the staff directory of office numbers.

WHAT HAPPENS WHEN IT IS MISHANDLED

Discipline is progressive but not slow. A first substantiated finding of unauthorized access ordinarily results in suspension. A finding of disclosure for personal gain results in dismissal and referral for prosecution. Federal penalties are assessed against the Department per violation and increase sharply where the conduct is found to be willful neglect left uncorrected. Where a breach exceeds the statutory threshold, the Department must notify affected persons individually, notify the Secretary, and issue a media notice.

Access logs are reviewed monthly. Every record opened is attributed to a user account.

SCENARIO REVIEW — mark T or F

___ 1. A staff member looks up a record to confirm an address before mailing a program notice. This is permitted because the mailing is program-related.

___ 2. A shredder is full, so a stack of intake forms is placed on top of it to be shredded in the morning. This is acceptable because the forms remain inside the office.

___ 3. A colleague from another division asks for a case count by census tract for a grant application. Providing the count is permitted.

___ 4. A caller identifying themselves as a physician's office requests a patient's immunization dates. The request may be filled after verifying the caller through the number on file for that practice.

___ 5. Fax cover sheets bearing a confidentiality notice make it acceptable to fax a chart to a number entered from memory.

___ 6. A record is left open on screen while a staff member steps away for five minutes. No one else is in the room, so no violation occurred.

Supervisors review answers at the next unit meeting. Direct questions to the Privacy Officer.
