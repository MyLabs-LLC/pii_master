SLIDE 2.1
VISUAL: Title only — "What Is Restricted?"
NARRATION: Restriction is determined by the category of the record, not by how sensitive it feels to the person holding it. A class roster is a restricted education record. A hallway conversation about a roster is a disclosure.
PRESENTER NOTE: Do not solicit examples from the floor at this point.

SLIDE 2.2
VISUAL: Six-row list — Education Records; Special Education and 504; Student Health; Employee Personnel; Directory Information; Food Service and Financial
NARRATION: Read each row. State the custodian for each. Emphasize that Directory Information is the only category with a default of release, and that the default is defeated by a written objection on file in the building office.
PRESENTER NOTE: Hold up the annual objection list binder.

SLIDE 2.3
VISUAL: Diagram — folder, cabinet, sign-out log
NARRATION: The handling standard has three parts: storage, access, and transfer. Storage means a locked cabinet or a password-protected directory. Access means a documented instructional or administrative need. Transfer means the sign-out log for paper and the authorization form for electronic extracts.
PRESENTER NOTE: Distribute Form HR-114 now if not already distributed.

SLIDE 2.4
VISUAL: Four-step ladder — Counseling; Written Reprimand; Suspension; Dismissal
NARRATION: Consequences are progressive but not automatic in sequence. A single disclosure involving a health or special education record may begin at the third step. Prompt self-reporting to the Records Custodian is a mitigating factor and is expected within one working day.
PRESENTER NOTE: Anticipated question — whether an accidental disclosure is treated as a violation. Answer: yes, the category and volume govern, not the intent.

SLIDE 2.5
VISUAL: "Questions to the Records Custodian"
NARRATION: Any request for records originating outside the district is routed to the building office without response. This includes requests from a parent claiming a right of access, from a law enforcement officer, and from another school district. Staff do not evaluate those claims.
