CLASS DEFINITIONS
  RED   Release outside the licensed area requires written authorization
        on file or standing approval of the Pharmacist-in-Charge.
  AMBER Access limited to workforce members with an assigned duty. No
        release outside the chain without the Privacy Official.
  GREEN Not used in this module. All Module 4 categories are restricted.

MINIMUM NECESSARY RULE
  Access only the fields required to complete the task at hand. Opening a
  profile for curiosity, for a relative, or for a co-worker is a Tier III
  occurrence regardless of whether information leaves the store.

COUNTER VERIFICATION SEQUENCE (ALL PICKUPS)
  1. Confirm two identifiers stated by the requester against the profile.
     Do not read identifiers aloud to prompt the requester.
  2. Confirm the will-call bag matches the vial label before handoff.
  3. Where the requester is not the patient, confirm the agent designation
     is recorded. If absent, refer to the Pharmacist-in-Charge.
  4. Record the signature log entry. Do not permit the requester to view
     prior entries on the log page; use the privacy overlay.

DISPOSAL
  All R-01 through R-05 output, including misprinted labels, register tape,
  fax cover sheets, and adjudication rejects, goes to the locked shred bin.
  General trash and cardboard bale are prohibited destinations. The bin key
  is held by the Records Custodian and the Pharmacist-in-Charge only.
