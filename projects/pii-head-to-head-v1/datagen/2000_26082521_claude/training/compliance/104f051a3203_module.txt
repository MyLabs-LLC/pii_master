MEMORANDUM

TO: All Escrow, Title, and Closing Staff
FROM: Privacy Officer
RE: Completion of the annual safeguards training and desk audit

The annual privacy and safeguards training has now been delivered at every branch. This memorandum records what is expected of each of you between now and the next cycle, and supplements rather than replaces the handout and quick reference distributed at the session.

Each staff member is responsible for knowing which fields on our standard forms carry restricted status. Restricted fields include, at minimum, the Social Security Number and Taxpayer Identification Number, Date of Birth, Driver's License Number, Bank Routing Number and Account Number, Escrow Trust Account Number, Loan Number, Payoff Account Number, Credit Score, and any wire beneficiary detail. Party names, telephone numbers, electronic mail addresses, mailing and forwarding addresses, employer names, and closing figures are confidential and are released only to a party to the transaction or an agent that party has authorized in writing. Recorded instruments, legal descriptions, parcel identification numbers, and the public index remain quotable.

Three practices require immediate attention based on what the desk audits found. First, several workstations were logged in and unattended during the lunch hour. Lock the workstation whenever you leave it, without exception. Second, paper containing restricted fields was found in ordinary wastebaskets at more than one branch. The locked shred consoles are the only permitted disposal route, and additional consoles have been ordered where the walk is inconvenient. Third, temporary coverage during absences has in some cases been handled by sharing a password. That practice ends now. Branch managers will request temporary rights from the systems administrator, who will grant them for a defined period and remove them on the return date.

Wire instruction verification remains the single highest-risk procedure in this office. Any wire instruction that arrives by facsimile or electronic mail, and any change to instructions already in the file, is verified by an outbound telephone call to a number taken from our own approved directory or from the original engagement record. A number printed on the incoming document is never used for verification. The verification, including who was reached and at what number, is noted in the file before funds are released.

Incidents are reported to the branch manager and to this office on the same business day they are discovered. Notification decisions concerning the underwriter, the lender, the affected parties, and the state insurance department are made by this office and not at the branch level. No employee has ever been disciplined here for a good-faith report that proved to be nothing; concealment is a separate and more serious matter.

Please sign the training log at your branch if you have not already done so. Branch managers should return completed logs by the end of the month.
