RETAIL PHARMACY GROUP — COMPLIANCE TRAINING HANDOUT
Form CT-114 (Rev. 2), effective 01 April 2021
Subject: Restricted Information — Categories, Handling Duties, and Sanctions

SECTION A — MODULE CONTROL
A.1 Module Code: CT-114
A.2 Delivery Format: Instructor-led, store back office
A.3 Duration: 45 minutes
A.4 Required Audience: Pharmacists, Pharmacy Technicians, Pharmacy Service Associates, Front Store Supervisors
A.5 Recertification Interval: 12 months
A.6 Content Owner: Office of the Privacy Officer
A.7 Training Records Retained By: the Records Custodian

SECTION B — RESTRICTED INFORMATION CATEGORIES
B.1 Category I — Protected Health Information. Any field linking a person to care received, including: Patient Name; Date of Birth; Member Identifier; Prescription Number; Drug Name and Strength; Prescriber Name; Diagnosis Code; Refill History; Immunization Record.
B.2 Category II — Payment Card Data. Including: Cardholder Name; Primary Account Number; Expiration Date; Card Verification Value; Magnetic Stripe Track Data.
B.3 Category III — Government Identifiers. Including: Social Security Number; Driver's License Number; Medicare or Medicaid Identifier; Passport Number.
B.4 Category IV — Colleague Records. Including: Employee Identification Number; Pay Rate; Benefits Election; Disciplinary History; Home Address; Personal Telephone Number.
B.5 Category V — Company Confidential. Including negotiated reimbursement rates, store dispensing volumes, unreleased pricing, camera placement, safe combinations, and controlled substance inventory counts.
B.6 Category VI — Records Restricted by Special Rule. Substance use disorder treatment records, HIV and reproductive health records, mental health records, and minors' records where state law imposes protection exceeding the federal standard.

SECTION C — HANDLING DUTIES
C.1 Access and disclose the minimum necessary to complete the task at hand.
C.2 Conduct counseling at the consultation window; do not read Drug Name aloud at the register queue.
C.3 Deposit all label stock, rejected labels, and printed profiles in the secure shred receptacle. General trash and recycling are prohibited.
C.4 Do not photograph screens, labels, or documents with a personal device.
C.5 Do not access your own record, or the record of a relative, colleague, or public figure, for any reason.
C.6 Lock the workstation when stepping away. Credentials are never shared or borrowed.
C.7 Do not transmit restricted information to personal email or messaging accounts.
C.8 Confirm the destination number before every fax transmission and use the approved cover sheet.
C.9 Report any suspected impermissible disclosure to the Pharmacy Manager and the Privacy Office within one hour of discovery.

SECTION D — CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING
D.1 Corrective action up to and including termination, available at first offense where the conduct is willful.
D.2 Civil monetary penalties assessed against the company on a per-violation, tiered basis.
D.3 Criminal referral where information is obtained or disclosed knowingly, with enhanced exposure where disclosure is for personal gain.
D.4 Disciplinary action against the individual license by the state board of pharmacy.
D.5 Mandatory notification to affected persons, to the regulator, and to media where an incident affects more than 500 residents of a state.
D.6 Card brand fines and loss of processing privileges for Category II incidents.

SECTION E — SCENARIO QUIZ (circle one response per item)
E.1 A caller identifies as the spouse of a person whose prescription is ready and asks which medication it is and what condition it treats. No authorization is on file.
 (a) State the drug name only (b) State the therapeutic class only (c) Decline and describe the pickup authorization process
E.2 A technician recognizes a customer as a neighbor and, after the sale is complete, reopens the profile to see whether an antibiotic was dispensed.
 (a) Permitted, as no disclosure occurred (b) Impermissible access, reportable (c) Permitted with manager approval
E.3 The store fax is out of service, and a colleague proposes photographing the screen and texting the image to the prescriber's office.
 (a) Acceptable if the image is deleted after (b) Acceptable for urgent therapy only (c) Prohibited; use the alternate approved transmission route
E.4 A card is declined at the front register and the associate begins writing the account number on a notepad to re-key later.
 (a) Permitted if the note is discarded (b) Prohibited; the number may not be recorded outside the terminal (c) Permitted with supervisor initials
E.5 A courier collects a delivery tote in which bags for other households are visible, each bearing a full label.
 (a) Acceptable, as the courier is contracted (b) Cover or seal the tote before release (c) Acceptable if the courier signs the manifest
