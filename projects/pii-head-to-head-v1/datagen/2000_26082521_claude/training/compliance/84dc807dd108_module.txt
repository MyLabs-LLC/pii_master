SCENARIO EXERCISE — SESSION 4
Complete individually. Ten minutes. Sheets are returned to Compliance Administration.

SCENARIO ONE. An inbound caller states that they are the processor at the lender's regional office, gives the correct property address and the correct closing date, and asks the escrow officer to read back the Social Security Number and Date of Birth shown on the loan application so the caller can correct a keying error at their end. The caller declines to be called back, citing a full voicemail box. What is the correct handling, and which category governs?

SCENARIO TWO. A real estate licensee representing the seller requests a copy of the buyer's loan approval correspondence, explaining that the seller is entitled to confirm that the buyer can perform. The licensee is a party's agent of record but not a party. Identify which fields, if any, may be released, and identify who must authorize the release.

SCENARIO THREE. A funding clerk transmits a payoff demand by facsimile. The receiving number is keyed from a handwritten note rather than from the file. No confirmation page is retained. Two days later the intended recipient reports non-receipt. State the immediate reporting obligation, the position to whom the report is made, and the record that should have existed.

SCENARIO FOUR. At the close of a busy afternoon, working copies of a settlement statement and a credit report summary are left in the copier output tray overnight. The office is locked and alarmed; the cleaning service holds a key. State whether an incident report is required and on what basis, without regard to whether the documents were in fact seen.

SCENARIO FIVE. A branch receives a subpoena directed to the escrow file. The requesting attorney's clerk telephones ahead and offers to collect the file that afternoon to save postage. State the correct disposition of both the subpoena and the offer.

SCENARIO SIX. An employee wishes to work over the weekend and asks to take three closed files home, undertaking to return them Monday morning. The files are closed, the transactions have funded, and the policies have issued. State whether closure of the transaction alters the restriction, and give the rule.

SCENARIO SEVEN. A former employee of the branch telephones a current employee, states that they are now employed by a mortgage broker, and asks for the depository account number used for a prior transaction on which they personally worked. State the handling and the reporting obligation, and state whether prior involvement in the transaction has any bearing.

Answer key is retained by the instructor. Discussion follows collection of the sheets.
