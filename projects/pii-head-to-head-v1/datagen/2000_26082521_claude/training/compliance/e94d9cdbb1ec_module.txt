SCENARIO EXAMINATION — INFORMATION HANDLING
Form IS-40 (Rev. 2004)
Administered: annually and upon hire
Passing score: 8 of 10
Failure: retake within 30 days; second failure referred to Human Resources

1. INSTRUCTIONS
1.1 Closed book. Twenty minutes. Mark one response per item.
1.2 Do not write member information of any kind on this form.

2. REFERENCE — RESTRICTED CATEGORIES
2.1 Identifiers: Social Security Number, Member Number, Account Number, Card Number, Personal Identification Number, Driver's License Number.
2.2 Authenticators: Mother's Maiden Name, Date of Birth, Home Banking Password.
2.3 Financial condition: Current Balance, Transaction History, Gross Monthly Income, Credit Score, Delinquency Status, Charge-Off Amount.
2.4 Relationship: the fact of membership, Joint Owner Name, Beneficiary Designation.
2.5 Statutory: Suspicious Activity Report and its existence.

3. REFERENCE — SANCTIONS
3.1 First unauthorized access: termination.
3.2 Negligent disclosure: written warning to suspension without pay.
3.3 Disclosure of a Suspicious Activity Report or its existence: termination and referral for federal prosecution.
3.4 All incidents reported to the fidelity bond carrier and to the Supervisory Committee.

4. SCENARIOS
4.1 A caller correctly recites an account number and asks for the current balance but cannot supply a second authenticator. (A) Provide the balance. (B) Decline. (C) Provide the available balance only.
4.2 A person appears at the counter with a court order directing disclosure of account records. (A) Produce the records. (B) Accept the document and route it to the Compliance Department without disclosure. (C) Telephone the issuing court and then produce the records.
4.3 A sworn law enforcement officer asks whether a named individual holds an account and whether a report was filed. (A) Answer both. (B) Answer neither; route the request to the Security Officer. (C) Answer the first only.
4.4 A staff member wishes to verify that her own paycheck deposited. (A) Look it up on the teller system. (B) Use the member-facing audio response unit or a branch as a member. (C) Ask a co-worker to look it up.
4.5 A vendor representative on site for hardware maintenance asks for a live report to test printing. (A) Provide one. (B) Provide a report with restricted columns suppressed by the Data Processing Manager. (C) Provide one and collect it afterward.
4.6 A member's account statement is returned by the post office and opened at the branch to identify the addressee. (A) Discard the envelope and contents in the wastebasket. (B) Log the return, secure the contents, and refer to the Records Custodian. (C) Retain it at the teller window pending the member's next visit.
