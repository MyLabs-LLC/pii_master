FORM HR-114 — STAFF TRAINING HANDOUT
CONFIDENTIALITY OF STUDENT AND EMPLOYEE RECORDS
ISSUING OFFICE: Records Management Unit, Office of the Superintendent
EFFECTIVE: School Year 1998-99
DISTRIBUTION: All certificated staff, classified staff, substitutes, and volunteers holding building keys
RETENTION OF SIGNED COPY: Personnel file, 7 years

SECTION A — PURPOSE
This handout states the categories of information that are restricted from disclosure, the required handling standard for each, and the disciplinary consequences of mishandling. Completion of the attached acknowledgment is a condition of continued building access. Training is delivered annually by the Records Custodian or a designee prior to the first instructional day.

SECTION B — RESTRICTED CATEGORIES AND FIELDS

CAT. I — EDUCATION RECORDS (CONFIDENTIAL)
  Fields: Student Name; Student Identification Number; Grade Level; Homeroom Assignment; Attendance Record; Report Card Marks; Standardized Test Scores; Disciplinary Referral Type
  Custodian: Building Principal
  Handling: Locked file cabinet; sign-out log required for removal from the building

CAT. II — SPECIAL EDUCATION AND SECTION 504
  Fields: Eligibility Category; Evaluation Report; IEP Goals and Objectives; Related Services Log; Placement Decision; Parent/Guardian Consent Status
  Custodian: Director of Pupil Services
  Handling: Access restricted to staff with documented instructional need; no photocopying without written authorization

CAT. III — STUDENT HEALTH
  Fields: Immunization Record; Medication Administration Log; Nurse Visit Note; Allergy or Condition Code; Physician Order; Emergency Contact Designation
  Custodian: District Health Services Coordinator
  Handling: Stored separately from the cumulative folder

CAT. IV — EMPLOYEE PERSONNEL
  Fields: Social Security Number; Home Address; Home Telephone; Certification Status; Salary Placement; Evaluation Rating; Leave Balance; Grievance File
  Custodian: Director of Personnel
  Handling: Central office only; no building-level duplicates

CAT. V — DIRECTORY INFORMATION (RELEASABLE ABSENT WRITTEN OBJECTION)
  Fields: Student Name; Participation in Officially Recognized Activities; Height and Weight of Athletic Team Members; Dates of Attendance; Awards Received; Most Recent School Attended
  Custodian: Building Principal
  Handling: Verify the annual objection list before any release

CAT. VI — FOOD SERVICE AND FINANCIAL
  Fields: Free and Reduced Meal Eligibility Status; Household Income Range; Cafeteria Account Balance
  Custodian: Food Service Supervisor
  Handling: Eligibility status may not be disclosed to instructional staff except where required for program administration

SECTION C — PROHIBITED PRACTICES
  1. Discussing any record listed in Categories I through IV in a corridor, workroom, cafeteria, or other area accessible to the public.
  2. Leaving a terminal logged in to the student information system while unattended.
  3. Transmitting a Category II, III, or IV record by facsimile without prior telephone confirmation that the receiving machine is attended.
  4. Removing diskettes containing extracted record data from the building.
  5. Sharing a network user identifier or password with any other person, including a supervisor.
  6. Disposing of printed records in a classroom or office wastebasket rather than the designated shredding receptacle.

SECTION D — CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING
  Level 1 — Verbal counseling by the immediate supervisor; retraining within 10 working days.
  Level 2 — Written reprimand placed in the personnel file.
  Level 3 — Suspension without pay; revocation of system access privileges.
  Level 4 — Recommendation for dismissal and, where applicable, referral to the certification authority.
  Level assignment considers the category involved, the number of records affected, and whether the disclosure was reported promptly by the employee.
