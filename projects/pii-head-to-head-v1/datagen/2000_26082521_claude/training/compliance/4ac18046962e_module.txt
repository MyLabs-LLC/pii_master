SLIDE 5 OF 11
  ON SCREEN:   THE CONSEQUENCE IS NOT HYPOTHETICAL
  PRESENTER:   State plainly that a substantiated willful disclosure is a
               discharge offense, that the Company is obligated to notify
               the Department of Insurance in the circumstances prescribed
               by the applicable code, and that the individual employee may
               be named in a civil action notwithstanding the Company's
               own liability. Pause for questions. Do not speculate as to
               the disposition of any pending matter.

SLIDE 6 OF 11
  ON SCREEN:   SCENARIOS -- SHOW OF HANDS
  PRESENTER:   Read each aloud; take the vote; then give the answer.

               (i)   A caller declines to give the authorization code and
                     asks only whether a claim "went through." Is the
                     yes-or-no answer a disclosure?     ANSWER: Yes.
               (ii)  A supervisor at a vendor asks for a spreadsheet of
                     claimants bearing the Diagnosis Code column so that
                     the vendor may "pre-sort" the work. Permissible?
                     ANSWER: No, absent an executed confidentiality
                     agreement and a Compliance Officer approval.
               (iii) A file drawer is left unlocked over a holiday
                     weekend. Reportable?     ANSWER: Yes, to the Records
                     Custodian on the next business day.
