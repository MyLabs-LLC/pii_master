RESTRICTED INFORMATION HANDOUT — PHARMACY AND FRONT STORE
Compliance Training Packet, Section 3
Issue Date: 02/97
Distribution: All licensed and non-licensed pharmacy personnel; front store management

1.0 PURPOSE
1.1 This handout states which categories of information are restricted, who may receive them, and what happens when they are mishandled.
1.2 It applies at all locations, in all departments, and during all hours of operation, including periods when the pharmacy department is closed and the front store remains open.

2.0 CATEGORIES OF RESTRICTED INFORMATION
2.1 Category A — Patient Identifying Information. Fields: Patient Name; Date of Birth; Sex; Street Address; Telephone; Social Security Number; Patient Account Number.
2.2 Category B — Dispensing Information. Fields: Rx Number; Original Fill Date; Refill Date; Drug Name; Strength; Dosage Form; NDC; Quantity Dispensed; Days Supply; Sig; Refills Authorized; Refills Remaining; Dispensing Pharmacist Initials.
2.3 Category C — Prescriber Information. Fields: Prescriber Name; Practice Address; State License Number; DEA Number; Telephone.
2.4 Category D — Third-Party and Payment Information. Fields: Plan Name; BIN; PCN; Cardholder ID; Group Number; Person Code; Relationship Code; Copay; Coordination of Benefits Indicator; Prior Authorization Number.
2.5 Category E — Clinical Notations. Fields: Allergy Code; Adverse Reaction Note; Chronic Condition Indicator; Counseling Notation; Refusal-of-Counseling Signature Line.
2.6 Category F — Controlled Substance Records. Documents: Perpetual Inventory Log; Schedule II Dispensing Register; DEA Form 222 file copies; Biennial Inventory; Theft or Loss Report (Form 106) working papers.
2.7 Category G — Associate Records. Fields: Employee Number; Position; Wage Rate; Social Security Number; Attendance Record; Disciplinary Notice; Leave and Medical Documentation.

3.0 WHO MAY RECEIVE
3.1 The patient, in person, after identity verification at the counter.
3.2 The prescriber or the prescriber's designated staff, for treatment purposes, by return call to the number on file for that practice.
3.3 The third-party processor, limited to the fields required to adjudicate the claim.
3.4 Another licensed pharmacy, for a lawful transfer, pharmacist to pharmacist only.
3.5 A regulatory inspector presenting credentials, with the Pharmacy Manager present.
3.6 No one else. Spouses, adult children, employers, attorneys, insurers other than the plan of record, and news media are refused and referred to the Pharmacy Manager.

4.0 HANDLING RULES
4.1 Hard copy awaiting filing is kept face down behind the counter and is never left on the outcounter or in the drive-thru drawer.
4.2 Will-call bags are stored so that the label is not readable from the customer side.
4.3 All discarded paper bearing any Category A through G field goes into the locked shred container. It does not go into the trash, the recycling bin, or the dumpster.
4.4 Terminals are logged off when unattended. Passwords are not shared, posted, or written near the workstation.
4.5 Profiles are accessed only in the course of filling, verifying, counseling, billing, or transferring a prescription.
4.6 Faxes are sent only to a number confirmed against the file, and only with the cover sheet supplied by the Records Custodian.

5.0 CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING
5.1 First-instance procedural lapses: documented coaching by the Pharmacy Manager and repeat certification.
5.2 Unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or removal of records: termination under Policy 402, without regard to length of service.
5.3 Referral to the state Board of Pharmacy, which may act against an individual license, intern registration, or technician registration.
5.4 Referral to law enforcement where controlled substance records are involved.
5.5 Civil exposure for the individual and for the company.

6.0 SCENARIO QUIZ
6.1 A caller identifying as a household member asks the pharmacy to confirm that a particular medication was picked up earlier that day.
6.2 A technician recognizes a name in the queue and opens the profile without a fill in process.
6.3 A third-party rejection slip prints and is left on the outcounter while the technician takes a call.
6.4 An insurance representative not associated with the plan of record requests a printed fill history.
6.5 A front store associate is asked by a vendor representative how many prescriptions the pharmacy filled for a named product line and for whom.
6.6 For each, state the correct action and the policy clause that governs it.
