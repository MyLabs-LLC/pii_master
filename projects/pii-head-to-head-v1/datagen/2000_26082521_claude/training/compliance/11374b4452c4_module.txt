QUICK REFERENCE CARD — RESTRICTED INFORMATION
Compliance Module 4A | 2021 Cycle | Post at every workstation

1. Do not open a record you are not actively working.
2. Do not write, repeat, or photograph any field below.
3. Shred printed matter before the shift ends.

CODE  CATEGORY                    EXAMPLE FIELD LABELS
----  --------------------------  ----------------------------------
P1    Dispensing record           Patient Name, Rx Number, NDC
P2    Insurance eligibility       Member ID, Group Number, BIN
P3    Payment instrument          Cardholder Name, PAN, Expiry
P4    Government identifier       SSN, Driver Licence Number
P5    Colleague file              Employee ID, Wage Rate

4. Consequence: first finding, documented coaching and re-certification; repeat
   finding, final written warning or termination; removal of records from the
   site, termination and regulatory referral.

5. SCENARIO CHECK

   5.1 A caller asks whether a prescription for another household is ready and
       offers a street address as proof. Do you confirm?
   5.2 A colleague on break asks you to look up a member number so they can call
       the payer on a friend's behalf. Do you comply?

6. ANSWERS

   6.1 No. An address offered by a caller is not an authorisation. Verify against
       the pickup authorisation record before naming anything.
   6.2 No. This is P2 access without a business need. Direct the request to the
       payer's own member services line.
