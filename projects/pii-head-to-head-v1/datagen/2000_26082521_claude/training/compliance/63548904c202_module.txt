REGISTRAR STAFF TRAINING — SLIDE SCRIPT
Module 2: Restricted Information and Release Decisions
Presenter: Associate Registrar for Compliance
Runtime: 20 minutes, 9 slides
Revision: Fall 2011

SLIDE 1 — TITLE
On screen: Student Records Confidentiality, Module 2
Say: This module covers what you may say, what you may not say, and what happens if you get it wrong. There is a quiz at the end and your attendance is recorded.

SLIDE 2 — THE DEFAULT RULE
On screen:
   Assume restricted. Release only by exception.
Say: The starting position for every field in the system is that it is restricted. You release only when you can point to the specific exception that permits it.

SLIDE 3 — RELEASABLE FIELDS
On screen:
   Name | Enrollment Status | Dates of Attendance | Major Field of Study | Degrees Awarded | Date of Conferral | Recognized Activities and Sports
Say: These are the designated directory fields. They are releasable only when the record carries no confidentiality hold. Check the hold indicator on the record header before you speak.

SLIDE 4 — RESTRICTED FIELDS
On screen:
   Social Security Number | Student Identification Number | Date of Birth | Race and Ethnicity | Gender | Home Address | Telephone Number | Electronic Mail Address | Grades and Grade Point Average | Class Rank | Course Schedule | Test Scores | Academic Standing | Disciplinary Sanctions | Financial Aid Award | Account Balance | Parent or Guardian Contact | Disability Accommodations | Immunization Records | Emergency Contact
Say: Read that list twice. Most complaints against this office involve the Course Schedule field and the Account Balance field, because staff treat them as harmless logistics. They are not.

SLIDE 5 — WHAT COUNTS AS A DISCLOSURE
On screen:
   Speaking it. Printing it. Faxing it. Leaving it on the screen. Confirming a guess.
Say: Confirming a guess is a disclosure. If a caller recites information and you say "that's correct," you have released it.

SLIDE 6 — CONSEQUENCES
On screen:
   System access suspended | Written reprimand | Termination | Federal complaint | Institutional funding review
Say: Consequences attach to the individual and to the office. There is no informal warning tier for a confirmed release of restricted fields.

SLIDE 7 — SCENARIO QUIZ, PART ONE
On screen:
   1. A caller who states they are a spouse asks for the current Course Schedule so they can plan childcare.
   2. A scholarship donor organization asks for the Grade Point Average of award recipients it funds.
   3. A department chair asks for Dates of Attendance for a student assistant timekeeping audit.
Say: Take thirty seconds. Write release or no release next to each.

SLIDE 8 — SCENARIO QUIZ, PART TWO
On screen:
   4. A staff member looks up the Home Address of a person they met socially, using their own credentials.
   5. A student requests their own Disciplinary Record in writing and presents a photo credential in person.
Say: Item four is the one that ends employment. Access without educational interest is a violation even when nothing is told to anyone.

SLIDE 9 — ANSWERS AND CLOSE
On screen:
   1. No release. 2. No release without signed consent. 3. Release, log the request. 4. Violation. 5. Release under inspection rights, coordinate with the Records Custodian.
Say: Sign the attendance roster on your way out. Questions go to the Records Custodian, not to your neighbor at the next window.
