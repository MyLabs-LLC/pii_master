PRIVACY AND INFORMATION HANDLING HANDOUT
Store Colleague Compliance Programme — Issue 3, Effective 01 March 2021
Retain at the pharmacy counter. Acknowledgement required within 14 days.

1. SCOPE

  1.1 This handout binds every colleague who holds a store system credential,
      including seasonal, agency, and float colleagues.
  1.2 Restricted information is any field listed in clause 2. The classification
      follows the field wherever it is displayed, printed, spoken, or stored.
  1.3 Authorised use means use that is necessary to fill, transfer, bill, or
      counsel on an active order, or to discharge a documented legal duty.

2. CATEGORY REGISTER

  CAT   INFORMATION CLASS              FIELD LABELS DECLARED IN SCOPE
  ====  =============================  ==========================================
  A     Protected health information   Patient Name; Date of Birth; Rx Number;
                                       NDC; Prescriber Name; Diagnosis Code
  B     Payment card data              Cardholder Name; PAN; Expiry Date; CVV
  C     Government identifiers         SSN; Driver Licence Number; Passport
                                       Number
  D     Controlled substance records   DEA Number; PMP Query ID; Schedule;
                                       Quantity Dispensed
  E     Colleague and payroll records  Employee ID; Wage Rate; Leave Reason Code
  F     Commercial and pricing data    Contract Rate; Rebate Tier; Vendor Terms

3. HANDLING DUTIES

  3.1 A field in Category A or D may be viewed only where the colleague is
      actively working the corresponding order. Curiosity is never a basis.
  3.2 A field in Category B is never written, never repeated aloud, and never
      retained on any medium after the transaction settles.
  3.3 A field in Category C is entered directly at the terminal by the person
      presenting the document. No photocopy is retained at store level.
  3.4 Category E enquiries are directed to Human Resources without exception.
  3.5 Category F is internal distribution only and is withheld from any vendor
      representative present in the store.
  3.6 Printed matter bearing any restricted field is deposited in the secure
      destruction bin before the end of the shift on which it was produced.

4. CONSEQUENCE MATRIX

 CLAUSE | INFRACTION CLASS                | FIRST FINDING              | REPEAT FINDING
========|=================================|============================|==========================
 4.1    | Disclosure to non-authorised party | Documented coaching     | Final written warning
 4.2    | Photograph of dispensing label  | Final written warning      | Termination
 4.3    | Access without business need    | Suspension pending review  | Termination and referral
 4.4    | Removal of records from site    | Termination                | Referral to regulator
 4.5    | Sale or transfer of data        | Termination and referral   | Civil and criminal action

  4.6 A finding under clause 4.2 or 4.3 is entered in the colleague file and is
      considered at the next scheduled performance review.
  4.7 The Records Custodian notifies the Privacy Office within one business day
      of any finding at Category A, B, C, or D.

5. SCENARIO ASSESSMENT

  5.1 A colleague working the drive-through recognises a vehicle and, without an
      order in progress, opens the profile to see what was last dispensed. State
      the category and the applicable clause.
  5.2 A person at the counter declines to hand over an identification document
      and asks the colleague to key the number from a photograph on a phone.
      State the correct handling step.
  5.3 A vendor representative restocking a display asks for the acquisition cost
      of a top-selling line. State whether disclosure is permitted.
  5.4 A supervisor is called away mid-transaction and the register remains at a
      screen displaying a cardholder name. State the required immediate action.

6. ANSWER KEY

  6.1 Category A; clause 4.3. Access without a business need. Report to the
      Pharmacy Manager and the Records Custodian.
  6.2 Decline the photograph. Under clause 3.3 the document is presented in
      person and the field is entered at the terminal.
  6.3 No. Clause 3.5 withholds Category F from vendor representatives.
  6.4 Lock the terminal immediately and record the interruption in the shift log.

7. ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

  7.1 Signature of colleague, printed role title, and store number are recorded
      on the counter acknowledgement register maintained by the Shift Supervisor.
