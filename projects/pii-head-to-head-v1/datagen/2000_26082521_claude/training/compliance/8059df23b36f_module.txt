CITY OF WESTBROOK MUNICIPAL UTILITIES DEPARTMENT
INTERNAL MEMORANDUM — INFORMATION HANDLING COMPLIANCE

TO:       All Utility Operations Staff, Customer Service Representatives, Meter Services, Billing
FROM:     Office of the Records Custodian
SUBJECT:  Mandatory Annual Refresher — Restricted Information Categories
COURSE:   IHC-101 (2016 cycle)
DURATION: 45 minutes, instructor-led

1. PURPOSE

This handout accompanies the 2016 information handling refresher. Attendance is mandatory for all staff with access to the Customer Information System (CIS), the Work Order Management System, or physical service files. Sign-in sheets are retained by the Training Coordinator.

2. RESTRICTED CATEGORIES

The following data elements are classified RESTRICTED. Staff may access them only where a documented business need exists.

  Category A — Customer Identity
    Fields: Customer Full Name; Date of Birth; Social Security Number;
    Driver License Number; Alternate Contact Name

  Category B — Financial
    Fields: Bank Routing Number; Bank Account Number; Payment Card Number;
    Card Expiration; Deposit Amount on File; Arrears Balance

  Category C — Premises and Location
    Fields: Service Address; Mailing Address; Meter Location Notes;
    Gate Access Code; Key Box Location

  Category D — Contact
    Fields: Primary Telephone; Mobile Telephone; Email Address

  Category E — Protected Status
    Fields: Medical Necessity Flag; Life Support Equipment Indicator;
    Low Income Assistance Enrollment; Domestic Violence Address
    Confidentiality Flag; Landlord/Tenant Dispute Marker

  Category F — Employee
    Fields: Employee ID; Home Address; Payroll Deduction Detail;
    Disciplinary Action Record

3. PROHIBITED HANDLING

  3.1  Transmission of RESTRICTED fields via personal email accounts.
  3.2  Storage on removable media not issued by Information Services.
  3.3  Printing of CIS account screens except to a departmental printer
       in a badge-controlled area.
  3.4  Disclosure to any party not verified under the Caller Verification
       Procedure (Ref: CSR-14).
  3.5  Lookup of accounts for non-business reasons, including accounts
       belonging to relatives, neighbors, elected officials, or oneself.
  3.6  Photographing screens or documents with any device.

4. CONSEQUENCES

  Tier 1 — Inadvertent, self-reported, no external disclosure:
           documented coaching; retraining within 30 days.
  Tier 2 — Negligent handling, or failure to report within one business day:
           written reprimand; CIS access reduced to read-only pending review.
  Tier 3 — Deliberate unauthorized lookup or disclosure:
           suspension without pay; termination recommended; referral to
           the City Attorney.
  Tier 4 — Disclosure involving Category E fields, or disclosure for
           personal gain: immediate access revocation; referral to law
           enforcement.

Note: Category E disclosures may create liability under state address
confidentiality statutes. There is no minor Category E incident.

5. REPORTING

Suspected or actual mishandling shall be reported to the Records Custodian
by telephone extension within one (1) business day. Do not attempt to
retrieve, recall, or delete misdirected material before reporting.

6. SCENARIO QUIZ

Circle the correct response. Completed sheets to the Training Coordinator.

Q1. A caller states they are the spouse of the account holder and requests
    the current balance and the last four digits of the payment card on
    file. The caller cannot provide the account PIN. The correct action is:
      (a) Provide the balance only.
      (b) Provide nothing; offer to mail a statement to the address of
          record.
      (c) Provide both, as the caller is a household member.

Q2. A field crew member finds a printed route sheet containing service
    addresses and meter location notes left in a City vehicle overnight.
    The correct action is:
      (a) Shred it and continue the route.
      (b) Return it to the dispatch desk and notify a supervisor.
      (c) Report the exposure to the Records Custodian and secure the
          document pending instruction.

Q3. A staff member is asked by a neighbor whether a particular household
    has had service disconnected. The correct action is:
      (a) Confirm only whether service is active.
      (b) Decline and direct the inquirer to the Public Records Office.
      (c) Look up the account but disclose nothing.

Q4. An account displays a Life Support Equipment Indicator. A local
    reporter requests a count of such accounts by neighborhood. The
    correct action is:
      (a) Provide aggregate counts, as no names are involved.
      (b) Refer the request to the Records Custodian and the City Attorney.
      (c) Decline and take no further action.

Q5. A staff member accidentally emails a billing export containing Bank
    Account Number and Service Address fields to an external vendor. The
    correct action is:
      (a) Recall the message and say nothing if the recall succeeds.
      (b) Ask the vendor to delete it, then report if they refuse.
      (c) Report immediately to the Records Custodian; do not attempt
          recall.

Answer key retained by the Training Coordinator. Passing score: 4 of 5.
Staff scoring below passing shall repeat IHC-101 within 15 days.

END OF MEMORANDUM
