MEMORANDUM

TO:      Division Supervisors
FROM:    Records Custodian
RE:      IHC-101 Delivery Notes and Common Objections
CYCLE:   2016

Supervisors delivering the refresher in-division should be prepared for the
following recurring objections. Approved responses follow.

OBJECTION: "It is public record anyway."
RESPONSE:  Certain utility records are disclosable under the state public
           records act. Determination of disclosability is made by the
           Public Records Office, not by operating staff. Fields including
           Social Security Number, Bank Account Number, Payment Card Number,
           Date of Birth, Medical Necessity Flag, and any record bearing an
           Address Confidentiality Flag are exempt or conditionally exempt.
           Staff do not make exemption calls at the counter.

OBJECTION: "I only looked, I did not tell anyone."
RESPONSE:  Access without business need is itself the violation. The audit
           log records the lookup. Whether disclosure followed affects the
           tier, not whether a violation occurred.

OBJECTION: "The customer gave me permission."
RESPONSE:  Consent must be recorded through the Authorized Party form and
           attached to the account. Verbal consent from an unverified caller
           is not consent.

OBJECTION: "It was faster to email it to myself."
RESPONSE:  Transmission to a personal account is a reportable exposure. The
           Department cannot certify deletion from systems it does not
           control. Expect a Tier 2 finding.

OBJECTION: "We have always done it that way."
RESPONSE:  Prior practice is not a defense and should be reported so the
           practice can be corrected without individual attribution.

DELIVERY NOTES

  - Do not use real account data in demonstrations. Use the training
    environment. Requests for training environment credentials go to
    Information Services.
  - Do not permit staff to discuss specific past incidents by name or
    account during the session. Redirect to the scenario set.
  - Collect completed quiz sheets. Do not grade in front of the group.
  - Forward the signed roster to the Training Coordinator within five
    business days of delivery.
  - Staff who decline to sign the acknowledgment shall be referred to
    Human Resources. Do not argue the point in session.
