QUICK REFERENCE CARD — RESTRICTED INFORMATION
Post at each pharmacy workstation. Card No. QR-11. Issued 2011.

RESTRICTED — NEVER LEAVE UNATTENDED
Hard copies | Will-call bags | Fax output | Insurance rejection printouts | Pseudoephedrine log | Payroll envelopes | Shred bin contents

FIELDS THAT MAKE A DOCUMENT RESTRICTED
Patient Name · Date of Birth · Address · Phone · Rx Number · Drug Name · Prescriber · Member ID · Group Number · Social Security Number · Card Account Number · Card Verification Code · Bank Routing Number

THREE HABITS
1. Close the screen before you step away.
2. Verify name and address at the register before releasing any bag.
3. Paper with a restricted field goes in the locked bin, never the trash.

THREE THINGS THAT END EMPLOYMENT
1. Looking up a record you have no business reason to open.
2. Sharing your login.
3. Removing restricted records from the store.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
Tell the Store Manager. Call the Compliance Hotline the same day. Write down what happened, when, and which fields were exposed. Do not attempt to recover a misdirected fax yourself.
