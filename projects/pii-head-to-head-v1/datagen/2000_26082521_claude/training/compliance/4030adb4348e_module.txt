MEMORANDUM

TO: Division Directors; Supervisors of Record
FROM: Records Custodian
RE: Supplemental Guidance — Verification Standard and Escalation Path for Restricted Information Requests
STATUS: Effective immediately; supersedes prior verbal guidance

Supervisors have inquired as to the standard of verification required before restricted elements may be released to a requesting party. The following shall govern.

1. AUTHENTICATION THRESHOLD. No element listed in Categories A through F of the annual compliance instruction shall be released to any caller, walk-in, or correspondent until the requesting party has satisfied the two-factor account verification protocol, consisting of (a) a matching Service Address, and (b) one additional non-public element selected by the representative from the approved challenge set. Recitation of the Service Address alone is expressly insufficient, that element being obtainable from public assessment rolls.

2. AUTHORIZED PARTY LIMITATION. Verification establishes identity; it does not establish authority. A verified individual who is not the Account Holder, a listed Authorized Contact, a holder of a recorded power of attorney, or a court-appointed fiduciary shall receive no restricted element regardless of the completeness of their recitation.

3. PROHIBITED READ-BACK. Under no circumstance shall a representative read aloud, transcribe, or otherwise reproduce a Bank Account Number, Bank Routing Number, or Payment Card Number, whether in full or in part, notwithstanding that the requesting party has been verified. Confirmation may be effected only by the party reciting the element and the representative responding affirmatively or negatively.

4. LEGAL PROCESS. Subpoenas, warrants, discovery demands, and law enforcement requests shall be routed unopened to the Office of the General Counsel. No Division shall respond directly. This applies with equal force to requests presented in person by an officer asserting exigency; in such case the officer shall be directed to the General Counsel's after-hours line, and the interaction shall be documented in an incident report the same business day.

5. PUBLIC RECORDS REQUESTS. Requests seeking customer-level data shall be logged and forwarded to the Records Custodian for exemption analysis. Divisions shall not perform their own redaction. The statutory response clock does not authorize expedited release of exempt material.

6. ESCALATION. Any request that a representative cannot resolve within the foregoing rules shall be escalated to the Supervisor of Record, and thereafter, if unresolved, to the Records Custodian. A request left unanswered pending escalation is a compliant outcome. A request answered incorrectly is not.

Supervisors shall acknowledge receipt in writing within five business days and shall confirm that this guidance has been read at shift briefing to all reporting staff.
