MEMBER RECORDS — CLASSIFICATION AND HANDLING
Policy Reference OPS-14. Supersedes OPS-14 dated 03/96. Effective 01/98.

1.0 SCOPE
    1.1 Applies to all employees, volunteers, Board members, Supervisory Committee members, and contracted service providers.
    1.2 Applies to records in all media: paper, microfiche, magnetic tape, CRT display, facsimile, and voice.

2.0 CLASSIFICATION
    2.1 CLASS A — Identifying
        2.1.1 Fields: Social Security Number; Date of Birth; Mother's Maiden Name; Member Number; PIN; Audio Response Access Code; Signature Card image.
        2.1.2 Handling: locked storage at close of business. No transmission by facsimile under any circumstance.
    2.2 CLASS B — Financial
        2.2.1 Fields: Share Balance; Share Draft Balance; Certificate Amount; Certificate Maturity Date; Loan Amount; Interest Rate; Payment Due Date; Days Delinquent; Charge-Off Indicator; Credit Score.
        2.2.2 Handling: disclosed to the member of record following verification against the signature card and one Class A field.
    2.3 CLASS C — Demographic
        2.3.1 Fields: Mailing Address; Home Telephone; Work Telephone; Employer Name; Occupation; Length of Employment; Joint Owner; Beneficiary.
        2.3.2 Handling: internal use only. Not furnished to affiliates or service providers absent contract clause 9(c).

3.0 PROHIBITED ACTS
    3.1 Access to any member record absent an assigned business purpose. The audit trail is reviewed monthly by the Records Custodian.
    3.2 Access to the record of a relative, household member, coworker, or one's own account. Route such transactions to a supervisor.
    3.3 Removal of any record from Credit Union premises.
    3.4 Retention of Class A fields on scratch paper, desk calendars, or the reverse of teller tickets.
    3.5 Sharing of terminal sign-on credentials, including with a supervisor.

4.0 CONSEQUENCES
    4.1 Progressive discipline per Personnel Manual Section 6.
    4.2 Termination for cause on first occurrence where disclosure was intentional or made for consideration.
    4.3 Notification of the NCUA Region and the bonding company where loss is indicated.
    4.4 Individual criminal and civil exposure. Legal defense is not furnished for acts outside the scope of employment.

5.0 CHECK YOUR UNDERSTANDING
    5.1 A member at the teller line asks the teller to read back the Mother's Maiden Name on file, citing a memory lapse. State the correct action.
    5.2 A vendor servicing the item processor requests a test file populated with live Member Number and Share Balance fields. State the correct action.
    5.3 A supervisor directs an employee to retrieve the Days Delinquent field on an account belonging to a coworker at another branch. State the correct action.
    5.4 A courier pouch arrives sealed but without the Class A stamp. State the correct action.
    5.5 Submit responses to the Compliance Officer within ten business days. Retain a copy at the workstation.
