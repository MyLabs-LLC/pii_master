PART II — PENALTY SCHEDULE
First occurrence, negligent: written reprimand and repeat training.
Second occurrence, negligent: suspension of one to five working days.
Any occurrence, intentional: termination and referral to the City Attorney.
Release of System Security material: termination, referral to the City Attorney, and notification of the state primacy agency.

PART III — SCENARIO QUIZ
Circle the correct release authority for each situation.

A. An attorney's office submits a written request for twelve months of consumption history on a residential account in connection with a property dispute.
   Records Custodian / Customer Service Supervisor / Refer to City Attorney

B. A caller asks whether the occupant of a specific service address is enrolled in the life-support registry, stating they are a concerned relative.
   Records Custodian / Customer Service Supervisor / No release

C. An engineering firm under contract requests the raw water intake schematic by electronic mail.
   General Manager / Data Processing Supervisor / No release

D. A supervisor asks Data Processing for a report listing all employees with wage garnishment deductions, to plan overtime.
   Human Resources Director / Finance Manager / No release
