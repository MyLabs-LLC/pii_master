ARTICLE I.  SCOPE AND APPLICATION
    This handout is promulgated pursuant to the Company's Information
    Practices Policy and to the privacy provisions of the insurance code of
    each jurisdiction in which the Company is admitted. It binds every
    salaried, hourly, temporary, and contracted person granted access to the
    policy administration system, the claims imaging system, the microfiche
    library, or any paper file room. Access granted for one function confers
    no right of access for any other function.
