BROWN-BAG IN-SERVICE — "WHAT NOT TO SAY"
County Health Department, Clinic Operations. 20 minutes. Presenter notes indented.

A. OPENING (2 min)
   (1) State the rule in one sentence: information about a person who came to us belongs to that person.
   (2) State the second rule: the county is answerable for every lookup recorded in the audit trail.

B. RESTRICTED, BY EXAMPLE OF FIELD (5 min)
   (1) On the face sheet: Patient Name, Date of Birth, Social Security Number, Address, Telephone Number.
   (2) On the encounter form: Medical Record Number, Chief Complaint, Diagnosis Code, Test Result.
   (3) On the billing screen: Health Plan Name, Policy Number, Group Number, Guarantor Name.
   (4) In the appointment book: the fact of the appointment itself, and the clinic that made it.
       - Emphasize (4). Presence in a specialty clinic is a disclosure by itself.

C. WHERE IT LEAKS (4 min)
   (1) Waiting-room sign-in sheets left face up.
   (2) Voice carrying over half-height partitions.
   (3) Facsimile transmittals to a stale directory entry.
   (4) Printouts abandoned at the shared device.
   (5) Case discussion in the break room with the door propped.

D. WHAT FOLLOWS (3 min)
   (1) Counseling, reprimand, suspension, termination, per the sanction schedule.
   (2) Federal civil monetary penalties per violation; criminal exposure for knowing disclosure.
   (3) Notification of the individual, and of the licensing board where applicable.
   (4) No exception for good intentions or family relationship.

E. SCENARIOS — SHOW OF HANDS (5 min)
   (1) A caller says a clinic appointment was missed and asks the front desk to confirm the reschedule date for a household member. Confirm?
   (2) A law enforcement officer appears at the counter without a subpoena, requesting the address on file for a person under investigation. Provide?
   (3) A vendor technician requests a live extract containing Client Index Number to test a new interface. Approve?
   (4) A staff member's own record is in the system. May the staff member view it directly?

F. ANSWER KEY
   (1) No. Do not confirm that any person is a client. Refer to the Records Custodian.
   (2) No. Route all requests to the Privacy Officer; document the contact.
   (3) No. Test environments use constructed data only. Refer to Information Systems.
   (4) No. Submit a written request for access under the Notice of Privacy Practices.

G. CLOSE (1 min)
   (1) Circulate the sign-in sheet.
   (2) Questions in writing to the Privacy Officer.
