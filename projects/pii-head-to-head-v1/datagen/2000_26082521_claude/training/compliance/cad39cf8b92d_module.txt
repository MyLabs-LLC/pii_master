1. AUTHORITY
1.1 NCUA Rules and Regulations Part 748 and Appendix A, Guidelines for Safeguarding Member Information.
1.2 Part 716, Privacy of Consumer Financial Information; annual and initial notice requirements.
1.3 Bank Secrecy Act confidentiality provisions, 31 CFR Part 103.
1.4 Fair and Accurate Credit Transactions Act of 2003, as adopted by Board Resolution 2004-11.
1.5 Employee Handbook, Section 9, Confidentiality.

2. CLASSIFICATION SCHEME
2.1 Class R — Restricted. Member nonpublic personal information in any medium, whether keyed, printed, filmed, spoken, or displayed. Designated fields include: Social Security Number; Taxpayer Identification Number; Member Number; Share Account Number; Share Draft Account Number; Loan Account Number; Card Number; Card Expiration Date; Personal Identification Number; Home Banking User ID; Home Banking Password; Date of Birth; Mother's Maiden Name; Driver's License Number; Current Balance; Available Balance; Transaction History; Direct Deposit Source; Payroll Deduction Amount; Gross Monthly Income; Employer Name; Credit Score; Loan Application Status; Adverse Action Reason; Delinquency Status; Charge-Off Amount; Joint Owner Name; Beneficiary Designation; Signature Card Image.
2.2 Class R also includes the fact of membership. Staff shall neither confirm nor deny that any person maintains a relationship with the Credit Union.
2.3 Class C — Confidential Institutional. Vault and ATM combinations; alarm codes; teller drawer limits; wire callback procedures; internal audit workpapers; fraud case files.
2.4 Class P — Statutorily Protected. Suspicious Activity Reports, including their existence, contents, and supporting documentation; Section 314(a) information requests; grand jury subpoenas.
2.5 Class G — General. Rate sheets, lobby notices, brochures, the published annual report.

3. HANDLING REQUIREMENTS
3.1 Clean desk at close of business. Class R secured in locked drawer or file room.
3.2 Facsimile transmission of Class R requires a cover sheet, verification of the destination number before sending, and telephone confirmation of receipt.
3.3 Diskettes, CD-R media, and microfiche containing Class R are logged with the Records Custodian. Removal from premises requires Form IS-12 signed by a department manager.
3.4 Class R shall not be placed in outbound Internet electronic mail.
3.5 Telephone requests require two authenticators from the Member Verification Schedule. Caller identification display is not an authenticator.
3.6 Disposal by cross-cut shredder or locked destruction bin only. Wastebaskets and recycling containers are not approved.
3.7 Workstations locked when unattended. Passwords are not shared, posted, or recorded on paper.

4. PROHIBITED ACTS
4.1 Accessing the record of one's own account, or that of a relative, co-worker, or acquaintance, through staff systems.
4.2 Releasing information to a spouse, parent, adult child, or employer absent documented authorization on file.
4.3 Discussing member matters in the lobby, teller line, break room, elevator, or parking area.
4.4 Removing Class R from the premises for work at home.

5. CONSEQUENCES
5.1 Discipline is progressive: documented counseling, written warning, suspension without pay, termination.
5.2 Unauthorized access is grounds for termination on first occurrence.
5.3 Referral to law enforcement and to the fidelity bond carrier. An employee reported to the carrier may be rendered unbondable and therefore ineligible for continued employment.
5.4 Costs of member notification, card re-issuance, and account renumbering are charged to the originating department.

6. SCENARIO QUIZ
6.1 Circle one response. Return to your supervisor within five business days.
6.2 A caller identifies himself as the employer of a member and asks that the direct deposit amount be confirmed for payroll reconciliation. (A) Confirm the amount. (B) Decline and refer the caller to the member. (C) Confirm only the deposit date.
6.3 A branch receives a walk-in request from a person stating she is the daughter of an elderly member and holds power of attorney. No documentation is presented. (A) Provide the balance. (B) Decline pending review of the instrument by the Records Custodian. (C) Provide the balance if the member number is recited correctly.
6.4 A teller finds a printed transaction register left on the copier at close of business. (A) Place it in the recycling bin. (B) Place it in the locked destruction bin and notify the supervisor. (C) Return it to the copier tray for the owner.
6.5 A co-worker asks to be told whether a former co-worker's loan application was approved. (A) State the status. (B) Decline and report the request. (C) State only that an application exists.
