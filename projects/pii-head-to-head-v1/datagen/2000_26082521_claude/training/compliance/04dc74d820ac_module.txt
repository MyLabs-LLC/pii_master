COMPLIANCE INSTRUCTION No. 2021-07
SUBJECT: Restricted Information Handling — Annual Workforce Instruction and Competency Check
ISSUED BY: Compliance Department, at the direction of the Escrow Operations Committee
APPLIES TO: All employees, temporary staff, and contracted personnel with access to Company systems of record
EFFECTIVE: Upon distribution; acknowledgment due within ten (10) calendar days

§ 1. SCOPE

1.1  This Instruction supplements, and does not supersede, the Written Information Security Program, the Wire Transfer and Disbursement Policy, and the Records Retention and Destruction Schedule.
1.2  For purposes hereof, "Restricted Information" means any populated data value falling within the inventory set forth at § 2, irrespective of medium, and irrespective of whether such value was obtained from a consumer, a lender, a real estate licensee, a public record, or a consumer reporting agency.

§ 2. INVENTORY OF RESTRICTED FIELDS

2.1  Tier I — Identity and Government Issued
     Social Security Number; Individual Taxpayer Identification Number; Driver's License Number; State Identification Card Number; Passport Number; Date of Birth; Mother's Maiden Name.
2.2  Tier II — Financial Instrument and Account
     Deposit Account Number; ABA Routing Number; Credit Card Account Number; Card Expiration Date; Card Verification Value; Escrow Trust Account Number; Loan Number; Payoff Balance; Wire Beneficiary Name and Beneficiary Account Number.
2.3  Tier III — Transactional and Derived
     Earnest Money Amount; Income; Credit Score; Consumer Report Contents; Seller Proceeds Amount; Commission Disbursement Detail; Notarial Journal Signature and Thumbprint Entry.
2.4  Column headings, form captions, and database field names enumerated above are themselves unclassified and are reproduced here deliberately, so that personnel may recognize the fields on sight. Classification attaches to the value only.

§ 3. HANDLING STANDARD

3.1  Transmission outside the Company network shall occur exclusively through the approved encrypted delivery portal.
3.2  Physical media bearing Restricted Information shall be secured under lock at the conclusion of each business day and destroyed by crosscut shredding or by the contracted destruction vendor under certificate.
3.3  Verbal disclosure is prohibited absent identity verification performed in accordance with the Disbursement Policy callback procedure.
3.4  Storage on personal devices, personal cloud accounts, or removable media is prohibited without exception.

§ 4. CONSEQUENCES

4.1  Employment consequences: documented counseling, suspension, or termination. Intent is not an element of the violation.
4.2  Regulatory consequences: examination findings, civil money penalties, referral to the state insurance department, the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, or the state attorney general.
4.3  Professional consequences: suspension or revocation of escrow officer licensure or notarial commission.
4.4  Contractual consequences: suspension or termination of underwriting agreements, and indemnity demand against the Company.
4.5  Personal consequences: individual civil liability where provided by statute.

§ 5. COMPETENCY CHECK

Respond in writing. Do not identify any transaction party by name in your response.

  5.1  A funding request arrives instructing that disbursement be redirected to a different beneficiary account, citing a bank error. The request originates from an address resembling the lender's. State every step required before any funds move.
  5.2  A remote worker stores a scanned identification document in a personal cloud folder for the duration of a single business day. Analyze under §§ 2.1 and 3.4.
  5.3  A closed file containing Tier II fields is placed in general refuse. Identify the violation, the notification obligation, and the responsible department.
  5.4  A colleague requests your system credentials to complete a funding deadline while you are away. State the correct response and the reporting obligation, if any.

§ 6. ACKNOWLEDGMENT

Signed acknowledgments shall be transmitted to the Records Custodian, who shall maintain them as part of the compliance training record for the retention period prescribed by the Records Retention and Destruction Schedule.
