INFORMATION SECURITY MODULE — PRESENTER SCRIPT
Runs 25 minutes with overhead transparencies. Read the SAY lines; the bullets are what appears on the screen.

SLIDE 1 — WHY WE ARE HERE
SAY: Every one of you can see more about a member's finances than that member's own family can. That access is the job. Protecting it is also the job.
ON SCREEN:
- Access is granted by role, not by curiosity.
- Members assume silence. Regulators require it.
- Examiners test us on this every cycle.

SLIDE 2 — WHAT IS RESTRICTED
SAY: Do not try to remember a list of secrets. Remember instead that everything on the account screen is restricted, including the fact that the screen exists.
ON SCREEN:
- Member Name, Member Number, Social Security Number, Date of Birth
- Street Address, Home Telephone, Employer Name
- Share Account Number, Loan Account Number, Card Number, PIN
- Balance, Transaction History, Payee Name, Check Serial Number
- Credit Bureau Score, Application Status, Delinquency Status
- Currency Transaction Report and Suspicious Activity Report contents
- Existence of the relationship itself

SLIDE 3 — THE FOUR PLACES IT LEAKS
SAY: In this credit union, in the last three examinations, information left the building in exactly four ways. Not one of them involved a burglar.
ON SCREEN:
- The mouth — lobby conversation, break room, telephone without verification.
- The paper — proof tape, printouts, mail returns, the wastebasket.
- The screen — unlocked terminal, monitor angled at the lobby.
- The wire — misdialed fax, modem file transfer, unattended printer.

SLIDE 4 — THE RULES, PLAINLY
SAY: If you learn nothing else today, learn these six.
ON SCREEN:
- Verify before you disclose. No exceptions for familiar voices.
- Look only at what your work requires.
- Lock the terminal when you stand up.
- Shred anything with a member field printed on it.
- Refer outside requests up, never sideways.
- Never mention a Suspicious Activity Report to anyone outside the reporting chain.

SLIDE 5 — WHAT HAPPENS IF YOU DON'T
SAY: I am not going to soften this part.
ON SCREEN:
- Unauthorized look-up: warning, retraining, inquiry rights suspended.
- Repeat or willful look-up: termination.
- Disclosure outside the credit union: termination, bond claim, law enforcement referral.
- Suspicious Activity Report disclosure: federal penalties against you personally.
- Every case goes to the Supervisory Committee.

SLIDE 6 — THREE SITUATIONS
SAY: Take a moment on each. Answer out loud. There is no trick in any of them.
ON SCREEN:
- A caller gives a member number and an address, then asks for the balance and a recent withdrawal amount. What do you do first?
- A relative of a member is standing at your window and says the member is in the hospital and needs the account closed today. What do you do?
- A coworker asks you to check whether a mutual acquaintance is behind on a car loan. What do you do, and what do you do next?

SLIDE 7 — CLOSING
SAY: The test is simple. If the member were standing behind you, would they be comfortable with what you are about to say, print, or type?
ON SCREEN:
- Verify. Limit. Lock. Shred. Refer.
- Questions to the Compliance Officer or the Records Custodian.
