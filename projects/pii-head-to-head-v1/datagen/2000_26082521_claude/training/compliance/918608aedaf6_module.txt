RIVERBEND FEDERAL CREDIT UNION
MEMBER PRIVACY TRAINING — HANDOUT 4B
Retain at workstation. Review quarterly. Revised 11/97.

  I. RESTRICTED CATEGORIES
      A. Tier 1 — Not disclosed outside the Credit Union absent written member authorization or lawful process.
          1. Social Security Number
          2. Date of Birth
          3. Mother's Maiden Name
          4. PIN / Audio Response Access Code
      B. Tier 2 — Disclosed to the member of record only, after positive identification.
          1. Member Number
          2. Share Balance / Share Draft Balance
          3. Loan Balance, Payment Due Date, Days Delinquent
          4. Credit Score and bureau file contents
      C. Tier 3 — Internal routing only.
          1. Employer Name; Payroll Deduction Amount
          2. Mailing Address; Home Telephone; Work Telephone
          3. Beneficiary Designation; Joint Owner

  II. CONSEQUENCES
      A. First occurrence — written warning, personnel file entry, mandatory retraining.
      B. Second occurrence — suspension without pay pending review.
      C. Willful disclosure for consideration — termination for cause and referral to NCUA and the U.S. Attorney.
      D. Civil liability attaches to the individual employee. The Credit Union's bond does not indemnify willful acts.

  III. QUIZ — mark T or F
      A. A caller recites the last four digits of a Social Security Number and requests a balance. Identification is complete. ___
      B. A person present at the teller line, named on no share, requests a loan payoff figure. Release is permitted. ___
      C. A telephone request to change the Mailing Address may be processed without written confirmation to the address of record. ___
      D. A printout bearing the Credit Score field may be discarded in the branch wastebasket once the loan is denied. ___
      E. A member of the Board may review any member record on request. ___
