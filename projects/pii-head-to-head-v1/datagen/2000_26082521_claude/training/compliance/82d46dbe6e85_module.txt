PRESENTER SCRIPT — MODULE 3: "WHAT MAY I SAY, AND TO WHOM?"
Annual Compliance In-Service, School Year 2004-2005

Distribution: Building Administrators and Designated Presenters Only

SLIDE 4 OF 11
TITLE ON TRANSPARENCY: "Directory Information Is the Exception, Not the Rule"
ON-SCREEN TEXT:
  - May be released without consent: Name; Address; Telephone Listing; Date and Place of Birth; Major Field of Study; Participation in Activities and Sports; Weight and Height of Athletic Team Members; Dates of Attendance; Degrees and Awards Received; Most Recent Educational Agency Attended
  - MAY NOT be released as Directory Information: Social Security Number; Student Identification Number; Grades; Disability Status; Discipline History
PRESENTER NARRATION (read verbatim):
"The categories displayed on the left were designated Directory Information by Board action and published in the annual notification to parents. That designation is not self-executing. A parent or an eligible student may file a written objection, and where such an objection has been filed, the elements on the left revert to restricted status in their entirety. Before you release any element on the left, you are obligated to verify the objection flag in the student information system. If you cannot verify it, you do not release it."
FACILITATOR NOTE: Pause. Solicit questions regarding yearbook and athletic program requests before advancing.

SLIDE 5 OF 11
TITLE ON TRANSPARENCY: "The Hallway, the Lounge, and the Parking Lot"
ON-SCREEN TEXT:
  - Oral disclosure is disclosure.
  - "Legitimate educational interest" is not "curiosity" and is not "I've known the family for years."
  - There is no exception for conversations among colleagues.
PRESENTER NARRATION (read verbatim):
"The single most frequent source of substantiated complaints received by this office is not the misfiled folder. It is the conversation conducted in a location where it may be overheard. Staff members are reminded that the standard applied is not whether disclosure was intended, but whether reasonable safeguards were employed. A conversation regarding a placement decision, conducted in the staff lounge in the presence of employees who bear no assigned responsibility for that student, constitutes a disclosure and will be treated as such."
FACILITATOR NOTE: Reference Section IV of Form HR-114. Do not discuss any pending investigation.
