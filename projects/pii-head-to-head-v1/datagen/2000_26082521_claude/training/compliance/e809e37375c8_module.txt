TRAINING SLIDE SCRIPT — "WHAT YOU MAY NOT SAY"
Module 2004-INF-02
Presenter: the Training Coordinator
Running time: 12 minutes
Audience: all new hires within 30 days of start date

SLIDE 1 — TITLE
1.1 Read the title aloud.
1.2 State that the module is mandatory under Part 748 and that attendance is recorded.

SLIDE 2 — WHAT IS RESTRICTED
2.1 Read the field list from the slide without elaboration: Social Security Number; Member Number; Account Number; Card Number; Personal Identification Number; Date of Birth; Mother's Maiden Name; Current Balance; Transaction History; Credit Score; Loan Application Status; Delinquency Status.
2.2 State: membership itself is restricted. We do not confirm that a person is a member.
2.3 Do not invite examples from the room. Examples drawn from live accounts are themselves a disclosure.

SLIDE 3 — WHAT HAPPENS
3.1 Read: written warning, suspension without pay, termination.
3.2 Read: unauthorized lookup of a known person's account is termination on first occurrence.
3.3 Read: referral to the bond carrier; referral to law enforcement where warranted.

SLIDE 4 — SCENARIO
4.1 Read aloud: a person telephones the branch, states that a joint owner has moved out, and asks that the other owner be removed from the account and that recent withdrawals be described.
4.2 Pause thirty seconds. Solicit two responses.
4.3 Correct answer: decline; refer to Member Services; a joint owner is not removed by telephone and account activity is not narrated to a caller.
4.4 Do not identify incorrect responses by the responder.

SLIDE 5 — CLOSE
5.1 Distribute Form IS-40.
5.2 Collect signed acknowledgments before dismissal.
