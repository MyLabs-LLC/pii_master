MODULE 4 — INFORMATION HANDLING
SPEAKER SCRIPT AND PARTICIPANT WORKSHEET
For use in new-hire orientation, week one. Presented by the Terminal Safety Coordinator.

A. Opening statement (read as written)
   1. "Everything you will see today on a screen, on a printed page, or on a trailer door is the property
      of the Company or of its customers, and is provided to you solely for the performance of your duties."
   2. "There is no category of information in this building that you are free to repeat at your discretion."

B. What is restricted
   1. Anything identifying a person
      a. Fields: Full Name, Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Home Address, Personal Telephone Number,
         License Number, Medical Certificate Number.
   2. Anything identifying a payment instrument
      a. Fields: Primary Account Number, Expiration Date, Card Verification Value.
   3. Anything identifying a customer's business terms
      a. Fields: Account Name, Contract Rate, Volume Commitment, Accessorial Charge.
   4. Anything identifying a shipment or its protection
      a. Fields: Consignee Name, Delivery Address, Commodity Description, Declared Value, Seal Number,
         Route Restriction, Screening Method.

C. What happens if it is mishandled
   1. To the individual: discipline up to and including termination for cause; loss of badge access;
      referral to law enforcement where the disclosure involves security-sensitive or export-controlled
      material; personal liability that is not discharged by resignation.
   2. To the Company: contractual indemnification obligations, chargebacks, regulatory penalty assessed per
      violation, and removal from customer approved-carrier lists.
   3. To the customer: cargo loss, competitive harm, and, in the case of workforce records, identity theft
      affecting a coworker.

D. Worksheet — scenario responses (complete and submit before leaving the room)
   1. Scenario A. A person telephones the terminal, states they are calling from a customer's corporate
      office, and asks for the contract rate applied to last month's shipments. What do you do?
   2. Scenario B. A coworker asks you to look up another employee's home address so that a card may be
      mailed. What do you do?
   3. Scenario C. You find an unsecured folder labeled "Driver Qualification Files" on a shared network
      drive accessible to the entire terminal. What do you do, and within what period?
   4. Scenario D. A vendor's service technician asks to photograph a dispatch screen "to document the
      configuration." What do you do?

E. Completion
   1. Worksheet fields to be entered by the participant: Employee Number, Job Title, Terminal Code, Date of
      Session.
   2. Worksheet fields to be entered by the presenter: Score, Pass or Fail, Signature, Date of Review.
   3. Failing scores require reattendance within thirty calendar days.
