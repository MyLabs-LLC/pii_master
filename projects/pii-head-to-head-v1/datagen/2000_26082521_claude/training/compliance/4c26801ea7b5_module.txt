RESTRICTED INFORMATION HANDLING
Annual Compliance Refresher — Module 3 of 5
Issuing function: Office of the Records Custodian
Revision 2021.2 | Effective 01 March 2021
Audience: Branch staff, corporate support functions, field supervisors
Delivery: Instructor-led, 45 minutes. Completion attestation required.

    1.0 SCOPE
        1.1 Personnel holding entitlements to the applicant tracking system (ATS), the payroll platform, or physical candidate files.
        1.2 All work locations, including approved remote workstations.
        1.3 Contract and temporary internal staff, effective from date of system provisioning.

    2.0 RESTRICTED CATEGORIES
        2.1 R1 — Government and tax identifiers
            2.1.1 Field labels in scope: Social Security Number; Individual Taxpayer Identification Number; Work Authorization Document Number; Driver's License Number.
            2.1.2 System of record: onboarding module and Form I-9 repository.
            2.1.3 Display rule: masked to final four characters in all list views and exports.
        2.2 R2 — Financial instructions
            2.2.1 Field labels in scope: Bank Routing Number; Deposit Account Number; Pay Card Number; Garnishment Case Reference.
            2.2.2 Change requests accepted only through the verified-caller workflow.
        2.3 R3 — Health, screening, and accommodation
            2.3.1 Field labels in scope: Drug Screen Result; Physical Capacity Restriction; Accommodation Request Type; Leave Reason Code; Vaccination Status.
            2.3.2 Storage: segregated medical file. Not permitted in the ATS candidate record or in requisition notes.
        2.4 R4 — Background and adjudication
            2.4.1 Field labels in scope: Consumer Report Reference; Criminal Record Disposition; Adjudication Outcome; Adverse Action Stage.
            2.4.2 Client disclosure limited to the fitness determination. Underlying report content is not transmitted.
        2.5 R5 — Direct identifiers and contact data
            2.5.1 Field labels in scope: Full Legal Name; Date of Birth; Home Address; Personal Telephone Number; Personal Email Address; Emergency Contact Name.

    3.0 HANDLING CONTROLS
        3.1 Transmission: encrypted portal only. Attachment of R1–R4 fields to outbound mail is blocked at the gateway.
        3.2 Storage: no local drives, personal cloud accounts, or unmanaged spreadsheets.
        3.3 Print and physical: locked at end of shift; cross-cut shredding at disposal.
        3.4 Disclosure: minimum necessary, on documented business need, logged in the disclosure register.

    4.0 CONSEQUENCES OF NON-CONFORMING HANDLING
        4.1 Individual: suspension of system entitlements pending review; corrective action up to and including termination; personal liability where statute provides.
        4.2 Organizational: contractual remedies under client master service agreements; notification obligations under state breach statutes; regulatory exposure under FCRA, ADA, GINA, and applicable state privacy law.
        4.3 Reporting: suspected exposure reported to the Records Custodian within one hour of discovery. Delay is itself a violation.

    5.0 SCENARIO ASSESSMENT (select one response per item)
        5.1 A client hiring manager requests the full background report for a placed worker rather than the fitness determination.
            (a) Forward the report. (b) Forward the determination only and log the request. (c) Forward with identifiers masked.
        5.2 A caller states a direct deposit account has changed and asks that payroll be redirected before the current cycle closes.
            (a) Update on the caller's statement. (b) Route to the verified-caller workflow. (c) Update and confirm afterward by mail.
        5.3 A branch coordinator maintains a personal spreadsheet of Date of Birth and Social Security Number to speed onboarding.
            (a) Permitted if password-protected. (b) Permitted on a managed device. (c) Prohibited; delete and report.
        5.4 A screening vendor returns a result to a shared branch mailbox that all recruiters may open.
            (a) Leave in place. (b) Move to the segregated medical file and request corrected routing. (c) Print and file locally.
        5.5 Key: 5.1 (b); 5.2 (b); 5.3 (c); 5.4 (b).
