MEMBER PRIVACY & RESTRICTED DATA — FACILITATOR SLIDE SCRIPT
Deck IS-101B | Runtime 12 minutes | Delivered at branch huddle and by remote broadcast

SLIDE 4 of 11 — "WHAT WE MEAN BY RESTRICTED"
ON SCREEN: Three stacked bands labeled RESTRICTED / CONFIDENTIAL / INTERNAL USE
SAY: "Please understand that the classification of a data element is determined by the harm its disclosure would foreseeably cause to the member, and not by the ease with which you happen to be able to retrieve it. The following field labels are Restricted in every context: Social Security Number, Member Number, Card Number, Card Verification Value, Online Banking User ID, Security Question Response, Date of Birth, and Driver's License Number."
PAUSE. ADVANCE ON CUE.

SLIDE 5 of 11 — "THE PROHIBITIONS"
ON SCREEN: Bulleted list, animate on click
SAY, reading each bullet verbatim:
  » "You shall not access a member record absent a documented business purpose arising from an assigned duty."
  » "You shall not access your own record, nor the record of a relative, coworker, neighbor, or public figure."
  » "You shall not photograph, screenshot, or transcribe a Restricted field to any medium not under credit union control."
  » "You shall not transmit a Restricted field by unencrypted electronic mail, by text message, or through any consumer messaging application."
  » "You shall not confirm or deny the existence of a Suspicious Activity Report to any person, including the member and including a subpoenaing party."

SLIDE 6 of 11 — "IF IT GOES WRONG"
ON SCREEN: Escalation ladder graphic
SAY: "Report any suspected mishandling to your supervisor and to the Information Security Officer within one hour of discovery, irrespective of the hour or day. Self-reporting is a mitigating factor. Concealment is an independent and terminable offense. Consequences may include termination, criminal referral, individual civil liability, and regulatory penalty assessed against the institution."

SLIDE 7 of 11 — "QUICK CHECK"
ON SCREEN: Scenario text, no advance until hands are raised
SAY: "A teller recognizes the name on a deposit slip as a neighbor and, after the transaction concludes, reopens the account to see the balance. Is this permissible?"
EXPECTED RESPONSE: No. The business purpose terminated when the transaction concluded; the subsequent access is unauthorized and independently reportable.
SAY: "A branch manager receives a telephone request from a caller asserting authority as a co-borrower and requesting the payoff figure. What is required?"
EXPECTED RESPONSE: Authentication in accordance with the Customer Identification Program and verification of the caller's status on the Loan Suffix record before any figure is disclosed.

CLOSE: Direct all participants to sign the roster before leaving the room.
