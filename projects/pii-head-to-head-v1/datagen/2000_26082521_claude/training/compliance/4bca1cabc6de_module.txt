SLIDE 3 OF 9 -- "WHY THE TIER MATTERS"

PRESENTER NOTE. Three points, then advance.

First, tier assignment is determined by the presence of a listed field label
in the record, not by the sensitivity a staff member assigns to the content.
A roster carrying only the columns Program Name and Aggregate Case Count is
Tier P. Add the column Date of Birth and the same roster becomes Tier 1.

Second, aggregation raises the tier. Two Tier P extracts that can be joined
on a common key are treated at the tier of the resulting joined set. The
Privacy Officer makes the determination; staff may not make it locally.

Third, the tier travels with the copy. Printing, screen capture, export to
removable media, and forwarding to a personal account each create a copy
that carries the original controls. There is no de-escalation by format.

SLIDE 4 OF 9 -- "WHAT MISHANDLING COSTS"

PRESENTER NOTE. Summarize; the sanction schedule is distributed separately
as Attachment B and is not read aloud.

Mishandling is addressed on three tracks that run independently. The
administrative track is progressive discipline applied by the Human
Resources Director. The regulatory track is a civil money penalty assessed
per violation against the Department, with an annual cap for identical
provisions. The criminal track applies to knowing disclosure, obtaining
records under false pretenses, and disclosure for personal gain or malicious
harm. A finding on one track does not bar a finding on the others.
