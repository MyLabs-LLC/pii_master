TELLER LINE REMINDER — POST AT EACH STATION
Compliance Bulletin 98-3

  • DO NOT SPEAK ALOUD
      – Social Security Number
      – Member Number
      – Share, Draft, or Loan Balance
      – Days Delinquent
      – Credit Score

  • DO NOT RELEASE BY TELEPHONE WITHOUT VERIFICATION
      – Verification means one Class A field plus one Class C field
      – Caller identification equipment is not verification
      – Correct recitation of the Mailing Address is not verification

  • DO NOT LEAVE UNATTENDED
      – Teller tickets bearing Member Number
      – Loan Application worksheets
      – A CRT session left signed on
      – Microfiche left in the reader

  • IF MISHANDLED
      – Notify the Branch Operations Manager the same day
      – Complete Incident Form 22 before end of shift
      – Failure to report is itself a violation subject to discipline

  • TWO-MINUTE QUIZ — HUDDLE USE
      – A caller states the line is poor and asks the teller to repeat the balance louder. Response?
      – A member requests that a statement copy be faxed to a workplace machine. Response?
      – A dispute form bearing the Card Number field is found in the copier feed tray. Response?

Discuss at Monday huddle. Initial the roster.
