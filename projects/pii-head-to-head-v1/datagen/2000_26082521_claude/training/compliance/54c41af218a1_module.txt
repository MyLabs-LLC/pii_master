QUICK REFERENCE CARD — RELEASE AUTHORIZATION MATRIX
POST AT EVERY BUILDING OFFICE COUNTER AND TELEPHONE STATION

BEFORE RELEASING ANY RECORD, CONFIRM ALL FOUR:
  [ ] Requester identity verified in person or by callback to a number on file
  [ ] Category of record identified (I through VI, Form HR-114)
  [ ] Written authorization on file, or requester is the record's custodian
  [ ] Objection list checked (Directory Information only)

ROUTE TO CUSTODIAN — DO NOT RESPOND DIRECTLY:
  Subpoena or court order .............. Superintendent's Office
  Law enforcement inquiry .............. Superintendent's Office
  Media inquiry ........................ Public Information Office
  Non-custodial parent request ......... Building Principal
  Transfer request, other district ..... Registrar
  Employee file request ................ Director of Personnel

FIELDS NEVER RELEASED BY TELEPHONE:
  Student Identification Number; Social Security Number; Meal Eligibility Status; Allergy or Condition Code; Evaluation Rating; Home Address

IF A DISCLOSURE HAS ALREADY OCCURRED:
  Notify the Records Custodian within one working day. Document the category, the approximate number of records, and the recipient. Do not contact the recipient without direction.
