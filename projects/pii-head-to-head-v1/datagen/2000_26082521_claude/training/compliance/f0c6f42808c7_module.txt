SECTION B — INCIDENT ROUTING
1. Discovering employee notifies the Privacy Officer before end of shift.
2. Privacy Officer opens a numbered incident file and assigns a tier.
3. Division Director provides a written statement of unit facts within three working days.
4. Personnel is consulted before any sanction at Tier II or above.
5. The Health Officer approves all Tier IV determinations.
6. The incident file is retained for six years from closure.
