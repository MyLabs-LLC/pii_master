ANNUAL PRIVACY CERTIFICATION — COLLEAGUE ATTESTATION
Form CT-114A (companion to Form CT-114) | Cycle: 2021

SECTION 1 — ATTESTATION STATEMENTS
1.1 I have received instruction on the six restricted information categories and can identify the field labels associated with each, including Patient Name, Date of Birth, Prescription Number, Primary Account Number, Social Security Number, and Employee Identification Number.
1.2 I understand that access to a record is limited to the minimum necessary for an assigned task, and that no personal interest, familiarity, or concern constitutes a business purpose.
1.3 I understand that I may not access my own record or that of a relative, colleague, or public figure through the pharmacy system, and that all access is logged and subject to audit.
1.4 I understand that credentials are individual and that permitting another person to work under my login is a sanctionable finding attributed to me.
1.5 I understand that suspected disclosure must be reported to the Pharmacy Manager and the Privacy Office within one hour of discovery, and that late reporting is separately sanctionable.
1.6 I understand that mishandling may result in termination, civil monetary penalties, criminal referral, and action against any professional license I hold.

SECTION 2 — SCENARIO ASSESSMENT
2.1 A colleague on an adjacent shift asks what medication a mutual acquaintance is taking, having seen them at the counter. The colleague has no assigned task involving that record. State the required action.
2.2 During a system outage, a technician begins recording pending prescription details on a personal notepad to re-enter later. State whether this is permitted and identify the approved alternative.
2.3 A caller states they are from an insurer and requests the full member identifier and date of birth for a person in the queue, but cannot supply a case reference. State the required action.
2.4 A will-call bag is handed to a person who states the named individual is waiting in the car. No authorization is on file. State the required action.
