SLIDE 1 — TITLE
On-screen: "Restricted Information: What It Is and Why It Matters"
Script: State the course code, the recertification interval, and the sign-in sheet requirement. Advance when the sheet has circulated.

SLIDE 2 — DEFINITION
On-screen: "Restricted = access limited to documented business need"
Script: Emphasize that business need is job-function based, not curiosity based, and not seniority based. A department director has no access right to a record outside the director's assigned function.

SLIDE 3 — THE CATEGORIES
On-screen bullet list: Protected Health Information; Communicable Disease Case Data; Vital Records; Substance Use Disorder Treatment Records; Human Resources and Payroll Data; Security and Infrastructure Data.
Script: Read each aloud. Note that Substance Use Disorder Treatment Records carry an additional redisclosure prohibition beyond the general privacy rule.

SLIDE 4 — IDENTIFIER FIELDS
On-screen table headers: Field Label | Category | Minimum Handling
Script: Walk the table. Reinforce that a health fact becomes Protected Health Information the moment it is joined to any field in the identifier list — Name, Medical Record Number, Date of Birth, Street Address, ZIP Code, Telephone Number, Email Address, Social Security Number, Account Number, Device Serial Number, IP Address, Biometric Identifier, Full-Face Photograph.

SLIDE 5 — CONSEQUENCES
On-screen: three columns headed Administrative | Civil | Criminal
Script: Administrative outcomes range from written reprimand to termination. Civil penalties are assessed per violation and tiered by culpability. Criminal exposure attaches to knowing disclosure for personal gain or malicious harm.

SLIDE 6 — SCENARIOS (ASK, PAUSE, REVEAL)
Scenario 1: A laptop containing an unencrypted case registry export is taken from a vehicle overnight. Ask: reportable or not? Reveal: reportable; encryption is the safe-harbor control and it was absent.
Scenario 2: A workforce member discusses an active investigation in a shared elevator without stating a name. Ask: permissible? Reveal: not permissible; condition plus location plus timing can re-identify.
Scenario 3: A records request arrives by fax from an address not on the approved partner list. Ask: process or hold? Reveal: hold and route to the Records Custodian for verification.
Scenario 4: A former employee's account remains active eleven days after separation. Ask: whose duty? Reveal: the separating supervisor initiates access termination on the last working day.

SLIDE 7 — REPORTING
On-screen: "Report within one business day"
Script: Direct attendees to the Privacy Officer and the Compliance Hotline entry in the intranet directory. Reporting is mandatory. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a disciplinary offense.

SLIDE 8 — CLOSE
Script: Collect quiz forms. Confirm every attendee signed Section E of Form PC-114. Return the packet to the Division Training Coordinator by close of business.
