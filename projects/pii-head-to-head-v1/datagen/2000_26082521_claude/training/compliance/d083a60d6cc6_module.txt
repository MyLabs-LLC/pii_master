FACILITATOR SLIDE SCRIPT — INFORMATION PROTECTION MODULE
DECK IDENTIFIER: TRN-2021-07
DELIVERY FORMAT: Instructor-led, forty (40) minutes
AUDIENCE: New hire cohort and annual recertification cohort
PREPARED BY: Compliance Education Unit

===========================================================
SLIDE 1 — TITLE
ON SCREEN: "What We Protect, and Why It Matters"
NARRATION: Good morning. Over the next forty minutes we will address three matters: first, which categories of information the Company classifies as restricted; second, the affirmative obligations that attach to those categories; and third, the consequences that follow from mishandling. Attendance is recorded and completion is a condition of continued system access.

===========================================================
SLIDE 2 — THE CATEGORIES
ON SCREEN (bulleted):
  - Social Security Number / Taxpayer Identification Number
  - Date of Birth
  - Member Identification Number / Subscriber Number
  - Claim Number / Policy Number / Group Number
  - Financial Account Number / Payment Card Number
  - Diagnosis Code / Procedure Code / Prescription History
  - Residential Address / Personal Telephone Number / Personal Electronic Mail Address
NARRATION: Read the list. Note that several of these elements are unremarkable in isolation and become restricted the moment they are paired with an identifier. A claim number alone is a reference. A claim number alongside a diagnosis code is protected health information.

FACILITATOR NOTE: Do not populate these fields with live values during demonstration. Use the training environment.

===========================================================
SLIDE 3 — OBLIGATIONS
ON SCREEN: "Minimum Necessary. Encrypt in Transit. Verify Before You Disclose. Report Within 24 Hours."
NARRATION: These four obligations are cumulative, not alternative. Satisfying one does not excuse failure of another. The twenty-four hour reporting clock begins at discovery, not at confirmation.

===========================================================
SLIDE 4 — CONSEQUENCES
ON SCREEN (two columns):
  TO THE COMPANY          |  TO THE INDIVIDUAL
  Civil monetary penalty  |  Termination for cause
  Examination finding     |  Personal civil liability
  Corrective action plan  |  Criminal referral
  Loss of plan sponsor    |  Loss of appointment
NARRATION: Emphasize the right-hand column. Individual liability is not theoretical. Knowing disclosure for personal gain is prosecutable independent of any action the Company takes.

===========================================================
SLIDE 5 — SCENARIO DISCUSSION (open floor)
SCENARIO A: A staff member working remotely prints a batch of correspondence to a household printer shared with others.
SCENARIO B: An associate receives an unsolicited message requesting immediate confirmation of a member's coverage effective date, styled as an urgent request from an executive.
SCENARIO C: A team observes that a departing colleague's system entitlements remain active three weeks after the last day worked.
NARRATION: Solicit responses. For each scenario, drive the discussion toward identification of the failed control, not toward assignment of blame.

===========================================================
SLIDE 6 — CLOSE
ON SCREEN: Privacy Officer extension and the Compliance Hotline reference are posted on the intranet landing page.
NARRATION: Collect the attestation forms before dismissal.
