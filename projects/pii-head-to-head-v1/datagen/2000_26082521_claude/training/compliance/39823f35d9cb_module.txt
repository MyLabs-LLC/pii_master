MERIDIAN TITLE & ESCROW SERVICES, INC.
OFFICE OF THE COMPLIANCE OFFICER
ANNUAL PRIVACY AND INFORMATION HANDLING TRAINING — PARTICIPANT HANDOUT
Course Code IH-04 · Revised March 2004 · Retain in branch training binder, Tab 3

1.0 Purpose and Application. This handout supports the annual instruction required of every officer, escrow assistant, title examiner, funder, courier, receptionist, and temporary or contract worker assigned to a branch or to the central plant. The instruction implements the Company's obligations under the privacy and safeguards provisions of the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act, the disposal and identity theft provisions of the Fair and Accurate Credit Transactions Act, the escrow recordkeeping rules of the states in which the Company is licensed, and the customer identification and reporting obligations arising under the USA PATRIOT Act. Nothing in this handout narrows a duty imposed by an underwriter's bulletin or by a closing instruction letter; where the instruments conflict, the more restrictive control governs and the Compliance Officer is to be consulted the same business day.

2.0 Categories of Restricted Information. The Company recognizes three tiers. Tier I consists of nonpublic personal financial information obtained in connection with an escrow, closing, or title order, and is identified by field label rather than by document. The following labels, wherever they appear — on an application, a settlement statement, a payoff demand, a lender's closing package, a fax cover sheet, or a screen in the escrow accounting system — mark Tier I content: Social Security Number; Taxpayer Identification Number; Date of Birth; Driver's License Number; Deposit Account Number; Routing/ABA Number; Credit Card Number; Loan Number; Payoff Balance; Credit Score; Gross Monthly Income; Employer Name; Mother's Maiden Name; Home Telephone Number; Home Address. Tier II consists of transaction work product, including the Escrow File Number, Title Order Number, Wire Beneficiary Account, Earnest Money Deposit Amount, Preliminary Report exceptions, and the contents of any lender instruction. Tier III consists of internal business confidential material, including rate filings, underwriter loss runs, plant indices, and employee personnel records. Tier I is restricted absolutely; Tier II is restricted to the file team and the parties of record; Tier III is restricted to the position that owns the record series.

3.0 Handling Duties. Restricted information is to be viewed only for a business reason connected to an open order, is to travel only by sealed courier pouch or by a fax confirmed by telephone before transmission, and is to be stored only in a locked drawer, a locked plant cabinet, or an access-controlled directory. Screens are to be cleared and workstations locked whenever a desk is left unattended. Superseded paper is to be cross-cut shredded, never placed in recycling, and microfiche is to be surrendered to the Records Custodian for destruction under log.

4.0 Consequences of Mishandling. Disclosure to an unauthorized person, whether careless or deliberate, exposes the Company to regulatory examination findings, civil penalty, license action against the escrow officer and the branch, and claims against the fidelity bond. The individual employee is subject to progressive discipline through termination, and, where the conduct involves misuse of an identifier for personal gain, to referral for criminal prosecution. Reinstatement of access after a substantiated finding requires the written approval of the Compliance Officer.

5.0 Scenario Examination. Read each situation and record the required action on the answer sheet. 5.1 A caller states that a payoff figure is needed immediately for a file and declines to give the escrow number, offering instead to confirm identity by reciting a Date of Birth. 5.2 A cover sheet bearing a Loan Number and a Deposit Account Number is discovered in the shared fax tray after business hours. 5.3 A real estate licensee requests a copy of the buyer's loan application because the file "is a joint deal." 5.4 A funder is asked by a caller to redirect a wire to a different Beneficiary Account after the closing has already been instructed. 5.5 A departing employee asks to take a diskette copy of a spreadsheet listing Escrow File Number, Payoff Balance, and Employer Name "for reference."

6.0 Acknowledgment. Completion is recorded by the branch manager and reported to the Office of the Compliance Officer within five business days of the session.
