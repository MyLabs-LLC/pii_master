DIVISION OF PUBLIC HEALTH
RESTRICTED INFORMATION TRAINING — FACILITATOR OUTLINE
Policy ADM-114 | Session length 45 minutes | Calendar year 2004

1.0  PURPOSE
     Establish a uniform standard for identifying restricted information,
     applying the correct release authority, and recognizing the
     administrative and legal consequences of mishandling.

2.0  CLASSIFICATION AND RELEASE AUTHORITY

TIER  CATEGORY OF INFORMATION        DISCLOSURE AUTHORITY        FORM
----  -----------------------------  --------------------------  --------
1     Communicable disease registry  Health Officer, in writing  HD-207
2     Clinical treatment record      Custodian of Records        HD-114
2     Vital records file             Registrar of Vital          HD-031
                                     Statistics
3     Eligibility determination      Program Manager             HD-556
3     Employee personnel file        Human Resources Director    HD-002
4     Aggregate statistical report   Public Information Officer  HD-901

2.1  Tier 1 and Tier 2 records carry the following field labels and are
     closed in their entirety: Patient Name, Date of Birth, Medical Record
     Number, Social Security Number, Diagnosis Code, Contact Name, Home
     Address, Telephone Number, Guardian Name.

2.2  Suppression of a single field does not reduce the tier. A record is
     reclassified to Tier 4 only after certification by the Custodian of
     Records that no direct or indirect identifier remains.

3.0  CONSEQUENCES

3.1  Administrative. Progressive discipline under the personnel rules:
     documented counseling, written reprimand, suspension without pay,
     revocation of information system credentials, dismissal.

3.2  Regulatory. Civil monetary penalties assessed against the department
     per violation, with separate counts for each record disclosed.

3.3  Criminal. Knowing disclosure is a misdemeanor; disclosure under false
     pretenses or for commercial advantage or personal gain is a felony.

3.4  Professional. Mandatory referral to the applicable licensing board for
     any credentialed employee.

4.0  SCENARIO EXERCISE — respond in writing, 10 minutes

4.1  A program assistant transcribes a list of clients due for follow-up
     onto a legal pad, carries the pad to a community outreach event, and
     leaves it in an unattended tote bag for the duration of the event.
     Identify the tier, the violation, and the required report.

4.2  A supervisor receives a subpoena from a private attorney demanding the
     complete clinic file of a party to a civil suit. The subpoena is not
     accompanied by a court order or a signed authorization. State the
     correct response and the office to which the document is routed.

4.3  An analyst merges an eligibility extract with a disease registry
     extract to study service gaps. The merged file retains a client
     identifier column. State whether the merged file is releasable to a
     university partner and under what instrument.

4.4  A staff member discovers that a departing colleague's system account
     remains active three weeks after separation and has been used to open
     records. State the two reports required and their deadlines.

5.0  ANSWER KEY (facilitator copy)
     4.1  Tier 2; unsecured storage outside a departmental facility;
          incident report to the Custodian of Records within one business
          day. 4.2  Do not produce; route to the Office of Legal Counsel
          for objection or protective order. 4.3  Not releasable; requires
          a data use agreement and either a limited data set or written
          authorization. 4.4  Security incident report and access audit
          request, both within one business day of discovery.
