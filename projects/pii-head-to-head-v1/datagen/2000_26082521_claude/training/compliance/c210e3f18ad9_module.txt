SLIDE SCRIPT — "WHAT WE PROTECT AND WHY"
New Hire Orientation, Segment 4. Nine minutes. Presenter: Branch Compliance Lead.

    SLIDE 1 — Title
        On screen: Restricted Information at the Branch
        Say: Four categories. Two rules. One reporting number.

    SLIDE 2 — The four categories
        On screen (labels only):
            Identifiers: Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Work Authorization Document Number
            Financial: Deposit Account Number, Bank Routing Number, Pay Card Number
            Health: Drug Screen Result, Accommodation Request Type, Vaccination Status
            Adjudication: Consumer Report Reference, Adjudication Outcome
        Say: If a field carries one of these labels, it is Restricted regardless of where you found it.

    SLIDE 3 — The two rules
        On screen:
            Rule 1 — Restricted data moves through the portal, never through mail or messaging.
            Rule 2 — Health and adjudication data never enters the candidate record or requisition notes.

    SLIDE 4 — Consequences
        On screen:
            Entitlements suspended on the day a violation is confirmed.
            Corrective action up to and including termination.
            Client notification and, where triggered, state breach notification.
        Say: The agency absorbs the fine. The individual absorbs the corrective action.

    SLIDE 5 — Two scenarios
        Read aloud, take a show of hands:
            One. A candidate texts a photograph of a Social Security card to a recruiter's work phone. What is the correct disposition?
            Two. A supervisor asks why a worker was placed on a restricted duty assignment. What may be disclosed?
        Answers: One — do not save or forward; direct the candidate to the portal; delete after the entry is made from the portal copy. Two — the restriction as it affects assignment fit; nothing from the medical file.

    SLIDE 6 — Close
        On screen: Report suspected exposure to the Records Custodian within one hour.
