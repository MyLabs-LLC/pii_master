COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
OFFICE OF THE PRIVACY OFFICER
STAFF COMPLIANCE HANDOUT — FORM HD-PRV-101 (Rev. 03/2004)
SUBJECT: CATEGORIES OF RESTRICTED INFORMATION AND STAFF DUTIES

SECTION I — PURPOSE AND AUTHORITY
1.1 This handout is issued under Department Policy 4-17 and implements the federal Standards for Privacy of Individually Identifiable Health Information, the state public health records act, and applicable county personnel rules.
1.2 This handout applies to all employees, temporary staff, students, contractors, and volunteers assigned to any division, clinic, or field program of the Department.
1.3 Completion of the annual session and execution of Section V is a condition of continued system access.

SECTION II — CATEGORIES OF RESTRICTED INFORMATION
2.1 Category A — Protected Health Information. Any information created or received by the Department that relates to the physical or mental health of an individual, the provision of care, or payment for care, and that is linked to any of the following data elements:
    (a) Name; (b) Street Address; (c) City, County, and ZIP Code; (d) Date of Birth; (e) Admission Date; (f) Discharge Date; (g) Date of Death; (h) Telephone Number; (i) Facsimile Number; (j) Electronic Mail Address; (k) Social Security Number; (l) Medical Record Number; (m) Health Plan Beneficiary Number; (n) Account Number; (o) Certificate or License Number; (p) Vehicle Identifier; (q) Device Identifier; (r) Internet Protocol Address; (s) Biometric Identifier; (t) Full-Face Photographic Image.
2.2 Category B — Specially Protected Records. Records subject to heightened restriction by statute, including communicable disease and HIV/AIDS case reports, sexually transmitted disease investigation files, tuberculosis directly observed therapy logs, substance abuse treatment records governed by federal confidentiality regulation, family planning program records, and child fatality review materials.
2.3 Category C — Vital Records. Certificates and worksheets of birth, death, fetal death, marriage, and dissolution held by the Registrar.
2.4 Category D — Program Eligibility Data. Applications, certification files, and benefit issuance records of nutrition, immunization assistance, and indigent care programs.
2.5 Category E — Administrative Confidential Data. Personnel files, employee health and injury reports, investigative files, sealed bid materials, and information security configurations.

SECTION III — MINIMUM HANDLING REQUIREMENTS
3.1 Access shall be limited to the minimum necessary to perform an assigned duty. Curiosity is not a duty.
3.2 Paper records shall be secured in a locked cabinet at the close of business and shall not be removed from the premises absent written authorization from the Records Custodian.
3.3 Facsimile transmission requires a cover sheet bearing the confidentiality legend and pre-verification of the receiving number.
3.4 Electronic mail shall not carry Category A or Category B information outside the Department network.
3.5 Portable media, including diskettes, compact discs, and laptop computers, shall be inventoried and encrypted where the capability exists.
3.6 Verbal disclosure in corridors, elevators, waiting areas, and the parking structure is prohibited.
3.7 Any suspected loss, theft, or improper disclosure shall be reported to the Privacy Officer before the end of the shift on which it is discovered.

SECTION IV — CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING
4.1 Progressive discipline under county personnel rules, up to and including dismissal.
4.2 Referral to the applicable licensing board.
4.3 Civil monetary penalties assessed against the Department and, in cases of knowing misuse, criminal penalties including fine and imprisonment.
4.4 Referral to the District Attorney or United States Attorney where the facts warrant.
4.5 Termination of contract and removal of premises access for non-employees.
