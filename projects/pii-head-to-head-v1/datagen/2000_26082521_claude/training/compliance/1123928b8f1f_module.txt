PARTICIPANT HANDOUT 4-A — QUICK REFERENCE ON RESTRICTED CATEGORIES
For retention at the work station. Not for distribution outside the Authority.

1.0  GOVERNING PRINCIPLE
     1.1  Access is granted on the narrowest basis consistent with the performance of assigned duties, and is withdrawn when that basis ceases to exist.
     1.2  The holder of a credential is accountable for every action performed under that credential, irrespective of who performed it.

2.0  CATEGORY TABLE — FIELDS THAT TRIGGER RESTRICTED HANDLING
     2.1  Rider systems: Cardholder Name | Fare Media Serial Number | Registered Account Number | Payment Instrument on File | Tap History (Station, Route, Timestamp)
     2.2  Paratransit: Applicant Name | Date of Birth | Eligibility Determination Code | Mobility Aid Type | Certifying Clinician | Residential Pickup Location
     2.3  Human resources: Employee Identification Number | Social Security Number | Date of Birth | Home Address | Direct Deposit Account Number | Grievance File Reference
     2.4  Security: Incident Report Number | CCTV Segment Identifier | Officer Assignment Roster | Facility Access Control Log
     2.5  Legal and procurement: Claim File Number | Settlement Terms | Sealed Bid Amount | Attorney Work Product Designation

3.0  PERMITTED AND PROHIBITED HANDLING
     3.1  Permitted: viewing within the system of record; printing to a secured device where a documented need exists; transmitting internally by encrypted means to a recipient with an equivalent need.
     3.2  Prohibited: photographing a screen; forwarding to a personal mail account; discussing case specifics in an operator break room, on a platform, or over an open radio channel; retaining a printed record beyond the business day without secured storage.
     3.3  Disposal: cross-cut shredding or deposit in a designated secure destruction receptacle. Ordinary refuse and recycling containers are not authorized.

4.0  ESCALATION PATH
     4.1  First tier — immediate supervisor.
     4.2  Second tier — Divisional Records Custodian.
     4.3  Third tier — Compliance Duty Officer, or the Ethics Hotline extension published on the intranet directory, which accepts anonymous reports.

5.0  ACKNOWLEDGMENT
     5.1  By recording completion of this module, the participant affirms that the foregoing obligations have been read and are understood, and that questions concerning application to particular duties have been directed to the Records Custodian.
