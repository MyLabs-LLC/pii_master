ITEM 1.
A caller telephones the main office, states that they are the noncustodial parent of a currently enrolled student, and requests the student's current class schedule and attendance record. The office does not have a court order on file addressing access to records. The appropriate response is:
  (a) Provide the record, as a parent is entitled to access.
  (b) Decline all access permanently.
  (c) Decline to release by telephone, verify parental status and the absence of a limiting order through the Registrar, and route the request in writing.
  (d) Refer the caller to the classroom teacher.

ITEM 2.
A staff member requires a roster of students receiving related services in order to prepare a bulletin board display recognizing progress. The staff member holds no assigned responsibility for the students named. The appropriate response is:
  (a) Provide the roster; the purpose is laudatory.
  (b) Provide the roster with last names removed.
  (c) Decline; the requester lacks a legitimate educational interest, and disability status is restricted irrespective of intent.
  (d) Provide the roster upon verbal approval of a colleague.

ITEM 3.
A cafeteria worker is asked by a volunteer why a particular student is not charged for meals. The appropriate response is:
  (a) Explain the eligibility determination.
  (b) State that meal eligibility status is confidential by statute and decline to discuss the matter.
  (c) Refer the volunteer to the student.
  (d) Confirm eligibility without stating the basis.

ITEM 4.
A staff member discovers that a printed report containing student identification numbers and assessment scores has been left in a shared workroom overnight. The appropriate response is:
  (a) Return the report to the originating office and take no further action.
  (b) Shred the report and take no further action.
  (c) Secure the report immediately and report the occurrence to the building administrator the same working day for entry in the incident log.
  (d) Determine first whether any unauthorized person actually viewed it.

ITEM 5.
A staff member wishes to transport an electronic file containing the medication administration log to a home computer in order to complete data entry outside of duty hours. The appropriate response is:
  (a) Permissible if the diskette is labeled.
  (b) Permissible with verbal supervisor approval.
  (c) Not permissible; restricted electronic records shall not be removed from District systems absent written authorization from the Records Custodian.
  (d) Permissible if the file is renamed.
