MEMORANDUM

TO: All Personnel, Divisions of Water Operations, Electric Distribution, Customer Accounts, and Field Services
FROM: Office of the General Counsel / Records Custodian
RE: Mandatory Annual Information Handling Compliance Instruction — Restricted Information Categories, Attendant Liabilities, and Competency Assessment
CLASSIFICATION: Internal Use Only — Not for Public Distribution

I. PURPOSE AND AUTHORITY

Pursuant to the Municipal Records Retention Ordinance, the state Public Records Act exemptions applicable to utility customer information, and the Authority's Board-adopted Information Governance Policy IG-114, all personnel are hereby required to complete this instruction and the appended competency assessment prior to the close of the current calendar quarter. Failure to complete constitutes an independent violation reportable to the employee's Division Director irrespective of whether any mishandling has in fact occurred.

II. CATEGORIES OF RESTRICTED INFORMATION

The following data elements, whether stored in the Customer Information System, in field service tablets, in printed work orders, or in any correspondence file, are designated RESTRICTED. Staff shall neither disclose, transmit, reproduce, nor discuss these elements outside the scope of a documented business necessity:

  Category A — Customer Identity Elements
      Field labels: Account Holder Name; Service Address; Mailing Address;
      Telephone Number (Primary / Alternate); Email Address; Date of Birth;
      Social Security Number (last four digits as stored); Driver License Number;
      Employer Name.

  Category B — Financial and Payment Elements
      Field labels: Bank Routing Number; Bank Account Number; Payment Card
      Number; Card Expiration; Autopay Authorization Reference; Delinquency
      Balance; Payment Arrangement Terms; Collections Referral Status.

  Category C — Protected Program Participation
      Field labels: Low-Income Assistance Enrollment Status; Medical Necessity
      Certification Flag; Life-Support Equipment Designation; Certifying
      Provider Name; Shutoff Protection Expiration.

  Category D — Confidential Address Designations
      Field labels: Address Confidentiality Program Participant Flag;
      Substitute Mailing Designation; Protective Order Reference Number.

  Category E — Critical Infrastructure and Operational Security
      Field labels: SCADA Endpoint Identifier; Reservoir Access Code;
      Substation Keying Schedule; Vulnerability Assessment Finding;
      Backflow Cross-Connection Survey Result.

  Category F — Personnel and Labor Relations
      Field labels: Employee Identification Number; Compensation Grade;
      Disciplinary Action Disposition; Occupational Injury Claim Number;
      Benefit Election.

III. CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING

Improper disclosure, whether willful or negligent, may result in: (a) progressive discipline up to and including termination under Personnel Rule 12.4; (b) personal civil liability under the state consumer records statute, for which the Authority expressly declines to provide indemnification where the conduct is found to be outside the course and scope of employment; (c) criminal referral where the disclosure involves Category D or Category E elements; (d) mandatory breach notification to affected customers and to the Attorney General, the cost of which shall be charged against the responsible Division's operating budget; and (e) forfeiture of system access credentials pending investigation.

IV. COMPETENCY ASSESSMENT

Respond in writing. Retain a copy; submit the original to the Records Custodian.

  1. A caller identifies as the spouse of an account holder, states the service
     address correctly, and requests the current balance and the last four
     digits of the payment card on file. The caller is not listed as an
     authorized party. What is the permissible response?

  2. A field crew member photographs a completed work order to document a meter
     condition. The photograph incidentally captures the Account Holder Name
     and Service Address fields. The crew member transmits the photograph via
     personal messaging application to a colleague. Identify each violation.

  3. A local reporter submits a public records request for a list of all
     accounts carrying a Medical Necessity Certification Flag within a
     specified service zone, asserting a public interest in shutoff practices.
     State the applicable exemption and the required routing of the request.

  4. A customer service representative observes an Address Confidentiality
     Program Participant Flag while processing a routine billing inquiry and
     mentions the flag aloud in an open service lobby. Characterize the
     severity and the required incident report timeline.

  5. A vendor engaged for meter data analytics requests a bulk extract
     containing Account Number, Consumption Interval, and Service Address.
     No executed data protection addendum is on file. State the required
     action prior to any extract being generated.

V. ACKNOWLEDGMENT

By completing the assessment, the undersigned affirms comprehension of the foregoing categories and consequences and acknowledges the continuing obligation of confidentiality, which obligation survives separation from employment.
