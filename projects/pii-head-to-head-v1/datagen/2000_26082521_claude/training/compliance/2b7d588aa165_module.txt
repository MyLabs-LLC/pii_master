INFORMATION SAFEGUARDS — ANNUAL STAFF TRAINING HANDOUT
Compliance Administration, Title and Escrow Operations
Revision 2, effective March 2004. Supersedes Revision 1 in its entirety.
Distribution: branch managers, escrow officers, title examiners, funding and recording clerks, receptionists, temporary and contract personnel.

1. SCOPE. This handout states the Company's classification of restricted information and the handling rules applicable to each class. It is issued under the Information Security Program adopted pursuant to the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act and the implementing Safeguards Rule, and under the terms of the Company's underwriter agency agreements. Observance is a condition of continued employment and of continued agency appointment. Interpretive questions are routed to the Records Custodian. No other position is authorized to grant an exception, and no exception is effective unless entered in the exception log maintained at the administrative office.

2. CATEGORY I — NONPUBLIC PERSONAL INFORMATION. Category I is information obtained from a consumer in connection with a settlement service and not otherwise lawfully of public record. The governing fields are: Borrower Name; Co-Borrower Name; Social Security Number; Taxpayer Identification Number; Date of Birth; Driver's License Number; Residence Telephone Number; Marital Status; Prior Address History; Employer and Position; Gross Monthly Income. Category I is disclosed only to a party to the transaction, that party's designated agent of record, the lender of record, the underwriter, or a governmental authority acting under compulsory process. Disclosure to a real estate licensee is limited to the fields necessary to close and does not extend to the first three fields listed above.

3. CATEGORY II — FINANCIAL ACCOUNT AND DISBURSEMENT DATA. Fields: Escrow File Number; Lender Loan Number; Payoff Demand Account Number; Depository Account Number; ABA Routing Number; Wire Beneficiary Account Number; Earnest Money Instrument Number; Credit Score. Category II is handled inside the funding function only. Verbal release of any Category II field over an inbound telephone call is prohibited without callback verification against the number of record in the file, and the callback is noted on the file activity log.

4. CATEGORY III — TRANSACTION WORK PRODUCT. Preliminary reports, chain of title abstracts, judgment and lien search results, escrow instructions, settlement statement line detail, commission disbursement schedules, and underwriter loss reserve correspondence. Release outside the transaction requires the written consent of the Records Custodian.

5. HANDLING. Files are returned to the locked file room at the close of business; desks are cleared. Files are not removed from the premises. Facsimile transmission requires the standard cover sheet and confirmation of the receiving number before the first page is sent. No Category I or Category II field is placed in an electronic mail subject line. Paper carrying any Category I or Category II field is destroyed by cross-cut shredder, not deposited in general waste, in accordance with the disposal requirements of the Fair and Accurate Credit Transactions Act. Diskettes and print ribbons are surrendered to the Records Custodian.

6. CONSEQUENCES. A first substantiated mishandling results in written warning and repeat training. A second results in suspension without pay. Disclosure for consideration, or disclosure enabling a fraudulent disbursement, results in termination and referral to the appropriate prosecuting authority. The Company's exposure is separate and additional: cancellation of agency appointment by the underwriter, examination findings and administrative penalty by the state Department of Insurance, and civil liability to the consumer. Employees are advised that individual liability is not indemnified where the conduct was intentional.
