MEMORANDUM

TO:      All Certificated and Classified Staff
FROM:    Office of the Records Custodian
CC:      Building Principals; Director of Technology Services
DATE:    August 22, 2011
RE:      Mandatory Annual Confidentiality Training — Restricted Information

Acknowledgment Form R-14 is due to the building secretary no later than September 9, 2011. Non-return will be reported to Human Resources and will suspend system credentials until cured.

1. PURPOSE
Annual training required under Board Policy 4360 and applicable federal law. Attendance at a building session does not substitute for the signed acknowledgment.

2. CATEGORIES OF RESTRICTED INFORMATION

   Tier 1 — Confidential Student Record Data. Applicable field labels include:
   Student ID; Date of Birth; Home Address; Guardian Telephone; Guardian
   Electronic Mail; Free/Reduced Meal Eligibility; Special Education
   Eligibility Code; IEP Goal Narrative; Section 504 Plan Status; Health
   Condition Note; Immunization Record; Medication Administration Log;
   Disciplinary Action Code; Attendance Reason Code; Home Language Survey
   Response; State Assessment Scale Score; Custody Order Flag.

   Tier 2 — Confidential Employee Data. Applicable field labels include:
   Employee ID; Social Security Number; Date of Birth; Home Address; Bank
   Routing Number; Bank Account Number; Direct Deposit Allocation; Insurance
   Dependent Roster; Evaluation Rating; Grievance File Number; Leave Reason
   Code; Background Check Disposition.

   Tier 3 — Directory Information. Releasable only where no opt-out is
   recorded on Form D-2. Verify opt-out status in the student information
   system before any release, including yearbook, athletics program, or press
   inquiry.

3. PROHIBITED PRACTICES
   3.1 Transmission of Tier 1 or Tier 2 fields through personal web-mail.
   3.2 Storage of any Tier 1 or Tier 2 export on an unencrypted flash drive,
       personal laptop, or home computer.
   3.3 Printing of class rosters, eligibility lists, or discipline reports to
       an unattended shared printer.
   3.4 Discussion of individual student matters in hallways, staff lounges,
       parking areas, or on social networking sites.
   3.5 Sharing of network credentials with a substitute, volunteer, student
       aide, or family member.

4. CONSEQUENCES
Mishandling is subject to progressive discipline: written reprimand;
suspension of system access; suspension without pay; recommendation for
termination; non-renewal. Certain disclosures carry referral to the county
prosecutor and to the state licensing board. The District is separately
obligated to notify affected households and, above the applicable threshold,
the state education agency. Cost of notification may be assessed against the
originating department budget.

5. SCENARIO REVIEW

   5.1 A paraprofessional forwards a spreadsheet of meal eligibility codes to
       a personal account to finish sorting at home.
       Improper. Tier 1 export off network. Report same day.

   5.2 A coach requests a roster showing name and grade level for a program
       insert.
       Permissible only after opt-out verification on Form D-2.

   5.3 A teacher leaves a printed discipline summary face-up in the shared
       workroom overnight.
       Improper. Unsecured Tier 1 output. Complete Form IR-2.

   5.4 A front office clerk is telephoned by a caller asserting guardian
       status and requesting an address on file.
       Do not release. Refer to the Records Custodian for identity
       verification.

   5.5 A building substitute asks to borrow a gradebook login for the day.
       Deny. Request a temporary account through Technology Services.

Direct questions to the Office of the Records Custodian, Administration
Building, Room 112.
