PART TWO — CLAUSES

1.0 A field label appearing anywhere on a page classifies the entire page, and the classification travels with every copy, extract, working note, and screen print made from it.

2.0 Access is granted by assignment to an open order and expires when the order closes or is cancelled. Curiosity, familiarity with a party, and the fact that a transaction is publicly discussed in the market are not business reasons and do not create access.

3.0 Outbound movement is permitted by sealed courier pouch, by confirmed fax preceded by a telephone call to the receiving station, or by hand delivery to a party of record who has been identified. No restricted content is carried on diskette or portable media outside the office without the written authorization of the Compliance Officer.

4.0 A suspected or actual disclosure is reported to the branch manager immediately and to the Office of the Compliance Officer in writing within one business day. The reporting employee is protected from retaliation. Failure to report is treated as an independent violation and is weighed more heavily in discipline than the underlying disclosure.

5.0 Consequences include regulatory examination findings and civil penalty against the Company, action against the escrow license held by the branch and its officers, claims against the fidelity bond, discipline of the employee through and including termination, and referral for criminal prosecution where an identifier has been misused for gain.

PART THREE — SCENARIO EXAMINATION

6.1 A caller states that a closing is at risk, declines to supply the Escrow File Number, and offers to verify identity by reciting a Date of Birth and the last digits of a Deposit Account Number. What is the required action?

6.2 A lender package containing pages labeled Loan Number, Gross Monthly Income, and Credit Score is found in the shared fax tray at the start of the following business day. What is the required action, and what is the reporting deadline?

6.3 A real estate licensee requests a copy of the buyer's loan application on the ground that the transaction is a shared listing. State the classification, the permitted recipients, and the response.

6.4 During funding, a caller instructs that the Wire Beneficiary Account be changed from the account on the signed instruction. State the two independent verifications required before any action is taken.

6.5 An employee accepting a position with a competitor asks to retain a spreadsheet containing Escrow File Number, Payoff Balance, and Employer Name. State the classification and the required response.

6.6 A title examiner is asked by a relative whether a particular property is in escrow with this office. State the applicable clause.

ANSWER KEY — 6.1 Decline; return call to the number of record in the file. 6.2 Secure the pages in a locked drawer, notify the branch manager immediately, report in writing within one business day. 6.3 Restricted; file team and parties of record only; decline and refer the request to the lender. 6.4 Callback to the party of record at the number in the file, plus written confirmation countersigned by the escrow officer. 6.5 Restricted and internal; refuse, retrieve any copy, and report under clause 4.0. 6.6 Clause 2.0; the existence of an order is itself not disclosed.
