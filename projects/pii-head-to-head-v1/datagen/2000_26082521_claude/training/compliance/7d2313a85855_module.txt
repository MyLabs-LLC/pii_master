BILLING OFFICE COMPLIANCE BULLETIN 04-09
Refresher Instruction — Restricted Information
Posted by the Privacy Officer for reading at the weekly staff huddle

(1) The following instruction is issued between annual training cycles and is effective on posting. Supervisors shall read it aloud at the huddle and shall obtain the acknowledgment below from each person present.

(2) Restricted information is not limited to the medical record. It includes every field label appearing on the face sheet, the itemized statement, the claim form, and the collection screen, among them Patient Name, Medical Record Number, Account Number, Date of Birth, Social Security Number, Guarantor Name, Subscriber Identification Number, Group Number, Diagnosis Code, Date of Service, and Patient Balance.

(3) Discussion of an account in the corridor, the elevator, the cafeteria, or the parking structure is a disclosure. The absence of an apparent listener is not a defense.

(4) Screens are to be cleared before a walk-up visitor is admitted to the work area. Reports are to be collected from the printer immediately and not at the end of the shift.

(5) A lapse is handled under progressive discipline. A deliberate access, or any disclosure for personal benefit, results in termination and referral, and carries personal civil and criminal exposure that the facility does not indemnify.

(6) Report any suspected disclosure the same business day.
