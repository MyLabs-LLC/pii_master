MEMORANDUM — SLIDE SCRIPT, MODULE 4 REFRESHER (FIVE MINUTES)
Office of Compliance and Records Governance | Branch Meeting Packet | June 2021

SLIDE 1 — WHAT IS RESTRICTED
Read aloud: 1. Four field groups are restricted at all times. 2. Identity and tax: Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Work Authorization Document Number. 3. Payroll: Bank Routing Number, Deposit Account Number, Garnishment Order Reference. 4. Screening: Drug Screen Result, Workers' Compensation Claim Number. 5. Contact: Residential Street Address, Personal Mobile Number, Personal Electronic Mail Address.

SLIDE 2 — WHAT LEAVES THE BUILDING
Read aloud: 6. Only these travel to a client: Assignment ID, Position Title, Start Date, Shift Code, Billing Rate. 7. Everything else is refused and referred to the Account Compliance Lead.

SLIDE 3 — WHAT HAPPENS IF YOU GET IT WRONG
Read aloud: 8. Counseling and re-certification on a first finding. 9. Entitlement suspension and possible termination where identity or payroll fields are involved. 10. Statutory notification costs and client contract penalties are charged to the branch.

SLIDE 4 — QUICK QUIZ, HANDS UP
Read aloud: 11. A branch prints a payroll register and leaves it on the counter during a client tour — which control failed? 12. A recruiter texts a start date and a personal mobile number to a client supervisor — which part is the violation? 13. An account manager is asked for the reason a worker failed screening — what is the answer?

Close: 14. Submit quiz cards to the Records Custodian before leaving.
