OFFICE OF THE UNIVERSITY REGISTRAR
STUDENT RECORDS CONFIDENTIALITY — ANNUAL STAFF TRAINING HANDOUT
Required of all registrar staff, student assistants, and temporary clerical hires
Academic year 2011-2012

BEFORE YOU HANDLE ANY RECORD
- Confirm you have completed the annual confidentiality acknowledgment on file with the Records Custodian.
- Confirm your student information system access level matches your current job duties. Access is granted by role, not by seniority or tenure.
- Confirm you have a legitimate educational interest in the record before you open it. Curiosity, personal acquaintance, and "checking on a friend" are not legitimate educational interests.

CATEGORY A — DESIGNATED DIRECTORY INFORMATION
May be released to a third party only when no confidentiality hold is present on the record.
- Full Name
- Enrollment Status (full-time / part-time / half-time)
- Dates of Attendance (term-level only, not daily attendance)
- Major Field of Study
- Degrees, Certificates, and Awards Received
- Date of Degree Conferral
- Participation in Officially Recognized Activities and Sports
- Height and Weight of Members of Athletic Teams
- Most Recent Previous Educational Institution Attended

CATEGORY B — RESTRICTED (NON-DIRECTORY) INFORMATION
Never released without prior written, signed, and dated consent from the student, or under a documented statutory exception approved by the Records Custodian.
- Social Security Number
- Student Identification Number
- Date of Birth
- Place of Birth
- Citizenship and Visa Status
- Race, Ethnicity, and Gender
- Permanent and Local Mailing Address
- Home and Mobile Telephone Number
- University-Assigned Electronic Mail Address
- Grades, Grade Point Average, and Class Rank
- Course Schedule, Room Assignment, and Meeting Times
- Test Scores (admissions, placement, and standardized)
- Academic Standing, Probation, and Dismissal Actions
- Disciplinary Records and Conduct Sanctions
- Financial Aid Award Amounts and Need Determination
- Student Account Balance, Payment History, and Third-Party Billing Authorization
- Parent or Guardian Name, Income, and Contact Information
- Disability Accommodation Records
- Immunization and Health Clearance Records
- Emergency Contact Information

CONFIDENTIALITY HOLDS
- A hold suppresses Category A as well as Category B. When a hold is present, the correct response is that no information is available for release. Do not confirm or deny enrollment.
- Holds are placed and removed only by the Records Custodian upon written request. Front-counter staff may not lift a hold, not even at the student's verbal request at the window.

CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING
- Immediate suspension of student information system credentials pending review.
- Written reprimand placed in the personnel file; repeat findings support termination of employment or dismissal of a student assistant.
- Referral to Human Resources and to University Counsel for review under the state public employee conduct standards.
- Complaint filed by the student with the federal Family Policy Compliance Office, which may investigate the office as a whole.
- Institutional exposure to loss of federal funding eligibility.
- Personal liability is not covered by the university when disclosure was willful.

SCENARIO CHECK — MARK RELEASE OR NO RELEASE
- A caller states they are a parent, provides the student's Student Identification Number correctly, and asks for current term grades. No consent form is on file.
- A university police officer requests a Course Schedule in connection with an active safety investigation and puts the request in writing.
- A prospective employer asks whether a former student received a degree and in what Major Field of Study. No hold is present.
- A faculty member from an unrelated department asks for the Permanent Mailing Address of a student who owes them a library item.
- A student at the counter asks staff to read their own Grade Point Average aloud, and presents a university photo credential.

Answers: no release; release upon written record and Records Custodian log entry; release; no release; release after identity verification.
