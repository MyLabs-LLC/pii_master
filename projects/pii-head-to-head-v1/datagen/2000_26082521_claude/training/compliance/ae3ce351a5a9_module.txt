STORE COMPLIANCE BRIEFING — RESTRICTED DATA AWARENESS
Briefing Number: SB-2021-19
Issued By: Office of the Privacy Officer
Distribution: All Store Locations — Pharmacy and Front Store
Posting Requirement: Break room board, 30 consecutive days

PART I — PURPOSE
i. This briefing instructs all colleagues on the classification of information handled in the course of duty, the obligations attaching to each classification, and the sanctions for mishandling.
ii. This briefing supplements and does not replace the annual certification recorded on Form CT-114.

PART II — CLASSIFICATION MATRIX
CLASS | REPRESENTATIVE FIELD LABELS | PERMITTED LOCATION | DESTRUCTION METHOD
R1 Health | Patient Name; Date of Birth; Prescription Number; Drug Name and Strength; Prescriber Name; Allergy List; Immunization Record | Pharmacy system; secured will-call bin | Secure shred
R2 Payment | Cardholder Name; Primary Account Number; Expiration Date; Card Verification Value | Payment terminal only | Not to be recorded outside terminal
R3 Identifier | Social Security Number; Driver's License Number; Insurance Member Identifier; Group Number | Enrollment screen; insurer portal | Secure shred
R4 Colleague | Employee Identification Number; Home Address; Personal Telephone Number; Pay Rate; Leave Status | Manager workstation | Secure shred
R5 Business | Reimbursement rate schedules; store volume reports; safe combination; camera layout | Manager workstation | Secure shred

PART III — OBLIGATIONS
iii. No class listed in Part II may be recorded on personal paper, a personal device, a personal notebook, or any surface outside an approved system.
iv. Class R2 may not be written down under any circumstance, including for the purpose of re-keying a declined transaction.
v. Class R1 held at will-call shall be stored label-inward and released only after identity verification and, where the requester is not the person named on the label, verification of the authorization on file.
vi. Vaccination administration records generated at the store are Class R1 and are transmitted only through the state registry interface. Photographs of completed record cards are prohibited.
vii. Screens facing a public aisle shall be angled or shielded. Privacy filters are stocked and available on request from the Pharmacy Manager.
viii. Drive-through and curbside handoffs are subject to the same verification standard as counter handoffs.

PART IV — SANCTION SCHEDULE
ix. First finding, negligent: documented coaching, retraining within 7 days.
x. First finding, willful access without a business purpose: termination and referral for regulatory reporting.
xi. Any finding involving Class R2 with evidence of exfiltration: termination, referral to law enforcement, and card brand notification.
xii. Failure to report a known incident within the reporting window is itself a sanctionable finding, independent of the underlying event.

PART V — SCENARIO CHECK
xiii. A person at the counter asks a colleague to look up whether a household member's prescription has been filled, stating that they will collect it. No authorization appears on the profile. State the required action.
xiv. A colleague finds a printed profile left on the copier by an earlier shift. State the required action and the required destination for the document.
xv. A prescriber's office calls and asks for the last four digits of a member identifier to complete a prior authorization. State whether this is a permitted disclosure and on what basis.
xvi. A district visitor requests a copy of the store's controlled substance count sheet by photograph, to send from a personal phone. State the required action.
