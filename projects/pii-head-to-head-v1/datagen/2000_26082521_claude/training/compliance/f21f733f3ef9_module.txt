SLIDE 1 of 8 — TITLE
ON SCREEN: "What You May See. What You May Say. What You May Send."
SCRIPT: 1.1 State the purpose of the session. 1.2 Advise attendees that the sign-in roster is the official attendance record and that late arrivals must initial the roster margin.

SLIDE 2 of 8 — THE FOUR QUESTIONS
ON SCREEN: Who is asking? What do they want? Are they entitled? How much is enough?
SCRIPT: 2.1 Explain that every request, internal or external, is answered by working these four questions in order. 2.2 Emphasize that a badge is not an entitlement; assignment is.

SLIDE 3 of 8 — RESTRICTED CATEGORIES
ON SCREEN: Categories A through E of Form HD-PRV-101.
SCRIPT: 3.1 Read the five categories aloud. 3.2 Note that Category B records carry statutory restrictions that survive a general authorization form. 3.3 Direct attendees to Section II of the handout.

SLIDE 4 of 8 — MINIMUM NECESSARY
ON SCREEN: "Open the chart to the page you need, not the chart."
SCRIPT: 4.1 Illustrate with the clinic pull list. 4.2 State that access logs on the registry system are reviewed monthly by the Records Custodian.

SLIDE 5 of 8 — THE FAX, THE CART, THE CAR
ON SCREEN: Three failure points.
SCRIPT: 5.1 Misdirected facsimile; verify the number before transmitting. 5.2 Unattended records cart in a public corridor. 5.3 Files carried home in a private vehicle without written authorization.

SLIDE 6 of 8 — WHAT HAPPENS WHEN IT GOES WRONG
ON SCREEN: Discipline. Licensure. Penalty. Prosecution.
SCRIPT: 6.1 Walk Section IV of the handout. 6.2 State plainly that dismissal has occurred for records browsing without any further disclosure.

SLIDE 7 of 8 — REPORTING
ON SCREEN: Same shift. Every time. No exceptions.
SCRIPT: 7.1 Explain that self-reporting is a mitigating factor and concealment is an aggravating one. 7.2 Identify the Privacy Officer as the single point of intake.

SLIDE 8 of 8 — QUIZ AND SIGN-OUT
SCRIPT: 8.1 Distribute Form HD-PRV-103. 8.2 Allow ten minutes. 8.3 Collect quizzes and acknowledgment pages together; do not release attendees until both are received.
