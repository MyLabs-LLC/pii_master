CONFIDENTIALITY OF PROTECTED INFORMATION — MANDATORY ANNUAL IN-SERVICE
Staff Handout and Acknowledgment of Receipt
Form No. HR-114 (Rev. 08/2004) — Supersedes Form No. HR-114 (Rev. 09/2001)
Issuing Authority: Office of the Records Custodian, Department of Student Services
Applicable To: All certificated staff, classified staff, substitutes, contracted service providers, student teachers, and authorized volunteers

SECTION I — PURPOSE AND STATUTORY AUTHORITY
This handout is promulgated pursuant to the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (20 U.S.C. § 1232g; 34 C.F.R. Part 99), the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (34 C.F.R. Part 300, Subpart F), the Protection of Pupil Rights Amendment (20 U.S.C. § 1232h), the privacy standards adopted under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act as applicable to the District's self-funded plan, and Board Policy 8330 (Student Records) and Policy 8350 (Confidentiality). Nothing herein shall be construed to diminish any obligation imposed by state statute, administrative regulation, or the applicable collective bargaining agreement.

SECTION II — DEFINITIONS
2.1 "Education Record" shall mean any record, in any medium, directly related to an enrolled student and maintained by the District or by a party acting for the District.
2.2 "Legitimate Educational Interest" shall mean a demonstrable need to review a record in order to fulfill a professional responsibility assigned by the District.
2.3 "Disclosure" shall mean permitting access to, or the release, transfer, or other communication of, personally identifiable information by any means, including oral communication.
2.4 "Records Custodian" shall mean the administrator designated by the Superintendent to maintain custody of a given category of record.

SECTION III — CATEGORIES OF RESTRICTED INFORMATION
The following data elements are designated RESTRICTED and shall not be disclosed absent prior written consent or an enumerated statutory exception:

  Category A — Student Identity Elements: Student Name; Student Identification Number; State Testing Identifier; Date of Birth; Place of Birth; Social Security Number; Home Address; Home Telephone Number; Parent/Guardian Name; Parent/Guardian Employer.
  Category B — Academic and Disciplinary: Cumulative Grade Point Average; Individual Assessment Scores; Retention Status; Suspension and Expulsion Records; Attendance and Truancy Referrals.
  Category C — Special Education and Related Services: Evaluation Team Report; Individualized Education Program; Section 504 Accommodation Plan; Related Services Log; Disability Category.
  Category D — Health and Medical: Immunization Record; Medication Administration Log; School Nurse Encounter Note; Diagnosis; Health Plan Member Identifier; Free and Reduced Price Meal Eligibility Status.
  Category E — Employee Records: Personnel File Contents; Evaluation Instruments; Payroll Account and Routing Numbers; Insurance Enrollment Data; Investigatory Materials.

SECTION IV — CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING
4.1 Administrative. A substantiated violation shall be addressed through progressive discipline, up to and including a recommendation for termination of employment or nonrenewal of contract, and shall be documented in the personnel file.
4.2 Institutional. The Family Policy Compliance Office is empowered to require corrective action and, in cases of policy or practice violation, to direct the withholding of federal funds.
4.3 Civil. The District and the responsible individual may be subject to suit for invasion of privacy, defamation, or negligent supervision.
4.4 Licensure. Conduct constituting a breach of professional ethics shall be referred to the state licensure board.
4.5 Criminal. Unauthorized access to a District computer system is a criminal offense under applicable state computer-crimes statutes.
