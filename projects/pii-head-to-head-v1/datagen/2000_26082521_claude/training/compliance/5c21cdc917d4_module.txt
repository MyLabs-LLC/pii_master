CONTRACTOR COMPLIANCE PROGRAM
HANDOUT 4-B — RESTRICTED INFORMATION: CATEGORIES, DUTIES, AND PENALTIES
Issued for the 2016 recertification cycle
Owner: the Director of Compliance • Custodian of record: the Records Custodian
Distribution: all terminal, dispatch, billing, and yard personnel

§ 1  DUTY OF CARE
     — Each person to whom this handout is distributed occupies a position of trust and is charged with an
       affirmative and continuing obligation to safeguard information entrusted to the Company by its
       customers, its employees, and the agencies before which it holds operating authority.
     — This obligation attaches upon receipt of the information, survives the completion of the shipment to
       which it relates, and continues in force after separation from employment.

§ 2  RESTRICTED CATEGORIES
     — Workforce records. Any file, screen, or report displaying the fields: Employee Number, Social
       Security Number, Date of Birth, Home Address, Personal Telephone Number, Emergency Contact Name,
       Beneficiary Designation, Wage Rate, Direct Deposit Account Number.
     — Qualification and testing records. Fields including: Driver License Number, Endorsement Codes,
       Medical Examiner's Certificate Number, Drug and Alcohol Test Result, Specimen Identification
       Number, Safety Performance History Inquiry Response.
     — Payment records. Fields including: Primary Account Number, Cardholder Name, Expiration Date, Card
       Verification Value, Billing Postal Code, Remittance Reference.
     — Customer commercial records. Fields including: Account Name, Contract Rate, Fuel Surcharge Index,
       Volume Commitment, Shipper Reference, Consignee Name, Delivery Address, Commodity Description,
       Declared Value.
     — Security and export records. Fields including: Security Program Number, Screening Method, Seal
       Number, Route Restriction, Export Control Classification Number, License or Exemption Cited.

§ 3  PROHIBITED ACTS
     — Transmission of any restricted category to a personal email account, personal device, or removable
       media not issued and encrypted by the Company.
     — Retention of Sensitive Authentication Data after authorization has been obtained.
     — Discussion of a load's commodity, value, or routing in any public area, including a truck stop, a
       receiving lobby, or an open radio channel.
     — Photography of any document, screen, or trailer seal by means of a personal camera or telephone.

§ 4  PENALTIES
     — Internal: written warning, suspension without pay, or termination for cause, as determined by the
       Director of Compliance without regard to length of service.
     — External: civil monetary penalty assessed per violation; contractual indemnification of the affected
       customer; disqualification of the Company from bid participation; criminal exposure for the
       individual in matters involving export-controlled or security-sensitive material.

§ 5  SCENARIO ASSESSMENT — circle one response per item and return this page to the Records Custodian
     — Item 1. A yard employee is offered payment by an unfamiliar visitor in exchange for identifying which
       trailers are loaded and unsealed. Correct action: (a) decline and continue work; (b) decline and
       report to the Terminal Manager within the shift; (c) refer the visitor to dispatch.
     — Item 2. A billing clerk is asked by a coworker to email a customer's rate schedule to that coworker's
       personal account. Correct action: (a) send it, as the coworker is an employee; (b) refuse and report;
       (c) send it in a password-protected file.
     — Item 3. A supervisor observes a printed drug and alcohol test result left on a shared printer.
       Correct action: (a) leave it for its owner; (b) discard it in the general waste stream; (c) secure it
       and notify the Records Custodian.
     — Item 4. A driver is asked at a delivery point to state the declared value of the freight. Correct
       action: (a) state the value from the bill of lading; (b) decline and direct the requester to the
       shipper; (c) estimate the value.
