SPEAKER SCRIPT — SESSION 4: "WHAT WE PROTECT AND WHY"
Twenty-five minutes. Branch conference room. Handout distributed before the session; do not read the handout aloud.

SLIDE 1 (title card). Open by stating the purpose without preamble. We hold information that the consumer had no practical choice but to give us. That is the entire basis of the rule. Nobody applies for title insurance in order to share a Social Security Number; they do it because the lender required a policy. Make the point in one sentence and move on.

SLIDE 2 (the four categories). Walk the categories in order and name the fields on the card: Social Security Number, Taxpayer Identification Number, Date of Birth, Driver's License Number, Marital Status, Employer and Position under Category I; Escrow File Number, Lender Loan Number, Depository Account Number, ABA Routing Number, Wire Beneficiary Account Number, Credit Score under Category II. Do not let the room debate the boundary between Category II and Category III during the session; note the question and refer it to the Records Custodian.

SLIDE 3 (the three failure points). Historically our findings arise in three places and only three. First, the telephone — an inbound caller who sounds like a processor and is not verified. Second, the facsimile — a transposed receiving number and no confirmation before transmission. Third, the wastebasket — working copies discarded at the desk rather than shredded. Emphasize that none of the three involved a computer. Staff expect a lecture about computers and will discount everything else if they receive one.

SLIDE 4 (consequences). State the progressive discipline sequence plainly: written warning, suspension, termination with referral. Then state the Company-level consequence: agency appointment cancelled by the underwriter, examination finding by the Department of Insurance, civil action by the consumer. Do not soften this slide and do not editorialize about the regulator.

SLIDE 5 (quiz). Distribute the scenario sheet. Allow ten minutes. Collect and forward all sheets to Compliance Administration; scores are not reported to branch management and are not placed in the personnel file. Announce that fact before the sheets are handed out or the answers will be worthless.

Close by directing staff to sign the acknowledgment before leaving the room.
