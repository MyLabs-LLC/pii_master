TRAINING SLIDE SCRIPT — "WHAT MAY I SAY, AND TO WHOM?"
Patient Financial Services / Quarterly In-Service
Presenter: Compliance Coordinator or designee
Running time: twelve minutes. Overheads numbered to match.

SLIDE 1 — ON SCREEN: "Three Kinds of Restricted Information"
SAY: First, anything that identifies the patient — Patient Name, Medical Record Number, Account Number, Health Insurance Claim Number, Patient Birthdate, Guarantor Name. Second, anything clinical — Principal Diagnosis Code, Procedure Code, Admission Source, Discharge Status. Third, our own business terms — the chargemaster and the payer rate schedules.

SLIDE 2 — ON SCREEN: "The Extra-Protected Four"
SAY: Substance abuse treatment, HIV test results, psychiatric care, and minors' confidential services. A general consent form does not reach these. Route every such request to the Records Custodian without exception.

SLIDE 3 — ON SCREEN: "What Happens If"
SAY: Careless handling brings counseling and retraining. Looking at a record you have no business reason to open brings suspension or termination, even if you tell no one. Disclosure for gain brings termination and prosecution; the federal fine begins at fifty thousand dollars.

SLIDE 4 — ON SCREEN: "Two Situations"
SAY: One — a caller gives the correct account number and asks for the diagnosis. Do you release it? Two — an employee asks you to confirm whether a former coworker is currently an inpatient. What do you say, and to whom do you report the request?

SLIDE 5 — ON SCREEN: "Sign the roster on your way out."
