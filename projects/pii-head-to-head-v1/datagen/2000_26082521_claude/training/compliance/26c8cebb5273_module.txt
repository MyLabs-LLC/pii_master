TOOLBOX TALK 2016-09 — "WHAT YOU MAY NOT REPEAT"
Fifteen-minute dock briefing. To be read verbatim by the Shift Lead.

1.0  STANDING RULE
     1.1  No information created, received, or observed in the course of duty may be disclosed to any party who has not been established, in advance, as having both authorization and a demonstrated business need.
     1.2  Verbal disclosure constitutes disclosure. So does a photograph, a text message, and a document left face-up.

2.0  RESTRICTED FIELDS — RECOGNIZE THEM ON SIGHT
     2.1  On the driver file: Social Security Number, Date of Birth, License Number, Medical Certificate Expiration.
     2.2  On the bill of lading and manifest: Consignee Name, Delivery Address, Commodity Description, Declared Value, Seal Number.
     2.3  On the payment record: Primary Account Number, Expiration Date, Card Verification Value.
     2.4  On any page marked SSI: every field, without exception, including the header.

3.0  CONSEQUENCES
     3.1  Disclosure of a customer's Declared Value or Delivery Address has resulted, industry-wide, in cargo theft and in the termination of the disclosing employee.
     3.2  The Company reserves the right to pursue recovery of losses attributable to an unauthorized disclosure.

4.0  QUIZ — RAISE YOUR HAND
     4.1  A caller states they are the consignee's dispatcher and asks which trailer holds the high-value load. Do you answer?
     4.2  A coworker photographs a manifest to send to a friend who "wants to see the truck." Is this reportable?
     4.3  A visitor asks a lumper what commodity is on the outbound. What is the correct reply?
