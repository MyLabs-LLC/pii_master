COURSE RM-101 — SCENARIO EXERCISE
Complete individually. Ten minutes. Return the sheet to the instructor.

ACTION CODES
  A   Refer the request to the Records Custodian; take no other action
  B   Release; the record is public
  C   Secure the record at once and tell the immediate supervisor
  D   Decline, then enter the request in the disclosure register
  E   Report to the Security Manager within one hour
