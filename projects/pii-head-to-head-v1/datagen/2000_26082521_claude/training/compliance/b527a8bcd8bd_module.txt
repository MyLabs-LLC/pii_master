TELLER STATION REFERENCE CARD — DISCLOSURE STOPS
Post at the window. Do not remove from the branch.

BEFORE YOU SPEAK
- Confirm you are on an authorized account before opening the record.
- Verify the caller or member against two identifying elements, never the account number alone.
- Say nothing that confirms a relationship exists until verification is complete.

NEVER RELEASE WITHOUT COMPLIANCE APPROVAL
- Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Mother's Maiden Name
- Card Number, PIN, Password, Security Question Answer
- Credit Bureau Score or Report contents
- Currency Transaction Report or Suspicious Activity Report contents
- Subpoena, Levy, or Garnishment contents

ROUTE THESE REQUESTS UP
- Attorney or process server: Compliance Officer.
- Law enforcement of any agency: Compliance Officer.
- Collection agency or skip tracer: Compliance Officer.
- Newspaper, radio, or television: Branch Manager, then Compliance Officer.
- Another financial institution requesting verification: Records Custodian.

END OF SHIFT
- Clear the counter of all printed member fields.
- Empty the proof tape and inquiry printouts into the shred bin.
- Sign off the terminal; do not leave it at the account screen.
- Lock the drawer, the file cabinet, and the fiche reader cabinet.

If you are unsure, stop and ask. No member has ever been harmed by waiting.
