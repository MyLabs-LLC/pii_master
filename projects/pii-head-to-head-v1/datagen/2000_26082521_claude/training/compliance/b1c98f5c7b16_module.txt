SCENARIO QUIZ — RESTRICTED INFORMATION HANDLING

Instructions: Circle one answer per scenario. Passing score is 4 of 5. Retraining is required for any score below passing. Submit to the Store Manager at the end of the session.

SCENARIO 1
A caller states they are picking up a prescription for a household member and asks which medication is ready. The caller is not listed on the profile.
A. Confirm the drug name only.
B. Confirm that something is ready but not what it is.
C. Decline and offer to note the request for the patient to authorize.
D. Ask the caller for the patient's date of birth and proceed.

SCENARIO 2
A technician recognizes a name in the queue as a former classmate and opens the profile to see what was prescribed. Nothing is printed and nothing is said to anyone.
A. No violation, because nothing was disclosed.
B. Violation of the minimum necessary rule; access alone is the violation.
C. Acceptable if the technician does not open the record again.
D. Acceptable if the technician later tells the Store Manager.

SCENARIO 3
A prior authorization fax intended for a prescriber's office transmits to a wrong number. The confirmation page shows the transmission completed.
A. Redial the correct number and take no further action.
B. Wait to see whether anyone calls about it.
C. Notify the Store Manager and the Compliance Hotline the same day.
D. Discard the confirmation page.

SCENARIO 4
A customer at the register reads a full card account number aloud so the cashier can key it in, and asks the cashier to write it on the receipt for their records.
A. Write it on the receipt as requested.
B. Key the number and decline to write it anywhere.
C. Write it on a note and shred it after the shift.
D. Enter it into the customer comment field.

SCENARIO 5
A pharmacist finds a bundle of completed hard copies and one pseudoephedrine log page in the general trash at close.
A. Leave them; the store is locked overnight.
B. Move them to the locked shred bin and report the finding.
C. Take them home and shred them there.
D. Return them to the counter for filing in the morning.
