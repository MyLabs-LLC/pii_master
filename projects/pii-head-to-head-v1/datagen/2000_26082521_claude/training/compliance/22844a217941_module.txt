INTERNAL MEMORANDUM
Office of Compliance and Records Governance
Distribution: All Staff | Issued: 18 August 2021 | Module 4, Abbreviated Handout

=====================================================================
PART A — THE RESTRICTED LIST
=====================================================================
1. Treat as restricted, in every format and on every device:
   1.1 Social Security Number; Individual Taxpayer Identification Number; Date of Birth; Government Photo Identification Number.
   1.2 Bank Routing Number; Deposit Account Number; Pay Rate; W-4 Withholding Elections.
   1.3 Drug Screen Result; Accommodation Request Detail; Workers' Compensation Claim Number.
   1.4 Residential Street Address; Personal Mobile Number; Personal Electronic Mail Address; Emergency Contact Name.
   1.5 Consumer Report Identifier; Adverse Action Notice Date.
2. Not restricted, and therefore the only fields shareable with a client: Assignment ID; Position Title; Start Date; Shift Code; Billing Rate.

=====================================================================
PART B — CONSEQUENCES
=====================================================================
3. Written counseling and re-certification follow any substantiated first incident.
4. Entitlement suspension and Disciplinary Panel review follow any incident touching Clause 1.1 or 1.2, and termination is available at first instance.
5. Where notification law is triggered, the agency bears notice costs, credit-monitoring costs, and client indemnity; the responsible branch bears any contract penalty against its operating budget.
6. Knowing misuse is referred to counsel and may be reported to law enforcement.

=====================================================================
PART C — SCENARIO QUIZ (circle one, return to the Records Custodian)
=====================================================================
7. A coordinator photographs an onboarding form with a personal phone to complete entry from the job site.
   PERMITTED / PROHIBITED — cite the clause.
8. A client contact asks why a worker was withdrawn from an assignment after screening.
   ANSWER THE QUESTION / REFER TO THE ACCOUNT COMPLIANCE LEAD — cite the clause.
9. A payroll register is left in the branch printer tray over a weekend.
   MINOR / REPORTABLE INCIDENT — state the reporting deadline.
10. A recruiter stores a list of Personal Mobile Number and Emergency Contact Name in a personal notes application for after-hours call-outs.
   PERMITTED / PROHIBITED — state the approved alternative.
11. A branch emails an assignment roster carrying Assignment ID, Position Title, and Shift Code to a client scheduler.
   PERMITTED / PROHIBITED — state why.

12. Completed forms are due within five business days of the session. Non-return is recorded as a training deficiency against the branch scorecard.
