NOTES

The level is set by the Director of Human Resources after review with the
Records Custodian. A level need not follow the one before it; a first finding
may be set at L-4 or L-5 where the facts support it.

Reporting a mishandling by another employee is not itself a violation and is
not entered in the reporting employee's file.

Contract staff and volunteers are removed from agency assignment at L-3 and
above, and the contracting officer is notified in writing.
