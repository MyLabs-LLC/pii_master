TELLER LINE QUICK CHECK — RESTRICTED INFORMATION
Post at station. Do not remove from branch.

BEFORE YOU DISCLOSE
• 1. Two authentication elements, every time, no exceptions for familiar voices.
• 2. Never confirm what a caller guesses. Silence is not confirmation, but neither is "close."
• 3. Restricted fields include: Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Member Number, Account Number, Card Number, PIN Offset, Balance, Loan Application Status, Credit Score.

BEFORE YOU STEP AWAY
• 4. Lock the terminal.
• 5. Clear the drawer, the counter, and the printer bin.
• 6. Check the fax tray on the way past.

BEFORE YOU DISCARD
• 7. Shred console only. Never the wastebasket, never the recycling bin.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
• 8. Do not attempt to retrieve or correct it yourself.
• 9. Tell the Branch Manager immediately; the Information Security Officer must be notified the same business day.
• 10. Concealment is treated more severely than the original error.

WHAT IT COSTS
• 11. Written warning and re-training on a first finding.
• 12. Termination on a repeated or willful finding.
• 13. Regulatory penalties against the credit union under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act.
