STAFF DEVELOPMENT MODULE 4: INFORMATION RESTRICTED FROM DISCLOSURE
Division of Business and Records Services

§1  SCOPE
    1.1   All employees. No exemption by classification or hours worked.
    1.2   Reviewed annually. Retention of the signed acknowledgment: employment plus three years.

§2  RESTRICTED CATEGORIES AND ASSIGNED CUSTODIANS
    2.1   Student education records — Registrar
              Fields: Student Name, Student Identification Number, Course History, Grade Point Average, Attendance Record
    2.2   Special education records — Director of Student Services
              Fields: Eligibility Category, Evaluation Report, IEP Goals, Placement Determination, Parent Consent Status
    2.3   Student health records — Health Services Coordinator
              Fields: Immunization Status, Allergy Notation, Medication Administration Log
    2.4   Meal program records — Food Services Director
              Fields: Free/Reduced Meal Eligibility Status, Household Size, Application Determination Date
    2.5   Personnel records — Human Resources Administrator
              Fields: Social Security Number, Home Address, Date of Birth, Evaluation Rating, Insurance Enrollment Election
    2.6   Survey and screening records — Building Principal
              Fields: Parental Consent Status, Instrument Title, Administration Date

§3  PROHIBITED PRACTICES
    3.1   Use of the Social Security Number as a classroom or roster identifier.
    3.2   Storage of restricted fields on a home computer or a personal diskette.
    3.3   Redisclosure of meal eligibility to any party outside the child nutrition program.
    3.4   Release of any field to a third party without a signed consent form on file.
    3.5   Access to a record where no legitimate educational interest exists, including records of a relative or neighbor.

§4  CONSEQUENCES
    4.1   First substantiated incident: written reprimand, mandatory retraining.
    4.2   Repeated or willful incident: suspension without pay, recommendation for termination.
    4.3   Unauthorized access to a record for personal reasons: treated as willful. No progressive discipline.
    4.4   District-level exposure: federal complaint, corrective action, funding review.

    5.2   A teacher may leave the student information system open on an unattended workstation during a fire drill. ___
    5.3   The front office may confirm meal eligibility status to a parent-teacher organization planning a fundraiser. ___
    5.4   A staff member who discovers a printout of employee salary placement in a shared workroom should place it in the recycling bin. ___
    5.5   Directory Information may be released for a student whose opt-out flag is set, provided the request is from a community organization. ___
    5.6   An employee who mishandles a record and reports it promptly is still subject to discipline. ___
