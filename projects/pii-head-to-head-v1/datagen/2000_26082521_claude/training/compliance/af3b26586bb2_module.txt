RETAIL PHARMACY OPERATIONS — ANNUAL COMPLIANCE CURRICULUM
MODULE 4: RESTRICTED INFORMATION CATEGORIES
Facilitator Slide Script | Curriculum Year 2021 | Runtime 20 minutes
Audience: Pharmacy Technicians, Front-Store Associates, Shift Supervisors

SLIDE 4.1 — PURPOSE (read as written)

1. This module identifies the categories of information the Company classifies
   as restricted, the handling duty attaching to each, and the disciplinary and
   regulatory consequences of mishandling.
2. A category is restricted where its disclosure outside an authorised channel
   would breach federal or state law, a payer contract, or Company policy.
3. Restriction attaches to the field, not to the medium. A field is equally
   restricted on a screen, a printed label, a fax cover sheet, or a bin tag.

SLIDE 4.3 — CONSEQUENCE (read as written)

4. A first substantiated finding at Tier R-1 through R-4 results in documented
   coaching and re-certification within ten business days.
5. A repeat finding, or any finding involving removal of records from the site,
   results in a final written warning or termination.
6. Findings at Tier R-2 are additionally reportable to the acquiring bank, and
   findings at Tier R-4 are reportable to the state board of pharmacy.

SLIDE 4.4 — SCENARIO CHECK (facilitator poll; show one at a time)

7. A caller states they are collecting a prescription for a household member and
   asks which medication is waiting. What is the correct response?
8. A colleague at the drop-off window photographs a completed dispensing label to
   confirm a directions field with a supervisor off-site. Which tier applies?
9. A supervisor asks a technician to look up the wage rate of a colleague on a
   different shift to settle a scheduling dispute. Is the lookup permitted?

SLIDE 4.5 — ANSWER KEY

10. Clause 7: decline; verify the requester against the pickup authorisation
    record before any medication is named.
11. Clause 8: Tier R-1. Photography of a dispensing label is prohibited on any
    device. Escalate by telephone using the Rx Number only.
12. Clause 9: no. Tier R-5 lookups are routed through Human Resources.
