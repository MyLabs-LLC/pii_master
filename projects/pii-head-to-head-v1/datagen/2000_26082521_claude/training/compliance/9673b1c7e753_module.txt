PRIVACY MODULE 3 — SLIDE SCRIPT
Presenter notes, Slides 9 through 11. Read verbatim where indicated.

SLIDE 9 — "What Is Restricted"
Talking points:
* Restricted means it does not leave this building, this system, or this conversation without an authorized purpose.
* Four buckets. Say them out loud with the class:
  * NPI — Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Residence Address, Annual Household Income
  * PHI — Member Identification Number, Diagnosis Code, Date of Service, Treatment Notes
  * Financial — Bank Routing Number, Account Number, Credit Card Number
  * Investigative — Claim Number, SIU Referral Reason, Recorded Statement Transcript
* Note for the class: a file is restricted even when only the label is visible on a folder tab.

SLIDE 10 — "What Happens If You Get It Wrong"
Talking points:
* Corrective action, including termination.
* License action by the Department of Insurance for licensed producers and adjusters.
* Penalties against the carrier under federal privacy rules.
* Personal criminal exposure where disclosure was for gain.

SLIDE 11 — "Quick Check" (poll the room, show of hands)
* A Mailroom Clerk finds an unsealed envelope of explanation-of-benefits statements. Discard, reseal, or route to the Privacy Officer?
* A Claims Adjuster is asked by a family member at home about a local accident claim. Confirm, deny, or decline?
* A Systems Contractor requests production data to test a new print job. Approve, refuse, or refer to the Data Steward?
