HANDOUT 3 — INFORMATION CLASSIFICATION, SANCTIONS, AND APPLIED SCENARIOS
County Health Department | Compliance and Privacy Program | Calendar Year 2021 Curriculum

I. CLASSIFICATION TIERS — the workforce member shall be able to state each tier without reference to notes.
 • Tier I — Public. Aggregate statistics released at a level of aggregation sufficient to prevent re-identification; no cell size below the threshold established by the Departmental Data Release Policy.
 • Tier II — Internal. Operational records containing no individually identifiable element, such as clinic schedules populated only by the column headings Appointment Slot, Service Type, and Assigned Station.
 • Tier III — Confidential. Any record containing one or more of the fields Patient Name, Date of Birth, Social Security Number, Medical Record Number, Member Identifier, Street Address, Telephone Number, or Electronic Mail Address.
 • Tier IV — Restricted by Statute. Communicable disease investigation files, sexually transmitted infection records, tuberculosis registry entries, substance use treatment records subject to 42 C.F.R. Part 2, and vital records certificates. Access is granted by name to designated position classifications only and is subject to periodic recertification.

II. SANCTIONS — the following are cumulative and not alternative.
 • First substantiated incident of negligent handling: written reprimand, retraining, and access review.
 • Second substantiated incident, or any incident involving Tier IV information: suspension without pay pending investigation and referral to the Human Resources Director.
 • Any access undertaken out of curiosity, personal interest, or for a purpose unrelated to assigned duties: presumptive termination, without regard to whether information was further disclosed.
 • Any sale, transfer, or use for commercial advantage or malicious harm: referral to the District Attorney and to federal authorities as required by law.

III. APPLIED SCENARIOS — respond in writing on the reverse; the Training Coordinator will collect and score.
 • A staff member is asked by a colleague in another division to look up whether a particular household has been contacted by the case investigation unit. The colleague states the inquiry is work-related but declines to identify the program purpose. State the correct response and the applicable minimum necessary standard.
 • A contracted interpreter requests a copy of an investigation record containing the fields Case Identifier, Onset Date, and Contact Name in order to prepare for an interview. Identify the instrument that must be executed prior to any transfer.
 • An investigator discovers that a weekly line list export has been delivered to an incorrect internal distribution list for the preceding three weeks. State the discovery date obligation, the risk assessment factors, and the office responsible for determining whether notification is required.
 • A workforce member wishes to publish a photograph taken inside a vaccination clinic on a personal social media account. State the categories of information that must be absent from the image before any posting is contemplated.
