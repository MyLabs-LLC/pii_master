MERIDIAN TITLE & ESCROW SERVICES, INC.
PRESENTER SCRIPT — "WHAT WE PROTECT AND WHY" (25 MINUTES)
Module IH-04A · For use with the overhead transparency set · 2004 cycle

SLIDE 1 — TITLE
ON SCREEN: What We Protect and Why
SAY: Welcome. This is the annual privacy session, and it is required of everyone in the branch, including part-time and contract staff. I am going to talk in terms of numbered rules, because that is how the examiner will read our file if we are ever asked to prove we trained you.

SLIDE 2 — THE THREE TIERS
ON SCREEN: Tier I / Tier II / Tier III
SAY: Rule 1. We do not classify by document, we classify by field label. If you see the words Social Security Number, Taxpayer Identification Number, Date of Birth, Driver's License Number, Deposit Account Number, Routing/ABA Number, Credit Card Number, Payoff Balance, or Credit Score anywhere on a page or a screen, that page is Tier I from that moment forward, and it does not matter whether the page came from the lender, the borrower, or our own accounting system. Rule 2. Tier II is our work product — Escrow File Number, Title Order Number, Wire Beneficiary Account, Earnest Money Deposit Amount — and it goes to the file team and the parties of record, nobody else. Rule 3. Tier III is company business: rate filings, plant indices, personnel records.

SLIDE 3 — HOW IT LEAKS
SAY: Rule 4. In our industry the loss almost never looks dramatic. It looks like a fax left in a tray overnight, a settlement statement mailed to the wrong party of an assignment, a payoff quoted to a caller who sounded convincing, a diskette carried home in a coat pocket, or a cabinet left unlocked over a long weekend. Rule 5. Verify before you speak. If the caller cannot give the escrow number and you did not initiate the call, you call back on the number in the file. That single habit prevents most of what I have described.

SLIDE 4 — WHAT IT COSTS
SAY: Rule 6. The Company faces examination findings, civil penalty, and bond claims. The branch faces license exposure. You personally face discipline up to termination and, where an identifier is misused for gain, referral for prosecution. I am not raising this to alarm anyone; I am raising it because people are usually surprised that the personal exposure is real.

SLIDE 5 — SCENARIOS (DISCUSS ALOUD)
SAY: Take these one at a time. First, a caller demands a payoff, refuses the escrow number, and offers a Date of Birth instead. Second, a courier pouch arrives unsealed with a lender package inside. Third, a licensee asks for the loan application because the deal is shared. Fourth, someone asks a funder to change the Wire Beneficiary Account after instructions are out. Tell me what you would do, then tell me who you would call.

SLIDE 6 — CLOSING
SAY: Rule 7. When in doubt, stop and route it to the Compliance Officer. Nobody in this company has ever been disciplined for asking first.
