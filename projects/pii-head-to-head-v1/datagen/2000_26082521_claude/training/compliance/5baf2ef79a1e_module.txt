PRESENTER SCRIPT — FALL 2011 STAFF DEVELOPMENT
SESSION 3: RESTRICTED INFORMATION
Running time: 20 minutes. Sign-in sheet circulates during Slide 1.

======================================================================
SLIDE 1 — WHY THIS SESSION
======================================================================
Read aloud: Board Policy 4360 requires annual training for every employee,
volunteer, and contractor with access to student or employee records.
[NOTE] Do not accept verbal attestation. Sign-in sheet is the audit record.

======================================================================
SLIDE 2 — THE THREE TIERS
======================================================================
Read aloud: Restricted information is classified in three tiers.

  TIER 1, student confidential. Field labels: Student ID, Date of Birth,
  Home Address, Guardian Telephone, Meal Eligibility, Special Education
  Eligibility Code, Section 504 Plan Status, Health Condition Note,
  Immunization Record, Disciplinary Action Code, Custody Order Flag.

  TIER 2, employee confidential. Field labels: Employee ID, Social Security
  Number, Bank Routing Number, Bank Account Number, Evaluation Rating,
  Leave Reason Code, Background Check Disposition.

  TIER 3, directory. Field labels: Name, Grade Level, Enrollment Dates,
  Athletic Participation, Honors Received. Subject to opt-out.

[NOTE] If asked whether a specific report is Tier 1: if the report can be
traced to one household, treat it as Tier 1 pending ruling.

======================================================================
SLIDE 3 — HOW LOSSES HAPPEN
======================================================================
Read aloud: Last cycle, reported incidents in districts of our size came from
five sources — flash drives, personal web-mail, unattended printouts, laptops
left in vehicles, and shared logins.
[NOTE] Do not identify buildings or individuals when taking questions.

======================================================================
SLIDE 4 — CONSEQUENCES
======================================================================
Read aloud, verbatim: Written reprimand. Loss of system access. Suspension
without pay. Recommendation for termination. Referral to the licensing board.
Referral for prosecution. Household notification at department expense.
[NOTE] Pause. Do not soften this slide.

======================================================================
SLIDE 5 — SCENARIO CHECK (show one at a time; solicit response)
======================================================================
  A. A teacher photographs a wall chart of intervention groups and posts the
     image to a professional networking site.
     ANSWER: Violation. Tier 1 by inference. Report within one business day.

  B. A secretary receives a call from a person identifying as a school
     official at a receiving district and requesting a transcript.
     ANSWER: Do not fax. Route to the Records Custodian for verification.

  C. A bus driver keeps a printed route list with home addresses in the
     vehicle overnight.
     ANSWER: Violation. Lists return to the transportation office at end of
     run.

  D. A staff member reuses a District password on a personal shopping site.
     ANSWER: Violation of the acceptable use agreement. Reset immediately.

======================================================================
SLIDE 6 — CLOSE
======================================================================
Read aloud: Form R-14 to your building secretary before you leave today.
Collect sign-in sheet. Deliver to the Office of the Records Custodian.
