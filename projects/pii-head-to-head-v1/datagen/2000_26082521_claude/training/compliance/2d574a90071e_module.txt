SECTION-BY-SECTION COMPLIANCE TRAINING HANDOUT
FORM REFERENCE: CMP-114 (Rev. 3)
SUBJECT: RESTRICTED INFORMATION CATEGORIES, HANDLING OBLIGATIONS, AND CONSEQUENCES OF UNAUTHORIZED DISCLOSURE
ISSUING AUTHORITY: Office of the Chief Compliance Officer
APPLICABLE BUSINESS UNITS: Underwriting; Claims Adjudication; Member Services; Agency Distribution; Actuarial Support
EFFECTIVE PERIOD: Plan Year 2021
REVIEW CYCLE: Annual, or upon material amendment of governing regulation
COMPLETION REQUIREMENT: Mandatory for all employees, temporary staff, and contracted personnel with system credentials

SECTION I — PURPOSE AND SCOPE
1.1 This handout is promulgated pursuant to the Company's Information Governance Policy and applicable state insurance data security statutes, the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act safeguards provisions, and, where the Company acts as a covered entity or business associate, the administrative safeguards required under HIPAA.
1.2 The obligations set forth herein apply without regard to the medium in which information is stored, transmitted, or displayed, including but not limited to core administration systems, imaged correspondence, facsimile transmissions, voice recordings, printed work product, and portable media.

SECTION II — CATEGORIES OF RESTRICTED INFORMATION
2.1 The following data elements, whether appearing individually or in combination with any identifier, are classified RESTRICTED and may not be accessed, reproduced, transmitted, or discussed except as required for a documented business purpose:
   (a) Social Security Number; Individual Taxpayer Identification Number
   (b) Date of Birth; Date of Death
   (c) Member Identification Number; Subscriber Number; Certificate Number
   (d) Policy Number; Group Number; Claim Number when paired with any of (a) through (c)
   (e) Financial Account Number; Payment Card Number; Routing Number; Premium Draft Authorization
   (f) Driver's License Number; State Identification Number; Passport Number
   (g) Diagnosis Code; Procedure Code; Treatment Notes; Prescription History; Behavioral Health Records
   (h) Biometric Identifiers; Genetic Test Results
   (i) Residential Street Address; Personal Telephone Number; Personal Electronic Mail Address
   (j) Underwriting Risk Score; Rating Class; Adverse Action Rationale
2.2 Substance use disorder records and behavioral health records are subject to heightened restriction and require separate written authorization prior to any disclosure not otherwise permitted by law.

SECTION III — HANDLING OBLIGATIONS
3.1 Minimum Necessary. Access shall be limited to the least amount of Restricted Information reasonably required to perform the assigned function.
3.2 Transmission. External transmission requires encryption in transit. Unsecured personal electronic mail accounts and consumer file-sharing services are prohibited without exception.
3.3 Physical Control. Work product containing Restricted Information shall be secured in a locked receptacle when unattended and destroyed by cross-cut shredding or deposited in a designated secure destruction container.
3.4 Verification. Prior to any telephonic disclosure, the associate shall complete caller authentication in accordance with the Member Services Authentication Standard.
3.5 Reporting. Any suspected or actual unauthorized acquisition, access, use, or disclosure shall be reported to the Privacy Officer within twenty-four (24) hours of discovery. Delay in reporting constitutes an independent violation.

SECTION IV — CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING
4.1 Employment Consequences: verbal counseling; written warning; suspension of system entitlements; termination for cause.
4.2 Regulatory Consequences: civil monetary penalties assessed against the Company; market conduct examination findings; corrective action plans imposed by the Department of Insurance.
4.3 Individual Consequences: personal civil liability; referral for criminal prosecution where the disclosure was made knowingly and for personal gain or malicious harm.
4.4 Contractual Consequences: indemnification obligations to plan sponsors; forfeiture of producer appointment.

SECTION V — SCENARIO-BASED KNOWLEDGE CHECK
5.1 A caller states that they are the spouse of a covered member and requests the status of a pending claim. The caller cannot complete the authentication script. The correct action is:
   ( ) Provide the claim status, as the relationship is plausible
   ( ) Decline to disclose and offer the written authorization process
   ( ) Transfer the call to any available associate
5.2 An associate discovers a printed claims register left on a shared conference table after a meeting. The correct action is:
   ( ) Return it to the general recycling bin
   ( ) Secure the document and notify the Records Custodian and the Privacy Officer
   ( ) Leave it in place for the originating department
5.3 A vendor representative requests a spreadsheet containing member identifiers to "assist with testing." The correct action is:
   ( ) Transmit the file if the vendor is under contract
   ( ) Decline and route the request to Vendor Risk Management for authorization review
   ( ) Transmit the file with a password shared in the same message
