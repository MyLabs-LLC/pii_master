METROPOLITAN TRANSIT AUTHORITY
OFFICE OF RECORDS AND INFORMATION GOVERNANCE
ANNUAL REFRESHER: HANDLING OF RESTRICTED INFORMATION
Slide Script — Facilitator Copy
Revision 4, February 2011
Running time: 30 minutes. Return the completed sign-in sheet to the Records Custodian within five business days.

SLIDE 1 — SCOPE
- Applies to all Authority employees, temporary staff, interns, and contractor personnel with badge access to Operations, Customer Service, Payroll, or Paratransit systems.
- Applies to information in any form: printed, handwritten, screen display, voicemail, magnetic media, or fare-collection device memory.
- Read aloud: "Restricted information is anything on the following four lists. If you are unsure which list an item belongs to, treat it as restricted until the Records Custodian tells you otherwise."

SLIDE 2 — CATEGORY A: EMPLOYEE AND PAYROLL RECORDS
- Restricted fields: Full Name, Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Home Address, Home Telephone, Personal E-mail, Direct Deposit Routing Number, Direct Deposit Account Number, Garnishment Order Number, Beneficiary Designation.
- Also restricted: DOT drug and alcohol test results, return-to-duty status, FMLA certifications, workers' compensation claim files, disciplinary correspondence, and background check dispositions.
- Access rule: Human Resources and Payroll staff by assigned role only. Supervisors see attendance and discipline for their own reporting unit; nothing else.

SLIDE 3 — CATEGORY B: CUSTOMER AND FARE MEDIA RECORDS
- Restricted fields: Cardholder Name, Fare Card Serial Number, Registered Account Number, Credit Card Number, Expiration Date, Card Verification Value, Autoload Bank Account Number, Billing Address, Contact Telephone.
- Trip history is restricted. Tap time, station identifier, and vehicle identifier tied to a card become location data about a person.
- Never write a card number or account number on a call log, sticky note, or shift handover sheet. Use the Case Reference Number generated by the customer system.

SLIDE 4 — CATEGORY C: PARATRANSIT AND REDUCED FARE ELIGIBILITY
- Restricted fields: Applicant Name, Client Identification Number, Date of Birth, Diagnosis, Functional Assessment Notes, Mobility Device Type, Physician Name, Physician Contact, Attendant Name, Pickup Address, Drop-off Address.
- These files carry protected health information. They do not travel outside the Paratransit Eligibility Unit without a written release on file.
- Dispatch and operators receive only what the trip requires: pickup point, drop-off point, mobility aid, and attendant flag.

SLIDE 5 — CATEGORY D: SECURITY SENSITIVE INFORMATION
- Restricted items: vulnerability assessments, camera coverage maps, alarm codes, radio encryption keys, revenue collection routes and schedules, key control inventories, and threat correspondence.
- Marked "SENSITIVE SECURITY INFORMATION — 49 CFR PARTS 15 AND 1520." Distribution is by name on the cover sheet and by covering signature only.
- Do not photograph, scan, or forward. Do not discuss on open radio channels or in the operator break room.

SLIDE 6 — CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING
- Progressive discipline under Personnel Rule 12: written warning, suspension without pay, or termination, depending on severity and intent.
- Contractor staff are removed from Authority premises and the sponsoring firm is notified in writing.
- Referral to the General Counsel and, where warranted, to law enforcement. Unauthorized release of protected health information and unauthorized access to motor vehicle records both carry personal civil liability.
- Notification costs, credit monitoring, and regulatory penalties are charged to the responsible department's budget.
- Every access to the customer and eligibility systems is logged with user identifier, terminal, and timestamp. Logs are reviewed monthly.

SLIDE 7 — SCENARIO QUIZ (facilitator reads; participants answer aloud)
- A caller states that a household member rides on a Reduced Fare card and asks for the card balance and the last three boarding locations. What is the correct response?
- A supervisor from another division asks you to pull the attendance and medical leave file for an operator in your unit, citing an internal investigation. What do you do?
- You find a printed roster on the break room table listing operator names beside Social Security Numbers. What are your two immediate actions?
- A local reporter asks for the camera coverage diagram for a rail platform, saying it is a public record. What do you say?
- An eligibility packet must reach the contracted assessment clinic by end of day and the secure courier has already run. May you send it by personal fax or personal e-mail?

SLIDE 8 — ANSWER KEY
- Verify only the caller's own registered account. Release nothing on a third party's card without a signed authorization in the file.
- Refer the request to Human Resources. Division supervisors have no standing access to another unit's personnel or medical records.
- Take custody of the roster immediately, then report the exposure to the Records Custodian the same shift.
- Refer to Public Affairs. Security sensitive material is exempt from disclosure and is not released at the counter.
- No. Use the secure courier on the next scheduled run or the encrypted transfer queue. Convenience is not an exception.
