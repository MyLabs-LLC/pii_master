Post at every workstation. Do not remove.

  Financial ........... Bank Routing Number, Bank Account Number,

  Confidential ........ Address Confidentiality Program Participant Flag,
                        Protective Order Reference Number
  Infrastructure ...... SCADA Endpoint Identifier, Reservoir Access Code,
                        Substation Keying Schedule
  Personnel ........... Employee Identification Number, Compensation Grade,
                        Disciplinary Action Disposition

THE FOUR RULES

  ONE     Verify identity by Service Address PLUS one challenge element.
  TWO     Verify authority separately. Identity is not authority.
  THREE   Never read back a stored financial credential. Ever.
  FOUR    Route all legal process and public records requests upward, unopened.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

  Do not attempt to recall, delete, or quietly correct the disclosure.
  Notify your Supervisor of Record immediately.
  File an incident report within 24 hours.
  Preserve all related records; retention holds are automatic upon report.

CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING

  Progressive discipline through termination · Personal civil liability
  Criminal referral for confidential-address and infrastructure categories
  Mandatory breach notification at Division cost · Credential suspension

QUICK SELF-CHECK

  Am I certain who is asking?
  Am I certain they are entitled to this element?
  Am I sending the minimum necessary, or a bulk extract?
  Is the container approved, or is it my own device?

  Any hesitation — stop and call the Records Custodian.
