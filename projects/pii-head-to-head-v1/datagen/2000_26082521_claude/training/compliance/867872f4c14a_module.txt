QUARTERLY INFORMATION SECURITY REFRESHER
Bulletin 2004-INF-03
To: Lending, Member Services, Card Services
From: Compliance Department
Posting: Departmental bulletin board, 90 days

1. SCOPE
1.1 This bulletin supplements Handout 2004-INF-01. It addresses restricted material in printed and on-screen form.

2. RESTRICTED MATERIAL BY MEDIUM
2.1 Reports. The following reports carry restricted column headings. Copies are numbered, signed out, and returned to the Records Custodian at day end. They do not leave the department.

    DL-102  Delinquent Loan Register
            MEMBER NO. | LOAN NO. | ORIG DATE | ORIG AMT | CURR BAL |
            DAYS DELQ | LAST PMT DATE | RISK GRADE

    NA-014  New Account Register
            MEMBER NO. | SSN | DOB | ACCT TYPE | OPEN DATE |
            INITIAL DEP | BRANCH CODE | OPENED BY

    CD-330  Card Activity Exception Listing
            CARD NO. | AUTH CODE | MERCH CAT | AMOUNT | DECLINE RSN

2.2 Files. Loan application packet; credit bureau report; adverse action notice; income verification; collateral title documents; deposit account signature card; deceased-member file.
2.3 Screens. Inquiry, teller, and collections screens display restricted fields. Monitors are angled away from the member side of the counter and from lobby seating.

3. HANDLING
3.1 Reports are shredded, not filed, once the retention period stated in the Records Retention Schedule expires.
3.2 Working notes containing any restricted field are treated as the underlying record and destroyed the same way.
3.3 Credit bureau reports are obtained only for a permissible purpose recorded on the inquiry log.
3.4 Collection calls disclose account details only to the obligor. Messages left with a third party state a return telephone number and nothing further.
3.5 Overnight courier pouches are sealed, numbered, and reconciled against the manifest on receipt.

4. CONSEQUENCES
4.1 Loss of a numbered report copy is reported to the Security Officer within one business hour of discovery.
4.2 Failure to report is treated more seriously than the loss.
4.3 Sanctions range to termination. Impermissible credit bureau inquiries carry statutory liability in addition to internal discipline.
4.4 Findings are reported to the Supervisory Committee in the quarterly compliance summary.

5. SCENARIO QUIZ
5.1 Answer in the space provided. Return to the department manager.
5.2 A collector reaches a household member who states the obligor is unavailable and asks how much is owed and how far behind the account is. What may be said?
5.3 A loan officer is asked by a neighbor, in a social setting, whether a local business banks with the Credit Union. What may be said?
5.4 A report copy signed out in the morning cannot be located at day end. State the required actions and the deadline for each.
5.5 A member service representative pulls a credit bureau report for a person who has not applied for credit, at the request of a co-worker. State the classification of this act and the sanction.

6. ROUTING
6.1 Department manager forwards completed responses to the Compliance Department within ten business days.
6.2 Non-response is recorded as a training exception on the departmental scorecard.
