SLIDE SCRIPT — "WHAT IS RESTRICTED AND WHY IT MATTERS"
Presenter: Records Custodian
Audience: All Building Staff, Opening Institute Day
Running Time: 20 minutes. Do not deviate from script.

  SLIDE 1 — TITLE
      SAY: This is a required session. Sign-in sheets go to Human Resources.
      SAY: Three points today. What is restricted. How it leaks. What happens to you.

  SLIDE 2 — THE FOUR BUCKETS
      Bucket 1: Education Records
          - Student Identification Number, Grade Level, Course History, Attendance Record, Disciplinary Referral Code
      Bucket 2: Confidential Program Records
          - Eligibility Category, IEP Goals, Related Services Minutes, Free/Reduced Meal Eligibility Status, English Language Proficiency Level
      Bucket 3: Health Records
          - Immunization Status, Allergy Notation, Medication Administration Log, Nursing Care Plan
      Bucket 4: Employee Records
          - Home Address, Home Telephone Number, Date of Birth, Salary Placement, Evaluation Rating
      SAY: Everything in Buckets 1 through 4 is closed. Directory Information is the only open list, and only until a parent opts out.

  SLIDE 3 — HOW IT LEAKS
      A.  The copier tray.
      B.  The unattended workstation.
      C.  The misdialed fax.
      D.  The e-mail attachment sent to a home account.
      E.  The conversation held where it can be overheard.
      F.  The diskette in a coat pocket.
      SAY: Six causes. Five of them are habits, not accidents.

  SLIDE 4 — WHAT HAPPENS
      To the district:
          - Written complaint to the Family Policy Compliance Office
          - Corrective action plan and follow-up review
          - Federal funding eligibility placed at risk
      To you:
          - Written reprimand placed in the personnel file
          - Suspension without pay
          - Nonrenewal or termination
          - Referral to the state professional standards board
      SAY: The district's exposure is money. Your exposure is your license.

  SLIDE 5 — SCENARIOS. HANDS UP FOR YES.
      1.  A staff member is asked by a neighbor whether a particular household receives meal assistance. Answer?
      2.  A substitute finds a cumulative folder left in a classroom desk drawer. Next step?
      3.  A department chair wants the disciplinary history of every student assigned to the department next term. Permitted?
      4.  A front office employee is handed a request for records by an individual claiming to be a noncustodial parent. Next step?
      5.  A teacher plans to carry a diskette of grade files home over the weekend. Permitted?
      SAY: Correct answers are: no, secure and notify the Registrar, no, verify custody documentation with the Building Principal, no.

  SLIDE 6 — CLOSING
      SAY: When in doubt, you do not release. You route it to the Records Custodian.
      SAY: Sign the roster on the way out. Unsigned rosters are followed up individually.
