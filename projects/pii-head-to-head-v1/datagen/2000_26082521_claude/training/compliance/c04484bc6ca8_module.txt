INTERNAL USE — RESTRICTED DISTRIBUTION
COURSE CMP-114: CLASSIFICATION AND HANDLING OF RESTRICTED INFORMATION
ANNUAL REFRESHER — FACILITATOR SLIDE SCRIPT
Revision 03, effective first quarter 2016
Facilitator of record: the Regional Compliance Officer
Attendance roster maintained by: the Training Records Custodian

    I. SLIDE 1 — PURPOSE AND AUTHORITY
        A. Script: "This course is delivered pursuant to Section 7.4 of the Employee Handbook and to the information-security clauses incorporated by reference into each customer transportation services agreement."
        B. Facilitator note: state that completion is a condition precedent to continued badge access to terminal and dock areas.

    II. SLIDE 2 — CATEGORIES OF RESTRICTED INFORMATION
        A. Category I — Personally Identifiable Information of employees, drivers, and applicants, being any record bearing the following fields:
            1. Full Name; Social Security Number; Date of Birth
            2. Commercial Driver's License Number; State of Issuance; Medical Examiner's Certificate Number
            3. Home Address; Personal Telephone Number; Personal Email Address
            4. Direct Deposit Routing Number; Direct Deposit Account Number; Garnishment Order Reference
        B. Category II — Protected Health Information arising from occupational health and DOT medical qualification programs:
            1. Medical Examiner Determination; Restriction Code; Return-to-Duty Status
            2. Controlled Substance Test Result; Specimen Identification Number; Substance Abuse Professional Referral
        C. Category III — Cardholder Data received in connection with freight payment:
            1. Primary Account Number; Cardholder Name; Expiration Date; Service Code
            2. Sensitive Authentication Data, comprising Card Verification Value, PIN Block, and full magnetic-stripe track data, which shall not be retained subsequent to authorization under any circumstance whatsoever.
        D. Category IV — Customer Proprietary and Commercially Sensitive Information:
            1. Rate Schedule; Fuel Surcharge Table; Contract Award Amount; Accessorial Matrix
            2. Consignee Name; Delivery Address; Commodity Description; Piece Count; Declared Value
        E. Category V — Sensitive Security Information as defined at 49 C.F.R. Part 1520, together with export-controlled technical data:
            1. Security Program Document Number; Vulnerability Assessment; Screening Protocol
            2. Export Control Classification Number; License Exception Cited; End-User Statement

    III. SLIDE 3 — CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING
        A. Employment: progressive discipline up to and including termination for cause, imposed without regard to the absence of malicious intent.
        B. Contractual: customer-invoked audit rights, chargeback of remediation cost, indemnification demand, and suspension from the approved-carrier list.
        C. Regulatory and civil: civil penalty per violation for unauthorized disclosure of Sensitive Security Information; assessment by the acquiring bank; referral for criminal prosecution in the case of willful export violations.
        D. Personal: individual liability is not extinguished by the termination of employment.

    IV. SLIDE 4 — SCENARIO QUIZ (facilitator reads aloud; participants respond on the answer card)
        A. Scenario 1: An individual at a consignee's receiving dock, displaying no credential, asks to photograph the delivery manifest "for their records." Identify the category implicated and the required response.
        B. Scenario 2: A dispatcher forwards an unencrypted spreadsheet titled "Driver Qualification Status" to a personal webmail account in order to finish a report at home. State each provision breached.
        C. Scenario 3: A billing clerk receives a voicemail from a caller claiming to represent a shipper and requesting the full Primary Account Number on file to "verify" a declined payment. State the permitted disclosure, if any.
        D. Scenario 4: A terminal supervisor discovers a printed vulnerability assessment excerpt in the break-room recycling bin. State the reporting deadline and the title of the person to whom the report is directed.
