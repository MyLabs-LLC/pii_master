METROPOLITAN TRANSIT AUTHORITY
FACILITATOR NOTES — MODULE 4 DELIVERY
Confidential to instructional staff

  §1  Session length is forty-five (45) minutes. Do not compress the consequences slide to recover time; compress the historical background instead.

  §2  Anticipated participant objections, and the responses to be given:
        (a) "I only looked, I did not tell anyone."
              Access itself is the violation. The audit log records the query, not the intention behind it.
        (b) "The rider asked me to look."
              An oral request from a rider does not authorize retrieval. Consent is administered through the Public Records Officer and is documented.
        (c) "My supervisor told me to send it."
              A verbal instruction does not displace the written-authorization requirement. Direct the participant to escalate to the Records Custodian and to document the instruction.

  §3  Do not permit participants to volunteer illustrative situations drawn from their own casework during the session. Redirect to the printed scenarios, which have been drafted for this purpose.

  §4  Attendance shall be certified by the facilitator and forwarded to the Training Registrar the same business day. Partial attendance shall be recorded as non-completion.

  §5  Questions arising in session that cannot be answered from the slide script shall be logged on the Facilitator Query Form and routed to the Office of the General Counsel; facilitators shall not extemporize on matters of legal interpretation.
