RESTRICTED INFORMATION — POCKET CHECKLIST
County Health Department | Privacy Office | Issued 2021

BEFORE YOU DISCLOSE, TRANSMIT, PRINT, OR DISCUSS — VERIFY ALL:
[ ] The requestor's identity and authority have been independently confirmed.
[ ] A written authorization, court order, or statutory permission is on file.
[ ] The disclosure is limited to the minimum necessary data elements.
[ ] The transmission method is encrypted and Department-approved.
[ ] The disclosure is logged in the Accounting of Disclosures register under the headings Date of Disclosure, Recipient Organization, Purpose, and Data Elements Released.

NEVER RELEASE WITHOUT PRIVACY OFFICE REVIEW:
[ ] Social Security Number
[ ] Date of Birth
[ ] Medical Record Number or Case Identifier
[ ] Home Address, Telephone Number, or Electronic Mail Address
[ ] Diagnosis, Test Result, or Specimen Accession Number
[ ] Immunization Registry Record Identifier

IF YOU SUSPECT AN IMPERMISSIBLE USE OR DISCLOSURE:
[ ] Report to the Privacy Officer within one business day of discovery.
[ ] Do not attempt independent remediation, deletion, or recall.
[ ] Preserve all logs, envelopes, facsimile confirmations, and message headers.

FAILURE TO COMPLY MAY RESULT IN: discipline up to termination; civil monetary penalty; criminal referral; loss of system access; licensure referral.
