SECTION 2. PROHIBITED PRACTICES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
 P-1  Transmission of any A, C, or D field to a personal mail account.
 P-2  Storage of any C field in a note, ticket, chat, or scanned image.
 P-3  Access to an account absent an assigned work queue entry.
 P-4  Removal of printed worklists from the department floor.
 P-5  Photography of any workstation display by any device.
 P-6  Sharing of credentials, badges, or session tokens.
 P-7  Disclosure to a caller who has failed identity verification.

 Civil penalties are assessed against the organization per violation and
 per category, with annual caps applied by tier of culpability. Individual
 liability attaches at severity 4 and above.
