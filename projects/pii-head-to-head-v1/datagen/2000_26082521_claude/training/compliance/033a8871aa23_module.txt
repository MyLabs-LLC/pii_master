NOTIFICATION. A confirmed breach of unsecured Tier 1 information requires
notification to each affected individual without unreasonable delay and no
later than 60 calendar days from discovery. Incidents affecting 500 or more
individuals require concurrent notice to the Secretary and to prominent
media serving the jurisdiction. The Privacy Officer is the sole authority
for issuing notice; no division may notify independently.
