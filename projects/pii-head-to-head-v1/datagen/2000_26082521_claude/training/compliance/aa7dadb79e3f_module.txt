PRE-SHIFT BRIEFING CARD — CONFIDENTIALITY
Post at the verification station. Read at the start of each shift change.

(1) Assume every screen, label, bag, and printout at this counter is restricted.
(2) Restricted fields include: Patient Name, Date of Birth, Address, Telephone, Social Security Number, Rx Number, Drug Name, NDC, Sig, Refills Remaining, Prescriber Name, DEA Number, Cardholder ID, Group Number, BIN, PCN, Copay, Allergy Flag.
(3) Do not confirm that any person is a patient here.
(4) Verify before you speak. Callers are called back at the number on file.
(5) Face hard copy down. Turn will-call labels inward.
(6) Everything with a restricted field goes in the shred container.
(7) Log off the terminal when you step away.
(8) Look up a profile only when you have a task on it. Access is logged by Employee Number.
(9) Report a suspected breach to the Pharmacy Manager before you leave the building.
(10) Violations are termination-level and are reportable to the Board of Pharmacy.
