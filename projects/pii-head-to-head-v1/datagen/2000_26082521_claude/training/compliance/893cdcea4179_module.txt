INFORMATION SECURITY REFRESHER — SUPERVISOR LEAD-IN
Fifteen-minute stand-up briefing, all divisions
Card 3 of 5, September 2011

READ THE FOLLOWING TO YOUR CREW:

[ ] Four categories are restricted. Personnel and payroll files. Customer accounts and fare media. Paratransit and reduced fare eligibility. Security sensitive operational material.

[ ] The following labels tell you a record is restricted no matter what form it is in: Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Home Address, Personal Telephone, Bank Account Number, Payment Card Number, Card Verification Value, Client Identification Number, Diagnosis, Physician Name, Pickup Address.

[ ] A record that names no one can still be restricted. Camera coverage maps, alarm codes, and revenue routes are restricted because of what they reveal about the system.

[ ] Access follows your assignment, not your curiosity. Looking up a neighbor, a relative, a coworker, or a public figure is a violation on the first occurrence, even if nothing is written down or repeated.

[ ] Every query is logged with user identifier, terminal, and timestamp. Logs are audited monthly and the reports go to the Division Superintendent.

[ ] Mishandling leads to discipline up to termination, removal of system access, referral to the General Counsel, and in some cases personal civil liability. Costs of notifying affected riders or employees are charged to the responsible division.

[ ] Report any exposure to your Supervisor the same shift and to the Records Custodian by close of the next business day. Reporting promptly is treated as a mitigating factor. Concealment is not.

ASK THE CREW (no written answers required):

[ ] A printout listing employee numbers beside Social Security Numbers is found in a copier tray. What are the first two steps?

[ ] A caller says a relative left a wallet on a vehicle and asks which stops that person's fare card was used at yesterday. What is released?

[ ] Someone in the break room asks you to look up whether a coworker's medical leave was approved. What is your answer?

[ ] A rider posts a photograph online showing a platform camera. Does the Authority confirm the coverage area?

CLOSE WITH:

[ ] If you are not certain, stop and call the Records Custodian at the extension posted on the division board. Nobody has ever been disciplined for asking first.
