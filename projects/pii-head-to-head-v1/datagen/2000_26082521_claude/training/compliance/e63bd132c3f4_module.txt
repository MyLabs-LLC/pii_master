INTERNAL MEMO

TO:       Store Managers and Pharmacists in Charge
FROM:     Regional Compliance Coordinator
SUBJECT:  Delivering the Confidentiality Module — Facilitator Notes
CC:       District Managers

You are delivering Module 4 at your next staff meeting. These notes are for you, not for distribution to staff.

TIMING. Twenty minutes of content. Allow ten more for the scenarios. Do not combine this with the register balancing discussion; people stop listening.

TONE. Procedural, not dramatic. The material is serious enough without being oversold. Read the categories, read the rules, state the consequences flatly, then run the scenarios. Staff remember the scenarios. They do not remember the categories.

THE POINT YOU MUST LAND. Two ideas, and if the room takes away nothing else, take these:

First, curiosity is not a business reason. The most common violation in this region is not a leak to an outsider. It is an employee opening a profile for a neighbor, a relative, or an ex. The system logs it. Loss Prevention pulls the report monthly. It is termination, and staff consistently do not believe that until they see it happen.

Second, we do not confirm that anyone is a customer. Staff over-focus on drug names and under-focus on the fact of the relationship. Confirming that a person fills prescriptions here is itself a disclosure. Say this twice.

SCENARIO HANDLING. Do not read the answer. Ask the room, wait through the silence, and let a peer correct a wrong answer. Peer correction holds. Your correction does not.

If a scenario produces a wrong answer with confidence from an experienced employee, stop and work it through fully. That is the highest-value moment in the session and it is worth running long.

QUESTIONS YOU WILL GET.

"What if it's a real emergency?" — The Pharmacist in Charge decides. Never the technician, never the clerk. Transfer the call.

"What if a police officer is standing at the counter?" — Be courteous. Take the agency name and badge number for the Records Request Log. The Pharmacist in Charge reviews the paperwork. Nothing is released on a verbal request.

"Can I look up my own record?" — No. Request it in writing like any customer.

"What about a spouse who always picks up?" — Pickup authorization is on the profile or it is not. Habit is not authorization.

DOCUMENTATION. Every employee signs the acknowledgment before leaving the room. Collect the sheets yourself. Keep them in the store compliance file for two years. Regional audit pulls these without notice, and a missing signature is written up against the store, not the employee.

STAFF WHO MISS THE MEETING. They complete the handout individually within seven days and sign. You initial the sheet confirming you reviewed it with them. Do not let this slide to the next quarter; the seven days is the whole control.

Call me if a situation comes up that these notes do not cover. Do not improvise a new rule and do not tell staff "it's probably fine." Refer it up.
