RIVERBEND COMMUNITY CREDIT UNION
OFFICE OF THE CHIEF COMPLIANCE OFFICER
ANNUAL INFORMATION SAFEGUARDS TRAINING — HANDOUT A
RESTRICTED INFORMATION CATEGORIES AND ATTENDANT HANDLING OBLIGATIONS
Course Code IS-101 | Required of All Employees, Volunteers, and Supervisory Committee Members

I. AUTHORITY AND SCOPE

- This handout is promulgated under the authority of the Board-approved Information Security Program adopted in furtherance of Section 501(b) of the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act, Part 1016 of the regulations of the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection (Regulation P), Part 748 of the NCUA Rules and Regulations and Appendix A thereto, the Identity Theft Red Flags Rule, and the recordkeeping and confidentiality provisions of the Bank Secrecy Act.
- Compliance is a condition of continued access to core system credentials. Nothing herein shall be construed to limit any additional obligation imposed by contract, by state law, or by the Employee Handbook.

II. TIER ONE — RESTRICTED (HIGHEST SENSITIVITY)

- Data elements which, whether standing alone or in combination, are sufficient to authenticate, impersonate, or effect a transaction upon a member's relationship, including without limitation the following field labels as they appear in the core and card management systems:
  o Social Security Number / Taxpayer Identification Number
  o Member Number; Share Suffix; Loan Suffix
  o Debit Card Number; Credit Card Number; Expiration Date; Card Verification Value
  o Online Banking User ID; Password; Security Question Response; Mother's Maiden Name
  o Driver's License or State Identification Number; Passport Number
  o Date of Birth; Place of Birth
- Handling obligation: encryption at rest and in transit; access strictly on a documented need-to-know basis; no reproduction to portable media; no transmission by unencrypted electronic mail or facsimile under any circumstance whatsoever.

III. TIER TWO — CONFIDENTIAL

- Balance and transaction history; Credit Score; Credit Report Data; Debt-to-Income Ratio; Employer Name; Annual Income; Beneficiary Designation; Signature Card Images; Safe Deposit Box Number.
- Suspicious Activity Report filings and any information that would reveal the existence thereof, disclosure of which is prohibited without regard to intent and without exception for the subject of the report.
- Handling obligation: retain within approved systems of record; discuss only in non-public areas; release externally only upon receipt of a legally sufficient process reviewed by the Records Custodian and countersigned by the BSA Officer.

IV. TIER THREE — INTERNAL USE

- Examination correspondence, vendor due diligence files, internal audit workpapers, fraud loss statistics, network topology documentation, and branch cash limits.
- Handling obligation: no dissemination outside the credit union absent written authorization of an executive officer.

V. CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING

- Corrective action up to and including immediate termination of employment, without regard to whether the disclosure was inadvertent.
- Referral to law enforcement where the facts suggest a violation of federal criminal law governing unauthorized access to financial records or aggravated identity theft.
- Personal civil liability, individual regulatory sanction, and the assessment of statutory penalties against the credit union, together with mandatory member notification, credit monitoring costs, and reputational harm.
- Access revocation pending investigation; forfeiture of incentive compensation; and permanent notation in the personnel file.
