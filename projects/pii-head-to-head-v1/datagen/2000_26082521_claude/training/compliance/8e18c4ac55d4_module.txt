MODULE CT-114 — QUICK REFERENCE CARD
Post at workstation. Do not remove from premises.

RESTRICTED IF THE FIELD IS LABELED:
    Social Security Number ......... Member ID
    Tax Identification Number ...... Group Number
    Date of Birth .................. Diagnosis Code
    Driver's License Number ........ Procedure Code
    Residential Address ............ Date of Service
    Telephone Number ............... Treating Provider
    Email Address .................. Claim Narrative
    Bank Routing Number ............ Policy Number
    Account Number ................. Claim Number
    Payment Card Number ............ Matter Number

BEFORE YOU SEND — FOUR CHECKS:
    1. Recipient verified against the Approved Recipient Register?
    2. Encryption gateway engaged for external delivery?
    3. Attachment opened and confirmed to be the intended file?
    4. Business reason exists and is documented?
    Any "no" stops the transmission.

NEVER:
    Personal email. Personal cloud storage. Personal device. Removable media.
    Home printer. Text message. Social media. Verbal confirmation to an
    unverified caller.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG:
    Stop. Do not attempt recall or cleanup.
    Report to the Privacy Office within one business hour.
    Preserve the message, the file, and the log. Do not delete.
    Await instruction from the Records Custodian.

DISPOSAL:
    Paper — cross-cut bin. Never the recycling stream.
    Media — Records Custodian, certified destruction, receipt retained.

CALLER VERIFICATION MINIMUM:
    Policy number plus two identifiers from the file. Relationship alone is not
    authorization. Written authorization required for third-party release.

Roster signature required. Retained seven years.
