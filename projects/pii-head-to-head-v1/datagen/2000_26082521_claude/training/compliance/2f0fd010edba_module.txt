RESTRICTED INFORMATION SCHEDULE AND SANCTION SUMMARY
Unit In-Service Outline — Handout 3 of 4
Distribution: All divisions. Retain in unit binder, Tab 6.

1.0 SCOPE
    1.1 Applies to employees, contractors, students, interns, and volunteers.
    1.2 Applies to paper, electronic, verbal, and imaged form.
    1.3 Obligation survives separation from county employment.

2.0 CATEGORIES OF RESTRICTED INFORMATION
    2.1 Tier I — Direct identifiers
        2.1.1 Patient Name; Parent or Guardian Name
        2.1.2 Social Security Number; Driver License Number
        2.1.3 Medical Record Number; Client Index Number
        2.1.4 Street Address; Telephone Number; Electronic Mail Address
        2.1.5 Date of Birth; Date of Service
    2.2 Tier II — Clinical content
        2.2.1 Diagnosis Code; Procedure Code; Test Result
        2.2.2 Medication Name; Dosage; Dispense Date
        2.2.3 Referral Source; Disposition
    2.3 Tier III — Statutorily protected programs
        2.3.1 Communicable disease investigation files
        2.3.2 HIV/AIDS counseling and testing records
        2.3.3 Substance abuse treatment records
        2.3.4 Mental health records
    2.4 Tier IV — Administrative
        2.4.1 Employee Identification Number; Payroll Deduction Code
        2.4.2 Applicant Reference File; Grievance File
        2.4.3 Sealed bid pricing during an open solicitation

3.0 ACCOUNTING OF DISCLOSURES
    3.1 Each non-routine disclosure is logged. Required columns:
        3.1.1 Date of Disclosure
        3.1.2 Name of Recipient
        3.1.3 Address of Recipient
        3.1.4 Description of Information Disclosed
        3.1.5 Purpose or Statutory Basis
        3.1.6 Staff Initials
    3.2 Log retained six years. Individuals may request the accounting.

4.0 SANCTIONS
    4.1 Level 1 — inadvertent, no further disclosure: documented counseling, retraining within 30 days.
    4.2 Level 2 — negligent handling, or repeat Level 1: written reprimand, access review.
    4.3 Level 3 — access without business purpose: suspension without pay, up to termination.
    4.4 Level 4 — disclosure for personal gain, curiosity in a publicized matter, or falsification of the log: termination, referral for prosecution, licensing board notification.
    4.5 Federal civil and criminal penalties apply independently of departmental action.

5.0 SCENARIO CHECK — DIVISION DISCUSSION
    5.1 A workstation in the intake area is left logged on while staff step to the copier; a member of the public is waiting at the counter. Level?
        Level 2. Report the exposure; do not wait for evidence of viewing.
    5.2 A supervisor requests a roster of clients enrolled in a treatment program to plan staffing. Permitted?
        Aggregate counts only. Individual identifiers exceed minimum necessary for the stated purpose.
    5.3 Staff discuss an unusual case at a professional association dinner, omitting the name but stating the ZIP code, occupation, and week of admission. Violation?
        Yes. The combination reasonably identifies the individual.
