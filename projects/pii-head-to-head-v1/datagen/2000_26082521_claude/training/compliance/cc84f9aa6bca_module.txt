RESTRICTED INFORMATION CATEGORIES — QUICK REFERENCE FOR CLOSING AND PROCESSING STAFF

This page accompanies the annual privacy training and should be kept at the desk. It lists the field labels that appear on our standard forms and states the handling class of each. When a field is not listed, treat it as restricted until the Privacy Officer says otherwise.

CLASS A — RESTRICTED, RELEASE ONLY ON WRITTEN AUTHORIZATION IN THE FILE

Social Security Number. Taxpayer Identification Number. Date of Birth. Driver's License Number and issuing state. Passport Number. Bank Routing Number. Bank Account Number. Escrow Trust Account Number. Loan Number. Payoff Account Number. Credit Score. Gross Monthly Income. Earnest Money Check Number. Wire Receiving Bank and Beneficiary Account. Signature image.

CLASS B — CONFIDENTIAL, RELEASE ONLY TO A PARTY TO THE TRANSACTION OR THEIR AUTHORIZED AGENT

Borrower Name. Seller Name. Home Telephone Number. Mobile Telephone Number. Electronic Mail Address. Mailing Address. Forwarding Address. Marital Status. Employer Name. Closing Figures and disbursement detail. Commission Amount. Title Commitment Schedule B exceptions before clearance.

CLASS C — PUBLIC RECORD, MAY BE QUOTED

Recorded Deed. Recorded Mortgage or Deed of Trust. Legal Description. Parcel Identification Number. Recording Date and Instrument Number. Assessed Value as published by the county. Chain of Title as it appears in the public index.

HANDLING RULES BY CHANNEL

Telephone. Class A is never released by telephone, regardless of what the caller can recite about the file. Class B is released by telephone only after the caller is identified against information already in the file that a stranger would not possess, and the release is noted in the file log.

Facsimile. Class A is not faxed except to a lender at a number printed in our own approved lender directory. Confirm the receiving machine is attended before sending. Retrieve the transmission report and place it in the file.

Electronic mail. Class A is not sent by electronic mail in any form, including as an attachment. This rule has no exceptions and no manager may waive it. Class B may be sent only to an address already used by that party in the file.

Paper in the office. Files containing Class A or Class B fields are returned to the locked cabinets at the close of business. Nothing goes into the recycling bin; the locked shred consoles are the only disposal route.

Paper leaving the office. Nothing leaves for home. Courier packets are sealed, logged out, and released only to a named recipient who signs the log.

Systems. One password per person. No shared logins. Coverage during absence is arranged by the branch manager through the systems administrator, who grants temporary rights and removes them on the return date.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

Stop. Do not attempt to retrieve the item yourself if that would mean a second disclosure. Write down what was sent or shown, to whom, by what channel, and at what time. Notify the branch manager and the Privacy Officer the same business day. The Privacy Officer determines whether the underwriter, the lender, the affected parties, and the state regulator must be notified, and that determination is not made at the branch level.
