INTEROFFICE MEMORANDUM

TO:       Branch Managers, Claims Supervisors, Agency Field Managers
FROM:     Office of Compliance
SUBJECT:  Mandatory Delivery — Module CT-114, Information Classification
COPIES:   Privacy Office; Records Custodian; Internal Audit

    1.  SCOPE OF DELIVERY

        1.1  All staff with system access, including temporary and contract
             personnel, complete Module CT-114 this quarter.

        1.2  Appointed agents with carrier portal credentials are included.
             Agency principals are responsible for their downlines.

        1.3  No exemptions. Prior-year completion does not satisfy the
             requirement.

    2.  DELIVERY METHOD

        2.1  Live session preferred. Presenter uses the CT-114 slide script
             without modification.

        2.2  Remote attendance permitted by conference bridge. Cameras not
             required; verbal roll call is.

        2.3  Session length 45 minutes. Do not compress below 30.

    3.  MATERIALS

        3.1  Handout outline — one per attendee.
        3.2  Quick reference card — one per workstation.
        3.3  Roster — one per session, returned same day.
        3.4  Answer key — presenter copy only. Not distributed.

    4.  RECORDKEEPING

        4.1  Rosters transmit to the Records Custodian by internal courier or
             secure interoffice envelope. Not by fax.

        4.2  Retention seven years from session date.

        4.3  Internal Audit samples rosters quarterly. Missing rosters are
             reported as a control deficiency against the branch, not the
             presenter.

    5.  ESCALATION

        5.1  Staff who fail to attend by quarter end are reported to the
             regional officer.

        5.2  Continued nonattendance results in suspension of system
             credentials until completion.

    6.  QUESTIONS

        6.1  Content questions — Privacy Office.
        6.2  Scheduling and roster questions — Compliance Coordinator.

Acknowledge receipt of this memorandum by return notation within five business
days.
