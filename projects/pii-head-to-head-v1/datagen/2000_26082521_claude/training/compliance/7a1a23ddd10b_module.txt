PART A — RESTRICTED CATEGORIES

• 1.1 Tier I, Member Identifying Information. The following fields are restricted whenever populated: Full Legal Name; Social Security Number; Taxpayer Identification Number; Date of Birth; Mother's Maiden Name; Driver's License Number; Residential Address; Home Telephone Number; Employer Name.
• 1.2 Tier I, Account and Instrument Identifiers. Member Number; Share Savings Suffix; Share Draft Account Number; ATM/Debit Card Number; Card Expiration Date; PIN Offset; Certificate Number; Safe Deposit Box Number; Audio Response Access Code.
• 1.3 Tier II, Credit and Underwriting Data. Credit Bureau Score; Tradeline Payment History; Debt-to-Income Ratio; Loan Application Status; Denial Reason Code; Charge-Off Indicator; Bankruptcy Flag; Collateral Appraised Value.
• 1.4 Tier II, Transactional Data. Available Balance; Ledger Balance; Transaction Description; Payee Name; Direct Deposit Source; Wire Instruction Detail; Check Image; Overdraft Occurrence Count.
• 1.5 Tier III, Institutional and Supervisory Data. The existence or contents of any Currency Transaction Report or Suspicious Activity Report; OFAC screening results; internal audit findings; network administrator credentials; vendor contract pricing.
• 1.6 Field labels and column headings are not themselves restricted. A blank application form may be reviewed and discussed openly at any workstation. The same form, once populated, becomes Tier I material and is governed by Part B.

PART B — MANDATORY HANDLING CONTROLS

• 2.1 Confirm caller identity by two independent authentication elements before releasing any Tier I or Tier II value over the telephone.
• 2.2 Clear all desktops, fax trays, printer output bins, and teller drawers of populated documents before leaving the work area for any period.
• 2.3 Route all populated documents scheduled for destruction to the locked shred consoles. Ordinary waste receptacles are not approved for any tier.
• 2.4 Transmit Tier I fields to external parties only by approved encrypted channel or by courier under dual control. Unencrypted electronic mail and unattended fax are prohibited.
• 2.5 Access member records only where a documented business purpose exists. Curiosity, personal interest, or accommodation of a coworker's request is not a business purpose.
• 2.6 Report any suspected disclosure to the Information Security Officer before the end of the business day on which it is discovered.

PART C — CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING

• 3.1 First substantiated finding: written warning, mandatory re-training, and ninety-day access review by the Records Custodian.
• 3.2 Repeated or willful findings: suspension of system credentials and termination of employment.
• 3.3 Regulatory exposure: examination findings and civil money penalties under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act, Title V, and NCUA Rules and Regulations Part 748.
• 3.4 Personal exposure: unauthorized access to member records may constitute a criminal offense; referral to law enforcement is at the discretion of the Board.
• 3.5 Institutional exposure: notification costs, credit monitoring obligations, and reputational loss borne by the membership.

PART D — SCENARIO QUIZ

• 4.1 A caller states that they are the spouse of a joint member, correctly gives the street name but not the account number, and requests the current available balance. Circle: Release / Decline and refer.
• 4.2 A branch employee is asked by a coworker in another department to look up the loan application status of an applicant with whom the coworker attends church. Circle: Permitted / Prohibited.
• 4.3 A completed membership application bearing the Social Security Number field is discovered in the lobby brochure rack after closing. Circle: Shred immediately / Log and report to the Information Security Officer.
• 4.4 A vendor technician on site to service the platform terminals asks a teller to remain logged in so the technician may test printing. Circle: Accommodate / Refuse and escalate.
• 4.5 A member requests that a payoff quotation be sent to a personal electronic mail address. Circle: Send as requested / Offer approved secure delivery only.
