METROPOLITAN TRANSIT AUTHORITY
MODULE 4 SCENARIO ASSESSMENT — ADMINISTERED FOLLOWING THE SLIDE SCRIPT
Instructions to the participant: Select the single response that most fully conforms to Administrative Directive 14-06. A score of eight (8) of ten (10) is required; participants scoring below the threshold shall be rescheduled within thirty (30) calendar days.

  A. SCENARIO ONE
     A booth attendant is approached at the close of a shift by an individual who states that a spouse's fare card was used at a station the individual did not expect, and who asks the attendant to display the tap history for that card.
       (1) Retrieve the Tap History and read the entries aloud, the requester being a family member.
       (2) Decline to retrieve the record, explain that account records are released only through the Public Records Officer, and provide the referral.
       (3) Retrieve the record but disclose only the Station field.
       (4) Refer the requester to the transit police for a records request.
     Correct response: (2). Familial relationship confers no right of access, and partial disclosure remains disclosure.

  B. SCENARIO TWO
     A scheduling clerk in the paratransit unit is asked by a colleague in a different division, who is preparing a route-planning presentation, for a spreadsheet of upcoming trips including the Applicant Name and Residential Pickup Location fields.
       (1) Provide the file, the requester being an Authority employee.
       (2) Provide the file with the Applicant Name column deleted.
       (3) Decline pending written authorization from the Records Custodian, and offer to supply aggregate trip counts by zone with no individual-level fields.
       (4) Provide the file and instruct the recipient to delete it afterward.
     Correct response: (3). Employment alone does not establish business necessity, and pickup location remains identifying after a name is removed.

  C. SCENARIO THREE
     A maintenance supervisor discovers an unattended printout in a shop area bearing the headings Employee Identification Number, Date of Birth, and Direct Deposit Account Number.
       (1) Return the printout to the nearest desk in the originating department.
       (2) Place the printout in the recycling container.
       (3) Secure the printout immediately, notify the immediate supervisor and the Compliance Duty Officer within one hour, and deposit it in a secure destruction receptacle upon instruction.
       (4) Take no action, the printout having been generated by another division.
     Correct response: (3). Discovery of an exposed record triggers the reporting duty regardless of the discoverer's division.

  D. SCENARIO FOUR
     A member of the press contacts an operations analyst directly, requesting the CCTV Segment Identifier and footage associated with a platform incident that has drawn public attention.
       (1) Describe the footage in general terms without releasing it.
       (2) Release the identifier only, the footage being withheld.
       (3) Confirm neither the existence nor the content of any record and refer the inquiry to the Public Records Officer.
       (4) Consult a colleague in security and relay that colleague's characterization.
     Correct response: (3). Confirmation of the existence of a security record is itself a disclosure.

  E. SCORING AND RECORDATION
     Completed assessments shall be transmitted to the Training Registrar, who shall record the result in the Learning Management System under the participant's Employee Identification Number and retain the instrument for three (3) years.
