ROUTING SLIP AND ACKNOWLEDGMENT — SESSION 4
Compliance Administration to all branches. Return by the fifteenth of the month following the session.

The branch manager is responsible for scheduling the session, for confirming attendance of every position listed in the distribution block of the handout, and for accounting for absentees. An employee absent from the scheduled session attends a make-up session at another branch within thirty days; no employee is excused on the ground of prior attendance in an earlier revision year, as the classification of Category II fields was amended at Revision 2.

Each attendee signs the acknowledgment below the fold, which recites that the employee has received the handout, has had the opportunity to ask questions, and understands that mishandling of restricted information carries discipline through termination. Signed acknowledgments are forwarded intact to Compliance Administration and are retained in the training file, not in the personnel file. Completed scenario sheets are forwarded under separate cover and are not scored by branch management.

Branches reporting no session held will be scheduled by Compliance Administration without further notice to the branch.
