PURPOSE

This handout supports the mandatory annual refresher for billing office personnel. Completion is tracked in the Learning Management System. Attestation is due within fourteen (14) calendar days of assignment.

SECTION 1 — CATEGORIES OF RESTRICTED INFORMATION

Category A — Protected Health Information (PHI). Any field that identifies, or could reasonably be used to identify, an individual and relates to care, payment, or eligibility. Restricted fields include:
- Patient Name
- Medical Record Number (MRN)
- Account Number / Guarantor Account Number
- Date of Birth
- Date of Admission / Date of Discharge / Date of Service
- Street Address, City, ZIP Code
- Home Phone, Mobile Phone
- Email Address
- Health Plan Beneficiary Number / Member ID
- Diagnosis Code and Narrative Description
- Procedure Code and Narrative Description

Category B — Financial Account Data. Restricted fields include Cardholder Name, Primary Account Number (PAN), Card Expiration Date, Card Verification Value, Bank Routing Number, Bank Account Number, Payment Plan Identifier.

Category C — Government Identifiers. Restricted fields include Social Security Number, Taxpayer Identification Number, Driver License Number, Medicare Beneficiary Identifier.

Category D — Workforce and Proprietary Data. Restricted fields include Employee Identification Number, Compensation Band, Payer Contract Rate Schedule, Negotiated Discount Percentage, Internal Audit Finding.

Category E — Legal Hold Material. Any record flagged by the Records Custodian under an active preservation notice, including correspondence, work papers, and claim images.

SECTION 2 — HANDLING RULES

2.1 Minimum necessary. Access only the records required to complete an assigned work queue item. Curiosity is not a business purpose.

2.2 Transmission. External transmission of Category A, B, or C data requires encryption. Personal email accounts and personal cloud storage are prohibited without exception.

2.3 Printing. Printed claim images, remittance advice, and work lists must be retrieved immediately and placed in secure shred bins at end of shift. Desks are subject to unannounced clean-desk inspection.

2.4 Verbal disclosure. Do not discuss account specifics in corridors, elevators, cafeteria, or shared waiting areas. Telephone workstations must be positioned away from public thoroughfares.

2.5 Storage media. Removable media is blocked at the endpoint. Requests for exception route to the Information Security Officer.

2.6 Vendor contact. Refer all outside collection agency and payer audit inquiries to the Revenue Cycle Supervisor. Do not confirm or deny that a record exists.

SECTION 3 — CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING

3.1 Organizational. Breach notification obligations to the affected individual, to the Secretary, and, where thresholds are met, to prominent media outlets. Civil monetary penalties assessed per violation with annual caps by culpability tier. Potential corrective action plan and monitoring period.

3.2 Individual. Progressive discipline up to and including termination. Referral for criminal prosecution where disclosure was made under false pretenses or for personal gain or malicious harm. Loss of system credentials pending investigation. Reporting to licensure boards where applicable.

3.3 Timeliness. Suspected incidents must be reported to the Privacy Officer within one (1) hour of discovery. Delay is itself a disciplinable failure.
