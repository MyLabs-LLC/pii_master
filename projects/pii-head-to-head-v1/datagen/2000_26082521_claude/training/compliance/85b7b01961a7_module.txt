CITY WATER & LIGHT DEPARTMENT
INFORMATION SECURITY AND RECORDS COMPLIANCE
Employee Training Handout — Form ISR-104
Revision Date: March 2004
Issuing Office: Office of the Records Custodian

SECTION 1 — PURPOSE
To instruct Department staff on the categories of information that may not be released, copied, or discussed outside authorized channels, and on the penalties for improper disclosure.

SECTION 2 — WHO MUST COMPLETE THIS HANDOUT
All full-time, part-time, seasonal, and contract personnel with access to customer service terminals, meter reading devices, billing files, personnel files, or plant control systems. Completion is required within thirty (30) days of hire and annually thereafter.

SECTION 3 — RESTRICTED CATEGORIES
3.1 Customer account information: account number, service address, Social Security number, date of birth, driver's license number, credit history, deposit amount, payment history.
3.2 Cardholder and bank data: card account number, expiration date, bank routing number, checking account number.
3.3 Employee information: personnel file contents, medical certifications, disciplinary records, payroll deduction records.
3.4 Life-support and medical equipment registry entries.
3.5 System security information: plant schematics, reservoir and well locations not already published, vulnerability assessment findings, SCADA access credentials, alarm response procedures.
3.6 Legal and investigative material: pending litigation files, claims investigations, internal audit working papers.

SECTION 4 — CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING
Verbal counseling, written reprimand, suspension without pay, or termination, depending on severity and repetition. Improper release of security information or customer financial data may also result in referral to the City Attorney and to law enforcement. Civil liability may attach to the employee individually.

SECTION 5 — SCENARIO QUIZ
Mark T or F.
5.1 ___ A caller states they are the spouse of an account holder and requests the current balance and the last payment date. Because the caller knows the service address, the representative may read the account history aloud.
5.2 ___ A local newspaper requests a list of all customers enrolled in the life-support equipment registry. The request must be routed to the Records Custodian rather than answered at the counter.
5.3 ___ A field crew leader photocopies a distribution main schematic to keep in a personal vehicle for convenience. This is an acceptable practice.
5.4 ___ A terminal is left signed on while the operator steps away from the counter for ten minutes. This is reportable to the supervisor.
