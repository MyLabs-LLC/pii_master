CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING. Any disclosure, transmission, reproduction, or
oral repetition of a Class I, II, or III item to a party lacking both a
business need and a documented authorization shall constitute a reportable
privacy incident. The employee shall report same to the Records Custodian
before the close of the business day on which the occurrence is discovered.
Substantiated incidents are subject to progressive discipline up to and
including discharge, and, where the applicable state insurance code so
provides, to referral for civil penalty. Ignorance of the classification of
a given field shall not excuse its disclosure; where classification is
uncertain, the employee shall withhold the item and consult the Unit
Supervisor.
