CONFIDENTIALITY AND INFORMATION SAFEGUARDS — ANNUAL WORKFORCE TRAINING
County Health Department | Division of Epidemiology, Public Health Nursing, and Vital Records
Facilitator Slide Script — Revision 4 | Effective First Quarter 2021 | Required of All Workforce Members, Volunteers, Students, and Contracted Personnel

SLIDE 1 — SCOPE AND GOVERNING AUTHORITY (read verbatim)
• This instruction is promulgated pursuant to the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act Privacy and Security Rules, the applicable provisions of the state public health code governing communicable disease reporting, the state vital statistics act, and Departmental Administrative Policy AP-114 (Information Classification and Handling).
• Attendance is mandatory and is documented in the Learning Management System under the fields Employee Number, Position Classification, Division Code, Completion Date, and Attestation Signature.
• Nothing in this instruction shall be construed to diminish any obligation arising under a separate confidentiality agreement, data use agreement, or business associate agreement to which the Department is a party.

SLIDE 2 — CATEGORIES OF RESTRICTED INFORMATION (checklist — confirm each is understood)
• Protected Health Information, including but not limited to the data elements labeled Patient Name, Date of Birth, Medical Record Number, Encounter Identifier, Diagnosis Code, Specimen Accession Number, and Treating Provider.
• Communicable disease investigation records, including the fields labeled Case Identifier, Onset Date, Exposure Site, Contact Name, Isolation Start Date, and Quarantine End Date, which are further protected from disclosure by state statute irrespective of any federal exemption.
• Vital records information, including the fields labeled Registrant Name, Certificate Number, Place of Birth, Informant Name, and Cause of Death.
• Individually identifiable financial and administrative data, including the fields labeled Social Security Number, Medicaid Member Identifier, Insurance Group Number, Payment Card Number, and Billing Account Number.
• Personnel and applicant records, including the fields labeled Employee Home Address, Personal Telephone Number, Personal Electronic Mail Address, Disciplinary Action Type, and Occupational Health Clearance Status.
• Direct contact identifiers appearing in any medium whatsoever, including the fields labeled Street Address, Apartment or Unit, Mobile Telephone Number, and Emergency Contact.

SLIDE 3 — CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING (state each consequence; do not paraphrase)
• Administrative discipline up to and including termination of employment, revocation of volunteer status, or termination of a contract for cause.
• Referral to the appropriate licensing board where the workforce member holds a professional credential.
• Civil monetary penalties assessed against the Department and, in cases of knowing acquisition or disclosure, individual criminal liability including fine and imprisonment.
• Mandatory breach notification to affected persons, to the Secretary of Health and Human Services, and, where the threshold is met, to prominent media outlets serving the jurisdiction.
• Permanent revocation of access credentials to the Electronic Disease Surveillance System, the Immunization Information System, and the Vital Records Registry.

SLIDE 4 — SCENARIO QUIZ (facilitator reads aloud; participants respond by show of hands)
• Scenario One: A caller identifying themself as a relative requests the isolation end date associated with a case under investigation. Permissible or impermissible, and on what authority?
• Scenario Two: A nurse in the immunization clinic photographs a screen containing the Patient Name and Medical Record Number columns in order to complete documentation from home. Identify each policy violated.
• Scenario Three: A line list is exported to a spreadsheet and transmitted to a community partner without execution of a data use agreement. Identify the required corrective actions and the notification timeline.
• Scenario Four: A workforce member observes a printed contact tracing roster left unattended at a shared printer. State the reporting obligation and the office to which the report is directed.
