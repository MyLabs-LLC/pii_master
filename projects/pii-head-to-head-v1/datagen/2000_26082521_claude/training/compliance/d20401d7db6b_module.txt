CONSTRUCTION OF THIS BULLETIN. The enumeration above is illustrative and
not exhaustive. Where a field label is not enumerated, the employee shall
treat the field as restricted until the Records Custodian has classified
it in writing. No verbal classification shall be relied upon.

PENALTY PROVISIONS. Each of the following is independently sufficient
ground for discipline: (a) release of a restricted field to a party who
has not been verified in accordance with the caller verification
procedure; (b) transmission of a restricted field to a facsimile machine
or modem line not designated as secure; (c) removal of a restricted
document from Company premises without a signed transit log entry;
(d) inspection of a file for a purpose other than the performance of an
assigned duty; and (e) failure to report an occurrence known to the
employee within one business day. Penalties range from documented
counseling to discharge, and, in the case of subsections (a), (d), and
(e), may be reported to the Department of Insurance where the applicable
code so requires.
