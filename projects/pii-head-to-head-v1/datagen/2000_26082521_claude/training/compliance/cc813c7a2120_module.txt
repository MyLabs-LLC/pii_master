SECTION B — RESTRICTED INFORMATION CATEGORIES
The following categories are designated Restricted. Access is limited to workforce members with a documented business need.

B.1 Protected Health Information (PHI)
Includes any health record element combined with an identifier. Identifier field labels include: Patient Name, Medical Record Number, Health Plan Beneficiary Number, Date of Birth, Admission Date, Discharge Date, Date of Death, Street Address, ZIP Code, Telephone Number, Fax Number, Email Address, Social Security Number, Account Number, Certificate or License Number, Vehicle Identifier, Device Serial Number, Web URL, IP Address, Biometric Identifier, Full-Face Photograph.

B.2 Communicable Disease Case Data
Includes reportable condition surveillance records, case investigation notes, contact tracing rosters, and laboratory result reports. Column names in the case registry include: Case ID, Reporting Provider, Condition Code, Onset Date, Interview Status, Contact Count, Jurisdiction Code.

B.3 Vital Records
Includes birth certificate applications, death certificate worksheets, fetal death reports, and amendment petitions.

B.4 Substance Use Disorder Treatment Records
Subject to heightened federal confidentiality protections. Redisclosure is prohibited absent specific written consent.

B.5 Human Resources and Payroll Data
Includes personnel action forms, disciplinary files, benefits enrollment records, and direct deposit authorizations.

B.6 Security and Infrastructure Data
Includes system credentials, network diagrams, access control rosters, and audit log extracts.

SECTION C — CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING
C.1 Administrative: Verbal counseling, written reprimand, suspension without pay, revocation of system access, or termination of employment or contract.
C.2 Civil: Departmental exposure to monetary penalties assessed per violation, tiered by degree of culpability. Individual workforce members may be named in civil actions.
C.3 Criminal: Knowing disclosure of individually identifiable health information for personal gain or malicious harm may result in fines and imprisonment under applicable federal statute.
C.4 Licensure: Referral to the applicable professional licensing board.
C.5 Notification: Breach affecting the confidentiality of Restricted Information triggers mandatory notification to affected individuals, the state health authority, and, above the applicable threshold, prominent media outlets serving the jurisdiction.
