MODULE 4 - PRESENTER SCRIPT

DELIVERED BY: STORE MANAGER OR DESIGNEE. SIGN-IN SHEET REQUIRED.
READ ALOUD. DO NOT PARAPHRASE THE CONSEQUENCE MATRIX.

SLIDE 1 - PURPOSE
  READ: This module covers restricted information, your handling duties,
  and the consequence schedule in force at this location.
  NOTE: Confirm every attendee has signed in before proceeding.

SLIDE 2 - WHAT IS RESTRICTED
  READ: Eight categories are listed on Form PHR-404 at the bench. If a
  field label appears on that schedule, the contents of that field are
  restricted. This applies on paper, on screen, on the phone, and by fax.
  READ: Restriction does not end at the door. It continues after your
  shift, after transfer, and after separation from the company.

SLIDE 3 - THE THREE FAILURE MODES
  READ: Most occurrences fall into one of three patterns.
    1. Talking. Discussing a fill within earshot of the sales floor.
    2. Leaving. Unattended screens, labels at the bench, paper in general
       trash, faxes left in the tray.
    3. Looking. Accessing a profile with no assigned duty.

SLIDE 4 - CONSEQUENCE MATRIX
  READ: The following schedule is applied by management without regard to
  intent. Intent affects the referral, not the tier.

  TIER | OCCURRENCE TYPE                | MINIMUM MANAGEMENT ACTION        | REFERRAL
  -----+--------------------------------+----------------------------------+-------------
  I    | Verbal disclosure, in-store    | Documented coaching; re-test     | Store Mgr
  II   | Unattended terminal / label    | Written warning; access audit    | District Mgr
  III  | Disclosure to third party      | Suspension pending review        | Privacy Off.
  IV   | Sale or transfer of records    | Termination; law enforcement     | Corp. Legal
  V    | Unauthorized C-II diversion    | Termination; DEA notification    | Corp. Legal
  -----+--------------------------------+----------------------------------+-------------

  READ: Tiers III through V carry external exposure. Civil monetary
  penalties and criminal referral under the federal privacy rule attach to
  the individual, not solely to the company.

SLIDE 5 - REPORTING DUTY
  READ: You are required to report a suspected occurrence by the end of
  the shift on which you observe it. Report to the Pharmacist-in-Charge.
  If the Pharmacist-in-Charge is the subject, report to the District
  Manager or the compliance line posted in the break room.
  READ: Failure to report a known occurrence is itself a Tier II matter.
  Good-faith reports are not grounds for discipline. Retaliation against a
  reporting employee is a separate terminable offense.

SLIDE 6 - HIGH-EXPOSURE POINTS AT THIS LOCATION
  READ: Drive-thru window, will-call bin visible from the register queue,
  the shared fax at the front end, the pseudoephedrine logbook, and the
  drop-off basket. Each is audited during the district walk.

SLIDE 7 - CLOSE
  READ: Complete the scenario check now. Closed book. Ten minutes.
  Attendees scoring below four of six repeat this module within seven days.
  NOTE: Collect all answer sheets. Route per Form PHR-404A. Do not leave
  completed sheets at the bench overnight.
