SECTION 3. SCENARIO REVIEW

1. A clerk in the immunization unit receives a telephone request from a
   caller who states they are a parent and asks for a child's shot record.
   The caller cannot supply the chart identifier. Correct action?
   (a) Read the record aloud. (b) Verify identity through the written
   release procedure. (c) Mail the record to the address given by phone.

2. A field nurse leaves a case report folder on the passenger seat of a
   parked vehicle for ninety minutes. Classify the event.
   (a) No incident. (b) Reportable security event. (c) Reportable only if
   the folder is later found missing.

3. An epidemiologist copies a disease registry extract to a diskette to
   finish an analysis at home. Applicable rule?
   (a) Permitted for public health purposes. (b) Prohibited absent written
   authorization from the Records Custodian. (c) Permitted if the diskette
   is returned the next business day.

4. A supervisor asks a payroll technician for a coworker's home address to
   send a card. Correct action?
   (a) Provide it. (b) Decline; refer to Human Resources. (c) Provide only
   the street name.
