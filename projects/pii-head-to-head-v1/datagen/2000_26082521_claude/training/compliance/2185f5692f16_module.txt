INFORMATION CLASSIFICATION BRIEFING
Retail Pharmacy Division | Learning Reference LR-2021-04
Issued by the Office of the Records Custodian | Review due: annually

PART ONE — STANDING RULES

  §1  Every colleague holds restricted information in trust. The trust is
      defined by role, not by access. A credential that opens a record does not
      authorise the opening of that record.
  §2  Classification attaches at the field level. Where a document mixes classes,
      the document takes the highest class present.
  §3  No restricted field leaves the store network. This includes personal
      devices, personal accounts, and messaging applications.

PART TWO — CATEGORY AND RULE

.--------------------------------------------------------------------------.
| §  | RESTRICTED CATEGORY        | HANDLING RULE                          |
:----+----------------------------+----------------------------------------:
| 1  | Patient dispensing data    | Access only to fill an active order    |
| 2  | Payment card data          | Never write down; never repeat aloud   |
| 3  | Government identifiers     | Enter at terminal; no paper copy       |
| 4  | Controlled substance log   | Dual sign-off; secure storage          |
| 5  | Colleague records          | Human Resources channel only           |
| 6  | Store operations data      | Internal distribution only             |
'--------------------------------------------------------------------------'

PART THREE — CONSEQUENCE

  §4  A substantiated finding is recorded in the colleague file and reported to
      the Privacy Office by the Pharmacy Manager within one business day.
  §5  Categories 1 through 4 carry external reporting duties. Depending on the
      category, notification runs to the state board of pharmacy, the acquiring
      bank, the affected payer, or the federal regulator.
  §6  Disciplinary outcome scales with class and repetition: coaching for a
      first non-repeat finding; final written warning where a record was copied
      or photographed; termination where a record left the site; termination
      with referral where information was transferred for value.
  §7  Regulatory penalty is assessed against the Company and, where the conduct
      was knowing, against the individual colleague.

PART FOUR — SCENARIO EXERCISE

  §8  A technician receives a telephone request from a person stating they are
      calling from a prescriber's office, asking for the full dispensing history
      of a patient. The caller cannot supply a callback number on the practice
      record. Identify the category and the required action.
  §9  A front-store associate finds a printed eligibility slip left on a counter
      after close. Identify the category and the required action.
  §10 A shift supervisor is asked by a colleague to disclose the wage band of a
      newly hired technician. Identify the category and the required action.
  §11 A district visitor requests an export of contract rates to a personal
      spreadsheet for a weekend review. Identify the category and the action.

PART FIVE — MODEL ANSWERS

  §12 On §8: Category 1. Decline, take the practice name, and return the call
      only on the number held on the prescriber record.
  §13 On §9: Category 1. Place the slip in the secure destruction bin and log
      the find in the shift log.
  §14 On §10: Category 5. Decline and direct the colleague to Human Resources.
  §15 On §11: Category 6. Decline the export. Provide access through the
      internal reporting portal only.
