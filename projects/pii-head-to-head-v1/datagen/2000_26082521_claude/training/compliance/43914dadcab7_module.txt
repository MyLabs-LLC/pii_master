I. PURPOSE AND GOVERNING AUTHORITY

This handout is issued pursuant to the Company's Written Information Security Program and is intended to satisfy the annual workforce training obligation imposed by Title V of the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act, the Safeguards Rule promulgated thereunder at 16 C.F.R. Part 314, the data security statutes and insurance department regulations of each jurisdiction in which the Company is licensed as a title insurance agent or escrow agent, the underwriting agreements to which the Company is a party, and Pillar Three of the ALTA Title Insurance and Settlement Company Best Practices. Attendance shall be documented by the Records Custodian and retained for a period of not less than five (5) years.

II. CATEGORIES OF RESTRICTED INFORMATION

The following data elements, whether appearing in an escrow file, a title commitment work-up, a closing disclosure worksheet, a wire instruction, a lien payoff demand, or any electronic system of record, constitute Nonpublic Personal Information ("NPI") and are Restricted:

  (a) Government identifiers, including the field labels Social Security Number, Individual Taxpayer Identification Number, Employer Identification Number, Driver's License Number, State Identification Card Number, Passport Number, and Permanent Resident Card Number;
  (b) Financial account identifiers, including Deposit Account Number, ABA Routing Number, Credit Card Account Number, Card Verification Value, Loan Number, and Escrow Trust Account Number;
  (c) Identity attributes, including Full Legal Name, Date of Birth, Maiden Name, Marital Status, and Mother's Maiden Name where collected for authentication purposes;
  (d) Contact and location attributes, including Property Street Address where linked to a natural person, Mailing Address, Telephone Number, and Electronic Mail Address;
  (e) Transaction attributes, including Credit Score, Income, Payoff Balance, Earnest Money Amount, Wire Instruction Beneficiary Detail, and any Consumer Report obtained from a consumer reporting agency.

Field labels and column headings are themselves unrestricted and appear throughout Company forms; it is the populated value that is Restricted.

III. CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING

Unauthorized access, disclosure, transmission, storage, or disposal of Restricted Information may result in: (i) corrective counseling, suspension, or termination of employment without regard to intent; (ii) referral to state insurance regulators, the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, or state attorneys general; (iii) individual civil liability under applicable state statutes; (iv) revocation of the individual's escrow officer or notary commission; (v) mandatory consumer notification at Company expense; and (vi) suspension or termination of the Company's underwriting agreements.
