BRANCH REFRESHER NOTICE — RESTRICTED INFORMATION
Distribute at the weekly production meeting · Second quarter, 2004

1. Every escrow and title file in this office contains restricted content, and the content is identified by field label, not by the color of the folder. The labels that trigger the highest handling standard are Social Security Number, Taxpayer Identification Number, Date of Birth, Driver's License Number, Deposit Account Number, Routing/ABA Number, Credit Card Number, Loan Number, Payoff Balance, and Gross Monthly Income.

2. Restricted content leaves a desk only in a sealed pouch or by confirmed fax, and it is stored only under lock. Superseded paper is cross-cut shredded. Nothing goes to recycling.

3. Disclosure to a person outside the file team, however casual, is a reportable event. Report it to the branch manager the same day and to the Compliance Officer in writing within twenty-four hours. Late reporting is treated more severely than the disclosure itself.

4. Consequences run to the Company as penalty and bond exposure, to the branch as license exposure, and to the employee as discipline through termination.

5. Consider before the meeting ends: a caller who will not supply the escrow number offers a Date of Birth instead; an unattended workstation is found displaying a payoff screen. State the required action for each.
