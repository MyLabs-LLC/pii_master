BRANCH QUICK REFERENCE — RESTRICTED DATA
Posted at: recruiter workstations, payroll desks, front-of-house intake counter
Maintained by: Office of the Records Custodian | 2021 edition

    - WHAT IS RESTRICTED
        - [R1] Identity and tax
            - Social Security Number / Individual Taxpayer Identification Number
            - Work Authorization Document Number / Driver's License Number
        - [R2] Money movement
            - Bank Routing Number / Deposit Account Number / Pay Card Number
            - Garnishment Case Reference
        - [R3] Health and screening
            - Drug Screen Result / Physical Capacity Restriction
            - Accommodation Request Type / Leave Reason Code
        - [R4] Background
            - Consumer Report Reference / Adjudication Outcome / Adverse Action Stage
        - [R5] Identity in the ordinary sense
            - Full Legal Name / Date of Birth / Home Address
            - Personal Telephone Number / Personal Email Address

    - WHERE IT LIVES
        - R1, R2, R4: system of record only. No local copy, no export without approval.
        - R3: segregated medical file. Never in the ATS candidate record.
        - R5: ATS candidate record. Masked in shared views and printed rosters.

    - WHAT GOES WRONG MOST OFTEN
        - Restricted fields pasted into a message to a client contact.
        - Screening documents filed with the general onboarding paperwork.
        - Intake forms left face-up at the counter during shift change.
        - Account changes accepted by telephone without verification.

    - WHAT FOLLOWS
        - Entitlements suspended the day the finding is confirmed.
        - Corrective action up to and including termination.
        - Client notification, and state breach notification where the statute is triggered.
        - Report suspected exposure to the Records Custodian within one hour. Late reporting is treated as a separate violation.

    - CHECK YOURSELF
        - 1. A caller asks a recruiter to confirm which of two candidates has the cleaner background report. Permitted?
        - 2. An intake form showing Social Security Number and Date of Birth is scanned, then the paper is placed in the recycling bin. Which control failed?
        - 3. A supervisor asks for the medical reason behind a duty restriction. What is disclosable?
        - Answers: 1. No. No comparative or underlying disclosure; fitness determination only, and only for the assigned requisition. 2. Physical disposal. Cross-cut shredding is required. 3. The restriction as it affects assignment fit. Nothing else.
