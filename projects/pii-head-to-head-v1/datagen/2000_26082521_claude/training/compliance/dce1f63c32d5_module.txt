MEMORANDUM — CONFIDENTIALITY REMINDER, WINTER RECESS

To:   All Building Staff
From: Records Custodian
Date: December 13, 2011

Effective at dismissal, December 16, 2011:

-  No paper record bearing Student ID, Date of Birth, Home Address, Guardian
   Telephone, Meal Eligibility, Special Education Eligibility Code, Health
   Condition Note, or Disciplinary Action Code may leave the building.
-  No District laptop may be taken home without an approved Form T-7 on file.
-  All flash drives holding student or employee exports are to be surrendered
   to the building secretary for secured storage.
-  Desk drawers and file cabinets containing cumulative folders are to be
   locked. Keys remain in the building.
-  Grade entry from home is performed through the remote portal only. Local
   downloads are prohibited.

Mishandling during recess is treated as a reportable incident. Sanctions range
from written reprimand to termination and, where disclosure occurs, referral
for prosecution. Notification costs are charged to the originating department.

SCENARIO: A staff member intends to grade at home over the break and copies
the class export, including Student ID and State Assessment Scale Score, to a
personal external drive.

RESPONSE: Prohibited. Use the remote portal. Any copy already made is to be
reported to the Records Custodian on the first business day of return and
destroyed under witness.

Building administrators will conduct a walkthrough on December 16 after
dismissal. Deficiencies are logged to the building compliance file.
