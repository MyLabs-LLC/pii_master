SANCTIONS
First substantiated incident: written reprimand, retraining, access review.
Repeat or aggravated incident: suspension without pay, recommendation for
termination, licensing board referral, prosecution referral. Household
notification costs charged to the originating department.

SELF-CHECK
1. A department secretary e-mails a building principal a list containing
   Student ID and Eligibility Code using the District system.
   Acceptable. Internal, need to know, managed system. Log the disclosure.

2. A volunteer asks for a class list with names and grade levels for a
   fundraiser mailing.
   Not acceptable until opt-out status is verified and a confidentiality
   agreement is on file.

3. A staff member carries a printed report containing Bank Routing Number to
   a satellite office in an unsealed folder.
   Not acceptable. Use interoffice sealed pouch, tracked.

4. A counselor is asked in a hallway by another employee about a student's
   Section 504 Plan Status.
   Not acceptable to answer. Need to know is established by assignment, not
   by curiosity.

Report suspected mishandling to the Office of the Records Custodian on Form
IR-2 within one business day of discovery.
