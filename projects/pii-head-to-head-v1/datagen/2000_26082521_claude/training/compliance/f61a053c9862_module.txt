PURPOSE

This handout supports the Part 1 session of the annual information handling training required under ALTA Best Practices Pillar 3 and the Company's Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA) Safeguards Program. It defines which categories of information are restricted, where each category may be stored, and who may release it. Sign the attendance roster at the end of the session; the Records Custodian will file rosters with the training log.

THREE HANDLING TIERS

Tier 1 — Nonpublic Personal Information (NPI). Any information about a consumer obtained in connection with a settlement service that is not publicly available. Encryption required at rest and in transit. Release only to the consumer of record, that consumer's written designee, or a party named in the settlement statement.

Tier 2 — Transaction Confidential. Information that is not NPI but would harm a party or the Company if disclosed. Internal systems only. No transmission outside the Company without the Escrow Manager's approval.

Tier 3 — Public Record. Recorded instruments and matters of public record. May be released without restriction, subject to the plant licensing terms.

STANDING RULES

1. Never place a Tier 1 field value in the subject line or body of an email. Use the secure portal link generated by the Escrow Platform.
2. Wire instructions are verified by outbound call to a number obtained independently of the request. Inbound-only verification is prohibited without exception.
3. Personal cloud storage, personal email, and personal messaging applications are not approved for any tier.
4. Documents printed for closing are logged out at the printer and either delivered to the file or placed in the locked shred bin the same business day.
5. Screen-lock is mandatory when leaving a workstation, including in the closing room.
6. Report any suspected disclosure to the Compliance Department within one hour of discovery. Do not attempt remediation first.

Questions on classification of a field not listed above go to the Compliance Department before the field is used or transmitted.
