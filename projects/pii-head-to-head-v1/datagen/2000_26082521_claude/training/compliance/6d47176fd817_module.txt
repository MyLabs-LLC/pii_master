MEMORANDUM

TO: All Branch Staff, Recruiters, Account Managers, On-Site Coordinators
FROM: Office of Compliance and Records Governance
DATE: 12 March 2021
RE: Module 4 Handout — Restricted Information Categories, Duties, and Consequences
CLASSIFICATION: Internal Use Only

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
1.1 This memorandum is the written handout for Module 4 of the annual compliance curriculum and shall be read in full before attendance at the live session.
1.2 It binds every person who creates, receives, transmits, stores, or destroys agency records, including temporary internal staff and contracted payroll processors.

2. RESTRICTED CATEGORIES
2.1 Tier 1 — Identity and Tax. Fields: Full Legal Name; Former Legal Name; Social Security Number; Individual Taxpayer Identification Number; Date of Birth; Government Photo Identification Number; Work Authorization Document Number; Form I-9 Section 2 Document Title and Expiration.
2.2 Tier 1 fields may be entered only in the applicant tracking system, and never reproduced in electronic mail, chat, local spreadsheets, or handwritten notes.
2.3 Tier 2 — Financial and Payroll. Fields: Bank Routing Number; Deposit Account Number; Pay Rate; Garnishment Order Reference; W-4 Withholding Elections.
2.4 Tier 3 — Health and Screening. Fields: Drug Screen Result; Physical Capacity Evaluation Outcome; Accommodation Request Detail; Workers' Compensation Claim Number.
2.5 Tier 4 — Contact and Household. Fields: Residential Street Address; Personal Mobile Number; Personal Electronic Mail Address; Emergency Contact Name and Relationship.
2.6 Tier 5 — Background Adjudication. Fields: Consumer Report Identifier; Disposition Code; Adverse Action Notice Date.

3. HANDLING DUTIES
3.1 The Records Custodian is the sole approver for any export containing Tier 1 or Tier 2 fields.
3.2 Workstations displaying restricted fields shall be locked when unattended, including during client walkthroughs of the branch.
3.3 Paper of any tier shall be deposited in the locked destruction console at close of business. Desk drawers are not approved storage.
3.4 A client request for Tier 1, Tier 3, or Tier 5 fields shall be refused and referred to the Account Compliance Lead. Clients receive roster fields only: Assignment ID; Position Title; Start Date; Shift Code; Billing Rate.
3.5 Photographing a screen or document with a personal device is prohibited without exception.

4. CONSEQUENCES
4.1 A first substantiated incident results in written counseling and re-certification within ten business days.
4.2 A second incident, or any incident involving Tier 1 or Tier 2 fields, results in suspension of system entitlements pending Disciplinary Panel review and may result in termination.
4.3 Disclosure triggering a state breach-notification statute obligates the agency to notify affected persons and the applicable Attorney General at agency cost, and to indemnify the client under Section 9 of the master services agreement.
4.4 Knowing misuse of Tier 1 fields may constitute identity theft under federal law and will be referred to counsel.

5. SCENARIO QUIZ
5.1 A recruiter forwards a completed onboarding packet to a personal address to finish data entry from home. Identify the tiers implicated and the correct alternative.
5.2 A client supervisor telephones the branch and asks for a drug screen result before approving a shift change. State the required response.
5.3 A roster showing Deposit Account Number is found on the shared printer after hours. State the first two required actions and the reporting deadline.
5.4 A coordinator keeps a private spreadsheet of Emergency Contact Name and Personal Mobile Number to speed call-outs. State whether this is permitted, and why.
5.5 Responses are due to the Records Custodian on the Module 4 form within five business days.
