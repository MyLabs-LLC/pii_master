MEMORANDUM

TO:    Billing Representatives, Cashiers, and Insurance Follow-Up Staff
FROM:  Director of Patient Accounts
DATE:  September 24, 1997
RE:    Screen-by-Screen Guide to Restricted Fields; Discipline Schedule; Self-Test

1.  The following is a field-level guide to the three screens used most often in this department. Every field listed is restricted. Restriction attaches to the field wherever it is displayed, printed, spoken aloud, or transmitted.

2.  ACCOUNT INQUIRY (Screen AR01)

        Column heading              Restricted
        ------------------------    ----------
        Patient Name                 Yes
        Medical Record Number        Yes
        Account Number               Yes
        Patient Birthdate            Yes
        Financial Class              Yes
        Guarantor Name               Yes
        Guarantor Employer           Yes
        Current Balance              Yes
        Last Payment Date            Yes

3.  CLAIM MAINTENANCE (Screen CL14)

        Column heading              Restricted
        ------------------------    ----------
        Type of Bill                 Yes
        Principal Diagnosis Code     Yes
        Other Diagnosis Codes        Yes
        Principal Procedure Code     Yes
        Attending Physician UPIN     Yes
        Insured's Unique ID          Yes
        Subscriber SSN               Yes
        Discharge Status             Yes

4.  AGED TRIAL BALANCE (Report AR-330). Every line of this report is restricted in its entirety. The report is numbered, logged out by the Records Custodian, and shredded on return. It does not leave the floor.

5.  Requests touching alcohol or drug treatment, HIV testing, psychiatric admission, or confidential services to a minor are not answered at the workstation under any circumstance. They are transferred to the Records Custodian.

6.  DISCIPLINE SCHEDULE

    6.1  Unattended active terminal, printout left in a common area, misdirected facsimile: documented counseling; second instance, written warning.

    6.2  Discussion of an account in the corridor, elevator, or cafeteria: written warning.

    6.3  Access without an assigned business purpose, including a record belonging to a relative, coworker, or acquaintance: suspension without pay pending review; termination is the ordinary outcome.

    6.4  Disclosure to a person outside the hospital without verified authorization: termination.

    6.5  Disclosure for money or favor, or removal of any list derived from the receivable file: termination, referral to law enforcement, and reporting to the applicable licensing board.

7.  SELF-TEST. Answer in writing; return to your supervisor by Friday.

    7.1  An attorney's office telephones and asks you to confirm the admission date and the principal diagnosis on a pending account, offering to send a subpoena afterward. What do you do first?

    7.2  You are asked by a coworker in another department for a copy of Report AR-330 "just for the totals." Is the request grantable? If not, name the alternative.

    7.3  While working a denial you notice that the account belongs to someone you know socially. Describe the required action and the person to whom you report it.
