INFORMATION CLASSIFICATION AND HANDLING
Annual Privacy and Security Training — Participant Handout
Corporate Compliance Office | Form CT-114 (Rev. 02/04)
Audience: All associates, contract staff, temporary personnel, and vendor representatives with badge access

SECTION 1 — RESTRICTED CATEGORIES
- Nonpublic Personal Information (NPI) under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act and the state Insurance Information and Privacy Protection Act. Field labels in this class: Applicant Name; Social Security Number; Taxpayer Identification Number; Date of Birth; Residence Address; Home Telephone; Employer Name; Annual Household Income.
- Protected Health Information (PHI) under the HIPAA Privacy Rule. Field labels in this class: Member Identification Number; Group Number; Diagnosis Code; Procedure Code; Date of Service; Attending Provider; Treatment Notes; Prior Authorization Reference.
- Financial account data. Field labels in this class: Bank Routing Number; Demand Deposit Account Number; Credit Card Number; Card Expiration; Premium Draft Authorization.
- Claim investigation material. Field labels in this class: Claim Number; SIU Referral Reason; Recorded Statement Transcript; Surveillance Log; Independent Medical Examination Report.
- Underwriting work product. Field labels in this class: MIB Code; Motor Vehicle Report; Attending Physician Statement; Lab Panel Result; Rating Class Assigned; Declination Reason.
- Personnel records held by Human Resources. Field labels in this class: Employee Number; Performance Rating; Compensation Band; Leave Category; Garnishment Order Type.

SECTION 2 — HANDLING REQUIREMENTS BY MEDIUM
- Paper. File under lock at end of shift. Clean-desk rule applies at all workstations, including cubicles inside the secured claims floor. Shred in the locked bins only; do not use open recycling containers.
- Facsimile. Confirm the destination number against the maintained directory before transmission. Use the approved cover sheet. Retrieve output from the machine immediately.
- Electronic mail. Restricted categories may not be sent to any address outside the corporate domain without Compliance approval and approved encryption. Distribution lists are not to be used for case-level correspondence.
- Removable media. Diskettes, CD-R, and zip cartridges containing restricted categories must be logged with the Records Custodian and stored in the media safe.
- Laptops and remote access. Full inventory tag required. Dial-in sessions terminate after fifteen minutes idle. No storage of extracts on local drives.
- Telephone. Verify caller identity against two authentication elements on file before disclosing anything beyond the existence of a file.
- Verbal. No discussion of file content in elevators, the cafeteria, the parking structure, or on speakerphone in open areas.

SECTION 3 — CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING
- Corrective action up to and including termination, per the Associate Handbook, Section 9.
- Referral to the state Department of Insurance, which may result in individual license suspension or revocation for licensed staff.
- Civil monetary penalties assessed against the carrier, and criminal referral where disclosure was made for personal gain or malicious intent.
- Contract termination and debarment for vendor personnel.
- Mandatory notification to affected persons and to regulators where a breach of an unsecured record set is confirmed.
