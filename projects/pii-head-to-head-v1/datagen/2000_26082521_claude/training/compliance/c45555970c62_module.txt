FRONT COUNTER REFERENCE — RESTRICTED FIELDS
Post at every service window. Registrar's Office, revised August 2011.

[ ] Check the confidentiality hold indicator before you speak.
[ ] Verify identity with a university photo credential for in-person requests.
[ ] Never verify identity by telephone using Social Security Number, Date of Birth, or Student Identification Number as a shared secret.
[ ] Never release: Grades, Grade Point Average, Class Rank, Course Schedule, Test Scores, Academic Standing, Disciplinary Record, Financial Aid Award, Account Balance, Home Address, Telephone Number, Electronic Mail Address, Date of Birth, Race and Ethnicity, Parent or Guardian Contact, Emergency Contact, Immunization Record, Disability Accommodation Record.
[ ] May release without a hold: Name, Enrollment Status, Dates of Attendance, Major Field of Study, Degrees Awarded.
[ ] Lock your workstation when you step away, including for a two-minute break.
[ ] Shred anything printed with a Student Identification Number before you leave your shift.

Consequences: credential suspension, written reprimand, termination, federal complaint against the office.

Quick check, answer on the back of this card:
[ ] A caller asks only whether a person is currently enrolled. No hold is present. Do you answer?
[ ] The same caller then asks which building the person's afternoon class meets in. Do you answer?
[ ] A coworker asks you to look up an Account Balance for someone not in your assigned queue. Do you comply?
