MUNICIPAL UTILITY SERVICES
ANNUAL CONFIDENTIALITY BRIEFING — PRESENTER SCRIPT
Course Code: CMP-201
Duration: 25 minutes
Room Setup: Overhead projector, sign-in roster, handout Form ISR-104

SLIDE 1 — TITLE
ON SCREEN: "What We Protect and Why"
SAY: This briefing is required for everyone who touches customer records, personnel records, or plant operations data. Sign the roster before you leave. There is a four-question quiz at the end and it is graded.

SLIDE 2 — THE FOUR BUCKETS
ON SCREEN: Customer Data / Employee Data / Financial Data / System Security Data
SAY: Everything we restrict falls into one of four buckets. If you cannot place a document in a bucket, treat it as restricted until the Records Custodian tells you otherwise.

SLIDE 3 — CUSTOMER DATA
ON SCREEN: Fields listed — Account Number, Service Address, Mailing Address, Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Telephone Number, Deposit Amount, Payment History, Disconnection Notices, Life-Support Registry Status
SAY: Under the Public Records Act, the customer's name and the fact of service may be releasable. The fields on this slide are not. Do not decide this at the counter. Route the request.

SLIDE 4 — EMPLOYEE DATA
ON SCREEN: Fields listed — Home Address, Home Telephone, Social Security Number, Payroll Deductions, Medical Certification, Grievance File, Performance Evaluation
SAY: Curiosity is not authorization. Pulling a coworker's file because you were asked at a barbecue is a disciplinary matter, not a favor.

SLIDE 5 — FINANCIAL DATA
ON SCREEN: Fields listed — Card Account Number, Card Expiration Date, Bank Routing Number, Checking Account Number, Authorization Code
SAY: Never write these on a scratch pad. Never leave them on a fax cover sheet. Payment authorization forms go directly into the locked drop box at the end of each shift.

SLIDE 6 — SYSTEM SECURITY DATA
ON SCREEN: Vulnerability Assessment, Plant Schematics, Well and Reservoir Detail, Chemical Storage Inventory, SCADA User ID and Password, Alarm Response Plan
SAY: Since the 2002 federal preparedness requirements, our vulnerability assessment is exempt from public release. It does not go home, it does not go in a truck, and it does not get discussed with vendors who lack a signed nondisclosure agreement.

SLIDE 7 — HANDLING RULES
ON SCREEN: Lock it / Shred it / Log it / Ask first
SAY: Diskettes and printouts go in the shred bin, not the wastebasket. Sign off your terminal when you step away. Log every records request in the Disclosure Log.

SLIDE 8 — CONSEQUENCES
ON SCREEN: Counseling — Written Reprimand — Suspension — Termination — Referral
SAY: The Department has terminated staff for browsing accounts. Assume every lookup is logged, because it is.

SLIDE 9 — QUIZ
SAY: Answer on the back of your handout, then pass it forward.
Q1: A caller identifying themselves as a landlord asks for the balance owed by the current tenant at a rental property. What do you do?
Q2: A contractor asks for a copy of the treatment plant layout to prepare a bid. What is required first?
Q3: You find a printed payment authorization form on the break room table. What are the two required actions?
Q4: A coworker in another division asks you to look up whether a neighbor is behind on their bill. What is your response, and to whom do you report the request?

SLIDE 10 — CLOSE
ON SCREEN: Records Custodian, Extension listed in the Department directory
SAY: Questions go to the Records Custodian. Roster must be signed.
