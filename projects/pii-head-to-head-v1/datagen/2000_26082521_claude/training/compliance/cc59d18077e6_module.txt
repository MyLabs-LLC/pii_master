MEMORANDUM

TO: Billing Office Team Leads
FROM: Privacy Officer
RE: Consequence Schedule — Restricted Information Mishandling
CLASSIFICATION: Internal Use Only

Team leads are directed to review the following schedule with their units during the next available huddle and to record the review in the unit log.

TIER 1 — PROCEDURAL LAPSE, NO DISCLOSURE
Examples: unattended printout recovered intact; screen left unlocked in a secured area; verbal discussion in a semi-private area with no listener present.
Response: documented coaching by team lead. Entry retained twelve months.

TIER 2 — DISCLOSURE TO AN UNAUTHORIZED INTERNAL PARTY
Examples: access to a record outside assigned work queue; forwarding a worklist to a colleague without a business purpose.
Response: written warning. Access review. Mandatory re-training within ten business days.

TIER 3 — DISCLOSURE TO AN EXTERNAL PARTY
Examples: misdirected fax or email containing Medical Record Number, Diagnosis Code, or Guarantor Account Number; transmission of Primary Account Number by unencrypted channel.
Response: suspension pending investigation. Breach risk assessment initiated. Notification determination made by the Privacy Officer. Termination presumed where the act was reckless.

TIER 4 — INTENTIONAL MISUSE
Examples: access for curiosity regarding a public figure or acquaintance; disclosure for personal gain; alteration of a record under legal hold.
Response: immediate termination. Referral for criminal prosecution. Notification to licensing authorities where applicable. Civil liability is personal.

AGGRAVATING FACTORS
- Failure to self-report within one hour of discovery.
- Prior entry at any tier within twenty-four months.
- Attempt to conceal, delete, or reconstruct the affected record.
- Volume: any incident involving more than one individual's record.

MITIGATING FACTORS
- Prompt self-report before discovery by another party.
- Full cooperation with the investigation.
- Documented ambiguity in the applicable procedure, which the Privacy Officer will correct.

Team leads have no authority to resolve Tier 3 or Tier 4 matters locally. Route immediately. Do not interview the employee. Do not preserve evidence by informal means; contact the Records Custodian for imaging.

Questions route through the compliance mailbox on the intranet directory.
