FACILITATOR GUIDE AND ANSWER KEY
Module 4: Restricted Information
For use by department supervisors conducting the quarterly session
Issued by the Compliance Department, revision of April 2004

I. PREPARATION CHECKLIST
• 1. Confirm the attendance roster against the department staffing list before the session opens.
• 2. Distribute one Staff Handout, Issue 04-02, and one blank acknowledgment sheet to each attendee.
• 3. Have available for demonstration: one blank membership application, one blank loan application, one shred console key.
• 4. Do not bring populated member documents into the training room for any purpose, including illustration.

II. TEACHING POINTS TO EMPHASIZE
• 5. The distinction between a field label and a field value is the single point most often missed on the post-test. Repeat it twice.
• 6. Restricted values include, at minimum: Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Mother's Maiden Name, Member Number, Account Number, Card Number, PIN Offset, Credit Score, Loan Application Status, Available Balance, Charge-Off Indicator.
• 7. Report filings under the Bank Secrecy Act may not be discussed with the subject of the filing, with coworkers outside the reporting chain, or with family members. This prohibition has no expiration.
• 8. Access without business purpose is a violation even when nothing is changed, copied, or repeated to anyone.

III. SCENARIO ANSWER KEY
• 9. Scenario 4.1 — Decline and refer. Joint ownership is not established by knowledge of a street name. Offer to take a callback number and route to the Branch Manager.
• 10. Scenario 4.2 — Prohibited. A coworker's personal acquaintance with an applicant is not a business purpose. The employee who was asked should decline and report the request.
• 11. Scenario 4.3 — Both, in order. Secure the document immediately, log the discovery, notify the Information Security Officer, and destroy only after the incident log entry is complete.
• 12. Scenario 4.4 — Refuse and escalate. Credentials are never shared or lent, including to authorized vendors under escort. Test printing is performed under a service account by the Systems Administrator.
• 13. Scenario 4.5 — Approved secure delivery only. A member's preference does not waive the safeguards requirement; offer branch pickup, courier, or the encrypted channel.

IV. CONSEQUENCES — STATE THESE VERBATIM
• 14. Findings are documented in the personnel record and reviewed at the next performance appraisal.
• 15. Access rights are revoked pending review upon any substantiated willful violation.
• 16. The credit union may be assessed penalties, and the Board may refer matters for prosecution.

V. CLOSING THE SESSION
• 17. Collect all acknowledgment sheets before attendees leave the room; incomplete sheets are not accepted.
• 18. Forward the signed sheets and the attendance roster to the Human Resources Administrator within three business days.
• 19. Record any attendee who could not attend on the make-up list and schedule within thirty days.
• 20. Return this guide to the locked supervisory file at the conclusion of the session.
