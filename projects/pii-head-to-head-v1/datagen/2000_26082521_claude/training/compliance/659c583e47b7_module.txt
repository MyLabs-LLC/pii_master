MEMORANDUM

TO: New Employees and Transferring Staff
FROM: Training Coordinator
SUBJECT: Module 3 of 6 — Information Handling. Read before your access account is activated.

You will not receive login credentials until your supervisor confirms you have completed this module and signed the Confidentiality Acknowledgment.

SECTION A. THE RULE IN ONE SENTENCE

You may access restricted information only when your assigned duties require it, only the fields your duties require, and only for as long as the task requires.

SECTION B. CATEGORIES YOU WILL ENCOUNTER

Depending on your assignment, you will handle one or more of the following. Each is restricted in full.

Clinical records. Field labels include Client Name, Medical Record Number, Date of Birth, Guarantor, Diagnosis, and Service Date.

Vital registrations. Field labels include Registrant Name, Certificate Number, Date of Event, Place of Event, and Parent Names.

Disease investigation files. Field labels include Case Name, Contact Name, Onset Date, and Exposure Site.

Employment records. Field labels include Employee Number, Home Address, Compensation Rate, and Direct Deposit Account Number.

Environmental health enforcement files that are under active investigation, including complainant identity.

SECTION C. WHAT MISHANDLING COSTS

The Department has three obligations when information is mishandled: contain it, report it, and notify those affected. You have one obligation: tell the Privacy Officer within one business day of learning of it. Failure to report is itself a disciplinary offense, separate from the underlying mishandling.

Sanctions range from written counseling for a first inadvertent lapse to dismissal and criminal referral for deliberate misuse. Civil penalties assessed against the County under federal privacy rules are substantial and are calculated per violation, which in a spreadsheet disclosure means per record, not per file.

SECTION D. SCENARIOS

Discuss each with your supervisor during your first week. There is no written answer sheet; the discussion is the exercise.

One. You are asked by a family member to check whether a neighbor's restaurant received a failing inspection score. Scores are posted publicly. Would your answer change if the file were under active complaint investigation?

Two. You find a printed appointment roster in the copier tray. Nobody is nearby. What do you do with it, and whom do you tell?

Three. A vendor performing maintenance on the records room asks you to hold the door open because their badge is not working. What is your response?

Four. You are copied on an email chain about a case that you have no role in. Are you permitted to read it? What should you do next?

Five. You need to send a line list to a state partner. The list contains a Name column you do not need for the analysis. What is the correct step before sending?

Six. You recognize a name on a case list as someone you know socially. What are you required to do, and what are you prohibited from doing?

Return the signed acknowledgment to the Training Coordinator. Retain this memo.
