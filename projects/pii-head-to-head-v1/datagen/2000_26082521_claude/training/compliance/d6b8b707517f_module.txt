MEMORANDUM

TO:      Pharmacy Staff, All Locations
FROM:    Office of the Compliance Officer
SUBJECT: Restricted Information — Quarterly Refresher and Certification
ACTION:  Read and sign. Manager holds signature sheet in store file for two years.

1. PURPOSE

This memo restates the chain's rules on restricted information. It replaces the memo issued last quarter. The rules have not changed. Enforcement has tightened.

2. RESTRICTED CATEGORIES

The following data elements are restricted. This list is not exhaustive; when in doubt, treat the element as restricted.

    Patient identifiers:      Name, Date of Birth, Address, Telephone
    Clinical data:            Drug Name, Strength, Directions, Diagnosis
    Dispensing data:          Rx Number, Fill Date, Quantity, Refills Remaining
    Financial data:           Cardholder ID, Group Number, Card Number, SSN
    Prescriber data:          Name, Practice Address, DEA Number, State License
    Controlled substances:    Schedule, Inventory Count, Theft Report Contents
    Employee data:            Wage Rate, Performance Rating, Disciplinary File
    Company data:             Reimbursement Rates, Store P&L, Vendor Cost

3. STANDING PROHIBITIONS

    3.1  No discussion of clinical or dispensing data outside the consultation area.
    3.2  No confirmation to any third party that a person is a customer.
    3.3  No release of records without Pharmacist in Charge review and log entry.
    3.4  No shared sign-ons. No unattended terminals. No written passwords.
    3.5  No restricted paper in general trash. Locked shred bin only.
    3.6  No removal of restricted material from the store premises in any form.
    3.7  No access to any record without a documented business reason.

4. CONSEQUENCES

Violations are graded. Careless handling with no disclosure results in a written warning and mandatory retraining. Repeat careless handling results in termination. Any deliberate disclosure, any access without business reason, and any disclosure in exchange for anything of value results in immediate termination. Licensed pharmacists and registered technicians are reported to the State Board in every case regardless of grade. The company will cooperate fully with any Board or law enforcement inquiry and will not indemnify an employee who acted outside these rules.

5. SCENARIO CERTIFICATION

Circle your answer. Wrong answers are reviewed with your manager, not held against you.

A. A caller will not give a date of birth but insists the matter is urgent and threatens to complain to corporate.
   ( ) Release the information  ( ) Refuse and transfer to the Pharmacist in Charge

B. A coworker asks you to look up a profile for someone they know personally, saying they will take responsibility.
   ( ) Look it up  ( ) Refuse and report the request

C. You find a printed profile on the floor behind the counter at close.
   ( ) Leave it for the morning shift  ( ) Shred bin immediately, tell the Pharmacist in Charge

D. A vendor representative asks for the store's reimbursement rates on a common generic.
   ( ) Provide it  ( ) Refuse and refer to the District Manager

E. A customer at the register asks what the person ahead of them was picking up.
   ( ) Answer generally  ( ) State that we do not discuss other customers
