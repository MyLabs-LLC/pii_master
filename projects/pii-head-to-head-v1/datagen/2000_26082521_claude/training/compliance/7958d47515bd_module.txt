METROPOLITAN TRANSIT AUTHORITY
OFFICE OF THE GENERAL COUNSEL — COMPLIANCE AND RECORDS DIVISION
ANNUAL INFORMATION SAFEGUARDING TRAINING, MODULE 4
FACILITATOR SLIDE SCRIPT — REVISION 3, EFFECTIVE MARCH 1, 2021

    I. SLIDE 1 — PURPOSE, SCOPE, AND AUTHORITY
        A. Read verbatim: "This module is delivered pursuant to Administrative Directive 14-06 and applies to all officers, employees, seasonal personnel, interns, volunteers, and contracted staff of the Authority, without exception."
        B. State that completion is a condition of continued access to Authority information systems.
        C. Note that nothing in this module shall be construed to diminish any obligation arising under applicable federal or state law, collective bargaining agreement, or executed confidentiality undertaking.

    II. SLIDE 2 — WHAT CONSTITUTES RESTRICTED INFORMATION
        A. Explain that "Restricted Information" means any record, in any medium, whether created by the Authority or received from a third party, falling within one or more of the following categories:
            1. Rider Account Records. Includes the fields Cardholder Name, Fare Media Serial Number, Registered Account Number, Autoload Payment Instrument, Billing Address, and Transaction History.
            2. Paratransit and Accessibility Records. Includes Applicant Name, Date of Birth, Functional Assessment Narrative, Certifying Clinician, Eligibility Determination Code, Conditional Eligibility Restrictions, and Trip Origin/Destination Log.
            3. Personnel and Labor Relations Records. Includes Employee Identification Number, Social Security Number, Home Address, Home Telephone Number, Direct Deposit Routing and Account Numbers, Benefit Enrollment Elections, Disciplinary File, and Occupational Injury Report.
            4. Security and Law Enforcement Records. Includes Incident Report Number, CCTV Retrieval Request, Camera Placement Schematics, Emergency Response Protocols, and Vulnerability Assessment Findings.
            5. Procurement and Proprietary Records. Includes Bidder Name, Sealed Bid Amount, Cost Buildup Worksheets, and Trade Secret Designations submitted under seal.
        B. Emphasize: the classification attaches to the field, not to the convenience of the requester.

    III. SLIDE 3 — THE OPERATIVE PROHIBITIONS
        A. No employee shall access Restricted Information absent a documented business necessity arising from that employee's assigned duties.
        B. Curiosity, familiarity with a rider, media interest, or an unsolicited request from a person outside the employee's chain of supervision does not constitute business necessity.
        C. Restricted Information shall not be transmitted to any non-Authority address, stored on any personal device, or reproduced in any form except as authorized in writing by the Records Custodian.
        D. Requests from members of the public, the press, or outside counsel shall be referred, without substantive response, to the Public Records Officer.

    IV. SLIDE 4 — CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING
        A. Administrative. Progressive discipline up to and including termination, and immediate suspension of system credentials pending inquiry.
        B. Contractual. Termination of the engagement and debarment of the contracting entity from future solicitations.
        C. Civil. Personal exposure to suit, and assessment of statutory damages against the Authority, for which indemnification is not guaranteed where the conduct was willful.
        D. Criminal. Referral to the appropriate prosecuting authority where unauthorized access, disclosure for pecuniary gain, or falsification of a record is indicated.
        E. State plainly: "The Authority does not treat unauthorized browsing as a minor infraction. It is an access violation and is logged as such."

    V. SLIDE 5 — AFFIRMATIVE DUTY TO REPORT
        A. Any suspected loss, unauthorized disclosure, or compromise shall be reported to the immediate supervisor and to the Compliance Duty Officer within one (1) hour of discovery.
        B. Advise that good-faith reporting is protected and that failure to report a known incident is itself an independently sanctionable offense.
        C. Close the module and direct participants to the scenario assessment.
