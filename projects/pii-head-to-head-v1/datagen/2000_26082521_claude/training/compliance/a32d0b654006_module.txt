SECTION 4 -- SCENARIO CHECK (COMPLETE BEFORE LEAVING SESSION)

A. A maintenance supervisor asks a payroll clerk to confirm whether a
   worker on the shop floor has a wage garnishment on file. The
   supervisor states the request is for scheduling purposes.
   REQUIRED: Decline. Refer to Benefits Coordinator. Category R6.

B. An operator finds a printed paratransit manifest left on a bench at
   a relief point. The manifest lists client identifiers and pickup
   locations.
   REQUIRED: Secure the document. Deliver to Mobility Svcs Manager.
   Report within one hour. Category R3.

NOTICE: Reporting duty is one hour from discovery to the Records
Custodian. Privacy Officer notification is 24 hours. State notification
is 72 hours. Failure to report is itself a Step 2 finding.
