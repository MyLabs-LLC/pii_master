SLIDE SCRIPT — "WHAT WE PROTECT AND WHY"
Module 2 of 4, Retail Pharmacy Compliance Series
Revision: 2011.1
Presenter: Store Manager or Pharmacy Supervisor
Runtime: 12 minutes
Materials Needed: Projector or printed slide set, sign-in sheet, Form HR-PRV-104

SLIDE 1
ON SCREEN: Four locked boxes labelled Health, Payment, Personnel, Proprietary.
SAY: Everything we handle falls into one of four boxes. Three of them are somebody's private life. The fourth is the company's business position. All four are restricted.

SLIDE 2
ON SCREEN: Header "Health Information — Field List." Rows: Patient Name / Date of Birth / Address / Phone / Rx Number / Drug and Strength / Prescriber / Insurance Member ID / Group Number.
SAY: If a field appears on this list, it is restricted whether it is on a screen, a label, a hard copy, a fax cover sheet, or a sticky note. The label sheet in the trash counts. The bag stapled to the wrong order counts.

SLIDE 3
ON SCREEN: Header "Payment Information — Field List." Rows: Cardholder Name / Account Number / Expiration Date / Verification Code / Track Data.
SAY: We never write these down, never store them outside the terminal, and never read them aloud. If a customer reads a card number to you over the phone, stop them.

SLIDE 4
ON SCREEN: Header "Personnel Information — Field List." Rows: Social Security Number / Employee ID / Routing Number / Account Number / Wage Rate.
SAY: The same rules protect you. Payroll paperwork left in the break room is the same violation as a prescription left on the counter.

SLIDE 5
ON SCREEN: "Minimum Necessary."
SAY: You may open the record you need for the task you are performing. Not the record of a neighbor. Not the record of a family member. Not the record of a person in the news. The system logs every lookup with your credentials, and those logs are reviewed.

SLIDE 6
ON SCREEN: Ladder graphic, bottom to top: Coaching / Written Warning / Suspension / Termination / Board Referral / Civil Penalty / Criminal Charge.
SAY: Consequences scale with severity and intent. An honest mistake reported promptly usually ends at the bottom of this ladder. Curiosity browsing does not. Selling information does not.

SLIDE 7
ON SCREEN: "Report Same Day."
SAY: Misdirected fax, lost hard copy, wrong bag handed out, screen left open — tell the Store Manager and call the Compliance Hotline before you leave your shift. We cannot make notification deadlines if we learn about an incident a week late. No one has ever been disciplined here for reporting.

SLIDE 8
ON SCREEN: "Quiz — 5 Scenarios."
SAY: Turn to the quiz. Work alone, five minutes, then we review as a group. Hand the completed sheet to me before you clock out.

TRAINER NOTE: Collect sign-in sheet. Record completion in the training log. Forward exceptions to the District Office within five business days.
