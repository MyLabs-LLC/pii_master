3. RULES OF HANDLING
3.1 Tiers 1 and 2 shall not be exported, printed, or attached to a message without prior written approval of the Records Custodian.
3.2 Tier 3 columns shall not be discussed with any client contact under any circumstance, including where the client asserts a safety interest.
3.3 Tier 4 columns may be used for internal scheduling only, from the system of record, and shall not be copied into local files.
3.4 Any file containing a Tier 1 or Tier 2 column shall reside only on the governed network share, which is retained and purged on the published schedule.

4. ENFORCEMENT
4.1 Quarterly scanning of branch drives and mailboxes is performed against the column names in Section 2.
4.2 A positive match generates an incident record assigned to the Branch Manager, with a five-business-day response requirement.
4.3 Sustained findings carry counseling, entitlement suspension, or termination, in the order set by the Disciplinary Panel, and are reportable to the affected client under the master services agreement.

5. SCENARIO QUIZ
5.1 A weekly report is titled "Headcount Summary" but carries DEPOSIT_ACCT_NBR. State the handling tier and the required disposition.
5.2 A branch is asked to certify to a client that all assigned workers cleared screening, and proposes to attach the screening feed. State the compliant response.
5.3 A supervisor exports the contact table to plan a holiday shift roster. State which columns must be removed before the export is permitted.
5.4 Return completed answers to the Records Custodian within five business days.
