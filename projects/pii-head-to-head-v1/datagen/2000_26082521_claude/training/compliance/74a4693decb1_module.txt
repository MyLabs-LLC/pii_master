ANNUAL CERTIFICATION — MODULE PC-2 SUPPLEMENT
CONFIDENTIALITY: CATEGORIES, CONSEQUENCES, AND CASE PRACTICE
For use by the Pharmacy Manager or designated trainer. Rev. 06/99.

I. RESTRICTED CATEGORIES

1. Patient identifiers. The profile header carries Patient Name, Date of Birth, Sex, Address, Telephone, and Patient ID. Any one of these, standing alone, is restricted.

2. Prescription detail. The fill record carries Rx Number, Fill Date, Drug Name, Strength, NDC, Quantity, Days Supply, Sig, Refills Remaining, and Dispensing Pharmacist Initials. Drug Name alone identifies a course of treatment and is treated as restricted.

3. Benefit and payment detail. The claim screen carries Plan Name, BIN, PCN, Cardholder ID, Group Number, Person Code, Relationship Code, Prior Authorization Number, and Copay.

4. Clinical notations. The intervention screen carries Allergy Code, Adverse Reaction Note, Chronic Condition Indicator, and the Counseling / Refusal signature line.

5. Controlled substance documentation. The perpetual inventory log, the Schedule II register, the DEA Form 222 file, and the biennial inventory are restricted and are also non-alterable records.

6. Associate records. Employee Number, wage rate, Social Security Number, attendance history, and any medical or leave document.

II. CONSEQUENCES

7. Coaching and re-certification for procedural lapses that create no disclosure.

8. Termination for unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, removal, or destruction of any record in clauses 1 through 6.

9. Board of Pharmacy referral, which may result in suspension or revocation of a license, intern registration, or technician registration.

10. Law enforcement referral where controlled substance records are implicated.

11. Personal civil liability. Company indemnification does not extend to conduct outside policy.

12. Loss of third-party network participation for the store, affecting every associate at that location.

III. CASE PRACTICE

Instructions: For each case, write the required action, the associate who must be notified, and the governing clause. Do not discuss cases outside the training room.

13. A caller states a relationship to a patient and asks the pharmacy to confirm whether a prescription in a specific therapeutic class was filled this month.

14. A technician opens a profile with no fill, refill, transfer, or billing task pending. The access appears in the weekly audit report.

15. A prescriber's office requests a fill history by fax and supplies a fax number that does not match the number on file for that practice.

16. Will-call bags for the afternoon are staged label-outward on a rack visible from the waiting area.

17. A shredder jams and the overflow of labels and rejection slips is bagged and set beside the receiving door overnight.

18. A regulatory inspector presents credentials at the counter during a shift when the Pharmacy Manager is off site.

19. An associate is asked by an outside party to look up whether a particular address appears in the patient file.

20. A former associate telephones and asks a current technician to read back a refill date from a profile.

IV. CERTIFICATION

21. The trainer reviews all responses and records completion on the Training Roster by Employee Number.

22. The associate signs the Certification Acknowledgment, which is retained by the Records Custodian for the period required by the state Board of Pharmacy.

23. Associates who cannot complete clauses 13 through 20 correctly repeat the module before returning to the counter.
