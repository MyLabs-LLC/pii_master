COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
INTEROFFICE MEMORANDUM

TO:       Division Directors and Program Supervisors
FROM:     Privacy Officer
SUBJECT:  Slide Script — "Restricted Information: Know It, Guard It, Report It"
          For use by presenters at branch-level in-service sessions

Presenters: read the script as written. Do not improvise examples using actual case files. Sessions run twenty-five minutes including the quiz.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 1 — TITLE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Say: "Everyone in this room handles information that a member of the public cannot lawfully obtain by asking for it. Today we cover three things: which categories are restricted, what happens when they are mishandled, and how to recognize a problem before it becomes an incident."

-----------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 2 — THE FOUR CATEGORIES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Display the table. Read each row.

  CATEGORY                 EXAMPLE FIELDS ON THE RECORD
  Protected Health         Patient Name, Medical Record Number,
  Information              Date of Birth, Diagnosis Code,
                           Health Plan ID, Service Date
  Vital Records            Registrant Name, Certificate Number,
                           Date of Event, Parent Names
  Communicable Disease     Case Name, Onset Date, Contact Name,
  Case Data                Reporting Provider, Test Result
  Personnel and Financial  Employee Number, Home Address,
                           Compensation Rate, Account Number

Say: "The column on the right lists field labels. If a document you are holding has those labels filled in, you are holding restricted information regardless of what the folder is called."

-----------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 3 — THE MINIMUM NECESSARY RULE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Say: "Access is granted by job function, not by curiosity or by rank. A director does not have broader clinical access than a nurse simply by being a director. Pull the minimum fields needed to do the task in front of you, and close the record when the task is done."

-----------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 4 — CONSEQUENCES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Read all four bullets. Do not soften them.

  * Discipline under County Personnel Rules, up to dismissal.
  * Federal civil monetary penalties assessed per violation
    against the Department, escalating for willful neglect.
  * Criminal exposure — fines and imprisonment — for obtaining
    or disclosing under false pretenses or for personal gain.
  * Breach notification to affected persons, the state
    registrar, and, above the threshold count, to the media.

Say: "The fourth bullet is the one that surprises people. Notification is a legal obligation, not a public relations decision."

-----------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 5 — SCENARIO QUIZ (show one at a time)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Read each aloud. Take a show of hands. Give the answer before moving on.

  A. A courier delivers a sealed envelope of lab results to the
     wrong floor. It is opened by a program assistant who has no
     clinical role. Incident or not?
     ANSWER: Incident. Report within one business day.

  B. Two staff discuss a case in an elevator without using names,
     but mention the neighborhood and the condition.
     ANSWER: Improper disclosure. Location plus condition can
     identify a person in a small community.

  C. A laptop containing an exported contact list is stolen from
     an office. The file was password protected but not encrypted.
     ANSWER: Reportable. Password protection is not encryption
     and does not qualify for safe harbor.

  D. A staff member forwards a work email containing a case
     summary to a personal webmail account to work from home.
     ANSWER: Prohibited. Use the remote access portal.

  E. A records request arrives for a birth certificate from a
     person who cannot document a direct and tangible interest.
     ANSWER: Refer to the Vital Records Custodian. Staff do not
     make eligibility determinations independently.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 6 — CLOSING
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Say: "Report to the Privacy Officer within one business day. Reporting early is treated as mitigation. Concealing is treated as aggravation. Sign the roster on your way out."

Presenters return signed rosters to the Training Coordinator within five business days.
