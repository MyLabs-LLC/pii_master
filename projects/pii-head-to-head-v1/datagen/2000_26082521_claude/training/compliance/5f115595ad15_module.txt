CONFIDENTIALITY OF STUDENT AND EMPLOYEE INFORMATION
Annual Staff In-Service — Handout A
Office of the Superintendent / Records Management Unit
School Year 2004-2005

I. PURPOSE
   A. Satisfies annual notification requirement, 34 CFR Part 99.
   B. Applies to all certificated staff, classified staff, substitutes, contracted service providers, and volunteers with building access.
   C. Signature page (Attachment 1) returns to the Building Principal within five (5) working days.

II. CATEGORIES OF RESTRICTED INFORMATION
   A. Education Records — Protected. Release only on legitimate educational interest.
      1. Fields include: Student Name; Student Identification Number; Grade Level; Course History; Grade Point Average; Standardized Test Scores; Attendance Record; Disciplinary Referral Code.
      2. Cumulative folder remains in the Registrar's file room. No overnight removal.
   B. Special Education Records — Protected, heightened.
      1. Fields include: Eligibility Category; Evaluation Report; IEP Goals and Objectives; Related Services Minutes; Placement Determination; Parent Consent Status.
      2. Custodian: Director of Student Services.
   C. Health Records — Protected, heightened.
      1. Fields include: Immunization Status; Medication Administration Log; Allergy Notation; Nursing Care Plan; Screening Result.
      2. Custodian: Health Services Coordinator.
   D. Program Eligibility Records — Protected. Federal restriction on redisclosure.
      1. Fields include: Free/Reduced Meal Eligibility Status; Household Size; Title I Service Designation; Migrant Status; Homeless Status; English Language Proficiency Level.
   E. Identifying Numbers — Protected. Never posted, never used as a class roster header.
      1. Fields include: Social Security Number; Medicaid Number; State Assigned Student Identifier.
   F. Employee Records — Protected.
      1. Fields include: Home Address; Home Telephone Number; Date of Birth; Salary Placement; Evaluation Rating; Leave Balance; Insurance Enrollment Election.
   G. Directory Information — Releasable unless opted out.
      1. Fields designated by Board Policy: Student Name; Participation in Activities; Weight and Height of Athletic Team Members; Diplomas and Awards Received; Most Recent School Attended.
      2. Opt-out flag appears on the student information system inquiry screen. Check before release.
      3. Military recruiter and postsecondary institution requests are governed by NCLB Section 9528. Route to the Records Custodian.

III. HANDLING REQUIREMENTS
   A. Do not transmit restricted fields by unencrypted e-mail attachment.
   B. Verify the receiving fax number before sending. Use the cover sheet on file.
   C. Log off the student information system before leaving the workstation.
   D. Diskettes and printouts containing restricted fields are shredded, not discarded.
   E. Do not discuss cases in hallways, workrooms, the parking lot, or the bus loop.
   F. Log every outside disclosure on the Record of Access form retained with the file.

IV. CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING
   A. Employee: verbal warning, written reprimand, suspension without pay, nonrenewal, or termination, per the negotiated agreement.
   B. District: complaint to the Family Policy Compliance Office; corrective action plan; suspension of federal funding eligibility.
   C. Certificated staff: referral to the state professional standards board.
   D. Volunteers and contractors: immediate revocation of building access.

V. SCENARIO REVIEW — Circle PERMITTED or NOT PERMITTED
   A. A classroom teacher posts graded assignments in the hallway with student identification numbers used in place of names.
   B. A bus driver asks the front office which riders qualify for the free meal program in order to plan a field trip collection.
   C. A guidance counselor shares an evaluation report with a teacher scheduled to implement the accommodations next semester.
   D. A parent volunteer offers to alphabetize cumulative folders in the file room during the lunch period.
   E. An office assistant faxes a withdrawal packet to a receiving district using a number written on a sticky note.
   F. A coach releases the roster with heights and weights to the local newspaper for a season preview.

VI. RETURN COMPLETED HANDOUT TO THE BUILDING PRINCIPAL.
