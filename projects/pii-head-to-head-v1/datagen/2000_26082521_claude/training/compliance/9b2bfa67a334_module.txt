SLIDE 5 -- SCENARIO DRILL

[READ EACH ALOUD. TAKE A SHOW OF HANDS. GIVE THE RULING.]

DRILL 1. A dispatcher notices that a workstation left logged in
displays an open personnel screen showing benefit deduction fields.
The dispatcher's own role carries no X in column R1 or R6.
RULING: Do not read. Lock the workstation. Notify the supervisor and
the Records Custodian. Log the event. Non-report is a Tier 1 finding.

DRILL 2. A vendor cleaner photographs a posted crew board that lists
route assignments and employee identification numbers.
RULING: Stop the activity. Escalate to the Transit Police Liaison.
Vendor access is governed by the contract confidentiality clause; the
Contract Administrator issues the cure notice.

DRILL 3. A station agent is asked by a local reporter to describe the
contents of an incident report filed after a platform disturbance.
RULING: No comment. Refer all press to Communications. Incident
narratives, camera identifiers, and report numbers are Category R4.

DRILL 4. An employee wishes to check their own fare account history
using the revenue terminal rather than the public web portal.
RULING: Prohibited. Self-lookup is still an unlogged-purpose lookup.
Use the public portal.
