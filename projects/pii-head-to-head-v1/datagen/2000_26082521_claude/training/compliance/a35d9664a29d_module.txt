STUDENT RECORDS COMPLIANCE — SELF-STUDY MODULE 4
Registrar's Office, State University
Complete and return the signature page to the Records Custodian within ten working days.

1. PURPOSE
   1.1 Confirm which fields are restricted from release.
   1.2 Confirm the consequences that follow a release made in error.
   1.3 Demonstrate judgment on five common counter and telephone situations.

2. RESTRICTED FIELDS — MEMORIZE THIS LIST
   2.1 Identifiers: Social Security Number, Student Identification Number, Date of Birth, Place of Birth, Citizenship Status.
   2.2 Contact: Permanent Address, Local Address, Telephone Number, Electronic Mail Address, Emergency Contact.
   2.3 Academic: Grades, Grade Point Average, Class Rank, Course Schedule, Test Scores, Academic Standing, Probation and Dismissal Actions.
   2.4 Financial: Financial Aid Award Amount, Need Determination, Account Balance, Payment History, Parent or Guardian Income.
   2.5 Protected status: Race and Ethnicity, Gender, Disability Accommodation Record, Immunization Record, Disciplinary Record.

3. CONSEQUENCES
   3.1 First confirmed release in error: credentials suspended, retraining required, written reprimand.
   3.2 Access without legitimate educational interest: termination or dismissal from the student assistant program.
   3.3 Willful release for personal benefit: referral to University Counsel and to law enforcement.
   3.4 Office-level exposure: federal complaint, corrective action plan, funding eligibility review.

4. SCENARIO QUIZ
   4.1 A person at the counter presents a valid university photo credential and asks for their own Disciplinary Record. What do you do?
   4.2 A caller states they pay the tuition and asks for the current Account Balance and Grade Point Average. No consent form is on file. What do you do?
   4.3 A dean's office sends an internal request for Enrollment Status on twelve records for a retention study. What do you do?
   4.4 A local newspaper asks whether a named individual is enrolled and in what Major Field of Study. The record carries a confidentiality hold. What do you do?
   4.5 You notice a colleague viewing a record with no queue assignment and no request in the log. What do you do?

5. ANSWER KEY
   5.1 Accept a written request; coordinate release with the Records Custodian under inspection rights.
   5.2 Release the Account Balance only with a signed third-party billing authorization on file. Never release the Grade Point Average without signed consent.
   5.3 Release for legitimate educational interest; record the requesting unit and purpose in the disclosure log.
   5.4 State that no information is available. Do not confirm or deny enrollment.
   5.5 Report to the Records Custodian the same day. Do not confront the colleague and do not discuss it with other staff.

6. ACKNOWLEDGMENT
   6.1 Print role title, sign, and date the attached page.
   6.2 Retain a copy at your workstation for the current academic year.
