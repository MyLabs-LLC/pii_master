RESTRICTED RECORDS QUICK REFERENCE — POST AT WORKSTATION
County Health Department | Policy ADM-114 | 2004

CATEGORY                  | RESTRICTED FIELD LABELS     | DISPOSITION
--------------------------+-----------------------------+-------------
Immunization registry     | Client Name; Date of Birth  | Shred
STD clinic encounter      | Case Name; Diagnosis Code   | Locked file
Environmental complaint   | Complainant Name; Address   | Closed record
Payroll register          | Employee Name; Account No.  | Restricted
Death certificate work    | Decedent Name; Cause Code   | Sealed

RULE: Access follows assignment. Curiosity is not a business purpose.

CONSEQUENCE SCHEDULE: counseling and retraining for inadvertent access;
suspension and loss of system rights for unauthorized access; termination
and outside referral for disclosure.

SCENARIO: A staff member covering the front desk during lunch opens a chart
belonging to a client of another program to check an appointment time that
is also printed on the daily schedule. Is the access permissible?

ANSWER: No. The appointment time was available from an authorized source.
Opening the chart exceeded the assignment and is a reportable event.

Report to the Records Custodian within one business day.
