CONFIDENTIALITY POCKET GUIDE — CARRY WITH BADGE
County Health Department. Supersedes prior edition. Do not photocopy.

  - BEFORE YOU OPEN A RECORD
      - Is the record assigned to me?
      - Do I need all of it, or one field?
      - If either answer is unclear: stop, ask the supervisor.

  - NEVER RELEASE WITHOUT AUTHORIZATION
      - Patient Name, Date of Birth, Social Security Number
      - Medical Record Number, Client Index Number, Case Number
      - Diagnosis Code, Test Result, Treatment Status
      - Street Address, Telephone Number, Electronic Mail Address

  - DO
      - Log off before leaving the workstation.
      - Face documents down at the counter.
      - Shred into locked consoles only.
      - Verify the recipient before sending a facsimile.

  - DO NOT
      - Discuss cases in corridors, elevators, or the parking structure.
      - Look up friends, relatives, coworkers, or persons in the news.
      - Store restricted files on removable media or home equipment.
      - Speak to media. Transfer to the Public Information Officer.

  - THREE QUESTIONS, ANSWERED IN UNIT MEETING
      1. A caller states they are a relative and requests a test result. Release?
         No. Verify authorization on file; otherwise refer to the Records Custodian.
      2. A colleague asks you to look up a record on their behalf while they are out. Permitted?
         No. Access follows the assignment, not the favor.
      3. A misdirected facsimile confirmation is received. Action?
         Notify the Privacy Officer, retain the confirmation sheet, do not re-send.

  - PRIVACY OFFICER, EXTENSION POSTED AT EACH NURSE STATION.
