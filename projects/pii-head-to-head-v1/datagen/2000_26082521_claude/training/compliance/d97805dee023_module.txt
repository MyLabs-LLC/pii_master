SLIDE SCRIPT — MODULE 7: RESTRICTED MEMBER INFORMATION
Presenter: Compliance Officer or designee
Running time: 25 minutes
Materials: transparencies 7-1 through 7-8, Handout 4B, sign-in sheet

SLIDE 7-1 — PURPOSE
    On screen:
        "Information Is Collateral. Treat It That Way."
    Say:
        - Module is required annually. Sign the attendance sheet before leaving the room.
        - Examiners test staff knowledge by telephone without notice. Assume every caller is an examiner.
    Do not:
        - Discuss any incident open before the Supervisory Committee.

SLIDE 7-2 — WHAT IS RESTRICTED
    On screen — three column headings, no entries:
        Identifiers | Financial Detail | Relationship Detail
    Say:
        - Column one: Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Mother's Maiden Name, Driver's License Number, Member Number.
        - Column two: Share Balance, Certificate Amount, Certificate Maturity Date, Loan Balance, Payment History, Days Delinquent, Credit Score, Debt-to-Income Ratio.
        - Column three: Mailing Address, Home Telephone, Employer Name, Occupation, Joint Owner, Beneficiary Designation.
        - Every field on the New Member Application is restricted. There is no unrestricted field.

SLIDE 7-3 — WHERE IT LEAKS
    Say:
        - Telephone. Highest volume, lowest control.
        - Facsimile. Confirm the receiving number by voice before transmission. Cover sheet mandatory.
        - Wastebasket. Shred bin only. No exception for adding-machine tape.
        - CRT screen angle. Reposition so the member queue cannot read the display.
        - Lunchroom and hallway conversation. Members, couriers, and vendors are present.

SLIDE 7-4 — LAWFUL RELEASE
    Say:
        - Written member authorization on file, unexpired, matching the signature card.
        - Subpoena, summons, or court order — route unopened to the Compliance Officer. Branch staff do not respond directly.
        - Credit bureau reporting in the ordinary course.
        - Verification of Deposit — standard form only, mailed to the requesting institution's published address only.

SLIDE 7-5 — CONSEQUENCES
    On screen: ladder graphic, five rungs.
    Say:
        - Documented counseling and retraining.
        - Suspension pending investigation.
        - Termination for cause.
        - Criminal referral where disclosure was made in exchange for anything of value.
        - Personal civil exposure, uninsured.

SLIDE 7-6 — SCENARIO ONE
    Read aloud:
        - A caller states they are the employer of a member and must confirm a payroll deduction before the next cycle. The caller states the Employer Name correctly.
    Ask: Release?
    Answer: No. Employer Name is not authentication. Offer to return the call to the Home Telephone on file.

SLIDE 7-7 — SCENARIO TWO
    Read aloud:
        - While pulling an unrelated file, a staff member observes a Days Delinquent entry and remarks on it to a second staff member at the drive-up window.
    Ask: Violation?
    Answer: Yes. Two findings — access without business purpose, and disclosure within earshot of the member lobby.

SLIDE 7-8 — SCENARIO THREE
    Read aloud:
        - A facsimile arrives requesting a Loan Payoff Amount. The cover sheet bears title company letterhead. No member authorization is attached.
    Ask: Release?
    Answer: No. Hold pending written authorization. Log the request in the Disclosure Register the same business day.

CLOSE
    - Collect sign-in sheet.
    - Forward the completion roster to Human Resources within five business days.
    - Unexcused non-attendance is reported to the Branch Operations Manager.
