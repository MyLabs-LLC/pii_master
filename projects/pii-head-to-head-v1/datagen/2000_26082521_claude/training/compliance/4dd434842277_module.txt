PART TWO — SCENARIOS

1. A loan officer, working from home, forwards a completed application to a personal electronic mail account in order to review it during the evening. The application contains the Social Security Number and Annual Income fields. Identify every violation and the required reporting action.

2. A member calls the contact center and, when the representative requests authentication, becomes angry and states that they have banked here for twenty years. The representative, wishing to de-escalate, discloses the current balance. State whether the disclosure was permissible and identify the governing obligation.

3. A supervisor is served with a document demanding production of transaction history and is advised by the requesting party that the matter is urgent. State the required sequence of review prior to any production whatsoever.

4. An employee observes a coworker photographing a screen displaying the Member Number and Date of Birth fields. State the employee's obligations, the deadline applicable to them, and the consequence of remaining silent.

5. A former member's relative appears at the teller line and inquires whether an account remains open. State the permissible response.

ANSWER KEY — FOR INSTRUCTOR USE

1. Unauthorized transmission of Restricted fields over an uncontrolled channel; creation of an unmanaged copy outside the system of record; probable violation of the remote work addendum. Report to the supervisor and to the Information Security Officer within one hour; do not delete the message, as preservation is required for the incident file.

2. Impermissible without exception. Authentication under the Customer Identification Program is a condition precedent to any disclosure; member dissatisfaction is not a recognized exception, and the representative should have escalated to a supervisor.

3. Route to the Records Custodian for a determination of legal sufficiency; obtain countersignature of the BSA Officer; confirm the scope of the demand; produce only the records enumerated and nothing further. Assertions of urgency by the requesting party do not abridge this sequence.

4. Report immediately to the supervisor and to the Information Security Officer; do not confront or attempt to resolve the matter independently. Silence constitutes concealment and is subject to corrective action up to and including termination.

5. Neither confirm nor deny the existence of any relationship. Direct the inquirer to the Member Services desk for a general explanation of privacy obligations.
