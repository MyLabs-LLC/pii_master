ITEM 1. A caller states that they are a relative of a clinic client and asks whether the client kept an appointment yesterday.
 (a) Confirm the appointment only.  (b) Neither confirm nor deny; refer the caller to the Health Information Unit.  (c) Confirm if the caller supplies the client's date of birth.

ITEM 2. A staff member in the immunization program recognizes a neighbor's file in the day's pull list and opens the record out of concern for the child's welfare.
 (a) Permissible; the motive is benign.  (b) Permissible if nothing is disclosed to anyone.  (c) Prohibited; access without an assigned duty is a violation regardless of motive and regardless of disclosure.

ITEM 3. A field investigator is asked by a law enforcement officer at the counter for the address associated with a communicable disease case report, without a subpoena or court order.
 (a) Release the address; law enforcement is exempt.  (b) Decline and refer the request to the Privacy Officer for legal review.  (c) Release the address if the officer signs the visitor log.

ITEM 4. A facsimile containing laboratory results is transmitted to a number that turns out to belong to an unrelated business, discovered forty minutes later.
 (a) Take no action; the transmission cannot be recalled.  (b) Note it in the unit log at month end.  (c) Report to the Privacy Officer before the end of the shift and request return or destruction from the receiving party.

ITEM 5. A supervisor asks a clerk to copy a program eligibility file for a county board member who has requested it informally.
 (a) Copy the file; the requester holds elected office.  (b) Copy the file with identifiers struck through by hand.  (c) Decline pending written authorization routed through the Records Custodian.

ITEM 6. A laptop assigned to the outreach team, containing a downloaded case management extract, is taken home overnight and left in a vehicle.
 (a) Acceptable; the vehicle was locked.  (b) Acceptable if the laptop is password protected.  (c) Unacceptable; removal requires written authorization and the incident is reportable.
