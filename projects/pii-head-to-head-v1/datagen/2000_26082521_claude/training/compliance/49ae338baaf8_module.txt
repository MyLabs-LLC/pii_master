SCENARIO 1
A caller identifies himself as the listing agent on a file closing tomorrow and asks for the seller's net proceeds figure and the deposit account the proceeds will be sent to.
   A. Provide both; the agent is a party to the transaction.
   B. Provide the net proceeds figure only.
   C. Provide neither without written authorization from the seller of record.

SCENARIO 2
A lender's processor emails asking for a copy of the borrower's driver's license, replying inside an existing thread that already contains the loan number.
   A. Attach the image to the reply.
   B. Send the image through the secure portal and reply that it has been posted.
   C. Fax the image to the number in the processor's signature block.

SCENARIO 3
Twenty minutes before a closing, an email arrives from the seller's known address stating that the wire instructions have changed and asking that the update be applied immediately.
   A. Apply the change; the address matches the file.
   B. Reply to the email to confirm, then apply the change.
   C. Call the seller at the number independently verified at file opening, and escalate to the Escrow Manager.

SCENARIO 4
A closer finds a printed settlement statement and a copy of a payoff statement left on the closing room table after the parties have gone.
   A. Recycle both.
   B. Place both in the locked shred bin and note the file.
   C. Leave them for the next closer to file.

SCENARIO 5
An employee wants to finish reviewing a title commitment at home and emails the file to a personal email address.
   A. Acceptable if the file is deleted afterward.
   B. Acceptable if no Tier 1 fields are included.
   C. Not permitted under any circumstances; use the approved remote connection.

SCENARIO 6
An employee realizes an escrow package was emailed to the wrong recipient forty minutes ago.
   A. Recall the message and take no further action.
   B. Notify the Compliance Department now, then follow instructions.
   C. Wait to see whether the recipient responds.

ANSWER KEY: 1-C, 2-B, 3-C, 4-B, 5-C, 6-B
