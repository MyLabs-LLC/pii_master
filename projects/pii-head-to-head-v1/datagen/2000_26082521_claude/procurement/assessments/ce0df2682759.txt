VENDOR SECURITY ASSESSMENT QUESTIONNAIRE
Issued by: Vendor Risk Management, Office of Corporate Compliance
Applicable to: Suppliers with access to escrow, closing, or settlement records
Effective: First Quarter 2021 · Supersedes: Fourth Quarter 2019 edition

ARTICLE I — SCOPE AND DEFINITIONS

    1.01  For purposes of this questionnaire, "Protected Information" means
          any information relating to a natural person that is obtained by
          the Supplier in connection with the Company's title, escrow,
          settlement, or recording operations, including information
          appearing on settlement statements, loan instruments, and
          identity verification records.

    1.02  "Supplier" includes any subcontractor, staffing agency, or
          affiliate through which the Supplier performs services.

    1.03  Responses given below are incorporated by reference into the
          master services agreement between the parties and shall be
          treated as continuing representations for the term thereof.
