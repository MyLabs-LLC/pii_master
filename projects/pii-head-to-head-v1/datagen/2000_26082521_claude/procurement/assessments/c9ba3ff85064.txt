1.  INSTRUCTIONS

1.1 The Supplier shall complete every response box at Sections 2 to 6 below.
    A response of "not applicable" must be supported by written justification
    recorded at Clause 7.
1.2 Where the Evidence Required column is marked "Y", the Supplier shall
    attach the named artefact to its return submission and cross-reference it
    by attachment number within the corresponding response box.
1.3 For the purposes of this questionnaire, "Personal Information" means any
    information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person that
    is collected, processed, transmitted or held by the Supplier in connection
    with the engagement, including policy administration data, claims data and
    enrollment data.
1.4 The completed questionnaire is to be returned to the Vendor Risk
    Management Office within twenty (20) business days of receipt.
