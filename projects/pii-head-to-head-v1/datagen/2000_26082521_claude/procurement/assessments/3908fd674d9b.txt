VENDOR SECURITY ASSESSMENT QUESTIONNAIRE — 2021 EDITION
Data Protection Section (Pillar 3 Alignment)
Distribution: Tier 1 and Tier 2 Suppliers · Response Deadline: Thirty (30) Calendar Days

PART A — COMPLETION REQUIREMENTS

    1.  General
        1.1   The Supplier shall respond to every clause in Part B. A clause
              left blank shall be scored as a control deficiency.
        1.2   Responses shall describe controls in force as of the date of
              completion and shall not describe planned or roadmap controls.
              Where a control is planned, the Supplier shall so state and
              shall provide the projected implementation quarter.
        1.3   The Company reserves the right to verify any response by
              on-site or remote examination upon reasonable notice.

    2.  Evidence
        2.1   The evidence column indicates whether documentation must be
              submitted with the response.
                  [E]  = evidence mandatory
                  [—]  = evidence discretionary
        2.2   Evidence shall be submitted through the secure portal
              identified in the transmittal notice. Evidence submitted by
              unencrypted electronic mail will be rejected and destroyed.
        2.3   Each evidence item shall be labeled with the clause number to
              which it responds.

PART B — STORAGE, ENCRYPTION, TRANSMISSION AND DISPOSAL

    3.  Storage of Customer Information                                    [E]

        3.2   State the geographic locations of all primary and secondary
              storage facilities, at the country and state level.
        3.3   State whether customer information is replicated to any

    5.  Transmission                                                       [E]
        5.1   Enumerate every channel through which customer information is
              conveyed to or from the Company.
        5.2   State the minimum protocol version accepted by Supplier
              endpoints and describe the mechanism by which deprecated
              versions are refused.
        5.3   Describe the controls applied to wire instruction data and to
              any communication capable of altering disbursement details.
        5.4   Describe the procedure for verifying the identity of a
              requesting party prior to release of customer information.

    6.  Retention and Disposal                                             [—]
        6.1   State the retention period applicable to customer information
              and identify the event from which the period runs.
        6.2   Describe the disposal method applied to solid-state media,
              magnetic media, and printed material.
        6.3   State whether disposal is performed internally or by a
              contracted provider, and identify the standard or
              certification governing that provider's work.
        6.4   Confirm that certificates of destruction are retained and are
              available to the Company upon request.
