MEMORANDUM

TO:      All Category Managers, Pharmacy Operations and Front Store
FROM:    Office of Information Security
SUBJECT: Handling of Returned Vendor Security Assessment Questionnaires
DATE:    Second Quarter 2016

Purpose

This memo sets out what category managers are to do when a supplier returns the Vendor Security Assessment Questionnaire. It does not change the questionnaire itself.

Routing

Returned questionnaires arrive through the supplier portal and are queued to Vendor Risk Management, not to the category manager. Do not accept a questionnaire sent to you directly. If a supplier sends one outside the portal, instruct their Records Custodian to resubmit and delete the copy you received.

Review sequence

1. Vendor Risk Management performs a completeness check within five business days. A questionnaire is incomplete if any numbered item has a blank response box, or if any item marked Evidence Required in the right-hand column has no corresponding attachment index.

2. Complete submissions move to substantive review. Reviewers score each section against the control baseline in force at the time of receipt.

3. Sections 2 and 4 — encryption and disposal — are scored first. A deficiency in either section holds the entire assessment regardless of scores elsewhere.

4. The category manager receives the outcome as one of three dispositions: cleared, cleared with conditions, or held.

Conditions and remediation

Where an assessment is cleared with conditions, the conditions are written as dated commitments and tracked by Vendor Risk Management. The category manager is copied but does not own the tracking. Do not negotiate remediation dates directly with the supplier; refer date changes to Vendor Risk Management.

Held assessments

A held assessment means no new personal information may be transmitted to the supplier until the hold is lifted. Existing flows continue only where an exception has been recorded in advance. Category managers are to confirm with the requesting business area that no new data feeds are being provisioned during a hold.

Records

Completed questionnaires, attachments, and scoring worksheets are retained by Vendor Risk Management for the term of the contract plus six years. Category managers should not retain local copies. Where a copy is needed for a business review, request an extract rather than storing the return package on a shared drive.

Questions

Route procedural questions to the Vendor Risk Management intake queue. Questions about a specific supplier's disposition should reference the supplier number only.
