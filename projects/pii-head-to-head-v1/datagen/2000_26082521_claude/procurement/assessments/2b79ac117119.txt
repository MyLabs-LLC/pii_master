VENDOR INFORMATION SECURITY ASSESSMENT — PROTECTED INFORMATION HANDLING
Riverbend Municipal Water & Power Authority — Office of Information Governance
Form IG-114 (Rev. 03/2011) — Return within twenty (20) business days of receipt

This questionnaire is issued to suppliers who, under an existing or contemplated agreement, receive, generate, transmit, store or dispose of protected information belonging to Authority customers, ratepayers or employees. For purposes of this form, protected information means any data element or combination of data elements that identifies or can reasonably be used to identify a natural person, including but not limited to full name, residential or mailing address, telephone number, electronic mail address, date of birth, government-issued identification number, taxpayer identification number, financial account number, payment card number, utility account number, meter identifier, service address, and any credential permitting access to the foregoing. Responses must describe the supplier's practices as actually implemented on the date of signature and must not describe planned, aspirational or roadmap controls except where a question expressly invites such a description.

Complete every numbered item in narrative form within the response box provided. Where a question is not applicable to the services rendered, state "not applicable" and explain in not fewer than two sentences why the exclusion is claimed; a bare assertion of inapplicability will be returned as non-responsive. The rightmost column indicates whether documentary evidence must accompany the response. Where evidence is required, attach the referenced artifact as a separate file and cite it by the supplier's own document control number and revision date in the body of the response. Screenshots are acceptable only where the questionnaire specifically permits them. The Authority reserves the right to request additional evidence, to conduct an onsite inspection on ten business days' notice, and to interview supplier personnel holding operational responsibility for the controls described.
