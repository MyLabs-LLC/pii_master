MEMORANDUM

TO      : Contract Supplier — Lockbox and Remittance Processing
FROM    : Records Custodian, Eastbay Regional Wastewater Authority
DATE    : 8 June 1999
SUBJECT : Annual Vendor Security Assessment — Questionnaire VSA/99

1.  This questionnaire is issued annually under Section 14 of the master
    agreement. It supersedes the form returned last year. Answer in the
    boxes; do not substitute a marketing brochure for a response.

2.  STORAGE

    2.1  Identify the system of record and media holding Customer Full
         Name, Account Number, Service Address, Bank Routing Number, and
         Check Serial Number.
         [                                                            ]
         Evidence required: system inventory listing.

    2.2  State the account and password controls on that system.
         [                                                            ]
         Evidence required: security parameter printout.

3.  ENCRYPTION

    3.1  Algorithm, mode, and effective key length applied to the stored
         Bank Routing Number and Account Number fields.
         [                                                            ]
         Evidence required: product specification, page cited.

    3.2  Whether images of remittance documents are enciphered on the
         optical platter as well as on fixed disk.
         [                                                            ]
         Evidence required: none.

4.  TRANSMISSION

    4.1  Method of delivery of the daily posting file to the Authority,
         and protection applied in transit.
         [                                                            ]
         Evidence required: current network diagram.

    4.2  Controls over remote maintenance access by the software vendor.
         [                                                            ]
         Evidence required: access log extract, one month.

5.  DISPOSAL

    5.1  Destruction method and interval for processed remittance
         documents and for superseded backup tapes.
         [                                                            ]
         Evidence required: destruction certificate specimen.

    5.2  Confirm that no field listed at 2.1 is retained beyond the
         schedule in Appendix C of the master agreement.
         [                                                            ]
         Evidence required: retention schedule as operated.

6.  Return the completed questionnaire and attachments to the Records
    Custodian within thirty days. Findings are reported to the Audit
    Committee at its next quarterly sitting.
