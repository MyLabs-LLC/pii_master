BRIAR CREEK AREA SCHOOL DISTRICT
Superintendent's Office — Pupil and Personnel Records
VENDOR SECURITY ASSESSMENT QUESTIONNAIRE (LONG FORM)
Form SR-4 (Rev. 02/99) — supersedes Form SR-3 (Rev. 05/96)

PURPOSE. This questionnaire is issued to every firm that will store, process,
transmit, or dispose of pupil or employee records on behalf of the District, in
accordance with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act and the District's
records policy. Responses become part of the contract file and are subject to
verification by the District's auditor.

INSTRUCTIONS. Answer in the space provided beneath each item. Where the right
margin shows [EVID], the response must be accompanied by the document named.
Mark the enclosed transmittal sheet to show which exhibits are attached. Return
the completed form to the Records Custodian, in a sealed envelope marked
CONFIDENTIAL — VENDOR ASSESSMENT.
