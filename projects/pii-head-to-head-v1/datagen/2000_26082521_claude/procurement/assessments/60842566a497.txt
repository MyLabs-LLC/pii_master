RIVERBEND MUNICIPAL WATER & POWER AUTHORITY
OFFICE OF INFORMATION GOVERNANCE

TRANSMITTAL — VENDOR SECURITY ASSESSMENT PACKET IG-114

Delivered with: Form IG-114 (Rev. 03/2011); Schedule A, Protected Information Definitions; Schedule B, Evidence Conventions.

The enclosed questionnaire forms part of the Authority's periodic supplier assurance cycle and is issued to every supplier holding an active agreement under which protected information is disclosed. Completion is a condition of continued performance under the agreement; it is not a solicitation and does not itself create, extend or modify any contractual obligation.

Suppliers are directed to observe the evidence conventions set out in Schedule B. In summary: attachments must be transmitted through the Authority's secure exchange facility rather than by ordinary electronic mail; each attachment must be identified in the response body by the supplier's own document control number and revision date; and no attachment may contain live protected information. Where an artifact necessarily contains such information — for example, a rotation log, an access review extract, or a certificate of destruction — the supplier must obscure the identifying values before transmission and state in the response what was obscured and by what method. An attachment received containing unobscured identifying values will be destroyed on receipt, the fact of destruction recorded, and the supplier required to resubmit.

Responses are to be given in narrative prose. The Authority does not accept marketing collateral, third-party audit summaries offered in substitution for a direct answer, or references to a public trust page in lieu of a written response. A third-party attestation report may accompany a response as supporting evidence but does not discharge the obligation to answer the question in the supplier's own words. Where a supplier relies on a subcontractor for a control described in any response, the supplier must identify the subcontractor by legal name and state whether the supplier has itself assessed that subcontractor within the preceding twelve months.

Questions concerning interpretation of any item should be directed to the Office of Information Governance through the contract administration channel identified in the agreement. Interpretive guidance given orally is not binding; suppliers seeking a binding interpretation must request it in writing and will receive a written response, which will be appended to the assessment file.

Incomplete packets are returned without assessment. A packet is incomplete if any numbered item lacks a response, if a claim of inapplicability is unexplained, if a required attachment is absent, or if the certification is unsigned or signed by a person without authority to bind the supplier. The twenty-business-day return period is not extended by the return of an incomplete packet.
