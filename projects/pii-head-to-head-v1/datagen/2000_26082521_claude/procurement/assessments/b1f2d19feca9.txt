SUBJECT:  Annual Reassessment — Data Lifecycle Controls Questionnaire

PURPOSE

This questionnaire supports the annual reassessment required under the institution's vendor management policy. It addresses the four lifecycle stages at which member personal information is exposed to risk: storage, encryption, transmission, and disposal. Responses are evaluated against the control baseline in effect for the current examination period.

RESPONSE CONVENTIONS

  - Enter narrative responses in the bracketed block beneath each question.
  - Enter "EVID:" followed by the attachment index where evidence is required.
  - Do not include live data, extracts, screenshots of production records, or
    any member-identifying content in your responses or attachments. Redact
    at source; unredacted submissions are destroyed on receipt and the item
    is scored as unanswered.
