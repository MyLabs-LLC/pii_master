§ 1. RECITALS

1.1 WHEREAS the Department is obligated by statute and by the terms of its funding agreements to obtain satisfactory assurances that its business associates and service providers will appropriately safeguard personal information; and

1.2 WHEREAS such assurances must be documented, particularized, and susceptible of verification; NOW THEREFORE the Respondent is directed to complete the following interrogatories.

§ 2. RESPONSE CONVENTIONS

2.1 A response consisting solely of "Yes," "No," or a reference to a policy title shall be deemed non-responsive and returned for cure.

2.2 The notation "[E]" appearing at the right margin of an interrogatory signifies that documentary evidence is required. The notation "[E–D]" signifies that evidence is required but may be deferred to the on-site or virtual validation session.
