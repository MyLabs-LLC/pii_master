I. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

The District collects personal information from riders in the course of administering registered fare cards, reduced-fare certification, paratransit scheduling, and employer pass programs. Where a vendor stores, processes, or transports that information on the District's behalf, the vendor is required under District Administrative Regulation 9-4 to complete this review prior to award and annually thereafter. The reviewer will record findings in the margin; the vendor is to confine its answers to the response areas and to attach evidence where the evidence column is marked as required. Answers consisting solely of "yes," "compliant," or a reference to an industry certification will be returned unscored.

II. STORAGE

(1) Identify each database, file share, application server, and archive in which personal information resides, and state the operating system and database platform of each. (2) State whether personal information is commingled with data belonging to other clients on shared hardware and, if so, describe the logical separation enforced. (3) Describe the physical security of each facility housing the identified systems, addressing perimeter access control, visitor escort, and access log retention. Response area: [__________________________________________________]  Evidence: required for (1) and (3).

III. ENCRYPTION

(4) State the encryption algorithms and key lengths applied to personal information at rest, distinguishing between production, test, and archived copies. (5) Confirm whether test and development environments contain live personal information and, if so, whether it is masked, scrambled, or truncated before use. (6) Describe key custody: who holds key material, how it is stored, and how dual control is enforced for key recovery. Response area: [__________________________________________________]  Evidence: required for (4) and (6).

IV. TRANSMISSION

(7) Describe all interfaces over which personal information leaves vendor control, including batch file transfers, real-time interfaces, courier movement of tape, and remote administrative sessions. (8) State the protection applied to each interface and identify any interface for which no cryptographic protection exists. (9) Describe how remote access by vendor staff and by third-party maintenance personnel is authenticated and logged. Response area: [__________________________________________________]  Evidence: required for (7) and (9).

(12) Describe the disposal of hardware, including hard drives resident in retired workstations, servers, ticket vending equipment, and onboard validators.
