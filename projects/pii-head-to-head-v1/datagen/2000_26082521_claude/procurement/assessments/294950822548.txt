QUARTERLY OPERATIONS REVIEW — MIDWEST DISTRIBUTION CENTER
Reporting Period: Q3
Prepared by: Operations Analytics Group
Distribution: Site Director, Regional Vice President, Continuous Improvement Lead

SUMMARY OF PERFORMANCE

Throughput for the quarter finished at 94.2% of plan, a decline of 2.6 points against the prior quarter. The shortfall is concentrated in the second shift, where staffing ran below authorized headcount for seven of thirteen weeks. Inbound receiving met plan in every week of the quarter; outbound pick-and-pack absorbed the entire variance.

DISCUSSION

Overtime consumption is the dominant cost driver. The site absorbed 1,270 additional overtime hours to protect the on-time ship rate, and still lost 2.3 points against it. The Continuous Improvement Lead has been asked to model whether a fourth pick line would reduce dependence on overtime at the current volume, or whether the constraint is upstream in put-away.

Order accuracy remains within tolerance but has drifted for three consecutive quarters. The pattern correlates with newly badged associates in their first sixty days. The Training Coordinator will report on revised certification gates at the next review.

Inventory shrink increased two basis points. Cycle count coverage was 88% against a 95% target; the gap traces to counters being redeployed to outbound during peak weeks.
