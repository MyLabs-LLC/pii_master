SUPPLIER INFORMATION SECURITY ASSESSMENT
PART C — HANDLING OF PERSONAL INFORMATION
Issue 3, 2011

INSTRUCTIONS TO THE RESPONDING SUPPLIER

This part of the assessment is concerned exclusively with personal information obtained from, or generated on behalf of, the contracting staffing agency. Personal information for the purposes of this Part comprises candidate registration records, right-to-work documentation, payroll instruction data, background screening outcomes, and any identifier capable of distinguishing one individual from another. The supplier's authorised respondent should complete each numbered item in continuous prose rather than by reference to marketing material or generalised policy statements. Where the third column carries a requirement flag, the cited artefact must be enclosed with the return; the assessment will be recorded as incomplete in its absence and will be reissued.
