MEMORANDUM

TO:      Vendor Risk Management Reviewers
FROM:    Office of Information Security
SUBJECT: Scoring Guidance — Sections 1 through 4, Vendor Security Assessment Questionnaire
DATE:    Second Quarter 2016

Apply the following when scoring returned questionnaires. Record the score for each section on the worksheet; do not annotate the supplier's response boxes.

SECTION 1 — STORAGE

  Adequate      Every storage location named. Shared environments identified with
                separation controls described. Inventory owner named by role and
                review frequency stated.
  Partial       Locations named but inventory owner or review frequency omitted.
  Inadequate    Any storage location described only in general terms, or shared
                environment declared without separation controls.

SECTION 2 — ENCRYPTION

  Adequate      Algorithm and key length stated and meeting the baseline. Key
                custody assigned to a named role. Rotation interval stated.
                Portable media encryption enforced by technical control.
  Partial       Baseline met at rest but enforcement on portable media is by
                policy alone.
  Inadequate    Algorithm not stated, key length below baseline, or key custody
                unassigned. Score inadequate and hold the assessment.

SECTION 3 — TRANSMISSION

  Adequate      All transmission methods listed. Minimum protocol version at or
                above baseline with retirement date given. Subcontractors listed
                by function and processing country.
  Partial       Methods listed but subcontractor register incomplete.
  Inadequate    Any transmission method omitted, or weaker protocol versions
                still permitted.

SECTION 4 — RETENTION AND DISPOSAL

  Adequate      Retention period stated. Destruction method and standard given
                for both electronic and paper. Certificates issued, retained,
                and assigned to a role. Termination interval stated.
  Partial       Destruction standard given for one medium only.
  Inadequate    No retention period, or destruction described without a standard.
                Score inadequate and hold the assessment.

EVIDENCE COLUMN

An item marked Evidence Required is scored on the attachment, not the response text. Where the attachment is a specimen document, confirm it carries no values in the personal information fields before filing. Return any attachment containing populated fields to the supplier's Records Custodian and record the return on the worksheet.

DISPOSITION

Two or more Partial scores, or any single Inadequate, prevents a clear disposition. Forward the worksheet to the reviewing manager with the recommended disposition and the conditions, if any, expressed as dated commitments.
