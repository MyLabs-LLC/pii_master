SCORING KEY

    0 = No response provided, or response non-responsive to the item
    1 = Response provided; no evidence supplied where evidence marked "Y"
    2 = Response provided; evidence supplied but incomplete or out of date
    3 = Response provided; evidence complete and current

DISPOSITION THRESHOLDS

    Aggregate 0–29 ....... Reject. Supplier not approved for Personal
                           Information handling. Escalate to Category Manager.
    Aggregate 30–44 ...... Conditional. Remediation plan required within
                           thirty (30) days. Interim controls to be documented.
    Aggregate 45–57 ...... Approved with observations. Re-test at next cycle.
    Aggregate 58–60 ...... Approved. No further action this cycle.

MANDATORY FAIL CONDITIONS (override aggregate score)

    (a) Item 2.1 scored 0 or 1 — encryption at rest unverified.
    (b) Item 3.3 answered affirmative with no compensating control described.
    (c) Item 3.4 incomplete — undisclosed subcontractor processing.
    (d) Item 1.5 answered affirmative for unmanaged devices.

    Any mandatory fail condition requires written waiver from the Head of
    Vendor Risk & Compliance prior to award or renewal. Waivers expire at the
    close of the assessment cycle in which they are granted and are not
    carried forward.
