VENDOR SECURITY ASSESSMENT QUESTIONNAIRE
Personal Information — Storage, Encryption, Transmission and Disposal
Reference series: VSA/PI/2011

Section 1 — Scope and basis of assessment

The contracting staffing agency is obliged, under the terms of its client framework agreements and under applicable data protection legislation, to satisfy itself that suppliers engaged in the processing of personal information maintain controls no less rigorous than those the Agency applies within its own estate. This questionnaire constitutes the Agency's principal means of discharging that obligation. It is issued in advance of contract execution and thereafter at intervals determined by the risk tier assigned to the supplier. The supplier is required to respond to each item in Section 2 in the space provided. Responses should describe the control as operated, not as intended or as documented in draft. Where the evidence column indicates that an artefact is required, that artefact must be enclosed; the Agency reserves the right to request further material or to conduct an onsite inspection where responses are insufficiently specific.
