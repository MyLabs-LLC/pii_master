PART II — GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS

  • One questionnaire per contract. Do not combine service lines.
  • Answer in the space provided. Continuation sheets must be numbered and referenced.
  • The Evidence Required column governs. Where marked, the questionnaire is not
    considered received until the corresponding document is on file.
  • Direct procedural questions to the Records Custodian.
