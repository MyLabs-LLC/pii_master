CONFIDENTIAL — PROCUREMENT SENSITIVE

ARTICLE I. PURPOSE AND APPLICABILITY

1.01 This Questionnaire is promulgated pursuant to the Bureau's authority to assess, prior to award and periodically thereafter, the administrative, physical, and technical safeguards maintained by any person or entity that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits personal information on behalf of the Department.

1.02 For purposes hereof, "personal information" shall bear the meaning ascribed to it in the applicable state information practices act, and shall include without limitation individually identifiable health information, whether in electronic, paper, or oral form.

1.03 Completion of this Questionnaire is a condition precedent to execution of any agreement, amendment, or task order. Refusal or material incompleteness shall constitute grounds for a determination of non-responsibility.

ARTICLE II. INSTRUCTIONS TO THE RESPONDENT

2.01 The Respondent shall answer each numbered interrogatory in the space provided. Where the space provided is insufficient, the Respondent shall continue the response on a separate sheet, plainly labeled with the corresponding clause number, and shall so indicate within the response box.

2.02 The column captioned "Evidence Required" specifies whether documentary substantiation must accompany the response. An entry of "Required" obligates the Respondent to attach the artifact described; an entry of "Upon Request" permits deferral until the Department so directs in writing.

2.03 All responses shall be current as of the date of certification and shall be updated by the Respondent within ten (10) business days of any material change.
