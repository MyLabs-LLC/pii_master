SECTION B — INSTRUCTIONS TO THE RESPONDENT

The Supplier is hereby required to complete each numbered item below in full. Responses shall be furnished in writing within thirty (30) calendar days of receipt. Where the column headed "Evidence Required" indicates "YES," the Supplier shall attach documentary substantiation, which may include but shall not be limited to written policies, executed procedures, audit attestations, certifications, or system configuration extracts. A response of "Not Applicable" shall be accompanied by a written explanation in the corresponding response box. Incomplete submissions may, at the sole discretion of the Company, result in suspension of onboarding activities or termination of the existing agreement in accordance with the terms thereof.

For purposes of this questionnaire, "Protected Information" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual that the Supplier creates, receives, maintains, or transmits on behalf of the Company, including protected health information as defined under 45 C.F.R. § 160.103, personally identifiable information, and cardholder data as defined by the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard.
