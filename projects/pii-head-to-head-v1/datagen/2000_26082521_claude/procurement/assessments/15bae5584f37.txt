HARROW & VANCE PHARMACY GROUP
SUPPLIER ASSURANCE PROGRAMME — INFORMATION HANDLING QUESTIONNAIRE
Reference: SAP/IHQ/2011  |  Revision 3  |  Restricted

PART A — PREAMBLE

A.1 The Company is a covered entity for the purposes of the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act and its implementing regulations, as amended by the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health Act. Suppliers performing functions involving protected health information are business associates and are contractually bound to the safeguards described in the executed business associate agreement. This questionnaire evidences the Company's ongoing diligence obligation and shall be completed annually and upon any material change of service.

A.2 Each clause in Part B is presented with an adjacent evidence column. Where the column bears the entry MANDATORY, the named artifact must accompany the response; where it bears ON REQUEST, the artifact must be produced within five business days of a written request. Responses are to be typed into the bracketed field following each clause.

PART B — CONTROL CLAUSES

B.1 Storage. Set out in narrative form the manner in which personal information is segregated from the data of other clients of the supplier, distinguishing between logical segregation within shared infrastructure and dedicated infrastructure, and state whether any personal information is replicated to environments used for development or testing.
    [                                                                    ]
    EVIDENCE: MANDATORY — architecture overview

B.2 Access. Describe the process by which access to personal information is granted, reviewed and revoked, the frequency of entitlement review, and the interval within which access is removed upon separation of a workforce member.
    [                                                                    ]
    EVIDENCE: MANDATORY — access review record specimen

B.3 Encryption. State whether personal information is encrypted at rest and in transit; identify the standard applied; and confirm whether the methodology renders the information unusable, unreadable or indecipherable such that the breach notification safe harbour is available.
    [                                                                    ]
    EVIDENCE: MANDATORY — encryption policy extract

B.4 Transmission. Describe the controls governing outbound transmission, including whether electronic mail containing personal information is permitted, whether content inspection is deployed at the network boundary, and how physical media in transit is protected.
    [                                                                    ]
    EVIDENCE: ON REQUEST

B.5 Disposal. Describe the destruction standard applied to electronic and hardcopy records, the role title accountable for authorising destruction, and the records maintained to evidence each event.
    [                                                                    ]
    EVIDENCE: MANDATORY — destruction log specimen

B.6 Incident Response. Describe the process for detecting and reporting suspected unauthorised acquisition, access, use or disclosure of personal information, and state the maximum interval within which the Company would be notified.
    [                                                                    ]
    EVIDENCE: MANDATORY — incident response plan

Completed forms are to be lodged through the Supplier Assurance intake queue. Incomplete submissions are returned unassessed.
