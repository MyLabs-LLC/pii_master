1. SCOPE AND DEFINITIONS

1.1  "Institutional Data" means any record, file, extract, index, report or
     derivative furnished by the University or generated by the Vendor on the
     University's behalf, including directory and non-directory student record
     elements as those terms are used at 34 CFR Part 99.
1.2  "Processing Site" means any owned, leased or subcontracted facility at
     which Institutional Data is stored, staged, printed, converted or backed up.
1.3  "Disposition" means destruction, degaussing, overwriting, return to the
     University, or transfer to a successor supplier.
