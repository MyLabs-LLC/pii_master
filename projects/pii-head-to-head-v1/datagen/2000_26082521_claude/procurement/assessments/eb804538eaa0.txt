1.0  PREAMBLE AND CONSTRUCTION

1.1  This Questionnaire is issued pursuant to and incorporated by reference into the Master Services Agreement then in force between the Company and the Supplier, and the responses recorded herein shall constitute representations and warranties of the Supplier for all purposes thereunder.

1.2  For the purposes of this Questionnaire, "Personal Information" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, whether such person be an employee, agent, consignee, consignor, or customer of the Company, and whether held in electronic, magnetic, optical, or physical form.

1.3  Each response shall be entered legibly within the response box provided. Where the Evidence Required column is marked "Y", the Supplier shall append the corresponding artefact and cross-reference it by exhibit number. A response of "Not Applicable" shall be accompanied by written justification.
