The Authority requires each bidder to describe, in narrative form, the administrative and technical controls it applies to personal information obtained through Authority contracts. The narrative shall be responsive to the six numbered inquiries set out below and shall be submitted on the bidder's letterhead with this attachment appended. The evidence column indicates whether documentary support must accompany the narrative; where support is required and not furnished, the response will be evaluated as though the control were absent. Bidders are cautioned not to include live records of any kind in the response package, whether as illustration, screen print, or sample output.

1. Describe the manner in which personal information is received from the Authority and the controls applied at the point of receipt, including validation, logging, and the segregation of inbound files from general-purpose storage.  Evidence: N
2. Describe the storage architecture, naming each host, database instance, and backup repository holding personal information, and state whether any such component resides outside a facility under the bidder's direct control.  Evidence: Y
3. Describe the cryptographic controls applied to personal information at rest and in transit, identifying algorithm, key length, mode of operation, and the standard against which the implementation has been validated.  Evidence: Y
4. Describe the population of personnel with access to personal information by role title, the basis on which access is granted, the frequency of access recertification, and the background screening applied prior to access.  Evidence: Y
5. Describe the destruction of personal information at the conclusion of the contract term, including the return or destruction election available to the Authority, the deadline for completion, and the form of attestation the bidder will provide.  Evidence: Y
6. Describe any circumstance under which the bidder would disclose personal information to a governmental body, a litigant, or an affiliate, and the notice the Authority would receive.  Evidence: N
