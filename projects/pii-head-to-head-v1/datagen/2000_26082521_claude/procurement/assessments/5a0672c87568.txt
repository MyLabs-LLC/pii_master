RE:      Annual Third-Party Due Diligence Questionnaire — Safeguarding of
         Member Nonpublic Personal Information (Cycle 2004-A)

I. PURPOSE AND AUTHORITY

This questionnaire is issued pursuant to, and in furtherance of, the obligations imposed upon this credit union by Title V, Subtitle A of the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (15 U.S.C. 6801 et seq.) and by Part 748 of the Rules and Regulations of the National Credit Union Administration, including without limitation Appendix A thereto. Said authorities require, inter alia, that this institution exercise appropriate due diligence in the selection of service providers and require by contract that each such provider implement measures adequate to protect member information against reasonably foreseeable threats.

II. INSTRUCTIONS TO THE RESPONDENT

Where the notation EVIDENCE REQUIRED appears, a narrative response standing alone shall not be deemed responsive; the Supplier must append the documentation described and identify it by exhibit designation.
