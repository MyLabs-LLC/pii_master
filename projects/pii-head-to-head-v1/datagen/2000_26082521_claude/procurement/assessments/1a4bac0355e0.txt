OFFICE OF THE UNIVERSITY REGISTRAR
RECORDS MANAGEMENT AND DISCLOSURE UNIT

VENDOR INFORMATION SECURITY ASSESSMENT QUESTIONNAIRE
Form REG-SEC-114 (Rev. 03/1997)

TO THE SUPPLIER: Pursuant to the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act of
1974, as amended, and to the administrative rules promulgated thereunder by the
Board of Regents, no contract for services involving access to Student Education
Records shall be executed, renewed, or extended until this questionnaire has been
completed in its entirety and returned to the Records Custodian. Responses shall
be typewritten within the boxes provided and shall not be continued into the
margins. Where the column headed EVIDENCE REQUIRED bears the notation "REQUIRED,"
the Supplier shall append documentary proof, indexed to the corresponding inquiry
number and certified as a true and correct copy. Submissions returned incomplete
shall not be reviewed, and the associated procurement shall be held in abeyance.
