INTERNAL — OFFICE OF INFORMATION GOVERNANCE
ASSESSMENT WORKING NOTE — IG-114 REVIEW STANDARD

Purpose of this note. Reviewers assess responses against the standard set out below rather than against personal judgment of what constitutes adequate security. The standard is applied consistently across suppliers; departures are recorded in the assessment file with reasons.

Adequacy. A response is adequate where it describes a control that is implemented, is specific enough that its operation can be tested, and is consistent with the evidence supplied. A response describing a control in the conditional or future tense is not adequate regardless of how sound the described control would be. A response that recites a standard without stating how the supplier conforms to it is not adequate; the recitation may be retained as context but the reviewer must record the substantive gap.

Consistency checks. Reviewers cross-read items 2, 4 and 6 to confirm that every environment named in item 2 is addressed in item 4 and that backup treatment in item 6 covers each such environment. Items 1 and 10 are cross-read to confirm that a destruction method is stated for every retention period claimed. Items 7 and 8 are cross-read to confirm that electronic mail, if disclaimed in item 8, does not reappear as a channel in item 7. Where a cross-read produces a contradiction, the packet is returned as deficient rather than accepted with conditions; a contradiction indicates the supplier does not hold an accurate picture of its own processing.

Separation of duties. Item 5 requires that key custody and authorization of key destruction rest with different role titles. Suppliers occasionally answer with the same role title for both, or with a single named role and a note that a second person is "usually" involved. Neither satisfies the item. The reviewer records the response as deficient and states the reason in one sentence.

Handling of received evidence. Evidence arriving with unobscured identifying values is not read beyond the point at which the reviewer recognizes the condition. The file is destroyed, the destruction recorded in the assessment file by artifact name and receipt date, and a resubmission request issued. Reviewers do not extract, quote or summarize such values into the assessment file, working papers, or correspondence. This applies equally where the values appear incidentally in a screenshot margin, a document footer, or a file name.

Accepted with conditions. This outcome is used where the substantive controls are adequate but an evidentiary or administrative element is outstanding — a missing revision date, an extract shorter than the stated period, a translation not yet supplied. Conditions are stated with a completion date and tracked to closure. The outcome is not used to accept an inadequate control on an undertaking to improve it.

Reassessment interval. Accepted suppliers are reassessed at three-year intervals or upon material change reported under the certification, whichever is earlier. Suppliers accepted with conditions are reassessed at the earlier of condition closure plus twelve months or the ordinary interval.
