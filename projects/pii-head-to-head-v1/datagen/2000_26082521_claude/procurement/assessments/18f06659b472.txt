1.0 PURPOSE AND APPLICABILITY. This questionnaire is issued to every supplier that creates, receives, maintains or transmits personal information on behalf of the Company, including protected health information arising from pharmacy dispensing, prescription benefit adjudication, immunization records, loyalty enrollment and photofinishing services. The supplier shall complete every clause below without exception. Where a clause is genuinely inapplicable to the services performed, the supplier shall write "Not Applicable" in the response box and state the basis for that conclusion in narrative form; a blank box will be treated as a non-response and will suspend the assessment.

2.0 INSTRUCTIONS FOR COMPLETION. Responses shall be entered in the box beneath each clause. Boxes may be expanded as required, provided the clause numbering is preserved. The right-hand notation EVIDENCE REQUIRED indicates whether supporting documentation must accompany the return: "Y" obliges the supplier to attach the artifact named in the clause, and "N" permits a narrative response alone. Attachments shall be labelled with the corresponding clause number and returned as a single package through the Supplier Portal. The Company reserves the right to request evidence for any clause marked "N" following review.
