TRANSMITTAL — VENDOR SECURITY ASSESSMENT PACKET

To:      Supplier Primary Contact (title on file)
From:    Vendor Risk & Compliance
Subject: Annual recertification — Personal Information handling controls

Enclosed:

    1. Vendor Security Assessment Questionnaire (Sections 1–5)
    2. Evidence submission index
    3. Return envelope, tracked service

Action required:

    Complete all seventeen numbered items. Items marked "Y" in the Evidence
    Required column will not be scored without attached documentation.
    Cross-references to prior-cycle submissions are not accepted; evidence
    must be current as at the date of attestation.

Return method:

    Secure file transfer portal (credentials issued separately by the Records
    Custodian) or tracked courier to the Vendor Risk & Compliance intake desk.
    Do not return by unencrypted electronic mail. Packets returned by
    unencrypted mail will be destroyed on receipt and a replacement packet
    issued, with the fifteen-day clock restarted.

Consequence of non-return:

    Suppliers failing to return a completed packet within the stated period
    will be suspended from new requisition distribution until the assessment
    is closed. Existing placements are unaffected pending disposition.

Queries:

    Direct all queries in writing to the Records Custodian. Telephone queries
    will not be answered. Extensions of time require the approval of the Head
    of Vendor Risk & Compliance and are granted once only.
