PART I — SCOPE AND INSTRUCTIONS

This questionnaire applies to every supplier that stores, processes, or
transmits student or employee personal information on behalf of the
District, including hosted applications, transportation routing services,
food service point-of-sale operators, and assessment vendors.

Responses are due within twenty (20) business days. Each numbered item has
a response box and an evidence designation. Where evidence is designated
REQUIRED, the response is treated as unsupported until the documentation is
received and logged by the Records Custodian.

Terms used below carry the meanings given in the District's Data Governance
Regulation and in FERPA, 34 CFR Part 99.
