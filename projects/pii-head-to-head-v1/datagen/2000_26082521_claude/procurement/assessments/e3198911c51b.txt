PREAMBLE. The District, being a recipient of federal financial
assistance and thereby subject to the provisions of 20 U.S.C. § 1232g
and the regulations promulgated thereunder at 34 C.F.R. Part 99, is
obliged to maintain direct control over education records placed in
the hands of its contractors. The interrogatories in the table below
are propounded for that purpose. Answers shall be inscribed within the
response field appertaining to each item. Where the column headed
"EVID. REQ'D" bears the entry "Y," the Respondent shall attach the
proof described in the far-right column and shall enumerate the same
upon the Schedule of Exhibits.
