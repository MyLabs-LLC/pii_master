PART 4 OF THE SUPPLIER ASSURANCE PACK — PERSONAL INFORMATION CONTROLS
For completion by the supplier's nominated respondent. 2011 issue.

Preliminary. The staffing agency issuing this pack places candidate and worker records with suppliers for the purposes of payroll administration, background screening, occupational health assessment, and the storage of engagement documentation. Records so placed comprise personal information within the meaning of the Agency's data protection policy, and the Agency remains accountable for them notwithstanding their transfer. This Part is directed at establishing, in specific and verifiable terms, how such records are held, protected in transit and at rest, and disposed of at the conclusion of their useful life. It is not a general enquiry into the supplier's security posture; Parts 1 to 3 address perimeter, endpoint and personnel controls respectively and should be completed separately. Responses framed as references to certification held, without description of the control as it operates upon Agency records specifically, will be returned unassessed.

4.1 The supplier should describe, in narrative form, the lifecycle of an Agency personal information record from the point of receipt to the point of destruction. The description should identify each system through which the record passes, each individual role with the ability to view or alter it, and each point at which a copy is created, whether deliberately or as a consequence of backup, replication or logging. Copies held in test or development environments should be identified expressly, together with the treatment applied to reduce their sensitivity.

4.2 The supplier should state the cryptographic controls applied to Agency personal information while at rest. The response should address the algorithm and key length in use, the boundary at which encryption is applied, the location of key material relative to the encrypted data, the role responsible for key custody, and the frequency and method of key rotation. Where the supplier relies upon encryption provided by a hosting or infrastructure provider, that arrangement should be described and the division of responsibility stated.
Response: [                                                                        ]
Evidence required: Yes

4.3 The supplier should describe the protections applied to Agency personal information in transit. The response should distinguish between transfers to and from the Agency, transfers to onward recipients such as screening bureaux or pension administrators, and transfers between the supplier's own sites or environments. For each, the protocol and cipher configuration should be stated. The supplier should confirm whether personal information is at any point transmitted by unencrypted electronic mail or carried on unencrypted portable media, and should describe the authorisation required before any such transfer occurs.
Response: [                                                                        ]
Evidence required: Yes

4.4 The supplier should describe its retention and disposal arrangements. The response should state the retention period applicable to each category of Agency personal information, the event from which the period runs, and the process by which expiry is detected and acted upon. The method of destruction should be stated separately for magnetic media, solid-state media, paper and backup sets. Where destruction is performed by a contracted provider, the provider's role should be described and the form of destruction confirmation obtained should be specified. The supplier should further state the maximum interval between the logical deletion of a record and its irrecoverable removal from all backup copies.

Evidence required: Yes
