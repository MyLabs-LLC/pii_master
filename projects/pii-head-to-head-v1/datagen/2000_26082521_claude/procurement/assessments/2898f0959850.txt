INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

TO:        All Department Managers
FROM:      Facilities Planning Office
SUBJECT:   Consolidation of Third-Floor Workspace — Phase 2
CLASSIFICATION: Internal Use Only

Phase 2 of the third-floor consolidation begins the week following the holiday closure. This memorandum sets out what each department must complete beforehand and what the Facilities Planning Office will handle.

WHAT IS CHANGING

The east wing of the third floor will be taken out of service for mechanical work lasting approximately eleven weeks. Departments currently seated in the east wing will relocate to the consolidated open plan in the west wing. Seat density in the west wing increases from 118 to 164 positions. Four enclosed offices become bookable focus rooms; two remain assigned.

DEPARTMENT RESPONSIBILITIES

Before the move date, each department manager must:

- Submit a seating map identifying which positions require dual monitors, standing desks, or accessibility accommodations. Accommodation requests should reference the request number issued by the Accommodations Office rather than describing the underlying need.
- Confirm the disposition of all filing cabinets. Cabinets containing records under active retention hold must be tagged; Facilities will move them under seal. Cabinets not tagged by the deadline will be treated as surplus.
- Identify one departmental move coordinator and one alternate by title.
- Clear all personal effects from the east wing. Items remaining after the cutoff will be boxed and held for thirty days at the loading dock, then donated.

WHAT FACILITIES HANDLES

Network drops, telephony reassignment, badge reader reprogramming, furniture disassembly and reinstallation, and the physical transport of sealed records cabinets. Do not arrange third-party movers independently; building insurance does not cover unapproved vendors on the freight elevator.

SCHEDULE

Seating maps due:              two weeks before closure
Cabinet tagging complete:      one week before closure
Personal effects cleared:      last business day before closure
Move weekend:                  first weekend after closure
Systems verification:          first Monday, all day
Normal operations resume:      first Tuesday

QUESTIONS

Direct questions through your departmental move coordinator, who will consolidate and forward them to the Facilities Planning Office. Individual inquiries sent directly will be redirected, which slows response.

A walkthrough of the west wing layout is available by appointment for managers who wish to review sightlines and storage before submitting seating maps.
