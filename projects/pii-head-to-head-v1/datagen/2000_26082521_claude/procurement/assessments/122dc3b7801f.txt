COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSION
SUPPLIER ASSURANCE PROGRAM
Personal Information Safeguards Questionnaire — Schedule D
Effective for solicitations issued on or after 1 July 2016

Purpose
    Schedule D is completed once per supplier per contract term. It records how the supplier holds, protects, moves and destroys personal information belonging to riders, applicants, employees and program participants of the Commission. The completed schedule is retained by the Contracts Office for the life of the contract plus six years.

How to complete
    Enter answers in the box under each question. Boxes expand as needed; do not attach separate narrative in place of a response.
    The column marked EVID indicates the evidence class due with this return:
        A — policy or procedure document, current and approved
        B — technical configuration record or system output
        C — third-party report, certificate or attestation
        — — no evidence due
    Enter the attachment reference for each item in the ATTACH REF box.
