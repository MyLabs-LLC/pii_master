COMPLETION RULES
================================================================
1. Each question must be answered in the response box beneath it. Do not
   substitute a marketing document for a written answer.
2. Where the Evidence Required column is marked "Y", the named artifact
   must accompany the questionnaire. Where it is marked "O", the artifact
   may be supplied at the Authority's later request.
3. Answers are incorporated by reference into the contract and must be
   updated within ten business days of any material change.
4. Direct questions on completion to the Authority contract administrator
   named in the transmittal letter.
