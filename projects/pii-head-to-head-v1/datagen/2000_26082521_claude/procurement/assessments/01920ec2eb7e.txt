3.0 SCOPE AND METHOD OF COMPLETION

3.0.1  This sheet supplements Section 4 of the master questionnaire and is to
       be completed by suppliers assessed at Tier 1 only.
3.0.2  For each clause the Supplier shall mark the Y/N/NA column, complete the
       response box, and supply the artefact named in the Evidence Required
       column. Responses recorded outside the boxes will not be assessed.
3.0.3  Terms not defined on this sheet carry the meaning given at Clause 1.3
       of the master questionnaire.
