SCHEDULE B — EVIDENCE CONVENTIONS
Applicable to Form IG-114 (Rev. 03/2011)

B.1 Purpose. This schedule states how documentary evidence is to be prepared, labelled and transmitted in support of responses to the vendor security assessment questionnaire. It is issued for the convenience of suppliers and does not vary the substance of any question.

B.2 Form of evidence. Evidence must be a document that exists in the supplier's own management system independently of this assessment. A document prepared solely to answer this questionnaire is a response, not evidence, and will be treated as such. Each artifact must bear the supplier's document control number, revision designation and effective date on its face. Where the supplier's system does not apply such identifiers, the supplier must state that fact in the response and provide the approval record for the document in its place.

B.3 Obscuring of identifying values. Evidence artifacts frequently contain fields whose labels are informative and whose values are not. The Authority requires the labels and requires the values to be removed. The following field labels are expected to appear in evidence and must be preserved: account holder name; service address; mailing address; telephone number; electronic mail address; date of birth; government identification number; taxpayer identification number; financial account number; payment card number; utility account number; meter identifier; premise identifier; credential identifier; key identifier. The corresponding values must be obscured by irreversible means. Blacking out on a printed page followed by rescanning is acceptable. Application of a graphical overlay to an electronic document is not acceptable where the underlying value remains recoverable from the file. The supplier must state in the response which method was used.

B.4 Column and header retention. Where evidence takes the form of a register, log or extract, the column headings must be retained in full so that the Authority may assess the completeness of the record. A log excerpt supplied with headings removed conveys nothing and will be rejected.

B.5 Extent of extracts. An extract need not comprise the entire record. Where an extract is supplied, it must be continuous, must cover a period stated in the response, and must not be selected so as to exclude exceptions. The supplier must state in the response the total number of entries in the source record for the stated period and the number supplied.

B.6 Screenshots. Screenshots are accepted only where the questionnaire expressly permits them, and then only as corroboration of a configuration state described in narrative. A screenshot must show the system identifier and the date on which it was taken. Screenshots of policy documents are not accepted; the document itself is required.

B.7 Language. Evidence in a language other than English must be accompanied by a translation prepared by a competent translator, together with a statement of the translator's role title and qualifications. The Authority assesses the translation; the original is retained in the assessment file.

B.8 Retention by the Authority. Evidence submitted becomes part of the assessment file and is retained for the term of the agreement and seven years thereafter. It is held as commercially confidential and is disclosed only as required by law, by lawful process, or to the Authority's external auditors under obligations of confidence.
