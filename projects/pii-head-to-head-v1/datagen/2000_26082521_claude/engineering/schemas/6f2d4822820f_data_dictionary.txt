ACCESS RESTRICTION
Category III columns are governed by authorization list MBRSEC3. *PUBLIC is
*EXCLUDE at object level; group profiles OPSCLK and TELLER hold *USE on the
MBRV01 logical view, which omits MBR_TIN, MBR_BIRTH_DT, MBR_MOTHER_MAIDN and
MBR_DRV_LIC_NBR. Direct file authority is held by four named operations
profiles and reviewed quarterly by the Records Custodian against the security
officer's authority report.

RETENTION
Rows persist five years past MBR_STAT_CD = C. Category III columns are nulled
at closure plus 24 months by job MBRPRG02 unless a litigation hold code is
present in the suspense file.

DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
MBRMAST feeds the statement print vendor (name and address only), the EFT
processor (member number and status), the credit bureau Metro submission
(name, address, TIN), and the monthly MCIF marketing file (opt-out honored,
TIN and birth date excluded by extract program, not by view).
