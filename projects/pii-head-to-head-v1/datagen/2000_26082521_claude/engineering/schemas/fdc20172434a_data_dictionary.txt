DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT
SCHOOL DISTRICT — DEPARTMENT OF INFORMATION SYSTEMS
FILE: HEALTH_SVC_MBR   RELEASE: 2004.2   SHEET: 4

PART I — GENERAL

1. This excerpt describes the member attributes of the Health Services
   registry as constituted at release 2004.2. It supersedes sheet 4 of
   release 2003.1 in its entirety.

2. Column definitions are binding on all programs reading the file. Any
   program that derives a value not listed below shall be documented on
   Form IS-DER-31 before promotion to production.

PART II — COLUMN DEFINITIONS

3. HSM_MEMBER_KEY
   3.1 Type: DECIMAL(9,0).  3.2 Nullable: No.
   3.3 Notes: Cross-reference to the student master. No re-use of retired
       keys is permitted.

4. HSM_NM_LAST / HSM_NM_FIRST
   4.1 Type: CHAR(30) / CHAR(20).  4.2 Nullable: No / No.
   4.3 Notes: Carried for print legibility on clinic logs only. Access
       restricted to the Health Services role and the Records Custodian.

5. HSM_DT_BIRTH
   5.1 Type: DATE.  5.2 Nullable: No.
   5.3 Notes: Governs immunization schedule calculation. Never rendered on
       any extract; only the derived age band is exported.

6. HSM_ID_MEDICAID
   6.1 Type: CHAR(12).  6.2 Nullable: Yes.
   6.3 Notes: Restricted class A. Excluded from every report definition.
       Write access limited to the Billing Clerk role.

7. HSM_CD_CONDITION_1 through HSM_CD_CONDITION_5
   7.1 Type: CHAR(4) each.  7.2 Nullable: Yes.
   7.3 Notes: Coded against CONDITION_REF. Free text is not accepted in
       these positions and shall not be introduced.

8. HSM_TXT_NOTE
   8.1 Type: VARCHAR(2000).  8.2 Nullable: Yes.
   8.3 Notes: Restricted class A. Excluded from all extracts without
       exception. Subject to review by the School Nurse Coordinator.

9. HSM_FL_CONSENT_SHARE
   9.1 Type: CHAR(1).  9.2 Nullable: Yes.
   9.3 Notes: A null value is processed as withheld consent.

10. HSM_TS_LAST_TOUCH
    10.1 Type: TIMESTAMP.  10.2 Nullable: No.
    10.3 Notes: Maintained by database trigger. Application updates are
         rejected.

PART III — ACCESS, RETENTION AND EXTRACTS

11. Restricted class A columns are readable only through stored procedure
    SP_HSM_CLINIC and only from workstations in the health office subnet.

12. Rows are retained for the period of enrollment plus seven years, after
    which the record is destroyed under the approved retention schedule.
    Note columns are purged at the close of each school year plus two.

13. Two downstream extracts are authorized: the immunization compliance
    count to the county, monthly, aggregate only; and the state audit
    file, annual, keyed and age-banded, carrying no name, identifier or
    contact column.

14. Requests for additional extracts shall be filed with the Records
    Custodian not less than thirty days before the required delivery date.
