CUSTOMER MASTER (CUST_MSTR) — DICTIONARY EXCERPT, SECTION 4.2

The CUST_MSTR file is the system of record for all active and inactive service relationships carried on the Customer Information System and is maintained as a VSAM KSDS with the composite key ACCT_KEY plus CUST_SEQ_NO. ACCT_KEY is PIC 9(10) COMP-3, non-null, assigned sequentially by the account-open transaction and never reused; CUST_SEQ_NO is PIC 9(2), non-null, defaulted at establishment and incremented when a second responsible party is added to the same premise. Neither element is derived from any externally issued number, a design decision recorded in the 1994 conversion notes and reaffirmed during the current remediation cycle.

Name elements occupy the first fixed block. CUST_LAST_NM is PIC X(30) and non-null; CUST_FRST_NM is PIC X(20) and non-null; CUST_MID_INIT is PIC X(01) and nullable, spaces-filled where the applicant declines to supply one. CUST_BUS_NM, PIC X(40), is nullable and populated only where ACCT_TYPE_CD indicates a commercial or institutional relationship, in which case the individual name fields carry the authorized signer for the account and are treated identically to residential name data for access purposes. Mailing and service location are carried in SVC_ADDR_LN1 and SVC_ADDR_LN2, both PIC X(35), the first non-null and the second nullable, together with MAIL_CITY PIC X(25), MAIL_ST PIC X(02), MAIL_ZIP5 PIC 9(05) and MAIL_ZIP4 PIC 9(04), the last nullable pending completion of the postal certification pass. Operations should note that SVC_ADDR_LN1 is not an addressable location in the geographic sense; PREM_ID, PIC 9(08) COMP-3 and non-null, is the only reliable join to the premise and meter tables, and reports keyed on address text have historically double-counted multi-unit structures.

Contact telephony is held in HOME_PHN and WORK_PHN, both PIC 9(10) and both nullable. Neither field is validated on entry beyond a length edit, and the Billing supervisor's standing instruction is that WORK_PHN may be used only for field-service coordination and never for collection contact. The two telephone fields, together with SSN_TIN PIC 9(09), BIRTH_DT, and DRV_LIC_NO PIC X(20), constitute the restricted block described in Section 6. Access to the restricted block is controlled by RACF group rather than by application menu, and any request to add a userid to that group is routed to the Records Custodian for written approval. BIRTH_DT was carried as PIC 9(06) in YYMMDD form through the 1997 cycle and was expanded to PIC 9(08) CCYYMMDD during date remediation; the conversion utility applied a fixed pivot, and records established before the pivot year should be treated as suspect until the audit pass completes.

Retention for the full record is six years following FINAL_BILL_DT, after which the annual purge job clears the restricted block in place, sets PURGE_IND, and retains the remaining financial and consumption elements indefinitely for rate-case support. The nightly extract to the collections vendor carries ACCT_KEY, CUST_LAST_NM, CUST_FRST_NM, the mailing elements, and balance fields only; SSN_TIN, BIRTH_DT and DRV_LIC_NO are suppressed at the extract step and not merely omitted from the vendor layout. The quarterly extract to the state weatherization program carries LIHEAP_ELIG_IND and the mailing elements under the standing interagency agreement and is written to labelled tape, logged, and hand-carried.
