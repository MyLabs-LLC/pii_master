SECTION C — ACCESS RESTRICTION
  C.1  Tier 1 columns are excluded from all standard reporting roles. Grants are
       issued to named service accounts only, with quarterly recertification by
       the Associate Registrar for Records.
  C.2  Tier 2 columns are released under the directory-information rules in the
       annual FERPA notice. Rows carrying DIRECTORY_SUPPRESS_IND = 'Y' are
       filtered at the view layer, not by the consuming application.
  C.3  Ad hoc query access is provisioned through the registrar's data request
       form and expires at the close of the requesting term.

SECTION D — RETENTION
  D.1  Active rows are retained for the life of the academic record.
  D.2  NATIONAL_ID_NUM is purged seven years after final enrollment or degree
       conferral, whichever is later; the column is set to NULL and the purge is
       logged in the retention audit table.
  D.3  Address and telephone columns are archived, not deleted, on separation.
  D.4  Non-production refreshes carry masked values for all Tier 1 and Tier 2
       columns; the mask job runs before the environment is released.

SECTION E — DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
  E.1  Nightly ODS load — all columns except NATIONAL_ID_NUM.
  E.2  National Student Clearinghouse submission — STUDENT_ID, name columns,
       BIRTH_DATE. Transmitted over SFTP.
  E.3  IPEDS aggregation — counts only; no identifying columns leave the schema.
  E.4  Emergency notification vendor — CAMPUS_EMAIL_ADDR, MOBILE_PHONE_NUM.
       Refreshed weekly. Contract review due at renewal.
