CUSTOMER PARTY SUBJECT AREA — DATA DICTIONARY
Extract from the Enterprise Data Catalogue, Book II, Clauses 7.1 through 7.14
Issued by the Manager of Data Administration; concurrence of the Privacy Officer recorded 22 September 2004

7.1 This clause set describes the Oracle 9i tables CUST_PARTY and PARTY_CONTACT within schema CUSTDW on instance PRD1, which together constitute the system of record for party demography in the personal lines environment. Both tables are loaded by the party consolidation cycle each night at 23:40 and are read by the policy administration system, the billing system, and the claims front end through synonyms in the respective application schemas. Where a definition below conflicts with a copybook or program comment elsewhere, this catalogue governs.

7.2 CUST_PARTY carries the surrogate key PARTY_ID, NUMBER(12), NOT NULL, generated by sequence CUST_PARTY_SEQ and never reused. PARTY_TYP_CD, VARCHAR2(2), NOT NULL, distinguishes individual from organisational parties and determines which of the attributes below are populated. SRC_SYS_CD, VARCHAR2(4), NOT NULL, records the contributing system, and SRC_PARTY_KEY_TXT, VARCHAR2(30), NOT NULL, preserves the natural key of that system for audit reconciliation.

7.3 The name attributes are LAST_NAME, VARCHAR2(35), NOT NULL for individual parties and null for organisational parties; FIRST_NAME, VARCHAR2(25), nullable on the same condition; MIDDLE_NAME, VARCHAR2(25), nullable; NAME_PREFIX_CD and NAME_SUFFIX_CD, both VARCHAR2(5), nullable; and ORG_LEGAL_NAME, VARCHAR2(60), populated only for organisational parties. The consolidation cycle does not standardise these values beyond case folding, and duplicate detection is performed downstream by the match utility rather than by constraint.

7.4 The sensitive attribute group consists of TAX_ID_NBR, VARCHAR2(9), nullable; NATL_ID_NBR, VARCHAR2(11), nullable and populated only for parties sourced from the group business; DATE_OF_BIRTH, DATE, nullable; DRV_LIC_NBR, VARCHAR2(20), nullable; and DRV_LIC_ST_CD, CHAR(2), nullable. These five columns are designated Restricted under the information classification standard and are subject to clause 7.9 without exception.

7.5 PARTY_CONTACT is keyed by PARTY_ID together with CONTACT_SEQ_NBR, NUMBER(3), NOT NULL, and carries CONTACT_TYP_CD, VARCHAR2(3), NOT NULL; ADDR_LINE_1, VARCHAR2(50), NOT NULL; ADDR_LINE_2 and ADDR_LINE_3, both VARCHAR2(50), nullable; CITY_NAME, VARCHAR2(35), NOT NULL; STATE_PROV_CD, CHAR(2), NOT NULL; POSTAL_CD, VARCHAR2(10), NOT NULL; COUNTRY_CD, CHAR(3), NOT NULL with default USA; PHONE_NBR, VARCHAR2(15), nullable; PHONE_EXT_NBR, VARCHAR2(6), nullable; FAX_NBR, VARCHAR2(15), nullable; and EMAIL_ADDRESS, VARCHAR2(80), nullable.

7.6 Marketing preference is carried on PARTY_CONTACT as DO_NOT_CALL_IND, DO_NOT_MAIL_IND, and DO_NOT_EMAIL_IND, each CHAR(1), NOT NULL with default N. These indicators are authoritative. No extract, campaign selection, or vendor file may be produced from this subject area without applying all three, and the Privacy Officer has directed that the suppression logic be evidenced in the job documentation of any process that reads these tables.

7.7 Effective dating is by ADDR_EFF_DT, DATE, NOT NULL, and ADDR_EXP_DT, DATE, nullable, the latter null denoting the current row. Applications must not assume a single current contact row per party; the consolidation cycle permits one current row per contact type.

7.8 Audit columns CRT_TS, CRT_USER_ID, UPD_TS, and UPD_USER_ID are present on both tables, all NOT NULL, and are maintained by database trigger. They record the batch or online identifier responsible for the change and are relied upon by the internal audit sampling performed under the Section 404 programme.

7.9 The columns named in clause 7.4, together with EMAIL_ADDRESS and PHONE_NBR, are accessible only through role CUSTDW_PII_R. The role is granted by name to individual database accounts on the written approval of the Privacy Officer, is reviewed semi-annually, and is never granted to application service accounts, to the reporting instance, or to any generic identifier. Copies of these tables taken to the development or test instances must be processed by the transformation utility DWSCRB before the copy is made available, and the operator is to record completion of that step on the refresh log.

7.10 Retention for CUST_PARTY is governed by the corporate schedule: a party row is retained while any policy, claim, or receivable references it, and thereafter for ten years from the last such reference, this period reflecting the longest applicable statute among the states in which the Company is licensed. PARTY_CONTACT history rows are retained for seven years from ADDR_EXP_DT. The annual purge is executed under change control and its output reconciled by Data Administration.

7.11 Downstream consumers of this subject area are the data warehouse load (job DW_PARTY_LD, full contact history, nightly), the actuarial extract (PARTY_ID and geographic attributes only, monthly), the printing and fulfilment vendor file (name and address groups, weekly, transmitted by secure file transfer under the agreement executed in 2003), and the reinsurance bordereau. The vendor file is the only extract permitted to leave the Company's network from this subject area, and it excludes every column named in clause 7.4.

7.12 Requests for additional extracts are submitted on form DA-14 and require the joint approval of the Manager of Data Administration and the Privacy Officer. Approval is recorded against this clause set and the extract is added to the register in Book IV.
