MEMORANDUM

TO:      Applications Development — Member Systems
FROM:    Database Administration
DATE:    09 April 1999
SUBJECT: MBRMST excerpt, columns added under release 4.3

Four columns are added to the Member Master this release. Definitions follow in
the standard stanza format. Copybook member MBRMSTC has been updated; recompile
before promotion.

Column:       MBR_ALT_LAST_NAM
Type:         CHAR(25)
Null:         Yes
Notes:        Prior or alternate surname, captured at the counter when a member
              reports a change. The current surname remains in MBR_LAST_NAM.
              Used by the duplicate detection job MBRD500 only. Not displayed on
              the counter screen and not carried in any extract.

Column:       MBR_DOB_VER_CD
Type:         CHAR(1)
Null:         No, default U
Notes:        Indicates whether the date of birth in MBR_DOB_CYMD was verified
              against presented documentation. Values V verified, S self
              reported, U unknown. Required by the immunisation program.

Column:       MBR_GUARD_ID
Type:         CHAR(11)
Null:         Yes
Notes:        Self-referencing to MBR_ID. Populated where the member is a minor.
              Access follows the parent row; no separate restriction applies, but
              the column is excluded from MBRX010 along with MBR_HOH_ID.

Column:       MBR_PRIV_REQ_CD
Type:         CHAR(1)
Null:         No, default N
Notes:        Set to Y when a member submits a written request for restricted
              handling. When Y, the row is suppressed from every extract without
              exception, including MBRX040, and the counter screen displays a
              handling indicator. Only the Records Custodian may reset this
              column; the on-line maintenance program does not expose it.

Retention for all four columns follows the parent row: twenty-four months on line
past MBR_LAST_ACT_DT once inactive, then archive to MBRARC. MBR_ALT_LAST_NAM is
cleared at archive rather than written to tape, on the instruction of the Records
Custodian.

Extract owners are asked to confirm in writing by 30 April that their job control
has been reviewed against MBR_PRIV_REQ_CD. Jobs not confirmed will be held out of
the May schedule.
