MEMBER DATABASE — DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT
Segment: MEMBER MASTER and MEMBER ADDRESS
Application: Loyalty and Third Party Administration
Custodian of record: the Records Custodian, Corporate Pharmacy Services
Distribution: Store Systems, Third Party Billing, Internal Audit

1. SCOPE
    1.1 This excerpt covers the two segments that hold member-identifying content. It does not cover prescription history segments, which are dictionaried separately under RXHIST.
    1.2 Physical implementation is VSAM KSDS on the host, shadowed nightly to the DB2 reporting copy. Column names below are the copybook names in MBRCPY01; the DB2 shadow uses the same names with underscores.

2. MEMBER MASTER SEGMENT
    2.1 Identifier columns
        2.1.1 MBR_ID — CHAR(10), NOT NULL. Cluster key. Generated by the enrollment program; no intelligence is embedded in the value.
        2.1.2 MBR_CARD_NBR — CHAR(16), NOT NULL. Encoded on the plastic. Reissue writes a new value and moves the prior value to MBR_CARD_PRIOR_NBR.
        2.1.3 MBR_CARD_PRIOR_NBR — CHAR(16), NULL ALLOWED. Retained one reissue deep for chargeback research.
        2.1.4 MBR_HH_ID — CHAR(10), NOT NULL. Household roll-up. Multiple members may share a household.
    2.2 Name and demographic columns
        2.2.1 MBR_LAST_NAM — CHAR(25), NOT NULL.
        2.2.2 MBR_FIRST_NAM — CHAR(15), NOT NULL.
        2.2.3 MBR_SUFX_CD — CHAR(3), NULL ALLOWED.
        2.2.4 MBR_DOB — DATE, NULL ALLOWED. Four-position year following remediation. The conversion program rejected any value that could not be resolved against the enrollment date and wrote it to the exception file for manual review at the store.
        2.2.5 MBR_GENDER_CD — CHAR(1), NULL ALLOWED.
        2.2.6 MBR_SSN — CHAR(9), NULL ALLOWED. Carried only where a third-party plan uses it as the subscriber key. Not to be used as a search argument on any new program.
    2.3 Contact columns
        2.3.1 MBR_HOME_PHON — CHAR(10), NULL ALLOWED.
        2.3.2 MBR_WORK_PHON — CHAR(10), NULL ALLOWED.
        2.3.3 MBR_EMAIL_TXT — VARCHAR(60), NULL ALLOWED. Added this release. Collection is optional at the counter and the field is not to be required.

3. MEMBER ADDRESS SEGMENT
    3.1 MBR_ADDR_LINE_1 — CHAR(30), NOT NULL.
    3.2 MBR_ADDR_LINE_2 — CHAR(30), NULL ALLOWED.
    3.3 MBR_CITY_NAM — CHAR(20), NOT NULL.
    3.4 MBR_STATE_CD — CHAR(2), NOT NULL.
    3.5 MBR_ZIP_CD — CHAR(9), NOT NULL. Five positions required; the plus-four is populated by the coding pass and may be blank on newly keyed rows.
    3.6 MBR_ADDR_STAT_CD — CHAR(1), NOT NULL. V = verified, U = unverified, B = returned undeliverable. Rows at B are suppressed from mailings until re-verified at the counter.

4. ACCESS RESTRICTION
    4.1 The segments are secured at the field group level. Group A covers 2.1; Group B covers 2.2 and 2.3; Group C covers Section 3.
    4.2 Store personnel receive Group A and Group C. Group B is granted by written request to the Records Custodian, countersigned by the district manager.
    4.3 MBR_SSN sits outside the group scheme and is individually authorized. The list of authorized identifiers is reviewed each quarter and stale entries are removed without notice to the holder.
    4.4 Ad hoc query tools are pointed at the DB2 shadow, where 2.2.6 and 2.3.1 through 2.3.3 are dropped from the view definition.

5. RETENTION
    5.1 Active rows are retained for the life of the membership.
    5.2 A member inactive for thirty-six consecutive months is moved to the archive cluster and the columns under 2.3 and Section 3 are blanked.
    5.3 Archive is retained seven years from the last dispensing event, after which the row is deleted and the deletion is logged by MBR_ID only.

6. DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
    6.1 MBRMAIL — weekly, to the co-operative mailer. Carries 2.2.1, 2.2.2, 2.2.3 and Section 3. Tape is logged out and logged back in by the Records Custodian.
    6.2 MBRPLAN — daily, to third-party adjudication. Carries 2.1.1, 2.1.2, 2.2.4, 2.2.5 and 2.2.6 where the plan key requires it.
    6.3 MBRANLY — monthly, to Category Management. Carries 2.1.1, 2.1.4, 3.4 and 3.5 truncated to five positions. No column from 2.2 or 2.3 is permitted on this extract, and requests to add one are refused at the Records Custodian without escalation.
