No tax identification number, birth date, telephone number, electronic mail
address or account identifier is written to MKTEXT under any routing code.
The extract program applies the opt-out suppression before write, and the
suppression list is reconciled against MBRMAST each cycle by the Records
Custodian. Routing B columns are dropped from the copy delivered to the
analysis vendor; that vendor receives keys, bands and counts only.

Files transmitted to routing A move by dial transfer to the vendor's host and
are confirmed by control totals returned the same day. Routing B output is
produced to a restricted output queue for branch marketing staff and is not
permitted to leave the building in machine-readable form. Routing C output is
aggregated to district level before printing.
