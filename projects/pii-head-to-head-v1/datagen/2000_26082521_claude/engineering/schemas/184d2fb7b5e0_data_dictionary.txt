DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT
Outbound Interface Layout — EXT_ASSIST_QTR
Form CIS-DD-021 / Customer Care & Billing

INTERFACE HEADER
  Feed name.......... EXT_ASSIST_QTR
  Direction.......... Outbound
  Consumer........... County energy assistance office
  Frequency.......... Quarterly, second business day
  Format............. Fixed-width, 412 bytes, EBCDIC-to-ASCII translated
  Transport.......... SFTP to the county drop, key-based authentication
  Source objects..... CUST_MSTR, BILL_PARTY, ASSIST_ENROLL

ACCESS RESTRICTION
  The generating job runs under the CIS batch account. The staging directory is
  readable by that account and by the Records Custodian only. Operations staff
  may confirm file arrival by record count; they hold no read privilege on
  content. Re-runs require a ticket and are logged in the interface register.

RETENTION
  Staging copies are deleted on transmission acknowledgment. The control
  register entry, which carries counts and hash totals but no field content, is
  retained three years. Source enrollment rows follow schedule LG-4200 and are
  purged six years after program termination; BIRTH_DT and SSN_LAST4 are nulled
  at 24 months past termination, which suppresses those positions on any
  subsequent reissue of a historical file.

NOTES
  Positions 084 and 092 were added at the county's request in the March 2011
  revision and are populated only where the applicant supplied them at intake.
  A blank in either position does not invalidate the record.
