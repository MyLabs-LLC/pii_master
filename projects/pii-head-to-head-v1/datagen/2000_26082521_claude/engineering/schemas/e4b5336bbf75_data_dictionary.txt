NOTE CODES
01  Maintained through the CICS billing screens. No batch update path exists.
02  Overwritten weekly by the address hygiene job BLJ0440. Manual keying is lost.
03  Restricted. Display authority is granted individually, not by department, and is
    logged. The log is reviewed monthly by the Records Custodian.
04  Participates in the duplicate member match. Do not blank to correct an error;
    use the merge transaction so the audit trail is preserved.
05  Dropped at unload time from every extract that leaves the building.
06  Carried on the claim submission extract only, and only for carriers whose
    trading partner agreement is on file in the Business Office.
07  Free text. No edit applied. Not suitable for reporting or selection criteria.
08  Eight position date, century expanded in release 4.0. Any program reading this
    column with a six position work field must be recompiled before conversion.

RETENTION
Active rows remain on line until the member has carried a zero balance for seven
years. The quarterly purge job BLJ0900 writes the selected rows to the archive
series BLARC, verifies the tape, and then deletes. Archive tapes are held for a
further three years in the offsite vault and are destroyed on the schedule kept by
the Records Custodian.

CHANGE CONTROL
Additions and length changes to this segment are logged on the maintenance sheet and
approved by the Business Office Manager before the copybook is promoted. Programs
listed on the cross reference report must be recompiled in the same promotion.
