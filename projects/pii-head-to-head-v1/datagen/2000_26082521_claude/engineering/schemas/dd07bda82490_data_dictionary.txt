DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT — SUBJECT AREA 04: REGISTERED PATRON MASTER
Physical Table: RDRMAST     Database: DB2 for MVS, Subsystem DSN1 (PROD)
Custodial Unit: Office of Revenue Systems / Records Management
Dictionary Version 4.2, superseding Version 4.1     Effective: 15 September 1998

1. Scope. This excerpt documents the columns of RDRMAST carrying identifying attributes of registered farecard holders, together with the physical characteristics, null treatment, and operational handling required of each. It does not document the transaction detail tables (RDRTXN, RDRTXN_ARCH), which are dictionaried separately under Subject Area 07. Where a column is referenced in a COBOL copybook, the copybook member name is given in the operational note for that column.

2. Key and control columns. PTRN_ID_NBR, DECIMAL(9,0), NOT NULL, is the system-assigned surrogate key generated by the sequence routine in module RDR0100; it is the only patron identifier permitted in join predicates written by application programs outside the Revenue Systems group. REG_STS_CDE, CHAR(1), NOT NULL WITH DEFAULT, carries the registration status domain (A active, S suspended, C closed, P pending certification). SRC_SYS_CDE, CHAR(4), NOT NULL, records the originating channel. LST_UPD_TS, TIMESTAMP, NOT NULL, and LST_UPD_USR_ID, CHAR(8), NOT NULL, are maintained by trigger and are not to be set by application logic.

3. Name and identity columns. PTRN_LAST_NAM, VARCHAR(30), NOT NULL; PTRN_FRST_NAM, VARCHAR(20), NOT NULL; PTRN_MDL_INIT, CHAR(1), nullable; PTRN_SFX_CDE, CHAR(4), nullable. SSN_NBR, CHAR(9), nullable, is populated only where the patron participates in the reduced-fare or employer-deduction programs and is retained solely to reconcile duplicate certifications. DOB_DTE, DATE, nullable, was converted from the packed six-digit representation under Y2K remediation project TR-9711 and no longer permits a two-digit century assumption; programs still reading the legacy PIC 9(6) field through copybook RDRMBIRT are to be retired before 30 June 1999.

4. Address and contact columns. RES_ADDR_LN_1 and RES_ADDR_LN_2, VARCHAR(40), the first NOT NULL and the second nullable; RES_CITY_NAM, VARCHAR(25), NOT NULL; RES_ST_CDE, CHAR(2), NOT NULL; RES_ZIP_CDE, CHAR(9), NOT NULL, right-padded where the add-on four is unknown. MAIL_ADDR_LN_1, MAIL_ADDR_LN_2, MAIL_CITY_NAM, MAIL_ST_CDE, and MAIL_ZIP_CDE are all nullable and are populated only where the mailing address differs from residence. TEL_HOME_NBR and TEL_WORK_NBR, CHAR(10), both nullable, are stored unformatted. EMAIL_ADR_TXT, VARCHAR(60), nullable, was added at Version 4.0 and is presently populated for fewer than one in nine active registrations; no notice may be issued exclusively by this channel.

5. Financial columns. FARECARD_SER_NBR, CHAR(16), NOT NULL, UNIQUE index IX_RDRMAST_02. FARECARD_ISS_DTE, DATE, NOT NULL. ACCT_BAL_AMT, DECIMAL(7,2), NOT NULL WITH DEFAULT. AUTOLOAD_BANK_RTN_NBR, CHAR(9), and AUTOLOAD_ACCT_NBR, CHAR(17), both nullable, are present only for patrons enrolled in automatic replenishment; both are stored enciphered under the ICSF key label held by Treasury and are unreadable through ordinary SPUFI or QMF access.

6. Access restriction. Base-table SELECT on RDRMAST is granted to RACF group RDR$SEC1 only. Customer service representatives are granted access through view VW_RDRMAST_SVC, which suppresses SSN_NBR, DOB_DTE, AUTOLOAD_BANK_RTN_NBR, and AUTOLOAD_ACCT_NBR and returns only the terminal four positions of FARECARD_SER_NBR. Marketing and Planning are granted VW_RDRMAST_GEO, which exposes no name or contact column and truncates RES_ZIP_CDE to five positions. Requests from the Transit Police Bureau, from counsel, or under the public records statute are not to be satisfied by direct query; they are logged and routed to the Records Custodian, who alone may authorize an unmasked retrieval and who maintains the disclosure register in accordance with Administrative Directive 12-04.

7. Retention. Rows in closed status are retained for seven years from the effective date in REG_CLS_DTE, after which quarterly purge job RDRP090 removes the row and writes a reduced audit stub (PTRN_ID_NBR, REG_CLS_DTE, ACCT_BAL_AMT) to RDRMAST_STUB. Contact columns are overwritten with low values at the eighteen-month mark following closure by job RDRP085 notwithstanding the longer row retention. Prior-generation microfiche of the registration application file is held by Records Management for the same seven-year term and is destroyed by certificate.

8. Downstream extracts. Nightly job RDRX041 produces the card-embossing file transmitted to the fulfillment vendor over the leased line; it carries name, mailing address, and FARECARD_SER_NBR and carries no financial or identity column. Monthly job RDRX062 produces the ridership analysis extract for Planning and is constrained by DDL to the columns exposed in VW_RDRMAST_GEO. Any new extract, and any change to the column list of an existing extract, requires the countersignature of the Records Custodian on Form RM-27 before the JCL member is promoted to production.
