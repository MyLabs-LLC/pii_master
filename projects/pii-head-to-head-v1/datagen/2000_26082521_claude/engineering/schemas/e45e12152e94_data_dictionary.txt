III. ACCESS RESTRICTION
   Elements marked Protected are readable only under the role CHD_PHI_READ. Bulk
   selection exceeding one thousand (1,000) rows requires prior written authorization
   of the Privacy Officer and is logged irrespective of outcome.

IV. RETENTION
   Registry rows are retained for the life of the member plus three (3) years, in
   accordance with the applicable State retention schedule. Disposition is suspended
   during any litigation hold and resumes only upon written release.

   EXT_SCHOOL_COMPLIANCE — status element and derived age band only.
   EXT_EPI_QUARTERLY — de-identified; all Protected elements suppressed.
