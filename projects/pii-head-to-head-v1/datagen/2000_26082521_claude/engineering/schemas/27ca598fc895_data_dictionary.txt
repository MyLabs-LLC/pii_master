NOTE 1 — ACCESS. Columns designated Restricted above are readable only by personnel
holding a current need-to-know determination issued by the Records Custodian, and
only through the roles enumerated in the access matrix appended to this document at
Schedule A. Direct query privileges against the base table are withheld from all
personnel other than the assigned database administrator; all other access shall be
effected through the secured views prefixed VW_RIDER_.

NOTE 2 — RETENTION. The row and all columns comprising it shall be retained for seven
(7) years measured from the value carried in ACCT_CLOSE_DT, after which the row shall
be purged by scheduled job PURGE_RIDER_MASTER in accordance with the Authority
Records Retention Schedule, item GS-4.2. Where ACCT_CLOSE_DT remains null the
retention period shall not be deemed to have commenced.

NOTE 3 — DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS. Restricted columns are suppressed from the monthly
ridership extract EXT-RIDERSHIP-MTHLY and from all extracts furnished to the
Planning Department. They are released, in the narrowest form sufficient for the
purpose, to the card personalisation extract EXT-CARDFUL-WKLY, which is transmitted
to the fulfilment contractor under the confidentiality provisions of the governing
contract. No further onward disclosure is authorised.
