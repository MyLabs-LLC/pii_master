SECTION 4. ACCESS RESTRICTION
  4.1  Base table access is denied to all interactive users. Access is granted
       through view V_MEMBER_MASTER_RPT, which omits Category 4 columns.
  4.2  Requests for name or address columns require a completed Form IS-27
       countersigned by the Privacy Office. Standing approvals expire annually.
  4.3  Developer access in non-production is permitted only against de-identified
       copies produced by job SCRUB_MBR_NIGHTLY.

SECTION 5. RETENTION
  5.1  Financial records: 7 years from ACCT_STATUS_CD transition to closed.
  5.2  Minors: retained to age of majority plus 7 years, per State schedule.
  5.3  Purge job PURGE_MBR_Q runs quarterly; suppressed for accounts flagged
       LEGAL_HOLD_IND.
