SECTION II — SCOPE AND CONSTRUCTION
  2.1  This excerpt enumerates only those columns of the table of record which have been
       designated Tier II (Restricted) or Tier III (Protected) under the Department's Data
       Classification Directive. Columns designated Tier I (Internal) are omitted and shall
       be obtained from the full dictionary maintained by the Informatics Unit.
  2.2  Nothing in this excerpt shall be construed to authorize access to, or disclosure of,
       any data element enumerated herein. Authorization is governed exclusively by Section IV.
  2.3  Where a conflict arises between this excerpt and the physical schema as deployed, the
       physical schema shall govern and the discrepancy shall be reported to the Data Steward
       within five (5) business days of discovery.

SECTION IV — ACCESS RESTRICTION
  4.1  Tier III columns are readable solely through the role CHD_PHI_READ, membership in which
       requires a completed Confidentiality Undertaking on file with the Records Custodian and
       annual re-attestation thereafter.
  4.2  Direct query access to the table of record is prohibited for all analytic personnel;
       such personnel shall be provisioned against the governed view V_MEMBER_ANALYTIC.
  4.3  All access, whether successful or denied, is logged and reviewed quarterly.

SECTION V — RETENTION AND DISPOSITION
  5.1  Records are retained seven (7) years following the value of ELIG_END_DT, or, where that
       column is null, seven (7) years following the last date of service.
  5.2  Upon expiry, Tier III columns are overwritten in place; the surrogate key and enrollment
       dates are retained indefinitely for statistical continuity.
  5.3  Litigation holds suspend all disposition until released in writing.

SECTION VI — DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
  6.1  EXT_STATE_IMM_WEEKLY — transmits name, date-of-birth and postal columns under the
       standing data-sharing agreement; requires CONSENT_FLG = 'Y'.
  6.2  EXT_OUTREACH_MONTHLY — transmits contact columns only; opt-outs excluded at source.
  6.3  EXT_EPI_QUARTERLY — de-identified; all Tier III columns suppressed or generalized.
