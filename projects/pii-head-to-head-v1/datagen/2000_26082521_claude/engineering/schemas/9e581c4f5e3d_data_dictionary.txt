4.1 Construction and Authority. The columns enumerated in this excerpt are governed by the Department's Information Access Policy as amended, by the Standards for Privacy of Individually Identifiable Health Information codified at 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164, and by the applicable County Records Retention Schedule as certified by the Board of Supervisors. Nothing in this excerpt shall be construed to confer upon any user, division, or contracted party a right of access broader than that conferred by an approved Access Request Form countersigned by the Records Custodian and the Divisional Privacy Coordinator. Where a conflict arises between the operational notes recorded herein and the aforementioned authorities, the aforementioned authorities shall govern, and the Data Administrator shall cause this excerpt to be amended within thirty (30) calendar days of receiving written notice of such conflict.

4.2 Primary Identity Columns. The column CLNT_ID is defined as NUMBER(10), NOT NULL, and is populated exclusively by sequence CLNT_ID_SEQ; it constitutes the sole permissible join key for all reporting and interface purposes and is not derived from, and shall not be reconstructed from, any other column in this table. The columns CLNT_LAST_NAM, VARCHAR2(30), NOT NULL; CLNT_FRST_NAM, VARCHAR2(20), NOT NULL; CLNT_MID_INIT, VARCHAR2(1), NULL permitted; and CLNT_SFX_CD, VARCHAR2(4), NULL permitted, are together designated Restricted Identity Elements. The columns CLNT_SSN_NBR, VARCHAR2(9), NULL permitted, and CLNT_BRTH_DT, DATE, NOT NULL, are designated Highly Restricted and are stored in the encrypted extent CLIN_SEC01. Query access to the two Highly Restricted columns is granted by role CHD_IDENT_R only, which role the Records Custodian shall review not less frequently than semiannually, and every such query is written to audit table AUD_IDENT_ACC with the invoking database account, the timestamp, and the statement hash.

4.3 Locator Columns. The columns CLNT_ADDR_LN_1, VARCHAR2(40), NOT NULL; CLNT_ADDR_LN_2, VARCHAR2(40), NULL permitted; CLNT_CITY_NAM, VARCHAR2(28), NOT NULL; CLNT_ST_CD, CHAR(2), NOT NULL; CLNT_ZIP_CD, VARCHAR2(9), NOT NULL; CLNT_HOME_PHN_NBR, VARCHAR2(10), NULL permitted; and CLNT_EMAIL_TXT, VARCHAR2(60), NULL permitted, are maintained solely for service delivery, appointment notification, and mandated follow-up. The column CLNT_EMAIL_TXT was added under Change Order 2003-114 and remains sparsely populated; no program shall treat it as a reliable contact channel absent written approval of the Division Director. Locator columns are updated by the intake application only; direct data manipulation language issued against them outside the application is prohibited and, where detected by the quarterly audit extract, shall be reported to the Records Custodian.

4.4 Retention. Client rows and all columns enumerated above shall be retained for the period prescribed by the retention schedule for clinical service records, being not less than seven (7) years from the date of last service as recorded in CLNT_LAST_SVC_DT, DATE, NULL permitted, and in the case of a client who received services as a minor, not less than seven (7) years from attainment of majority. Upon expiration, the purge utility PURGE_CLNT.PRC shall null the Restricted and Highly Restricted columns in place and shall preserve CLNT_ID, CLNT_ZIP_CD truncated to five positions, and the aggregate service counters for statistical continuity.

4.5 Downstream Extracts. Three standing extracts draw upon this table. The monthly State Health Department transmittal (job STXTR_MO) carries CLNT_ID, CLNT_BRTH_DT, CLNT_ZIP_CD, and program participation flags, and is transmitted by secure file transfer under the executed data use agreement. The immunization registry interface (job IZREG_NGHT) carries the Restricted Identity Elements and CLNT_BRTH_DT as required for probabilistic matching. The Board reporting extract (job BRD_QTR) shall carry no column designated Restricted or Highly Restricted, and the Data Administrator shall verify this condition prior to each quarterly release.
