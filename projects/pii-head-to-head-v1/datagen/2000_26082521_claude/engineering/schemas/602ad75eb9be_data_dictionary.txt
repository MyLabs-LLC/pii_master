SECTION B — SCOPE
B.1  This excerpt covers the base member table only. Address history (CUST_ADDR_HIST), consignment linkage (CONS_MBR_XREF) and card scheme tables are dictionaried separately.
B.2  Columns marked "R" at Section D are restricted and must not be surfaced in any report, screen or file outside the roles listed.

SECTION C — COLUMN REGISTER

COLUMN NAME          | TYPE          | NULL | OPERATIONAL NOTE
---------------------+---------------+------+-----------------------------------
MBR_ID               | NUMBER(12)    | N    | Surrogate key, sequence MBR_SEQ. Not reused on deletion.

DT_OF_BIRTH          | DATE          | Y    | R. Captured for age-restricted goods handling only.
NAT_INS_NO           | CHAR(9)       | Y    | R. Legacy column, populated only for accounts migrated from the 1998 carrier system. Do not populate on new records.
ADDR_LINE_1          | VARCHAR2(60)  | N    | R.
ADDR_LINE_2          | VARCHAR2(60)  | Y    | R.
ADDR_LINE_3          | VARCHAR2(60)  | Y    | R.

POSTCODE_OUT         | VARCHAR2(4)   | Y    | Derived nightly. Used for route density reporting.
HOME_TEL_NO          | VARCHAR2(20)  | Y    | R.
MOBILE_TEL_NO        | VARCHAR2(20)  | Y    | R. Source for SMS delivery notification.
EMAIL_ADDR_1         | VARCHAR2(120) | Y    | R.
DELIV_INSTR_TXT      | VARCHAR2(255) | Y    | R. Free text. Known to contain names of neighbours and key-safe details; treat as restricted in full.

CONSENT_CAPTURE_DT   | DATE          | Y    | Must be present where MKTG_OPT_IN_FLG = 'Y'.
LAST_AMEND_DT        | DATE          | N    | System-maintained.
LAST_AMEND_USER_ID   | VARCHAR2(12)  | N    | Operator identifier, not the member.

SECTION D — ACCESS RESTRICTION
D.1  Restricted columns ("R") are exposed only through view V_MBR_CSR, which is granted to database roles RL_CSR_FULL and RL_DEPOT_COUNTER.
D.2  Role RL_MI_ANALYST is granted V_MBR_ANON only. That view returns MBR_ID, MBR_STATUS_CD, POSTCODE_OUT and consent flags; all other restricted columns are excluded at view definition, not by masking.
D.3  Direct SELECT on the base table is held by the DBA group and by the Records Custodian. Grants are reviewed quarterly and evidenced on Form AC-11.
D.4  Ad-hoc query tools must connect via the reporting service account, which has no grant on the base table.

SECTION E — RETENTION AND DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
E.1  Records at MBR_STATUS_CD = 'CL' are purged 24 months after closure by job PRG_MBR_M24. NAT_INS_NO is nulled at 6 months regardless of status.
E.2  Nightly extract EXT_MBR_DLV (fixed-width, to the depot handhelds) carries SURNAME, ADDR_LINE_1 to 3, POSTCODE and DELIV_INSTR_TXT. Handheld copies are wiped at shift end.
E.3  Extract EXT_MBR_MI (weekly, to the Cognos warehouse) is sourced from V_MBR_ANON and carries no restricted column.
E.4  No extract is permitted to third-party carriers without written instruction from the Divisional Compliance Officer.
