>> SECTION III — ACCESS RESTRICTION
1. Schema PFS_STAGE is not published to the reporting catalog.
2. SELECT is held by interface service accounts and by two analysts in
   Interface Operations. No end-user grants exist or will be issued.
3. Error queue review is performed at the console; extracts of held rows
   require Privacy Office approval on Form IS-27.
4. Trace-level interface logging must not be enabled in production. Where
   enabled under an incident ticket, logs are deleted at ticket closure.

>> SECTION IV — RETENTION
1. Table rows: cleared daily as above.
2. Interface engine message archive: 30 days on disk, 90 days on tape.
3. Rejected message store: 180 days, then destroyed. Extension requires
   written direction from the Records Custodian.

>> SECTION V — DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
None permitted directly from this schema. Promoted rows land in
PFS_CORE.MEMBER_MASTER and are governed by Form PFS-DD-04. Any request to
read staging content for reconciliation is logged as an exception and
reported in the quarterly access review.
