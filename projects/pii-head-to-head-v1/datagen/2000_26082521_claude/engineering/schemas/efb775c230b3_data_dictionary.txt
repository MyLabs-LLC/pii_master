MEMBER MASTER FILE — DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT
Core Processing System / Member Services Division
Prepared by the Records Custodian, Information Systems

The MEMBER MASTER table (physical file MBRMST on the production library) carries one row per member relationship and is the authoritative source for all downstream extracts. The primary key is MBR_KEY, a nine-digit packed decimal assigned sequentially by the account origination program; it is NOT NULL, has no business meaning, and is never reused following relationship closure. The secondary key MBR_LEGACY_NBR (CHAR 10, nullable) was carried forward from the pre-conversion system and remains populated only for relationships opened before the 1994 conversion. Operations staff are reminded that MBR_LEGACY_NBR is not unique across the branch network and must not be used as a join key.

Name and identity fields occupy the first segment of the row. MBR_LAST_NAME (CHAR 30, NOT NULL), MBR_FIRST_NAME (CHAR 20, NOT NULL), MBR_MIDDLE_INIT (CHAR 1, nullable) and MBR_NAME_SUFFIX (CHAR 4, nullable) are keyed at the teller platform and are the only fields permitted to feed the statement rendering job. MBR_SSN (CHAR 9, NOT NULL for primary owners, nullable for authorized signers) is subject to restricted access: read privileges are granted only to the Compliance Officer, the Records Custodian, and the batch identity used by the annual tax reporting job. The field is masked to the last four positions on all inquiry screens except CIF07 and CIF09. MBR_DOB (DATE, NOT NULL) is used solely for age-based product eligibility and share certificate beneficiary logic; it is excluded from the marketing extract by an explicit column list in the extract program rather than by view definition, and any change to that program requires sign-off from the Records Custodian.

Contact fields comprise MBR_ADDR_LINE_1 and MBR_ADDR_LINE_2 (CHAR 40, first NOT NULL), MBR_CITY (CHAR 25, NOT NULL), MBR_STATE (CHAR 2, NOT NULL), MBR_ZIP (CHAR 9, NOT NULL, stored unformatted), MBR_PHONE_HOME and MBR_PHONE_WORK (CHAR 10, both nullable), and MBR_EMAIL_ADDR (VARCHAR 60, nullable, added in the 1998 maintenance release and populated for a small minority of the file). MBR_ADDR_BAD_FLAG (CHAR 1) is set by the returned-mail clerk and suppresses inclusion in all bulk mail extracts. MBR_DO_NOT_SOLICIT (CHAR 1, NOT NULL, default 'N') must be honored by every outbound extract without exception.

Relationship and status fields include MBR_OPEN_DATE (DATE, NOT NULL), MBR_CLOSE_DATE (DATE, nullable), MBR_BRANCH_CD (CHAR 3, NOT NULL), MBR_STATUS_CD (CHAR 1, NOT NULL, values A/C/D/R), and MBR_LAST_MAINT_TS (TIMESTAMP, NOT NULL, set by trigger). MBR_DECEASED_FLAG (CHAR 1) suppresses solicitation and routes the relationship to the estate queue.

Retention: closed relationships remain in MBRMST for seven years from MBR_CLOSE_DATE, after which the purge utility moves the row to the archive tape series and blanks MBR_SSN, MBR_DOB and all contact columns in any retained summary row. Downstream extracts are limited to three: the nightly core-to-datamart feed (excludes MBR_SSN), the quarterly regulatory extract (includes MBR_SSN under encryption in transit), and the marketing selection file (excludes MBR_SSN, MBR_DOB and MBR_EMAIL_ADDR). No ad hoc query tool is authorized against MBRMST directly; all reporting access is through the MBRMST_RPT view, which omits the restricted columns entirely.
