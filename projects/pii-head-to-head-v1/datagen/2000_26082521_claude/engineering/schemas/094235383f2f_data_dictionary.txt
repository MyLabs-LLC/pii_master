DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT AND CONTROL INSTRUMENT
FORM RM-207, SCHEDULE B ATTACHMENT
OBJECT: CIS_PROD.MBR_ENROLL — Customer Assistance Program Enrollment
CUSTODIAN OF RECORD: Office of the Records Custodian, Department of Municipal Utilities
EFFECTIVE: Second quarter, calendar year 2016

ARTICLE I — PURPOSE AND CONSTRUCTION

1.01 This instrument enumerates the columns of the above-named object and prescribes, as to each, the datatype, the nullability, and the operational conditions attaching thereto.

1.02 Where a provision of this instrument conflicts with the general provisions of the master dictionary, the more restrictive provision shall govern.

1.03 The terms "restricted attribute" and "controlled attribute" bear the meanings assigned to them in the Department's information classification standard.

ARTICLE II — COLUMN REGISTER

2.01 MBR_ENROLL_ID. Datatype NUMBER(12). Nullability: NOT NULL. Primary key; assigned by sequence and not reused upon deletion.

2.02 CUST_ID. Datatype NUMBER(12). Nullability: NOT NULL. Foreign key to CIS_PROD.CUST_MASTER; enforced by constraint, cascade prohibited.

2.03 MBR_FIRST_NAME and MBR_LAST_NAME. Datatype VARCHAR2(60), each. Nullability: NOT NULL. Restricted attributes. Values are transformed to a surrogate token in every non-production environment, without exception and without waiver.

2.04 MBR_DOB. Datatype DATE. Nullability: NULL permitted where eligibility is established by household composition rather than by age. Restricted attribute.

2.05 MBR_TAX_ID_NUM. Datatype VARCHAR2(11). Nullability: NULL permitted. Controlled attribute of the highest sensitivity; stored under application-layer encryption; the plaintext column is not exposed by any view.

2.06 MBR_MAIL_ADDR_LINE_1, MBR_MAIL_ADDR_LINE_2, MBR_MAIL_POSTAL_CD. Datatypes VARCHAR2(80), VARCHAR2(80), CHAR(9). Nullability: NOT NULL as to the first and third; NULL permitted as to the second.

2.07 MBR_CONTACT_PHONE. Datatype CHAR(10). Nullability: NULL permitted. Populated only where the applicant has elected telephone notice.

2.08 HH_SIZE_CNT and HH_INCOME_BAND_CD. Datatypes NUMBER(2) and CHAR(3). Nullability: NOT NULL. The band code, and not any stated figure, shall be the operative eligibility value.

2.09 ENROLL_EFF_DT and ENROLL_EXP_DT. Datatype DATE. Nullability: NOT NULL as to the former; NULL permitted as to the latter pending termination.

2.10 VERIF_DOC_IMG_REF. Datatype VARCHAR2(64). Nullability: NULL permitted. Pointer to the imaging repository; the image itself is not stored in this object.

ARTICLE III — ACCESS RESTRICTION

3.01 Access is granted by role and not by individual. The roles so authorized are ASSIST_INTAKE_CLERK, ASSIST_PROGRAM_ADMIN, and CIS_AUDIT_READ.

3.02 The columns identified at 2.03, 2.04, and 2.05 require an additional column-level grant, requested in writing and approved by the Records Custodian.

3.03 All read operations against 2.05 are written to the fine-grained audit trail, which is reviewed monthly by the Information Security Officer.

ARTICLE IV — RETENTION AND DISPOSITION

4.01 Enrollment rows shall be retained three (3) years following the date recorded in ENROLL_EXP_DT.

4.02 Upon expiration of the retention period, the columns identified at 2.04 and 2.05 shall be overwritten irreversibly, the remaining columns being retained in de-identified form for program evaluation.

ARTICLE V — DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS

5.01 A monthly extract is furnished to the State weatherization office. Permitted columns: CUST_ID, HH_SIZE_CNT, HH_INCOME_BAND_CD, ENROLL_EFF_DT, MBR_MAIL_POSTAL_CD. All other columns are withheld.

5.02 A quarterly extract is furnished to the Council's finance staff in aggregate form only; no row-level column is transmitted.

5.03 No extract of this object shall be delivered by unencrypted transport, and none shall be written to removable media.
