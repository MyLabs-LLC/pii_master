SECTION C — ACCESS RESTRICTION
C.1  Table-level SELECT is granted to roles SIS_REGISTRAR, SIS_COUNSEL and
     SIS_REPORT only. Individual grants are prohibited.
C.2  Name columns are exposed to SIS_REPORT solely through view
     V_STU_ROSTER, which masks all columns listed under clause C.3.
C.3  SSN_NBR, PHONE_HOME, PHONE_EMERG and all ADDR_ columns are withheld
     from every reporting role. Ad hoc query tools have no grant.
C.4  CD_MEAL_ELIG may not be disclosed outside Nutrition Services except in
     aggregate counts of ten or more.
C.5  Any grant change requires written approval of the Records Custodian
     and is logged on Form MIS-AC-22.

SECTION D — RETENTION
D.1  Active rows are retained for the duration of enrollment.
D.2  Withdrawn rows move to STU_MASTER_HIST at fiscal year close.
D.3  Transcript-bearing attributes are retained permanently.
D.4  SSN_NBR is purged from history rows three years after withdrawal.
D.5  Contact columns are purged one year after withdrawal.
D.6  Nightly tape cycles are held six weeks; year-end tapes, seven years.

SECTION E — DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
E.1  State enrollment file, fixed-width, monthly, 14 columns, no contact data.
E.2  Nutrition Services interface, weekly, keyed on STU_ID_LOCAL.
E.3  Transportation routing file, quarterly, ADDR_ZIP only.
E.4  Library circulation load, term start, roster columns only.
E.5  Each extract is registered on Form MIS-EX-07 and expires annually.
