OFFICE OF THE UNIVERSITY REGISTRAR
DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT — MEMBER DIRECTORY VIEW
Form RMU-7 | Review cycle: annual | Current review closed 2016-09-30

1.0  OBJECT
     1.1  View name ......... STUDENT.V_MEMBER_DIRECTORY
     1.2  Base tables ....... MEMBER_MASTER, MEMBER_ENROLLMENT, MEMBER_ADDRESS
     1.3  Refresh ........... Materialized, rebuilt nightly at 02:15
     1.4  Steward ........... Associate Registrar for Records
     1.5  Custodian ......... the Records Custodian

2.0  COLUMN LIST

     COLUMN                 TYPE           NULL   NOTE
     ---------------------  -------------  -----  --------------------------
     MEMBER_KEY             NUMBER(10)     NO     Join key only.
     DISPLAY_NAME           VARCHAR2(120)  NO     Concatenation of chosen or
                                                  legal first name and legal
                                                  last name.
     CAMPUS_EMAIL           VARCHAR2(128)  NO     Directory item.
     PRIMARY_MAJOR_CODE     VARCHAR2(4)    YES    Directory item.
     CLASS_STANDING_CODE    CHAR(2)        YES    Directory item.
     ENROLL_STATUS_CODE     CHAR(1)        NO     Full/part-time flag.
     DEGREE_AWARDED_CODE    VARCHAR2(6)    YES    Populated at conferral.
     CONFERRAL_TERM         VARCHAR2(6)    YES    Directory item.
     CAMPUS_LOCATION_CODE   VARCHAR2(4)    YES    Main, regional, or online.

3.0  COLUMNS DELIBERATELY EXCLUDED

     The following base-table columns are not carried into the view and must not
     be added without a signed change request: SSN_ENCRYPTED, DATE_OF_BIRTH,
     PERM_ADDR_LINE1 through PERM_ZIP, MAIL_ADDR_LINE1 through MAIL_ZIP,
     PRIMARY_PHONE, ALT_PHONE, PERSONAL_EMAIL, RESIDENCY_DETERMINATION_CODE,
     FINANCIAL_HOLD_IND.

4.0  ACCESS RESTRICTION

     4.1  The view is readable by the campus directory application service
          account and by the registrar staff role.
     4.2  Rows where FERPA_BLOCK_IND = 'Y' are filtered during the nightly
          rebuild. The filter is applied in the materialization, so a stale
          rebuild cannot expose a newly suppressed row; the rebuild is monitored
          and a failure raises a page to the on-call analyst.
     4.3  Suppression requests received after the nightly window are applied by
          manual delete from the materialized view, and the change is recorded
          on the daily exception sheet.

5.0  RETENTION

     5.1  The view holds currently enrolled members plus members conferred
          within the prior two terms.
     5.2  No independent retention obligation attaches to the view; it is
          rebuilt from the base tables and carries no data of record.

6.0  DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS

     6.1  Campus directory web application — live query, no copy retained.
     6.2  Commencement program file — DISPLAY_NAME, DEGREE_AWARDED_CODE,
          PRIMARY_MAJOR_CODE. Produced once per conferral term and released to
          the printing vendor after review by the Records Custodian.
     6.3  Student government election roster — MEMBER_KEY, CAMPUS_EMAIL,
          CLASS_STANDING_CODE. Released once per academic year under a signed
          data use agreement.
     6.4  No extract from this view is permitted to third parties for
          solicitation purposes.

7.0  CHANGE CONTROL
     Additions to Section 2.0 require the signature of the Associate Registrar
     for Records and a security review by Information Security. Removals may be
     made by the custodian and reported at the next review.
