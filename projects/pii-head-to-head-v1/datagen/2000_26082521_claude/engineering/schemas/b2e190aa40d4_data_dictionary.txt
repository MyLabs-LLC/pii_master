The Environmental Health client registry table EH_CLIENT_REG is defined for dictionary purposes as follows, and program staff are directed to observe the conditions stated. The columns EH_CLIENT_ID_SK (NUMBER(10), NOT NULL), COMPLAINANT_NAME_TXT (VARCHAR2(80), NULL), COMPLAINANT_PHONE_NBR (VARCHAR2(15), NULL), COMPLAINANT_EMAIL_ADDR_TXT (VARCHAR2(120), NULL), SITE_ADDR_LINE1_TXT (VARCHAR2(80), NOT NULL), SITE_PARCEL_NBR_TXT (VARCHAR2(20), NULL), and ANONYMITY_REQUESTED_IND (CHAR(1), NOT NULL) constitute the restricted portion of the registry. Where ANONYMITY_REQUESTED_IND carries the affirmative value, the three complainant columns shall be treated as exempt from disclosure under the public records act to the fullest extent the act permits, and the Records Custodian shall so certify upon any request for inspection; the site columns remain disclosable and are routinely disclosed in the inspection docket. Retention follows the inspection series: seven years from CASE_CLOSE_DT for routine matters and permanently for matters resulting in an abatement order, in which event the complainant columns are nonetheless nullified at the seven-year mark by the annual disposition run. The only standing extract from this table is the weekly file supplied to the County Geographic Information Systems Office, which draws SITE_ADDR_LINE1_TXT, SITE_PARCEL_NBR_TXT, and CASE_STATUS_CD, and which draws no complainant column under any circumstance.
