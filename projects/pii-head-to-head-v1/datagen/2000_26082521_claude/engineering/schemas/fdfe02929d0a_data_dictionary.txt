DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT — MEMBER MASTER
Enterprise Data Management / Pharmacy Retail Operations
Document Reference: EDM-DD-LMP-0114 | Revision 6 | Effective 2021-04-19

SECTION A — OBJECT IDENTIFICATION
  System of Record ............ Loyalty & Member Platform (LMP)
  Physical Schema ............. LMP_CORE
  Table ....................... MBR_MASTER
  Platform .................... Oracle 19c, Enterprise Edition, TDE tablespace
  Grain ....................... One row per enrolled member, current state
  Load Cadence ................ CDC micro-batch, 15-minute interval
  Data Classification ......... Restricted — Tier 2 (PII; PHI-adjacent when RX_LINK_IND is set)
  Business Steward ............ the Member Data Steward, Enterprise Data Management
  Records Custodian ........... the Records Custodian, Information Governance
  Privacy Reviewer ............ the Privacy Office designee

SECTION B — COLUMN INVENTORY

  COLUMN NAME          TYPE             NULL   OPERATIONAL NOTES
  MBR_SK               NUMBER(18)       NO     Surrogate key; sequence-generated; never reused.
  MBR_NBR_TKN          CHAR(32)         NO     Format-preserving token for the member number. Detokenization requires vault role LMP_DETOK.
  FIRST_NM             VARCHAR2(60)     YES    Restricted. Masked in all non-production clones.
  LAST_NM              VARCHAR2(80)     NO     Restricted. Masked in all non-production clones.
  MID_INIT             CHAR(1)          YES    Sourced from POS enrollment only.
  NM_SFX_CD            VARCHAR2(10)     YES    Domain-validated against REF_NAME_SUFFIX.
  DOB_DT               DATE             YES    Restricted. Column-level grant only; excluded from all marketing extracts. Age band derived downstream as AGE_BAND_CD.
  GNDR_CD              CHAR(1)          YES    Self-reported at enrollment; not clinically authoritative.
  EMAIL_ADDR_TXT       VARCHAR2(254)    YES    Restricted. Hashed copy maintained in EMAIL_ADDR_HASH for match/merge.
  MOBILE_PHONE_NBR     VARCHAR2(20)     YES    Restricted. E.164 normalized on load. SMS eligibility governed by MKTG_OPT_IN_IND.
  ADDR_LINE_1_TXT      VARCHAR2(100)    YES    Restricted. Current mailing address; history retained in MBR_ADDR_HIST.
  ADDR_LINE_2_TXT      VARCHAR2(100)    YES    Restricted.
  POSTAL_CD            VARCHAR2(10)     YES    First five positions only are exposed to analytics views.
  GOVT_ID_TKN          CHAR(32)         YES    Populated solely for controlled-substance purchase logging. Vault-tokenized at ingest; cleartext never persisted.
  PREF_LANG_CD         CHAR(3)          YES    Drives label and counseling-material language selection.
  ENROLL_DT            DATE             NO     Anchors retention clock.
  MBR_STAT_CD          CHAR(1)          NO     A=active, I=inactive, C=closed, S=suspended.
  MKTG_OPT_IN_IND      CHAR(1)          NO     Default N. Consent evidence linked via CNSNT_EVENT_ID.
  HIPAA_AUTH_IND       CHAR(1)          NO     Gates any join to pharmacy fulfillment tables.
  RX_LINK_IND          CHAR(1)          NO     Y indicates the member record is linked to a dispensing profile; elevates row to PHI handling.
  SRC_SYS_CD           VARCHAR2(8)      NO     POS, WEB, MOBL, CALL, or MIGR.
  ETL_LOAD_TS          TIMESTAMP(6)     NO     Populated by the loader; not business-meaningful.
  ETL_UPD_TS           TIMESTAMP(6)     NO     Drives incremental extraction watermarks.
  REC_DEL_IND          CHAR(1)          NO     Soft-delete marker; purge job honors this flag.

SECTION C — ACCESS RESTRICTION
  Base table grants are prohibited outside role LMP_CORE_OWNER. Analyst access is provisioned exclusively through view V_MBR_MASTER_MSK, which redacts FIRST_NM, LAST_NM, DOB_DT, EMAIL_ADDR_TXT, MOBILE_PHONE_NBR, ADDR_LINE_1_TXT, ADDR_LINE_2_TXT and GOVT_ID_TKN. Requests for unmasked access require Privacy Office approval and are time-boxed to 30 days. All SELECT activity against restricted columns is captured by Unified Audit policy AUD_LMP_PII.

SECTION D — RETENTION
  Active members: retained for the life of the enrollment. Closed enrollments: 7 years from MBR_STAT_CD transition to C, then purged by job PRG_MBR_CLOSED. GOVT_ID_TKN is purged at 2 years regardless of member status. Rows with RX_LINK_IND = Y are held to the pharmacy record schedule and are excluded from the standard purge.

SECTION E — DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
  EXT_MBR_MKTG_DAILY — masked view source; excludes DOB_DT and GOVT_ID_TKN; suppression applied on MKTG_OPT_IN_IND = N.
  EXT_MBR_CDP_HOURLY — hashed identifiers only; no cleartext direct identifiers permitted.
  EXT_MBR_FIN_RECON_MONTHLY — MBR_SK and MBR_NBR_TKN only.
  No extract may be written to a landing zone outside the governed transfer path without a signed data movement request.

SECTION F — CHANGE CONTROL
  Column additions require schema change ticket and Privacy Office impact assessment. This excerpt is reviewed semi-annually by the Records Custodian.
