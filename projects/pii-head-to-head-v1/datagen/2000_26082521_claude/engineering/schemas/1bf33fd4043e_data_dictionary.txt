Enterprise Data Management / Data Governance Office
Issue 3.2 — effective 01 MAR 2016. Supersedes Issue 3.1 (14 SEP 2015).

1. Scope. This section governs RTL_CUST.MBR_MASTER on EDW-PRD-01 (Oracle 11.2.0.4), tablespace CUST_DAT01. Grain: one row per enrolled loyalty member per banner. Row count at issue: approx. 41.6M.

3. Nullability. Columns marked N are enforced at DDL. Columns marked Y are not defaulted; downstream consumers must handle NULL explicitly. Empty string is not a permitted substitute for NULL and is rejected by CHK_MBR_NOEMPTY.

4. Load. Source: POS enrolment feed (hourly), preference centre feed (daily 02:15), address hygiene return file (weekly, Sun 04:00). Informatica workflow wf_mbr_master_ld. Late-arriving rows are held in MBR_MASTER_STG for a maximum of 72 hours before rejection to MBR_MASTER_ERR.

5. Access restriction. R1 (unrestricted internal) applies to all columns not otherwise marked. R2 requires role RL_CUST_PII, granted only on approved AR-form and reviewed quarterly by the Records Custodian. R3 requires role RL_CUST_PII_SENS and additional written approval from the Privacy Officer; grants are time-boxed to 90 days and expire without renewal. Direct SELECT by named individual accounts is prohibited; all access is by role. R2/R3 access is logged to AUD_CUST_ACCESS and retained 24 months.

6. Retention. PURGE_ELIG_DT is set to LAST_TXN_DT + 84 months, or ENROLL_DT + 84 months where LAST_TXN_DT is NULL. The purge job pg_mbr_master runs monthly and nullifies all R2/R3 columns in place, retaining MBR_SK, MBR_NBR and aggregate counters for continuity of historical reporting. Rows are not deleted. Where HIPAA_AUTH_FLG = Y, purge is deferred to the pharmacy record retention schedule and must not be executed from this job.

7. Downstream extracts. Six standing extracts consume this table: EXT-104 (marketing campaign selection, R1 plus hashed contact keys only), EXT-107 (statement print vendor, R2, SFTP, PGP-encrypted, 30-day vendor retention), EXT-112 (clinical outreach, R2/R3, restricted to HIPAA_AUTH_FLG = Y), EXT-118 (Teradata analytic mart, R1 only), EXT-121 (fraud analytics, tokens only), EXT-133 (state reporting). All extracts apply the OPT_OUT_SHARE_FLG filter at source. New extracts require Data Governance Office registration prior to scheduling; unregistered extracts are disabled on detection.
