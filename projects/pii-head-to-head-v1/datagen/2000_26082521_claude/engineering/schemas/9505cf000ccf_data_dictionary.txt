DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT — PROD_ESCROW.TBL_PARTY_CONTACT
Issued by the Data Governance Office. Applies to production and to the reporting replica.

1. Purpose. TBL_PARTY_CONTACT holds repeating contact points for a party. One row per contact point. The table is child to TBL_PARTY_MASTER by PARTY_ID.

2. Column register.

  COLUMN                  TYPE            NULL   NOTE
  CONTACT_ID              NUMBER(12)      NO     Surrogate key.
  PARTY_ID                NUMBER(12)      NO     FK to TBL_PARTY_MASTER.
  CONTACT_TYPE_CD         CHAR(3)         NO     HPH, MPH, WPH, EML, FAX, MAL.
  CONTACT_VALUE           VARCHAR2(160)   NO     Format governed by CONTACT_TYPE_CD.
  MAIL_ADDR_LINE_1        VARCHAR2(80)    YES    Populated for MAL rows only.
  MAIL_ADDR_LINE_2        VARCHAR2(80)    YES
  MAIL_CITY               VARCHAR2(40)    YES
  MAIL_STATE_CD           CHAR(2)         YES
  MAIL_POSTAL_CD          VARCHAR2(10)    YES
  PREFERRED_IND           CHAR(1)         NO     Y/N. One Y per CONTACT_TYPE_CD.
  DO_NOT_CONTACT_IND      CHAR(1)         NO     Y/N. Default N.
  VERIFIED_BY_USER_ID     VARCHAR2(20)    YES    Network identifier of verifier.
  VERIFIED_TS             TIMESTAMP(6)    YES
  SOURCE_SYSTEM_CD        CHAR(4)         NO     ORD, PRT, IMP, MAN.
  REC_STATUS_CD           CHAR(1)         NO     A/I/P.

3. Operational notes.
3.1 CONTACT_VALUE is the only column holding the contact point itself; the discrete mailing columns are retained solely for legacy address parsing and are frozen to new development.
3.2 Rows sourced IMP originate from lender or exchange imports and are not authoritative until VERIFIED_TS is set.
3.3 Wire instruction confirmation must not rely on a row where SOURCE_SYSTEM_CD equals EML or IMP. Confirmation procedure is stated in the wire policy and is not repeated here.

4. Access restriction. The table is not granted to reporting roles. Access is by view: VW_PARTY_CONTACT_OPS returns rows for open files only; VW_PARTY_CONTACT_MKTG returns CONTACT_TYPE_CD EML and MAL where DO_NOT_CONTACT_IND is N. Direct table grants require written approval of the Records Custodian.

5. Retention. Contact rows follow the retention of the parent party record. Rows carrying DO_NOT_CONTACT_IND equal to Y are exempt from purge and are retained indefinitely as suppression evidence.

6. Downstream extracts. Two standing extracts exist: the escrow disbursement notice feed, restricted to MAL rows, and the post-closing satisfaction feed, restricted to EML rows and suppressed per clause 4. Ad hoc extracts expire ninety days from approval.
