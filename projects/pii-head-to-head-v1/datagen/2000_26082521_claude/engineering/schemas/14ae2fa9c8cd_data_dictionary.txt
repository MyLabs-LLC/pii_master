DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT — CIS_PROD.CUST_CONTACT_PREF
Prepared by the Data Administration Unit / Reference DD-2011-031

TABLE PURPOSE: One row per customer per notification channel. Populated by the
web self-service portal and by counter maintenance screen CS210.

COLUMN: PREF_ID
  Type: NUMBER(12)   Null: No
  Notes: Surrogate key. Composite unique on CUST_ID + CHANNEL_CD.

COLUMN: CUST_ID
  Type: NUMBER(12)   Null: No
  Notes: FK to CUST_MSTR. Cascade is not enabled; orphans are swept monthly.

COLUMN: CONTACT_NM
  Type: VARCHAR2(60)   Null: Yes
  Notes: Free text. May differ from the account holder of record. Restricted.

COLUMN: NOTIFY_PHONE_NBR
  Type: VARCHAR2(14)   Null: Yes
  Notes: Fed to the outbound dialer. Do-not-call scrub applied at extract time.

COLUMN: NOTIFY_EMAIL_ADDR
  Type: VARCHAR2(60)   Null: Yes
  Notes: Confirmed by double opt-in since release 7.3. Restricted.

COLUMN: NOTIFY_SMS_NBR
  Type: VARCHAR2(14)   Null: Yes
  Notes: Added October 2010. Carrier gateway lookup performed nightly.

COLUMN: CHANNEL_CD
  Type: CHAR(3)   Null: No
  Notes: PHN, EML, SMS, PPR. FK to CHANNEL_REF.

COLUMN: OPT_IN_TS
  Type: TIMESTAMP(6)   Null: Yes
  Notes: Evidence of consent. Required before any extract release.

COLUMN: OPT_OUT_TS
  Type: TIMESTAMP(6)   Null: Yes
  Notes: Non-null suppresses the row from every outbound feed.

ACCESS: SELECT to CIS_CSR_ROLE and CIS_DBA_ROLE. The three contact columns are
masked in the reporting replica. No ad hoc query tool is licensed against this
table.

RETENTION: Purged 24 months after OPT_OUT_TS, or with the parent account under
LG-4200, whichever occurs first.

EXTRACTS: EXT_OUTAGE_RT (outage notification platform, near real time) and
EXT_DIALER_DAILY. Both are transmitted over SFTP to the vendor drop and are
deleted from the staging directory after transmission acknowledgment.
