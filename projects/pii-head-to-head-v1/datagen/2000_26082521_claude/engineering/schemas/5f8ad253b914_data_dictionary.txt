MEMORANDUM

TO:      Distribution
FROM:    Office of the Records Custodian
DATE:    2 November 1998
RE:      Addendum to DA-DD-1998-041 — Two (2) Columns Added at Release 4.3

The following two columns have been added to CUSTMAST effective with the 4.3 install and are hereby incorporated into the dictionary of record. Departmental abstracts should be corrected accordingly.

     PREFERRED_CONTACT_CD
          Type:        CHAR(1)
          Nullable:    Yes
          Note:        Indicates the channel by which the party has asked to
                       be reached. Does not itself carry contact particulars.
                       Unrestricted. Present in the branch productivity
                       extract.

     SOLICITATION_OPT_OUT_IND
          Type:        CHAR(1)
          Nullable:    No, defaults to 'N'
          Note:        Set by the closing officer of record upon written or
                       oral instruction of the party. Once set to 'Y' the
                       value may not be reversed by any user profile other
                       than ESCADM, and any such reversal is written to the
                       audit journal. This column is to be honored by every
                       downstream extract that feeds a mailing or telephone
                       campaign, without exception and without inquiry into
                       the age of the instruction.

Neither column is to be treated as a substitute for the maintenance of the underlying instruction in the file jacket. The paper instruction remains the record copy and is retained under Schedule R notwithstanding the disposition of the row.
