SUBJECT:   Data Dictionary Excerpt, CUST_MEMBER Subject Area, With Notation of Access Restriction, Retention Obligation, and Downstream Extract Disposition

I.  PRELIMINARY STATEMENT

The Records Custodian, having been requested by the Loyalty Marketing organization to certify the definitional content of the CUST_MEMBER subject area in advance of the fourth-quarter extract cycle, hereby circulates the excerpt set forth in Part II below. The excerpt is confined to those columns which are, or may reasonably be expected to be, implicated in an extract to a system of record other than the originating instance. Columns of a purely technical or audit character have been omitted as immaterial to the present inquiry.

II.  COLUMN DEFINITIONS

     CUST_MEMBER_ID
        Type:            NUMBER(12)
        Nullability:     NOT NULL
        Note:            Immutable surrogate identifier. Shall not be reused following purge.

     LEGAL_LAST_NAME
        Type:            VARCHAR2(40)
        Nullability:     NOT NULL
        Access:          Restricted. CRM_PII_READ.
        Note:            Suppressed in all datamart views. Not eligible for extract to third parties.

     LEGAL_FIRST_NAME
        Type:            VARCHAR2(40)
        Nullability:     NOT NULL
        Access:          Restricted. CRM_PII_READ.

     PREFERRED_NAME
        Type:            VARCHAR2(40)
        Nullability:     NULL permitted
        Access:          Restricted. Treated as equivalent to LEGAL_FIRST_NAME for all handling purposes.

     DATE_OF_BIRTH
        Type:            DATE
        Nullability:     NULL permitted
        Access:          Restricted, highest class. PHARM_OPS_READ and CRM_PII_READ only.
        Note:            Where the member record is linked to a pharmacy patient record, this column is subject to the pharmacy retention schedule and may not be purged on the loyalty cycle.

     RESIDENCE_ADDRESS_LINE_1 / RESIDENCE_ADDRESS_LINE_2
        Type:            VARCHAR2(60) each
        Nullability:     NULL permitted
        Access:          Restricted.
        Note:            Overwritten in place by the address hygiene process; prior values are not versioned.

     RESIDENCE_POSTAL_CODE
        Type:            VARCHAR2(10)
        Nullability:     NULL permitted
        Note:            Extracted at five-position granularity only.

     CONTACT_PHONE_NBR
        Type:            VARCHAR2(20)
        Nullability:     NULL permitted
        Access:          Restricted. Suppression list applied prior to any outbound file.

     CONTACT_EMAIL_ADDR
        Type:            VARCHAR2(120)
        Nullability:     NULL permitted
        Access:          Restricted. Extract conditioned upon EMAIL_CONSENT_IND.

     EMAIL_CONSENT_IND
        Type:            CHAR(1)
        Nullability:     NOT NULL
        Note:            Default is refusal. A null in the source feed shall be resolved to refusal.

     PHARMACY_LINK_IND
        Type:            CHAR(1)
        Nullability:     NOT NULL
        Note:            Where set, the row is excluded from all marketing extracts without exception.

     LAST_ACTIVITY_DT
        Type:            DATE
        Nullability:     NULL permitted
        Note:            Sole basis for dormancy and purge eligibility.

III.  RETENTION AND DISPOSITION

Rows not bearing PHARMACY_LINK_IND are eligible for purge thirty-six (36) months following LAST_ACTIVITY_DT. Rows bearing that indicator are retained in accordance with the applicable state board of pharmacy schedule and shall not be purged by the loyalty process under any circumstance. Upon purge, restricted columns are overwritten and the residual row is retained for aggregate reporting only.

IV.  EXTRACTS

The nightly file to the marketing datamart carries no restricted column. Any request to vary this condition shall be submitted in writing to the undersigned and shall be acted upon only with the concurrence of the Privacy Officer and of Internal Audit — Information Systems.
