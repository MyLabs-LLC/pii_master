DATA DICTIONARY — STUDENT MEMBER MASTER
SUPPLEMENT 2 OF 5: PERSON-LEVEL COLUMNS
Issued by the Office of the University Registrar, Records Management Unit
Document control no. RMU-DD-2016-02 | Supersedes RMU-DD-2015-07

1. SCOPE

This supplement documents the person-level columns of STUDENT.MEMBER_MASTER.
Enrollment, term, and academic-history columns are documented in Supplements 3
and 4. Entries are ordered as they appear in the physical table.

2. FIELD ENTRIES

2.1  MEMBER_KEY
     Type: NUMBER(10)   Null: NO
     Primary key, populated from sequence MEMBER_SEQ. Stable across name and
     status changes. Cited in all defect tickets in place of any identifying
     column.

2.2  UNIVERSITY_ID
     Type: VARCHAR2(9)  Null: NO
     Printed on the campus card and used for in-person service desk lookup.
     Unique. Not reissued after separation.

2.3  LEGAL_LAST_NAME / LEGAL_FIRST_NAME / LEGAL_MIDDLE_NAME
     Type: VARCHAR2(60) Null: NO / YES / YES
     Source of record for transcripts, diplomas, and enrollment certification.
     Updated only on receipt of documentation at the service counter; the
     web self-service channel cannot write to these columns.

2.4  CHOSEN_FIRST_NAME
     Type: VARCHAR2(60) Null: YES
     Added for term 201620 under the chosen-name policy. Populated by student
     self-service. Propagates to the learning management system and the class
     roster view; does not propagate to the Clearinghouse extract.

2.5  SSN_ENCRYPTED
     Type: RAW(64)      Null: YES
     Restricted Tier 1. Stored under transparent data encryption. Decryption is
     available to two service accounts, both used by scheduled jobs; no
     interactive session holds the decrypt grant. Every access writes to
     AUDIT_TIER1_ACCESS.

2.6  DATE_OF_BIRTH
     Type: DATE         Null: YES
     Restricted Tier 1. Required for the Clearinghouse and veteran benefits
     files. Excluded from all self-service reporting subject areas.

2.7  PERM_ADDR_LINE1 / PERM_ADDR_LINE2 / PERM_CITY / PERM_STATE / PERM_ZIP
     Types: VARCHAR2(75), VARCHAR2(75), VARCHAR2(50), CHAR(2), VARCHAR2(10)
     Null: YES on all five.
     Denormalized copy of the current active row in MEMBER_ADDRESS. Refreshed by
     the nightly address-sync job. Downstream consumers are directed to the
     normalized table; the denormalized copy exists for counter-service response
     time and is not authoritative.

2.8  MAIL_ADDR_LINE1 / MAIL_CITY / MAIL_STATE / MAIL_ZIP
     Same types as 2.7. Null: YES.
     Falls back to the permanent address when no active mailing row exists.

2.9  PRIMARY_PHONE / ALT_PHONE
     Type: VARCHAR2(20) Null: YES
     Format is not enforced at the column level; the cleansing routine runs
     monthly and writes exceptions to a review queue worked by the Records
     Custodian.

2.10 CAMPUS_EMAIL / PERSONAL_EMAIL
     Type: VARCHAR2(128) Null: NO / YES
     PERSONAL_EMAIL is collected at application and retained for admitted
     applicants who do not enroll. It is not used for official notice.

2.11 FERPA_BLOCK_IND
     Type: CHAR(1)      Null: NO   Default: 'N'
     Set 'Y' on written request. Once set, the row is withheld from directory
     inquiries, degree verification, and the printed commencement program.

2.12 LAST_ENROLLED_TERM / RECORD_STATUS / UPDATE_TIMESTAMP
     Types: VARCHAR2(6), CHAR(1), TIMESTAMP. Null: YES / NO / NO.
     RECORD_STATUS drives the retention clock described in Section 4.

3. ACCESS

Tier 1 columns (2.5, 2.6) are excluded from the reporting views and from the
data warehouse load. Tier 2 columns (2.3, 2.7 through 2.10) are available to the
registrar staff role and to advising staff for assigned students only.
Recertification is performed each September.

4. RETENTION

Records with RECORD_STATUS = 'S' (separated, no degree) are purged of Tier 1
columns after five years. Degree-conferred records retain the academic core
permanently; Tier 1 and contact columns are purged seven years after
conferral. Purges are executed by the quarterly retention job and countersigned
by the University Registrar.

5. EXTRACTS

Nightly to the operational data store; weekly to the Clearinghouse; term-based
to the state higher education coordinating board (aggregate only); monthly to
the alumni system on degree conferral. The alumni extract carries name, mailing
address, and personal email; it does not carry SSN_ENCRYPTED or DATE_OF_BIRTH.
