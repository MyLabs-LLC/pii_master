DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT — PLAN AND CARDHOLDER CROSS-REFERENCE
File: MBRPLXR, library RXPRDDTA
Owner: Third Party Billing
Reviewed with: the Records Custodian

A. Purpose
    1. Holds the association between a member and each third-party plan under which that member may be adjudicated. One member may carry several rows; one plan carries many members.
    2. The file is written by the plan enrollment program and by the nightly load from the plan sponsor tape. It is not maintained from the dispensing screen.

B. Columns
    1. XR_SEQ_NBR — DEC(9,0), NOT NULL. Row identifier, assigned by the file. No business meaning.
    2. MBR_ID — CHAR(10), NOT NULL. Foreign key to the member master.
    3. PLAN_BIN_CD — CHAR(6), NOT NULL. Routing code supplied by the sponsor.
    4. PLAN_GRP_CD — CHAR(15), NULL ALLOWED. Blank where the sponsor does not group.
    5. CARDHLDR_ID — CHAR(20), NOT NULL. The identifier the sponsor uses on its own file. Format varies by sponsor and is not edited beyond length.
    6. CARDHLDR_LAST_NAM — CHAR(25), NOT NULL. Held because several sponsors match on name as well as identifier.
    7. CARDHLDR_FIRST_NAM — CHAR(15), NOT NULL.
    8. CARDHLDR_DOB — DATE, NULL ALLOWED. Required by sponsors that use date of birth as part of the match key.
    9. DEPND_REL_CD — CHAR(2), NOT NULL. Relationship of the member to the cardholder.
    10. EFF_DT — DATE, NOT NULL.
    11. TERM_DT — DATE, NULL ALLOWED. Null means open coverage.

C. Access restriction
    1. The file carries authorization list RXTPBAL. Read is limited to Third Party Billing and to the two rebilling clerks at each regional office.
    2. Store personnel have no direct read. Coverage questions at the counter are answered from the adjudication response, not from this file.
    3. Columns B.6 through B.8 are excluded from the reporting shadow. Requests for them go to the Records Custodian in writing and are granted for a named study with a stated end date.

D. Retention
    1. A row with a populated TERM_DT is retained twenty-four months past that date to support retroactive rebilling, then deleted.
    2. Rows for a sponsor that has ended its contract are deleted ninety days after final reconciliation, and the tape received from that sponsor is destroyed on the same schedule.

E. Downstream extracts
    1. TPBRECON — monthly, back to each sponsor. Carries only the columns that sponsor supplied, filtered to that sponsor's BIN.
    2. TPBRJCT — daily, to the rebilling queue. Carries B.1 through B.5 and the reject reason. No name or date column is written to this extract.
    3. No extract from this file is permitted to any marketing destination.
