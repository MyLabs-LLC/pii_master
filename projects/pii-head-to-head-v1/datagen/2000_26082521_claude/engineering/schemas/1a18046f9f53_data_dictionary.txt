ACCESS RESTRICTION
Both files inherit authorization list MBRSEC3. Class III columns are excluded
from every logical view available to branch and call center group profiles.
MBRXREF carries an additional constraint: no group profile holds *READ on the
base physical file, and access is mediated solely through service program
XRFSRV01, which logs each lookup to the security journal. Journal receivers
are retained on line for 90 days and on tape for one year. Ad hoc query
tooling is not authorized against either file; report requests are queued
through the Records Custodian and satisfied from the reporting library copy,
which is created with Class III columns dropped.

RETENTION
MBRJOIN rows are retained six years past JNT_REMOVE_DT to align with the
signature card retention schedule and the applicable statute of limitations
on deposit contracts. MBRXREF is fully rebuilt each quarter from current
MBRMAST content; prior generations are not archived. Rows for members whose
MBR_STAT_CD is D are excluded from rebuild after the estate closing code is
posted.

DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
MBRJOIN supplies name fields to the year-end tax reporting job for JT and CU
roles, and supplies role and document locator fields to the account services
audit report. Neither extract carries JNT_TIN outside the tax reporting
transmission path. MBRXREF is not extracted; its output is limited to the
householding counts written to the monthly management summary, which are
aggregate only.
