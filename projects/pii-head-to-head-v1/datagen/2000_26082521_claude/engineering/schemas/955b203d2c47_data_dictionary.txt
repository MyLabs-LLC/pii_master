SECTION D — ACCESS RESTRICTION
Direct table SELECT is granted to the Application DBA and to the CHCR service
account only. Clinical staff reach the table through view V_CLIENT_LOOKUP, which
omits SSN and MEDICAID_ID. Requests for the unmasked columns are routed to the
Records Custodian and require written supervisor justification, retained for the
life of the account. Column-level reads are logged to AUDIT_COLUMN_ACCESS.

SECTION E — RETENTION
Rows are retained seven years past the last documented service encounter. For
clients served as minors, retention runs seven years past the eighteenth
birthday. Purge is executed annually by the Application DBA against the Records
Custodian's approved disposition list; merged rows are retained as tombstones.

SECTION F — DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
1. Nightly ETL to CHD_WAREHOUSE. Name, address, phone, email, and SSN columns are
   dropped at the staging step; MEMBER_NUM is replaced by a hashed key.
2. Quarterly epidemiology extract. County-level counts by ZIP5, age band derived
   from DOB, RACE_CD, and ETHNICITY_CD. No direct identifiers.
3. State registry upload, HL7 2.3.1, weekly. Transmitted via secure FTP under the
   data use agreement on file with the Records Custodian.
4. Ad hoc extracts require a completed Data Release Request and are logged in the
   extract register maintained by the Data Steward.
