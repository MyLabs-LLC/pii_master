STUDENT MASTER — SRS.STDNT_MSTR (excerpt, dictionary release 4.2)

The table holds one row per person to whom an institutional record has been assigned, whether or not enrollment followed. STDNT_PIDM, NUMBER(8), NOT NULL, is the surrogate primary key; values are drawn from sequence SRS_PIDM_SEQ, are never reused after purge, and carry no external meaning. STDNT_ID_NBR, VARCHAR2(9), NOT NULL, is the printed campus identifier and is the value carried on transcripts and on the card office feed. SSN_NBR, VARCHAR2(9), nullable since the Fall 2001 conversion, is held only where federal aid processing or 1098-T reporting requires it; SELECT is granted exclusively through role SRS_RESTRICTED_R, approved case by case by the Records Custodian, and the column is excluded from every reporting view by construction rather than by masking. BIRTH_DT, DATE, NULL, is similarly restricted and is exposed to the institutional research schema only as a derived birth-year integer. LEGAL_LAST_NM, LEGAL_FIRST_NM and LEGAL_MIDL_NM, VARCHAR2(30), are NOT NULL with the exception of the middle-name column; PREF_FIRST_NM, VARCHAR2(30), NULL, is maintained by student self-service and does not propagate to transcript or diploma output. FERPA_SUPPRESS_IND, CHAR(1), NOT NULL, defaults to 'N' and determines eligibility for the directory extract. Rows persist under the permanent academic record provision of the state retention schedule; application-only rows with no term activity are purged after five inactive years.
