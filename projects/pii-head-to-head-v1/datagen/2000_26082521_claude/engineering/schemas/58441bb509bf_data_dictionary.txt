SECTION C — ACCESS RESTRICTION
Base table SELECT is granted to role CIS_DBA_ROLE only. Counter and call-center
staff read through view CUST_MSTR_V, which masks TAX_ID_ENCR, DL_NBR_HASH and
BIRTH_DT and returns SSN_LAST4 only when the session holds CIS_IDVERIFY_ROLE.
Grants to individual accounts are prohibited under Administrative Directive 09-6;
all grants are role-mediated and reviewed quarterly by the Records Custodian.
Fine-grained auditing is enabled on the four masked columns; audit rows land in
AUD$ and are exported to the security archive weekly.

SECTION D — RETENTION
Rows are retained six years following ACCT_CLOSE_DT under the state Local
Government Records Retention Schedule, series LG-4200 (Utility Customer Account
Records), then purged by job PRG_CUST_MSTR_ANNUAL. Rows carrying an open
collections referral or an unreleased deposit are exempted from the purge until
the flag clears. BIRTH_DT is nulled at 24 months past program termination
irrespective of account status.

SECTION E — DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
EXT_PRINT_DAILY (print and mail vendor) — name, mailing address, language code.
EXT_COLL_MONTHLY (collections agency) — name, mailing address, SSN_LAST4.
EXT_ASSIST_QTR (energy assistance office) — name, BIRTH_DT, household counts.
EXT_DW_NIGHTLY (enterprise warehouse) — all columns except the two encrypted or
hashed identifiers, which are replaced by a warehouse-local surrogate.

SECTION F — CHANGE CONTROL
Structural change requires a signed DA-22 and regression of all four extracts.
