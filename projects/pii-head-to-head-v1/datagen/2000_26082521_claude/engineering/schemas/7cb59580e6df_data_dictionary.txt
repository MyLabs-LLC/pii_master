DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT — TABLE CHS_CLIENT_MSTR (CLIENT MASTER)
County Health Department, Division of Public Health Informatics
Records Series HD-114 — Dictionary Revision 4.2, effective 01 February 2016

1. SCOPE AND CONTROLLING AUTHORITY. This excerpt describes those columns of the client master table that are subject to restricted handling by operation of the privacy and security rules promulgated under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act, the substance use disorder confidentiality provisions codified at 42 C.F.R. Part 2, the communicable disease reporting statutes of the State, and the retention schedule adopted by the Board of Supervisors. Nothing herein shall be construed to enlarge any right of access; where an operational note stated below conflicts with a more restrictive provision of the Department's Privacy and Security Policy Manual, the more restrictive provision governs. Grants and revocations of access to the columns enumerated below are executed by the Records Custodian upon written request countersigned by the requesting program's supervising manager, and are recertified not less frequently than annually.

2. KEY AND CONTROL COLUMNS. CLIENT_ID_SK, defined as NUMBER(12) and NOT NULL, is the system-assigned surrogate primary key, carries no intrinsic meaning, is populated by database sequence, and shall not be reused, reassigned, or reproduced in correspondence directed outside the Department. MPI_MERGE_STATUS_CD (CHAR(1), NOT NULL) records disposition within the master index reconciliation cycle, and MPI_SURVIVOR_ID_SK (NUMBER(12), NULL) is populated only where a record has been superseded by merge, in which event the subordinate row is retained in place for audit purposes and suppressed from every downstream extract. RECORD_STATUS_CD (CHAR(2), NOT NULL) and RECORD_SOURCE_SYS_CD (VARCHAR2(8), NOT NULL) are maintained exclusively by the nightly load and may not be updated by application users.

3. RESTRICTED IDENTITY COLUMNS. The columns LAST_NAME_TXT (VARCHAR2(60), NOT NULL), FIRST_NAME_TXT (VARCHAR2(40), NOT NULL), MIDDLE_NAME_TXT (VARCHAR2(40), NULL), NAME_SUFFIX_TXT (VARCHAR2(10), NULL), MAIDEN_NAME_TXT (VARCHAR2(60), NULL), DOB_DT (DATE, NOT NULL), SSN_TXT (VARCHAR2(9), NULL), MEDICAID_RECIPIENT_ID_TXT (VARCHAR2(20), NULL), and DRIVER_LICENSE_NBR_TXT (VARCHAR2(20), NULL) are designated Category I and are visible only through the role CHS_ROLE_IDENTITY_FULL. SSN_TXT is collected solely where a benefits determination requires it, is stored encrypted at the column level, and is never selected into an extract; the derived column SSN_LAST4_TXT (VARCHAR2(4), NULL) exists for verification purposes only.

4. CONTACT AND RESIDENCE COLUMNS. RESIDENCE_ADDR_LINE1_TXT, RESIDENCE_ADDR_LINE2_TXT, RESIDENCE_CITY_TXT, RESIDENCE_STATE_CD, RESIDENCE_ZIP5_TXT, RESIDENCE_ZIP4_TXT, MAILING_ADDR_LINE1_TXT, MAILING_CITY_TXT, MAILING_ZIP5_TXT, PRIMARY_PHONE_NBR, ALT_PHONE_NBR, and EMAIL_ADDR_TXT are nullable in all cases except RESIDENCE_ZIP5_TXT, which is required for jurisdictional determination. The paired consent indicators PHONE_CONTACT_OK_IND, TEXT_CONTACT_OK_IND, and EMAIL_CONTACT_OK_IND (each CHAR(1), NOT NULL, defaulting to the negative value) shall be evaluated by every outreach process before a contact column is read; a contact column read in the absence of the corresponding affirmative indicator constitutes a reportable privacy incident. RESIDENCE_CENSUS_TRACT_TXT (VARCHAR2(11), NULL) is derived by geocoding and is the only geographic element permitted in analytic extracts.

5. RETENTION AND EXTRACTS. Rows are retained seven years from LAST_SERVICE_DT, or, where MINOR_AT_SERVICE_IND is affirmative, until the twenty-first anniversary of DOB_DT plus three years, whichever is later, after which the quarterly purge job nullifies all Category I columns and preserves the analytic shell. The nightly HL7 v2.5.1 interface to the State immunization registry transmits identity columns under the executed data use agreement; the monthly eligibility file transmits identity and benefit columns only; the quarterly epidemiology extract transmits neither, and suppresses all columns enumerated in Sections 3 and 4 except RESIDENCE_CENSUS_TRACT_TXT.
