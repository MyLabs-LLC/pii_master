Two variants are cut nightly by MBREXT40. The unmasked variant is written to
a restricted output queue and released only to the credit bureau transmission
job. The masked variant, in which MBR-TIN and MBR-BIRTH-DT are space filled,
is the sole version supplied to the statement vendor, the card processor and
the marketing analysis tape. Tapes are logged out through the Records
Custodian, retained for two cycles, and degaussed on return. Requests for the
unmasked variant require written approval from the security officer.
