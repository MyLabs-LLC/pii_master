FILE: MEM_ACCT (MEMBERSHIP AND ACCOUNT DETAIL) — DICTIONARY EXCERPT PREPARED FOR THE DP STEERING COMMITTEE

This excerpt covers the elements most frequently misread by report writers and is organized by the operational question each group of columns is intended to answer. It supersedes the September pages for the identification and financial blocks; the consumption block is unchanged and is not reproduced here.

(a) Identification. MEM_NO, DECIMAL(9,0), NOT NULL, is the membership number assigned at enrollment and is the sole primary key. It is distinct from ACCT_KEY on the billing side, and the two are related through the XREF_MEM_ACCT table, which permits many-to-many because a single membership may hold service at several premises and a single premise may pass through several memberships across time. Report writers who join MEM_ACCT to the billing extract on MEM_NO alone and omit the effective-dating on XREF_MEM_ACCT will produce inflated counts; this has been the origin of three of the last five reconciliation discrepancies. MEM_LAST_NM CHAR(30) NOT NULL, MEM_FRST_NM CHAR(20) NOT NULL and MEM_MID_INIT CHAR(1) NULL carry the individual identification. SSN_TIN CHAR(9) NULL was collected as a matter of course through the 1995 enrollment form and is nullable thereafter; approximately sixty percent of rows carry a value, and the field is not to be used as a join key, a sort key, or a match key under any circumstance. BIRTH_DT DATE NULL is collected only where a senior or medical rate classification is claimed.

(b) Financial. DEPOSIT_AMT DECIMAL(9,2) NOT NULL DEFAULT ZERO, DEPOSIT_DT DATE NULL, and DEPOSIT_REFUND_DT DATE NULL together support the deposit aging report. EFT_ROUT_NO CHAR(9) NULL and EFT_ACCT_NO CHAR(17) NULL support the direct-payment program. The two EFT elements are stored in the clear on the production tablespace, a condition documented in the internal audit finding of last April and scheduled for remediation in the next release; in the interim, the tablespace carries a restricted authorization identifier, the DBA has revoked SELECT from all general reporting identifiers, and unload authority is held only by the two production support staff. No extract, report, or screen outside the payment application is permitted to reference either column, and the DBA has been asked to run the catalog scan quarterly to confirm this.

(c) Status and lifecycle. MEM_STAT_CD CHAR(2) NOT NULL, ENROLL_DT DATE NOT NULL, TERM_DT DATE NULL and TERM_RSN_CD CHAR(3) NULL govern the record lifecycle. A row with a populated TERM_DT remains fully queryable for the balance of the fiscal year in which it terminated plus six additional years, which is the period specified by the records schedule for utility account documentation. At the close of that period the annual disposition job sets DISP_IND and overwrites MEM_LAST_NM, MEM_FRST_NM, MEM_MID_INIT, SSN_TIN, BIRTH_DT, DRV_LIC_NO, HOME_PHN, EFT_ROUT_NO and EFT_ACCT_NO with the standard fill pattern. The row itself is retained because the consumption history is required for rate-case testimony and cannot be reconstructed once discarded. The disposition job produces a control report listing counts by TERM_RSN_CD, which is signed by the Records Custodian and filed; the report lists no element values.

(d) Downstream. Three regular extracts read this file. The monthly conservation-program mailing takes MEM_NO, the name and mailing elements, and the premise identifier. The annual regulatory filing takes counts and aggregates only and dereferences no individual row. The bad-debt referral takes the name, mailing and balance elements and is transmitted on labelled tape under the vendor agreement. Any fourth extract requires a completed data release form and the Custodian's countersignature before the JCL is promoted.
