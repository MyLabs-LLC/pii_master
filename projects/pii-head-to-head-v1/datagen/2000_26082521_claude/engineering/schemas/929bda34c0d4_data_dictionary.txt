III. NOTE LEGEND
    (a) Populated by the load process; not sourced from the production object.
    (b) One-way hash. The cleartext account number is not staged, is not
        indexed, and shall not be reconstructed by any downstream process.
    (c) Restricted attribute. Replaced by surrogate values in every environment
        other than production staging.
    (d) Subject to the column-level grant provisions of Form DD-114, Section C.
        No grant issued upon the production object is inherited here; a separate
        grant is required and must be separately justified.
    (e) Excluded from every outbound file described at Part V below.

IV. ACCESS RESTRICTION
    a. The schema CIS_STG is not accessible to interactive sessions. Access is
       confined to the batch account ETL_LOAD_SVC and to the role CIS_ETL_ADMIN.
    b. Ad hoc query privileges upon this object are prohibited. Requests for
       investigative access are directed to the Records Custodian and are granted
       for a fixed term not exceeding five business days.
    c. Failed load attempts leave the prior contents in place; operators shall not
       remedy a failure by manual update of restricted columns.

V.  DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
    a. Collections work queue, nightly. Columns 02, 04, 07, 08, 09, 11, 12, 13,
       and 15. Delivered to the internal secure directory.
    b. Print and mail vendor, cycle-based. Columns 04, 07, 08, 09, and 12,
       delivered under the standing confidentiality agreement then in force.
    c. Delinquency statistics, monthly. Columns 13 and 15 in aggregate; no
       row-level attribute leaves the Division.

VI. RETENTION
    The object is truncated at the commencement of each load. Load audit rows in
    CIS_STG.LOAD_CTL are retained eighteen (18) months. Outbound files described
    at Part V are purged from the transfer directory after fourteen (14) days,
    the purge being verified by the operations log.
