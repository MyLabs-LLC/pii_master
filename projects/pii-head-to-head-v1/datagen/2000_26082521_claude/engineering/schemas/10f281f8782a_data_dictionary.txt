DATA DICTIONARY MAINTENANCE NOTE — TABLE MBR_ENRL_HIST
Prepared for the file of the Records Custodian

The Bureau records the following amendment to the enrollment history table, the same having been approved at the meeting of the Data Governance Committee and entered into the change log under sequence 2004-0063. The column MBR_ID, NUMBER(10), NOT NULL, remains the foreign key to CLNT_MSTR and is not itself restricted. The column MBR_ENRL_EFF_DT, DATE, NOT NULL, and the column MBR_ENRL_END_DT, DATE, NULL permitted where enrollment is open, together establish the effective period upon which all eligibility determinations depend, and no process shall infer eligibility from any other column. The column MBR_HSHLD_SIZE_QTY, NUMBER(2), NULL permitted, and the column MBR_INCM_LVL_CD, VARCHAR2(3), NULL permitted, are financial screening elements and are made available to the eligibility unit alone by role CHD_ELIG_R; they are excluded by construction from every extract other than the federal program reimbursement file. The column MBR_MEDCD_NBR, VARCHAR2(14), NULL permitted, is designated Restricted and is masked in all views prefixed VW_RPT_. Rows in this table are never deleted; the retention obligation attaching to the parent client row governs, and superseded rows are marked by MBR_ROW_STAT_CD, CHAR(1), NOT NULL, rather than removed.
