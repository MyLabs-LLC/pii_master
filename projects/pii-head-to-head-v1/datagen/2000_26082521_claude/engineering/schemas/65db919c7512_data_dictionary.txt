MEMORANDUM

TO:      Data Governance Committee
FROM:    Records Custodian
SUBJECT: Data Dictionary Excerpt — MEMBER_CONTACT_HIST
DATE:    9 June 2016
FILE:    DG-DD-2016-021

1.0  BACKGROUND

1.1  MEMBER_CONTACT_HIST retains the prior-value history of contact attributes maintained on MEMBER_MASTER. The table was created in response to Internal Audit finding IA-2015-08 concerning the absence of a demonstrable change trail on address of record.

1.2  The table is append-only. Update and delete privileges are granted to no role, including the schema owner, outside of an approved maintenance window.

2.0  STRUCTURE

2.1  CONTACT_HIST_SEQ
     2.1.1  Datatype: NUMBER(14)
     2.1.2  Nullability: NOT NULL
     2.1.3  Note: Sequence-generated key; gaps are expected and are not defects.

2.2  MBR_NBR
     2.2.1  Datatype: NUMBER(10)
     2.2.2  Nullability: NOT NULL
     2.2.3  Note: Foreign key to MEMBER_MASTER; indexed with CHANGE_TS.

2.3  ATTRIBUTE_NAME
     2.3.1  Datatype: VARCHAR2(30)
     2.3.2  Nullability: NOT NULL
     2.3.3  Note: Records the schema name of the altered column, drawn from the set MBR_ADDR_LINE_1, MBR_ADDR_LINE_2, MBR_CITY, MBR_STATE_CD, MBR_POSTAL_CD, MBR_HOME_PHONE, MBR_MOBILE_PHONE, MBR_EMAIL_ADDR.

2.4  PRIOR_VALUE_ENC
     2.4.1  Datatype: RAW(256)
     2.4.2  Nullability: YES
     2.4.3  Note: Restricted. Encrypted with the contact history key; null where the attribute was previously unpopulated.

2.5  NEW_VALUE_ENC
     2.5.1  Datatype: RAW(256)
     2.5.2  Nullability: NOT NULL
     2.5.3  Note: Restricted. Encrypted as above.

2.6  CHANGE_CHANNEL_CD
     2.6.1  Datatype: CHAR(3)
     2.6.2  Nullability: NOT NULL
     2.6.3  Note: Branch, contact centre, home banking or bulk maintenance.

2.7  CHANGE_OPERATOR_ID
     2.7.1  Datatype: VARCHAR2(12)
     2.7.2  Nullability: NOT NULL
     2.7.3  Note: Operator identifier; the reserved literal for member-initiated home banking changes is documented in Appendix C.

2.8  CHANGE_TS
     2.8.1  Datatype: TIMESTAMP
     2.8.2  Nullability: NOT NULL
     2.8.3  Note: Server time, not adjusted for daylight saving.

3.0  ACCESS RESTRICTION

3.1  Read access is limited to role COMPLIANCE_RO and to the Fraud Investigator role. Decryption of PRIOR_VALUE_ENC and NEW_VALUE_ENC requires a case reference recorded in the investigations log.

3.2  Standing reporting access is not granted. Ad hoc requests are approved individually by the Records Custodian.

4.0  RETENTION

4.1  History rows are retained seven years from CHANGE_TS. The purge job runs on the first Sunday of each calendar quarter.

4.2  Rows bearing an active investigations hold are exempt from purge until the hold is released in writing.

5.0  DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS

5.1  No downstream extract of this table is authorized. Any request to establish one shall be treated as a new interface and subjected to vendor due diligence.
