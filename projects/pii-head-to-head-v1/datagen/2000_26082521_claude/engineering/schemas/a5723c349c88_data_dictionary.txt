MEMORANDUM

TO:      Manager of Core Systems; Business Intelligence Analyst
FROM:    Records Custodian
SUBJECT: Data Dictionary Excerpt — ODS Staging Layer, Table STG_MEMBER_DAILY
DATE:    2 September 2016
FILE:    DG-DD-2016-029

[1]  APPLICATION

     This excerpt governs the staging structure loaded nightly from the core
     platform into the operational data store. Staging content is transient and
     is not a system of record.

[2]  COLUMNS

     -- STG_LOAD_ID
        NUMBER(12), NOT NULL. Batch identifier; joins to the load control table.

     -- STG_BUSINESS_DT
        DATE, NOT NULL. Effective processing date of the source extract.

     -- MBR_NBR
        NUMBER(10), NOT NULL. Natural key carried forward from the core.

     -- MBR_LAST_NAME_HASH
        CHAR(64), NOT NULL. One-way hash substituted at extract time; the
        cleartext column is not carried into staging.

     -- MBR_POSTAL_CD_3
        CHAR(3), NOT NULL. Truncated to three positions for geographic
        segmentation. Full postal code is not carried into staging.

     -- MBR_AGE_BAND_CD
        CHAR(2), NOT NULL. Derived at extract time from MBR_DOB in the source.
        The underlying date column is not carried into staging.

     -- MBR_TENURE_MONTHS
        NUMBER(4), NOT NULL. Derived from MBR_OPEN_DT in the source.

     -- MBR_BRANCH_CD
        CHAR(4), NOT NULL. Home branch of record.

     -- MBR_PRODUCT_COUNT
        NUMBER(3), NOT NULL. Count of open share and loan relationships.

     -- MBR_AGG_BALANCE
        NUMBER(13,2), NOT NULL. Aggregate deposit balance; negative values are
        rejected by the load.

     -- MBR_CHANNEL_PREF_CD
        CHAR(2), NULL. Null where no preference has been recorded.

[3]  ACCESS RESTRICTION

     3.1  The staging schema is readable by role BI_ANALYST. No Restricted
          column as defined in DG-DD-2016-011 is present, and none may be added
          without amendment of this excerpt.

     3.2  Re-identification of hashed or banded columns by join to any external
          source is prohibited.

[4]  RETENTION

     4.1  Staging partitions are dropped after fourteen days. Downstream marts
          hold their own retention schedules.

[5]  DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS

     5.1  Feeds the membership growth mart and the branch performance
          dashboard. Both are internal; neither leaves the institution's
          network.
