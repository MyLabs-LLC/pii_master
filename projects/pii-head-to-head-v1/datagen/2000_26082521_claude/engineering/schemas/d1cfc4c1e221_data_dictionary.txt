DATA DICTIONARY — WORKING COPY
Subject Area: Assignment & Payroll Profile
Table: PLC.WORKER_PAY_PROFILE
Dictionary Ref: DD-207 | Version 2.9 | Issued 14-JUN-2016
Owner of Record: the Payroll Operations Supervisor
Data Steward: the Records Custodian
Sensitivity: Restricted (contains Tier 3 elements)

1. PURPOSE
This table holds the payroll and disbursement attributes for any candidate who
has reached assignment status. It is populated by the intake handoff job
(JOB_INTK_PROMOTE) and is not directly editable from the recruiter desktop.

3. OPERATIONAL NOTES

 2.1  PAY_PROFILE_ID is generated by SEQ_PAYPROF. CANDIDATE_ID is enforced as
      unique per open effective period; overlapping periods are rejected by
      TRG_PAYPROF_OVERLAP.

 2.2  TAX_ID_NUMBER is stored encrypted. Decryption occurs only inside the
      payroll batch schema. No unmasked select is permitted from any reporting
      tool, including the Cognos catalogue. Requests for decrypted output are
      logged and require dual approval by the Payroll Operations Supervisor and
      the Director of Compliance.

 2.3  Withholding columns are refreshed from the annual form cycle. Stale rows
      (EFFECTIVE_TO_DT in the past) are excluded from payroll selection.

 2.4  Banking columns are write-once through the disbursement setup screen and
      are never displayed in full on any interface. The trailing four positions
      of BANK_ACCOUNT_NUMBER are exposed to branch staff through a derived
      column in V_PAY_SUMMARY. PAYCARD_TOKEN is a vendor-issued surrogate and
      carries no cardholder data.

 2.5  DATE_OF_BIRTH is duplicated from CANDIDATE_MASTER for benefits age
      banding. Divergence between the two is reported weekly on RPT-DQ-08.

 2.6  EEO self-identification columns are collected voluntarily. They are
      physically separated from recruiting screens and are visible only to the
      EEO Reporting Analyst role. They must not be used in any selection,
      assignment, or rate decision.

 2.7  Emergency contact columns hold third-party personal data and follow the
      same restriction tier as candidate contact columns.

 2.8  GARNISHMENT_ORDER_REF is an internal file reference. Court documents are
      held outside the database in the secured records room.

 2.9  ROW_HASH supports change detection for the nightly extract.

4. RETENTION
Payroll profile rows are retained 7 years past the final payroll period in
which the profile was used. Banking columns are nulled 90 days after the
DIRECT_DEPOSIT_FLAG is cleared. Purge is executed by JOB_PURGE_PAYPROF and
countersigned by the Records Custodian.

5. DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
 - FEED_ADP_WEEKLY: full profile, SFTP, encrypted at rest, vendor DPA on file.
 - FEED_BENEFIT_ADMIN: CANDIDATE_ID, age band, STATE_TAX_JURISDICTION.
 - FEED_EEO1_ANNUAL: aggregate counts only; no row-level identifiers leave the
   EEO reporting schema.
 - DW_STAGING: all Tier 3 columns are dropped at the staging boundary.
