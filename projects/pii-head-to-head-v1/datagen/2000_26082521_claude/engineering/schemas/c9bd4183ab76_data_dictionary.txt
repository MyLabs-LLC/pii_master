FORM RM-22 — DATA ELEMENT SCHEDULE
DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH, OFFICE OF RECORDS AND INFORMATION GOVERNANCE
Subject Table: CHD_CORE.MEMBER_CONTACT_HIST
Schedule Date: 15 September 2021

ARTICLE 1. PURPOSE
  1.1. This Schedule describes, for governance and audit purposes, each column of the
       subject table, together with its declared type, its nullability as deployed, and
       the operational conditions attaching thereto.
  1.2. This Schedule is incorporated by reference into the Departmental Data Inventory
       and shall be reviewed not less than annually.

ARTICLE 2. COLUMN SCHEDULE

  2.1. CONTACT_HIST_SK — BIGINT — NOT NULL
       Surrogate key. Populated by sequence. Never reissued following deletion.

  2.2. MBR_SK — BIGINT — NOT NULL
       Foreign key to MEMBER_MASTER. Enforced; cascading deletion is disabled by design.

  2.3. CONTACT_TYPE_CD — CHAR(3) — NOT NULL
       Constrained to the domain maintained in CHD_REF.CONTACT_TYPE_CD.

  2.4. CONTACT_NAME_TXT — VARCHAR(120) — NULLABLE
       Restricted element. Populated where the member designates an alternate contact.
       Not exposed in any analytic view.

  2.5. CONTACT_PHONE_NBR — VARCHAR(20) — NULLABLE
       Restricted element. Subject to the Department's outreach opt-out register; the
       register shall be consulted prior to each campaign without exception.

  2.6. CONTACT_EMAIL_ADDR_TXT — VARCHAR(254) — NULLABLE
       Restricted element. Written only where affirmative consent is recorded upstream.

  2.7. CONTACT_ADDR_TXT — VARCHAR(180) — NULLABLE
       Restricted element. Standardization is performed in place; the pre-standardization
       value is retained in the audit schema for ninety (90) days and then purged.

  2.8. RELATIONSHIP_CD — CHAR(2) — NULLABLE
       Domain-constrained. Null permitted for legacy rows migrated in 2019.

  2.9. EFF_START_TS / EFF_END_TS — TIMESTAMP — NOT NULL / NULLABLE
       Bitemporal pair. A null end timestamp denotes the currently effective row.

ARTICLE 3. ACCESS
  3.1. Columns designated Restricted in Article 2 are accessible only to holders of the
       role CHD_PHI_READ and to the Records Custodian in the discharge of official duties.
  3.2. No credential shall be shared, and no service account shall be granted the role
       absent written approval of the Privacy Officer.

ARTICLE 4. RETENTION
  4.1. Rows are retained six (6) years from EFF_END_TS. Currently effective rows are not
       eligible for disposition.
  4.2. Disposition is executed by the quarterly purge job and evidenced in the purge log.

ARTICLE 5. DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
  5.1. EXT_OUTREACH_MONTHLY consumes CONTACT_TYPE_CD, CONTACT_PHONE_NBR and
       CONTACT_EMAIL_ADDR_TXT for currently effective rows only.
  5.2. No other extract, report, or interface is authorized to read the Restricted columns
       of this table. Any proposed addition requires a completed Form RM-30.
