DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT — EMPLOYER PASS AND PAYROLL DEDUCTION PROGRAM
Physical Table: EMPPASS     Dictionary Version 1.9     Owner: Office of Revenue Systems

1. General.

   1.1 EMPPASS records the individual participants enrolled through participating employers under the pre-tax transit benefit program. The employer entity itself is recorded in EMPMAST and is not personal data; the columns dictionaried below are, and the distinction governs everything in clause 4.

   1.2 The table is refreshed from employer-supplied enrollment files on a monthly cycle. Employers submit on cartridge or, since March 1998, by dial-up transfer to the enrollment mailbox; the layouts differ by submission method and are reconciled by module EMP0300 into the common internal format described here.

2. Columns.

   2.1 EMP_ENROLL_NBR, DECIMAL(9,0), NOT NULL, primary key. EMPLOYER_CTL_NBR, DECIMAL(7,0), NOT NULL, foreign key to EMPMAST.

   2.2 PARTIC_LAST_NAM, VARCHAR(30), NOT NULL; PARTIC_FRST_NAM, VARCHAR(20), NOT NULL; PARTIC_MDL_INIT, CHAR(1), nullable.

   2.3 EMPLOYEE_ID_TXT, VARCHAR(20), NOT NULL, is the identifier assigned by the employer and is not under the Authority's control; its format varies by employer and it must not be parsed. SSN_NBR, CHAR(9), nullable, is accepted only where the employer submits no other stable identifier and is used solely to match against a prior month's enrollment.

   2.4 WORK_ADDR_LN_1, VARCHAR(40), NOT NULL; WORK_ZIP_CDE, CHAR(9), NOT NULL; RES_ZIP_CDE, CHAR(5), nullable, supplied by roughly a third of employers and used for corridor analysis. TEL_WORK_NBR, CHAR(10), nullable. EMAIL_ADR_TXT, VARCHAR(60), nullable.

   2.5 PASS_TYP_CDE, CHAR(3), NOT NULL; FARECARD_SER_NBR, CHAR(16), nullable until first issuance; DEDUCT_AMT, DECIMAL(5,2), NOT NULL WITH DEFAULT; BENEFIT_AMT, DECIMAL(5,2), NOT NULL WITH DEFAULT; ENROLL_EFF_DTE and ENROLL_TERM_DTE, DATE, the latter nullable; PARTIC_STS_CDE, CHAR(1), NOT NULL.

3. Null treatment. Where an employer's submission omits a nullable column, the prior month's value is not carried forward; the column is set to null and the omission is reported on the exception listing EMPR040 for follow-up by the program coordinator. Carrying values forward was the practice through Version 1.6 and was discontinued because it masked terminations.

4. Access, retention, and extracts.

   4.1 Access to the name, identity, and contact columns is limited to RACF group EMP$ADMN. The employer relations staff work through VW_EMPPASS_ROSTER, which is further constrained by a row-level predicate on EMPLOYER_CTL_NBR so that no representative may retrieve the roster of an employer other than those assigned to that representative.

   4.2 No participant-level data is returned to the employer beyond that which the employer itself submitted, together with PASS_TYP_CDE and the enrollment dates. Employer requests for ridership or travel detail at the participant level are refused as a matter of standing policy; aggregate counts are supplied where the enrolled population exceeds twenty-five.

   4.3 Terminated enrollments are retained four years from ENROLL_TERM_DTE to satisfy the record-keeping obligation attaching to the pre-tax benefit, then purged by job EMPP070. SSN_NBR is overwritten at twelve months following termination irrespective of the longer row retention.

   4.4 The only recurring extract is EMPX020, the monthly reconciliation file returned to each employer over the same channel by which the enrollment was received. Ad hoc extracts require Form RM-27 and the countersignature of the Records Custodian.
