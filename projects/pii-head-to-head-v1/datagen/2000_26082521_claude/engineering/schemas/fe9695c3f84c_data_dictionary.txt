ACCESS RESTRICTION
The stage layer is readable only by the ETL service account and the Data Warehouse Lead. No reporting tool connects to it. Any grant beyond these two is approved in writing by the Records Custodian and expires after thirty days.

RETENTION
Stage rows are truncated after the mart load confirms, and in no case held beyond fourteen days. The mart retains hashed party attributes for seven years from CLOSING_DT, matched to the file retention rule. The quarterly purge job removes DOB_DT-derived age bands at five years.

DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
Two extracts read from the mart, not from this table. The volume and cycle-time report carries no party attributes. The remittance reconciliation file carries FILE_NBR and ENTITY_LEGAL_NAME only. Requests for a new extract containing any hashed party column are logged and reviewed by the Compliance Officer before the job is scheduled.

OPEN ITEM
PHONE_MOBILE is still received in clear from one lender feed and hashed after landing. The Data Warehouse Lead is to move the hash upstream to the feed handler.
