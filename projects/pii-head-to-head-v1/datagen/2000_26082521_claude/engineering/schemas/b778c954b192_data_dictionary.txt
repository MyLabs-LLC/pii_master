ACCESS RESTRICTION
Base tables carry no interactive grants. Three consumption views exist:
MBR_SERVICE_V (Sections A identity keys plus B, for the service center),
MBR_ACTUARIAL_V (Sections A and C with DOB_DT replaced by AGE_BAND_CD and all
Section B columns suppressed), and MBR_ZIP3_V (Class 1 only). Class 4 columns
are readable exclusively through role R_PII_ELEV, which is time-boxed to a
single business day per approval and recertified quarterly. All Class 4 SELECT
activity is written to the database audit trail and reviewed monthly.

RETENTION
Operational retention is the coverage period plus seven years, measured from
COV_TERM_DT. On expiry the annual purge nulls Class 3 and Class 4 columns and
retains PARTY_KEY, GRP_NBR, PLAN_CD, and the coverage dates. Rows carrying a
litigation or regulatory hold are excluded until the hold is released by the
Records Custodian.

DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
Four recurring extracts read this area. The stop-loss carrier file carries no
Section B columns and substitutes a hashed member key. The state all-payer
submission carries DOB_DT and ZIP_CD under the filing exemption. The wellness
vendor file carries name, email, and preferred language only, gated on the
communication consent flag. The internal reporting mart carries Section C in
full and no Class 4 column. Any new consumer requires a data-sharing review
before the ETL request is scheduled.
