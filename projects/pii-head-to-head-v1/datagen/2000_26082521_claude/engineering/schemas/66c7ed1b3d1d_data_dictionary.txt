DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT — CUSTMAST
ESCROW ACCOUNTING SYSTEM (EAS), RELEASE 4.2
VSAM KSDS, PRODUCTION CLUSTER PROD.EAS.CUSTMAST
ISSUED BY THE OFFICE OF THE RECORDS CUSTODIAN — EFFECTIVE 11 MARCH 1998

1. This excerpt states the leading fields of CUSTMAST only. The complete record layout is carried in copybook CUSTM01 of the production copy library and governs in the event of conflict.

2. CUST-KEY. PIC 9(10) COMP-3. NOT NULL. Primary key. Assigned by the sequence routine at record creation. Not derived from and not reconcilable to any government-issued identifier.

3. CUST-LAST-NM. PIC X(30). NOT NULL. Restricted, Class B. Upper case on input; embedded punctuation retained.

4. CUST-FIRST-NM. PIC X(20). NULL permitted where CUST-TYPE-CD indicates a corporate or trust party.

5. CUST-MID-INIT. PIC X(01). NULL permitted.

6. CUST-TIN-SSN. PIC X(09). NULL permitted. Restricted, Class A. Nine digits, unformatted; punctuation stripped at edit. Field is display-suppressed at all terminals except those carrying the CMPL group profile. Excluded from every downstream extract without exception.

7. CUST-BIRTH-DT. PIC 9(08), CCYYMMDD. NULL permitted. Restricted, Class A. Expanded from six-byte YYMMDD under the century remediation project, September 1997.

8. CUST-ADDR-LN-1, CUST-ADDR-LN-2. PIC X(40). Line 1 NOT NULL. Restricted, Class B.

9. CUST-HOME-PHON. PIC X(10). NULL permitted. Restricted, Class B.

10. Retention: seven years from the CUST-LAST-ACTV-DT of the terminating file. Class A fields are overwritten with low-values at the fifth year by job EASPRG40.

11. Extract authority: nightly extract EASX010 to the title plant index carries clauses 2, 3, 8 only. All other extracts require written authorization of the Records Custodian.
