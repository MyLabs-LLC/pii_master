DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT
SCHEMA: DEP_CORE          TABLE: SHARE_ACCOUNT
REVISION: 6.1             EFFECTIVE: 2021-09-20
OWNER: the Deposit Operations Manager
TECHNICAL STEWARD: the Database Administrator on record

I.   TABLE DEFINITION
     A.  Grain: one row per share or certificate account, all suffixes included.
     B.  Volume: partitioned monthly on ACCT_OPEN_DT.
     C.  Source: nightly posting from the core banking platform; no direct user insert.

II.  IDENTITY AND LINKAGE
     A.  SHARE_ACCT_SK          BIGINT          NOT NULL
         1.  Surrogate key, system generated, stable across core conversions.
     B.  SHARE_ACCT_NBR         VARCHAR(17)     NOT NULL
         1.  Restricted column. Presented to service staff in full; masked to trailing four positions in reporting views and in every environment below production.
         2.  Never permitted as a join key to externally sourced files.
     C.  ACCT_SUFFIX_CD         CHAR(4)         NOT NULL
     D.  PRIMARY_MEMBER_SK      BIGINT          NOT NULL
         1.  Foreign key to MBR_CORE.MEMBER_MASTER.
     E.  JOINT_OWNER_MEMBER_SK  BIGINT          nullable
         1.  Populated only for jointly held accounts; multi-party holdings above two owners are carried in ACCT_OWNER_BRIDGE.

III. PRODUCT AND STATUS ATTRIBUTES
     A.  PRODUCT_CD             CHAR(6)         NOT NULL
     B.  ACCT_STATUS_CD         CHAR(2)         NOT NULL
     C.  ACCT_OPEN_DT           DATE            NOT NULL
     D.  ACCT_CLOSE_DT          DATE            nullable
     E.  DORMANCY_FLAG          CHAR(1)         NOT NULL
         1.  Set by the escheatment job; manual override requires dual authorisation.
     F.  ESCHEAT_ELIGIBLE_DT    DATE            nullable

IV.  BALANCE AND RATE ATTRIBUTES
     A.  CURRENT_BAL_AMT        DECIMAL(15,2)   NOT NULL
     B.  AVAILABLE_BAL_AMT      DECIMAL(15,2)   NOT NULL
     C.  HOLD_AMT               DECIMAL(15,2)   NOT NULL, defaults to zero
     D.  DIVIDEND_RATE_PCT      DECIMAL(7,5)    nullable
     E.  MATURITY_DT            DATE            nullable, certificates only

V.   ACCESS RESTRICTION
     A.  Clause II.B is classified Confidential — Member Financial. Select is granted by role, never by individual account.
     B.  Balance columns under section IV are readable by the finance reporting role in aggregate views only; row-level access requires membership in DEP_ANALYST_ROW.
     C.  All decryption and unmasking events are written to the database audit trail and reviewed monthly by the Records Custodian.

VI.  RETENTION
     A.  Active rows retained indefinitely.
     B.  Closed accounts retained seven years past ACCT_CLOSE_DT to satisfy the deposit records schedule, then moved to the archive schema with clause II.B tokenised.
     C.  Dormant and escheated rows are exempt from clause VI.B until the state remittance is confirmed.

VII. DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
     A.  DEP_EXT_02 — nightly to the enterprise warehouse. Carries all of section III and IV; clause II.B is tokenised, clause II.D is passed as a surrogate only.
     B.  DEP_EXT_05 — monthly to the regulatory reporting utility. Aggregated by PRODUCT_CD and branch; no account-level column leaves the boundary.
     C.  DEP_EXT_11 — quarterly to the external auditor. Full section II is included under the engagement letter; the file is delivered by managed transfer and expires at thirty days.
     D.  No extract not enumerated in this section may be scheduled against this table.
