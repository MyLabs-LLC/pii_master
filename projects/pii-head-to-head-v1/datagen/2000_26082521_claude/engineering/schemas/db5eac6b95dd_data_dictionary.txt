SUPPLEMENTAL NUTRITION PROGRAM (WIC) — DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT
Table: PARTICIPANT_XREF | Database: WICLOCAL (SQL Server 2008 R2) | Rev. 1.4 | 2011-06-13

COLUMNS
PARTICIPANT_SK      | INT          | NOT NULL | surrogate key
STATE_WIC_ID        | VARCHAR(10)  | NOT NULL | assigned by state system, unique
LOCAL_CASE_NUM      | VARCHAR(8)   | NULL     | legacy clinic numbering, frozen
PARTICIPANT_LNAME   | VARCHAR(35)  | NOT NULL | as printed on benefit instrument
PARTICIPANT_FNAME   | VARCHAR(25)  | NOT NULL |
DOB                 | DATE         | NOT NULL | drives category and certification end
GUARDIAN_LNAME      | VARCHAR(35)  | NULL     | infant and child records only
RESIDENCE_ZIP5      | CHAR(5)      | NOT NULL | clinic assignment
CONTACT_PHONE       | CHAR(10)     | NULL     | digits only
CATEGORY_CD         | CHAR(2)      | NOT NULL | PG, BF, PP, IN, CH
CERT_END_DT         | DATE         | NOT NULL |
INCOME_PROOF_CD     | CHAR(2)      | NOT NULL | documentation type, not amount
REC_STATUS_CD       | CHAR(1)      | NOT NULL | A, T terminated, X transferred

ACCESS: Restricted to WIC certifiers and the Clinic Supervisor by application
role. No general department read. The Records Custodian approves any export.

RETENTION: Three years following the end of the fiscal year in which the
certification closed, per federal program rule, then destroyed on the Records
Custodian's annual disposition authorization.

EXTRACTS: Nightly upload of certification and issuance rows to the state WIC
system. Monthly participation counts by category and clinic to the Program
Manager; counts only, no name, DOB, or phone columns included.
