DEPARTMENTAL DATA DICTIONARY — ARTICLE VII
COMMUNICABLE DISEASE CASE REGISTRY (CD_CASE_SUBJECT)
Adopted by the Data Governance Committee, June 2016

ARTICLE VII, SECTION 1 — CONSTRUCTION. The definitions set forth in this Article are binding upon every unit of the Department, upon every contractor performing work on the case registry, and upon every person granted a database credential thereto. The terms "restricted," "controlled," and "open" bear the meanings assigned to them in Article I, and a column not expressly designated in this Article shall be presumed controlled until the Privacy Officer determines otherwise in writing.

SECTION 2 — SUBJECT COLUMNS. (a) CASE_SUBJECT_ID_SK, NUMBER(12), NOT NULL, is the surrogate key and is open. (b) STATE_CASE_NBR_TXT, VARCHAR2(20), NULL, is assigned by the State reporting system upon acceptance of the initial report and is controlled; it shall be quoted in correspondence with the State only. (c) SUBJECT_LAST_NAME_TXT (VARCHAR2(60), NOT NULL), SUBJECT_FIRST_NAME_TXT (VARCHAR2(40), NOT NULL), SUBJECT_DOB_DT (DATE, NOT NULL), SUBJECT_SSN_LAST4_TXT (VARCHAR2(4), NULL), SUBJECT_MRN_TXT (VARCHAR2(20), NULL), SUBJECT_PHONE_NBR (VARCHAR2(15), NULL), SUBJECT_ADDR_LINE1_TXT (VARCHAR2(80), NULL), and SUBJECT_ZIP5_TXT (VARCHAR2(5), NOT NULL) are restricted, and access thereto is confined to investigators assigned to the case and to their supervising manager, such assignment being recorded in the CD_CASE_ASSIGNMENT table and audited monthly.

SECTION 3 — CONDITION AND CONTACT COLUMNS. (a) CONDITION_CD, VARCHAR2(10), NOT NULL, is drawn from the State reportable conditions list in effect on REPORT_RECEIVED_DT. (b) Where CONDITION_CD denotes a sexually transmitted infection, human immunodeficiency virus infection, or tuberculosis, the columns enumerated in Section 2(c) become subject to the heightened confidentiality provisions of State law, and the row is excluded from every extract other than the State disease surveillance transmission, notwithstanding any contrary instruction from a requesting unit. (c) CONTACT_TRACE_ID_SK, NUMBER(12), NULL, links a named contact to the index record; the contact's own identity columns reside in CD_CONTACT_SUBJECT and are restricted on the same terms, and the linkage shall not be resolved in any analytic environment.

SECTION 4 — RETENTION. Rows are retained for the period prescribed by the disease-specific schedule, being permanent for tuberculosis and human immunodeficiency virus infection, twenty-five years for vaccine-preventable conditions, and ten years from CASE_CLOSE_DT for all others. Upon expiration of the applicable period, the disposition process nullifies the columns enumerated in Section 2(c) and preserves CONDITION_CD, SUBJECT_ZIP5_TXT truncated to three digits, ONSET_DT, and the outcome columns.

SECTION 5 — EXTRACTS. Three extracts are authorized: the nightly State surveillance transmission, carrying the columns required by the State message specification then current; the weekly internal investigation worklist, carrying restricted columns and remaining within the Department network; and the monthly public reporting aggregate, carrying counts only and no column enumerated in Section 2(c). Creation of any further extract requires the prior written approval of the Health Officer and the Privacy Officer, jointly.
