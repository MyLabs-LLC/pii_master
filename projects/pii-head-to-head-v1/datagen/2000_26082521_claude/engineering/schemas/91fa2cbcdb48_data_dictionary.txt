DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT — THIRD PARTY PLAN ENROLMENT
SCHEMA PHRMCY_MBR . TABLE MBR_PLAN_ENRL . NCPDP 5.1 ALIGNED

1.0 PURPOSE
1.1 This excerpt documents the plan enrolment table used at claim
    adjudication. Column naming follows the NCPDP 5.1 field names where a
    direct correspondence exists.
1.2 The table is written by the eligibility interface and by counter
    maintenance. It is not writable from the office network.

2.0 COLUMN INVENTORY

 COLUMN NAME          | TYPE          | NULL | OPERATIONAL NOTE
 ---------------------+---------------+------+----------------------------
 PLAN_ENRL_ID         | NUMBER(12)    | NO   | Primary key.
 PT_PROFILE_ID_FK     | NUMBER(12)    | NO   | Cascade on profile merge.
 PAYER_BIN            | CHAR(6)       | NO   | Routing. Not restricted.
 PROC_CTRL_NBR        | VARCHAR2(10)  | YES  | Routing. Not restricted.
 GRP_NBR              | VARCHAR2(15)  | YES  | Restricted at the view.
 CARDHOLDER_ID_NBR    | VARCHAR2(20)  | NO   | Restricted. Class 2.
 PERSON_CD            | CHAR(3)       | YES  | Dependent sequence.
 PT_RELTN_CD          | CHAR(1)       | YES  | Coded domain. Edit checked.
 COB_SEQ_NBR          | NUMBER(1)     | NO   | Coordination of benefits.
 PLAN_EFF_DT          | DATE          | NO   | Adjudication window start.
 PLAN_TERM_DT         | DATE          | YES  | Null denotes open enrolment.
 ELIG_VERIF_TS        | TIMESTAMP     | YES  | Last successful E1 response.
 LAST_MAINT_USER_ID   | VARCHAR2(8)   | NO   | Operator id for audit.
 REC_STAT_CD          | CHAR(1)       | NO   | Active, termed, or suspended.

3.0 ACCESS RESTRICTION
3.1 CARDHOLDER_ID_NBR and GRP_NBR are exposed in full only to the
    adjudication service account and to roles PHRM_RPH and PHRM_TECH.
3.2 All other consumers read through V_PLAN_ENRL_MASKED, which returns the
    trailing four positions of CARDHOLDER_ID_NBR and suppresses GRP_NBR
    entirely. Direct table grants to reporting accounts are prohibited.
3.3 The audit trail on this table captures the reading account for every
    unmasked select and is retained for six years.

4.0 RETENTION
4.1 A row terminated by PLAN_TERM_DT is held eighteen months against
    claim reversal and audit recovery, then archived.
4.2 Archived rows are recoverable only by the Records Custodian on written
    request from the Pharmacy Systems Manager or from counsel.
4.3 ELIG_VERIF_TS is not retained beyond the archive step and is dropped
    at archive write.

5.0 DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
5.1 The nightly warehouse extract carries PAYER_BIN, PROC_CTRL_NBR,
    COB_SEQ_NBR, PLAN_EFF_DT, PLAN_TERM_DT, and REC_STAT_CD.
5.2 No extract, scheduled or ad hoc, carries CARDHOLDER_ID_NBR, GRP_NBR,
    PERSON_CD, or PT_RELTN_CD. This clause admits no exception under the
    90 day provision applied elsewhere in this dictionary.
5.3 Plan level reporting supplied to a payer under contract is drawn from
    the warehouse copy and never from this table.
