DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT — LENDING SCHEMA
LND_ORIG.LOAN_APPLICATION
Revision 3.4 | Effective 2021-11-08 | Supersedes Revision 3.3
Business Owner: the Chief Lending Officer
Data Steward: the Loan Servicing Supervisor

1. Purpose and Grain
   a. One row per credit application, whether booked, declined, withdrawn or expired.
   b. Applications spawning multiple credit decisions are versioned by DECISION_SEQ_NBR; the row is not overwritten.
   c. This table is the system of record for adverse action reporting and for the fair-lending analysis file.

2. Key Columns
   a. LOAN_APP_SK — BIGINT, NOT NULL. Surrogate primary key.
   b. LOAN_APP_NBR — VARCHAR(14), NOT NULL, unique. Restricted; masked below production.
   c. APPLICANT_MEMBER_SK — BIGINT, nullable. Null where the applicant is not yet a member at the time of application.
   d. CO_APPLICANT_MEMBER_SK — BIGINT, nullable.
   e. BOOKED_LOAN_ACCT_NBR — VARCHAR(17), nullable. Populated at funding only. Classified Confidential — Member Financial.

3. Applicant Attributes Held on This Table
   a. APPLICANT_LEGAL_NAME_FIRST, APPLICANT_LEGAL_NAME_LAST — VARCHAR(40), NOT NULL.
   b. APPLICANT_TAX_ID_ENC — VARBINARY(256), NOT NULL. Encrypted at column level; the decryption grant is held by the underwriting role and by no reporting role.
   c. APPLICANT_DATE_OF_BIRTH — DATE, NOT NULL. Used solely for identity verification and age-of-majority checks.
   d. APPLICANT_MAILING_POSTAL_CD — CHAR(10), NOT NULL. Retained for geographic distribution analysis; the remaining address lines are held on the origination platform and are not replicated here.
   e. GROSS_MONTHLY_INCOME_AMT — DECIMAL(13,2), nullable.
   f. EMPLOYMENT_STATUS_CD — CHAR(2), nullable.

4. Decision Attributes
   a. PRODUCT_CD — CHAR(6), NOT NULL.
   b. REQUESTED_AMT, APPROVED_AMT — DECIMAL(15,2); APPROVED_AMT nullable until decision.
   c. CREDIT_SCORE_VAL — SMALLINT, nullable. Sourced from the bureau interface.
   d. CREDIT_BUREAU_CD — CHAR(3), nullable.
   e. DTI_RATIO_PCT — DECIMAL(6,3), nullable.
   f. DECISION_CD — CHAR(2), NOT NULL after the underwriting queue releases the application.
   g. DECISION_TS — TIMESTAMP, nullable.
   h. ADVERSE_ACTION_REASON_CD_1 through _4 — CHAR(3), nullable. Domain maintained in REF_AA_REASON and reviewed annually by the Compliance Officer.
   i. UNDERWRITER_ROLE_CD — CHAR(4), NOT NULL. Records the deciding role, not the individual; individual attribution is carried in the platform audit log.

5. Access Restriction
   a. Clauses 3.b and 3.c are readable only through the stored procedure interface. Ad hoc select against these columns is revoked at the schema level.
   b. Clause 4.c and clause 4.h together constitute the regulated decision record. Any query touching both is logged with the requesting role and the stated purpose.
   c. Development and test environments receive this table with clauses 3.a through 3.d generated synthetically by the masking utility; production values are never propagated below the production boundary.

6. Retention
   a. Declined and withdrawn applications: twenty-five months from DECISION_TS, per the adverse action retention requirement, then purged.
   b. Booked applications: retained for the life of the loan plus seven years, then archived with clauses 2.b, 2.e and 3.b tokenised.
   c. Purge jobs run monthly and produce a certificate of destruction filed with the Records Custodian.

7. Downstream Extracts
   a. LND_EXT_03 — nightly to the warehouse. Excludes all of section 3 except clauses 3.d, 3.e and 3.f.
   b. LND_EXT_08 — annual fair-lending file. Includes clause 3.d and section 4 in full; delivered to counsel under privilege.
   c. LND_EXT_12 — servicing handoff at funding. Includes clause 2.e; transmitted by managed file transfer with per-file keys.
   d. Requests for any extract beyond clauses 7.a through 7.c are submitted to the data governance committee and are approved for a single named run.
