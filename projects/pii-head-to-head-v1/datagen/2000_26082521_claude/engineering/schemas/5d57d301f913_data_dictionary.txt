SECTION 3 — ACCESS RESTRICTION
Tier 2 and above require an approved Data Access Request (form DAR-04) countersigned by the requester's director. Tier 4 columns are excluded from the standard analyst role EDW_ANLYST_RO and are available only through role EDW_PII_ELEV, reviewed quarterly. Ad hoc SELECT against Tier 3 and Tier 4 columns is logged to the database audit trail and reviewed monthly by Information Security.

SECTION 4 — RETENTION
Active member rows retained for the life of coverage. Terminated segments retained ten (10) years following COVG_TERM_DT per state insurance record retention requirements, then purged by job PRG_MBR_RETN. Tier 4 columns are overwritten with nulls at year seven under the early-suppression standard.

SECTION 5 — DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
EXT-114 Actuarial Reserving Extract — age band only; name and address columns excluded.
EXT-207 Wellness Vendor File — subscriber identifier tokenized; SFTP delivery.
EXT-330 Regulatory Filing Support — state and postal prefix only.
Any new extract consuming Tier 3 or Tier 4 columns requires Privacy Office sign-off before scheduling.
