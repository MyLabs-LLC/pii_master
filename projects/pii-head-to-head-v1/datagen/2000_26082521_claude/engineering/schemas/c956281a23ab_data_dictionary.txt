DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT — MARKETING EXTRACT VIEW
SCHEMA PHRMCY_RPT . VIEW V_MBR_MKTG_EXTRACT

COLUMN NAME          TYPE        NULL  OPERATIONAL NOTE
-------------------  ----------  ----  ----------------------------------
MBR_KEY_HASH         CHAR(32)    NO    One way. Not reversible to MBR_ID.
ENROLL_STORE_NBR     CHAR(5)     NO    Store of first enrolment.
POSTAL_CD_5          CHAR(5)     YES   Five positions. +4 not projected.
AGE_BAND_CD          CHAR(2)     YES   Derived from MBR_DOB at build.
GNDR_CD              CHAR(1)     YES   Passed through unaltered.
MKTG_OPT_IN_FLG      CHAR(1)     NO    View predicate. Affirmative only.
LAST_PURCH_QTR_CD    CHAR(5)     YES   Calendar quarter, front end only.
BASKET_DECILE_CD     CHAR(2)     YES   Front end spend. Excludes pharmacy.
SEG_CD               CHAR(3)     YES   Assigned by the scoring job.
BUILD_DT             DATE        NO    Stamped by the extract job.

1. CONSTRUCTION
   1.1 The view selects from MBR_MASTER and from the front end sales
       summary. It does not join to PT_PROFILE, to MBR_PLAN_ENRL, or to
       any fill history structure, and the join privileges to do so are
       not held by the account that builds it.
   1.2 MBR_LAST_NAM, MBR_FRST_NAM, MBR_DOB, ADDR_LINE_1, ADDR_LINE_2,
       HOME_PHN_NBR, EMAIL_ADDR, and SSN_NBR are not projected by this
       view under any role. AGE_BAND_CD is the only derivation permitted
       from a suppressed column.
   1.3 The view predicate requires an affirmative MKTG_OPT_IN_FLG and a
       record status of active. Rows failing either test are absent, not
       nulled.

2. ACCESS RESTRICTION
   2.1 Select is granted to the marketing analysis role and to the
       extract service account. No grant is made to any vendor account.
   2.2 The hash key is salted per build. Two builds are not joinable to
       one another, and the salt is held by Data Administration.

3. RETENTION
   3.1 The materialised copy is dropped fourteen days after BUILD_DT.
   3.2 Any file produced from this view and released to an outside
       agency is scheduled for certified destruction at ninety days, and
       the destruction certificate is filed with the Records Custodian.

4. AMENDMENT
   4.1 Addition of a column to this view requires the joint approval of
       the Records Custodian and the Vice President of Marketing, and is
       recorded in the schema change register before the build job is
       promoted.
