SUBJECT: MBR_CONTACT_PREF — Dictionary Excerpt

1. Scope. This excerpt covers the contact preference child table only. Parent definitions remain as published for MBR_MASTER.

2. Columns.

COLUMN | TYPE | NULL | NOTES
PREF_ID | BIGINT | N | Surrogate key.
MBR_KEY | BIGINT | N | FK to MBR_MASTER; cascade on purge.
CHANNEL_CD | CHAR(2) | N | Values held in CHANNEL_REF.
CONTACT_POINT_ENC | VARBINARY(256) | Y | Encrypted at column level; holds the phone number or email address for the stated channel. Decryption is available to the statement generator service account only.
CONTACT_POINT_LAST4 | CHAR(4) | Y | Display aid for service desk verification; restricted.
PREF_LANGUAGE_CD | CHAR(3) | N | Drives statement template selection.
CONSENT_FLG | CHAR(1) | N | Y or N; no default.
CONSENT_CAPTURED_TS | TIMESTAMP | Y | Null where consent has never been recorded.
SUPPRESS_FLG | CHAR(1) | N | Defaults to N. Overrides CONSENT_FLG when set.
SUPPRESS_REASON_CD | CHAR(3) | Y | Mandatory where SUPPRESS_FLG is Y.
UPDATED_BY_ROLE | VARCHAR(30) | N | Role identifier, not a user identifier.
UPDATED_TS | TIMESTAMP | N | Set by trigger.

3. Access. The columns CONTACT_POINT_ENC and CONTACT_POINT_LAST4 are restricted. No developer role holds read access in production; defect investigation is performed against the masked copy in the lower environment.

4. Retention. Preference rows follow the parent account and are purged with it. Superseded preference rows are retained for twenty-four months in PREF_HISTORY to evidence consent at the time of each contact.

5. Extracts. The statement generator receives a same-day feed of PREF_ID, MBR_KEY, CHANNEL_CD, PREF_LANGUAGE_CD, CONSENT_FLG and SUPPRESS_FLG. CONTACT_POINT_ENC is resolved inside the service boundary and is not written to any file, log or queue.

6. Action. Analysts holding queries built before Release 4.2 are to confirm by 10 September that no such query selects the restricted columns. Confirmations are to be lodged with the Records Custodian.
