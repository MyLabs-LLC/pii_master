3. ACCESS RESTRICTION
   3.1 Read access by ticket only, one request per purpose, expiring at
       ticket closure. No standing grants.
   3.2 Bulk SELECT (over 1000 rows) blocked by resource plan; override
       requires DBA action and is reported to the quarterly access review.
   3.3 Columns 2.4 through 2.11 are excluded from every published view.

4. RETENTION
   4.1 Base schedule: 7 years from VALID_TO_TS.
   4.2 Rows where LEGAL_HOLD_IND is set are exempt from purge without
       exception; release requires written notice from Legal.
   4.3 Annual certification of purge completion filed on Form RM-08.
