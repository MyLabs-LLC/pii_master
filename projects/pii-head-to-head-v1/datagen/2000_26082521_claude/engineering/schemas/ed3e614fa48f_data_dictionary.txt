DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT — MEMBER CONTACT PREFERENCE, TABLE MBRPREF
Oracle 8i instance PHRM2, schema CUSTOWN. Issued by the Data Administration desk, 14 JUN 2004.

MBRPREF is a dependent of CUSTMAST and inherits its restriction class. MBR_KEY_ID, NUMBER(11), NOT NULL, foreign key to the master; PREF_SEQ_NBR, NUMBER(3), NOT NULL, completes the composite key. PREF_CHNL_CD, CHAR(2), NOT NULL, identifies the contact channel and is validated against REFCHNL. OPT_OUT_IND, CHAR(1), NOT NULL, defaulted to the negative value at insert; the affirmative value suppresses the row from every outbound selection without exception and may not be reversed by store personnel. OPT_OUT_DT, DATE, NULL permitted, required whenever the indicator is affirmative. OPT_OUT_SRC_CD, CHAR(3), NULL permitted, records whether the election arrived by written request, telephone request to the service desk, or in-store form.

MBR_MAIL_NAM, VARCHAR2(60), NULL permitted, holds the addressee line as printed and is not to be parsed for matching purposes. EMAIL_ADR_TXT and HOME_PHON_NBR are duplicated here for extract convenience and are refreshed nightly from the master; they are read-only to all application accounts.

Retention follows the master. Suppression elections are retained indefinitely and are excluded from all purge routines by design. The sole authorized extract is XMKT01. Access is by role CUSTREAD; grants to individual accounts are not permitted.
