TO: Data Stewards Council
FROM: Office of the University Registrar, Data Administration
SUBJECT: Dictionary excerpt in support of the annual access review — SRS.STDNT_MSTR and dependents

The columns below are those for which access, retention, or extract treatment differs from the system default, and are the columns on which the council is asked to act. Everything else in the student master follows the standard rule: SELECT to SRS_STDNT_R, retention per the permanent academic record provision, and inclusion in extracts at the discretion of the extract owner.

Restricted columns. SSN_NBR, VARCHAR2(9), NULL; BIRTH_DT, DATE, NULL. Both are excluded from the reporting schema entirely; there is no masked variant and no view through which a partial value can be obtained. Grants are held by eleven accounts at present, of which four are batch accounts belonging to financial aid and student accounts, and the remainder are staff accounts in the records unit. The Records Custodian approves each grant in writing and the council reviews the list annually. Statement-level auditing is enabled on both columns and the audit extract is retained for eighteen months.

Conditionally released columns. ADDR_LINE_1_TXT, ADDR_LINE_2_TXT, ADDR_LINE_3_TXT, ADDR_CITY_NM, ADDR_STATE_CDE, ADDR_POSTL_CDE, TELE_AREA_CDE, TELE_NBR, all in SRS.STDNT_ADDR_TELE and all nullable except the first address line and city; and EMAIL_ADDR_TXT, VARCHAR2(60), NULL, in SRS.STDNT_EMAL. These are directory information under the published notice and are therefore releasable, but only for rows whose parent FERPA_SUPPRESS_IND, CHAR(1), NOT NULL, is 'N'. The suppression flag is evaluated at extract time and not at request time; an extract taken before a suppression request is honored will not be recalled, which is why the directory file is regenerated nightly rather than weekly as it was through the prior year.

Columns with non-standard retention. SSN_NBR, purged sixty months after the last reportable event, as described in the retention section of the dictionary. HOLD_CMNT_TXT, VARCHAR2(240), NULL, in SRS.STDNT_HOLD, purged one year after the hold is released. APPL_ESSAY_TXT, CLOB, NULL, in ADM.APPL_SUPP, purged at the close of the admission cycle for applicants who do not matriculate and retained for one year for those who do.

Extract inventory. Six scheduled jobs read the restricted or conditionally released columns named above: the enrollment reporting transmission, the federal aid origination file, the tax reporting file, the directory file, the card office load, and the library patron load. Three of the six carry no restricted column at all and are candidates for reclassification, which would remove them from the quarterly certification and reduce the review burden accordingly. The council is asked to act on that reclassification at this meeting and to confirm the eleven standing grants.
