SECTION 2 -- ACCESS RESTRICTION

The columns marked restricted above are excluded from the general reporting view
PFS_MEMBER_V. Direct table select is granted only to role PFS_FULL, which is held by the
Records Custodian, the two collections supervisors, and the compliance analyst. Grants are
recertified in January and July; the signed recertification sheet is filed with the
Business Office Manager. Ad hoc query tools connect through the view and cannot see the
restricted columns regardless of role. Any request to widen access is submitted in writing
and approved by the Director of Patient Financial Services before the grant is issued.
