PARTY MASTER — DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT
Schema: ESCROWPRD.PARTY_MASTER
Excerpt covers identity, contact and disbursement columns only.

SECTION 1 — REGISTER CONTROL
  Business owner ......... the Records Custodian
  Technical owner ........ the Database Administration Lead
  Data classification .... Restricted (Tier 2)
  Review cycle ........... Semi-annual, with the Compliance Officer
  Revision ............... 2021.02

SECTION 2 — KEY AND LINKAGE COLUMNS
COLUMN | TYPE | NULL | OPERATIONAL NOTE
PARTY_ID | NUMBER(12) | N | Surrogate key, sequence-generated. Never reused.
FILE_NBR | VARCHAR2(18) | N | Escrow file. Joins to FILE_HEADER.
ORDER_NBR | VARCHAR2(18) | Y | Title order. Null until title is opened.
PARTY_ROLE_CD | CHAR(3) | N | BUY, SEL, BOR, LDR, AGT. Validated against ROLE_REF.
SOURCE_SYS_CD | CHAR(4) | N | Origin system for the row.
BRANCH_CD | CHAR(5) | N | Drives row-level access filtering.

SECTION 3 — IDENTITY ELEMENTS (RESTRICTED)
COLUMN | TYPE | NULL | OPERATIONAL NOTE
PARTY_LAST_NAME | VARCHAR2(60) | Y | Null for entity parties. Masked in all non-production copies.
PARTY_FIRST_NAME | VARCHAR2(40) | Y | Masked in all non-production copies.
PARTY_MIDDLE_NAME | VARCHAR2(40) | Y | Vesting text is built from this column; do not trim.
ENTITY_LEGAL_NAME | VARCHAR2(120) | Y | Populated when PARTY_TYPE_CD = 'E'.
TIN_SSN_ENC | RAW(64) | Y | Column-level encryption. Decrypt grant is by named role only.
TIN_TYPE_CD | CHAR(1) | Y | S = individual, E = entity.
DOB_DT | DATE | Y | Collected for CIP only. Excluded from all reporting views.
GOVT_ID_TYPE_CD | CHAR(2) | Y | Identification type captured at signing.
GOVT_ID_NBR_ENC | RAW(64) | Y | Encrypted. Purged on the CIP retention schedule.

SECTION 4 — CONTACT COLUMNS
COLUMN | TYPE | NULL | OPERATIONAL NOTE
MAIL_ADDR_LINE_1 | VARCHAR2(80) | Y | Mailing, not subject property. Property sits in PARCEL.
MAIL_CITY_NAME | VARCHAR2(40) | Y | Standardized nightly by the address hygiene job.
MAIL_STATE_CD | CHAR(2) | Y |
MAIL_POSTAL_CD | VARCHAR2(10) | Y | Nine-digit form permitted.
EMAIL_ADDR | VARCHAR2(120) | Y | Used for secure-link delivery. Not exported to marketing.
PHONE_MOBILE | VARCHAR2(20) | Y | Digits only; extensions go in PHONE_EXT.
ESIGN_CONSENT_DT | DATE | Y | Null blocks electronic delivery.

SECTION 5 — DISBURSEMENT COLUMNS
COLUMN | TYPE | NULL | OPERATIONAL NOTE
BANK_ROUTING_ENC | RAW(64) | Y | Encrypted. No screen displays the clear value.
BANK_ACCT_NBR_ENC | RAW(64) | Y | Encrypted. Entry restricted to the Escrow Officer role.
WIRE_CALLBACK_DT | DATE | Y | Must be populated before a wire is released.
WIRE_VERIF_BY_ROLE | VARCHAR2(30) | Y | Records the verifying role, not the individual.

SECTION 6 — ACCESS RESTRICTION
Read access to Sections 3 and 5 is granted through the ESCROW_PII_READ role, approved by the Records Custodian and recertified quarterly. Analysts hold ESCROW_ANALYTIC_READ, which resolves to the masked view V_PARTY_ANALYTIC. Ad hoc query tools are pointed at the view, never the base table.

SECTION 7 — RETENTION
Party rows follow the file: seven years after disbursement, or the longer state period where applicable. GOVT_ID_NBR_ENC and DOB_DT are nulled five years after closing by the quarterly purge job. Purge counts are reported to the Compliance Officer.

SECTION 8 — DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
  1099-S reporting file — includes TIN_SSN_ENC and name columns; SFTP to the reporting vendor, January cycle.
  OFAC screening feed — name and address columns only; daily.
  Underwriter remittance — no Section 3 or Section 5 columns.
  CRM feed — masked view only; EMAIL_ADDR suppressed.
