CUSTOMER MASTER FILE — DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT
System: Pharmacy Front End, Release 4.2
Physical file: CUSTMST, library RXPRDDTA (DB2/400)
Maintained by: Store Systems Group, under the Records Custodian

    I. Control columns
        A. CUST_KEY — CHAR(12), NOT NULL. Primary key. Assigned by the enrollment sequence generator at the point of first fill. Never reused, never reassigned on merge.
        B. STORE_ORIG_NO — DEC(4,0), NOT NULL. Store of original enrollment. Used for district reporting only; does not follow the customer on transfer.
        C. ENRL_DT — DATE, NOT NULL. Century-expanded in the March remediation pass. Any row still carrying the two-position form is trapped by the nightly edit job and written to CUSTERR.
        D. REC_STAT_CD — CHAR(1), NOT NULL. A = active, I = inactive, D = deceased, M = merged-away. Rows at D or M are excluded from all outbound extracts without exception.

    II. Identity columns
        A. CUST_LAST_NAM — CHAR(25), NOT NULL.
        B. CUST_FIRST_NAM — CHAR(15), NOT NULL.
        C. CUST_MID_INIT — CHAR(1), NULL ALLOWED.
        D. CUST_BIRTH_DT — DATE, NULL ALLOWED. Populated at the counter only when the plan requires age verification.
        E. CUST_SSN — CHAR(9), NULL ALLOWED. Legacy column. No new writes are permitted; the entry field was removed from the enrollment screen in the prior release.

    III. Access
        A. Columns under II are governed by authorization list RXCUSTAL. Read access is granted by store role, not by user request.
        B. CUST_SSN is masked in all inquiry displays and is readable only by the Records Custodian and the Third Party Billing supervisor.
        C. Every read of CUST_SSN writes a row to the journal receiver RXAUD; receivers are retained eighteen months.

    IV. Retention and extracts
        A. Rows at REC_STAT_CD I for thirty-six months are purged in the January cycle.
        B. The weekly extract CUSTEXT carries CUST_KEY and the columns under II only in the mailing-house variant, and CUST_KEY alone in the analytic variant.
