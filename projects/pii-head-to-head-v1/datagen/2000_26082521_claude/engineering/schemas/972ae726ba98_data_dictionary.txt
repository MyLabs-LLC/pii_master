MEMORANDUM

TO:      Data Governance Committee
FROM:    Records Custodian, Operations Support
DATE:    14 March 2011
SUBJECT: Data Dictionary Excerpt — MEMBER_MASTER (MBR01), Core Host Release 2010.01
CLASS:   Internal Use Only / Restricted Distribution

1. Purpose. This excerpt documents the member identity segment of MEMBER_MASTER as it stands following the February maintenance window. It supersedes the excerpt circulated 09 November 2010.

2. Column inventory. The following columns are within scope of this excerpt.

   COLUMN NAME            TYPE           NULL  OPERATIONAL NOTE
   MBR_SEQ_ID             NUMBER(12)     NO    Surrogate key; host-assigned, never reused.
   MBR_NBR                CHAR(10)       NO    Natural key; restricted display.
   MBR_LAST_NAME          VARCHAR2(40)   NO    Restricted column (see cl. 4).
   MBR_FIRST_NAME         VARCHAR2(30)   NO    Restricted column.
   MBR_MID_INIT           CHAR(1)        YES   Sparsely populated pre-1998 conversions.
   MBR_SSN                CHAR(9)        YES   Restricted; encrypted at rest.
   MBR_DOB                DATE           YES   Restricted; used by Red Flags scoring.
   MBR_ADDR_LINE_1        VARCHAR2(60)   NO    Restricted; CASS-standardised nightly.
   MBR_ADDR_LINE_2        VARCHAR2(60)   YES   Suite/unit designator only.
   MBR_POSTAL_CD          CHAR(9)        NO    ZIP+4; hyphen not stored.
   MBR_HOME_PHONE         CHAR(10)       YES   Restricted; NPA/NXX validated on entry.
   MBR_EMAIL_ADDR         VARCHAR2(80)   YES   Restricted; verified flag held separately.
   MBR_OPEN_DT            DATE           NO    Drives retention clock (cl. 5).
   MBR_CLOSE_DT           DATE           YES   Null implies open relationship.
   MBR_STATUS_CD          CHAR(2)        NO    Domain held in CODE_TABLE 'MSTAT'.
   REC_LAST_MAINT_TS      TIMESTAMP      NO    Populated by host trigger; not editable.

3. Nullability. Columns marked NO are enforced at the database level. Application-side "required" edits are broader than the physical constraints and must not be relied upon by extract developers.

4. Access restriction. Columns annotated "Restricted" are granted by role, not by individual. Grants are held by the Records Custodian and reviewed quarterly under Part 748 Appendix A. Non-production refreshes must pass through the masking routine before any developer schema is opened; unmasked copies outside the production zone are prohibited without written waiver from the Information Security Officer.

5. Retention. Records remain live for the duration of the relationship and for seven years after MBR_CLOSE_DT, consistent with Retention Schedule RS-4.2. BSA-relevant identity elements are retained five years from the date of the last covered transaction, whichever is later. Purge candidates are staged monthly and released only on Custodian sign-off.

6. Downstream extracts. The following consume this segment: EXT_MCIF_001 (nightly, marketing datamart, restricted columns tokenised); EXT_STMT_PRINT (cycle-driven, name and address only, transmitted over managed file transfer); EXT_CRA_REPORT (monthly bureau furnishing); EXT_OFAC_SCAN (daily). No new extract may be scheduled against MEMBER_MASTER without a completed Data Release Request.

7. Change control. Requests to alter type, length or nullability route through the Change Advisory Board. Direct DDL against production is reserved to the database administration group during an approved window.
