SECTION C — ACCESS RESTRICTION
  Tier 3 columns are excluded from all ad-hoc query roles. Grants to RL_PAYSEC
  are issued for a named position, not an individual, and are re-certified each
  quarter by the Director of Compliance. Branch recruiters hold RL_RECRUIT,
  which resolves CANDIDATE_MASTER through the view V_CANDIDATE_SAFE.

SECTION D — RETENTION
  Active rows retained for the duration of the placement relationship. On
  STATUS_CODE = AR (archived), Tier 3 columns are nulled after 7 years from the
  last assignment end date. Full row purge at 10 years, subject to a legal hold
  register check by the Records Custodian.
