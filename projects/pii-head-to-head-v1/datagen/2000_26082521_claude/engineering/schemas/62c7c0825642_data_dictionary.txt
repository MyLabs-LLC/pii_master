REGISTRAR DATA DICTIONARY — ADDENDUM A
TABLE: STUDENT.MEMBER_CONTACT_HIST

PURPOSE
Row-versioned history of contact columns. One row per change; no updates in
place.

COLUMNS
HIST_KEY            | NUMBER(12)    | NO  | Sequence-populated.
MEMBER_KEY          | NUMBER(10)    | NO  | Foreign key to MEMBER_MASTER.
ADDR_TYPE_CODE      | VARCHAR2(4)   | NO  | PERM, MAIL, BILL, EMER.
ADDR_LINE1          | VARCHAR2(75)  | YES | Tier 2.
ADDR_LINE2          | VARCHAR2(75)  | YES | Tier 2.
CITY                | VARCHAR2(50)  | YES | Tier 2.
STATE_PROV_CODE     | CHAR(2)       | YES | Null for international rows.
POSTAL_CODE         | VARCHAR2(10)  | YES | Feeds the residency determination.
COUNTRY_CODE        | CHAR(3)       | YES |
PHONE_NUMBER        | VARCHAR2(20)  | YES | Tier 2.
EMAIL_ADDRESS       | VARCHAR2(128) | YES | Tier 2.
EFFECTIVE_FROM_DATE | DATE          | NO  |
EFFECTIVE_TO_DATE   | DATE          | YES | Null indicates the current row.
CHANGED_BY_ROLE     | VARCHAR2(30)  | NO  | Role title, not operator identity.

ACCESS
Read access is limited to the registrar staff role and to Internal Audit on
request. The table is not exposed to the reporting layer.

RETENTION
History rows are kept three years past the closing of the record. Emergency
contact rows (ADDR_TYPE_CODE = 'EMER') are purged at separation.

EXTRACTS
None scheduled. Point-in-time pulls for address disputes are performed by the
Records Custodian and logged on the data request form.
