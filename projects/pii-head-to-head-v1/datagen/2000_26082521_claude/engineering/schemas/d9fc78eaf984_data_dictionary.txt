Retention. Every row of this table, together with each column enumerated above,
shall be retained for a period of seven years reckoned from the value held in
ACCOUNT_CLOSED_DATE, and shall thereafter be destroyed by the scheduled purge
routine without further authorisation, save where a litigation hold has been
placed upon the account by the Office of the General Counsel, in which event the
purge routine shall bypass the affected rows until the hold is released in writing.

Access. No extract, report, or ad hoc query touching a column marked Restricted
may be created, scheduled, or amended except upon the written approval of the
Records Custodian, which approval shall record the purpose, the recipient, the
columns concerned, and the date upon which the authorisation expires.
