MEMORANDUM

TO:      Data Governance Committee; Manager of Core Systems
FROM:    Records Custodian
SUBJECT: Controlled Data Dictionary Excerpt — MEMBER_MASTER (Core Release 2016.2)
DATE:    14 March 2016
FILE:    DG-DD-2016-011

1.0  PURPOSE
1.1  This memorandum publishes the controlled excerpt of the MEMBER_MASTER table as constituted under Core Release 2016.2, superseding excerpt DG-DD-2015-047 in its entirety.
1.2  All schema change requests affecting the columns enumerated in Clause 3.0 shall be routed through the Data Governance Committee prior to promotion into the production instance.

2.0  SCOPE
2.1  This excerpt covers MEMBER_MASTER only. Companion tables (SHARE_ACCT, LOAN_ACCT, CARD_SERVICE) are documented under separate cover.
2.2  Columns designated Restricted in Clause 3.0 are governed by the institution's Gramm-Leach-Bliley safeguards program and by Regulation P notice obligations.

4.0  ACCESS RESTRICTION
4.1  Columns marked Restricted are exposed only through role MBR_SVC_FULL and role COMPLIANCE_RO. Branch teller roles receive MBR_SSN_TIN masked to the final four positions by view V_MEMBER_TELLER.
4.2  Direct table-level SELECT on MEMBER_MASTER is withdrawn from all interactive accounts. Analytical work shall be performed against the reporting views.
4.3  Quarterly entitlement recertification is performed by the Records Custodian and evidenced to Internal Audit within ten business days of quarter close.

5.0  RETENTION
5.1  Rows bearing a populated MBR_CLOSE_DT are retained for five years from that date, after which the Restricted columns are overwritten by the purge utility and the row is retained in truncated form for statistical continuity.
5.2  MBR_OFAC_SCAN_DT and MBR_ID_VERIF_CD are retained five years beyond relationship termination in satisfaction of BSA recordkeeping requirements, notwithstanding Clause 5.1.

6.0  DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
6.1  Nightly extract EXT-CORE-014 delivers MBR_NBR, MBR_STATUS_CD, MBR_BRANCH_CD and MBR_OPEN_DT to the data warehouse. No Restricted column is included.
6.2  Extract EXT-STMT-003 delivers name and address columns to the statement print vendor under the executed service agreement; transmission is by SFTP with key rotation each June.
6.3  Extract EXT-MKTG-009 delivers MBR_EMAIL_ADDR and MBR_MOBILE_PHONE to the marketing platform for members not bearing an opt-out indicator. Suppression logic is reviewed by Compliance semi-annually.
6.4  No extract not enumerated above may be scheduled without written authorization of the Chief Information Officer.

7.0  EFFECTIVE DATE
7.1  This excerpt takes effect upon the promotion of Core Release 2016.2 and remains in force until superseded.
