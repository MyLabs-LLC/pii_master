3. Classification legend. A — restricted. B — confidential. C — internal. D — unclassified.

4. Name columns are stored as keyed. No parsing, standardization or householding is performed within the database. Any such processing is the responsibility of the receiving application.

5. Class A columns are revoked from PUBLIC and from role MSV_USER. SELECT is granted to role MSV_COMPL only. Direct grants to individual accounts are prohibited. Requests for exception are logged by Data Administration and reviewed quarterly.

6. Class A columns are masked in all views prefixed MSV_V_ and are omitted from the column list of every extract cursor. Where a downstream system requires a stable person-level key, MEMBER_ID is to be supplied in lieu of MEMBER_SSN.

7. Downstream extracts of record:
   a. MSVX100, nightly, to the escrow accounting interface. Carries clauses 2 columns of class C and D only.
   b. MSVX250, monthly, to the outside fulfillment vendor for renewal mailings. Carries name, address and ZIP columns under the confidentiality rider on file. No class A column is carried.
   c. MSVX900, annual, to the actuarial file. Age is carried as a derived integer computed at extract time; MEMBER_DOB is not carried.

8. Retention. Rows are retained on line for thirty-six months following TERM_DT, thereafter archived to the offline member history file for a further four years. Class A columns are nulled at archive. Purge is executed by job MSVPRG99 with a control report to the Records Custodian.

9. Any change to the columns named in clause 2, including widening, is a controlled change and requires an approved DA change request prior to migration.
