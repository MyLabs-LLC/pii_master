3.0 ACCESS RESTRICTION
    3.1 Columns 6, 7 and 9 are readable only through package PKG_ID_VERIFY,
        which logs caller, timestamp and business reason to ID_ACCESS_LOG.
    3.2 Column 16 is excluded from all grants pending its removal in 7.5.
    3.3 Developers hold no privileges in CISPRD. Defect analysis is performed
        against the scrambled copy in CISDEV, refreshed on request.

4.0 RETENTION
    4.1 Base retention: six years past OCCUP_END_DT (schedule LG-4200).
    4.2 Deposit-related identity columns: nulled 12 months after deposit refund
        or application to final bill, whichever is earlier.
    4.3 Partitions older than the retention horizon are dropped, not deleted.

5.0 DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
    5.1 EXT_DEPOSIT_SCORE — weekly to the credit bureau interface; carries
        columns 3, 4, 6, 7, 13 through 15. Encrypted channel, vendor-held key.
    5.2 EXT_LANDLORD_RVT — monthly reversion notices; columns 3, 4, 13, 14, 15.
    5.3 EXT_DW_NIGHTLY — warehouse load; identity columns suppressed at source.
    5.4 Any new extract requires a completed DA-31 and steward countersignature.
