--------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ACCESS RESTRICTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 Columns marked Restricted are exposed only through views. Base table
    authority is limited to the database administration profile and the
    nightly batch profile.

2.2 TAX_ID_NBR carries an additional control: reads are logged to the
    security journal and reconciled weekly by the Records Custodian. Any
    read outside a documented business process is reported to the
    Information Security Officer within one business day.

2.3 Test and training environments receive masked copies only. The masking
    routine substitutes format-preserving values for all Restricted columns
    and nulls DRIVERS_LIC_NBR entirely.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. RETENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 Rows persist while the underlying membership is open.

3.2 On closure, the row is retained seven years from the closure date, then
    archived to offline media for a further three years before destruction.

3.3 FORMER_NAME and DRIVERS_LIC_NBR are purged at closure plus five years,
    ahead of the parent row, per the Compliance Officer's standing
    instruction of 11 January 2011.

3.4 Litigation holds suspend all purge activity for the affected rows. Holds
    are recorded in the hold register maintained by the General Counsel's
    office.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 STMTX01 — statement print and mail vendor. Name and mailing columns.
    Cycle-driven. Encrypted transmission, key rotated annually.

4.2 MCIFX03 — marketing datamart load. Nightly. Restricted identifiers are
    replaced with a surrogate token before the file leaves the host.

4.3 CRAX02 — consumer reporting furnishing. Monthly. Includes identity
    columns as required by the Metro 2 layout.

4.4 CIPX07 — identity verification vendor. Event-driven at account opening.

4.5 No extract may be added, altered or redirected without a Data Release
    Request approved by both the Records Custodian and the Compliance
    Officer. Ad hoc query output containing Restricted columns is treated as
    an extract for this purpose.
