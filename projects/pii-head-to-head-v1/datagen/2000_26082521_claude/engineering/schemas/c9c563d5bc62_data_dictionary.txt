SUBJECT: Data Dictionary Excerpt — CUSTMAST (Customer Master), Release 4.2

The following excerpt covers the personal-data bearing segment of CUSTMAST only. Columns outside the range CUST_ID through EXTR_SUPR_FLG are documented in the full dictionary binder held by the Records Custodian. Direct any correction requests to the DBA Group through the standard change form.

Access note 1: Columns marked Restricted are readable only under the security group PROD.CUSTMAST.PII.READ. Application programs requiring these columns must bind through the restricted plan. Ad hoc query IDs are not eligible.

Access note 2: CTC_DOB_DT and CTC_TAX_ID_NBR are write-once from the account opening screen and are not displayed on any inquiry panel. Amendment requires a signed correction form countersigned by the Records Custodian.

Retention note 3: PURGE_ELIG_DT is calculated as LAST_ACTV_DT plus seven years for closed accounts. The quarterly purge utility clears restricted columns to low-values and retains the balance of the row for statistical use. Rows carrying an unresolved claim indicator are excluded from purge until released by Legal.

Note 4: Where EXTR_SUPR_FLG is set, the row is withheld from the marketing extract and from the carrier partner file. It is not withheld from billing, EDI acknowledgement, or the operations routing extract, which remain necessary to perform the contract.

Downstream extracts drawing on this segment: nightly billing interface, weekly EDI 810 companion file, monthly marketing extract, quarterly carrier partner file. Only the billing interface receives restricted columns, and only BILL_ADR_LN1 through BILL_POST_CD.
