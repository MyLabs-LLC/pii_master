DATA DICTIONARY EXCERPT — PARATRANSIT REGISTRANT FILE (PARREG)

1. PARREG is the registrant file supporting scheduling under the complementary paratransit service. Its columns are: REG_CTL_NBR, DECIMAL(9,0), NOT NULL, primary key; REG_LAST_NAM and REG_FRST_NAM, VARCHAR(30) and VARCHAR(20), both NOT NULL; DOB_DTE, DATE, NOT NULL; PICKUP_ADDR_LN_1 and PICKUP_ADDR_LN_2, VARCHAR(40), the second nullable; PICKUP_ZIP_CDE, CHAR(9), NOT NULL; TEL_PRIM_NBR and TEL_ALT_NBR, CHAR(10), the second nullable; MOBIL_AID_CDE, CHAR(2), NOT NULL, domain in PARCODE table 03; ATTEND_REQ_IND, CHAR(1), NOT NULL WITH DEFAULT; EMERG_CNTC_NAM and EMERG_CNTC_TEL_NBR, VARCHAR(50) and CHAR(10), both nullable; ELIG_TYP_CDE, CHAR(1), NOT NULL; ELIG_EXP_DTE, DATE, NOT NULL; REG_STS_CDE, CHAR(1), NOT NULL.

2. The file is closed to all groups except PAR$SCHD and PAR$ADMN. Contract carriers receive only the manifest extract produced by job PARX010, which carries pickup address, TEL_PRIM_NBR, MOBIL_AID_CDE, and ATTEND_REQ_IND for the service day in question, and which carries neither DOB_DTE nor the emergency contact columns. Carrier files are transmitted the evening prior and the carrier is under contractual obligation to destroy them within thirty days.

3. Registrant rows are retained for the duration of eligibility and for four years thereafter. Manifest history in PARMANF is retained two years for complaint and on-time-performance investigation, after which it is summarized and the identifying columns dropped. Requests for a registrant's travel history are directed to the Records Custodian and are not answered from the scheduling terminals.
