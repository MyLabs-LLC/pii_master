SECTION A — APPLICABILITY
This excerpt is issued under the Department's records administration procedure and describes only those columns enumerated in Section B. Columns not enumerated remain governed by the master dictionary maintained by the Records Custodian and are not the subject of this instrument.

SECTION B — COLUMN REGISTER

COLUMN NAME              TYPE           NULL   OPERATIONAL NOTE

CUST_ALT_NAME            VARCHAR2(120)  YES    Doing-business-as or authorized alias.

BILL_EMAIL_ADDR          VARCHAR2(160)  YES    Suppressed in all extracts to outside parties.
BILL_PHONE_PRIMARY       CHAR(10)       YES    Digits only; formatting applied at presentation.
ACCT_OPEN_DT             DATE           NO     Immutable after posting.
ACCT_STATUS_CD           CHAR(2)        NO     Domain maintained by Billing Operations.

SECTION C — ACCESS RESTRICTION
Read access is limited to the roles CIS_BILLING_CLERK and CIS_SUPERVISOR. Column-level grants upon CUST_TAXID_LAST4, CUST_DOB, and BILL_EMAIL_ADDR shall issue only upon the written authorization of the Records Custodian, and shall be reviewed semiannually.

SECTION D — RETENTION
Rows are retained seven (7) (7) years following the date recorded in ACCT_CLOSE_DT, whereupon disposition proceeds under the approved schedule. Suspension of disposition occurs upon notice of litigation hold.

SECTION E — DOWNSTREAM EXTRACTS
(1) Nightly feed to the Collections work queue; name and address columns transmitted, identifiers masked.
(2) Monthly feed to Meter Services routing; service-address columns only.
(3) Quarterly aggregate to the Rate Study file; no restricted column included.
