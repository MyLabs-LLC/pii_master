SECTION III — OPERATIONAL NOTES
  1. PT_SSN is populated for approximately half of active registrants. It is
     not a reliable join key and shall not be used as one.
  2. PT_ZIP is truncated at load. The nine-digit form is not carried in this
     table.
  3. Updates are applied on-line only. There is no batch demographic update.
  4. History rows are written by trigger on any change to a Class A column.

SECTION IV — RESTRICTION AND RETENTION
  1. RESTRICT_CD values 'CD', 'FP' and 'BH' invoke suppression. Suppressed rows
     do not appear in any view, report or extract, and are not counted in
     denominators supplied to outside agencies.
  2. Access to Class A columns is limited to clinical staff sign-ons and to the
     Records Custodian. The Information Systems staff hold no standing access.
  3. Demographic rows are retained ten years past PT_LAST_SEEN_DT, then
     archived. Archive tapes are held in the departmental vault.

SECTION V — EXTRACTS IN EFFECT
  E-1  Reportable disease transmittal to the state, daily, Class A columns
       permitted under the state health code only.
  E-2  Clinic utilization file to the Bureau of Planning, monthly, Class C
       columns plus PT_ZIP and banded age.
  E-3  Outside laboratory interface, real time, Class A columns limited to
       those required for specimen identification.

  Requests for additional extracts are made on Form IS-33 and are acted upon by
  the Committee. No extract is created on verbal authorization.
