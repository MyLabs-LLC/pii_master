1. Jurisdiction. The complaint was received by the District office on February 9, 2011 on the standard intake form (Form HR-1312.3-A, rev. 08/09) and was date-stamped by the Records Custodian on the same day. The Compliance Officer reviewed the allegations against the categories enumerated in Board Policy 1312.3 and 5 CCR §4610 and determined on February 11, 2011 that the matter fell within the scope of the Uniform Complaint Procedure as a general complaint of employee conduct, and did not present an allegation of unlawful discrimination requiring referral under AR 4030. Written acknowledgement of receipt was mailed to the address of record on February 14, 2011.

2. Notice. Notice of investigation was served on the respondent on February 15, 2011 and on the exclusive representative on the same date pursuant to the collective bargaining agreement, Article XI, Section 4. The respondent acknowledged receipt on February 17, 2011 and elected representation. No request for extension of the sixty-day investigative period was made by either party.

3. Evidence gathered. The Compliance Officer conducted six interviews between February 22 and March 18, 2011: the complainant, the respondent, two classified staff assigned to the same site, the site administrator, and one former site administrator. Interview summaries were prepared contemporaneously and returned to each witness for correction; four of six were returned with signatures, and two were closed as unreturned after the fourteen-day window expired on April 1, 2011. Documentary evidence consisted of the site sign-in log for the period at issue, two written referrals filed under the site discipline protocol, and the respondent's prior evaluation of record dated May 2010, which contained no comparable finding.

4. Analysis. The allegation as framed required a determination whether the conduct described departed from the professional standards articulated in Board Policy 4119.21 and, if so, whether the departure was of a degree warranting formal action under Education Code §44932. The evidence established that an interaction of the general character alleged occurred on or about February 3, 2011. The accounts diverged materially as to tone, duration, and the presence of third parties. The two corroborating accounts obtained were partial, in that neither witness observed the entirety of the interaction, and one placed the interaction outside the window described by the complainant.

5. Determination. The Compliance Officer found the allegation partially substantiated. The finding is limited to a determination that the interaction occurred and that the manner of the interaction was inconsistent with Board Policy 4119.21. The record does not support a finding of conduct within the meaning of Education Code §44932(a).

6. Disposition and appeal rights. A written decision was issued to both parties on April 21, 2011, within the sixty-day period prescribed by 5 CCR §4631(e). The decision directed the site administrator to conduct a documented conference with the respondent, memorialized as a memorandum of expectation and not as a disciplinary instrument. The complainant was advised of the right of appeal to the California Department of Education within fifteen days of receipt. No appeal was filed and the file was closed on May 9, 2011. Retention: five years from closure per the District records retention schedule, Class 2 — Optional.
