MEMORANDUM — OCCUPATIONAL INJURY AND RETURN TO WORK

Case reference:      OH/2004/0592

Employee name:       [SUPPRESSED]
Payroll number:      ████████
Occupational health record no.:  [SUPPRESSED]
Date of report:      7 December 2004

1. Incident

1.1 On 4 October 2004 a warehouse operative sustained a soft tissue injury to the lower back while manually handling a part-collapsed pallet in the returns bay.

1.2 The incident was entered in the accident book the same day and reported to the Health and Safety Adviser at 15:10.

2. Process Steps

2.1 4 October 2004 — First aid administered on site; employee stood down from duty for the remainder of the shift.

2.2 6 October 2004 — Fit note received certifying unfitness for work for two weeks.

2.3 18 October 2004 — Referral made to the occupational health provider under reference OH/2004/0592.

2.4 8 November 2004 — Occupational health opinion received, recommending phased return with a manual handling restriction of ten kilograms for six weeks.

2.5 22 November 2004 — Return-to-work meeting held with the Warehouse Manager. Phased plan agreed at four hours per day rising to full hours over three weeks.

3. Adjudication and Findings

3.1 The investigation concludes that the pallet had been accepted into the returns bay in a damaged condition and had not been re-wrapped or segregated in accordance with the returns procedure.

3.2 No individual fault is attributed. The contributing factor is recorded as a procedural gap at the point of goods receipt.

3.3 The restriction expires on 20 December 2004, at which point a review is to be scheduled. Unrestricted duties are not to be resumed before that review is recorded on file.

4. Actions

4.1 The returns procedure is to be reissued with a mandatory segregation step, owner: the Health and Safety Adviser, target date 31 January 2005.

4.2 Toolbox briefings on damaged pallet handling are to be delivered across all shifts before the end of the current period.
