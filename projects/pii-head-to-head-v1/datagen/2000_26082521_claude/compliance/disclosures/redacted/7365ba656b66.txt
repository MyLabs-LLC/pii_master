1.  PURPOSE. This action transfers designation as Records Custodian for
    the student education records of the institution, as that term is used
    in Registrar Procedure RP-099 and in the annual notification issued
    under 34 CFR 99.7.

2.  BACKGROUND. The prior designee separated from the office effective
    17 September 2004. Custody has been held on an interim basis by the
    University Registrar since that date, an arrangement contemplated by
    RP-099 §4 for a period not exceeding thirty days.

3.  REVIEW STEPS COMPLETED.

      3.1  Position description reviewed by Human Resources ... 07 Sep 2004
      3.2  Classification confirmed, grade AD-11 ............... 14 Sep 2004
      3.3  Background verification returned, no exception ..... 21 Sep 2004
      3.4  Confidentiality undertaking executed, Form C-3 ..... 24 Sep 2004
      3.5  Systems access request approved, Ticket IT-41902 ... 28 Sep 2004

4.  SCOPE OF AUTHORITY. The designee is authorized to (a) approve release
    of education records under consent or statutory exception; (b) certify
    transcripts and enrollment; (c) approve corrections following a hearing
    under RP-101; and (d) maintain the disclosure register required at
    34 CFR 99.32. Authority to waive fees remains with the Registrar.

5.  ACCESS PROVISIONING. Role SIS-CUSTODIAN-01 replaces role SIS-TRAN-04.
    The superseded role shall be revoked by Information Technology no later
    than the effective date. Revocation is confirmed on Ticket IT-41902.
