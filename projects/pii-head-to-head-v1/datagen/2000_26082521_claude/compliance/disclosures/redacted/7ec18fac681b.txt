STATEMENT OF DISPOSITION

The grievance asserts that the Department, in assigning emergency call-out work
on eleven occasions between 2 January and 5 February 2011, departed from the
rotational seniority roster prescribed by Article XI, Section 3, of the
collective bargaining agreement then in force, and thereby deprived the grievant
of overtime opportunity properly accruing to the grievant.

Upon review of the call-out logs, the dispatch tapes and the testimony adduced
at the Step Two hearing, the Hearing Officer finds that on four of the eleven
occasions specified the roster was not consulted, the Shift Supervisor having
proceeded instead upon a list of personal availability maintained informally.
That practice is contrary to the plain language of the Agreement and is directed
to cease forthwith. On the remaining seven occasions the departure is found
justified by the emergency-skill exception at Article XI, Section 3(c).

The remedy awarded is payment of four call-out minimums at the applicable
premium rate, together with restoration of the grievant's position in the
rotation. The Department's answer at Step One is modified accordingly. This
disposition is issued without prejudice and shall not be cited as precedent.
