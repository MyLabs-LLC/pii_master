1. On 12 February 2004 this Office received a written request seeking inspection of the complete education record maintained under the requesting party's name, together with a request that two entries in the academic history be amended.

2. The request was logged the same business day and assigned the file reference shown above. Receipt was acknowledged by first-class mail on 13 February 2004 to the mailing address of record then on file.

3. Under institutional policy the Office is required to permit inspection within forty-five (45) calendar days of receipt. The controlling deadline was calculated as 28 March 2004.

4. Inspection was scheduled for 2 March 2004 in the reading room of the Registrar's Office. The requesting party attended and was supervised throughout by a member of the records staff. No copies were released at that sitting, consistent with the policy then in force limiting reproduction where an outstanding financial obligation is recorded.

5. The two contested entries were identified at inspection as (a) a grade recorded for a course taken in the fall term of 2002, and (b) the effective date of a change of major.

6. As to entry (a), the Office determined that a challenge to the substance of an assigned grade is not within the scope of the records amendment procedure and must be pursued under the academic grievance procedure administered by the college of enrollment. The requesting party was so advised in writing on 9 March 2004.

7. As to entry (b), the Office reviewed the underlying change-of-major form, the departmental transmittal, and the term-by-term enrollment file. The transmittal was found to have been posted one term later than the date of departmental approval.

8. A correction to entry (b) was approved on 17 March 2004 and posted to the academic history on 19 March 2004. A notation of correction was entered in the audit log under transaction code AH-COR-0331.

9. The requesting party was notified of the disposition of both entries by letter dated 22 March 2004, which set out the right to request a hearing on any portion of the record the Office declined to amend.

10. No hearing request was received within the thirty (30) day period allowed. The file was closed on 26 April 2004.

11. Any statement of disagreement subsequently submitted shall be filed with the education record and released with it whenever the contested portion is disclosed.

12. This determination is certified as a true record of the actions taken by this Office in the above-referenced file.
