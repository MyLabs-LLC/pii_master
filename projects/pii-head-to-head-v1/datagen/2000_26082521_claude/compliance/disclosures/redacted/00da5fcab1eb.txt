BASIS OF APPEAL
Applicant contests the trip-by-trip condition limiting paratransit use to
periods when the heat index exceeds 90 degrees or when accumulated snow or ice
obstructs the path of travel. Applicant asserts that fatigue and balance
symptoms are episodic and not reliably predicted by weather.

PANEL FINDINGS
The functional assessment of 09 August 2016 recorded a sustained walking
distance below the fixed-route access threshold on two of three trials. The
treating clinician's statement, received 20 September 2016, describes symptom
variability inconsistent with a weather-triggered condition. The panel finds
the conditional restriction not supported by the record.

DETERMINATION
Appeal GRANTED. Unconditional eligibility, valid three years from the date
below. Service to commence within five business days.
