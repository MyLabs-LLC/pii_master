  NARRATIVE
      The crew was engaged in scheduled fastener renewal under Track Bulletin
      96-338, which established a four-hour work window with adjacent-track
      traffic suspended. The mechanized rail lifter assigned to the gang was
      reported out of service at the start of shift and no substitute was
      dispatched. Rail positioning proceeded by hand with a three-person lift.
      The employee reported acute onset of lower back pain on the second lift
      and ceased work. The employee was walked to the roadway access point and
      transported by the supervisor's vehicle to the designated occupational
      clinic, arriving at 03:40.

  CORRECTIVE ACTION
      1.  Root cause identified as the dispatch of a work gang without the
          mechanized lifting equipment specified in the job briefing package.
      2.  General Order 96-22 amended effective 01 December 1996 to require the
          supervisor to suspend the fastener renewal task, rather than proceed
          manually, where the specified lifting equipment is unavailable.
      3.  Equipment availability now confirmed at the pre-shift job briefing and
          recorded on Form WS-14.
      4.  Case reviewed at the Departmental Safety Committee, minute 96-11-04.
