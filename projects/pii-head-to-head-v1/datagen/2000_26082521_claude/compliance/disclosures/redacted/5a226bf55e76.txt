II. CLAUSES

    1.  The claimant contends that seventeen hours of authorised
        overtime worked in the pay periods ending 02 October 2016 and
        09 October 2016 were paid at the standard hourly rate rather
        than at the enhanced rate provided for by clause 6.2 of the
        assignment schedule.

    2.  The claimant further contends that a deduction of £48.00
        described on the payslip as "equipment" was made without
        written authority.

    3.  The dispute was raised through the branch on 21 October 2016
        and escalated to central payroll on 24 October 2016 in
        accordance with the two-stage procedure.

    4.  Records examined comprised: the signed timesheets for both
        periods; the client's authorisation log; the assignment
        schedule as varied on 12 September 2016; the payslips as
        issued; and the equipment issue register held at the branch.

    5.  As to the first contention, the enhanced rate under clause 6.2
        is triggered by hours worked beyond thirty-nine in a single
        week. The timesheets record forty-four and forty-one hours
        respectively. The enhancement was therefore due on five hours
        in the first period and two hours in the second, being seven
        hours in total rather than the seventeen claimed. The
        remainder of the hours claimed fell below the threshold.

    6.  The underpayment at clause 5 arose from a mapping error in the
        rate table applied to the client account following the
        variation of 12 September 2016. The error was systemic and
        affected the account generally, not this claimant alone.

    7.  As to the second contention, the equipment issue register
        records the issue of protective footwear but contains no
        signature in the authorisation column. No separate written
        authority has been produced. The deduction accordingly fails
        for want of authority.

III. DETERMINATION

    8.  The first contention is upheld in part, to the extent of seven
        hours. Arrears to be paid at the enhanced differential in the
        next available pay run.

    9.  The second contention is upheld in full. The sum of £48.00 is
        to be refunded without deduction.

   10.  Interest is not payable, the correction falling within the
        ninety-day window under clause 11.4.

   11.  The rate table for the client account is to be re-mapped and a
        retrospective audit of all associates on the account for the
        period 12 September 2016 to 30 November 2016 is directed, to
        report by 20 January 2017.

   12.  The claimant is advised of the right to seek external review
        within three months of this determination.
