- 1. The loss was reported to the first-notice unit on 3 March 2021 at 09:41 under intake script FNOL-AUTO-2. The reporting party described a rear-impact collision at a signalised intersection during the afternoon commute, with both vehicles drivable and no ambulance dispatched to the scene.
- 2. Coverage was confirmed on 4 March 2021 against form PA-0001 (04-19) with endorsements PA-2110 and PA-4472 attached. The policy was in force on the date of loss with premium paid through the current instalment cycle. No lapse, reinstatement or mid-term cancellation appears in the billing history.
- 3. Liability was investigated between 5 March and 22 March 2021. The file contains a recorded statement from the insured driver (transcript ref. RS-2021-0388), a recorded statement from the adverse driver (transcript ref. RS-2021-0391), the responding agency's collision report, and eleven photographs of vehicle damage indexed PH-01 through PH-11.
- 4. Liability was accepted at one hundred per cent against the insured on 24 March 2021. The adjudication note records that the physical damage pattern, the point of rest of both vehicles and the adverse driver's account were mutually consistent, and that no comparative fault argument was supportable on the evidence gathered.
- 5. Property damage was appraised on 29 March 2021 by an independent appraiser under vendor agreement VA-3306. The vehicle was declared a constructive total loss. Actual cash value was determined by a market valuation report (ref. MVR-21-8802) and settled on 12 April 2021, less the applicable deductible under Coverage D.
- 6. A bodily injury demand was received on 6 May 2021 supported by treatment records covering eleven weeks of conservative care. The reserve was strengthened on 10 May 2021 following medical review under protocol MR-7. Settlement authority was granted at the supervisory tier on 2 June 2021.
