- Committee findings
  - The applicant satisfies the twelve-month physical presence requirement.
  - The applicant satisfies four of the five secondary indicia enumerated in Board Policy 3-201(d).
  - The applicant was claimed as a dependent on an out-of-state return for the most recent tax year, which raises the rebuttable presumption of derivative domicile.
  - The applicant rebutted that presumption with documentation of self-support exceeding fifty percent of annual expenses, including wage records and a statement of independent financial responsibility.
  - Enrollment in the preceding academic year does not, standing alone, defeat the claim, the applicant having been employed full time in the state prior to matriculation.

  - Classification flagged for routine reverification at the point of any enrollment gap exceeding two consecutive terms.
  - Decision recorded as non-precedential; committee vote 3–0.
  - File retained under the residency schedule for five years from the last term of enrollment.
