2. INCIDENT PARTICULARS

2.1 Date of incident: 27 February 1997. Time recorded as 05:42, being the second hour of the early shift.
2.2 The claimant was engaged in the manual transfer of palletised goods from a docked trailer to the inbound sortation belt.
2.3 The dock levelling plate is recorded in the maintenance log (asset ref. DK-11-B) as having been reported non-compliant on 9 January 1997 and again on 3 February 1997. No corrective work order was raised against either report.
2.4 The claimant sustained a lower-back injury while manoeuvring a load across the levelling plate. The injury was reported to the shift supervisor at 06:10 and to the depot occupational health desk at 07:55.

3. PROCEDURAL HISTORY

3.1 First notification of loss: 14 March 1997.
3.2 Acknowledgement issued to claimant: 18 March 1997 (letter ref. ACK/97/0442).
3.3 Statement taken by the Field Investigator: 2 April 1997.
3.4 Independent medical examination arranged: 21 April 1997; report received 6 May 1997 under ref. IME/97/118.
3.5 Reserve set at first review: 12 May 1997.
3.6 File referred to the Liability Panel: 3 June 1997.

4. FINDINGS OF THE LIABILITY PANEL

4.1 The Panel accepts that the levelling plate was in a defective condition at the material time and that the defect was known to depot management.
4.2 The Panel finds no evidence that the claimant departed from the manual handling method set out in Standing Instruction MH-04.
4.3 The Panel notes that refresher training in manual handling had lapsed at the depot; the last recorded session predates the incident by nineteen months.
4.4 Contributory negligence is not asserted.

5. ADJUDICATION

5.1 Liability is admitted in full on behalf of the contractor.
5.2 Quantum to be assessed on receipt of the consolidated loss-of-earnings schedule from Payroll (requested 9 June 1997, outstanding at date of this note).
5.3 The reserve is to be reviewed at the next quarterly sitting.

6. ACTIONS ARISING

6.1 Work order to be raised against asset DK-11-B within seven days of this determination.
6.2 The depot is to reinstate the manual handling refresher cycle and confirm completion to the Safety Coordinator by 30 September 1997.

Determination recorded by the Claims Adjudicator, 11 June 1997.
