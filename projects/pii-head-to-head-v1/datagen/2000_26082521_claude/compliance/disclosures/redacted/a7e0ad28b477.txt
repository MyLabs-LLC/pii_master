  * Determination: the allegation is sustained in part. The conduct complained of, insofar as it is established by the weight of the evidence, contravenes the agency's standards of conduct but does not, in the judgment of the Reviewing Authority, rise to the threshold of unlawful harassment as defined by the applicable statute.
  * Corrective action was taken with respect to the respondent and is recorded in the respondent's file under separate reference; the particulars thereof are not disclosed to the complainant beyond confirmation that action was taken.
  * No adverse action of any description was taken against the complainant at any point in the pendency of this matter, and the anti-retaliation provisions were reiterated to the complainant in writing on 31 July 2004.
  * A follow-up contact is diarised for 30 October 2004 to confirm the absence of retaliatory conduct, and this matter shall not be treated as concluded for retention purposes until that contact is recorded.
