I.  MATTER PRESENTED

    1.  Appeal from a Fifteen-Day Notice of Discontinuance issued 18 October 2021
        for an arrears balance of $1,482.36 accrued over eleven billing cycles.
    2.  The appellant seeks continuation of service under the medical hardship
        protections of Tariff Rule 12, and in the alternative a levelized
        payment plan under Rule 14.

II. FINDINGS

    3.  Service has been continuously rendered at the premises since 2016. No
        prior discontinuance has issued.
    4.  A completed Form MC-3 was received on 02 November 2021 certifying that a
        member of the household relies on electrically powered durable medical
        equipment. The certifying practitioner's licence was verified against the
        state registry on 04 November 2021 and is current.
    5.  The certification is timely as to the notice under appeal but, under Rule
        12.4, confers protection for sixty days only, renewable twice in any
        twelve-month period.
    6.  The appellant's declared household income places the household below the
        threshold for the Winter Moratorium Program, for which no application has
        been filed.

III. DETERMINATION

    7.  The Notice of Discontinuance dated 18 October 2021 is STAYED through
        08 January 2022.
    8.  The Authority shall enroll the account in the Life-Support Registry and
        shall provide the priority-restoration flag applicable to that registry.
    9.  Arrears shall be amortized over twenty-four months at the zero-interest
        rate prescribed by Rule 14.2, the first installment due with the January
        2022 bill.
    10. The Customer Assistance Office shall transmit a Winter Moratorium
        application to the correspondence address of record within five business
        days and shall document receipt or non-receipt in the docket.
    11. Failure to renew the medical certification before expiry, or default on
        two consecutive installments, restores the Authority's discontinuance
        remedies upon fresh notice.

IV. REVIEW

    12. This determination is final agency action. Review lies to the Utilities
        Board on the written record within twenty days of service.
