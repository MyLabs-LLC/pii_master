The injured worker, employed by the Authority in the classification of Track Maintainer and assigned at all material times to the Maintenance of Way Department, sustained an injury arising out of and in the course of employment on the date stated above while engaged in the removal of tie plates in the vicinity of Milepost 14.2 on the Western Subdivision. The mechanism of injury, as reported to the Supervisor of Track and as subsequently recorded upon the Employer's First Report of Injury, was a lifting event without mechanical assistance, the worker reporting immediate onset of lumbar symptoms.

The worker was transported to an occupational medicine facility on the date of injury and was released with a work restriction prohibiting lifting in excess of ten pounds. The Authority, being unable to accommodate the said restriction within the worker's home classification, placed the worker in a temporary transitional assignment in the Stores Department effective 12 April 1999, which assignment continued until 03 June 1999, on which date the treating physician imposed a total temporary disability status. Indemnity benefits commenced accordingly.

A controversion was considered and rejected by this office, there being no substantial evidence to rebut the presumption of compensability. The claim is accepted as compensable in full.

An independent medical examination was conducted on 21 July 1999, the report of which is filed at tab 7 of the file. The examiner concurred in the diagnosis and opined that maximum medical improvement had not been attained and would not be anticipated prior to the fourth quarter of the calendar year. Vocational rehabilitation referral was recommended and has been effected under referral number VR-99-0812.

RESERVE RECOMMENDATION: Indemnity reserve to be increased to reflect an anticipated period of total temporary disability extending through 31 December 1999, with medical reserve to be increased to accommodate the projected course of conservative treatment and one anticipated surgical consultation. Expense reserve unchanged.
