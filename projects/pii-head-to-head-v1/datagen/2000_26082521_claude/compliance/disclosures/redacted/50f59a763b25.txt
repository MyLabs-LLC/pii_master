2. MATTER UNDER CONSIDERATION

2.1 Breach of Schedule 4 of the Operating Handbook (Tachograph Discipline), in that on three separate trunk runs the recording equipment was operated without a chart inserted for part of the driving period.

2.2 Runs affected: 11 August 1998 (run code TR-224), 18 August 1998 (run code TR-231), 26 August 1998 (run code TR-247).

3. INVESTIGATION

3.1 Chart audit conducted by the Compliance Clerk on 2 September 1998 under audit cycle Q3/98.
3.2 Discrepancy notice issued to the operating centre on 4 September 1998.
3.3 Investigatory meeting held 10 September 1998, chaired by the Transport Manager. The employee attended and was accompanied by a representative of the recognised union.
3.4 The employee's account, recorded in the meeting note, was that the charts had been inserted but had failed to register owing to a known fault in the head unit of vehicle fleet no. [SUPPRESSED].
3.5 The vehicle defect book was examined. Two entries in the relevant period refer to intermittent recording failure; neither entry was signed off by the workshop.

4. DISCIPLINARY HEARING

4.1 Notice of hearing issued 15 September 1998; hearing convened 23 September 1998.
4.2 Panel: the Depot General Manager (chair), a representative of Human Resources, and a note-taker.
4.3 The employee submitted that the fault was reported verbally to the shift fitter on each occasion.
4.4 The panel accepted that a mechanical fault was present but found that the employee did not complete the written defect entry required by paragraph 4.2 of Schedule 4 on any of the three occasions.

5. OUTCOME

5.1 A first written warning is recorded, to remain live on file for twelve months from 23 September 1998.
5.2 The employee is to undertake tachograph refresher instruction before the next trunk allocation.
5.3 A separate engineering instruction is raised requiring the head unit to be replaced and the defect book entries to be closed by the workshop.

6. APPEAL

6.1 The right of appeal was explained at the close of the hearing. Any appeal is to be lodged in writing within five working days.
6.2 No appeal received as at 5 October 1998. File closed to the Records Custodian on that date.
