1. Adjudication History

1.1 The claim was received on 19 March 1996 and entered pended status on 21 March 1996 pending confirmation of eligibility for the date of service, the eligibility file having shown a lapse between 1 February 1996 and 29 February 1996.

1.2 An eligibility inquiry was transmitted to the Eligibility Determination Unit on 22 March 1996 under trace 96-ED-2207. Response was received on 8 April 1996 confirming continuous coverage, the February lapse having been an artifact of a delayed recertification posting.

1.3 The claim was released from pend on 9 April 1996 and adjudicated on 11 April 1996. Line one was allowed at the Tier 2 fee schedule rate effective for the date of service. Line two was denied under edit code D-41, service not covered under the applicable benefit plan.

2. Reconsideration

2.1 The provider filed a request for reconsideration of the line two denial on 6 May 1996, asserting that the service was ancillary to the allowed procedure. Reconsideration was docketed as RC-96-0831.

2.2 The Medical Review Officer reviewed the submitted operative note on 28 May 1996 and upheld the denial, finding that edit code D-41 applies without regard to the ancillary character of the service, the exclusion being categorical under the plan document then in force.

2.3 The provider was notified of the reconsideration outcome on 4 June 1996. No further appeal was received within the sixty day window, and the claim reached final status on 6 August 1996.
