ADJUDICATION NOTES

Application submitted over the counter with the prescribed fee. Initial review
found the affidavit properly executed but the documentary support insufficient,
as only one qualifying record was tendered where the correction category requires
two established more than five years prior to the application date.

A deficiency notice was mailed allowing thirty days for cure. The applicant
tendered a second qualifying record within the period. Both documents were examined
against the original certificate and found consistent with the correction sought.

The Local Registrar determined that the discrepancy arose from a transcription
error at the time of original registration and is therefore within the clerical
correction authority rather than requiring court order. The amendment was approved
and the certificate reissued with the statutory amendment notation. Transmittal to
the State Registrar completed within the required interval. Original application
and supporting affidavits retained permanently by the Records Custodian.
