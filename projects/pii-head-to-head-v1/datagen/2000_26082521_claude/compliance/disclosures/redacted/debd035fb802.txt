1. JURISDICTION AND FILING

1.1 The claim was presented to the Office of the City Clerk on 3 May 2021 and date-stamped at intake under sequence CLK-IN-2021-1188.

1.2 The Authority finds the claim timely presented within the six-month period prescribed by the State Tort Claims Act and by Municipal Code § 12.44.030, the operative loss having occurred on 27 March 2021.

1.3 No defect in form is asserted. The claim form bore all required attestations at the signature block.

2. NATURE OF THE LOSS

2.1 The claimant alleges damage to interior finishes, stored household goods and one detached accessory structure arising from a distribution main failure on the 8-inch cast iron main serving Pressure Zone 3.

2.2 Field Operations Work Order WO-2021-33417 confirms a circumferential break at a joint of 1958 vintage, reported by the on-call dispatcher at 04:12 hours and isolated at 05:47 hours.

2.3 Claimed loss as stated in the presentation: itemised at Schedule A, comprising eleven line items, with supporting receipts numbered 1 through 9.

3. INVESTIGATION

3.1 A site inspection was conducted on 11 May 2021 by the Authority's field investigator, accompanied by a representative of the Distribution Maintenance Section.

3.2 Photographic documentation, twenty-eight frames, is retained in the imaging system under asset reference IMG-CLM-0873.

3.3 The Maintenance Section produced a five-year break history for the affected segment on 2 June 2021, disclosing two prior failures within 400 feet, in 2016 and again in 2019.

3.4 The claimant was interviewed by telephone on 18 May 2021. The interview memorandum is filed at Tab 6.

4. ANALYSIS

4.1 The Authority has considered whether the condition constituted a dangerous condition of public property of which the Authority had actual or constructive notice a sufficient time in advance to have taken protective measures.

4.2 The break history at clause 3.3, taken together with the segment's placement on the deferred replacement list for Fiscal Year 2019–20 and its subsequent deferral, is found to support constructive notice.

4.3 Design immunity is not asserted, no discretionary approval of the plan or design having been located in the archived project files.

4.4 Comparative fault is not asserted against the claimant. The private service line was found intact and no unpermitted alterations were observed.

5. DETERMINATION

5.1 The claim is ALLOWED IN PART.

5.2 Depreciation is applied to stored household goods at the schedule adopted by Administrative Order 2019-11. Line items 7 and 10 of Schedule A are disallowed as unsubstantiated.

5.3 The accessory structure is compensated at repair cost rather than replacement cost, the structure being repairable in the opinion of the inspecting investigator.

6. DISPOSITION AND NOTICE

6.1 Payment authority is granted under delegated settlement authority, the allowed sum falling below the threshold requiring Council concurrence.

6.2 A release in the Authority's standard form is required prior to disbursement. Warrant issuance is to follow receipt of the executed release.

6.3 Notice of determination was mailed to the claimant of record on 15 October 2021. The claimant is advised of the limitations period commencing upon service of notice.

6.4 This file is closed to further adjudication and transferred to Records Retention under retention category RC-07 (Claims, Allowed), a retention period of ten years from closure applying.
