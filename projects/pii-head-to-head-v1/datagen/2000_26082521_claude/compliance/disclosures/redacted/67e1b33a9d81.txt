3. NARRATIVE OF PROCEEDINGS

An incident report was filed with the Division Superintendent on 3 March 2016 following a reported lost-time injury sustained during a mid-route coach changeover at the Eastside Division yard. The report was logged the same shift and assigned incident code INC-16-0339.

Initial adjudication opened 11 March 2016. The Claims Adjudicator requested supplementary medical documentation on 18 March 2016; documentation was received 6 April 2016 and found sufficient. Wage-replacement eligibility was calculated on the basis of the twenty-six-week average preceding the incident date, consistent with Schedule C of the collective bargaining agreement then in force.

A determination of compensable injury was entered 22 April 2016 under adjudication code ADJ-C-2. Benefits commenced retroactive to the eighth calendar day following the incident. The Authority did not contest causation.

A modified-duty assignment was offered 30 June 2016 and accepted, with return-to-work recorded 11 July 2016. The file was administratively closed 29 August 2016 under closure code CLS-04 (return to full duty, no residual restriction).

4. NOTES ON SUPPRESSION METHODOLOGY

Suppression was applied at the field level rather than by page. Where a name or identifier appeared within continuous narrative text, the term has been replaced by the corresponding role title. Reference codes, adjudication codes, and dates of process steps have been retained, as these do not in themselves identify the individual and are necessary to render the file intelligible.

No responsive material has been withheld in full. No fee has been assessed.
