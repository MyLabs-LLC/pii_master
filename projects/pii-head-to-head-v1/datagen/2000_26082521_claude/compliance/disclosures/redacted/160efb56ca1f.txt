CLAUSE 2 — INTAKE

  (a) The complaint was received by telephone on 5 May 2011 at 15:20 and
  entered in the complaint queue the same afternoon.
  (b) The complaint alleged improper holding temperatures at a permitted food
  service establishment and a recurring pest sighting in a storage area.
  (c) The complaint was triaged as Priority 2, requiring a field response
  within three working days.

CLAUSE 3 — FIELD RESPONSE

  (a) First unannounced inspection conducted 9 May 2011. Four violations were
  documented, of which two were classified as critical.
  (b) The critical violations concerned cold holding temperature and the
  absence of a documented corrective action log.
  (c) Correction was ordered on site. A written notice was served on the
  responsible person, who signed the acknowledgement line on Form EH-22.
  (d) Reinspection scheduled for 16 May 2011.

CLAUSE 4 — REINSPECTION AND SUBSEQUENT ACTION

  (a) Reinspection conducted 16 May 2011. The two critical violations were
  found corrected. One non-critical violation remained open.
  (b) A second reinspection on 27 May 2011 found all items corrected.
  (c) An administrative penalty of the amount set out in the penalty schedule
  for a first repeat critical violation was assessed on 2 June 2011.

CLAUSE 5 — ADJUDICATION NOTES

  (a) The permit holder requested an informal conference on 9 June 2011. The
  conference was held 21 June 2011 before the Division Director.
  (b) The Director affirmed the assessment, finding the inspection record
  adequate and the classification consistent with the county code and with
  prior determinations in the same permit class.
  (c) Payment was recorded 8 July 2011 and the enforcement action closed the
  same day. The permit was not suspended at any point.
