1. The claim was received on 21 April 2004, being twenty-nine days after the last day of the term for which relief is sought.

2. Supporting documentation was submitted comprising a signed practitioner's statement, a copy of the withdrawal form bearing the departmental date stamp, and a written narrative from the claimant.

3. The Office verified that enrolment was terminated on 2 March 2004, after the sixty per cent point of the term, at which point no refund arises under the ordinary schedule.

4. The medical exception provision permits pro rata relief where the interruption is documented, involuntary, and continuous through the remainder of the term. The panel found each element satisfied on the record before it.

5. Relief is calculated on the unattended portion of the term, being forty-one per cent, applied to assessed tuition and to those mandatory fees not already consumed by service delivery.

6. The health service fee and the transportation fee are excluded from relief, both having been fully earned at the point of assessment. The technology fee and the activity fee are included.

7. The approved refund is to be applied first against any outstanding balance on the account and the remainder disbursed by cheque to the address of record.

8. Financial aid awarded for the term is subject to separate return-of-funds calculation by the Office of Student Financial Aid. Nothing in this adjudication determines that question, and the net amount payable to the claimant may be reduced accordingly.

9. Grades recorded for the term are converted to administrative withdrawal notations. The term is excluded from satisfactory academic progress computation.

10. The claimant was notified of this determination on 14 May 2004 and advised that any dispute as to the calculated proportion must be raised in writing within thirty days.

11. No dispute having been received, the claim was settled and closed on 24 June 2004 under settlement reference SET-04-0902.
