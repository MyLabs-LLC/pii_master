3. INVESTIGATIVE FINDINGS.
   3.1  The CCTV survey identified a mature root intrusion at joint 3+42 downstream of the claimant's lateral connection, occupying an estimated 60% of the pipe cross-section.
   3.2  Maintenance records establish that the segment was scheduled for root treatment on a thirty-six month cycle and that the treatment due in the second quarter of 2020 was deferred and not rescheduled.
   3.3  Rainfall for the twenty-four hours preceding the loss totalled 0.41 inches, well below the design storm, and does not support an act-of-God defence.
   3.4  No backwater valve was installed at the premises. Installation is recommended but not mandated for structures permitted before 1998.

4. LIABILITY ANALYSIS. Notice of the defect is imputed to the Authority by its own deferred-maintenance record. Immunity for discretionary functions is not available where the omission is one of routine maintenance rather than policy-level judgment. Comparative fault attributable to the absence of a backwater valve is assessed at 10%.

5. DAMAGES. The adjuster values the loss at $16,220 after depreciation of contents and exclusion of pre-existing moisture staining documented at the rear foundation wall.

6. RECOMMENDATION. Settlement authority is requested in the amount of $14,598, representing 90% of the adjusted valuation, conditioned on execution of a full release and on the claimant's written acknowledgment that future coverage of like losses is contingent on installation of a backwater device.

7. REMEDIAL REFERRAL. The root-treatment schedule for basin 6 is referred to the Collections Superintendent for audit of all deferred cycles since 2019. A written response is requested by 31 January 2022.
