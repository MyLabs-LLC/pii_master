1. NATURE OF ACTION. This record documents a request for position reclassification submitted by the incumbent and the disposition reached by the classification review panel.

2. BASIS OF REQUEST. The incumbent asserted that duties assigned since the reorganisation of the records unit in July 2003 exceeded the classification specification for Records Technician II, in particular the assumption of responsibility for degree audit exception processing and for supervision of two term-appointed clerical staff.

3. SCHEDULE OF PROCESS STEPS.
 Request received by Human Resources — 19 January 2004
 Position description questionnaire returned — 6 February 2004
 Supervisory statement received — 13 February 2004
 Desk audit conducted — 25 February 2004
 Panel convened — 11 March 2004
 Written determination issued — 26 March 2004

4. DESK AUDIT FINDINGS. The auditing analyst confirmed that degree audit exception processing occupied an estimated thirty-five per cent of working time and that the incumbent exercised assignment and review authority over term-appointed staff, though not authority over hiring or discipline.

5. PANEL DETERMINATION. The panel found the duties consistent with the specification for Records Analyst I in respect of exception processing, but found the supervisory element insufficient to support the lead-worker designation sought. Reclassification to Records Analyst I was approved.

6. EFFECTIVE DATE AND COMPENSATION. The reclassification takes effect at the beginning of the pay period commencing 4 April 2004. Placement is at the entry step of the assigned range. No retroactive adjustment is authorised, the governing rule limiting effect to the date of panel action.

7. RIGHT OF REVIEW. The incumbent was advised of the right to seek review by the Director of Classification and Compensation within twenty (20) working days of the written determination.

8. SUBSEQUENT ACTION. A request for review was filed on 15 April 2004 confined to the question of the lead-worker designation. The Director affirmed the panel on 21 May 2004. The affirmation exhausts administrative remedies within the classification process.

9. FILE NOTATIONS. The position description on file is superseded. The revised description is filed at PD-2004-0063R. Departmental budget records were adjusted by transfer notice BT-04-2211.

10. RECORDS DISPOSITION. This action file is retained in the departmental personnel file for the duration of employment plus five years, and thereafter destroyed in accordance with the approved retention schedule.
