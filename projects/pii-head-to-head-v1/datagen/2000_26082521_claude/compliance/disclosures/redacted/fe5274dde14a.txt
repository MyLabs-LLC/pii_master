I.   Basis of Review
     A.  A referral was received from the Escrow Accounting group on 18 August 2016
         following the monthly three-way reconciliation for the branch trust account.
     B.  The referral identified two disbursements released ahead of collected-funds
         status, contrary to Escrow Policy 4.2 (Good Funds).

II.  Procedure Followed
     A.  Notice of review was issued 22 August 2016.
     B.  An interview was conducted 25 August 2016 in the presence of the HR Business
         Partner. The employee was advised of the right to submit a written response.
     C.  A written response was received 29 August 2016 and is retained at Tab 4.

III. Findings
     A.  Both disbursements were released against deposits that had not cleared. The
         shortfall was covered within the same cycle and no third party sustained loss.
     B.  The employee acknowledged the sequence of events and attributed it to pressure
         to meet a recording deadline.
     C.  No evidence of personal benefit, concealment, or falsification of records was
         found. The conduct is assessed as a procedural lapse rather than misconduct.

IV.  Determination
     A.  Documented verbal counseling, effective 02 September 2016.
     B.  Reassignment of disbursement release authority to the branch manager for a
         period of ninety days, subject to review on 01 December 2016.
     C.  Completion of the Good Funds refresher module by 30 September 2016.

V.   Retention
     A.  This record is retained in the confidential employee relations file and is not
         filed in the general personnel folder.
