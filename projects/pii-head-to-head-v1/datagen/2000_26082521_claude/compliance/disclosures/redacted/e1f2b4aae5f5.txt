The appellant filed a timely appeal on 11 January 2011, within the sixty-day window running from the posting of final grades on 20 December 2010. The stated ground was arbitrary application of the stated grading scheme, specifically that the syllabus weighting for the final examination was applied at a percentage other than that published at the commencement of the term. The appeal did not allege discrimination and was therefore not referred to the Office of Equal Opportunity.

The Committee obtained the syllabus of record from the departmental file on 24 January 2011 and the instructor's grade book extract on 7 February 2011. The instructor of record submitted a written response on 18 February 2011 acknowledging a mid-term adjustment to the examination weighting, communicated orally in class but not reduced to writing. The appellant did not dispute that an oral announcement was made but asserted absence on the date in question.

The Committee convened on 21 March 2011. Four voting members were present; one seat was vacant. The Committee found that the mid-term adjustment, however communicated, was not reflected in any written amendment to the syllabus and that the burden of documenting such an amendment rests with the instructor. The Committee declined to substitute its own judgment as to the merits of the work submitted.

Determination: appeal sustained in part. The course grade is to be recomputed under the weighting published at the commencement of the term. Recomputation is remanded to the department chair, to be completed within thirty days. The Committee makes no finding as to the appellant's remaining requests for tuition remission, which fall outside its jurisdiction.

Transmitted to the Office of the University Registrar for grade change processing, 4 April 2011. Recomputed grade posted 19 April 2011. Docket closed.
