1.   Chronology of Events
     1.1  On 12 September 2016 the escrow branch received wiring instructions purporting
          to originate from the seller party, transmitted by electronic mail.
     1.2  The instructions superseded instructions delivered at the time of contract and
          directed proceeds to an institution outside the state of closing.
     1.3  Closing occurred 14 September 2016. Disbursement was released 15 September 2016
          at 10:42 local time.
     1.4  On 19 September 2016 the seller party contacted the branch to report non-receipt
          of proceeds. The branch escalated to Compliance the same day.

2.   Immediate Response
     2.1  A recall request was transmitted to the originating institution on 19 September
          2016 within four hours of escalation.
     2.2  A hold was placed on the receiving account by the receiving institution on
          20 September 2016. Partial funds were confirmed frozen.
     2.3  A report was filed with the appropriate federal complaint center on
          20 September 2016 under confirmation number [SUPPRESSED].
     2.4  Local law enforcement was notified 21 September 2016. Agency case number
          XXXXXXXXX.

3.   Forensic Review
     3.1  Message headers from the superseding instructions were preserved and imaged on
          22 September 2016 under evidence tag EV-16-0912-A.
     3.2  The review established that the message did not originate from the domain of
          record associated with the seller party and that the display name had been
          altered to resemble it.
     3.3  No compromise of the company's own mail environment was identified. Endpoint
          and mailbox audit logs for the branch were reviewed for the period
          01 August 2016 through 20 September 2016 with negative results.
     3.4  Interviews were conducted with the escrow officer of record and the escrow
          assistant on 23 September 2016. Both stated that the callback verification
          step required under Escrow Policy 6.1 was attempted using contact details
          contained in the electronic mail rather than the file of record.

4.   Control Failure Identified
     4.1  The verification callback was performed against a telephone number supplied
          within the suspect communication. This is a direct departure from Policy 6.1,
          which requires use of the contact details captured at opening.
     4.2  The secondary authorization step for changed wiring instructions was recorded
          in the file but bears no evidence of independent review.

5.   Remediation
     5.1  Effective 26 September 2016, all changes to wiring instructions in Region II
          require dual authorization by a branch manager and a member of Escrow
          Accounting.
     5.2  A mandatory refresher on Policy 6.1 was assigned across the region with a
          completion deadline of 31 October 2016.
     5.3  Notice was given to the fidelity carrier on 27 September 2016 under the
          company's crime policy. Carrier claim reference [SUPPRESSED].

6.   Status
     6.1  Recovery efforts continue. Frozen funds remain subject to the receiving
          institution's hold pending instruction from law enforcement.
     6.2  Case remains open. Next review 31 October 2016.
