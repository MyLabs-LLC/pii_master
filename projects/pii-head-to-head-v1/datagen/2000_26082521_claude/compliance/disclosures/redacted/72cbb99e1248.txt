1. The claim seeks reclassification to resident status for tuition
   assessment purposes for the academic year shown, together with an
   adjustment to the bursar account in the amount of the non-resident
   differential assessed for the fall term.

2. The governing standard requires twelve consecutive months of domicile
   in the state immediately preceding the first day of classes, coupled
   with objective evidence of intent to make the state a permanent home.

3. The Claimant established physical presence from 01 August 2010,
   producing a residential lease, utility statements for nine of twelve
   months, and payroll records reflecting continuous in-state
   employment at not less than half time.

4. The first-level reviewer denied the petition on 10 June 2011 on the
   ground that the Claimant had been claimed as a dependent on an
   out-of-state income tax return filed for tax year 2010, which under
   the standard creates a rebuttable presumption of domicile elsewhere.

5. On appeal the Claimant produced a sworn statement of financial
   independence, bank records covering the full lookback period, and a
   statement from the out-of-state filer withdrawing the dependency
   claim by amended return filed 30 June 2011.

6. The Committee finds the presumption rebutted. The amended return, the
   continuity of in-state employment, and the timing of the vehicle
   registration and voter registration together satisfy the intent
   element of the standard.

7. The Committee further finds that physical presence commenced on
   01 August 2010 and that the twelve-month period closed before the
   first day of classes for the fall term.

8. DECISION: Reclassification to resident status granted, effective for
   the fall term 2011 and prospectively. The Bursar shall credit the
   non-resident differential assessed for that term. Reclassification
   shall not be applied retroactively to any term preceding the
   effective date.

9. The decision of the Committee is final. Notice was mailed to the
   address of record on 22 July 2011 and a copy placed in the file.
