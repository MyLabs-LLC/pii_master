DISPOSITION. Upon inspection conducted 05 April 2016 by the Field
Investigations Unit, and upon review of the maintenance and televising
histories maintained for Main Segment 22-B, the Adjudicating Officer finds
that the obstruction giving rise to the surcharge complained of was
occasioned by root intrusion within a private lateral lying wholly beyond
the Department's point of maintenance responsibility as that point is
fixed by the Standard Conditions of Service, Article XI, § 4. No act,
omission or want of ordinary care on the part of the Department or its
agents having been demonstrated, the claim is DENIED in its entirety
pursuant to Municipal Code § 13.44.070(c).
