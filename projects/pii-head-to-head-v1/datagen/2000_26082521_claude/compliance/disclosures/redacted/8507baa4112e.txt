  1. This memorandum is issued in disposition of the grievance filed under Article 11
     of the Collective Bargaining Agreement (1996–1999 term), the said grievance having
     been presented in writing on 12 May 1998 and having been processed through the
     First and Second Stages without resolution.

  2. The grievance as pleaded asserts that the Company, in issuing a written warning
     dated 06 May 1998 in respect of an alleged failure to complete a pre-trip
     inspection report, acted without just cause and in contravention of Articles 11.2
     and 14.4 of the said Agreement.

  3. The Company's answer, filed 26 May 1998, maintains that the disciplinary measure
     was progressive in nature, proportionate to the conduct alleged, and consistent
     with prior application of the pre-trip inspection standard published in Operations
     Bulletin OB-95/22.

  4. A Second Stage hearing convened on 09 June 1998 at the divisional office. Minutes
     of that hearing are appended hereto as Exhibit A. The Second Stage answer, denying
     the grievance, issued 17 June 1998.

  5. Notice of appeal to the Third Stage was served 24 June 1998, within the ten working
     days prescribed by Article 11.6(c), and is accordingly deemed timely.

  6. The Panel convened 22 July 1998 and received the following evidence: (i) the
     vehicle inspection log for the period 01 May 1998 to 07 May 1998; (ii) the
     dispatch record for tractor unit ██████; (iii) oral testimony of the responding
     manager; and (iv) oral testimony of the grievant, taken under the customary
     assurance that no adverse inference would be drawn from any refusal to answer.

  7. The Panel finds, on the balance of probabilities, that the pre-trip inspection
     report for the shift commencing 05 May 1998 was not completed in the manner
     required by OB-95/22. The Panel further finds, however, that the standard was not
     uniformly enforced at the home terminal during the relevant period, and that at
     least three comparable omissions during the same quarter attracted no disciplinary
     response whatsoever.

  8. DETERMINATION: The grievance is sustained in part. The written warning of
     06 May 1998 shall be reduced to a documented verbal counselling and shall be
     expunged from the personnel record upon the expiry of six months from the date
     hereof, provided no further infraction of like character occurs. No back pay or
     other monetary remedy arises, none having been claimed.

  9. This determination is final and binding upon the parties in accordance with
     Article 11.9 and shall not be cited as precedent in any subsequent proceeding.
