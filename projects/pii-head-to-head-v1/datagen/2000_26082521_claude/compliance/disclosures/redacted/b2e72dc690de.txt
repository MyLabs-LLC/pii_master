1. The grievance was filed at Level I on 21 February 2011 and denied by the site administrator on 04 March 2011. Level II review was requested on 09 March 2011 and denied by the Assistant Superintendent, Human Resources, on 28 March 2011. The Level III appeal was timely under Article XVII §5, which allows fifteen (15) working days from receipt of the Level II response.

2. The grievant alleges that the involuntary reassignment effective 03 January 2011 was made without regard to unit seniority and without the consultation meeting required by Article XIV §3(b).

3. The District's position, stated through the Director of Certificated Personnel, is that the reassignment arose from a mid-year enrollment decline of 47 students at the sending site, that the action was a lateral transfer within the same classification and salary schedule placement, and that no vacancy posting obligation was triggered.

4. The following documents were entered into the record at hearing: the reassignment notice dated 16 December 2010; the site enrollment reconciliation report of 10 December 2010; the seniority roster published 01 October 2010; and the notes of the consultation meeting of 21 December 2010. Names and employee numbers appearing in these exhibits have been suppressed in the disclosure copy.

5. Testimony was received from the grievant, the site administrator, and one departmental colleague. Witness identifiers are suppressed and are recorded in the sealed hearing index under Exhibit Nos. GR-11-0418-W1 through W3.

6. The record establishes that a consultation meeting was in fact convened on 21 December 2010, that the grievant attended, and that the grievant declined to state a site preference at that meeting.

7. The record does not establish that alternative placements were presented in writing, as contemplated by Article XIV §3(b)(2). The Hearing Officer treats this as a procedural defect.

8. The seniority roster of 01 October 2010 shows three unit members at the sending site with less service credit than the grievant. The District offered no evidence that credential authorization prevented the reassignment of any of the three.

9. Accordingly, the grievance is SUSTAINED IN PART as to Article XIV §3(b)(2) and Article IX §2, and DENIED IN PART as to the allegation that no consultation occurred.

10. Remedy: the grievant shall be offered, in writing, the next lateral vacancy for which the grievant holds authorization at the sending site or a comparable site, such offer to issue no later than 30 June 2011. No back pay is awarded, the reassignment having been lateral with no loss of compensation.

11. The District shall issue written guidance to site administrators on the documentation requirements of Article XIV §3(b)(2) before the opening of the 2011–2012 school year.

12. This determination is final at the District level. Either party may invoke arbitration under Article XVII §7 by written notice within twenty (20) working days of the date shown above.

13. A copy of this determination has been placed in the grievance file and, pursuant to Article XVII §9, is not filed in the personnel record of any employee.

14. Custody of the unredacted record remains with the Office of Employee Relations under retention schedule EMP-04 (seven years from close).
