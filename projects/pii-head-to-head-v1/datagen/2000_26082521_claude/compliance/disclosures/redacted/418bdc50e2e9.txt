3. INVESTIGATION

   The lien in question was recorded eleven days after the date of policy. The
   claimant asserts commencement of work predating the insured deed. Escrow
   file contains a signed owner's affidavit and indemnity in the standard form
   then in use at the branch. Signature block on that affidavit is suppressed
   in the production copy; the underlying instrument is retained in the
   original file.

   Branch closing notes reflect that no notice of commencement was of record
   at the time of the search, and no mechanic's lien exception was raised on
   Schedule B. Interviews with the handling escrow officer and the branch
   title examiner were completed on 05/24/2016 and 06/01/2016 respectively.

4. COVERAGE ANALYSIS

   Covered Risk 2(c) and Covered Risk 10 are implicated. Exclusion 3(a) is not
   available on this record: nothing indicates the insured created, suffered,
   assumed, or agreed to the lien, nor that the insured had knowledge not
   disclosed to the Company. The Company's rights against the affiant under
   the indemnity are preserved and are not conditioned on this determination.

5. DETERMINATION

   Coverage is AFFIRMED. Defense and clearance are to be undertaken at
   Company expense. No reservation is continued as to the lien claim itself;
   reservation is maintained solely as to any consequential damages asserted
   beyond the policy amount.
