RESIDENCY CLASSIFICATION APPEAL — DETERMINATION AND FILE SUMMARY

Petition No.: RES-2011-A-0311
Petitioner: ████████████████
Student ID: XXXXXXXXX
Term of Application: Spring 2012
Classification of Record: Nonresident
Classification Requested: Resident, for tuition purposes
Reviewing Officer, First Instance: Residency Officer, Office of the University Registrar
Appellate Body: Residency Appeals Panel

1. 3 October 2011 — Petition received electronically through the student portal, together with the standard documentary schedule. Filed within the deadline for the Spring 2012 term, which closed 15 October 2011.

2. 12 October 2011 — Documentary sufficiency check completed. Four of the six required categories were satisfied. The petition was deficient as to proof of twelve months' continuous domicile and as to evidence of financial independence for the preceding tax year.

3. 14 October 2011 — Deficiency notice issued to the address of record, allowing twenty-one days for supplementation.

4. 1 November 2011 — Supplemental packet received. The packet established continuous physical presence but did not, in the view of the Residency Officer, rebut the presumption of continuing out-of-state domicile arising from the petitioner's declared dependency status on a nonresident parent's return.

5. 9 November 2011 — First-instance determination issued: petition denied. Notice of appeal rights transmitted with the determination.

6. 21 November 2011 — Notice of appeal filed, timely. The appeal asserted that the presumption had been misapplied and that the Residency Officer had given no weight to the petitioner's continuous full-time employment within the state during the qualifying period.

7. 8 December 2011 — Residency Appeals Panel convened. Three members sat. The petitioner appeared and was heard for approximately fifteen minutes. No witnesses were called.

The Panel found that continuous full-time employment within the state, sustained across the full twelve-month qualifying period and constituting the petitioner's principal means of support, is materially inconsistent with the dependency presumption relied upon below. The Panel noted that dependency as declared for federal tax purposes is evidence of domicile but is not conclusive of it, and that the first-instance determination treated it as dispositive without reaching the remaining indicia enumerated in Policy 3-104.

Determination: appeal granted. The petitioner is reclassified as a resident for tuition purposes effective with the Spring 2012 term. Reclassification is prospective only; no adjustment is made to charges assessed for prior terms, and the Panel expressly declines to disturb the Fall 2011 assessment.

Transmitted to Student Accounts for billing adjustment, 9 December 2011. Adjustment confirmed posted 16 December 2011. Petition file closed and retained under schedule GS-11.
