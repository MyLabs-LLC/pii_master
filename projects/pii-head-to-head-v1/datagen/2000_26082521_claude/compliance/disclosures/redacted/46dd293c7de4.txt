1.0  Nature of Claim
     1.1  Notice of claim was received in the Claims Administration Unit on 09 June 2016
          and docketed the same business day under the reference number shown above.
     1.2  The claimant asserts that an unreleased deed of trust of record, recorded prior
          to the Date of Policy, constitutes a defect in title insured against under
          Covered Risk 2 of the policy form.

2.0  Investigation
     2.1  A supplemental search of the public records was ordered on 13 June 2016 and
          returned to the file on 21 June 2016.
     2.2  The search confirmed the recorded instrument identified in the notice of claim
          and confirmed that no release, satisfaction, or reconveyance appears of record.
     2.3  The originating escrow file was retrieved from off-site storage on 24 June 2016.
          The payoff demand contained in the file bears a demand expiration date preceding
          the disbursement date, and no updated demand was obtained prior to closing.
     2.4  The closing instructions, settlement statement, and disbursement ledger were
          reviewed in full. Signature pages and wire confirmations have been withheld from
          this production.

3.0  Coverage Analysis
     3.1  The defect asserted existed as of the Date of Policy and was not disclosed in
          Schedule B of the policy.
     3.2  No exclusion under Section 3 of the Conditions is available on these facts. The
          matter was neither created, suffered, assumed, nor agreed to by the insured.
     3.3  Coverage is therefore accepted without reservation.

4.0  Loss Determination
     4.1  The outstanding principal secured by the unreleased instrument, together with
          accrued interest calculated to 30 June 2016, forms the measure of loss.
     4.2  Loss is within policy limits and does not implicate the coinsurance provisions.

5.0  Disposition

     5.2  Authority to settle by payoff and recordation of release was granted 05 July 2016
          under the delegated authority schedule then in effect.
     5.3  Recovery referral opened against the settlement agent's errors and omissions
          coverage on 08 July 2016.

6.0  Closing Note
