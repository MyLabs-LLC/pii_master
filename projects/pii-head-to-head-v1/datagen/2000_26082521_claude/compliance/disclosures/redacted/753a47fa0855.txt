    3.  INCIDENT NARRATIVE
        3.1  The claimant was a fare-paying passenger aboard a northbound coach
             operating on a weekday afternoon schedule. The coach executed a
             service stop at a mid-route boarding island. While the claimant was
             traversing the aisle toward the rear exit, the operator resumed
             motion from the stop. The claimant reports losing footing and
             striking the stanchion at the rear door well, sustaining injury to
             the left shoulder and lower back.
        3.2  Emergency medical service was summoned by the operator via radio at
             15:47. Transport to a receiving hospital was declined at scene. The
             claimant presented independently for treatment the following
             morning.
        3.3  No mechanical defect was reported by the operator on the pre-trip or
             post-trip inspection sheet for that assignment.
