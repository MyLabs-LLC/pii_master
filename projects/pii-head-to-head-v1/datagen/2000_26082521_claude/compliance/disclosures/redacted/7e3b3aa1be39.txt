   Sequence of events
     -  Load tendered and sealed at origin, 03 October 2011
     -  Seal recorded as intact at the destination gate, 05 October 2011
     -  Damage noted at unloading and marked on the delivery receipt
     -  Claim submitted by the consignee, 19 October 2011
     -  Acknowledged in writing by this office, 21 October 2011
     -  Inspection carried out by the appointed surveyor, 28 October 2011

   Nature of loss
     -  Four cartons on the nose of the trailer showed crush damage to
        the outer packaging. Contents of two cartons unsalvageable.
     -  Surveyor attributed the damage to insufficient blocking and
        bracing at loading rather than to handling in transit.

   Adjudication
     -  Loading was performed by the shipper. The driver signed the
        bill of lading as shipper load and count.
     -  Liability is declined on that basis. The declination letter was
        issued 04 November 2011.
     -  Claimant advised of the internal appeal route and the sixty-day
        window in which to use it.
     -  File held open to 03 January 2012 pending any appeal.
