SECTION B — NUMBERED FINDINGS

1. Mechanism of injury. On 11 February 2021 the worker sustained a lumbar strain while relocating cases of bottled product from a delivery cage to the stockroom shelving. No mechanical aid was in use at the time.

2. Notice. Notice was given verbally to the shift supervisor on the day of injury and reduced to writing on Form INJ-01 on 12 February 2021. Notice is accepted as timely.

3. Initial treatment. The worker attended an occupational health clinic on 12 February 2021 and was placed on modified duty with a twenty-pound lifting restriction.

4. Modified duty. Modified duty was accommodated at the assigned location from 15 February 2021. Accommodation records are held on the store roster and are consistent with the restriction issued.

5. Course of treatment. Conservative management comprising physiotherapy over eight weeks was authorised on 24 February 2021. A follow-up assessment on 21 April 2021 recorded partial improvement.

6. Independent medical examination. An examination was arranged for 09 June 2021. The examiner assessed the condition as work-related, with maximum medical improvement reached on or about 26 July 2021.

7. Impairment. A permanent partial impairment rating of four percent to the whole person was assigned.

8. Indemnity. Temporary partial disability benefits were paid for the period 15 February 2021 to 26 July 2021 at the statutory compensation rate applicable to the worker's average weekly wage. Payment ledger is held under the claim file reference above.

9. Return to work. The worker returned to unrestricted duty on 02 August 2021. No subsequent lost time has been recorded.

10. Contested issues. None outstanding. Compensability was accepted without reservation on 19 February 2021 and was not withdrawn at any stage.

SECTION C — DISPOSITION

11. The claim is recommended for closure on a settled basis, subject to the statutory approval of the compromise agreement executed on 18 November 2021.

12. Medical benefits remain open in accordance with the terms of that agreement.

13. Loss prevention referral: the location has been added to the Q1 2022 manual handling audit schedule, and cage-to-shelf handling has been added to the district safety briefing cycle.
