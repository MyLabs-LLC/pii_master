PART B — SUMMARY OF ALLEGATION
----------------------------------------------------------------
The complainant asserts that the monthly payment increased by
$187.42 effective 01 February 2016 without adequate notice, that
the annual escrow analysis statement was not received, and that
two attempts to resolve the matter by telephone in January 2016
produced inconsistent explanations from staff. The complainant
further asserts that a shortage spread was applied over twelve
months when the complainant had requested a lump-sum payoff of
the shortage instead.

----------------------------------------------------------------
PART D — FINDINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Allegation of non-delivery of the escrow analysis statement:
   SUBSTANTIATED. Statement was addressed to a superseded address
   of record as a result of a system propagation defect.
2. Allegation of inadequate notice of payment change:
   SUBSTANTIATED in effect, as a consequence of finding 1. The
   statement itself was timely produced on 22 December 2015.
3. Allegation of inconsistent staff explanations:
   PARTIALLY SUBSTANTIATED. The 07 January call was accurate. The
   19 January call misdescribed the shortage spread as mandatory.
4. Allegation regarding refusal of lump-sum payoff:
   NOT SUBSTANTIATED as to policy, SUBSTANTIATED as to service.
   The option existed and was not offered.

----------------------------------------------------------------
PART E — REMEDIATION AND ADJUDICATION NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------
Payment recalculated with shortage removed following voluntary
lump-sum payment tendered 15 April 2016. Two months of the
increased payment differential refunded, $374.84, posted
20 April 2016. No adverse credit reporting occurred; verified
against the reporting extract for February and March 2016.

The Compliance Officer notes that the propagation defect is a
systemic control weakness rather than an individual servicing
error, and that the remedy for the complainant does not discharge
the institution's obligation to the remaining forty affected
loans. Corrective action item CA-2016-019 opened accordingly,
owner Information Systems Analyst, due 30 June 2016. Coaching
delivered to the representative on the 19 January call; no formal
corrective action warranted.
