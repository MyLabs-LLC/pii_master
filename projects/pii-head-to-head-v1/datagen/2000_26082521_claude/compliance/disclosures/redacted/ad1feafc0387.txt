STATEMENT OF THE MATTER

Be it recorded that on 19 January 1998 the Superintendent of the Northside Division did cause to be served upon the above-named employee a Notice of Investigation, in accordance with Article XIV, Section 4 of the collective bargaining agreement then in force between the District and Local 589, said Notice alleging that the employee had on 11 January 1998 departed the terminal loop in advance of the scheduled departure time by an interval of not less than four minutes, and had thereafter failed to make the prescribed report of the deviation to the Line Supervisor.

The investigation was convened on 26 January 1998 at the Northside Division conference room at 0900 hours. Present were the Hearing Officer, the Superintendent as charging party, the employee, and the Local Chairman appearing as the employee's designated representative pursuant to the aforesaid Article XIV. The employee was advised of the right to remain silent and of the right to call witnesses, which advisements were acknowledged on the record.

The record before the Hearing Officer consisted of the automatic vehicle location data extract for Run 2214 covering the period 0600 to 0930 hours on the date in question, bearing extract reference AVL-98-0111-2214; the Line Supervisor's contemporaneous log; and the employee's own statement given under Article XIV protections.

FINDINGS

The Hearing Officer found the first charge sustained upon the weight of the automatic vehicle location data, which was determined to be reliable and unrebutted. The second charge was found not sustained, the Hearing Officer accepting the employee's uncontradicted testimony that the Line Supervisor's radio channel was occupied for the duration of the relevant interval.

DISCIPLINE ASSESSED

Written reprimand, to be entered in the employee's service record and to remain of record for a period of twelve (12) months from the date hereof, after which the same shall be expunged in accordance with Article XIV, Section 11. No suspension. No loss of compensation.
