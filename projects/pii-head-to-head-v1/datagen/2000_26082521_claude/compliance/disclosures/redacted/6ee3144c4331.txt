PERSONNEL ACTION RECORD — OFFICE OF THE UNIVERSITY REGISTRAR
Action No. HR-REG-2011-0038. Employee: [SUPPRESSED]. Employee ID: XXXXXXX. Position Classification: Records Assistant II, non-exempt, benefits-eligible. Supervisor of Record: Assistant Registrar, Transcript Services. Action Type: Written Corrective Action, Step 2.

On 8 August 2011 the system access audit for the second quarter identified thirty-one queries against student records outside the assigned work queue, executed between 6 June and 22 July 2011. The employee was interviewed on 11 August 2011 in the presence of a union representative and acknowledged the queries, stating they were undertaken to assist colleagues in other units without a routed service ticket. No evidence of external disclosure was found. A Step 1 verbal counseling had issued 3 February 2011 on a related matter concerning unrouted work.

The Assistant Registrar issued written corrective action on 19 August 2011, directing that all record queries be initiated only from an assigned ticket and that the employee complete FERPA refresher training by 30 September 2011. Training completion was recorded 14 September 2011. The employee declined to submit a written rebuttal within the ten-day period provided. This record is to be maintained in the supervisory file and is eligible for removal from active consideration after twenty-four months without further action.
