3. FIELDS RETAINED

3.1 Case reference, file opening date, incident date, run or route code, depot code, procedural dates, reserve movements, panel composition by role, and adjudication outcome are retained in full. These are required for actuarial reporting and for the defence of any subsequent proceeding.

4. HANDLING

4.1 Requests for the withheld fields are to be directed to the Data Protection Officer and are not to be actioned at archive level.
4.2 The reconciliation key linking suppressed values to case references is held separately under lock in the strong room and is not part of this series.

5. REVIEW

5.1 Retention period: seven years from the date of file closure, save where litigation is pending.
5.2 Next scheduled review of this series: September 2006.
5.3 Files marked for destruction at review are to be listed and countersigned by the Claims Adjudicator before disposal.
