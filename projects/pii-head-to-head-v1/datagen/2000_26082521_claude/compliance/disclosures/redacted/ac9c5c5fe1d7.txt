3. CHRONOLOGY OF PROCESS STEPS

3.1 22 April 2021 — Prescription received electronically and entered into the dispensing system at 16:42 local time.

3.2 22 April 2021 — Product selection, labelling and final verification completed within a single transaction cycle. The verification scan was recorded as bypassed under override code OV-7.

3.3 06 May 2021 — Claimant contacted the store by telephone reporting symptom recurrence. Call logged to the Patient Contact Register.

3.4 07 May 2021 — Incident opened by the Pharmacy Manager and escalated to Regional Quality Assurance under escalation tier 2.

3.5 19 May 2021 — Root cause review conducted. Findings recorded on Form QA-31.

3.6 03 June 2021 — Formal claim received by the Claims Intake Unit and acknowledged in writing within the five-business-day service standard.

3.7 26 August 2021 — Matter placed before the Committee for adjudication.

4. FINDINGS

4.1 The Committee finds that a strength-selection error occurred at the product selection step and was not intercepted at final verification.

4.2 The Committee further finds that the barcode verification override was applied without a contemporaneous entry in the override justification field, contrary to clause 6.4 of PHR-SOP-114.

4.3 Staffing records for the shift in question show technician coverage below the district planning benchmark for the volume dispensed. The Committee treats this as a contributing systemic factor rather than a proximate cause.

4.4 No evidence was identified of prior similar events at this location within the preceding twenty-four months.

5. ADJUDICATION

5.1 The claim is ACCEPTED in part. Liability is admitted as to the dispensing error described at clause 4.1.

5.2 Quantum is remitted to the Claims Settlement Unit for assessment against the schedule in force at the date of the incident. Reserve category: C2.

5.3 Recovery against the dispensing pharmacist personally is not pursued.

6. REMEDIAL DIRECTIONS

6.1 The Regional Director shall, by 31 October 2021, confirm that override code OV-7 has been restricted to supervisory credentials at all sites within the district.

6.2 Refresher training on final verification shall be delivered to all pharmacy staff at the affected location and completion certified on Form TR-08.

6.3 A compliance audit of override usage across the district shall be scheduled in the fourth-quarter assurance plan.

7. RECORD RETENTION

7.1 This file is retained for ten years from the date of adjudication under the corporate retention schedule.
