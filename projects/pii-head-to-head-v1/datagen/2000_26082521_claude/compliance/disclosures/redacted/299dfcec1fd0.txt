SECTION C — PROCEEDING
Contest filed 22 February 2016, within the twenty-one (21) day window prescribed
by Adjudication Rule 4.2. Hearing convened 06 April 2016 before the Hearing
Officer. Respondent appeared and elected an oral hearing on the record. The
issuing inspector did not appear; the citation and the inspector's
contemporaneous field notes were admitted under Rule 6.5 as records kept in the
ordinary course of business.

SECTION D — FINDINGS
Respondent produced a reduced fare card and a validation receipt bearing a
timestamp four (4) minutes prior to the inspection. Tap history retrieved from
the fare collection system for the card of record confirms a valid entry tap at
the origin station on the date in question. Maintenance log MW-2016-0211
documents a validator outage at STN-046 Platform B from 16:55 to 18:20 the same
evening. The Hearing Officer finds the inspection occurred during that
documented outage and that the presumption established at FC-2.11(c) is
rebutted. No adverse credibility finding is made.

SECTION E — DISPOSITION
Citation dismissed with prejudice. No penalty assessed. Any sum collected to be
refunded to the account of record within thirty (30) days per Rule 9.1.
Respondent advised in open hearing that no further obligation attaches and that
the dismissal is not reportable to collections.
