2. NATURE OF THE MATTER
   - Dispute over disbursement of a holdback established at close of escrow for completion of repairs identified in an inspection report.
   - Amount in controversy carried on the claims register; not reproduced.

3. CHRONOLOGY
   - 05/28/1998 — Escrow closed; holdback agreement executed and holdback funds retained in trust.
   - 07/15/1998 — Deadline for completion of repairs under the holdback agreement.
   - 07/22/1998 — Competing written demands received from both parties.
   - 07/23/1998 — Disbursement suspended; funds segregated on the trust ledger.
   - 08/06/1998 — Notice of conflicting claims issued to both parties and to counsel.
   - 09/30/1998 — Mediation attempted; no resolution.
   - 11/12/1998 — Interpleader authorized by the Regional Counsel.
   - 12/03/1998 — Complaint in interpleader filed; case number suppressed.
   - 03/18/1999 — Funds deposited with the clerk of court; receipt number suppressed.
   - 05/07/1999 — Order discharging the Company entered; costs and fees awarded from the fund.
   - 06/22/1999 — Trust ledger closed to zero.
   - 09/29/1999 — Distribution among the parties completed by the court; the Company took no position.

4. REVIEW FINDINGS
   - Holdback agreement was properly executed, witnessed, and made part of the escrow instructions.
   - Escrow officer acted within instructions in withholding disbursement upon receipt of conflicting demands.
   - Notice of conflicting claims was timely under the operations manual, section 12.2.
   - One procedural exception: the segregation entry was posted one business day after suspension. No prejudice resulted; no interest attribution issue arose.
   - No coverage exposure under any policy of title insurance. Matter arises solely from escrow function.

5. ADJUDICATION NOTES
   - Claim closed without indemnity payment. Expense payments limited to counsel fees, recovered in part from the fund.
   - No referral to the state department of insurance. No consumer complaint filed.
   - Recommendation adopted: revise the holdback instruction form to require a stated deadline, a named disbursement condition and an acknowledgment of the interpleader remedy. Revision issued as Form ES-227 rev. 08/1999 and distributed to all branches.
