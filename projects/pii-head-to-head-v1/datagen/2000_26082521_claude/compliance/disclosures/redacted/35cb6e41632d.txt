This memorandum records the disposition of the investigatory leave imposed in this matter and is placed in the personnel file pursuant to Education Code §44031, which affords the employee the right to inspect the file and to attach a written response.

The employee was placed on paid administrative leave effective September 26, 2011 by letter of the same date, delivered by hand with a witness present. The leave was imposed as a non-disciplinary measure to permit an orderly inquiry, and the letter so stated. Consistent with Article XV, Section 7 of the collective bargaining agreement, the employee was directed to remain available during contract hours, was relieved of all site duties, and was advised that District keys, the site identification badge, and the assigned network credential would be held by the Assistant Superintendent, Human Resources for the duration.

The inquiry was conducted by the Director of Classified and Certificated Personnel between September 27 and November 4, 2011. The employee participated in one investigatory interview on October 11, 2011, having been furnished a written statement of the subject matter on October 4, 2011 and having been advised in writing of the right to representation. Representation was requested and provided. A second interview scheduled for October 20, 2011 was cancelled at the request of the representative and was not rescheduled, the investigator having determined that the additional testimony was cumulative.

The inquiry concluded that the conduct described in the initiating report was not established by a preponderance of the evidence as to the principal allegation, and that a secondary matter concerning adherence to the site sign-out procedure was established but did not warrant action under Education Code §44932. No Notice of Unprofessional Conduct under Education Code §44938 was issued, and no statement of charges was prepared.

The employee was returned to the assignment of record effective November 7, 2011. Keys, badge, and network credential were reissued on November 4, 2011 and logged by the Records Custodian. No loss of salary, benefit, or seniority credit was incurred, and the period of leave is recorded as paid duty time for all purposes including retirement service credit.

The initiating report, interview summaries, and investigator's notes are retained separately from this file in the confidential investigative file under reference HR-11-0088 and are not part of the personnel record. The employee was furnished a copy of this memorandum on November 7, 2011 and declined at that time to attach a written response, reserving the right to do so.
