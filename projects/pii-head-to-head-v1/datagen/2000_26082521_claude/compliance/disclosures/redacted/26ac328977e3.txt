SECTION C — FINDINGS
C.1  The timekeeping records relied upon were drawn from the client's gate-access system and cross-checked against the agency's own timesheet portal. A discrepancy of between nine and twenty-two minutes was identified on four of the eleven shifts examined.
C.2  The associate advanced an explanation relating to the availability of the shuttle transport serving the site, which was accepted in part. The reviewing officer accepted that two of the four instances were attributable to circumstances outside the associate's control.
C.3  The allegation of early departure from the goods-inwards area was not substantiated. The supervisor's written account could not be reconciled with the shift rota for the dates in question, and no corroborating witness was available.
C.4  No issue of conduct affecting safety, property or the interests of the client account was found.

SECTION D — DETERMINATION
D.1  The allegation at C.3 is dismissed in full.
D.2  The allegation at C.1 is upheld in part, to the extent of two instances.
D.3  A written reminder as to timekeeping obligations is recorded and shall remain live on file for six months from the date of the appeal outcome.
D.4  The assignment is not terminated. The associate is returned to the client account without break in service.
D.5  The client's request for removal from the account is declined, the request having been made in reliance on the allegation dismissed at C.3.
