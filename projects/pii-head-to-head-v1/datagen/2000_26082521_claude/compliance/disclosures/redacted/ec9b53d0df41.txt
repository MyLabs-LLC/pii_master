1. The grievance was filed at Step One on 21 June 2021 and denied by the immediate supervisor on 30 June 2021. Appeal to Step Two was received within the ten working days prescribed by Article 14, Section 3 of the Memorandum of Understanding.

2. The grievance alleges that the assignment of weekend standby rotation for the period commencing 5 June 2021 departed from the seniority order required by Article 9, Section 2.

3. A hearing was convened on 27 July 2021. The grievant appeared with representation. The Plant Superintendent appeared for the District.

4. The District produced the standby rotation logs for the calendar quarter, together with the qualification matrix maintained for the facility.

5. It is found that two operators senior to the grievant were not certified for the Grade III process control tasks required during the period in question, and were therefore properly passed over under the qualification proviso at Article 9, Section 2(c).

6. It is further found that the posting of the rotation occurred four days late, contrary to the seven-day notice requirement at Article 9, Section 5.

7. The grievance is SUSTAINED IN PART as to the late posting and DENIED as to the seniority allegation.

8. As remedy, the District shall post standby rotations not less than seven days in advance for the remainder of the contract term, and shall confirm compliance to the Union in writing each quarter. No monetary remedy is awarded, no loss of compensation having been demonstrated.

9. The grievant is advised of the right to advance this matter to Step Three within ten working days of receipt of this determination.
