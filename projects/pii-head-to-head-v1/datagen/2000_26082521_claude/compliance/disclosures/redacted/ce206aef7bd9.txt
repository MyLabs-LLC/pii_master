PROCEDURAL HISTORY

The incident giving rise to this matter occurred on October 5, 2011. The site administrator suspended the student for five school days effective October 6, 2011, following an informal conference conducted the previous afternoon at which the student was informed of the basis for the suspension and afforded the opportunity to present a version of the events, as required by Education Code §48911(b). Notice of suspension was mailed and telephoned to the parent/guardian of record on October 5, 2011; telephone contact was documented as completed at the second attempt.

The site administrator recommended expulsion on October 7, 2011. The Superintendent's designee reviewed the recommendation and extended the suspension pending hearing on October 12, 2011 in accordance with Education Code §48911(g), following a conference at which the parent/guardian participated by telephone with an interpreter present on the District line.

Written notice of hearing was mailed by certified mail on October 14, 2011, thirteen calendar days in advance of the scheduled hearing date, and included the statement of charges, a copy of the disciplinary rules alleged to have been violated, notice of the right to inspect all documents to be presented, notice of the right to be represented, and notice of the obligation of the parent/guardian to notify a subsequent district of enrollment of any expulsion order. Return receipt was recorded on October 17, 2011.

The student's records were reviewed on October 18, 2011 for evidence of a suspected disability. The student was not identified under the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act or Section 504, and no prior referral was located in the cumulative file. A manifestation determination was accordingly not required.

HEARING AND FINDINGS

The hearing convened on October 27, 2011 in closed session at the District office. The parent/guardian and the student attended and were accompanied by a non-attorney advocate. An interpreter certified by the District was present throughout and was sworn. The presentation consisted of the site administrator's report, three written witness statements taken on October 5 and 6, 2011, and the testimony of two employees. The student testified. Two character witnesses were presented by the family.

The panel found by a preponderance of the evidence that the conduct charged occurred, that it constituted a violation of Education Code §48900(k), and that other means of correction had been attempted and documented on three prior occasions in the current and preceding school year. The panel further found that the presence of the student constituted a continuing danger to the physical safety of others within the meaning of Education Code §48915(b).

ORDER

The panel recommended expulsion for the balance of the fall semester and the succeeding spring semester. The Board adopted the recommendation in closed session on November 8, 2011 and, on the panel's further recommendation, suspended enforcement of the order for a period of one calendar year under Education Code §48917, conditioned on enrollment in the District's community day program, weekly attendance verification, and completion of a counseling referral. The rehabilitation plan was issued the same date with a review date of May 2012. Notice of the order, the suspended enforcement, the conditions, and the right of appeal to the County Board of Education within thirty days was mailed November 9, 2011. No appeal was filed.
