BASIS OF CLAIM

The claimant sought reimbursement of annual permit and re-inspection fees paid
during the 2015 permit year, asserting that two re-inspections were conducted in
error following a plan review that had already resolved the underlying deficiency.
The claim was filed within the ninety-day window prescribed by the fee ordinance.

ADJUDICATION NOTES

Division records confirm that the plan review was closed prior to the first
re-inspection. The fee for that re-inspection is allowed in full, together with
the associated administrative surcharge.

The second re-inspection was prompted by a separate complaint received through the
public intake line and documented under an independent activity number. That
inspection was properly noticed and the fee is not reimbursable; the corresponding
amount is disallowed.

The claimant additionally sought reimbursement of the base annual permit fee. The
ordinance does not provide for refund of the annual fee where the permit remained
active and the facility operated throughout the permit year. That portion is
disallowed.

Determination issued by the Environmental Health Director. The claimant was advised
in writing of the right to appeal to the Board within thirty days of the mailing
date. No appeal was received before the period expired, and the determination is
final. Allowed amount forwarded to the Auditor-Controller for payment processing
against the current fiscal year appropriation.
