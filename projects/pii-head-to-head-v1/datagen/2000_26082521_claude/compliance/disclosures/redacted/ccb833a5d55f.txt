PART B. NATURE OF THE GRIEVANCE

The Grievant alleges that the assignment of mandatory overtime hours during the pay periods ending 01/17/2004 and 01/31/2004 was effected without regard to the seniority-based rotation required by Article 14, Section 14.3(d) of the Memorandum of Understanding then in force between the County and the certified bargaining representative. The Grievant further alleges that the resulting distribution of hours constituted an inequitable allocation of premium compensation opportunity within the meaning of Article 14, Section 14.5.

PART D. FINDINGS OF FACT

1. The overtime rotation log maintained by the Division for the period in question was produced and admitted as Departmental Exhibit 1.
2. The log reflects that overtime was offered in descending order of classification seniority on nine (9) of the eleven (11) occasions at issue.
3. On the two (2) remaining occasions, the offering supervisor invoked the emergent-staffing exception set forth at Article 14, Section 14.3(f).
4. Contemporaneous shift documentation corroborates that census levels on both occasions exceeded the threshold specified in that subsection.

PART E. DETERMINATION

The grievance is SUSTAINED IN PART and DENIED IN PART. The Department is directed to make the Grievant whole for premium compensation associated with one (1) of the eleven (11) occasions, the record being insufficient to establish that the emergent-staffing exception was properly invoked as to that instance. The remainder of the grievance is denied. This determination is final at the departmental level and does not preclude timely demand for arbitration under Article 22.
