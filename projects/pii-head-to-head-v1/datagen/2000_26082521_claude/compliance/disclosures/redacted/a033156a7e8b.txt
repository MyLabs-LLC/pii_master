FINDINGS

- The three-way reconciliation for the April cycle did not clear on first pass. The bank balance, book balance, and trial balance of open file liabilities differed by an amount carried as an unidentified credit for eleven days.
- The credit was traced on 2004-05-06 to an incoming wire posted to the trust account without a matching file assignment. The originating instruction referenced a file that had closed and disbursed on 2004-04-13.
- Review of the closed file shows that a payoff figure was overstated in the payoff demand relied upon at closing. The lienholder refunded the overage directly to the branch rather than to the disbursing party.
- The refund sat in suspense for eleven days because the suspense aging report was run but not reviewed by the branch manager during the reporting week. The report itself functioned correctly.

DISPOSITION

- 2004-05-07   Overage identified and coded to the originating file. Suspense entry cleared.
- 2004-05-10   Refund check issued to the party entitled under the settlement statement. Payee and check number suppressed in this record.
- 2004-05-12   Reconciliation re-run. All three balances agreed. Signed reconciliation filed with the cycle package.
- 2004-05-14   Branch manager acknowledged the aging report review requirement in writing.

CONCLUSION

No shortage occurred and no client funds were used for any purpose other than the transaction for which they were deposited. The exception is one of timeliness in clearing suspense, not of trust integrity, and does not meet the threshold for notification to the state regulator under the applicable escrow regulations. The item is closed for the April cycle and will be tested again in the July cycle. Aging report review is added to the branch manager's monthly certification checklist effective June 1, 2004.
