DECISION AND ORDER (SUMMARY). Having considered the whole of the record,
the Hearing Officer determines that the arrearage asserted is correct in
amount, the meter having tested within the tolerance prescribed by
Commission Rule 4.03, and that no billing error occurred within the period
under review. The Hearing Officer further determines, upon the sealed
certification lodged as Exhibit A, that discontinuance during the period
1 July through 30 September 2016 would occasion a serious hazard to the
health of a resident of the premises, and that the protections of Rule 9.05
are accordingly engaged.

IT IS ORDERED that discontinuance be stayed to and including 30 September
2016; that the arrearage be liquidated in twelve (12) equal monthly
installments commencing with the billing cycle of August 2016, each such
installment to be paid together with current charges; and that default in
any two installments shall dissolve this stay without further notice. Any
party aggrieved may petition the Commission for review within twenty (20)
days of the date of issuance.
