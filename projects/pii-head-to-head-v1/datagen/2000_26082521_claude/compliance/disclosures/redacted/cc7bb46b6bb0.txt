Clause 1. Scope of Production
     (a)  This certificate accompanies the materials assembled in response to the request
          referenced above and describes the treatment applied to each category of record.
     (b)  The production comprises the escrow file, the trust account ledger extract for
          the disbursement cycle, internal escalation correspondence, and the compliance
          case narrative.

Clause 2. Method of Suppression
     (a)  Suppression was applied field-by-field rather than by wholesale withholding of
          documents, so that the operative narrative of each record remains legible.
     (b)  Where a field carried an identifying value, the value was removed and the field
          label preserved in place. Three markings are used and are not interchangeable:
          bracketed notation for individually identifying values, solid blocking for
          entity and location values, and character substitution for numeric strings.
     (c)  Signature blocks, notarial certificates, and imaged instruments bearing
          endorsements were withheld in their entirety and are itemised at Schedule B.

Clause 3. Categories Suppressed
     (a)  Party and personnel names in every capacity, including witnesses, brokers, and
          institutional contacts.
     (b)  Account numbers of every description, including trust, operating, and receiving
          accounts, together with routing identifiers and confirmation strings.
     (c)  Property addresses, legal descriptions, parcel identifiers, and recording book
          and page references.
     (d)  Telecommunication and electronic contact details appearing anywhere in the body
          of the produced records.

Clause 4. Categories Retained
     (a)  Dates of every process step, including receipt, escalation, disbursement,
          notification, interview, and review.
     (b)  Internal reference codes, case numbers, evidence tags, and policy citations.
     (c)  Findings, adjudication notes, control assessments, and remediation directives
          in their original wording.

Clause 5. Certification
     (a)  The undersigned has caused a page-by-page review to be conducted and confirms
          that the suppressions described are complete as to the categories listed at
          Clause 3.
     (b)  A suppression log recording each instance by page and field is maintained
          separately under the same production reference and is available on request of
          the requesting authority.
     (c)  Executed at the offices of the company on 14 October 2016.
