SUMMARY

Claim arises from an alleged fall aboard a revenue vehicle during deceleration at a mid-block stop. Notice of claim was timely served. A statutory hearing was noticed for 4 March 2016 and held 22 March 2016 following one adjournment at the request of claimant's counsel.

INVESTIGATION

The Field Investigator obtained the vehicle event recorder download on 26 January 2016. Deceleration values recorded at the time index in question fell within normal service range and did not indicate an emergency application. Onboard camera media was preserved under retention hold RH-16-0044 and remains available. The Operator of Record submitted a written statement on 2 February 2016 consistent with the recorder data.

Two passenger witnesses were identified. Contact information is suppressed. Statements taken 5 February 2016 and 11 February 2016 are attached with identifying fields withheld.

ADJUDICATION NOTES

Liability is assessed as doubtful. The recorder data materially undercuts the pleaded mechanism of injury, and no maintenance defect was recorded against the fleet number in the ninety days preceding the incident. Medical specials as pleaded are unverified pending authorization for records release, which was requested 14 April 2016 and has not been returned.

RECOMMENDATION

Deny at the administrative level under disposition code DISP-D-1 and reserve at nominal value pending suit. Re-evaluate reserve if suit is filed within the limitations period ending 28 December 2016. No settlement authority is requested at this time.

Referred to the Claims Review Panel for disposition. Panel action to be entered under the file reference above.
