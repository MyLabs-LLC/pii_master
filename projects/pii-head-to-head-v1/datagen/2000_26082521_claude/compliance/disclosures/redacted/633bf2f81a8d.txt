I. BASIS OF THE NOTICE

1. This notice issues under Education Code provisions governing evaluation of certificated employees and under Article XI of the Master Agreement.

2. The employee received an overall rating of "Does Not Meet Standard" in Standard 2 (Creating and Maintaining Effective Environments for Student Learning) and Standard 4 (Planning Instruction and Designing Learning Experiences) on the summative evaluation dated 25 February 2011.

3. Observations informing the rating were conducted on 12 October 2010 (formal, announced), 18 November 2010 (formal, unannounced), and 26 January 2011 (formal, announced). Post-observation conferences were held within five working days of each observation.

II. PARTICULARS

6. No allegation of misconduct is made. This notice concerns instructional performance only.
