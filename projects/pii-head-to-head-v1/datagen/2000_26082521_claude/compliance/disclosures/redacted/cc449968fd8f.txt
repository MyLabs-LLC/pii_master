SECTION C — NARRATIVE OF OCCURRENCE
1.  On the date stated at A.4, a refill authorization was received by electronic transmission at 10:42 and entered into the dispensing system by the technician identified at B.7.
2.  The prescription directed a maintenance dose of levothyroxine sodium 25 mcg once daily. The product selected at the shelf was levothyroxine sodium 50 mcg, thirty (30) tablets.
3.  Product verification was performed at 11:07 by the pharmacist identified at B.6. The National Drug Code was scanned; the scan returned a mismatch alert, which was overridden under override code 14 ("stock rotation / repackage").
4.  The prescription was released at the drive-through window at 16:20 on the same date. Patient counselling was declined at the point of sale, and the declination was recorded in the signature log.
5.  The variance was identified on 15 March 2016 at 09:15 when the patient contacted the pharmacy to query a change in tablet colour and imprint.

SECTION D — IMMEDIATE ACTIONS TAKEN
6.  The patient was advised to discontinue the dispensed product pending prescriber contact. Corrected product was prepared and delivered at no charge the same day.
7.  The prescriber of record was contacted by telephone at 09:48 on 15 March 2016 and confirmed no clinical follow-up was indicated beyond routine thyroid function monitoring at the next scheduled interval.
8.  The remaining stock bottle was quarantined and reconciled. No further discrepancy was identified in the perpetual inventory count.

SECTION E — CONTRIBUTING FACTORS
9.  Adjacent shelf placement of two strengths of the same molecule in identical manufacturer packaging.
10. Override of the barcode mismatch alert without secondary confirmation, contrary to Standard Operating Procedure PH-DIS-018 §4.2.
11. Staffing at the time of verification recorded at one pharmacist and one technician against a prescription volume of 312 for the shift.

SECTION F — CORRECTIVE ACTION PLAN
12. Tall-man lettering shelf tags applied to all thyroid hormone strengths; completed 22 March 2016.
13. Override code 14 restricted to pharmacist-in-charge credentials at the store level; system change effective 01 April 2016.
14. Documented re-training on SOP PH-DIS-018 for all pharmacy staff at the store; completed 15 April 2016, attendance roster filed under separate cover.

SECTION G — ADJUDICATION
15. The Quality Assurance Committee met on 28 April 2016 and determined the event to be a system-contributed variance. No individual disciplinary referral was made.
16. No claim was tendered by or on behalf of the patient within the notice period. No reserve was established.
17. Reportability was assessed against state board rules and found not to meet the mandatory reporting threshold, the event having resulted in no documented harm.

SECTION H — RETENTION
18. Retain ten (10) years from the date at A.7. Identifier fields at B.1–B.10 withheld from this copy.
