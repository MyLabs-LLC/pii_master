NARRATIVE AND PROCEDURAL HISTORY

The claimant, being at all material times an employee of the Company within the meaning of Article 3(1) of the Collective Agreement then in force, sustained a lumbar strain injury on 11 April 1997 in the course of manual handling operations at the inbound receiving bay. A First Report of Injury was lodged with the Depot Supervisor on the same date and transmitted to this office on 14 April 1997.

Medical certification from the attending practitioner was received 18 April 1997, certifying the claimant unfit for duties involving lifting in excess of ten kilograms for a period of six weeks. Wage replacement benefits commenced 21 April 1997 at the statutory rate of sixty-six and two-thirds per centum of average weekly earnings, calculated in accordance with Schedule B of the Company's compensation policy.

An independent examination was scheduled for 09 June 1997 and duly attended. The examining physician's report, filed 16 June 1997, concluded that maximum medical improvement had been reached and that no permanent partial impairment rating was warranted.

ADJUDICATION NOTE: Claim accepted as compensable. No contributory negligence found. Return to modified duties effected 23 June 1997; return to full duties effected 07 July 1997. File recommended for closure subject to the two-year reopening period prescribed by statute.
