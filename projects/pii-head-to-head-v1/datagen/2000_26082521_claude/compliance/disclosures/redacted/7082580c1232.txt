SECTION D — NARRATIVE
The quarterly application access review compared assigned system roles
against the position role matrix approved by the Compliance Committee on
2015-11-18. The review identified that the employee retained the
"Adjustment — Unrestricted" role and the "Charge Master Inquiry" role,
neither of which is provisioned to the Representative II classification.
Both entitlements had been granted on 2014-09-30 during a temporary
coverage assignment and were not revoked when that assignment concluded.

Transaction sampling covering 2016-01-01 through 2016-04-30 was performed
by the Internal Audit Analyst. Ninety-two adjustment transactions were
posted under the elevated role. All ninety-two carried supervisor
approval documentation in the workflow queue and fell within the dollar
thresholds of policy FIN-19. No transaction was posted to an account
associated with the employee, and no pattern of small-balance write-off
was detected. The finding was therefore classified as an entitlement
control deficiency rather than misuse.

Written counseling was issued for failure to report the retained access
during the two prior annual attestations, which the employee
acknowledged. The employee stated the attestation screens were completed
without review of the itemized role list. This explanation is consistent
with the attestation completion times recorded in the identity management
log and with similar findings across the cost center.

Corrective action at the department level: the Patient Accounts Manager
requested that the attestation workflow be modified to require line-item
acknowledgment of each role rather than a single confirmation. The
request was submitted to Information Services as change ticket CHG-2016-
0774 and scheduled for the fourth-quarter release.

Access rights were reduced to the standard Representative II profile
effective 2016-06-24. No interruption to production duties resulted; the
employee's adjustment work is now routed to the supervisor queue.

The ninety-day follow-up review found no further variance. The file is
closed at the department level and forwarded to Human Resources for
retention under schedule HR-R-07 (seven years from separation).
