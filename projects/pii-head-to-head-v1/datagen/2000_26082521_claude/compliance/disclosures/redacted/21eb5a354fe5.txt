SECTION A — BACKGROUND

A.1 The consignment was collected on 12 August 2004 from the customer's manufacturing site and consigned for trunking overnight to the regional cross-dock, with final delivery scheduled for 13 August 2004 before noon.

A.2 The consignment was trunked as planned and arrived at the cross-dock at 02:55 on 13 August 2004. It was broken down onto a delivery vehicle at 05:20 the same morning.

A.3 Delivery was effected at 11:40 on 13 August 2004. The receiving warehouse signed the delivery note claused "damage to outer packaging, contents unchecked".

SECTION B — CLAIM AS PRESENTED

B.1 A written claim was received on 2 September 2004, within the twenty-one day contractual notification period, alleging water ingress affecting part of the consignment and shortage of two cartons.

B.2 The claim was quantified by reference to the customer's invoice value. The carriage conditions in force limit liability by weight, and the claimed sum exceeds that limit.

SECTION C — INVESTIGATION

C.1 Trailer inspection on 3 September 2004 identified a repaired tear in the roof sheet approximately 1.2 metres from the rear header. The repair pre-dates the movement and was recorded on the trailer defect log on 4 July 2004.

C.2 Weather records for the trunking corridor confirm sustained rainfall between 23:00 on 12 August and 04:00 on 13 August 2004.

C.3 The cross-dock scan record accounts for all pallets but does not evidence carton-level counts. The shortage element is therefore neither confirmed nor excluded by system data.

C.4 The night shift check sheet for 12 August 2004 was completed but the roof condition box was left blank.

SECTION E — ADJUDICATION

E.1 Liability is admitted in respect of the water-damaged cartons at lines 1 and 2. The defect log entry of 4 July 2004 establishes that the roof condition was known and that the trailer was released to service without effective repair.

E.2 The shortage element at line 3 is repudiated. The clean pallet-level scan at the cross-dock and the absence of any carton count at the point of receipt leave the claim unsupported on the evidence available.

E.3 Settlement is to be calculated at the weight-based limitation figure and not at invoice value. The Claims Handler is authorised to communicate the offer once the limitation calculation has been checked by the Contracts Adviser.

E.4 Two remedial actions are directed: the defect log escalation threshold is to be reviewed by the Fleet Engineer before 30 November 2004, and the night shift check sheet is to be amended to make the roof condition entry mandatory.
