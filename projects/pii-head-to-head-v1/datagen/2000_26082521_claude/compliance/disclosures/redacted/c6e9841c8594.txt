BASIS OF CLAIM. Claimant asserts that the suspension of a retail food facility health permit, effected by field order on 05/26/2004 following a routine inspection scored below the closure threshold, was imposed without the notice contemplated by the County Food Protection Ordinance §8.04.190, and seeks damages for business interruption during the seven-day period of suspension.

ADMINISTRATIVE REVIEW SEQUENCE.
06/15/2004 — Claim docketed; acknowledgment of receipt transmitted under Gov. Code §910.8.
06/28/2004 — Inspection file retrieved from Bureau records; field notes, scoring sheet and photographic log compiled for review.
07/09/2004 — Statement obtained from the inspecting Registered Environmental Health Specialist.
07/16/2004 — Matter referred to County Counsel for advisory opinion, Reference No. CC-ADV-XXXX.
08/04/2004 — Advisory opinion returned, concluding that verbal notice of suspension delivered on-site, followed by written confirmation within twenty-four hours, satisfied the ordinance.
08/11/2004 — Recommendation of rejection prepared by the Bureau Chief.

DISPOSITION. Claim REJECTED in its entirety by action of the Board of Supervisors, Agenda Item No. 47, adopted 08/24/2004. Notice of rejection served upon Claimant by certified mail on 08/26/2004, incorporating the warning required by Gov. Code §913(b) as to the six-month limitation period for the filing of a court action.
