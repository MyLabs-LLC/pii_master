1. This action arises from the annual position audit conducted across
   Transcript Services between 03 January and 25 February 2011.

2. The audit questionnaire was completed by the incumbent on 19 January
   2011 and countersigned by the Records Custodian on 24 January 2011.

3. The classification analyst conducted a desk audit on 09 February 2011
   and recorded that the incumbent had assumed standing responsibility
   for the degree-audit exception queue, a duty not contemplated in the
   position description last revised 12 June 2008.

4. The Associate Registrar confirmed that the exception queue was
   reassigned to the incumbent on or about 15 August 2010 following a
   vacancy in the unit, and that the assignment has continued without
   interruption since that date.

5. The analyst determined that the added duty is properly allocated to
   the Records Analyst I specification and that the volume of such work
   exceeds thirty percent of the incumbent's assigned time.

6. ACTION APPROVED: Reclassification from Records Assistant III to
   Records Analyst I, effective at the start of the pay period beginning
   03 July 2011. Salary placement at the entry step of the receiving
   range, with no adjustment to service credit or leave accrual.

7. The incumbent was notified in writing on 06 May 2011 and
   acknowledged receipt on 10 May 2011. No appeal was filed within the
   fourteen-day window prescribed by the classification appeal rule.

8. The position description of record shall be reissued to reflect the
   allocated duties. The prior description is retained in the file as a
   superseded document.
