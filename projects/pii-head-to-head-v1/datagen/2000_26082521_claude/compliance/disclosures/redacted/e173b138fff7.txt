SECTION B — PROCEDURAL CHRONOLOGY

  Pursuant to the Company's Attendance Control Programme as revised and promulgated
  01 July 1995, the subject accumulated eight chargeable occurrences within the rolling
  twelve month measurement period ending 31 October 1996. The following steps were taken
  in the ordinary course:

    02 AUG 96 ....... Verbal counselling recorded at fourth occurrence
    06 SEP 96 ....... Written notice issued at sixth occurrence
    14 OCT 96 ....... Final written notice issued at eighth occurrence
    21 OCT 96 ....... Request for review submitted by the subject
    28 OCT 96 ....... Review meeting convened; union representation present
    05 NOV 96 ....... Medical documentation tendered in respect of three occurrences

SECTION C — FINDINGS AND DISPOSITION

  It is the finding of this office that three of the eight occurrences recorded, being
  those of 19 August 1996, 20 August 1996 and 23 September 1996, are supported by
  contemporaneous medical certification and are therefore excludable from the count in
  accordance with paragraph 5(b) of the Programme, which provides that absences
  certified by a licensed practitioner and arising from a single continuous condition
  shall be treated as one occurrence and shall not be chargeable where certification is
  produced within thirty days.

  The remaining five occurrences stand as recorded. The subject's total is accordingly
  adjusted to five chargeable occurrences, which falls below the threshold at which
  final written notice is warranted under the Programme.

  DISPOSITION: The final written notice of 14 October 1996 is rescinded and shall be
  removed from the active personnel file. The written notice of 06 September 1996
  stands and shall lapse by operation of the rolling measurement period on 06 September
  1997. The subject is to be advised in writing of this disposition, and the Payroll
  Section is to be notified that no adjustment to earnings arises.

  No further action is required of the reporting supervisor at this time. This record is
  to be retained for the period prescribed by the Company's retention schedule and
  thereafter destroyed in the manner there specified.
