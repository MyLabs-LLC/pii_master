    II. SUMMARY OF FINDINGS
        A. The Q1 audit sampled 340 completed prescriptions filled between
           02 January 2004 and 28 February 2004 at the subject location. Of these,
           eleven records showed a discrepancy between the quantity entered at
           point of sale and the quantity recorded in the perpetual inventory
           log for schedule III controlled substances.
        B. The discrepancies fell into two patterns. Seven involved a partial
           fill that was never closed out in the system, leaving an open balance
           that aged past the 72-hour window required under standard operating
           procedure SOP-DIS-114. Four involved an inventory adjustment entered
           without a corresponding supervisor countersignature.
        C. No evidence of diversion was identified. The reconciliation performed
           on 09 March 2004 accounted for the full quantity of product in each
           of the eleven instances. The variance was administrative in nature.
