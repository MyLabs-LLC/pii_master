    3. FINDINGS
        3.1 On three separate transactions during the period 01 March through 23 April 2021, wiring instructions were released to the funding desk without the second-approver signature required by Treasury Directive TD-11.
        3.2 In each instance the funds reached the intended recipient and no loss occurred. The control failure was procedural rather than financial.
        3.3 The Internal Audit Unit identified the pattern during the routine quarterly sample and referred it under audit finding AF-2021-Q1-062.
        3.4 The employee acknowledged the omissions during the fact-finding meeting held 28 April 2021 and attributed them to volume pressure during the March refinance surge.

    4. CORRECTIVE MEASURES
        4.1 Mandatory re-certification on TD-11 and the wire-fraud prevention module, to be completed by 04 June 2021.
        4.2 All wire releases originated by this employee subject to supervisory pre-review for ninety days, through 02 August 2021.
        4.3 Weekly one-to-one review with the reporting manager during the pre-review period.

    5. ADJUDICATION NOTES
        5.1 The reviewing panel considered whether the March volume constituted a mitigating operational condition. It found the condition real but not exculpatory, as the escalation path in TD-11 §4 was available and unused.
