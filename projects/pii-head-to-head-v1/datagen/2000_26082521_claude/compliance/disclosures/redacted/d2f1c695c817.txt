INVESTIGATION NARRATIVE

The report was received by the Unit through the electronic laboratory reporting
interface on the date shown above and was queued for triage under the standing
Category II protocol, which requires investigator assignment within two business
days. Assignment was made the following morning by the Unit Supervisor.

A first telephone contact attempt was unsuccessful; a voicemail message was left
in accordance with the Unit's confidentiality script, which does not disclose the
condition under investigation. A second attempt two days later reached the client,
and the standard interview instrument was completed in a single session lasting
approximately forty minutes. The client was cooperative and reported no barriers
to treatment access. Symptom onset was stated as occurring approximately eleven
days before the report date.

Supplementary laboratory results consistent with the initial report were received
on 22 March 2016, satisfying the confirmatory criteria in the current surveillance
case definition. The case was reclassified from Probable to Confirmed on that date
by the Unit Supervisor and the change was entered in the surveillance system.

Contact elicitation produced three named contacts, each of whom was entered as a
separate record and is not further described here. Two were reached and offered
evaluation; one was referred to an adjacent jurisdiction under the reciprocal
notification agreement. No cluster linkage was identified.
