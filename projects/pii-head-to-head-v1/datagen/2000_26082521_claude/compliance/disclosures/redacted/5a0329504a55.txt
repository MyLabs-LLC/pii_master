    B. DESCRIPTION
        A refill was dispensed at the correct drug and correct dosage form but
        at a strength one increment above the strength written. The error was
        identified at the point of pickup by the patient, who observed that the
        tablet imprint differed from the prior fill. The bag was not removed
        from the counter. No dose was taken.

    C. IMMEDIATE ACTIONS
        1. Product quarantined at 5:15 PM on the day of occurrence.
        2. Correct strength dispensed and counseled the same visit.
        3. Prescriber contacted by the pharmacy manager for notification. No
           change to therapy directed.
        4. Incident entered in the quality event system before end of shift.

    D. CONTRIBUTING FACTOR ANALYSIS
        Both strengths of the product were stocked in adjacent bins with
        near-identical label presentation. The refill was processed during the
        4:00 PM to 5:00 PM window, the highest-volume hour at this location.
        The verification scan was performed but the scan confirms NDC family
        rather than full package identity under the version of the software in
        use at this store as of the date of occurrence.

    E. CORRECTIVE ACTION
        1. Bin separation implemented 12 October 2004. Confirmed by district
           audit 19 October 2004.
        2. Software configuration ticket raised: TKT-2004-44117. Full package
           identity verification enabled chain-wide in the November release.
        3. No disciplinary action. Event classified as system-attributable
           under the non-punitive reporting policy.
