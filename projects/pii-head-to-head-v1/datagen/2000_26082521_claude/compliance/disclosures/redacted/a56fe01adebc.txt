1. Purpose

1.1 This memorandum accompanies the disclosure bundle assembled in respect of the incident of 22 June 2004 and records the steps taken between notification and provisional adjudication.

1.2 The bundle comprises the incident report, the tachograph analysis, the vehicle examiner's report dated 5 July 2004, and correspondence with the third-party insurer.

2. Chronology of Process Steps

2.1 22 June 2004, 06:40 — Incident reported to the Traffic Office by the driver via the depot radio channel.

2.2 22 June 2004, 09:15 — First notification of loss lodged with the insurer under reference MVA/04/0231.

2.3 24 June 2004 — Tractor unit recovered to the contracted repair agent. Trailer released for service after inspection.

2.4 5 July 2004 — Vehicle examiner's report received. Damage confined to the nearside front quarter and mirror assembly.

2.5 19 July 2004 — Third-party correspondence received alleging liability in full.

2.6 30 August 2004 — Tachograph charts for the seven days preceding the incident retrieved from depot archive and analysed.

3. Findings

3.1 The tachograph record shows no breach of driving-hours limits in the shift concerned. The rest period preceding the shift exceeded the required minimum by fifty-five minutes.

3.2 The route taken was consistent with the assigned run schedule. No unauthorised deviation is evidenced.

3.3 Road conditions at the time were recorded as wet, with surface water noted on the approach to the roundabout.

4. Adjudication Notes

4.1 The Panel is invited to note that liability is contested. The third-party account and the driver's account diverge materially as to lane position at the point of contact.

4.2 In the absence of independent witness evidence, the Claims Handler recommends a without-prejudice apportionment offer at 50/50, subject to insurer concurrence.

4.3 No disciplinary process has been opened. The Transport Manager has confirmed that the driver's licence check and periodic assessment were both current at the date of incident.

5. Retention

5.1 This file is to be retained for six years from the date of final settlement, after which it falls due for review under the standard destruction schedule.
