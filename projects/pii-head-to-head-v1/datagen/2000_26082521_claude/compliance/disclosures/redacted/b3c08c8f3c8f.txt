PART 3. ISSUES PRESENTED
===============================================================================
Issue 1. Whether the district failed to implement the individualized education program
         as written during the period from January 2004 through June 2004, specifically
         with respect to the frequency and duration of designated instruction services.

Issue 2. Whether the district failed to convene an IEP team meeting within thirty days
         of a written parental request.

Issue 3. Whether the district's assessment plan, offered in the spring of 2004, was
         sufficient in scope to address all areas of suspected disability, and whether
         an independent educational evaluation at public expense is warranted.

PART 5. ADJUDICATION NOTE AND DISPOSITION
===============================================================================
Issue 1 — Decided for the parent, in part. Service logs established a shortfall in
delivered minutes across two reporting periods. The shortfall was found material rather
than de minimis. Compensatory services ordered: 24 hours, to be delivered by a
non-district provider selected from the approved vendor list, scheduled by January 31, 2005.

Issue 2 — Decided for the district. The written request was received during the summer
recess period; the thirty-day count excludes days of school vacation in excess of five
days. The team meeting convened within the adjusted timeline.

Issue 3 — Decided for the district. The assessment plan addressed each area supported by
the record. Request for independent educational evaluation at public expense denied.
