You are advised that the above-captioned action was served upon the Commission through its designated agent for service on 02 October 1996, the summons and complaint having been date-stamped by this office on the same day. The complaint sounds in negligence and premises liability and arises out of an occurrence alleged to have taken place on 21 November 1995 upon the mezzanine level of the Central Interchange Station, in the vicinity of the northbound escalator bank designated ESC-CI-N3 in the Commission's facilities inventory.

The gravamen of the complaint is that the Commission, its agents and servants, negligently permitted an accumulation of water to remain upon the mezzanine floor surface for an unreasonable period of time, and further failed to place warning devices or to cordon the affected area, notwithstanding actual or constructive notice of the condition. Plaintiff pleads special damages and general damages in amounts set forth at paragraphs 14 through 19 of the complaint.

You are directed to institute an immediate litigation hold upon all records, in whatever medium maintained, relating to the following: escalator bank ESC-CI-N3 and the mezzanine level of Central Interchange Station for the period 01 September 1995 through the present; all custodial and janitorial service logs for the said station for the same period, including contractor service tickets under contract JAN-95-114; all incident reports, whether or not resulting in a claim, pertaining to falls at the said station for the period commencing 01 January 1994; all closed-circuit surveillance recordings, if any survive the ordinary retention interval of thirty (30) days; and all maintenance work orders in series WO-95-CI and WO-96-CI.

An answer is due to be interposed on or before 22 October 1996. This office intends to serve a general denial together with affirmative defenses of comparative negligence, absence of notice, and the notice-of-claim limitations set forth in the enabling statute. Preliminary evaluation of exposure will be transmitted under separate cover upon completion of the document collection directed hereinabove.

Kindly acknowledge receipt of this advice and confirm implementation of the hold not later than 15 October 1996.
