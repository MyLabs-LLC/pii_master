SECTION 2 — CLAUSES

1. This review was opened on the reactivation request logged 8 February 1999, following a dormancy of eleven months.

2. Under clause 6 of the Registration Standard (rev. 06/1998), reactivation after twelve months requires a full re-check; after six months and under twelve, a confirmatory check only. The dormancy being eleven months, a confirmatory check was commissioned on 11 February 1999 under vendor order VC-99-0774.

3. The vendor returned its result on 2 March 1999 disclosing one discrepancy: the dates of a prior engagement as stated on the registration form differed by two months from the dates confirmed by the referee.

4. The subject was invited to comment by letter of 4 March 1999 and responded on 15 March 1999, producing a payslip series that reconciled with the referee's dates.

5. The reviewing officer records that the discrepancy arose from a transcription error at the point of registration and was not a misstatement by the subject. The registration record has been amended, and the amendment audited under change ticket CHG-99-1180.

6. Determination: eligible for re-engagement without restriction, effective 26 March 1999. Skills grade carried forward unchanged. The next confirmatory check falls due on 26 March 2002, or on the next dormancy exceeding six months, whichever is the earlier.

7. No adverse entry has been made to the register, and no notification to client undertakings is required under clause 11.
