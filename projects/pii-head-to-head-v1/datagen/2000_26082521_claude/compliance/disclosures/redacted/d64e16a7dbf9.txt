PART C — FINDINGS AND ORDER

1. The Panel finds that the challenge was timely lodged within the
   period prescribed by the Records Challenge Procedure and that all
   preliminary steps required by that Procedure were exhausted.

2. The Petitioner appeared and was heard. The Petitioner was
   accompanied by an adviser of the Petitioner's choosing, who did not
   address the Panel.

3. The teaching department appeared through its designated
   representative and produced the section grade book, the syllabus in
   force for the term, and the departmental grade-review memorandum
   dated 21 January 2011.

4. The Panel received nine exhibits, marked P-1 through P-4 and D-1
   through D-5. Exhibits P-2 and D-4 were admitted over objection.

5. The Petitioner contended that the recorded grade did not reflect the
   weighted total shown on returned coursework and that a laboratory
   score entered on 06 December 2010 was attributed to the wrong roster
   line.

6. The department conceded a transcription discrepancy of eleven points
   in the laboratory component but maintained that the discrepancy did
   not alter the letter grade under the published scale.

7. The Panel independently recomputed the weighted total using the
   syllabus weights in force. The corrected component score raises the
   term percentage by 1.4 points.

8. The corrected percentage falls below the threshold for the next
   letter grade by 0.6 points. The Panel therefore finds no basis to
   disturb the letter grade as recorded.

9. The Panel further finds that the education record is inaccurate as to
   the component score and that the inaccuracy is subject to correction
   notwithstanding the outcome on the letter grade.

10. ORDER: The Registrar shall amend the component score of record to
    the corrected figure within ten (10) business days. The letter grade
    of record shall stand. The Petitioner may place a statement in the
    file commenting on the contested portion of the record, and such
    statement shall be disclosed whenever the contested portion is
    disclosed.

11. This determination is final within the Office of the Registrar. The
    Petitioner was advised in writing on 26 April 2011 of the right to
    file a complaint with the appropriate federal office.
