4. SUMMARY OF EVIDENCE
The Authority presented timekeeping extracts for the pay periods in question,
the shop sign-in sheets, and testimony of the assigning Foreperson that no
call-out was recorded on the two May dates. The Union produced a call-centre
confirmation number for 04 May 2016 and argued the third occurrence fell within
an approved intermittent leave certification then pending in the Benefits
Office. The Authority conceded at Step 2 that the certification had been
received 22 April 2016 and was not decided until 13 June 2016, after the notice
of discipline issued.

5. ADJUDICATION AND DISPOSITION
The occurrence of 27 May 2016 is expunged as covered by the certification, once
approved, retroactive to the date of filing. The occurrence of 04 May 2016 is
reduced to a documented late call, non-chargeable. The occurrence of 05 May 2016
stands. Discipline is modified from a five-day suspension to a written warning
with time held out of service to be restored as paid. Payroll adjustment
processed on the pay period ending 13 August 2016. The written warning remains
active twelve (12) months from 14 June 2016 and is thereafter removed from the
active file under CBA Art. 12.9.
