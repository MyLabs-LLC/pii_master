The claimant was at all material times engaged upon duties involving the manual handling of palletised freight within the inbound goods area, and had received the standard induction and the biennial manual-handling refresher, the latter last completed on 22 January 2004.

- **Incident narrative** — On the night shift commencing 11 May 2004, at approximately 02:40, the claimant sustained an injury to the lumbar region whilst attempting to reposition a partially collapsed pallet load in Bay 7. It is recorded that the mechanical handling aid ordinarily allocated to that bay was, on the night in question, out of service and awaiting a replacement hydraulic seal, that fact being corroborated by Maintenance Defect Log entry MDL-04-0918 raised on 9 May 2004. The claimant reported the matter to the shift supervisor within the shift and the Accident Book entry was completed the same night under reference AB/NW114/04/207.

- **Process chronology** — Internal notification received 11 May 2004; first medical attendance 11 May 2004; formal claim intimated to the insurer 26 May 2004; acknowledgement issued 2 June 2004; liability investigation opened 4 June 2004; witness statements obtained 17 and 19 June 2004 (deponent identities [SUPPRESSED]); occupational health assessment conducted 8 July 2004; interim payment authorised 30 July 2004; case conference convened 21 September 2004.

- **Adjudication note** — The adjudicating officer, having considered the maintenance records, the training register and the accounts of those present, finds that the employer's system of work was, on the night in question, deficient in that no alternative handling method was formally substituted upon the withdrawal of the mechanical aid. Contributory negligence is not made out on the evidence available. Liability is accordingly admitted in full, subject to quantum, and the file is remitted for medical evidence and a schedule of loss.
