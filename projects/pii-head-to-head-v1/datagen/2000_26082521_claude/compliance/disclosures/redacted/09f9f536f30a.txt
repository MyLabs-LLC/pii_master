1.  JURISDICTION. This matter arises under Academic Regulation 4.11 (Dismissal
    for Failure to Maintain Minimum Cumulative Standing) and is properly before
    the Committee on Academic Standing pursuant to Regulation 4.14(b).

2.  PROCEDURAL HISTORY.

      2.1  Notice of academic dismissal issued .............. 19 January 2004
      2.2  Written petition for reinstatement received ...... 12 February 2004
      2.3  Petition acknowledged; file docketed ............. 17 February 2004
      2.4  Supplemental documentation requested ............. 24 February 2004
      2.5  Supplemental documentation received .............. 09 March 2004
      2.6  Committee convened, closed session ............... 25 March 2004
      2.7  Decision letter mailed, certified ................ 06 April 2004

3.  GROUNDS ASSERTED. The petitioner asserted that a documented medical
    interruption during the second half of the Fall 2003 term materially
    impaired course performance in three enrolled sections, and that
    withdrawal deadlines passed while the petitioner was under care.

4.  EVIDENCE CONSIDERED. The Committee reviewed the certified academic
    history, the petition and its four attachments, a statement from the
    Office of Disability Services (Ref. ODS-04-0231), and a memorandum from
    the department chair of the major unit dated 18 March 2004. Attending
    practitioner correspondence was reviewed in camera and is retained in
    the confidential medical annex, which is not part of this extract.

5.  FINDINGS.

      5.1  The medical interruption is substantiated for the period
           commencing on or about 27 October 2003.
      5.2  Two of the three deficient grades fall within that period.
      5.3  The remaining deficient grade predates the interruption and is
           not excused by it.
      5.4  No prior petition under Regulation 4.14 appears in this file.

6.  DISPOSITION. Dismissal is VACATED. The petitioner is reinstated on
    Academic Probation for the Fall 2004 term, subject to the conditions
    recorded at Clause 7. Retroactive withdrawal is GRANTED as to the two
    sections identified in the confidential annex and DENIED as to the third.

7.  CONDITIONS OF REINSTATEMENT. (a) Minimum term grade point average of
    2.30; (b) enrollment capped at twelve credit hours; (c) three documented
    advising contacts, the first no later than the tenth instructional day;
    (d) failure to satisfy any condition returns the matter to the Committee
    without further notice.

8.  RECORD ANNOTATION. The Records Custodian shall enter code AP-04 against
    the Fall 2004 term and code WR-RETRO against the two sections named in
    the annex. No annotation shall appear on the official transcript beyond
    the standard probation notation required by Regulation 4.16.

9.  RETENTION. Seven years from date of closure, per Records Schedule R-14.
