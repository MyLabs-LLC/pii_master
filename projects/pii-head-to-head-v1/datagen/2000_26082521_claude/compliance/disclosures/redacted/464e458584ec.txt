1.  AUTHORITY. The Panel acts under Board of Regents Policy 610.04 and
    the implementing Registrar Procedure RP-610, which vest initial
    classification in the Office of Admissions and appellate review in
    this Panel. The burden of proof rests on the petitioner and is
    satisfied only by clear and convincing evidence.

2.  CHRONOLOGY OF PROCEEDINGS.

      Initial classification issued .............. 22 March 2004
      Written appeal received .................... 14 June 2004
      Appeal deemed complete ..................... 28 June 2004
      Documentary supplement requested ........... 02 July 2004
      Documentary supplement received ............ 26 July 2004
      Oral presentation, held telephonically ..... 11 August 2004
      Panel deliberation, closed ................. 11 August 2004
      Determination issued ....................... 20 August 2004

3.  DOCUMENTATION OF RECORD. The Panel received and examined the
    following, each of which has been de-identified in this extract but
    remains in the case file in unaltered form: (a) a lease instrument
    covering twenty months; (b) two payroll statements from an in-state
    employer; (c) a state income tax return for tax year 2003 filed as a
    full-year resident; (d) a motor vehicle registration issued in the
    state; (e) a voter registration acknowledgement; (f) an affidavit of
    financial independence executed before a notary; and (g) a parental
    tax return excerpt bearing on dependency status.

4.  ISSUE PRESENTED. Whether the petitioner established domicile within
    the state for the twelve consecutive months immediately preceding the
    first day of instruction of the Fall 2004 term, and whether presence
    in the state was for purposes other than enrollment at an institution
    of higher education.

5.  FINDINGS OF FACT.

      5.1  Continuous physical presence in the state is established from
           a date not later than 01 June 2003.
      5.2  Employment averaging thirty-two hours weekly is established
           for the period 14 July 2003 through the date of the appeal.
      5.3  The petitioner was not claimed as a dependent on any federal
           return for tax year 2003.
      5.4  Indicia of domicile — tax filing, vehicle registration, voter
           registration — are consistent and precede the twelve-month
           qualifying window by no less than sixty days.
      5.5  No evidence suggests that presence in the state was incident
           to enrollment; enrollment postdates establishment of domicile.

6.  CONCLUSIONS OF LAW. The petitioner has met the burden imposed by
    Policy 610.04(c). The presumption arising under Policy 610.04(f)
    against students entering the state within twelve months of
    enrollment is inapplicable on these facts and is not invoked.

7.  DETERMINATION. The appeal is SUSTAINED. The petitioner is classified
    as a Resident for Tuition Purposes effective with the Fall 2004 term.
    Classification is prospective only; no refund of prior-term
    differential tuition is authorized under Policy 610.04(h).

8.  IMPLEMENTATION. The Records Custodian shall post classification code
    RES-1 effective 2004-90 and notify Student Financial Services and the
    Bursar within five business days. The Panel retains no jurisdiction
    following posting.

9.  FURTHER REVIEW. A determination of this Panel is final within the
    institution. Nothing herein limits any remedy available at law.
