ADJUDICATION NOTES

Causation memo ENG-16-0074 attributes the failure to circumferential fracture of a
cast-iron main installed in 1948, consistent with graphitic corrosion observed on
the recovered pipe section. No prior break history is recorded on this segment
within the ten-year lookback window. Pressure telemetry for the district shows no
transient event preceding the failure; surge-induced rupture was therefore excluded
as a contributing cause.

Site inspection confirmed water migration into a below-grade storage area by way of
a foundation penetration. Damage was limited to floor covering, gypsum board to a
height of approximately eighteen inches, and stored household goods. Documentation
submitted comprised three contractor estimates and one paid invoice; the median
estimate was adopted for reserve purposes.

Liability was accepted on a strict-liability theory applicable to the failure of a
department-owned distribution facility. Comparative fault was not asserted, the
claimant having no maintenance responsibility for the main. Depreciation was applied
to stored goods at the schedule rate. Subrogation potential was evaluated and found
absent; no third-party excavation permit was open within the affected block during
the six months preceding the loss.

The determination letter offered settlement in full and final satisfaction of all
claims arising from the incident. Release REL-16-0417 was executed without
reservation. The file is closed for indemnity and expense. Retention class R-7;
destruction eligible 2023-07-29 subject to litigation hold review.
