1. Origin and Initial Response

1.1 A complaint alleging inadequate refrigeration and improper holding temperatures at a permitted food service establishment was received by the Division on 7 January 1999. The complaint was assigned the same day and dispatched to the District Sanitarian under the routine forty-eight hour response standard applicable to temperature-related allegations.

1.2 An unannounced inspection was conducted on 8 January 1999. Two critical violations were recorded, being cold holding above the permitted maximum in one reach-in unit and the absence of a functioning thermometer in a second unit. A correction notice issued on site, with a compliance deadline of 15 January 1999.

2. Follow-Up and Enforcement

2.1 A reinspection conducted on 15 January 1999 found the first violation corrected and the second uncorrected. An administrative citation issued under schedule EH-3, item 7, with the penalty amount fixed at the first-offence tier.

2.2 The permit holder requested an informal conference on 22 January 1999. The conference was convened on 4 February 1999 before the Division Chief, who heard evidence that a replacement thermometer had been ordered on 11 January 1999 but was not delivered until 19 January 1999.

2.3 The Division Chief found the delay documented and outside the permit holder's control, and reduced the citation to a written warning by order dated 9 February 1999. Compliance was verified at a final reinspection on 18 February 1999 and the matter closed on 22 February 1999.

3. Disclosure

3.1 Complainant and premises identifying fields have been suppressed in accordance with the confidentiality afforded to complainants under Division policy. Inspection findings, dates, citation schedules and the enforcement determination are released intact.

3.2 Released by the Records Custodian, 3 April 1999, under request DR-99-0088.
