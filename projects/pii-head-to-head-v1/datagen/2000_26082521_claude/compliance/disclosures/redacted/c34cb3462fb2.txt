FINDINGS AND ADJUDICATION

1.  The grievance was timely filed. The Level I filing occurred eleven working
    days after the notice of reassignment was served, within the fifteen-day
    window prescribed by Article XVII, Section 8.1.

2.  The District asserted that the reassignment was effected under the staffing
    authority reserved in Section 3.2(a) and was therefore not arbitrable. That
    assertion is rejected. Section 3.2(c) conditions the exercise of that
    authority on the consultation steps set out in subsection (d).

3.  The record establishes that the site administrator issued written notice on
    January 31, 2011, and that no consultation meeting was offered prior to
    that date. The consultation requirement of Section 3.2(d) was not met.

4.  The Association's further contention that the reassignment was retaliatory
    is not sustained. No evidence was introduced connecting the reassignment to
    the grievant's protected activity, and the two witnesses called on this
    point offered no direct testimony.

5.  The requested remedy of full rescission is denied. The receiving assignment
    is within the grievant's credential authorization and equivalent in salary
    placement, hours, and benefit eligibility.

6.  The District shall convene the consultation required by Section 3.2(d) no
    later than twenty working days from the date of this decision, and shall
    document the meeting on Form PER-114.

7.  Any documentation of this proceeding shall be maintained separately from
    the grievant's personnel file in accordance with Article XVII, Section 11.

8.  This decision is final at the district level. Either party may invoke
    binding arbitration under Section 9.3 by written demand served within
    thirty calendar days of May 11, 2011.
