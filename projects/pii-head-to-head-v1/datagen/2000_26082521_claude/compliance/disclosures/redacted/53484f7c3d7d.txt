This memorandum is prepared pursuant to Section 9.14 of the Authority's Claims Handling Manual (rev. January 1996) and is intended to memorialize the adjudicative posture of the above-referenced matter as of the date of transmittal to the Office of the General Counsel.

The claim arises out of an incident alleged to have occurred aboard Coach No. 4412 while said coach was operating in revenue service on Route 27 (Eastbound), between the hours of 1640 and 1655 on the date of loss set forth above. The claimant, whose particulars appear at the fields suppressed hereinabove, asserts that the operator of the aforementioned coach effected a sudden and unanticipated deceleration in the vicinity of the intersection identified in Operations Incident Report No. OIR-97-0311-27E, and that as a direct and proximate consequence thereof the claimant was caused to be projected forward against the stanchion assembly, sustaining the injuries particularized in the claimant's Notice of Claim.

Investigation was commenced on 31 March 1997 and proceeded in the ordinary course. The Authority obtained and reviewed the following: (i) the Operator's Statement, executed 15 March 1997 and bearing internal control number OS-97-1188; (ii) the Supervisor's Field Report of the same date; (iii) the vehicle maintenance history for Coach No. 4412 covering the twelve-month period preceding the date of loss, retrieved under work order series WO-96-4412; and (iv) the passenger manifest and farebox transaction log for the run in question, insofar as the same were retained under the Authority's then-applicable retention schedule. The claimant's medical documentation was received on 22 May 1997 under cover of correspondence from claimant's counsel, whose identifying particulars appear at the suppressed fields of the correspondence index.

Upon review, the Adjuster determined that the mechanical condition of Coach No. 4412 was not causally implicated, the brake system having been inspected on 06 February 1997 under work order WO-96-4412-0211 with no defects noted. The question of comparative negligence remains open, the Operator's Statement asserting that a private vehicle entered the coach's path without signal, and the claimant asserting that she was seated at all material times.

The matter was referred to the Claims Review Committee on 09 September 1997. The Committee, sitting in regular session, directed that a reserve in the amount previously established be maintained without adjustment pending receipt of the independent medical examination scheduled for 04 November 1997, and further directed that no offer of settlement be extended prior to the said examination. The Committee's determination is recorded at Minute Book entry CRC-97-142.
