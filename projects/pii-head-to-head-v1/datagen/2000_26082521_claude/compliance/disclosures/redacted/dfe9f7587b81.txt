SECTION III — SUBSTANCE OF THE PETITION

  • Petitioner asserts the recorded final grade does not reflect the arithmetic total of scored assessments as returned during the term.
  • Petitioner further asserts that a laboratory practical submitted on the published due date was entered as missing.
  • The instructional unit states the practical was received after the posted deadline and was assessed the standard late reduction described in the course syllabus.
  • The instructional unit produced the original scoring worksheet and the departmental late-submission log for the term.
  • Neither party disputes the scores recorded for the three midterm examinations or the final examination.

SECTION IV — FINDINGS

  • The panel finds the late-submission log entry corroborated by the departmental date stamp and by the teaching assistant's contemporaneous notation.
  • The panel finds one arithmetic error in the transfer of the second midterm score from the worksheet to the final grade computation, understating the composite by 2.4 points.
  • Corrected composite crosses the published threshold for the next letter grade.
  • The panel finds no evidence of arbitrary or capricious grading within the meaning of Academic Regulation 6.14(c).

SECTION V — DISPOSITION AND ACTIONS

  • Petition granted in part on the arithmetic ground; denied in part on the late-submission ground.
