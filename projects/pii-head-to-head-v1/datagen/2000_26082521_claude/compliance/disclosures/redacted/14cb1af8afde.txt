III. CIRCUMSTANCES OF INJURY
The operator was assigned to Run 214 departing Northgate at 14:36. At the
terminal loop the wheelchair ramp failed to deploy under power. Following the
procedure at OPS Bulletin 2015-44, the operator attempted manual deployment
using the override handle. The ramp bound at approximately half travel and the
operator reported immediate onset of low back and left shoulder pain while
applying force. Service was held; a supervisor attended at 14:58 and completed
Form OPS-9. The coach was pulled from service and tagged under work order
XXXXXXX. Maintenance subsequently confirmed a seized pivot bearing.

VI. ADJUDICATION NOTES
Compensability was accepted without hearing; no §29 defense was asserted, the
mechanism of injury being corroborated by the supervisor's report and the
maintenance work order. Claimant's counsel filed a claim for increased
impairment 08 July 2016. The Authority relied on the independent examination
and did not concede the higher rating. The parties resolved the dispute at
mediation on 19 September 2016 on a stipulated 5% award with medical left open
for two years. Stipulation approved by the Board 04 October 2016. Attorney fee
approved at the statutory percentage, payable from the award. No third-party
recovery is anticipated; the ramp assembly is Authority-owned and no vendor
warranty claim survives.
