II. SUBSTANCE OF THE REQUEST

The requester seeks amendment of a clinical progress note entered in the immunization module of the encounter record, contending that a vaccine administration documented therein was not in fact administered at this Department but at an out-of-county provider, and that the entry as it stands overstates the Department's role in the immunization series.

III. PROCESSING CHRONOLOGY

    10/04/2004 ...... Request logged; written acknowledgment issued.
    10/07/2004 ...... Request routed to the Health Information Manager.
    10/18/2004 ...... Originating clinician queried; response received 10/26/2004.
    11/02/2004 ...... Immunization registry cross-check performed.
    11/15/2004 ...... Determination drafted for supervisory review.
    11/22/2004 ...... Determination approved and issued.

IV. DETERMINATION

The request is GRANTED IN PART. The Department finds that the disputed entry is inaccurate insofar as it identifies this Department as the administering site. Pursuant to 45 C.F.R. §164.526(c), the Department will append an amendment to the designated record set and will link the amendment to the original entry, which shall not be expunged. The requester's further demand that the entry be deleted in its entirety is DENIED, the regulation providing for amendment rather than deletion of clinical documentation.

V. NOTIFICATION AND DISTRIBUTION

Notice of this determination has been transmitted to the requester at the address of record. Consistent with the identification of relevant persons furnished with the request, the amendment shall be transmitted to the out-of-county provider of record and to the State Immunization Registry.
