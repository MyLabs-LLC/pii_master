MEMORANDUM
Ref: HR-DISC-2021-0913
Date: 18 October 2021

To:      Regional Human Resources Business Partner
From:    Employee Relations Casework Unit
Subject: Closure of disciplinary file — Pharmacy Services

1. Employee Name: [SUPPRESSED]. Employee Number: XXXXXXX. Position: Certified Pharmacy Technician. Location: ██████████████.

2. The allegation concerned repeated failure to complete controlled substance count reconciliation at shift close on three occasions between 12 July 2021 and 09 August 2021.

3. An investigation meeting was convened on 24 August 2021. The employee was accompanied by a colleague and was provided with the evidence pack seven calendar days in advance.

4. The employee accepted that the reconciliations were not completed as recorded and cited workload pressure during the period of reduced staffing.

5. The investigating manager concluded that the conduct was established but that no diversion or loss occurred. The count discrepancies were resolved on subsequent audit.

6. A written warning was issued on 06 September 2021, to remain live on file for twelve months. The employee was advised of the right of appeal within five working days.

7. No appeal was lodged. The file is accordingly closed.

8. Recommended follow-up: review of shift-close staffing at the location, referred to the District Manager.
