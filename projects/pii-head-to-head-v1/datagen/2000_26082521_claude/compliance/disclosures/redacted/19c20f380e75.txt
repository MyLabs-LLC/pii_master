DETERMINATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.  The request was made in writing and identified the records sought with
    sufficient particularity to permit a search of the cumulative file and the
    student information system.

2.  Requester's status as a parent of record was verified against the current
    enrollment declaration on file. No court order restricting access was
    located in the file at the time of verification.

3.  Inspection was afforded within the five business days required by state law
    and within the forty-five day federal maximum. Copies were furnished in
    lieu of on-site inspection at the requester's election.

4.  Two documents were withheld in full. The first consists of an
    investigatory memorandum concerning a third-party student and constitutes
    the education record of that other student. The second consists of
    handwritten instructional notes retained in the sole possession of the
    originating certificated staff member and not shared with any other person
    except a substitute.

5.  Three documents were released with the identifying particulars of other
    students obscured. Obscured content is limited to names, identification
    numbers, and narrative passages from which identity could reasonably be
    determined.

6.  The requester was advised in writing of the right to a hearing to challenge
    the content of the records and of the procedure for placing a statement of
    disagreement in the file.

7.  A log entry recording this disclosure has been made in the disclosure
    register maintained with the student's cumulative file, as required.

Record closed by the Records Custodian on September 26, 2011.
