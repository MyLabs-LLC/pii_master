FINDINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The customer of record appeared and was heard. The Department appeared by the
Collections Supervisor. The account is shown to be in arrears in the amount of
$1,412.86, of which $486.20 represents consumption billed upon an estimated
reading subsequently corrected. The customer disputes the estimated portion and
asserts that a leak in a private service line, since repaired, accounts for the
excess consumption recorded in the fourth quarter of the preceding year.

A plumber's invoice bearing a repair date of 4 February 2011 was tendered and
received in evidence. The Department's meter test of 1 March 2011 certifies the
meter accurate within tolerance and no adjustment on that ground is warranted.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DETERMINATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Termination of service is STAYED upon the following terms, breach of any of
which shall vacate the stay without further notice: (1) the leak adjustment
provided by tariff Rule 22 shall be applied to one billing period, reducing the
arrears by $311.40; (2) the balance shall be paid in twelve equal monthly
installments commencing 1 April 2011, in addition to current charges as billed;
(3) no penalty shall accrue upon the deferred balance while the terms are
observed. The customer of record is advised of the right to appeal to the Board
of Utility Commissioners within fifteen days.
