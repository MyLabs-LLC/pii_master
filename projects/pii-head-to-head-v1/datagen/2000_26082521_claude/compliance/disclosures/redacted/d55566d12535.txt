3. SUBSTANCE OF THE GRIEVANCE

3.1 The grievance alleged inequitable allocation of weekend shifts over a fourteen-week period and a failure by local management to apply the published rota rotation.

3.2 A secondary allegation concerned the handling of two earlier informal complaints said to have gone unanswered.

4. PROCESS

4.1 Stage 1 hearing convened 22 July 2021. Outcome issued 29 July 2021: grievance not upheld.
4.2 Appeal lodged 04 August 2021 within the ten-day window.
4.3 Stage 2 hearing convened 14 September 2021 before a deciding officer with no prior involvement.
4.4 Outcome issued 21 October 2021.

5. ADJUDICATION NOTES

5.1 Rota extracts for the fourteen-week period were examined. The deciding officer found a measurable imbalance in weekend allocation, though below the threshold that would evidence deliberate exclusion.

5.2 The grievance was accordingly upheld in part: the allocation failure was established; the allegation of improper motive was not.

5.3 The deciding officer criticised the absence of any written record of the two informal complaints and directed that the local log be reinstated.

6. OUTCOME AND UNDERTAKINGS

6.1 Rota rotation to be recalculated and published four weeks in advance at the affected location from the period commencing 01 December 2021.

6.2 Local management to complete refresher training on the grievance procedure by 31 January 2022.

6.3 The matter is exhausted internally. No further stage of appeal is available.
