CERTIFICATION OF THE RECORDS CUSTODIAN
Production Ref: DISC-2016-0921-B        Date of Certification: December 8, 2016
Requesting Authority Ref: [SUPPRESSED]        Governing Order Dated: October 4, 2016

1. The undersigned is employed as Records Custodian for the Company and has
   custody and control of escrow, title production, and claims records
   maintained in the ordinary course of business.

2. The records produced under this reference were assembled from the escrow
   order file, the title production file, the claims file, and the trust
   accounting ledger for a single transaction closed in 2016. The transaction
   identifiers, the parties, and the land are suppressed throughout.

3. Assembly and review proceeded as follows:

     10/07/2016  Request logged; scope confirmed with Regional Legal.
     10/19/2016  Source files pulled from branch and offsite storage.
     11/02/2016  Page-level review completed; suppression schedule applied.
     11/21/2016  Second-pass quality review by Compliance.
     12/05/2016  Production set finalized, Bates range applied.

4. The following field classes were suppressed in every instance in which
   they appeared, by role and not by exception:

     Party names (buyer, seller, borrower, guarantor, affiant)
     Signature blocks and notarial acknowledgment content
     Street address, unit designation, and parcel identifier
     Policy number, order number, loan number
     Deposit and disbursement account numbers, routing numbers
     Taxpayer identification and government identification numbers
     Dates of birth and marital status entries
     Telephone numbers, electronic mail addresses, mailing addresses
     Employee identification numbers of Company personnel
     Recording book, page, and instrument numbers

5. Suppression was performed against the working copy only. Original records
   remain intact, unaltered, and in Company custody at their designated
   retention locations. The retention schedule applicable to these records is
   RM-6 (escrow, seven years from close) and RM-9 (claims, ten years from
   closure of file).

6. No record within the identified scope was withheld in full. No record was
   destroyed, modified, or created in connection with this production. Page
   count as produced: 412. Native-format items produced separately: 3
   spreadsheets, 1 accounting export.

7. Narrative entries, adjudication notes, process dates, internal reference
   codes, and dollar amounts were retained as written. Where a narrative entry
   named a party, the name alone was suppressed and the surrounding text was
   left unedited.

8. Questions regarding scope, method, or the suppression schedule are to be
   directed to Regional Legal, which retains the unsuppressed comparison set
   under separate control.

Executed this 8th day of December, 2016.
