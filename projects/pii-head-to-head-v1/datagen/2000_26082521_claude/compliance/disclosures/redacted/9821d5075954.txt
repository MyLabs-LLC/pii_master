III.  CHARGES AS SPECIFIED
============================================================================
Charge One:   Failure to observe minimum approach distances in the
              energized-conductor work described in Safety Rule 302.4,
              constituting a violation of Rule 302.4 and of the General
              Order on Live-Line Practice.
Charge Two:   Failure to complete and lodge the pre-job tailboard briefing
              form required by Standing Instruction 11-06, the same being an
              administrative rather than a safety-critical omission.

============================================================================
IV.  FINDINGS OF THE HEARING OFFICER
============================================================================
Charge One is SUSTAINED. The record, including the tailboard log, the crew
statements and the recorded telemetry from the district recloser, supports
by a preponderance of the evidence the conclusion that the approach was
undertaken without the cover-up required by the rule. The Hearing Officer
notes in mitigation the absence of injury, the absence of any prior
sustained discipline, and the employee's prompt and candid acknowledgment
of the deficiency at interview.

Charge Two is NOT SUSTAINED. The documentary evidence establishes that the
briefing was in fact conducted, the form having been lodged out of sequence
by reason of a clerical practice then prevailing in the district office.

============================================================================
V.  DISPOSITION AND CERTIFICATION
============================================================================
The proposed suspension of five (5) working days is reduced to a written
reprimand together with mandatory re-certification in live-line practice,
such re-certification to be completed on or before 30 June 2016. The
reprimand shall be removed from the active personnel file eighteen (18)
months from 04 March 2016 absent intervening discipline of like character,
in accordance with Article 19.9. The grievance is withdrawn with prejudice
as to the matters herein and without precedent as to any other matter.
