IV. LIABILITY ANALYSIS
   1. The claimant alleges a slip and fall on a wet floor surface arising from a leaking refrigerated display unit.
   2. Sweep logs record inspections at 17:30 and 18:00, the latter documented as clear. CCTV shows a spill originating at 18:04 from a customer-carried container and the claimant traversing the area at 18:12.
   3. Notice was therefore constructive at best, with an interval of approximately eight minutes between origin and occurrence. Comparative fault was assessed at forty percent (40%) on the basis of footwear and posted caution signage at the aisle entry.
   4. Maintenance work orders for the display unit disclose no reported leak in the twelve months preceding the date of loss.

V. DAMAGES AND RESERVES
   1. Special damages presented: emergency department attendance, radiography, and eight sessions of physical therapy. No surgical intervention. No wage loss claimed.
   2. Indemnity reserve established 01 July 2016 and revised 09 September 2016. Expense reserve carried for defence counsel and records retrieval.
   3. Resolution reached by negotiated settlement inclusive of the asserted lien, without admission of liability, and subject to a general release and confidentiality clause.

VI. FILE DISPOSITION
   1. Subrogation reviewed and declined; no viable third party identified.
   2. Recommendation forwarded to Store Operations for quarterly refrigeration seal inspection.
   3. Retention: seven (7) years from closure. Claimant identifiers at Section II withheld from this copy.
