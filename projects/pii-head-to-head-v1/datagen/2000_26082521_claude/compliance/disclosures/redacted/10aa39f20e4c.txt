ADJUDICATION NOTES OF THE RECORDS CUSTODIAN

The request, as received, sought "all files, notes, photographs and reports
concerning the water main break" of March last. The request being reasonably
descriptive of an identifiable body of records, no clarification was demanded
and the twenty-day period was not tolled.

Each responsive record was reviewed page by page for the presence of exempt
material. Where a record contained both exempt and non-exempt matter, the exempt
matter was excised and the remainder produced, in conformity with the principle
that exemption attaches to information and not to documents in gross. Excisions
comprise the name, residential address, telephone number, electronic mail
address, utility account number and personnel identification number of natural
persons appearing in the file. The identity of Departmental employees acting in
their official capacities has been suppressed only in the two instances in which
the safety exemption at §4(1)(f) was found applicable upon the recommendation of
the Field Superintendent; elsewhere employees are designated by title alone.

Items 006 and 007 are withheld in full as prepared in anticipation of litigation
and reflecting the mental impressions and legal theories of the Department and
its counsel. No segregable factual portion of either record could be produced
without disclosing the protected impressions themselves. Item 009 is withheld in
full as a customer billing record the disclosure of which is prohibited absent
the written consent of the customer of record.

The requester was advised in writing of the exemptions asserted, of the page
count withheld under each, and of the right to seek review by the City Attorney
within thirty days. No such petition had been docketed as of the date hereof.
