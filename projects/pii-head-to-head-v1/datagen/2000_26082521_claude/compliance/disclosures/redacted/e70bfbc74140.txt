MEMORANDUM — CARGO LOSS AND DAMAGE CLAIM
Claim Reference: CL-99/0863
Loss Date: 17 February 1999
Prepared: 30 March 1999
Prepared By: The Cargo Claims Administrator

SUMMARY OF LOSS

A consignment of packaged dry goods moving under bill of lading terms was tendered at
the origin dock on 16 February 1999 and reported short by fourteen cartons upon delivery
on 17 February 1999. A clear exception was noted on the delivery receipt at the time of
tender. The consignee filed written notice of claim on 02 March 1999, within the nine
month period prescribed by the governing bill of lading.

ADJUDICATION NOTE

Liability is admitted in part. The consignment moved at a released value of fifty cents
per pound, the shipper having declined to declare a higher value at the time of tender
and the applicable tariff provision having been plainly disclosed on the face of the bill
of lading. Settlement is accordingly authorised at the released rate rather than at
invoice value, and a settlement offer letter is to issue within ten business days.
Salvage: none. Subrogation potential: none identified. File to remain open pending
receipt of executed release.
