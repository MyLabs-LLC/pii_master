STATEMENT OF THE DISPUTE

The grievance contests a three-day suspension imposed for failure to follow the
confined-space entry procedure during a valve vault inspection, and further
contests the assignment of the associated overtime callout to a junior employee.
The Department maintains that atmospheric monitoring was not performed before
entry and that no attendant was posted, both being mandatory under Standard
Operating Procedure 402. The Union maintains that the vault was classified as
non-permit-required at the time of entry, that the classification was made by the
Section, and that the grievant relied on it in good faith.

FINDINGS AND DECISION

The record establishes that the vault carried a non-permit classification dated
2014 and that the classification had not been re-verified following the adjacent
sewer connection completed in 2015. Responsibility for re-verification rests with
the Section, not the operator. Notwithstanding, entry without a posted attendant
departs from SOP 402 irrespective of classification, and the grievant acknowledged
the omission in the investigatory interview.

The suspension is reduced from three days to one day, with two days' pay restored.
The written reprimand stands. The overtime claim is sustained; the callout roster
was applied out of order and the grievant is made whole at the applicable premium
rate for four hours. The Section is directed to re-verify vault classifications
across the district within ninety days. This decision is issued without precedent
as to the classification question and does not resolve the parties' broader dispute
under Article 19.
