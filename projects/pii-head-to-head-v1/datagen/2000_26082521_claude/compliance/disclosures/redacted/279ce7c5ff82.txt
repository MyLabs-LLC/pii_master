PART II — CLAUSES

1. The grievance was lodged in writing on 21 October 1997 and acknowledged within the three working days required by clause 4.3.

2. The matter complained of was the withdrawal of a booked assignment on 17 October 1997 at four hours' notice, and the subsequent allocation of that booking to another registered worker.

3. The panel received:
   3.1 the branch booking ledger for the weeks commencing 6 and 13 October 1997;
   3.2 the client's written instruction of 17 October 1997 recording the change;
   3.3 the grievant's statement dated 21 October 1997; and
   3.4 the respondent manager's response dated 4 November 1997.

4. It was common ground that the withdrawal was instructed by the client and was not initiated by the branch.

5. The panel found that clause 9.2 of the standard terms permits withdrawal at any notice, but that the division's own service standard requires a telephone call to the affected worker before reallocation. No such call was made, and none is logged in the ledger at 3.1.

6. The panel accordingly upheld the grievance in part:
   6.1 the withdrawal itself was proper and no compensation arises under clause 9.2;
   6.2 the failure to notify before reallocation was a breach of the service standard.

7. Remedy directed: payment of the four-hour minimum call-out rate as an ex gratia sum, authorised 24 November 1997; and a written restatement of the service standard to all branch coordinators, issued 1 December 1997.

8. The determination was communicated in writing on 21 November 1997. The right of appeal under clause 5 expired on 5 December 1997 without an appeal being lodged.

9. Retention: this record is held for six years from the date of determination and falls due for destruction in November 2003.
