2.0 BASIS OF THE INVESTIGATION

    2.1 A written complaint was received by the Office of Human Resources on
    3 February 2011 alleging failure to follow the standing clinic protocol for
    vaccine storage temperature logging over an eight-day period in January 2011.
    2.2 The complaint was screened for jurisdiction on 4 February 2011 and
    accepted for investigation under Personnel Rule 12.4(b).

3.0 PROCESS STEPS

    3.1 Notice of investigation issued to the employee: 7 February 2011.
    3.2 Documents secured from the clinic: 8 February 2011, comprising the
    temperature log book, the digital data logger export, and the two most
    recent protocol acknowledgement forms.
    3.3 Employee interview conducted: 15 February 2011, with a union
    representative present at the employee's request.
    3.4 Two witness interviews conducted: 16 and 17 February 2011.
    3.5 Draft findings circulated to the Division Director: 25 February 2011.
    3.6 Predisciplinary conference held: 8 March 2011.

4.0 FINDINGS

    4.1 The investigator found that manual log entries were absent for six of
    the eight days in question and that the digital logger recorded no
    excursion outside the acceptable range during that period.
    4.2 The employee stated that the log book had been moved during a partial
    refit of the clinic room and was not located until the following week. That
    account was corroborated by one witness.
    4.3 No vaccine loss occurred, and no doses were withdrawn from inventory as
    a result of the lapse.

5.0 ADJUDICATION

    5.1 The allegation is sustained as to the recordkeeping requirement and not
    sustained as to any allegation of harm to inventory.
    5.2 Disposition: written reprimand, to be placed in the personnel file for
    eighteen months, together with a directive to complete the cold chain
    refresher module before 30 April 2011.
    5.3 Appeal rights under Personnel Rule 15 were explained at the conference
    and acknowledged in writing on 8 March 2011. No appeal was filed within the
    ten-day window.
