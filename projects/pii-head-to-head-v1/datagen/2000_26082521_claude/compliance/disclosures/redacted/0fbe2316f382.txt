Date of Determination: 2 December 2021

1. SCOPE OF THE REQUEST

1.1 The requester seeks the complete investigative file arising from a tampering referral opened on 4 February 2021 in respect of a single residential premise.

1.2 The Department has identified 62 pages as responsive, together with four photographic exhibits and one meter interval data extract.

2. BASIS OF SUPPRESSION

2.1 Disclosure is made subject to the personal information exemption of the state public records statute, as applied by Departmental Administrative Order 2016-04.

2.2 Suppression has been applied at field level rather than by withholding whole pages, in order to preserve the narrative and procedural content of the file.

2.3 No exemption is asserted over investigative findings, correspondence dates, work order references or adjudication notes, and none has been applied.

4. TREATMENT OF EXHIBITS

4.1 Photographic exhibits 1 through 4 are released with the meter register and premise placard obscured, the placard bearing the premise identifier.

4.2 The meter interval data extract is released in full. The Department finds that fifteen-minute consumption values, absent the account and premise identifiers suppressed under clause 3, do not identify a natural person.

5. RETAINED NARRATIVE

5.1 The investigative chronology is released intact, including the referral date of 4 February 2021, the field inspection of 19 February 2021, the laboratory bench test of the removed meter completed 8 March 2021, and the backbill determination issued 6 April 2021.

5.2 The adjudication notes of the Revenue Protection Supervisor are released intact at pages 44 through 51, including the finding that the observed register condition was consistent with mechanical failure rather than interference, and the consequent cancellation of the tampering penalty.

6. NOTICE TO THE REQUESTER

6.1 No fee is assessed, the volume falling below the threshold at the adopted fee schedule.

6.2 The requester may seek review of any suppression applied under clause 3 by written petition to the City Attorney within thirty days.

6.3 A copy of this determination is filed with the disclosure log maintained under the statute and is itself a public record.
