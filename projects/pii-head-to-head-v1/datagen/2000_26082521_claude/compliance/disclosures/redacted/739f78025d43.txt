ANALYSIS AND ADJUDICATION NOTES
The lien is a matter affecting title that first attached prior to the date of policy and was not disclosed in Schedule B. Exclusion 3(a) is available only on proof that the insured created, suffered, assumed, or agreed to the encumbrance, or had actual knowledge of it and failed to disclose. The file does not presently support that proof. The two missing lien waivers are a settlement-practice deficiency attributable to the closing branch rather than evidence of insured knowledge, and the absence of the waivers weakens, rather than strengthens, any denial premised on the insured's conduct.

Covered Risk 7 and Covered Risk 8 are both engaged. The duty to defend is broader than the duty to indemnify and is triggered by the allegations of the lien foreclosure complaint as pleaded.

RECOMMENDATION
1. Withdraw the reservation of rights under Exclusion 3(a); confirm defense without reservation.
2. Assign defense counsel from the approved panel and set the initial litigation budget for committee approval.
3. Adjust the reserve from Category B to Category A and post the revision to the reserve ledger this cycle.
4. Refer the waiver-collection deficiency to Escrow Compliance for branch-level review under the agency audit protocol.
5. Diary the file for status at sixty days.

Disposition: Coverage confirmed. Claim remains open pending resolution of the underlying foreclosure action.
