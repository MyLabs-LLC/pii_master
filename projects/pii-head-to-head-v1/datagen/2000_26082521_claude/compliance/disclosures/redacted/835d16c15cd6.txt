The claim was presented within the six-month period prescribed by Government Code §911.2 and was accepted as timely by the Risk Manager on April 5, 2011. The claim alleges damage to a private vehicle parked in a District lot during an after-hours facility use event authorized under a Civic Center Act permit. Two estimates were submitted with the claim form; a third was obtained by the adjuster on April 26, 2011.

Investigation established that the permit holder, not the District, exercised control of the lot at the time of loss, and that the permit on file carried the required certificate of insurance naming the District as additional insured. The adjuster's report of May 10, 2011 recommended tender to the permit holder's carrier and denial as to the District.

The Board acted on the recommendation at its regular meeting of June 14, 2011 and rejected the claim in full. Notice of rejection was mailed June 16, 2011 and included the warning prescribed by Government Code §913(b). Tender was accepted by the permit holder's carrier on July 1, 2011. Reserve closed August 2, 2011. No suit was filed within the six-month limitation period.
