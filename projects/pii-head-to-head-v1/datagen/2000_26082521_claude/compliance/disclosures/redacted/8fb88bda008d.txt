  III.  MATTER IN DISPUTE
        A.  The grievant was charged with an avoidable contact with fixed
            infrastructure while reversing from a layover berth, resulting in
            body damage to the assigned coach and no reported injury.
        B.  The Authority relied on the Accident Review Board finding of
            preventable under Standard ARB-3, together with the post-incident
            damage estimate of $1,140.
        C.  The Local contended that the berth in question lacked a functioning
            mirror assembly on the date of the incident and that Maintenance
            Work Order 98-2231 corroborates a repair completed eleven days
            after the event.

   IV.  FINDINGS
        A.  The mirror deficiency is established on the record and is a
            mitigating condition, though it does not excuse the requirement of
            Rule 27 that an operator obtain a ground guide or dismount to
            inspect clearance before reversing.
        B.  The grievant's service record reflects no prior chargeable accident
            within the thirty-six month look-back period.

    V.  DISPOSITION
        A.  Suspension reduced from three days to one day.
        B.  Two days' pay to be restored through the payroll cycle ending
            29 May 1998.
        C.  Record entry to be amended to "preventable — mitigated."
        D.  Settlement is non-precedential per Article 8.09.
        E.  Signed by the Hearing Officer, Labor Relations, 12 May 1998.
