ADJUDICATION NOTES

The claim as presented alleges damage to finished basement improvements and to
stored personal property arising from the failure of a twelve-inch cast iron
distribution main at or about 03:40 hours on the date of loss. The Claims
Adjuster inspected the premises on 6 April 2011 in the company of the Field
Superintendent and photographed the affected areas in eleven exposures, which
exposures are indexed at Tab C of the file.

The engineering report of 21 April 2011 concludes that the subject main, placed
in service in 1948, failed by circumferential fracture attributable to
longitudinal beam stress consequent upon differential soil settlement, and that
no defect in maintenance, inspection or operation contributed to the failure.
The Department's maintenance history for the affected segment discloses no prior
break, no prior complaint of low pressure, and no deferred work order.

The Division is advised that liability for damage occasioned by the failure of a
water distribution facility attaches only upon proof of actual or constructive
notice of a defective condition and a failure to remediate the same within a
reasonable time. No such notice being shown, and the doctrine of res ipsa
loquitur being inapplicable to a municipal water utility in these circumstances,
the claim is DENIED in its entirety. The reserve of record is hereby closed and
the file coded for retention under Schedule 14, Item 9 (six years from closure).

APPEAL RIGHTS

determination letter, file a written request for reconsideration with the Office
of the City Clerk, stating with particularity the grounds relied upon and
attaching any documentary evidence not previously submitted. Reconsideration is
not a condition precedent to the commencement of an action at law.
