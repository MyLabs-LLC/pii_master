1. Nature of Action

1.1 This record concerns a written reprimand issued on 6 February 1998 and the grievance subsequently filed under Article 14 of the collective bargaining agreement then in effect. The action arose from documented deficiencies in the timeliness of home visit charting during the fourth quarter of the preceding calendar year.

1.2 The reprimand was preceded by a documented counselling session on 14 November 1997 and a follow-up expectations memorandum dated 5 December 1997, both retained in the supervisory working file.

2. Grievance Chronology

2.1 A Step One grievance was filed with the Bureau Director on 13 February 1998, within the ten working day window prescribed by the agreement. The Step One response, denying the grievance, issued on 24 February 1998.

2.2 A Step Two grievance was filed with the Director of Administrative Services on 3 March 1998. A hearing was convened on 18 March 1998; present were the grievant, the union steward, the Nursing Services Supervisor and the Personnel Officer. The Step Two determination issued on 31 March 1998, modifying the reprimand to a documented counselling notation.

2.3 The union advised on 9 April 1998 that the matter would not be advanced to arbitration. The file was accordingly closed on 15 April 1998.

3. Adjudication Notes

3.1 The hearing officer accepted that the charting backlog was substantially attributable to a caseload increase of approximately thirty per cent following the vacancy in a companion position, unfilled between September 1997 and January 1998. Mitigation was found on this basis.

3.2 The hearing officer nonetheless found that the expectations memorandum of 5 December 1997 had been clear and that partial non-compliance persisted through January 1998. A documented counselling notation was therefore held appropriate and proportionate.

3.3 Pursuant to the retention schedule, the modified notation is to be removed from the active personnel file eighteen months from the date of the Step Two determination, that is, on or about 30 September 1999, and thereafter retained only in the closed grievance series.
