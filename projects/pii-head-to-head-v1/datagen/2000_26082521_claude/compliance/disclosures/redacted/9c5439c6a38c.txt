3. NATURE OF THE DISPUTE. The customer of record asserts that consumption billed for the period 28 April 2021 through 28 May 2021, recorded at 41,300 gallons against a twelve-month premises average of 4,850 gallons, is not attributable to any use at the premises and requests that the charge be vacated in its entirety.

4. FINDINGS OF FACT.
   4.1  The field re-read of 21 June 2021 confirmed the register reading transcribed on the disputed bill; no transposition error was identified.
   4.2  Bench testing at three flow rates returned accuracy of 99.4%, 100.1%, and 99.8%, all within the tolerance prescribed by § 14-211(a). The meter is accordingly presumed accurate.
   4.3  The plumbing contractor's invoice submitted at the informal conference documents replacement of a failed supply line serving an exterior irrigation loop, with work performed on 24 May 2021.
   4.4  Consumption returned to 5,110 gallons in the succeeding cycle, consistent with repair of a continuous-flow loss rather than with meter malfunction.

5. CONCLUSIONS OF LAW.
   5.1  Water passing through an accurate meter is chargeable to the customer of record irrespective of whether it was beneficially used.
   5.2  The premises nonetheless qualifies for the single-occurrence leak adjustment authorized by § 14-208(c), the customer having repaired the defect within thirty days of the read date and having claimed no such adjustment within the preceding thirty-six months.

6. DETERMINATION. The charge is AFFIRMED AS TO ACCURACY and ADJUSTED AS TO AMOUNT. Volume in excess of the twelve-month premises average shall be rebilled at the commodity rate net of sewer volumetric charges, yielding a credit of $312.48 to be applied to the account of record within two billing cycles. The request to vacate the charge in its entirety is DENIED.

7. PAYMENT ARRANGEMENT. The residual balance shall be amortized over six equal monthly installments without interest. Default on any installment reinstates the stayed collection remedies without further notice.

8. APPEAL. Written appeal to the Rates and Adjustments Panel lies within thirty calendar days of service of this determination. Filing an appeal does not stay the installment obligation established at clause 7.
