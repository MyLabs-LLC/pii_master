  ADJUDICATION NOTES
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Liability is assessed as probable but shared. The evidence establishes
  that the valve box was reset to a grade approximately one and one-quarter
  inches below finished pavement, a condition constituting a defect within
  the meaning of the Authority's Restoration Standard, § 5.2. The condition
  was occasioned by the contractor in the first instance; the Authority's
  exposure arises solely from the acceptance inspection performed on
  14 July 2016, which failed to record the deviation. Comparative
  allocation is provisionally set at contractor 80 / Authority 20.

  Settlement authority in the sum of $3,000.00, inclusive of all costs and
  in full and final release of all claims arising out of the said incident,
  is recommended to the Claims Committee for its meeting of 14 December
  2016. Should settlement not be effected, the matter is to be referred to
  the Office of the Municipal Attorney not later than 31 January 2017, the
  limitations period expiring 22 August 2018.
