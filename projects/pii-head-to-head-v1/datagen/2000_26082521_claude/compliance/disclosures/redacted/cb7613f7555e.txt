4.0  NARRATIVE OF LOSS
     4.1  Claimant boarded at a mid-route stop and proceeded toward the rear
          seating area while the coach was stationary. Operator initiated
          departure on a green indication. Claimant reports losing balance in
          the articulation zone and striking the stanchion at the rear door.
     4.2  Operator reported no unusual acceleration. Onboard event recorder
          shows departure acceleration within normal parameters; no hard
          braking event logged within 60 seconds either side of timestamp.
     4.3  Video from cameras 2 and 5 retained under litigation hold; camera 4
          out of service at time of loss per Maintenance Work Order 2011-06622.
     4.4  Claimant declined ambulance transport at scene. Supervisor completed
          field report; witness contact details collected on Form RM-14 and
          appear at Attachment C.
