ADJUDICATION NOTES

The Division submitted the reclassification request on the ground that duties had
materially changed following the consolidation of two program caseloads in the
prior fiscal year. The Classification Analyst conducted a desk audit at the
assigned work site and interviewed both the incumbent and the immediate supervisor.

The Analyst's report concluded that the position now meets the distinguishing
characteristics of the higher classification, specifically the independent
management of a defined caseload without routine supervisory review, and the
provision of technical guidance to lower-classified staff. Two duties cited in the
original request were found to be incidental and were not credited.

The recommended action is placement at Step 3 of the receiving range rather than
Step 1, applying the salary-on-promotion rule that guarantees a minimum increase
of five percent over the prior rate. The union was notified within the timeline
required by Article 14 of the memorandum of understanding and filed no objection
before the comment period closed.

Approved by the Human Resources Director. Effective at the beginning of the pay
period following Board consent. Retroactivity was requested by the Division and
denied; the denial is consistent with prior practice for classification actions
under Rule 6.12.
