3.0  STATEMENT OF FACTS
     3.1  The claimant was engaged in coupling a loaded trailer to a
          yard tractor on the east apron of the terminal when the
          landing gear crank handle released under load.
     3.2  The claimant reported striking the left forearm against the
          trailer frame and continued the shift for approximately two
          hours before notifying the shift lead.
     3.3  The shift lead completed the incident worksheet the same
          evening. Two co-workers on the apron were interviewed by the
          Safety Coordinator on 16 February 2011; both statements are
          held in the appendix with identifying fields suppressed.
     3.4  No mechanical defect was recorded against the trailer in the
          preceding thirty days. The unit was placed out of service on
          15 February 2011 pending inspection and returned to service
          on 18 February 2011.
