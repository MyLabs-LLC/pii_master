BASIS FOR ACTION
A quarterly file review conducted the week of July 26, 2004 sampled twelve closed escrow files handled by the employee. Three of the twelve files were found to contain settlement statements signed by the parties before the final figures had been reconciled against the lender's closing instructions. In two of those three files, the reconciliation was completed on the following business day and the corrected statements were mailed for signature. In the third file, no corrected statement was located.

The review also noted that trust account disbursements in two files were released the same day funds were received by wire, without the funding confirmation entry required by Escrow Procedure 4.10 (rev. 1/03). No shortage resulted in either instance, and both files balanced at close.

MEETING RECORD
  2004-08-16   Review findings transmitted to the branch manager.
  2004-08-19   Counseling meeting held. Present by role: the employee, the Branch Manager, and the Human Resources Generalist. The employee acknowledged the sequencing errors and attributed them to file volume during the June closing peak.
  2004-08-19   Employee declined union or other representation; declination noted on the meeting form.
  2004-08-24   Written summary provided to the employee for signature. Signature received; signature block suppressed in this record.

EXPECTATIONS AND FOLLOW-UP
1. All settlement statements are to be reconciled to lender instructions before presentation for signature, without exception.
2. Funding confirmation entries are to be posted before any disbursement is released, per Procedure 4.10.
3. Refresher training module ESC-210 to be completed by September 24, 2004.
4. A second file sample of ten files to be drawn in November 2004. Results to be documented in this file.

No change to compensation, title, or licensed status results from this action. This is a documented verbal counseling and does not constitute a written warning under the progressive discipline policy.
