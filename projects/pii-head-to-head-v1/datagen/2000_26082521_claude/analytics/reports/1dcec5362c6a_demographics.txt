CLAUSE 4 — SUPPRESSION

4.1 Any Cell of value below the Threshold is withheld and marked "<5". Where a single withheld Cell in a row would be recoverable by arithmetic against the row total, a second Cell in that row is likewise withheld.

4.3 The Contractor holds no field of a personal character in the extract underlying this Return. Fields captured operationally — Consignee Name, Contact Number, Delivery Address, Signature Reference — are severed at extraction and take no part in any figure above.
