SECTION D — SUPPRESSION AND RELEASE CONDITIONS

  D.1  Districts returning fewer than fifty records are not published individually and are
       rolled into the aggregate row shown in Section A. This threshold is set above the
       general institutional minimum of ten because postal district in combination with any
       second attribute presents a higher re-identification risk.
  D.2  No cross-tabulation of postal district against age band, sex, race and ethnicity,
       language, or income bracket is released from this schedule.
  D.3  Geography below the three-digit postal district is not released in any form. Requests
       for finer geography, including full postal code or municipality, are directed to the
       Records Custodian and are evaluated against the institutional disclosure policy and the
       applicable federal education records rule.
  D.4  Percentages are computed on the domestic denominator of 23,562 and rounded to one
       decimal place. Rounding may cause columns to depart from 100.0 by up to 0.1.
