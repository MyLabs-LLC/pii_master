PURPOSE

To record the geographic distribution of the covered population at the coarsest level approved for internal circulation. This is the final report in the plan year 2003 series and closes file reference STAT-2004-014 through STAT-2004-026.

BASIS

Full postal codes are held in the source system and are not carried into this tabulation. Members with more than one address on file are assigned to the address flagged as primary for correspondence.

SUPPRESSION AND AGGREGATION

Postal district is the finest geography released. Requests for tabulation at full postal code, census tract, or municipality are declined by standing instruction of the Records Custodian.

Districts with fewer than eleven members are not printed as separate rows; those members are folded into the out-of-area line and the fold is recorded in the working papers retained by this unit. Where a district row would be combined with any of the demographic fields reported earlier in this series — age band, sex, ethnicity, preferred language, or household income band — the same threshold of eleven applies to every resulting cell, and complementary suppression is applied so that a withheld cell cannot be recovered from published margins.

RETENTION

Working papers, including the fold record and the suppression log, are retained for six years under the unit's records schedule. The underlying extract is purged from the reporting environment thirty days after the final report in a series is issued.
