DESHLER COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
OFFICE OF THE HEALTH OFFICER

MEMORANDUM

TO:      The Board of Supervisors, Committee on Health and Human Services
FROM:    The Health Officer
THROUGH: The Deputy Director for Program Planning and Evaluation
SUBJECT: Transmittal of the Quarterly Demographic Profile of the Served Population, Quarter Ending December 1998
DATE:    January 1999

There is transmitted herewith, in accordance with the Committee's standing request of prior session and without waiver of any privilege attaching thereto, the demographic profile of the population served by the Department's clinical and categorical programs for the quarter ending December 1998.

The Committee is respectfully advised that the profile is an aggregate compilation only. The tabulation was constructed by the Records Custodian from program rosters upon the fields Age Band, Sex, Race and Ethnicity, Primary Language Spoken in the Home, Household Income Bracket as a Proportion of the Federal Poverty Guidelines, and Postal District of Residence at the three-digit level, no field of narrower geographic resolution having been carried forward for any purpose. The unduplicated served count for the quarter was 31,884, of which 4,102 persons (12.9 percent) were of an age of sixty-five years or greater and 8,377 persons (26.3 percent) were of an age of seventeen years or less; 54.8 percent were reported female and 45.2 percent male; and 22.1 percent reported a Primary Language Spoken in the Home other than English, the most frequently reported such language having been Spanish at 16.4 percent of the served population.

The Committee's attention is directed to the suppression convention in force. Any cell of fewer than ten persons is withheld and marked [S], and a complementary cell is likewise withheld where withholding one alone would permit recovery by arithmetic. Suppressed values are nonetheless carried in all totals. Requests for disaggregation below the levels presented cannot be honored.
