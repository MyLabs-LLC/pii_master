SIERRA VALLEY MUTUAL HEALTH ASSURANCE
BUREAU OF ACTUARIAL STATISTICS
DEMOGRAPHIC COMPOSITION OF THE SERVED POPULATION, CALENDAR YEAR 1998
Report No. AS-1998-11 · Approved by: the Chief Statistician

The following checklist constitutes the whole of the authorized tabulation. Each numbered clause is to be read as a condition of the clause preceding it.

1. Governing conventions.
   (a) The unit of enumeration is the covered life, counted once and only once irrespective of the number of certificates under which coverage is afforded.
   (b) The enumerated total for the period is 147,930 covered lives, against which all percentages herein are computed.
   (c) Percentages are carried to one decimal place; the reader is cautioned that column sums may depart from 100.0 by reason of such rounding, and no significance whatsoever attaches to any such departure.

2. Suppression of small cells.
   (a) Any cell of the tabulation reporting fewer than six (6) covered lives is suppressed and shown as "—".
   (b) Suppression having been applied within any margin, complementary suppression is applied to a second cell within the same margin, notwithstanding that such second cell may exceed the threshold, so that the suppressed value cannot be recovered by subtraction from the margin total.
   (c) Postal geography is reported at the three-digit prefix, districts of insufficient magnitude being combined into the line designated "Balance of Service Area"; no finer geographic breakdown is prepared, held, or releasable at this level of report.
