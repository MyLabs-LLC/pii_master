MERIDIAN TITLE & ESCROW SERVICES
ANNUAL DEMOGRAPHIC TABULATION — CALENDAR YEAR 2010
Issued by the Statistical Reporting Unit, March 2011
Statewide, all branches

1. SCOPE AND METHOD

1.1 The reference population is every party of record on a file disbursed by a Meridian branch between January 1 and December 31, 2010. Reference population: 26,540 parties across 15,882 files.

1.2 Sex, ethnicity, and elected settlement language are taken from the demographic addendum executed at signing. Where the addendum was not executed, the party is carried in the "not recorded" or "declined to state" line, as applicable to the field.

1.3 No individual-level extract was produced in the course of this tabulation. Counts were compiled by aggregation query against the closed-file warehouse and reconciled to the disbursement ledger.

5. SUPPRESSION

5.1 The suppression threshold for this tabulation is eleven. Any cell with a count of one through ten is withheld and reported as an em dash.

5.2 Where withholding a single cell would allow its value to be recovered by subtracting the published cells from the published total, the next smallest cell in the same table is withheld as well.

5.3 Suppression is applied before rounding. A cell at or above the threshold is not suppressed on account of a small percentage.

5.4 The "Other, interpreter engaged" line in Clause 4 is a combined line. Its component languages fall below the threshold individually and are not published separately.

6. ROUNDING

6.1 Percentages are computed on unrounded counts and rounded to one decimal place. Column totals in Clauses 2 and 4 reflect rounding and do not equal 100.0.

7. RETENTION AND REUSE

7.1 The aggregate tables in this document are retained for seven years by the Records Custodian.

7.2 The underlying query definitions are held by the Statistical Reporting Unit. Re-execution against a later closed-file period requires written direction from the Compliance Officer.

7.3 Onward disclosure of any table in this document to a party outside the company is routed through the Compliance Officer.
