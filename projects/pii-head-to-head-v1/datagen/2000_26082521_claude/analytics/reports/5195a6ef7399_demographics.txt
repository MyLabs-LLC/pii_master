•  ITEM 5 — SUPPRESSION OF SMALL CELLS
   ○  A minimum cell threshold of eleven (11) is applied throughout. Cells falling below the threshold are suppressed and displayed as "—".
   ○  Where suppression of a single cell would leave the withheld value derivable from the published row or column margin, a second cell is suppressed as a complementary measure.
   ○  Suppressed values continue to contribute to all margins and to the grand total; margins are never recomputed on the suppressed subset.
   ○  Percentages are rounded to one decimal place, and columns may not therefore total precisely 100.0.

•  ITEM 6 — LIMITATION ON GRANULARITY
   ○  No cross-tabulation of three or more dimensions is published where any resulting cell would fall below the threshold in Item 5.
   ○  Geography is not reported below the outward postal code.
