[X] 8. CROSS-TABULATIONS LIMITED
       Only two-way crossings are published: age band by sex, and postal district by income bracket. Three-way crossings were prepared for internal planning and are held in the unit; they are not part of this release.

[X] 9. SMALL-CELL SUPPRESSION APPLIED
       Any published cell containing fewer than 11 persons is withheld and displayed as an em dash. Suppressed counts remain inside the published row and column totals.
       Where a single withheld cell could be recovered by subtracting the published cells from a total, a second cell in the same row is withheld. Four complementary suppressions were made in the postal district by income bracket crossing, all in the 466 and outside-county rows.
       Percentage cells are withheld whenever the underlying count is withheld. Percentages are not published for any denominator below 50.

[X] 10. ROUNDING CONFIRMED
       Counts are exact. Percentages are rounded to one decimal, so columns may not sum to exactly 100.0.

[X] 11. AGGREGATE-ONLY OUTPUT CONFIRMED
