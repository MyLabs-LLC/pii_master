DEMOGRAPHIC PROFILE OF REGISTERED WORKERS
Branch return, six months to 30 June 2016
Compiled by the Branch Manager

The branch registered 1,946 individuals as available for work during the period. All figures below are aggregate counts with the corresponding percentage of the total. Fields are taken from the registration record and, where applicable, from the earnings history held by the Payroll Administrator.

The age profile is set out below.

  Age Band          Count     Per cent
  16 to 24            486         25.0
  25 to 34            622         32.0
  35 to 49            553         28.4
  50 to 64            261         13.4
  65 and over          24          1.2

The band structure differs from the divisional return, which splits 35 to 44 and 45 to 54; the branch uses the wider 35 to 49 band because the narrower split produced cells below the threshold in earlier periods.

On Sex, the split is close to even, with a modest majority of male registrations, consistent with the industrial and warehouse assignments that make up most of the branch's work. On Ethnicity, White British remains the largest single group at a little over half the total, with Asian or Asian British and White Other the next largest; two further headings have been combined for publication. On First Language, four fifths of registrations record English, and the remainder are spread across a number of languages, none of which individually exceeds eight per cent.

On Gross Weekly Earnings Band, registrations are concentrated in the £200 to £299 and £300 to £399 bands, which between them account for approximately three fifths of the total. On Postal District, five districts account for slightly under half of all registrations, and the balance is reported as All Other Districts.

Cells containing fewer than five individuals are not published, and where suppression of one cell would permit its recovery from the published total, a second cell has been suppressed in addition. Geography is not reported below postal district. The Compliance Officer has confirmed that the return meets the standing requirements for aggregate-only reporting.
