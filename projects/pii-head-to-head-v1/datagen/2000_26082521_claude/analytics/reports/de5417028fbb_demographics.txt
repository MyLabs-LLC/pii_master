1.  PREAMBLE

1.1 This document tabulates the demographic composition of persons served by
    departmental programs during the program year ended 30 SEP 97.

1.2 Total unduplicated served population: 18,772. All panels employ this
    denominator unless otherwise stated.

1.3 Figures are drawn from the consolidated program register as of the fourth
    quarterly close and are not adjusted for late entry.

4.  DISCLOSURE CONTROL

4.1 The publication threshold is ten. No cell in Sections 2 or 3 fell below the
    threshold; no suppression was therefore required in the tables as printed.

4.2 In the underlying age-by-postal-district matrix, fourteen cells fell below
    threshold. Those cells, together with seven cells designated for complementary
    suppression, are withheld, and the matrix is consequently not reproduced here.

4.3 Geography is not published at any level finer than the five-digit postal
    district. Requests for finer geography are referred to the Bureau Chief and are
    granted only in aggregate form.

5.  NOTES

5.1 Percentages are rounded to one decimal and may not sum exactly to 100.0.

5.2 Language reflects the language recorded for the household at intake, not
    proficiency in English.

5.3 The file of record is held by the Records Custodian.
