      Female .................... 6,338     51.1%
      Male ...................... 5,981     48.2%

      Total .................... 12,406    100.0%

5. Percentages are computed on the total in Clause 2 and are rounded to one decimal place. Columns may not sum to 100.0% by reason of rounding.

6. Age band is derived at file opening and is not recalculated thereafter.

7. Any cell with a count below 5 is suppressed and rolled into the adjacent band or into the "Not reported" line, so that no cell in this report represents fewer than 5 parties. No suppressed cell is published separately, and no complementary cell is published where its publication would permit the suppressed count to be recovered by subtraction.

8. Underlying detail is retained by the Records Custodian and is not distributed with this report.
