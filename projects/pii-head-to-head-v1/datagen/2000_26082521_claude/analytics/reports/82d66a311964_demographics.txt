ANNUAL DEMOGRAPHIC PROFILE OF THE SERVED POPULATION
Retail Pharmacy Operations — All Regions
Calendar year 2011 (01 January to 31 December)
Custodian of record: the Records Custodian, Corporate Data Governance

I.  SCOPE AND METHOD

    A.  Basis of enumeration
        1.  Served population, distinct individuals: 1,204,700
        2.  Stores contributing data: 1,163
        3.  Extraction date: 14 February 2012

    B.  Derivation rules
        1.  Age band is calculated as at 31 December 2011.
        2.  Income band is assigned from the published median household income
            of the geographic unit of residence. It is not collected from the
            individual and is not held at individual level.
        3.  Language reflects the communication preference flag recorded at the
            point of service.

III.  SMALL-CELL SUPPRESSION

    A.  Threshold
        1.  Cells with a count of fewer than 11 are suppressed and rendered "—".

    B.  Secondary suppression
        1.  Complementary cells are suppressed where a primary suppression could
            be reversed by arithmetic against a published total or margin.

    C.  Cross-tabulation
        1.  Two-way tables crossing race/ethnicity, language or income with
            geography are produced at census-division level only. Requests for
            a finer geographic cross-tabulation are declined by the Records
            Custodian.
