MEMBERSHIP DEMOGRAPHICS REPORT — MEMORANDUM

TO: Board Governance Committee; Community Development Officer
FROM: Research and Statistics Unit
SUBJECT: Annual membership demographic tabulation, reporting year ended 31 December 2016
CLASSIFICATION: Internal — Board Distribution

1. PURPOSE

This memorandum tabulates the demographic composition of the served membership as at the reporting-year close. It is prepared to support the Community Development Review and the field-of-membership assessment filed with the regulator. All figures are aggregate counts drawn from the core banking extract and the membership application register. No individual records are reproduced or referenced in this memorandum.

2. BASIS OF PREPARATION

Population: all members holding at least one open share account at year end. Total membership base: 41,286.

Source fields used in tabulation: Age at Reporting Date (derived), Sex, Ethnicity (self-reported), Primary Language (self-reported), Household Income Bracket (self-reported at application or last review), Postal District of Record.

Self-reported fields are optional at the point of application. Non-response is reported as a distinct category and is not imputed. Percentages are calculated against the total membership base and may not sum to 100.0 owing to rounding.

9. SUPPRESSION

Cells with a count below 10 are suppressed and reported in the residual "Other" or "Outside area" line for the relevant table. Where suppression in one cell would permit derivation of a suppressed value by subtraction, the next-smallest cell in the same table is also suppressed. Postal geography is reported at district-group level only; no reporting is performed below that level in this memorandum.

10. RETENTION
