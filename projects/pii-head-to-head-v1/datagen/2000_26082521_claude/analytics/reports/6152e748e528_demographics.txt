SECTION I — SUPPRESSION AND ROUNDING
Any cell with a count below 11 is suppressed and shown as "<11". Where a

total, the next smallest cell in the same table is also suppressed
(complementary suppression). No table in this return is published at a
geography finer than the three-digit postal prefix. Cross-tabulations of
three or more characteristics are not released outside the Analytics Unit.
Percentages are computed on the published total and may not sum to 100.0
because of rounding.
