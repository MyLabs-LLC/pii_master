HAULAGE & DISTRIBUTION SERVICES (NORTHERN) LIMITED
BENEFICIARY PROFILE RETURN — CONTRACT YEAR 2003/04, QUARTER 4
Prepared under Schedule 6, Clause 14 (Statistical Returns) of the Distribution Services Agreement
Issued to the Commissioning Authority, 19 April 2004
Return reference: BPR/2004/Q4/N — Status: Final

1. SCOPE OF RETURN. This return tabulates the demographic composition of the population served under the household delivery contract during the three months ending 31 March 2004. The unit of count is the individual resident within a served household, as recorded at the point of round scheduling. The served population for the quarter was 41,268 individuals across 16,942 households. Figures are drawn from the round management system extract taken on 5 April 2004 and reconciled by the Data Quality Lead against depot manifest totals. No individual-level records accompany this return; the Records Custodian retains the source extract under the retention schedule.

2. AGE BAND. Distribution by the contract age bands is set out below. Percentages are of the served population and are subject to rounding.

3. SEX. The recorded field takes three values. Female accounted for 21,604 (52.3 per cent), Male for 19,631 (47.6 per cent) and Not Stated for 33 (0.1 per cent). The Not Stated proportion has remained below one half of one per cent across four consecutive quarters and no remedial action is proposed.

4. ETHNIC GROUP. Categories follow the 2001 Census classification and are not re-based. White British accounted for 25,940 (62.9 per cent) and White Other for 3,318 (8.0 per cent). Asian or Asian British accounted for 5,884 (14.3 per cent), Black or Black British for 3,401 (8.2 per cent), Mixed for 1,072 (2.6 per cent) and Chinese or Other Ethnic Group for 812 (2.0 per cent). A residual 841 records (2.0 per cent) carry the value Not Stated, principally where scheduling was completed by telephone rather than by returned form.

5. MAIN LANGUAGE. English was recorded as main language for 34,102 individuals (82.6 per cent). The principal other languages recorded were Urdu 1,540 (3.7 per cent), Bengali 1,203 (2.9 per cent), Polish 902 (2.2 per cent), Somali 741 (1.8 per cent) and Cantonese 388 (0.9 per cent). The residual grouping Other or Not Recorded accounted for 2,392 (5.8 per cent). The Contract Compliance Officer notes that interpreter-supported deliveries were requested by 4.1 per cent of households, which is consistent with the language profile.

6. HOUSEHOLD INCOME BRACKET. Brackets are annual gross household income as self-declared at registration and are not indexed within the contract year. Under £5,000 accounted for 5,133 (12.4 per cent); £5,000 to £9,999 for 11,208 (27.2 per cent); £10,000 to £14,999 for 9,764 (23.7 per cent); £15,000 to £19,999 for 6,015 (14.6 per cent); £20,000 to £29,999 for 4,332 (10.5 per cent); and £30,000 and over for 1,690 (4.1 per cent). Not Stated accounted for 3,126 (7.6 per cent).

7. GEOGRAPHY. Geography is reported at postal district and no finer. The five largest served districts were BD3 (4,412; 10.7 per cent), BD5 (3,986; 9.7 per cent), BD8 (3,340; 8.1 per cent), LS9 (2,977; 7.2 per cent) and LS11 (2,614; 6.3 per cent). All remaining districts are aggregated as Remainder of Contract Area (23,939; 58.0 per cent).

8. SUPPRESSION. Any cell with a count of fewer than five is suppressed and shown as a hyphen. Where a single suppressed cell would be recoverable by subtraction from published row or column totals, secondary suppression has been applied to the next smallest cell in the same row or column. Suppressed cells remain included in all totals and percentages. Cross-tabulations of ethnic group by postal district have been withheld in full for this quarter on the same grounds and are available to the Commissioning Authority on written request at coarser geography only.
