NOTES

1. Attribute fields not completed at intake are carried as "Not reported" or "Declined to state" and are not imputed.

2. Small-cell suppression. Cells with counts below eleven are not published. Where suppression of one cell would permit recovery of its value by subtraction, a second cell is suppressed in the same table. Suppressed counts remain within the universe of 8,760; published categories may accordingly fall short of the universe total.

3. Geography is not published below the three-digit postal district. Aggregation to district level occurs before tabulation; file-level location values are not carried into this report or its work product.

4. Age band is fixed at the date the file was opened and is not restated on closing.

5. Income bracket boundaries were revised effective 01 January 2021. Figures for prior periods are not comparable to those above without restatement.

6. This tabulation is produced quarterly on a rolling twelve-month basis. Queries and requests for reissue are directed to the Records Custodian.
