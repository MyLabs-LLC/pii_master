Prepared: Office of the Records Custodian
Reviewed: Director of Pharmacy Operations
Filed with: Corporate Compliance; Regulatory Affairs

I. BASIS

1. This report tabulates the demographic composition of the population served by 143 retail pharmacy locations operated by the Company during the reporting year.
2. Unit of count is the individual served at least once in the year, de-duplicated across locations. Total: 214,900.
3. All percentages are computed on the total at clause 2 and rounded to one decimal place.
4. No tabulation in this report is derived from, or resolves to, an individual record. Income band is assigned at census-tract level and rolled up to the geography shown at clause 12.

III. SUPPRESSION AND RELEASE

13. Primary suppression: any cell with a frequency below 11 is withheld and printed as "n/p".
14. Complementary suppression: where a single withheld cell could be recovered by subtraction from a published total, a second cell in the same row is withheld, or categories are collapsed as at clause 8. Withheld cells remain in the totals; published cells accordingly need not sum to the total at clause 2.
15. Cross-tabulations of race or language against geography are withheld below three-digit ZIP prefix in all cases, without regard to cell size.
16. Release outside the Company requires written authorization of the Director of Pharmacy Operations and countersignature of Corporate Compliance.
