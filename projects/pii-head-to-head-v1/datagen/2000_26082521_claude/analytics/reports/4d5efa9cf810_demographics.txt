NOTES

1.  Residence is reported at the three-digit postal district. Finer
    geography is not compiled for reporting purposes and will not be
    released to requesting parties, internal or external.

2.  Small-cell suppression. Cells containing fewer than 10 records are
    suppressed and shown as an asterisk. Where suppression of a single
    cell leaves that value derivable from the published row or column
    total, a second cell is suppressed in complement. In Table 3 the
    suppressed cell is not recoverable, as the corresponding row total
    is withheld by design and the column total absorbs the residual.

3.  Income bracket is a declared figure and is not verified except in the
    sample selected for benefit-application verification under federal
    rule. The verification sample is not reported here.

4.  The "Not reported / no application" line is not to be treated as an
    income category. Direct certification through the assistance agency
    match accounts for a portion of this line; the Committee is cautioned
    against reading it as an estimate of non-eligible households.

5.  Queries to the Records Custodian. Reproduction of Tables 1 through 3
    outside the district requires prior authorization.
