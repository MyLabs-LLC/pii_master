Ref:     STAT-2004-018 (companion to STAT-2004-014)

1. SCOPE

Same extract and same denominator as Report 1 of this series: 128,450 covered lives, enrollment extract dated January 31, 2004. Ethnicity and language are self-reported on the enrollment application and on the annual renewal form. Household income band is taken from the most recent subsidy or rating determination on file. All three fields are optional at enrollment; non-response is reported as its own category rather than imputed or dropped.

5. SUPPRESSION

Cells below a threshold of eleven are withheld and printed as a hyphen. Complementary suppression is applied within any row or column where a single withheld cell could be recovered by subtraction. The threshold applies to the counts as published; it is not relaxed for internal audiences. Categories carrying a suppressed count are retained in the table so that the absence of a value is visible.
