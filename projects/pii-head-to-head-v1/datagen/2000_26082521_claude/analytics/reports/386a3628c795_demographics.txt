CALEDONIAN FREIGHT & CONTRACT SERVICES LIMITED
Statistical Report on the Served Population
Concessionary Fuel and Winter Supplies Delivery Contract CFS-2004-11
Submitted to the Contracting Authority, November 2004

A.  SCOPE AND METHOD
    A.1  This Report profiles the 30,884 recipients registered for delivery under the Contract as at the end of the 2004 registration window.
    A.2  The counting unit is the registered recipient. Where two or more recipients share a delivery point, each is counted separately.
    A.3  Percentages are computed on the total at A.1 and stated to one decimal place. Rounding may cause components to depart from 100.0.
    A.4  Income is banded from the benefit award category evidenced at registration; it is not independently verified by the contractor.

F.  DISCLOSURE CONTROL AND SMALL CELLS
    F.1  The threshold for publication is a cell count of five. Cells below the threshold are suppressed and shown as "‡".
    F.2  Secondary suppression is applied to a further cell in the same table wherever the primary suppression could otherwise be recovered from the published total.
