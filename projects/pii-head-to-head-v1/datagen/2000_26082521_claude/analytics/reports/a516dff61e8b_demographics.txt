DEMOGRAPHIC STATISTICAL REPORT — REPORTING YEAR 2021
Report series VG/STAT/2021/07
Issuing unit: Population Analytics; retention owner: the Records Custodian

PART I — SCOPE AND BASIS

1.1 This report tabulates the demographic composition of the population served by the Plan across its five-county operating territory for the year ended 31 December 2021.

1.2 The unit of count is the covered life. The denominator for all tables is 61,204, being those enrolled for one day or more in the reporting year.

1.3 Source fields are drawn from the enrolment record as follows: Date of enrolment; Age band at year end; Sex; Race/ethnicity (self-reported); Preferred spoken language; Annual household income band; County of residence. No record-level attribute is carried into this report.

PART III — DISCLOSURE CONTROL

3.1 The suppression threshold for this series is eleven. Any cell of fewer than eleven covered lives is withheld and is shown as an em dash, as at clause 2.3.

3.2 Withheld counts remain included in the denominator at clause 1.2 and in every marginal total. Accordingly, the published categories in a table may not reconcile to the stated total by the amount withheld.

3.3 Where a single withheld cell in a table could be recovered by subtraction from a published margin, the next smallest category in that table is withheld also. This provision was not engaged in Part II, the residual for the withheld category at clause 2.3 being masked by the aggregation of the Not reported category.

3.4 Geography is published at county level only. Requests for tabulation below county level, or for any cross-tabulation of county with race, ethnicity, language or income, are directed to the Records Custodian and are subject to the same threshold applied to each interior cell.

3.5 Percentages are rounded to one decimal place against the denominator at clause 1.2.
