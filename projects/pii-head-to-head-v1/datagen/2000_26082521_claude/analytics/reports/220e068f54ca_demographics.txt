NORTH COAST FREIGHT SERVICES LTD
Return type: Quarterly demographic profile, served population
Contract lot: 7 (Rural and Coastal)
Period: quarter ending 30 September 2004
Reference: NCF/Q3/04/L7
Prepared by: the Data Quality Lead
Certified by: the Contract Compliance Officer
Distribution: Commissioning Authority; Records Custodian (file copy)

The served population for the quarter was 7,296 individuals in 3,488 households. Counts are aggregate and are drawn from the registration extract of 3 October 2004.

By age band, 0 to 4 accounted for 612 (8.4 per cent), 5 to 17 for 1,448 (19.8 per cent), 18 to 64 for 3,911 (53.6 per cent) and 65 and over for 1,325 (18.2 per cent). By sex, Female accounted for 3,876 (53.1 per cent) and Male for 3,420 (46.9 per cent); no records in this lot carry Not Stated. Ethnic group is reported in collapsed form: White groups 93.7 per cent, all other groups combined 4.1 per cent, Not Stated 2.2 per cent. Main language was English for 96.4 per cent, with all other languages aggregated at 3.6 per cent; no individual language other than English reaches the disclosure threshold in this lot and none is itemised.

On household income bracket, under £7,500 accounted for 2,204 (30.2 per cent), £7,500 to £14,999 for 2,760 (37.8 per cent), £15,000 to £24,999 for 1,313 (18.0 per cent), £25,000 and over for 421 (5.8 per cent) and Not Stated for 598 (8.2 per cent).

Geography for this lot is reported at local authority district level rather than postal district, the postal districts in the contract area being sparsely populated and several falling below the threshold when crossed with any other field. District 1 accounted for 3,102 (42.5 per cent), District 2 for 2,547 (34.9 per cent) and District 3 for 1,647 (22.6 per cent).

Cells below a count of five are suppressed and shown as a hyphen; secondary suppression is applied within the affected row or column where the primary suppression would otherwise be recoverable from published totals. Suppressed values are retained in all totals and percentages. Percentages may not sum to 100.0 owing to rounding.
