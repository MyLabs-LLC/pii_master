NORTHGATE REGIONAL TRANSIT AUTHORITY
Office of Planning and Performance
Annual Service Area Demographic Return — Reporting Year 2016

1. Scope and Basis
    1.1 This return states the composition of the resident population within
        the Authority's designated service area as at 30 June 2016.
    1.2 Figures are aggregate counts derived from the census base file and
        updated against the mid-year estimate series. No unit-level records
        are held in support of this return.
    1.3 Service area resident population: 412,600.

5. Disclosure Control
    5.1 Cells containing fewer than ten persons are suppressed and displayed
        as an em dash.
    5.2 Where a single cell in a row or column has been suppressed under
        clause 5.1, complementary suppression is applied to the next-smallest
        cell in that row or column to prevent recovery by subtraction.
    5.3 All counts are rounded to the nearest 100 and percentages expressed
        to one decimal place. Component percentages may not sum to the stated
        total by reason of rounding.
    5.4 Cross-tabulation of any two or more characteristics in this return is
        released only at service-area level and not below.
