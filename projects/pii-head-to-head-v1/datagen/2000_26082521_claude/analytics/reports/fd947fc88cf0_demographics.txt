MERIDIAN TITLE & ESCROW SERVICES
STATISTICAL REPORTING UNIT
CLOSED FILE POPULATION SUMMARY — SECOND QUARTER 2011
Northern Operations Region

1. This summary covers escrow files disbursed and closed between April 1 and June 30, 2011 in the Northern Operations Region. Closed files: 4,318. Parties of record across those files: 7,006.

2. Counts are drawn from the party-role and demographic addendum fields on the settlement worksheet. Each party of record is counted once per file. A party appearing on two files in the period is counted twice.

3. Distribution by age band, computed from the age-at-closing field:

    Age band        Count    Percent
    Under 25          143        2.0
    25 to 34        1,268       18.1
    35 to 44        1,779       25.4
    45 to 54        1,655       23.6
    55 to 64        1,290       18.4
    65 and over       811       11.6
    Not recorded       60        0.9
    Total           7,006      100.0

4. Cells containing fewer than eleven parties are suppressed and shown as an em dash. Where a single suppression in a row would permit recovery of the suppressed value by subtraction from the row total, a second cell in that row is suppressed as well. No suppressed cell appears in Clause 3.

5. Percentages are rounded to one decimal place and may not sum to exactly 100.0.

6. Questions on derivation of the age-at-closing field are directed to the Records Custodian. Requests to re-run the tabulation on an alternative banding are directed to the Statistical Reporting Unit through the branch manager of record.
