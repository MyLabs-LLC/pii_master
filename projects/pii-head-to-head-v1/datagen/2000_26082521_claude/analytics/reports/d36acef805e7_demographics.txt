MIDLAND CARRIAGE PARTNERS — STATISTICAL SUMMARY, SERVED POPULATION
Half-Year Return, period ending 30 June 2004. Reference MCP/HY/04-02.

The served population for the period was 12,884 individuals in 5,317 households across three county areas. By age, under 16 accounted for 3,004 (23.3 per cent), 16 to 64 for 7,655 (59.4 per cent) and 65 and over for 2,225 (17.3 per cent). By sex, Female accounted for 6,742 (52.3 per cent), Male for 6,131 (47.6 per cent) and Not Stated for 11 (0.1 per cent). Ethnic group is reported in collapsed form for this return: White groups combined 78.9 per cent, all other groups combined 18.4 per cent, Not Stated 2.7 per cent. English was recorded as main language for 88.1 per cent. On household income bracket, below £10,000 accounted for 41.2 per cent, £10,000 to £19,999 for 36.8 per cent, £20,000 and over for 13.5 per cent, and Not Stated for 8.5 per cent. Geography is reported at county level only for this contract lot: County Area A 5,912 (45.9 per cent), County Area B 4,338 (33.7 per cent), County Area C 2,634 (20.4 per cent). Cells below five are suppressed; where suppression is defeasible by subtraction, a second cell in the same row has been suppressed. Ethnic group is collapsed rather than published in full because several detailed categories fall below the threshold in two of the three county areas. Queries to the Records Custodian.
