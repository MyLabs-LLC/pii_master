NORTHSTAR DRUG STORES, INCORPORATED
PROFESSIONAL SERVICES DIVISION — STATISTICAL REPORTING UNIT

SERVED POPULATION DEMOGRAPHIC PROFILE, CALENDAR YEAR 1998
REPORT SERIES PSU-D, ISSUE 14
DISTRIBUTION: DIVISION OFFICERS; DISTRICT MANAGERS; THE RECORDS CUSTODIAN

1. This report is issued in satisfaction of the standing instruction of the Vice President, Professional Services, requiring an annual tabulation of the demographic composition of the population served by the Company's retail pharmacy units. It supersedes Issue 13 in all particulars and is effective upon distribution.

2. The served population for the reporting period comprises 1,284,660 distinct served persons across 412 operating units in six states. Enumeration proceeds by unduplicated count derived from the dispensing system extract taken to magnetic tape on the fourth business day following period close. A served person is counted once, irrespective of the number of units patronized or the number of transactions recorded, and no household or family rollup is applied at any stage of the tabulation.

3. Distribution by age band, computed from AGE_BAND as of the last day of the reporting period, is as follows: under 18 years, 214,338 (16.7 per cent); 18 to 34 years, 268,494 (20.9 per cent); 35 to 49 years, 289,049 (22.5 per cent); 50 to 64 years, 254,363 (19.8 per cent); 65 to 79 years, 187,560 (14.6 per cent); and 80 years and over, 70,856 (5.5 per cent). The Unit draws attention to the fact that the two senior bands together account for 20.1 per cent of the served population but 41.3 per cent of dispensed volume, a divergence consistent with prior issues.

4. Distribution by SEX_CODE is 692,432 female (53.9 per cent), 588,374 male (45.8 per cent), and 3,854 not recorded (0.3 per cent). Item nonresponse in this field arises principally at units operating the older point-of-sale terminals, where the field is not enforced at enrollment.

5. Distribution by ETHNIC_GRP, a self-reported and expressly optional item, is: white, 63.1 per cent; Black or African American, 14.2 per cent; Hispanic or Latino, 12.4 per cent; Asian or Pacific Islander, 4.3 per cent; American Indian or Alaska Native, 0.8 per cent; other, 1.6 per cent; and declined or not recorded, 3.6 per cent. Percentages are computed on the full base and not on respondents alone.

6. Distribution by LANG_PREF, being the language elected for counselling and auxiliary labelling, is: English, 86.4 per cent; Spanish, 8.1 per cent; Chinese, 1.4 per cent; Vietnamese, 0.9 per cent; Russian, 0.7 per cent; Korean, 0.5 per cent; Polish, 0.4 per cent; all other languages in aggregate, 1.6 per cent.

7. Distribution by INC_BAND is imputed at the census block group level and is not solicited from any served person. The bands report as: under $15,000, 13.8 per cent; $15,000 to $24,999, 17.2 per cent; $25,000 to $34,999, 18.6 per cent; $35,000 to $49,999, 21.0 per cent; $50,000 to $74,999, 18.4 per cent; $75,000 and over, 8.7 per cent; not determinable, 2.3 per cent.

8. Geographic tabulation is carried at POSTAL_DIST, being the first three positions of the postal code, across 148 districts, with rollup to REGION_CD comprising the Metro East, Metro West, Northern Tier, River Valley and Gulf Coast regions. No finer geography is carried into this report or into any working file supporting it.

9. Small-cell suppression is applied throughout. Any cell with a count below 25 is suppressed and shown as a hyphen; complementary suppression is applied to the next smallest cell in the affected row and column so that suppressed values cannot be recovered by subtraction from published marginals. Percentages are withheld where the denominator falls below 100. Suppression is applied after rounding, and marginals are published as computed before suppression; rows may therefore not sum to the stated totals.

10. Requests for tabulation beyond the cross-classifications published here are to be directed to the Statistical Reporting Unit through the Records Custodian, and will be honoured only at the age-band, region and language levels.
