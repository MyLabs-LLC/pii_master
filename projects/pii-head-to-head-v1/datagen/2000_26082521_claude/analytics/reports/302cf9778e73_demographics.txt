ARTICLE ONE -- CONSTRUCTION AND SCOPE

    (a) This Return is made by the Statistical and Information Governance Unit
        in discharge of the Authority's statutory reporting duties and of such
        further duties as the Authority has assumed by resolution.
    (b) Every figure appearing in this Return is an aggregate figure. Nothing
        in this Return is to be construed as constituting, containing, or
        permitting the reconstruction of, any record relating to an identified
        or identifiable individual.
    (c) Total roll at the Census Point: 21,207.

ARTICLE EIGHT -- SUPPRESSION AND ROUNDING

    (a) The Unit applies a minimum cell threshold of ten. Any cell falling
        below that threshold is suppressed and shown as a hyphen.
        (i)  Secondary suppression is applied within the same analysis where a
             residual would otherwise be recoverable by subtraction from a
             published total.
        (ii) Where an entire analysis would fall below the threshold, the
             analysis is withheld in its entirety and the fact of withholding
             is recorded in the working papers retained by the Unit.
    (b) Percentages are calculated upon the total roll stated at Article One
        and are rounded to one decimal place; columns may therefore not total
        exactly one hundred.
