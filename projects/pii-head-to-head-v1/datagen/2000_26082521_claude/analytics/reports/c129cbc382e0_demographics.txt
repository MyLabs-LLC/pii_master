MERIDIAN TITLE & ESCROW SERVICES
BUYER-SIDE INCOME BAND TABULATION
Reporting period: files closed January 1 through December 31, 2010
Prepared by the Statistical Reporting Unit

1. Population. The tabulation covers 3,974 buyer-side files carrying a lender transmittal summary. Files closed without lender financing are outside the population and are not counted in any line below.

2. Source field. The income band is derived from the qualifying household income figure stated on the lender transmittal summary at the time of closing. The figure is banded on receipt; the underlying amount is not carried into the reporting warehouse.

3. Distribution by qualifying household income band:

      (a) Under $35,000 .................... 214 ....... 5.4%
      (b) $35,000 to $49,999 .............. 397 ...... 10.0%
      (c) $50,000 to $74,999 .............. 892 ...... 22.4%
      (d) $75,000 to $99,999 .............. 963 ...... 24.2%
      (e) $100,000 to $149,999 ............ 899 ...... 22.6%
      (f) $150,000 to $199,999 ............ 341 ....... 8.6%
      (g) $200,000 and over ............... 198 ....... 5.0%
      (h) Not stated on transmittal ........ 70 ....... 1.8%
      ---------------------------------------------------------
          Total ......................... 3,974 ..... 100.0%

4. Reading notes.

      (a) Bands are inclusive of the lower bound and exclusive of the upper bound.
      (b) A file with two or more qualifying incomes is banded on the combined figure and counted once.
      (c) Line (h) covers files where the transmittal summary was received without the income figure completed, and files where the summary was received after disbursement.

5. Suppression. Cells below eleven are withheld. No cell in Clause 3 falls below the threshold, and no suppression has been applied to this table. Where this tabulation is re-run at branch level rather than statewide, several bands are expected to fall below the threshold and will be withheld, with complementary withholding of a second cell in the affected row.

6. Cross-tabulation. This table is not to be crossed with the geographic table or the age table without prior review by the Compliance Officer, as the resulting cell sizes fall below the threshold in the majority of combinations.

7. Custody. The banded extract supporting Clause 3 is held by the Records Custodian under the closed-file retention schedule. Requests for the extract are made in writing to the Records Custodian and are logged.
