MEMORANDUM — RESEARCH AND STATISTICS UNIT

Subject:   Demographic tabulation, small-cell suppression note
Reporting period:   Year ended 31 December 2016
Distribution:   Compliance Officer; Community Development Officer

This note accompanies the demographic tabulation circulated separately and records the suppression rule applied to it. It is filed so that the rule is available to anyone re-running the tabulation in a later period.

Fields tabulated: Age Band, Sex, Ethnicity, Primary Language, Household Income Bracket, Postal District of Record. Membership base for the period: 41,286.

The threshold is a count of 10. Any cell falling below the threshold is not published; the count is added to the residual category of its table ("Other", "Two or more", or "Outside area" as applicable) and the suppression is recorded in the working papers rather than marked in the published table.

Complementary suppression applies. Where a single suppressed cell in a table could be recovered by subtracting the published cells from the published total, the next-smallest cell in that table is also suppressed and rolled into the same residual line. In the current period this was triggered once, in the ethnicity table, where the Native Hawaiian or Pacific Islander count fell below threshold.

Cross-tabulation is not published. The tabulation reports each field against the membership total only. Age by postal district, ethnicity by income bracket, and similar two-way tables produce cells at or near the threshold across much of the grid and are not prepared for circulation. Requests for cross-tabulated output should be referred to this unit, which will assess disclosure risk before any figures are released.

Geography is reported at district-group level. District groups aggregate several postal districts each; the smallest group in the current period carries 2,469 members. Reporting below district-group level is not performed.

Non-response is reported as a distinct "Not stated" category on every self-reported field and is neither imputed nor apportioned across the stated categories. The suppression threshold does not apply to the "Not stated" line, which in every table exceeds it by a wide margin.

Percentages are computed against the membership base and rounded to one decimal place. Where suppression has moved a count into a residual line, the residual percentage reflects the moved count.

The tabulation workbook, the query definitions and the suppression log for the period are held by the Records Custodian on the standard retention schedule. Re-runs at alternative bandings are available on request to this unit.
