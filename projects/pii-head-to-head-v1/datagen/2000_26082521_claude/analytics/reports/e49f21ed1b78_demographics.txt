Coastline Mutual Health Assurance Company
Statistical Digest SD-2021-04
Enrolled Population Demographics, Plan Year 2021
Issued by the Actuarial Reporting Unit

1. This digest reports the demographic composition of the enrolled population of the Company's individual and small-group lines for the reporting year ended 31 December 2021. All figures are aggregate counts drawn from the enrolment master extract.

2. The reporting denominator is the number of covered lives enrolled for one day or more during the reporting year, being 88,412.

3. Age band at close of reporting year: 0–17, 17,240 (19.5%); 18–44, 28,655 (32.4%); 45–64, 27,981 (31.6%); 65 and over, 14,536 (16.4%).

4. Sex as recorded at enrolment: Female, 45,318 (51.3%); Male, 42,905 (48.5%); Not stated, 189 (0.2%).

5. Race and ethnicity, self-reported: White, 57,984 (65.6%); Hispanic or Latino, 11,933 (13.5%); Black or African American, 8,930 (10.1%); Asian, 5,127 (5.8%); all other categories combined, 2,564 (2.9%); Not reported, 1,874 (2.1%).

6. Preferred spoken language: English, 74,109 (83.8%); Spanish, 9,637 (10.9%); all other languages combined, 3,682 (4.2%); Not recorded, 984 (1.1%).

7. Annual household income band, as declared on application: Under $35,000, 21,459 (24.3%); $35,000 to $74,999, 33,806 (38.2%); $75,000 and over, 28,772 (32.5%); Not stated, 4,375 (4.9%).

8. Geography by postal district group: Northern districts, 32,715 (37.0%); Central districts, 28,304 (32.0%); Southern districts, 18,566 (21.0%); Outlying districts, 8,827 (10.0%).

9. Small-cell suppression. No cell containing fewer than eleven covered lives is published. Where suppression of a single cell would permit its value to be derived by subtraction, complementary suppression is applied to the next smallest cell in the same row or column. Suppressed values remain within all published marginal and grand totals. Percentages are computed on unsuppressed denominators and rounded to one decimal place; rows may therefore not sum to exactly 100.0.
