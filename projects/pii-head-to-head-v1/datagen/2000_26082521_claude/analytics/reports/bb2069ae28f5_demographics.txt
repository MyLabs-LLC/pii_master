Column note: the "Total" row for the three percentage columns is a
population-weighted or household-weighted service-area rate, not a column sum.

Suppression rule. Any district-level cell derived from fewer than 50 weighted
persons, or fewer than 25 weighted households, is withheld. Where a single
withheld cell in a row could be recovered from the row total, a second cell in
that row is withheld as well. No district in the current tabulation triggered
either rule; the rule is stated because it governs all derivative extracts
produced from this series, including the ward-level and corridor-level
breakouts distributed separately.

Rounding. Population and household counts are rounded to the nearest hundred;
percentages carry one decimal place. Shares may not sum to 100.0.

Source and method. Weighted five-year pooled sample, reweighted to the
authority's 2021 service-boundary control totals. Vehicle availability and
household language are household-level characteristics; age is a person-level
characteristic. Districts 113 through 117 lie outside the fare-integrated
boundary and are not tabulated here.
