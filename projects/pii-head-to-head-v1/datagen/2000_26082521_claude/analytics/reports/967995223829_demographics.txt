STATISTICAL PROFILE OF SERVED POPULATION — CONTRACT SERVICES DIVISION
Reporting Period: 1 January 2011 – 31 December 2011
Prepared pursuant to Schedule 7, Clause 12 (Demographic Reporting Obligations) of the Master Services Agreement

1. PREAMBLE AND SCOPE OF UNDERTAKING

1.1 This report is submitted by the Contractor in satisfaction of the obligations set forth at Schedule 7, Clause 12.1 through 12.9 inclusive, of the Master Services Agreement subsisting between the Contractor and the Commissioning Authority.

1.2 The population to which this report relates comprises all persons in respect of whom a consignment was received, held, transferred or delivered by the Contractor during the Reporting Period, and in respect of whom the Commissioning Authority has furnished demographic attributes under the data-sharing protocol at Annex C.

1.3 The denominator for all proportional figures stated herein is the Total Served Population, being 184,206 records, save where a field-specific denominator is expressly stated by reason of non-response.

2. METHODOLOGY AND DERIVATION OF FIGURES

2.1 Counts were derived by aggregation over the fields Record Reference, Service Date, Age Band, Sex, Ethnic Group, Principal Language, Household Income Bracket and Postal District, as those fields are defined at Annex C, Table 2.

2.2 Percentages are expressed to one decimal place and are calculated upon the applicable denominator stated at Clause 1.3. Column totals may not sum to 100.0 by reason of rounding, and no inference shall be drawn from such variance.

2.3 No individual-level attribute has been extracted, retained or reproduced in the preparation of this report. Direct and indirect identifying fields — including Full Name, Date of Birth, Member Identifier, Contact Telephone, Contact Email and Delivery Address Line — were excluded at the point of extraction and do not appear in any working set.

8. SMALL-CELL SUPPRESSION

8.1 In accordance with Schedule 7, Clause 12.7, any cell yielding a count of fewer than ten (10) has been suppressed and is denoted by an em dash. Where suppression of a single cell would permit derivation of the suppressed value by subtraction from a stated total, secondary suppression has been applied to the next-smallest cell in the same row or column.

8.2 Suppressed counts remain included in all row and column totals. Accordingly, the sum of published cells within any table may fall below the stated total, and the Commissioning Authority is directed not to treat such shortfall as evidence of omission or error.

8.3 The Records Custodian retains the unsuppressed derivation under controlled access and shall produce it upon written requisition by the Commissioning Authority's audit function.
