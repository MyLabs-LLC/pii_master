NOTES

1. Basis: all members of record holding at least one open share
   account at the close of business on the reporting date. Closed
   and dormant-purged accounts are excluded.

2. Age is derived from the birth-date field on the master share
   file and computed as at the reporting date. The derived value
   is retained; the source field is not carried into this report.

3. Sex is taken from the sex code field as captured at account
   opening. The code is not revalidated at renewal.

4. Suppression: any cell containing fewer than five members is
   withheld and shown as a dash, with a second cell in the same
   row suppressed where the withheld figure would otherwise be
   recoverable by subtraction. No suppression was required in
   this table.

5. Percentages are of all bands and may not sum to 100.00 owing
   to rounding.
