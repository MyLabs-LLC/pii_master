DEMOGRAPHIC CROSS-TABULATION MEMORANDUM
Series DEM-2003-B — companion to DEM-2003-A
Title and Escrow Group, Statistical Unit
Issued 2 April 2004

I. PURPOSE
   (a) To present the Ethnicity Code by Household Income Bracket cross-tabulation for the 2003 closed-file population.
   (b) The base is unchanged from DEM-2003-A: N = 14,207 closed residential files, one record per file.
   (c) This memorandum publishes no dimension not already published in marginal form in DEM-2003-A.

II. COLLAPSING RULES
   (a) The seven published income brackets are collapsed to four tiers: under $50,000; $50,000–$99,999; $100,000 and over; not stated.
   (b) The categories "American Indian or Alaska Native", "Other", and "Information not provided" are combined into a single row, "Other or not stated", because at least one interior cell of each source row fell below the suppression threshold.
   (c) No further collapsing was required. All published interior cells carry a frequency of 11 or greater.

IV. NOTES ON INTERPRETATION
   (a) Row and column totals reconcile to the marginals published at clauses 5 and 7 of DEM-2003-A.
   (b) The "not stated" income column reflects files on which the Household Income Bracket field was left blank at intake; it is not an income category and should not be treated as one in any derived measure.
   (c) Percentages are deliberately omitted from the matrix. Where a percentage basis is required, it should be stated explicitly as row, column, or grand total.

V. SUPPRESSION
   (a) The suppression threshold is a frequency below 11, applied at the interior-cell level before any marginal is published.
   (b) Suppression is effected by row collapsing, as at II(b), rather than by cell masking, so that no residual value is recoverable by subtraction from a published marginal.
   (c) Three source rows were absorbed under this rule. Their combined frequency is published; their individual frequencies are not.

VI. DISTRIBUTION
