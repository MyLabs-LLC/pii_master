CRAVENHILL COUNTY DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH SERVICES
DIVISION OF PLANNING, RESEARCH AND STATISTICS
STATISTICAL BULLETIN 99-06
DEMOGRAPHIC CHARACTERISTICS OF THE SERVED POPULATION
Reporting Period: Calendar Year 1998

1. INTRODUCTORY STATEMENT. This Bulletin is issued by the Division of Planning, Research and Statistics under the general supervision of the Director of Health Services and constitutes the annual statistical accounting of the demographic characteristics of the population served, required to be maintained and made available for inspection under the terms of the Department's several categorical grant agreements then in force. The compilation was performed upon a data extract prepared by the Records Custodian, from which extract all identifying particulars were removed before transmittal to the Division; the Division neither requested nor received any record capable of being associated with an identified or identifiable person, and the Division's analysts are without means to effect any such association.

2. FIELDS TABULATED. The following fields, and no others, were carried into the tabulation: Age Band; Sex; Race and Ethnicity; Primary Language Spoken in the Home; Household Income Bracket (Percent of Federal Poverty Guidelines); and Service Area Quadrant, being an aggregation of postal districts and the finest geography maintained for statistical purposes.

4. NOTE ON SUPPRESSION. It is to be understood, in the reading of the foregoing table and of every table appended hereto, that no cell containing fewer than five persons is displayed. Such cells are suppressed and denoted by the symbol ‡, and where the suppression of a single cell would permit its value to be derived by subtraction from a displayed marginal total, the next-smallest cell within the affected row or column is suppressed in complement. Suppressed cells are included within all marginal totals notwithstanding their suppression, and percentages have been computed upon full denominators. By reason of rounding to the nearest tenth, columns may not sum precisely to one hundred percent. No cross-tabulation of three or more of the fields enumerated in paragraph 2 above is published, nor shall any such cross-tabulation be prepared upon request, the risk of re-identification at that resolution having been determined by the Director to be unacceptable.
