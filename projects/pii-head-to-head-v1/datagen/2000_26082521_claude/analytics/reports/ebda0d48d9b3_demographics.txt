Clause 1 — Purpose and Coverage
    (a) This profile records the characteristics of the population served by
        District fixed-route and paratransit operations during fiscal year
        2016.
    (b) Reference population: 233,900 persons; 96,400 households.
    (c) Reference date: 1 April 2016.
    (d) The profile is compiled from published aggregate statistical series.
        The District maintains no register of individuals in connection with
        this compilation.

Clause 5 — Small-Cell Suppression
    (a) A cell is suppressed where the underlying count is fewer than ten.
    (b) Suppression under sub-clause (a) triggers the following:
            (i)  withholding of the associated percentage; and
            (ii) withholding of one further cell in the same row and one in
                 the same column, selected as the smallest available value.
    (c) Suppressed cells are shown as "S". Cells with a zero count are shown
        as "0" and are not treated as suppressed.
    (d) Counts are rounded to the nearest 100. Percentages are calculated on
        unrounded values and may not sum to 100.0.
    (e) Tabulations combining ethnicity, language and income are released at
        district level only and are not disaggregated by postal district.
