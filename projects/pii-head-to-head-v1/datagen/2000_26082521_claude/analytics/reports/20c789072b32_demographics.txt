PACIFIC COAST TITLE AND ESCROW CORPORATION
ANNUAL STATISTICAL REPORT ON THE SERVED POPULATION
Calendar Year 2004
Prepared and certified by the Office of the Records Custodian

CLAUSE 1 — SCOPE

1. This report tabulates all parties served on files disbursed between January 1, 2004 and December 31, 2004 across the Company's six branch offices.
2. The unit of count is the distinct party of record. A party appearing on multiple files within the year is counted once, at the demographic values recorded on the earliest such file.
3. Total parties in scope: 15,720. This figure is the denominator for every percentage stated below.

CLAUSE 8 — SUPPRESSION AND ROUNDING

6. No cell representing fewer than 10 parties is published. Suppressed counts are aggregated into the "All other," "Declined to state," or "Not stated" line of the same table.
7. Where a table contains exactly one suppressed cell, a second cell is suppressed and aggregated in the same line, so that no suppressed value may be recovered by subtraction from the published total.
8. Cross-tabulation of any two or more of Clauses 2 through 7 is not published in this report and is not to be produced on request from the reporting extract.
9. Percentages are rounded to one decimal place; totals stated as 100.1 reflect rounding only.
