CITY ELECTRIC AND WATER COMMISSION
BUREAU OF SERVICE PLANNING
ANNUAL STATISTICAL REPORT ON THE CHARACTERISTICS OF THE SERVED POPULATION
Fiscal year ended 30 June 1999 — Document SP/99/07

SECTION I — AUTHORITY AND PURPOSE

1. This report is laid before the Commission in discharge of the requirement of Rule 9 of the Rules of Service, which directs the Bureau to report annually upon the composition of the population served, to the end that rate relief programmes, notice practices, and conservation outreach may be directed where they are of most effect. Nothing in this report identifies any premises or occupant, the whole being compiled from grouped returns.

2. The Commission supplied, at the close of the fiscal year, 97,530 residential accounts and 8,240 non-residential accounts, together serving an estimated resident population of 241,600 persons across seven postal districts and two annexed townships.

SECTION II — AGE AND SEX

3. The distribution by AGE BAND is: Under 18, 62,570 persons (25.9 per cent); 18–24, 21,020 (8.7 per cent); 25–44, 68,780 (28.5 per cent); 45–64, 55,090 (22.8 per cent); 65 and over, 34,140 (14.1 per cent). The distribution by SEX is Female, 123,700 (51.2 per cent) and Male, 117,900 (48.8 per cent). Households headed by a person aged 65 or over, a class of particular relevance to the winter moratorium on disconnection, number 19,410, or 19.9 per cent of residential accounts.

SECTION III — ETHNICITY AND LANGUAGE

4. The distribution by ETHNICITY is: White, 58.6 per cent; Black, 24.4 per cent; Asian or Pacific Islander, 7.2 per cent; American Indian or Alaska Native, 0.7 per cent; Other or two or more categories, 9.1 per cent. Persons of HISPANIC ORIGIN, enumerated without regard to the foregoing, constitute 19.8 per cent of the served population.

5. The distribution by HOUSEHOLD LANGUAGE, as indicated upon application for service and upon any subsequent request for correspondence in a language other than English, is: English, 72.9 per cent; Spanish, 16.4 per cent; Korean, 3.3 per cent; Arabic, 2.6 per cent; Hmong, 1.4 per cent; all other languages, 3.4 per cent. The Bureau accordingly recommends that the notice of estimated reading, the notice of arrears, and the conservation bulletin be printed in English, Spanish, and Korean for general distribution, and in the remaining languages upon request.

SECTION IV — INCOME

6. The distribution by HOUSEHOLD INCOME BRACKET is: under $15,000, 20.7 per cent; $15,000–$24,999, 17.9 per cent; $25,000–$34,999, 18.2 per cent; $35,000–$49,999, 19.4 per cent; $50,000–$74,999, 15.1 per cent; $75,000 and over, 8.7 per cent. Of accounts in the lowest two brackets, 41.3 per cent are enrolled in the percentage-of-income payment plan, leaving a substantial body of eligible accounts unenrolled, to which the Bureau invites the Commission's attention.

SECTION V — GEOGRAPHY

7. The distribution by POSTAL DISTRICT is: District 05, 11.9 per cent; District 09, 16.2 per cent; District 13, 14.7 per cent; District 16, 18.4 per cent; District 20, 13.1 per cent; District 24, 15.8 per cent; District 28, 9.9 per cent. No division finer than the postal district is prepared, tabulated, or released by the Bureau, whether internally or upon request.

SECTION VI — SUPPRESSION AND ROUNDING

8. Every cell containing fewer than ten accounts is suppressed and printed as a dash. Complementary suppression is applied within the affected row and column where the primary suppression would otherwise be recoverable from published margins. Percentages are struck upon full unsuppressed totals; counts are rounded to the nearest ten and percentages to one decimal place, whence columns may not sum exactly to the stated totals.
