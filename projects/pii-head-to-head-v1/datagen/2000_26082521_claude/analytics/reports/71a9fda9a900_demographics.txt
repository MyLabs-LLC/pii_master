BASIS OF CLASSIFICATION

Ethnic classification is self-reported on the membership
application and is optional. Members who leave the item blank are
counted under "Declined to state"; the field is not imputed and is
not updated from any external source. Change figures in the final
column of Table 2.1 are percentage-point movements against the
corresponding quarter of the prior year and are computed on the
published percentages, not on counts.

Primary language of correspondence is the operative field for
statement and notice production. It is set at account opening and
amended only on written request. Where no code is present the
member receives English-language correspondence by default; such
members are counted under "Not recorded" rather than under
"English".

SUPPRESSION

Cells containing fewer than five members are not published. Eleven
languages, each recorded for fewer than five members, have been
combined into "Other recorded" for this reason; the constituent
counts are not separately reportable and are not held in the
published extract. Where a single suppressed cell would be
recoverable from a published row or column total, the next
smallest cell in that row is suppressed in complement.

Percentages are computed on the full membership denominator and
may not sum to 100.00 owing to rounding.
