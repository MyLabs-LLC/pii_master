MERIDIAN TITLE GUARANTY & ESCROW
SERVED POPULATION STATISTICS — SUMMARY TABULATION
Period: Fiscal Year Ended September 30, 2004
Custodian of record: Records and Reporting Unit

PART I — BASIS

1. Counts below are of distinct parties served on closed files during the fiscal year. Total in scope: 6,204.
2. All figures are aggregate. No file-level or party-level detail accompanies this tabulation.

PART II — GEOGRAPHY

3. Distribution by service region, the coarsest geographic unit maintained in the reporting extract: Northern service region, 1,742 (28.1%); Central service region, 2,318 (37.4%); Southern service region, 1,551 (25.0%); Out-of-region, 593 (9.6%).

6. Distribution by sex: Female, 3,151 (50.8%); Male, 3,012 (48.6%); Not stated, 41 (0.7%).

PART IV — INCOME BRACKET

8. The two lowest brackets otherwise maintained in the Household Income field have been collapsed at Clause 7 because the lower of the two fell below the publication floor stated at Clause 11.

PART V — ETHNICITY AND LANGUAGE

9. Distribution by ethnicity, self-reported: White, non-Hispanic, 2,880 (46.4%); Hispanic or Latino, 1,613 (26.0%); Black or African American, 546 (8.8%); Asian, 502 (8.1%); All other categories combined, 249 (4.0%); Declined to state, 414 (6.7%).
10. Distribution by language of correspondence: English, 5,204 (83.9%); Spanish, 807 (13.0%); All other languages combined, 193 (3.1%).

PART VI — SUPPRESSION AND ROUNDING

11. The publication floor is 6. No cell representing fewer than 6 parties appears in this tabulation; such counts are combined into an "All other" or "Not stated" line, as at Clauses 9 and 10.
12. Where a single suppression would leave the suppressed value recoverable by subtraction, the next smallest cell in the same distribution is suppressed and combined with it.
13. Percentages are computed on the total at Clause 1 and rounded to one decimal place.
