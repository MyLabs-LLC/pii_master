EASTGATE LOGISTICS GROUP
ANNUAL DEMOGRAPHIC RETURN — SERVED POPULATION
Contract Lot 4 (Metropolitan). Year ending 31 March 2004. Ref ELG/ADR/04/L4.

(a) Basis. The return covers 58,431 individuals in 23,905 served households. Counts are of individuals resident at a served address as recorded on the delivery register at year end, not of deliveries made. Households entering and leaving the register during the year are counted once. Reconciliation against the depot control totals was completed by the Data Quality Lead on 12 April 2004; a variance of 0.3 per cent against the round manifests is attributed to late deregistrations and is accepted.

(b) Age band. The recorded distribution was: 0 to 17, 14,120 (24.2 per cent); 18 to 34, 13,908 (23.8 per cent); 35 to 54, 15,776 (27.0 per cent); 55 to 74, 10,449 (17.9 per cent); 75 and over, 4,178 (7.2 per cent). Age is derived from the age-band field completed at registration; no finer age detail is held in the reporting extract.

(c) Sex. Female 30,617 (52.4 per cent); Male 27,777 (47.5 per cent); Not Stated 37 (0.1 per cent).

(d) Ethnic group and main language. White British accounted for 54.8 per cent, White Other 9.6 per cent, Asian or Asian British 19.1 per cent, Black or Black British 9.4 per cent, Mixed 3.2 per cent, Chinese or Other 2.1 per cent, Not Stated 1.8 per cent. Main language was recorded as English for 74.3 per cent, with Urdu, Punjabi, Bengali, Somali and Polish together accounting for a further 17.6 per cent and all remaining languages, including Not Recorded, for 8.1 per cent. Individual language counts below the threshold are not itemised.

(e) Household income bracket. Under £5,000, 14.1 per cent; £5,000 to £9,999, 29.8 per cent; £10,000 to £14,999, 22.4 per cent; £15,000 to £19,999, 13.3 per cent; £20,000 and over, 12.2 per cent; Not Stated, 8.2 per cent. The bracket boundaries are those specified in the contract and have not been uprated.

(f) Geography. Reported at postal district: M9, 8,332 (14.3 per cent); M14, 7,109 (12.2 per cent); M40, 6,540 (11.2 per cent); OL9, 4,988 (8.5 per cent); SK5, 3,776 (6.5 per cent); all other districts aggregated, 27,686 (47.4 per cent). No output below postal district is produced under this contract and no coordinate or route-level geography is released.

(g) Suppression. The disclosure threshold is a count of five. Cells at or below the threshold are shown as a hyphen and are excluded from itemisation but retained in totals. Secondary suppression is applied where a primary suppression could be recovered by arithmetic. Two-way tables combining ethnic group, language or income bracket with postal district are not published; requests are handled by the Contract Compliance Officer at ward level or coarser.
