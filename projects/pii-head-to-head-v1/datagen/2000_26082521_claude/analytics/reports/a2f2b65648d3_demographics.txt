CLAUSE 8. SMALL-CELL SUPPRESSION. Any category with a count below eleven is
withheld from publication. Withheld counts are consolidated into the "Other or
multiple" or "Not reported" line of the same dimension so that dimension totals
reconcile to the denominator at Clause 1. Where consolidation would leave a single
residual cell from which a withheld value could be derived by subtraction, a
second cell is suppressed by complement. In the present issue, three categories in
Clause 5 and one category in Clause 4 were consolidated under this rule.

CLAUSE 9. GEOGRAPHIC FLOOR. No tabulation in this document is published below the
three-digit postal district. Cross-tabulations of geography with any second
dimension are prepared only where every resulting cell exceeds the threshold at
Clause 8; none met that condition in the present period and none is published.

CLAUSE 10. ROUNDING. Percentages are computed against the denominator at Clause 1
and rounded to one decimal place. Columns may not sum to exactly 100.0.

CLAUSE 11. STATUS. Approved for internal circulation by the Director of Statistical
Services. Distribution: Health Officer; Deputy Health Officer for Programs; Section
Chiefs; Records Custodian. Source extracts are retained by the Records Custodian
for the period prescribed in the retention schedule and are not circulated.
