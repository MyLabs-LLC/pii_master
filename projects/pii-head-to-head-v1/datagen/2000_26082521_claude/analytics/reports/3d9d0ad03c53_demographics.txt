VIII. SMALL-CELL SUPPRESSION
    A. Primary rule: counts below 11 are withheld and shown as "S".
    B. Percentages derived from a suppressed count are also withheld.
    C. Secondary suppression is applied to the next smallest cell in the same
       row or column where a single suppressed cell would otherwise be
       recoverable by subtraction.
    D. Suppressed counts remain in every marginal and in the denominator at
       I.C. Published category counts therefore sum to no more than the
       denominator.
    E. The suppression pass is run before the compilation leaves the
       aggregation layer. Suppression decisions are logged by table and cell
       reference and retained by the Records Custodian for seven years.
