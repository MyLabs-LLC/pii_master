H.  SMALL-CELL SUPPRESSION

    H.1  Threshold. Any cell of fewer than 10 is suppressed and printed
         as an asterisk. The threshold is fixed and is not adjusted for
         the size of the base population.
    H.2  Complementary suppression. Where one suppressed cell in a row
         or column is recoverable by subtraction from the published
         margin, the next smallest cell in that margin is suppressed.
         No fewer than two cells are ever suppressed in a single line.
    H.3  Sections C through G are marginal distributions and are not to
         be combined by the reader. Cross-tabulation of any two sections
         reconstructs cells below threshold and is prohibited.
    H.4  Suppression precedes rounding. Percentages are computed on the
         unsuppressed base and may not sum to the stated total.
