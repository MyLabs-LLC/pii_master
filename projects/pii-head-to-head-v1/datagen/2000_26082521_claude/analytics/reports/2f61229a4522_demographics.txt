Prepared by: the Workforce Data Analyst
Countersigned by: the Regional Compliance Officer
Extract taken from: Candidate Registration System, table CAND_PROFILE

I. SCOPE AND METHOD
    A. Population counted
        1. Candidates holding an active registration at any point in the period
        2. Total in scope (N): 9,764
        3. Records excluded as duplicate registrations: 118
    B. Source fields used
        1. Age at registration (derived to band; no underlying value carried into this return)
        2. Sex
        3. Ethnic group (self-assigned, ONS 2011 category set)
        4. Main language
        5. Gross annual income bracket (self-declared)
        6. Postcode district (outward code only)
    C. Rounding
        1. Percentages are given to one decimal place
        2. Column percentages may not total 100.0 owing to rounding

VIII. SUPPRESSION
    A. Cells with a count of fewer than five are not published. Where a cell has been
       suppressed on this ground it is shown as "-" and the affected category is rolled
       into the adjacent "Other" or "All other" line.
    B. Secondary suppression is applied where a single suppressed cell could be
       recovered by subtraction from a published total.
    C. Cross-tabulations below regional level are not produced from this return.
    D. Requests for finer geography are directed to the Records Custodian and are
       answered only at aggregate level.
