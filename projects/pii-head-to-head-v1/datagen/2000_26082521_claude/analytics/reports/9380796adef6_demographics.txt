MEMORANDUM

MUNICIPAL WATER AND LIGHT DEPARTMENT — RECORDS AND DISCLOSURE

DATE:  8 October 2004
TO:    Distribution — Planning and Analysis; Customer Operations;
       Office of the City Attorney (Public Records Unit)
FROM:  the Records Custodian
RE:    Tabulation dictionary and disclosure-control standard applicable
       to reports PA-04-217 through PA-04-219

1. APPLICATION. This standard governs all demographic tabulations of the
   served population released outside the originating division.

2. PERMITTED DIMENSIONS. Tabulations may be constructed only from the
   following fields. Field labels are published; underlying values are
   held in the source system and are not released.

     AGE_BAND               seven-category, five- and ten-year bands
     SEX                    two-category
     ETHNICITY_CODE         OMB Directive 15 categories
     PRIMARY_LANGUAGE       preferred language of correspondence
     INCOME_BRACKET         eight-category, annual household
     POSTAL_DISTRICT        internal carrier-route grouping
     SERVICE_CLASS          residential, small commercial, other
     TENURE_BAND            years of continuous service
     ASSISTANCE_FLAG        program participation, yes or no

3. PROHIBITED DIMENSIONS. Tabulations shall not be constructed from, and
   shall not be released alongside, any of the following: account number,
   meter or premise identifier, service address, mailing address, name of
   account holder, telephone number, electronic mail address, date of
   birth, taxpayer identification number, or any other field by which a
   single account or person may be distinguished.

4. THRESHOLD. The minimum publication threshold is eleven (11). A cell
   containing one to ten units is suppressed primary and marked "S". A
   cell containing zero may be published as zero only where the row and
   column margins do not permit inference of a suppressed neighbour;
   otherwise it is suppressed as well.

5. COMPLEMENTARY SUPPRESSION. Where a single primary suppression occurs
   in a row or column, at least one further cell in that row or column
   shall be suppressed, selected to minimise information loss. The
   originating division shall verify by audit that no suppressed value is
   recoverable by subtraction from published margins, and shall retain
   the audit worksheet for three years.

6. GEOGRAPHY FLOOR. Postal district is the finest geography permitted.
   Requests for finer geography shall be refused at division level and
   referred to this office.

7. ROUNDING. Counts are published unrounded above threshold. Percentages
   are published to one decimal place.

8. EXCEPTIONS. Any release departing from paragraphs 4 through 7 requires
   written approval of the Records Custodian and prior notice to the
   Public Records Unit.

Distribution: three copies; one retained.
