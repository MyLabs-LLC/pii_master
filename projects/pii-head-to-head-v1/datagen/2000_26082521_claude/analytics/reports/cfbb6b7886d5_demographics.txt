SECTION 8 — SUPPRESSION
Figures are published as aggregates only. No cell is released below the postal-district level. Any cell with a count under 500 is withheld and shown as "S"; where a single withheld cell could be recovered by subtraction, the next smallest cell in the same table is also withheld. Withheld counts remain in the stated totals. Percentages are computed on the unsuppressed total and may not sum to 100 owing to rounding.
