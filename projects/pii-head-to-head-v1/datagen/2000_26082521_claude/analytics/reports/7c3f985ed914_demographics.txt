BRIARFIELD INDEPENDENT SCHOOL DISTRICT

STATISTICAL REPORT UPON THE DEMOGRAPHIC COMPOSITION
OF THE POPULATION SERVED BY THE DISTRICT
For the Instructional Year Commencing August 2015 and Concluding June 2016
Snapshot Date: 30 October 2015 -- Report Series RIS-16-002

RECITALS

    WHEREAS the Board of Trustees has by standing resolution directed the
    Department of Research and Information Services to prepare and lay before
    it an annual statement of the composition of the population served; and

    WHEREAS the several reporting instruments to which the District is subject
    require the enumeration of the served population by age, by sex, by
    ethnicity, by language, by household economic circumstance, and by area of
    residence; and

    WHEREAS the District is bound to observe the disclosure-avoidance
    obligations arising under applicable federal and state law governing
    education records, and to publish in aggregate form only;

    NOW THEREFORE the following statement is laid before the Board.

SECTION 8 -- SUPPRESSION OF SMALL CELLS

    8.1  It is the standing practice of this Department that no Cell reflecting
         a count of fewer than ten shall be published. Each such Cell is
         denoted by an asterisk in the tables above, and the corresponding
         percentage is likewise withheld.
    8.2  Where the withholding of a single Cell would nonetheless permit its
         value to be inferred by subtraction from a published total or
         subtotal, the Department shall withhold such further Cells as are
         necessary to defeat the inference.
    8.3  All percentages are calculated upon the total stated at Section 1.3
         and rounded to the nearest one-tenth of one percent; by reason of
         rounding and of any suppression applied, the several columns may not
         sum precisely to one hundred percent.
