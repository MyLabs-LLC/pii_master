1. SCOPE
1.1 This return tabulates the demographic composition of the served population for the twelve months ended 31 December 2011.
1.2 Served population is defined as the count of distinct dispensing relationships active at any point in the reporting period.
1.3 Total served population: 1,284,600.
1.4 All figures are aggregate. No record-level extract accompanies this return.

8. SUPPRESSION
8.1 Any cell with a count below 5 is suppressed and shown as "—".
8.2 Where a single suppressed cell within a row or column would be recoverable by subtraction from the published total, a second cell in that row or column is suppressed. Secondary suppression is applied to the next-smallest cell.
8.3 Suppressed values are excluded from percentage bases. Percentages may therefore not sum to 100.0 owing to rounding at one decimal place.
8.4 Totals are published unsuppressed in all cases.
