CALDER COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
DIVISION OF EPIDEMIOLOGY AND VITAL STATISTICS
ANNUAL DEMOGRAPHIC PROFILE OF THE SERVED POPULATION
CALENDAR YEAR 1998 | REPORT CY98-DEM-02

1. AUTHORITY AND PURPOSE. This tabulation is prepared under the standing
   instruction of the Board of Health to report, annually, the composition of the
   population receiving departmental services. It supersedes no prior issue.

2. BASIS OF COUNT. Service episodes recorded in the departmental register were
   reduced to unduplicated persons by deterministic linkage. Denominator for all
   panels: 31,940.

6. SMALL-CELL SUPPRESSION. No category is published at a count below ten.
   Categories falling below that threshold are consolidated into "Other or
   multiple" or "Not reported" as appropriate to the dimension, and complementary
   suppression is applied to any second cell whose value would permit the withheld
   figure to be reconstructed. Geography is not published below the three-digit
   postal district.
