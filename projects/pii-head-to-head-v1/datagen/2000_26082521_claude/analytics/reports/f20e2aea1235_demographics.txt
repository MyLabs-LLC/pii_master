[2] BASIS
    Districts are three-digit postal prefixes. Assignment is by
    the service district of the dispensing store, not by any
    individual location. Bracket assignment is imputed from
    census block-group median household income. No income figure
    is collected from, or held against, any served individual.

[4] DISTRICTS NOT SHOWN
    Sixty-one districts carrying fewer than three stores each are
    consolidated into the "All" line and are not printed
    separately. Their combined served count is 387,590. Printing
    them individually would in several instances yield cells
    below the release threshold and would permit inference of the
    withheld figures from the published margins.

[5] SUPPRESSION RULE
    Threshold: cell frequency of 11. Cells at or below the
    threshold are withheld and marked ++. Complementary
    suppression is applied within the affected row and column
    where a single withheld cell could be recovered by
    subtraction. No suppression was required on Schedule 2 at the
    district level of aggregation printed above; suppression does
    apply to the unpublished store-level working papers from
    which this schedule is derived.

[6] RETENTION
    Working papers are held by the Population Analysis Unit for
    thirty-six months from issue and are then destroyed. Printed
    schedules are retained indefinitely.
