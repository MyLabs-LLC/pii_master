1. SCOPE AND BASIS OF PREPARATION

1.1 This Return is furnished by the Contractor to the Authority in satisfaction of the Contractor's continuing obligations under the aforementioned Clause, and comprises aggregate statistical measures only, derived from the operational service records maintained in the ordinary course of business during the Reporting Period.

1.2 The denominator population ("the Served Population") comprises all discrete service recipients to whom one or more scheduled delivery, collection or assisted-uplift movements were completed within the Reporting Period. Where a recipient was served on multiple occasions, that recipient is counted once and once only.

1.3 Percentages are computed against the total Served Population of 41,882 and are stated to one decimal place. By reason of independent rounding at each row, column totals may not sum precisely to 100.0.

1.4 Fields recorded but not disclosed in aggregate herein: Recipient Reference; Service Address; Contact Telephone; Date of Birth; National Insurance Number. Disclosure of the foregoing is neither required nor permitted by Clause 14.3.

2. SMALL-CELL SUPPRESSION

2.1 In accordance with the disclosure control protocol adopted at Clause 14.3(f), any cell having a frequency of fewer than ten (10) is suppressed and denoted by the symbol [S]. Where suppression of a single cell within a category would permit derivation of the suppressed value by subtraction from the category total, a secondary cell of next-lowest frequency is likewise suppressed. Suppressed frequencies remain incorporated within all stated category totals.
