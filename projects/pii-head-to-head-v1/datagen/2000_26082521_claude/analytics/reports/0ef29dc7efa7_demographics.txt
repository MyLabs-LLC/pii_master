INTEROFFICE MEMORANDUM

TO:      District Managers, Metro East and River Valley Regions
FROM:    Manager, Statistical Reporting Unit
COPY:    The Records Custodian; Director of Store Planning
SUBJECT: Second-Quarter 1997 Supplement to the Served Population Profile

1. The supplement transmitted herewith restates the served population count at 1,236,904 for the twelve months ended 30 June 1997, an increase of 2.4 per cent over the corresponding period.

2. Two districts previously reported separately have been merged into a single POSTAL_DIST grouping following the closure of four units. The merged grouping carries 41,208 served persons, or 3.3 per cent of the total. Prior-period figures for the constituent districts are not restated and should not be compared directly against the merged line.

3. The LANG_PREF tabulation shows Spanish preference at 11.6 per cent in the merged grouping against 7.9 per cent chain-wide. District Managers are asked to review auxiliary label stocking against this figure before the third-quarter order cycle.

4. Cells falling below the suppression threshold of 25 are shown as a hyphen in the attached tabulation, with complementary suppression applied so that the withheld figures cannot be derived from the published row and column totals. Three cells in the ETHNIC_GRP by INC_BAND table are affected.

5. Questions on method are to be raised with this Unit in writing before 15 August.
