Compiled: Office of the Records Custodian
Approved: Regional Controller

1. SCOPE AND METHOD

1.1  The return covers 214 pharmacies trading in the Region for the whole of the period. Branches opened or closed mid-period are excluded and are listed in Annex C.
1.2  Unit of count is the individual served, de-duplicated across branches by internal linkage key. Occasions of service are not counted here; see Return SWR-2004-H1-V.
1.3  Total served population, all branches: 486,900.
1.4  Percentages are of the total at 1.3 and are rounded to one decimal place. Rounding may cause columns to depart from 100.0 by up to 0.1.

8. SMALL-CELL SUPPRESSION

8.1  Any cell with a frequency below 10 is withheld and printed as "n/p".
8.2  Complementary suppression is applied within the same row and column where a residual would otherwise be recoverable by subtraction. Suppressed cells are included in row and column totals; published cells therefore need not sum to the stated total.
8.3  Cross-tabulations combining ethnic group, language and geography are suppressed below postal-area level in all cases, irrespective of cell size.
8.4  Income band is assigned from postal-sector indices. It is not recorded against any individual and is not to be represented as such.

9. RETENTION

9.1  Source extracts are held for 24 months from the closing date at 1.1 and are then destroyed under the Regional Retention Schedule.
