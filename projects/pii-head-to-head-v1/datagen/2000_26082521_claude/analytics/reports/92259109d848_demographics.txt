METROPOLITAN WATER BOARD — DIVISION OF PLANNING AND FORECASTS
BIENNIAL PROFILE OF THE SERVED POPULATION
Reporting period: 1 January 1995 to 31 December 1996
Series WB-DEM, Issue 6
=====================================================================

1. Scope and method.

1.1 The Board serves an estimated resident population of 338,900 persons through 128,470 metered connections, of which 121,860 are residential and 6,610 are commercial or institutional. This profile is prepared biennially for the guidance of the Board in matters of tariff design, capital programming, and the discharge of its obligations under the language-assistance ordinance.

1.2 Demographic characteristics are not collected in the ordinary course of billing. They are estimated by apportioning the decennial census characteristics of each postal district to the Board's connection counts within that district, adjusted by the housing-unit change series maintained by the Division. All results are published in aggregate.

2. Composition of the served population.

2.1 By age band: Under 5, 6.9 per cent; 5–17, 18.2 per cent; 18–34, 24.1 per cent; 35–54, 27.6 per cent; 55–64, 8.9 per cent; 65–74, 7.8 per cent; 75 and over, 6.5 per cent.

2.2 By sex: Female, 172,930 persons (51.0 per cent); Male, 165,970 persons (49.0 per cent).

2.3 By ethnicity: White, 54.7 per cent; Black, 26.1 per cent; Asian or Pacific Islander, 9.8 per cent; American Indian or Alaska Native, 0.6 per cent; Other or two or more categories, 8.8 per cent. Hispanic origin, enumerated separately and crossing the foregoing categories, stands at 21.4 per cent.

2.4 By household language: English, 70.2 per cent; Spanish, 17.6 per cent; Chinese, 4.1 per cent; Russian, 2.8 per cent; Haitian Creole, 2.2 per cent; all other languages, 3.1 per cent. Households in which no member aged 14 or over speaks English well are estimated at 6.4 per cent, this figure governing the printing quantities for translated notices of interruption.

3. Economic characteristics.

3.1 By household income bracket: under $15,000, 22.3 per cent; $15,000–$24,999, 18.1 per cent; $25,000–$34,999, 17.5 per cent; $35,000–$49,999, 18.9 per cent; $50,000–$74,999, 14.6 per cent; $75,000 and over, 8.6 per cent.

3.2 Households at or below 150 per cent of the federal poverty guideline, being the threshold of eligibility for the reduced service charge, are estimated at 27.9 per cent of residential connections.

4. Geographic distribution.

4.1 By postal district: District 03, 14.8 per cent; District 06, 19.2 per cent; District 08, 12.6 per cent; District 12, 17.1 per cent; District 15, 21.0 per cent; District 19, 15.3 per cent.

4.2 By pressure zone, a coarser division employed for operational purposes: North Zone, 34.1 per cent; Central Zone, 41.7 per cent; South Zone, 24.2 per cent.

5. Suppression.

5.1 Cells of fewer than fifteen connections, and any cross-tabulation of three or more characteristics at district level, are withheld from publication. Where withholding one cell would permit its recovery from published margins, a further cell is withheld in the same row and in the same column.

5.2 Marginal totals are published in full, percentages being computed upon those totals. Figures are rounded to one decimal place and may not sum precisely to one hundred.
