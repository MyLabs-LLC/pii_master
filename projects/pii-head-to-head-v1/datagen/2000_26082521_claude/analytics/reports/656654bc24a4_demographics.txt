»  SMALL-CELL SUPPRESSION
   -  The threshold is eleven (11). Any cell beneath it is withheld and rendered as "—" in every schedule.
   -  Complementary suppression is effected wherever a single withheld cell would otherwise be recoverable by subtraction from a published margin, and the cell selected for complementary suppression is the next-smallest in the affected row or column.
   -  Withheld counts remain incorporated in all subtotals, marginals and the grand total, such that totals are complete notwithstanding the suppression of their constituent cells.
   -  Percentages are calculated prior to suppression and rounded to one decimal place; consequently, published columns may depart from 100.0% by a rounding residual.
