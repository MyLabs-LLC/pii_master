8. SUPPRESSION OF SMALL CELLS

8.1 A cell of fewer than five users is not published. The count is shown as a hyphen and no percentage is given.

8.2 Where the withheld figure could be inferred from the published total and the remaining cells, the smallest remaining cell in that table is withheld also.

8.3 The rule is applied to every table in this return, including those in which no cell currently falls below the threshold, so that the presence or absence of a hyphen carries no additional meaning.

9. NOTES ON INTERPRETATION

9.1 Income is recorded at household level and attributed to each registered user in that household; the income table therefore counts users, not households, and is not a distribution of household incomes.

9.2 The 12.0 per cent recorded as income not stated at clause 6 reflects an optional question on the review questionnaire. No imputation has been carried out.

9.3 Percentages are rounded to one decimal place. Columns may not sum exactly to 100.0.
