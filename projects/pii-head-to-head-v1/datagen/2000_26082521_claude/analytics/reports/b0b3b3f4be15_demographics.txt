SUBJ:    Service Population Demographic Profile, FY 2003-04
REF:     Report PA-04-217; supersedes PA-03-198

1.  PURPOSE. Transmits the annual demographic tabulation of the resident
    population within the combined water and electric service territory,
    as required under Board Resolution 2001-44, Sec. 6(b).

2.  BASIS. Resident counts are apportioned from Census 2000 block-group
    tabulations to the service territory boundary in effect 30 June 2004,
    with intercensal adjustment applied at the postal-district level.
    Estimated total served resident population: 248,610.

4.  DISCLOSURE CONTROL. All figures are aggregate. No cell in Tables 1
    through 3 falls below the Department's minimum publication threshold
    of eleven (11). Where a cross-tabulation of these dimensions would
    yield a cell of ten (10) or fewer, that cell is suppressed and marked
    "S", and one or more complementary cells in the same row and column
    are suppressed to prevent recovery by subtraction. Suppressed cells
    remain included in marginal totals. Requests for unsuppressed detail
    must be directed in writing to the Records Custodian and are subject
    to review under Sec. 9 of the Department's data release policy.

5.  NOTES. Percentages are computed on the total column and may not sum
    to 100.0 owing to rounding. Age is as of 1 July 2004. Ethnicity
    categories follow OMB Directive 15 as revised. Counts are estimates
    and carry sampling and apportionment error not stated here; standard
    errors are available on request from Planning and Analysis.

6.  ACTION. No Board action required. For information only. Companion
    tabulations covering language, income and geography are transmitted
    separately as PA-04-218 and PA-04-219.
