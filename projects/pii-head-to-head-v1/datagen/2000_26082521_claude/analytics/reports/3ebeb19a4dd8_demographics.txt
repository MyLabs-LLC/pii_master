9. Suppression. The publication threshold is a cell count of six. Cells falling below the threshold are withheld and shown as an em dash. Where a single withheld cell could be recovered by subtracting the published cells from the stated total, the next smallest cell in that row is withheld in addition. Postal prefixes returning fewer than fifty closed orders are reported within the "all other prefixes" line and not separately. Withheld counts remain within the stated totals.

10. Rounding. Percentages are computed on the counted-party base at Clause 1 and rounded to one decimal place. Columns may not sum to exactly 100.0 percent.

11. Retention and access. Branch worksheets supporting Clauses 3 through 8 are held in the statistical file for seven years. Requests for detail finer than the levels published here are directed to the Records Custodian.
