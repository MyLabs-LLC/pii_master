MERIDIAN TITLE & ESCROW SERVICES
GEOGRAPHIC DISTRIBUTION OF CLOSED FILES
First half 2011 (January 1 through June 30)
Statistical Reporting Unit — Report No. 2011-G2

1. Population and unit of count. The unit of count is the closed file, not the party. Files closed in the period: 5,612. Each file is assigned to the postal district of the subject property, taken from the legal description block on the settlement statement.

2. Level of geography. Distribution is reported at the postal district level, meaning the first three positions of the postal code only. Full postal codes are not carried into this report and are not reported at any level below the district.

4. County rollup. The eight named districts roll up to four counties as follows: 085 and 086 to the northern county; 087 and 088 to the central county; 089 to the eastern county; 070, 071, and 072 to the southern county. The rollup is published as a separate table on request to the Statistical Reporting Unit.

5. Withheld line. The "Withheld, combined" line at Clause 3 aggregates four postal districts, each carrying between one and ten closed files in the period. These districts are not named. The combined figure is published because it stands at or above the suppression threshold; the component districts are not published individually and are not published in the county rollup at Clause 4.

6. Suppression rule. The threshold is eleven. A district falling below the threshold in a given period is folded into the combined withheld line for that period. Folding is applied per period and is not carried forward; a district withheld in one period may be published in the next.

7. Out-of-region line. Files on subject property outside the operating footprint are combined into a single line without district detail, regardless of count.

8. Rounding. Percentages are rounded to one decimal place on unrounded counts. The column total reflects rounding.

9. Distribution. This report is circulated to branch managers of record and to the Compliance Officer. Copies are held by the Records Custodian under the reporting retention schedule.
