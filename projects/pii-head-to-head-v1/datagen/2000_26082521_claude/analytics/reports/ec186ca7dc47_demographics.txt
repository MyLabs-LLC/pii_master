MUNICIPAL GAS AND LIGHT UNDERTAKING
ANNUAL DEMOGRAPHIC ENUMERATION OF THE SERVICE AREA
Year ended 31 December 1998

TO: The Committee on Rates and Service Extension
FROM: The Superintendent of Statistical Services
COPY: The Records Custodian; the District Engineer

1. It is respectfully reported that as at the close of the year the Undertaking supplied 61,240 residential accounts serving an estimated resident population of 146,880 persons. The enumeration is derived from the annual account review, the tri-annual premises survey, and the language-preference indications recorded at the time of service application, all such returns having been aggregated before tabulation.

2. As to AGE BAND, the population is composed of Under 18, 25.8 per cent; 18–34, 21.4 per cent; 35–54, 28.9 per cent; 55–64, 10.2 per cent; and 65 and over, 13.7 per cent. As to SEX, Female stands at 50.9 per cent and Male at 49.1 per cent, a distribution unchanged in any material degree from the preceding year.

3. As to ETHNICITY, the categories returned are White, 68.4 per cent; Black, 14.9 per cent; Asian or Pacific Islander, 8.1 per cent; American Indian or Alaska Native, 0.4 per cent; and Other, 8.2 per cent, with Hispanic origin separately enumerated at 12.6 per cent. As to PRIMARY HOUSEHOLD LANGUAGE, English is recorded for 79.3 per cent of accounts, Spanish for 11.2 per cent, Vietnamese for 3.1 per cent, Polish for 2.4 per cent, and all remaining languages for 4.0 per cent.

4. As to HOUSEHOLD INCOME BRACKET, self-reported at application and updated upon any request for the low-income tariff, the counts and proportions are:

      Income bracket                Accounts        Per cent
      Under $15,000                  11,020           18.0
      $15,000 – $24,999              10,410           17.0
      $25,000 – $34,999              11,630           19.0
      $35,000 – $49,999              12,860           21.0
      $50,000 – $74,999               9,800           16.0
      $75,000 and over                5,520            9.0
      Total                          61,240          100.0

5. As to GEOGRAPHY, the service area is reported at postal-district level, no finer division being published: District 22, 28.4 per cent of accounts; District 25, 24.1 per cent; District 27, 21.7 per cent; District 31, 16.3 per cent; and the unincorporated fringe served under the extension agreement, 9.5 per cent.

6. In conformity with the Committee's standing direction of 12 March 1996, no cell containing fewer than ten accounts is published; such cells are suppressed together with a second cell in the same row and column sufficient to prevent recovery by arithmetic. The suppression rule is applied before percentages are struck, and totals are shown as unsuppressed sums.
