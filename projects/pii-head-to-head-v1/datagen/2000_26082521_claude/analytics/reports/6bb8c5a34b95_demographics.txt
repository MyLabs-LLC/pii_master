HARBIN COUNTY BOARD OF HEALTH
REPORT OF THE HEALTH OFFICER RESPECTING THE COMPOSITION OF THE SERVED POPULATION
For the Fiscal Year Ended June 30, 1997

(a) The Health Officer, being charged by the ordinances of this County with the duty of rendering an annual account of the persons served by the programs of the Department, and having caused the Records Custodian to prepare an aggregate compilation for that purpose, does hereby report as follows.

(b) That the number of unduplicated persons served during the fiscal year aforesaid was 52,940; that the said number represents an increase of 3.1 percent over the corresponding figure for the preceding fiscal year; and that the said increase is attributable chiefly to the expansion of the immunization and prenatal programs, the caseloads of the remaining programs having been substantially unchanged.

(c) That the fields upon which the said compilation was made are the following, namely: Age Band; Sex; Race and Ethnicity, the categories thereof conforming to the Federal standard then prescribed; Primary Language Spoken in the Home; Household Income Bracket, expressed as a percentage of the Federal Poverty Guidelines then in effect; and Postal District of Residence, expressed at no resolution finer than the three-digit sectional prefix; and that no other field was extracted, retained, or examined.

(d) That of the persons so served, 30.2 percent were of an age of seventeen years or less, 44.9 percent were of an age between eighteen and fifty-four years inclusive, and 24.9 percent were of an age of fifty-five years or greater; that 55.6 percent were reported female and 44.4 percent male; that 18.7 percent reported a Primary Language Spoken in the Home other than English; that 63.4 percent reported a Household Income below 185 percent of the Federal Poverty Guidelines; and that the four postal districts comprising the northern tier of the County together accounted for 41.8 percent of the served population, being a proportion in excess of that tier's share of the general population as last enumerated.

(e) That in the preparation of the tables appended hereto the following rule of suppression was observed without exception: every cell of fewer than five persons was withheld from display and marked ‡; every such withholding was accompanied, where necessary to prevent recovery by subtraction, by the withholding of the next-smallest cell in the affected row or column; and all withheld values were nevertheless carried into the marginal totals, so that the totals herein reported are complete notwithstanding the suppressions.

(f) All of which is respectfully submitted for the consideration of the Board.
