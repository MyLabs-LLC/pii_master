MEMORANDUM — MUNICIPAL LIGHT AND POWER

To:      Chief Financial Officer
From:    Rates and Load Research Section
Date:    8 August 2011
Ref:     LR-2011-14
Subject: Mid-Year Demographic Summary, Residential Service Class

1. The following summary characterises the population within the electric service boundary. Resident total 138,900; occupied household total 54,220.

2. Age band — Under 18: 31,947 (23.0%); 18 to 34: 32,974 (23.7%); 35 to 54: 36,514 (26.3%); 55 to 74: 27,363 (19.7%); 75 and over: 10,102 (7.3%).

3. Sex — Female: 71,003 (51.1%); Male: 67,897 (48.9%).

4. Ethnicity — White: 78,846 (56.8%); Black or African American: 26,391 (19.0%); Hispanic or Latino: 20,973 (15.1%); Asian: 8,056 (5.8%); Two or more races: 3,195 (2.3%); All other categories combined: 1,439 (1.0%).

5. Household primary language — English: 45,981 (84.8%); Spanish: 4,988 (9.2%); Somali: 1,301 (2.4%); Hmong: 921 (1.7%); All other: 1,029 (1.9%).

6. Household income bracket — Under 25,000: 12,809 (23.6%); 25,000 to 49,999: 14,368 (26.5%); 50,000 to 74,999: 11,821 (21.8%); 75,000 to 99,999: 8,133 (15.0%); 100,000 and over: 7,089 (13.1%).

7. Geography, by service district — District A: 19,193 (35.4%); District B: 15,455 (28.5%); District C: 12,182 (22.5%); District D: 7,390 (13.6%).

8. Any cell falling below ten households has been rolled up to the next broader category rather than published. Where a rollup would still permit derivation of the withheld figure, the neighbouring cell has likewise been rolled up.

9. Comparable figures for the prior year are held by the Records Custodian and may be requested through the usual channel.
