1. PURPOSE

To transmit the 2021 base-year demographic tabulation for the authority's
defined service area (all census blocks whose centroid lies within 0.5 mile of
a fixed-route stop or 0.75 mile of a rail station portal). This tabulation
supersedes OPA-2019-087 and is the reference dataset for Title VI service and
fare equity analyses through the 2024 base-year refresh.

6. SUPPRESSION AND ROUNDING

Cells with a weighted count below 50, or with a relative standard error above
30 percent, are suppressed and shown as "--". Where suppression of a single
cell within a table would permit derivation of that cell by subtraction, the
next-smallest cell in the same table is also suppressed (complementary
suppression). Suppressed values remain included in table totals; component rows
therefore may not sum to the stated total. All counts are rounded to the nearest
hundred and percentages to one decimal place, so percentage columns may not sum
to exactly 100.0.

No record-level extract underlies this memorandum. All figures are weighted
aggregates produced from the pooled five-year sample and released only at the
levels shown.

7. DISPOSITION

The underlying aggregate tables are held by the Records Custodian under
retention schedule PL-06 (ten years). Requests for cross-tabulations not shown
above should be routed to this office in writing; cross-tabulations that would
yield cells below the suppression threshold will not be produced.
