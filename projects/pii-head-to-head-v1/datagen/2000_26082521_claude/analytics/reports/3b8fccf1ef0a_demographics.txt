PART III. KNOWN LIMITATIONS

III.1  Ethnicity is self-reported at registration and is not revalidated on re-enrollment. The "Not reported" category includes forms returned with the item left blank.

III.2  Income bracket is derived from meal benefit applications only. Households not applying are counted under "No application on file" and are not imputed. This category is not equivalent to "above 250% FPL."

III.3  Postal district reflects the residence recorded at the official count date. Mid-year moves within the reporting period are not reflected.

III.4  Home language reflects the response to the enrollment survey and is not a measure of proficiency. English learner designation, reported separately on RAA-14B, is based on assessment.

PART IV. SUPPRESSION APPLIED TO THIS FORM

IV.1  No cell in Parts I–II falls below the suppression threshold of 10.
IV.2  Where a completeness rate would identify fewer than 10 records as missing, the rate is published as ">99.9" rather than as a count.
IV.3  Suppression rules are those stated in Form RAA-14, Section H.
