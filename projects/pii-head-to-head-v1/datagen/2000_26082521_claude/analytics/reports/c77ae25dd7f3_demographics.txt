Return for the Twelve Months Ended 31 March 1997
Compiled and lodged by the Contractor in accordance with Clause 22 (Information Covenants)

A. INTERPRETATION
   1. In this Schedule, "served population" bears the meaning ascribed to it at Clause 1.1 of the Agreement, namely the aggregate of all delivery points activated at any time within the return period, being 27,415 in number.
   2. "Preferred language" denotes the language recorded at the point of service activation as that in which the household elects to receive written notifications, and no inference as to competence in any other tongue is to be drawn therefrom.
   3. "Income bracket" denotes equivalised net weekly household income as declared at activation and is not verified by the Contractor.

D. SMALL-CELL SUPPRESSION
   1. The Contractor has applied a minimum cell threshold of five throughout.
      (i) Any language recorded for fewer than five households has been aggregated into the line at Paragraph B.7 above.
      (ii) Any income bracket falling below the threshold has been merged with the bracket immediately adjoining it, and the resulting composite band is shown as published.
   2. Where primary suppression alone would leave a suppressed value recoverable by arithmetic from the published totals, the Contractor has additionally suppressed one or more further cells for that purpose.
   3. Nothing in this Schedule is to be construed as authorising the Authority to request, and the Contractor shall not supply, any tabulation at a level of disaggregation finer than that presented herein.

E. RESERVATION
   1. The Contractor reserves the right to restate any figure in this Schedule upon the correction of an aggregation error, notice of which shall be given to the Authority within twenty-eight days of its discovery by the Records Custodian.
