ANNUAL DEMOGRAPHIC STATISTICAL REPORT
Title Insurance and Escrow Services Division
Reporting Period: 1 January 2003 – 31 December 2003
Issued: 12 March 2004
Prepared by: the Records Custodian
Reviewed by: the Compliance Officer
Report Reference: DEM-2003-A

1. SCOPE
1.1 This report tabulates aggregate demographic characteristics of transacting parties recorded on residential escrow and settlement files closed by the Division during the reporting period.
1.2 The reporting base is the closed-file count, N = 14,207. Each file contributes one record, taken from the primary party position.
1.3 Cancelled files, re-opened files, and commercial transactions are excluded from the base.

2. SOURCE FIELDS
2.1 Values are extracted from the following intake fields: Date of Birth; Sex; Ethnicity Code; Preferred Language of Correspondence; Household Income Bracket; Property Postal Code.
2.2 Age is derived from the Date of Birth field as of the closing date and is released in banded form only.
2.3 Geography is truncated to the first three digits of the Property Postal Code field prior to tabulation.

9. SUPPRESSION AND ROUNDING
9.1 Any cell with a frequency below 11 is suppressed and folded into the adjoining "All other" or "All other districts" category. Where a single suppression would permit recovery of the suppressed value by subtraction from a published total, a complementary cell in the same row is suppressed in the same manner.
9.2 Percentages are computed against the unrounded base and rounded to one decimal place. Columns may therefore differ from 100.0 by up to 0.2.
9.3 No published cell in this report rests on fewer than 11 records.

10. RETENTION
10.1 Working tabulations are retained five years from issue. Underlying intake records remain governed by the Division retention schedule.
