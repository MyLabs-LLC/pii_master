Paragraph 1 — Preliminary

Paragraph 4 — Suppression of Small Cells
    – A threshold of five has been observed. No published cell falls beneath it.
    – Two categories which would otherwise have been separately enumerated at Paragraph 3 have accordingly been folded into the residual line "Other, or not stated", and the Contractor declines to identify which, such identification being itself capable of defeating the suppression.
    – Cross-tabulation of sex against ethnic group is withheld in its entirety, the Contractor having determined upon inspection that a material proportion of the resulting cells would fall beneath the threshold and that secondary suppression sufficient to protect them would render the remaining table of no analytical utility.

Paragraph 5 — Custody
    – The underlying records from which these aggregates are drawn remain in the custody of the Records Custodian and are retained for the period stipulated by the Instrument of Award, after which they are to be destroyed by the means therein specified.
