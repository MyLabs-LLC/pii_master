1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
1.1 This return tabulates the demographic composition of the population for which the Billing Office opened, adjudicated or closed a guarantor account during the reporting period.
1.2 The denominator is the count of distinct served individuals (n = 48,312) unless a clause states otherwise.
1.3 All figures in this return are aggregate counts and derived percentages. No record-level extract accompanies this return.

8. SMALL-CELL SUPPRESSION
8.1 Any cell with a count of fewer than eleven is suppressed and shown as [S].
8.2 Where a single suppressed cell would be recoverable by subtraction from a published total, the next smallest cell in that table is also suppressed as a complementary measure.
8.3 Percentages for suppressed cells are withheld. Column totals are published as originally computed and therefore exceed the sum of published rows.
8.4 Cross-tabulation of any two or more breakdowns in this return is not authorised at this level of aggregation.
