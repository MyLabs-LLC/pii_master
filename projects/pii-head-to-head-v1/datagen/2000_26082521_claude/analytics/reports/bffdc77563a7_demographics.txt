1. PURPOSE AND AUTHORITY

1.1 This return is made under Schedule 9, paragraph 4, of the contract, which requires the Contractor to submit an annual profile of the population served.

1.2 The return reports aggregate counts and percentages only. No case-level extract accompanies it.

2. PERIOD AND BASIS OF COUNT

2.1 The reporting period runs from 1 April 2003 to 31 March 2004 inclusive.

2.2 A person is counted once if a delivery was completed to that person's household at any point in the period. Households are counted at their delivery point of record on the final day of the period.

2.3 Served population: 38,640 individuals in 16,940 households.

9. DATA QUALITY

9.1 Ethnicity, language and income are self-declared at the point of registration and are not verified.

9.2 Non-response is carried in an explicit "not stated" category rather than imputed. Denominators therefore include non-respondents throughout.

10. SMALL-CELL SUPPRESSION

10.1 No cell derived from fewer than ten individuals or five households is published. Suppressed cells appear as "n/p" and the associated percentage is omitted.

10.2 Secondary suppression is applied so that a suppressed cell cannot be reconstructed from a published total. Where secondary suppression would remove more than half of a row, the row is collapsed to a broader category instead.

10.3 Geography is not reported below postal-district level under any circumstances, and no cross-tabulation of geography with ethnicity, language or income is released.

11. RETENTION OF WORKING FILES

11.1 The tabulation source files are held by the Records Custodian for six years from the close of the reporting period, after which they are destroyed under the divisional disposal schedule.
