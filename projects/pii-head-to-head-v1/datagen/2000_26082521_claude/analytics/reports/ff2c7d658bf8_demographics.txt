GEOGRAPHIC DISTRIBUTION OF SERVED POPULATION
Series DEM-2003-C
Title and Escrow Group, Statistical Unit
Issued 16 April 2004

§1. Basis. Closed residential escrow files, calendar year 2003: 14,207. Geography is assigned from the Property Postal Code field, truncated to three digits and then aggregated to the reporting regions defined at §2. Files with an unresolved or out-of-state Property Postal Code are carried in Region IV.

§2. Region definitions.
    Region I   — postal districts 900, 902, 906
    Region II  — postal districts 913, 917
    Region III — postal districts 926, 928
    Region IV  — all remaining districts, aggregated

§3. Distribution by region.

    Region        Closed files    Percent of base
    Region I             3,945               27.8
    Region II            3,757               26.4
    Region III           4,664               32.8
    Region IV            1,841               13.0
    Total               14,207              100.0

§4. Language concentration. Counts below record files on which the Preferred Language of Correspondence field held a value other than English. The percentage is taken against the region base, not the reporting base.

    Region        Non-English    Percent of region
    Region I              512                 13.0
    Region II             691                 18.4
    Region III            481                 10.3
    Region IV             179                  9.7
    Total               1,863                 13.1

§5. Suppression. Individual postal districts with fewer than 250 closed files are not published separately and are carried within Region IV; nine districts were absorbed on this basis in the reporting period. Within the published regions, no cell falls below the standard threshold of 11. Percentages are rounded to one decimal place.

§6. Cross-reference. Region totals at §3 reconcile to the three-digit district table at clause 8 of DEM-2003-A. The non-English total at §4 reconciles to the residual of clause 6 of that report. Any variance should be reported to the Records Custodian before the figures are relied upon for branch staffing or translation-service planning.
