9. SUPPRESSION. Any cell with a count fewer than six is suppressed and denoted by the symbol (S). Where suppression of a single cell within a dimension would permit its recovery by subtraction from a published marginal total, the next-smallest cell in that dimension is likewise suppressed. No cell in Clauses 3 through 8 met the suppression criterion in this period.

10. CROSS-TABULATION. Requests for tabulation of two or more dimensions in combination are subject to the same threshold applied at the level of the joint cell, and are directed to the Bureau Statistician.

11. ROUNDING. Percentages are computed to two decimal places and presented to one. Column sums may depart from 100.0 by up to 0.2 for this reason.

12. NON-RESPONSE. Ethnicity, language, and income items are voluntary. Non-response is reported as a discrete category and is not imputed.

13. CUSTODY. The source extract and the derivation worksheets are held by the Records Custodian under retention schedule H-4 (seven years).
