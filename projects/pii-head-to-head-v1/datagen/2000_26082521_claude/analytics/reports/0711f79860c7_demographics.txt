BOROUGH WATER AND SEWER AUTHORITY
OFFICE OF RATES AND STATISTICS
Report RS-97/3 — Demographic Profile of the Served Population
Quarter ended 30 September 1997

1. The Authority reports for the quarter under review a served resident population of 212,470 persons occupying 84,310 metered premises within the five postal districts comprising the service territory. The figures are compiled from the quarterly meter census, reconciled against the 1990 decennial enumeration and the housing-unit estimates of the Regional Planning Commission, and are stated in aggregate only.

2. By AGE BAND the population divides as follows: Under 18, 56,090 (26.4 per cent); 18–34, 49,080 (23.1 per cent); 35–54, 59,070 (27.8 per cent); 55–64, 19,760 (9.3 per cent); and 65 and over, 28,470 (13.4 per cent). By SEX the division is Female, 109,640 (51.6 per cent) and Male, 102,830 (48.4 per cent).

3. By ETHNICITY the proportions recorded are White, 61.2 per cent; Black, 22.7 per cent; Asian or Pacific Islander, 6.4 per cent; American Indian or Alaska Native, 0.5 per cent; and Other or two or more categories, 9.2 per cent. Persons of Hispanic origin, being a category of origin rather than of race, are enumerated separately at 17.9 per cent of the whole.

4. By HOUSEHOLD LANGUAGE the returns show English, 74.8 per cent; Spanish, 15.1 per cent; Portuguese, 3.6 per cent; Chinese, 2.2 per cent; and All other languages, 4.3 per cent. By HOUSEHOLD INCOME BRACKET the distribution is: under $15,000, 19.6 per cent; $15,000–$24,999, 17.2 per cent; $25,000–$34,999, 18.4 per cent; $35,000–$49,999, 20.1 per cent; $50,000–$74,999, 15.8 per cent; and $75,000 and over, 8.9 per cent.

5. By POSTAL DISTRICT the served population is apportioned: District 4, 22.1 per cent; District 7, 26.8 per cent; District 9, 18.3 per cent; District 11, 19.4 per cent; and District 14, 13.4 per cent.

6. Any cell whose count falls below ten persons has been suppressed and appears as a dash. Where a single suppression would permit derivation by subtraction, complementary suppression has been applied to the next smallest cell in the same row. Percentages are computed upon unsuppressed totals and may not sum to one hundred by reason of rounding.
