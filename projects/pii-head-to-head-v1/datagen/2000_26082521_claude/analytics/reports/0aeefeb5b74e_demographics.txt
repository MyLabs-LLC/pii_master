DEMOGRAPHIC COMPOSITION OF THE SERVED POPULATION
Retail Pharmacy Chain — Consolidated Statistical Report
Period covered: 01 January 2011 to 31 December 2011
Prepared by: the Records Custodian
Reviewed by: the Chair of the Data Governance Committee
Classification: Internal — Management Use

Clause 1.  Purpose and standing
    (a)  This report satisfies the annual reporting obligation of the Records
         Custodian to the Data Governance Committee.
    (b)  It describes the composition of the population served across the trading
         estate for the period stated above.
    (c)  It is an aggregate statistical product. Every figure below is a count of
         individuals or a percentage of a stated denominator.

Clause 2.  Basis of enumeration
    (a)  Denominator: 2,318,940 distinct individuals served.
    (b)  Contributing sites: 2,417 retail pharmacy locations.
    (c)  Fields drawn upon:
         (i)    Age band
         (ii)   Sex
         (iii)  Race and ethnicity
         (iv)   Preferred language
         (v)    Household income band (area-derived)
         (vi)   Geography of residence, three-digit postal prefix

Clause 9.  Small-cell suppression
    (a)  Primary rule. A cell with a count of fewer than 11 is suppressed and
         printed as "—". The rule applies to every table in this report and to
         any derivative extract produced from it.
    (b)  Secondary rule. Where a single suppressed cell in a row or column could
         be recovered by subtraction from a published margin, the next smallest
         cell in that row or column is suppressed as well.
    (c)  Percentage rule. A percentage is withheld wherever its numerator has
         been suppressed. Percentages are computed against the full denominator
         at Clause 2(a) and rounded to one decimal place.
    (d)  Geographic floor. No output below three-digit postal prefix is prepared,
         held or released.
    (e)  Any request to lower a threshold in this clause requires the written
         authority of the Data Governance Committee, recorded in its minutes.
