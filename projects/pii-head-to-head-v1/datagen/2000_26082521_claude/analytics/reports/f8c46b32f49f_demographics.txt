ANNUAL DEMOGRAPHIC RETURN 2015/16
Compiled by the Data and Reporting Officer; reviewed by the Compliance Officer

1. Scope and method

The return covers 7,204 individuals who were registered and available for work at any point in the twelve months to 31 March 2016. Each individual is counted once. Where a field was left blank at registration and not completed at review, the record is assigned to the relevant Not Stated category rather than excluded, so that the denominators are consistent across all tables.

2. Age Band and Sex

The age distribution is: 16 to 24, 1,656 individuals or 23.0 per cent; 25 to 34, 2,241 or 31.1 per cent; 35 to 44, 1,510 or 21.0 per cent; 45 to 54, 1,141 or 15.8 per cent; 55 to 64, 578 or 8.0 per cent; and 65 and over, 78 or 1.1 per cent. On Sex, 4,109 individuals or 57.0 per cent are recorded as male and 3,095 or 43.0 per cent as female.

3. Ethnicity and First Language

Ethnicity is reported against the six standard headings used in the previous return, with a Not Stated category retained. Rather than publish the full table, which contained two headings below the threshold, the two smallest headings have been combined into Other and Not Stated, which together account for 213 individuals or 3.0 per cent. On First Language, 5,742 individuals or 79.7 per cent record English, and 1,462 or 20.3 per cent record another language. The detailed language breakdown is held on file but is not published in this return.

4. Gross Weekly Earnings Band

Earnings are averaged across weeks worked. The distribution is: under £200, 981 individuals or 13.6 per cent; £200 to £299, 2,164 or 30.0 per cent; £300 to £399, 2,238 or 31.1 per cent; £400 to £499, 1,183 or 16.4 per cent; and £500 and above, 638 or 8.9 per cent.

5. Geography

Geography is published at a coarser level than in the quarterly returns because several districts would otherwise fall below the threshold across the smaller fields. Individuals resident in inner-city districts number 3,388 or 47.0 per cent; those in outer districts number 2,507 or 34.8 per cent; and those resident outside the travel-to-work area number 1,309 or 18.2 per cent.

6. Suppression

The threshold for this return is ten. Any cell falling below it has been withheld or merged with an adjacent category, and complementary suppression has been applied where a withheld cell could be derived from published row or column totals. Percentages are calculated on published denominators and may not sum to 100 owing to rounding and suppression.
