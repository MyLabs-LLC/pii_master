MEMORANDUM

TO:      Board of Commissioners, Rate and Equity Subcommittee
FROM:    Office of Statistical Services
THROUGH: The Records Custodian
DATE:    14 March 2011
SUBJECT: Service-Population Demographic Tabulation, Calendar Year 2010

1. PURPOSE. This memorandum transmits the annual demographic tabulation of the population resident within the Authority's water and sewer service territory, prepared in support of the affordability review scheduled for the second quarter.

2. BASIS OF COMPILATION.
   2.1 Resident counts are derived from the decennial enumeration apportioned to service-territory boundaries by the Planning Section.
   2.2 Household counts are derived from the active residential billing file as of 31 December 2010, deduplicated by premise.
   2.3 Income and language characteristics are modelled from the five-year community survey and are reported at household level only.

9. SUPPRESSION. Cells with a denominator below eleven households are withheld and folded into the adjacent "other or not stated" line. Complementary suppression has been applied to the next-smallest cell in any row or column where a single withheld cell would otherwise be recoverable by subtraction. Percentages are computed on unsuppressed totals and may not sum exactly owing to rounding.
