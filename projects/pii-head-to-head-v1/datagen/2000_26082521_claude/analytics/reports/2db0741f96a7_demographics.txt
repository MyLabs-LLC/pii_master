MUNICIPAL WATER AND LIGHT DEPARTMENT
INTERNAL MEMORANDUM — CUSTOMER SERVICE DIVISION

DATE:    22 September 2004
TO:      the Supervisor of Billing Operations
FROM:    the Manager of Planning and Analysis
SUBJECT: Preferred Language of Correspondence — Account Base, Q3 2004
FILE:    PA-04-218a

Tabulation of active residential and small commercial accounts by the
preferred-language field recorded at enrollment or last account update.
Base: 96,240 active accounts as of 31 August 2004.

  English ......................... 79,382 ....... 82.5%
  Spanish ......................... 11,741 ....... 12.2%
  Vietnamese ....................... 1,829 ........ 1.9%
  Chinese (Cantonese or Mandarin) .. 1,251 ........ 1.3%
  Russian ............................ 674 ........ 0.7%
  Tagalog ............................ 481 ........ 0.5%
  Somali ............................. 289 ........ 0.3%
  All other or not stated ............ 593 ........ 0.6%
                                     ------      -------
  TOTAL ........................... 96,240 ...... 100.0%

Counts are aggregate at territory level. Language groups producing fewer
than eleven accounts in any postal district are collapsed into "All other
or not stated" for district-level reporting; no district breakout of this
table is published.

Bill inserts and disconnection notices are currently produced in English
and Spanish only. The above indicates a further 5.3% of the account base
with a stated preference in a third language. Recommend the Division cost
a translated shutoff-notice pilot for the top three non-English groups for
inclusion in the FY 2005-06 budget request.
