3. SUPPRESSION OF SMALL CELLS
   3.1 Where any cell in the foregoing tabulations would otherwise disclose a count of fewer than five, that cell has been suppressed and its contents subsumed within the adjacent residual category designated "Other" or "Remainder of contract area", as the case may be.
   3.2 Secondary suppression has been applied to such further cells as would, by subtraction from a published row or column total, permit the reconstitution of a primarily suppressed cell.
   3.3 No cross-tabulation of two or more of the characteristics enumerated at Paragraph 2 is furnished herewith, the same having been withheld on the ground that the resulting cell counts would in numerous instances fall below the threshold specified at Paragraph 3.1.
