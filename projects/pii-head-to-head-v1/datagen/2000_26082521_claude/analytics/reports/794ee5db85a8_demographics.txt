- Handling of low counts
  - Any cell falling below five is withheld from publication.
  - The withheld count is absorbed into the "not stated" or "all other" line of the
    same breakdown so that section totals continue to reconcile to N.
  - Where a section contains a single withheld cell, a second cell is withheld to
    prevent recovery by subtraction.
  - Breakdowns are published one dimension at a time. Combined breakdowns, for
    example ethnic group by district, are not issued for this population at this
    volume.

- Reconciliation
  - Each section totals to N before rounding.
  - Percentages are rounded to one decimal place and may not sum to 100.0.
  - Queries on figures are directed to the Operations Reporting Officer; queries on
    the underlying register are directed to the Records Custodian.
