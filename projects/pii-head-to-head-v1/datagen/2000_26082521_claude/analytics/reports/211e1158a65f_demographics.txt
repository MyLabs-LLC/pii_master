MARWOOD COUNTY DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC HEALTH
OFFICE OF EPIDEMIOLOGY AND VITAL STATISTICS
ANNUAL ENUMERATION OF THE SERVED POPULATION, FISCAL YEAR 1996–1997
Report No. EPI-97-114; Received and Ordered Filed by the Board of Health, September 1997

ARTICLE I. AUTHORITY AND PURPOSE. Pursuant to the standing directive of the Board of Health and the reporting obligations imposed upon local health jurisdictions under the Public Health Code, Chapter 141, Subchapter C, the Department is required annually to compile, and to transmit to the Board and to the State Registrar, a tabulation of the demographic composition of the population served by its clinical, categorical, and environmental programs during the preceding fiscal period. This report is prepared and submitted in satisfaction of that obligation. Nothing contained herein shall be construed to enlarge, diminish, or otherwise modify any obligation of confidentiality imposed upon the Department by law.

ARTICLE II. METHOD OF COMPILATION. The enumeration herein reported was derived by the Office of Epidemiology from encounter records maintained in the ordinary course of Departmental business, which records were extracted, deduplicated, and aggregated by the Records Custodian, acting under the supervision of the Health Officer, prior to any analytic use. The unit of enumeration is the unduplicated individual served, and not the encounter, the visit, or the episode of care; accordingly, the totals reported in Article III below will not reconcile to the utilization totals reported in the companion service-volume report. The fields carried forward into aggregation, and no others, were: Age Band at First Service in Period; Sex; Race and Ethnicity; Primary Language Spoken in the Home; Annual Household Income as a Percentage of the Federal Poverty Guidelines; and Postal District of Residence, expressed at the three-digit sectional level. All direct and indirect identifying particulars were stripped in the extraction step and were not made available to the analytic staff.

ARTICLE III. FINDINGS. The unduplicated served population for the period was one hundred eighteen thousand four hundred seventy-two (118,472). Distribution by Age Band was as follows: under five years, 9,241 (7.8 percent); five through seventeen years, 22,318 (18.8 percent); eighteen through thirty-four years, 30,402 (25.7 percent); thirty-five through fifty-four years, 28,776 (24.3 percent); fifty-five through sixty-four years, 11,905 (10.0 percent); and sixty-five years and over, 15,830 (13.4 percent). Distribution by Sex was female, 64,109 (54.1 percent), and male, 54,363 (45.9 percent). Distribution by Race and Ethnicity, the categories being those prescribed by Statistical Policy Directive No. 15, was: White, not of Hispanic origin, 71,846 (60.6 percent); Black, not of Hispanic origin, 24,190 (20.4 percent); Hispanic origin, 15,377 (13.0 percent); Asian or Pacific Islander, 5,208 (4.4 percent); American Indian or Alaskan Native, 971 (0.8 percent); and Not Ascertained, 880 (0.7 percent). Distribution by Annual Household Income was: below 100 percent of the Federal Poverty Guidelines, 41,556 (35.1 percent); 100 through 184 percent, 33,190 (28.0 percent); 185 through 249 percent, 19,832 (16.7 percent); 250 percent and above, 14,304 (12.1 percent); and Declined to State or Not Ascertained, 9,590 (8.1 percent).

ARTICLE IV. SUPPRESSION. In every table constituting a part of this report, any cell the value of which is greater than zero but fewer than five has been suppressed and denoted by the symbol ‡; and where a single suppression within a row or column would permit the suppressed value to be recovered by subtraction, the next-smallest cell in that row or column has likewise been suppressed. Suppressed cells remain included in all marginal totals. Percentages are computed upon unsuppressed denominators and may not sum to one hundred by reason of rounding.
