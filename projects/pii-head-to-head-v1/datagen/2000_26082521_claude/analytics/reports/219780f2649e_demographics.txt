PENNINE HAULAGE & DISTRIBUTION (CONTRACTS) LIMITED
Annual Demographic Statement
Welfare Food Scheme Distribution Contract WFS/04
Reporting year 1 April 2003 to 31 March 2004

1     PURPOSE AND BASIS
1.1   This Statement discharges clause 14 of the Contract, which requires an annual profile of the population served.
      1.1.1  Base population: 22,415 beneficiaries recorded as served on at least one occasion in the reporting year.
      1.1.2  Each table is compiled from the following fields of the referral record: Referral Number; Surname; Forename; Date of Birth; Sex; Ethnic Group; Main Language of Household; Gross Weekly Household Income; Postal District; Depot Catchment.
      1.1.3  Only derived aggregates are carried into this Statement. Age band is calculated at the date of first delivery in the year.

3     DISCLOSURE CONTROL
3.1   The suppression threshold for this Statement is a cell count of five.
      3.1.1  Cells at or below the threshold are withheld and denoted "‡".
      3.1.2  Where a primary suppression occurs, a secondary suppression is applied to the smallest remaining cell in the same table so that the withheld figure cannot be derived from the published total.
      3.1.3  Suppressed cells remain within their table total; totals are not adjusted.
3.2   Categories with persistently small counts are combined into "Other" rather than published separately.
