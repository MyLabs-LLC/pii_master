SECTION 8 — DISCLOSURE CONTROL
8.1 Cells with a value of fewer than ten are suppressed and shown as an asterisk. Where a single suppressed cell would be recoverable by subtraction from a published total, a second cell in the same row or column is suppressed as a complement.
8.2 Percentages are computed on unsuppressed totals and rounded to one decimal place; columns may not sum to exactly 100.0.
8.3 Geography is not published below the three-digit postal district. Requests for finer geography are referred to the Records Custodian for review under the institutional disclosure policy.
8.4 Cross-tabulations beyond two dimensions are not released from this schedule.
