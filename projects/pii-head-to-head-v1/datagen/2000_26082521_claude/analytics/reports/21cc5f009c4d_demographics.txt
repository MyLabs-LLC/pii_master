ANNUAL DEMOGRAPHIC RETURN — CALENDAR YEAR 1996
Compiled by the Office of the Records Custodian
File Reference: ADR/96

CLAUSE 1 — COVERAGE
   (a) The return covers all title and escrow orders closed by the Company in its three operating regions during calendar year 1996.
   (b) Counted parties: 41,655. Orders closed: 26,104.
   (c) Where an order records more than one party, each party is counted once. Parties appearing on more than one order in the year are counted once per order.

CLAUSE 2 — INSTRUMENT
   (a) Demographic items are gathered on Form 41-D, the voluntary supplement issued with the order confirmation.
   (b) Items are self-designated by the responding party. Branch staff do not observe, infer, or correct entries.
   (c) Unreturned or partially completed forms produce a "not stated" entry for the affected item only.

CLAUSE 4 — LEVEL OF DETAIL
   (a) Geography is reported at metropolitan division. Three-digit postal prefix tabulations are prepared for internal underwriting use and are not carried in this return.
   (b) Cross-tabulations of two or more demographic items are not published. Only the marginal distributions at Clause 3 are released.

CLAUSE 5 — SMALL-CELL SUPPRESSION
   (a) The publication threshold is a cell count of six. Cells below the threshold are withheld and marked with an em dash.
   (b) Where withholding a single cell would allow its recovery by subtraction from a published total, the next smallest cell in the same row or column is withheld as well.
   (c) Categories that fall below the threshold in every region are collapsed into the "other" or "all other" line for the item, and the collapse is recorded in the working papers.
   (d) Withheld counts remain in all stated totals and bases.

CLAUSE 6 — ROUNDING AND COMPARABILITY
   (a) Percentages are computed on the 41,655 counted-party base and rounded to one decimal place; columns may not sum to exactly 100.0.
   (b) The income brackets at 3.5 were revised at the start of 1996. Comparison with returns for 1994 and 1995 is limited to the three lowest brackets.

CLAUSE 7 — CUSTODY
   (a) Working papers, including branch-level worksheets, are held by the Records Custodian for seven years from the date of this return.
   (b) Requests for tabulations at a finer level than published are referred to the Records Custodian and require the written approval of the Compliance Officer.
