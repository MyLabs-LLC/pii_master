Percentages are rounded to one decimal place and may not sum to 100.0.

Suppression. Any cell containing fewer than eleven members is withheld and shown as a hyphen. Where a single suppressed cell within a row would be recoverable by subtraction from the row total, the next-smallest cell in that row is also suppressed. Suppression is applied before the table leaves this unit; suppressed values are not restored for downstream reporting.

Fields carried in the source extract but not tabulated here — ethnicity, written and spoken language preference, household income band, and postal district of residence — appear in the companion reports of this series. Requests for cross-tabulations beyond two dimensions should be routed through the Records Custodian, who will assess disclosure risk before release. No member-level extract is produced in support of these tabulations.

Questions on method should be directed to this unit. Corrections received after April 15, 2004 will be carried into the plan year 2004 series rather than restated here.
