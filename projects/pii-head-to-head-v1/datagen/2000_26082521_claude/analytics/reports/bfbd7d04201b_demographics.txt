1. SCOPE

1.1  This report tabulates the population of parties for whom the Company acted as settlement agent, title insurer, or escrow holder during the reporting period. Universe N = 24,600.

1.2  Source records are the settlement intake form, the government monitoring information section of the loan application where one exists, and the escrow instructions. Where an attribute was collected more than once for the same file, the earliest value controls.

1.3  Attributes are reported one at a time. No cross-tabulation is included in this issue.

3. SUPPRESSION RULE

3.1  Threshold is eleven. A cell with a count of one through ten is marked "S" and its value withheld, together with the corresponding percentage.

3.2  Complementary suppression is applied where a lone suppressed cell could be derived from the published total and the remaining published cells. In such a case the smallest remaining cell is also marked "S".

3.3  Suppressed counts are retained in the universe total. Published categories in a table therefore may not sum to N.

3.4  The three-digit postal district is the finest geography published. Requests for finer geography, or for any cross-tabulation, are denied at the Unit level and may be appealed in writing to the Chief Compliance Officer.

4. ROUNDING

Percentages are computed on unrounded counts and rounded to one decimal place. Totals shown as 100.0 may reflect rounding.
