NORTHSTAR DRUG STORES, INCORPORATED
BOARD SUBMISSION — SERVED POPULATION SUMMARY, THIRD QUARTER 1999
SUBMITTED BY THE PROFESSIONAL SERVICES DIVISION

PART I — SUMMARY

1. The served population stood at 1,331,470 at quarter end, distributed across 148 postal districts and five regions. Growth of 3.6 per cent over the year is attributable in the main to the Gulf Coast region, which now carries 18.2 per cent of the total against 15.9 per cent twelve months prior.

2. The composition of the population by principal band is set out below.

    AGE_BAND          COUNT        PER CENT
    Under 18          219,692        16.5
    18 to 34          274,283        20.6
    35 to 49          300,912        22.6
    50 to 64          267,625        20.1
    65 to 79          193,063        14.5
    80 and over        75,895         5.7

    TOTAL           1,331,470       100.0

PART II — OBSERVATIONS

3. The proportion of the population in the two senior bands has risen by four-tenths of a point over four quarters. Store Planning is asked to weigh this against the compounding growth in the Gulf Coast region, whose age profile is materially younger than the chain average.

4. LANG_PREF other than English now stands at 14.9 per cent chain-wide against 13.6 per cent in the corresponding quarter. Eleven districts exceed 30 per cent.

5. INC_BAND remains an imputed neighbourhood measure and is presented at region level only in this submission. The Division declines to present it against ETHNIC_GRP at any geography finer than region.

PART III — SUPPRESSION

6. Cells below a count of 25 are suppressed and printed as a hyphen, with complementary suppression applied within the affected rows and columns to prevent recovery by subtraction. Percentages are withheld on bases below 100. Two cells in the region by language table are suppressed on this occasion.

7. The detailed tabulations supporting this submission are held by the Statistical Reporting Unit and may be consulted on application to the Records Custodian.
