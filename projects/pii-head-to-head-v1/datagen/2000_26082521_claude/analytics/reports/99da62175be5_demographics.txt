Period: calendar year 2016
Distribution: divisional management, pharmacy operations, compliance

A. PREPARATION
    1. The extract is drawn from the dispensing ledger on the fifth business
       day after period close.
    2. Direct identifiers are removed in the extract step. The aggregation
       layer holds category codes and counts only.
    3. Counts are of distinct persons served, not of prescriptions filled.
    4. Divisional denominator: 233,700.

H. SMALL-CELL SUPPRESSION
    1. A count below 11 is suppressed and printed as "S".
    2. The derived percentage for a suppressed count is suppressed with it.
    3. Where one suppressed cell in a row or column could be recovered by
       subtracting the published cells from the marginal, the next smallest
       cell in that row or column is also suppressed.
    4. Suppressed counts stay in all marginals and in the denominator at A.4.
       Published categories therefore sum to at most the denominator, and
       percentage columns may total less than 100 per cent.
    5. The suppression pass runs on every table before release. No table
       leaves the aggregation layer without it.

I. RETENTION AND REVIEW
    1. The compiled tables are retained for seven years.
    2. The suppression log is retained with the tables and is available to
       compliance on request.
    3. Corrections to a released table are issued as a replacement table
       carrying the same period label and a revision number.
