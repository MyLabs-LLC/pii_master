OFFICE OF THE UNIVERSITY REGISTRAR
INTERIM CERTIFICATION OF AGGREGATE COUNTS
Graduate and Professional Division — Fall 2011 Census
Ref. REG-IR-2011-04G | Issued by the Institutional Research Analyst

• 1. Certified headcount for the division on the census date: 3,914. All figures below are aggregates drawn from the frozen census extract; detailed frequency tables are held as Appendix G to this reference.

• 2. Age band. The 25–34 band predominates at 48.1 per cent, followed by 21–24 at 22.6 per cent and 35–49 at 21.3 per cent. The 50-and-over band accounts for 7.1 per cent and the under-21 band for 0.9 per cent.

• 3. Sex. Female, 2,142 (54.7 per cent); male, 1,761 (45.0 per cent); not reported, 11 (0.3 per cent).

• 4. Race and ethnicity. White, 2,461 (62.9 per cent); nonresident alien, 588 (15.0 per cent); Black or African American, 251 (6.4 per cent); Asian, 244 (6.2 per cent); Hispanic or Latino, 238 (6.1 per cent); two or more races, 82 (2.1 per cent); American Indian or Alaska Native, 21 (0.5 per cent). The Native Hawaiian or Other Pacific Islander cell and the unknown cell are withheld under clause 8 below.

• 5. Primary language of the household. English, 87.4 per cent; Chinese, all varieties, 3.9 per cent; Spanish, 2.6 per cent; Hindi, 1.2 per cent; all other languages combined, 3.6 per cent; not reported, 1.3 per cent.

• 6. Household income bracket. Reported on 1,908 records bearing a completed federal aid application. Under $20,000, 22.4 per cent; $20,000–$39,999, 18.1 per cent; $40,000–$59,999, 16.9 per cent; $60,000–$79,999, 14.2 per cent; $80,000–$99,999, 11.8 per cent; $100,000 and over, 16.6 per cent.

• 7. Geography, by three-position postal district. District 537, 31.2 per cent; district 535, 9.8 per cent; all other in-state districts, 24.1 per cent; out-of-state domestic, 19.9 per cent; international, 15.0 per cent.

• 8. Suppression. Cells below a frequency of ten are withheld and shown as —S—; a complementary cell is withheld in the same dimension wherever the withheld figure would otherwise be recoverable from the published marginal total.
