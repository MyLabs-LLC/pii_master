RESEARCH AND COMPLIANCE

FIELD OF MEMBERSHIP ANALYSIS: GEOGRAPHY BY INCOME
Twelve months ended 31 December 1998
Report RC-98-11

1. SCOPE

   Primary members aged 18 and over, resident within or adjacent
   to the chartered service area, holding an open share account
   on the reporting date. Joint members, minors and estate
   accounts are out of scope. Geography is assigned from the
   three-digit postal district of the residence field on the
   master share file; the remaining digits are discarded at
   extraction and are not carried forward.

3. SUPPRESSION

   The publication threshold is ten. Cells falling below the
   threshold are shown as "-" and the underlying counts are held
   only in the working extract. Two cells in the "Other" row are
   below threshold; their combined value is 9 and neither is
   individually recoverable. Column totals are not published for
   this table, since publishing them alongside a fully populated
   column would defeat the suppression of the affected cells. The
   row total and the grand total are published.

4. DERIVATION

   Income band is taken from the gross annual household income
   field as declared at application or at the most recent credit
   review, whichever is later. The field is not verified against
   payroll or tax records and is not updated on a fixed cycle;
   approximately one member in nine carries a declaration more
   than four years old. Band boundaries follow the ranges used in
   the 1994 analysis and are unchanged, so the series remains
   comparable year on year.

   Percentages have not been computed at cell level for this
   table on account of the suppressed cells. Row percentages are
   available on request from the Records Custodian in aggregate
   form only.
