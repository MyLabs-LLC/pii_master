NOTE FOR FILE — MONTHLY DEMOGRAPHIC SUMMARY, AUGUST 2016

The Records Custodian has completed the monthly summary of the served population for the branch. In the month, 1,203 individuals held at least one live assignment. Counts and percentages have been produced for the standard fields: Age Band, Sex, Ethnicity, First Language, Gross Weekly Earnings Band and Postal District. Headline figures are 654 workers or 54.4 per cent recorded as male and 549 or 45.6 per cent as female, with the age profile concentrated in the 25 to 34 and 35 to 44 bands, which together account for just over half the total.

Two rows in the Ethnicity table and four rows in the First Language table returned counts below five and have been suppressed, with the affected records rolled into the relevant Other category rather than published separately. Postal District is reported at district level only. The summary is aggregate throughout and contains no individual-level output. It has been filed in the branch reporting folder and copied to the Regional Operations Board in the usual way.
