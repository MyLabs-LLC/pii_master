» SUPPRESSION
   1. Cells of fewer than eleven covered lives are withheld and printed as
      an asterisk. This applies to the cross-tabulations first, since that
      is where the thin cells fall.
   2. Where a row or column carries exactly one suppressed cell, suppress
      the next smallest cell on that line so the withheld figure cannot be
      recovered by subtraction.
   3. Totals are published as computed and include suppressed cells.
   4. Percentages are not published on any base under 25 covered lives. The
      base is printed in its place.
   5. The checker initials the suppression pass separately from the
      tabulation pass.
