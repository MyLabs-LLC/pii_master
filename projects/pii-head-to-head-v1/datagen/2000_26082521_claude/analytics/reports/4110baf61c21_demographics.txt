SILVER CREEK CASUALTY & HEALTH
Actuarial Services — Population Characteristics Report
Twelve months ended 31 December 2016

(1) The report describes the characteristics of members covered under all lines of health business during the reporting period. Membership at year end stood at 61,340 lives.

(2) By age band, the population divided as follows: under 18, 13,556 lives or 22.1 per cent; 18 to 34, 11,900 lives or 19.4 per cent; 35 to 49, 14,599 lives or 23.8 per cent; 50 to 64, 14,047 lives or 22.9 per cent; and 65 and over, 7,238 lives or 11.8 per cent.

(3) By sex, the population comprised 31,897 female members (52.0 per cent), 29,259 male members (47.7 per cent) and 184 members whose sex was not stated (0.3 per cent).

(4) By ethnicity, as self-reported at enrolment: White, 43,674 (71.2 per cent); Black or African American, 6,993 (11.4 per cent); Hispanic or Latino, 5,459 (8.9 per cent); Asian, 2,208 (3.6 per cent); two or more races or other, 1,656 (2.7 per cent); not stated, 1,350 (2.2 per cent).

(5) By preferred language for member communications: English, 56,003 (91.3 per cent); Spanish, 3,190 (5.2 per cent); Somali, 675 (1.1 per cent); Arabic, 491 (0.8 per cent); all other languages, 736 (1.2 per cent); not stated, 245 (0.4 per cent).

(6) By annual household income bracket: under $25,000, 11,409 (18.6 per cent); $25,000 to $49,999, 16,746 (27.3 per cent); $50,000 to $74,999, 14,783 (24.1 per cent); $75,000 to $99,999, 8,710 (14.2 per cent); $100,000 and over, 5,337 (8.7 per cent); not stated, 4,355 (7.1 per cent).

(7) By geography, taken at the three-digit postal prefix of the residence on file: 553, 19,322 (31.5 per cent); 554, 15,212 (24.8 per cent); 550, 11,164 (18.2 per cent); 551, 8,526 (13.9 per cent); all other prefixes combined, 7,116 (11.6 per cent).

(8) A small-cell rule of fewer than ten members applies to every table above. Affected cells are suppressed at source and their counts are carried into the "other" or "not stated" line of the same table, so that published totals remain reconcilable to the figure at clause (1). Where suppression of one cell alone would allow its value to be inferred, the next smallest cell in the same table is suppressed as well.

(9) Percentages are struck against year-end membership and rounded to one decimal place. No cross-tabulation of two or more characteristics is published in this report; requests for such tabulations are directed to the Records Custodian and are subject to the same threshold.
