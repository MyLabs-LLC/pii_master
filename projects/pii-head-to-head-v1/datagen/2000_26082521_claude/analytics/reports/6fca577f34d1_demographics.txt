PACIFIC MERIDIAN TITLE & ESCROW COMPANY
OFFICE OF THE RECORDS CUSTODIAN
SERVED POPULATION SUMMARY — CALENDAR YEAR 1997
Report No. SR-97-04

1. Scope. This report tabulates the aggregate demographic composition of parties to title and escrow orders closed through the Company's branch network between 1 January 1997 and 31 December 1997. Closed orders: 14,208. Counted parties: 22,946.

2. Source. Counts are drawn from the demographic supplement to the order intake questionnaire retained in the order jacket. Completion of the supplement is voluntary. Non-response is carried as "Declined / Not Stated" and is not imputed.

9. Suppression. Any cell with a count below six is withheld and shown as an em dash. Where a single suppressed cell in a row or column would permit recovery of the withheld count by subtraction, the next smallest cell in that row or column is also withheld. Withheld counts remain in the "All Other" line and in the stated totals.

10. Rounding. Percentages are computed on the counted-party base and rounded to one decimal place. Columns may not sum to exactly 100.0 percent.

11. Retention. This report is retained in the statistical series for seven years. Requests for tabulations below the levels shown are directed to the Records Custodian.
