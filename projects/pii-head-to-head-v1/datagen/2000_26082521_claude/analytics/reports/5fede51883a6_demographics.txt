RELEASE

  • Geography is published at service district and county group only.
    Requests for finer geography are referred to the Actuarial Supervisor.
  • Suppression: cells of fewer than eleven covered lives are withheld and
    printed as an asterisk. Where one suppressed cell in a row or column can
    be recovered by subtraction from the published total, the next smallest
    cell on that line is suppressed as well. Suppressed cells remain in the
    totals; totals are never adjusted to match the printed detail.
  • Percentages carried to one decimal, forced to 100.0 on the largest cell.
  • Two copies to the Records Custodian, one to the Underwriting Committee
    file, one retained by the unit for the seven-year cycle.
