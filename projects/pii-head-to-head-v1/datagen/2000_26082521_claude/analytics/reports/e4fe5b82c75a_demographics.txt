SECTION H — SUPPRESSION AND RELEASE
Publication is at aggregate level only. The smallest geography released is the postal district; requests for output at sector, unit postcode or property level are refused. Cells of fewer than 400 persons, or fewer than 200 households, are withheld and shown as "S". Complementary suppression is applied where a withheld cell could be derived by subtraction from a published margin. Withheld values are retained in row and column totals. Percentages are struck on the total shown and may not sum to 100 after rounding. Retention of the underlying survey returns follows the schedule held by the Records Custodian.
