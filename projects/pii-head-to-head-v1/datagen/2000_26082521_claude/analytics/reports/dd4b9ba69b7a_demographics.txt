CELL SUPPRESSION WORKSHEET
Form DS-121 — Population Analysis Unit

Applies to ................ Forms DS-114, DS-116 and attachments
Cycle ..................... CY1997 annual compilation
Completed by .............. the Statistical Reporting Analyst
Reviewed by ............... the Records Custodian

RULE SET
  Primary threshold ....... frequency < 11
  Marker .................. ++
  Complementary rule ...... applied to next smallest cell in the
                            affected row and in the affected
                            column
  Rounding ................ percentages to one decimal; counts
                            unrounded
  Margins ................. published as computed before
                            suppression

CYCLE TALLY
  Cells tabulated ......... 1,412
  Primary suppressions .... 47
  Complementary ........... 39
  Total withheld .......... 86  (6.1%)
  Tables affected ......... 9 of 24

GEOGRAPHIC FLOOR
  Lowest published unit is the three-digit postal district.
  Store-level, block-group and street-level tabulations are
  working papers and are not released.

DISPOSITION
  Requests for withheld cells are refused. Requesters may be
  offered a coarser tabulation in which the cell clears the
  threshold. Approval of any such re-tabulation rests with the
  Records Custodian.
