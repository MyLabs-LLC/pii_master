SCHEDULE C: LANGUAGE AND FINANCIAL NEED, UNDERGRADUATE POPULATION
SPRING 2016 CENSUS

A. SCOPE AND METHOD
   Population: undergraduate students with an active registration on the Spring 2016 census
   point. Headcount N = 18,406. Language derived from the home-language field on the
   admission application; financial need derived from the need-analysis result carried on the
   aid record for the corresponding aid year. Both fields are optional at point of collection.
   Nonresponse is reported as its own category rather than imputed.

E. GEOGRAPHY
   Reported at three-digit postal district only. In-state districts account for 87.6 percent of
   the population; out-of-state and foreign origin account for the remainder. District-level
   detail is issued on Schedule D and is not repeated here.

F. DISCLOSURE CONTROL
   The minimum reportable cell size on this schedule is ten. Cells falling below that threshold
   are suppressed and marked with an asterisk. Where suppression of a single cell would permit
   recovery of the withheld figure from a published row or column total, the next smallest cell
   in that row or column is also suppressed. Row 4 of Section D is published at the stated values
   because no constituent cell fell below the threshold. Percentages are computed against the
   stated denominator and rounded to one decimal place.
