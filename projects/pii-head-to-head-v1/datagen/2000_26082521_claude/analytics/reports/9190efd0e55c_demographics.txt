CONTRACTOR'S ANNUAL DEMOGRAPHIC STATEMENT
Served Population Analysis — Southern Distribution Contract
Statement No. 3 of 2011 · Issued to the Commissioning Authority's Statistical Office

ARTICLE I — RECITALS

WHEREAS the Contractor is obliged under Clause 19.4 of the Distribution Services Contract to furnish an annual statement of the demographic composition of the population served; and

WHEREAS the parties have agreed at Annex F that such statement shall consist exclusively of aggregate counts and derived proportions, and shall at no point comprise individual-level records or extracts thereof;

NOW THEREFORE the Contractor furnishes the following.

ARTICLE II — BASIS OF COMPILATION

Section 2.01. The compilation covers the twelve calendar months ending 31 December 2011.

Section 2.02. The Served Population is stated at 97,655 persons. This figure is the count of distinct service relationships and not the count of service events, of which there were 412,880 in the Period.

Section 2.03. The fields carried into the aggregation are, and are limited to: Age Band; Sex; Ethnic Group; Principal Language; Household Income Bracket; Postal District (outward code). Fields present in the operational system but not carried forward include Given Name, Family Name, Date of Birth, National Insurance Number, Customer Account Number, Telephone, Electronic Mail Address and Premises Address; none of these has been read, copied or referenced in the compilation.

ARTICLE IV — SUPPRESSION OF SMALL CELLS

Section 4.01. Consistent with Annex F, Paragraph 9, no cell of count less than ten is published. Such cells are shown as a hyphen.

Section 4.02. Cross-tabulations of two or more of the fields at Section 2.03 are not published in this Statement, on the ground that the resulting cell sizes cannot be assured above the suppression threshold at postal-district granularity. Where the Commissioning Authority requires a cross-tabulation, the Contractor will supply it only at a geography no finer than the banded groupings at Section 3.06, and subject to the same threshold.

Section 4.03. Suppressed values are retained in marginal totals. The Contractor will not confirm or deny the magnitude of a suppressed cell upon informal enquiry; requests are to be directed in writing to the Records Custodian.
