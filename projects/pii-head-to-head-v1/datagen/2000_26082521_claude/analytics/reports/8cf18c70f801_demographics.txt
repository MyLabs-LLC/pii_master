G. NOTES ON PREPARATION
   G.1  Counts are unduplicated at the participant level within the program year.
   G.2  Percentages are computed on the full N in each section, including the
        "not reported" line, and may not sum to 100.0 because of rounding.
   G.3  Small-cell suppression: any cell of one to four participants is withheld
        and merged into the section's "all other" or "not reported" line. Where
        withholding one cell would allow its recovery from a published total, the
        next smallest cell in that section is withheld as well.
   G.4  Geography is published no finer than the three-digit ZIP prefix.
   G.5  Cross-tabulation of two or more of sections A through F is not released
        outside the agency. Internal requests go to the Records Custodian.
