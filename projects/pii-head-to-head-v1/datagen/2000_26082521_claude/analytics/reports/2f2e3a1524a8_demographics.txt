NOTES

1. Postal districts are the Department's internal groupings of carrier
   routes and are the finest geography at which figures are released.
   Sub-district, block, premise and meter-route breakouts are not
   published and are not available on request.

2. Suppression: any cell of the A-by-B cross-tabulation containing ten
   or fewer accounts is suppressed, together with sufficient complementary
   cells to prevent derivation from published margins. Under this rule,
   fourteen cells of the 8-by-8 matrix are withheld, chiefly in PD-13 and
   PD-18 at the upper income brackets. Marginal totals shown above are
   unaffected by suppression.

3. The two lowest brackets together account for 24.5% of residential
   accounts and 41.2% of arrears balances over 90 days. This ratio has
   widened for four consecutive quarters and is referred to the Assistance
   Program Coordinator for comment.

4. Rounding: percentages to one decimal; columns may not sum exactly.
