HARBOR METRO TRANSIT COMMISSION
Internal Memorandum — Finance and Equity Analysis

Date:     8 April 2021
Subject:  Household income distribution, service area, 2021 base year
File:     FEA-2021-019

1. Scope. Household income is tabulated for the 559,900 households within the
service boundary. Income is total household income in the twelve months
preceding enumeration, expressed in 2021 dollars. Income is not tabulated
below the three-digit postal district; the table below is service-area total.

2. Distribution by income bracket.

   Bracket                        Households    Percent    Cumulative
   ------------------------------------------------------------------
   Under $15,000                      84,300      15.1          15.1
   $15,000 to $24,999                 61,700      11.0          26.1
   $25,000 to $34,999                 58,900      10.5          36.6
   $35,000 to $49,999                 72,400      12.9          49.5
   $50,000 to $74,999                 92,600      16.5          66.0
   $75,000 to $99,999                 66,800      11.9          77.9
   $100,000 to $149,999               68,300      12.2          90.1
   $150,000 and over                  54,900       9.8          99.9
   ------------------------------------------------------------------
   Total                             559,900     100.0

   Median household income:  $52,400
   Households below the federal poverty threshold: 96,700 (17.3%)
   Households below 150% of threshold:            148,300 (26.5%)

3. Interpretation. The two lowest brackets together account for 26.1% of
households, against 21.4% at the 2019 base year. The shift is concentrated in
the districts with the highest no-vehicle rates and is material to the
low-income fare program eligibility forecast filed under separate cover.

4. Suppression. Bracket counts below 25 weighted households are withheld;
complementary suppression is applied where a withheld cell could otherwise be
recovered from the total. No cell in the table above met the threshold for
withholding. Cross-tabulation of income bracket against race, ethnicity or
language at any geography finer than the service-area total will produce cells
below the threshold and is not released.

5. Rounding. Counts rounded to the nearest hundred; percentages to one decimal.
The cumulative column reflects rounded components and closes at 99.9.

6. Custody. Aggregate tables and the weighting documentation are held by the
Records Custodian, retention schedule FN-04 (seven years). The source sample is
accessed only under the executed data use agreement and is not held by this
commission in record-level form.
