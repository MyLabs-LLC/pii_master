1  BASIS AND AUTHORITY
   1.1  This Return is compiled and published in satisfaction of the obligations
        imposed upon the District by the state education code and by the terms
        of the District's assurances to its federal grantors, and constitutes
        the official statement of aggregate composition for the year stated.
   1.2  All figures herein are aggregate figures derived from the certified
        enrollment extract taken as of the Reference Date. No individual-level
        record is reproduced, appended, or referenced.
   1.3  Total Enrolled Membership on the Reference Date: 8,764.

3  SUPPRESSION OF SMALL CELLS
   3.1  Where any cell would otherwise disclose a count of fewer than ten
        pupils, that cell has been suppressed and marked with an asterisk.
   3.2  Where suppression of a single cell would permit its value to be
        recovered by arithmetic operation upon the remaining published cells,
        the next-smallest cell in the same dimension has been suppressed in
        addition. Suppression is applied before, and not after, the computation
        of percentages, which are stated to one decimal place.
