        obligation for service-area characteristics.

              scheduled fixed-route stop.

SECTION 7 — SMALL-CELL SUPPRESSION
    7.1 Primary suppression applies to any cell with a count below 25.
    7.2 Secondary suppression is applied within the affected row and column

        suppressed under clause 7.1 or 7.2.
