MEMORANDUM

TO:    Board Governance Committee
FROM:  Research and Statistics Unit
RE:    Demographic tabulation — summary of principal findings, 2016

The full tabulation is circulated separately. This memorandum summarises the principal figures for the committee and records the fields on which the tabulation is built.

Membership base at year end: 41,286.

Fields tabulated: Age at Reporting Date (derived), Sex, Ethnicity, Primary Language, Household Income Bracket, Postal District of Record.

Age. The membership is broadly even across the working-age bands. The 25–34 band is the largest at 18.3 per cent, followed by 45–54 at 17.5 per cent and 35–44 at 16.9 per cent. Members aged 65 and over account for 17.3 per cent taken together, and members under 18 for 4.5 per cent.

Sex. Female 51.7 per cent; male 46.5 per cent; not stated 1.8 per cent.

Ethnicity. White 50.7 per cent; Black or African American 21.2 per cent; Hispanic or Latino 14.6 per cent; Asian 5.3 per cent; all remaining categories together 4.1 per cent; not stated 4.1 per cent.

Language. English is the primary language of 78.6 per cent of the membership and Spanish of 14.2 per cent. No other single language reaches 2 per cent.

Income. Just under a quarter of members declare household income under $25,000 and a further 32.1 per cent between $25,000 and $49,999. Members declaring $100,000 or more account for 5.6 per cent. The not-stated rate on this field is 7.9 per cent, the highest of any field tabulated.

Geography. Central and North district groups together account for 47.6 per cent of the membership. The Outside area line, covering members whose postal district of record falls beyond the field of membership, stands at 6.0 per cent.

Suppression. Cells below a count of 10 are suppressed and rolled into the residual line for their table, with complementary suppression applied where a suppressed figure could be recovered by subtraction. Geography is reported at district-group level and no lower. Cross-tabulations are not prepared for circulation.

Ethnicity, language and income are optional self-reported fields; non-response is reported as "Not stated" and is not imputed. Percentages are rounded and may not total 100.0.
