METHOD

Figures are drawn from the master share file as at the close of
the final processing day of the year. The extraction routine
reads the following fields: MEMBER NUMBER, SURNAME, GIVEN NAME,
DATE OF BIRTH, SEX CODE, ETHNIC CODE, PREFERRED LANGUAGE CODE,
GROSS HOUSEHOLD INCOME BAND, RESIDENCE POSTAL CODE, COUNTY CODE,
BRANCH OF RECORD and MEMBERSHIP OPEN DATE. Identifying fields are
discarded at the point of extraction; only derived classification
codes and the county code pass into the aggregation step, and the
intermediate file is deleted on completion of the run.

Ethnic classification is collected but is not tabulated in this
item; it is reported separately under the fair lending schedule
in accordance with the reporting calendar approved by the Board
in March.

SUPPRESSION

The threshold for publication is ten members. Any classification
falling below the threshold is folded into the corresponding
"Other recorded" line rather than shown separately, and no
residual line is published from which it could be recovered.
Panel E reflects four languages so folded. Cross-tabulations of
two or more panels are not published at any level of detail;
requests for cross-tabulated figures are referred to the
Operations Committee.

Percentages are of total members of record. Panel E does not sum
to 100.00 owing to rounding.
