1.0  SCOPE

1.1  This memorandum tabulates the ethnicity and home language composition
     of the served population. Age, sex, income and geography are reported
     in the companion tabulation.
1.2  Counts are unduplicated. Percentages are of the base population.
1.3  Percentages may not sum to 100.0 owing to rounding.

4.0  SUPPRESSION AND CROSS-TABULATION

4.1  Cells below the threshold of 10 are suppressed. The threshold applies
     to every published table, including marginals.
4.2  Ethnicity-by-language cross-tabulations are withheld in their entirety.
     At the observed cell sizes, complementary suppression would void the
     majority of the matrix and the residual carries a disclosure risk that
     the office does not accept.
4.3  Language counts below 74 are consolidated into line 3.4, "All other
     languages, combined," and are not itemized at any level of request.
