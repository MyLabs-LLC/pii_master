MERIDIAN COAST INSURANCE GROUP
SERVED POPULATION STATISTICAL REPORT
Reporting Year 2021 (1 January to 31 December 2021)
Report number MC-PS-2021-02 · Series: Annual · Classification: Internal
Compiled by the Population Statistics Section · Custodian: the Records Custodian

CLAUSE 1 — PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 This report tabulates the demographic composition of the population served by the Group across its commercial, individual and managed-care lines for the reporting year.

1.2 The report is prepared on an aggregate basis. The tables below present counts and percentages only; no interior cell is derived from, or resolvable to, fewer than the threshold stated at Clause 6.

1.3 The denominator throughout is 327,915 covered lives, being all persons enrolled for one day or more during the reporting year, counted once irrespective of the number of enrolment segments held.

CLAUSE 2 — SOURCE FIELDS

2.1 Attributes are taken from the following enrolment fields: Age band; Sex; Race; Ethnicity; Preferred spoken language; Annual household income band; Postal district of residence (first three characters).

2.2 Where an attribute is unpopulated at the annual freeze date, the covered life is assigned to the Not stated or Not recorded category of the relevant table rather than excluded from the denominator.

CLAUSE 6 — SMALL-CELL SUPPRESSION AND ROUNDING

6.1 The suppression threshold for this series is eleven covered lives. Any cell falling below the threshold is withheld and shown as an em dash.

6.2 Where withholding a single cell would permit its recovery by subtraction from a published total, complementary suppression is applied to the next smallest cell in the same row or column until recovery is no longer possible.

6.3 Withheld counts remain within all marginal and grand totals. Published categories may therefore not reconcile to the stated total.

6.4 Percentages are calculated against the denominator at Clause 1.3 and rounded to one decimal place; columns may not sum to exactly 100.0.

6.5 Any request for tabulation finer than that published here, including cross-tabulation of geography with race, ethnicity, language or income, is referred to the Records Custodian for threshold assessment before release.
