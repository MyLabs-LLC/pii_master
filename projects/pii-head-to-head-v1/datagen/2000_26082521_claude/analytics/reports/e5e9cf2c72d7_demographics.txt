REGIONAL TITLE SERVICES COMPANY
STATISTICAL REPORT — DEMOGRAPHIC COMPOSITION OF SERVED POPULATION
Semiannual Report, Period Ending December 31, 2004
Issued by: Compliance and Records Section

1. PURPOSE AND BASIS

1.1 This report states the demographic composition of the population served by the Company's title and escrow operations for the period stated above.

1.2 The count basis is the distinct party of record on a disbursed file. Parties appearing on more than one file are counted once.

1.3 Total parties in scope for all tabulations below: 8,742.

5. SUPPRESSION

5.1 Cells with a count below 11 are not published. Such counts are combined into the "All other" or "Declined to state" line of the relevant table, as shown in Clause 3.1.

5.2 Where suppression of a single cell would permit its recovery from the published total, a second cell in the same table is suppressed and combined in the same manner.

6. ROUNDING

6.1 Percentages are carried to one decimal place. Totals are stated as 100.0% notwithstanding rounding differences in the constituent lines.
