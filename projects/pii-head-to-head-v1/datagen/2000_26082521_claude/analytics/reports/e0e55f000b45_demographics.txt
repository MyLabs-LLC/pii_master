SECTION H — DISCLOSURE CONTROL
================================================================
All figures are aggregate counts. Any cell with a frequency of
fewer than 11 is suppressed and shown as double dagger. Where a
single suppression would permit recovery of the withheld value
by subtraction from a published margin, the next smallest cell
in the same row and column is suppressed as complementary. Row
and column totals are published as computed prior to
suppression; consequently published cells may not sum to the
stated total.
