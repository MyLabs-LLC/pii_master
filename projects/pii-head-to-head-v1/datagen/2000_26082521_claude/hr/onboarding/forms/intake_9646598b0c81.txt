MEMORANDUM

SUBJECT:   Form RT-09 (Rev. 09/2016), Application for Company Sponsorship of Pharmacy Technician Trainee Registration
ISSUED BY: Office of Professional Licensure Compliance
APPLIES TO: All Non-Licensed Personnel Seeking Sponsored Registration

PREAMBLE AND INSTRUCTIONS

1. This form is provided for the use of any individual employed by the Company in a non-licensed capacity who seeks the Company's sponsorship of an initial or renewal application for pharmacy technician trainee registration before the applicable state board of pharmacy.

2. The applicant shall complete Articles I through IV below in the applicant's own hand. The applicant shall not delegate completion to a supervisor, co-worker, or family member, it being the position of the Company that the accuracy of the representations made herein is personal to the applicant and non-delegable.

3. The applicant is placed on notice that sponsorship, if extended, is discretionary in all respects; that the Company undertakes no obligation to advance, reimburse, or indemnify the applicant with respect to any fee, fine, or assessment levied by any board of pharmacy; and that the withdrawal of sponsorship, for cause or without cause, shall not give rise to any claim against the Company.

4. Any field left blank without the notation "N/A" shall cause the application to be returned unprocessed, and the applicant shall bear the consequences of any resulting delay, including without limitation the lapse of any existing registration.
