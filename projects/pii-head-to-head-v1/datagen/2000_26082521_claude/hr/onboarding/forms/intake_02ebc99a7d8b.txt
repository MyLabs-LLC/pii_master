INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS FORM.  The party applying for the
opening of an escrow, or a duly authorized representative acting on behalf of
such party, shall complete each and every item set forth hereinbelow, in ink
and in legible block capitals.  No item may be left vacant; where an item is
inapplicable, the notation "N/A" shall be inscribed in the space provided.
The particulars furnished hereunder will be relied upon by the Company in the
preparation of the preliminary report, in the giving of all notices required
under the escrow instructions, and, where applicable, in the issuance of a
policy of title insurance.  Corrections shall be effected by a single line
struck through the erroneous entry and initialed in the margin; correction
fluid shall not be employed.  Upon completion, this form shall be delivered
to the escrow officer to whom the file has been assigned.
