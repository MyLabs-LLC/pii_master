MUNICIPAL WATER & SEWER DEPARTMENT
FIELD SERVICES AND METER READING

FORM UW-331 (REV. 01/04)

TO:      Party Requesting Discontinuance or Transfer of Responsibility
FROM:    Office of the Records Custodian
SUBJECT: Notice of Vacancy, Final Meter Reading, and Transfer of Responsibility

PURPOSE

1. This form is used to record a request for a final meter reading, to close a service account upon vacancy, and where applicable to transfer responsibility for the premises to an incoming party.
2. Responsibility for all consumption at the premises continues until the date of the final reading recorded by the Department. Verbal notice is not sufficient.

INSTRUCTIONS

3. Give not less than three (3) full business days' notice of the requested reading date.
4. Ensure the meter pit or interior meter is accessible on the requested date. Where access cannot be obtained, an estimated reading will be rendered and a re-read fee may be applied.
5. Complete Part I in all cases. Complete Part II only where an incoming party has agreed to assume responsibility.
