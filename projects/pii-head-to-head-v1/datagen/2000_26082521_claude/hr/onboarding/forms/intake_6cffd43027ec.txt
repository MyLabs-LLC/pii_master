HARLAN COUNTY UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT
BUSINESS SERVICES DIVISION — VENDOR AND CONTRACTOR RELATIONS

FORM BS-31 (2021) | VOLUNTEER AND CONTRACTED SERVICE PROVIDER SITE ACCESS REGISTRATION

This registration is completed by any individual who is not a district employee and who seeks recurring access to a district campus during instructional hours, whether as an unpaid volunteer, as a contracted service provider, or as an agent of an entity holding a services agreement with the district. The registration is completed once per academic year and does not transfer between campuses; an individual seeking access at more than one campus completes a separate registration for each. The individual completing this registration presents it in person to the site Attendance Technician, who verifies the entries in Section 1 against a government-issued photographic identification and initials the office-use block accordingly. The Attendance Technician does not retain a copy of the identification presented.
