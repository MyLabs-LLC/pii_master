UNDERWRITING SUPPLEMENT — HOUSEHOLD AND ADDITIONAL INTEREST SCHEDULE
Form UW-63S (Rev. 02/11)

PURPOSE AND INSTRUCTIONS
This supplement is required whenever the application lists more than one resident of the household aged fifteen (15) years or older, or whenever any party other than the named applicant holds a financial interest in the insured property. List every qualifying individual, including those not requesting coverage. If an individual is excluded from coverage, complete the exclusion column and obtain a separate signed exclusion endorsement. Rows must be completed in full; partial entries will be returned to the producer without action. Use additional copies of this form if more than six individuals must be listed and number each sheet in the space provided at the foot of the page.
