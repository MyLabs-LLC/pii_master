MUNICIPAL UTILITIES DEPARTMENT
FORM UD-206 (REV. 09/04)
APPLICATION FOR TRANSFER OF ACCOUNT RESPONSIBILITY BETWEEN PROPERTY OWNER AND OCCUPANT

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE. This form is used where responsibility for a metered service is to pass from a property owner to an occupant, or from an occupant back to the property owner, without interruption of supply. It is not used to open a new account at an unserved premises; Form UD-114 governs that case. One form is required for each meter.

2. INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS FORM. Complete every paragraph of Sections 3 through 6. Print in block capitals in ink. Where a paragraph does not apply, enter the notation N/A rather than leaving the line vacant; vacant lines are treated as omissions and the form is returned. Both parties must sign at Section 6 before the form is lodged. The completed form is lodged with the Records Custodian, who date-stamps the original and returns no copy. Processing is not undertaken until the arrears position of the existing account is settled or a deferment has been authorised under Form UD-311.
