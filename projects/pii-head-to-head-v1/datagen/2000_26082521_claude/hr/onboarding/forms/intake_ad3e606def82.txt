PHARMACY TECHNICIAN TRAINEE — REGISTRATION AND CONTACT RECORD
Form PT-07 (Rev. 08/2011)
Pharmacy Services Division

PURPOSE
This record establishes the trainee file held at store level and duplicated at the division office. It must be completed before the first supervised shift is rostered.

NOTES FOR THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS FORM
* Complete all items. An item left blank will cause the registration to be returned.
* Use full legal spellings. Nicknames belong only in the "preferred name" item.
* Any change to the items in Part 2 must be reported to the supervising pharmacist within five business days.
* Do not staple documents to this form. Use the paper clip provided.
