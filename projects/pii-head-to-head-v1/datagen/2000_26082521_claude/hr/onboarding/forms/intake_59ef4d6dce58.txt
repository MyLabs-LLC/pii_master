FORM REV:  CA-101 (Rev. 04/04). Supersedes CA-101 (Rev. 11/99), which shall not be accepted after the close of the current fiscal quarter.

PART I — INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS FORM

The applicant is hereby advised that no service shall be commenced, restored, or transferred at any premises situated within the corporate limits, or within such extraterritorial service area as the governing body may from time to time designate, until such time as this application has been completed in its entirety, subscribed by the applicant or by a duly authorized agent thereof, and lodged with the Customer Service Counter during posted business hours. All entries shall be made in ink. Erasures are not permitted; a correction shall be effected by striking the erroneous entry with a single horizontal line and placing the initials of the person completing the form in the adjacent margin. An application tendered with omitted, illegible, or internally inconsistent entries shall be returned without action, and computation of any required deposit shall be held in abeyance pending resubmission.
