INTEROFFICE MEMORANDUM

TO:      Person Completing Form CA-407
FROM:    Office of the Records Custodian
RE:      Authorization for Release of Customer Account Information to a Third Party
FORM:    CA-407 (Rev. 02/04)

Be advised that the Department is prohibited from disclosing information concerning a customer account to any person other than the customer of record, save upon the written authorization of that customer or upon lawful process. This form constitutes such written authorization. It must be completed by hand, in ink, and presented at the counter by the customer of record, who shall produce identification satisfactory to the receiving clerk.
