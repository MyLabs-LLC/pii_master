RETENTION AND DISPOSAL SCHEDULE — PERSONNEL AND INTAKE RECORDS
Carrier Services Division / Issue 4 / Supersedes Issue 3

SCOPE

This schedule governs paper records created or received by Depot Administration in
connection with applications for employment, engagement, and termination. It does
not govern payroll records, which are scheduled separately under FIN/RET/02.

CUSTODY

The Records Custodian holds custody of all records within scope. Depot clerks act
as agents of the Custodian for the purpose of receipt, entry in the register, and
filing. Removal of a record from the intake cabinet or the personnel cabinet
requires an entry in the movement log showing file number, date out, initials of
the person taking the record, and date returned.

SCHEDULE

    Record class                          Retention period               Disposal method
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Applicant Particulars Record          12 months from outcome date    Cross-cut shred
      (unsuccessful applicants)
    Applicant Particulars Record          Transferred to personnel       n/a — transferred
      (successful applicants)             file on engagement
    Induction papers                      6 years from engagement        Cross-cut shred
    Licence and entitlement checks        Duration of engagement + 6 yrs Cross-cut shred
    Intake register sheets                10 years from sheet closure    Cross-cut shred
    Movement log                          3 years from last entry        Cross-cut shred
    Correspondence with applicants        12 months from despatch        Cross-cut shred

REVIEW AND DESTRUCTION

Files falling due for destruction are to be listed on a disposal certificate showing
file number, record class, retention class, and due date. No personal particulars are
to be transcribed onto the certificate beyond the file number. The certificate is to
be signed by the clerk carrying out the destruction and countersigned by the Records
Custodian. Certificates are retained for ten years.

Where a file is subject to a dispute, a claim, or an enquiry by an external body,
destruction is suspended and the file is marked "HOLD — DO NOT DESTROY" on the front
cover, with the reason and the date of review entered in the hold register. Holds are
reviewed every six months by the Custodian.

EXCEPTIONS

Any departure from this schedule requires the written authority of the Division
Secretary. A record of the authority is to be filed with the disposal certificate.
