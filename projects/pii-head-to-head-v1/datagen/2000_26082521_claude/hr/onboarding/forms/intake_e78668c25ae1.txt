TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS FORM

This form opens your permanent file with the division and is the source from which every subsequent record is built. The information you enter here will be transcribed by the Records Custodian onto the master card and into the dispatch system, and it will not be re-verified against you afterward, so accuracy at this stage matters more than speed. Take as long as you need. If you require assistance in reading or completing any part of this form, ask the receptionist, who will arrange for a coordinator to sit with you. Enter one character per box where boxes are provided. Where a line is provided instead, print within the line and do not run over into the margin, as the margins are trimmed when the form is microfilmed at year end.
