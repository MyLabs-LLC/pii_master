REQUEST FOR CHANGE TO EMPLOYEE CONTACT RECORD
Form HR-207 (Rev. 03/2011)

READ BEFORE COMPLETING

1. This form is used to correct or update the contact record held by the Office of Human Resources. It does not change the record held by the benefits carrier, the retirement system, or the credit union. Separate notice must be given to each of those bodies.
2. Complete Section A in full, even where an entry is unchanged. An incomplete Section A prevents matching to the personnel file.
3. In Section B, complete only those lines that are changing. Rule through any line that is not changing.
4. Sign Section C. An unsigned form has no effect and will be returned.
5. Submit to the Records Custodian, Office of Human Resources. Requests received after the fifteenth of the month may not take effect until the following pay cycle.
