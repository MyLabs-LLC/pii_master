INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS APPLICATION. The applicant, or a duly authorized agent of the applicant acting under written authority filed with this Department, shall complete each and every part of the following instrument in legible black or blue-black ink, or by typewriter, and shall not omit, strike through, or otherwise render illegible any portion hereof except by drawing a single line through the erroneous entry and placing the initials of the person making such correction immediately adjacent thereto. Where a field is inapplicable to the applicant's circumstances, the applicant shall enter the notation "N/A" rather than leaving such field vacant, it being the position of this Department that a vacant field constitutes an incomplete application and shall be returned without action pursuant to the administrative provisions governing the acceptance of applications for permits of this character. No application shall be deemed filed, and no period of review shall be deemed to commence, until such time as the Permit Intake Clerk has stamped the instrument received and the applicable fee has been tendered in a form acceptable to the County Treasurer.
