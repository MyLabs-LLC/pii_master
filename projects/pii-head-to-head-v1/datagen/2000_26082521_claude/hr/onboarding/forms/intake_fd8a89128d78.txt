OFFICE OF THE REGISTRAR — RECORDS UNIT
PETITION FOR CORRECTION OR AMENDMENT OF THE PERMANENT STUDENT RECORD
Form RG-208 (Rev. 09/2021)

ARTICLE I — PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 This petition is used to request a correction, amendment, or update to identity or contact information carried on the permanent academic record.
1.2 A separate petition is required for each category of change requested.
1.3 This petition does not effect a change to payroll, benefits, or housing records maintained by other offices.

ARTICLE II — INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS FORM

2.1 Print legibly. Enter one character per box where boxes appear.
2.2 Leave any field blank that is not being changed. Do not enter "same" or "no change."
2.3 Attach one item of supporting documentation for every entry made in Article IV. Original documents will be examined and returned; photocopies will not be accepted at the counter.
2.4 Present the completed petition to the Records Custodian during posted counter hours. Petitions left in unattended drop boxes will not be date-stamped.
2.5 Retain the applicant copy (yellow) for your records.
