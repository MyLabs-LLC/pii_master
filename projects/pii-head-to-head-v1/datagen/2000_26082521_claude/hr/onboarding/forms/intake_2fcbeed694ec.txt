TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS RECORD:

     Pursuant to the customer identification procedures maintained by the
     Company, the particulars set forth hereinbelow must be obtained from each
     applicant before an escrow may be opened or a policy of title insurance
     may be bound. The applicant is requested to furnish each particular, and
     the receiving officer is directed to record the same verbatim, without
     abbreviation and without substitution of any nickname, familiar form, or
     trade style for the legal name of the applicant. Where the applicant
     declines to furnish a particular, the receiving officer shall so note in
     the margin, shall initial the notation, and shall refer the matter to the
     Compliance Officer of the day before proceeding further. Nothing herein
     shall be construed to authorize the opening of a file upon an incomplete
     record.
