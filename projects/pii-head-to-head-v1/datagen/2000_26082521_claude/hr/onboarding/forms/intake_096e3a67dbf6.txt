I.  PURPOSE AND FILING INSTRUCTIONS
    A.  This form is filed by a party seeking to assume, relinquish, or add responsibility for an existing metered service account.
    B.  A separate form shall be filed for each premises.
    C.  The party relinquishing responsibility and the party assuming responsibility shall each complete the sections designated to them. An incomplete form is returned without action.
    D.  Attach the following:
        1.  Documentation of lawful occupancy or ownership of the premises.
        2.  Written authorization, where the request is submitted by an agent.
