NETWORK ACCOUNT AND DEVICE ASSIGNMENT REQUEST — NEW PERSONNEL

Completion of this request is the responsibility of the hiring supervisor, not of the individual for whom the account is sought, and the request is not accepted from the individual directly. The supervisor completes the request no fewer than five working days before the individual's first day of duty; requests submitted inside that window are processed on a best-effort basis and the supervisor should not represent to the individual that credentials will be available at the start of duty. The supervisor forwards the completed request to Technology Services through the interoffice mail or delivers it to the service desk. Technology Services does not accept this request by electronic transmission, as the request bears an original supervisory signature.

The supervisor enters, in Block 1, the individual's legal name as it appears in the payroll system of record. A discrepancy between the entry in Block 1 and the payroll record will cause the request to be returned without action; Technology Services does not reconcile such discrepancies on the supervisor's behalf.
