FIDELITY NATIONAL-STYLE ESCROW INTAKE — APPLICANT IDENTITY AND CONTACT PARTICULARS

FORM EI-114 (REV. 03/2021) — ESCROW APPLICANT IDENTIFICATION WORKSHEET
Retention Class: EI-3 | Governing Procedure: Escrow Operations Manual, §4.2 (Applicant Onboarding)

PURPOSE AND SCOPE OF THIS INSTRUMENT

This worksheet is completed at the opening of any escrow file in which the applicant is not an existing customer of record, or in which an existing customer's identifying particulars have changed since the last verified file. The information gathered here supports the identity verification obligations set out in the Escrow Operations Manual and is used by the escrow officer to establish the applicant of record before any funds instruction is accepted. The worksheet does not itself constitute an escrow instruction, an order for title evidence, or a commitment of any kind. It is an internal record only and shall not be furnished to any party outside the branch except upon direction of the Records Custodian or in response to lawful process.

INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS FORM

Complete every field in Sections A through D in permanent black or blue ink, or by typed entry. Do not leave a field blank; where an item does not apply, enter the notation "N/A" in the space provided. Where a field calls for a legal name, enter the name exactly as it appears on the identification document presented, including any suffix or middle initial, and do not substitute a familiar or shortened form. Corrections shall be made by a single strike-through with the correcting party's initials adjacent; do not obliterate, overwrite, or apply correction fluid to any entry. When the applicant is an entity rather than a natural person, complete Section A for the authorized signatory and attach the entity authorization documents called for at Section D. The completed worksheet is submitted to the escrow officer assigned to the file, who reviews it for legibility and completeness before the file number is assigned.
