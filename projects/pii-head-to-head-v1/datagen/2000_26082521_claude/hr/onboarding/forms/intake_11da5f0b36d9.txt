OFFICE OF THE REGISTRAR
AUTHORIZATION TO RELEASE ACADEMIC RECORDS TO A THIRD PARTY
Form RG-331 (Rev. 06/2021)

1. INSTRUCTIONS

   (a) Complete every clause of Parts 2 through 5. An incomplete authorization is void.
   (b) Print in block capitals using black or blue ink.
   (c) An authorization remains in effect for twelve (12) months from the date of signature unless a shorter period is stated in Clause 4(d).
   (d) To revoke an authorization already on file, submit a written revocation to the Records Custodian. Revocation is not retroactive to disclosures already made.
   (e) Do not use this form to request a transcript for your own use. Use Form RG-102 for that purpose.
