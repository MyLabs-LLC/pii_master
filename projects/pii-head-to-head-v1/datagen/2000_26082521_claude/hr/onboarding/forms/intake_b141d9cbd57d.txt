INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS APPLICATION. Read the
whole of the following instructions before making any entry. (1) All
entries shall be made in black or dark blue ink and shall bear
sufficient pressure to produce a legible impression upon the pink
carbon copy. (2) Every numbered item shall receive a response; where
an item does not apply to the circumstances of the applicant, the
applicant shall enter the notation "NONE" or "N/A" as the case may
require, and shall not leave the space blank. (3) The applicant shall
not write in any space designated for office use. (4) This
application shall be delivered to the cashier's window or deposited
in the locked drop box adjacent thereto; applications transmitted by
facsimile shall not be accepted for processing. (5) The applicant is
advised that submission of this application does not suspend the
accrual of any balance nor the ordinary course of collection activity
until such time as written notice of approval has been issued by the
Section.
