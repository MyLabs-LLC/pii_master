DEPOT ADMINISTRATION — FORM 27B
APPLICANT PARTICULARS AND CONTACT REGISTER
Issue 4, March 2021. Supersedes Issue 3 (August 2019).

PURPOSE AND USE OF THIS FORM

This form captures the identifying and contact particulars of persons applying for engagement as drivers, forklift operators, dock hands, or general depot labour, whether engaged directly or through an approved labour supplier. It is the first document opened on an applicant file and must be completed before any other onboarding paperwork is issued. The information recorded here is used to establish the applicant's file, to verify licences and entitlements against the issuing authorities, and to contact the applicant during the assessment period. It is not used for rostering until the file has been cleared by the Operations Manager.

INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS FORM

Complete every section personally. This form may not be completed on your behalf by a recruitment agent, a family member, or a depot employee, except where a reasonable adjustment has been agreed in advance with the Records Custodian and noted in the office-use block below. Print clearly in block capitals. Use one character per box where boxes are provided, and one line per entry where ruled lines are provided. Do not use correction fluid; strike through any error with a single line and initial the correction in the margin. If you require more space than the form allows, continue on a separate sheet, head that sheet with the section number, and sign and date it. Unsigned continuation sheets are not accepted. Return the completed form to the depot office in person or by internal mail. Forms received by facsimile are not accepted.
