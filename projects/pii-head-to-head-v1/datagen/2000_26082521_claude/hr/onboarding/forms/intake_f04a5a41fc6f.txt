APPLICANT PARTICULARS — SUPPLEMENT TO ESCROW OPENING PACKET

Form 41-A, revision of February 2021. To be filed as page two of the escrow opening packet in all files opened after the effective date of the revision.

The supplement exists because the escrow opening packet in its principal form records the transaction but not the person, and the division has determined that the particulars of the applicant should be captured on a single leaf that can be pulled and verified without disturbing the rest of the packet. The escrow assistant completes the supplement in the presence of the applicant where practicable, and otherwise from the information furnished by the applicant in writing. Each blank is filled; a blank left empty is treated as an incomplete file by the reviewer and is returned. Entries are made in ink. The applicant is asked to review the completed leaf before signing it, and any correction the applicant requests is made on the face of the leaf and initialed by both the applicant and the assistant. The supplement, once signed, is imaged with the packet and the paper original is held in the working file until the file is closed and forwarded to the Records Custodian for retention.
