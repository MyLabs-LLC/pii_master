REGIONAL TRANSIT AUTHORITY — DEPARTMENT OF SECURITY AND FACILITIES
FORM SEC-208-A (Issue 2, effective 01 July 2011)
REQUEST FOR ISSUANCE, RENEWAL, OR REPLACEMENT OF A
RESTRICTED-AREA FACILITY ACCESS CREDENTIAL
===============================================================

PART I — DIRECTIVES TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS REQUEST

  – This request shall be completed by the individual for whom the credential is sought (hereinafter "the Requestor") and by no other person acting on the Requestor's behalf, save where the Requestor is physically unable to do so, in which event the assisting party shall record that fact at Part VI.
  – One (1) form shall be completed per Requestor per credential class. Consolidated or multi-party submissions shall not be accepted under any circumstances.
  – All entries shall be typewritten or hand-printed in block capitals. Cursive script shall be treated as illegible and shall constitute grounds for return of the request.
  – Where a field is not applicable to the Requestor, the Requestor shall enter the notation "N/A" in full; the field shall not be left vacant.
  – Alterations, if unavoidable, shall be effected by a single horizontal strike-through, shall be initialled and dated in the margin, and shall not be effected by correction fluid, correction tape, or overwriting of any kind.
  – The completed request shall be transmitted to the Credentialing Unit through the Requestor's supervising authority and not by direct submission.
