COPIES:   Compliance Officer; Records Custodian; Supply

ARTICLE 1. APPLICATION
   1.1  This form registers a new applicant in the policyholder index prior to
        the assignment of a policy number.
   1.2  One entry is made for the primary applicant. A separate entry is made
        for each co-applicant and is cross-referenced under Article 4.

ARTICLE 2. INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS FORM
   2.1  Complete the register line by line. Do not skip lines.
   2.2  Enter dates in numeric form only.
   2.3  Where the applicant supplies a correction after submission, do not
        alter this form. Prepare a new form and mark the superseded copy
        "REPLACED" in the margin.
   2.4  Do not detach the office-use block.
