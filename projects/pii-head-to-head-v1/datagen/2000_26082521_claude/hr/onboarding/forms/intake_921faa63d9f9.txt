* GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS
  * Complete this application in full before approaching the cashier window.
  * Print all entries. Do not abbreviate except where an item permits it.
  * Applicants must complete the approved food safety orientation prior to card issuance. Proof of completion is required at the time of application.
  * A health card is valid for three (3) years from the date of issuance and is not transferable between individuals.
  * Applicants seeking renewal must present the expiring card. If the card is unavailable, complete the Lost Card Declaration in Part F.
  * The card remains the property of the department and must be surrendered upon revocation or suspension.
