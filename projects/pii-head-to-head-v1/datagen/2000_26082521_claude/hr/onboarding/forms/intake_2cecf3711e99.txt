APPLICANT PARTICULARS AND CORRESPONDENCE RECORD
Distribution: Original — Branch File; Duplicate — Regional Records Center

  I.  SCOPE AND COMPLETION INSTRUCTIONS
      A.  Purpose
          1.  This record captures applicant identity and correspondence data
              required for assignment dispatch, payroll setup, and statutory
              reporting.
          2.  It does not constitute an application for employment and does not
              record skills, references, or wage history. Those items are
              captured on Forms OCP-25 and OCP-26.
      B.  Who Completes This Record
          1.  Parts II through V are completed by the applicant.
          2.  Part VI is completed by branch staff only.
          3.  Where an applicant requires assistance, the assisting employee
              records their initials and the reason in Part VI.G.
      C.  Manner of Completion
          1.  Type, or print legibly in ink. One character per box where boxes
              are supplied.
          2.  Do not abbreviate street names, municipalities, or given names.
          3.  Leave no field blank. Enter "N/A" or "NONE" as appropriate.
          4.  Corrections: single strikethrough, correction adjacent, initials
              of the person making the correction. Correction fluid, erasure,
              and overwriting are not permitted.
          5.  Submit the completed record to the Records Custodian on the day
              of completion.
