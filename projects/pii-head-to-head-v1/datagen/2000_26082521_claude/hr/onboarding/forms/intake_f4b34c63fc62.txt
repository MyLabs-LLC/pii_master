MEMORANDUM

TO:       Customer Service Counter Staff and Applicants
FROM:     Billing and Collections Section
SUBJECT:  Form CB-330 (Rev. 07/2004) — Designation of Authorized Agent for
          Account Inquiry and Correspondence
DISTRIBUTION: Original to file; carbon copy to designating party on request.

GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS

A. This designation authorizes a named agent to inquire into account status, to
   receive duplicate billing statements, and to submit payment arrangements on
   behalf of the party of record. It does not confer authority to discontinue
   service, to alter the party of record, or to release a deposit.
B. The designating party must execute Item 12 in the presence of counter staff
   or before a notarial officer. Unwitnessed forms are not accepted.
C. One agent per form. Submit a separate form for each additional agent.
D. The designation remains effective until revoked in writing or until the
   account is closed, whichever occurs first.
E. Do not complete Items 13 through 19.
