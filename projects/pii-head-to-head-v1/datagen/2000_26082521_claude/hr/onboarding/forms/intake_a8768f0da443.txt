OWNER-DRIVER ONBOARDING QUESTIONNAIRE

Introduction and how to complete this questionnaire

Every owner-driver proposing to carry freight under a Harbour Line consignment note is required to complete this questionnaire before a contract carrier number is allocated. The questionnaire is completed once at the commencement of the engagement and again at each three-yearly review, or earlier if the contracting entity changes. It is completed by the driver personally, not by an intermediary, and is written in ink rather than pencil so that entries cannot be altered after signing. Please read each section through before writing, as several sections ask you to answer differently depending on whether you contract as an individual, as a partnership, or through a company. Where a section does not apply to you, rule a single diagonal line through the whole section and write your initials beside it. Completed questionnaires are lodged with the contracts clerk at the depot from which you will principally operate. The clerk checks the questionnaire for completeness only; questions about the substance of the engagement should be directed to the Fleet Contracts Officer.
