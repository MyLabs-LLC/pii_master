TERMS OF THE ARRANGEMENT

1. An arrangement may be granted on an account in arrears where the customer tenders an initial instalment at the counter on the day of application.
2. The balance outstanding after the initial instalment is divided into equal monthly instalments, which are added to the current charges on each statement.
3. The arrangement is void, and the whole balance becomes immediately due, if any instalment or any current charge is not paid within fifteen (15) days of the statement date.
4. Only one arrangement may be in force on an account at any time.
