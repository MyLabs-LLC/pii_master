REMITTANCE

Fees are payable at the time of filing and are not refundable in whole or in part following the assignment of an application number, irrespective of the disposition subsequently entered by the reviewing officer. Cash, check, and money order are accepted at the intake window. Checks shall be drawn payable to the County Treasurer and shall bear the application number in the memorandum line where such number has been assigned. Fee waivers are available only to organizations holding a current certificate of exemption on file with the Records Custodian, and the applicant shall so indicate in Part I under "Capacity in Which the Applicant Applies."

Questions concerning classification of an operation under the foregoing categories should be directed to the Permit Intake Unit prior to filing. Classification determinations made at the counter are provisional and subject to revision by the assigned Field Sanitarian, in which event any difference in fee shall be collected or credited at the time of inspection.
