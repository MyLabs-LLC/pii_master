PRELIMINARY INSTRUCTIONS — READ IN FULL BEFORE MAKING ANY ENTRY

 [_] This Statement is to be completed by the candidate personally. It may not be completed by a spouse, relative, agent, or other representative, and no portion of it may be completed by Commission staff on the candidate's behalf.
 [_] Type all entries or print plainly in black ink. Should additional space be required for any item, continue the response on a plain sheet of the same size, identify the item number to which the continuation pertains, and attach the sheet to the rear of this Statement.

 [_] This Statement, once filed, becomes the property of the Commission and will not be returned to the candidate.
