RECORDING AND POST-CLOSING TRANSMITTAL LOG

Form TE-341 (Rev. 11/2021)
Recording Department · One log per file · Continuation sheets permitted

INSTRUCTIONS TO PREPARER

Complete Blocks 1 through 4 at the time the closing package is released to the recording desk. Do not pre-date entries. Where an instrument is rejected by the recorder, open a new line rather than amending the original line; the original line remains as filed. Illegible entries are returned to the preparer for rewrite. The Records Custodian retains the log with the file jacket and does not release it outside the department without written authorization.
