DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC UTILITIES
FORM UD-428 (REV. 06/04)
DESIGNATION OF AUTHORISED AGENT AND THIRD-PARTY NOTIFICATION REGISTRY

PURPOSE
     A customer of record may designate one agent to receive duplicate notices, to make enquiries as to the account, and to be notified before disconnection. Designation does not transfer liability for charges, which remains with the customer of record in all events. A designation stands until revoked in writing or until the account is closed, whichever first occurs.

INSTRUCTIONS
     Complete Parts I, II and III. Print in ink. Do not enter particulars for more than one agent; a second designation supersedes the first and the earlier registration is struck from the registry without notice to the person named in it. The form must be signed by the customer of record and not by the agent. Lodge the completed form with the Records Custodian at the Customer Accounts counter or by post to the Division's registered box. Telephone designations are not accepted in any circumstance.
