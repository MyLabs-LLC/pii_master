WORKSHEET TE-2 — NEW APPLICANT PARTICULARS (TITLE AND ESCROW DIVISION)
Effective 01/2021. Supersedes Worksheet TE-2 dated 06/2018.

This worksheet is prepared by the receiving clerk at the counter, or by the escrow assistant when an application is taken by telephone, and is retained in the working file behind the title order. Its function is limited to the orderly capture of applicant particulars at intake so that the escrow officer need not gather the same information a second time. The person completing the worksheet writes legibly, uses one character per box where boxes are provided, and does not abbreviate except where an abbreviation is called for on the face of the form. Any field for which the applicant declines to provide information is marked "DECLINED" and initialed by the person taking the application; the escrow officer is notified before the file proceeds. The worksheet is not transmitted outside the division and is not reproduced for the applicant.
