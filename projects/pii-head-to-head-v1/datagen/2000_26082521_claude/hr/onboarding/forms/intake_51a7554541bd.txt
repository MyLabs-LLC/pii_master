SECTION I — HOW TO COMPLETE THIS FORM

  - Complete all sections in ink. Illegible forms are returned unprocessed.
  - Enter one participant per form. Do not list additional persons in the margins.
  - Where a field is marked (REQUIRED), the form cannot be processed if left blank.
  - Do not staple. Use the paper clip provided at the counter.
  - The participating employer's benefits coordinator must sign Section V before
    submission. Forms submitted directly by the participant are not accepted.
  - Enrollment closes on the fifteenth day of the month. Forms received after
    the closing date take effect in the following benefit month.
