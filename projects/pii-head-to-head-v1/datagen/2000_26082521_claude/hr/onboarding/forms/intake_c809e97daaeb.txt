CALDER & VANCE LOGISTICS — PERSONNEL ADMINISTRATION
CONTRACTOR IDENTITY AND CONTACT DECLARATION
Form PA-233 (Rev. 6) — effective 01 July 2016 — supersedes Rev. 5
Retention class: PER-3 | Sheets: 2 | Ink: black or blue-black only

PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This declaration opens a personnel administration file for an individual
engaged under a contract for services at any depot, cross-dock or
transhipment site operated by the company. It is not a contract of
employment and confers no engagement.

INSTRUCTIONS
(a) Complete every numbered field. Fields marked [M] are mandatory and a
    file will not be opened where they are left blank.
(b) Enter one character per cell in celled fields. Do not overwrite cell
    borders.
(c) Do not attach loose documents to this form. Supporting documents are
    handed to the Records Custodian separately and logged in Section 5.
(d) Where an entry changes after submission, submit form PA-234 (Change
    of Particulars). Do not amend a lodged declaration.
