GUARANTOR / RESPONSIBLE PARTY UPDATE
Billing Office Form G-30 · Rev. 09/2021

  HOW TO COMPLETE THIS FORM
    – Use this form only to change or correct the guarantor of record on an existing account.
    – Complete all boxes. Marks made outside the boxes are not scanned.
    – One form per account. Do not list more than one guarantor.
    – A change of guarantor does not transfer a balance already in collection.
    – Hand the finished form to the clerk at Window 3, or place it in the sealed intake box beside the elevator.
