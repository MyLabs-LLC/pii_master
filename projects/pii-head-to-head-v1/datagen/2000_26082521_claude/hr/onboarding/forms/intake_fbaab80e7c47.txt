SECTION 1 — GENERAL

1.1 This application is made under Article 14 of the General Conditions of Contract. It shall be completed by the individual to be credentialed and countersigned by the Authority's Contract Administrator of record.
1.2 One application is required for each individual. Group or firm-wide applications are not accepted.
1.3 Type or hand-print. Illegible applications shall be returned to the sponsoring firm without action.
1.4 Allow fourteen (14) working days for processing. Expedited processing is not available.

SECTION 5 — DECLARATIONS

5.1 The applicant declares that the particulars given are correct and consents to verification of employment, licensure and criminal history to the extent permitted by law.
5.2 The credential remains the property of the Authority, shall be worn visibly above the waist at all times within Authority premises, and shall be surrendered on completion of the work, on demand, or on separation from the sponsoring firm, whichever first occurs.
5.3 Loss or theft shall be reported to the Communications Desk immediately and confirmed in writing within twenty-four (24) hours.
5.4 Right-of-way access is conditional on current Track Safety Certification. An expired certification voids the credential automatically.
