TEMPORARY WORKER REGISTRATION — PART ONE: PERSONAL PARTICULARS

Form TW/1 (03/16). Part One is completed by the worker. Parts Two and Three, which cover availability, trade skills and referee details, are issued separately by the Placement Desk once Part One has been accepted.

HOW TO COMPLETE THIS PART

Read the whole of Part One before entering anything. Every question in Sections A to D must be answered, and the form must be signed and dated at Section E in the presence of a member of branch staff. Where a tick box is printed, place a single cross in one box only; where a line is printed, print in capitals and keep your writing within the line. If you run out of space, continue on the reverse of this sheet, marking clearly which section the continuation belongs to. Registration cannot proceed until the original of your identification document has been produced at the counter; photocopies brought from home are not accepted, and no assignment will be offered until the counter check has been completed and initialled by the Records Custodian.
