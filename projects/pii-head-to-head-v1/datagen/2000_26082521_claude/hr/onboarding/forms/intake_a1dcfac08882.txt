RELIEF PHARMACIST CASUAL REGISTER
APPLICATION AND CONTACT RECORD — FORM RP-58 (Rev. 11/2011)
Workforce Planning, Pharmacy Services

WHY THIS FORM EXISTS
- The casual register is the list from which unplanned shift vacancies are filled.
- Placement on the register is not an offer of employment and does not guarantee any minimum hours.
- An applicant remains on the register for twelve months from the date of acceptance, after which this form must be completed again.

HOW TO COMPLETE THIS FORM
- Complete in ink. Corrections are to be ruled through once, dated, and initialled. Do not use correction fluid.
- Where a bullet offers boxes, tick one box only unless the bullet says otherwise.
- Attach a copy of the current registration certificate and current professional indemnity cover. Do not attach originals.
- Hand the completed form to the district pharmacy manager or post it to the Workforce Planning desk at the support centre.
- Retain the tear-off acknowledgement slip at the foot of this form.
