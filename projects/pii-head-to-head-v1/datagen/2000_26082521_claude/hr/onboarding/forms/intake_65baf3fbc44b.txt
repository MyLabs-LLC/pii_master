REGISTRATION OF A VOLUNTEER AND APPLICATION FOR CRIMINAL HISTORY CLEARANCE
Form VOL-2 (Rev. 06/04)

1. GENERAL. Every person who proposes to render volunteer service upon District property, or who proposes to accompany pupils upon any school-sponsored excursion, shall register with the Office of School Safety and Community Participation prior to the commencement of such service, and shall renew such registration in each successive school year.

2. COMPLETION. The applicant shall complete Articles 4 through 7 inclusive in ink. The applicant shall not complete Article 8, which is reserved to the District. Where an article affords insufficient space, the applicant shall so indicate and shall append a signed continuation sheet.

3. FEES. The fee assessed by the State Bureau of Identification for the processing of fingerprint impressions shall be borne by the applicant, except where the applicant is the parent or guardian of a pupil then enrolled in the District.
