BEFORE YOU BEGIN
Read all notes below before entering anything on this form. The form is
completed once, at first attendance, and is then lodged with Personnel
Administration. Entries are transcribed to the operating system by the
Records Custodian; illegible forms are returned unprocessed to the
originating site and delay clearance to work.

NOTES FOR THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS FORM
 1. Write in capitals, one character per cell. Do not use abbreviations
    for names of places.
 2. Enter dates as day, month, four-digit year throughout.
 3. Rule a single diagonal line through any section that has been left
    blank deliberately and initial it in the margin.
 4. Sections 1 to 4 are completed by the individual. Section 5 is
    completed by the site and Section 6 by Personnel Administration.
 5. Do not staple. Do not photocopy a completed form.
 6. Queries on completion are directed to the Records Custodian at the
    originating site, not to the transport office.
