INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS FORM
Sections 1 and 2 are completed by the requesting Site Supervisor. Section 3 is completed by the worker in the presence of a site representative. One form per worker. Do not combine assignments. Submit to the Staffing Desk no later than 48 hours before the first shift. Incomplete forms will be returned unprocessed. Entries must be handwritten unless the form is being completed on the terminal workstation.
