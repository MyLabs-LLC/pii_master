Instructions to the Applicant

1. This form records a change of legal name, mailing address, or telephone of record. It is not used to correct grades, credit hours, or degree audit entries.
2. Print legibly. Entries that cannot be read are treated as omitted.
3. Documentary evidence must accompany a name change. Acceptable evidence is enumerated at clause 15.
4. The applicant retains no copy. A confirmation notice is mailed to the permanent address on file after posting.
