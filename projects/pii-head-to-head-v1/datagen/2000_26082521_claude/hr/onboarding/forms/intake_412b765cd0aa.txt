RECORDS RETENTION SCHEDULE — ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH SERVICES
Series EHS-100 through EHS-199 (Permit Administration)
Issued by the Records Custodian, 2011

This schedule governs the retention and lawful disposition of the record series enumerated below and supersedes all prior schedules touching upon the same series. Departure from this schedule requires the written concurrence of the Records Custodian and, where the record is subject to pending litigation or a formal public records request, the further concurrence of County Counsel.

SERIES EHS-114 — Temporary Food Facility Permit Applications

Description: Completed applications upon Form EHS-114, together with attachments, deficiency notice cards, fee receipts, and the office-use-only disposition block.

Retention: Three (3) years from the expiration date of the permit issued, or from the date of the denial disposition where no permit issued, whichever is later. Applications returned at the counter for incompleteness are not records of this series, no application number having been assigned, and shall not be retained.

Disposition: Confidential destruction. Applications contain identifying particulars furnished by the applicant and shall not be placed in ordinary waste. Destruction shall be witnessed and logged.

SERIES EHS-118 — Intake Registers

Description: Bound or electronic registers recording application number, date received, category code, fee tendered, and receipt number.

Retention: Ten (10) years from close of the fiscal year in which the register was opened.

Disposition: Transfer to the County Archive. Registers constitute the primary index to the EHS-114 series and shall not be destroyed while any application within the corresponding period remains subject to retention.

SERIES EHS-122 — Field Inspection Reports and Correction Notices

Retention: Five (5) years from the date of the final inspection recorded thereon. Where an inspection gives rise to an enforcement action, the report shall be retained for five (5) years from the final resolution of that action.

Disposition: Confidential destruction, subject to the enforcement exception above.

SERIES EHS-131 — Procedural Issuances and Interoffice Memoranda

Retention: Permanent for issuances of general application. Superseded issuances shall be retained in the historical file and shall be marked as superseded upon the face of the document, with the date of supersession and the initials of the Records Custodian.

Disposition: Retain in place. No routine destruction.

Requests for access to any record in the foregoing series shall be directed in writing to the Records Custodian and shall identify the series, the period, and the purpose of the request. Access to Series EHS-114 by persons other than the applicant or the applicant's authorized agent is subject to review for exempt content prior to release.
