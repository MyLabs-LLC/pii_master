DISTRIBUTION: Academic Deans; Bursar; Financial Aid Office

ARTICLE I — SCOPE AND INSTRUCTIONS

1. This application is required of any degree-seeking student who has not been enrolled for two (2) or more consecutive regular terms, exclusive of summer sessions.
2. A student who has attended any other collegiate institution during the period of absence shall arrange for an official transcript to be furnished directly to this Office by the issuing institution. A transcript furnished by the applicant will not be accepted.
3. Applications are considered in the order received. The deadline for each term is published in the Academic Calendar.
4. Readmission is not automatic. A student who departed while on academic probation, or who was academically dismissed, must additionally obtain the written endorsement of the dean of the college in which reentry is sought.
5. Complete all items. Enter "N/A" where an item does not apply. Do not leave any item blank.
