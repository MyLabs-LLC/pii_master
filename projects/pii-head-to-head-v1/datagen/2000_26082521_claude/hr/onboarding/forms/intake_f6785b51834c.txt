PURPOSE AND SCOPE OF THIS FORM

This form is used by the Staffing Unit to establish an applicant record for placement on the district substitute eligibility roster for the current fiscal year. The applicant completes Sections I through IV in their entirety and returns the form to the Records Custodian at the district administrative annex during posted intake hours. Entries must be printed legibly in blue or black ink, or typed. Correction fluid is not accepted; strike a single line through an error, enter the corrected value adjacent to it, and initial the margin. An application returned with any field in Sections I through III left blank is considered incomplete, and the Records Custodian will place it in suspense pending applicant contact rather than forwarding it to the credential verification queue.

SECTION I — APPLICANT IDENTITY

The applicant enters their legal name exactly as it appears on the credential or permit under which they intend to serve. Where a preferred name differs from the legal name, the applicant records it in the space provided; the district uses the preferred name for site rosters and building directories only.
