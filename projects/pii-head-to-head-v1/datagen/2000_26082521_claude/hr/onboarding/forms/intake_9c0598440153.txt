BRANCH INTAKE SHEET — APPLICANT IDENTITY AND CONTACT
Title and Escrow Operations, Form BI-7 (2021)

This sheet is completed once per applicant per file and is placed at the front of the working file. The escrow assistant taking the information writes in ink, prints all names, and does not abbreviate a legal name. Where the applicant is not present in the office, the assistant records the manner in which the information was received. No field is left empty; where an item does not apply, the assistant enters a dash. The completed sheet is signed by the assistant and reviewed by the escrow officer before the file is worked.
