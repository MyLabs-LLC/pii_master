NOTICE TO THE APPLICANT

You are asked to complete this form in full at the time of your first visit to the branch. It is not an application for a particular job. It is the record by which the branch identifies you, reaches you when work becomes available, and keeps your file separate from the files of other applicants with similar names, of whom there are more than you might suppose. For that reason the branch asks for several pieces of identifying information that may seem redundant, and for more than one way of reaching you. Nothing on this form is optional unless the item itself says so. Where an item does not apply, write the words DOES NOT APPLY. Do not leave an item blank, do not write SEE ABOVE, and do not attach a résumé in place of an answer, as résumés are filed separately and are not consulted by the dispatch desk. Complete the form in ink. Corrections should be made by drawing a single line through the incorrect entry and initialing beside it; do not use correction fluid, as this form is photographed.

ITEM 1 — NAME

Print your full legal name exactly as it appears on your Social Security card. If the card shows a name you no longer use, print that name here anyway and then print the name you now use on the second line, and bring the card and any court order or marriage certificate to the counter so the Records Custodian may sight them.
