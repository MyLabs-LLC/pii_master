ESCROW OPERATIONS — DISBURSEMENT AUTHORIZATION INSTRUCTION SHEET
Form TE-227 (Rev. 08/2021) · Retain original in file jacket · Copy to Accounting

PURPOSE
This sheet records the disbursement instructions applicable to a single escrow file. It is completed by the assigned escrow officer following receipt of signed settlement statements and is countersigned by the funding desk prior to release of funds.
