INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS FORM

1. This form shall be completed in its entirety by the applicant personally and by no other party purporting to act on the applicant's behalf, save where a reasonable accommodation has been authorized in advance and in writing by the Records Custodian or a duly designated deputy thereof. Entries shall be made in ink of black or dark blue color, or by typewritten or machine-printed impression.

2. No item shall be left vacant. Where an item is inapplicable to the circumstances of the applicant, the applicant shall inscribe the notation "N/A" in the space provided; a blank space shall be construed as an omission and shall cause the form to be returned as deficient.

3. Erasures are prohibited. Any correction shall be effected by a single horizontal strike-through, adjacent to which the applicant shall place their initials and the date of correction. Correction fluid, correction tape, and overwriting are not accepted.

4. The completed instrument shall be transmitted under sealed cover to the District Office, Attention: Records Custodian, not later than the close of business on the third (3rd) business day following the date of the conditional offer of employment.
