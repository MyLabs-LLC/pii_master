SECTION I — INSTRUCTIONS TO THE APPLICANT

Read this section before writing anything. This worksheet is the only record the branch keeps of how to reach you, and every assignment offered to you proceeds from it. Use a black or blue pen and print in the spaces provided. Do not use abbreviations except for state names. If a question does not apply to you, write the letters N/A in the space rather than leaving it empty, so that the Records Custodian can distinguish an answer from an omission. When you have finished, return the worksheet to the staffing coordinator at the counter, who will review it with you before it is filed. Do not fold or staple this form.
