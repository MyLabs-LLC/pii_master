NOTICE AND INSTRUCTIONS

The applicant is hereby advised that no person shall be admitted to any area situated behind the pharmacy counter, nor to any stockroom, receiving dock, or controlled-substance storage area, unless and until a credential has been issued pursuant to this application and is displayed conspicuously upon the outer garment at all times while upon the premises. Completion of this application confers no right of entry. The application shall be delivered to the Records Custodian not fewer than five (5) business days prior to the date upon which access is first sought, together with such supporting documentation as may be required by the sponsoring department.
