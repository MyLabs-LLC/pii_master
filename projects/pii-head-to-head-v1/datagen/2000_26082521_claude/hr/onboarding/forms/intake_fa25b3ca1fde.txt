INSTRUCTIONS TO THE INTAKE CLERK AND TO THE APPLICANT.

 (1) This worksheet shall be prepared at the time an order is first taken,
     whether such order be taken in person, by telephone, or by facsimile
     transmission, and shall precede the opening of any escrow account.

 (2) Every item appearing in Part One shall be obtained from the applicant
     directly and not from any prior file, it being the policy of the Company
     that particulars be verified anew upon each order taken.

 (3) Part Two constitutes the register of those persons whom the applicant has
     authorized to receive information concerning the order.  No disclosure
     shall be made to any person not entered upon the register, and no entry
     shall be added to the register except upon the written direction of the
     applicant lodged with the escrow officer of record.

 (4) Where an item cannot be furnished at the time of intake, the clerk shall
     enter the notation "TO FOLLOW" and shall bring the deficiency to the
     attention of the escrow officer within one business day.

 (5) No item within the office-use block shall be completed by the applicant.
