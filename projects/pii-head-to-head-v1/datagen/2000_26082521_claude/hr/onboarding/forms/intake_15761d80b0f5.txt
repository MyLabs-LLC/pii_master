RETENTION: Seven (7) years following account closure

INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS FORM
1. Print legibly in black or blue ink. Do not use pencil. Typewritten entries are acceptable.
2. Complete every field in Sections 1 through 3. Enter "N/A" where a field does not apply. Do not leave a field blank.
3. Leave Section 4 blank. Section 4 is completed by Division staff only.
4. Submit the completed form in person at the Customer Service counter or by first-class mail to the Customer Accounts Division. Faxed applications are not accepted for new connections.
5. Attach one (1) legible photocopy of a government-issued photographic identification credential and one (1) instrument of property interest (deed, recorded lease, or executed purchase agreement).
6. Incomplete applications are returned without processing and do not establish a place in the connection queue.
