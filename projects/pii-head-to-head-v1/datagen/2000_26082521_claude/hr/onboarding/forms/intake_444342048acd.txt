REQUEST FOR ITEMIZED STATEMENT AND COPIES OF BILLING RECORDS
Hospital Billing Office — Form BR-47 (Rev. 11/2021)

A.  PURPOSE AND INSTRUCTIONS
    1.  Use this form to request an itemized statement of charges, a payment history, or copies of billing correspondence.
    2.  This form does not release clinical records. Requests for clinical records go to the Health Information Management department on the ground floor.
    3.  Print clearly. The Billing Office cannot process a request it cannot read.
    4.  Complete Sections B, C, and D. Sign and date Section E.
    5.  The first itemized statement in a twelve-month period is furnished without charge. A per-page fee applies to additional copies and is quoted before the copies are produced.
    6.  Allow ten (10) business days from the date of receipt.
    7.  Direct questions to the Billing Office during posted hours or to the Records Custodian for questions about older records.
