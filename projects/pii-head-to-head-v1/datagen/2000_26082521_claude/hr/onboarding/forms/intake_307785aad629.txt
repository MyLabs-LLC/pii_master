INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS FORM

1. Complete Sections A through C in blue or black ink. Do not use pencil. Do not use correction fluid; strike a single line through an error, enter the correction beside it, and initial in the margin.

3. The applicant must complete Sections A and B in the applicant's own hand. Staff may complete Section C on the applicant's behalf where the applicant so requests, but the applicant must initial each line so completed.
4. Do not detach the office-use block at the foot of this form.
