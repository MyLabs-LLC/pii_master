PART I — INSTRUCTIONS TO THE APPLICANT

1. Print legibly in black or blue ink. Applications completed in pencil shall be returned unprocessed.
2. Complete Sections A, B and C in full. Enter "N/A" in any item that does not apply. Do not leave items blank.
3. Do not write in shaded areas or below the double rule. Those areas are reserved to the Authority.
4. Attach one (1) unmounted photograph, head and shoulders, not larger than 2" x 2", to the reverse of this form with the clip provided. Do not staple.
5. Present this application in person at any Card Services counter during posted hours, together with the documentary proof required under Tariff Rule 9.04. Applications received by mail are not accepted.
6. Retention of this application by the Authority does not constitute issuance of a card. No fare privilege attaches until the card is produced and countersigned.
