REGISTRY USER ACCESS REQUEST AND CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT
Form IZ-08 | Rev. 06/2011 | Page 1 of 1

PURPOSE
• This form establishes an individual user account in the county immunization information system. One form is required per user. Group or shared accounts are not issued under any circumstance.

COMPLETION INSTRUCTIONS
• Type or print legibly. Illegible submissions are returned to the requesting site.
• All items in Sections 1 through 4 are mandatory unless marked optional.
• The Site Administrator must sign Section 5 before the form is forwarded to the Immunization Program.
• Forward the completed original by interoffice mail or facsimile to the Immunization Program office. Retain a copy at the requesting site.
• Allow ten (10) business days for account provisioning.
