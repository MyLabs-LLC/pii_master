INTEROFFICE MEMORANDUM
Division of Environmental Health Services — Permit Intake Unit

TO: All Intake Clerks and Field Sanitarians
FROM: The Program Supervisor, Permit Intake Unit
SUBJECT: Handling of Form EHS-114 Applications Returned for Incompleteness
EFFECTIVE: Immediately upon distribution

It has come to the attention of this office that a substantial and growing proportion of applications submitted upon Form EHS-114 are being accepted at the counter notwithstanding the presence of vacant fields, and are thereafter returned to the applicant at a later stage of processing, occasioning both delay to the applicant and unnecessary handling within this Unit. Staff are reminded that the instruction printed at the head of the form is not advisory in character but operates as a condition precedent to filing.

Accordingly, the following procedure shall be observed without exception.

First, upon presentation of an application at the counter, the receiving clerk shall conduct a facial review of Parts I through III, confirming only that each field bears an entry or the notation "N/A." The clerk shall not undertake any evaluation of the substantive sufficiency of the entries, that function being reserved to the assigned Field Sanitarian.

Second, where a vacant field is observed, the clerk shall decline to stamp the instrument received, shall return it across the counter to the person presenting it, and shall furnish a copy of the deficiency notice card, indicating thereon by circle the part or parts requiring completion. No fee shall be accepted in such circumstances.

Third, where the application is facially complete, the clerk shall apply the received stamp, accept the fee, issue a numbered receipt, and enter the assigned application number in the intake register. The office-use-only block shall be completed to the extent of the entries then known, and the remainder left vacant for the reviewing officer.

Fourth, in no case shall a clerk complete any portion of Parts I through III on behalf of an applicant, whether at the applicant's request or otherwise. Where an applicant requires assistance, the clerk may read the field labels aloud and may direct the applicant to the interpreter service maintained by this Department, but the entries shall be made in the applicant's own hand.

Questions concerning the application of this memorandum should be directed to the Program Supervisor. A copy shall be posted at each intake window and a further copy transmitted to the Records Custodian for filing with the current-year procedural issuances.
