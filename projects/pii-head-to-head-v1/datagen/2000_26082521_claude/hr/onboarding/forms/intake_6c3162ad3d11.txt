INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS FORM

1. Complete every field in Sections A through D in black ink and in block capitals. Do not use pencil.

4. Alterations shall be struck through with a single line and initialled in the margin. Correction fluid is not permitted.
5. The completed form is to be handed to the Recruitment Coordinator or placed in the sealed personnel tray at the depot office.
