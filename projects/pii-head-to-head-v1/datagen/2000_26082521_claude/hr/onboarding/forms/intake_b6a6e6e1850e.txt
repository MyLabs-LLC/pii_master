PURPOSE AND GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS

This form is used to open a registration file for any person seeking temporary or permanent placement through this branch. It is completed by the applicant at the counter, checked by the Registration Clerk before the applicant leaves the premises, and countersigned by the Records Custodian at the close of business on the day of registration. Applicants should allow twenty minutes for completion and should bring the original of one photographic identification document and one document evidencing their current address. The form is to be completed in ink throughout. Entries made in pencil, entries obscured by correction fluid, and forms bearing unsigned alterations will be rejected and the applicant asked to complete a fresh copy. Where a question does not apply, enter the letters N/A across the space so that it is clear the question has been read and considered rather than overlooked. Applicants who require the questions to be translated or read aloud should say so before beginning, and the branch will arrange assistance at no charge.
