      A. COMPLETION INSTRUCTIONS
            1. Complete in blue or black ink. Do not use pencil.
            2. Print legibly. One character per box where boxes are provided.
            3. All items are mandatory unless marked (Opt.).
            4. Strike a single line through corrections. Initial each strike-through. Do not obliterate.
            5. Do not detach Part D. Part D is completed by escrow staff only.
            6. Return the completed worksheet to the Records Custodian within five (5) business days of file opening.
            7. Attach continuation sheet TE-114A if space is insufficient.
