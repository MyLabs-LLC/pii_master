  I. NOTICE AND INSTRUCTIONS TO THE REQUESTING PARTY
  ==================================================
  ( ) This request shall be tendered to the Office of the Records Custodian not fewer than ten (10) working days in advance of the date upon which access is first required. Requests tendered in a lesser interval may be denied without further consideration.

  ( ) The Authority reserves the unqualified right to deny, suspend, condition, or revoke any authorization issued hereunder, at any time and without prior notice, in the interest of the safe and efficient operation of the transit system.
  ( ) Retain the applicant's copy. It must be produced upon request at any controlled point of entry.
