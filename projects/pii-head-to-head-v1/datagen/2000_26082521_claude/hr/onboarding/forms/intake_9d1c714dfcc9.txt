Instructions to the person completing this form. This form is completed by the parent or legal guardian of the pupil whose transfer is requested, or by the pupil themselves where the pupil has attained the age of majority and is enrolled in their own right. The requesting party should read the entirety of the form before entering any value, as the sequence of sections does not correspond to the sequence in which the department evaluates them. Every field carrying the notation (req.) must bear an entry. Fields not so marked may be left blank where the applicable circumstance does not obtain, but the requesting party should not enter a dash, the notation "N/A," or any similar mark in place of a value, as the department's intake procedure treats any non-blank entry as an assertion of fact. The completed form is submitted to the Enrollment Coordinator at the pupil services counter. Submissions received after the fifteenth day of the month are evaluated in the following month's cycle.
