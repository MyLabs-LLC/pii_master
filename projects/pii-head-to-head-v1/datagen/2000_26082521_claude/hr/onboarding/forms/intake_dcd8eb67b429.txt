INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS FORM

1. Complete Sections A through E in blue or black ink. Print legibly in block capitals. Do not use pencil.
2. Every field marked with an asterisk (*) is required. An application returned with a required field left blank will be held in suspense and no service order will be issued.
3. Where a field does not apply, enter "N/A". Do not leave the field empty.
4. Present one item of photo identification and one item of proof of occupancy at the service counter. Identification is inspected and returned; the Department does not retain photocopies.
5. Corrections shall be made by drawing a single line through the entry and initialling beside it. Correction fluid is not accepted.
6. The completed form is to be lodged at the counter or placed in the drop box. Applications received after 3:00 p.m. are processed on the following business day.
