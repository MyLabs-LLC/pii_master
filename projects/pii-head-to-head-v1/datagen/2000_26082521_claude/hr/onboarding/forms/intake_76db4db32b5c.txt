MEMORANDUM

TO:      Applicants for Reclassification of Residency Status
FROM:    Office of the Registrar — Residency Determination Unit
SUBJECT: Petition for Reclassification to Resident Status for Tuition Purposes
FORM:    REG-330 (Rev. 01/2011)

PREAMBLE

1. This petition is filed by a student who has been classified as a nonresident for tuition purposes and who asserts that the classification should be changed.
2. The burden of proof rests upon the petitioner. The Residency Determination Unit will decide the petition upon the written record submitted.
3. A petition must be received not later than the last business day preceding the first day of instruction of the term for which reclassification is sought. A petition received thereafter will be considered for the following term only.
4. Supporting documentation must be attached. Original documents will not be returned.
