OFFICE OF THE REGISTRAR
FORM RG-341 (Rev. 03/2004)
APPLICATION FOR READMISSION AND REACTIVATION OF STUDENT RECORD

1.0 GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS
   • Submit one application per intended term of return.
   • Complete Sections 2.0 through 5.0 in full. Applications with omitted items are held in suspense for thirty calendar days and thereafter destroyed.
   • Applicants absent for four or more consecutive terms are subject to the catalog in force at the time of readmission.
   • Applicants with an outstanding obligation to any university unit will not be reactivated until the obligation is cleared by the responsible office.
   • Attach official transcripts from every institution attended during the period of absence. Transcripts must arrive under separate cover from the issuing institution.
