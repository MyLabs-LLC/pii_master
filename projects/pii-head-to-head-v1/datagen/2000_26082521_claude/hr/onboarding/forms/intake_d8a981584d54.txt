Form CS-462 (Rev. 01/99)                                  Sheet 1 of 1

1.  ELIGIBILITY. The applicant must (a) have held continuous service at the
    point of service named below for not less than twelve consecutive billing
    periods, (b) carry no arrears exceeding one billing period at the date of
    application, and (c) be billed on a residential schedule.

2.  HOW TO COMPLETE THIS FORM. Print in ink. Enter one character per box
    where boxes are shown. Leave unused boxes blank. Do not abbreviate the
    name of record. Return the completed sheet to the Billing Services
    Section not later than the fifteenth day of the month in which enrolment
    is to commence.
