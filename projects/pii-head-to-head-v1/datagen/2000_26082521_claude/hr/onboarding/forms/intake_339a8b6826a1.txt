INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PETITIONER
The parent, legal guardian, or other person holding lawful custody of the pupil (hereinafter "the Petitioner") shall complete every part of this petition. The petition shall be filed with the Office of Pupil Assignment during the open filing period established annually by the Governing Board, being the first business day of February through the last business day of March. Petitions filed outside the open filing period shall be considered only upon a showing of changed circumstances arising after the close of that period. The Petitioner is advised that transportation shall not be furnished by the District to a pupil attending a school other than the school of residence, and that approval, if granted, is granted for a single academic year and does not vest any right of continued enrollment.
