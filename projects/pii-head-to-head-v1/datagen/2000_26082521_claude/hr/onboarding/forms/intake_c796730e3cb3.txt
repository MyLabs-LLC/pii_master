**INSTRUCTIONS TO THE REQUESTING PARTY**

* Complete every item set forth in Blocks 1 through 4 hereunder. A request that omits any required item shall be deemed deficient and shall be returned to the requesting party without prejudice to resubmission.
* Describe the record or records sought with such particularity as will permit a custodial employee of ordinary diligence to locate the same without recourse to conjecture. Requests framed in general or categorical terms shall be construed narrowly.
* Be advised that fees for search, retrieval, review, and reproduction are assessed in accordance with the published Schedule of Charges then in effect, and that the Authority may require payment or a deposit in advance of processing.
* Submit this form by hand delivery to the Office of the Records Custodian, or by first-class mail, addressed to the Office at the address posted upon the Authority's public notice board.
