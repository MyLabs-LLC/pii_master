OFFICE OF THE REGISTRAR — RESIDENCY CLASSIFICATION UNIT

PETITION FOR RECLASSIFICATION OF RESIDENCY STATUS FOR TUITION PURPOSES

PURPOSE AND FILING INSTRUCTIONS
- [ ] This petition establishes the record of a request to reclassify tuition residency status. It does not itself alter any assessment already posted to a student account.
- [ ] Filing deadline is the last business day prior to the first day of instruction for the term in which reclassification is sought. Petitions filed after that date are held for the subsequent term.
- [ ] One petition per term. Duplicate filings are consolidated under the earliest date stamp.
- [ ] All items are mandatory unless expressly marked optional. Incomplete petitions are returned unprocessed with a deficiency notice.
- [ ] Supporting documentation must be submitted as legible photocopies. Original documents are not retained by this office.
- [ ] Do not staple. Use the binder clip provided at the service counter.
- [ ] Entries below the double rule are made by the Residency Officer only.
