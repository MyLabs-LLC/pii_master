PART ONE — DIRECTIONS TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS APPLICATION

• Print legibly in black or blue ink. Applications completed in pencil, or containing erasures, strikeovers, or correction fluid, will be rejected in their entirety and must be resubmitted.
• Answer every item. Where an item is inapplicable, enter the notation "N/A"; do not leave any item blank.
• An application which is unsigned, undated, or unaccompanied by the certification required under Part Four shall be deemed abandoned sixty (60) calendar days after receipt.
• Do not submit currency by mail. Fees are payable only at a Customer Service Center.
