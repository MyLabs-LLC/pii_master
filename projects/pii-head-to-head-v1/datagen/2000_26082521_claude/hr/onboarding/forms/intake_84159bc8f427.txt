MERIDIAN TITLE AND ESCROW COMPANY
ESCROW APPLICATION, PART B -- CO-APPLICANT PARTICULARS AND ADDRESS FOR THE
SERVICE OF NOTICES.  FORM ET-114B (REV. 09/97).

DIRECTIONS.  Part B is required only where two or more parties are to be named
as applicants upon a single order.  The co-applicant, or a representative duly
empowered in writing to act for the co-applicant, shall furnish each of the
particulars enumerated below.  Where the co-applicant maintains an address for
the service of notices differing from the mailing address stated at Part A,
such address shall be set forth at items B-5 and B-6, and all notices shall
thereafter be directed thereto.  An entry once made shall not be altered save
by a further writing executed with the same formality as the original.
