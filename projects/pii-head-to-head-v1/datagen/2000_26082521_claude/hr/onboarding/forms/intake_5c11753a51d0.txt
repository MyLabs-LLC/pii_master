THE HOSPITAL BUSINESS OFFICE
              DESIGNATION OF AUTHORIZED REPRESENTATIVE
              FOR PURPOSES OF ACCOUNT ADMINISTRATION
                     FORM PFS-088 (REV. 04/96)

DIRECTIONS. The person seeking designation as an authorized
representative (hereinafter "the applicant") shall complete Parts A
through D below and shall present this instrument in person to the
Records Custodian, together with one form of photographic
identification, which identification shall be examined but not
retained. No entry shall be made by the applicant in Part E. This
instrument confers no authority whatsoever until the entries in
Part E have been executed by the Business Office.
