APPLICATION FOR READMISSION FOLLOWING WITHDRAWAL OR ABSENCE OF TWO OR MORE CONSECUTIVE TERMS

ARTICLE I — GENERAL

1. A former student whose enrollment has lapsed for two or more consecutive regular terms, summer sessions excepted, shall apply for readmission on this form prior to registration.
2. Application shall be filed not later than the fifteenth (15th) day of the month preceding the opening of the term sought.
3. A non-refundable readmission service charge is payable at the Cashier's window at the time of filing. The Cashier's receipt shall be stapled to the face of this form.
4. Readmission is granted under the catalog in force at the term of re-entry unless continuity of catalog is separately petitioned.
