SUBJECT: Form HR-402 (Rev. 09/2016) — Authorization to Amend Identity and Contact Particulars of Record

1. PURPOSE. This instrument is provided for the use of any colleague who desires that the identity or contact particulars maintained in the personnel record be amended. It shall not be used to effect changes to benefit elections, direct deposit instructions, or withholding designations, each of which is governed by a separate instrument.

2. SUBSTANTIATION. An amendment to a legal name shall be supported by a certified copy of the instrument effecting such change. An amendment to a residential or mailing address requires no substantiation but shall be attested by the colleague below.

3. EFFECTIVE DATE. An amendment shall take effect upon entry into the personnel system by the Records Custodian and not upon submission.
