INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS INSTRUMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. This statement is to be completed in its entirety by, or at the express
     direction of, each applicant party to the contemplated transaction, and is
     to be returned to the Underwriting Intake Unit not later than the third
     business day following the date upon which it was tendered.

  2. Each field is to be typed or hand-printed in block capitals, one character
     to each ruled position where positions are indicated. Entries which extend
     beyond the ruled field shall be continued upon the Continuation Sheet
     appended hereto and shall be cross-referenced by item number.

  3. Where an applicant party is other than a natural person, the identity of
     the signatory and the instrument conferring authority upon that signatory
     shall be recited at Item 9, and evidence of such authority shall accompany
     this statement upon its return.

  4. Omissions, illegible entries, and internally inconsistent entries will
     occasion the return of this statement without action, and no commitment
     shall issue until a conforming statement has been lodged with the Company.

  5. The person completing this instrument shall not inscribe any entry within
     the block appearing at the foot hereof, the same being reserved to the
     Company. Corrections shall be effected by single strike-through, initialed
     and dated by the party making the same; correction fluid is not permitted.
