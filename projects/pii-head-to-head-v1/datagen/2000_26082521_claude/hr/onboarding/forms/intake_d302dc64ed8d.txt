APPLICATION FOR READMISSION AND REACTIVATION OF AN INACTIVE RECORD
Form REG-52 (Rev. 6/99)                          Fee: nonrefundable, payable at the Bursar

READ BEFORE COMPLETING

1.  This application is required of any former student whose record has been inactive for two or more consecutive regular terms, and of any student separated by academic action, administrative withdrawal, or leave of absence.
2.  Type or print in black ink. Sign at clause 27. Unsigned applications are not processed and are not held.
3.  Deadlines: sixty days before the first day of classes for the fall term; forty-five days for the spring term; thirty days for either summer session.
4.  An application is not complete until the Bursar has cleared all prior indebtedness and the Library has released all holds. Clearance is verified by this office; the applicant need not obtain signatures.
5.  Transcripts of all work attempted elsewhere during the period of separation must be requested by the applicant from the issuing institution and sent directly to this office. Hand-carried transcripts are not accepted.
