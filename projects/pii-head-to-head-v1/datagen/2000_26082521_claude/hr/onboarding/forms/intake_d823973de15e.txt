MEMORANDUM

FROM:     Office of the Records Custodian
TO:       Applicants Seeking Relief Under the Levelized Payment Program
FORM:     CA-233 (Rev. 09/04) — Application for Enrollment in the Levelized Payment Program and, in the Alternative, for Hardship Deferral

1.  STATEMENT OF PURPOSE AND APPLICABILITY

1.1  This form is provided for the use of any residential customer of record who desires to have the amounts billed for water, sanitary sewer, and stormwater utility service levelized over a rolling twelve-month period, or who, being unable to satisfy a current obligation, seeks a deferral of collection activity pursuant to the hardship provisions of the utility regulations.

1.2  Enrollment is not automatic. Enrollment is conditioned upon the applicant's account being free of any unsatisfied final notice at the time of tender, or upon the applicant's execution of a separate arrearage agreement in a form acceptable to the Supervisor of Customer Accounts.

2.  INSTRUCTIONS

2.1  Complete Sections 3 and 4 in full. Where an item is inapplicable, enter the notation "N/A"; do not leave an item blank.
2.2  Attach such documentary proof as is described in Section 5. Original documents will not be retained; copies will be made at the counter and the originals returned.
2.3  Retain the applicant's carbon copy. The Department will not furnish duplicates.
