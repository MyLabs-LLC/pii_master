1. This worksheet accompanies every consumer loan application on which a second party is named. It is not itself an application and confers no obligation upon the credit union.
2. Prepare one worksheet per co-applicant. Where more than one co-applicant is named, number the worksheets consecutively in the space at clause 9 below.
3. Print legibly in block capitals. One character per space where boxed spaces are provided.
