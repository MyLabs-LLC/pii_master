SUBJECT: Request for Official Academic Transcript

PART I — INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS FORM

1. This form authorizes the release of an official academic transcript. Requests submitted by telephone or by electronic mail cannot be accepted.
2. Print legibly. Illegible or incomplete requests will be returned without action.
3. A processing fee is assessed for each copy requested. Requests bearing an outstanding financial obligation to the University will be held until the obligation is satisfied.
4. Allow five (5) to seven (7) business days for processing during ordinary periods, and up to fifteen (15) business days during the two weeks following the close of any term.
5. Requests must be signed by the record holder. A third party may not sign on the record holder's behalf absent a properly executed power of attorney on file with the Records Custodian.
