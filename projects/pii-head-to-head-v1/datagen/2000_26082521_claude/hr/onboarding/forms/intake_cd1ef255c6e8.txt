CLAIM FOR REFUND OR RESTORATION OF VALUE
LOST, STOLEN, DEFECTIVE OR CAPTURED FARE MEDIA

READ BEFORE COMPLETING

1. A claim will be considered only where the media was registered with the Authority before the date of loss. Unregistered media carries no refund right.
2. File within sixty (60) days of the occurrence. Late claims are barred under Tariff Rule 12.11 and will not be reviewed.
3. Complete Items 5 to 14 in ink. Strike through errors with a single line and initial; do not erase or use correction fluid.
4. Do not enclose currency, fare media or original receipts. Enclose photocopies only.
