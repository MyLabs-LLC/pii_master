  A.  FILING INSTRUCTIONS
      1.  Submit within ten (10) working days of the change. Retroactive corrections beyond one payroll cycle require Unit Manager approval at Item F.4.
      2.  Complete Section B in all cases. Complete only those subsequent sections affected by the change.
      3.  Enter prior and new entries side by side. Strike-throughs are not accepted; submit a new form.
      4.  Name changes require presentation of a supporting legal document to the Payroll Window. The document is examined and returned.
      5.  Forward the completed original to the Payroll and Benefits Unit through the site administrator. Do not transmit by electronic mail.
