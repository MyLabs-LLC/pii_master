1.  INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS SHEET
    1.1  This sheet establishes the master contact record used for assignment
         dispatch and payroll correspondence. Accuracy is the responsibility of
         the person completing it.
    1.2  Complete all shaded and unshaded items in Parts 2 through 4.
    1.3  Where a check box is provided, mark one box only unless the item states
         otherwise.
    1.4  Strike through any error with a single line, enter the correction
         adjacent to it, and initial the correction. Do not use correction fluid.
    1.5  Submit the completed sheet to the branch Records Custodian. Retain no
         photocopy.
