REQUEST FOR INSPECTION OR COPIES OF DEPARTMENTAL RECORDS
Form RC-03 (Rev. 11/2016)

1.  INSTRUCTIONS TO THE REQUESTER

    1.1  Complete every clause of Parts 2, 3 and 4. Parts 5 and 6 are completed by departmental staff.
    1.2  Describe the records sought with sufficient particularity to permit an officer unfamiliar with the matter to locate them. A request framed in general terms may be returned for clarification, and the statutory response period runs afresh from the date any clarified request is received.
    1.3  A requester is not obliged to state a reason for the request. Clause 4.5 is optional and is used only to determine eligibility for a fee waiver.
    1.4  Copying charges are assessed at the schedule posted at the public counter. Where the estimated charge exceeds twenty-five dollars, the requester is contacted for authorisation before the work is undertaken.
    1.5  Lodge the completed form at the public counter, by post, or by electronic transmission to the address published on the departmental website.
