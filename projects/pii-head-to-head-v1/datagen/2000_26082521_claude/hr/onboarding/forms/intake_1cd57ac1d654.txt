INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

TO:        All Depot Administration Clerks, Carrier Services Division
FROM:      The Records Custodian
SUBJECT:   Withdrawal of Form DR-109; adoption of Form DR-114 (Rev. 08/97)

1.  EFFECT OF THIS MEMORANDUM

    Form DR-109 (Applicant Particulars Record, Rev. 03/94) is withdrawn with
    immediate effect. Stocks held at depot level are to be returned to
    Depot Administration for destruction. No DR-109 is to be issued to an
    applicant after the date of this memorandum.

2.  PRINCIPAL DIFFERENCES

    2.1  Section B has been restructured. The single free-text address block on
         DR-109 is replaced by discrete labelled lines (postal address line 1,
         postal address line 2, town/city, county/state, postcode). Clerks are
         reminded that the postcode field is boxed and takes one character per box.

    2.2  The emergency contact field has been reduced to relationship only.
         Clerks are not to solicit a name, number or address for this field at
         intake stage. That particular is collected at induction, on Form IN-22,
         and held on the personnel file rather than the intake file.

    2.3  Section E (office use) now carries a retention class and a destruction
         interval. Both are to be completed at the point of filing, not later.

3.  HANDLING OF COMPLETED FORMS

    3.1  A completed DR-114 is to be date-stamped on receipt and entered in the
         intake register on the same working day.

    3.2  Forms are to be held in the locked intake cabinet. They are not to be
         left in the shift tray overnight, nor carried between depots in an
         unsealed envelope.

    3.3  Where an applicant is not progressed, the form is retained under
         retention class C and destroyed at the interval entered at E.7.
         Destruction is by cross-cut shredder only.

4.  ACTION REQUIRED

    Depot clerks are to acknowledge receipt of this memorandum by initialling the
    circulation sheet attached to the depot copy and returning it to Depot
    Administration within five working days.

Circulation: Depot Managers; Depot Administration Clerks; Transport Office
