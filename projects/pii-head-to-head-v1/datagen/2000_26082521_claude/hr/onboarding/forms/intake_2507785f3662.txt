INSTRUCTIONS TO APPLICANT
  1. Complete every field in BLOCK CAPITALS. Black ink only.
  2. Enter one character per box. Leave one box clear between words.

     Correction fluid is not accepted.
  5. Hand the completed form to the front desk together with two
     forms of identification. Do not detach the lower block.
