REQUEST FOR ACCESS TO STUDENT RECORDS BY PARENT OR ELIGIBLE STUDENT
Form SR-22 (Rev. 01/2004)
Custodian of Records — District Administrative Center

1. SCOPE

1.1 This form is used to request inspection of, or copies from, the cumulative record maintained by the district.

1.2 A request is deemed received on the date it is stamped by the Records Custodian under Section 6.

2. INSTRUCTIONS

2.1 The requester shall complete Sections 3, 4 and 5 in full and present photographic identification at the time of submission.

2.2 A separate form is required for each student whose record is sought.

2.3 Copying charges are assessed per page in accordance with the fee schedule posted at the counter. Fees are payable before release.
