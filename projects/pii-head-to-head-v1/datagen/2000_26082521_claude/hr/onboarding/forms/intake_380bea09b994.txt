INTEROFFICE MEMORANDUM

TO:        Field Agents; Branch Managers; Records Custodians
FROM:      Office of the Vice President, Personal Lines Underwriting
SUBJECT:   Issuance of Form PL-207, Applicant Profile and Correspondence Worksheet
EFFECTIVE:  First business day of the current quarter
FORM CONTROL: PL-207 (04/2016). Prior editions are withdrawn and are not to be ordered from Supply.

CLAUSE 1 — SCOPE
1.1 Form PL-207 records the identity and correspondence particulars of each individual applying for coverage under the Personal Lines program.
1.2 The form accompanies, but does not replace, the signed application and any supplementary questionnaires required by the underwriting guide.

CLAUSE 2 — COMPLETION
2.1 The person completing this form shall do so in the presence of the applicant wherever practicable.
2.2 Entries shall be made in ink. Typewritten entries are acceptable provided the carbon set remains legible.
2.3 The completing party shall verify each identity entry against a government-issued photographic document and record the document type in Part IV.
2.4 No field may be left vacant. Enter "N/A" where a field does not apply and "REFUSED" where the applicant declines to answer.

CLAUSE 3 — CUSTODY
3.1 The original is forwarded to Home Office Records. The pink copy remains with the branch. The yellow copy accompanies the rating file.
3.2 Completed forms are confidential business records and are to be stored in a locked cabinet outside of business hours.
