SECTION 1 — INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS FORM

Complete every section of this record in black or blue ink before your registration interview begins. Print clearly within the ruled spaces provided. Where a field does not apply to you, write "N/A" rather than leaving the space empty, as blank spaces will be treated as incomplete and the record will be returned to you at the front desk. Do not write in the shaded block at the foot of the form. If you require assistance in completing any part of this record, ask the Registration Clerk on duty, who will read the questions aloud and enter your responses on your behalf.
