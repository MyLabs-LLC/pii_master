        DESIGNATION OF AUTHORISED AGENT AND THIRD-PARTY NOTIFICATION
                       Form CS-341 (Rev. 07/98)

PURPOSE. This form authorises the Department to disclose account particulars
to, and to accept instructions from, the persons named below, and to direct
a duplicate of any notice of arrears to a third party nominated by the
customer of record.

INSTRUCTIONS. Type or print. A designation remains in force until revoked in
writing by the customer of record. Designation of an agent does not relieve
the customer of record of liability for charges. Lodge the completed form
with the Records Custodian.
