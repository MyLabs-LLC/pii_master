MEMORANDUM — RECORDS DEPARTMENT

FORM RD-042: ANNUAL MEMBER FILE VERIFICATION SHEET (Rev. 09/2004)

1. GENERAL

1.1  This sheet is completed at the annual file review of each member account and is filed as the topmost document in the member's physical folder.
1.2  Complete one sheet per member file. Do not combine joint owners on a single sheet.
1.3  Entries are made in ink by the reviewing officer only. Members do not complete any part of this sheet.

4. VERIFICATION FINDINGS

4.1  Against each line at Sections 2 and 3, enter one of the following codes in the margin: V (verified against source document), D (discrepancy noted), or M (missing from file).
4.2  Where code D or M is entered, describe the exception at clause 4.3 and raise Form MS-118 or Form MS-114 as appropriate.

5. ACTION AND ESCALATION

5.1  A file bearing two or more exceptions at clause 4.3 is referred to the Records Custodian within five (5) business days of review.
5.2  A file for which the identification document of record has expired is placed on restricted status pending re-verification. Restriction is applied by the Records Custodian and by no other officer.
5.3  A file in which the residential address falls outside the field of membership is referred to Membership Services for eligibility determination; the account is not closed by the reviewing officer.
5.4  No entry at Section 2 or Section 3 is amended on this sheet. Amendments are made only on the forms cited at clause 4.2.

6. RETENTION

6.1  The completed sheet is retained for seven (7) years from the date of review.
6.2  The superseded sheet from the prior review is transferred to the archive box for the review year and is not destroyed at branch level.
