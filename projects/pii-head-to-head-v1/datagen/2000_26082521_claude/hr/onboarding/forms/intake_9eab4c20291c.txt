GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS FORM

Read all instructions before making any entry. Type or print legibly in black or dark blue ink. Every numbered item must carry an entry; where an item does not apply, enter the characters "N/A". A blank item is treated as an omission and the form will be returned. Do not erase. To correct an entry, draw a single line through it, enter the correct information above or beside it, and initial in the margin. This form must be completed by the applicant or, where the applicant is represented, by the representative holding written authority, in which case Item 22 must be completed. Submit the original to Central File; retain no photocopies outside the escrow jacket.
