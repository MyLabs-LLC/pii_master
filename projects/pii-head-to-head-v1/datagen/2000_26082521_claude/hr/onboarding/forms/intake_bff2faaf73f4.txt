APPLICANT PARTICULARS AND ENGAGEMENT RECORD
Document control: HR-INT-021 | Revision 4 | Effective 1 September 2021
Retain original at branch. Do not detach any part of this record.

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

    1.1 This record captures the identity, contact and availability particulars of an
        individual seeking engagement through the agency on a temporary, fixed-term
        or permanent basis.
    1.2 The record is to be completed once at first registration and reviewed on each
        anniversary of registration.
    1.3 All clauses at sections 3, 4 and 5 are mandatory.

2. INSTRUCTIONS TO THE APPLICANT

    2.1 Read every clause before entering any particulars.
    2.2 Write clearly within the ruled space provided. Entries extending beyond the
        ruled space shall be continued at clause 8.1 and cross-referenced.
    2.3 Do not use correction fluid. Errors shall be struck through once, corrected
        alongside, and initialled by the applicant.
    2.4 Sections marked "Office Use Only" shall not be completed by the applicant.
    2.5 The applicant shall notify the branch in writing of any change to the
        particulars recorded at sections 4 and 5 within five working days of the change.
