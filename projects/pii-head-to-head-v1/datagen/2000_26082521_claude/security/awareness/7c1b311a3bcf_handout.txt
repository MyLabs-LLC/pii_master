  Social Security Number ................ always
  Date of Birth ......................... always
  Card Number / Expiry / CVV ............ always
  Government ID Number .................. always

WHAT NEVER GOES IN E-MAIL
  Any field named above ................. no exception

  Loan packet ........................... portal only

  It claims to be staff you cannot find . verify first
  It gives half a number and waits ...... confirm nothing
