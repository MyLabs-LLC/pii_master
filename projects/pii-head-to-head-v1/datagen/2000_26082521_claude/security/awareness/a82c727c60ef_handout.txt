NORTHRIDGE VALLEY UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT
OFFICE OF RECORDS AND INFORMATION GOVERNANCE

ADMINISTRATIVE GUIDANCE HANDOUT 2016-04
PROTECTION OF PERSONALLY IDENTIFIABLE INFORMATION IN DAILY OPERATIONS

TO: All Certificated and Classified Staff; Site Clerical Personnel; Volunteers with Records Access
FROM: The District Records Custodian
ISSUED: August 2016
SUPERSEDES: Administrative Guidance Handout 2013-09
REVIEW CYCLE: Annual, on or before the first duty day of each school year

1. Purpose and scope. This handout describes the ordinary handling expectations for personally identifiable information held by the District in student, employee, and applicant records. It applies wherever such information is created, viewed, transmitted, printed, stored, or disposed of, including at school sites, at the District Office, at off-site meeting locations, and on District-issued portable equipment. Staff who encounter personally identifiable information incidentally, such as when covering a front counter or assisting during registration, are equally bound by these expectations.

2. Categories of protected identifiers. For purposes of this handout, protected identifiers include the following fields wherever they appear on a form, roster, screen, or report: Social Security Number; Date of Birth; Student Identification Number; State Student Identifier; Employee Identification Number; Home Address; Home Telephone Number; Personal Email Address; Parent or Guardian Name; Bank Routing Number; Bank Account Number; Payment Card Number; Driver License Number; Medicaid Number; Health Plan Member Number; Immigration or Visa Status; Free and Reduced Price Meal Eligibility Status; Special Education Eligibility Category; Disciplinary Incident Code. The combination of any two or more of these fields with a person's name is treated as a heightened-sensitivity record regardless of the medium in which it is held.

3. Materials requiring destruction by shredding. Any paper bearing a protected identifier is to be destroyed by cross-cut shredding rather than placed in recycling, trash, or classroom paper-reuse bins. This includes, without limitation, printed class rosters and seating charts containing the Student Identification Number field; attendance verification printouts; meal eligibility applications and their supporting income documentation; enrollment and withdrawal packets; health office logs and medication administration records; substitute teacher folders after the assignment concludes; payroll registers, direct deposit authorization forms, and benefits enrollment worksheets; transcript requests and their fax cover sheets; screening and referral forms generated by student support teams; and any handwritten note in which a staff member has transcribed a Date of Birth, a Social Security Number, or an Account Number for temporary convenience. Draft and duplicate copies are destroyed on the same schedule as originals, and no protected identifier is to remain on a whiteboard, an easel pad, or a sign-in sheet at the close of the business day.

4. Transmission restrictions. Protected identifiers are not to be sent by electronic mail, whether to an internal or an external recipient, and are not to be entered into instant messaging, personal cloud storage, personal devices, or any web form not published by the District. The prohibition extends to attachments, to screenshots, and to text placed in a subject line. Where a colleague requests information that would require such transmission, staff are to respond by directing the requester to the approved secure transfer facility maintained by the Technology Services Department, or by arranging a physical transfer under signature. A request to "just email it this once" is refused as a matter of course, and no exception is created by the seniority of the requester or by the urgency asserted.

5. Recognition of pretexting contacts. A pretexting call is one in which a caller adopts a false or unverifiable identity in order to obtain information that would not be released to a stranger. Such calls are commonly characterised by a manufactured deadline, by flattery or by expressed sympathy for the recipient's workload, by a claim of authority that cannot be checked in the District directory, by reluctance to provide a callback number, by a request to bypass the ordinary written request process, and by the caller supplying part of an identifier and inviting the recipient to confirm or correct the remainder. Confirmation is disclosure. Staff are to obtain the caller's name and organisation, decline to verify any field, terminate the call courteously, and report the contact.

6. Reporting and internal contacts. Suspected pretexting, misdirected correspondence, lost paper, or a missing device is reported the same duty day to the site Principal or designee and to the District Records Custodian. Questions concerning the release of student records are directed to the Director of Student Services. Questions concerning employee or payroll records are directed to the Director of Human Resources. Questions concerning devices, accounts, and secure transfer are directed to the Chief Technology Officer. Contact details for each office are published in the internal directory.
