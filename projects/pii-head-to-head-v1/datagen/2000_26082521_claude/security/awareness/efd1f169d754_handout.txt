HALSTEAD COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
OFFICE OF THE PRIVACY OFFICER
GUIDANCE HANDOUT NO. HCHD-PRIV-114
SAFEGUARDING OF PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS IN THE ORDINARY COURSE OF DEPARTMENTAL BUSINESS
Issued: March 2021. Supersedes Handout No. HCHD-PRIV-097 (rev. 2018).

PURPOSE AND AUTHORITY

This handout is promulgated by the Privacy Officer pursuant to the Department's administrative rules governing the confidentiality of health and enrollment records, and is directed to all employees, temporary staff, interns, volunteers, and contracted personnel who, in the discharge of their assigned duties, may foreseeably come into the possession of records bearing personal identifiers. For purposes hereof, the term "personal identifier" shall be construed broadly and shall include, without limitation, any data element recorded under the field labels Full Name, Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Medical Record Number, Member Identification Number, Case Number, Home Address, Home Telephone Number, Personal Email Address, Driver License Number, Bank Account Number, and Payment Card Number, together with any combination of lesser elements which, taken in the aggregate, would permit a reasonably diligent person to ascertain the identity of a particular individual. The obligations set forth herein attach without regard to the medium in which the identifier appears and without regard to whether the record originated within the Department or was received from an external agency, provider, or member of the public.

MATERIALS REQUIRING DESTRUCTION BY CROSS-CUT SHREDDING

Every employee is directed to deposit into the locked destruction consoles, and under no circumstances into ordinary waste or single-stream recycling receptacles, the following categories of material once their administrative or programmatic utility has been exhausted: intake and registration worksheets; appointment rosters and clinic day sheets; laboratory requisition carbons and result printouts; immunization record printouts; eligibility determination worksheets; benefit and premium notices; correspondence drafts bearing addressee blocks; sign-in sheets; mailing labels and label overrun sheets; facsimile cover sheets and transmission confirmations; handwritten notes containing any field enumerated in the preceding paragraph; and photocopy misfeeds, test pages, and alignment sheets generated during the reproduction of any of the foregoing. Staff shall not remove such materials from the premises for destruction elsewhere, shall not permit destruction consoles to remain unlocked or overfilled, and shall not attempt to retrieve material once deposited. Where a console is unavailable, the material shall be secured in a locked drawer and the condition reported the same business day to the Records Custodian.

PROHIBITED TRANSMISSION BY ELECTRONIC MAIL

No employee shall transmit, by electronic mail, in the body of a message or as an attachment thereto, any Social Security Number, Date of Birth in combination with a name, Medical Record Number, Member Identification Number, Bank Account Number, Payment Card Number, or any clinical result or diagnosis attributable to an identifiable person, to any address outside the Department's own mail domain, nor to any personal or non-departmental address whatsoever, including the employee's own. Internal transmission of such elements shall be avoided as a matter of routine practice and, where genuinely unavoidable, shall be limited to the minimum necessary, shall employ the approved encryption designation in the subject line, and shall be directed only to the individual holding the position requiring the disclosure. The forwarding, auto-forwarding, or rule-based redirection of departmental mail to any external service is prohibited absolutely.

RECOGNITION OF PRETEXT CONTACTS

Staff are advised that persons seeking unauthorized disclosure commonly present themselves by telephone as a member, a family member acting on a member's behalf, a treating provider's office, a laboratory courier, a vendor's billing department, an auditor, a technology support technician, or a senior official of the Department itself. The characteristic indicia of such a contact include the assertion of urgency; the invocation of authority coupled with an unwillingness to be called back through the published directory; a request that the employee read back, confirm, or "verify" an identifier which the caller purports already to possess; a request to disable, reset, or share credentials; an expressed preference for an unrecorded channel; and an appeal to the employee's helpfulness accompanied by mild reproach at any hesitation. Employees confronted with any such contact shall decline to confirm or deny the existence of any record, shall offer to return the call through the internal directory listing for the relevant program area, shall terminate the call if pressed, and shall reduce the substance of the contact to writing before the close of the same business day.

INTERNAL REPORTING BY ROLE

Questions concerning the retention, storage, or destruction of records shall be directed to the Records Custodian. Suspected or actual disclosure of personal identifiers, whether by misdirected mail, lost media, unattended documents, or pretext contact, shall be reported immediately and without prior investigation by the reporting employee to the Privacy Officer, who shall notify the Health Officer and the Information Security Manager as circumstances warrant. Matters concerning workstation configuration, encryption designations, or suspected malicious electronic mail shall be reported concurrently to the Information Security Manager. Program-specific disclosure questions shall be routed through the employee's Program Supervisor to the Privacy Officer. No employee shall be subject to retaliation for a report made in good faith under this handout.
