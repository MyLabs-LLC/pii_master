METROPOLITAN TRANSIT AUTHORITY
INTEROFFICE MEMORANDUM

TO:       All Divisional Clerks, Timekeepers, and Fare Media Staff
FROM:     Privacy Coordinator
SUBJECT:  Identifier Handling — Consolidated Checklist
COPIES:   Records Custodian; Divisional Security Officers; Internal Audit Manager

Retain in the divisional procedures binder behind Tab 4.

A. DAILY — CLEAR DESK

   * Lock all files bearing Social Security Number, Date of Birth, or Home Address before leaving the counter, including for meal relief.
   * Clear the facsimile tray. Unattended output is treated as a disclosure.
   * Clear the photocopier glass and the document feeder.
   * Log off the personnel terminal. Do not leave a session open under another clerk's sign-on.

B. DAILY — DESTRUCTION

   * Cross-cut shred: drafts, photocopies, misprints, adding machine tapes, facsimile confirmation sheets, sign-in sheets bearing Employee Number.
   * Cross-cut shred: paratransit trip manifests once reconciled.
   * Do not shred diskettes or tape media. Route under Form DP-22 to the Data Processing Manager.
   * Bins are emptied under observation. A clerk witnesses and initials the destruction log.

C. ELECTRONIC MAIL — DO NOT SEND

   * Social Security Number
   * Date of Birth in combination with Employee Number
   * Bank Routing Number, Direct Deposit Account Number
   * Operator Licence Number
   * Medical Certification Number, restriction codes, or return-to-duty status
   * Registrant lists or payroll extracts in any format, including attachments

   Reference records by Case File Number. Route the substance by internal mail in a sealed envelope marked RESTRICTED.

D. PRETEXTING — CHECKLIST FOR THE CLERK RECEIVING THE CALL

   * Does the caller refuse a return call to a published extension?           If yes — terminate.
   * Does the caller supply a partial identifier for confirmation?            If yes — terminate.
   * Does the caller cite a senior title without a work order number?         If yes — terminate.
   * Does the caller request facsimile to an unlisted machine?                If yes — terminate.
   * Is the call outside business hours to relief staff?                      If yes — terminate.
   * Has a similar call reached another extension this week?                  If yes — report immediately.

   Neither confirm nor deny that a file exists. Do not explain the refusal. Do not accept a promise of written authorisation to follow.

E. REPORTING — BY ROLE

   * Divisional Security Officer — verbal report, same shift, all suspected pretexting.
   * Records Custodian — Form RM-6, within one business day.
   * Privacy Coordinator — pattern reports, external written inquiries, rider complaints.
   * Data Processing Manager — terminal access revocation, session logs, media destruction.
   * Director of Human Resources — employee file access disputes.
   * General Counsel — subpoenas, court orders, and all press contact.

F. VERIFICATION

   * Divisional Superintendents certify on Form RM-9 each quarter.
   * The Internal Audit Manager conducts unannounced bin and desk inspections.
   * Findings are reported to the Deputy General Manager, Administration.
