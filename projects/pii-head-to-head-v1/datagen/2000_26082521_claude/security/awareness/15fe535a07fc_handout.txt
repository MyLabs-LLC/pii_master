MERIDIAN FREIGHT SYSTEMS, INC.
CORPORATE COMPLIANCE DEPARTMENT

HANDOUT PR-3: SAFEGUARDING PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS
Effective 01 June 2004. Review cycle: annual.
Applies to: dispatch, dock, driver services, payroll clerical, terminal
administration, contract courier personnel, third-party dock labour.
Acknowledgement slip PR-3A to be signed and returned to the Records
Custodian within ten (10) working days of receipt.

1. SCOPE

1.1 This handout governs the handling of any record containing one or
more of the following fields, whether printed, handwritten, faxed,
imaged, or held on magnetic media:

    Full Name              Social Security Number
    Date of Birth          Commercial Driver Licence Number
    Home Address           Medical Examiner Certificate Number
    Employee Number        Bank Routing Number
    Account Number         Payment Card Number
    Mother's Maiden Name   Personal Identification Number

1.2 Presence of a single field above brings the whole record within
scope. Partial redaction does not remove a record from scope.

2. DESTRUCTION REQUIREMENTS

2.1 Shred at the point of use. Cross-cut consoles are located in each
terminal office and each payroll suite. Strip-cut shredders withdrawn
from service 01 April 2004 are not to be reinstated.

2.2 Shred, without exception:
    - Superseded driver qualification files and annual review forms
    - Payroll registers, stub carbons, garnishment notices
    - Benefit enrolment forms and dependant schedules
    - Photocopies made for convenience, including illegible ones
    - Fax transmission reports listing recipient identifiers
    - Adding-machine tapes prepared against a payroll register
    - Sign-in sheets and visitor logs from unattended lobby desks

2.3 Do not place in-scope records in the dumpster, the baler, the
recycling tote, or the trailer used for cardboard removal.

2.4 Diskettes, tape cartridges, and hard drives are not shredded
locally. Route them to the Information Systems Supervisor under
transfer receipt IS-11.

3. ELECTRONIC MAIL — PROHIBITED CONTENT

3.1 The following must never be transmitted by electronic mail,
internally or externally, in the body, in the subject line, or as an
attachment:
    - Any field enumerated at 1.1
    - Images or scans of licences, medical cards, cheques, or cards
    - Spreadsheets containing an unmasked identifier column
    - Password or PIN reset material
    - Personnel action forms, discipline records, medical notes

3.2 Where transmission is operationally unavoidable, request an
approved courier or secure file transfer through the Information
Systems Supervisor. Verbal approval is not sufficient.

3.3 Automatic forwarding of a company mailbox to any outside address
is prohibited. Existing rules to be removed on receipt of this handout.

4. RECOGNISING A PRETEXTING CALL

4.1 Indicators. The caller:
    - Asserts authority (audit, headquarters, insurer, regulator) but
      is not listed in the internal directory
    - Manufactures urgency: a load held, a payroll deadline, an
      inspection under way
    - Offers partial data and asks only for confirmation
    - Requests transmission to a number or address they supply verbally
    - Objects to the callback procedure or to being placed on hold
    - Calls outside normal office hours to reach relief staff

4.2 Required response, in order:
    (a) Disclose nothing. Confirm nothing, including spelling.
    (b) Advise that requests are returned through the published
        internal directory.
    (c) End the call.
    (d) Complete Incident Note SEC-4 the same shift.
    (e) Notify the Terminal Manager before end of shift.

4.3 The same procedure applies to callers at the dock window and to
persons presenting unverified carrier identification.

    Records retention, destruction schedules ....... Records Custodian

    Suspected pretexting, badge misuse ..... Corporate Security Officer

Route by role title through the terminal switchboard. Do not route
