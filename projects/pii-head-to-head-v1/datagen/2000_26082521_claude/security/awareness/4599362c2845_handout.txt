UTILITIES DIVISION
STAFF GUIDANCE — RECOGNITION OF PRETEXT CONTACT
2016 Revision

1. Purpose. This guidance states the minimum handling standard for customer and employee identifiers held by the Division and applies to all classified, unclassified and temporary personnel, and to contractors having incidental access.

2. Covered fields. Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Driver's License Number, Bank Routing Number, Bank Account Number, Payment Card Number, Customer Account Number when paired with Account Holder Name, Service Address when paired with any of the preceding, and any medical certification supporting a life-support or hardship designation.

3. Destruction. Material bearing a covered field is destroyed by cross-cut shredding or by deposit in a locked console. Draft printouts, screen prints, sticky notes, register tapes, counter forms abandoned by an applicant, and address labels are within scope. Material is not to be held overnight on an open surface pending destruction.

4. Electronic mail. Covered fields are not sent by electronic mail in any circumstance, including to a customer at the customer's own request and including to another employee of the Division. A request from a customer to receive their own identifiers by electronic mail is declined and the alternative channels are offered.

5. Pretext contact. A pretext contact is a telephone or counter approach in which the party misrepresents identity or purpose in order to obtain a covered field. Indicators are urgency, escalation to a threat of disconnection, refusal to permit a call-back, claimed authority as a supervisor, auditor, vendor, contractor or law enforcement officer, partial knowledge offered as proof of legitimacy, and requests to bypass an established verification step. Employees do not verify, confirm, deny or read back a covered field to any party who has not satisfied the standard authentication procedure. A call-back to the number of record is the accepted method of verification and is not to be waived for convenience or seniority.

6. Escalation. The employee terminates the contact, makes a contemporaneous written note of the time, channel and content, and notifies the Customer Service Supervisor immediately. The Supervisor notifies the Information Security Coordinator and the Privacy Officer the same business day. Suspected internal misuse is reported directly to the Privacy Officer, bypassing the immediate supervisor where necessary.

7. Non-compliance. Departure from this guidance is a matter for the Human Resources Director under the applicable disciplinary policy.
