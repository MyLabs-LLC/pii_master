SECTION C — INTERNAL REFERRAL BY ROLE

ROLE TITLE                        RESPONSIBILITY
--------------------------------  -------------------------------------------
The Records Custodian             Authorises release of any student record.
The Payroll Supervisor            Holds all R1 payroll and benefit fields.
The Director of Human Resources   Receives every suspected disclosure.
The Network Administrator         Secures mailboxes, drives, and backups.
The Site Principal                First point of contact at each site.
The Custodial Services Lead       Supplies and empties locked shred bins.
The Public Information Officer    Answers requests from outside the district.
The Business Manager              Approves the disposal contract and log.

A red bin that is full is reported to the Custodial Services Lead the same
day. Bins are not to be propped open, and material is not to be stacked on
top of a bin. The disposal log is countersigned by the Records Custodian at
each pickup and filed with the Business Manager.
