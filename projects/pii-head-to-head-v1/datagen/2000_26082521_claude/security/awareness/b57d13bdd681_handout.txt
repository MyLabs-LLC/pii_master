NEVER BY ELECTRONIC MAIL

  Any field listed above, in the body, in the subject line, or in an
  attachment. Screen captures. Spreadsheet extracts. Word-processed
  letters awaiting signature. Scanned correspondence. Worklists.

  Mail is not a record of disclosure. Where a disclosure is required,
  raise the paper request and route it for authorisation.

CALL SCREENING — IF / THEN

  IF   the caller offers part of an identifier and asks you to confirm
       the remainder
  THEN confirm nothing. End the call. Log it.

  IF   the caller states that a procedure or discharge is being held up
  THEN take the department name only, and call back on a listed line.

  IF   the caller states they are calling from another department and
       needs an account read out
  THEN verify against the internal directory and call back.

  IF   the caller becomes insistent, abusive, or repeats the request
       under a different name
  THEN end the call and notify the Privacy Officer the same shift.

  IF   the caller asks for the office fax number and the name of the
       staff member handling an account
  THEN give neither.
