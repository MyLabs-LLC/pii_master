3. PAPER AND WORKSTATION CONTROL

    a.  Printers are cleared at the end of each task, not at the end of the
        shift. An unclaimed page is placed in the locked console by the person
        who finds it.
    b.  The screen is locked whenever the workstation is left, including for a
        transfer of a call to another desk.
    c.  Work papers are turned face down when a visitor is at the counter and
        are stored in the locked drawer overnight. The desk surface is clear
        at close of business.
    d.  Nothing bearing a listed field leaves the department: no notebook, no
        scratch paper, no photograph of a screen, no personal storage device,
        and no work taken home.
    e.  A shared or written-down password is a reportable incident, not a
        convenience.

4. TELEPHONE PRETEXTING - SHORT TEST

    A caller who resists verification, presses for speed, offers a partial
    identifier and asks for the remainder, claims internal authority while
    absent from the internal directory, or asks for delivery to a destination
    of their own choosing is treated as a pretexting attempt. The clerk
    verifies nothing further, ends the call, and records it on the Suspicious
    Inquiry Log. The caller is never told which element failed, because that
    answer instructs the next attempt.

5. WHOM TO CONTACT

    Privacy Officer ................... suspected or actual disclosure
    Information Security Officer ..... device, account, or system loss
    Records Custodian ............... consoles, storage, chart control
    Patient Accounts Supervisor .......... routine questions and doubt
    HIM Director .................... authorizations and record copies
    Revenue Cycle Director ............ agency, vendor, and exceptions
    Compliance Hotline Coordinator .................. anonymous report

    Contact is by role title through the internal directory. A staff member
    who is unsure whether an event is reportable reports it. No disciplinary
    action follows a report made in good faith.
