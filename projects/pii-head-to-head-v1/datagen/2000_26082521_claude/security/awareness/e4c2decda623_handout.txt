COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
              PROTECTING YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION
                   Information for Clients

(1)  When you attend a clinic, the Department records only what is
     needed to provide your care and to meet reporting duties. Fields
     collected may include Name, Date of Birth, Address, Telephone
     Number, Insurance Policy Number, and Medicaid Identification
     Number.

(2)  Please keep the following in mind:

     -  Staff will never telephone you to ask for your Social
        Security Number or bank details. If such a call is
        received, end it and contact the Privacy Officer.
     -  The Department does not send test results, diagnoses, or
        identification numbers by electronic mail, and asks that
        you do not send them to us that way.
     -  Documents you bring to an appointment and no longer need
        may be placed in the shredding console at the reception
        counter. Do not leave them on the counter or in the
        waiting-room bins.
     -  Check that any letter you receive from us carries your
        correct Case Number. Report a mismatch at once.

(3)  Requests to see, copy, or correct your record are made in
     writing to the Records Custodian at the reception counter.
     Complaints concerning the handling of your information are
     directed to the Privacy Officer.
