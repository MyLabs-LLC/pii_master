METROPOLITAN TRANSIT AUTHORITY
STAFF HANDBOOK — SECTION 7 (EXTRACT)
PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS: HANDLING, RETENTION AND DISPOSAL
Effective 1 April 2021. Supersedes Section 7 dated June 2018.

7.1 SCOPE AND DEFINITIONS

    7.1.1 "Personal identifier" means any field which, alone or with another field held by the Authority, identifies a living individual.

    7.1.2 This section applies to concession applications, staff records, incident reports, CCTV disclosure requests and refund casework.

7.2 CLEAR DESK AND DISPOSAL

    7.2.1 At the close of each shift the following are removed from desks, counters and mess rooms and placed in a locked cabinet or destroyed:
        7.2.1.1 concession application forms and their supporting evidence;
        7.2.1.2 incident report carbon copies;
        7.2.1.3 CCTV disclosure request forms;
        7.2.1.4 printed extracts from the cardholder database.

    7.2.2 Destruction is by cross-cut shredder to the standard specified in the Authority's disposal contract. Strip-cut machines withdrawn in 2019 are not to be used.

    7.2.3 Bulk destruction is witnessed by two officers, one of whom must be the Records Custodian or a person nominated in writing by that post-holder. A Certificate of Destruction is retained for six years.

    7.2.4 Under no circumstances is material within clause 7.2.1 to be removed from Authority premises for disposal elsewhere.

7.3 TRANSMISSION CONTROLS

    7.3.1 Email is not an approved channel for personal identifiers. The prohibition is absolute for:
        7.3.1.1 National Insurance Number;
        7.3.1.2 full Card Number, Security Code, Bank Sort Code, Account Number;
        7.3.1.3 Date of Birth where the message also contains Home Address or Cardholder Name;
        7.3.1.4 Photograph, Medical Exemption Reference, or Occupational Health Case Reference;
        7.3.1.5 any scanned identity document.

    7.3.2 Approved channels are the secure document exchange, internal sealed despatch, and direct entry to the relevant system of record.

    7.3.3 Where a colleague requests information by email, the request is answered by reference to the case number only, with the substance placed in the system of record.

    7.3.4 Attachments are not to be forwarded outside the Authority without the written authority of the Information Governance Manager.

7.4 SOCIAL ENGINEERING AND PRETEXTING

    7.4.1 Pretexting is the construction of a false context in order to obtain information which would not be given on a plain request.

    7.4.2 Indicators, to be treated cumulatively:
        7.4.2.1 assertion of seniority, or of acting on behalf of a named senior post, without a case reference;
        7.4.2.2 manufactured deadline, frequently tied to a board meeting, an audit, or a passenger complaint said to be escalating;
        7.4.2.3 request that standard verification be waived "on this occasion";
        7.4.2.4 flattery, sympathy, or reference to the recipient's recent absence or shift pattern;
        7.4.2.5 caller display showing an internal presentation, which is trivially falsified and carries no evidential weight;
        7.4.2.6 request for the structure of a record rather than its content, for example which fields a concession record contains.

    7.4.3 Required response:
        7.4.3.1 do not confirm or deny that a record exists;
        7.4.3.2 offer to return the call through the internal directory and terminate;
        7.4.3.3 make a contemporaneous note of the time, the claimed identity, the case or account referenced, and the exact information sought;
        7.4.3.4 submit the note to the line manager before the end of the shift.

7.5 ROLES

    7.5.1 Records Custodian — day-to-day control of physical files, destruction certificates and the Disclosure Log.
    7.5.2 Information Governance Manager — approves disclosures outside the Authority and maintains this section.
    7.5.3 Data Protection Officer — statutory point of contact; determines whether an incident is notifiable.
    7.5.4 Information Security Manager — technical containment and account suspension.
    7.5.5 Duty Control Room Manager — the sole out-of-hours point of contact for all four posts above.

7.6 INCIDENT REPORTING

    7.6.1 Suspected loss, theft or improper disclosure is reported immediately to the line manager and, in parallel, to the Data Protection Officer.
    7.6.2 The reporting obligation is not discharged by rectifying the error, and delay in reporting is itself a disciplinary matter.
