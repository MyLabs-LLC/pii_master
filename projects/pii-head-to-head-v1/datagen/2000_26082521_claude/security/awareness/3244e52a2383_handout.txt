METROPOLITAN TRANSIT AUTHORITY
OFFICE OF THE RECORDS CUSTODIAN
ADMINISTRATIVE BULLETIN NO. AB-97-14

SUBJECT:  DESTRUCTION OF RECORDS BEARING PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS
POSTING:  ALL BULLETIN BOARDS, ALL DIVISIONS, ALL SHIFTS
EFFECT:   FIRST BUSINESS DAY FOLLOWING THE DATE OF POSTING

Pursuant to the authority reserved to the Records Custodian under Article IX
of the Authority's Administrative Code, and without derogation of any
retention period imposed by statute, by regulation, or by any collective
bargaining agreement then in force, the schedule of destruction set forth
below is hereby promulgated and shall be observed by every employee, agent,
and contracted person having custody of Authority records. No employee may
substitute a method of destruction less stringent than that prescribed.
Where a single document bears more than one identifier field, the most
stringent method applicable to any one such field shall govern the
disposition of the document in its entirety.

+--------------------------------------+------------------------------+--------------------------+
| DOCUMENT CLASS                       | IDENTIFIER FIELDS BORNE      | PRESCRIBED METHOD        |
|                                      | THEREON                      | OF DESTRUCTION           |
+--------------------------------------+------------------------------+--------------------------+
| Employment applications (declined)   | Social Security Number       | Cross-cut shred, on site |
| Payroll registers and stub copies    | Employee Identification No.  | Cross-cut shred, on site |
| Fare media refund request forms      | Fare Card Account Number     | Cross-cut shred, on site |
| Reduced-fare eligibility packets     | Date of Birth; Home Address  | Locked bin; pulping      |
| Group insurance enrollment forms     | Dependent Name; Member No.   | Locked bin; pulping      |
| Incident/claim investigation files   | Claimant Name; Home Address  | Shred, witnessed by two  |
| Contractor tax certification forms   | Taxpayer Identification No.  | Cross-cut shred, on site |
| Duplicate personnel memoranda        | Employee Name; Badge Number  | Cross-cut shred, on site |
+--------------------------------------+------------------------------+--------------------------+

Documents awaiting destruction shall be held in the locked bin assigned to
the division and shall not be placed in any refuse receptacle, recycling
container, or unattended cart at any time or for any interval, however
brief. A supervisor observing an identifier field in open refuse shall
recover the document, complete a Report of Improper Disposal, and forward
the same to the Records Custodian without delay. Failure to observe this
schedule constitutes a violation of the Administrative Code and shall be
addressed in the ordinary course of the Authority's disciplinary procedure.
