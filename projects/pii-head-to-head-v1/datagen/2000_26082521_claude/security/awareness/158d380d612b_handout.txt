PROTECTING YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION
A NOTICE TO CUSTOMERS OF THE DEPARTMENT OF WATER, SEWER AND ELECTRIC SERVICE
Issued 2016 by the Office of the Privacy Officer

WHAT WE HOLD

To establish and maintain your service, the Department collects and retains a limited set of fields. These are Account Holder Name, Co-Applicant Name, Service Address, Mailing Address, Date of Birth, Social Security Number or Individual Taxpayer Identification Number, Driver's License or State Identification Number, Telephone Number, Electronic Mail Address, Employer Name, Bank Routing Number and Bank Account Number where automatic payment has been elected, Payment Card Number where card payment has been elected, Meter Number, Customer Account Number, and Deposit Amount. No field is collected that is not required for billing, credit assessment, service connection or lawful reporting. Retention periods are set by the applicable records retention schedule and are not subject to variation at the request of staff or customers.

WHAT TO DESTROY

Customers are advised to destroy by cross-cut shredder any document bearing the fields named above once the document has served its purpose and no longer needs to be retained for tax or dispute purposes. This includes closed-account statements, deposit refund advices, disconnection and reconnection notices, budget billing reconciliation statements, returned check notices, and any correspondence bearing the Customer Account Number. Strip-cut shredding, tearing and burning in an open container are not recommended. Documents relating to a pending billing dispute should be retained until the dispute is closed in writing.

WHAT IS NEVER SENT BY ELECTRONIC MAIL

The Department does not request, and customers should never send, the Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Driver's License Number, Bank Routing Number, Bank Account Number or Payment Card Number by electronic mail. Electronic mail is not a secure channel and is not treated as one by the Department. Any message purporting to originate from the Department and requesting these fields should be disregarded and reported. Photographs or scans of identity documents should likewise not be transmitted by electronic mail or by text message. Where such information is required, it is to be provided in person at the Administration Building service counter during posted business hours, by telephone using the published main number listed on the billing statement and dialed by the customer, or through the payment portal reached from the Department's public website.

HOW TO RECOGNISE A PRETEXTING CALL

Pretexting is the practice of obtaining information by adopting a false identity or a false reason for calling. The recognised indicators are as follows. The caller asserts that disconnection is imminent and will occur within the hour. The caller demands payment by prepaid card, wire transfer or a similar irrevocable instrument. The caller instructs the customer not to contact the Department to verify the matter. The caller already possesses part of an identifier and requests confirmation of the remainder. The caller requests the Social Security Number or Date of Birth as a condition of avoiding interruption of service. The Department does not conduct business in this manner. Field personnel do not collect payment, and no employee is authorised to accept payment by prepaid card. Customers receiving such a call should end the call and report it.

WHOM TO CONTACT

Reports of suspected pretexting, of suspected misuse of a customer record, or of correspondence not recognised by the customer should be made to the Customer Service Supervisor at the service counter, who will refer the matter to the Privacy Officer. Requests for correction or inspection of a customer record are directed to the Records Custodian. Requests relating to a payment instrument are directed to the Billing Manager. Written communications should be addressed by role title and delivered to the Administration Building.
