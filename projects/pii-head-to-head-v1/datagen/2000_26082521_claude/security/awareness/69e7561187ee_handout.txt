MERIDIAN FREIGHT SYSTEMS, INC.
CORPORATE SECURITY AND RECORDS ADMINISTRATION
HANDOUT IH-1  /  REVISION 3  /  ISSUED SECOND QUARTER 2004
SUBJECT:      HANDLING OF PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS IN TERMINAL AND OFFICE AREAS
DISTRIBUTION: ALL TERMINAL STAFF, DOCK SUPERVISORS, COUNTER AND BILLING CLERKS
POSTING:      BULLETIN BOARD (DRIVERS' ROOM), BILLING OFFICE, CUSTOMER COUNTER

SECTION 1 — PURPOSE
    1.1  To state the minimum handling requirements for records containing
         personal identifiers held in the course of freight, payroll, and
         customer account administration.
    1.2  To be read in conjunction with the Records Retention Schedule and
         the Terminal Access Control Standard.

SECTION 2 — COVERED FIELDS
    The following field labels denote covered content wherever they appear,
    on paper or on screen, in whole or in part:

         Employee Name                    Social Security Number
         Date of Birth                    Commercial Driver's Licence Number
         Home Address                     Home Telephone Number
         Bank Routing Number              Deposit Account Number
         Payment Card Number              Card Expiry Date
         Customer Account Number          Consignee Contact Name
         Claim Reference and Claimant     Medical Certificate Number

SECTION 3 — DESTRUCTION REQUIREMENTS
    3.1  Cross-cut shredding is required for: payroll registers and stubs;
         driver qualification file extracts; annual wage statements; benefit
         enrolment forms; credit application worksheets; declined card
         authorisation slips; delivery manifests bearing consignee contact
         details; visitor logs; and any photocopy, fax cover sheet, or
         handwritten note reproducing a field named in Section 2.
    3.2  Strip-cut shredders are withdrawn from service and are not to be
         used for material under 3.1.
    3.3  Material awaiting destruction is held only in the locked consoles
         provided. Open recycling bins, dock waste skips, and desk-side
         wastebaskets are not acceptable interim storage.
    3.4  Console keys are held by the Records Custodian. Consoles are not to
         be forced, tipped, or emptied by terminal staff.

SECTION 4 — ELECTRONIC MAIL RESTRICTIONS
    4.1  The following are never to be placed in the body of an electronic
         mail message, in a subject line, or in an attachment: Social
         Security Number; Date of Birth; Bank Routing Number; Deposit
         Account Number; Payment Card Number; Card Expiry Date; Card
         Verification Value; Commercial Driver's Licence Number.
    4.2  This restriction applies to internal messages equally. Internal
         addressing is not a control.
    4.3  Where a business need exists, the record is transferred by internal
         courier pouch, sealed, and logged on the Pouch Transfer Register.
    4.4  Requests received by electronic mail asking for a field listed at
         4.1 are not answered by reply. Forward to the Records Custodian
         without comment and without quoting the requested value.

SECTION 5 — TELEPHONE ENQUIRIES: PRETEXT INDICATORS
    5.1  Treat a call as a suspected pretext where the caller does any of
         the following:
         (a)  states urgency and asks that normal verification be waived;
         (b)  declines to accept a return call on the number held on file;
         (c)  asks the recipient to read back a Social Security Number,
              account number, or date of birth "to confirm";
         (d)  claims to be calling from payroll services, a bank fraud
              department, a licensing authority, or corporate audit, but
              cannot cite the internal reference format in use;
         (e)  asks that a document be faxed or mailed to a destination not
              already recorded on the account;
         (f)  applies pressure by naming a supervisor or by referring to
              disciplinary consequences.
    5.2  Response: obtain the caller's stated name, stated organisation, and
         stated reason. State that the enquiry will be returned through the
         recorded contact point. End the call. Do not confirm or deny that
         any record exists.
    5.3  Complete the Suspected Pretext Call Log at the counter the same
         shift.
