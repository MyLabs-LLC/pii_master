BRANCH PROCEDURE PRV-207 -- TELEPHONE HANDLING OF IDENTIFIERS
Effective 2021. Supersedes PRV-198. Applies to every line answered in a
title or escrow branch, including reception and the after-hours rota.

CALL SCRIPT MATRIX

STEP | WHAT THE CALLER ASKS FOR          | REQUIRED RESPONSE
-----+-----------------------------------+----------------------------------
1    | Any part of an SSN or DOB         | Decline; offer the portal
2    | Confirmation of a name or address | Neither confirm nor correct
3    | A payoff figure or loan number    | Route to Escrow Operations
4    | Wire or account instructions      | Route to Wire Verification Desk
5    | A document re-sent by email       | Portal only; log the request
6    | Speed, because closing is today   | Callback on the number on file
7    | Your direct line or your rota     | Give the main line only

NOTES

1. Identity is proved by callback, never by what the caller recites.
   File numbers, property descriptions and lender names circulate
   widely and are not secrets.
2. Take the caller's name, their employer, their role, and the reason
   for the call. Write it down while the caller is still speaking.
3. Do not narrow a guess for a caller. "Not that one" is a disclosure.
4. Paper produced during the call -- notes, printed screens, fax covers
   -- goes into the desk cross-cut unit before you leave the station.
5. None of the following travels by email: Social Security Number, Date
   of Birth, Driver's Licence Number, Bank Account Number, Routing
   Number, Card Number, or a scan showing any of them. The secure portal
   is the only outbound channel and a bonded courier the only paper one.
6. A caller who turns friendly, then urgent, then aggravated inside one
   call is following a script. End the call and report it.

ESCALATION

TRIGGER                        | ROLE TO NOTIFY
-------------------------------+-----------------------------
Pretexting suspected           | Privacy Officer
Identifier sent to wrong party | Privacy Officer
Shred console left unlocked    | File Room Supervisor
Missing file or loose paper    | Records Custodian
Wire detail change requested   | Escrow Operations Manager
Portal or scanner outage       | Information Security Lead

Reports are made within one business hour. Where the duty role is
absent, the record goes to the Branch Manager and is repeated to the
Privacy Officer at the next open.
