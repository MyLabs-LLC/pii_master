TECHNOLOGY SERVICES ADVISORY TS-2021-07
RECOGNITION AND HANDLING OF PRETEXTING CONTACTS
Distribution: all campuses, central office, and transportation

ARTICLE I — DEFINITIONS

1. "Pretexting" means the use of a fabricated identity, role, or circumstance to induce a staff member to disclose, confirm, or alter protected information.
2. "Protected element" means any of the following field labels held in a District system: Student Identification Number, State Testing Identifier, Date of Birth, Social Security Number, Home Address, Parent/Guardian Name, Custody Status, Disability Category, Medical Alert, Meal Eligibility Status, Bus Route Assignment.
3. "Verified channel" means a return call to a number listed in the published directory, an in-person visit with photographic identification, or a message originating within the District domain.

ARTICLE II — INDICATORS

4. The following indicators, singly or in combination, warrant refusal and referral:
   4.1 The caller supplies part of a protected element and asks the staff member to confirm or complete the remainder.
   4.2 The caller asserts a deadline expiring the same day, a pending audit, or an emergency involving a named child.
   4.3 The caller declines a return call, states that the line is unavailable, or supplies an alternative number.
   4.4 The caller claims to be a systems vendor, insurance administrator, state agency reviewer, or law enforcement officer, without prior written notice held by the campus.
   4.5 The caller requests that the matter be kept from a supervisor.
   4.6 The caller asks for a directory export, a class roster, or a transportation manifest.

ARTICLE III — REQUIRED RESPONSE

5. Staff shall neither confirm nor deny the existence of a record.
6. Staff shall record the substance of the request, the claimed affiliation, and the time of the call. No identifier read aloud by the caller shall be written into the log.
7. Staff shall terminate the call and re-establish contact only through a verified channel.
8. Where a protected element has been disclosed, the disclosure shall be reported within one hour to the Data Privacy Officer and the Chief Technology Officer.

ARTICLE IV — ELECTRONIC EQUIVALENTS

9. Messages requesting credential entry, urgent wire transfer, or gift purchase shall be reported to the Help Desk Supervisor using the report function in the mail client, and shall not be forwarded.
10. Protected elements shall not be entered into external translation services, unapproved artificial intelligence tools, or personal cloud storage.

ARTICLE V — CONTACTS

11. Immediate incident report: the Help Desk Supervisor.
12. Determination of breach: the Data Privacy Officer.
13. Notification to families: the Superintendent's designee, on the recommendation of the Data Privacy Officer.
14. Media and public inquiry: the Communications Officer. Staff shall make no statement.
