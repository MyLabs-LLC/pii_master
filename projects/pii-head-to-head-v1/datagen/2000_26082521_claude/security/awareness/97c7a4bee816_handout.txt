PATIENT FINANCIAL SERVICES — PRIVACY BULLETIN
Bulletin No. PFS-2021-04
Effective Date: 15 February 2021
Issued By: the Office of the Revenue Cycle Director
Distribution: Billing Office, Registration, Collections, Cashiering

    I.  PURPOSE
        A.  This bulletin is promulgated to remind all personnel of their
            continuing obligations with respect to the safeguarding of
            protected health information and personally identifiable
            information encountered in the ordinary course of billing duties.

    II. DESTRUCTION OF PAPER
        A.  The following shall be deposited exclusively in the locked
            confidential-destruction consoles and never in ordinary waste:
            1.  Registration face sheets bearing the fields Patient Name,
                Date of Birth, and Medical Record Number.
            2.  Explanation of Benefits printouts bearing the fields
                Member ID, Group Number, and Claim Number.
            3.  Payment slips bearing the fields Cardholder Name,
                Primary Account Number, and Expiration Date.
            4.  Handwritten notes, sticky notes, and scratch paper on which
                any such field has been transcribed, whether legible or not.

    III.PROHIBITED TRANSMISSION
        A.  No employee shall transmit by unencrypted electronic mail any
            record bearing the fields Social Security Number, Bank Routing
            Number, Account Number, or Diagnosis Code, notwithstanding any
            request by the recipient that the same be expedited.

    IV. INQUIRIES
        A.  All questions arising hereunder shall be directed to the
            Records Custodian or, in that person's absence, to the
            Privacy Officer.
