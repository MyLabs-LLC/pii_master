DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC UTILITIES — CUSTOMER ACCOUNTS SECTION
HANDOUT FOR COUNTER AND TELEPHONE STAFF, 2016

The Section holds records that identify individuals, and the handling of those records is a condition of employment rather than a matter of individual judgement. The fields treated as identifiers are Account Holder Name, Authorized User Name, Service Address, Mailing Address, Date of Birth, Social Security Number, State Identification Number, Telephone Number, Electronic Mail Address, Bank Routing Number, Bank Account Number, Payment Card Number, Payment Card Expiration Date, Customer Account Number, Meter Number and Deposit Amount. A record containing two or more of these fields in combination is treated as sensitive irrespective of the sensitivity of any field taken alone.

Paper bearing an identifier is destroyed rather than discarded. Locked consoles are provided at each workstation cluster and at the counter. Everything printed from the billing system in the course of assisting a customer is deposited in a console before the workstation is vacated, including duplicate statements not collected by the customer, application forms begun and abandoned, credit check results, arrangement worksheets, and the counter log where it records an identifier. Envelopes and mailing labels are included. Nothing bearing an identifier is placed in a recycling container, taken from the premises, or retained in a personal file.

Electronic mail is not an approved channel for identifiers. Staff do not send an identifier by electronic mail, do not forward a customer message containing one without first removing it, and do not respond to an inbound request for one. Where a customer has transmitted an identifier by electronic mail unprompted, the message is forwarded to the Privacy Officer for handling and is then deleted from the mailbox and from deleted items.

Telephone contacts intended to extract identifiers are common and are rehearsed by the caller. The caller will typically manufacture urgency, will resist a call-back, will supply a fragment of an identifier and ask that the remainder be confirmed, will claim to be calling on behalf of a supervisor, a contractor or an agency, and will object when the standard verification questions are put. None of these circumstances relieves the employee of the verification requirement. The employee does not read back, confirm or deny any field, does not disclose whether an account exists, and terminates the contact where verification fails. Pressure applied by the caller is itself the indicator.

Reporting is by role title as follows. Suspected pretexting and any disclosure made in error are reported to the Customer Service Supervisor without delay and in writing the same day. Loss of paper, of a laptop or of a portable drive is reported to the Information Security Coordinator. Requests to inspect, amend or destroy a record, and all subpoenas and public records requests, are referred unopened to the Records Custodian. Questions of interpretation are directed to the Privacy Officer. No employee is to determine independently that an exception applies.
