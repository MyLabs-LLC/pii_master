2.  DESTRUCTION. Paper carrying any field marked above is placed in
    the cross-cut shredder or the locked destruction bin. This
    includes day sheets, laboratory slips, facsimile cover pages,
    label backing, envelopes bearing a mailing address, and drafts.
    Diskettes and compact discs go to the destruction bin whole.

3.  PRETEXTING. A caller seeking an identifier is treated as
    unverified until each test below is met.

    3.1  The caller states a role title and a division.
    3.2  The caller accepts a return call to a listed extension.
    3.3  The caller does not press for an immediate answer.
    3.4  The caller has not quoted a fragment of a record in order
         to obtain the remainder.

    Where any test fails, end the call, write down what was asked
    for, and notify the roles at clause 4 before the shift closes.
