PROCEDURE PS-4
RECOGNITION AND HANDLING OF PRETEXT TELEPHONE CONTACT
Issued by the Office of the Privacy Official

1. PURPOSE

1.1 To establish a uniform response to telephone contact intended to obtain Personal Identifiers under a false identity or false claim of authority.

2. INDICATORS

2.1 Treat a call as suspect where two or more of the following are present:

     (a) The caller asserts urgency and applies time pressure.
     (b) The caller claims to be a district, regional, or corporate officer but cannot be located in the internal directory.
     (c) The caller asks the colleague to read back a Social Security Number, Date of Birth, or Payment Card Account Number rather than supplying it for verification.
     (d) The caller requests that a record be faxed or electronically mailed to a destination not previously on file.
     (e) The caller discourages the colleague from placing the call on hold or from consulting a supervisor.
     (f) The caller claims a system outage and asks for information to be supplied verbally as a workaround.
     (g) The caller requests a password, terminal identifier, or override code for any reason.
     (h) The caller has partial identifying information and seeks confirmation or completion of the remainder.

3. REQUIRED RESPONSE

3.1 Do not confirm, deny, or complete any identifier held by the caller.

3.2 State that the request will be returned through a verified channel. Obtain no further detail.

3.3 Terminate the call. Do not transfer a suspect call to the pharmacy department.

3.4 Where the caller claims internal authority, telephone the claimed office using the internal directory only. Do not use a number supplied by the caller.

4. REPORTING

4.1 Complete an Incident Contact Record the same business day. The record carries the fields: Date of Contact, Time of Contact, Department Receiving, Claimed Identity of Caller, Information Requested, Information Disclosed Y/N, Indicators Observed, Action Taken, Reporting Colleague Role Title.

4.2 Submit the record to the Store Manager for countersignature.

4.3 The Store Manager shall forward the record to the District Loss Prevention Manager within one business day.

4.4 Where clause 4.1 records a disclosure, the Store Manager shall additionally notify the Privacy Official by telephone before the close of business and shall not wait for the written record to be processed.

5. RETENTION

5.1 Incident Contact Records are held at the location pending transfer to the Records Custodian and are not destroyed at store level.
