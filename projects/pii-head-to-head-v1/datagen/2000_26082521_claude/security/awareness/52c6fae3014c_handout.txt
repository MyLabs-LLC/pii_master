MERIDIAN COAST TITLE & ESCROW COMPANY
RECORDS HANDLING AND DESTRUCTION — BRANCH PROCEDURE
Owner: Records Custodian

1.0  CLEAN DESK
     1.1  At every departure of thirty minutes or more, file material bearing
          any of the labels below returns to a locked drawer or the file room:
              Borrower Name          Social Security Number
              Seller Name            Date of Birth
              Loan Number            Driver's License Number
              Escrow Number          Deposit Account Number
              Payoff Amount          Routing Number
     1.2  Screens are locked, not minimised.
     1.3  Signing rooms are swept by the Closing Coordinator after each
          appointment; anything left behind goes to the Records Custodian,
          not into the next file.

2.0  PRINT, COPY, AND FAX
     2.1  Retrieve print jobs immediately. Do not send to a device you cannot
          see from your desk.
     2.2  Misfeeds and test pages go into the shred console, not the bin.
     2.3  Incoming faxes are collected hourly during business hours and at
          close of business by the Escrow Assistant on duty.

3.0  DESTRUCTION
     3.1  Paper past its retention period is destroyed by cross-cut shredding
          or by the certified vendor under a Certificate of Destruction held
          by the Records Custodian.
     3.2  Electronic media is destroyed physically. Deleting a file or
          reformatting a drive is not destruction.
     3.3  No box leaves the branch except on the vendor manifest, signed by
          the Records Custodian or the Branch Escrow Manager.

4.0  TRANSMISSION LIMITS
     4.1  Identifiers do not travel by open email, personal web mail, personal
          fax, or unencrypted portable media.
     4.2  Secure portal, courier, or sealed certified mail only.
     4.3  Wire instruction changes are verified by outbound callback to the
          number of record before any funds move — no exceptions, regardless
          of who is waiting.

5.0  ESCALATION
     5.1  Suspected pretexting — Branch Escrow Manager and Information
          Security Officer, same day.
     5.2  Lost, missing, or mis-sent material — Privacy Coordinator, same day.
     5.3  Retention questions — Records Custodian.
     5.4  Vendor or subcontractor access questions — Compliance Officer.
