TRI-VALLEY PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY
DIVISION OF ADMINISTRATION — PERSONNEL AND PASSENGER RECORDS
STAFF GUIDANCE HANDOUT PR-11 (2021 REVISION)
SUBJECT: Identifier Hygiene, Media Destruction, and the Detection of Social Engineering
CLASSIFICATION: Internal Use — Not for Public Posting

    §1  SCOPE AND CONSTRUCTION
        (a) This Handout governs the handling of records containing personal identifiers by
            personnel assigned to Operations, Maintenance, Fare Administration, Human
            Resources, Paratransit Eligibility, and Risk Management.
        (b) Where a provision of this Handout conflicts with a prior divisional practice,
            this Handout shall control, and the divisional practice shall be deemed
            superseded to the extent of the inconsistency.
        (c) Nothing herein shall be construed to diminish any obligation arising under
            applicable state or federal law, under the collective bargaining agreement, or
            under the terms of any grant assurance to which the Authority is a party.

    §2  DESTRUCTION OF PHYSICAL MEDIA
        (a) Cross-cut shredding, at a minimum security level of P-4, is required for:
            (1)  Employment applications and background investigation summaries, which
                 bear the fields Social Security Number, Date of Birth, and Driver's
                 License Number;
            (2)  Paratransit eligibility files, which bear the fields Client Identifier,
                 Diagnosis Code, Emergency Contact Name, and Home Address;
            (3)  Payroll registers and garnishment notices, which bear the fields Employee
                 Number, Gross Wages, and Bank Routing Transit Number;
            (4)  Incident and claim files, which bear the fields Claimant Name, Policy
                 Number, and Date of Loss;
            (5)  Any handwritten note, telephone message slip, or dispatch log entry upon
                 which an identifier has been transcribed, however transiently.
        (b) Sealed destruction consoles shall be used where provided. Consoles shall not be
            propped open, overfilled, or relocated from their assigned positions.
        (c) Optical media, magnetic media, and solid-state media shall not be shredded in
            paper equipment but shall be surrendered to the Information Technology Asset
            Coordinator for degaussing or physical disintegration under witnessed
            certificate.

    §3  MATTER NEVER TO BE SENT BY ELECTRONIC MAIL
        (a) The following shall not be transmitted by electronic mail under any
            circumstance: Social Security Number; Taxpayer Identification Number; Payment
            Card Primary Account Number; Card Verification Value; Bank Account Number;
            Routing Transit Number; Date of Birth in combination with Full Legal Name;
            Diagnosis Code; Driver's License Number; system credentials of any kind.
        (b) The addition of the notation "CONFIDENTIAL" to a subject line does not
            constitute encryption and confers no protection whatsoever.
        (c) Forwarding to a personal mailbox for the purpose of completing work from a
            remote location is prohibited and constitutes a reportable event irrespective
            of the good faith of the employee concerned.

    §4  RECOGNITION OF A PRETEXTING CALL
        (a) The pretexting caller typically presents in one of the following guises:
            (1)  A purported employee of the Authority's benefits administrator seeking
                 verification of a Date of Birth or Social Security Number;
            (2)  A purported vendor representative seeking an update to a Remittance
                 Account Number on file;
            (3)  A purported law enforcement officer demanding immediate release of a
                 passenger's travel history without process;
            (4)  A purported information technology technician requesting a one-time
                 passcode in order to "complete a migration."
        (b) Common levers employed are urgency, flattery, invocation of authority, and the
            manufactured appearance of prior familiarity with the Authority's internal
            terminology.
        (c) Verification shall be accomplished by independent callback to a number obtained
            from the internal directory, never to a number supplied by the caller.

    §5  MANDATORY REPORTING, BY ROLE
        (a) Suspected pretexting or successful disclosure: the Chief Information Security
            Officer, and concurrently the employee's Division Superintendent.
        (b) Loss, theft, or misplacement of paper records: the Records Custodian.
        (c) Improperly transmitted electronic mail: the Data Protection Officer, who shall
            determine whether notification obligations are triggered.
        (d) Media pending destruction: the Information Technology Asset Coordinator.
        (e) Retaliation for a good-faith report is prohibited and shall be referred to the
            Director of Human Resources.
