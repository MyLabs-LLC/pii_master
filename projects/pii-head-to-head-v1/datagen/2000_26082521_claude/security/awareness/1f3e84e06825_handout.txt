MEMBER AND EMPLOYEE GUIDANCE CONCERNING THE SAFEGUARDING OF PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS
Issued by the Office of the Compliance Officer under authority of the Information Security Program
Revision 2 — Calendar Year 2004

ARTICLE I. PURPOSE AND DEFINITIONS

1.1 This guidance is promulgated pursuant to Part 748 of the NCUA Rules and Regulations and Appendix A thereto, and shall be construed to supplement, and in no event to diminish, any obligation otherwise imposed upon the reader.

1.2 The term "Personal Identifier" shall mean any of the following data elements, whether appearing singly or in combination: Full Legal Name; Social Security Number; Taxpayer Identification Number; Date of Birth; Mother's Maiden Name; Member Number; Share or Share Draft Account Number; Debit or ATM Card Number; Personal Identification Number; Home Banking User ID; Driver's License Number; Residence Address; Electronic Mail Address.

ARTICLE II. DESTRUCTION OF RECORDS

2.1 Every paper record bearing a Personal Identifier shall, upon expiration of its applicable retention period, be rendered unreadable by cross-cut shredding. Strip-cut destruction is not deemed sufficient and is prohibited.

2.2 The following are expressly subject to Clause 2.1: Periodic Statement of Account; Consumer Loan Application; Membership Application; Signature Card duplicate; Credit Bureau Report printout; Deposit Ticket and Withdrawal Slip carbon; expired plastic card; unused Convenience Check; Wire Transfer Request; Teller Proof Sheet; and any screen print taken from the core processing system.

2.3 Pending destruction, such records shall be deposited only in a locked destruction receptacle. Deposit into an open wastebasket, recycling container, or unattended carton is prohibited without exception.

2.4 The Records Custodian shall maintain a Certificate of Destruction bearing the following column headings: Record Series; Container Identifier; Volume; Method of Destruction; Date of Destruction; Title of Witnessing Employee.

ARTICLE III. ELECTRONIC MAIL

3.1 No employee shall transmit, and no member shall be invited to transmit, any Personal Identifier by unencrypted electronic mail, whether in the body of a message or as an attachment thereto.

3.2 Where a member volunteers a Personal Identifier by electronic mail, the responding employee shall not quote the incoming message, shall respond only by telephone or by secure message within Home Banking, and shall thereafter delete the original from all folders, including Sent Items and Deleted Items.

3.3 Forwarding of member records to any personal or non-Credit Union mail account, and the storage of such records upon home computers or removable media, is prohibited.

ARTICLE IV. PRETEXTING

4.1 "Pretexting" means the obtaining of member information by false, fictitious, or fraudulent statement, as contemplated by Title V, Subtitle B of the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act.

4.2 The following constitute indicia of a pretext call: (a) asserted urgency or threatened loss; (b) a caller purporting to be a member yet unable to satisfy standard authentication; (c) a caller purporting to be an employee, examiner, vendor, or peace officer who declines verification; (d) a request that ordinary procedure be waived as a courtesy; (e) a request that information be directed to an address or facsimile number not of record.

4.3 An employee encountering such a call shall neither confirm nor deny the existence of any relationship, shall decline to proceed, and shall offer to return the call at a number appearing in the Credit Union's own records. A number supplied by the caller shall not be used.

4.4 The occurrence shall be reported to the Information Security Officer not later than the close of the same business day.

ARTICLE V. INTERNAL CONTACTS

5.1 Retention and destruction: the Records Custodian.
5.2 Suspected pretexting, lost media, or unauthorized disclosure: the Information Security Officer, and in that officer's absence, the Branch Operations Manager.
5.3 Privacy notices and opt-out elections: the Compliance Officer.
5.4 Suspected fraud warranting a Suspicious Activity Report: the Bank Secrecy Act Officer.
5.5 No employee shall suffer adverse action by reason of a good-faith report made under this Article.
