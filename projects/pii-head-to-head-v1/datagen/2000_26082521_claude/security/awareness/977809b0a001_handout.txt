CORRIDOR STAFFING SERVICES
NOTICE TO APPLICANTS AND ASSIGNED ASSOCIATES
Protecting the information you give us

1. Information we collect from you
   1.1 To register you for assignment and to pay you, we record data under these field
       labels: Full Name; Home Address; Home Telephone; Social Security Number;
       Date of Birth; Driver's License Number; Work Authorisation Document Type;
       Bank Routing Number; Bank Account Number; Emergency Contact; Skills and
       Certifications; Assignment History.
   1.2 We ask for nothing beyond what payroll, tax withholding, work authorisation,
       and client placement require.

2. How we hold it
   2.1 Paper files are held in locked cabinets in a staff-only area and are not left
       on desks overnight.
   2.2 Screens are locked when unattended. Terminal access is limited to staff whose
       duties require it.
   2.3 Superseded paper is destroyed by cross-cut shredder or in a locked destruction
       console. It is never placed in ordinary waste or recycling.

3. What we will not do
   3.1 We will not send your Social Security Number, Date of Birth, or bank account
       details by electronic mail, and we ask that you do not send them to us that way.
   3.2 We will not disclose your file to a client beyond what the assignment requires.
   3.3 We will not confirm or deny any detail of your file to a caller who cannot be
       verified — including a caller who claims to be you. This protects you.

4. What we ask of you
   4.1 Bring original documents to the branch rather than posting or faxing copies.
   4.2 Collect your pay stub from the branch or have it mailed; do not ask for it to
       be forwarded to a personal address you have not registered with us in writing.
   4.3 Tell us in person or by telephone when your address or bank details change.
       Changes are not accepted by email or by a third party on your behalf.
   4.4 Shred any agency paperwork you no longer need, including pay stubs and
       assignment confirmations.

5. Watch for pretexting
   5.1 Callers may pose as a recruiter, a payroll clerk, a bank, or a government
       office in order to obtain your identifiers.
   5.2 We will never telephone you to ask for your full Social Security Number, your
       bank account number, or a password. If a caller does, end the call and
       telephone the branch on a number you look up yourself.

6. Who to contact
   6.1 Questions about what is held in your file, or a request to correct it — ask for
       the Records Custodian.
   6.2 A concern that your information has been misused, or a suspicious call — ask
       for the Branch Manager, who will refer the matter to the Privacy Officer.
   6.3 Questions about payroll deductions and withholding — ask for the Payroll
       Supervisor.
