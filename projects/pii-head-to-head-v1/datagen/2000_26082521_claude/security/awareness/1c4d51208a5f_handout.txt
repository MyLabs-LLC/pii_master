METROPOLITAN TRANSIT AUTHORITY
CONTACT CENTRE BRIEFING NOTE, SEPTEMBER 2021
IDENTITY VERIFICATION AND CALLER PRETEXTING
For display at every workstation

I. THE RECORD YOU ARE LOOKING AT

    (i) A cardholder record contains these fields and no others: Cardholder Name, Correspondence Address, Date of Birth, Concession Category, Card Serial, Photograph, Journey History, Refund Status, Bank Sort Code, Account Number.

    (ii) You may confirm that a record exists only after verification is complete. Before that point, the existence of the record is itself confidential.

II. VERIFICATION

    (i) Verification is by three fields drawn from different categories, requested in a different order on each call.

    (ii) You ask; the caller answers. You do not read a field aloud for the caller to confirm, and you do not accept a partial answer.

    (iii) Verification may not be waived by any post-holder, including the Head of Customer Services.

III. CALLS THAT SHOULD STOP

    (i) The caller cannot verify but supplies context — a recent journey, a complaint reference, a bereavement — as a substitute.

    (ii) The caller asks you to change the Correspondence Address and then, in the same call, to reissue the card.

    (iii) The caller states that a colleague has already verified them and asks you to proceed from that point.

    (iv) The caller asks which fields the record holds, or how many attempts the system permits.

    (v) The caller becomes conciliatory after pressure fails; this is a change of technique, not a change of intent.

IV. HANDLING

    (i) End the call courteously. Do not explain which indicator prompted you.
    (ii) Log the account reference, the time, and the information sought.
    (iii) Notify the Team Leader immediately; the Team Leader notifies the Revenue Protection Lead.
    (iv) Where a change was made before the concern arose, the Data Protection Officer is informed the same day.

V. PAPER

    (i) Notepads used on shift are shredded at the workstation shredder before you leave the floor.
    (ii) Screen prints are prohibited. No exception exists for training or for handover.
