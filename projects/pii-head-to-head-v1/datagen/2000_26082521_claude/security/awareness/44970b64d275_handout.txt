BILLING OFFICE DESK REFERENCE — SAFEGUARDING IDENTIFIERS
Posted at each workstation by direction of the Revenue Cycle Director
Review Cycle: Annual | Current Posting: October 2021

    A.  FIELDS TREATED AS SENSITIVE IN THIS DEPARTMENT
        The columns enumerated below appear routinely upon departmental
        reports and are, in each instance, to be regarded as sensitive:

            Report Column                  Source Screen
            ------------------------------ ----------------------------
            Patient Name                   Registration
            Date of Birth                  Registration
            Social Security Number         Registration
            Medical Record Number          Registration
            Account Number                 Account Inquiry
            Guarantor Name                 Guarantor Maintenance
            Member ID                      Insurance Verification
            Group Number                   Insurance Verification
            Claim Number                   Claim Status
            Primary Account Number         Payment Posting
            Bank Routing Number            Payment Posting
            Date of Service                Charge Detail

    B.  DISPOSAL
        -   Every printed page bearing any column named in Section A shall
            be shredded, whether or not the page was in fact used.
        -   Test prints, alignment pages, and duplicate copies produced in
            error are subject to the foregoing requirement in full.
        -   Carbon interleaves and pressure-sensitive receipt copies shall
            be shredded and not merely discarded.

    C.  ELECTRONIC MAIL
        -   Nothing bearing a Social Security Number, a Primary Account
            Number, or a Bank Routing Number shall be sent by electronic
            mail, internally or externally, under any circumstance.
        -   Attachments shall not be forwarded to a personal mailbox for
            the purpose of working from home.
        -   Automatic forwarding rules to any external domain are forbidden.

    D.  TELEPHONE PRETEXTING — SIGNS TO HEED
        -   Refusal to be transferred, or refusal of a return call.
        -   Insistence that the verification script be waived "just this once."
        -   Claimed affiliation with a vendor, auditor, or payer that the
            caller cannot substantiate upon request.
        -   Pressure applied through flattery, urgency, or implied sanction.

    E.  WHOM TO TELL
        -   Immediately: the Patient Financial Services Supervisor.
        -   Same day: the Privacy Officer.
        -   Where a password may have been disclosed: the Information
            Security Officer, in addition to the foregoing.
