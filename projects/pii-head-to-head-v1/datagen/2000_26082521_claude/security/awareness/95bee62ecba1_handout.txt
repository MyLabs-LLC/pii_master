MEMORANDUM

TO:       All Branch, Lending, and Operations Staff
FROM:     Office of the Compliance Officer
DATE:     March 15, 2004
RE:       Safeguarding Member Identifying Information — Required Practices

1. PURPOSE

This memorandum states the credit union's required handling practices for member identifying information under our Information Security Program, adopted pursuant to NCUA Rules and Regulations Part 748, Appendix A. It supersedes the handling guidance circulated with the prior teller procedures binder. Every employee, including part-time and temporary staff, is responsible for compliance. Questions of interpretation go to the Compliance Officer.

2. INFORMATION TREATED AS PROTECTED

The following fields are protected wherever they appear — on a form, a screen print, a fax cover, a note pad, or a spreadsheet:

     Member Name
     Member Number
     Social Security Number / Taxpayer Identification Number
     Date of Birth
     Mother's Maiden Name
     Driver's License or State Identification Number
     Share, Share Draft, and Loan Account Numbers
     ATM or Debit Card Number, PIN, PIN Offset
     Home Address, Mailing Address, Home Telephone Number
     Employer Name and Wage Information
     Credit Report Data and Credit Score

3. WHAT MUST BE SHREDDED

Cross-cut shred, at the point of use, before end of business:

     - Teller reject items, deposit slip carbons, and voided withdrawal tickets
     - Screen prints of member inquiry, statement, or card maintenance screens
     - Loan application worksheets, credit bureau reports, and denial worksheets
     - Adding machine tapes bearing account numbers
     - Returned mail and undeliverable statements after disposition is recorded
     - Old signature card photocopies, expired identification photocopies
     - Any handwritten note bearing a field listed in Section 2

Do not place these items in the wastebasket, in the recycling bin, or in the dumpster. Locked shred consoles are placed in every branch work area. If a console is full, notify the Branch Operations Manager; do not stack material on top of it.

Records still within their retention period are not to be shredded. Retention questions go to the Records Custodian before disposal, not after.

4. WHAT MUST NEVER BE SENT BY ELECTRONIC MAIL

Internet electronic mail is not a secure channel. Staff may not place any of the following in the body of a message, in a subject line, or in an attachment sent outside our network:

     - Social Security Number or Taxpayer Identification Number
     - Full account, loan, or card number
     - PIN, password, security code, or challenge answer
     - Date of Birth combined with Member Name
     - Credit report data of any kind
     - Scanned images of signature cards or identification documents

Internal messages between staff should reference the Member Number only where the business purpose requires it, and never the Social Security Number. Members who send protected fields to us by electronic mail are to be answered by telephone or letter; do not reply in kind.

5. RECOGNIZING A PRETEXT CALL

Pretexting is the practice of obtaining member information by impersonation. Callers may claim to be the member, a spouse, an attorney, an employer, a law enforcement officer, a collection agency, or an employee of another department of this credit union.

Warning signs include: urgency and time pressure; refusal or inability to answer verification questions; a request that you read back a number the caller "already has"; flattery or hostility directed at the employee; a caller who says a supervisor has already approved the request; background noise suggesting a call center; and a request to send information to an address or fax number not on file.

Verify every caller against the identifiers on file before disclosing anything. If verification fails, offer to mail the information to the address of record. Never confirm or deny a number the caller reads to you.

6. REPORTING AND INTERNAL CONTACTS

Report any suspected pretext call, lost file, misdirected fax, or unsecured document immediately. Do not wait until the end of shift and do not investigate on your own.

     Suspected pretext calls and identity theft ......... Security Officer
     Disposal, retention, and file requests ............. Records Custodian
     Electronic mail and system access questions ........ Information Systems Manager
     Member disputes and disclosure requests ............ Member Services Supervisor
     Policy interpretation and regulatory questions ..... Compliance Officer
     Suspicious activity reporting ...................... Bank Secrecy Act Officer

Extensions for each role are listed on the internal directory posted at every workstation. After hours, contact the Security Officer through the answering service.
