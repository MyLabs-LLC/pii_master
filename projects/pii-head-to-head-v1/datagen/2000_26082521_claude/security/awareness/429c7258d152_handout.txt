PROTECTING YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION
Customer Notice — Pharmacy Services
Available at the counter on request. Copies may be taken.

1. WHAT WE COLLECT AND WHY
   - Name, Date of Birth, and Address, to match you to the correct record
   - Prescriber Name and NPI, to verify the order
   - Insurance Carrier, Member ID, Group Number, and BIN, to bill your plan
   - Allergy and medication history, to screen for interaction
   We do not require your Social Security Number to fill a prescription. If a plan requests it, ask the Pharmacy Manager on Duty for the alternate identifier procedure.

2. WHAT YOU SHOULD DESTROY AT HOME
   Cross-cut shred before disposal; do not place in curbside recycling:
   - Pill bottle labels and outer bag tags
   - Explanation of Benefits statements
   - Pharmacy receipts showing Rx Number or partial card data
   - Expired insurance cards and prescription discount cards
   - Mail-order shipping labels and packing slips
   - Any correspondence listing Member ID or Policy Number

3. WHAT WE WILL NEVER ASK YOU TO EMAIL
   This pharmacy does not accept the following by email under any circumstance:
   - Social Security Number
   - Full card number, expiration, or security code
   - Photographs of insurance cards or identification documents
   - Date of Birth combined with Member ID
   Any message requesting these items is not from us, regardless of the logo, sender name, or signature block it carries.

4. HOW TO RECOGNISE A PRETEXTING CALL
   A pretexting caller impersonates a trusted party to extract identifiers. Indicators:
   - The caller already knows part of an identifier and asks you to confirm the rest
   - The caller states your prescription, benefit, or refund is suspended pending verification
   - The caller creates time pressure and discourages you from calling back
   - The caller requests remote access to your computer to "correct a claim"
   - The caller asks for payment by prepaid card or wire
   Response: end the call. Do not confirm, correct, or deny any detail offered. Contact the pharmacy directly using the number printed on your bag tag or bottle label.

5. IF YOU SUSPECT MISUSE
   - Notify the Pharmacy Manager on Duty in person or by the number on your label
   - Request a copy of your dispensing history from the Records Custodian
   - Request a fraud alert from each nationwide consumer reporting agency
   - Request in writing that an accounting of disclosures be prepared

6. WHO TO ASK
   - Records or history requests: the Records Custodian
   - Billing and plan identifier questions: the Third-Party Billing Lead
   - Privacy complaints: the Store Privacy Coordinator, escalating to the Regional Compliance Officer
   Complaints may be submitted verbally at the counter or in writing. No customer will be denied service for filing one.
