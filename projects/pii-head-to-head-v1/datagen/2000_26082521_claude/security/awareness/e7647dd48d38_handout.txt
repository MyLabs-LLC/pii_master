COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT — DIVISION OF ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES
PERSONAL IDENTIFIER HANDLING STANDARD
Standard No. AS-2021-11 | Review cycle: annual | Supersedes: AS-2019-07

ARTICLE I — PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 This standard prescribes minimum handling, transmission, and destruction controls for records containing personal identifiers held by the Department in any medium.

1.2 The standard applies without exception to clinical, environmental health, vital records, and administrative units, and to any workstation, home office, mobile device, or vehicle in which Department records are held.

1.3 Where a program-specific requirement is more restrictive than this standard, the more restrictive requirement governs.

ARTICLE II — CLASSIFICATION OF FIELDS

2.1 The following field labels are classified Restricted. Their values shall not be displayed, printed, transmitted, or spoken outside the minimum necessary workflow.

    FIELD LABEL                         CLASSIFICATION   DISPOSAL ROUTE
    Client Name                         Restricted       Locked console
    Date of Birth                       Restricted       Locked console
    Social Security Number              Restricted       Locked console
    Medical Record Number               Restricted       Locked console
    Medicaid / Member Identification    Restricted       Locked console
    Home Address                        Restricted       Locked console
    Telephone Number                    Restricted       Locked console
    Electronic Mail Address             Restricted       Locked console
    Financial Account Number            Restricted       Locked console
    Employer Identification Number      Internal         Locked console
    Facility Permit Number              Internal         Standard recycling
    Program Activity Code               Internal         Standard recycling

2.2 A record carrying one Restricted field is classified Restricted in its entirety, irrespective of the classification of the remaining fields.

ARTICLE III — PHYSICAL DESTRUCTION

3.1 Restricted paper shall be destroyed by cross-cut shredding to a particle size not exceeding the specification held by the Facilities Coordinator, or by deposit into a locked destruction console serviced under contract.

3.2 The following are expressly enumerated as within scope: intake worksheets, laboratory result printouts, appointment rosters, sign-in sheets, mailing labels, envelopes bearing addressee blocks, fax transmission reports, sticky notes, and any handwritten transcription of a Restricted field.

3.3 Destruction consoles shall not be over-filled, propped open, or relocated. Where a console is full, staff shall notify the Facilities Coordinator and shall retain the material in a locked drawer pending service.

3.4 Destruction of records under legal hold is prohibited. Legal hold status is confirmed by the Records Custodian.

ARTICLE IV — ELECTRONIC TRANSMISSION

4.1 Restricted values shall not be sent by unencrypted electronic mail, by personal electronic mail accounts, by short message service, or through consumer file-sharing services.

4.2 Restricted values shall not be entered into the subject line of any message, encrypted or otherwise.

4.3 Where an external recipient requires Restricted data, the sending unit shall use the secure transfer gateway, shall verify the recipient through a channel independent of the request, and shall retain the transmission receipt.

4.4 Screen captures containing Restricted values shall not be pasted into ticketing systems, chat channels, or training materials.

ARTICLE V — SOCIAL ENGINEERING AND PRETEXTING

5.1 Pretexting is the elicitation of protected information by a caller who misrepresents identity, affiliation, or entitlement.

5.2 Indicators include: assertion of an emergency requiring immediate release; a claim of internal authority combined with unfamiliarity with internal procedure; a request that staff confirm, correct, or complete a partial identifier; flattery or hostility directed at the receiving employee; refusal to accept call-back through the published main line; and a request to bypass the release-of-information process.

5.3 Staff shall not confirm or deny the existence of a record to an unverified caller, and shall not disclose the internal structure of record systems.

5.4 Staff shall terminate the contact, record the observed indicators on the Suspicious Contact Log, and notify the Privacy Officer.

ARTICLE VI — INTERNAL NOTIFICATION

6.1 Suspected disclosure, loss, or theft: the Privacy Officer, immediately and in all cases.

6.2 Compromise of a device, credential, or mailbox: the Information Security Officer.

6.3 Questions of retention, legal hold, or destruction eligibility: the Records Custodian.

6.4 Requests from the press or the public: the Public Information Officer. No other employee is authorized to respond.

6.5 Failure of a console, lock, or badge reader: the Facilities Coordinator.
