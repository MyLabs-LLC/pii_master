CLAUSES
  1.  The Company will never request your social security number,
      bank account number or personal identification number by
      electronic mail. Please do not send these to us by that means.
  2.  When you telephone us, you will be asked several questions to
      confirm your identity. This protects your file. Please do not
      regard these questions as a discourtesy.
  3.  If someone telephones you claiming to represent the Company and
      asks you to confirm your account number, birth date or mother's
      maiden name, decline politely, hang up, and telephone the
      servicing branch shown on your declarations page.
  4.  Destroy, preferably by shredding, any premium notice, benefit
      statement, draft stub or identification card you no longer
      require. Do not place these whole in household refuse.
  5.  Report a lost identification card or a missing benefit statement
      to the servicing branch at once, and ask that a notation be
      placed on your file.
  6.  Requests to change the address of record, to change a
      beneficiary, or to release file material to a third party must
      be made in writing over your signature.

A written request for a copy of the Company's information practices
may be addressed to the Consumer Affairs Coordinator at the servicing
branch shown above.
