NOT ACCEPTABLE: recycling bin, general waste, tearing by hand, a personal
desk-side strip-cut shredder, or removal from the department for destruction
at another site.

Console keys are held by the Records Custodian. An overflowing or unlocked
console is reported to the Patient Accounts Supervisor within the same shift.
Material is not stacked on top of a console. The witnessed destruction log and
the vendor certificate are filed by the Records Custodian and retained under
the departmental retention schedule.
