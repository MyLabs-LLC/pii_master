BUSINESS AND FINANCIAL SERVICES
PROCEDURE BFS-114: PROTECTION OF EMPLOYEE AND VENDOR IDENTIFIERS
Issued 2021 | Review cycle: annual | Owner: the Director of Business and Financial Services

SECTION 1 — APPLICATION

1. This procedure governs payroll, benefits, accounts payable, and vendor onboarding functions.
2. Where this procedure conflicts with a departmental practice, this procedure controls.

SECTION 2 — CLASSIFICATION AND CHANNEL

3. Each data element handled by this department is classified below. Staff shall determine the classification before selecting a transmission or destruction method.

FIELD LABEL                     CLASSIFICATION   PERMITTED CHANNEL      DESTRUCTION
Social Security Number          Restricted       Secure portal only     Cross-cut shred
Employee Identification Number  Confidential     Internal mail, portal  Cross-cut shred
Bank Routing Number             Restricted       Secure portal only     Cross-cut shred
Bank Account Number             Restricted       Secure portal only     Cross-cut shred
Date of Birth                   Confidential     Internal mail, portal  Cross-cut shred
Home Address                    Confidential     Internal mail, portal  Cross-cut shred
Dependent Name                  Confidential     Secure portal only     Cross-cut shred
Health Plan Member Identifier   Restricted       Secure portal only     Cross-cut shred
Taxpayer Identification Number  Restricted       Secure portal only     Cross-cut shred
Position Title                  Public           Any                    Recycling

4. No element classified Restricted may leave the department by electronic mail, facsimile to an unattended device, or unencrypted removable media.

SECTION 3 — PAPER CONTROL

5. Direct deposit authorization forms, benefit enrollment forms, and tax withholding forms shall be secured in a locked cabinet at the close of each business day.
6. Printers in shared areas shall be released using badge authentication. Unclaimed output found at a shared printer shall be consoled and reported to the Payroll Supervisor.
7. Shred consoles shall be serviced by the contracted vendor under a certificate of destruction. Staff shall not remove material from a console.

SECTION 4 — VENDOR AND CALLER VERIFICATION

8. A request to change banking details for an employee or vendor shall be verified by outbound contact using details already held in the master file, and never using details supplied within the request.
9. A caller who cites urgency, an audit deadline, or a senior official's authority, and who declines a return call, shall be treated as unverified. The call shall be ended without disclosure.
10. Attempted account changes that fail verification shall be logged and referred to the Internal Auditor.

SECTION 5 — CONTACTS

11. Routine questions on classification: the Payroll Supervisor.
12. Suspected fraudulent change request: the Internal Auditor and the Data Privacy Officer.
13. Retention and disposal scheduling: the Records Custodian.
