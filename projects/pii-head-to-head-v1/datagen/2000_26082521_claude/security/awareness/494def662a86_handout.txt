OFFICE OF THE UNIVERSITY REGISTRAR
STAFF GUIDANCE, QUESTION AND ANSWER FORM
Issued November 18, 1996

The questions below have been received with sufficient frequency to warrant a
written response. The responses are binding upon all staff of this Office.

1.  Q. A caller states that he is calling from another University department
    and needs a student's Social Security Number to complete a form. May I
    read it to him?

    A. No. Departmental affiliation asserted by a caller is not verification
    of that affiliation. Advise the caller that the element will be
    transmitted by inter-campus courier envelope to his office upon written
    request bearing the signature of his supervising officer, and terminate
    the call. A caller who objects to this procedure is to be reported to the
    Associate Registrar for Compliance the same business day.

2.  Q. May identifiers be sent by electronic mail if the message is marked
    confidential?

    A. No. A marking of confidentiality confers no protection whatsoever upon
    the message, which passes through relay hosts outside the control of this
    Office and is retained on backup media indefinitely. The prohibition
    extends to the Social Security Number, the Date of Birth, the Bank
    Routing Number, the Deposit Account Number, the Loan Account Number, the
    Disciplinary Action Code, and any Individual Course Grade, and applies
    equally to the subject line and to any attached file.

3.  Q. What is to be done with a transcript that misfeeds in the printer?

    A. It is to be placed directly in the cross-cut shredder. It is not to be
    set aside for later attention, placed in a wastebasket, or deposited in a
    recycling container. The same requirement applies to alignment
    impressions, superseded rosters, partial label sheets, and working notes.

4.  Q. How is a pretexting call recognized?

    A. By its pattern rather than its content. The caller manufactures
    urgency, claims an authorization he cannot document, offers one
    identifier in the hope of obtaining a second, expresses familiarity with
    staff not personally known to the recipient, and resists any proposal to
    return the call to a published extension.

5.  Q. To whom are questions directed?

    A. Verification procedure and release forms, to the Assistant Registrar
    for Records. Destruction schedules and shredder service, to the Records
    Custodian. Suspected pretexting and any disclosure believed to have been
    made in error, to the Associate Registrar for Compliance. Legal process
    of every description, to the Office of University Counsel.
