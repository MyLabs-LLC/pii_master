STANDING RULES
 1. Verify by call-back to the listing in the district directory. No exceptions.
 2. Release no identifier to a caller who cannot be verified in the directory.
 3. Attachments bearing identifiers travel by interoffice pouch, sealed.
 4. Clear desks nightly. Lock all files. Do not leave fax output unattended.
 5. Recycling bins are not confidential. Shred consoles are emptied weekly.
 6. Direct all press or parent complaints to the Superintendent's Office.
