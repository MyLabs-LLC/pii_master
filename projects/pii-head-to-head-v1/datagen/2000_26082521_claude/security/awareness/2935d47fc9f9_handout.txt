NOTICE TO ALL BILLING OFFICE PERSONNEL

SUBJECT: HANDLING OF PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS
STATUS: MANDATORY — ACKNOWLEDGEMENT REQUIRED
YEAR OF ISSUE: 2011

1. SHREDDING

1.1 Every sheet leaving your hands is either filed, sealed for internal delivery, or shredded. There is no fourth option.

1.2 Shred: statements, claim forms, remittance advices, aged-trial-balance printouts, insurance verification notes, payment-plan copies, adding-machine tapes, fax cover sheets, fax confirmation slips, envelopes from returned mail, and any scrap bearing Patient Name, Date of Birth, Social Security Number, Medical Record Number, Guarantor Account Number, Insurance Member Identifier, or Claim Number.

1.3 Do not stack material on top of a bin. If the slot is blocked, tell the Billing Office Supervisor.

2. ELECTRONIC MAIL

2.1 Do not place any of the fields at clause 1.2 in an electronic mail message, its subject line, or an attachment.

2.2 Card Number, Card Expiry Date, Card Verification Code, Bank Routing Number, and Bank Account Number are prohibited in electronic mail without exception, including internally.

2.3 Use the secure file transfer service. If you do not have access, request it through the Billing Office Supervisor rather than working around it.

2.4 Do not forward hospital records to a personal account, and do not copy them to a memory stick.

3. PRETEXTING CALLS

3.1 A pretexting caller is not usually rude. They are helpful, plausible, and in a hurry.

3.2 Stop and verify where the caller:

    — will not give a callback route held in our own records
    — claims authority you cannot confirm on the internal directory
    — asks you to read back a Social Security Number or Bank Account Number
    — presses you to act before the end of your shift
    — asks for a file to be sent outside the hospital
    — asks that the contact go unrecorded

3.3 Follow the identity script every time. It is not discourteous to verify, and no legitimate caller will object.

3.4 If verification fails, end the call and complete an incident sheet the same day.

4. REPORTING

4.1 Billing Office Supervisor — routine queries and bin collection.
4.2 Privacy Officer — suspected pretexting, wrongful disclosure, misdirected statements.
4.3 Records Custodian — file retrieval, retention, destruction certificates.
4.4 Information Security Officer — lost badge, laptop, or memory stick.
4.5 Compliance Hotline — anonymous reports.

5. ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

5.1 Sign the acknowledgement sheet held by the Billing Office Supervisor. The sheet records: Employee Role Title; Department; Shift; Date Briefed; Briefing Officer Role; Acknowledgement Received.
