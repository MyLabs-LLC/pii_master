NOTICE TO ALL WINDOW AND TELEPHONE STAFF
          Post at each cashier station and collection desk.

          1.  Shred at the window: statements returned by the post
              office, voided receipts, deposit worksheets, and any note
              bearing an Account Number or Social Security Number.

          2.  Clear the counter before calling the next party. A face
              sheet left face up is a disclosure.

          3.  Nothing carrying a Social Security Number, Date of Birth,
              Medical Record Number, or Policy Number leaves this office
              by electronic mail. There are no exceptions for urgency,
              for management request, or for outside counsel.

          4.  Verify before you speak. Where the caller cannot state the
              Account Number and confirm the Guarantor Address already on
              file, take a message and return the call to the number of
              record.

          5.  A caller who will not accept a return call is not a caller
              you may assist. Note the date, the hour, and the fields
              requested.

          6.  Refer at once to the Patient Accounts Manager. In that
              officer's absence, refer to the Business Office Director.
              Suspected fraud is referred to the Corporate Compliance
              Officer.

          By order of the Business Office Director.
