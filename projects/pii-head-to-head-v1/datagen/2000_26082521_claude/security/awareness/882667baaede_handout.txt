FIELD CLASSIFICATION AND PERMITTED CHANNEL SCHEDULE

Annex B to the Personal Identifier Handling Standard. 2021 revision.
Maintained by the Records Custodian. Interpretation queries to the Data Protection Lead.

1. This Schedule classifies each field held on the worker record and states the only channels through which it may be moved. Where a field does not appear below, treat it as Restricted until the Records Custodian confirms otherwise.

2. Classification table.

   FIELD LABEL                          CLASSIFICATION   PERMITTED CHANNEL        DISPOSAL
   Full Legal Name                      Internal         System, portal, email    Cross-cut shred
   Former Name                          Restricted       System only              Cross-cut shred
   Date of Birth                        Restricted       System only              Cross-cut shred
   National Insurance / SSN             Highly Restricted System only              Cross-cut shred
   Passport Number                      Highly Restricted System only              Not to be printed
   Driving Licence Number               Highly Restricted System only              Not to be printed
   Right-to-Work Document Reference     Restricted       System, client portal    Cross-cut shred
   Home Address                         Restricted       System, mail house       Cross-cut shred
   Personal Telephone Number            Restricted       System only              Cross-cut shred
   Personal Email Address               Restricted       System only              Cross-cut shred
   Bank Sort Code                       Highly Restricted Payroll transfer only    Not to be printed
   Bank Account Number                  Highly Restricted Payroll transfer only    Not to be printed
   Payroll Reference                    Internal         System, payroll transfer Cross-cut shred
   Next of Kin Name                     Restricted       System only              Cross-cut shred
   Next of Kin Telephone Number         Restricted       System only              Cross-cut shred
   Occupational Health Reference        Highly Restricted System only              Not to be printed

3. "Not to be printed" means the field may not be rendered to paper, to a local file, or to a screen capture. Where a business process appears to require printing, the process is defective and shall be referred to the Branch Manager.

4. "System" means the placement management system. "Payroll transfer" means the secure transfer facility operated by the payroll function. "Client portal" means a portal formally provisioned under a signed data processing schedule; an ad hoc upload link supplied by a client contact is not a client portal.

5. A field classified Internal does not become Internal-by-association. A worker's Full Legal Name transmitted alongside a Payroll Reference and a Date of Birth is a Highly Restricted transmission and is governed by the strictest classification present.

6. Requests to add, retire, or reclassify a field are made in writing to the Records Custodian and approved by the Data Protection Lead. Local spreadsheets holding fields outside this Schedule are prohibited and shall be deleted on discovery.
