REGIONAL TRANSIT AUTHORITY
PASSENGER SERVICES: REDUCED FARE AND ACCESSIBILITY PROGRAMMES

INFORMATION HANDOUT FOR APPLICANTS, CARDHOLDERS AND THEIR DULY
AUTHORISED REPRESENTATIVES CONCERNING THE PROTECTION OF PERSONAL
IDENTIFIERS SUBMITTED TO OR HELD BY THE AUTHORITY

Form PS-118 / Issue 3 / Revised October 2016 / Retain for your records

I. PURPOSE AND CONSTRUCTION

   This handout is furnished in satisfaction of the notification
   obligations of the Authority and shall be construed as supplementary
   to, and not in derogation of, the terms and conditions printed upon
   the reverse of the application form. Nothing contained herein shall
   be deemed to create, extend or abridge any right of action not
   otherwise existing at law, nor to constitute a waiver of any
   immunity available to the Authority.

II. IDENTIFIERS COLLECTED AND THEIR DISPOSAL

   The Authority collects only those identifiers strictly necessary to
   the adjudication of eligibility. Each field is retained for the
   period stated and is thereafter destroyed by the method stated, such
   destruction being effected under the supervision of the Records
   Custodian and evidenced by a certificate of destruction.

   FIELD LABEL ON APPLICATION      RETENTION      METHOD OF DISPOSAL
   ==============================  =============  ==================
   Applicant Full Name             36 months      Cross-cut shred
   Date of Birth                   36 months      Cross-cut shred
   Social Security Number          Not retained   Shred at intake
   Medicare Claim Number           Not retained   Shred at intake
   Certifying Practitioner Data    36 months      Cross-cut shred
   Residential Address             36 months      Cross-cut shred
   Photograph of Applicant         Card lifetime  Secure erasure
   Fare Card Serial Number         Card lifetime  Secure erasure
   Bank Account and Routing Data   Not retained   Shred at intake

III. MEANS OF TRANSMISSION WHICH ARE NOT PERMITTED

   Applicants and cardholders are advised, and are requested to advise
   any representative acting upon their behalf, that the Authority does
   not accept and will not act upon any of the fields listed in Part II
   where the same is conveyed by electronic mail, by social messaging,
   by facsimile transmitted to an unattended machine, or by voicemail.
   Material so conveyed will be destroyed unread so far as is
   practicable, and no acknowledgement of receipt will issue.

IV. RECOGNITION OF PRETEXTING SOLICITATIONS

   Employees of the Authority will never initiate a telephone call in
   which a cardholder is asked to recite in full a Social Security
   Number, a Bank Account Number, a Payment Card Primary Account
   Number, or a Payment Card Verification Value. A caller who requests
   such a recitation, who declines to permit a return call to a
   published Authority line, who asserts an emergency requiring
   immediate compliance, or who already appears to possess a fragment
   of an identifier and seeks confirmation of the remainder, should be
   treated as a pretexting solicitation. The cardholder should end the
   call without confirming or denying any particular and should report
   the matter at any staffed station.

V. DESTRUCTION OF PAPERS HELD BY THE CARDHOLDER

   Correspondence, expired certificates, superseded photographic cards
   and duplicate application drafts should be destroyed by cross-cut
   shredding. Tearing by hand is not sufficient for any paper bearing a
   Social Security Number, a Date of Birth, an account number or a
   certificate number.

VI. OFFICERS TO BE CONTACTED

   NATURE OF THE ENQUIRY               OFFICER HOLDING RESPONSIBILITY
   ----------------------------------  ------------------------------
   Correction of a recorded field      Customer Records Manager
   Suspected pretexting solicitation   Data Protection Coordinator
   Loss or theft of a fare card        Fare Media Administrator
   Request for a copy of a file        Records Custodian
   Complaint as to handling            Privacy Officer
   Refusal of a request for a file     General Counsel

VII. EFFECT OF NON-COMPLIANCE

   Failure of an applicant to furnish a required field may result in
   the suspension of adjudication until the deficiency is cured. Any
   failure by the Authority to observe Part II shall be reported to the
   Internal Audit Lead in accordance with standing instructions.
