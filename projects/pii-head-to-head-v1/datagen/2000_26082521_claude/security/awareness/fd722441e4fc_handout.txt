PART 3 -- TELEPHONE INQUIRIES

  Verification is by call-back only, to the listing carried in the district
  directory or in the student's file of record. A number supplied by the
  caller is not verification.

  Indicators of a pretext call:
    ( ) Urgency, deadline, threatened penalty, or claimed audit
    ( ) Refusal to accept a call-back or to be placed on hold
    ( ) Partial data recited, with a request that the employee complete it
    ( ) Request to redirect mail or fax to an unrecorded destination
    ( ) Claimed authority of an administrator the caller cannot name in role
    ( ) Call placed outside office hours or on a non-student day

  If two or more indicators are present, end the call and complete the log.
