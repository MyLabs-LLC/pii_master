COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
    PERSONAL INFORMATION PROTECTION — HANDOUT FOR CLIENTS AND VISITORS

    A. WHY WE ASK FOR INFORMATION
        1. We collect only what is needed to provide services and to meet
           reporting requirements.
        2. The fields we ask for are: Name; Date of Birth; Address; Telephone;
           Insurance or Member ID; Emergency Contact.
        3. You may ask any staff member why a field is required.

    B. WHAT WE DO WITH PAPER
        1. Forms are kept behind the counter, not on it.
        2. Working copies are shredded on site in locked consoles.
        3. Records are stored in a locked room with restricted access.

    C. WHAT WE DO NOT DO
        1. We do not send your Social Security number, member number, or date
           of birth by ordinary email.
        2. We do not confirm to a caller that you are known to this office.
        3. We do not sell or trade contact lists.

    D. PROTECTING YOURSELF
        1. Shred at home:
            a. Explanation of benefits statements
            b. Insurance cards being replaced
            c. Appointment reminders showing a member number
            d. Pre-approved credit offers
        2. Never give out by telephone or email:
            a. Your Social Security number
            b. Your full member or account number
            c. Your date of birth combined with your address
        3. Recognise a pretexting call:
            a. The caller already knows part of your information and wants the
               rest "for verification."
            b. The caller says a benefit will lapse unless you act now.
            c. The caller refuses to send anything in writing.
            d. The caller gives you a number to call back that is not the one
               printed on your card or statement.
        4. What to do:
            a. Hang up.
            b. Call back using the number printed on your own card or bill.
            c. Tell us if the caller claimed to be from this department.

    E. WHO TO ASK HERE
        1. Records Custodian — copies of your record, corrections, releases
        2. Privacy Officer — complaints, or concern that information was shared
           improperly
        3. Office Manager — anything you find left out in a waiting area

    F. YOUR RIGHTS
        1. To see and copy your record.
        2. To ask that an error be corrected.
        3. To receive a list of certain disclosures.
        4. To file a complaint without affecting your care.
