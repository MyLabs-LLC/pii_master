COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT — PRIVACY OFFICE

IDENTIFIER HANDLING: SUPERVISOR BRIEFING OUTLINE

I.  Scope
    A.  All program areas, all clinics, all field staff.
    B.  Covers paper, electronic, telephone, and verbal disclosure.

II. The four rules to teach at unit meetings
    A.  Rule one — minimum necessary
        1.  Ask only for the fields the form requires.
        2.  Do not copy an identification document unless the program
            manual requires the copy.
    B.  Rule two — shred by default
        1.  Anything printed with an identifier goes to a console.
        2.  Included: rosters, worksheets, call slips, mailing labels,
            fax confirmations, envelopes with visible name and address.
        3.  Excluded: original legal record documents — route these to the
            Records Custodian instead.
    C.  Rule three — email carries nothing that identifies
        1.  Forbidden in body, subject line, and attachment: Social Security
            Number; Date of Birth with Name; Member ID with Name; Chart
            Number with Name; scanned identification.
        2.  Case number alone is acceptable internally.
        3.  Secure transfer is arranged by the Information Systems
            Coordinator.
    D.  Rule four — verify before you speak
        1.  No identifier is released by telephone.
        2.  All external requests route to the Records Custodian in writing.

III. Pretexting — what supervisors should drill
    A.  Scenario: caller claims to be a case worker at a partner agency and
        needs a date of birth "to match a record."
    B.  Scenario: caller claims to be from a billing office and reads back a
        partial member number, asking staff to supply the remaining digits.
    C.  Scenario: caller claims to be an internal employee locked out of a
        system and asks for a colleague's login.
    D.  Correct answer in all three: no confirmation, take details, refer to
        the Records Custodian, report to the Privacy Officer.
    E.  Reinforce that staff are never disciplined for refusing a request in
        good faith.

IV. Escalation by role
    A.  Immediate, same shift — Privacy Officer.
    B.  Records and legal process — Records Custodian.
    C.  Systems, accounts, lost devices — Information Systems Coordinator.
    D.  Physical security, consoles, keys — Office Manager.
    E.  External communication — Health Officer only.

V.  Documentation supervisors must keep
    A.  Attendance at the annual briefing.
    B.  Date and summary of any suspected pretexting attempt reported.
    C.  Console service exceptions.
