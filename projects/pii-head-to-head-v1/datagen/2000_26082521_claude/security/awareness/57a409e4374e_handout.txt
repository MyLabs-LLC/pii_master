SECTION 1 — SCOPE ------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  This sheet governs the handling, transmission, and destruction of personal identifiers appearing in underwriting, claim, billing, and enrollment records held by the Company.
1.2  It applies to paper, facsimile, magnetic media, optical media, microfiche, and terminal print output without distinction.

SECTION 2 — IDENTIFIER CLASSES ------------------------------------------------
2.1  Class A (Restricted). Social Security Number; Taxpayer Identification Number; Driver License Number; Bank Routing Number; Deposit Account Number; Credit Card Account Number.
2.2  Class B (Confidential). Member Identification Number; Group Certificate Number; Claim File Number; Policy Number; Date of Birth; Mother's Maiden Name; Medical Record Number; Diagnosis Code; Provider Identification Number.
2.3  Class C (Internal). Mailing Address; Residence Telephone Number; Employer Name; Occupation Code; Beneficiary Designation.

SECTION 3 — DESTRUCTION REQUIREMENTS ------------------------------------------
3.1  Cross-cut shredding at DIN Level 3 or above is mandatory for any paper bearing a Class A or Class B field. This includes, without limitation:
     (a) claim worksheets, adjuster field notes, and reserve calculation sheets;
     (b) enrollment applications, change forms, and their carbon interleaves;
     (c) premium remittance advices and returned check photocopies;
     (d) explanation-of-benefits stubs and coordination-of-benefits inquiries;
     (e) policy declaration pages and endorsement drafts;
     (f) underwriting scoring sheets and motor vehicle report abstracts.
3.2  The following are frequently overlooked and are likewise to be shredded: adding machine tapes; facsimile cover sheets and transmission confirmation reports; photocopier misfeeds, jams, and test copies; terminal screen prints; courier manifests; addressed window envelopes and mailing labels; desk calendars and message pads bearing caller identifiers.
3.3  Class A and Class B material is not to be placed in wastebaskets, recycling containers, or unlocked accumulation bins under any circumstance. Locked consoles are provided on each floor.
3.4  Diskettes, cartridge tapes, hard disk units, and microfiche are not to be introduced into paper shredding equipment. Route these to the Records Custodian for degaussing or witnessed incineration under Form AD-119.

SECTION 4 — ELECTRONIC MAIL PROHIBITIONS ---------------------------------------
4.1  No Class A field may be entered into the body, subject line, or attachment of any electronic mail message, internal or external, encrypted or otherwise.
4.2  No Class B field may be transmitted over any Internet mail gateway. Internal mail carrying Class B fields must be limited to named recipients within the responsible department.
4.3  Cite the internal file locator in place of an identifier. The locator alone is sufficient for all interdepartmental correspondence.
4.4  Spreadsheet extracts, report downloads, and "print to file" output are subject to the same prohibitions as typed text.
4.5  Blind copy distribution, departmental distribution lists, and automatic forwarding to residence or vendor mailboxes are not permitted for any message referencing a policyholder file.
