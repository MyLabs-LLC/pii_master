PART A — WHAT COUNTS AS AN IDENTIFIER
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
The following field labels are identifiers wherever they appear: on screen,
on paper, on a facsimile, on removable media, or spoken aloud at a counter.

   Patient Name                Guarantor Name           Account Number
   Medical Record Number       Social Security Number   Date of Birth
   Insurance Member ID         Group Number             Claim Number
   Home Address                Home Telephone           Employer Name
   Card Account Number         Cheque Number            Admission Date

A partial value is an identifier. The last four characters of any field
above are an identifier. A field is not made safe by abbreviation.

PART B — DISPOSAL MATRIX
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   ROUTE                  ACCEPTS                       DOES NOT ACCEPT
   ---------------------  ----------------------------  ----------------
   Locked shred console   Paper bearing any Part A      Media, binders
   Desk cross-cut unit    Loose sheets, label stock     Bound volumes
   Media destruction bin  Diskette, CD-R, backup tape   Paper
   Open recycling         Blank stock, vendor flyers    Any Part A field
   Waste basket           Wrappers, cups, packaging     Any Part A field
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Consoles are collected under contract. Staff do not empty, unlock, or
transport consoles. A full console is reported, not left overnight.

PART C — ELECTRONIC MAIL
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   RULE 1   No Part A field leaves the hospital network by mail.
   RULE 2   No Part A field is placed in a subject line, internal or not.
   RULE 3   Attachments carry the same restriction as the message body.
   RULE 4   Rules that forward mail to an outside mailbox are not permitted
            and are removed when found.
   RULE 5   Personal or home mail accounts are not used for any hospital
            business, including drafts and work carried home.
   RULE 6   Mail sent in error is reported the same shift. The message is
            not recalled, deleted, or resent by the sender.

PART D — RECOGNISING A PRETEXT CALL
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   SIGNAL                                     WEIGHT     RESPONSE
   -----------------------------------------  ---------  ---------------
   Caller supplies part of a field and asks
   staff to confirm the balance               High       Terminate
   Caller cites urgency, surgery, discharge   High       Terminate
   Caller declines a call-back on a listed
   department line                            High       Terminate
   Caller claims internal department, asks
   for an account to be read aloud            Medium     Verify, call back
   Caller asks who is working the account     Medium     Decline, log
   Caller asks for the department fax number
   in order to receive a statement            Medium     Decline, log
   Caller repeats the request later under a
   different name                             High       Terminate, report
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Staff do not confirm spelling, do not confirm that an account exists, and
do not confirm that a person is or is not known to the department. Silence
is an acceptable answer. Every terminated call is logged before shift end.
