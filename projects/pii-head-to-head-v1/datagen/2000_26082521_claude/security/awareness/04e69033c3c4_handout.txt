ASSOCIATE AND CLIENT GUIDANCE HANDOUT — SAFEGUARDING OF PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS
Issued by the Office of the Privacy Officer
Revision: First Quarter 2021
Applicability: All Branch Personnel, Field Associates on Assignment, and Client Site Supervisors

    1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
        1.1 This handout is promulgated pursuant to the Agency's Information Handling Policy and shall govern the collection, transmission, retention, and destruction of any record bearing a Personal Identifier, as that term is defined at Section 2 below.
        1.2 Nothing herein shall be construed to diminish any obligation imposed by applicable federal or state law, by a client's own security addendum, or by the terms of an executed staffing services agreement. Where a conflict arises, the more restrictive requirement shall control.
        1.3 Compliance is a condition of continued assignment. Failure to observe these provisions may result in corrective action up to and including removal from assignment and termination of the employment relationship.

    2. DEFINITION OF PERSONAL IDENTIFIER
        2.1 For purposes of this handout, "Personal Identifier" means any of the following data elements, whether appearing alone or in combination, and whether in paper, electronic, or verbal form:
            2.1.1 Social Security Number or Individual Taxpayer Identification Number;
            2.1.2 Date of Birth;
            2.1.3 Driver's License Number or State Identification Card Number;
            2.1.4 Passport Number, Permanent Resident Card Number, or Employment Authorization Document Number;
            2.1.5 Bank Routing Number, Deposit Account Number, or Payment Card Number;
            2.1.6 Home Address, Personal Telephone Number, and Personal Email Address;
            2.1.7 Wage Rate, Garnishment Order Reference, and Benefit Enrollment Election;
            2.1.8 Background Check Reference Number and Drug Screen Collection Identifier.
        2.2 The field labels enumerated above are the operative subject of this handout. Associates are reminded that the label alone is innocuous; it is the populated value that carries risk.

    3. DESTRUCTION REQUIREMENTS — MATERIALS TO BE SHREDDED
        3.1 The following shall be deposited in a locked shred console and shall under no circumstances be placed in a recycling stream, a general waste receptacle, or a desk-side bin:
            3.1.1 Printed applications, resumes, and skill assessment sheets after data entry has been verified;
            3.1.2 Form I-9 supporting documentation photocopies, including any legible reproduction thereof;
            3.1.3 Form W-4 worksheets, direct deposit authorization forms, and voided instruments submitted for account verification;
            3.1.4 Timecards, per-diem receipts, and expense substantiation bearing an associate's identifying number;
            3.1.5 Interview notes, reference call notes, and any handwritten memorandum reciting a Personal Identifier;
            3.1.6 Fax cover sheets, transmission confirmations, and misprinted or duplicate pages produced at the multifunction device.
        3.2 Materials awaiting destruction shall not remain unattended on any work surface at the conclusion of the business day. A clean-desk inspection may be conducted by the Branch Operations Manager without prior notice.

    4. TRANSMISSION PROHIBITIONS — MATERIALS NEVER TO BE SENT BY ELECTRONIC MAIL
        4.1 No associate shall transmit, forward, reply with, or attach to any electronic mail message a Social Security Number, a Date of Birth, a Bank Routing Number, a Deposit Account Number, a Payment Card Number, or an image of a government-issued identity document.
        4.2 The prohibition at Section 4.1 applies irrespective of the recipient's identity, and applies with equal force to internal correspondence, to messages exchanged with a client contact, and to messages an associate may wish to send to a personal address for convenience.
        4.3 Where transmission of such an element is genuinely necessary to complete an onboarding or payroll function, the associate shall use the Agency's secure document portal, and shall record the transfer in the transmittal log maintained by the Records Custodian.
        4.4 Personal cloud storage, personal messaging applications, and unencrypted removable media are prohibited channels without exception.

    5. RECOGNITION OF PRETEXTING CONTACT
        5.1 "Pretexting" denotes a contact in which the caller assumes a false identity or invents a false circumstance in order to induce disclosure of information the caller is not entitled to receive.
        5.2 Indicia of a pretexting contact include, without limitation:
            5.2.1 An asserted urgency, coupled with an instruction that established verification steps be bypassed "just this once";
            5.2.2 A claim of authority the caller cannot substantiate, such as a purported executive, auditor, or client security officer unknown to the branch;
            5.2.3 A request that the associate read back a Social Security Number, a Date of Birth, or a Deposit Account Number that the caller states is already on file;
            5.2.4 Flattery, sympathy, or intimation of adverse consequence directed at the associate personally;
            5.2.5 A request to redirect a payment instruction, alter a Direct Deposit Election, or update a Home Address on the basis of the call alone;
            5.2.6 Refusal or inability to provide a call-back reference that can be independently verified against the internal directory.
        5.3 Upon encountering any such indicium, the associate shall decline to confirm or deny the existence of any record, shall terminate the call courteously, and shall initiate the notification sequence at Section 6.

    6. INTERNAL NOTIFICATION SEQUENCE
        6.1 Suspected pretexting contact, misdirected correspondence, or apparent loss of a document: notify the Branch Operations Manager without delay, and in no event later than the close of the same business day.
        6.2 Confirmed or suspected disclosure of a Personal Identifier to an unauthorized party: notify the Privacy Officer, who shall assume direction of the matter.
        6.3 Questions regarding retention periods, shred console servicing, or file room access: direct to the Records Custodian.
        6.4 Suspicious electronic mail, credential prompts, or device compromise: report to the Information Security Lead through the internal directory listing posted at each workstation.
        6.5 Client-imposed handling requirements that appear inconsistent with this handout: refer to the Compliance Coordinator for reconciliation prior to acting.
        6.6 No associate shall conduct an independent investigation, contact the purported caller, or communicate with any external party concerning an incident absent express written authorization from the Privacy Officer.

    7. ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
        7.1 Each recipient shall execute the acknowledgement receipt distributed with this handout and return it to the Branch Operations Manager for filing with the Records Custodian.
