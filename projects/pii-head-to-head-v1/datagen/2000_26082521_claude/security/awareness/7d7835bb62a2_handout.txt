COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
DIVISION OF ADMINISTRATION

SAFEGUARDING IDENTIFIERS — QUICK REFERENCE FOR FRONT COUNTER STAFF

1.0  PURPOSE
     1.1  This handout sets out the minimum handling rules for any paper or
          electronic item that carries a personal identifier.
     1.2  It applies to all staff, temporary staff, students, and volunteers.

2.0  DEFINITIONS
     2.1  "Identifier" means any field that names or narrows to an individual,
          including: Name; Date of Birth; Social Security Number; Member ID;
          Chart Number; Address; Telephone; Email.
     2.2  "Field label" means the printed heading only. Labels may be discussed
          freely. The values entered beneath them may not.

3.0  DESTRUCTION
     3.1  Shred:
          -  Superseded intake forms
          -  Duplicate printouts
          -  Sticky notes bearing a chart number
          -  Envelopes showing a name and address through the window
          -  Printer test pages and misfeeds
     3.2  Do not shred anything that is part of the legal record. If in doubt,
          route it to the Records Custodian rather than the console.
     3.3  Consoles are emptied on the vendor's posted schedule. Do not stack
          overflow on top of or beside a console.

4.0  ELECTRONIC MAIL
     4.1  Email is not a secure channel and is not to be treated as one.
     4.2  Never place in an email: Social Security Number; Date of Birth with a
          name; Member ID with a name; scanned identification; roster
          attachments.
     4.3  Use the secure transfer service for anything above. Request access
          from the Information Systems Coordinator.
     4.4  Auto-forwarding of departmental mail to any outside account is
          prohibited without written approval from the Privacy Officer.

5.0  TELEPHONE REQUESTS AND PRETEXTING
     5.1  Assume the caller is unverified until proven otherwise.
     5.2  Warning signs: urgency; flattery; claimed authority; a request to
          "just confirm" details the caller recites; reluctance to submit a
          written request; a callback number that differs from the one on file.
     5.3  Response script: "I am not able to confirm any record information by
          telephone. Requests are handled by our Records Custodian, and I can
          give you the process for submitting one in writing."
     5.4  Log the attempt. Report to the Privacy Officer before end of shift.

6.0  CONTACTS BY ROLE
     6.1  Records Custodian — record requests, releases, legal process
     6.2  Privacy Officer — incidents, suspected pretexting, training questions
     6.3  Information Systems Coordinator — secure transfer, passwords, devices
     6.4  Office Manager — supplies, consoles, workspace security

7.0  REVIEW
     7.1  This guidance is reviewed annually by the Privacy Officer.
