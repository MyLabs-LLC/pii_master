    I.   WHAT WE HOLD ABOUT YOU

         A.  To provide water, wastewater, and electric service we maintain
             a customer record containing, among other entries:
                 1.  Account Number
                 2.  Service Address and Mailing Address
                 3.  Telephone Number and, where you have supplied one,
                     Email Address
                 4.  Date of Birth and the last portion of your Social
                     Security Number, held solely for identity verification
                 5.  Bank Routing Number and Bank Account Number, where you
                     have enrolled in automatic payment

         B.  We do not sell this information, and we do not disclose it to
             third parties except as required by law or as necessary to
             collect a lawful debt.

    II.  WHAT YOU SHOULD DESTROY RATHER THAN DISCARD

         A.  Cross-cut shred, rather than bin, the following:
                 1.  Old billing statements and payment receipts.
                 2.  Disconnection and final-notice letters.
                 3.  Deposit refund advices and enrolment confirmations.
                 4.  Any correspondence from us bearing your Account Number.

         B.  Statements older than the current tax year need not be retained
             unless you require them as proof of residency.

    III. WHAT WE WILL NEVER ASK YOU TO SEND BY EMAIL

         A.  Do not place any of the following in an email message to the
             Department, and disregard any message that requests them:
                 1.  Social Security Number, in whole or in part
                 2.  Payment Card Number, Expiration Date, or Security Code
                 3.  Bank Routing Number or Bank Account Number
                 4.  A photograph or scan of a driver's licence

         B.  Payments and documents may be lodged through the secure customer
             portal, at the payment kiosk in the lobby, by post, or in person
             at the business office.

    IV.  HOW TO RECOGNISE A PRETEXTING CALL

         A.  A pretexting call is a fraudulent call designed to make you
             surrender information or money. Common signs:
                 1.  The caller says your service will be cut off within the
                     hour unless you pay immediately.
                 2.  The caller demands payment by prepaid card, wire
                     transfer, or gift card. We accept none of these.
                 3.  The caller already claims to know part of your account
                     details and asks you to "confirm the rest".
                 4.  The caller refuses to let you ring the Department back.

         B.  Hang up. Ring the business office number printed on your
             statement — not a number given to you by the caller — and ask
             for the Customer Service Supervisor.

    V.   WHO TO CONTACT

         A.  Billing disputes and suspected fraudulent calls — the Customer
             Service Supervisor.
         B.  Requests for copies of your own record, or corrections to it —
             the Records Custodian.
         C.  Concerns about how your information is handled — the Privacy
             Officer, Office of the Director.
