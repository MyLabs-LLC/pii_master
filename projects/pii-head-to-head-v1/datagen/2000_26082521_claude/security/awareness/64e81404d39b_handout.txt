INFORMATION PROTECTION STANDARD — PRETEXTING AND IDENTIFIER HANDLING

Standard Reference:  ISP-7-09
Supersedes:          ISP-7-09, Revision 1 (2019)
Effective Date:      14 June 2021
Review Cycle:        Annual
Owner (Role Title):  Chief Compliance Officer
Applies To:          All branch, contact centre, and back-office personnel

1.  PURPOSE

1.1 This Standard establishes the minimum controls governing the retention,
    destruction, and transmission of personal identifiers held by the Credit
    Union, and prescribes the response required of personnel upon receipt of a
    communication bearing the characteristics of a pretexting attempt.

2.  DESTRUCTION REQUIREMENTS

2.1 Any physical medium bearing a Member Account Number, Social Security
    Number, Date of Birth, Government ID Number, card number, signature
    specimen, or Online Banking credential shall be destroyed by cross-cut
    shredder or deposited in a locked destruction console. Deposit in an open
    waste receptacle, a recycling stream, or an unattended bin is prohibited.

2.2 The prohibition in clause 2.1 extends to teller proof tapes, adding-machine
    tapes, wire confirmations, card carriers, credit report extracts, notes
    taken during a member interview, and any working paper on which an
    identifier has been transcribed in whole or in part.

2.3 A destruction console shall not be left unlocked, propped, or overfilled at
    any time. Custody of the console key rests with the Records Custodian.

3.  TRANSMISSION REQUIREMENTS

3.1 Personal identifiers shall not be placed in electronic mail under any
    circumstance, including replies, forwards, calendar invitations, and
    automatic signature blocks. Members requesting delivery of documents by
    e-mail shall be redirected to the secure member portal and the redirection
    shall be noted in the service log.

4.  PRETEXTING INDICATORS AND PRESCRIBED RESPONSE

INDICATOR OBSERVED                  PRESCRIBED RESPONSE
==================================  ==========================================
Caller asserts urgency or penalty   Terminate call; log with Fraud Watch Desk
Caller seeks full SSN readback      Refuse; readback is prohibited absolutely
Caller declines verified callback   Terminate call; treat as a pretexting act
Caller claims internal authority    Verify through Branch Operations Manager
Caller requests e-mail of records   Refuse; direct to the secure portal only
Caller supplies partial identifier  Do not confirm or correct any character
Caller invokes regulator or court   Refer to the Chief Compliance Officer

5.  ESCALATION

5.1 Escalation shall proceed in the following order and shall not be abridged:
    the Fraud Watch Desk Supervisor; thereafter the Branch Operations Manager;
    thereafter the Chief Compliance Officer. Where the contact concerns
    lending documentation, the Lending Compliance Officer shall be included.

5.2 Failure to escalate within the same business day constitutes a reportable
    control deficiency and shall be disclosed to the Supervisory Committee.

6.  ENFORCEMENT

6.1 Non-observance of this Standard may result in corrective action up to and
    including termination of employment or of contract, without prejudice to
    any further remedy available to the Credit Union at law.
