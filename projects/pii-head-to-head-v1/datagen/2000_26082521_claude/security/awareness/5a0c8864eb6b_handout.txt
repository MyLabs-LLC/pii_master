CEDAR VALLEY UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT
INTERIM PROCEDURE 2004-06
SUSPECTED DISCLOSURE OF A PERSONAL IDENTIFIER
Effective on issue. Applies to all classified and certificated staff.

STEP  ACTION                                        RESPONSIBLE ROLE
----  --------------------------------------------  --------------------------
  1   Stop work; secure the page or the screen.     Employee who observes
  2   Write down what was seen and when.            Employee who observes
  3   Deliver the note to the site office.          Employee who observes
  4   Log the report; issue a receipt number.       The Site Principal
  5   Identify which field labels were exposed.     The Records Custodian
  6   Secure the file, drive, or mailbox.           The Network Administrator
  7   Decide whether written notice is required.    The Dir. of Human Resources
  8   Prepare any notice to affected persons.       The Public Information Off.
  9   Report the outcome to the cabinet.            The Business Manager
 10   File the closed report for retention.         The Records Custodian

WHAT COUNTS AS A DISCLOSURE
  - A page bearing an R1 or R2 field label left on a copier, a counter, or a
    printer tray.
  - An electronic mail message containing any field listed in Desk Card 4.
  - A file cabinet, health office, or records room found unlocked overnight.
  - A telephone caller who obtained a field before the call was ended.
  - A district computer, diskette, or backup tape that cannot be located.

DO NOT
  - Do not attempt to recall a message by writing again to the recipient.
  - Do not delete a log, a message, or a file until the Network Administrator
    releases it.
  - Do not discuss the matter outside the reporting chain shown above.
  - Do not speak to a caller, a vendor, or a member of the press about it.

Steps 1 through 3 are completed on the day of the observation. Steps 4 and 5
are completed within one working day. The Director of Human Resources reports
the status of every open item at the weekly cabinet meeting until it is closed.
