CLASS KEY
  R1  Restricted.  Held only by the assigned custodian. Never duplicated.
  R2  Confidential. Released only on written authorisation already on file.
  R3  Internal.    Not for public posting or outside distribution.

SHRED — WHAT AND HOW
  Cross-cut shredding is required for every page bearing an R1 or R2 field
  label: drafts, surplus copies, fax cover sheets, mailing labels, routing
  slips, and printer test pages. Strip shredders may not be used for R1
  fields. No R1 or R2 page may be placed in a recycling tote, a classroom
  scrap box, or an unlocked collection bin. Bagged shred waste is held in the
  locked custodial closet until the contracted pickup.

NEVER SEND BY ELECTRONIC MAIL
  District electronic mail is not encrypted and is retained on the server.
  Do not type an R1 or R2 value into a message body, a subject line, an
  attached spreadsheet, or a calendar entry. Do not forward a message that
  already contains one. Print it, send the paper by interoffice mail, then
  delete the message from the sent folder as well as the inbox.

TELEPHONE REQUESTS
  A pretexting call sounds ordinary. The usual patterns: the caller claims to
  be from the county office, a benefits carrier, or a district vendor and asks
  you to "verify" a field the caller appears to hold already; the caller reads
  out most of a value and asks only for the remainder; the caller presses a
  deadline; the caller asks you to stay on the line while you read a screen;
  the caller offers a return number rather than accepting yours. Take the
  caller's stated organisation and reason. State that the district returns
  such calls only through numbers already on file. End the call and refer the
  matter as shown below.
