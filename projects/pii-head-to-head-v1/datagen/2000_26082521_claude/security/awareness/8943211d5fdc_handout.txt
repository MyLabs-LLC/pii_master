BRANCH BULLETIN — HANDLING OF IDENTIFYING DATA
Office of the Compliance Coordinator | 2021

    I. GENERAL OBLIGATION
        A. Every associate is charged with the safekeeping of identifying data entrusted to the Agency, and shall exercise not less than reasonable care in the handling thereof.

    II. SHRED — WITHOUT EXCEPTION
        A. Application packets and interview notes
        B. Copies of identity and work authorization documents
        C. Direct deposit authorizations and payroll adjustment slips
        D. Any misprint, duplicate, or discarded page bearing the field labels Social Security Number, Date of Birth, or Account Number

    III. NEVER BY ELECTRONIC MAIL
        A. Social Security Number
        B. Date of Birth
        C. Bank Routing Number or Account Number
        D. Photographed or scanned identity credentials
        E. Spreadsheets containing the foregoing columns, whether or not password-protected

    IV. PRETEXTING — WARNING SIGNS
        A. Unverifiable claim of authority
        B. Manufactured urgency and pressure to skip verification
        C. Request to read back data the caller says is already held
        D. Request to change a payment instruction by telephone

    V. WHOM TO TELL
        A. Immediately — the Branch Operations Manager
        B. For any suspected disclosure — the Privacy Officer
        C. For records and retention questions — the Records Custodian
