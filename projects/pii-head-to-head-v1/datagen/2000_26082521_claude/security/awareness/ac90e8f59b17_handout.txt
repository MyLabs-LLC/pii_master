OPERATIONS NOTICE — HANDLING AND DISPOSAL OF IDENTIFYING DOCUMENTATION

1. Application. This notice governs all staff at branch and administrative locations who create, receive, image, or dispose of documentation bearing member identifiers. It supplements, and does not displace, the retention schedule maintained by the Records Custodian.

2. Field inventory. The following labels denote restricted content wherever they appear on a form, screen, report, or worksheet: Member Name; Member Number; Social Security Number; Individual Taxpayer Identification Number; Date of Birth; Mother's Maiden Name; Driver's License Number and Issuing State; Home Address; Mailing Address; Home Telephone; Mobile Telephone; Personal Email Address; Employer Name; Account Number; Card Number; Card Expiration; Personal Identification Number; Online Banking Username; Security Question and Answer. A document containing any one of these fields is restricted for its entire life, irrespective of the remaining content.

3. Clear desk. Restricted documentation is not to remain on a work surface, in an open tray, on a printer, or at a fax device outside the immediate period of use. At the close of each shift, all such material is secured in a locked drawer or returned to the file room. Workstations are to be locked whenever unattended, including for brief absences from the teller line.

4. Destruction. Material identified for destruction is placed in the locked consoles provided, and not in waste receptacles or recycling containers. Console keys are held by the Branch Operations Manager. Destruction by the contracted vendor is performed under the certificate of destruction procedure, and the certificates are filed by the Records Custodian for the period prescribed in the retention schedule. Ad hoc shredding at the branch is permitted only with cross-cut equipment and only for volumes that cannot await the scheduled collection.

5. Transmission. Restricted fields are not to be entered into the body or subject line of an outbound electronic mail message, are not to be included in a message to a personal address of a member or a member of staff, and are not to be transmitted to any external party except through the approved secure channel with prior authorisation. Internal distribution follows the minimum necessary principle: restricted material is provided to the individual whose duties require it and to no wider audience.

6. Reporting. A suspected or actual disclosure is reported immediately to the Information Security Officer, and in the absence of that officer, to the Compliance Officer. Reporting is not discretionary and is not to be delayed pending internal investigation or confirmation of harm. Retaliation against a member of staff who reports in good faith is prohibited.
