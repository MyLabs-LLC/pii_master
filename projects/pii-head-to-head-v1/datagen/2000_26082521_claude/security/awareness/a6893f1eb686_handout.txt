COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
OFFICE OF THE HEALTH OFFICER
IDENTIFIER PROTECTION NOTICE IP-04 — ISSUED 2021
Applies to: all divisions, clinics, and field programs

1.0 Application. This notice binds staff, volunteers, students, and contracted personnel who create, receive, transport, or destroy records containing personal identifiers.

2.0 Destruction. Any paper bearing a Client Name, Date of Birth, Social Security Number, Medical Record Number, Case Number, or Insurance Member ID shall be deposited in a locked destruction console. Desk-side cross-cut shredding is permitted for single sheets only. Recycling bins, general waste, and unattended carts are not approved destruction routes. Draft worksheets, appointment slips, fax cover sheets, and printed screen captures fall within this clause.

3.0 Electronic transmission. The following field values shall not appear in the body, subject line, or attachment of unencrypted electronic mail: Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Medical Record Number, Immunization Registry ID, Home Address, Telephone Number, Financial Account Number, Driver License Number. Where transmission is operationally necessary, use the secure gateway and obtain concurrence of the Privacy Officer for any external recipient.

4.0 Pretexting. A caller who asserts urgency, claims an authority not shown on the organizational chart, offers a partial identifier and asks staff to complete it, or resists transfer to the Records Custodian shall be treated as a pretext caller. Staff shall neither confirm nor deny that a record exists. Terminate the call and enter the stated affiliation and time on the Suspicious Contact Log.

5.0 Reporting. Report within one business day to the Privacy Officer; in that officer's absence, to the Records Custodian or the Information Security Officer.
