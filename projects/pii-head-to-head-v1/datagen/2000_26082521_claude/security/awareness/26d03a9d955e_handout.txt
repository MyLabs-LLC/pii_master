PERSONAL IDENTIFIER HANDLING STANDARD — BRANCH AND ON-SITE OPERATIONS

Issue 4. Effective from the first working day of Q3 2021. Supersedes Issue 3.
Applies to: branch consultants, on-site coordinators, payroll administrators, compliance vetting staff, and all workers registered on the agency payroll.
Document owner: the Data Protection Lead. Technical custodian: the Records Custodian.

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
1.1 This Standard sets the minimum controls for the collection, transmission, storage and destruction of personal identifiers held by the agency in the course of placement, vetting and payroll activity.
1.2 It is binding on permanent staff, temporary staff, and contracted third parties with access to the placement management system.
1.3 Where a client site imposes a stricter control, the stricter control applies.

2. DEFINITIONS
2.1 "Personal identifier" means any data field capable, alone or in combination, of identifying a natural person. The controlled field set is: Full Legal Name; Former Name; Date of Birth; National Insurance / Social Security Number; Passport Number; Driving Licence Number; Right-to-Work Document Reference; Visa or Share Code Reference; Home Address; Personal Telephone Number; Personal Email Address; Bank Sort Code; Bank Account Number; Payroll Reference; Next of Kin Name; Next of Kin Telephone Number; Occupational Health Reference.
2.2 "Restricted field" means any field listed at clause 2.1 other than Full Legal Name and Payroll Reference.
2.3 "Pretexting" means an attempt to obtain personal identifiers by asserting a false identity, false authority, or false urgency.

3. COLLECTION
3.1 Restricted fields shall be collected only through the registration portal or the vetting workstation. Collection by handwritten note is prohibited.
3.2 Staff shall collect the minimum field set required for the stated purpose. Recording a Passport Number where a Right-to-Work Document Reference suffices is a breach of this clause.
3.3 Identity documents presented in person shall be verified visually, recorded as a verification outcome, and returned immediately. Photocopying of identity documents at branch counters is prohibited.

4. TRANSMISSION
4.1 The following shall never be transmitted by email, whether in the body, in an attachment, or in a calendar invitation: National Insurance / Social Security Number; Bank Sort Code; Bank Account Number; Passport Number; Driving Licence Number; Date of Birth; Occupational Health Reference.
4.2 The prohibition at clause 4.1 applies irrespective of recipient, including transmission between agency mailboxes.
4.3 Permitted channels for restricted fields are: the placement management system; the payroll secure transfer facility; and the client portal where one has been formally provisioned.
4.4 Restricted fields shall not be transmitted by SMS, instant messaging, personal messaging applications, or photographs of screens or documents.
4.5 Where a client requests restricted fields by email, the request shall be refused and referred to the Account Compliance Manager.

5. STORAGE AND PRINTING
5.1 Restricted fields shall not be stored on local drives, removable media, or personal devices.
5.2 Printing of documents containing restricted fields requires a stated operational reason and shall use secure release printing only.
5.3 Printed material containing restricted fields shall not be left unattended at any time, including during short absences from the desk.

6. DESTRUCTION
6.1 The following shall be cross-cut shredded at the point they cease to be required: registration forms; timesheets bearing Payroll Reference and Bank Account Number; vetting checklists; interview notes recording Date of Birth or Next of Kin details; envelopes bearing Home Address; printed payslips; rejected or superseded bank mandate forms.
6.2 Strip-cut shredding does not satisfy clause 6.1.
6.3 Confidential waste sacks shall be sealed at the close of each business day and lodged in the secure waste cage. Confidential waste shall not be placed in general recycling under any circumstances.
6.4 Failure of the shredding equipment shall be reported to the Facilities Coordinator and the affected material retained in the locked cabinet pending disposal.

7. PRETEXTING AND UNSOLICITED CONTACT
7.1 Staff shall treat as suspicious any inbound call exhibiting one or more of the following: an assertion of internal authority without a verifiable identity; pressure to bypass the callback procedure; a stated deadline framed as a payroll cut-off; a request to confirm rather than supply a Bank Account Number; or a request for a worker's Date of Birth as a "verification" step.
7.2 Staff shall not disclose any restricted field on an inbound call. The caller shall be advised that the branch will return the call using the number held on file.
7.3 The callback shall use the contact record in the placement management system only. A number supplied by the caller shall not be used.

8. REPORTING
8.1 Suspected pretexting shall be reported to the Data Protection Lead on the same working day.
8.2 Actual or suspected disclosure of a restricted field shall be reported to the Data Protection Lead immediately and in any event within one hour of discovery.
8.3 Loss of printed material or removable media shall be reported to the Records Custodian and the Branch Manager.
8.4 Queries concerning worker access requests shall be directed to the Data Protection Lead. Queries concerning client contractual terms shall be directed to the Account Compliance Manager.

9. NON-COMPLIANCE
9.1 Breach of clauses 4.1, 5.1 or 6.4 shall be referred to the Branch Manager and may be treated as a disciplinary matter.
