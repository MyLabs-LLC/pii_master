PROTECTING YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION
A Notice to Our Pharmacy Patients

1. We collect only the information needed to fill your prescription and bill your health plan. This includes your name, date of birth, address, plan member number, and prescriber details.

2. We will never ask you for your Social Security number by telephone.

3. We will never ask you for a payment card number, card expiration date, or bank account number by telephone or by electronic mail.

4. Please do not send us your date of birth, member number, or card details by electronic mail. Electronic mail is not a secure channel. Bring the information to the pharmacy counter or telephone the number printed on your prescription receipt.

5. If a caller claims to represent this pharmacy and asks you to confirm identifying details, hang up and call the pharmacy directly using a number you already have.

6. Shred, rather than discard, any paper carrying your name together with a prescription number, member number, or account number. This includes pharmacy receipts, prescription labels peeled from containers, and insurance correspondence.

7. Remove or destroy the label before recycling an empty prescription container.

8. Ask for your printed medication profile to be handed to you directly. We do not leave profiles at the counter unattended.

9. To ask a question about how your information is used, or to request a copy of our Notice of Privacy Practices, speak to the Pharmacist-in-Charge at this location.

10. To make a formal privacy complaint, ask the Pharmacist-in-Charge to refer you to the Privacy Official.
