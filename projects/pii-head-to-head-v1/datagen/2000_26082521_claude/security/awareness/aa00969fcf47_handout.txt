RECOGNIZING A PRETEXT CALL
Front Counter and Escrow Assistant Handout
Prepared by the Compliance Department, 2011

A. What a pretext call is
   1. A caller invents a role and a reason in order to draw out information we would not otherwise release.
   2. The caller is usually pleasant, well prepared, and in a hurry. Pressure and courtesy arrive together.

B. Common covers
   1. The lender's funder needing "confirmation" of wire instructions before a cutoff.
   2. A relative of the seller who says the seller is traveling and cannot be reached.
   3. Someone from our own company at another branch, or from the "corporate audit team," who needs a file read aloud.
   4. A courier or notary asking for the Property Address and Borrower Name together so a package can be delivered.

C. Signals worth stopping for
   1. Urgency tied to a deadline the caller supplies.
   2. A request that you read back a field rather than answer yes or no. Requests to confirm the last digits of a Bank Account Number belong here.
   3. Background that does not match the claim, such as an office claim with public noise behind it.
   4. Refusal to be called back at a number we already hold on file.
   5. Small partial information offered first, so that you supply the rest.

D. What to do
   1. Do not confirm or deny that a file exists.
   2. Take the caller's name, company, and callback number, and end the call.
   3. Call back only on a number drawn from the file or the lender's published main line, never on the number the caller gives.
   4. Verify through the escrow officer of record before any information moves.
   5. Write the attempt up the same day.

E. Wording you may use
   1. When asked to confirm a file:
      "I'm not able to discuss whether we hold a file. I can take your information and have the escrow officer return your call."
   2. When pressed on a deadline:
      "I understand the timing. Our verification step doesn't change. I'll pass this along now."
   3. When the caller claims to be internal:
      "I'll reach you through the internal directory. What is your role title and branch?"

F. Reporting
   1. Tell the Escrow Branch Manager immediately.
   2. Send the written note to the Compliance Officer, copying the Information Security Manager if wire instructions were mentioned at any point.
   3. Do not warn the caller that a report is being made.
