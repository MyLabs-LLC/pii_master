MEMORANDUM

TO:        All Terminal, Dock, Dispatch, and Administrative Personnel
FROM:      Office of the Records Custodian

DATE:      3 May 2004
SUBJECT:   Protection and Disposal of Personal Identifiers
CONTROL:   RC-2004-017 (supersedes RC-2002-009)

1. SCOPE

This memorandum applies to every location operating under the company's motor carrier and warehouse authorities, including leased dock space and satellite dispatch offices. It governs any record — paper, microfiche, diskette, CD-R, or network file — carrying one or more of the data elements listed in Section 2.

2. DEFINITION

A personal identifier is any of the following data elements when held in association with a named individual:

   Social Security Number
   Driver's License Number / Issuing State
   Date of Birth
   Bank Routing Number and Account Number
   Payment Card Number / Expiration Date / Card Verification Value
   Employee Number
   Medical Examiner's Certificate Number
   Home Address and Home Telephone Number
   Consignee Contact Name and Telephone Number

3. DESTRUCTION REQUIREMENTS

Cross-cut destruction is mandatory for the following once the retention period stated in the Records Retention Schedule has elapsed:

   - Working copies of driver qualification files
   - Employment applications, W-4 and I-9 worksheets, road test certificates
   - Motor vehicle record printouts and annual review forms
   - Controlled substance testing chain-of-custody copies
   - Payroll registers, garnishment notices, benefit enrollment forms
   - Workers' compensation first report of injury forms
   - Misprinted or voided delivery receipts and bills of lading bearing consignee contact fields
   - Fax cover sheets, courier manifests, gate and visitor logs

Strip-cut shredders are withdrawn from service effective this quarter and are not to be used for any item above. Material awaiting destruction is to be held in the locked console at each terminal. Consoles are not to be propped open. Personal identifiers are never to be placed in recycling toters, dock dumpsters, or trailer sweep. A Certificate of Destruction is retained by this office for each console service.

4. TRANSMISSION PROHIBITIONS

The elements listed in Section 2 are not to be placed in the body, subject line, or attachment of Internet electronic mail under any circumstances. This includes payroll spreadsheets exported from the personnel system, scanned qualification documents, and driver rosters compiled for customer bid packages. External web mail services are not to be used for company business, and mailbox auto-forwarding rules directing mail off the corporate domain are prohibited. Approved channels are the EDI gateway, the secure file transfer host, and the interoffice courier pouch. Facsimile transmission is permitted only to an attended machine, with a transmission report retained.

5. RECOGNISING A PRETEXTING CALL

Pretexting is the practice of obtaining identifiers by impersonation. Typical markers:

   - Caller asserts urgency and a supervisor's authority but cannot cite a role title
   - Caller volunteers a partial identifier and asks staff to "confirm the rest"
   - Caller claims a system outage, a lost fax, or an audit deadline
   - Caller requests the file be read aloud, faxed to an outside number, or mailed to a residence
   - Caller resists a return call to a number listed in the internal directory

Terminate collection of the request, obtain the stated company and reason, offer a call-back, and report the contact the same shift.
