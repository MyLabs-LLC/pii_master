METROPOLITAN TRANSIT AUTHORITY
OFFICE OF RECORDS MANAGEMENT
STAFF NOTICE 98-11 — PROTECTION OF PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS

Post at every work station. Do not remove.

SHRED — CROSS-CUT MACHINE ONLY
- Employment applications, interview notes, reference check worksheets
- Monthly pass applications bearing Social Security Number, Date of Birth, or Home Address
- Paratransit eligibility forms and attached physician certifications
- Payroll registers, garnishment notices, benefit enrolment forms
- Carbon sets, used ribbon cartridges, and misfeeds from the personnel printer
- Fare media replacement requests bearing Card Account Number

NEVER TRANSMIT BY ELECTRONIC MAIL
- Social Security Number, in whole or in part
- Employee Number in combination with Date of Birth
- Bank Routing Number or Direct Deposit Account Number
- Operator Licence Number or endorsement status
- Medical restriction codes or disability certification status
- Scanned or facsimile images of any item listed above

PRETEXTING — RECOGNITION INDICATORS
- Caller asserts urgency and declines a return call
- Caller recites a partial identifier and asks staff to confirm the remainder
- Caller claims a senior title but cannot produce a work order number
- Caller requests transmission by facsimile to a temporary machine
- Caller telephones outside business hours to reach relief personnel
- Caller states that the request has "already been cleared upstairs"

REQUIRED ACTION
- Terminate the call. Neither confirm nor deny any element.
- Record the time, the extension reached, and the wording of the request.
- Notify the Divisional Security Officer during the same shift.
- Lodge Form RM-6 with the Records Custodian within one business day.

Direct all questions to the Records Custodian, Office of Records Management.
