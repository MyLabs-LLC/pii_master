CONFIDENTIAL WASTE — COUNTER AND BACK-OFFICE INSTRUCTION

Posted notice. Retain at the shredding station. 2021.

1. Everything listed at paragraph 2 goes to cross-cut shred. Nothing listed at paragraph 2 goes to recycling, general waste, or home.

2. Shred:
   - registration and re-registration forms
   - bank mandate forms, including spoiled and superseded copies
   - timesheets and timesheet correction slips
   - printed payslips and payslip reprints
   - vetting checklists and right-to-work verification sheets
   - interview and reference notes
   - printed emails containing Full Legal Name against any other field
   - address labels, envelopes and courier dockets
   - handwritten notes bearing a Payroll Reference

3. Do not shred documents subject to a live retention hold. If a document carries a hold marker, route it to the Records Custodian unopened.

4. Sacks are sealed at close of business by the person holding the counter keys. Sealed sacks go to the secure cage. Sacks are not stored under desks overnight.

5. If the shredder jams or fails, stop. Do not remove material from the machine. Lock the pending material in the counter cabinet and notify the Facilities Coordinator.

6. Never send Bank Account Number, Bank Sort Code, National Insurance / Social Security Number, Passport Number or Date of Birth by email. There is no exception for internal recipients.

7. Report anything you shredded in error to the Records Custodian the same day.
