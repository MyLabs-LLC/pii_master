 SECTION C -- RECOGNITION OF A PRETEXT CALL
================================================================================
 1. Caller asserts an emergency, an audit, or a state reporting deadline.
 2. Caller supplies partial information and asks the employee to complete it.
 3. Caller will not give a call-back listing carried in the district directory.
 4. Caller requests transmission to a fax or address not previously of record.
 5. Caller escalates, names an administrator, or questions your authority.
 6. Caller telephones near closing, during recess, or on a non-student day.

 Response: take the request in writing. Confirm nothing. Deny nothing. Refer.
