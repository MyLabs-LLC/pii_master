CARDINAL VALLEY MUTUAL INSURANCE COMPANY
POLICYHOLDER NOTICE PS-8 (REV. 01/99)
PROTECTING THE IDENTIFIERS IN YOUR POLICY FILE

A.  WHAT WE HOLD
    Your file contains a Policy Number, a Member Identification Number, a Social Security Number, a Date of Birth, a Mailing Address, and, where a claim has been submitted, a Claim File Number and associated treatment coding. Access is restricted to personnel with a servicing need.

B.  WHAT TO DESTROY AT HOME
    Cross-cut shred, rather than discard whole, any of the following once you no longer require them: premium notices and billing statements; explanation-of-benefits stubs; policy declaration pages superseded by a later endorsement; correspondence bearing your Member Identification Number; and unsolicited insurance offers, which frequently carry a pre-assigned reference tied to your name.

C.  WHAT NOT TO SEND BY ELECTRONIC MAIL
    Do not send us your Social Security Number, Taxpayer Identification Number, Deposit Account Number, or Credit Card Account Number by electronic mail. Internet mail passes through systems we do not control and is not treated by this Company as a secure channel. Correspond by post, by facsimile to the number printed on your billing statement, or by telephone. Where an identifier is required, cite your Policy Number only; we will locate the file from it.

D.  TELEPHONE CALLS YOU DID NOT PLACE
    Company personnel will not telephone you and request your Social Security Number, your bank account details, or your mother's maiden name in full. A caller who asserts that your policy will lapse today, who reads a partial number and asks you to complete it, or who declines to let you call back is not acting for this Company. End the call and telephone the number printed on your policy declarations page.

E.  WHOM TO CONTACT
    Billing and address changes ....... Policyholder Services Supervisor
    Suspected fraudulent contact ...... Special Investigations Unit Coordinator
    Request for a copy of your file ... Records Custodian
    Questions about this notice ....... Privacy Compliance Officer

Direct written correspondence to the role title above at the branch address shown on your billing statement.
