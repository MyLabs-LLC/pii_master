OFFICE OF THE UNIVERSITY REGISTRAR
STAFF AND CUSTOMER GUIDANCE HANDOUT RG-2004-07
PROTECTION OF PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS HELD IN STUDENT EDUCATION RECORDS

Issued by ......... the University Registrar
Concurrence ....... the Associate Vice President for Enrollment Services
Effective ......... first day of instruction, Fall Term 2004
Supersedes ........ Handout RG-2002-03
Distribution ...... all Registrar staff; posted at the public service counter

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
   1.1 This handout states the mandatory handling rules for protected identifiers
       appearing in student education records maintained by this Office, as
       required by the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act and by the
       University information security program adopted under the Safeguards Rule.
   1.2 "Protected identifier" means any of the following fields, whether printed,
       displayed, or stored on removable media: Social Security Number, Student
       Identification Number, Date of Birth, Mother's Maiden Name, Bank Account
       Number, Driver's License Number, and Personal Identification Number.
   1.3 This handout binds all regular staff, student assistants, and temporary
       employees. Compliance is a condition of continued access to the Student
       Information System.

2. DESTRUCTION OF PAPER AND MEDIA
   2.1 No item listed in Table A may be placed in a wastebasket, a recycling
       container, or an unattended out-tray at any time, including after hours.
   2.2 Shredding is performed at the point of creation and is not deferred to a
       later shift. Strip-cut devices are not acceptable for these materials.
   2.3 Locked destruction bins are opened only in the presence of the Records
       Custodian or a written designee.

TABLE A -- DISPOSITION OF MATERIALS BEARING PROTECTED IDENTIFIERS

+--------------------------------+------------------------------------+----------------------------+
| Material                       | Identifier Fields Present          | Required Disposition       |
+--------------------------------+------------------------------------+----------------------------+
| Printed class roster           | Name; SSN; Student ID; Grade       | Cross-cut shred            |
| Transcript request form        | Name; SSN; Date of Birth           | Cross-cut shred            |
| Enrollment verification stub   | Name; Student ID; Term Code        | Cross-cut shred            |
| Fee payment remittance slip    | Name; Bank Account Number          | Cross-cut shred            |
| Diskette or CD-R (SIS extract) | All fields in the extract          | Physical destruction       |
| Fax cover sheet and pages      | Name; SSN; Mailing Address         | Cross-cut shred            |
| Screen print of SIS record     | Name; SSN; Hold Code               | Cross-cut shred            |
| Draft correspondence           | Name; Student ID                   | Cross-cut shred            |
| Printer ribbon or carbon set   | Any field printed that day         | Locked destruction bin     |
+--------------------------------+------------------------------------+----------------------------+

3. ELECTRONIC MAIL
   3.1 The fields named in clause 1.2 shall never appear in the body, the subject
       line, or an attachment of an electronic mail message, internal or external.
   3.2 A request received by electronic mail is answered by campus mail or in
       person at the service counter. The reply repeats no protected field.
   3.3 Extracts drawn from the Student Information System move only by the secure
       file transfer service approved by the Information Security Officer.
   3.4 Automatic forwarding of University mail to a personal account is prohibited.

4. RECOGNISING A PRETEXTING CALL
   4.1 Treat as a pretexting attempt any caller who asserts urgency, invokes rank,
       supplies part of a protected field and invites staff to confirm the
       remainder, declines a return call, or asks which fields the record contains.
   4.2 Staff neither confirm nor deny a value offered by a caller.
   4.3 Terminate the call and return it using the listing published in the
       University directory. Do not use a number offered by the caller.
   4.4 Record the attempt on the Suspicious Contact Log: Date, Time, Channel,
       Fields Requested, Pretext Claimed, Action Taken, Staff Initials.

5. INTERNAL CONTACTS
   5.1 Suspected disclosure: the Records Custodian, immediately.
   5.2 System access and passwords: the Registrar Security Coordinator.
   5.3 Subpoenas and law-enforcement demands: the University Registrar.
   5.4 Lost or stolen media: the Information Security Officer.
