IDENTITY SAFEGUARDS — PHARMACY COUNTER CHECKLIST
Issue 3. Post at the fill station. Do not remove or annotate.

SHRED (LOCKED CONSOLE ONLY — NOT TRASH, NOT RECYCLING, NOT DUMPSTER)
- Misprinted, voided, or rejected prescription labels
- Third-party rejection slips bearing Member ID, Group Number, or BIN
- Claim reversal printouts and adjudication worklists
- Signature log sheets once the posted retention period has run
- Fax cover sheets, transmission reports, and misdirected inbound faxes
- Labels stripped from returned, expired, or recalled stock
- Register tape and terminal receipts showing truncated card data
- Any handwritten note bearing Patient Name, Date of Birth, or Rx Number
- Delivery manifests and will-call bag tags after pickup is confirmed

NEVER TRANSMIT BY EMAIL — INBOUND OR OUTBOUND, INTERNAL OR EXTERNAL
- Social Security Number, in whole or in part
- Date of Birth paired with any other identifier
- Cardholder ID, Member ID, Policy Number, Account Number
- Prescription Number paired with Patient Name
- Scanned images or photographs of any of the above
- Spreadsheets of patient rosters, refill lists, or transfer logs

If a sender emails such data to you: do not forward, do not reply with the data quoted, do not print. Report to the Store Privacy Coordinator the same shift.

PRETEXTING CALL — TERMINATE AND REPORT IF THE CALLER
- Claims to be corporate, IT, or audit and will not be called back
- Cites urgency, threatened termination, or a "system outage"
- Requests a patient list, a full profile, or bulk data
- Supplies a partial identifier and asks you to confirm the remainder
- Refuses to provide a verifiable prescriber DEA or NPI on file
- Objects when told the call will be returned to the number on record

Return calls only to the number already held in the system record. Never the number offered by the caller.

ESCALATION
- Suspected pretexting: Pharmacy Manager on Duty, immediately
- Confirmed disclosure: Store Privacy Coordinator, within the shift
- Lost or open shred console: District Loss Prevention Manager
- All incidents log to the Regional Compliance Officer within 24 hours
