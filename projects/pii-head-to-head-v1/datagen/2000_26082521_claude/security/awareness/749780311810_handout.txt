COUNTY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY
OFFICE OF THE DATA PROTECTION OFFICER
HANDOUT DP-4:  RECOGNITION OF THE PRETEXTING TELEPHONE CALL
ISSUED TO ALL CUSTOMER SERVICE, PERSONNEL, PAYROLL, AND PASS SALES STAFF

A pretexting call is a telephone call in which the calling party assumes a
false character, whether that of an employee, of an official, of a carrier,
or of a customer, for the purpose of inducing an employee of the Authority to
disclose a personal identifier held in the Authority's records. The calling
party is under no obligation to answer truthfully and frequently possesses a
fragment of genuine information, obtained from a discarded document or from a
prior call, by which the balance of a record is sought to be extracted. The
conditions enumerated below have been observed in calls reported to this
Office and shall be treated by every employee as sufficient grounds to
suspend the transaction.

No employee is authorized to read aloud, to confirm, to deny, or to correct
any value appearing under the labels Social Security Number, Date of Birth,
Home Address, Employee Identification Number, Fare Card Account Number, or
Pension Member Number, irrespective of the apparent authority of the caller.
An employee who declines a request under this handout acts within the scope
of employment and shall not be subject to criticism, correction, or
discipline on account of that refusal, and any complaint arising therefrom
shall be directed to the Data Protection Officer for disposition.
