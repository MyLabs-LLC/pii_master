WORKSHEET CS-22
CORPORATE SECURITY DEPARTMENT
RECOGNITION AND REPORTING OF PRETEXT TELEPHONE CONTACTS

PART I — PURPOSE
  1.  A pretext call is a telephone contact in which the caller adopts
      a false identity in order to obtain personal information about
      an insured, a claimant, an employee or a producer. The caller is
      typically well spoken, well prepared, and in possession of one
      or two genuine details of the file.

PART II — INDICATORS (mark each observed)
  [ ]  2.1  Caller supplies a partial identifier and invites you to
            supply or correct the remainder.
  [ ]  2.2  Caller claims to be an adjuster, attorney, physician's
            office, employer or state examiner but cannot produce a
            file reference in the form used by the Company.
  [ ]  2.3  Caller asserts urgency: a hearing, a surgery, a funeral,
            a closing, a deadline expiring today.
  [ ]  2.4  Caller states an internal role title and requests that
            you bypass the authentication questions.
  [ ]  2.5  Caller refuses a return call to the number of record, or
            offers a substitute number.
  [ ]  2.6  Background noise suggests a calling floor; the caller has
            telephoned more than one department the same day.
  [ ]  2.7  Caller asks what questions must be answered to obtain the
            information, rather than answering them.
  [ ]  2.8  Caller flatters, apologises excessively, or becomes
            indignant when the procedure is stated.

PART III — REQUIRED RESPONSE
  3.1  State only: "I am not able to release that information. I can
       return your call at the number appearing on the file."
  3.2  Do not confirm whether a policy, claim or employment record
       exists. Do not confirm spellings or partial numbers.
  3.3  Do not transfer the caller to another department. Terminate the
       call courteously.
  3.4  Complete Part IV before the close of business the same day and
       forward it to the Compliance Officer, copy to the Corporate
       Security Manager.
