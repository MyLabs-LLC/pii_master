MERIDIAN COAST TITLE & ESCROW COMPANY
FOR OUR CUSTOMERS: HOW WE HANDLE YOUR INFORMATION

    What we will ask you for
        - Identification at the signing table, viewed and returned to you
        - Confirmation of the property address shown in your file
        - Your instructions for the disbursement of funds, given in person or
          by a call we place to you

    What we will never ask you for
        - Your Social Security Number by email
        - Your online banking password or security questions
        - A change of wire instructions accepted by email alone

    If someone contacts you claiming to be from this company
        - Ask for the file number and the escrow officer's title
        - Hang up and call the branch number printed on your closing packet
        - Do not use a number, link, or address supplied in the message

    Protecting your own copies
        - Shred settlement statements, loan disclosures, and identification
          photocopies when you no longer need them
        - Store originals in a locked place, not in a vehicle
        - Collect mail promptly during the escrow period

    If you believe your information has been misused
        - Contact the Escrow Officer handling your file
        - Ask that your concern be referred to the Privacy Coordinator
        - Place a fraud alert with the credit reporting agencies
