REGISTRAR RECORDS BULLETIN
Handling Rules for Identifier-Bearing Material · 2011

The table below summarises the treatment of the data elements handled most often in this office. It is posted at each workstation and at the imaging station. Where a situation is not covered, the more protective rule applies, and the Records Custodian is consulted before the material moves.

Data Element                     | Open Email | Fax        | Paper Disposal
Social Security Number           | Never      | Never      | Cross-cut shred
Student Identification Number    | Never      | Confirmed  | Cross-cut shred
Date of Birth (full)             | Never      | Confirmed  | Cross-cut shred
Legal Name and Former Legal Name | Restricted | Confirmed  | Cross-cut shred
Permanent and Local Address      | Restricted | Confirmed  | Cross-cut shred
Bank Account and Routing Number  | Never      | Never      | Cross-cut shred
Immigration and Visa Status      | Never      | Never      | Cross-cut shred
Course Schedule and Grade Detail | Restricted | Confirmed  | Cross-cut shred

"Confirmed" means the receiving machine sits in a locked office and the receiving department has confirmed by telephone that a person is standing at it. "Restricted" means the element may be sent only within the university domain, only to a business address, and only where the recipient has a documented need arising from an assigned duty.

The principal risk to this office is not the misfiled document but the plausible telephone call. A pretexting caller does not break a rule; the caller invites a member of staff to break it on their behalf, usually by manufacturing urgency and offering a sympathetic reason. A parent describing a medical emergency, a person claiming to be a graduate whose employer needs verification before the end of business, and a caller who says an administrator has already approved the release are all familiar shapes of the same approach. The correct response in each case is identical and requires no assessment of sincerity: state that identifiers cannot be released or confirmed by telephone, offer the written request procedure, and do not open the record while the caller is on the line. Where the caller persists, take the call no further and transfer it to the Associate Registrar for Records.

Any staff member who believes an identifier has been disclosed in error reports the matter the same day. There is no penalty for a prompt report and no expectation that the staff member investigate. The report goes to the Information Security Officer, with notice to the University Registrar and to the FERPA Compliance Coordinator. Where the disclosure involves a departmental system outside this office, the Data Steward for Student Records is included in the notice. Questions about how a particular form should be routed, retained, or destroyed go to the Records Custodian, and questions about the retention schedule itself go to the University Archivist.
