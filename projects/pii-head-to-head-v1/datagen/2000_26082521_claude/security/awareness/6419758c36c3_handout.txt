NOTICE TO MEMBERS

HOW TO RECOGNISE AND DEFEAT A PRETEXT TELEPHONE CALL
Prepared at the direction of the Privacy Officer

CLAUSE 1. A pretext call is a telephone call in which the caller assumes a false identity in order to obtain your identifying particulars, and thereafter uses those particulars to obtain credit, funds, or services in your name.

CLAUSE 2. You should regard a call as suspect where the caller: (a) states that your account is at immediate risk and that you must act before the call concludes; (b) already possesses one particular concerning you and asks you to supply the remainder; (c) asks for your Personal Identification Number, your Home Banking User ID, your password, or the answer to your security question; (d) asks you to confirm your Social Security Number, your Date of Birth, or your Mother's Maiden Name; (e) declines to permit you to return the call; or (f) asks that a statement, a card, or a check be directed to an address other than your address of record.

CLAUSE 3. The Credit Union will not, in the course of an outbound telephone call, request that you disclose the particulars enumerated in Clause 2, subparagraphs (c) and (d). An employee placing such a call already possesses that information.

CLAUSE 4. Upon receiving a suspect call you should decline to answer, terminate the call, and place a return call to the Credit Union at the number appearing upon your periodic statement or upon the reverse of your card. You should not use a number, an internet address, or a mailing address furnished by the caller.

CLAUSE 5. You should destroy by cross-cut shredder, and should not place in ordinary refuse, the following: periodic statements; deposit and withdrawal receipts; expired cards; unused convenience checks; pre-approved credit solicitations; loan and insurance correspondence; and any printed matter bearing your Member Number or Account Number.

CLAUSE 6. You should not send your identifying particulars by electronic mail. Correspondence of that nature should be conducted through the secure message facility within Home Banking, by telephone, or in person at a branch.

CLAUSE 7. A member who believes that a pretext call has occurred, or that identifying particulars have been obtained without authority, should ask for the Fraud Investigations Coordinator. A member wishing to review the Credit Union's privacy practices, or to record an opt-out election, should ask for the Privacy Officer.
