IDENTITY THEFT PREVENTION PROGRAM — STORE-LEVEL HANDOUT
Distribution: all pharmacy and front-end staff. Read and initial the training roster.
Supersedes all prior counter postings on this subject.

SCOPE
This handout implements the covered-account provisions of the Program and the disposal standard. It applies to every associate with access to the dispensing system, the point-of-sale terminal, the fax line, or the will-call area.

SECTION A — MATERIALS FOR SECURE DESTRUCTION
Deposit in the locked shred console. Do not overfill. Do not remove contents.
- Prescription labels: misprinted, voided, transferred, or stripped from returned stock
- Adjudication printouts, rejection slips, prior authorisation worksheets
- Signature capture overflow sheets and manual logs past retention
- Fax cover sheets, confirmations, and misdirected inbound transmissions
- Will-call bag tags, delivery manifests, courier receipts
- Register journal tape, void slips, chargeback correspondence
- Handwritten notes, sticky notes, and scratch pads bearing any identifier
- Obsolete insurance card photocopies held in the pending file
Media: diskettes, CDs, and USB drives are not shredded at store level. Route to the District Loss Prevention Manager for certified destruction. Do not reformat and reuse.

SECTION B — PROHIBITED EMAIL CONTENT
The following must not appear in the body, subject line, or attachment of any message:
- Social Security Number or Taxpayer Identification Number
- Date of Birth in combination with Name, Member ID, or Rx Number
- Card Number, Expiration Date, Security Code, or Track Data
- Member ID, Group Number, BIN, PCN, Policy Number
- Patient rosters, refill queues, or transfer logs in any format
- Images or scans reproducing any of the above
Approved channels for these elements: the dispensing system, the secure payer portal, and the encrypted transfer utility. Personal webmail is prohibited on store equipment without exception.

SECTION C — PRETEXTING INDICATORS
Treat as a pretext attempt where the requester:
- Withholds a verifiable prescriber, pharmacy, or payer credential
- Requests bulk records or an unrestricted profile
- Presents urgency, authority, or sympathy as a substitute for verification
- Asks staff to bypass the return-call procedure
- Requests credentials, remote access, or a password reset
- Approaches at shift change, during a rush, or near closing
Counter-measure: verification is one-directional. Staff confirm what the requester states; staff do not supply, correct, or hint at any identifier.

SECTION D — ESCALATION ROUTING

  MATTER                                  ROUTE TO                          WITHIN
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Suspected pretexting call               Pharmacy Manager on Duty          Immediately
  Confirmed improper disclosure           Store Privacy Coordinator         Same shift
  Records or accounting-of-disclosure     Records Custodian                 3 business days
  Console breach, tampering, theft        District Loss Prevention Manager  Immediately
  Payer or PBM data dispute               Third-Party Billing Lead          Next business day
  Program interpretation, training gap    Regional Compliance Officer       5 business days
  System access or credential misuse      Information Security Desk         Immediately

SECTION E — ASSOCIATE OBLIGATIONS
- Lock the terminal when leaving the fill station, regardless of duration
- Do not share credentials, including with a supervisor
- Position monitors and label printers away from the customer sightline
- Clear the counter of paper before the shift handover
- Report a suspected incident even where no disclosure is believed to have occurred

Non-compliance is addressed under the disciplinary standard. Retaliation against an associate who reports in good faith is separately prohibited.
