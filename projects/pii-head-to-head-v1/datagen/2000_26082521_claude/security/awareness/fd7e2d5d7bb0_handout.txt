MEMORANDUM

TO:     All Staff
FROM:   Security Officer
DATE:   June 7, 2004
RE:     Quick Reference — Member Information Handling

Post this at your workstation.

SHRED IT
--------
Screen prints. Credit bureau reports. Reject items and carbons.
Adding machine tapes with account numbers. Photocopies of member
identification. Returned mail. Any note listing Member Name,
Member Number, Social Security Number, or Date of Birth.

NEVER EMAIL IT
--------------
Social Security Number. Full account or card number. PIN or
password. Date of Birth with a name. Credit report data.
Scanned identification or signature cards.

STOP THE CALL
-------------
The caller is in a hurry.
The caller cannot answer verification questions.
The caller asks you to read a number back.
The caller says someone here already approved it.
The caller wants information sent somewhere new.

Verify first. Disclose second. When in doubt, mail it to the
address of record and tell your supervisor.

TELL SOMEONE
------------
Pretext calls and lost records ....... Security Officer
Shredding and file retention ......... Records Custodian
Email and system questions ........... Information Systems Manager
Policy questions ..................... Compliance Officer

Reporting a mistake promptly is not a disciplinary matter.
Concealing one is.
