CORRIDOR STAFFING SERVICES — DESK CARD
SEVEN CLAUSES ON HANDLING IDENTIFIERS

Clause 1. Lock the drawer before you leave the desk, even for a moment. Applicant folders stay closed and face down when a visitor is at the counter.

Clause 2. Nothing bearing a Social Security Number, Date of Birth, or Bank Account Number leaves the branch in a briefcase, a car, or a coat pocket.

Clause 3. Shred it the same day: working application copies, screening printouts, superseded withholding forms, message slips, fax confirmations, returned mail.

Clause 4. Never by email: Social Security Number, Date of Birth, bank routing and account fields, screening results, images of identity documents.

Clause 5. A caller who will not accept a call-back on a number you look up is not who they say they are. Hang up courteously and report it.

Clause 6. Do not confirm a partial record. Saying "yes, that's the right one" is a disclosure.

Clause 7. Report the same day. Records Custodian for filing and destruction; Branch Manager for anything lost, taken, or suspicious; Privacy Officer where the Branch Manager is unavailable.

Keep this card at the workstation. Replace it when it becomes illegible.
