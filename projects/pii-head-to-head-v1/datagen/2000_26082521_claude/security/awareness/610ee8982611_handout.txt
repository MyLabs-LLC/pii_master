PERSONAL IDENTIFIER HANDLING: BRANCH DESK REFERENCE

Scope: branch coordinators, recruiters, payroll clerks, on-site
supervisors, and assigned associates with file-room access.

1. CLASSIFICATION AND HANDLING SCHEDULE

+--------------------------------+-------+----------------------+------+------+
| FIELD LABEL                    | CLASS | PERMITTED STORAGE    | RETN | DISP |
+--------------------------------+-------+----------------------+------+------+
| Social Security Number         | R1    | Locked cabinet only  |   84 | D1   |
| Taxpayer Identification Number | R1    | Locked cabinet only  |   84 | D1   |
| Date of Birth                  | R1    | HRIS record only     |   84 | D3   |
| Driver's License Number        | R2    | Locked cabinet only  |   36 | D1   |

RETN = retention in months from assignment end.
D1 = cross-cut shred at branch; destruction log entry required.
D2 = cross-cut shred; deposit in vendor console pending pickup.
D3 = electronic purge, executed by the Records Custodian only.
R1 = restricted.  R2 = confidential.

2. DESTRUCTION RULES

Strip-cut and single-pass shredders are withdrawn from service.
Any document bearing an R1 field label is shredded whole; do not
separate cover sheets. Consoles are not general waste bins and
are not to be used for catalogues, food packaging, or client
marketing material. Console keys remain with the Branch
Operations Manager. Overflow is held in the locked cabinet, never
stacked beside the console.

3. TRANSMISSION PROHIBITIONS

The following are never sent by email, internal or external, in
the message body, in an attachment, or in a screenshot: Social
Security Number, Taxpayer Identification Number, Date of Birth,
Bank Routing and Account Number, Payment Card Number, Work
Authorization Number, Background Check Reference Number, Medical
Restriction Note. Password-protecting an attachment does not
lift this prohibition. Fax to an unverified destination is
treated as email for the purpose of this section.

4. PRETEXTING INDICATORS

A pretexting call presents a plausible role and an artificial
deadline. Treat the following as sufficient grounds to end the
call: the caller cannot supply a case or ticket reference; the
caller asks staff to read an identifier back for "confirmation";
the caller states that a prior colleague already approved the
release; the caller redirects delivery to a channel not held on
the account record. Verification is by outbound call to the
number held on file only. No identifier is ever confirmed,
partially confirmed, or denied to an inbound caller.
