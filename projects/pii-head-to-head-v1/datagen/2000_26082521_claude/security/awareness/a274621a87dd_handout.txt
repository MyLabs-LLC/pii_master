OFFICE OF THE UNIVERSITY REGISTRAR
INTERNAL ROUTING SCHEDULE R-4 (2004 REVISION)
WHOM TO CONTACT WHEN A PERSONAL IDENTIFIER IS AT RISK

ROLE TITLE                            MATTER REFERRED                          FIRST CONTACT WITHIN
====================================  =======================================  ====================
the Records Custodian                 Any suspected disclosure of a field      Immediately
the Registrar Security Coordinator    SIS access, passwords, screen prints     Same business day
the Associate Registrar for Records   Third-party transcript requests          Same business day
the FERPA Compliance Officer          Directory-information opt-out disputes   Two business days
the University Registrar              Subpoenas and law-enforcement demands    Immediately
the Information Security Officer      Lost media, virus, or system misuse      Immediately
the Internal Audit Director           Pattern of repeated pretext calls        Five business days
the Shredding Contract Administrator  Overflowing or unlocked bins             Same business day

1. Contact is made by role title through the University directory listing. Staff
   do not route a report through a colleague, a voicemail box, or an electronic
   mail message that restates the field at issue.
2. The initial report states only: the channel of the contact, the fields
   requested, the pretext claimed, and whether anything was released. It does
   not reproduce the value of any field.
3. Where a release has occurred, the reporting employee retains the working
   papers intact and does not shred them until the Records Custodian authorises
   destruction in writing.
4. A caller declining to be returned a call at the published listing is refused
   and reported under line 1 of the schedule above.
5. This schedule is reviewed each August by the University Registrar and is
   posted at every workstation with access to the Student Information System.
