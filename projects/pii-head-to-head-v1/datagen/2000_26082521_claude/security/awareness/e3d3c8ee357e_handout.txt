BILLING OFFICE — IDENTIFIER PROTECTION GUIDANCE
Issued by: Patient Financial Services            Effective: 01 JUN 2004
Supersedes: guidance issued 02 FEB 2003          Review cycle: annual
Distribution: Billing, Cashiering, Collections, Registration, Cash Post

SECTION 1.  SHREDDING
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
MATERIAL                       BIN            METHOD          FREQUENCY
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Superbill, worked              Grey console   Cross-cut       End of shift
Explanation of Benefits        Grey console   Cross-cut       End of shift
Denial correspondence          Grey console   Cross-cut       End of shift
Printer test page              Grey console   Cross-cut       End of shift
Adhesive label sheets          Desk shredder  Cross-cut       Immediate
Fax cover sheet with MRN       Desk shredder  Cross-cut       Immediate
Carbon, imprinter slip         Desk shredder  Cross-cut       Immediate
Diskette, CD-R with extract    Media locker   Physical destr. On surrender
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Strip-cut units are withdrawn from service and are not to be requisitioned.
Console material is not to be placed in recycling, tote boxes, or vehicles.
Consoles are not to be propped, wedged, or emptied by department staff.

SECTION 2.  ELECTRONIC MAIL
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
FIELD                          INTERNAL MAIL      INTERNET MAIL
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Patient Name with Account No.  Approved request   Prohibited
Social Security Number         Prohibited         Prohibited
Date of Birth                  Prohibited         Prohibited
Card Account Number            Prohibited         Prohibited
Insurance Member ID            Approved request   Prohibited
Scanned image of statement     Approved request   Prohibited
Statistics, no identifiers     Permitted          Approved request
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Approved request means a written request logged with the Privacy Officer
before transmission. Auto-forward rules to outside mailboxes are barred.
Attachments are not exempt. Subject lines are not exempt.

SECTION 3.  PRETEXT CALLS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
INDICATOR                                       REQUIRED ACTION
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Caller states an identifier and asks to verify  Do not confirm. Terminate.
Caller cites urgency or names a manager         Do not confirm. Terminate.
Caller refuses call-back on a listed line       Terminate. Log attempt.
Caller claims to be from an internal department Verify by internal
                                                directory. Call back.
Caller requests a full account history by voice Refer. Do not read out.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
