HALSTEAD COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT — ADMINISTRATIVE GUIDANCE
Identifier Protection: Obligations of Staff and Guidance to Members
Reference HCHD-PRIV-114B / Effective July 2021

§ 1. Construction. This guidance is to be read in conjunction with, and not in derogation of, the Department's Notice of Privacy Practices and the confidentiality provisions of applicable state and federal law. Where a provision hereof imposes an obligation more protective of personal identifiers than that imposed by another departmental instruction, the more protective provision shall govern.

§ 2. Destruction of Paper. Any document bearing a Full Name in combination with any of the field labels Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Medical Record Number, Member Identification Number, Account Number, or Home Address shall be destroyed by cross-cut shredding or by deposit into a locked destruction console maintained under contract for that purpose. Members of the public who inquire are to be advised that the same practice is recommended for benefit notices, explanation-of-benefits statements, immunization printouts, prescription labels, insurance correspondence, and any envelope bearing an addressee block, and that whole-page tearing, ordinary strip shredding, and household recycling afford no meaningful protection whatsoever.

§ 3. Electronic Mail. Electronic mail shall be presumed insecure. No staff member shall transmit thereby, and no member of the public shall be invited or instructed to transmit thereto, any Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Medical Record Number, Member Identification Number, Bank Account Number, Payment Card Number, photographic image of an identity document, or clinical result. Where a member proposes to furnish such an element by electronic mail, staff shall interrupt the proposal, decline the transmission, and direct the member to the secure member portal, to the postal mail intake address published on departmental correspondence, or to in-person delivery at a service counter. A message received in contravention hereof shall not be forwarded; it shall instead be reported to the Privacy Officer, who shall direct its disposition.

§ 4. Pretext Contacts. A pretext contact is any communication in which the caller misrepresents identity, affiliation, or purpose in order to induce disclosure. The Department does not initiate telephone contact for the purpose of soliciting a Social Security Number, Payment Card Number, Bank Account Number, or account credential, and does not condition the provision of services upon immediate payment by telephone. Staff shall so advise any member who reports having received such a call, shall counsel the member to terminate the call and to reinitiate contact through the number appearing on official departmental correspondence, and shall document the report.

§ 5. Referral. Reports arising under §§ 2 through 4 shall be directed to the Privacy Officer. Records retention and destruction inquiries shall be directed to the Records Custodian. Matters implicating departmental systems, credentials, or electronic mail security shall be directed to the Information Security Manager. Matters implicating suspected financial exploitation of a member shall additionally be referred to the Adult Services Program Manager.
