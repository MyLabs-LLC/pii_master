CARDINAL VALLEY MUTUAL INSURANCE COMPANY
FORM SIU-22 (REV. 06/97) — PRETEXT CONTACT RECOGNITION AND REPORT

PART I — INDICATORS
A caller engaged in pretexting seeks identifiers by impersonation rather than by entitlement. Mark each indicator observed.

[ ] Caller asserts urgency and applies time pressure ("before the file closes today").
[ ] Caller claims internal status but cannot state department, reporting role title, or workflow queue.
[ ] Caller asks the clerk to read back an identifier the caller should already hold.
[ ] Caller supplies a partial identifier and invites correction of the remainder.
[ ] Caller requests transfer of the file, or the mailing of a duplicate, to an address of record different from the one on file.
[ ] Caller objects to standard verification, cites inconvenience, or invokes a senior title.
[ ] Caller requests confirmation only, i.e. "just tell me whether it ends in the digits I have."
[ ] Background is inconsistent with the claimed setting; caller repeats the request after transfer.
[ ] Caller identifies as a relative, employer, attorney, or investigator without written authorization on file.
[ ] Repeat calls to different clerks within the same day regarding the same file.

PART II — REQUIRED RESPONSE
1.  Do not confirm, deny, correct, or complete any identifier. Silence on a partial identifier is itself disclosure; state only that the item cannot be discussed.
2.  Verify by challenge from the file, never by recitation to the caller.
3.  Offer the outbound alternative: advise that the department will return the call to the number of record. Do not accept a number supplied by the caller.
4.  Do not transfer the caller to another department. Terminate courteously.
5.  Complete Part III below within one business hour and deliver it to the Special Investigations Unit Coordinator. Notify the Privacy Compliance Officer if any identifier was disclosed before the pretext was recognized.
