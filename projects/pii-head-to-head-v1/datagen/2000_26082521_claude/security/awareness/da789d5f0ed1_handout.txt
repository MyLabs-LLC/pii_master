MUNICIPAL UTILITIES DEPARTMENT
ADMINISTRATIVE BULLETIN — RECORDS AND INFORMATION PRACTICES SERIES
Effective: January 2016. Supersedes all prior bulletins in this series.
Issued by: Office of the Records Custodian. Approved by: the Utilities Director.

Personnel are advised that the customer account record contains protected identifiers, including but not limited to Account Holder Name, Service Address, Mailing Address, Date of Birth, Social Security Number, Driver's License Number, Bank Routing Number, Bank Account Number, Payment Card Number, Telephone Number and Electronic Mail Address. These fields are collected for billing, deposit assessment and service continuity only. No other use is authorized.

Working papers bearing any of the fields named above shall be deposited in the locked shred consoles maintained in each work area at the close of business. Consoles are serviced under contract on a scheduled cycle; personnel shall not remove material once deposited. Recycling bins, general waste receptacles and unattended desk trays are not approved for this purpose. Tearing by hand does not constitute destruction. Items requiring destruction include printed account histories, deposit worksheets, returned payment notices, meter set orders bearing Service Address, and any photocopy of an identity document presented at the counter.

Identifiers shall not be transmitted by electronic mail, whether internally or externally, and shall not be entered in the body or subject line of a message, in a calendar entry, or in an attachment. Requests arriving by electronic mail that solicit such fields shall be referred to the Privacy Officer without reply.

Personnel receiving telephone contact from a party asserting urgency, claiming supervisory or vendor authority, or requesting confirmation of fields already in the caller's possession shall terminate the transaction and record the circumstances. Verification of the caller is the obligation of the employee, not of the caller.

Questions concerning this bulletin shall be directed to the Records Custodian through the internal directory.
