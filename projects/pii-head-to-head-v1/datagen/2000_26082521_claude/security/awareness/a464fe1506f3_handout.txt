NOTES
1.  "0 days" denotes destruction at end of shift on the day of creation.

3.  Console keys are issued to the floor warden and the Records Custodian only. Report a full or jammed console; do not force material into the throat.
4.  Witnessed destruction certificates are filed by quarter and are subject to review by the Internal Audit Manager.
