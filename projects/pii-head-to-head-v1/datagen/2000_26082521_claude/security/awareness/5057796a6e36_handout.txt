RE:      Year-End Purge — Identifier Handling Refresher

The year-end purge of expired files begins the first full week of December. Read this before opening a retention box.

WHAT GOES IN THE CONSOLE

   [ ] Driver qualification working copies past retention
   [ ] Motor vehicle record printouts and annual review forms
   [ ] Employment applications from candidates not hired
   [ ] W-4, I-9, and direct deposit authorisation worksheets
   [ ] Payroll registers, garnishment notices, benefit enrollment forms
   [ ] Voided delivery receipts and bills of lading with consignee contact fields
   [ ] Gate logs, visitor logs, fax cover sheets, courier manifests
   [ ] Diskettes and CD-R media holding personnel or payroll extracts

Media are placed in the marked bin beside the console, not in the paper slot. Nothing on this list goes into a recycling toter, a dumpster, or a trailer sweep, whole or torn.

WHAT NEVER GOES INTO ELECTRONIC MAIL

Social Security Number. Date of Birth. Driver's License Number. Bank Routing and Account Number. Payment Card Number, Expiration Date, Card Verification Value. Employee Number. Home Address and Home Telephone Number. This applies to the message body, the subject line, and every attachment, and it applies whether the recipient is inside or outside the company. Use the secure transfer host or the courier pouch.

WHAT A PRETEXT CALL SOUNDS LIKE

The caller is friendly, in a hurry, and already knows something. They have the pro number but want the driver's license number. They read out four digits and ask you to confirm the rest. They mention an adjuster's deadline, a claim hearing, or a fax that "never came through." They ask you to send it to a number they will give you. They do not want to be called back at a listed number.

Any of these: stop, take the caller's stated employer and what they asked for, and offer a call-back. Do not confirm, deny, or correct anything they have said.

WHO TO TELL

   Pretext calls, mail and network questions .... Information Security Coordinator
   Boxes, retention dates, certificates ......... Records Custodian
   Personnel and payroll files .................. Human Resources Administrator
   After hours .................................. Terminal Manager on duty
