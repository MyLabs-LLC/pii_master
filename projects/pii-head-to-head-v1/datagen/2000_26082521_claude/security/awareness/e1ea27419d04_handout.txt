PERSONAL IDENTIFIER PROTECTION — STAFF GUIDANCE
Document class: Internal / Controlled
Revision 3 · Supersedes Revision 2 · Review due Q1 2012
Owner: the Data Protection Coordinator
Distribution: all sites, all shifts, notice board and induction pack

1. SCOPE

1.1 This guidance applies to every record held on behalf of the Company or on behalf of a client, in paper or electronic form, at a depot, in a cab, or at a home working location.

1.2 A personal identifier is any field which, alone or with another field, identifies a living individual. The following field labels are treated as identifiers without exception:
   - Full Name; Former Name
   - Date of Birth
   - National Insurance Number
   - Passport Number; Driving Licence Number
   - Home Address; Postcode
   - Sort Code; Account Number
   - Payroll Number; Employee Reference
   - Consignee Name; Consignee Address
   - Signature image

2. RETENTION AND DESTRUCTION

2.1 Shred the following at the point they cease to be required for the current operational task:
   - Route sheets and manifests carrying Consignee Name and Address
   - Proof of delivery duplicates and signature strips
   - Recruitment paperwork: application forms, right-to-work photocopies, reference letters
   - Payroll enquiries, deduction notices, attachment of earnings correspondence
   - Handwritten notes taken during a personnel or customer telephone call
   - Contents of the returns tote where labels remain legible

2.2 Cross-cut shredders only. Strip-cut units are withdrawn from service.

2.3 Where a locked console is provided, deposit through the aperture. Do not stack alongside the console. Do not remove material from a console once deposited.

2.4 Screens, keyboards and desk surfaces are cleared at end of shift. Nothing bearing an identifier is left on a printer, a fax tray or a cab dashboard.

3. ELECTRONIC TRANSMISSION

3.1 Prohibited by email in all circumstances:
   - National Insurance Number, Tax Reference, Passport Number
   - Sort Code, Account Number, Card Number
   - Date of Birth against a named individual
   - Bulk extracts of the Personnel Master File or the Customer Address File
   - Scanned identity or entitlement documents

3.2 Prohibited on unencrypted removable media. USB sticks are issued by the IT Service Desk Lead and are logged against a Media Serial Number.

3.3 Attachments to clients travel by the approved secure transfer facility only. A password sent in a second email does not constitute encryption.

3.4 Do not forward, reply-all, or copy a personal home mailbox. Do not print to an unattended device on another floor or site.

4. PRETEXTING — RECOGNITION AND RESPONSE

4.1 Pretexting is the use of a false context to obtain identifiers. It is directed at staff, not at systems.

4.2 Recognise the following:
   - Unsolicited call presenting as head office, HR, an insurer, a bank, an auditor or a client contact
   - Refusal or inability to state a Depot Cost Centre Code or Purchase Order Reference
   - Urgency, deadline pressure, or reference to a senior manager who cannot be reached
   - Request that you confirm a partial identifier read aloud by the caller
   - Request to be transferred internally, or to be given a colleague's extension
   - A second call from a different caller reinforcing the first

4.3 Response, in order:
   - Supply nothing, including confirmation that a person is employed here
   - Record stated name, stated organisation, time, and the request made
   - End the call
   - Re-establish contact through the published switchboard or the client's contracted account route
   - Notify the Depot Security Officer

5. INTERNAL CONTACTS BY ROLE

   - Records Custodian — retention periods, archive recall, destruction certificates
   - Data Protection Coordinator — subject access requests, misdirected correspondence, client notification
   - Depot Security Officer — suspected pretexting, tailgating, console tampering
   - IT Service Desk Lead — encrypted media, mailbox restrictions, secure transfer accounts
   - Transport Shift Supervisor — driver documentation, agency worker briefings
   - Compliance Manager — audit findings, corrective action closure

6. NON-COMPLIANCE

6.1 Failure to observe this guidance is a disciplinary matter and may constitute an offence under data protection legislation.

6.2 Reporting an error you have made attracts no penalty. Concealment does.
