OPERATIONS BULLETIN — HANDLING OF MEMBER IDENTIFYING DATA IN THE SERVICE CHANNELS
From the Office of the Vice President of Operations
To: Member Service Representatives, Loan Officers, Call Center Staff

§ 1. Application. This bulletin governs the treatment of the fields enumerated in Schedule A below, in every channel through which the Credit Union receives or delivers member communications.

§ 2. Channel Restrictions.
 (a) The fields listed in Schedule A shall not be placed in outbound electronic mail, nor in the subject line thereof, nor in any facsimile cover sheet.
 (b) Such fields may be transmitted only within the core processing system, within the secure message facility of Home Banking, or upon paper delivered by hand or by first-class mail to the address of record.
 (c) A member requesting delivery to an address other than the address of record shall be advised that the change of address must first be effected and shall thereafter be subject to the customary waiting interval.

§ 3. Retention at the Desk. No document bearing a Schedule A field shall remain upon a desk, credenza, printer tray, or facsimile machine after the close of business. The Records Custodian shall conduct an unannounced sweep of each work area not less than monthly and shall log the following column headings: Work Area; Item Description; Field Types Exposed; Disposition; Title of Reviewing Officer.

§ 4. Pretext Recognition. A representative shall regard the following as sufficient cause to terminate an inquiry: an unexplained refusal to be called back; a claimed emergency accompanied by a request to bypass authentication; a caller who supplies one field correctly and asks the representative to supply the remainder; a caller who asks what fields the Credit Union maintains; or a caller who asserts authority derived from a relationship not evidenced in the record.

§ 5. Reporting. Every occurrence described in § 4 shall be memorialized in the incident register and reported to the Information Security Officer. Where the occurrence concerns a loan file, the Lending Compliance Analyst shall be copied. Where a member alleges that identity theft has occurred, the matter shall be referred immediately to the Fraud Investigations Coordinator, who alone is authorized to place a block or an alert upon the relationship.

SCHEDULE A — RESTRICTED FIELDS
 Full Legal Name
 Social Security Number
 Date of Birth
 Mother's Maiden Name
 Member Number
 Share Account Number
 Share Draft Account Number
 Loan Account Number
 Debit Card Number
 Card Expiration Date
 Personal Identification Number
 Home Banking User ID
 Security Question and Answer
 Driver's License Number
 Residence Address
 Employer Name and Position

§ 6. Any exception to this bulletin shall be granted in writing by the Vice President of Operations upon the concurrence of the Compliance Officer, and shall be limited in duration and in scope.
