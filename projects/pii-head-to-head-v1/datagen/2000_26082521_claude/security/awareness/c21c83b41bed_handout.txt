MEMORANDUM

TO:       Branch Managers; Operations Supervisors
FROM:     Records Custodian
DATE:     September 2, 2004
RE:       Disposal Schedule by Record Type — Effective Immediately

The following schedule governs destruction of member records at the branch level. It is issued in advance of the federal disposal rule and applies now. Retain nothing beyond its period; destroy nothing before it.

RECORD TYPE                     FIELDS PRESENT                              DISPOSAL METHOD
-----------------------------   -----------------------------------------   ---------------------
Membership application          Name, SSN, Date of Birth, Address           Cross-cut shred
Signature card (superseded)     Name, Member Number, Signature              Cross-cut shred
Loan application worksheet      Name, SSN, Employer, Income                 Cross-cut shred
Credit bureau report            Name, SSN, Account Numbers, Score           Cross-cut shred
Teller reject / carbon          Member Number, Amount                       Cross-cut shred
Card maintenance form           Card Number, PIN Offset, Member Number       Cross-cut shred
Screen print, any system        Varies                                      Cross-cut shred
Returned mail envelope          Name, Mailing Address                       Cross-cut shred
Wire transfer worksheet         Name, Account Number, Beneficiary Name      Cross-cut shred, log
Safe deposit access ledger      Name, Box Number, Signature                 Vault retention only

NOTES

Cross-cut shred means the locked console or the branch shredder. It does not mean the recycling bin, the tear-in-half method, or the dumpster behind the drive-up.

Wire worksheets require an entry in the branch destruction log before shredding. The log records: Record Type, Volume, Date Destroyed, Employee Initials, Witness Initials. It does not record member identifiers.

Vault retention only means the item is never removed from the branch and never destroyed at the branch. Send a written request to the Records Custodian.

Shred consoles are serviced by a bonded vendor on a scheduled route. Staff are not to open a console, remove material from it, or allow the vendor into the work area unescorted. The Branch Operations Manager escorts each pickup and signs the certificate of destruction.

Boxed records sent to storage must be labeled with Box Number, Record Type, and Destruction Eligible Date on the outside. Do not label a box with member names.

Direct schedule questions to the Records Custodian. Direct questions about a pending legal hold or subpoena to the Compliance Officer before you destroy anything.
