DISTRICT ADMINISTRATIVE CIRCULAR AC-2021-07
SUBJECT: SAFEGUARDING OF PERSONALLY IDENTIFIABLE INFORMATION
ISSUING OFFICE: Office of the Superintendent
DISTRIBUTION: All school sites; all central departments; contracted and temporary personnel
EFFECTIVE: First instructional day, 2021-22 school year
REVIEW CYCLE: Annual, by the Data Privacy Officer

SECTION I — SCOPE

1. This circular governs the creation, transmission, storage, and destruction of any record containing personally identifiable information (PII) of students, employees, applicants, volunteers, or household members.

2. Compliance is a condition of continued access to District network resources. Access is provisioned by the Chief Technology Officer and revoked without notice upon confirmed violation.

3. For purposes of this circular, PII includes but is not limited to the following data elements: Social Security Number; Local Student Identifier; State Student Identifier; Date of Birth; Home Address; Guardian Contact Number; Free and Reduced Meal Eligibility Status; Special Education Eligibility Flag; Immunization History; Medicaid Billing Identifier; Bank Routing Number; Direct Deposit Account Number; Employee Identification Number; Credential Number; Driver License Number.

SECTION II — DESTRUCTION OF PHYSICAL RECORDS

4. The following shall be placed in a locked shred console and never in recycling, classroom paper reuse bins, or open waste receptacles:

 4.1 Class rosters, seating charts, and attendance printouts bearing student identifiers.
 4.2 Meal application worksheets and eligibility determination notices.
 4.3 Health office logs, medication administration sheets, and nurse referral slips.
 4.4 Emergency contact cards superseded by a current card.
 4.5 Substitute folders, sign-in sheets, and visitor badge stubs.
 4.6 Payroll registers, timesheet corrections, garnishment notices, and benefit enrollment forms.
 4.7 Applicant screening notes, reference call sheets, and interview scoring rubrics.
 4.8 Any draft, working copy, or misprint of the above.

5. Consoles are serviced under contract. Staff shall not remove material from a console once deposited. A console found unlocked or overfull shall be reported to the Site Custodial Lead the same working day.

6. Desktop cross-cut shredders may be used only for single-page incidental disposal. Strip-cut devices are not approved.

SECTION III — ELECTRONIC TRANSMISSION

7. The following shall never be sent by electronic mail, including internal mail, and shall never be attached in unencrypted spreadsheet or portable document form:

 7.1 Social Security Number, whole or truncated.
 7.2 Bank Routing Number or Direct Deposit Account Number.
 7.3 Payment card data of any kind.
 7.4 Any file whose header row contains the column names SSN, SSN_LAST4, DOB, DL_NUM, ROUTING, ACCT_NUM, or MEDICAID_ID.
 7.5 Combined extracts pairing Local Student Identifier with Date of Birth and Home Address.

8. Approved transfer methods are the secure file exchange service and the student information system's native report delivery. Requests for either are submitted to the Help Desk Supervisor.

9. Photographs of screens or paper records taken on personal devices are prohibited.

SECTION IV — PRETEXTING

10. A pretexting call is an attempt to obtain PII by asserting a false identity or a false urgency. Indicators include: refusal to provide a callback extension; a claim to be calling from the student information system vendor during an unannounced outage; pressure to bypass the ticketing process; a request to read back a Date of Birth or account field for "verification"; a caller who supplies partial information and asks staff to confirm the remainder.

11. Staff shall terminate the call, record the time and the substance of the request, and notify the Data Privacy Officer. No exception is made for callers asserting authority as a District administrator.

SECTION V — CONTACTS BY ROLE

12. Records requests and record corrections: the Records Custodian.
13. Suspected disclosure, loss, or theft: the Data Privacy Officer, within one working hour.
14. Account, device, and access matters: the Chief Technology Officer, through the Help Desk Supervisor.
15. Employee and applicant data: the Director of Human Resources.
16. Compensation and banking data: the Payroll Supervisor.
17. Student health data: the Health Services Coordinator.
18. Site-level questions: the Building Principal, who shall escalate to the Director of Student Services.
