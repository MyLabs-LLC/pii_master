NORTHGATE FREIGHT & DISTRIBUTION LTD
DEPOT NOTICE — HANDLING OF PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS
Ref NFD/SEC/11-04 · Issue 2 · Effective 1 March 2011
Applies to: all depot, transport, warehouse and agency personnel

SHRED — PLACE IN LOCKED CONSOLE, NOT IN GENERAL WASTE
- Driver files containing Full Name, Date of Birth, National Insurance Number, Driving Licence Number
- Payroll advice slips, P45/P46 copies, Payroll Number listings
- Bank mandate forms showing Sort Code and Account Number fields
- Agency booking sheets bearing Home Address and Next of Kin fields
- Customer delivery manifests showing Consignee Name, Address Line 1, Postcode
- Failed-delivery cards, signature capture printouts, POD overprints
- Visitor logs, gatehouse passes, temporary badge registers
- Any misprint, test print or duplicate of the above

NEVER SEND BY EMAIL — INTERNAL OR EXTERNAL
- National Insurance Number, Tax Reference, Passport Number, Driving Licence Number
- Sort Code, Account Number, Card Number, Card Security Code
- Date of Birth in combination with Full Name
- Scanned identity documents of any type
- Spreadsheets exporting the Personnel Master File in whole or in part
Use the internal courier envelope, sealed and signed for. No exceptions on grounds of urgency.

PRETEXTING CALL — INDICATORS
- Caller claims head office, audit, or insurer authority but cannot quote the Depot Cost Centre Code
- Caller applies time pressure ("the run is held", "payroll cut-off is in ten minutes")
- Caller asks you to confirm rather than supply — reading back a partial identifier for agreement
- Caller requests a callback on a number they provide
- Caller flatters, apologises repeatedly, or threatens escalation
Response: take the caller's stated name and organisation, terminate the call, verify through the published switchboard only.

REPORT WITHIN ONE WORKING SHIFT
- Suspected pretexting: the Depot Security Officer
- Lost or mislaid paperwork: the Records Custodian
- Misdirected email or fax: the Data Protection Coordinator
- Agency worker concerns: the Transport Shift Supervisor
