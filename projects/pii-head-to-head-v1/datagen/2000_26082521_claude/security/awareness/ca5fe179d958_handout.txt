INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

TO:       All Personnel and Volunteer Officials
FROM:     Office of the Chief Compliance Officer
SUBJECT:  Reaffirmation of Controls Over Personal Identifiers
DATE:     09 September 2021
FILE:     ISP-7 / MEMO-11

Following the annual review of the Information Security Programme, the
following controls are reaffirmed and shall be observed as written. This
memorandum is to be read in conjunction with Standard ISP-7-09 and does not
supersede it.

RETENTION AND DESTRUCTION SCHEDULE

RECORD CLASS                 FIELD LABELS CARRIED          MINIMUM RETENTION
---------------------------  ----------------------------  ------------------
Membership application       Full Name; SSN; DOB; ID No.   Life of membership
Signature card               Full Name; Signature          Life of membership
Loan file                    SSN; Income; Account No.      25 months post-pay
Card issuance record         Card No.; Expiry; CVV         Destroy on issue
Wire confirmation            Account No.; Routing No.      7 years
Teller proof tape            Account No.; Amount           Destroy same day
Member correspondence        Account No.; Address          3 years
Access log                   User ID; Timestamp            6 years

Every item above, upon expiry of its retention period, is to be surrendered to
the Records Custodian for destruction by cross-cut shredder. No employee is
authorised to destroy a record independently, and no record is to leave the
premises for destruction elsewhere.

ELECTRONIC MAIL

The prohibition on transmitting identifiers by electronic mail remains
absolute. It applies equally to internal correspondence between colleagues,
because internal mailboxes are subject to discovery, to export, and to
compromise. Where a colleague requests an identifier by e-mail, the request is
to be declined in writing and the exchange forwarded to the Chief Compliance
Officer.

PRETEXTING

The most common approach observed this year has been a caller presenting as a
member locked out of the online channel, offering a partial account number and
inviting the employee to complete it. Employees are reminded that completing,
confirming, or correcting a partial identifier is itself a disclosure. Callers
who resist a return call placed to the number already of record are to be
treated as hostile without further discussion.

REPORTING

Report to the Fraud Watch Desk Supervisor on the same business day. Where the
Supervisor is unavailable, report to the Branch Operations Manager. The Chief
Compliance Officer is to be copied in all cases. Retention questions go to the
Records Custodian; card questions go to the Card Services Manager.

Chief Compliance Officer
For the Board of Directors
