BUSINESS OFFICE — POSTED NOTICE 2004-07
IDENTITY THEFT PREVENTION AT THE COLLECTION WINDOW

BEFORE YOU SPEAK: The caller must supply, unprompted, two (2) of the following — guarantor account number, full mailing address of record, date of last payment. You supply nothing first. If the caller asks you to "just confirm" an identifier, the call is a pretext and is to be ended.

BEFORE YOU SEND: Electronic mail is an open medium and is not to be used for any social security number, account number, card number, or balance associated with a name, whether the recipient is a patient, an employer, an attorney, or an insurer. There is no exception for a signed authorization. Direct all such requests to the Records Custodian for release in hard copy under seal.

BEFORE YOU DISCARD: Statements, explanations of benefits, remittance advices, fax cover sheets, screen prints, message slips, calculator tapes, and window envelopes go to the locked destruction bin. Nothing bearing an identifier leaves this office in an ordinary trash receptacle.

REPORT WITHIN ONE HOUR TO: Billing Office Supervisor (first report) — Privacy Officer (all suspected pretexting and all misdirected faxes) — Information Security Administrator (lost diskette, laptop, or password compromise) — Compliance Officer (any question you cannot answer with certainty).
