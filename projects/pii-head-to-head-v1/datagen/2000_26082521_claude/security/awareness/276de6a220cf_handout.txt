SCHEDULE D TO ADMINISTRATIVE BULLETIN — INTERNAL REFERRAL ROUTING
Personal Identifier Matters: Whom to Contact, by Role Title

  1.  Staff shall route the matters below to the role title indicated.
      Contact shall be by internal telephone or by inter-office
      envelope marked CONFIDENTIAL — TO ADDRESSEE ONLY. Electronic
      mail shall not be used to report an incident.

  2.  Where the role title indicated is vacant or unavailable, the
      matter passes to the escalation tier shown, and not laterally.

ROUTING GRID

Matter | Responsible role title | Division | Internal extension | Escalation tier
-------+------------------------+----------+--------------------+----------------
Destruction schedules; litigation holds | Records Custodian | Corporate Secretary | ______ | Second
Suspected pretext call | Compliance Officer | Legal & Compliance | ______ | First
Lost, stolen or missing file material | Corporate Security Manager | Corporate Security | ______ | First
Diskette, tape or terminal security; password reset | Information Systems Security Administrator | Information Systems | ______ | Second
Facsimile misdirection | Branch Operations Supervisor | Regional Operations | ______ | Third
Policyholder complaint concerning privacy | Consumer Affairs Coordinator | Policyowner Services | ______ | Second
Producer or agency conduct | Agency Compliance Manager | Agency Department | ______ | Second
Medical or provider information | Claims Service Manager | Claims | ______ | Second
Subpoena, court order or regulatory demand | Regional Counsel | Legal & Compliance | ______ | First

  3.  A First tier matter shall be reported the same business day. The
      reporting employee shall not conduct an independent enquiry, and
      shall not contact the caller, the insured or the producer.

  4.  The Compliance Officer shall maintain the register of reported
      incidents and shall report quarterly to the Privacy Practices
      Committee.

  5.  Extensions are recorded in the current Internal Directory. Staff
      shall verify the extension before transmitting any material and
      shall not rely upon an extension supplied by a caller.
