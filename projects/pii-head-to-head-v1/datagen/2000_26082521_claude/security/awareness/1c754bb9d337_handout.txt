PATIENT FINANCIAL SERVICES — BILLING OFFICE
GUIDANCE HANDOUT: PROTECTION OF PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS

Issued by: Billing Office Manager, Patient Financial Services
Approved by: Privacy Officer
Classification: Internal — general distribution; copies released to patients on request
Effective: first working day of March 2011
Supersedes: identifier-handling guidance issued 2009
Next scheduled review: March 2012

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 This handout sets out the minimum handling rules for personal identifiers created, received, or stored by the Billing Office in the course of claims preparation, statement production, and account collection.

1.2 It applies to all permanent, temporary, and agency staff assigned to the Billing Office, and to any clinical or clerical staff who forward billing paperwork to this office.

1.3 The following field labels are treated as personal identifiers for the purposes of this handout: Patient Name; Date of Birth; Social Security Number; Medical Record Number; Guarantor Name; Guarantor Account Number; Insurance Member Identifier; Group Number; Claim Number; Bank Routing Number; Bank Account Number; Card Number; Card Expiry Date; Card Verification Code; Driver's Licence Number; Home Address; Home Telephone Number; Employer Name.

2. MATERIAL THAT MUST BE SHREDDED

2.1 The following are placed in the locked grey confidential-destruction bins and never in general waste or recycling: printed statements and dunning letters; remittance advices and explanation-of-benefits printouts; claim forms and claim rejection reports; encounter forms and charge tickets; insurance verification worksheets; payment-plan agreements; returned mail and its envelopes; adding-machine tapes recording account numbers; handwritten notes bearing any field listed at clause 1.3; fax cover sheets and fax confirmation reports; test prints and misfeeds from the statement printer.

2.2 Bins are not to be overfilled. When a bin is full, the Billing Office Supervisor is notified and the bin is sealed pending collection.

2.3 Destruction of bulk records outside the routine bin collection is logged. The destruction log carries these columns: Date Received; Batch Reference; Document Type; Identifier Fields Present; Volume (boxes); Destruction Method; Witnessing Role; Certificate Reference.

3. MATERIAL THAT IS NEVER SENT BY ELECTRONIC MAIL

3.1 Ordinary electronic mail is not an approved channel for personal identifiers. Staff do not send, forward, or reply with any of the fields at clause 1.3 in the body of a message, in a subject line, or in an unprotected attachment.

3.2 Spreadsheets, account extracts, and scanned correspondence are transferred only through the approved secure file transfer service, or by internal courier in a sealed envelope marked for the addressee role.

3.3 Card Number, Card Expiry Date, and Card Verification Code are never accepted by electronic mail under any circumstance. Where a patient supplies these by mail, the message is deleted without forwarding and the patient is invited to use the telephone payment line or the cashier's window.

3.4 Personal or home electronic mail accounts are not used for any hospital business, and hospital records are not stored on personally owned computers, portable drives, or unencrypted memory sticks.

4. RECOGNISING A PRETEXTING CALL

4.1 Pretexting is a caller's use of a false identity or false urgency to obtain identifiers to which they are not entitled.

4.2 Treat a call as suspect where the caller: refuses to give a callback route; claims to be a supervisor, auditor, or vendor engineer unknown to the office; presses for immediate action before a shift ends; asks staff to bypass the verification script; requests Social Security Number or Bank Account Number to "confirm" a record they claim already to hold; or asks that the call not be logged.

4.3 Staff verify every caller against the standing identity script before any account detail is discussed, and terminate the call politely where verification fails.

4.4 Every suspect call is recorded on the incident sheet and reported the same day.

5. INTERNAL CONTACTS BY ROLE

5.1 Routine questions on this handout: the Billing Office Supervisor.
5.2 Requests for records, retention questions, and destruction certificates: the Records Custodian.
5.3 Suspected pretexting, misdirected mail, or lost paperwork: the Privacy Officer.
5.4 Lost or stolen laptops, memory sticks, or badges: the Information Security Officer.
5.5 Anonymous reports: the Compliance Hotline, published on the staff noticeboard.
