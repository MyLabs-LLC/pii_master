REGIONAL TRANSIT AUTHORITY
DIVISION OF INFORMATION SYSTEMS, IN CONCURRENCE WITH THE OFFICE OF GENERAL COUNSEL
PROCEDURE IS-06, REVISION 2
GOVERNING THE CONVEYANCE OF PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS BY ELECTRONIC MAIL

1.0   AUTHORITY AND SCOPE

1.1   This Procedure is issued under the general administrative authority of
      the Executive Director, is binding upon all employees, upon all agents,
      and upon all persons performing services under contract to the
      Authority, and supersedes in its entirety Procedure IS-06, Revision 1.

1.2   For purposes of this Procedure, "electronic mail" means any message
      composed, transmitted, forwarded, or stored upon the Authority's
      messaging system, and further includes any message directed to or
      received from any address outside the Authority's messaging system by
      whatever gateway, service, or intermediary.

2.0   FINDINGS

2.1   The Division has determined that electronic mail is transmitted and
      stored without protection against interception, that it is retained
      upon backup media beyond the control of the sender, that it may be
      forwarded, printed, or quoted by any recipient without the knowledge or
      consent of the sender, and that it therefore affords no assurance of
      confidentiality whatsoever with respect to personal identifiers.

3.0   GENERAL PROHIBITION

3.1   No employee shall enter into the body of an electronic mail message,
      into the subject line thereof, or into any attachment, enclosure, or
      appended file thereto, any value corresponding to a field designated
      PROHIBITED in the table set forth at Section 4.0.

3.2   The prohibition established at Section 3.1 applies without regard to
      the identity of the recipient, without regard to whether the recipient
      is an employee of the Authority, and without regard to any urgency
      asserted by the recipient or by any third party.

3.3   A field designated RESTRICTED may be conveyed by electronic mail only
      upon the prior written authorization of the Records Custodian, which
      authorization shall be particular to a single transmission and shall not
      be construed as a continuing permission.

4.0   SCHEDULE OF FIELDS AND AUTHORIZED CONVEYANCES

FIELD LABEL OR DATA ELEMENT        | MAIL STATUS    | AUTHORIZED CONVEYANCE
======================================================================================
Social Security Number             | PROHIBITED     | Interoffice sealed envelope
Employee Identification Number     | PROHIBITED     | Interoffice sealed envelope
Date of Birth                      | PROHIBITED     | Interoffice sealed envelope
Home Address, Home Telephone       | PROHIBITED     | Hand delivery to Custodian
Fare Card Account Number           | PROHIBITED     | Encrypted diskette, courier
Bank Routing / Deposit Account     | PROHIBITED     | Encrypted diskette, courier
Driver License Number              | PROHIBITED     | Interoffice sealed envelope
Medical Diagnosis / Restriction    | PROHIBITED     | Occupational Health file only
Dependent or Beneficiary Name      | RESTRICTED     | Benefits Office, sealed
Pension Member Number              | PROHIBITED     | Pension Office, sealed
Photograph / Badge Image File      | RESTRICTED     | Security Office, hand carry
Payroll Deduction Detail           | PROHIBITED     | Payroll Office, sealed
======================================================================================

5.0   PERMITTED REFERENCES

5.1   Nothing in this Procedure prohibits the use in electronic mail of a
      field label standing alone, of a file or docket reference assigned by
      the Authority, or of a description of a record sufficient to permit the
      recipient to retrieve that record from a controlled location.

6.0   REPORTING OF INADVERTENT TRANSMISSION

6.1   An employee who has transmitted a PROHIBITED field by electronic mail,
      or who has received such a field from any sender, shall not forward the
      message, shall not print the message, and shall notify the Data
      Protection Officer in writing upon the same business day.

6.2   Deletion of the message by the employee does not discharge the
      obligation imposed by Section 6.1, the Division having advised that
      copies subsist upon backup media notwithstanding such deletion.
