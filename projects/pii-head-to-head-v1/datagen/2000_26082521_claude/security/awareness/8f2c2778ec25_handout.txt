MUNICIPAL UTILITIES DEPARTMENT — OFFICE OF THE DIRECTOR
ADMINISTRATIVE DIRECTIVE AD-2016-07
SUBJECT: Safeguarding of Customer and Employee Personal Identifiers
EFFECTIVE: 1 October 2016 — Supersedes AD-2011-04

    1.0  PURPOSE
         1.1  To establish uniform handling, transmission, and destruction
              requirements for records containing personal identifiers held
              by the Department in the course of billing, metering, service
              connection, collections, and employment administration.

    2.0  SCOPE
         2.1  This Directive binds all regular, seasonal, and temporary
              personnel, all contract meter readers, and all vendors granted
              access to the Customer Information System.
         2.2  It applies without regard to medium. Paper, microfiche, network
              file shares, portable media, and printed work queues are
              treated identically.

    3.0  DEFINITIONS
         3.1  "Personal Identifier" means any value recorded under the
              following field labels, alone or in combination:
                   3.1.1  Social Security Number
                   3.1.2  Date of Birth
                   3.1.3  Driver's Licence Number or State Identification Number
                   3.1.4  Bank Routing Number and Bank Account Number
                   3.1.5  Payment Card Number, Expiration Date, Security Code
                   3.1.6  Customer Account Number and Premises Identifier
                   3.1.7  Employee Payroll Number
         3.2  "Working Copy" means any extract, printout, screen capture, or
              spreadsheet derived from a system of record.

    4.0  MATERIAL REQUIRING CROSS-CUT DESTRUCTION
         4.1  The following shall be deposited in a locked destruction console
              and never in a waste bin, recycling stream, or open box:
                   4.1.1  Service application forms and deposit waiver forms.
                   4.1.2  Returned mail bearing the Service Address label block.
                   4.1.3  Cheque photocopies, remittance stubs, and voided drafts.
                   4.1.4  Delinquency and disconnection work queues, including
                          any page bearing the columns Account Number,
                          Customer Name, Service Address, Amount Past Due.
                   4.1.5  Fax cover sheets and confirmation reports.
                   4.1.6  Handwritten notes bearing any value under 3.1.
         4.2  Working Copies shall be destroyed at the close of the shift in
              which they were produced. Retention of a Working Copy overnight
              requires written authorisation of the Division Manager.

    5.0  PROHIBITED ELECTRONIC TRANSMISSION
         5.1  No personal identifier enumerated at 3.1 shall be placed in the
              body, subject line, or unencrypted attachment of an electronic
              mail message, whether internal or external.
         5.2  The prohibition is absolute and is not relieved by customer
              request, supervisory instruction, or operational urgency.
         5.3  Where a customer transmits identifiers by electronic mail, staff
              shall not reply in kind, shall not quote the original message,
              and shall forward the item to the Records Custodian for
              controlled deletion.
         5.4  Approved alternatives are the secure document portal, internal
              courier in a sealed confidential envelope, and hand delivery.

    6.0  TELEPHONE PRETEXTING
         6.1  A pretexting call is an attempt to obtain identifiers by
              impersonation. Indicators include:
                   6.1.1  Asserted urgency coupled with a threatened
                          disconnection or field visit.
                   6.1.2  A claim of internal authority the caller cannot
                          substantiate through the staff directory.
                   6.1.3  A request that the employee read back, rather than
                          verify, a stored value.
                   6.1.4  Refusal to accept a call-back to the number of record.
         6.2  Staff shall verify identity only by the challenge questions
              configured in the Customer Information System, shall never
              disclose a stored identifier in whole or in part, and shall
              terminate the call if verification fails.

    7.0  INTERNAL REPORTING
         7.1  Suspected pretexting, misdirected mail, or lost media shall be
              reported the same business day to the Records Custodian, with a
              copy to the immediate Supervisor.
         7.2  The Records Custodian shall notify the Privacy Officer and, where
              system access is implicated, the Information Security Manager.
         7.3  Questions of interpretation shall be directed to the Privacy
              Officer. Questions of retention schedule shall be directed to
              the Records Custodian.
