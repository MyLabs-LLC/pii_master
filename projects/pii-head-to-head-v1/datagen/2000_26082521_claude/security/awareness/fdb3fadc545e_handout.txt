NORTHRIDGE REGIONAL MEDICAL CENTER
OFFICE OF PATIENT FINANCIAL SERVICES
HANDOUT PFS-22: SAFEGUARDING YOUR INFORMATION — AND OURS

A. WHY YOU HAVE RECEIVED THIS HANDOUT
This Office maintains records containing identifiers which, if wrongfully obtained, may be employed to open credit, obtain services, or secure treatment in another person's name. This handout states the practices of this Office and the corresponding expectations of persons doing business with it.

B. WHAT THIS OFFICE WILL NEVER DO
This Office will never initiate a telephone call requesting that you recite a social security number, payment card number, card verification value, or bank account number. This Office will never request an identifier by electronic mail, nor direct you to a website to enter one. This Office will never demand payment by wire transfer or prepaid card, nor threaten arrest, deportation, or immediate legal process in the course of a collection call.

C. WHAT YOU SHOULD DESTROY
Statements, explanations of benefits, receipts, insurance correspondence, and preapproved credit offers should be destroyed by cross-cut shredding rather than discarded intact. Envelopes bearing an account number through the window are to be treated in the same manner.

D. WHAT SHOULD NEVER BE SENT BY ELECTRONIC MAIL
Do not transmit to this Office, or to any party representing itself as this Office, any of the following by electronic mail: social security number; medical record number; patient or guarantor account number; payment card number; bank routing or account number; date of birth; insurance identification number. Correspondence containing such elements should be delivered in person to the cashiering window or by first-class mail.

E. HOW TO RECOGNIZE A PRETEXT CALL
The caller already knows part of your information and asks you to confirm the remainder. The caller creates urgency. The caller refuses to state a call-back extension. The caller asks for information "for verification" that a legitimate creditor would already possess. End the call and initiate contact yourself using the number printed on your statement.

F. WHOM TO CONTACT
Billing questions and disputed balances: Billing Office Supervisor.
Privacy concerns and suspected pretexting: Privacy Officer.
Copies of, or amendments to, your record: Records Custodian.
Unresolved matters: Director of Patient Financial Services.
