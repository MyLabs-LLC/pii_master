PROTECTION OF PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS: SUMMARY OF STANDING OBLIGATIONS

It is the settled policy of the Authority, adopted by resolution and
binding upon all personnel and upon all persons transacting business
at any window of the Authority, that the fields specified in the left
column below shall in no case be committed to electronic mail, and
shall be conveyed, if at all, solely by the channel specified opposite.

+------------------------------------+------------------------------------+
| IDENTIFIER NEVER SENT BY EMAIL     | SANCTIONED ALTERNATIVE CHANNEL     |
+------------------------------------+------------------------------------+
| Social Security Number             | Sealed inter-office envelope       |
| Taxpayer Identification Number     | Sealed inter-office envelope       |
| Date and Place of Birth            | Verbal report in person only       |
| Bank Account or Routing Number     | Encrypted records terminal         |
| Payment Card Primary Account No.   | Encrypted records terminal         |
| Reduced Fare Certificate Number    | Locked courier pouch               |
| Employee Payroll File Number       | Sealed inter-office envelope       |
| Residential Address of a Patron    | Locked courier pouch               |
+------------------------------------+------------------------------------+

SHREDDING. Every paper bearing any field named above shall be placed,
before the close of the shift in which it was generated, in a locked
destruction receptacle. Deposit in general waste, in a recycling bin,
or in an unattended tray constitutes a reportable breach irrespective
of whether any loss is shown to have followed.

TELEPHONE ENQUIRIES. A caller seeking any field named above is to be
refused. No employee is required to justify such refusal, and no
employee shall be subject to adverse consequence for making it.

ESCALATION LADDER (in the order stated, without omission of a rung):

   First rung ....... Station Supervisor on Duty
   Second rung ...... Data Protection Coordinator
   Third rung ....... Privacy Officer
   Fourth rung ...... Chief Information Security Officer
   Record copy to ... Records Custodian and Internal Audit Lead

This Notice remains in force until expressly rescinded in writing by
the Privacy Officer.
