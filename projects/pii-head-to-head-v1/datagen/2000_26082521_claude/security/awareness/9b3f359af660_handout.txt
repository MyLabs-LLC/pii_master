COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
INFORMATION GOVERNANCE PROGRAMME
PROCEDURE IG-9: SAFEGUARDING PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS IN DAILY OPERATIONS
Effective 2021 | Owner: Privacy Officer | Distribution: all programme managers

1. Programme managers shall ensure that each employee under their supervision reads this procedure at onboarding and at each annual refresher, and shall retain the acknowledgement in the unit training file.

2. For the purposes of this procedure, a personal identifier is any value recorded under the following field labels, alone or in combination: Client Name; Date of Birth; Social Security Number; Medical Record Number; Case Number; Member or Medicaid Identification; Home Address; Mailing Address; Telephone Number; Electronic Mail Address; Financial Account Number; Biometric Reference; Photograph.

3. Collection shall be limited to the minimum necessary for the programme purpose. Where a form solicits a field not required by the programme, the manager shall submit the form to the Privacy Officer for revision.

4. Paper handling.
   a. Records in transit shall be carried in a closed, opaque folder or a locked transport case.
   b. Records shall not remain on desks, printers, or counters when the workspace is unattended.
   c. Records shall not be stored in a personal vehicle overnight.

5. Destruction.
   a. Approved routes are the locked destruction console and the departmental cross-cut shredder.
   b. Prohibited routes are recycling bins, general waste, home shredders, and burn without authorisation.
   c. Destruction shall not proceed where the record is subject to a retention period that has not elapsed or to a legal hold. The Records Custodian determines eligibility.

6. Electronic mail.
   a. No personal identifier shall be placed in the body, subject line, or attachment of an unencrypted message.
   b. Personal mail accounts, personal devices, and consumer storage services shall not be used to send, receive, or hold departmental records.
   c. Distribution lists shall be verified before sending; auto-completion of a recipient is a recognised cause of misdirected disclosure.
   d. Where the secure gateway is unavailable, the transmission shall be deferred, not downgraded.

7. Telephone enquiries.
   a. Staff shall establish the caller's entitlement before any discussion of a record.
   b. Verification shall be conducted by call-back to a number of record, not to a number supplied by the caller.
   c. Staff shall neither confirm nor deny the existence of a record to an unverified caller.

8. Pretexting indicators. Manufactured urgency; claimed internal authority coupled with ignorance of internal procedure; a request to confirm or complete a partial identifier; an offer to bypass the release-of-information process; escalation to hostility or flattery when procedure is invoked; a request for the names or duties of colleagues; a request for information about record systems, software, or vendors.

9. On identifying any indicator at clause 8, the employee shall terminate the contact, complete the Suspicious Contact Log, and notify the Privacy Officer the same working day.

10. Internal routing.

    Suspected or actual disclosure ............ Privacy Officer
    Device, mailbox, or credential compromise . Information Security Officer
    Retention, holds, subpoenas, and releases . Records Custodian
    Facilities, locks, consoles, and badges ... Facilities Coordinator
    Enquiries from press or public ............ Public Information Officer
    Contractor and vendor agreements .......... Contracts Administrator

11. Escalation beyond the roles at clause 10 is made by the Privacy Officer to the Health Officer. Employees shall not escalate directly.

12. Non-compliance is addressed under the Department's disciplinary policy and, where applicable, is reportable to the relevant oversight authority by the Privacy Officer.
