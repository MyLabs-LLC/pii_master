PRIVACY DESK CARD -- PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS      PRV-104-A      REV. 2021

ACTION            APPLIES TO                        ESCALATE TO
----------------  --------------------------------  ----------------------
SHRED SAME DAY    Loose notes bearing Name or SSN   File Room Supervisor
SHRED AT CLOSE    Duplicate ID scans, fax covers    Records Custodian
NEVER EMAIL       SSN, DOB, Account or Card No.     Privacy Officer
NEVER READ OUT    Identifiers to an inbound caller  Privacy Officer
PORTAL ONLY       Scans, payoff PDFs, ID images     Escrow Operations
CALL BACK FIRST   Any wire or account change        Wire Verification Desk
HANG UP, REPORT   Caller who resists a callback     Privacy Officer
LOCK OVERNIGHT    Files and unshredded paper        Branch Manager

A caller who already knows the file number has proved nothing. Take the
caller's name and role, end the call, and dial the number recorded in
the file. Identifiers leave this office through the secure portal or a
bonded courier, and by no other route. Where two rows could apply, the
stricter row governs. Report the same day; the Privacy Officer closes
the entry, not the branch.
