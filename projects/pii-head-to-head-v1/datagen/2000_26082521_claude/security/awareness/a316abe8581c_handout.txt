CEDAR VALLEY UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT
DESK CARD 4 — TELEPHONE REQUESTS FOR PERSONAL INFORMATION
Post beside every district telephone. Revised March 2004.

+---------------------------+-----------------------------+
| IF THE CALLER ...         | THEN YOU ...                |
+---------------------------+-----------------------------+
| Refuses to give a name    | End the call.               |
| Presses a deadline        | Ignore the deadline.        |
| Supplies part of a value  | Confirm nothing.            |
| Asks you to "verify only" | Give no field at all.       |
| Offers a return number    | Use the number on file.     |
| Asks for a screen reading | Turn the screen away.       |
| Names a senior officer    | Refer to that officer.      |
| Sounds routine and calm   | Follow this card anyway.    |
+---------------------------+-----------------------------+

Never give out a Social Security Number, a Date of Birth, a Home Address, a
Home Telephone Number, a Student Identification Number, or a Deposit Account
Number over the telephone, to any caller, for any reason, however the request
is worded.

After any such call, write down the date, the time, what was asked, and what
you said. Give the note to the Site Principal the same day. The Site Principal
notifies the Records Custodian. No employee is disciplined for refusing a
telephone request.
