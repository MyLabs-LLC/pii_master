METROPOLITAN TRANSIT AUTHORITY
INFORMATION GOVERNANCE NOTICE IG-04
PROTECTION OF PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS — STATION AND DEPOT STAFF
Issue 2, February 2021. Owner: the Data Protection Officer. Review: February 2022.

1. Application
    1.1 This notice binds all staff who handle passenger, applicant or colleague records in any format.

2. Destruction at the point of use
    2.1 Cross-cut shred, before the end of the shift on which it is created, any paper bearing:
        (a) Passenger Name; Home Address; Date of Birth;
        (b) Concession Card Number; Smartcard Serial; Photograph;
        (c) Payroll Number; Bank Sort Code; Account Number.
    2.2 Confidential waste sacks are provided for bulk clearance only. A single loose sheet is shredded, not bagged.
    2.3 Handwritten notes taken during a passenger enquiry fall within clause 2.1 and are not to be retained as an aide-memoire.

3. Prohibited transmission
    3.1 The following fields are never to be sent by email, whether internal or external, and never entered into a calendar invitation, chat message or ticketing note:
        (a) full Card Number or Security Code;
        (b) National Insurance Number;
        (c) Medical Exemption Reference;
        (d) Date of Birth in combination with Home Address.
    3.2 Where transfer is unavoidable, use the secure document exchange and record the transfer in the Disclosure Log.

4. Telephone approaches
    4.1 Treat as a pretexting attempt any call combining urgency, claimed seniority and a request to bypass identity checks.
    4.2 Terminate the call, record the time and the account referenced, and report to the Duty Station Supervisor.

5. Escalation
    5.1 The Duty Station Supervisor notifies the Records Custodian, who informs the Data Protection Officer where a disclosure may have occurred.
