NORTHGATE STAFFING SERVICES
BRANCH OPERATIONS BULLETIN 2016-04
DESTRUCTION SCHEDULE AND ESCALATION MATRIX

PART A: WHAT IS SHREDDED

  Shred, cross-cut only, any item bearing one or more of the
  field labels below:

    Social Security Number ............ Date of Birth
    Taxpayer Identification Number .... Home Street Address
    Driver's License Number ........... Personal Telephone Number
    Bank Routing / Account Number ..... Personal Email Address
    Payment Card Number ............... Medical Restriction Note
    Work Authorization Number ......... Background Check Ref. No.
    Wage Garnishment Reference ........ Emergency Contact Details

  Also shredded regardless of apparent content: interview note
  pads, timesheet carbons, reference call worksheets, printed
  HRIS extracts, unclaimed pay stubs, returned mail, badge
  request forms, and any handwritten note taken during a
  verification call.

PART B: WHAT IS NOT SHREDDED

  Blank forms, published rate cards, client marketing material,
  training handouts, and vendor catalogues. These are recycled.
  Placing them in a destruction console causes overflow and
  delays scheduled pickup.

PART C: TIMING

  Item type                     Hold before destruction
  ---------                     -----------------------
  Interview note pad            End of week
  Printed HRIS extract          Same day, end of shift
  Timesheet carbon              7 days after payroll close
  Returned mail, unopened       30 days
  Assignment file, closed       Per retention code in RC-114

PART E: STANDING INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Desks are cleared of printed identifiers at the end of every
     shift. Nothing bearing an R1 field label remains on a desk,
     in a tray, or on a printer overnight.

  2. Print jobs containing identifiers are collected immediately.
     Uncollected output is shredded by the last person to leave.

  3. Consoles are emptied only by the contracted vendor, under
     observation by the Branch Operations Manager or delegate.
     Branch staff do not remove material from a console once
     deposited, including material placed there in error.

  4. The destruction log is completed at the point of shredding,
     not retrospectively. Entries record item type, count, and
     the initials of the staff member performing destruction.

  5. Questions on classification are directed to the Records
     Custodian before disposal, not after.
