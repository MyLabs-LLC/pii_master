METROPOLITAN REGIONAL TRANSIT AUTHORITY
OFFICE OF THE RECORDS CUSTODIAN
ADVISORY NOTICE RC-2021-14
SUBJECT: Safeguarding of Personally Identifiable Information Held in the Custody of Operating Divisions
EFFECTIVE: First business day following posting
DISTRIBUTION: All Divisions — post at crew report locations and customer service windows

    1.0  AUTHORITY AND PURPOSE
         1.1  This Advisory is promulgated under the Records Retention and Information
              Security Policy of the Authority and shall be construed as a mandatory
              directive and not as guidance of a merely hortatory character.
         1.2  The obligations set forth herein attach to every employee, agent, contractor,
              and volunteer who, in the ordinary course of assigned duties, comes into
              possession of records bearing personal identifiers.

    2.0  MATERIALS REQUIRING CROSS-CUT DESTRUCTION
         2.1  Any printed, photocopied, or facsimile-reproduced document bearing, in whole
              or in part, a field captioned:
                   (a)  Social Security Number
                   (b)  Taxpayer Identification Number
                   (c)  Date of Birth
                   (d)  Driver's License or State Identification Number
                   (e)  Reduced Fare Applicant Identifier
                   (f)  Employee Badge Number
                   (g)  Payment Card Primary Account Number, Expiration Date, or Card
                        Verification Value
                   (h)  Bank Account Number and Routing Transit Number
         2.2  Drafts, working copies, mis-prints, transmittal cover sheets, and printer
              output abandoned at shared devices shall be treated as originals for the
              purposes of Section 2.1 and destroyed accordingly.
         2.3  Deposit of the foregoing into open recycling receptacles, refuse containers,
              or unattended collection bins is prohibited without exception, the
              convenience of the depositing employee notwithstanding.

    3.0  TRANSMISSIONS PROHIBITED BY ELECTRONIC MAIL
         3.1  No identifier enumerated at Section 2.1 shall be transmitted by electronic
              mail, whether in the body of a message, in an attachment, in a subject line,
              or in a calendar invitation, irrespective of the internal or external status
              of the recipient.
         3.2  The prohibition extends to photographic images of documents captured by
              personal devices and to screen captures of records management systems.
         3.3  Where transmission is operationally unavoidable, the sender shall route the
              record through the secure file transfer facility administered by the Office
              of Information Security, and shall record the transfer in the disclosure log.

    4.0  INDICIA OF A PRETEXTING CONTACT
         4.1  A caller who asserts an urgent deadline, a threatened service suspension, or
              the displeasure of a senior official as grounds for immediate disclosure.
         4.2  A caller who declines to be called back at a number appearing in the internal
              directory.
         4.3  A caller who supplies a partial identifier and invites the employee to confirm
              or complete it.
         4.4  Employees shall terminate such contacts without argument, make a
              contemporaneous written note, and report as provided in Section 5.0.

    5.0  INTERNAL REPORTING
         5.1  Suspected pretexting: the Security Operations Duty Officer.
         5.2  Improper destruction or disclosure: the Records Custodian.
         5.3  Questions of interpretation: the Data Protection Officer.
