PERSONALLY IDENTIFIABLE INFORMATION — HANDLING NOTICE
Office of the Data Privacy Officer
Effective: 01 September 2021
Supersedes: Handling Notice of 2019

1. Scope
1.1 This notice binds all employees, substitutes, contracted personnel, and approved volunteers who access student or employee records in any format.

2. Destruction of Paper
2.1 Cross-cut shredding is required for any printout bearing Student Identification Number, State Testing Identifier, Date of Birth, Social Security Number, Meal Eligibility Status, Home Address, or Parent/Guardian Name.
2.2 Material awaiting destruction shall be held in a locked shred console. Classroom waste containers, recycling bins, and open transfer boxes are not approved holding points.
2.3 Duplicate class rosters generated for a single meeting shall be collected at the close of that meeting and consoled the same day.

3. Electronic Mail
3.1 The following field types shall not be transmitted by electronic mail, whether in the message body, in a subject line, or as an attachment: Social Security Number; Bank Routing Number; Bank Account Number; Driver License Number; Medical Diagnosis Code; Individualized Education Program contents; Immigration Status.
3.2 The approved alternative is the District secure transfer portal, with link expiry set to seven calendar days and download notification enabled.
3.3 Forwarding District records to a personal mail account is prohibited without exception.

4. Telephone Requests
4.1 Staff receiving a telephone request for records shall not verify, confirm, correct, or complete any identifier read aloud by the caller.
4.2 The request shall be returned through a number obtained from the published District directory, never a number supplied by the caller.

5. Internal Contacts
5.1 Records access and release: the Records Custodian.
5.2 Suspected unauthorized disclosure: the Data Privacy Officer, same business day.
5.3 Device loss or theft: the Help Desk Supervisor, immediately.
