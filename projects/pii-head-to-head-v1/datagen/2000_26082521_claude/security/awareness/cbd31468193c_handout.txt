DESK PROCEDURE PFS-11.4(a)
PRETEXTING CALL RECOGNITION AND LOGGING
Effective: March 2004. Read in conjunction with Directive PFS-11.4, section 5.0.

1. Scope.
   (a) Applies to every inbound call in which the caller seeks information about an account, a balance, a coverage record, or a guarantor.
   (b) Applies whether the caller claims to be the patient, a family member, an employer, a payer representative, an attorney's office, or another department of this facility.

2. Standing rule.
   (a) Identity is established by the caller answering, not by the caller asserting.
   (b) Staff shall not read back, complete, correct, or confirm any field supplied by the caller.
   (c) A caller who fails verification is offered a callback to the number of record. No exception is granted for claimed urgency.

3. Indicators.
   (a) Pressure, deadline, or claimed authority of a manager not known to the department.
   (b) Refusal or inability to supply a direct callback number.
   (c) Callback number inconsistent with the payer or agency held in the account record.
   (d) Request for a batch, list, or report rather than a single account.
   (e) Request to have the Guarantor Mailing Address changed during the same call in which payment details are discussed.
   (f) Caller asks which fields are required to pass verification.
   (g) Call transferred repeatedly, or a second caller resumes the same request within the shift.

4. Immediate action.
   (a) Release nothing further.
   (b) Terminate the call courteously. Do not accuse.
   (c) Do not process any address change, coverage change, or refund request arising from the call.
   (d) Complete the pretexting log entry before the end of shift.

5. Log columns.
   Date of Call | Time of Call | Extension Receiving | Caller's Stated Name | Caller's Stated Organization | Callback Number Offered | Account Reference | Fields Requested | Fields Released (Y/N) | Action Taken | Staff Initials | Supervisor Review Date

6. Escalation.
   (a) The completed entry is passed to the Patient Financial Services Manager.
   (b) The Manager forwards all entries to the Privacy Officer weekly, and immediately where any field was released.
   (c) Where a release has occurred, the Privacy Officer determines notification. Staff shall not contact the patient or guarantor directly on their own initiative.

7. Review.
   (a) The log is reviewed monthly by the Privacy Officer and the Patient Financial Services Manager.
   (b) Recurring indicators are circulated to staff at the departmental meeting.
   (c) Completed logs are retained by the Records Custodian under the departmental retention schedule.
