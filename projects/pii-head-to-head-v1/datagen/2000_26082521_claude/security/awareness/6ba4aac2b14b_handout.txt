SCHOOL DISTRICT ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICES
GUIDANCE HANDOUT -- STAFF, PARENTS, AND GUARDIANS
IDENTITY PROTECTION: HANDLING OF PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS
Series 700 (Records) ......... Item 704-G ......... Revision 3, 2004

1.0  SCOPE
     Applies to every employee, volunteer, and contractor with access to
     student or employee files, in paper or electronic form. Applies to the
     main office, health room, payroll, transportation, and food services.

2.0  DESTRUCTION
     Paper carrying any identifier is destroyed at the point of use.

     MATERIAL                                  DISPOSITION
     ----------------------------------------  ----------------------------
     Working copies and printouts              Cross-cut shred, same day
     Carbon sets, mailing labels, envelopes    Cross-cut shred, same day
     Class rosters bearing student numbers     Shred at term close
     Benefit and payroll notices               Locked console, weekly pickup
     Diskettes, tapes, hard drives             Return to Data Processing
     Fax confirmations and misfeeds            Cross-cut shred, same day

     Strip-cut shredders are withdrawn. Recycling is not destruction.

3.0  ELECTRONIC MAIL
     The following field labels are not to appear in electronic mail, in the
     subject line, the message body, or any attached file:

       Social Security Number ........ Date of Birth
       Student Identification Number . Home Address
       Bank Routing / Account Number . Driver's License Number
       Medicaid / Health Plan Number . Meal Benefit Eligibility Status

     Electronic mail leaves the district network unprotected and is retained
     on servers outside district control. Use the interoffice pouch, sealed.

4.0  PRETEXTING
     A pretext caller impersonates a parent, an agency, a vendor, or a
     district employee in order to obtain identifiers by telephone.

     IF THE CALLER ...                        THEN THE EMPLOYEE SHALL ...
     ---------------------------------------  ------------------------------
     States a deadline and presses for haste  Take the request in writing only
     Declines to give a call-back number      End the call; log it; notify below
     Claims to be from a state agency         Verify via the district directory
     Asks you to confirm data he recites      Confirm nothing; correct nothing
     Requests a fax to a new number           Refuse; route to the Custodian
     Threatens to involve a supervisor        Refer the caller, release nothing

5.0  INTERNAL CONTACTS
     MATTER                                   ROLE
     ---------------------------------------  ------------------------------
     Student records, transcripts             Building Registrar
     Payroll, benefits, wage statements       Payroll Supervisor
     Health, immunization, medication         Lead School Nurse
     Computer accounts and passwords          Network Administrator
     Suspected improper disclosure            District Privacy Officer
     Subpoenas and public records requests    District Records Custodian
     Press or public inquiry                  Superintendent's Office

6.0  RETENTION
     Retain this handout at the workstation. Replaced copies are shredded.
