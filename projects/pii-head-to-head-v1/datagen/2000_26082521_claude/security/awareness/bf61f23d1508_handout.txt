CORRIDOR STAFFING SERVICES
BRANCH BULLETIN — TELEPHONE ENQUIRIES AND CALLER VERIFICATION
Post at every telephone position

§1. Standing rule
No personal identifier is released over the telephone until the caller has been verified under §3. This applies to every caller without exception, including a caller who identifies as an officer of this agency.

§2. What callers ask for
Requests most often concern the following columns of the associate record: SSN; DOB; HOME_ADDR; HOME_PHONE; BANK_ROUTING; BANK_ACCT; PAY_RATE; ASSIGNMENT_CLIENT; SCREEN_RESULT; HIRE_DATE; TERM_DATE. None of these is read aloud, spelled out, or confirmed on an unverified call.

§3. Verification steps
 1. Take the caller's name, organisation, and stated reason.
 2. State that you will return the call.
 3. End the call.
 4. Look up the number independently — the client file, the branch directory, or the published listing. Never the number the caller gave you.
 5. Call back and ask for the person by name.
 6. Where the caller cannot be reached at an independently sourced number, the request is refused and referred to the Branch Manager.

§4. Indicators of pretexting
 (a) Pressure — a payroll cut-off, an audit, a client emergency.
 (b) Flattery or familiarity, particularly use of a first name and small talk about the branch.
 (c) A partial record offered for you to complete or correct.
 (d) An unusual delivery route — "just fax it", "read it to me", "send it to my home".
 (e) Objection to the call-back procedure, or an appeal to seniority to bypass it.
 (f) Background noise inconsistent with the claimed office.

§5. Recording the incident
Complete the Suspicious Contact Log at the branch under the headings: Date and Time Received; Position Taking Call; Caller's Stated Name; Caller's Stated Organisation; Information Requested; Number Offered by Caller; Action Taken; Referred To. Submit the entry to the Branch Manager before close of business.

§6. Referral
 6.1 Branch Manager — first referral for all suspicious contacts.
 6.2 Privacy Officer — repeat contacts, contacts naming a specific associate, or any suspected disclosure.
 6.3 Systems Administrator — where the caller sought passwords, terminal access, or dial-in details.
 6.4 Compliance Coordinator — where a client insists on a route these procedures do not permit.

§7. No exceptions are granted at branch level. Do not agree to an alternative arrangement in order to end a difficult call.
