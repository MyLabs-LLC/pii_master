MERIDIAN FREIGHT SYSTEMS, INC.
TERMINAL SECURITY BULLETIN 04-17
IDENTIFIER PROTECTION — EMPLOYEE CHECKLIST
Issued March 2004. Supersedes Bulletin 02-09.
Distribution: all terminals, dock offices, driver ready rooms.

SHRED — cross-cut console only, never the recycling tote
- Driver qualification packets bearing the fields Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Commercial Driver Licence Number, Medical Examiner Certificate Number
- Payroll registers and stub carbons showing Employee Number and Bank Routing / Account Number
- Consignee delivery manifests showing Signatory Name and Home Address
- Fax cover sheets, misprints, jammed originals, carbon backing paper
- Obsolete badge applications and their photographs

NEVER SEND BY ELECTRONIC MAIL
- Any field listed above, in the body, subject line, or attachment
- Scanned licences, medical cards, void cheques, insurance cards
- Spreadsheets containing an unmasked Account Number column
- Personnel action forms of any grade

PRETEXTING — REFUSE AND REPORT
- Caller claims to be from headquarters, audit, or a carrier partner and cannot be found in the internal directory
- Caller cites urgency, threatens delay of a load, or asks you to bypass the callback rule
- Caller already "has most of it" and wants only the last four digits confirmed
- Caller requests a fax to a number they supply verbally
- Correct response: take no information, offer no confirmation, terminate the call, log the attempt

INTERNAL CONTACTS — BY ROLE TITLE ONLY
- First report: Terminal Manager
- File and retention questions: Records Custodian
- Suspected pretexting or badge misuse: Corporate Security Officer
- Mailbox and access rights: Information Systems Supervisor

Failure to observe this bulletin is a disciplinary matter under Rule 9.
