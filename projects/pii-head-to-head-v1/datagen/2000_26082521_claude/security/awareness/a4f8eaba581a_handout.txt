OFFICE OF THE REGISTRAR
INTERNAL GUIDANCE MEMORANDUM

TO: All Registrar Staff, Student Employees, and Departmental Records Liaisons
FROM: The University Registrar
DATE: Fall Term 2011
SUBJECT: Routine Handling of Personal Identifiers

This office holds custody of the student record for the institution, and every person who touches that record carries the same obligation regardless of title or hours worked. The guidance below restates existing practice under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act and the university's identity theft prevention program. It is not new policy; it is the working method we expect at every counter, telephone, and workstation in the suite.

Anything printed that carries a Social Security Number, Student Identification Number, Date of Birth, Permanent Address, Bank Routing Number, or Parent Taxpayer Identification Number is destroyed in a cross-cut shredder at the end of the shift in which it was produced. This includes screen prints from the student information system, transcript proof copies, worksheets used to reconcile enrollment counts, fax cover sheets, appointment sign-in slips bearing more than a first initial, and the discard stack from the imaging scanner. Strip-cut shredders remain in the building for general office paper and are not acceptable for identifier-bearing output. Material awaiting destruction stays in the locked consoles, never on top of them and never in a recycling bin.

Electronic mail is not a secure channel and is never used to send a Social Security Number, a full Date of Birth, a Driver's License Number, a Financial Aid Award Detail, or a scanned image of any government-issued identification. This restriction applies to messages between campus accounts as fully as it applies to messages sent outside the domain, and it applies to attachments, spreadsheets, and calendar invitations. Where a colleague requests such data by email, reply that the request must move to the secure document exchange or to interoffice mail in a sealed envelope, and do not repeat the requested identifier in your reply.

Telephone callers who describe an emergency, decline a return call, or ask you merely to confirm an identifier they have already recited are to be transferred to the Associate Registrar for Records without further discussion. Questions about a specific record belong to the Records Custodian; questions about a suspected compromise belong to the Information Security Officer and, in the same hour, to the FERPA Compliance Coordinator.
