GRANITE HILLS SCHOOL DISTRICT
CUSTOMER AND STAFF GUIDANCE — PROTECTION OF PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS
Adopted by the Superintendent's Cabinet, February 2016

I. GENERAL OBLIGATION

1. Every employee, contractor, and volunteer who handles District records holds those records in trust for the families and staff described in them. The obligation attaches to information in any form and does not end when a document leaves a desk.

II. DESTRUCTION

2. Paper bearing any of the following column headings is destroyed by cross-cut shredder and never by ordinary disposal: Last Name; First Name; Social Security Number; Date of Birth; Student ID; Employee ID; Home Address; Home Phone; Guardian Name; Account Number; Routing Number; Medicaid Number; Meal Eligibility Status; IEP Status.

3. The requirement applies with equal force to partial documents, misprints, jammed-printer sheets, and to any handwritten transcription a staff member has made for temporary convenience.

III. TRANSMISSION

4. None of the headings enumerated at clause 2 is transmitted by electronic mail, personal messaging application, personal cloud account, or unencrypted removable media. Where transfer is genuinely necessary, it is arranged through the Technology Services Department using the approved secure channel and recorded in the transfer log.

5. A District office will never ask a family to send a Social Security Number, an Account Number, or a Payment Card Number by email, and families receiving such a request should treat it as fraudulent.

IV. PRETEXTING CALLS

6. A pretexting caller constructs urgency and authority in order to make refusal feel unhelpful. Characteristic signs are an unverifiable job title, an insistence on speed, an offer to "just confirm what I already have," a refusal to accept a written process, and a request directed to a front counter rather than to the responsible department.

7. The uniform response is to decline all verification, to record the caller's stated name and organisation, to end the call politely, and to report it. No employee is criticised for declining a request that later proves to be legitimate.

V. INTERNAL CONTACTS

8. Reports of suspected pretexting, lost paper, misdirected mail, or a missing device go to the District Records Custodian, with a copy to the site Principal.

9. Student record access and amendment requests go to the Director of Student Services. Employee and payroll record questions go to the Director of Human Resources. Technical questions concerning accounts, encryption, and secure transfer go to the Chief Technology Officer. Escalations and questions of interpretation go to the Assistant Superintendent for Business Services.
