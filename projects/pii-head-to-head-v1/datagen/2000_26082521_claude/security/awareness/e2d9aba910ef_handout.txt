BRANCH INSTRUCTION: RECOGNISING AND HANDLING PRETEXTING CALLS

Issued to all consultants, coordinators and reception staff. 2021.

I. NATURE OF THE THREAT
   (a) A pretexting caller does not attempt to defeat a technical control. The caller attempts to persuade a member of staff to defeat it on their behalf.
   (b) The most common objective is a change to the Bank Sort Code and Bank Account Number held against a worker record before the payroll cut-off.
   (c) The second most common objective is the confirmation of a Date of Birth or National Insurance / Social Security Number already partially known to the caller.

II. INDICATORS
   (a) The caller asserts a senior role but is unknown to the branch and is not in the internal directory.
   (b) The caller supplies a return number and asks that it be used in place of the number on file.
   (c) The caller frames a deadline in terms of a worker being unpaid, and attributes fault to the branch.
   (d) The caller asks you to confirm a field rather than supply it, offering a partial value for agreement.
   (e) The caller objects to the callback procedure, or asks that it be waived "just this once".
   (f) Background audio is inconsistent with the stated location, or the call transfers between speakers mid-request.
   (g) The request arrives shortly before close of business, on a Friday, or during a known system outage.

III. REQUIRED RESPONSE
   (a) Disclose nothing. Do not confirm, deny, or partially confirm any field listed in Annex B.
   (b) State that the branch will return the call on the number held on the record. Do not negotiate this point.
   (c) End the call. Do not accept a transfer to a supervisor offered by the caller.
   (d) Retrieve the contact number from the placement management system. Do not use the number displayed by the telephone system and do not use a number offered during the call.
   (e) Where the caller claimed to be a worker, verify through the registered contact record before discussing the account at all.
   (f) Where the caller claimed internal authority, verify through the internal directory before discussing the account at all.

IV. BANK DETAIL AMENDMENTS
   (a) A change to Bank Sort Code or Bank Account Number is never actioned on a telephone instruction, an emailed instruction, or a messaging application instruction.
   (b) Amendments are actioned only through the worker portal or a completed mandate form verified by the payroll function.
   (c) A change requested within five working days of a payroll cut-off is held and referred to the Payroll Controller.

V. ESCALATION
   (a) Report the call to the Data Protection Lead on the same working day, whether or not anything was disclosed.
   (b) Where any field was disclosed, notify the Data Protection Lead immediately and the Branch Manager thereafter.
   (c) Where the caller referenced a specific client engagement, notify the Account Compliance Manager.
   (d) Record the attempt in the incident log. Log the time, the claimed identity, the fields requested, and the outcome. Do not record the caller's supplied number in any field used for outbound dialling.
