SECTION B — DEFINITIONS
  1.1  "Personal Identifier" means any of the following data elements
       held by the Company in any medium: social security number;
       taxpayer identification number; driver's licence number;
       policy, certificate or group number; claim file number; date
       of birth; mother's maiden name; medical record or provider
       number; bank, draft or credit card number; and personal
       identification number or password.
  1.2  "Working Paper" means any printout, worksheet, adding-machine
       tape, carbon, microfiche jacket, diskette, facsimile
       transmittal or draft correspondence bearing a Personal
       Identifier, whether or not it forms part of the file of record.

SECTION C — MATERIAL REQUIRING DESTRUCTION BY CROSS-CUT SHREDDER
  2.1  The following shall be placed in a locked destruction console
       and shall not be deposited in wastebaskets, recycling bins or
       corrugated cartons:
         (a) rating worksheets, underwriting scratch sheets and
             premium audit calculations;
         (b) claim intake notes, adjuster field notes and independent
             medical report drafts;
         (c) explanation-of-benefits printouts, remittance advices
             and voided drafts;
         (d) applications, change requests and beneficiary
             designation forms not accepted into the file of record;
         (e) computer printouts, green-bar reports and screen prints
             from the policy master or claims system;
         (f) mailing labels, returned envelopes and address-change
             notices;
         (g) carbons, ribbons and one-time-use typewriter cartridges;
         (h) diskettes and back-up tapes, which shall be surrendered
             to the Information Systems Security Administrator for
             degaussing rather than shredded.
  2.2  Working Papers awaiting destruction shall be secured overnight
       in a locked drawer. Consoles shall not be permitted to overfill.
  2.3  Destruction of material subject to a litigation hold or a
       records retention order is prohibited. Enquiries shall be
       directed to the Records Custodian.

SECTION D — RESTRICTIONS ON ELECTRONIC MAIL AND FACSIMILE
  3.1  Personal Identifiers shall not be transmitted by electronic
       mail, whether internally or across the Internet gateway.
  3.2  Where a matter must be discussed electronically, staff shall
       reference the file by internal work item number only and shall
       omit the insured's name and all elements listed at clause 1.1.
  3.3  Electronic mail shall not be used to acknowledge receipt of an
       enrolment form, to confirm coverage, or to relay medical
       information under any circumstance.
  3.4  Facsimile transmission is permitted only to a machine confirmed
       in advance to be located in a controlled area. The sender shall
       verify the destination number by voice contact before
       transmitting and shall retain the confirmation sheet.
  3.5  Documents shall not be left unattended on facsimile machines,
       printers or copier glass.

SECTION E — TELEPHONE ENQUIRIES
  4.1  Staff shall not disclose a Personal Identifier to a caller.
       Staff may confirm information supplied by the caller only after
       the caller has satisfied the authentication questions
       prescribed by the Branch Operations Supervisor.
  4.2  A caller who presses for urgency, who declines to be called
       back at the number of record, or who claims internal authority
       shall be referred without discussion to the Compliance Officer.
