RECORDS SECURITY BULLETIN — PROTECTION OF CUSTOMER AND EMPLOYEE IDENTIFIERS
Issued by the Office of the Compliance Officer
Distribution: All branch escrow, title, and closing personnel
Effective on receipt. Supersedes all prior bulletins on this subject.
Post at each workstation cluster and in the file room.

I. SHRED — CROSSCUT DEVICE ONLY. NO WASTEBASKET DISPOSAL.
- Escrow worksheets and scratch calculations bearing Social Security Number, Taxpayer Identification Number, or Date of Birth.
- Draft and superseded settlement statements showing Buyer Name, Seller Name, Loan Number, or Lender Loan Reference.
- Photocopies of Driver's License, State Identification Card, Passport, Resident Alien Card, or Military Identification taken for signature verification.
- Voided checks, deposit slips, and wire request forms showing Bank Routing Number, Account Number, or Account Holder Name.
- Payoff demand statements and beneficiary statements bearing Loan Number and Property Address.
- Credit reports, verification-of-employment forms, and any page bearing the field Employer, Employer Address, or Gross Monthly Income.
- Fax cover sheets, fax confirmation reports, and fax journal printouts.
- Adhesive notes, message slips, and desk calendar pages bearing any of the above field labels.
- Obsolete address labels, envelope windows, and mailing lists.
- Diskettes and backup tapes are NOT shred items. Route to the Records Custodian for degaussing.

II. NEVER TRANSMIT BY INTERNET MAIL
Internet mail is an unsecured medium. The following are prohibited in the body of a message, in an attachment, or in a subject line:
- Social Security Number; Taxpayer Identification Number.
- Date of Birth; Place of Birth; Mother's Maiden Name.
- Driver's License Number; State Identification Number.
- Bank Routing Number; Account Number; Wire Instruction detail of any kind.
- Escrow File Number combined with Buyer Name or Seller Name.
- Credit report content, in whole or in part.
- Scanned images of executed documents bearing signatures or notarial acknowledgments.
Approved alternatives: interoffice courier in a sealed envelope marked CONFIDENTIAL; hand delivery; recorded-delivery mail; facsimile to a machine confirmed in advance to be in a locked area and attended at the receiving end.

III. RECOGNISING A PRETEXT CALL
Treat as suspect any caller who exhibits two or more of the following:
- Claims urgency and states a deadline that expires the same business day.
- Refuses to give a call-back number, or offers a number differing from the one in the file.
- Claims to be an employee of this company but cannot state their branch, department, or supervising officer by title.
- Claims to be from a lender, county office, or courier and requests the whole file "for verification."
- Asks the employee to confirm rather than supply an identifier ("I have the last four, can you read me the rest?").
- Asks who else in the office handles the file, who is on vacation, or when the manager is away.
- Becomes hostile, flattering, or familiar when refused.
Required response:
- Do not confirm, deny, or correct any identifier.
- Do not state whether a file exists.
- Take the caller's name, employer, and stated purpose. Advise that a return call will be placed to a number of record.
- Terminate the call. Do not transfer it.
- Prepare a written memorandum of the contact the same day.

IV. INTERNAL CONTACTS — BY ROLE TITLE
- Suspected pretext call, lost file, or lost diskette: the Branch Manager immediately, then the Information Security Coordinator.
- File retrieval, off-site storage, destruction certificates, media degaussing: the Records Custodian.
- Requests from law enforcement, subpoenas, and demands for file production: the Underwriting Counsel. No employee shall respond directly.
- Questions on permissible disclosure to lenders and real estate licensees: the Compliance Officer.
- Employee identifier and personnel file inquiries: the Human Resources Administrator.
- Shredder faults, console overflow, or a console found unlocked: the Facilities Coordinator.
