METROPOLITAN TRANSIT AUTHORITY
ADMINISTRATIVE BULLETIN — SERIES 400 (RECORDS AND INFORMATION)
SUBJECT: HANDLING OF PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS BY DIVISIONAL STAFF
SUPERSEDES: BULLETIN 400-6

1. APPLICATION

1.1 This bulletin applies to all salaried and hourly personnel of the Authority, to seasonal fare inspection staff, and to contract clerical personnel assigned to divisional offices.
1.2 Compliance is a condition of continued access to the personnel system, the fare media system, and the paratransit registration system.

2. IDENTIFIERS SUBJECT TO CONTROL

2.1 The following field labels denote controlled data wherever they appear, on paper, on microfiche, on diskette, or on screen:
   - Social Security Number
   - Date of Birth
   - Home Address / Mailing Address
   - Home Telephone Number
   - Employee Number
   - Operator Licence Number
   - Direct Deposit Account Number and Bank Routing Number
   - Paratransit Registrant Number
   - Fare Card Account Number
   - Medical Certification Number
2.2 A record containing any two of the above in combination is classified RESTRICTED and may not leave the divisional office without written release.

3. DESTRUCTION SCHEDULE

3.1 Shred, cross-cut, at end of shift:
   - Working copies, drafts, and photocopies of any RESTRICTED record
   - Printer misfeeds, alignment sheets, and banner pages from the personnel printer
   - Adding machine tapes generated against payroll registers
   - Facsimile confirmation sheets showing transmitted field content
   - Sign-in sheets bearing Employee Number
3.2 Strip-cut shredders are withdrawn from service. Do not requisition replacement blades.
3.3 Diskettes and tape cartridges are not to be shredded. Route to the Data Processing Manager for degaussing under Form DP-22.
3.4 Nothing on the above list is to be placed in recycling containers, in the operators' room, or in any unattended bin.

4. ELECTRONIC MAIL — PROHIBITIONS

4.1 The electronic mail system is not an approved channel for RESTRICTED data. It is not encrypted, is retained on backup media, and is readable by system administration staff.
4.2 Do not place in the body of a message, in a subject line, or in an attachment:
   - Social Security Number
   - Date of Birth
   - Direct Deposit Account Number or Bank Routing Number
   - Medical Certification Number or restriction codes
   - Lists of registrants or employees indexed by any of the above
4.3 Do not forward externally addressed mail requesting the above. Print it, annotate the time received, and route to the Privacy Coordinator.
4.4 Reference a record by Case File Number only. The Case File Number is not a controlled identifier.

5. PRETEXTING — TELEPHONE AND COUNTER

5.1 A pretexting call is an attempt to obtain controlled data by impersonation. Indicators:
   - Claimed identity as a benefits carrier, auditor, or systems vendor, with no work order number
   - Claimed identity as a senior officer, calling from off site, refusing a return call
   - A partial identifier recited, with a request that staff supply or verify the balance
   - Pressure, deadline language, or reference to a grievance or a disciplinary matter
   - Request that material be faxed, read aloud, or held at a counter for collection
   - Repeat calls to different extensions within the same division
5.2 Staff shall:
   - Decline the request without explanation
   - Offer the caller only the published address of the Office of Records Management
   - Take no action on any promise of written authorisation to follow
   - Complete Form RM-6 and forward to the Records Custodian the same day
5.3 Counter requests require photographic identification and a completed release. Verbal authorisation is not accepted from any source, internal or external.

6. INTERNAL CONTACTS BY ROLE

   - Records Custodian — releases, retention questions, Form RM-6 intake
   - Privacy Coordinator — suspected pretexting, external inquiries, complaint intake
   - Divisional Security Officer — same-shift reporting, counter incidents
   - Data Processing Manager — media destruction, access revocation, system logs
   - Director of Human Resources — employee file access disputes
   - General Counsel — subpoenas, court orders, press inquiries
   - Internal Audit Manager — quarterly compliance review

7. ENFORCEMENT

7.1 Divisional Superintendents shall certify compliance quarterly on Form RM-9.
7.2 Failure to comply is a disciplinary matter under the Authority's rules of conduct.
