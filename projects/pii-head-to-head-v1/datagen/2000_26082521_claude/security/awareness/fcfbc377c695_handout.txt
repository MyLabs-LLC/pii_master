HALSTEAD COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
Supplement to Guidance Handout HCHD-PRIV-114
Subject: Verification Practice and Internal Points of Contact
Distribution: All Program Areas / Rev. 2 (November 2021)

It has been observed in the course of routine monitoring that the majority of improper disclosures occurring within the Department arise not from any deficiency in its technical safeguards but from ordinary courtesy extended to an unverified caller. Staff are accordingly instructed that the burden of establishing identity and authority rests at all times upon the person seeking the disclosure, that this burden is not discharged by the caller's recitation of an identifier already in that caller's possession, and that no employee shall be regarded as having acted improperly, discourteously, or in derogation of the Department's service obligations by declining a request pending verification through the internal directory. Verification shall be effected by returning the call to the published listing for the office claimed, and never to a number supplied by the caller; and where the caller claims internal departmental affiliation, verification shall be effected through the internal directory alone, irrespective of the seniority asserted.

Employees are further reminded that the prohibition upon transmitting identifiers by electronic mail extends to the incidental inclusion of such elements in a subject line, a calendar invitation, a meeting note, an out-of-office reply, or a screenshot appended for illustrative purposes, and that documents bearing the field labels Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Medical Record Number, or Member Identification Number are to be conveyed internally by secure portal, by hand within a closed folder, or by interoffice envelope sealed and addressed to a position title rather than to a work area at large. Printed material awaiting such conveyance shall not be left upon a printer, a facsimile machine, a conference table, or a vehicle seat, and shall be shredded rather than retained once conveyance is complete.

Reports shall be made the same business day on which the circumstance is discovered. The reporting employee shall not undertake any independent investigation, shall not contact the person whose identifiers are implicated, and shall preserve all related material in place pending direction from the Privacy Officer.
