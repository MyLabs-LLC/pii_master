DOCUMENT DESTRUCTION AND SHRED STANDARD
Title and Escrow Group — Branch Operations
Effective July 1, 2011. Supersedes the destruction memo of February 2009.
Review cycle: annual, by the Compliance Officer.

  I. Purpose
     A. To set one destruction method for all paper and media holding customer identifiers, consistent with the disposal obligations that apply to consumer report information.
     B. To make destruction routine rather than occasional, so that nothing accumulates in unsecured form.

 II. What must be shredded
     A. Closing and title work product
        1. Working copies of the settlement statement, the buyer and seller worksheets, and any draft carrying the Loan Number.
        2. Preliminary reports and commitments marked superseded.
        3. Payoff demand statements and their fax cover sheets.
     B. Identity documents
        1. Photocopies of a driver's license, passport, or military identification.
        2. Any page bearing the Social Security Number or Taxpayer Identification Number field, including the signature page of a tax certification.
     C. Funding and disbursement paper
        1. Wire instruction sheets, including drafts corrected by hand.
        2. Deposit slips, check copies, and any page displaying the Bank Account Number or ABA Routing Number fields.
        3. Voided checks of every kind.
     D. Incidental paper
        1. Printer misfeeds, test pages, and duplicate prints.
        2. Handwritten notes taken during a call that record any identifier.
        3. Envelopes and mailing labels that show a returned-mail forwarding block.

III. What must not be shredded
     A. Anything under a litigation hold notice issued by the Legal Department.
     B. Original recorded instruments, original notes, and original policies.
     C. Files inside the retention period set in the retention schedule. If in doubt, route to the Records Custodian rather than to the bin.

 IV. Method
     A. Cross-cut only. Strip-cut machines were removed from service and must not be returned to the floor.
     B. Locked consoles
        1. One console per floor, keyed to the Records Custodian.
        2. Consoles are for destruction only. They are not overflow storage and are not to be opened to retrieve a document. A document placed in a console is treated as destroyed.
     C. Vendor pickup
        1. Scheduled twice monthly. The Records Custodian or a designated backup must witness the load.
        2. The Certificate of Destruction is filed with the vendor contract. A missing certificate is reported to the Compliance Officer within five business days.
     D. Media
        1. Thumb drives, CD-R, and backup tapes are not shredded at the branch. They go to the Information Security Manager for degaussing or physical destruction.
        2. A printer, copier, or fax machine leaving the branch must have its drive cleared by the Information Security Manager first, whatever the reason for the move.

  V. Daily practice
     A. Shred at the point of creation, not at the end of the week.
     B. Empty personal recycling of any customer paper before leaving.
     C. Report a full console, a jammed console, or a console found unlocked to the Records Custodian the same day.
