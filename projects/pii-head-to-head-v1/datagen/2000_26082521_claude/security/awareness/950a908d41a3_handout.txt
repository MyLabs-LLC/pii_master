PROTECTING CUSTOMER IDENTIFIERS
Desk Handout — Escrow and Title Operations
Issued by the Compliance Department, March 2011

1. What we treat as a protected identifier
   1.1 Any value recorded in these fields: Social Security Number, Taxpayer Identification Number, Date of Birth, Driver's License Number, State ID Number, Bank Account Number, ABA Routing Number, Credit Card Number, Loan Number.
   1.2 A Borrower Name or Seller Name becomes protected the moment it appears beside any field in 1.1.
   1.3 Copies count. A photocopy of a driver's license carries the same handling rules as the original.

2. Handling at the desk
   2.1 Keep files closed and turned face down when you step away.
   2.2 Clear the counter before the next appointment. Nothing from one file may remain visible during another.
   2.3 Lock your workstation when leaving the desk for any reason, including short breaks.
   2.4 Do not carry closing files home. Files leave the branch only in the locked courier bag, logged out by the Records Custodian.

3. Storage
   3.1 Working files go in the escrow cabinet, locked at close of business.
   3.2 Thumb drives and CD-R media containing identifiers must be checked in to the Records Custodian the same day they are created.
   3.3 Do not keep identifiers in desk drawers, personal notebooks, or on adhesive notes.

4. Questions
   4.1 If you are unsure whether a document holds a protected identifier, treat it as though it does, and ask the Compliance Officer before releasing it.
