PART C. NOTES
    C.1  "SEALED" denotes a tamper-evident envelope, addressed to a role
         title, entered on the Pouch Transfer Register at both ends.
    C.2  A field marked NO is not transmitted by that channel under any
         approval, including verbal approval by a supervisor. Requests to
         override are reported to the Information Security Coordinator.
    C.3  Truncation does not remove a field from this matrix. Partial
         figures are treated as the whole.
    C.4  Where a record contains any field marked NO in a row above, the
         entire record adopts the most restrictive row.
    C.5  Screen displays containing Part B fields are cleared before a
         workstation is left unattended and are not positioned within view
         of the public counter.
