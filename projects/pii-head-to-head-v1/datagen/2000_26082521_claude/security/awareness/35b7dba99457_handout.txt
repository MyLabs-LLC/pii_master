METROPOLITAN TRANSIT AUTHORITY
OFFICE OF THE PRIVACY OFFICER

NOTICE TO ALL PERSONNEL CONCERNING THE HANDLING, TRANSMISSION
AND DESTRUCTION OF RECORDS BEARING PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS

Policy Reference: PRIV-04 (Rev. 2)        Effective: 01 April 2016
Supersedes: PRIV-04 (Rev. 1)              Next Review: 01 April 2018

Pursuant to the obligations imposed upon the Authority by applicable
state and federal privacy enactments, and without limitation to any
further or more stringent duty arising under contract or collective
agreement, the schedule set out below shall govern the treatment of
every record in which any of the enumerated fields appears.

+--------------------------+---------------------+---------------------+
| IDENTIFIER FIELD         | PERMITTED CHANNEL   | DISPOSITION METHOD  |
+--------------------------+---------------------+---------------------+
| Social Security Number   | Sealed inter-office | Cross-cut shred     |
| Date of Birth            | Sealed inter-office | Cross-cut shred     |
| Fare Card Account Number | Encrypted terminal  | Locked shred bin    |
| Bank Routing Number      | Encrypted terminal  | Witnessed shred     |
| Driver Licence Number    | Sealed inter-office | Cross-cut shred     |
| Home Street Address      | Sealed inter-office | Locked shred bin    |
| Reduced-Fare File No.    | Counter intake only | Cross-cut shred     |
| Payroll Reference Number | Sealed inter-office | Witnessed shred     |
+--------------------------+---------------------+---------------------+

No field enumerated in the foregoing schedule may be transmitted by
electronic mail, by facsimile, or by any unencrypted messaging service,
whether within the Authority or beyond it, under any circumstance
whatsoever and irrespective of the asserted seniority or apparent
urgency of the requesting party. The absence of an express prohibition
in respect of a field not enumerated shall not be construed as
permission to transmit that field by such means.

Enquiries arising under this Notice shall be directed in the first
instance to the Records Custodian and, in the event of that officer's
unavailability, to the Deputy Privacy Officer.
