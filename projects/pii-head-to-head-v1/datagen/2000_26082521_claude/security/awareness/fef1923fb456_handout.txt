OPERATIONS BULLETIN 2004-14
SUBJECT: DOCUMENT DISPOSAL AND MEDIA DESTRUCTION SCHEDULE
DISTRIBUTION: STORE MANAGERS, PHARMACISTS-IN-CHARGE
ACTION REQUIRED: POST AT PHARMACY WORKSTATION AND SERVICE DESK

1. Effective immediately, all locations shall dispose of identifier-bearing material in accordance with the schedule at clause 4. Ordinary waste streams may not be used.

2. Each location shall maintain not fewer than two operable cross-cut shredders, one sited within the pharmacy department and one at the service desk. Strip-cut units are withdrawn from approved status and shall be removed from service.

3. The Store Manager shall verify shredder operability at the opening of each trading week and shall record the check on the Weekly Controls Log. The log carries the columns: Check Date, Device Location, Operable Y/N, Waste Bag Sealed Y/N, Initials of Checker.

4. DISPOSAL SCHEDULE

   Material                          Method            Timing
   -------------------------------   ---------------   ------------------
   Label overruns / misprints        Cross-cut shred   Point of generation
   Adjudication printouts            Cross-cut shred   End of shift
   Third-party rejection slips       Cross-cut shred   End of shift
   Counselling notes                 Cross-cut shred   End of shift
   Signature logs                    Cross-cut shred   On retention expiry
   Register tape (voided)            Cross-cut shred   End of shift
   Fax confirmations                 Cross-cut shred   Point of generation
   Photo order envelopes             Cross-cut shred   On order closure
   Diskettes / backup cartridges     Degauss           Forward to Custodian
   Hard drives from retired units    Degauss + punch   Forward to Custodian

5. Material awaiting collection shall be held in the locked stockroom cage. Bags shall be sealed with numbered ties. The tie sequence is recorded on the Destruction Manifest, which carries the columns: Manifest Line, Material Class, Bag Seal Sequence, Date Sealed, Date Collected, Vendor Receipt Reference.

6. The Destruction Manifest is retained at the location for the period specified by the Records Custodian and is subject to audit by the District Loss Prevention Manager without prior notice.

7. Under no circumstances shall identifier-bearing material be:

   7.1 Placed in the cardboard baler or the compactor.
   7.2 Removed from the premises by a colleague for disposal elsewhere.
   7.3 Reused as scratch paper, message pad, or shelf tag backing.
   7.4 Retained in personal lockers, vehicles, or apron pockets past shift end.

8. Where a shredder fails and cannot be returned to service the same trading day, identifier-bearing material shall be bagged, sealed, and secured in the cage pending repair. Accumulation in open totes at the workstation is prohibited.

9. Questions concerning retention periods, manifest handling, or vendor collection shall be directed to the Records Custodian. Questions concerning shredder procurement and repair shall be directed to the Store Manager, who holds the facilities requisition authority.
