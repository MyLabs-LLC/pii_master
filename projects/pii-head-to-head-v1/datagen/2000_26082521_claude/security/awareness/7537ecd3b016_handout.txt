NOTICE TO ALL COUNTER AND TELEPHONE STAFF -- POST AND RETAIN

Office of the University Registrar                      March 3, 1997

The undersigned directs the attention of all staff to the following
requirements, which are in force during all hours the counter is attended.

-  Do not read aloud, confirm, or deny any of the following over the
   telephone: Social Security Number, Student Identification Number, Date
   of Birth, Permanent Address, Grade Point Average, or Course Grade.

-  Do not accept a caller's recitation of an identifier as proof of
   identity. A caller in possession of one element is frequently seeking a
   second.

-  Offer to return the call to the number appearing in the student's record
   or in the internal directory. A caller who declines a return call is to
   be treated as unverified, and the contact reported.

-  Shred, in the cross-cut unit only: spoiled transcript stock, superseded
   rosters, discarded label sheets, scratch notes, and printer ribbons.
   Nothing bearing an identifier is to enter a wastebasket or recycling bin.

-  Send no identifier by electronic mail, in the message, the subject line,
   or an attachment, to any recipient whatsoever.

-  Clear the counter surface and lock the drawer before leaving the station
   unattended for any interval.

Direct questions concerning verification procedure to the Assistant
Registrar for Records. Report any suspected pretexting attempt the same
business day to the Associate Registrar for Compliance.

THE UNIVERSITY REGISTRAR
