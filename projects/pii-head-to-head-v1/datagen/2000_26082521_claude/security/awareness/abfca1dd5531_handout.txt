COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
CLINIC FRONT-COUNTER GUIDANCE — PROTECTING IDENTIFIERS
Bulletin 2021-06 / Post at each reception station

(1) WHAT GOES IN THE LOCKED CONSOLE

Everything printed at the counter that carries a Client Name, Date of Birth, Social Security Number, Medical Record Number, or Insurance Member ID. This includes sign-in sheets, wristband misprints, label sheets, appointment reminder slips, superseded forms, and any note written on scratch paper. Do not use the blue recycling bin. Do not leave material on top of the console.

(2) WHAT NEVER GOES OUT BY EMAIL

    Social Security Number ......... never
    Date of Birth .................. never
    Medical Record Number .......... never
    Home Address ................... never
    Telephone Number ............... never
    Insurance Member ID ............ never
    Photograph of a document ....... never

Routing to a colleague inside the Department does not create an exception. Use the record system's internal note function, which is auditable; electronic mail is not.

(3) RECOGNISING A PRETEXT CALL

A pretext caller wants you to fill a gap. Listen for: "I only need you to confirm..."; "I'm calling from the office upstairs and I don't have my code"; "this is urgent, the patient is in transit"; "your supervisor already approved it"; a caller who becomes angry when asked to be called back on the published main line; a caller who supplies most of an identifier and asks whether the remainder is correct.

Correct response: do not confirm that a record exists. State that release of information is handled through the established process. End the call. Write down the time, the stated name of the organisation, and what was asked for, on the Suspicious Contact Log.

(4) WHO TO TELL

    Suspected release of information ....... Privacy Officer
    Lost badge, laptop, or paper file ...... Information Security Officer
    Console full or lock broken ............ Facilities Coordinator
    Records request or subpoena ............ Records Custodian
    Media enquiry .......................... Public Information Officer

(5) STANDING RULES

Clear the counter at close. Lock the drawer. Log off, do not lock the screen, when leaving the station for the day. Turn documents face down when a member of the public approaches. Never read an identifier aloud where the waiting area can hear it.
