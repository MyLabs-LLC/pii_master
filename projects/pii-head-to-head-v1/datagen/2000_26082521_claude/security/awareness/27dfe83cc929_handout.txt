NOTICE TO STAFF — TO BE POSTED AT EACH BILLING WORKSTATION
Patient Financial Services / Revised 04-2004 / Form PFS-A9

(1) Clear your desk at every break. Face-down is not secure.

(2) Lock your workstation before leaving it. Do not share your sign-on.

(3) Shred bin, not wastebasket. Cross-cut console at the end of each aisle.

(4) Never place any of the following in electronic mail: Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Medical Record Number, Account Number, Policy Number, Payment Card Number, Bank Routing Number, Checking Account Number.

(5) Verify before you release. Callers who will not give a callback number get nothing.

(6) Do not confirm information the caller supplies. Confirmation is disclosure.

(7) Retrieve printer and fax output immediately. Report orphaned output to the Records Custodian.

(8) Report any suspected pretexting call to the Patient Financial Services Manager before the end of shift.

(9) Do not remove protected paper from the department for any reason.

(10) Visitors are escorted. Vendors sign the departmental log at the reception desk.

When in doubt, release nothing and refer the caller to the Privacy Officer.
