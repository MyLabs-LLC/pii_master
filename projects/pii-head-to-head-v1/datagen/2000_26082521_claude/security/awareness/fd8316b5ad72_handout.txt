COASTAL COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT
CUSTOMER PRIVACY HANDOUT — SERIES 2021
"WHAT WE ASK FOR, AND WHAT WE WILL NEVER ASK FOR"
Available at all staffed stations, the Customer Service Center, and upon request from any Fare Inspector

    I.   INFORMATION THE DISTRICT LAWFULLY COLLECTS
         A.  In connection with an application for a discounted fare instrument, the
             District is authorized to collect and retain the following fields:
             1.  Applicant Full Legal Name
             2.  Residential Address of Record
             3.  Date of Birth
             4.  Reduced Fare Category Code
             5.  Certifying Physician or Agency Reference Number
         B.  In connection with a registered fare card, the District retains:
             1.  Card Serial Number
             2.  Autoload Payment Card Last Four Digits
             3.  Billing Address of Record

    II.  INFORMATION THE DISTRICT WILL NEVER REQUEST BY TELEPHONE OR ELECTRONIC MAIL
         A.  The District's employees are prohibited, absolutely and without exception, from
             soliciting any of the following by unsolicited outbound telephone call or by
             electronic mail:
             1.  Social Security Number, in whole or in part
             2.  Payment Card Primary Account Number
             3.  Card Verification Value or Expiration Date
             4.  Bank Account Number or Routing Transit Number
             5.  Online account password, personal identification number, or one-time
                 passcode transmitted to a customer's device
         B.  Any communication purporting to originate with the District and requesting the
             foregoing should be regarded as fraudulent notwithstanding the apparent
             legitimacy of the letterhead, logotype, caller identification display, or
             return address employed therein.

    III. RECOGNISING A PRETEXTING CALL
         A.  Hallmarks commonly observed:
             1.  An assertion that a fare instrument will be deactivated within a stated
                 and unreasonably short interval.
             2.  An offer of a refund, credit, or fare adjustment conditioned upon the
                 immediate provision of account credentials.
             3.  A refusal to permit the customer to terminate the call and to re-initiate
                 contact through a published District number.
             4.  An instruction that the customer refrain from discussing the matter with
                 station personnel.
         B.  The recommended response is to end the communication, to make no confirmation
             of any identifier however innocuous it may appear, and to report the contact.

    IV.  DISPOSAL OF DISTRICT-ISSUED PAPER
         A.  Expired fare instruments, application receipts, eligibility determination
             letters, and monthly statements should be shredded rather than discarded
             intact, each such document bearing at minimum an Account Number field and a
             Name of Record field.

    V.   WHOM TO CONTACT
         A.  To report a suspicious call: the Customer Privacy Coordinator, through the
             Customer Service Center.
         B.  To correct an identifier held in District records: the Records Custodian.
         C.  To obtain a copy of the District's retention schedule: the Public Records
             Officer.
