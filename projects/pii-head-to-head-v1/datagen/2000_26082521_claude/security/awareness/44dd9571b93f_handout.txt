PATIENT FINANCIAL SERVICES — BUSINESS OFFICE
IDENTIFIER PROTECTION HANDOUT BO-14
Issued: Fourth Quarter 1998.  Supersedes BO-11.
Distribution: all business office staff, registration clerks, cashiers.

  1.0  SCOPE
       1.1  This handout governs every field carried on the guarantor
            record and the aged trial balance, including Patient Name,
            Medical Record Number, Account Number, Social Security
            Number, Date of Birth, Guarantor Address, Subscriber
            Identification, Group Number, and Employer Name.
       1.2  The obligation attaches to the field, not to the medium.
            A figure written on a scrap of note paper is protected to
            the same degree as the same figure on the mainframe.

  2.0  DESTRUCTION
       2.1  Cross-cut shred at the end of each shift: superbills,
            voided encounter forms, remittance advice worksheets,
            carbon and carbonless copies, ledger cards, adding-machine
            tapes bearing an account number, and any green-bar report
            printed for reconciliation.
       2.2  Do not place identifier-bearing paper in the blue recycling
            barrels, the general waste stream, or the courier pouch.
       2.3  Diskettes, tape cartridges, and microfiche are surrendered
            to the Records Custodian for destruction. They are never
            shredded at the desk.

  3.0  ELECTRONIC MAIL
       3.1  Never place a Social Security Number, Date of Birth, or
            Account Number in the body, subject line, or attachment of
            any message leaving the house network.
       3.2  Spreadsheet extracts of the accounts receivable file are
            not to be mailed, attached, or carried home by diskette.

  4.0  TELEPHONE PRETEXT
       4.1  Treat as suspect any caller who declines a return call to a
            published number, cites urgency, or asks you to read back an
            identifier the caller should already hold.
       4.2  Terminate the call courteously and record what was asked.

  5.0  REPORTING
       5.1  Report to the Patient Accounts Manager within the shift; if
            unavailable, to the Business Office Director or, after
            hours, the House Supervisor.
