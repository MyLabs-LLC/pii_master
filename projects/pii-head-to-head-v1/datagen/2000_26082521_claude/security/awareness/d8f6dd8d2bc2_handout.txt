DATA GOVERNANCE PROCEDURE DG-4
IDENTIFIER PROTECTION — SITE AND DEPARTMENT PRACTICE
Owner: Data Privacy Officer
Concurrence: Chief Technology Officer; Director of Human Resources

1.0 CLASSIFICATION

1.1 Records are classified Restricted where they carry any element in the table below.

  ELEMENT                        SYSTEM COLUMN NAME
  Social Security Number         SSN
  Truncated Social Security No.  SSN_LAST4
  Local Student Identifier       LASID
  State Student Identifier       SASID
  Date of Birth                  DOB
  Home Address                   ADDR_LINE1 / ADDR_LINE2
  Guardian Contact Number        GDN_PHONE
  Meal Eligibility Status        MEAL_STATUS
  Special Education Flag         IEP_FLAG
  Medicaid Billing Identifier    MEDICAID_ID
  Direct Deposit Account Number  ACCT_NUM
  Bank Routing Number            ROUTING
  Employee Identification No.    EMPL_ID

1.2 A record is Restricted if any one element is present. Removal of a name does not declassify a record where LASID, DOB, or ADDR_LINE1 remain.

2.0 DISPOSAL

2.1 Restricted paper is destroyed by locked-console shredding only.

2.2 Retention periods are set by the Records Custodian. Destruction before the stated period is prohibited; retention beyond it without written hold is likewise prohibited.

2.3 Whiteboards, chart paper, and sticky notes bearing identifiers are Restricted paper.

3.0 TRANSMISSION

3.1 Electronic mail is not an approved channel for Restricted content, inbound or outbound.

3.2 Where a message is received containing Restricted content, the recipient shall not forward it. The recipient shall reply requesting resubmission through the secure file exchange, then delete the message and empty the deleted items folder.

3.3 Exports from the student information system shall be limited to the columns required for the stated purpose. Full-table extracts require written approval of the Data Privacy Officer.

3.4 Restricted files shall not be stored on personal cloud accounts, removable media, or home equipment.

4.0 PRETEXTING RECOGNITION

4.1 Staff shall assume that any unsolicited caller requesting identifiers is unverified.

4.2 Recognised indicators:
  a. Urgency framed around payroll, enrollment deadlines, or an audit.
  b. A caller who names a department but cannot name a role title within it.
  c. A request to "verify" data the caller purports to already possess.
  d. Insistence that the matter not be logged or escalated.
  e. Contact arriving outside published office hours.

4.3 Required response: decline, terminate, document, report to the Data Privacy Officer.

5.0 ESCALATION

5.1 Level 1 — Building Principal or department supervisor.
5.2 Level 2 — Data Privacy Officer.
5.3 Level 3 — General Counsel and the Risk Manager, convened as the incident panel.
