HARBOR CITY TRANSIT COMMISSION
BULLETIN OF THE OFFICE OF THE DATA PROTECTION OFFICER
NO. 2021-06 — PROTECTION OF PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS
FOR POSTING: Operator Ready Rooms, Fare Vault, Eligibility Intake Counter

    A.  GENERAL OBLIGATION
        Every officer and employee of the Commission is charged with an affirmative and
        continuing duty of care with respect to personal identifiers encountered in the
        course of employment, which duty survives transfer, reassignment, and separation.

    B.  SHRED — WITHOUT EXERCISE OF DISCRETION
        (i)   Fare subsidy applications bearing the fields Applicant Name, Date of Birth,
              and Household Income.
        (ii)  Vendor correspondence bearing the fields Taxpayer Identification Number and
              Remittance Account Number.
        (iii) Any register, roster, or manifest bearing the field Employee Social Security
              Number, including legacy forms retaining that field in unredacted form.
              PROVIDED, HOWEVER, that no record subject to an active litigation hold shall
              be destroyed, and where doubt exists as to the existence of such a hold the
              record shall be sequestered and the matter referred to the Records Custodian.

    C.  NEVER SEND BY ELECTRONIC MAIL
        (i)   Social Security Number or any fragment thereof.
        (ii)  Payment Card Primary Account Number, Expiration Date, or Card Verification
              Value.
        (iii) Bank Account Number or Routing Transit Number.
        (iv)  Passwords, personal identification numbers, or one-time passcodes.
        (v)   Photographs or screen captures depicting any of the foregoing fields.

    D.  A PRETEXTING CALL MAY BE IDENTIFIED BY
        (i)   Manufactured urgency coupled with a request that ordinary verification be
              waived "just this once."
        (ii)  Solicitation of confirmation rather than disclosure, the caller reciting a
              partial identifier and inviting correction.
        (iii) Resistance to a callback through the internal directory.
        (iv)  Escalating displeasure upon refusal, which is a tactic and not evidence of
              legitimacy.

    E.  CONTACT, BY ROLE TITLE
        (i)   Data Protection Officer — interpretation of this Bulletin.
        (ii)  Records Custodian — destruction, holds, and retention.
        (iii) Security Operations Duty Officer — suspected pretexting, at any hour.
        (iv)  Division Superintendent — notification concurrent with any of the foregoing.
