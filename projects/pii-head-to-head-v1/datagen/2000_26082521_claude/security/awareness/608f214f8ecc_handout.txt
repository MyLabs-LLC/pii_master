    1.0  AUTHORITY AND SCOPE
         1.1  This procedure is adopted pursuant to the administrative
              safeguards required of the institution and applies, without
              exception or limitation, to every employee, agent, temporary
              worker, student, volunteer, and contracted representative who
              is granted access, whether incidental or routine, to the
              billing systems of this institution.
         1.2  Nothing contained herein shall be construed to diminish any
              stricter obligation imposed by departmental practice, by the
              terms of a business associate agreement, or by applicable law.

    2.0  DEFINITIONS
         2.1  "Identifier" means any data element which, standing alone or
              in combination with other data elements, is reasonably capable
              of identifying an individual, including but not limited to the
              fields Patient Name, Guarantor Name, Date of Birth, Home
              Address, Telephone Number, Electronic Mail Address, Social
              Security Number, Medical Record Number, Account Number,
              Member ID, Subscriber ID, Claim Number, Date of Service, and
              Primary Account Number.
         2.2  "Pretexting" means the practice of obtaining Identifiers by
              means of a fabricated identity, a fabricated circumstance, or
              a fabricated authority.

    3.0  DESTRUCTION OF MEDIA
         3.1  The following categories of material shall be destroyed by
              cross-cut shredding, or by deposit into a locked destruction
              console pending vendor pulverization, and shall under no
              circumstances be placed in recycling bins, desk-side waste
              receptacles, or unattended collection boxes:
              3.1.1  Aged trial balance reports, collection worklists, and
                     any report bearing a Guarantor Name column.
              3.1.2  Insurance verification worksheets bearing Member ID
                     and Group Number columns.
              3.1.3  Returned mail, including envelopes, where the mailing
                     panel discloses a Patient Name.
              3.1.4  Facsimile cover sheets, transmission confirmations,
                     and misdirected facsimile output of every description.
              3.1.5  Adhesive labels, wristband proofs, and armband misprints.
         3.2  Material awaiting destruction shall not be permitted to
              accumulate upon any work surface at the close of a shift.

    4.0  RESTRICTIONS UPON ELECTRONIC TRANSMISSION
         4.1  The following shall never be transmitted by electronic mail,
              by personal messaging application, by short message service,
              or by any consumer file-sharing service, irrespective of the
              apparent urgency of the request:
              4.1.1  Any value populating the field Social Security Number.
              4.1.2  Any value populating the fields Primary Account
                     Number, Card Verification Value, or Expiration Date.
              4.1.3  Any value populating the fields Bank Routing Number or
                     Depositor Account Number.
              4.1.4  Screenshots, photographs, or scans of any registration
                     screen, remittance advice, or ledger detail.
         4.2  Where external transmission is genuinely necessary, the
              approved secure-messaging portal shall be employed, and the
              recipient shall be verified against the payer contact record
              maintained by the Revenue Cycle Director in advance of sending.

    5.0  RECOGNITION OF PRETEXTING
         5.1  Personnel shall treat the following as indicia of a pretexting
              attempt, the presence of any one of which shall suffice to
              warrant termination of the call:
              5.1.1  The caller asserts an emergency and disclaims the
                     ability to be called back at a published number.
              5.1.2  The caller declines or fails to supply the verification
                     elements required by the standard identity script.
              5.1.3  The caller purports to hold authority over the employee
                     and invokes the displeasure of an unnamed executive.
              5.1.4  The caller requests that the account note be suppressed,
                     omitted, or worded so as to conceal the inquiry.
              5.1.5  The caller seeks confirmation rather than disclosure,
                     inviting the employee merely to affirm a partial value.
         5.2  The employee shall not confirm, deny, correct, or complete any
              Identifier recited by an unverified caller.

    6.0  REPORTING
         6.1  Suspected pretexting shall be reported without delay to the
              Patient Financial Services Supervisor, who shall in turn
              notify the Privacy Officer within one business day.
         6.2  Suspected compromise of a system credential shall be reported
              concurrently to the Information Security Officer.
         6.3  Requests for records from law enforcement or from counsel
              shall be referred, without response, to the Records Custodian.
