MERIDIAN FREIGHT SYSTEMS, INC.
DOCK OFFICE POSTING — IDENTIFIER HANDLING
Post at each printer, fax station, and dispatch counter. Do not remove.

=====================================================================
PAPER IN HAND                    | ACTION
=====================================================================
Driver qualification file        | Locked cabinet; shred on supersession
Medical Examiner Certificate     | Locked cabinet; shred on supersession
Payroll register / stub carbon   | Shred same shift
Benefit enrolment form           | Return to Human Resources Coordinator
Delivery receipt, signed         | Retain per schedule RT-2
Fax misprint or jam sheet        | Shred immediately
Photocopy made for convenience   | Shred immediately
Visitor log, completed page      | Shred at month end
Diskette, tape, drive            | Transfer receipt IS-11
=====================================================================

FIELDS THAT TRIGGER THIS POSTING
Social Security Number . Date of Birth . Home Address . Employee Number
Commercial Driver Licence Number . Bank Routing Number . Account Number
Payment Card Number . Mother's Maiden Name . Personal Identification Number

ELECTRONIC MAIL — NO EXCEPTIONS
* No identifier field in body, subject line, or attachment.
* No scanned licence, cheque, medical card, or insurance card.
* No unmasked identifier column in any spreadsheet.
* No forwarding of a company mailbox to an outside address.
* Approved transfer only, arranged through the Information Systems
  Supervisor. Verbal approval is not sufficient.

TELEPHONE — PRETEXTING INDICATORS
* Unlisted caller claiming audit, headquarters, or carrier authority.
* Urgency: load held, payroll cut-off, inspector waiting.
* "I have most of it — just confirm the last four."
* Fax or address supplied verbally by the caller.
* Objection to callback or to being placed on hold.
* Call placed to relief staff outside office hours.

RESPONSE: Confirm nothing. Spell nothing. End the call. Log it on
SEC-4. Tell the Terminal Manager the same shift.

CONTACTS — ROLE TITLE ONLY
Records Custodian ................ retention, destruction, file pulls
Terminal Manager ................. first report, all incidents
Corporate Security Officer ....... pretexting, badge misuse, theft
Information Systems Supervisor ... mailboxes, media, transfers
Human Resources Coordinator ...... personnel file access

Unattended paper is an incident. Report it.
