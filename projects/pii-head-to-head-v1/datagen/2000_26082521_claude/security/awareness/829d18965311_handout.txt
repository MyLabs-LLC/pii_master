NOTICE TO GUARANTORS AND VISITORS TO THE BILLING OFFICE
Issued by the Office of the Privacy Officer
Posting Reference: PRV-NOT-07 (2021)

    1.  CONCERNING WHAT THIS OFFICE WILL AND WILL NOT ASK OF YOU

        (a)  When you telephone this office, a representative will request a
             limited number of verification elements, which may include the
             fields Patient Name, Date of Birth, and Account Number as the
             same appear upon your statement.

        (b)  This office will not, in the ordinary course, request that you
             recite your Social Security Number in its entirety, and will
             never request your online banking credentials, the Card
             Verification Value printed upon a payment card, or any password
             of any description whatsoever.

    2.  CONCERNING CALLS YOU MAY RECEIVE

        A person telephoning you and purporting to represent this
        institution should be regarded with caution where:

             (i)    the caller demands immediate payment by prepaid card,
                    money transfer, or a payment application;
             (ii)   the caller threatens the involvement of law enforcement
                    or the immediate withdrawal of care;
             (iii)  the caller is unwilling to provide a statement reference
                    that you may verify against correspondence in your
                    possession;
             (iv)   the caller solicits your Bank Routing Number or your
                    Primary Account Number before having established, to
                    your satisfaction, the legitimacy of the balance claimed.

        You are entitled in every such instance to terminate the call and to
        contact this office directly through the number printed upon your
        statement of account.

    3.  CONCERNING PAPER IN YOUR POSSESSION

        Statements, itemised bills, remittance advices, and explanation of
        benefits notices bear the fields Member ID, Claim Number, and Date
        of Service, and should be shredded rather than discarded intact once
        the matters to which they pertain have been concluded.

    4.  CONCERNING ELECTRONIC MAIL

        This office does not accept, and requests that you do not send,
        photographs or scans of payment cards, of identification documents,
        or of any correspondence bearing a Social Security Number. Secure
        transmission is available through the patient portal.

    5.  CONCERNING COMPLAINTS AND CONCERNS

        Any concern respecting the handling of your information may be
        raised with the Patient Financial Services Supervisor, and may be
        escalated, at your election, to the Privacy Officer, whose office is
        charged with the investigation of such matters and with the issuance
        of a written response. Requests for copies of records are directed
        instead to the Records Custodian.
