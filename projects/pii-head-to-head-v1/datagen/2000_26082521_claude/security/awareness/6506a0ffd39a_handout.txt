CUSTOMER AND STAFF HANDOUT — PROTECTING YOUR IDENTIFIERS
Issued by the Compliance Office · Contract Logistics Division · 2011

WHAT WE HOLD ABOUT YOU
Collection Name / Consignee Name · Delivery Address · Postcode · Account Reference · Signature image · Contact preference. We do not require and will not ask for: Date of Birth · National Insurance Number · Card Number · Card Security Code · Sort Code · Account Number.

SHRED IT
- Delivery notes, failed-delivery cards, returns labels
- Invoices and statements bearing an Account Reference
- Anything showing your Full Name against your Home Address
- Envelopes and packing slips — the address label is the identifier

DO NOT EMAIL IT
- Bank details of any kind. We will never request them by email.
- Copies of a passport, driving licence or utility bill
- A Date of Birth supplied "to confirm your identity"
- Photographs of documents taken on a mobile handset

RECOGNISE A PRETEXTING CALL
A caller who has some of your details is not thereby genuine. Treat the following as warning signs:
- Claims a parcel is held pending a payment or a "verification"
- Asks you to confirm details they read out
- Will not give a Consignment Reference, or gives one that does not match your paperwork
- Presses for an immediate answer, or asks you to stay on the line
- Offers a number for you to call back on

WHAT TO DO
- Give nothing. Ending a call is not rudeness.
- Note the time, the stated name and the stated organisation.
- Call back only on the number printed on your delivery paperwork.
- Report the contact.

WHO TO CONTACT
- The Customer Records Custodian — corrections, deletions, copies of what we hold
- The Data Protection Coordinator — misuse of your details, misdirected mail
- The Depot Security Officer — suspicious calls or callers claiming to represent this company
- The Duty Transport Supervisor — a driver or vehicle you cannot account for

Staff: the same rules bind you. Identifiers leave the depot by approved courier or approved secure transfer. Never by personal mailbox, personal handset, or unencrypted media.
