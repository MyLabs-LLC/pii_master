STAFF BULLETIN — TELEPHONE VERIFICATION

Call volume associated with pretexting attempts has increased across the branch network during the current quarter, and the pattern warrants restating the standing rule. No member identifier is to be confirmed to an inbound caller under any circumstance, including confirmation by implication. A caller who recites a partial Social Security Number, a Date of Birth, or a Member Number and asks whether it is correct is to receive no answer either way; the burden of authentication rests entirely with the caller, and the credit union discloses nothing until authentication is complete. Where a caller fails verification but presents a credible account of a service problem, the appropriate disposition is to invite the member to attend a branch with government-issued photographic identification. Staff are reminded that an assertion of employment by a vendor, by a card processor, by a regulator, or by another department of the credit union does not exempt a caller from verification, and that internal transfers are not to be initiated on a caller's instruction. Any call exhibiting these characteristics is to be logged and referred to the Fraud Investigations Coordinator before the close of business, with a copy of the log entry retained by the Member Services Supervisor.
