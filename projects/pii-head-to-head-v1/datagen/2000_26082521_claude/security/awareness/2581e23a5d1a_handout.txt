TELLER LINE STANDING INSTRUCTION — PROTECTION OF IDENTIFYING PARTICULARS
Posted at each station by direction of the Branch Operations Manager

1. Any item bearing a Member Number, Social Security Number, Date of Birth, or Account Number shall be placed in the locked destruction bin at the conclusion of its use, and not otherwise.

2. Cash drawer proof sheets, void tickets, and card carriers shall be treated as records within the meaning of Clause 1.

3. No Personal Identifier shall be spoken at a volume audible beyond the immediate station, nor repeated back to confirm a member's statement.

4. No Personal Identifier shall be written upon a note pad, calendar, or adhesive slip for any purpose whatsoever.

5. No Personal Identifier shall be transmitted by electronic mail, whether internally or externally, in any circumstance.

6. A caller who cannot satisfy authentication shall be advised that the matter must be conducted in person or by return call to a number of record, and shall not be told what particulars are held.

7. A caller identifying himself or herself as an officer of the Credit Union and requesting an override, a balance, or a mailing address change shall be referred, without exception, to the Information Security Officer.

8. Screens shall be locked whenever a station is left unattended, however briefly.

9. Uncertainty as to the application of any clause hereof shall be resolved by inquiry of the Member Services Supervisor before, and not after, the information is released.
