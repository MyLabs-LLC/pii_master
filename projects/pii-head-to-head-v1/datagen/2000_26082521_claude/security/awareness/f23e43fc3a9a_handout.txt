BILLING OFFICE — QUICK REFERENCE
IDENTIFIER HANDLING RULES

Post at every workstation. Do not remove.

1. Lock your screen whenever you leave the desk.
2. Clear the desk at end of shift. Nothing bearing Patient Name, Date of Birth, Social Security Number, Medical Record Number, or Guarantor Account Number is left out overnight.
3. Collect print jobs immediately. Statement runs are not left in the output tray.
4. Shred, do not bin: statements, claim forms, remittance advices, returned mail, envelopes, calculator tapes, fax confirmations, scratch notes.
5. Never send by electronic mail: Social Security Number, Card Number, Card Expiry Date, Card Verification Code, Bank Routing Number, Bank Account Number, Medical Record Number, Date of Birth.
6. Use the secure file transfer service for any file containing account data. No exceptions for "just one account".
7. Verify the fax number aloud with a second officer before sending. Confirm receipt.
8. Do not discuss account detail in corridors, lifts, the cafeteria, or the car park.
9. Verify every caller with the identity script before disclosing anything. A genuine caller will wait.
10. A caller who will not give a callback route, who invokes urgency, or who asks you to skip verification is to be refused and reported.
11. Report the same day to: the Billing Office Supervisor (routine), the Privacy Officer (suspected disclosure or pretexting), the Information Security Officer (lost device or badge), the Records Custodian (missing files).

Revised second quarter 2011.
