NOTES

  1.  The verification set consists of these field labels: Patient Name as
      filed, Date of Birth, Account Number, Guarantor Name, Date of Service,
      and Insurance Plan Name. The caller supplies each element unprompted.
      Staff do not read an element aloud in order to have it confirmed.

  2.  Two failed elements end the inquiry. The clerk states only that the
      account cannot be discussed by telephone and offers the written request
      procedure. The clerk does not say which element failed.

  3.  Caller identification display is not a verification element. Neither is
      knowledge of the patient name alone, a service date alone, or the
      balance shown on a recent statement.

  4.  Every refused or terminated call is entered on the Suspicious Inquiry
      Log at the end of the shift and forwarded by the Patient Accounts
      Supervisor to the Privacy Officer within one business day.

  5.  No exception is granted at the request of a caller. Release outside the
      standard procedure is authorized only by the Privacy Officer or, in that
      officer's absence, the Revenue Cycle Director.
