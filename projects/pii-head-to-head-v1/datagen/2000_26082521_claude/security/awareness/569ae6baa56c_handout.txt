COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
RECORDS MANAGEMENT UNIT
DESTRUCTION OF IDENTIFIABLE MATERIAL — DESK GUIDE

ARTICLE I. MATERIAL REQUIRING DESTRUCTION

  1. Any page, file, or medium bearing one or more of the fields
     below is destroyed by cross-cut shredder or locked console.
     Nothing bearing these fields enters recycling or waste:

     > Client Name
     > Social Security Number
     > Date of Birth
     > Home Address / Mailing Address
     > Home Telephone Number
     > Case Number / Chart Number
     > Medicaid Identification Number
     > Insurance Policy Number / Group Number
     > Immunization Registry Number
     > Lab Accession Number
     > Diagnosis Code / Test Result

  2. The rule applies equally to drafts, duplicates, and material
     that appears illegible or partially printed.

ARTICLE II. TIMING

  3. Desk-level material is cleared at the close of each business
     day. Desks are not left bearing identifiable paper overnight.

  4. Filed records are destroyed only on the authority of the
     Records Custodian and only after the retention period stated
     in the County schedule has expired. Staff do not destroy filed
     records on their own initiative, and do not retain them beyond
     schedule to "be safe".

ARTICLE III. MEDIA AND EQUIPMENT

  5. Diskettes, backup tapes, compact discs, and hard drives are
     not shredded on site. They are logged and surrendered to the
     Information Systems Manager for destruction.

  6. Photocopiers and facsimile machines are checked for abandoned
     originals at the close of business by the unit supervisor.

ARTICLE IV. CONSOLES

  7. Consoles are kept locked, are not propped open, and are not
     emptied by staff. A full console is reported to the Records
     Custodian, not left to overflow.

  8. Certificates of destruction issued by the contracted vendor
     are forwarded to the Records Custodian for retention.

ARTICLE V. ENQUIRIES

  9. Questions on retention periods, console servicing, or file
     retrieval: the Records Custodian.

 10. Questions on disclosure, breach, or client complaint: the
     Privacy Officer.
