INTERNAL CONTROL NOTICE TC-3 (2016)
TRANSMISSION OF IDENTIFIERS: PERMITTED AND PROHIBITED CHANNELS

     CHANNEL                        R1 FIELDS    R2 FIELDS    LOGGED
     Internal email ...........     PROHIBITED   PROHIBITED   n/a
     External email ...........     PROHIBITED   PROHIBITED   n/a

     Instant message ..........     PROHIBITED   PROHIBITED   n/a

3.2  FIELDS DESIGNATED R1

     Social Security Number; Taxpayer Identification Number; Date
     of Birth; Bank Routing and Account Number; Payment Card
     Number; Work Authorization Number; Background Check
     Reference Number; Medical Restriction Note.

3.3  CLAUSES

     3.3.1  A password applied to an attachment does not change
            the classification of the channel. The prohibition in
            3.1 stands.

     3.3.2  Partial masking is not a permitted workaround. Final
            four digits of an account or identification number
            remain R1 and are not transmitted by email.

     3.3.3  Screenshots, photographs of a screen, and photographs
            of a printed record inherit the classification of the
            fields shown.

     3.3.4  Where a client insists on email delivery, the request
            is referred to the Privacy Officer. Branch staff do
            not grant exceptions.

     3.3.5  A misdirected transmission is reported to the
            Information Security Lead within one business day.
            Do not attempt recall and do not forward the message
            to a third party as evidence.

3.4  REVIEW

     This notice is reviewed annually by the Compliance
     Coordinator. Retention and disposal codes are held
     separately in RC-114.
