SECTION B - NEVER SENT BY ELECTRONIC MAIL

    Social Security Number
    Medicare or Medicaid Identifier
    Health Plan Member Identifier
    Medical Record Number
    Date of Birth in combination with Patient Name
    Guarantor Home Address
    Payment Card Number, expiration, or verification code
    Bank Routing and Account Number
    Driver License or State Identification Number
    Diagnosis or procedure description tied to an account
    A scanned image of any of the above

The prohibition covers the subject line, the message body, the file name, and
every attachment. It applies equally to a message addressed to a co-worker,
since an internal message may be forwarded outside the domain without the
sender's knowledge. A request that arrives by e-mail is answered by telephone
or by letter, and the incoming message is not quoted back in the reply.

workstation, so that coverage is unbroken during an absence.
