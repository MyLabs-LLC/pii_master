TRANSIT AUTHORITY OF THE COUNTY
DIVISION OF CUSTOMER RECORDS AND FARE ADMINISTRATION

STAFF GUIDANCE HANDOUT No. 12
SUBJECT: RECOGNITION OF PRETEXTING SOLICITATIONS AND THE
         SAFEGUARDING OF PATRON AND EMPLOYEE IDENTIFIERS

Issued: Second Quarter 2016
Classification: Internal Use, All Divisions and Depots

1. SCOPE. This handout applies, without exception, exemption or
   waiver, to all permanent, provisional, seasonal and contracted
   personnel who in the ordinary course of their assigned duties may
   come into possession of, or be afforded sight of, a record bearing
   a personal identifier of a patron, an applicant, a pensioner, a
   claimant or a fellow employee.

2. DEFINITION. "Pretexting" means the procurement, or the attempted
   procurement, of a personal identifier by a party who assumes a
   false identity, a false authority, or a false urgency, for the
   purpose of inducing disclosure by an employee acting in apparent
   good faith. The offence is complete upon the attempt; no actual
   disclosure need occur.

3. INDICATORS. Personnel shall treat the concurrence of any two or
   more of the following indicators as conclusive grounds for the
   immediate termination of the contact and its referral upward.

  OBSERVED DURING TELEPHONE CONTACT          | WEIGHT | REQUIRED ACTION
  -------------------------------------------+--------+------------------
  Caller refuses a callback to a listed line | High   | Terminate call
  Caller asserts unverifiable internal rank  | High   | Terminate call
  Caller invokes urgency or a fixed deadline | Medium | Escalate at once
  Caller seeks a full identifier by voice    | High   | Terminate call
  Caller objects to the taking of notes      | Medium | Escalate at once
  Caller supplies a partial identifier       | High   | Terminate call
  Caller requests transfer to a named desk   | Medium | Escalate at once

4. PROHIBITED TRANSMISSION. Under no circumstance shall any employee
   place into the body of an electronic mail message, into the subject
   line thereof, or into any attachment thereto, a Social Security
   Number, a Taxpayer Identification Number, a Date of Birth, a Bank
   Account Number, a Bank Routing Number, a Payment Card Primary
   Account Number, a Payment Card Verification Value, a Driver Licence
   Number, a Medicare Claim Number, a Reduced-Fare Certificate Number,
   a Payroll File Number, or the Residential Address of any patron or
   employee. This prohibition subsists notwithstanding any instruction
   to the contrary given by a superior, and any employee in receipt of
   such an instruction shall decline it and report it.

5. DESTRUCTION. Working papers bearing any field named in clause 4
   shall be deposited, at the conclusion of the shift in which they
   were created, into a locked destruction receptacle. Such papers
   shall not be placed in general waste, in recycling containers, or
   in unattended trays, and shall not be removed from Authority
   premises for any purpose.

6. REFERRAL BY ROLE. Suspected pretexting shall be reported to the
   Station Supervisor on Duty, who shall record the matter and refer
   it without delay to the Data Protection Coordinator. Where an
   identifier is believed to have been disclosed, the Data Protection
   Coordinator shall additionally notify the Privacy Officer and the
   Chief Information Security Officer within the same working day.
