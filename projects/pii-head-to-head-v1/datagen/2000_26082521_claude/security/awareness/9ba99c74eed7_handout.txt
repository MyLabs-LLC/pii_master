MEMORANDUM OF GUIDANCE

TO:      All Staff and Contract Associates
FROM:    The Office of the Privacy Officer
RE:      Minimum Standards for the Protection of Personal Identifiers
YEAR:    2021

    ARTICLE ONE — DESTRUCTION
        Sec. 1.01. All paper bearing a Personal Identifier shall be destroyed by cross-cut
            shredding or deposited in a locked shred console pending vendor collection.
        Sec. 1.02. The obligation extends to, and is not limited by, the following: application
            forms; identity document photocopies; tax withholding worksheets; direct deposit
            authorizations; timecards; interview and reference notes; assignment rosters; and any
            page bearing the field labels Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Driver's License
            Number, Bank Routing Number, Account Number, or Home Address.
        Sec. 1.03. No such material shall be retained beyond the applicable retention period as
            published by the Records Custodian, nor discarded before it.

    ARTICLE TWO — TRANSMISSION
        Sec. 2.01. Electronic mail is not an approved channel for any Personal Identifier and shall
            not be used for that purpose under any circumstance, internal or external.
        Sec. 2.02. The secure document portal is the sole approved channel. Its use shall be logged.
        Sec. 2.03. Personal email accounts, personal devices, personal cloud storage, and
            unencrypted removable media are prohibited without qualification.

    ARTICLE THREE — PRETEXTING
        Sec. 3.01. A pretexting call is one in which the caller adopts a false identity or invents a
            false circumstance to obtain information.
        Sec. 3.02. Indicia include manufactured urgency; unverifiable claims of authority; requests
            to bypass verification; requests to read back data purportedly already held; and requests
            to alter a payment or address record by telephone.
        Sec. 3.03. Staff shall neither confirm nor deny the existence of any record, shall end the
            call, and shall document the request as made.

    ARTICLE FOUR — REPORTING
        Sec. 4.01. First report: the Branch Operations Manager.
        Sec. 4.02. Suspected disclosure: the Privacy Officer, immediately and directly.
        Sec. 4.03. Technical indicators: the Information Security Lead.
        Sec. 4.04. Records, retention, and destruction certificates: the Records Custodian.
        Sec. 4.05. No independent inquiry shall be undertaken by any staff member absent the
            written direction of the Privacy Officer.
