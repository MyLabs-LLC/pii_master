INFORMATION HANDLING BULLETIN
Issued by the Information Security Coordinator
Reference IS-04-11 / Posting required at all dispatch windows and break rooms

Effective immediately, no employee is to read out, fax, key into an Internet mail message, or otherwise release a Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Driver's License Number, Employee Number, or Bank Account Number to any caller, visitor, or correspondent who is not identified in the internal directory by role title. This restriction applies without exception to callers who identify themselves as insurance adjusters, background screening vendors, factoring companies, lease finance representatives, or law enforcement. Requests from those parties are routed unanswered to the Compliance Officer.

Three points of practice:

First, paper. Any sheet bearing the field labels above is destroyed by cross-cut shredder or placed in the locked destruction console. This includes the discard stack at the fax machine and the printer misfeed tray, which are checked at end of shift by the closing supervisor.

Second, mail. Company electronic mail crosses the public Internet unprotected. Treat every message as though it were a postcard read by a stranger. Personnel and payroll extracts move by the secure transfer host or courier pouch only.

Third, telephone. A caller who supplies part of an identifier and asks you to supply or confirm the remainder is running a pretext. So is a caller who cannot state their own role title, who insists a manager has already approved the release, or who asks that material be sent to a number or address they read out over the line. End the collection of details, take the caller's stated employer and reason, and advise that a return call will be placed to a listed number.

Report any such contact the same day to the Information Security Coordinator, with a copy to the Records Custodian. Failure to report is itself a reportable event.
