NOTICE TO STUDENTS, ALUMNI, AND OTHER RECORD HOLDERS

The Office of the Registrar limits what it will discuss and what it will accept over open channels, and this notice explains those limits so that a request is not delayed.

Requests for a transcript, an enrollment verification, or a change of legal name are accepted in person at the service counter, by mail, or through the secure online request form. They are not accepted by electronic mail, because a message sent to this office cannot be authenticated and cannot be protected in transit. Do not include a Social Security Number, a Date of Birth, a Bank Account Number, or a scanned identification document in any message to this office; a request containing those items is deleted without action and must be resubmitted through an accepted channel.

Staff at this counter will not confirm a Social Security Number or a Date of Birth that a caller reads aloud, and will not release information to a parent, spouse, employer, or prospective landlord without a signed consent on file. This applies even where the caller already appears to know the details in question.

If someone contacts you claiming to represent this office and asks for identifiers, do not supply them. Report the contact to the Records Custodian, who will confirm whether the request originated here.
