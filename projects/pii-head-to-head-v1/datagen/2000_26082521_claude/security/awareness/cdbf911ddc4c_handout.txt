OFFICE OF THE UNIVERSITY REGISTRAR
DIVISION OF ACADEMIC RECORDS AND ENROLLMENT SERVICES

MEMORANDUM

TO:      All Personnel Assigned to Records Custody, Transcript Production,
         Enrollment Verification, and Public Counter Duty
FROM:    The University Registrar
DATE:    October 6, 1998
RE:      Safeguarding of Personally Identifiable Information; Mandatory
         Procedures Governing Destruction, Transmission, and Caller
         Verification

I. AUTHORITY AND APPLICABILITY

1.01  This memorandum is promulgated under the authority vested in the
      University Registrar as designated custodian of education records
      pursuant to the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act of 1974, as
      amended (20 U.S.C. Sec. 1232g), the implementing regulations codified
      at 34 C.F.R. Part 99, and the records retention and disposition
      schedule adopted by the Board of Regents.

1.02  The procedures set forth herein are mandatory and are not to be
      construed as advisory. Deviation from any provision of Sections III
      through V requires the prior written authorization of the Associate
      Registrar for Compliance. Failure to observe these provisions may
      constitute grounds for corrective action under the applicable
      personnel policy, without prejudice to any liability arising under
      state or federal law.

II. IDENTIFIERS SUBJECT TO PROTECTION

2.01  For purposes of this memorandum, "personal identifier" includes any
      of the following data elements, whether appearing singly or in
      combination, in paper, microform, or machine-readable form:

         Full Legal Name
         Former or Maiden Name
         Social Security Number
         Student Identification Number
         Date and Place of Birth
         Permanent Mailing Address; Local Mailing Address
         Telephone Number of Record
         Name of Parent or Legal Guardian
         Financial Aid Award Amount; Loan Account Number
         Bank Routing Number and Deposit Account Number
         Grade Point Average; Individual Course Grades
         Disciplinary Action Code
         Visa Classification and Immigration Status Code
         Emergency Contact Name and Relationship

2.02  The designation of an item as directory information does not exempt
      that item from Section III where it appears on the same page as any
      non-directory element enumerated above.

III. DESTRUCTION OF PAPER AND MICROFORM MEDIA

3.01  The following shall be destroyed by cross-cut shredder and by no
      other means: transcript stock spoiled in printing; alignment and test
      impressions; one-time carbon ribbons and printer ribbons removed from
      service; grade rosters superseded by a subsequent posting; mailing
      label sheets discarded in whole or in part; adding-machine tape
      bearing account references; obsolete microfiche; and working notes of
      any description bearing an element listed at 2.01.

3.02  Deposit of any item described at 3.01 into a wastebasket, recycling
      container, corridor bin, or loading-dock receptacle is prohibited
      absolutely.

3.03  Each destruction event shall be witnessed and entered in the
      Certificate of Destruction log under the columns provided: Date of
      Destruction; Record Series; Volume in Linear Inches; Method of
      Destruction; Custodian Initials; Witness Initials.

IV. ELECTRONIC MAIL

4.01  Electronic mail traverses unattended relay hosts, is retained on
      backup media beyond the control of this Office, and may be forwarded
      or printed without the knowledge of the sender. No expectation of
      confidentiality attaches to it.

4.02  The following shall under no circumstances be placed in the body,
      subject line, or attachment of an electronic mail message: Social
      Security Number; Date of Birth; Bank Routing or Deposit Account
      Number; Loan Account Number; Individual Course Grades; Disciplinary
      Action Code. Transmission to any address outside the University
      domain is prohibited irrespective of content.

V. TELEPHONE PRETEXTING

5.01  A pretexting caller obtains identifiers by impersonation. Indicators
      include: assertion of urgency; claimed authority as an employee of
      another department who "cannot get to a terminal"; refusal to accept
      a return call to a published extension; possession of one identifier
      offered as proof in order to elicit a second; and objection to the
      standard verification script.

5.02  Staff shall terminate the call, decline to confirm or deny any
      element, and report the contact the same business day.

VI. INTERNAL REFERRAL

      Verification scripts and release forms:  Assistant Registrar for
      Records. Suspected pretexting or unauthorized disclosure:  Associate
      Registrar for Compliance, with concurrent notice to the University
      Privacy Officer. Shredder service and destruction logs:  Records
      Custodian. Subpoenas and court orders:  refer without response to the
      Office of University Counsel.
