SECTION 4.  RECOGNITION OF A PRETEXTING CALL

+-------+---------------------------------------------------------+
| CL.   | INDICATOR AND REQUIRED RESPONSE                         |
+-------+---------------------------------------------------------+
| 4.01  | Caller declines to give a role title or division.       |
| 4.02  | Caller asserts urgency and discourages callback.        |
| 4.03  | Caller offers partial data and asks for the rest.       |
| 4.04  | Caller claims authority from an unnamed superior.       |
| 4.05  | Request arrives outside published counter hours.        |
| 4.06  | Terminate, record the request, notify a supervisor.     |
