GUIDANCE NOTE 2016-11
RECOGNISING AND REPORTING PRETEXTING
Issued by the Privacy Officer for circulation to all divisions

    § 1.  Nature of the risk

          1.1  Pretexting is the practice of assuming a false identity in
               order to obtain personal identifiers from an employee who
               believes the request to be legitimate. It succeeds through
               courtesy and haste, not through technical skill.

    § 2.  Common pretexts encountered by this Department

          2.1  A caller representing themself as a landlord seeking the
               Social Security Number or Date of Birth of a tenant.
          2.2  A caller representing themself as a field crew supervisor
               requesting the Service Address list for a route.
          2.3  A caller representing themself as an employee of the finance
               office requesting Bank Routing Number and Bank Account Number
               "to correct a failed draft".
          2.4  A caller representing themself as a law enforcement officer
               and declining to provide a call-back number.
          2.5  A caller representing themself as a customer, unable to answer
               the challenge questions, offering alternative proof.

    § 3.  Behavioural indicators

          3.1  Manufactured urgency; a deadline that leaves no time to verify.
          3.2  Flattery, sympathy, or intimidation directed at the employee.
          3.3  Background noise suggesting a call centre.
          3.4  A request phrased as confirmation rather than disclosure.
          3.5  Escalation to a demand for a supervisor when refused.

    § 4.  Required response

          4.1  Disclose nothing. Confirm nothing. Deny nothing that would
               itself reveal a stored value.
          4.2  Offer to return the call to the number of record only.
          4.3  Record the time of the call, the claimed identity, and the
               information sought. Do not record any identifier the caller
               volunteered.
          4.4  Terminate the call without argument.

    § 5.  Reporting

          5.1  Report the same business day to the Records Custodian.
          5.2  Where the caller claimed internal authority, report also to
               the Information Security Manager.
          5.3  Where the caller claimed law enforcement authority, refer the
               matter to the Privacy Officer, who alone may respond.
          5.4  No employee shall be subject to criticism for refusing a
               request that later proves genuine.
