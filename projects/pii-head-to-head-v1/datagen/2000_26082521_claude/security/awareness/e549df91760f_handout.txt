CORPORATE POLICY 7.4 -- PROTECTION OF PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS
Pharmacy and Front-End Operations Manual
Revision 3, issued First Quarter 2004
Supersedes Revision 2. Applies to all store colleagues, pharmacy staff, district personnel, and temporary labour.

1. SCOPE AND AUTHORITY

1.1 This clause governs the handling, transmission, retention, and destruction of Personal Identifiers held by the Company in any medium, including paper, microfiche, magnetic media, and terminal display.

1.2 Compliance is a condition of continued access to the pharmacy management system. Access rights are granted by the Information Security Officer and revoked by the same office.

2. DEFINITION OF PERSONAL IDENTIFIER

2.1 A Personal Identifier is any data element populating one or more of the following fields, alone or in combination:

     Patient Surname
     Patient Given Name
     Date of Birth
     Social Security Number
     Health Plan Member Identification Number
     Group Number
     Prescription Number
     Prescriber DEA Registration Number
     Residential Street Address
     Home Telephone Number
     Payment Card Account Number
     Card Expiration Date
     Signature Log Entry

2.2 The field names enumerated at 2.1 are not themselves restricted. The values entered against them are restricted without exception.

3. DESTRUCTION REQUIREMENTS

3.1 The following must be destroyed by cross-cut shredder at the point of generation and must not be placed in general waste, recycling, or cardboard bale:

     3.1.1 Prescription labels, label overruns, and misprinted label stock.
     3.1.2 Patient counselling notes and hard-copy medication profiles.
     3.1.3 Third-party rejection slips and adjudication printouts.
     3.1.4 Signature log sheets past retention.
     3.1.5 Register tape containing truncated or untruncated card data.
     3.1.6 Fax cover sheets and confirmation reports.
     3.1.7 Pharmacy voicemail transcription pads.

3.2 Shredder waste is bagged, sealed, and retained in the locked stockroom cage pending collection by the contracted destruction vendor.

3.3 Magnetic media, including diskettes and backup cartridges, is not shredded on site. It is logged on the Media Transfer Register and forwarded to the Records Custodian for degaussing.

4. ELECTRONIC MAIL RESTRICTIONS

4.1 The Company electronic mail system transmits in clear text across public network segments and is not an approved channel for Personal Identifiers.

4.2 The following must never be sent by electronic mail, whether in the message body, the subject line, or an attached file:

     4.2.1 Any value populating a field listed at 2.1.
     4.2.2 Scanned or photographed prescription hard copy.
     4.2.3 Spreadsheets extracted from the dispensing system without prior de-identification.
     4.2.4 System passwords, terminal identifiers, or override codes.

4.3 Password protection applied within a word processing or spreadsheet application does not constitute encryption and does not satisfy this clause.

4.4 Approved alternatives are the internal encrypted file transfer facility, the dedicated pharmacy fax line to a confirmed recipient, and interoffice courier pouch under seal.

5. PRETEXT CONTACT

5.1 A pretext contact is an attempt to obtain Personal Identifiers by assuming a false identity or false authority.

5.2 Colleagues shall terminate any telephone contact exhibiting the indicators set out in Procedure PS-4 and shall report the contact the same business day.

6. INTERNAL CONTACTS

6.1 Suspected pretext contact: the Store Manager, and thereafter the District Loss Prevention Manager.

6.2 Suspected disclosure of Personal Identifiers: the Privacy Official.

6.3 Records retention and media destruction: the Records Custodian.

6.4 System access, password reset, and terminal security: the Information Security Officer.

6.5 Patient complaint concerning privacy practices: the Pharmacist-in-Charge, who shall log the matter and refer it to the Regional Compliance Coordinator.
