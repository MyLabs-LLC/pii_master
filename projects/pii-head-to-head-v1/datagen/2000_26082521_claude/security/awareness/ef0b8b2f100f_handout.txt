BRANCH ADDENDUM — PRETEXTING INDICATORS AND CALLBACK PROCEDURE

Pretexting calls directed at branch staff generally follow one of three patterns. In the first, the caller represents themselves as the member and supplies an identifier obtained from a data breach or from discarded documentation, then requests a change to the address of record, the mailing of a replacement card, or the release of a balance. In the second, the caller represents themselves as a member of staff at another branch or at a central function and requests assistance with a transaction said to be time-critical, relying on collegial deference to bypass verification. In the third, the caller represents themselves as an external authority — a card network, a law enforcement agency, or an examiner — and asserts that verification cannot be performed for reasons of confidentiality. All three patterns share an aversion to a callback, and the callback is accordingly the control on which staff are to rely.

Where a call is terminated for callback, the number used is drawn from the core system record or from the published internal directory, never from a number supplied by the caller. The following entry is completed and forwarded to the Fraud Investigations Coordinator:

  Date of Call | Time Received | Branch | Receiving Staff Role | Claimed Identity of Caller | Stated Purpose | Identifiers Requested | Identifiers Disclosed | Verification Outcome | Callback Number Source | Disposition | Escalated To

The column headed Identifiers Disclosed is expected to record none in the ordinary case; any other entry initiates a review by the Information Security Officer. Completed entries are retained by the Member Services Supervisor and summarised monthly for the Chief Risk Officer as part of the reporting required under the Identity Theft Prevention Program.
