CORRIDOR STAFFING SERVICES
IDENTITY INFORMATION PROTECTION — BRANCH AND FIELD STAFF HANDOUT
Issue 2 · Effective first quarter 2004 · Retain in the Branch Operations Binder

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
1.1 This handout states the minimum handling rules for personal identifiers held by the agency in the course of recruiting, placing, and paying temporary and contract personnel.
1.2 It applies to all branch staff, field recruiters, on-site coordinators, and payroll clerks, whether working at a branch office, at a client site, or from a home office.
1.3 "Personal identifier" means any data element recorded under the following field labels: Social Security Number; Date of Birth; Driver's License Number; State ID Number; Alien Registration Number; Bank Routing Number; Bank Account Number; Home Address; Home Telephone; Mother's Maiden Name; Emergency Contact.

2. DOCUMENTS TO BE SHREDDED
2.1 Cross-cut shredding is required — strip-cut shredders are withdrawn from service as of this issue.
2.2 Shred, at the close of each business day, all of the following once they are no longer in active use:
     (a) working copies of the Employment Application and Skills Sheet;
     (b) photocopies of I-9 supporting documents, including passport and licence copies;
     (c) superseded W-4 and state withholding forms;
     (d) background screening and drug screen result printouts;
     (e) payroll registers, check stubs, and direct deposit authorisation forms;
     (f) fax cover sheets and transmission confirmations bearing applicant data;
     (g) handwritten notes, message slips, and interview scratch pads;
     (h) mailing labels, envelope windows, and returned mail.
2.3 Material awaiting shredding is held in the locked destruction console only. It is not placed in recycling bins, under desks, or on top of the console.
2.4 Diskettes, CD-R media, and printer ribbons carrying applicant data are surrendered to the Records Custodian for physical destruction. They are not shredded at the branch.

3. TRANSMISSION RESTRICTIONS
3.1 The following are never sent by electronic mail, whether in the message body or as an attachment: Social Security Number; Date of Birth; Bank Routing Number; Bank Account Number; background screening results; a scan or image of any I-9 supporting document.
3.2 An applicant or associate file is not carried on a laptop hard drive, diskette, or USB memory device without written release from the Branch Manager.
3.3 Where a client requires identifier data, it is sent through the payroll interface or by bonded courier. The Account Manager arranges the transfer; branch staff do not improvise a method.
3.4 Faxes containing identifier data are sent only after telephone confirmation that a person is standing at the receiving machine.

4. RECOGNISING A PRETEXTING CALL
4.1 A pretexting call is an attempt to obtain identifier data by posing as someone entitled to it — a client contact, a bank officer, a government inspector, an associate, or an employee of this agency.
4.2 Treat as suspicious any caller who: refuses to be called back on a number you look up yourself; cites urgency, an audit, or a payroll deadline; already knows part of a record and asks you to confirm the rest; claims to be from head office but is unknown to the branch; or becomes hostile when asked to verify.
4.3 Confirming a detail the caller supplies is a disclosure. Do not confirm, correct, or complete it.
4.4 End the call politely, record the request, and report it the same day.

5. INTERNAL CONTACTS BY ROLE
5.1 Day-to-day questions on filing, retention, and destruction — the Records Custodian.
5.2 Suspected pretexting, lost media, or missing files — the Branch Manager, and then the Privacy Officer.
5.3 Client requests for identifier data — the Account Manager.
5.4 System access, passwords, and terminal lockouts — the Systems Administrator.
5.5 Refusal by a caller to accept these procedures — refer to the Compliance Coordinator. Branch staff do not grant exceptions.
