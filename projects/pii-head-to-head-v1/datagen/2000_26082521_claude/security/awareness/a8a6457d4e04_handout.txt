INFORMATION PRACTICES CHECKLIST
Title and Escrow Operations / Issued under the authority of the Information Security Coordinator
Employee acknowledgement required. Return signed copy to the Human Resources Administrator.

SECTION A — DESTRUCTION
[ ] I place the following in the locked shred console, never in trash or recycling:
    [ ] Superseded drafts of any document bearing the fields Buyer Name, Seller Name, Social Security Number, Date of Birth, or Loan Number
    [ ] Identification photocopies (Driver's License Number, Passport Number, State Identification Number)
    [ ] Wire request forms and voided checks (Bank Routing Number, Account Number)
    [ ] Credit reports, employment verifications, and pages bearing Employer or Income fields
    [ ] Fax cover sheets, transmission reports, and telephone message slips
    [ ] Printer misfeeds, test pages, and abandoned output at the shared printer
[ ] I do not remove shred material from the console for reuse as scratch paper.
[ ] I surrender diskettes, tapes, and hard-copy microfiche to the Records Custodian. I do not erase or discard them myself.

SECTION B — ELECTRONIC MAIL
[ ] I treat Internet mail as a postcard.
[ ] I do not place in any message, attachment, or subject line: Social Security Number; Taxpayer Identification Number; Date of Birth; Mother's Maiden Name; Driver's License Number; Bank Routing Number; Account Number; wire instruction detail; credit report content.
[ ] I do not reply to an outside request for identifiers by electronic mail, including a request that appears to come from a lender or a party to the transaction.
[ ] I use interoffice courier, sealed and marked CONFIDENTIAL, or facsimile to an attended machine confirmed in advance.
[ ] I do not use a home computer, personal modem account, or online service account for company files.

SECTION C — TELEPHONE PRETEXT
[ ] I regard the following as warning signs:
    [ ] Same-day urgency and pressure to bypass the file's authorised parties
    [ ] Caller cannot state their branch, department, or supervising officer by title
    [ ] Caller asks me to confirm or complete a partial identifier
    [ ] Caller requests the entire file "for verification" or "for the auditor"
    [ ] Caller asks about staffing, absences, or approval limits
    [ ] Refusal to accept a call-back to the number of record
[ ] On suspicion I disclose nothing, do not confirm the file exists, end the call without transferring it, and prepare a written memorandum the same day.

SECTION D — REPORTING
[ ] Immediate verbal notice: the Branch Manager
[ ] Written memorandum within one business day: the Information Security Coordinator
[ ] File custody and destruction certificates: the Records Custodian
[ ] Legal process, subpoenas, government demands: the Underwriting Counsel
[ ] Interpretation of this checklist: the Compliance Officer
