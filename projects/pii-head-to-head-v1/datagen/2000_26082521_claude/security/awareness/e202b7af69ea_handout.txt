OFFICE OF THE UNIVERSITY REGISTRAR
PUBLIC SERVICE COUNTER -- STANDING NOTICE SN-11
IDENTIFIER PROTECTION: THE FOUR RULES

+-----+--------------------+------------------------------------------+
| No. | Subject            | Standing Requirement                     |
+-----+--------------------+------------------------------------------+
| 1   | Shredding          | Cross-cut shred; never a recycling bin.  |
| 2   | Electronic mail    | No SSN, Date of Birth, or Account No.    |
| 3   | Telephone callers  | Call back before releasing any field.    |
| 4   | Escalation         | Report at once to the Records Custodian. |
+-----+--------------------+------------------------------------------+

1. The four rules are not discretionary and are not waived for a caller
   asserting urgency, seniority, or a family relationship.
2. Fields treated as protected at this counter are: Social Security Number,
   Student Identification Number, Date of Birth, Mother's Maiden Name, Bank
   Account Number, and Personal Identification Number.
3. A caller who supplies part of a protected field and invites staff to supply
   the remainder is treated as a pretexting attempt, without exception.
4. Staff neither confirm nor deny that a record exists until identity is
   established by callback to the listing in the University directory.
5. Requests arriving by electronic mail are answered by campus mail or in
   person. No protected field is placed in the reply.
6. Every refused or suspicious contact is entered on the Suspicious Contact Log
   held at the counter and reported before the close of business.
7. Questions on this notice are directed to the Registrar Security Coordinator.

Posted by direction of the University Registrar.
