GUIDANCE BULLETIN 99-11
ISSUED:  April 12, 1999
SUPERSEDES:  Guidance Bulletin 97-04
TO:  Unit Supervisors, Records Custodians, and Their Designees
SUBJECT:  Disposition of Media Bearing Personal Identifiers

PART A -- STATEMENT OF PURPOSE

It having come to the attention of this Office that materials bearing
personally identifiable information have been recovered from unsecured
receptacles within the building, all supervisors are directed to review the
disposition table appearing at Part B with each employee under their
direction and to record that review in the unit training file. The table
governs notwithstanding any contrary local practice of longer standing.

PART C -- ELECTRONIC TRANSMISSION

No data element appearing in the following list shall be transmitted by
electronic mail under any circumstance, nor shall it be entered in a message
subject line, which is recorded in system logs irrespective of message
deletion:  Social Security Number; Date of Birth; Loan Account Number; Bank
Routing Number; Deposit Account Number; Disciplinary Action Code; Individual
Course Grades. Where transmission between offices is unavoidable, the sealed
inter-campus courier envelope shall be used, addressed to a role title and
not to an individual, and logged out under the columns Date Dispatched,
Record Series, Receiving Office, and Dispatching Initials.

PART D -- CALLER VERIFICATION

Any caller who asserts urgency, who represents that a supervisor has
authorized an exception, who offers a partial identifier in the expectation
that staff will complete it, or who resists a return call to a published
extension, shall be treated as a pretexting attempt. Staff shall neither
confirm nor deny the accuracy of any element recited by such a caller. The
contact shall be reduced to writing and forwarded the same business day.

PART E -- REFERRAL

Interpretation of Part B:  Records Custodian. Interpretation of Parts C and
D, and all reports of suspected pretexting:  Associate Registrar for
Compliance. Requests received from law enforcement or accompanied by legal
process:  Office of University Counsel, without response of any kind.
