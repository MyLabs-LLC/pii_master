PATIENT FINANCIAL SERVICES
DEPARTMENTAL DIRECTIVE PFS-11.4
SAFEGUARDING OF PATIENT AND GUARANTOR IDENTIFIERS

Issued: March 2004. Supersedes PFS-11.3 (August 2002).
Applies to: pre-registration, insurance verification, coding, billing, cashiering, collections, and all temporary or agency personnel assigned to the department.

1.0 PURPOSE
1.1 To state the minimum handling standard for identifiers held by this department under the HIPAA Privacy Rule and the Fair and Accurate Credit Transactions Act.
1.2 This directive is a condition of continued system access. Non-compliance is a disciplinary matter.

2.0 PROTECTED FIELDS
2.1 The following fields, alone or in combination, are treated as protected regardless of the medium on which they appear:

     Patient Name
     Date of Birth
     Social Security Number
     Medical Record Number
     Account Number
     Guarantor Name
     Guarantor Mailing Address
     Subscriber Identification Number
     Group Number
     Policy Number
     Payment Card Number
     Card Expiration Date
     Card Verification Value
     Bank Routing Number
     Checking Account Number
     Employer Name
     Driver License Number
     Diagnosis and Procedure Codes

2.2 Screen prints, worklists, aging reports, remittance advices, and handwritten notes containing any field at 2.1 fall within this directive.

3.0 DESTRUCTION
3.1 All paper bearing a field listed at 2.1 shall be deposited in a locked destruction console. Cross-cut shredding is required; strip-cut shredders are withdrawn from service.
3.2 Prohibited: placing protected paper in desk-side wastebaskets, recycling bins, general refuse, or unattended carts.
3.3 Consoles shall not be overfilled. A full console is reported to the Records Custodian for unscheduled collection.
3.4 Diskettes, CD-R media, carbon interleaves, and used ribbon cartridges are surrendered to the Records Custodian. Staff shall not attempt disposal by other means.
3.5 Retention periods are set in the departmental retention schedule. Destruction before the stated period is prohibited.

4.0 ELECTRONIC MAIL AND FACSIMILE
4.1 No field listed at 2.1 shall be entered in the body, subject line, or attachment of an electronic mail message, whether internal or external.
4.2 Account references in mail shall be by internal work-queue reference only.
4.3 Instant messaging, personal web-mail, and home computers are not approved for departmental work of any kind.
4.4 Facsimile transmission requires the approved cover sheet, pre-confirmed destination, and verbal confirmation of receipt. Programmed speed-dial entries shall be verified quarterly.

5.0 TELEPHONE INQUIRIES
5.1 Staff shall not release information to an inbound caller on the strength of the caller's stated identity alone.
5.2 Indicators of a pretexting attempt include: urgency or claimed supervisory authority; refusal to give a callback number; a callback number not matching the payer or agency of record; requests to "verify" a Social Security Number already held by the caller; background call-center noise combined with an unfamiliar payer name; and requests for a full account listing rather than a single account.
5.3 On any indicator at 5.2, the call is terminated courteously, a callback is placed to the number of record, and the incident is logged.

6.0 REPORTING
6.1 Suspected pretexting, lost paper, or misdirected transmission is reported the same shift to the Patient Financial Services Manager.
6.2 The Privacy Officer is notified by the Manager in all cases. Staff may notify the Privacy Officer directly.
6.3 Media and destruction questions: the Records Custodian.
6.4 Anonymous reports may be made to the Compliance Hotline extension listed in the departmental telephone directory.

7.0 ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
7.1 Each employee signs the annual acknowledgement form and returns it to the Department Secretary. The signed form is filed in the departmental training record.
