I.  AUTHORITY AND SCOPE

Pursuant to the obligations imposed upon this institution by applicable federal
and state statute governing the safeguarding of member information, and in
furtherance of the Board-approved Information Security Programme, the following
schedule is promulgated and shall be observed by all employees, contractors,
volunteers, and members of the supervisory committee without exception. Nothing
herein shall be construed to diminish any stricter obligation arising under a
separate policy, regulatory directive, or lawful order.

III. TRANSMISSION RESTRICTIONS

No identifier designated "Prohibited" in the third column above shall be
transmitted by electronic mail, facsimile, short message service, or any
consumer messaging application, whether in the body of the message, in an
attachment, in a subject line, or in a file name, and irrespective of whether
the recipient is internal or external to the institution. Partial masking does
not cure the prohibition. Where transmission is operationally necessary, the
secure member portal shall be the sole permitted channel, and the transmitting
employee shall record the disclosure in the access log maintained by the
Records Custodian.

IV. RECOGNITION OF A PRETEXTING CONTACT

A pretexting contact is any communication in which the initiator adopts a false
identity, a false authority, or a false purpose in order to induce disclosure.
The following characteristics, singly or in combination, shall be treated as
sufficient grounds to terminate the interaction: the imposition of artificial
urgency; a demand that verification be waived; a request that an identifier be
read back aloud; refusal to accept a return call placed to the number of record;
an assertion of internal authority not evidenced in the staff directory; or a
request that documents be forwarded to an address not already of record. Under
no circumstance shall an employee confirm, deny, or correct any digit or
character of an identifier offered by the caller.

V.  INTERNAL REFERRAL

All suspected pretexting shall be reported the same business day to the Fraud
Watch Desk Supervisor, with a contemporaneous copy to the Chief Compliance
Officer. Questions of retention or destruction shall be directed to the Records
Custodian. Questions of member notification shall be directed to the Chief
Compliance Officer, whose determination shall be final.
