CLAUSE 1.  IDENTIFIER INVENTORY
           Every label below is printed on Division stationery. The entry
           written against a label is a personal identifier and is governed
           by clauses 2 to 5.

           FIELD LABEL                     MEDIUM OF ORIGIN
           ..............................  ..........................
           Applicant Full Name             Paper application
           Date of Birth                   Paper application
           Social Security Number          Paper application
           Parent or Guardian Name         Registration index
           Certificate Number              Registry ledger
           Place of Birth                  Registry ledger
           Mailing Address                 Mail request envelope
           Requester Identification        Counter log

CLAUSE 2.  DESTRUCTION AT END OF USE
           2.1  Cross-cut shredder, never the waste basket: clinic day
                sheets, appointment lists, laboratory slips, facsimile
                cover sheets, adhesive label backing, draft
                correspondence, superseded rosters.
           2.2  Locked destruction cabinet followed by witnessed
                destruction: diskettes, compact discs, printer ribbon,
                microfiche.
           2.3  A full bin is reported to the Facilities Supervisor and
                is not left unlocked overnight.

CLAUSE 3.  ELECTRONIC MAIL
           3.1  No field named at clause 1 may be typed into an
                electronic mail message, its subject line, or any
                attachment to it.
           3.2  Case discussion is conducted by internal note or by
                telephone to a listed extension.
           3.3  Where a colleague sends an identifier by electronic mail,
                delete it and report the event to the Privacy Officer the
                same working day.

CLAUSE 4.  RECOGNITION OF A PRETEXTING CALL

           INDICATOR                       COUNTER ACTION
           ..............................  ..........................
           No role title given             Ask for role and division
           Refuses a return call           End the call
           Quotes a partial record         Volunteer nothing further
           Cites an unnamed superior       Refer to a supervisor
           Presses for immediate answer    Slow the call down
           Calls outside counter hours     Take no identifier
