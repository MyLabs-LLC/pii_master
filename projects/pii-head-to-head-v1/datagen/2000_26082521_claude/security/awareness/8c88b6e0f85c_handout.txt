MERIDIAN FREIGHT SYSTEMS, INC.
BRIEFING SHEET IH-1(a) — QUESTIONS RAISED AT TERMINAL BRIEFINGS
ISSUED BY: RECORDS ADMINISTRATION

Q.1  A shipper's clerk asks me to read back the account number so they can
     confirm they have the right file. Is that permitted?
A.1  No. Verification runs one way. The caller supplies the reference; the
     recipient confirms or declines. A caller who cannot supply the
     reference is offered a callback to the recorded contact point.

Q.2  Payroll paperwork arrived in the wrong pouch. What is the correct step?
A.2  Reseal without further reading, mark the envelope "MIS-ROUTED", and
     return it to the Payroll Administrator through the pouch. Record the
     event on the Pouch Transfer Register. Do not re-address it locally.

Q.3  May I scan a wage statement and send it to a colleague who needs the
     figure urgently?
A.3  No. Wage statements carry fields prohibited from electronic mail and
     fax. The figure is confirmed by the Payroll Administrator directly.

Q.4  A caller states they are from the bank's fraud department and needs the
     driver's Social Security Number to stop a fraudulent transaction.
A.4  This is the standard pretext pattern: external authority, urgency, and
     a request for a prohibited field. Take the stated name and stated
     organisation, end the call, log it, and notify the Information Security
     Coordinator the same shift.

Q.5  The shredder is full and the console is locked. May documents be left
     on top of the console until morning?
A.5  No. Material is retained in a locked drawer under the control of the
     shift supervisor and deposited when the console is emptied.

Q.6  Whom do I tell if I believe a record has been disclosed in error?
A.6  The Information Security Coordinator, without delay, and the Terminal
     Manager if the disclosure involved a person present on site. Do not
     attempt to recover the document from the recipient.
