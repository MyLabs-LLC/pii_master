STAFF NOTICE — PII HANDLING
POSTED: Workroom, main office, health office
AUTHORITY: Data Privacy Officer

1. Shred, do not recycle: rosters, meal applications, health logs, emergency cards, payroll registers, applicant notes.

2. Never email: Social Security Number, Bank Routing Number, Direct Deposit Account Number, Driver License Number, or any file containing the columns SSN, DOB, or ACCT_NUM.

3. Clear the printer tray before leaving the workroom. Unclaimed output is treated as a disclosure.

4. Lock the screen when leaving the desk. Do not share a login.

5. Callers who will not give a callback extension, who invoke an emergency, or who ask you to confirm a Date of Birth or Home Address they already claim to hold are to be refused.

6. Do not confirm a student's enrollment status, Guardian Contact Number, or Free and Reduced Meal Eligibility Status by telephone.

7. Report any suspected pretexting attempt to the Data Privacy Officer the same day. Note the time and what was requested.

8. Report a lost device or a misdirected message to the Help Desk Supervisor immediately.

9. Direct all records requests to the Records Custodian. Staff do not release records.
