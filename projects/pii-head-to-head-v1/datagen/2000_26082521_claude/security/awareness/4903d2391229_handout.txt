=====================================================================
COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT — DIVISION OF ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES
TELEPHONE AND FRONT-COUNTER GUIDANCE: PRETEXT CALLS
Distribution: reception, medical records, billing, nursing stations
=====================================================================

4.1  Definition
     A pretext call is an approach in which the caller adopts a false
     identity or false purpose in order to obtain identifiers held by
     the Department. Callers are frequently courteous, well briefed,
     and in possession of one or two genuine details already.

4.2  Warning signs — treat any of the following as a pretext until
     established otherwise:
     •  The caller declines to give a call-back number, or gives a
        number that cannot be matched to a known agency
     •  The caller already holds a partial identifier and asks staff
        merely to "confirm" or "complete" it
     •  Manufactured urgency: a closing deadline, an ambulance en
        route, a benefit about to lapse
     •  Claimed seniority within the County, coupled with irritation
        at being asked to verify
     •  A request to read out fields rather than to post or fax them
        to a file address
     •  Sequential calls to different desks, each seeking one further
        field, so that no single member of staff sees the whole
        request
        o  a second caller referring to the first by role only
        o  a caller who knows the internal extension pattern

4.3  Fields never to be released or confirmed over the telephone:
     •  Social Security Number
     •  Date of Birth
     •  Home Address
     •  Medicaid Identification Number, Medicare Claim Number
     •  Insurance Policy Number, Group Number
     •  Mother's Maiden Name
     •  Test results and diagnoses, including confirmation that a
        named person is or is not a client of the Department

4.4  Required handling
     •  Take the caller's stated name, stated agency, and stated
        purpose
     •  Advise that the Department returns such calls, and terminate
        the conversation courteously
     •  Obtain the agency's number from the published directory, not
        from the caller, and call back
     •  Where the caller cannot be verified, make no disclosure and
        make no denial

4.5  Reporting
     •  Complete an incident note the same day and forward it to the
        Privacy Officer
     •  Notify the Front Counter Supervisor so that other desks may
        be alerted within the hour
     •  Where identifiers were disclosed before the pretext was
        recognised, notify the Privacy Officer immediately and the
        Records Custodian in writing; do not attempt to recall the
        information yourself
     •  Requests arriving by facsimile or letter that display the
        same characteristics are handled under this clause
