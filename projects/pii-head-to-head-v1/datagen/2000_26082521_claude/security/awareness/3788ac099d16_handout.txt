FRONT COUNTER — STANDING INSTRUCTION No. 3
Post at each cashier station. Review at quarterly briefing.

    1.  BEFORE YOU SPEAK A VALUE ALOUD
        (a)  Confirm the person at the window is the account holder or a
             person listed under Authorised Contact.
        (b)  Never read back a Social Security Number, Date of Birth, or
             Bank Account Number to confirm it. Ask the customer to state
             it; you confirm match or no match only.

    2.  BEFORE YOU HAND ANYTHING ACROSS THE COUNTER
        (a)  Check that the printout carries no columns other than those the
             customer is entitled to see.
        (b)  Redact nothing by pen. Reprint from the correct report instead.

    3.  BEFORE YOU LEAVE THE STATION
        (a)  Clear the desk of every form bearing Customer Name, Service
             Address, or Account Number.
        (b)  Place all such paper in the locked console. Nothing goes in the
             recycling bin, not even a blank-backed sheet.
        (c)  Lock the terminal. Lock the drawer.

    4.  IF A CALLER PRESSES YOU
        (a)  Anyone genuinely entitled to the information will accept a
             call-back to the number of record. A pretexter will not.
        (b)  End the call politely and notify the Customer Service Supervisor,
             who will log it with the Records Custodian.
