METROPOLITAN TRANSIT AUTHORITY
CUSTOMER ASSISTANCE CENTRE
PROTECTING YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION — RIDER HANDOUT

WHAT WE ASK FOR, AND WHY

[ ] Monthly Pass Application — Name, Mailing Address, Pass Serial Number
[ ] Reduced Fare Certification — Name, Date of Birth, Certifying Authority
[ ] Paratransit Registration — Name, Address, Registrant Number, Medical Certification Number
[ ] Lost Card Replacement — Card Account Number, Date of Loss

We do not require your Social Security Number for any fare product. Any form or caller demanding it is not acting for the Authority.

WHAT TO DESTROY AT HOME

[ ] Expired fare cards — cut through the magnetic stripe before disposal
[ ] Pass receipts and renewal notices bearing your Card Account Number
[ ] Carbon copies of paratransit trip vouchers
[ ] Correspondence bearing your Registrant Number

Tear or shred. Do not place intact in kerbside recycling.

WHAT NEVER TO SEND BY ELECTRONIC MAIL

[ ] Social Security Number
[ ] Card Account Number
[ ] Bank Account Number or Cheque images
[ ] Date of Birth
[ ] Medical Certification Number

Electronic mail is not a secure channel. Use the counter or the postal service.

RECOGNISING A PRETEXTING CALL

[ ] The caller says your pass or registration will be cancelled today
[ ] The caller already has part of your Card Account Number and asks you to complete it
[ ] The caller offers a refund and requests your Bank Account Number
[ ] The caller refuses to give a work order number or to be called back
[ ] The caller asks you to fax documents to a number they supply

The Authority does not telephone riders to request identifiers. Refunds are issued by cheque to the address on file.

IF YOU RECEIVE SUCH A CALL

[ ] End the call
[ ] Note the day and hour
[ ] Report it at any staffed station to the Station Supervisor
[ ] Or write to the Customer Assistance Supervisor, Customer Assistance Centre

Corrections to your file are made in person at the Customer Assistance Centre on presentation of photographic identification.
