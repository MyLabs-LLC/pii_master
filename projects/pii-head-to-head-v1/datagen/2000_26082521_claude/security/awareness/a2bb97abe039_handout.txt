STAFF GUIDANCE: SAFEGUARDING IDENTIFIERS IN DAILY PRACTICE
Human Resources and Student Services, joint issue, 2021
Reference: District Policy on Confidentiality of Records; federal student privacy law

1. PURPOSE
   (a) To state the minimum handling requirements for identifiers held by the District.
   (b) To describe the destruction, transmission, and verification steps required of every staff member.
   (c) To identify the role holders responsible for each category of incident.

2. IDENTIFIERS IN SCOPE
   (a) Student records: Student Identification Number, State Testing Identifier, Date of Birth, Enrollment Date, Home Address, Parent/Guardian Name, Custody Status, Disability Category, Medical Alert, Meal Eligibility Status, Discipline Record Reference.
   (b) Employee records: Employee Identification Number, Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Home Address, Bank Routing Number, Bank Account Number, Health Plan Member Identifier, Dependent Name, Evaluation Rating, Leave Balance.
   (c) Applicant and vendor records: Taxpayer Identification Number, Driver License Number, Background Check Reference, Certification Number.

3. DESTRUCTION
   (a) Cross-cut shredding is required for all paper bearing any element listed at clause 2. Strip-cut shredding is not accepted.
   (b) Interim storage shall be a locked shred console. Material shall not be removed from a console once deposited.
   (c) Sticky notes, sign-in sheets, nurse logs, substitute folders, and hall passes are within scope where they bear a listed element.
   (d) Electronic media awaiting disposal shall be surrendered to the Technology Services depot for wiping. Staff shall not attempt deletion as a substitute for wiping.

4. TRANSMISSION
   (a) Prohibited by electronic mail in all cases: Social Security Number; Taxpayer Identification Number; Bank Routing Number; Bank Account Number; Driver License Number; Health Plan Member Identifier; Medical Alert; Individualized Education Program contents; Custody Status.
   (b) Permitted by internal electronic mail between District accounts, where operationally necessary and limited to the minimum required: Student Identification Number; Employee Identification Number; Position Title; Building Assignment.
   (c) Any transmission outside the District domain shall use the secure transfer portal with expiry and download logging enabled.
   (d) Automatic forwarding rules to external addresses are disabled at the server and shall not be requested.
   (e) Screen captures of record system pages shall not be attached to mail, pasted into chat, or stored on personal devices.

5. VERIFICATION OF REQUESTS
   (a) A caller shall be asked to state the purpose and authority for the request before any record is retrieved.
   (b) Staff shall not confirm, correct, or complete an identifier offered by a caller.
   (c) Urgency, claimed seniority, refusal of a return call, and the offer of an alternative contact number are each grounds for refusal.
   (d) Contact shall be re-established only through the published directory or an in-person visit with photographic identification.
   (e) The refusal shall be logged; the log shall record the claimed affiliation and the time, and no identifier supplied by the caller.

6. ASSIGNED ROLES

   MATTER                              ROLE TITLE
   Record access and release           the Records Custodian
   Retention and disposal schedule     the Records Custodian
   Suspected disclosure or breach      the Data Privacy Officer
   Device loss, theft, or malware      the Help Desk Supervisor
   Fraudulent payment or bank change   the Internal Auditor
   Employee file questions             the Human Resources Director
   Campus-level enquiries              the Building Principal
   Family notification decisions       the Superintendent's designee
   External and media enquiries        the Communications Officer

7. ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
   (a) Each staff member shall complete the annual confidentiality acknowledgement in the learning management system before the tenth instructional day.
   (b) Supervisors shall confirm completion for their assigned personnel and report exceptions to the Human Resources Director.
