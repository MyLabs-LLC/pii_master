MUNICIPAL TRANSIT AUTHORITY -- INTERNAL REFERRAL ROSTER BY ROLE TITLE
ISSUED WITH PROCEDURE DP-1; TO BE AFFIXED AT EACH SUPERVISOR'S DESK

The referral roster below is furnished so that a matter touching upon a
personal identifier may be directed, in the first instance and without
intervening inquiry, to the role bearing custodial responsibility for that
matter. An employee shall refer by role title only; no referral shall be made
to an individual by reason of acquaintance, and no matter shall be discussed
with any person whose role does not appear below.

.--------------------------------.------------------------------------.--------------------------.
| ROLE TITLE OF REFERRAL         | SUBJECT MATTER REFERRED            | MEANS OF REFERRAL        |
:--------------------------------+------------------------------------+--------------------------:
| the Records Custodian          | Retention, disposal, subpoena      | Form RC-1, interoffice   |
| the Data Protection Officer    | Suspected disclosure of data       | Written memorandum       |
| the Payroll Services Manager   | Deduction and earnings fields      | Form PY-4, sealed        |
| the Benefits Administrator     | Dependent and member fields        | Form BN-2, sealed        |
| the Fare Media Administrator   | Fare card account fields           | Form FM-6, sealed        |
| the Chief of Transit Police    | Pretexting; attempted entry        | In person, immediately   |
| the Duty Superintendent        | After-hours incident of any kind   | By radio to Control      |
| the General Counsel            | Legal process; press inquiry       | Written memorandum       |
| the Inspector General          | Misuse by an employee or agent     | Sealed, marked Personal  |
'--------------------------------'------------------------------------'--------------------------'

A referral made in good faith under this roster is a protected act. An
employee who refers a matter shall retain no working copy of any document
bearing an identifier field and shall record the fact of referral, the role
title to which referral was made, and the hour thereof in the division log.
