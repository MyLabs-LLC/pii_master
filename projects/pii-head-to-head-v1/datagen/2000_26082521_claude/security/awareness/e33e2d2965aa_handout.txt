WESTFIELD CONSOLIDATED SCHOOL DISTRICT — GUIDANCE FOR FAMILIES AND STAFF
Safeguarding Personal Information — Issued Spring 2016

1. The District collects only the information it needs in order to enrol, instruct, feed, transport, and employ. Fields such as Student Identification Number, Date of Birth, Home Address, Parent or Guardian Name, Social Security Number, and Health Plan Member Number are collected under specific authority and are not disclosed casually.

2. Paper carrying any of those fields is destroyed by cross-cut shredder. Registration packets, meal benefit applications, health forms, bus route rosters, and payroll documents are placed in the locked shred consoles located in each main office, never in a recycling bin.

3. The District does not request or accept a Social Security Number, a Bank Account Number, or a Payment Card Number by email, and staff do not send those fields by email either. Anyone who receives a message appearing to come from the District and asking for such information should not reply to it.

4. Callers occasionally claim to be from a benefits administrator, a records clearinghouse, or a District department, and ask a front-office employee to read back a Date of Birth or an Account Number "for verification." No such verification is ever required by the District. The correct response is to decline and to take the caller's details.

5. Concerns about a suspicious call, letter, or message are reported to the school Principal and to the District Records Custodian. Requests to inspect or amend a record are made in writing to the Director of Student Services.
