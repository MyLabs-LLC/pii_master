BEFORE ANY IDENTIFIER IS SPOKEN ALOUD, CONFIRM ALL FOUR:

    [ ]  1.  The caller was reached on, or is being returned to, the contact
             point already recorded on the account.
    [ ]  2.  The caller supplied the file reference without being prompted.
    [ ]  3.  The enquiry falls within the caller's recorded authority on the
             account (shipper, consignee of record, or authorised agent).
    [ ]  4.  The disclosure is limited to shipment status. Personal fields
             are not disclosed by telephone in any circumstance.

IF ANY BOX IS UNCHECKED — STOP.

    SAY:   "That enquiry is handled through our records office. I will take
            your reference and we will call you back on the number we hold."
    THEN:  Take stated name, stated organisation, stated reason.
    THEN:  End the call. Do not read back any figure. Do not confirm whether
           a record exists.
    THEN:  Complete the Suspected Pretext Call Log before end of shift.
    THEN:  Notify the Information Security Coordinator.

NEVER DISCLOSE BY TELEPHONE:
    Social Security Number  •  Date of Birth  •  Deposit Account Number
    Bank Routing Number  •  Payment Card Number  •  Card Expiry Date
    Commercial Driver's Licence Number  •  Home Address  •  Home Telephone

PRESSURE IS THE SIGNAL. Urgency, seniority, and threat of complaint are the
three most common features of a pretext call. A genuine caller accepts a
callback. Escalate to the Terminal Manager if pressure continues.
