MEMORANDUM

TO:      Branch Managers, Department Heads
FROM:    The Records Custodian
RE:      Quarterly Destruction Cycle — Preparation Instructions
ACTION:  Required before the close of the cycle

The quarterly destruction cycle begins shortly. The certified destruction vendor collects from all locations. The following applies to every department holding member records.

BEFORE THE PICKUP

1. Purge only against the current retention schedule. If you are unsure whether an item is eligible, do not guess and do not destroy — send the query to this office. Destruction of a record still under retention, litigation hold, or examination request is a reportable event.

2. Do not open a console to retrieve something. If a document was placed in error, notify this office and the Facilities Coordinator; the console will be opened under dual control and the retrieval logged.

3. Consoles must be locked at all times and positioned within staff-only areas. A console in a lobby, a vestibule, or beside an exterior door is a finding. Report placement problems to the Facilities Coordinator.

4. Overflow does not go in cardboard boxes on the floor. Request additional locked bags in advance.

ITEMS THAT MAY NOT GO INTO A CONSOLE

Consoles are for paper. The following require separate handling through the Information Systems Manager:

  - diskettes, CD-R and CD-RW media, backup tapes
  - hard drives from retired workstations, laptops, and servers
  - microfiche and microfilm
  - typewriter and printer ribbons
  - card embossing waste and PIN mailer stock

Media is degaussed or physically destroyed under separate certificate. Do not place it in a trash receptacle at any stage.

WITNESSING

Destruction is witnessed. The Branch Manager or a designated officer observes loading, confirms that all consoles at the location were emptied, and signs the vendor's manifest. Do not sign a manifest for a count you did not observe. Retain your copy; the certificate of destruction is filed with this office and is produced for examiners.

FIELDS COMMONLY OVERLOOKED

Departments frequently forget that the following carry protected identifiers and must be captured in the purge: teller balancing tapes, item processing reject listings, ATM journal rolls, night deposit logs, safe deposit entry registers, wire callback logs, IRA distribution worksheets, and collection call sheets. Review your drawers and overhead bins for these specifically.

QUESTIONS

Retention periods and eligibility — the Records Custodian. Console supply, placement, and pickup scheduling — the Facilities Coordinator. Electronic media and drive disposal — the Information Systems Manager. Litigation holds and examination requests — the Vice President of Compliance.
