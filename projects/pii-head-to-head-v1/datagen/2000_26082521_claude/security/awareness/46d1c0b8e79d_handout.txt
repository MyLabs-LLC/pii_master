HALSTEAD COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT
                  NOTICE TO STAFF — POST AND MAINTAIN
                 Reception, Intake, and Clinic Work Areas
                          Form HCHD-PRIV-114A

Personnel assigned to counter and telephone duty are reminded that no identifier collected under the field labels Social Security Number, Date of Birth, Medical Record Number, Member Identification Number, Case Number, or Payment Card Number is to be read aloud, repeated back for confirmation, or displayed upon any screen visible from the public side of the counter. Work in progress shall be inverted or covered whenever the workstation is left unattended, and all such material shall be secured in a locked drawer at the conclusion of each shift, the desk surface being left clear of paper of every description.

Callers who decline to be returned through the internal directory, who assert urgency, or who ask that an identifier be confirmed rather than supplied, are to be treated as unverified irrespective of the authority they claim, up to and including a claim of senior departmental office. Such calls are to be concluded courteously and the substance thereof reported to the Privacy Officer before the close of business, with a copy of the report furnished to the Front Office Supervisor.
