VALLEY GENERAL HOSPITAL
PATIENT FINANCIAL SERVICES — DEPARTMENTAL INSTRUCTION
IDENTIFIER SAFEGUARD PROCEDURE
Form PFS-114 (Rev. 04/2004) — Supersedes PFS-114 (Rev. 09/2002)
Distribution: All Billing Office Personnel; Cashiering Window (posted copy); Off-Site Follow-Up Unit

SECTION I. AUTHORITY AND PURPOSE
This Instruction is issued pursuant to the Standards for Privacy of Individually Identifiable Health Information, 45 C.F.R. Parts 160 and 164, and the administrative safeguard requirements adopted thereunder, and is further issued in furtherance of the Hospital's obligations respecting the safeguarding of consumer financial information. The purpose of this Instruction is to prescribe the mandatory handling, transmission, disclosure, and destruction requirements applicable to Protected Identifiers coming into the possession of employees, contracted collectors, students, and volunteers assigned to the Billing Office.

SECTION II. DEFINITIONS
2.1 "Protected Identifier" means, without limitation, any of the following data elements when associated with an individual: name; social security number; medical record number; patient account number; guarantor account number; subscriber or member identification number; date of birth; admission or discharge date; street address; telephone or facsimile number; electronic mail address; payment card number; bank routing or deposit account number; photographic image; or any other element enumerated at 45 C.F.R. 164.514(b)(2).
2.2 "Pretexting" means the procurement or attempted procurement of a Protected Identifier by means of a false, fictitious, or fraudulent statement, representation, or assumed identity.

SECTION III. MATERIALS REQUIRING DESTRUCTION BY CROSS-CUT SHREDDING
No item bearing a Protected Identifier shall be deposited in any wastebasket, recycling receptacle, or unsecured container. The following shall be placed in the locked gray Secure Destruction bin or shredded at the point of use: superseded itemized statements and dunning notices; explanation of benefits documents; remittance advices and check stubs; facsimile cover sheets and transmittal confirmations; charge tickets and encounter forms; insurance verification worksheets; credit report printouts; carbonless copies of payment receipts; adding-machine tapes bearing account numbers; screen prints from the patient accounting system; address correction returns and envelopes bearing window-visible identifiers; handwritten notes, message slips, and post-it notations; obsolete floppy diskettes, compact discs, and microfiche (routed instead to the Information Security Administrator for physical destruction).

SECTION IV. PROHIBITED ELECTRONIC TRANSMISSION
4.1 No Protected Identifier shall be transmitted by electronic mail to any address outside the Hospital's internal messaging system, without exception, and irrespective of any request, consent, or insistence by the recipient.
4.2 The following shall not be placed in the body, subject line, or attachment of any electronic mail message: social security number; full account number; payment card number or card verification value; bank account or routing number; diagnosis, procedure, or account balance in combination with a name.
4.3 Internal messages containing a Protected Identifier shall bear the subject-line designation CONFIDENTIAL — PFS and shall employ the minimum necessary identifier, preferably the last four digits of the account number only.
4.4 Instant messaging clients, personal web-mail accounts, and home computers are prohibited media for any Protected Identifier.

SECTION V. RECOGNITION OF A PRETEXTING CALL
The following indicia, singly or in combination, shall be treated as presumptive evidence of a pretexting attempt and shall require immediate termination of the call: the caller supplies the account number and requests that the employee confirm the name, address, or social security number; the caller asserts urgency, threatened litigation, or supervisory authority to overcome ordinary verification; the caller claims to be calling from another department but cannot state a valid employee identification number; the caller requests that information be faxed or read aloud to a number not of record; the caller requests confirmation of employment, balance, or coverage on behalf of an unnamed third party; background noise indicative of a call center is present in a purportedly personal call. Under no circumstances shall an employee volunteer any identifier not first supplied and verified by the caller.

SECTION VI. INTERNAL NOTIFICATION — BY ROLE TITLE
Suspected pretexting, misdirected facsimile, or lost document: notify the Billing Office Supervisor immediately and the Privacy Officer within one (1) business hour.
Suspected unauthorized system access or lost portable media: notify the Information Security Administrator.
Requests for records from law enforcement, attorneys, or the media: refer without comment to the Records Custodian.
Questions concerning permissible disclosure or minimum necessary: consult the Compliance Officer.
Employee discipline or clarification of this Instruction: Director of Patient Financial Services.
