MERIDIAN FREIGHT SYSTEMS, INC.
INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

TO:      All Personnel, Terminal and Clerical Grades
FROM:    Corporate Compliance Officer
SUBJECT: Identifier Protection — Shift Checklist, Revision C
DATE:    September 2004
FILE:    CMP/PR/04

Revision C replaces Revision B in its entirety. Retain one copy at the
dispatch counter. Sign the acknowledgement panel below and forward it
to the Records Custodian.

PART A — BEFORE YOU HANDLE THE RECORD
[ ] Identify the fields present. In-scope fields: Social Security
    Number, Date of Birth, Home Address, Employee Number, Commercial
    Driver Licence Number, Medical Examiner Certificate Number, Bank
    Routing Number, Account Number, Payment Card Number.
[ ] Confirm you have a business reason to view the record.
[ ] Confirm the recipient, if any, holds the same authority.
[ ] Work at a counter not visible from the dock window or lobby.

PART B — DURING THE SHIFT
[ ] Clear the printer tray after each job.
[ ] Clear the fax tray after each transmission.
[ ] Do not leave a record face-up when leaving the counter.
[ ] Do not carry in-scope records off premises.
[ ] Do not photograph, re-key, or transcribe identifiers to a notepad,
    day book, wall board, or handheld device.

PART C — END OF SHIFT
[ ] Return active files to the locked cabinet.
[ ] Cross-cut shred superseded files, carbons, misprints, photocopies,
    adding-machine tapes, and completed visitor log pages.
[ ] Confirm the shredder bin is not overfull; report if it is.
[ ] Confirm no in-scope paper has entered the recycling tote, the
    dumpster, or the cardboard trailer.
[ ] Lock the counter drawer and the cabinet. Sign the close-out sheet.

PART D — ELECTRONIC MAIL
[ ] No in-scope field is sent by electronic mail, in any part of the
    message or as an attachment, internally or externally.
[ ] No scan of a licence, cheque, card, or medical certificate.
[ ] No unmasked identifier column in a spreadsheet.
[ ] Approved transfer arranged in writing through the Information
    Systems Supervisor where transmission is unavoidable.

PART E — TELEPHONE ENQUIRIES
[ ] Caller located in the internal directory before anything is said.
[ ] No confirmation of partial data, including spelling or last digits.
[ ] No transmission to a number the caller supplies verbally.
[ ] Callback rule applied without exception, whatever the urgency.
[ ] Refused attempt logged on SEC-4 and reported to the Terminal
    Manager the same shift.

PART F — REPORTING
Retention and destruction ............ Records Custodian
Any incident, first report ........... Terminal Manager
Pretexting, badge misuse, theft ...... Corporate Security Officer
Mailbox and media questions .......... Information Systems Supervisor
Personnel file access ................ Human Resources Coordinator
