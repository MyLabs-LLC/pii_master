VII. NOTES ON PREPARATION
        A. Figures are aggregate counts. No record-level output is produced by this report.
        B. Percentages are computed against the divisional denominator and rounded to one
           decimal place; columns may not total exactly 100.0 because of rounding.
        C. Small-cell suppression: cells with a count of 10 or fewer are withheld. Secondary
           suppression is applied within the same table so that a withheld cell cannot be
           derived from the row total, the column total, or the remaining cells.
        D. Geography is reported no finer than market region in this edition. Requests for
           postal-district detail are routed to the Records Custodian and are released only
           after the suppression rules above are applied to the finer grouping.
        E. Retention: this report and its extract are held for six years, then destroyed
           under the divisional retention schedule.
