7.0  READING THE TABLES
     7.1  Every figure is an aggregate. The report contains no individual records and
          no identifiers of any kind; the member key used to deduplicate the extract is
          discarded before the first table is built.
     7.2  Percentages are of the 88,240 individuals served and are rounded to one
          decimal place.
     7.3  Line 4.5 is suppressed under the small-cell rule described in 8.0. Its count
          is carried in the column total but is not shown at line 4.6.

8.0  SMALL-CELL SUPPRESSION
     8.1  Threshold: any category with fewer than 11 individuals is suppressed and
          printed as an em dash.
     8.2  Complementary suppression is applied where a single suppressed value could
          be recovered by subtracting the published rows from the table total.
     8.3  Geography is published no finer than the three-digit postal district. Store
          level and five-digit postal detail are not released in this report.
     8.4  Requests for a suppressed figure are directed to the Records Custodian and
          are answered only in a further-aggregated form.
