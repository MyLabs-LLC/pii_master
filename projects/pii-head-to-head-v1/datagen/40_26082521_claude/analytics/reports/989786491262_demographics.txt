SERVED POPULATION DEMOGRAPHIC REPORT — 2016
Retail Pharmacy Chain, Midwest and Mid-Atlantic Operating Regions
Compiled by the Records Custodian; reviewed by the Director of Pharmacy Operations
Extract date: first business day following year-end close
Population base: 1,046,300 individuals with at least one dispensing event

- METHOD
    - Source: nightly dispensing extract, deduplicated on the internal member key,
      which is dropped before tabulation.
    - Unit of count: the individual, not the prescription and not the transaction.
    - Fields tabulated: age band, sex, ethnicity, preferred language, household
      income bracket, state, postal district (three digits).
    - Output: counts and percentages only. No listings, no cross-tabulations below
      two dimensions, no free-text fields.

- SUPPRESSION AND ROUNDING
    - Primary rule: a cell is withheld when its count is fewer than 11. Withheld cells
      print as an em dash and are not released in any supporting file.
    - Complementary rule: when one cell in a table is withheld, a second cell is also
      withheld so the first cannot be recovered from the total.
    - Postal-district rows are released only where the district count is 1,000 or more;
      all remaining districts are combined into the "all other" line.
    - Percentages are rounded to one decimal place. Totals may differ from 100.0%.
    - Counts are not rounded, but no combination of published rows resolves to a group
      smaller than the primary threshold.
