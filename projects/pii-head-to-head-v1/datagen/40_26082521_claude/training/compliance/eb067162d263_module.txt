OFFICE OF THE COUNTY HEALTH OFFICER
DIVISION OF PRIVACY, RECORDS, AND INFORMATION GOVERNANCE
ANNUAL CONFIDENTIALITY REFRESHER — PRESENTER SCRIPT AND PARTICIPANT HANDOUT
Policy Reference: HD-PRIV-004 (Restricted Information Handling), Rev. 7 — Effective Calendar Year 2021

SLIDE 1 — PURPOSE AND SCOPE OF THIS INSTRUCTION
- This instruction is issued pursuant to the retained authority of the County Health Officer and applies, without exception and without regard to classification, appointment status, or duration of service, to all permanent employees, limited-term employees, extra-help personnel, interns, volunteers, contracted vendors, and any agent acting on behalf of the Department.
- Completion of this module, together with execution of the annual Confidentiality Acknowledgment, is a condition precedent to continued authorization to access Departmental information systems.
- Nothing herein shall be construed to diminish any duty imposed by federal law, state law, County ordinance, collective bargaining agreement, or professional licensure obligation; where obligations conflict, the more restrictive obligation controls.

SLIDE 2 — CATEGORIES OF RESTRICTED INFORMATION (PRESENTER: READ EACH ALOUD)
- Protected Health Information, being any information, in any medium, that relates to physical or mental health, the provision of care, or payment therefor, and that is individually identifiable, including but not limited to the following record fields: Patient Name; Date of Birth; Medical Record Number; Encounter Number; Diagnosis Code; Specimen Identifier; Ordering Clinician.
- Communicable Disease and Investigation Records, including the following case-management columns: Case ID; Disease Condition; Report Date; Interview Status; Contact Linkage ID; Isolation or Quarantine Disposition.
- Personally Identifiable Information maintained for administrative purposes, including: Social Security Number; Driver License Number; Home Street Address; Mailing Address; Home Telephone Number; Mobile Telephone Number; Personal Email Address.
- Financial and Payment Information, including: Payer Name; Member Identification Number; Group Number; Claim Number; Bank Routing Number; Account Number; Payment Card Number.
- Human Resources and Occupational Health Records, including: Employee Identification Number; Position Classification; Leave Category; Accommodation Status; Immunization Status; Fitness-for-Duty Determination.
- Vital Records data elements, including: Certificate Number; Registrant Name; Informant Name; Place of Occurrence; Cause of Death Literal Text.
- Law Enforcement and Legal Hold materials, including: Subpoena Docket Number; Custodian Affidavit; Litigation Hold Identifier.

SLIDE 3 — CONSEQUENCES OF MISHANDLING
- Administrative consequences, imposed following investigation and applicable due process, may include counseling, written reprimand, suspension without pay, demotion, revocation of system credentials, and termination of employment or contract.
- Civil consequences may include monetary penalties assessed against the Department and, in circumstances of individual culpability, indemnification demands against the responsible party.
- Criminal consequences may attach where disclosure is made knowingly, under false pretenses, or for purposes of commercial advantage, personal gain, or malicious harm.
- Professional consequences may include mandatory report to the applicable licensing board.
- Notification consequences: any unauthorized acquisition, access, use, or disclosure triggers a mandatory risk assessment and may compel individual, media, and regulator notification at Departmental expense.

SLIDE 4 — SCENARIO-BASED KNOWLEDGE CHECK (SELECT PERMIT, PROHIBIT, OR ESCALATE)
- A staff member receives a telephone inquiry from a caller asserting a familial relationship to a case under investigation and requesting the Disease Condition field.
- A contact tracer photographs a monitor displaying an interview queue in order to continue work from a personal residence.
- A supervisor forwards a spreadsheet containing the columns Member Identification Number and Diagnosis Code to a personal webmail account because the network file share is unavailable.
- A clerk locates a printed roster bearing the columns Registrant Name and Certificate Number in an unattended common-area printer tray.
- An employee accesses a record for a household matter unrelated to any assigned duty, views it, and closes it without further action.
- A vendor technician requests live production data containing Social Security Number for the purpose of validating a software patch.

PRESENTER CLOSING: Direct all uncertainty to the Records Custodian or the Privacy Officer prior to acting. When in doubt, do not disclose.
