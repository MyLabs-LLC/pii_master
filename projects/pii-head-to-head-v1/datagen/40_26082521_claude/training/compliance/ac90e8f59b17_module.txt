SECTION I — DUTIES OF THE WORKFORCE MEMBER
[ ] I acknowledge that access is granted solely on the principle of minimum necessary, and that authorization to access a system does not constitute authorization to access any particular record within that system.
[ ] I acknowledge that curiosity, familiarity, concern for the welfare of another, or a good-faith belief that disclosure would be beneficial constitutes no defense whatsoever to an allegation of improper access.
[ ] I acknowledge that the duty of confidentiality survives the termination of my employment or engagement in perpetuity.

SECTION II — ENUMERATED RESTRICTED CATEGORIES
[ ] Category A — Individually Identifiable Health Information. Illustrative fields: Patient Name; Date of Birth; Medical Record Number; Admission Date; Discharge Disposition; Diagnosis Code; Procedure Code.
[ ] Category B — Reportable Condition Surveillance Data. Illustrative columns: Case ID; Disease Condition; Onset Date; Investigation Status; Contact Linkage ID; Outbreak Cluster Identifier.
[ ] Category C — Sensitive Program Information subject to heightened statutory protection, including records of communicable disease testing, reproductive health services, behavioral health services, and substance use disorder treatment. Illustrative fields: Program Enrollment Identifier; Consent-on-File Indicator; Test Result.
[ ] Category D — Identity and Contact Data. Illustrative fields: Social Security Number; Home Street Address; Home Telephone Number; Personal Email Address; Emergency Contact Name.
[ ] Category E — Financial and Benefits Data. Illustrative fields: Member Identification Number; Group Number; Bank Routing Number; Account Number.
[ ] Category F — Security Data. Illustrative fields: User Account Name; Password; Multifactor Token Serial; Badge Credential Number.

SECTION III — SANCTION SCHEDULE
[ ] Tier 1 — Negligent handling without disclosure beyond the workforce: documented counseling and mandatory retraining.
[ ] Tier 2 — Negligent handling resulting in disclosure outside the Department: written reprimand, suspension, and credential review.
[ ] Tier 3 — Intentional access without a business justification: suspension or dismissal, and referral to the appropriate licensing authority.
[ ] Tier 4 — Disclosure for personal gain, commercial advantage, or malicious harm: dismissal, referral for criminal prosecution, and civil recovery of all costs incurred by the County.

SECTION IV — APPLIED JUDGMENT EXERCISE
[ ] Scenario One: A workforce member overhears a conversation in an elevator in which two staff discuss an active investigation using enough descriptive detail that a bystander could infer identity. Identify the violation and the required response.
[ ] Scenario Two: A request arrives from a sister agency seeking a data extract containing the columns Patient Name and Diagnosis Code, citing an oral agreement. Identify the instrument that must exist before any transfer occurs.
[ ] Scenario Three: A laptop assigned to a field team cannot be located at the close of the shift. Identify the reporting deadline and the office to be notified.
[ ] Scenario Four: A workforce member is asked by a family member to confirm whether a household is subject to isolation. Identify the correct response and the applicable prohibition.
[ ] Scenario Five: A shared drive folder is discovered with permissions set to allow all authenticated users to read files bearing the column Social Security Number. Identify the immediate containment step.
