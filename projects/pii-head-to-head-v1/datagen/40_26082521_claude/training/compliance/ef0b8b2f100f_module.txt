MEMORANDUM — MANDATORY IN-SERVICE
TO: All Divisions, Programs, and Contracted Personnel
FROM: The Privacy Officer, in consultation with the Records Custodian and County Counsel
RE: Restricted Information — Categories, Sanctions, and Competency Verification (2021)

1. AUTHORITY AND APPLICABILITY
   a. This in-service is promulgated under Departmental Administrative Directive and is mandatory for every individual holding a Departmental network account.
   b. Failure to complete the competency verification below within thirty calendar days shall result in suspension of system credentials without further notice.

2. INFORMATION THAT IS RESTRICTED AND MAY NOT BE ACCESSED, COPIED, TRANSMITTED, PRINTED, PHOTOGRAPHED, DISCUSSED, OR REMOVED FROM COUNTY PREMISES ABSENT WRITTEN AUTHORIZATION
   a. Clinical and case records containing any of the following fields: Patient Name; Date of Birth; Medical Record Number; Case ID; Diagnosis Code; Specimen Identifier; Laboratory Result.
   b. Identity records containing any of the following fields: Social Security Number; Driver License Number; Home Street Address; Telephone Number; Personal Email Address.
   c. Payment records containing any of the following fields: Account Number; Payment Card Number; Bank Routing Number; Claim Number; Member Identification Number.
   d. Employment and occupational health records containing any of the following fields: Employee Identification Number; Leave Category; Fitness-for-Duty Determination; Immunization Status.
   e. Vital records containing any of the following fields: Certificate Number; Registrant Name; Informant Name; Cause of Death Literal Text.
   f. Aggregate or de-identified products that have not been reviewed for small-cell suppression and re-identification risk shall be treated as restricted until such review is documented.

3. CONSEQUENCES, WHICH ARE CUMULATIVE AND NOT ALTERNATIVE
   a. Discipline in accordance with the applicable personnel rules, up to and including dismissal.
   b. Immediate and permanent revocation of access, and where applicable, termination of the contracting instrument.
   c. Civil monetary penalties, corrective action plans, and mandated independent monitoring.
   d. Criminal referral where the conduct is knowing, willful, or undertaken under false pretenses.
   e. Personal liability for costs where conduct falls outside the course and scope of employment.

4. COMPETENCY VERIFICATION — INDICATE THE REQUIRED ACTION FOR EACH SCENARIO
   a. A workforce member is asked by a supervisor to send a line list to a personal device so that work may continue during an evening shift.
   b. A visitor to a public counter can view a screen displaying the columns Case ID and Disease Condition while awaiting service.
   c. A departing employee copies a program folder to removable media, asserting that the folder contains only their own work product.
   d. A telephone caller states that they are calling from another government agency and requests confirmation of a Test Result field, declining to provide a written request.
   e. Documents bearing the column Registrant Name are placed in a general recycling receptacle rather than a locked destruction bin.

5. DIRECTION
   a. Escalate all uncertainty to the Records Custodian before acting; the absence of a prohibition is not a permission.
   b. Report any suspected unauthorized access, use, or disclosure immediately upon discovery, without regard to whether harm is believed to have occurred, and without independent investigation by the reporting party.
