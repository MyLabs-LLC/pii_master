CONFIDENTIALITY QUICK-REFERENCE CARD — POST AT WORKSTATION
County Health Department | Privacy Program | 2021 Cycle

RESTRICTED CATEGORIES — DO NOT DISCLOSE WITHOUT WRITTEN AUTHORIZATION
  • Clinical: Patient Name, Date of Birth, Medical Record Number, Diagnosis Code
  • Surveillance: Case ID, Disease Condition, Contact Linkage ID
  • Identity: Social Security Number, Driver License Number, Home Street Address
  • Financial: Account Number, Payment Card Number, Claim Number
  • Personnel: Employee Identification Number, Accommodation Status

CONSEQUENCES — CUMULATIVE AND NON-EXCLUSIVE
  • Progressive discipline up to and including dismissal
  • Civil monetary penalty and regulatory corrective action plan
  • Criminal referral where the act is knowing or for personal gain
  • Mandatory breach notification at Departmental cost

THIRTY-SECOND SCENARIO CHECK — ANSWER YES OR NO
  • A colleague asks you to look up whether a neighbor appears in the case-management system. Permitted?
  • You must transport a printed line list bearing the column Specimen Identifier between two County buildings in a personal vehicle. Permitted without a sealed transport envelope and a logged chain of custody?
  • A reporter requests aggregate counts by ZIP code with no cell suppression applied. Permitted without Public Information Office review?
  • You discover an unlocked file drawer labeled with a program name after hours. Is silence permitted?

If any answer is uncertain, cease the activity and contact the Records Custodian before proceeding. Verbal authorization does not substitute for written authorization.
