I. STATEMENT OF THE PURPOSE FOR WHICH THIS INSTRUMENT IS EXECUTED

The person executing this instrument (hereinafter "the Applicant") thereby petitions the Division of Environmental Sanitation for the issuance of a certificate of registration authorizing the Applicant to construct, install, alter, repair, or abandon on-site wastewater treatment systems within the unincorporated territory of the County, and within such incorporated municipalities as have by interlocal agreement delegated their sanitation authority to the County. The Applicant is placed on notice that registration hereunder is a privilege and not a vested property right; that it is issued in reliance upon the representations made herein; and that it may be suspended or revoked upon a showing, after notice and an opportunity to be heard before the Sanitation Hearing Officer, that the work performed by the Applicant has failed to conform to the standards prescribed by the County Sanitary Code as the same may from time to time be amended.

II. DIRECTIONS TO THE APPLICANT

Read the whole of this form before making any entry upon it. Every item in Articles III and IV must be completed by the Applicant personally, in ink, and no item may be left vacant; where an item is inapplicable, write the word "NONE" across the space provided. Do not attach business cards, letterhead, or promotional literature in lieu of a written response, as such attachments will be detached and destroyed by intake staff and will not be treated as a response to any item. The Applicant should note that the surety bond and certificate of liability insurance required by the Sanitary Code are separate submissions and are not to be enclosed with this application. Applications bearing erasures or correction fluid within Article III will be rejected; a correction shall instead be made by a single line struck through the erroneous entry, with the correct entry written adjacent thereto and initialed.
