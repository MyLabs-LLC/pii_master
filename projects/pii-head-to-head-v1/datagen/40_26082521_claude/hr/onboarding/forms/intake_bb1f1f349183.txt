INSTRUCTIONS TO THE REQUESTING PARTY. This form must be completed in its entirety, in ink, by the individual who is the subject of the record or by a personal representative whose authority to act is evidenced by documentation acceptable to the Records Custodian. An incomplete submission shall be returned without action, and the sixty-day period for departmental response shall not be deemed to have commenced until a complete submission is received at the intake window during posted business hours.
