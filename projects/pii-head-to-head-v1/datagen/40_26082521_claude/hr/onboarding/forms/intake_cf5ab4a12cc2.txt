ARTICLE ONE. PURPOSE AND EFFECT OF THIS APPLICATION.

This application is submitted by a licensed practitioner (hereinafter "the Applicant") who seeks designation as an enrolled provider authorized to receive publicly purchased vaccine from the Bureau. The Applicant is advised that enrollment creates a continuing custodial obligation with respect to publicly furnished biologicals, that such obligation runs to the Applicant personally and to the designated site jointly and severally, and that acceptance of this application by the Bureau shall not be construed as a waiver of any storage, handling, reporting, or accountability requirement imposed by state regulation or by the terms of the annual provider agreement executed contemporaneously herewith.

ARTICLE TWO. INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PERSON COMPLETING THIS FORM.

Complete every article in sequence, using black ink or a typewriter, and do not abbreviate any name or professional designation. Where a signature is required, it must be affixed by the Applicant and not by office staff acting under a general authorization. Should the space provided for any item prove insufficient, the Applicant shall continue the response upon a separate sheet, plainly identifying the article and item to which the continuation pertains, and shall initial each such sheet. Submission by facsimile is permitted only where the original is forwarded by mail within five business days.
