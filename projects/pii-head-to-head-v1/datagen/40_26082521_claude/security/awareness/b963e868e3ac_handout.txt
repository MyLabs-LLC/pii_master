OFFICE OF THE UNIVERSITY REGISTRAR
STAFF NOTICE RG-04-11: PROTECTION OF PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS
Issued Fall Term 2004        Supersedes RG-03-07        Page 1 of 1

CL.  MATERIAL OR FIELD LABEL          REQUIRED HANDLING
---  -------------------------------  ---------------------------------
1.1  Social Security Number           Cross-cut shred the same day;
     (any printout or worksheet)      no recycling, no waste basket
1.2  Date of Birth; Student           Shred working copies; return
     Identification Number            originals to the file room
1.3  Bank Routing Number and Bank     Shred and report the printout
     Account Number on refund forms   to the Records Custodian
1.4  Grade rosters, transcript        Lock in the desk pedestal at
     drafts, degree audit sheets      the close of each business day

2.1  Electronic mail shall not carry any value entered against the field
     labels named in clauses 1.1 through 1.3, whether in the message
     body, in the subject line, or in an attached file.
2.2  Facsimile transmission of the same fields requires the prior
     approval of the Associate Registrar for Records.
2.3  Diskettes and CD-R media leaving the suite are logged out and
     logged in with the Records Custodian.

3.1  A caller who presses for speed, who already knows part of a record
     and asks you to confirm the rest, or who cites a senior officer as
     authority for release, is treated as a pretexting caller.
3.2  Direct every question, and every suspected disclosure, to the
     Records Custodian; in that officer's absence, to the Associate
     Registrar for Records.
