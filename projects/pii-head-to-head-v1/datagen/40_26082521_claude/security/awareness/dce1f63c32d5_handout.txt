SECTION 2. ELECTRONIC TRANSMISSION

2.1  Values entered against the labels listed in Section 1 shall not be
     placed in electronic mail, in the subject line of electronic mail,
     or in any attachment to it, whether the recipient is internal or
     external to the University.
2.2  Where such a value must reach another office, it travels by campus
     mail in a sealed envelope marked for that office by title, or it is
     entered directly into the student information system.
2.3  Facsimile transmission is permitted only with the prior approval of
     the Associate Registrar for Records, and only to a machine confirmed
     to stand in a locked office.
2.4  Portable media are logged out and logged in with the Records
     Custodian. No identifier is written to a diskette, CD-R, or portable
     drive that leaves the building.
2.5  No member of staff writes a password on paper or repeats one to a
     caller for any reason whatever.

SECTION 3. TELEPHONE INQUIRIES

3.1  A pretexting call is an attempt to obtain identifiers by
     impersonation. The behaviors below are treated as pretexting unless
     and until the caller is verified through the steps stated.
