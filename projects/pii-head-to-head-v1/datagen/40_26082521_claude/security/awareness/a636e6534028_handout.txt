===============================================================================
OFFICE OF THE UNIVERSITY REGISTRAR
DEPARTMENTAL HANDOUT RG-04-12: IDENTIFIERS HELD OUTSIDE THE REGISTRAR SUITE
For academic department offices and college service centers, Fall Term 2004
===============================================================================

1.  SCOPE

1.1 This handout applies to every department office that receives class
    rosters, advising files, or verification requests from this Office.
1.2 Departments hold identifiers under the same conditions as the
    Registrar suite. Nothing in local practice reduces those conditions.

2.  FIELDS THAT MAY NOT LEAVE THE DEPARTMENT OFFICE ON PAPER

      Field label                     Carrier permitted
      ------------------------------  ---------------------------------
      Social Security Number          none; use the student number
      Bank Account Number             none
      Date of Birth                   sealed campus mail, by title
      Student Identification Number   sealed campus mail, by title
      Grade of Record                 sealed campus mail, by title

3.  TRANSMISSION

3.1 Electronic mail is not an approved carrier for any value entered
    against the labels in Section 2. This holds for the message body,
    the subject line, and every attachment.
3.2 Where a roster must be corrected, the correction is entered in the
    student information system and the paper draft is shredded at once.
3.3 Voicemail is not an approved carrier. A value left on a voicemail
    system is reported to the Information Security Officer.

4.  DAILY AND PERIODIC CONTROLS

CLAUSE | CONTROL POINT                | RESPONSIBLE ROLE         | FREQUENCY
=======+==============================+==========================+===========
4.1    | Cross-cut shredder emptied   | Department Records Clerk | Daily
4.2    | Fax machine tray cleared     | Front Counter Supervisor | Daily
4.3    | Screen locked when absent    | All staff                | Continuous
4.4    | Media log reconciled         | Records Custodian        | Weekly
4.5    | Pretexting drill reviewed    | Info Security Officer    | Termly

5.  PRETEXTING

5.1 Treat as pretexting any caller who presses for an immediate answer,
    who offers part of a record for confirmation, who claims authority
    from a named senior officer, or who asks that the answer be faxed or
    read aloud before a written request is received.
5.2 Take the caller's stated office and title only. Ring that office
    back on an extension taken from the campus directory, never on an
    extension supplied by the caller.
5.3 Record the attempt in the office day book and notify the Records
    Custodian the same day.

6.  INTERNAL CONTACTS BY ROLE

    Records Custodian ................ routine questions, shredding,
                                       suspected disclosure
    Associate Registrar for Records .. release approvals, exceptions
    Information Security Officer ..... compromised accounts, voicemail
    Department Chair ................. local staffing and access lists
    University Registrar ............. appeals and policy interpretation
    Office of University Counsel ..... subpoenas and outside demands
