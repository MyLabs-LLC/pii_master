UNIVERSITY REGISTRAR . . . . . . . . . . . . . COUNTER NOTICE C-9 (Rev. 2004)
PROTECTING YOUR PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS

1.  The Office holds the fields listed below against your student record.
    Only the field labels are shown here. Values are released to you in
    the manner stated in the right-hand column.

    Field label                            Released by
    -------------------------------------  ------------------------------
    Social Security Number ...............  counter, in person only
    Student Identification Number ........  counter or campus mail
    Date of Birth ........................  counter, in person only
    Permanent Address ....................  counter or campus mail
    Term Grade Record ....................  counter or campus mail
    Financial Aid Award Code .............  counter, in person only

2.  Photographic identification is required at the counter. Staff at the
    counter will not accept identification held up to the window by a
    third party.

3.  Destroy at home, by cross-cut shredder or by tearing across twice in
    both directions, any paper of ours you no longer need that carries a
    value against the labels in clause 1. Fee statements, award letters,
    and enrollment verifications all carry such values.

4.  Do not send us any of those values by electronic mail, and do not put
    them in the subject line of a message. We will not send them to you
    by electronic mail either. A request that reaches us by mail is
    answered at the counter or by campus mail.

5.  Recognizing a pretexting call:

    5.1  The caller claims to be from this Office, from a lender, or from
         a department, and asks you to confirm a value we already hold.
    5.2  The caller supplies part of a value and invites you to complete
         it.
    5.3  The caller states that your registration, your award, or your
         transcript will be canceled unless you answer immediately.
    5.4  In each case, end the call. Ring the Office back on the number
         printed in the campus directory and report what was asked.

6.  Report a lost identification card, a suspected disclosure, or a call
    of the kind described in clause 5 to the Records Custodian at the
    counter. Matters not resolved there are taken by the Associate
    Registrar for Records, and thereafter by the University Registrar.
