NOTES

1.  Periods run from the close of the fiscal year in which the
    triggering event falls.
2.  "Permanent" designates a holding that may not be disposed of and
    that shall be transferred to microform or to an approved electronic
    format upon closure of the active file.
3.  Where a single file contains material from two categories, the
    longer period governs the file and the more restrictive access class
    governs its handling.
4.  A holding under litigation or audit hold shall be marked on the file
    cover and on the shelf list and shall be excluded from the annual
    disposal run until the hold is released in writing.
5.  Requests to vary a period shall be submitted on Form F2 to the
    Director of Records Management not later than the first Friday in
    April.
6.  This schedule is reviewed biennially. Amendments are effective on
    the date of publication and are not retroactive to holdings already
    disposed of.
