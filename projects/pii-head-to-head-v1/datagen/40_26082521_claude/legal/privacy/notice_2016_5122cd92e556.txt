REGULATION 5125.4-P
DISPOSAL PROCEDURE AND CERTIFICATE OF DISPOSAL
Records Management Unit
Effective: March 2004

PART 1 — PROCEDURE

1.  Identification.
    (a) In April of each year the Records Custodian shall prepare a
        disposal list identifying each category eligible for disposal
        under Attachment A.
    (b) The list shall state category name, medium, volume in linear
        feet or in gigabytes, and shelf or system location.
    (c) The list shall not describe the contents of any individual
        record.

2.  Hold clearance.
    (a) The list shall be transmitted to the Office of General Counsel.
    (b) Counsel shall strike any category subject to a litigation or
        audit hold and shall return the list within fifteen working
        days.

3.  Authorization.
    (a) The site administrator or department head shall authorize the
        cleared list in writing.
    (b) Class 4 holdings require the additional written authorization of
        the Superintendent or designee.

4.  Method.
    (a) Paper, Access Class 1 or 2: recycling by the approved vendor
        under a signed confidentiality undertaking.
    (b) Paper, Access Class 3 or 4: cross-cut shredding on District
        premises, or mobile destruction observed to completion.
    (c) Microform: incineration or shredding by the approved vendor.
    (d) Electronic media for reuse: overwriting by the approved utility,
        not fewer than three passes.
    (e) Electronic media for retirement: degaussing followed by physical
        destruction of the platter or chip.
    (f) Deletion, reformatting, and placement in a recycle container are
        not approved methods for any class.

5.  Witnessing.
    (a) Disposal of Class 3 and Class 4 holdings shall be witnessed by
        an employee who is not the Records Custodian.
    (b) The witness shall observe the destruction to completion.

6.  Certification. A certificate shall be executed at Part 2 below on
    the day the disposal is effected.

7.  Filing. The certificate shall be filed with the Records Management
    Unit within five working days and retained for seven years.
