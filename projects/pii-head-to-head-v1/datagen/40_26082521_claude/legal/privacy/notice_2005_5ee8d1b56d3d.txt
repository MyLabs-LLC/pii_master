BOARD ADMINISTRATIVE STANDARD 5125.4
STANDARD FOR THE CLASSIFICATION, ACCESS, RETENTION AND DISPOSAL OF
PERSONAL INFORMATION HELD BY THE DISTRICT

Approved by the Superintendent's Cabinet — March 2004
Custodian of this Standard: Director of Records Management
Review Cycle: Biennial

ARTICLE I — PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.01  This Standard governs every holding of personal information
      created, received or maintained by any site or department of the
      District, in any medium.
1.02  This Standard applies to holdings relating to pupils, parents and
      guardians, employees, applicants, volunteers, and contractors.
1.03  Where a provision of this Standard conflicts with a controlling
      statute or with an executed collective bargaining agreement, the
      controlling instrument governs and the conflict shall be reported
      to the Director of Records Management.

ARTICLE II — CATEGORIES

2.01  Personal information is classified into the categories enumerated
      on Form F1 (Personal Information Category Declaration).
2.02  Each category is defined by the administrative purpose for which
      the holding exists, not by the identity of any subject and not by
      the site at which it is stored.
2.03  A holding that serves more than one purpose shall be assigned to
      the category carrying the more restrictive access class.
2.04  Categories shall be described in inventories by category name and
      by the names of the data elements comprising them. Inventories
      shall not reproduce, quote or paraphrase the contents of any
      record.
2.05  The Director of Records Management may establish a new category
      upon written request from a site or department. New categories
      take effect only upon publication of an amended Form F1.

ARTICLE III — ACCESS CLASSES

3.01  Class 1 — Open. Accessible to any employee in the performance of
      assigned duties. Applies to the Pupil Enrollment and Directory
      Category only, and only to those data elements not subject to a
      parental or guardian objection on file.
3.02  Class 2 — Restricted. Accessible to the assigned Records
      Custodian, the site administrator, and instructional or
      administrative staff having a documented educational interest.
3.03  Class 3 — Confidential. Accessible to the assigned Records
      Custodian and to the specific licensed or credentialed positions
      named in the access register for that category. Applies to the
      Health, Immunization and Nursing Category, the Special Education
      Assessment Category, the Employment Screening and Clearance
      Category, and the Volunteer and Contractor Screening Category.
4.04  Class 4 — Sealed. Accessible only upon the written authorization
      of the Superintendent or designee, or upon lawful process.
3.05  Access by a parent, guardian, eligible pupil, or employee to a
      holding concerning that person is governed by Regulation 5125.1
      and is not restricted by the classes above.
3.06  Each Records Custodian shall maintain an access register recording,
      for each disclosure outside the assigned class, the category, the
      requesting position, the authorizing position, the date, and the
      statutory or administrative basis.

ARTICLE IV — RETENTION

4.01  Retention periods are fixed by the schedule appended to this
      Standard as Attachment A.
4.02  Retention periods run from the close of the fiscal year in which
      the triggering event occurs, unless the schedule provides
      otherwise.
4.03  No holding shall be retained beyond its scheduled period except
      under a litigation hold or audit hold issued in writing by the
      Office of General Counsel.
4.04  Holds shall be recorded on Form F3 and released only in writing.

ARTICLE V — DISPOSAL

5.01  Disposal shall be effected only by the methods specified in
      Regulation 5125.4-P (Disposal Procedure).
5.02  Disposal of Class 3 and Class 4 holdings requires witnessing.
5.03  A certificate of disposal shall be executed for every disposal
      action and shall be retained for seven years.
5.04  Certificates shall identify the category, the volume, the method,
      and the authorizing and witnessing positions. Certificates shall
      not enumerate the subjects of the disposed holding.

ARTICLE VI — RESPONSIBILITY

6.01  Each principal and department head shall designate a Records
      Custodian in writing.
6.02  The Records Custodian shall complete Form F1 annually.
6.03  The Director of Records Management shall audit not fewer than four
      sites in each fiscal year and shall report findings to the
      Superintendent's Cabinet.
