RULE 60 — STANDARDS FOR SAFEGUARDING CUSTOMER INFORMATION

60.1  Authority and purpose.
      This rule is adopted under the general rulemaking authority of the
      Commissioner and implements the safeguarding provisions of the
      Insurance Information and Privacy Protection Act. Its purpose is to
      establish standards for the administrative, technical, and physical
      safeguarding of customer information held by licensees.

60.2  Definitions.
      (a) "Licensee" means a person or entity licensed, authorized, or
          registered under the insurance laws of this State.
      (b) "Customer information" means nonpublic personal information about
          a customer, whether in paper, electronic, or other form, that is
          maintained by or on behalf of the licensee.
      (c) "Customer information systems" means the electronic or physical
          methods used to access, collect, store, use, transmit, protect,
          or dispose of customer information.
      (d) "Service provider" means a person or entity that maintains,
          processes, or is otherwise permitted access to customer
          information through its provision of services directly to the
          licensee.

60.3  Information security program.
      Each licensee shall implement a comprehensive written information
      security program that includes administrative, technical, and
      physical safeguards appropriate to the size and complexity of the
      licensee, the nature and scope of its activities, and the sensitivity
      of the customer information at issue. The program shall be designed
      to:
      (1) ensure the security and confidentiality of customer information;
      (2) protect against any anticipated threats or hazards to the
          security or integrity of such information; and
      (3) protect against unauthorized access to or use of such information
          that could result in substantial harm or inconvenience to any
          customer.

60.4  Assessment of risk.
      The licensee shall identify reasonably foreseeable internal and
      external threats to customer information systems; assess the
      likelihood and potential damage of those threats, taking into
      consideration the sensitivity of each category of customer
      information maintained; and assess the sufficiency of policies,
      procedures, systems, and other safeguards in place to control the
      identified risks. The assessment shall be documented and reviewed not
      less than annually.

60.5  Oversight of service providers.
      The licensee shall exercise appropriate due diligence in selecting
      service providers and shall require them by contract to implement
      appropriate measures designed to meet the objectives of this rule.
      Contracts entered into after the effective date of this rule shall
      contain such a requirement.

60.6  Adjustment of the program.
      The licensee shall monitor, evaluate, and adjust the information
      security program in light of any material change in technology, the
      sensitivity of customer information, internal or external threats,
      and the licensee's own changing business arrangements, including
      mergers, acquisitions, alliances, joint ventures, outsourcing
      arrangements, and changes to customer information systems.

60.7  Effective date.
      This rule takes effect on the first day of the calendar quarter
      following adoption. Licensees shall achieve compliance not later than
      twelve months after that date.
