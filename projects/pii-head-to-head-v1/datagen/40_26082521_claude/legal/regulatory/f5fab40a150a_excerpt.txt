Subject: Disposal obligations and penalty schedule

Sections 11 through 13 of the Act are extracted below. Department examiners have cited these sections in recent market conduct examinations of carriers of comparable size.

Sec. 11. DISPOSAL OF RECORDS.
A holder of records containing personal information shall, when the record is no longer to be retained under the holder's retention schedule, dispose of the record by shredding, erasing, or otherwise modifying the personal information so as to render it unreadable or undecipherable by any means. Disposal by a contracted vendor does not relieve the holder of responsibility under this section.

Sec. 12. RECORDKEEPING.
The holder shall maintain a disposal log for a period of three years recording, for each disposal event: Record Series, Volume Disposed, Method of Disposal, Date of Disposal, Authorizing Officer Title, and Vendor Name where applicable. The log shall be produced to the Commissioner upon request.

Sec. 13. PENALTIES.
(a) A holder that violates Sec. 11 or Sec. 12 is subject to a civil penalty not to exceed five hundred dollars for each violation, provided that the aggregate penalty for violations arising from a single disposal event shall not exceed fifty thousand dollars.
(b) A knowing and willful violation is subject to a civil penalty not to exceed five thousand dollars for each violation and, in the discretion of the Commissioner, suspension or revocation of the certificate of authority.
(c) Penalties under this section are in addition to any other remedy available at law. Nothing in this section creates a private right of action.
(d) The Commissioner may accept a plan of corrective action in lieu of penalty for a first violation not involving actual disclosure of personal information to an unauthorized person.

Action required: file-room supervisors are to reconcile the disposal log against the retention schedule monthly and forward exceptions to the Records Custodian.
