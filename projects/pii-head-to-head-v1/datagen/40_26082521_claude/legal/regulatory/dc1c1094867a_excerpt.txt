RECORDS MANAGEMENT BULLETIN RM-2004-3
Issued by: Records Custodian
To be posted at each imaging and file-room station.

Subject: Notification statute — definition of covered identifier categories

Sections 3 and 5 of the notification statute are set out below. Section 4 (form and timing of notice) is issued separately as Bulletin RM-2004-4.

Sec. 3. DEFINITIONS.
(1) "Personal information" means an individual's first name or first initial and last name in combination with any one or more of the data elements listed in subsection (2), when either the name or the data element is not encrypted or redacted.
(2) Covered data elements are:
    (A) Social Security Number;
    (B) Driver's License Number or State Identification Card Number;
    (C) Financial Account Number, Credit Card Number, or Debit Card Number, in combination with any required Security Code, Access Code, or Password that would permit access to an individual's financial account.
(3) "Personal information" does not include information lawfully made available to the general public from federal, state, or local government records.
(4) "Breach of the security of the system" means unauthorized acquisition of computerized data that compromises the security, confidentiality, or integrity of personal information maintained by the holder of the record. Good-faith acquisition by an employee or agent of the holder for the purposes of the holder is not a breach, provided the information is not subject to further unauthorized disclosure.

Sec. 5. RECORD CLASSIFICATION.
Each holder of records shall classify stored fields against the categories in Sec. 3(2). The following classification applies to the policy administration and claims extract files:
