INSURANCE INFORMATION AND PRIVACY PROTECTION ACT
(Excerpt: §§ 2 and 4)

§ 2. Definitions.
As used in this Act, unless the context otherwise requires:
  (a) "Institution" means any corporation, association, partnership, reciprocal exchange, or other legal entity engaged in the business of insurance in this State.
  (b) "Personal information" means individually identifiable information gathered in connection with an insurance transaction from which a judgment may be made about an individual's character, habits, avocations, finances, occupation, general reputation, credit, or health. The term includes an individual's name in combination with any data element enumerated in § 4(b).
  (c) "Record" means any item, collection, or grouping of personal information maintained by an institution, whether in paper, electronic, microform, or other medium.
  (d) "Records custodian" means the officer or employee designated under § 4(a).

§ 4. Custody obligations.
  (a) Each institution shall designate in writing a records custodian, who shall be an officer or a person reporting directly to an officer.
  (b) The custodian shall maintain a current inventory of data elements collected, identified by field label, including at minimum: Full Legal Name; Mailing Address; Date of Birth; Social Security Number; Driver's License Number; Policy Number; Claim Number; Group Certificate Number.
  (c) The inventory shall state, for each field label, the business purpose of collection and the retention period applied.
