DETERMINATIONS

  1. The appeal was filed within the fifteen (15) day period prescribed by
     Ordinance § 13.28.140(B) and is properly before this Office.

  2. The Department established the fact of arrearage in the sum reflected
     upon the billing history received in evidence as Exhibit D-1, and
     further established that the notices required by § 13.28.140(A) were
     mailed on the dates therein recited.

  3. The Appellant established, by the certification received October 21,
     2011 and marked Exhibit A-3, that a resident of the premises is
     dependent upon life-sustaining equipment within the meaning of
     § 13.28.150(C). Discontinuance is accordingly prohibited during the
     certified period.

  4. The Appellant's contention that consumption was overstated by reason of
     a defective meter is not sustained. The field test performed
     September 19, 2011 (Ref. MTR-11-8806) returned registration within the
     tolerance prescribed by Tariff Rule 17.

ORDER

  The notice of discontinuance is VACATED. The account shall be placed upon
  a levelized amortization arrangement of twelve (12) monthly installments
  commencing with the November 2011 billing cycle, in addition to current
  charges. Failure to remit any installment within ten (10) days of its due
  date shall entitle the Department to proceed upon fresh notice. This
  determination is final for purposes of further review.
