I. PROCEDURAL HISTORY

The within claim was presented to the Office of the City Clerk on April 4, 2011, and transmitted to this Office by internal referral on April 7, 2011, within the twenty (20) day period prescribed by administrative directive. Acknowledgment of receipt issued April 11, 2011. The claim asserts damage to real and personal property alleged to have arisen from the failure of a cast-iron distribution main of nominal eight-inch diameter, said failure having been reported to the Emergency Dispatch Center at approximately 02:41 hours on March 14, 2011, and isolated by field crews at 04:55 hours the same date.

II. INVESTIGATION

Field investigation was conducted March 16 and March 22, 2011. The Distribution Maintenance Section produced work order WO-11-3392 together with the associated valve-exercise history for the affected pressure zone. Maintenance records disclose that the segment in question was installed in or about 1928, that it had sustained two prior circumferential breaks (recorded under WO-06-1187 and WO-09-2704), and that it had been placed on the Section's deferred replacement schedule in the capital plan then in force. Photographic documentation, soil-condition notes and pressure-recorder tapes for the period March 12 through March 15, 2011 are maintained in the file and are indexed at Tabs 4 through 9 inclusive.

III. ADJUDICATION NOTES

The Adjudicator finds the notice of claim timely presented and the claimant's interest in the affected premises sufficiently established by the documentation submitted. On the question of liability, the Adjudicator observes that the two antecedent failures of the same segment, coupled with the deferral of scheduled replacement, are sufficient to defeat the defense of ordinary inspection and repair, and that the doctrine of design immunity has no application to a maintenance decision of this character. The quantum of loss remains unresolved: the itemized estimate submitted with the claim exceeds the independent restoration estimate obtained by this Office on May 2, 2011 by a material margin, and no supporting proof of value has been furnished as to certain items of personal property claimed.

IV. DISPOSITION

Recommended for compromise settlement within the delegated authority of the Deputy Director, subject to execution of a general release in the approved form. Referred to the Office of the City Attorney for concurrence on May 18, 2011. Concurrence received June 6, 2011 (Ref. CA-OP-11-0219). File scheduled for closure upon disbursement and thereafter for retention in accordance with Records Retention Schedule L-7 (seven years from closure).
