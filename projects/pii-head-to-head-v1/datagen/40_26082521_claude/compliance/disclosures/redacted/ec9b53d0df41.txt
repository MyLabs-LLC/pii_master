STATEMENT OF CHARGES AS SUSTAINED IN PART

The charging document alleged (a) operation of a departmental vehicle without the endorsement required by Administrative Rule 9.14(c); (b) failure to complete the pre-trip inspection log for the tour of duty commencing January 11, 2011; and (c) discourtesy toward a member of the public at a service excavation. Charge (b) was admitted. Charge (a) was contested on the ground that the endorsement had lapsed during a period of approved leave and was restored on January 20, 2011.

FINDINGS AND ADJUDICATION NOTES OF THE BOARD

The Board finds that the Department carried its burden as to Charges (a) and (b) by a preponderance of the evidence, and further finds that Charge (c) is not sustained, the sole percipient account having been received in hearsay form and not subjected to cross-examination. The Board notes that progressive discipline was not observed in that no written reprimand preceded the suspension, contrary to the practice acknowledged by the Labor Relations Officer in testimony of April 15, 2011, and contrary to Article 14, Section 3(b) of the collective bargaining agreement then in effect.

DECISION

The five-day suspension is reduced to a written reprimand together with one (1) day of suspension, with back pay for the four (4) days remitted, payable on the payroll cycle next following the date hereof. The Department is directed to note the modification upon the service record. The decision is final and binding under Article 14, Section 9, subject only to such judicial review as the law allows.
