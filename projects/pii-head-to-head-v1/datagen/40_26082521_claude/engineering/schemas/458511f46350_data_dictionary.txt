SUBJECT: Data Dictionary Excerpt — MBR_MASTER (Patient Accounting, Release 9.4)
CLASS:   Internal — Restricted Handling

1. Purpose. This excerpt records the authoritative definitions for the columns of MBR_MASTER retained under the Release 9.4 schema. It supersedes the entry circulated with the Release 9.2 upgrade notes.

2. Authority. Column-level access grants are issued by the Records Custodian on the recommendation of the Billing Office Supervisor. No grant survives a change of role assignment.

3. Column register.

4. Retention. Rows remain active for seven years from the date recorded in LAST_ACTIVITY_DT on the associated account. On expiry, the retention job populates PURGE_ELIGIBLE_DT; identifier columns named in clause 3 as Restricted are overwritten in place ninety days thereafter, and the remaining financial columns are retained for statistical reporting.

5. Downstream extracts. Three extracts draw from this table: the collections file, the payer reconciliation file, and the quality reporting file. Column lists for each are fixed in the extract control table and may not be widened by the scheduling team. Any request to add a column carried in clause 3 as Restricted must be presented in writing to the Records Custodian and, where the request concerns MBR_SSN_LAST4, to the Privacy Officer as well.

6. Review. This entry is reviewed on the anniversary of the release date or upon any change to the registration interface, whichever occurs first.
