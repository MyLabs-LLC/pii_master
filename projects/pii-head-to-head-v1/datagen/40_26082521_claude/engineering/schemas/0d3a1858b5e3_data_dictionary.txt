MEMORANDUM

TO:       Enrollment Services; Interface Support Desk

SUBJECT:  Data Dictionary Excerpt — COVERAGE_ENROLLMENT
REFERENCE: DG-2021-114

1.0  Table description

     1.1  COVERAGE_ENROLLMENT holds one row per member per coverage
          segment. Segments are non-overlapping within a payer.

     1.2  The table is loaded by the eligibility interface and is not
          maintained by direct entry except under the correction
          procedure held by the Enrollment Services Manager.

2.0  Key and identifier columns

     2.1  ENROLL_SK — NUMBER(18), NOT NULL. Surrogate key.

     2.2  MBR_SK — NUMBER(18), NOT NULL. Foreign key to MBR_MASTER.
          Carries no meaning outside this system.

     2.3  SUBSCRIBER_ID — VARCHAR2(30), NOT NULL. Restricted. Assigned
          by the payer. Appears in the payer reconciliation extract in
          full, as required by the trading partner agreement; appears
          nowhere else outside the source table.

     2.4  MBR_DOB_DT — DATE, NOT NULL. Restricted. Held here for
          eligibility matching only. Read access is confined to the
          eligibility service account and to the Interface Support Desk
          role group.

     2.5  MBR_SSN — VARCHAR2(11), NULLABLE. Restricted — highest tier.
          Populated only where the payer requires it for matching.
          Column-level encryption applies at rest. Excluded from every
          extract, view and report without exception; the exclusion is
          enforced in the extract control table and cannot be waived by
          the scheduling team.

3.0  Coverage columns

     3.1  PAYER_CD — VARCHAR2(8), NOT NULL, reference-coded.

     3.2  PLAN_CD — VARCHAR2(12), NOT NULL, reference-coded.

     3.3  GROUP_NO — VARCHAR2(20), NULLABLE. Employer group identifier.

     3.4  COVERAGE_FROM_DT — DATE, NOT NULL.

     3.5  COVERAGE_THRU_DT — DATE, NULLABLE. A null denotes open
          coverage and is expected on active segments.

     3.6  COB_ORDER_NO — NUMBER(1), NOT NULL. Coordination of benefits
          sequence.

     3.7  ELIG_VERIFIED_TS — TIMESTAMP, NULLABLE. Set by the nightly
          verification job.

4.0  Retention

     4.1  Closed segments are retained for seven years from
          COVERAGE_THRU_DT.

     4.2  On expiry, SUBSCRIBER_ID, MBR_DOB_DT and MBR_SSN are
          overwritten in place. PAYER_CD, PLAN_CD and the two date
          columns are retained indefinitely for actuarial reporting.

5.0  Downstream extracts

     5.1  Payer reconciliation file — daily. Carries the columns at
          2.3, 3.1 through 3.6.

     5.2  Denials analysis file — weekly. Carries 2.2, 3.1, 3.2, 3.4,
          3.5. No direct identifier is included.

     5.3  Additions to either column list require the written approval
          of the Records Custodian and are recorded against this
          reference number.
