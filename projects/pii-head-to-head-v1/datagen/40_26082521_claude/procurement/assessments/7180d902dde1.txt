PART I — INSTRUCTIONS

This questionnaire is issued to every supplier that will create, receive, maintain, or transmit personal information belonging to riders, reduced-fare program participants, or employees of the Authority in the course of performing the referenced contract. Responses are to be furnished in narrative form and are to describe the controls actually in place on the date of preparation rather than controls planned or under procurement. Where the Evidence Required column is marked Y, the supplier shall attach the corresponding exhibit and cite its reference in the Exhibit Ref. field of the evidence schedule. A response of "not applicable" is acceptable only where accompanied by an explanation of why the condition does not arise. Submissions that leave any response box blank will be returned unscored and the review clock will restart upon resubmission.
