QUARTERLY FACILITIES BULLETIN — Building Operations

The loading dock resurfacing project remains on schedule for completion in the
third quarter. Contractors will stage equipment in the north lot between 07:00
and 16:00 on weekdays. Badge readers at entrances 2 and 4 will be offline for
approximately two hours during the changeover; signage will be posted.

Recycling collection moves to Thursdays effective next month. Questions about
building operations should be directed to the facilities desk during normal
business hours.
